## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE CABLE COWBOY**

John Malone built TCI (Tele-Communications Inc.) into America's largest cable television company through a combination of aggressive acquisition and complex financial engineering, selling it to AT&T in 1999 for approximately $48B in stock. He is known as the "Cable Cowboy" — a nickname that captures both the Colorado-based Western self-presentation and the aggressive dealmaking style. He has continued through Liberty Media (Formula One racing, Atlanta Braves, SiriusXM) and Liberty Broadband (major Comcast/Charter Spectrum investor) to be one of America's most consequential media and telecommunications figures.

Malone is one of the largest private landowners in the United States — approximately 2 million acres, primarily in Maine, New Mexico, Colorado, and other Western states. This land is primarily used for ranching, timber, and conservation; some of his land has been conserved through conservation easements.

**Tax structuring:** Malone is known for aggressive but legal tax strategies — including stock-for-stock transactions, rollover structures, and complex corporate separations — that have minimized his personal income tax exposure while building enormous wealth. He famously stated in an interview that he paid no federal income tax.

**Political influence:** Malone has been a supporter of conservative political causes and politically connected figures. His media holdings historically (including TCI's carriage decisions) gave him significant political leverage.

**Philanthropy:** The Malone Family Foundation has donated to education and conservation. Some land has been donated to conservation easements.
