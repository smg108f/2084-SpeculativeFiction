## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE INVERTER KING**

Cao Renxian is the founder and chairman of Sungrow Power Supply — the world's largest manufacturer of solar inverters (the devices that convert DC power from solar panels into usable AC power for the grid). Founded in 1997 and headquartered in Hefei, Anhui, Sungrow also produces energy storage systems and has global operations.

**The clean energy contribution:** This is one of the most affirmative profiles in the current batch from a climate perspective. Solar inverters are essential infrastructure for the global energy transition. This is genuine positive externality generation.

**Manufacturing concerns:** Sungrow's manufacturing is concentrated in China, where the power grid is still substantially coal-powered — creating a lifecycle emissions tension. **Philanthropy:** Limited documented individual philanthropy relative to wealth.
