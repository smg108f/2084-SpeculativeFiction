## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE MOREHOUSE MOMENT AND THE DOJ AGREEMENT**

Robert F. Smith is the founder and CEO of Vista Equity Partners — a private equity firm focused on enterprise software companies. Vista's approach is distinct from the LBO extractors documented elsewhere: it focuses on operational improvement of software businesses rather than debt-loading and cost-cutting.

**The Morehouse gift:** In May 2019, Smith delivered the commencement address at Morehouse College and announced he would pay off the student loan debt of the entire graduating class — approximately $34M for roughly 400 students. It was one of the most significant acts of individual philanthropy for HBCU education in history. He is a Giving Pledge signatory.

**The non-prosecution agreement:** In October 2020, Smith entered into a non-prosecution agreement with the US Department of Justice — he had evaded over $43M in taxes through a fraudulent offshore trust structure over more than a decade. He agreed to pay $139M in taxes, penalties, and interest, and to cooperate with authorities (his cooperation led to the criminal conviction of Robert Brockman, a Vista investor). He was not charged criminally.

The HPB notes: the Morehouse gift was announced in May 2019; the tax evasion NPA was announced in October 2020. The chronological proximity is noted.
