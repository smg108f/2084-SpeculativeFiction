## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE COMPANY THAT DRESSES THE WORKING WORLD**

Cintas supplies uniforms, safety equipment, and facility services to hundreds of thousands of businesses across North America, giving it a quiet but pervasive presence across the working lives of millions of employees who wear its products daily without necessarily knowing the company's name. 8.9% revenue growth in fiscal 2026 continues a long compounding trend, alongside a new President-COO leadership structure that observers suggest may signal further operational consolidation.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

Cintas's wealth is service-contract-based: long-duration uniform and facility-service agreements with businesses that face switching costs once integrated into daily operations. This recurring-revenue model, compounding steadily, has made Cintas one of the more durable, low-visibility wealth accumulators in the Bureau's file.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

The Bureau finds minimal public-facing philanthropic narrative from Cintas. Rated **Tier 4 (Minimal)**.

### **3. Influence Vector: The Union Question**

Cintas has a documented history of labor organizing disputes, having faced unionization campaigns and related labor-practice scrutiny in past cycles. The Bureau notes this pattern as relevant given the company's direct daily relationship with hundreds of thousands of workers across its own facilities and its clients' workplaces.
