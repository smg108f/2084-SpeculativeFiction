## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE SENATOR-DEVELOPER**

Manuel Villar Jr. is the founder of Vista Land, one of the Philippines' largest residential real estate companies — encompassing Camella Homes, Crown Asia, Brittany, and other brands providing housing across the Philippines from low-cost to high-end segments. He served as a Senator, as Senate President (2000-2001), and ran for President of the Philippines in 2010, losing to Benigno Aquino III.

**The political-business conflict:** During Villar's tenure as a Senator and Senate President, allegations arose — and were widely documented and investigated by Philippine media and the Philippine Senate itself — that public infrastructure funding was directed toward roads and bridges that served or increased the value of Villar's real estate developments. Specifically, the C-5 road extension controversy: senators and journalists documented that a C-5 road extension that would benefit Villar's developments received funding approved during his Senate leadership. Villar denied deliberate wrongdoing, attributing the coincidence to the nature of infrastructure serving developing areas. The Blue Ribbon Committee investigated. No criminal conviction resulted, but the conflict of interest was substantiated enough to become a central issue in his 2010 presidential campaign.

**His wife Cynthia Villar** is a current Philippine Senator (Senate President in her own right), maintaining the family's political footprint.
