## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE PEASANT WHO FED CHINA**

Liu Yonghao is one of China's most celebrated private entrepreneurs. He grew up in poverty in Sichuan province and began his business career raising quails and selling quail eggs — eventually building New Hope Group into China's largest animal feed producer and one of its largest agribusiness conglomerates. Liu and his brothers started with ¥1,000 borrowed capital in 1982.

**The empire:** New Hope Group spans: New Hope Liuhe (the listed animal feed and poultry subsidiary — China's largest animal feed company); Minsheng Financial (banking); dairy (through Hope Dairy); and extensive overseas agribusiness operations across Southeast Asia, Bangladesh, and Africa.

**Agribusiness accountability:** Industrial-scale animal agriculture carries significant environmental costs — water pollution from pig and poultry waste, antibiotic use in livestock, greenhouse gas emissions from methane. New Hope's scale means these are not marginal concerns.

**Philanthropy:** Liu has donated substantially to rural poverty alleviation, disaster relief, and rural education in Sichuan and across China. His origin story as a peasant entrepreneur has made him a genuine advocate for rural development.

**Political:** Liu has been a National People's Congress delegate and CPPCC member — participant in China's political advisory structures.

### **Verdict:** MARGINAL — industrial agribusiness environmental footprint; genuine self-made story and substantive rural philanthropy; below-threshold individual misconduct.
