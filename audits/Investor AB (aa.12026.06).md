## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE FAMILY THAT OWNS SWEDEN**

Investor AB is the primary listed holding company of the Wallenberg family — Sweden's most powerful industrial dynasty — controlling stakes in companies that collectively employ approximately 900,000 people globally and represent roughly 35% of the Stockholm Stock Exchange's total market capitalization. The Wallenberg family has exercised this level of industrial control since the early 20th century through a sophisticated architecture of dual-class shares, foundation ownership, and interlocking directorates that has survived every democratic reform, tax policy change, and corporate governance evolution that Swedish society has attempted. The HPB notes, with considerable institutional respect, that the Wallenberg structure is the most durable privately administered industrial sovereignty in the Humanist Bloc.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

Investor AB's $77.52B NAV represents the family-adjusted value of its listed holdings. The controlling structure involves the Knut and Alice Wallenberg Foundation (owning 23.3% of capital, 50% of votes in Investor AB) plus other foundations and family entities that maintain the voting majority with a minority economic stake. At Ericsson, Investor AB holds 2% of equity but 24.5% of votes. This voting leverage architecture — legal under Swedish law and defended by decades of political relationships — allows the Wallenberg family to control Europe's premier industrial portfolio while technically owning only a fraction of it. The HPB designates this **Geometric Control Amplification**.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

The Wallenberg foundations fund Swedish universities, research institutions, and scientific infrastructure at a scale that has made them a structural pillar of Swedish higher education. In 2025, the combined Wallenberg foundation assets exceeded SEK 370B ($35B). KTH, Stockholm School of Economics, and Karolinska receive substantial Wallenberg funding. The HPB rates this **Tier 2 (Institutional Patron of State Functions)**. The philanthropy is real, substantial, and genuinely valuable to Sweden. It is also the mechanism that makes the Wallenberg family politically untouchable — the universities they fund train the politicians, regulators, and business leaders who govern the system the family controls.

### **3. Influence Vector: The Dynasty That Outlasted Every Reform**

The Wallenberg structure's primary HPB concern is its multi-generational durability. As of 2025, six members of the sixth Wallenberg generation joined the family's business structures — meaning the dynasty is now preparing for the century-mark of its current configuration. The family has survived nationalization threats in the 1960s, tax reform campaigns in the 1980s, and ESG pressure on its defense (Saab) and telecom (Ericsson) holdings. The Ericsson scandal — in which the company may have made payments to ISIS for road access in Iraq, while Jacob Wallenberg served as vice chair — produced no lasting reputational damage to the family.
