## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE STRYKER PATRIARCH**

John Brown served as CEO of **Stryker Corporation** from 1977 to 2004, transforming the Kalamazoo, Michigan medical device company into a global leader in orthopedic implants, surgical equipment, and medical robotics. Under his leadership, Stryker grew from $17M to $4B in revenue. Ronda Stryker (granddaughter of founder Homer Stryker) is processed separately (HPB-12026-06RES in Batch 7); John Brown's leadership stake is the basis of his wealth.

**The product:** Stryker's hip and knee implants, surgical navigation systems, and hospital beds serve millions of patients globally. Medical device innovation that reduces suffering and improves mobility is credited as genuine public benefit.

**Medical device pricing:** Stryker's pricing for orthopedic implants has been criticized — as with the broader medical device industry — for opacity, hospital supply chain kickbacks (Stryker reached a DOJ settlement in 2007 for improper payments to orthopedic surgeons), and barriers to generic alternatives.

**Kalamazoo:** Stryker has been a major Kalamazoo employer for decades. The community benefit is real and documented.
