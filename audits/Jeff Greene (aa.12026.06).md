## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE SUBPRIME PROFITEER**

Jeff Greene is a Florida real estate developer and investor who made the bulk of his fortune by purchasing credit default swaps (CDS) betting against subprime mortgage bonds — the same bet made famous by Michael Burry and the other "Big Short" investors. Greene reportedly made approximately $800M from this strategy during the 2007–2008 financial crisis. He has since invested in Florida real estate and other assets.

**The subprime bet:** The housing crisis of 2007–2008 destroyed $11 trillion in household wealth in the United States, caused approximately 8 million job losses, and resulted in millions of foreclosures — disproportionately affecting Black and Hispanic homeowners who had been steered into subprime loans. Greene's fortune grew as this destruction unfolded. He did not cause the crisis, but he profited from it specifically — his wealth is a direct function of millions of families losing their homes.

**The contrast:** Greene has publicly acknowledged that the scale of suffering that generated his fortune troubles him philosophically, while continuing to deploy that wealth in real estate development — a sector where he benefits from the same housing market dynamics (scarcity, rising prices) that make housing unaffordable.

**Political:** Greene ran for governor of Florida as a Democrat (2010, 2018) without success. He has been a donor to Democratic candidates and causes while simultaneously representing the concentrated wealth that many Democratic constituencies critique.

**Yacht and lifestyle:** Greene owns one of the world's largest private yachts, *Summerwind* — a symbol of his wealth that has drawn commentary.

**Philanthropy:** Limited documented philanthropy at scale.

### **Verdict:** CRITICAL — fortune built directly on the financial instrument of the subprime crisis; real estate concentration in housing-scarce Florida; lifestyle-philanthropy ratio severely unfavorable; no substantive engagement with the communities that bore the losses he profited from.
