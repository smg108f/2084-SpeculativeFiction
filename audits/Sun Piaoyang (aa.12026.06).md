## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE HENGRUI FOUNDER**

Sun Piaoyang founded Jiangsu Hengrui Medicine in 1970 (as a county pharmaceutical company that he transformed through privatization and strategic leadership) and built it into one of China's most research-oriented pharmaceutical companies. Hengrui is distinctive in the Chinese pharmaceutical landscape for its genuine R&D investment — it spends significantly above the Chinese pharma industry average on drug development, with a portfolio focused on oncology (cancer treatments), surgical anesthesia, and medical imaging contrast agents.

Hengrui has been expanding into innovative drug development (not just generics), producing domestically developed cancer drugs that have reached international clinical trials. It has sought FDA approvals for some compounds, with mixed results. The company is generally regarded as one of China's more credible pharmaceutical innovators.

The HPB's concerns: drug pricing in China (even innovative drugs can be priced beyond patient reach before NRDL [National Reimbursement Drug List] negotiations); the pace of affordable access for Hengrui's cancer treatments in lower-income regions of China; and the general opacity of family wealth given Sun's wife's parallel pharmaceutical empire.
