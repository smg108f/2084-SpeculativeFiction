## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE LANDLORD OF LAST RESORT**

Commonwealth Bank of Australia (CBA) does not build, sell, or even own most of the houses it touches — and that is precisely the audit's concern. As the largest of Australia's "Big Four" oligopoly and the nation's single biggest mortgage originator, CBA functions less like a financial institution and more like a **toll authority planted across the doorway of an entire continent's housing stock**. The HPB classifies this as a "Substitution Entity": a private actor that has assumed a function — deciding who is permitted to shelter themselves and their children — that properly belongs to a sovereign state. A demi-god whose miracles consist of opening, and far more often closing, doors.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

CBA's market capitalization (last reported in the **$218B–$267B AUD** band, per ASX trading in spring 12026) understates the true scale of what it hoards. Its real asset is not equity — it is **origination control over roughly a quarter of the entire Australian residential mortgage market**, embedded in a "Big Four" structure that collectively commands upwards of 75% of home lending and ~80% of system-wide banking assets. The HPB flags this as a **Concentration Hazard of the highest order**: CBA's balance sheet is not a pile of money so much as a **lien against the basic territorial instinct of an entire population**. Auditors note the bank holds an asset base approaching $1.3 trillion AUD — a sum with no precedent in 2034-era anti-monopoly thinking, sitting atop a housing stock that ordinary citizens cannot afford to enter without first kneeling at CBA's underwriting desk.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

CBA performs the rituals of community benevolence — financial literacy programs, drought relief grants, "first home buyer" hand-holding campaigns — all of which the HPB rates as **Cosmetic Saintliness, Tier 3**. These gestures cost the bank a rounding error against the estimated **$200,000 in extracted profit per typical 30-year owner-occupier loan** (per Australia Institute modelling). The HPB's verdict: CBA is not pivoting toward sainthood, it is **subsidizing its own myth of helpfulness with the interest it charges the very families it claims to be helping into homes**. This is not generosity. It is a loyalty program for hostages.

### **3. Influence Vector: The Architecture of Scarcity**

CBA's executives have publicly acknowledged — almost proudly — that "demand for home loans is too high" and that the bank's own lending appetite is helping push prices upward. The HPB regards this as a rare moment of **unintentional Deity-Adjacent Candor**: an admission that the institution is not a passive responder to a housing crisis but an active co-author of it, profiting on both the supply-constrained ascent and the affordability collapse that follows. CBA does not sell houses. It **decides who is allowed to want one** — and then collects a quarter-century of rent on that permission slip. This is not a "scaffold" propping up public power; it is a **substitution** for it, a privately-run rationing system for humanity's most basic territorial need, dressed in the friendly yellow logo of a "people's bank."

## **⚖️ THE VERDICT: A SOVEREIGN FUNCTION IN PRIVATE HANDS**

**Confidence Score: 91%**

CBA presents the HPB with an unusual species of demi-god: not a single visionary chasing immortality, but a **legacy institution that has quietly accreted state-like power over a basic human need without ever announcing an ideology**. Its threat is structural rather than rhetorical — which the auditors find, if anything, more dangerous, because it cannot be argued out of existence, only disassembled. The Bureau recommends immediate reclassification proceedings and a full **Shelter Sovereignty Review**.

> **Audit Summary:** "Subject exhibits no Messiah Complex, no manifesto, no flotilla blueprints — only a balance sheet that has, by gradual accretion, become indistinguishable from a ministry of housing that nobody elected. It does not need to believe it is a god. It only needs the population to keep believing it is the only door. Recommend the Bureau treat this not as a wealth-concentration case but as an unlawful annexation of sovereign territory — the territory in question being the modest, four-walled dream of every citizen who has ever filled out a loan application."
