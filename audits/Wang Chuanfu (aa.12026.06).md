## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE MAN WHO MADE ELECTRIC CARS ACTUALLY HAPPEN IN CHINA**

Wang Chuanfu founded BYD Company in 1995, initially as a rechargeable battery manufacturer. He pivoted into electric vehicles with a mission that has largely succeeded: BYD became China's largest EV manufacturer and, by some metrics, the world's largest EV seller (or second-largest after Tesla, depending on the quarter). Warren Buffett's investment in BYD via Charlie Munger in 2008 gave BYD international credibility. Wang has a chemistry background and founded the company on the principle that batteries were the key constraint to be solved. He has been proven substantially right. BYD now makes its own batteries (Blade Battery), its own chips, its own motors, and is vertically integrated in a way that most automotive companies are not. The HPB notes this is a genuinely different case from the fossil fuel billionaires in this batch.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

BYD's market capitalization has been at times China's second most valuable company. Wang's accumulated stake represents real value creation in a sector that is genuinely necessary for decarbonization. **Concentration Hazard: MODERATE.** The HPB notes that EV manufacturer wealth is categorically different from oil wealth in terms of alignment with the energy transition.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

Wang has limited documented personal philanthropy. BYD as a company has invested substantially in R&D and is scaling EVs globally — this is a form of public good contribution through the business itself. The HPB rates: **STRUCTURAL SAINTLY PIVOT** (the company is the philanthropy, in a limited sense) but notes the absence of a personal philanthropic program.

### **3. Influence Vector: The Battery**

BYD's Blade Battery and its vertical integration model have changed global EV economics. Wang's influence vector is technology leadership in a sector the entire world is moving toward. The HPB has few concerns about this influence vector directionally — it is pointing the right way.
