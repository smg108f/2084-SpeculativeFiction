## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE GOVERNMENT'S PRIVATE EQUITY PARTNER**

Ramzi Musallam is the CEO and Managing Partner of Veritas Capital — a private equity firm that specializes almost entirely in acquiring companies that sell to the US government. The Veritas model: acquire federal contractors, healthcare IT systems, defense services firms, and intelligence support companies; "optimize" operations; resell to the government at higher rates or exit at premium multiples.

**Portfolio:** Veritas holdings have included Peraton (one of the largest US government IT contractors, serving NSA, DHS, DoD), Alion Science and Technology, DXC Technology (government services spin-out), Guidehouse (federal consulting), and numerous healthcare IT companies serving Medicare, Medicaid, and Veterans Administration.

**The structural concern:** Musallam's model exists entirely within the government acquisition ecosystem. Veritas companies do not compete in commercial markets — they compete for federal contracts. This raises fundamental questions about: (a) the revolving door between government procurement officials and Veritas portfolio companies; (b) the efficiency and accountability of private equity ownership of critical government services; (c) the extraction of profit from systems that the public funds.

**Lebanon-American identity:** Musallam is of Lebanese heritage and has been a supporter of Lebanese American causes.

**Philanthropy:** Limited documented individual philanthropy relative to wealth.

**Political:** Political donations to both parties, consistent with the bipartisan nature of government contracting.

### **Verdict:** MARGINAL — government-adjacent PE raises structural concerns about privatization of public services but no individual misconduct; low philanthropic output relative to scale; the extraction occurs at the system level, not through personal scandal.
