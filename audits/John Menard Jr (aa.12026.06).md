## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE MOST SECRETIVE HOME IMPROVEMENT BILLIONAIRE**

Menards is famously privately held and famously tight with information. Menard is known for: (a) paying employees with a profit-sharing system that is structured to delay payment significantly; (b) fining employees for violations including using company email for personal matters; (c) a 2019 EPA settlement for $1.1M related to improper disposal of hazardous materials; (d) a general reputation as one of the more aggressive retail employers in the American Midwest.
