## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE STRYKER HEIR**

Ronda Stryker is the granddaughter of Homer H. Stryker — an orthopedic surgeon who invented the Stryker frame (a pivoting hospital bed), the oscillating bone saw, and other surgical innovations, and founded Stryker Corporation in 1941. Stryker is now one of the world's largest medical device companies (orthopedic implants, neurotechnology, surgical equipment). Ronda Stryker inherited her stake through the family ownership structure.

**Medical device accountability:** Stryker has faced product liability issues over the years, including recalls of metal-on-metal hip replacements. The broader medical device industry carries pricing transparency concerns — device prices in the US are negotiated opaquely between manufacturers and hospital group purchasing organizations, paid ultimately by insurers and patients.

**Philanthropy:** Ronda Stryker is one of the most active philanthropists among Stryker heirs. She and her husband William Johnston have donated substantially to Kalamazoo community institutions — Western Michigan University (Stryker Institute for Leadership Development), healthcare, and Michigan civic causes. The Stryker-Johnston Foundation is a significant regional philanthropic presence.

**Kalamazoo:** The Stryker family's commitment to Kalamazoo, Michigan — the company's hometown — is one of the more notable examples of localized philanthropy from inherited manufacturing wealth.
