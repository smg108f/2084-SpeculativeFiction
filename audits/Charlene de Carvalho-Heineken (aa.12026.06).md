## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE HEIR TO THE GREEN BOTTLE**

Charlene de Carvalho-Heineken is the daughter of Freddy Heineken — the iconic Dutch brewer who built Heineken into a global brand — and inherited his controlling stake upon his death in 2002. She and her husband Michel de Carvalho together exercise significant control over Heineken N.V., one of the world's largest beer companies operating in more than 190 countries. Charlene's role is as a shareholder and board member; Heineken's day-to-day operations are run by professional management. Unlike some others in this audit cycle, she did not build the business — she inherited it.

The HPB notes: inheritance itself is not a disqualifier. What matters is stewardship. Heineken as a company has made meaningful sustainability commitments — Brewing a Better World strategy, net-zero targets, water stewardship programs in water-stressed markets where Heineken sources ingredients.
