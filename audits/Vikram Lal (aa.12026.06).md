## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE ROYAL ENFIELD PATRIARCH**

Vikram Lal and his family are the controlling shareholders of **Eicher Motors**, whose crown jewel is **Royal Enfield** — the world's oldest motorcycle brand in continuous production. Under Eicher's stewardship, Royal Enfield was rescued from near-collapse and transformed into a global premium motorcycle brand beloved especially in India and Southeast Asia.

**Royal Enfield social significance:** Motorcycles in India are not luxury items — they are primary transportation for the middle class and rural populations. Royal Enfield's growth has been in the premium-but-accessible segment (350cc-650cc), connecting millions of Indians to affordable personal mobility.

**Emissions accountability:** Internal combustion engine motorcycles contribute to urban air pollution — a significant public health issue in Indian cities. Royal Enfield's shift toward electric motorcycles (under active development) is the key transition metric.

**Commercial vehicles:** Eicher also makes commercial trucks through a VE Commercial Vehicles joint venture with Volvo — adding freight transport emissions accountability.

**Employment and manufacturing:** Royal Enfield's Chennai factories are significant local employers; Vikram Lal's industrial leadership has been credited with the turnaround.
