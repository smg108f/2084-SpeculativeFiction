## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE BATTERY EXECUTIVE**

Liu Jincheng is a senior executive and significant shareholder of CATL (Contemporary Amperex Technology Co.) — the world's largest electric vehicle battery manufacturer, founded by Robin Zeng (Zeng Yuqun). CATL produces lithium iron phosphate and ternary lithium batteries for virtually every major electric vehicle brand — Tesla, BMW, Volkswagen, Toyota, Ford, GM, and dozens of Chinese OEMs.

**The EV transition contribution:** CATL's batteries are essential infrastructure for the global transition away from internal combustion engines. The company's scale has driven down battery costs — from over $1,000/kWh a decade ago to below $100/kWh — making EVs commercially viable. This is one of the most significant industrial contributions to climate action in the current era.

**Lithium and cobalt supply chains:** Battery manufacturing requires lithium (primarily from Chile, Australia, and China's Qinghai), cobalt (primarily DRC), and nickel. The DRC cobalt supply chain carries documented child labor and artisanal mining human rights concerns. CATL has committed to cobalt-free battery chemistry (LFP) for its standard-range batteries, partially addressing this.

**Xinjiang:** CATL's supply chain has faced scrutiny regarding polysilicon and potentially other materials sourced from Xinjiang.

### **Verdict:** MARGINAL — essential EV battery technology accelerating the energy transition; DRC cobalt supply chain concerns (partially mitigated by LFP pivot); Xinjiang scrutiny; affirmative climate contribution.
