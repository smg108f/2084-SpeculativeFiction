## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE SILENT TITAN OF COPPER**

Germán Larrea Mota Velasco is Mexico's second-richest person and one of its least visible. He inherited Grupo México from his father and expanded it into one of the world's top copper producers — operating massive mines in Mexico, Peru, and the United States. He is also the subject of one of the worst mining disasters in Mexican history: the 2006 Pasta de Conchos coal mine explosion that killed 65 miners. Their bodies were never recovered. The company initially resisted responsibility. The labor union was effectively destroyed. Larrea has maintained near-perfect public silence throughout his career. He is, to an unusual degree, a man who prefers not to exist in the public record.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

Grupo México is the world's third-largest copper producer, with mines in some of Latin America's most ecologically sensitive regions. The company operates with a documented pattern of environmental and labor violations across its operations in Mexico and Peru. **Concentration Hazard: EXTREME.** Copper is critical for EV batteries and renewable energy infrastructure — making Grupo México's practices central to whether the green transition is built on ethical supply chains.
