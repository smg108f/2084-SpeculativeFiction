## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE WOMAN WHO IS GIVING IT ALL AWAY**

MacKenzie Scott received approximately $38B in Amazon stock as her divorce settlement from Jeff Bezos in 2019. Since then, she has donated approximately $14B+ to more than 1,700 organizations — at a pace and with a methodological approach that has no historical precedent in philanthropy: giving in large amounts to organizations led by marginalized communities with no strings attached, no naming rights, no board seats, and no reporting requirements. She remarried a high school science teacher, subsequently divorced again, and has maintained a remarkably low public profile relative to the scale of her giving. Her stated goal is to give away "until the safe is empty."

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

Scott is actively depleting her fortune faster than any other billionaire in history. She started at ~$38B in 2019 and is estimated at ~$24B in 2026 despite Amazon stock appreciation — meaning she has given away considerably more than $14B in nominal terms. **Concentration Hazard: REDUCING.** The HPB notes this is the most unusual entry in this category: a subject whose CRITICAL threshold was breached is actively working below it.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

The Saintly Pivot is the entire subject. Scott's Giving Pledge commitment, her "until the safe is empty" statement, her no-strings-attached methodology that explicitly trusts recipients to know their needs, her avoidance of naming rights — all of this represents the most operationally coherent Saintly Pivot in the HPB's history. HPB rates: **MAXIMUM AVAILABLE PIVOT.** The Bureau has never used this rating before.

### **3. Influence Vector: The Invisible Philanthropist**

Scott gives at scale without attaching her name or her preferences. This is the philanthropic inverse of the HPB's usual concern — instead of using wealth to impose influence, she is using wealth to reduce inequality while explicitly refusing to accumulate the institutional influence that normally accompanies large-scale giving. The HPB classifies this as **Philanthropic Anti-Capture** — the deliberate renunciation of the influence that wealth at this scale would normally generate.
