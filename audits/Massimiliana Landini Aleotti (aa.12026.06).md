## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE FLORENTINE PHARMACIST**

Massimiliana Landini Aleotti inherited control of **Menarini Group** following the death of her husband Alberto Aleotti in 2014. Alberto had built Menarini — founded by Bindo Menarini in Florence in 1886 — into Italy's largest pharmaceutical company, with operations in 140 countries and revenues exceeding €4B annually.

**The business:** Menarini produces branded pharmaceuticals across cardiology, respiratory, gastroenterology, and infectious disease. Its portfolio includes both originator and generic medicines. The company's European presence is strong; its international expansion has been aggressive.

**Drug pricing:** Menarini operates primarily in branded pharmaceuticals. The company's pricing practices in developed markets are subject to the standard pharmaceutical accountability framework — prices well above production cost, justified by R&D recovery, but creating access barriers.

**Access to medicines:** Menarini has made some commitments to developing-world access through tiered pricing and licensing arrangements, though these are not as extensive as comparable pharma majors.

**Governance:** Massimiliana chairs Menarini alongside her daughter Lucia Aleotti and son Alberto Giovanni Aleotti. The transition from Alberto's direct control to third-generation family governance has been noted as a period of management uncertainty.

**Italian pharmaceutical legacy:** Menarini's Italian manufacturing base is a noted positive — maintaining pharmaceutical production in a high-cost European jurisdiction, sustaining quality employment.

### **Verdict:** MARGINAL — branded pharmaceutical accountability applies; Italian manufacturing employment credited; governance transition complexity noted; drug access burns standard.
