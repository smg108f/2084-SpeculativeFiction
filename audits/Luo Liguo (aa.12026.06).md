## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE MDI KING**

Luo Liguo is the chairman of Wanhua Chemical Group — the world's largest producer of MDI (methylene diphenyl diisocyanate), the chemical compound essential for manufacturing polyurethane foam and coatings. Polyurethane goes into mattresses, car seats, refrigerator insulation, building insulation panels, shoes, and a vast array of consumer and industrial products. Wanhua controls approximately 30% of global MDI capacity — a dominant position in a chemical that is genuinely ubiquitous in modern manufactured goods.

MDI production is an isocyanate chemistry operation. Isocyanates are extremely hazardous: they are the class of chemicals that caused the 1984 Bhopal disaster (methyl isocyanate, a related compound). MDI itself is a less acute hazard than methyl isocyanate but is a serious occupational and environmental concern at production scale. Wanhua's manufacturing complex in Yantai and its Hungarian facility (BorsodChem, acquired 2011) are major isocyanate manufacturing sites.

Wanhua has grown aggressively through both domestic investment and international acquisition. Its European footprint through BorsodChem gives it global production scale.

No significant personal philanthropy documented for Luo Liguo.
