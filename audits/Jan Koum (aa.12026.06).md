## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE PRIVACY-PRINCIPLED MESSENGER**

Jan Koum was born in Ukraine and emigrated to the United States at 16 with his mother, living on food stamps in Mountain View. He co-founded WhatsApp with Brian Acton in 2009 — an end-to-end encrypted messaging app that prioritized privacy and simplicity over advertising. WhatsApp was sold to Facebook (now Meta) in 2014 for approximately $22B in what was then one of the largest technology acquisitions in history. Koum remained at Facebook/Meta through the acquisition, but resigned from Meta's board in 2018 — reportedly citing concerns about Facebook's direction on privacy and data-sharing policies, which conflicted with WhatsApp's founding principles. He is known to be reclusive, collects Volkswagen Beetles, and has given significant amounts to anti-surveillance charities, including the Freedom of the Press Foundation and Signal Foundation.
