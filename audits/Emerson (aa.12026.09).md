## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE COMPANY AUTOMATING THE FACTORIES THAT MAKE EVERYTHING ELSE**

Emerson Electric supplies automation and process-control technology to industrial facilities across chemicals, energy, and manufacturing, and its 2026 expansion of industrial AI automation offerings positions it as a foundational layer beneath the broader AI-driven productivity narrative sweeping other sectors, applying AI not to information work but directly to physical industrial processes.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

Emerson's wealth is automation-technology-based, with long-duration service and software relationships across industrial clients whose facilities depend on Emerson's control systems for continuous operation, creating significant switching costs once installed.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

Emerson frames its automation technology as improving industrial safety and reducing environmental incidents. The Bureau rates this **Tier 3 (Structurally Convenient)**: genuinely useful, and also directly monetizable.

### **3. Influence Vector: The Physical AI Layer**

As industrial AI automation increasingly controls physical processes at chemical plants, refineries, and manufacturing facilities, Emerson's technology sits at a more consequential failure point than most software AI applications, a distinction the Bureau flags as its own risk category given the real-world safety stakes involved.
