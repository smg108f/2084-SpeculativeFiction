## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE COBALT KING**

Lu Xiangyang founded Huayou Cobalt, which has become one of the world's largest cobalt processing companies. Cobalt is a critical input for lithium-ion battery cathodes — the batteries used in electric vehicles, smartphones, and energy storage. Huayou sources cobalt primarily from the Democratic Republic of Congo (DRC), which produces the majority of the world's cobalt. DRC cobalt mining has been extensively documented for: child labour in artisanal and small-scale mining (ASM); dangerous working conditions; environmental destruction; and community displacement. Huayou has been specifically named in investigations by Amnesty International and other bodies regarding its DRC supply chain. The company has made commitments to responsible sourcing, including participation in the Responsible Minerals Initiative (RMI), but independent assessments of compliance have varied.
