## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE THIRD INEOS FOUNDER**

John Reece is a co-founder of Ineos Group alongside Jim Ratcliffe and Andy Currie — the three men who created one of the world's largest private chemical companies through a series of leveraged acquisitions of chemical plants divested by BP, ICI, and other majors beginning in the 1990s.

**The Ineos model:** Ineos acquired mature petrochemical assets at discounted prices, extracted operational efficiencies, and built an integrated chemical empire. The company is privately held with minimal public disclosure. It manufactures petrochemicals, specialty chemicals, and industrial gases across 26 countries.

**Environmental record:** Ineos has faced persistent environmental challenges at its facilities — including the Grangemouth refinery in Scotland (a major industrial emissions site, also associated with labor disputes), and operations in Belgium, Germany, and the United States. Ineos is also associated with advocacy for shale gas development in the UK.

**Offshore structure:** Ineos is incorporated in Switzerland and uses tax structures that limit UK tax exposure — despite building its business through UK and European chemical assets.

**Individual profile:** Reece maintains a lower public profile than Ratcliffe. He is not publicly associated with major philanthropic activities at scale.
