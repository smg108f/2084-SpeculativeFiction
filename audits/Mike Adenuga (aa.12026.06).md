## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE NIGERIAN TELECOM MOGUL**

Mike Adenuga Jr. is the founder of **Globacom** — Nigeria's second-largest mobile telecommunications network — and **Conoil Producing**, an oil company. He is among Nigeria's wealthiest individuals and one of Africa's most powerful business figures.

**Globacom:** Globacom introduced per-second billing to Nigeria's mobile market in 2003, disrupting MTN's dominance and forcing industry-wide pricing reform. This is credited as genuine consumer benefit — per-second billing saved Nigerian mobile users billions in annual charges compared to the per-minute model.

**Conoil:** Oil production in Nigeria carries accountability for: Niger Delta environmental damage, community relations in oil-producing regions, and participation in an industry whose product contributes to climate change.

**Legal history:** Adenuga was arrested in 2006 by the Economic and Financial Crimes Commission (EFCC) on allegations of fraud and money laundering related to foreign exchange violations. Charges were later dropped. He was also expelled from Côte d'Ivoire in 2012 amid allegations of involvement in the country's political affairs — he was subsequently readmitted. These episodes are part of the record.

**Niger Delta:** Nigeria's oil industry has caused profound environmental and community harm in the Niger Delta. Conoil's specific environmental record is part of a broader industry accountability that all Nigerian oil producers share.

**Philanthropy:** Adenuga has donated to education and charitable causes in Nigeria, though not at a scale commensurate with his wealth relative to Nigeria's development needs.
