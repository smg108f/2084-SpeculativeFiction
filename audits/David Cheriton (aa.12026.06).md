## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE PROFESSOR WHO FUNDED GOOGLE**

David Cheriton is a Canadian-born computer scientist and Stanford professor who introduced Larry Page and Sergey Brin to Andy Bechtolsheim, and who personally invested $100,000 in Google before it had a name and a business model. That investment became worth billions. He also co-founded Arastra (now Arista Networks) — a major data networking company that went public in 2014 and competes with Cisco in high-performance data center networking.

Cheriton is notable for his extreme personal frugality: he famously cuts his own hair, drives an unremarkable car, and lives simply despite his enormous wealth. This is not a calculated public persona — it appears to be genuine personal temperament.

He has donated significantly to Stanford's School of Engineering — the David Cheriton School of Computer Science at the University of Waterloo (his alma mater) bears his name following a $25M donation. He has also supported Stanford computer science programs.

His wealth was not built on consumer exploitation or environmental harm — it was built on being a computer scientist who recognized Google's potential when almost no one else did, and on founding a legitimate enterprise networking company.
