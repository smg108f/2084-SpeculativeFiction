## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE COMPANY REBRANDING ADDICTION AS HARM REDUCTION**

British American Tobacco continues its long-running strategic pivot from combustible cigarettes toward vaping, heated tobacco, and nicotine pouches, framed publicly as "harm reduction" for existing smokers. Solid H1 2026 growth suggests this transition strategy continues generating shareholder value even as the underlying product category — nicotine addiction — remains fundamentally unchanged in its target customer relationship.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

BAT's wealth remains substantially combustible-cigarette-derived globally even as its growth narrative centers on "New Category" reduced-risk products, a familiar tension the Bureau has observed across the tobacco sector: legacy revenue funding a transition story that has not yet fully displaced it.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

BAT frames its vaping and nicotine-pouch products explicitly as public-health harm reduction. The Bureau rates this **Tier 4 (Contested Framing)**: genuine relative harm reduction for existing smokers is plausible, but the same products have also demonstrably expanded nicotine addiction among populations, including youth, who were not previously smokers — a documented pattern the Bureau does not consider a true Saintly Pivot.

### **3. Influence Vector: The New Addiction Pipeline**

The Bureau's core concern is not BAT's harm-reduction claim for existing smokers but the well-documented expansion of nicotine product marketing and appeal to non-smoking populations through flavored vaping and pouch products, effectively replacing a shrinking combustible customer base with a newly recruited one.
