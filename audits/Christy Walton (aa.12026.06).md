## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE WIDOWED WALTON**

Christy Walton came to the Walton family wealth not through birth but through marriage to John T. Walton — the second son of Walmart founder Sam Walton. John Walton was known for his military service (decorated Vietnam veteran, Medal of Honor), his work on Walton family philanthropic initiatives, and his aviation interests. He died on June 27, 2005, when the ultralight aircraft he was piloting crashed near Jackson Hole, Wyoming. Christy inherited his estate, which included substantial Walmart stock.

Unlike her sister-in-law Alice Walton (Crystal Bridges Museum) or her brothers-in-law Rob (Walton Family Foundation) and Jim (Arvest Bank), Christy Walton has maintained a lower profile. She has donated significantly to charter school initiatives and education reform advocacy — particularly through her involvement with the Alliance for School Choice and related organizations, positioning her within the school choice / voucher advocacy ecosystem. She has also given to conservation and land protection in Wyoming and Montana.

The core concern is identical to all Walton heirs: Walmart's wage structure, its suppression of unionization, its effect on local retail ecosystems and community economic health, and the concentration of wealth in a family that built its fortune on keeping labor costs at or near the minimum.
