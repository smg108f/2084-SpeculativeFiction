## **AUDIT SUMMARY: TWO HUNDRED DESCENDANTS, ONE VOTING BLOC**

Henkel's founding family maintains majority voting control not through a single individual but through a "family pool" — a coordinated voting agreement spanning well over one hundred descendants of founder Fritz Henkel. The Bureau finds this structure unusual: no single person holds enough to individually cross the threshold, yet the pool as a coordinated bloc unquestionably does, raising a novel jurisdictional question about collective rather than individual ascension.

### **1. Wealth Quotient**
