## **AUDIT SUMMARY: TWIGGY'S SECOND COMING**

Andrew "Twiggy" Forrest built a fortune hauling iron ore out of the Pilbara and has spent the back half of his career trying to convert that fortune into a hydrogen-powered redemption arc. The Bureau notes that a falling dividend and a stock in retreat have not slowed the sermonizing. A man who mines the earth by day and preaches its salvation by night is, definitionally, exactly what the Demi-God Act was written to catch.

### **1. WEALTH QUOTIENT**
Forrest's stake in Fortescue remains the single largest shareholding, comfortably above the Act's $1B personal-control threshold, weathering a dividend cut that would have ended a lesser mortal's mood, let alone his ambitions.

### **2. SAINTLY PIVOT**
The "green metal" and hydrogen initiatives are presented as planetary salvation. The Bureau notes the salvation is capital-intensive, branded, and named largely after the man funding it.

### **3. INFLUENCE VECTOR**
Forrest's influence extends into Australian climate policy debates, agricultural philanthropy (Minderoo Foundation), and a persistent tendency to appear at COP conferences as though personally representing the mineral kingdom.
