## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE TEMP AGENCY PATRIARCH**

Frits Goldschmeding founded **Randstad** in 1960 — growing it into the world's second-largest staffing and HR services company, operating in 38 countries and placing approximately 2 million people in work weekly. He stepped back from operations but remains a significant shareholder.

**The staffing industry accountability:** Like Allegis (Stephen Bisciotti, HPB-12026-06SB3), Randstad's business model depends on the existence of a contingent workforce. Temporary staffing provides flexibility for employers and access to work for many employees — but structurally reduces the bargaining power of workers, limits benefit access, and contributes to labor market precarity.

**Randstad's specific record:** Randstad is generally regarded as among the more responsibly run staffing companies — it has invested in worker training and has been involved in workforce development programs. Its **Randstad Foundation** supports vulnerable workers seeking employment.

**Dutch labor market context:** The Netherlands has among the highest rates of flexible and part-time work in Europe — a market that staffing companies like Randstad have both responded to and helped create. Dutch housing and pension systems are under stress partly due to the flexibilization of labor.

**Philanthropy:** Goldschmeding and his family support Christian Democratic social causes and community organizations in the Netherlands.
