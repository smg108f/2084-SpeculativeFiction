## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE HOME DEPOT GODFATHER**

Ken Langone (age 71 per CSV — note: actual age ~89 as of 12026) co-founded **The Home Depot** with Bernie Marcus and Arthur Blank, famously backing the venture when others wouldn't. He also runs **Invemed Associates**, a boutique investment bank. His NYU Langone Medical Center (now NYU Langone Health) represents one of the largest medical philanthropy gifts in U.S. history.

**Home Depot's role:** Home Depot democratized home improvement and building supplies, transforming the retail landscape and enabling millions of homeowners. The social utility is real. It also contributed to the destruction of small independent hardware retailers at scale.

**NYSE governance controversy:** Langone served as chairman of the NYSE Compensation Committee when Richard Grasso received a $187.5M pay package. NY Attorney General Eliot Spitzer sued Langone (and Grasso) for breach of fiduciary duty. The case was ultimately dismissed in 2008. The HPB notes the episode as a significant governance accountability question.

**NYU Langone Medical Center:** Langone donated $200M+ to NYU Medical Center, which renamed itself in his honor. The hospital serves a vast New York patient population including charity care. This is among the most significant medical philanthropic contributions of his generation.

**Republican politics:** Langone has been a prominent Republican donor and was an early backer of Rudy Giuliani's presidential aspirations. He has also been a vocal critic of income inequality rhetoric (famously warning that vilification of the wealthy could trigger a "Hitler"-style response — a remark he later walked back).
