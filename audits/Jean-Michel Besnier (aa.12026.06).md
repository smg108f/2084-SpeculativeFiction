## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE CHEESE EMPIRE'S SILENT HEIR**

Jean-Michel Besnier is a co-heir to Lactalis Group — the world's largest dairy company, headquartered in Laval, France. Lactalis produces Président butter, Galbani cheese, Parmalat, and hundreds of other dairy brands, with operations in 50+ countries. The company was founded by Michel Besnier (father); it is now controlled by his three children.

**The 2017 Salmonella contamination scandal:** This is the defining accountability event of the Lactalis era. In late 2017, Lactalis infant formula produced at its Craon factory was found to be contaminated with Salmonella Agona. At least 36 infants in France were confirmed infected; 12 million boxes were recalled across 83 countries. Subsequent investigations revealed that Lactalis management had been warned of contamination risks at the Craon factory dating to 2005 and had failed to address them. More damning: evidence emerged that Lactalis executives knew of the outbreak before making a public recall — and that the recall, when it happened, was chaotic, with contaminated product remaining on shelves for weeks.

**Opacity:** Lactalis is one of the most secretive major companies in the world. It does not publish accounts. The Besnier family has consistently avoided media attention. No consolidated audited financial statements are publicly available.

**French legal proceedings:** Lactalis executives were placed under formal investigation in France. The case dragged through courts for years; the company reached a settlement with families.

### **Verdict:** CRITICAL — infant formula Salmonella contamination; management prior knowledge; recall mismanagement; company opacity that prevents full accountability assessment. Shared accountability with sibling Marie Besnier Beauvalot.
