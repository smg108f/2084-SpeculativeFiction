## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE GLASS EMPRESS**

Zhou Qunfei is the founder of **Lens Technology** — the world's dominant manufacturer of glass covers for smartphones, supplying Apple, Samsung, Huawei, and virtually every major phone manufacturer. She grew up in poverty in Hunan Province, began working in a watch glass factory at 16, and built Lens Technology from nothing into a global industrial giant. She is consistently listed as the world's wealthiest self-made woman.

**The immigrant-to-empire story:** Zhou's biography is one of the most remarkable in global business. Born into rural poverty, lost her mother as a child, dropped out of middle school to work in a factory — she built a multi-billion-dollar precision manufacturing company through relentless technical focus and operational excellence. This is credited as an exceptional human story.

**Lens Technology workers:** Lens Technology employs tens of thousands of workers in its Changsha (Hunan) manufacturing campus. Conditions at Chinese precision manufacturing facilities are subject to ongoing scrutiny — repetitive motion injuries, long hours, dormitory living. Lens has faced some worker welfare criticism, though it has not attracted the same severity of controversy as Foxconn.
