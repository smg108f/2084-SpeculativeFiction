## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE DEATH LANDLORD**

Wang Laisheng is the co-founder and chairman of **Fu Shou Yuan International Group**, the largest provider of funeral and burial services in China. The company operates cemeteries, funeral homes, and cremation services across major Chinese cities. It is listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange.

**The business model:** In China, land for burial is acutely scarce — particularly in urban areas. Fu Shou Yuan occupies a uniquely powerful position as a premium cemetery operator, charging prices for burial plots in Shanghai and Beijing that can exceed apartment prices per square meter. In a country where filial piety and proper burial are deeply culturally important, the ability to charge premium prices for death is a significant form of market power.

**Regulatory environment:** Fu Shou Yuan operates in a heavily state-regulated industry. Its expansion has been enabled by land grants and favorable regulatory treatment from local governments. The relationship between funeral industry operators and local government land allocation creates opacity around how land rights are obtained and at what price.

**Social benefit:** The company has professionalized China's funeral industry — improving standards of care, introducing modern cremation facilities, and developing "green burial" alternatives (tree burials, sea burials) that reduce land consumption. These innovations are genuine contributions.

**Death care access:** Critics note that Fu Shou Yuan's premium positioning makes dignified burial inaccessible to lower-income families in the cities it serves — effectively creating a two-tier death-care system based on wealth.
