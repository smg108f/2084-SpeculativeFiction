## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE CEMENT PATRIARCH**

Benu Gopal Bangur is the chairman of Shree Cement — one of India's largest and most efficient cement manufacturers, headquartered in Kolkata. Shree Cement is known within the industry for its operational efficiency — it achieves among the lowest energy consumption per tonne of cement produced among Indian manufacturers, and has been recognized for its environmental management relative to sector peers.

**Cement and climate:** Cement production accounts for approximately 8% of global CO2 emissions — driven primarily by the calcination of limestone (a chemical reaction that is not a combustion process and cannot be fully eliminated by switching to renewable energy). Shree Cement's efficiency is a real virtue within this context, but cement's climate footprint remains inherently significant.

**India's infrastructure demand:** India's infrastructure development — roads, housing, bridges, urban construction — drives enormous cement demand. Shree Cement is a beneficiary and enabler of India's growth story.

**Philanthropy:** The Bangur family has a tradition of philanthropy through the Bangur Foundation, supporting healthcare, education, and community development in Rajasthan and Bengal. The family has a long history in Indian business.
