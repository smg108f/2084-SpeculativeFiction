## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE HOTEL CHAIN THAT MAPS THE CLASS DIVIDE**

Marriott's 2026 earnings commentary offers the Bureau an unusually clean data point on economic bifurcation: luxury leisure spending remains resilient even as government and business travel contract. A single hospitality company's occupancy patterns now function as a real-time indicator of which segments of society are still spending freely and which have pulled back — precisely the kind of "Whuffie economy" precursor signal the 2084 setting anticipates.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

Marriott's wealth is asset-light by design — it owns few hotels outright, instead licensing its brand and management expertise across a global franchise network. This makes its value a function of brand control and booking-data access rather than physical holdings, a form of wealth the Bureau finds harder to seize under conventional Apotheosis Protocol procedures.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

Marriott's charitable and disaster-relief initiatives are standard hospitality-sector practice. The Bureau rates this **Tier 3 (Routine)**: present, unremarkable, not a significant factor in the audit.

### **3. Influence Vector: The Travel Barometer**

The stock's 53% run-up alongside a revenue miss driven by falling government and business travel suggests investors are pricing in a widening gap between a resilient luxury-consumer class and a contracting middle-travel segment. Marriott does not create this bifurcation, but its earnings calls now narrate it with unusual clarity, making the company a useful — if unwitting — index of the Humanist Bloc's internal economic stratification.
