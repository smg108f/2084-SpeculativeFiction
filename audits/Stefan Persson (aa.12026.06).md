## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE FAST FASHION PATRIARCH**

Stefan Persson is the son of Erling Persson, who founded H&M (Hennes & Mauritz) in Sweden in 1947. Stefan took over as chairman in 1982 and oversaw H&M's global expansion into one of the world's largest fast fashion retailers — over 4,000 stores in 70+ countries. He is now honorary chairman; his son Karl-Johan Persson is the active chairman. H&M Group has been a central figure in the fast fashion industry's most documented problems: (a) its sustainability claims — including a "Conscious" collection — have been found by consumer regulators to be greenwashing; (b) its supply chain has been linked to exploitative labour conditions in Bangladesh, Cambodia, and other producing countries; (c) fast fashion's environmental footprint — textile waste, water use, synthetic fiber pollution — is significant.
