## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE TOYOTA DISTRIBUTOR TYCOON**

Dan Friedkin inherited and leads the **Friedkin Group**, whose core asset is **Gulf States Toyota** — the exclusive distributor of Toyota vehicles across Texas, Arkansas, Louisiana, Mississippi, and Oklahoma. This is among the most lucrative automotive distributorships in the world. Friedkin has diversified into film production (Imperative Entertainment), conservation, and most visibly, Italian football (acquiring **AS Roma** in 2020).

**Gulf States Toyota — distribution lock-in:** The Gulf States Toyota distributorship was granted to Dan's father Thomas Friedkin and gives the Friedkin Group a monopoly position on Toyota distribution in a massive five-state market. This inherited structural advantage generates extraordinary returns without competitive risk. The HPB notes that distribution monopolies of this scale are inherently accountability-bearing.

**AS Roma acquisition:** Friedkin purchased AS Roma in 2020, investing substantially in a historic Italian football club and guiding it through a Europa Conference League title (2022). AS Roma is a cultural institution for millions of Romans, and Friedkin's stewardship has been broadly positive.

**Conservation:** Dan Friedkin is a serious wildlife conservation philanthropist, particularly in Africa. He has personally funded anti-poaching operations and wildlife conservation programs across multiple African nations.

**Film:** Imperative Entertainment has produced notable films including *The Mauritanian* (2021). This is a constructive cultural contribution.

**Fossil fuel adjacency:** As a Toyota distributor, Friedkin Group has profited from ICE vehicle sales during the peak fossil fuel automotive era. Toyota's own EV transition pace has been slower than some peers.
