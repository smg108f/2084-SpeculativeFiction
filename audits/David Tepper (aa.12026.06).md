## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE VULTURE CAPITALIST WITH A FOOTBALL TEAM**

David Tepper is the founder of Appaloosa Management, a hedge fund that specializes in distressed debt investing — buying the securities of companies (and occasionally countries) that are in financial distress or bankruptcy at deep discounts, and profiting when they recover or are restructured. Tepper became famous for enormous bets during the 2008-2009 financial crisis, when he purchased distressed bank debt that recovered spectacularly — he reportedly earned $7B personally in 2009, making it one of the most profitable single-year individual trades in investment history. He bought the Carolina Panthers NFL franchise in 2018 for a record $2.275B. He has given significantly to Carnegie Mellon University (the business school is now named after him) and to other institutions. He is known for being aggressive, profane, and direct.
