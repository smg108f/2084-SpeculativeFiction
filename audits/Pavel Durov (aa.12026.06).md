## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE ENCRYPTED MESSENGER IN TROUBLE**

Pavel Durov founded VKontakte (VK), Russia's dominant social network, was forced out by political pressure and sold it, then founded Telegram — now one of the world's most widely used messaging applications with 900M+ users globally. Telegram is known for: (1) end-to-end encrypted messaging (by default in Secret Chats only); (2) massive group channels with up to 200,000 members; (3) minimal content moderation, which has made it a preferred platform for: privacy advocates, journalists, political dissidents, but also organized crime, terrorism, drug trafficking, child sexual abuse material (CSAM) distribution, and disinformation operations. In August 2024, Durov was arrested in France at Le Bourget airport and charged with complicity in allowing criminal activities on Telegram — specifically allegations that Telegram facilitated drug trafficking, fraud, and other crimes through inadequate moderation. Durov was released on bail and is subject to French legal process.

However, the documented use of Telegram for CSAM distribution, drug sales, and criminal organization is not a free-speech issue; it is a facilitation-of-harm issue. The French arrest crystallizes this concern.
