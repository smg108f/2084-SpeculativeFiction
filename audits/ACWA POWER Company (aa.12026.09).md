## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE COMPANY THAT MAKES THE DESERT DRINKABLE, ON SAUDI ARABIA'S TERMS**

ACWA Power, majority-backed by Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund, has become one of the world's largest developers of water desalination and power generation infrastructure, with SAR 455 billion in assets under management funding an expanding global portfolio. Unlike Sberbank or China Telecom's more overt state-fusion, ACWA Power's structure blends sovereign wealth fund backing with an actively expanding international commercial footprint, giving Saudi state capital direct influence over water and power infrastructure in multiple countries.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

ACWA Power's wealth is desalination-and-generation-infrastructure-based, funded substantially by Saudi sovereign capital and deployed across water-stressed regions globally, control over water access being, in the Bureau's assessment, among the most fundamental forms of leverage a single entity can hold over a population.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

Providing water access in water-stressed regions carries genuine public-benefit weight. The Bureau rates this **Tier 2 (Genuine but Sovereign-Leveraged)**: real infrastructure benefit that simultaneously extends Saudi state financial influence into the essential-resource infrastructure of client countries.

### **3. Influence Vector: Water as the Ultimate Chokepoint**

The Bureau considers water infrastructure control a categorically distinct risk from energy or financial chokepoints, populations cannot substitute or defer their need for water the way they might for other commodities, making sovereign-backed control over desalination capacity in water-stressed nations a uniquely high-leverage form of influence.
