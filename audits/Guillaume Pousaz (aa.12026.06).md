## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE PAYMENTS ARCHITECT**

**The payments business:** Payment processing is infrastructure — unglamorous but essential. Checkout.com competes with Stripe, Adyen, and Worldpay for enterprise e-commerce payment processing. The business model is transaction fees on payment volume — a toll on commerce.

**The valuation correction:** Checkout.com's valuation fell sharply from its 2022 peak, reflecting both the broader tech valuation correction and specific questions about the company's profitability and growth trajectory. **Philanthropy:** Limited documented philanthropy at scale for a billionaire of his wealth level.
