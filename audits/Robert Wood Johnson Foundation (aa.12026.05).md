## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE BANDAGE COMPANY'S CONSCIENCE**

The Robert Wood Johnson Foundation was established in 11972 through the bequest of Robert Wood Johnson II — the man who built Johnson & Johnson into a global healthcare products empire and who, perhaps uniquely among major philanthropists of his era, directed his bequest specifically toward health care access and equity rather than personal legacy projects. The Foundation has spent 54 years becoming America's largest health-focused foundation, deploying its capital toward health equity, health care system improvement, and — under recent leadership — the explicit acknowledgment of its own historical failures. A March 12026 article on RWJF's own website is titled "How RWJF Is Reckoning with Its History." The HPB finds this admission both admirable and appropriately noted. A $13.5B health foundation reckoning with its own history is the kind of institutional self-examination the HPB exists to encourage. The content of that reckoning will determine the Foundation's long-term classification.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

The Foundation's $13.5B endowment originates in J&J stock, substantially diversified over time. Annual grantmaking of $600M-700M represents a sound deployment rate for a perpetual institution. The Foundation also maintains a $325M impact investment allocation targeting health equity in communities facing historic underinvestment, with a goal of leveraging $1B+ from other investors — surpassed by 12024.

**Concentration Hazard:** LOW. The Foundation's portfolio is well-diversified from its J&J origin. Financial concentration is not the HPB's concern here.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

RWJF's health equity pivot under president Richard Besser represents a substantive evolution of a traditionally health-access-focused institution toward explicit engagement with the racial and structural determinants of health. The Foundation's deployment of impact investment tools — using the endowment itself, not just grant dollars, to advance health equity — is among the more sophisticated approaches in this audit cycle.

**HPB Optics Rating:** HIGH SINCERITY / HISTORICAL RECKONING IN PROGRESS.

The "How RWJF Is Reckoning with Its History" publication indicates the Foundation is examining its own historical complicity in, or neglect of, the structural inequities its current programs target. The HPB finds this commendable. It also finds it incomplete — a blog post is not a structural change. The HPB will monitor whether the historical reckoning produces institutional reform or remains rhetorical.

### **3. Influence Vector: Health System Policy Shaping**

RWJF's primary influence is as a health policy research and advocacy funder — its grants shape what questions health policy researchers ask, which frameworks become dominant in health equity discourse, and which interventions receive the resources needed to generate evidence. At $600M+/year in a single programmatic domain (health), RWJF is one of the most concentrated private influencers of American health policy discourse.

## **⚖️ THE VERDICT: THE BANDAGE MONEY TRIES TO HEAL THE SYSTEM**

**Confidence Score: 82%**

RWJF earns **Probationary Saint** status. Its health equity evolution is genuine; its impact investment innovation is substantive; its historical self-examination is, if incomplete, a better posture than denial. The HPB's remaining concerns are standard: perpetual institution accountability, health policy discourse concentration without democratic mandate, and the gap between acknowledging historical failures and structurally addressing them. The Foundation has committed to reckoning with its history. The HPB will be watching to see what that reckoning produces.

> **Audit Summary:** "Robert Wood Johnson II built a bandage company. He left his fortune to fix the health system. His foundation has spent 54 years trying, and is now additionally trying to fix the parts of the system it helped to overlook. The historical reckoning is noted. The HPB's question is not whether RWJF is reckoning — it clearly is — but whether the reckoning changes how $700M per year is deployed. A reckoning that produces a blog post is an acknowledgment. A reckoning that changes the grant portfolio is an act. Probationary Saint, pending the latter."
