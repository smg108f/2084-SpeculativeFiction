## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE FRANCHISE BISHOP**

Edir Macedo built a $2 billion personal fortune by teaching five million Brazilians, and roughly five million more across 100+ countries, that their money placed "on God's altar" would return to them multiplied. Little of it multiplied for anyone but him. He is, structurally, exactly what the Demi-God Act was written to catch: a man who converted a spiritual claim into a media conglomerate, a bank, and a political party, and who insists — pulpit-first, weekly, on his own television network — that his wealth is evidence of divine favor rather than a Wealth Quotient violation.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

Subject's holdings resist clean categorization by design. Direct assets include a controlling stake in **Grupo Record** (Brazil's second-largest broadcast network, ~R$4.3B in annual revenue), a majority stake in **Banco Digimais**, and a global real estate portfolio anchored by the **Temple of Solomon** replica in São Paulo — an 11-story, two-city-block megachurch that cost roughly $300M (R$680–700M) to build, financed largely by congregant tithes. Above all of this sits a **perpetual, self-replenishing revenue stream**: weekly tithing from millions of followers, structured theologically as an obligation rather than a donation. The HPB flags this as a **Concentration Hazard of the first order** — Subject has built a wealth-generation engine that requires no further labor, product, or innovation on his part, only continued belief.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

Subject's most recent large-scale "generosity" is instructive: in late 2025 and early 2026, Macedo personally injected over **R$1.75 billion** into Banco Digimais to keep it solvent amid a Central Bank fraud investigation into inflated assets and concealed insolvency. This is not philanthropy — it is a shareholder propping up his own failing bank to avoid seizure. The HPB rates the sincerity of Subject's public-good posture as **LOW**. A São Paulo prosecutor has gone further, stating on record that Macedo's promise of tithe-fueled riches "amounts to fraud." Subject has faced upward of 20 criminal investigations since the 1990s — money laundering via black-market currency operators (*doleiros*), fraud, and *curandeirismo* (charlatanism) among them — the great majority dismissed for lack of evidence or expired under statute of limitations. A pattern of investigation without conviction is not innocence, in HPB terms; it is **evidentiary friction**, and friction this consistent, sustained across three decades, reads less like persecution and more like a very good legal team.

### **3. Influence Vector: Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric**

This is the section the HPB flags in bold. Subject is not merely wealthy — he is a **literal bishop** who tells his congregation, in his own words, that they are placed "on God's altar, just as Jesus was God's tithe for humanity," and that material prosperity flows back to those who give to him specifically. This is Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric in its purest, least metaphorical form: a human being explicitly positioning himself as the conduit through which divine favor becomes bank-account balance. The **Human Humility Act of 2031** exists precisely to catch language like this, and Subject has been broadcasting it, uncontested, on his own national television network for over three decades. Layered on top is a **Political Agency** violation: in 2005 Subject founded the **Republicanos** party (formerly PRB) as a direct electoral extension of the church, and his 2018 endorsement of Jair Bolsonaro is widely credited by scholars as decisive — nearly 70% of Brazilian evangelicals voted for Bolsonaro, a bloc analysts argue he would not have won without. A man who claims to distribute God's favor, and who also controls a television network and a political party built to convert that favor into votes, is not a "Saint" in the 2084 taxonomy. He is a **parallel state church** wearing the paperwork of a nonprofit.
