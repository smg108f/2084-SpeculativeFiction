## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE MAN WHO ROCKETIZED MORTGAGES**

Dan Gilbert founded Quicken Loans in 1985 and built it into the largest retail mortgage lender in the United States — eventually renamed Rocket Mortgage and taken public via Rocket Companies in 2020. He is the majority owner of the Cleveland Cavaliers NBA franchise (which won its only championship in 2016 behind LeBron James), and a substantial downtown Cleveland real estate investor — he has purchased dozens of downtown Cleveland properties and positioned himself as the single most important investor in Cleveland's urban revitalization. He suffered a stroke in 2019 that significantly affected his capacity; he has continued at a reduced operational pace. He has given substantially to the Cavaliers Community Foundation and to Detroit-area causes (though Quicken Loans / Rocket is headquartered in Detroit). His company has also faced scrutiny: in 2021, Rocket agreed to pay $3.5M to settle allegations of Fair Housing Act violations and discriminatory lending practices.

The HPB notes that the mortgage lending industry, and Quicken/Rocket specifically during the 2000s subprime crisis period, warrants specific scrutiny — Quicken was less exposed to the worst subprime practices than some competitors, but it operated in an industry that caused significant community harm.
