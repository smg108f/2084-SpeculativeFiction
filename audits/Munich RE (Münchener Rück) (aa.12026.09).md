## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE COMPANY THAT PRICES THE PLANET'S RISK**

Munich Re is one of the world's largest reinsurers, meaning it does not merely insure individual risks but reinsures the insurers who do, giving it a position at the apex of the global risk-pricing hierarchy. A fifth consecutive year of surpassed profit guidance, record half-year profit, and a substantial buyback, delivered alongside the company's own explicit climate warning, place Munich Re in a uniquely powerful position: profiting from and publicly forecasting the same climate volatility reshaping global insurance markets.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

Munich Re's wealth is reinsurance-premium-based, compounding through a pricing cycle where climate-driven catastrophe risk increasingly determines global insurance affordability and availability. Five consecutive years of surpassed guidance during a period of escalating climate volatility suggests the company's risk models are pricing this volatility profitably.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

Munich Re publishes extensive climate research and catastrophe modeling made available to policymakers and the insurance industry broadly. The Bureau rates this **Tier 2 (Genuine, Structurally Central)**: real public value in climate risk understanding, though the same modeling directly informs the company's own profitable pricing.

### **3. Influence Vector: The Climate Oracle Problem**

As one of the world's foremost authorities on climate-driven catastrophe risk, Munich Re's proprietary models effectively determine which regions become uninsurable, a form of soft sovereignty over where people can afford to live and build, that operates several steps removed from any individual policyholder's understanding.
