## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE SON OF ENZO**

Piero Ferrari is the illegitimate son of **Enzo Ferrari**, the legendary founder of Ferrari. He holds approximately 10% of **Ferrari N.V.** — the most iconic automotive brand in the world. Piero serves as Vice Chairman of Ferrari and has been a quiet but present steward of the Ferrari legacy.

**Ferrari as cultural institution:** Ferrari is not merely an automobile company — it is a motorsport institution (Scuderia Ferrari / Formula 1), an Italian cultural symbol, and a luxury goods company that commands extraordinary brand loyalty and economic premium. Its contribution to Italian industrial identity and F1 is genuine and significant.

**Luxury emissions accountability:** Ferrari produces high-performance, high-emissions internal combustion engine vehicles for ultra-high-net-worth buyers. Per-vehicle emissions are significantly above standard automobiles. Ferrari has committed to hybridization (LaFerrari, SF90) and announced plans for its first fully electric Ferrari — accountability for the pace of this transition is appropriate.

**Inheritance and role:** Piero Ferrari's wealth is derived from his birthright as Enzo's son — he did not found or build the enterprise. His role has been as a supportive steward and institutional voice rather than an operational executive. The HPB notes this passive inheritance dynamic.

**Maranello community:** Ferrari's Maranello factory is the economic backbone of a community in Emilia-Romagna. Ferrari's role as a local industrial anchor and employer is a genuine community contribution.

**Whuffie credit:** Ferrari cultural institution stewardship; F1 legacy; Maranello community anchoring; Italy's most recognized industrial brand.

### **Verdict:** MARGINAL — luxury high-emissions vehicle production; EV transition pace; inherited wealth; offset by Ferrari's cultural and community significance; standard burns.
