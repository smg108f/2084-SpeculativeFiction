## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE ONLY RAILROAD THAT DOESN'T STOP AT THE BORDER**

Canadian Pacific Kansas City — rebranded following the 2023 merger that created the first single-line rail network connecting Canada, the United States, and Mexico — is a unique geopolitical artifact: a private corporation that physically stitches together three sovereign nations. Where CN Rail controls the northern spine, CPKC controls the continental meridian. The HPB notes that CPKC's founding value proposition was explicitly geopolitical — that USMCA trade would flow more efficiently if one private company owned the tracks from Winnipeg to Mexico City. The regulators who approved this merger created, in the HPB's assessment, a **Privately Held Trade Sovereignty Engine**.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

CPKC's wealth is concentrated in physical infrastructure (approximately 20,000 route miles of track across three countries), terminal assets, and long-term contracts with automotive manufacturers, grain exporters, and chemical shippers. The Mexico operations — formerly Kansas City Southern — give CPKC access to Mexican manufacturing corridors that are among the fastest-growing freight volumes in North America due to nearshoring trends. The company has benefited from U.S.-China trade tensions redirecting supply chains through CPKC's exact geographic corridor. The HPB classifies this as **Geopolitical Windfall Accumulation**: CPKC grew wealthier because nations fought.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

CPKC has made emissions pledges (locomotive hydrogen conversion programs) and funds community programs along its corridor. CEO Keith Creel cultivated a reputation as an operational efficiency patriarch — the "Precision Scheduled Railroading" doctrine he championed reduces dwell time and fuel consumption simultaneously. The HPB rates these **Tier 2 (Substantive, Operationally Motivated)**. The efficiency doctrine that Creel champions is not altruism; it is the extraction of maximum value from minimum asset use. It serves shareholders first; the environmental benefits are incidental.

### **3. Influence Vector: The USMCA Chokepoint**

CPKC's most alarming characteristic is its structural position at the intersection of USMCA trade flows. The single-line network means that a CPKC work stoppage — or a CPKC board decision to prioritize certain cargo types — has direct effects on automotive production in Michigan, grain exports from Saskatchewan, and manufactured goods shipments from Monterrey. In 2021 and 2024, rail labor disputes forced federal government intervention in both Canada and the United States. The HPB classifies CPKC as a **Tri-Sovereign Pressure Point**: an entity capable of extracting concessions from three governments simultaneously by doing nothing.

## **⚖️ THE VERDICT: THREE COUNTRIES SHARE ONE CHOKEPOINT**

**Confidence Score: 89%**

CPKC is, in the HPB's assessment, a slightly more geographically ambitious version of its CN Rail peer — and therefore a slightly more acute threat. Where CN Rail can pressure two governments, CPKC can pressure three. The merger that created it was approved on the premise that it would serve the public interest by lowering trade costs; the HPB notes that lower trade costs for shareholders are not the same thing as public benefit, and that the infrastructure enabling those costs was built on the same public land grants that should preclude private ownership.

> **Audit Summary:** "The railroad that connects three nations was approved because the regulators decided that USMCA trade efficiency outweighed the structural risk of a private entity controlling a continental meridian. The HPB was not consulted. It is consulting now."
