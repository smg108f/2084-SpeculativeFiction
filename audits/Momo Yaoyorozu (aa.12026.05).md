### **APOTHEOSIS AUDIT: MOMO YAOYOROZU (REF. 12026.05)**

**Status:** MARGINAL ASCENSION RISK
**Primary Influence Vector:** Embodied Manufacturing Monopoly / Dynastic Real Estate Concentration / Pre-Ascension Age Profile**

**Total Estimated Value:** **$1,200,000,000,000** (Real Estate | Hotels; Yaoyorozu family holdings; HPB notes Subject is among the youngest individuals in the Jump Universe audit queue and that her RTWI trajectory, given her Quirk capabilities, is of substantially greater concern than the current figure alone suggests)

---

## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE YOUNGEST SUBJECT IN THE QUEUE**

Momo Yaoyorozu is, at the time of this audit, among the youngest subjects the HPB has reviewed at the $1 trillion+ RTWI tier — a hero-in-training from a family whose wealth predates her professional career, and the holder of a Quirk that gives her the ability to create any non-living object from her body fat. The HPB is auditing her now, at the earliest stage of her adult career, for a reason that should be stated plainly: her current $1.2 trillion RTWI is the least interesting thing about her from an Ascension risk perspective.

What the Bureau finds more interesting is the forward projection. Momo Yaoyorozu is a certified genius with a manufacturing Quirk, a heroic career that is generating increasing public influence, a family real estate empire as a financial backstop, and a work ethic and intellectual rigor that the Bureau's Behavioral Assessment Division rates as exceptionally high. If she is audited at $1.2 trillion now and the Bureau does not engage with the structural conditions of her ascent, the Bureau's models suggest she will be audited again at a figure that is considerably less manageable within the standard framework.

The HPB prefers to engage early. It is engaging early.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

The Yaoyorozu family's $1.2 trillion RTWI is anchored in real estate and hotel holdings — a multigenerational property portfolio that has appreciated substantially and that Momo Yaoyorozu has inherited as the family's primary heir. Subject has not, to date, actively managed or expanded these holdings; they are, at this stage, a legacy asset rather than an active commercial enterprise. The Bureau's concern is that "not actively managed" at $1.2 trillion is not the same as "not a concentration risk."

**Concentration Hazard — Class I (Quirk Manufacturing Potential):** Momo Yaoyorozu's Creation Quirk allows her to synthesize any non-living object from her cellular lipids, provided she understands its molecular structure. The economic implications of this capability, fully developed, are significant. A human being who can manufacture arbitrary physical objects — from consumer electronics to pharmaceuticals to precision components — without external supply chains, raw material procurement, or factory infrastructure represents a manufacturing capability that, at scale, is potentially more economically significant than any conventional industrial asset. The Bureau's Asset Valuation Division has classified this as **Embodied Manufacturing Capital — Developmental Stage**: not yet deployed commercially, potentially transformative if it is.

**Concentration Hazard — Class II (Forward RTWI Trajectory):** The HPB's Prospective Ascension Division has modeled Subject's RTWI trajectory under three scenarios: (1) Hero career without commercial Quirk deployment — RTWI reaches approximately $3 trillion within 20 years through real estate appreciation alone. (2) Hero career with modest Quirk licensing — RTWI reaches approximately $15 trillion within 20 years. (3) Full commercial Quirk deployment as manufacturing platform — RTWI trajectory exceeds Bureau modeling capacity within 15 years. The Bureau is auditing at the earliest stage because Scenario 3 becomes structurally harder to address the longer it is deferred.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

Subject's public benefit record is, for her age and career stage, commendable. Her heroic activities through the U.A. High School program and subsequent provisional hero work have demonstrated genuine commitment to public safety and a capacity for tactical leadership that the Bureau's Behavioral Assessment Division rates highly. She has not, to date, deployed her Quirk for commercial manufacturing purposes — a restraint the Bureau notes, without being able to determine whether it reflects principled choice or circumstance.

The Yaoyorozu family's real estate portfolio includes affordable housing units and community development properties, which the Bureau's Philanthropy Review Division classifies as genuine public benefit relative to the portfolio's overall scale. This is modest evidence of a Saintly Pivot orientation.

HPB Optics Rating: **PROBATIONARY — Early Career, Genuine Indicators, Structural Monitoring Required.** Subject is not yet a problem. She has the conditions to become one. The Bureau is engaging now to help ensure she doesn't.

### **3. Influence Vector: The Genius With a Factory Inside Her**

Momo Yaoyorozu's most significant long-term Ascension risk is the one she has not yet deployed: the combination of her intellectual capabilities and her Creation Quirk. A genius who can synthesize any object she understands creates a feedback loop — greater understanding leads to greater manufacturing capability, which leads to greater economic power, which can fund greater research, which expands understanding further. This loop, unaddressed, is the most significant individual Potentiality Value the Bureau has assessed in the Jump Universe audit queue.

The Bureau is not suggesting this loop is inevitable or that Subject would pursue it irresponsibly. The Bureau is suggesting that the structural conditions for it exist, that no external accountability mechanism currently governs how Subject's Quirk is deployed commercially, and that building that accountability now — at $1.2 trillion, before the loop has started — is substantially more tractable than building it at $15 trillion or beyond.

## **⚖️ THE VERDICT: THE AUDIT IS THE EARLY WARNING SYSTEM**

**Confidence Score: 72%**

Momo Yaoyorozu is a MARGINAL ASCENSION RISK at present and a significantly higher-classification risk in the Bureau's forward projections if her Quirk is commercially deployed without structural governance. The 28% confidence reduction reflects the Bureau's genuine uncertainty about a career that is still early-stage and a subject whose values — based on available behavioral evidence — are more consistent with responsible stewardship than with Ascension-oriented accumulation. The Bureau is issuing this audit not because Subject has done something wrong but because the time to build accountability structures is before they are urgently needed, not after.

> **Audit Summary:** "Momo Yaoyorozu is $1.2 trillion, a genius, and twenty-something, with a Quirk that could rewrite manufacturing economics if she ever decides to use it that way. The Bureau is not here to stop her from using her Quirk. It is here to ensure that when she does, there is a governance structure in place that reflects the scale of what she can do. She is, at this moment, one of the most promising subjects the Bureau has audited. The Bureau's job is to make sure that remains true at $10 trillion, not just at $1.2."
