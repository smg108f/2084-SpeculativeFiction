## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE ELDER STATESMAN OF BEAUTY**

Leonard Lauder is the son of Estée and Joseph Lauder and the former CEO and longtime chairman of Estée Lauder Companies, which he grew from a family business into one of the world's leading luxury beauty conglomerates. He is 91 years old, has essentially retired from operational roles, and has spent much of his later life with two consuming passions: Cubist art (he donated 78 masterworks — the largest donation of Cubist art in history — to the Metropolitan Museum of Art in 2013, estimated at $1B+) and the Breast Cancer Research Foundation, which his late wife Evelyn founded and which has raised over $800M for research. He also donated $131M to the Whitney Museum of American Art and $75M to Penn Medicine. He is one of the most substantive art and medical research philanthropists in America.
