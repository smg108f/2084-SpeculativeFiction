## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE REFINER THAT BOUGHT ITSELF BACK**

Marathon Petroleum's 2026 has been defined less by expansion than by concentration: near-record refinery utilization drove a massive Q2 profit jump, and the company has run an unusually aggressive share buyback program, reportedly nearing completion. The effect is fewer shares representing the same critical fuel-refining capacity, concentrating both value and voting power among a shrinking shareholder base even as the underlying refining assets remain as structurally essential as ever.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

Near-record utilization in 2026 means the company is running its chokepoint infrastructure at close to maximum capacity while simultaneously retiring shares, a combination that compounds shareholder value without expanding the underlying public-facing capacity base.
