## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE TANKER KING**

He built his fortune through Frontline, the world's largest oil tanker company, and expanded into offshore drilling (Seadrill — which went through two Chapter 11 bankruptcies), LNG shipping (Flex LNG), and dry bulk (Golden Ocean). Fredriksen is the quintessential shipping financier: he profits from global oil trade, has navigated multiple commodity cycles, and has been willing to use bankruptcy restructuring aggressively to protect his equity. He was also accused of breaching UN sanctions by transporting Iranian oil in the 1980s-90s — a matter that generated a Norwegian investigation but resulted in no conviction. His wife Inger Astrup Fredriksen was listed on the Forbes list in prior years; his daughters Cecilie and Kathrine Fredriksen are both billionaires through their own Geveran Trading holdings.
