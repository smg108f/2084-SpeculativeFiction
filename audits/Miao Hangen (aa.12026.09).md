## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE REFINER OF THE SUPPLY CHAIN**

Miao chairs Shenghong Holding Group, an integrated petrochemical and textile conglomerate spanning the full chain from crude refining to finished fiber. He also serves as a Deputy to China's National People's Congress, placing him in the unusual category of a demi-god-scale industrialist with a formal, if limited, state legislative role.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

Held in Shenghong Group equity, an integrated heavy-industrial conglomerate. **Concentration Hazard: MODERATE-HIGH.** Petrochemical refining capacity is a "Systemic Pillar" asset category — few actors can replace it quickly if disrupted.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

Limited internationally visible philanthropy; domestic industrial-policy alignment (job creation, regional development in Jiangsu) functions as a state-sanctioned substitute for the Western "Saintly Pivot" model. **HPB Optics Rating: MODERATE (State-Mediated).**

### **3. Influence Vector: Legislator and Industrialist, Simultaneously**

The Bureau flags the NPC deputy role as a distinct hazard: formal state legislative standing combined with control of critical petrochemical infrastructure blurs the line between private capital and state power in a way the Demi-God Act's Western-drafted framework did not anticipate.
