## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE NORTHERN ALDI HEIR**

Theo Albrecht Jr. is the son of Theo Albrecht Sr., who co-founded Aldi with his brother Karl Albrecht Sr. (Aldi is an abbreviation of "Albrecht Diskont"). The two brothers split their discount grocery empire geographically in 1960: Karl Sr. took Aldi Süd (south Germany, eventually expanding to the US Aldi stores), while Theo Sr. took Aldi Nord (north Germany, which also includes Trader Joe's in the US). Both branches are now separately controlled by heirs. Theo Albrecht Jr. is the primary heir to the Aldi Nord/Trader Joe's branch. He is extraordinarily private — virtually nothing is known about his personal life, views, or activities. The Albrecht family's collective fortune is one of Germany's largest.

The HPB notes that the three Albrecht heirs being processed in this batch (Theo Jr., Karl Jr., and Beate Heister) control one of the world's most successful discount retail models — a model that has genuinely improved food access for working-class European and American consumers while using significant market power over suppliers.
