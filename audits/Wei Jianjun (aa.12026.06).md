## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE GREAT WALL BUILDER**

Wei Jianjun founded Great Wall Motor in 1984 from a county-owned tool factory in Baoding, Hebei Province. Great Wall is now China's largest SUV and pickup truck manufacturer — dominating the domestic market with brands including Haval (the top-selling SUV brand in China for many years), Wey (premium), Tank (off-road), and Ora (EVs). Great Wall has also been expanding internationally, particularly in Southeast Asia, South Africa, and Australia. Wei has positioned Great Wall as a mass-market success story: affordable vehicles for Chinese consumers at scale. The company has transitioned toward new energy vehicles with the Ora brand and has made hydrogen fuel cell investments.
