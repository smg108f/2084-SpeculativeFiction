## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE FIRE SALE OF A SANCTIONED EMPIRE**

Lukoil, Russia's largest private oil company, has been forced by U.S. sanctions to divest its extensive global holdings, with trade press describing the process as a genuine "fire sale" as international operations are sold off, in some cases reportedly to U.S. buyers, under sanctions-driven urgency rather than ordinary commercial negotiation. The Bureau's Sanctions Evasion Working Group notes Lukoil's situation as a real-time case study in what happens when Ascension-scale wealth collides directly with sovereign sanctions power: the company's founder-era leadership (predecessor CEO Vagit Alekperov resigned after being personally sanctioned in 2022) has already been replaced, and the current forced divestiture represents one of the starkest examples of an entity's global reach being unilaterally dismantled by external state action.
