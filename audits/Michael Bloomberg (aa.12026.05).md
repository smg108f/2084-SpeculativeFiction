The audit therefore concerns not just the man but the machine.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

Bloomberg LP is estimated to be worth $80–90B of Bloomberg's total $109B net worth. Because the firm is **entirely privately held** — no public shareholders, no SEC filings, no traded equity — standard RTWI (Real-Time Wealth Index) calculation tools are degraded. The HPB's AI has flagged Bloomberg as a **Class-II Opacity Risk**: his true potentiality value is likely understated. Bloomberg LP commands approximately 330,000 terminal subscriptions globally at ~$27,000/year — a recurring revenue floor of roughly $8.9B annually — making it not just a media company but a **Toll Booth on Reality**. The HPB notes with concern that Bloomberg's wealth is structurally *resistant to conventional divestiture*: the terminal network's value is inseparable from network effects. Selling it does not reduce the Ascension risk — it merely transfers the Information Sovereignty hazard to another entity, potentially one less visible to the Bureau.

**Concentration Hazard:** Bloomberg LP controls real-time financial data for virtually every major sovereign treasury and central bank on Earth. Under the HPB's **Infrastructure Doctrine of 2041**, this constitutes a **Tier-1 Systemic Dependency** — equivalent to owning a private electrical grid that every government hospital runs on.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

Bloomberg Philanthropies is the most politically directional philanthropy in the HPB's current dataset. Subject has deployed capital in the following verticals:

- **Climate:** Bloomberg Philanthropies has pledged to close every coal plant in the United States and has committed over $500M to the Sierra Club's Beyond Coal campaign. HPB Rating: **High Optics, High Influence**.
- **Gun Control:** Everytown for Gun Safety, funded primarily by Bloomberg, has operated as a shadow legislative architecture — funding state-level ballot initiatives across 30+ states without requiring elected office. HPB Rating: **Critical Policy Capture Risk**.
- **Public Health:** Anti-tobacco, anti-obesity, and anti-opioid campaigns funded at the municipal level across dozens of cities globally. HPB Rating: **Moderate Saintly Credit**.
- **Arts & Urban Development:** High optics, low influence threat. HPB Rating: **Acceptable**.

**HPB Assessment:** Bloomberg Philanthropies is not a "Saintly Pivot." It is a **Policy Operating System** disguised as charity. Subject is not giving money away to reduce his Ascension risk; he is converting dollars into regulatory outcomes, electing allies to state legislatures, and building a durable policy infrastructure that will operate on his behalf indefinitely. The philanthropy *increases* his Shadow Policy score rather than neutralizing it. The 2084 HPB classifies this pattern as **"Donor-Sovereign Laundering"** — the transformation of taxable capital into untaxable political will.

**2020 Presidential Campaign Note:** Subject voluntarily expended an estimated **$1.06 billion** of personal funds on a presidential campaign lasting approximately 99 days, yielding zero delegates before suspension. The HPB's Political Agency Index recognizes this as the **single most expensive individual Political Agency expenditure** in the pre-Demi-God Act era. The HPB reads this not as a failure but as a **proof-of-concept**: Subject demonstrated the capacity to unilaterally purchase a national political apparatus from private funds — and treated the loss as acceptable. This alone would trigger a Summons to Ascend under current statute.

### **3. Influence Vector: Information Sovereignty**

The Bloomberg Terminal is the HPB's most structurally complex audit target. Unlike social media platforms (which influence opinion) or venture capital portfolios (which influence innovation), the terminal influences the **speed and direction of capital itself**. Every basis-point move in interest rates, every sovereign debt auction, every currency intervention is conducted by parties whose perception of financial reality is mediated by Bloomberg's proprietary data architecture.

**The Core Risk:** Bloomberg LP is not ideologically neutral infrastructure. The editorial layer of Bloomberg News, Bloomberg Intelligence, and Bloomberg Opinion shapes how capital actors *interpret* the data they receive through the terminal. Subject thus controls both the raw signal (market data) and the interpretive layer (editorial framing). This is the HPB's definition of an **Information Moat**: a single entity that controls both the pipe and the meaning of what flows through it.

**Phantasim Classification Note:** At 84, Subject is biologically classified as **phantasim** — subject to natural mortality within the audit horizon. However, Bloomberg LP's Information Sovereignty risk does not diminish on biological expiration. The HPB's **Institutional Ascension Protocol** applies: the firm itself may require independent reclassification as a **Demi-God Entity (Institutional Class)** regardless of the founder's mortality status.
