## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE CARDBOARD KING**

Anthony Pratt is the chairman of Pratt Industries (US) and Visy Industries (Australia) — a family business that his father Richard Pratt built into one of Australia's and America's largest paper and cardboard packaging companies. Anthony, based predominantly in the US, has expanded Pratt Industries in America aggressively, building recycled cardboard mills across the country and positioning the company as a major employer. He made prominent investments in American manufacturing during and after the Trump administration, pledging to create significant US manufacturing jobs.

The Pratt family has been involved in scandal: Richard Pratt (Anthony's father) was found to have engaged in cartel behavior — price-fixing of cardboard boxes — resulting in the largest ever fine under the Australian Competition Act. Richard Pratt later settled. Anthony was not personally charged. Additionally, the Pratt family has been involved in litigation regarding Richard Pratt's extramarital affairs and estate disputes.
