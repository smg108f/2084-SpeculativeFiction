## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE POWER STRIP EMPRESS**

Ruan Liping co-founded **Gongniu Group** (Chinese: 公牛集团; brand: Bull) with her brother Ruan Xueping in 1995 in Zhejiang Province. Gongniu became China's — and arguably the world's — dominant manufacturer of power strips, electrical outlets, and electrical safety accessories. The Bull brand controls approximately 60% of China's retail power strip market.

**The product:** Power strips and electrical outlets are safety-critical consumer products. Substandard electrical accessories cause fires and deaths. Gongniu's success is partly attributable to building a reputation for quality and safety in a market historically plagued by counterfeit and unsafe products. This is genuine and important consumer benefit.

**Manufacturing accountability:** Gongniu's manufacturing employs tens of thousands of workers in Zhejiang Province. No major labor controversies are documented in public records.

**Market concentration:** Gongniu's 60% market share in China's power strip market represents a dominant position in a product category that every household needs. While this reflects genuine brand trust, the concentration warrants accountability for pricing and access.

**International expansion:** Gongniu has expanded into smart home electrical products. The electrical safety accountability of its expanding international product range warrants monitoring.

### **Verdict:** MARGINAL — power strip quality and safety leadership credited as genuine consumer benefit; market concentration noted; manufacturing employment credited; standard burns.
