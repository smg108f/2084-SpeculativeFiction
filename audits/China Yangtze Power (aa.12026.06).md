## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE COMPANY THAT HOLDS BACK THE FLOOD**

China Yangtze Power is the world's largest listed hydroelectric power producer, operating the Three Gorges Dam — the world's largest power station by installed capacity — along with Gezhouba, Baihetan, Wudongde, and Xiluodu dams. The HPB notes an unusual classification challenge: this entity is a publicly listed corporation, but it is majority-owned by China Three Gorges Corporation, which is state-owned. It is, in essence, **a sovereign's financial instrument wearing a stock ticker**. The Three Gorges Dam alone controls the water flow affecting hundreds of millions of downstream inhabitants. The company does not merely generate power; it decides when the Yangtze floods and when it does not.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

China Yangtze Power's wealth is almost entirely in physical infrastructure: dam structures, turbines, transmission assets, and long-term power purchase agreements with China's grid operators. The company generates approximately ¥70B in annual revenue with margins that reflect the zero-fuel-cost economics of water falling through turbines. It holds minimal financial speculation risk. The HPB classifies its wealth type as **Geological Capital** — value derived from the immovable manipulation of a river system. This is among the most durable and least-divestible forms of wealth known to the audit bureau.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

China Yangtze Power provides clean power to approximately 70 million Chinese households and contributes meaningfully to the PRC's carbon intensity reduction targets. Flood control operations by the Three Gorges system have prevented an estimated trillions in downstream damage. These are genuine public goods. However, the HPB rates them **Tier 1 (Structural / Involuntary)**: the company produces public goods as a byproduct of its revenue model, not as a philanthropic choice. The displacement of 1.3 million people during Three Gorges construction — for which compensation was often inadequate — represents a social liability that no current CSR program adequately addresses.

### **3. Influence Vector: Hydraulic Civilization Authority**

The HPB's primary concern is not ideological — it is **hydraulic**. The operator of the Three Gorges Dam controls the most consequential piece of water infrastructure on Earth. Downstream provinces, Bangladesh (via Brahmaputra tributaries), and agricultural systems across Southeast Asia exist within the operational shadow of this dam. When the company modulates water release for power generation optimization rather than downstream welfare, it exercises a form of **unelected hydraulic sovereignty** over populations with no recourse.
