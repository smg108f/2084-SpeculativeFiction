## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE CARTON, THE CONVICTION**

Finn Rausing is co-heir, with siblings Jörn and Kirsten, of the Gad Rausing branch of the Rausing family. Ruben Rausing invented Tetra Pak's aseptic packaging system in 1952. Tetra Pak packages approximately 200 billion cartons per year globally — milk, juice, soup, wine — with the distinctive multi-layer construction (paper, polyethylene, aluminum foil) that gives aseptic shelf stability but creates a recycling challenge.

**The Eva Rausing incident:** In July 2012, Finn Rausing's wife, Eva Rausing (née Kemeny), died of a drug overdose in their London home at 36 Cadogan Place. She had long struggled with substance abuse; Finn Rausing had also documented substance abuse issues. Finn Rausing did not report Eva's death. He kept her body in the bedroom of their home for approximately two months before police discovered it during a house call in August 2012. He was charged with and convicted of preventing a lawful burial. He received a ten-month suspended sentence.

This conviction is in the public record. It does not constitute a Corporate Crime in the HPB's AP 7.4(c) framework, but it does represent a documented personal criminal conviction involving gross moral failure at the time of a family tragedy.

**Tetra Pak packaging:** The multi-layer aseptic carton — paper, LDPE, aluminum foil — is technically difficult to recycle. Tetra Pak has invested in recycling infrastructure but the recycling rate for aseptic cartons globally remains low. At 200 billion cartons per year, the packaging waste dimension is significant.
