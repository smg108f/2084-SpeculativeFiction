## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE CHEESE KING WHO DOESN'T EXIST**

Emmanuel Besnier is the third-generation owner and CEO of Lactalis — the world's largest dairy company, with brands including President butter and cheese, Galbani, Pulmuone, Stonyfield, and dozens of others in 100+ countries. Lactalis has revenues of approximately €28B. Besnier inherited control from his father and has expanded it aggressively through acquisition. He is, by most journalistic accounts, among the most reclusive billionaires in the world — he does not give interviews, avoids public appearances, and has achieved a visibility-to-power ratio that rivals Gérard Wertheimer. Lactalis was at the center of a major infant formula contamination scandal in France in 2017-2018 that sickened dozens of infants, resulting in criminal charges and a court finding of negligence.

Lactalis supplies dairy products consumed by hundreds of millions of people globally, with particular presence in infant nutrition (an especially sensitive category). The company is entirely privately held with no public financial disclosures. **Concentration Hazard: HIGH.** The 2017 contamination scandal and criminal negligence finding is the HPB's primary concern.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

### **3. Influence Vector: The Invisible Dairy Empire**

The 2017 contamination scandal demonstrated that Lactalis's quality control failures have direct infant health consequences. The HPB classifies this as **Food Infrastructure Negligence Risk** — the private control of food essential supply with documented failure to meet the safety standards the product's vulnerability demands.
