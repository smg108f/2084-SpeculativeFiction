## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE SHOE FACTORY PATRIARCH**

Zhang Congyuan is the founder of Huali Industrial Group — one of the world's largest contract footwear manufacturers, making shoes for Nike, Adidas, and other global brands at facilities primarily in Vietnam and China. Huali is the largest single supplier of sports footwear in Vietnam.

**The contract manufacturing model:** Huali sits in the middle of the global footwear value chain — not a brand, but the factory. This position means Huali captures the manufacturing margin while brand owners capture the far larger brand premium. The labor conditions and wages paid to Huali's factory workers in Vietnam are thus the core accountability question.

**Vietnam operations:** The majority of Huali's workforce is Vietnamese. Vietnamese factory workers at large export manufacturers earn wages that are above local minimums but remain a small fraction of the retail value of the shoes produced. Working conditions at Huali factories have generally been considered adequate within the Vietnamese manufacturing context, but "adequate within context" and "fair share of value created" are different standards.

**Age:** At 82, Zhang is in an advisory capacity.

**Philanthropy:** Limited documented individual philanthropy at scale.
