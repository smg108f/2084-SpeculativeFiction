## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE MAN WHO OUTRUNS EVERY OTHER TRADER**

The Bureau's file raises a distinct concern from ordinary investment wealth: profit generated primarily through speed and algorithmic advantage over other market participants, rather than through building or improving any underlying enterprise.
