## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE HOME DEPOT PATRIARCH**

Bernie Marcus co-founded The Home Depot in 1978 after being fired from Handy Dan Home Improvement — one of the more famous "fired and then built something bigger" stories in American business. Home Depot is the world's largest home improvement retailer, and Marcus's founding vision (the big-box warehouse format, employee expertise, low prices) transformed American retail.

**The philanthropy — substantial and specific:**
- **Georgia Aquarium:** Marcus donated $250M to build the Georgia Aquarium in Atlanta — at opening, the largest aquarium in the world. A genuine public good.
- **Marcus Autism Center:** Significant funding for autism research and treatment — Marcus's family has personal connection to autism, and the Marcus Autism Center at Children's Healthcare of Atlanta is a nationally significant resource.
- **Marcus Foundation:** Has given hundreds of millions to healthcare, education, and Jewish causes.

**Conservative political giving:** Marcus is a major Republican donor — contributed approximately $7M to Donald Trump's 2020 campaign and has been a vocal supporter of conservative political causes. He called critics of Trump's business community support "schmucks." The political giving funds candidates and policies the HPB considers to undermine the democratic and regulatory institutions that constrain billionaire power.

### **Verdict:** MARGINAL — genuine and substantial philanthropy on the positive side; conservative political giving that works against institutional accountability on the other. Burns scale up the philanthropy and require a political contribution transparency commitment.
