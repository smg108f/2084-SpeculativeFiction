## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE GHOST IN THE PARROTHEAD MACHINE**

Subject expired 09/01/2023 (Merkel cell carcinoma, natural causes, no Ascension-related flight risk). This is the HPB's first fully posthumous Apotheosis Audit, and the desk has no clean precedent for it: the man is gone, but the royalty stream, the ~20% equity stake in a hospitality empire, and two warring trustees fighting over a $275M trust are all still very much alive. Buffett cannot flee to a Flotilla. His trust, on the other hand, has never stopped moving.

### **1. The Testamentary Ledger (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

The HPB's registry still lists Subject at **~$1,000,000,000** as of the last live snapshot (12024.03) — six months **after** his death, an embarrassing lag that HPB Data Integrity has since flagged. The actual corpus left behind is smaller and messier than the headline figure: a **$275M testamentary trust** holding song royalties, a minority (~20%) stake in Margaritaville Enterprises, real estate, and $2M in musical equipment, all willed to widow Jane Slagsvol Buffett and their three children.

- **Concentration Hazard:** The gap between the "$1B legend" and the "$275M corpus" is itself instructive. Most of Buffett's lifetime valuation was never liquid — it was **brand equity**, the kind of "Potentiality Value" the HPB's AI is trained to distrust. Dead, that brand equity didn't evaporate. It kept licensing itself.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot, Posthumously (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

While living, Subject built modest but genuine Whuffie: co-founder of the **Save the Manatee Club** (1981) and the **Singing for Change Foundation**, both still operating on his name and, nominally, his money.

- **HPB View:** Charitable output has stalled, not from bad faith but from litigation gridlock — every dollar currently flowing out of the trust is more likely headed to outside counsel than to a manatee. The "Saintly" ledger is frozen mid-gesture. Until the trust dispute resolves, philanthropy is a reputation asset the estate owns but cannot spend.

### **3. Influence Vector: The Undead Franchise**

Buffett poses zero **Political Agency** risk — dead men do not join Shadow Cabinets. His risk is entirely structural: the **Margaritaville licensing empire** (30 restaurants, 20 hotels, vacation clubs, casinos, home furnishings lines, and the **Latitude Margaritaville** 55+ retirement communities now expanding into Texas, South Florida, and — as of this year — Mexico) keeps compounding under his name without him. The brand carries an estimated 75% U.S. recognition rate. This is "Cognitive Impact" on a scale most living demi-gods would envy, generated entirely by a man who can no longer sign a contract.

- **The Real Hazard:** Not the $275M sitting in the trust today, but what happens if the co-trustee war ends, distributions resume compounding, and the Margaritaville enterprise's continued global expansion pushes the collective heirs' claim back toward the $1B line his brand already touched once, in death, by accident.

## **⚖️ THE VERDICT: A CORPSE CANNOT ASCEND, BUT HIS TRUST CAN**

**Confidence Score: 64%**

The living man is permanently cleared — the HPB has no jurisdiction over the deceased, and Subject poses no personal Ascension risk of any kind. His **estate**, however, is a different case file entirely: a $275M corpus attached to an appreciating, globally-expanding licensing machine, managed by two co-trustees currently burning trust assets on litigation rather than growing them responsibly. The HPB classifies this as **Marginal Ascension Risk by Proxy** — not because the Buffett Trust is close to $1B today, but because nothing currently prevents it from getting there once the lawsuits end and the Margaritaville royalty stream is allowed to compound unsupervised.

> **Audit Summary:** "Subject is deceased and therefore unreachable. The corpus he left behind is not. Recommend the trust — not the man — be placed under Mortal Recertification review before its co-trustees stop fighting each other long enough to notice how fast Margaritaville is growing without either of them."
