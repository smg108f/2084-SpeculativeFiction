## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE VENTURE CAPITALIST WHO READ THE CLIMATE REPORT**

John Doerr is the defining figure of Silicon Valley venture capital — the general partner at Kleiner Perkins who backed Google ($12.5M for ~12% in 1999), Amazon, Netscape, Intuit, Compaq, and dozens of others, accumulating $8.8B in returns that essentially trace the entire arc of the tech economy. He popularized OKRs (Objectives and Key Results) — the goal-setting framework from Intel's Andy Grove — through his 2018 book *Measure What Matters*, which is now standard curriculum in tech management globally.

**The climate turn:** Doerr has become one of the most significant climate philanthropists in America. In 2022, he and his wife Ann Doerr donated $1.1B to Stanford University to establish the **Doerr School of Sustainability** — the largest gift in Stanford's history and the first new school Stanford had created in 70 years. The school focuses on climate science, clean technology, and sustainability policy. He published *Speed & Scale: An Action Plan for Solving Our Climate Crisis* (2021), arguing for a concrete OKR-structured climate action plan. He is a Giving Pledge signatory.

**The tech industry's downstream effects:** Doerr's VC career helped build companies (Amazon in particular) whose labor practices, market concentration, and economic effects are the subject of ongoing HPB scrutiny. He did not design those companies' labor structures, but he provided the capital that scaled them.

**Political engagement:** Doerr has been an active Democratic donor and technology policy advocate, with genuine policy influence in climate and technology.
