## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE PROPERTY PRINCESS IN CRISIS**

Yang Huiyan received approximately 57% of Country Garden Holdings from her father Yang Guoqiang in 2007, making her briefly the richest woman in Asia. Country Garden was China's largest property developer by contracted sales at its peak — building residential communities across 200+ Chinese cities and internationally (Forest City, Malaysia).

**The 2021-2023 property crisis:** Country Garden was one of the three largest developers at the center of China's property market collapse — alongside Evergrande (which defaulted) and Sunac (which restructured). Country Garden came close to default in 2023, missed coupon payments on dollar bonds, and faced a liquidity crisis affecting hundreds of thousands of pre-sold but unfinished homes. The company scrambled to complete housing units that buyers had pre-purchased (as is standard in Chinese real estate) but that remained unbuilt.

**Unfinished homes:** Hundreds of thousands of Chinese households paid deposits and installments on Country Garden homes that were not completed when the financial crisis hit. These families are "mortgage slaves" — paying mortgages on homes they cannot occupy. The scale of human harm is extraordinary.

**Forest City, Malaysia:** A planned city for 700,000 people on artificial islands near Singapore — built to sell primarily to Chinese buyers, which became politically toxic after China-Malaysia relations soured and COVID restrictions eliminated buyers. As of 2024, Forest City had approximately 9,000 residents out of 700,000 capacity.
