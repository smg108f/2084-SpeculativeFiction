## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE GROCERY DISTRIBUTOR**

Rick Cohen is the chairman and CEO of C&S Wholesale Grocers, the largest wholesale grocery distributor in the United States. C&S is the invisible infrastructure of American food retail — it supplies grocery chains, independent supermarkets, and military commissaries with products from a network of highly automated distribution centers. The company is private and opaque; revenue estimates exceed $30B annually.

C&S under Cohen's leadership has been at the forefront of grocery warehouse automation — deploying robotic systems from Symbotic and other automation companies to reduce manual labor in distribution centers. This has generated significant labor conflict. The United Food and Commercial Workers (UFCW) union has organized against C&S's automation-driven workforce reductions. Workers who previously held unionized warehouse jobs — sorting, picking, and moving grocery products — have been displaced by robotic systems.

C&S has also been involved in major grocery sector consolidations. In 2023, when Kroger announced its proposed $25B acquisition of Albertsons (pending regulatory review), C&S was positioned to acquire certain Albertsons stores as a divestitures package — an arrangement that raised additional questions about market concentration in grocery supply chains.

Cohen's personal philanthropic record is not public.
