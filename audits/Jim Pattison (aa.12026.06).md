## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE EMPIRE BUILDER FROM EAST HASTINGS**

James Allen Pattison began his business career selling used cars from a single dealership in Vancouver. Over sixty years, he built the Jim Pattison Group into one of Canada's largest private companies — a conglomerate spanning grocery retail (Save-On-Foods, Overwaitea, Buy-Low Foods), auto dealerships, media (radio and television stations), outdoor advertising (the largest in Canada), entertainment (Ripley's Believe It or Not!, Guinness World Records), fishing and seafood, and financial services. The group employs approximately 45,000 people across Canada and internationally.

Pattison is known for two things: his business longevity (he has been CEO for over sixty years, into his nineties) and his motivational management philosophy — famously, he historically fired his lowest-performing car salesperson each month, a practice that attracted attention and controversy. He has attributed his work ethic to his Christian faith and his origins in East Hastings, Vancouver — a working-class neighborhood.
