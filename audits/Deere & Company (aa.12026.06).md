## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE GREEN GOD OF THE GRANARY**

Deere & Company is the rare subject that does not merely hoard wealth — it has fused itself into the literal mechanism by which the species eats. Through a combine of patient acquisition and proprietary firmware, it has positioned itself as the unacknowledged toll-keeper of the planet's caloric supply chain. The HPB does not classify this as "ambition." It classifies it as **load-bearing godhood**: remove the subject and the harvest does not merely slow — in many regions, it stops.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

The subject's wealth is not parked in art or islands; it is **embedded in the machines themselves**. Equity, dealer-network real estate, and — most concerning to auditors — an enormous and growing stockpile of **proprietary repair software (Service ADVISOR™)** that functions less like intellectual property and more like a **toll booth bolted to the world's tractors**. HPB analysts flag this as a textbook **Concentration Hazard**: the asset cannot be divested without functionally starving the customer base that depends on it. You cannot "sell off" a kill switch without first admitting one exists.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

Subject's public posture leans on "feeding the world" rhetoric — sustainability pledges, precision-agriculture efficiency gains, philanthropic grants to rural communities. HPB rates this Saintly Pivot as **largely cosmetic**. Each efficiency gain is sold back to the farmer as a subscription; each "rural community grant" arrives wrapped in dealership-exclusive service contracts. The optics read as generosity. The architecture reads as **recurring rent on the right to grow food**.

### **3. Influence Vector: The Kill-Switch Pastoral**

The subject's defining structural risk is this: it does not need to threaten anyone. It only needs to remain the sole entity capable of authorizing a repair. A regulatory dispute, a missed payment, a geopolitical disagreement — any of these could, in theory, leave a fleet of combines bricked mid-harvest, by design rather than accident. The HPB's **2026 Repair-Monopoly Settlement** ($99M, ten-year forced software access) is read internally not as accountability but as a **negotiated tariff** — the cost of doing business as a demi-god who has, in auditors' words, "put a leash on the plow and called it progress." This is not a scaffold for human flourishing. It is a **substitution for the right to feed oneself**, quietly inserted between humanity and its own harvest.

## **⚖️ THE VERDICT: THE GRANARY GOD MUST BE UNCHAINED FROM THE GRAIN**

**Confidence Score: 91%**

The HPB rarely encounters a subject whose threat is not its mobility but its **immovability** — Deere cannot flee to a Flotilla because its power is rooted in the dirt of every continent it serves. This is, paradoxically, the most dangerous profile the Bureau audits: a demi-god that has made itself indispensable to biological survival, then quietly reserved the right to revoke that survival on a billing cycle. Subject is hereby classified **CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK**, with mandatory divestment of all repair-authorization infrastructure as the precondition for any future "Saintly" status.

> **Audit Summary:** "Subject did not merely sell the world the machine that feeds it. Subject ensured that the machine could still, on a remote command and a missed invoice, *starve the world that bought it* — and called the resulting dependency 'innovation.' The Bureau finds no precedent for a demi-god whose temple is the tractor shed. Recommend immediate structural decoupling of repair authorization from ownership, full stop, before the next planting season makes the question moot."
