## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE CMA CGM CHAIRMAN**

Rodolphe Saadé is the chairman and CEO of CMA CGM — the world's third-largest container shipping company, founded in Marseille in 1978 by his Lebanese-born father Jacques Saadé Sr. (died 2018). Following his father's death, Rodolphe assumed operational leadership while the family's ownership structure spans him, his brother Jacques Jr., and his sister Tanya Saadé Zeenny. CMA CGM operates over 600 vessels and serves 420+ ports globally.

CMA CGM reported extraordinary profits during 2021–2022 when global supply chain disruptions drove container freight rates to record levels. CMA CGM's net profit in 2021 alone was approximately $17.9B — the result of a global crisis that disrupted supply chains and inflated shipping costs for goods ranging from medical supplies to food. The company donated $1.2B to humanitarian causes in 2021; the HPB notes this is approximately 6.7% of that year's profit.

Rodolphe Saadé has led CMA CGM into aggressive media acquisitions in France — purchasing La Provence (Marseille's main newspaper), BFM TV, RMC (news radio), L'Express, and other French media assets.
