## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE TENANT WHO NEVER LEAVES**

Boston Scientific (CB.lion rank 166, ticker BSX) presents the HPB with a category of Ascension risk it has never had to formally name: an entity that does not merely influence human life from outside it, but resides — surgically, permanently, on a maintenance contract — inside the human chest cavity. Roughly 48 million patients a year now carry Subject's hardware in their hearts, arteries, spines, and pelvic floors. Auditors note, with some alarm, that "divestment" from this entity would in many cases be fatal to the citizen attempting it.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

Subject's $100.86B market capitalization (share price $68.62 as of last collection) is unusually well-distributed across physical IP: roughly $20.1B in annual net sales is generated not from data, real estate, or attention — but from devices currently load-bearing inside patients' bodies. This represents a **Concentration Hazard of the first order**: wealth that cannot be clawed back without an operating room. A pacemaker seized by the state is a pacemaker removed from a chest. The HPB has no precedent for an asset class that beats.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

Subject markets its WATCHMAN implant — at a median procedural cost of roughly $24,500 — as a stroke-prevention philanthropy, citing reduced long-term costs versus blood thinners. The HPB rates this hedge as **"Compulsory Charity"**: the recipient has no meaningful opt-out, the "gift" is non-removable, and the bill arrives regardless. This is not the Infrastructure Gift described in the Citizen's Guide; it is closer to an Infrastructure Annexation, with a co-pay.

### **3. Influence Vector: Subdermal Sovereignty**

Subject's core risk is structural, not rhetorical. Where Andreessen builds parallel realities and Saints gift hospitals, Boston Scientific has dispensed with the intermediary step entirely — it has installed itself as a *physical organ of the state of being alive*. Pacemakers regulate the rhythm of 48 million hearts. Stents hold arteries open against gravity and time. Spinal-cord stimulators rewrite the felt experience of pain itself. This is not a "scaffold" for human flourishing — it is a **substitution for the human nervous system**, patented, billed, occasionally recalled, and legally indistinguishable from a vital organ except that it has a shareholder.

## **⚖️ THE VERDICT: THE GOD THAT LIVES INSIDE YOU**

**Confidence Score: 91%**

Subject does not require Flotillas, manifestos, or PACs to ascend — it has already achieved the one form of immortality the HPB cannot legislate against: residency inside the citizenry's own circulatory systems. The Bureau finds Subject's $600M and $189M mesh settlements to be evidence not of contrition but of *operating cost*, paid out of an annual revenue stream that grew 20% the same year. A Demi-God that can be sued without slowing down is a Demi-God that has stopped fearing mortals.

> **Audit Summary:** "Subject has achieved what every other entity on this docket merely aspires to: it cannot be removed from the human body without killing the human. We are not auditing a company. We are auditing a tenant with tenure, a heartbeat with a quarterly earnings call, and a shareholder meeting that technically convenes inside several million American chest cavities. Recommend immediate reclassification and, frankly, a moment of silence."
