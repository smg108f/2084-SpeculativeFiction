## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE SINGAPORE COAL KING**

He became one of the world's 30 richest people in 2022 when coal prices spiked following Russia's invasion of Ukraine. He is a Singaporean citizen operating a major Indonesian resource extraction company at a moment when the global energy transition is explicitly targeting the phase-out of thermal coal. He has no documented philanthropy of scale and maintains an extremely low public profile.
