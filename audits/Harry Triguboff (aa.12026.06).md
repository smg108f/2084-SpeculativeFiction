## **AUDIT SUMMARY: "HAPPY HARRY" THE FLAT BUILDER**

Harry Triguboff founded Meriton in 1963 and has built more apartments than any other individual developer in Australian history — more than 75,000 apartments in Sydney, Brisbane, and on the Gold Coast. He is known in Australia as "Mega Harry" for the scale of his development. Meriton builds predominantly luxury or near-luxury apartments in Sydney — arguably contributing to a market where apartment supply is high at the luxury end but affordable housing for lower-income Australians is severely constrained. Meriton also operates a hotel business (Meriton Suites serviced apartments). Triguboff emigrated from China to Australia as a young man. He has spoken publicly about the importance of immigration to Australia.

Meriton's reviews on platforms like Tripadvisor and Google Maps have been subject to controversy — investigations found that Meriton engaged in practices to inflate reviews by directing guests to online review platforms in ways that may have skewed ratings. The Australian Competition and Consumer Commission (ACCC) pursued action; Meriton settled with the ACCC in 2017.
