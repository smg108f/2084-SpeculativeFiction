## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE HALF-OWNED HALO**

Rihanna is a genuinely novel case file. Her wealth is not classic solo-hoarding — it is a 50/50 joint-venture stake in a French luxury conglomerate's cosmetics subsidiary, meaning her Ascension risk rises and falls on decisions made in a boardroom she does not chair. Compounding the puzzle, a foreign sovereign state has already, in effect, deified her: Barbados named her its 11th National Hero in 2021, three years before the HPB could have acted, making this the first file in which a rival government beat the Bureau to the punch.

### **1. The "Minority Stake" Paradox (The Hoarding Metric)**

Subject's wealth is unusually — almost defiantly — non-solo. **Fenty Beauty**, her dominant asset, is a 50/50 joint venture with LVMH's Kendo Brands division; independent valuations place the brand between $1B and $2.8B, meaning her direct half alone could run $500M–$1.4B depending on which banker's spreadsheet you trust. LVMH is currently exploring an outright sale of its 50% stake (JPMorgan and Evercore have floated €1.5B–€2.5B for the half), which means Subject's personal RTWI could be repriced overnight by a transaction she does not control and may not even get to approve. Add **Savage X Fenty** (a 30% stake in a company last valued near $1B, ~$300M) and an early-stage **Fenty Hair** minority position, and roughly 85%+ of her estimated net worth sits inside two co-owned consumer-goods ventures. Music royalties — the asset that made her famous — are now a rounding error against the cosmetics conglomerate's balance sheet. Real estate is modest by demi-god standards: a $13.8M Beverly Hills family compound (a second adjoining parcel was sold in 2023 for $10.3M), no seastead prep, no known Flotilla holdings. **Concentration Hazard flag: HIGH** — not because she owns too much outright, but because too much of what she owns is priced by someone else.

### **2. The Clara Lionel Doctrine (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

Subject's Saintly Pivot credentials are unusually strong for a file this size. The **Clara Lionel Foundation**, founded 2012 and named for her grandparents Clara and Lionel Braithwaite, has directed north of $100M into climate resilience, health equity, education, and women's entrepreneurship across the Caribbean, the U.S., and East Africa — including a recent $15M climate-justice pledge — and Subject was named to the inaugural **TIME100 Philanthropy** list in 2026. HPB optics assessment: sincere, well-regarded, low-cynicism. The one structural gap auditors flag: the Foundation remains a private family trust, which means the "gift" is still technically recallable. Until it is converted to irrevocable public governance, it counts as goodwill, not surrender.

### **3. Dual-Sovereignty: The Ambassador's Halo (Sovereign Ideology)**

This is the section that makes the file interesting. In 2018, Barbados appointed Subject its **Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary** — an active diplomatic title. In 2021, Prime Minister Mia Mottley named her the nation's **11th National Hero**, a permanent, state-conferred honorific with no mechanism for revocation by any authority, including the HPB. Subject has also shown a willingness to exercise cross-border soft power outside music entirely (see: the 2021 India farmer-protest tweet, which triggered a formal diplomatic complaint). HPB Legal has nicknamed the resulting jurisdictional tangle the **"Barbados Problem":** can the United States strip "personhood" from someone a friendly sovereign nation has already, and permanently, declared a legend? No precedent exists. This file is the test case.
