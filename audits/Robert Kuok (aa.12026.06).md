## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE SUGAR KING**

Robert Kuok — known as Malaysia's "Sugar King" for his early dominance in Malaysian sugar trading and refining — is Southeast Asia's most senior surviving billionaire patriarch. Now in his early 90s, he built a conglomerate spanning sugar, palm oil (Wilmar International is the world's largest agribusiness company and palm oil processor), Shangri-La luxury hotels, South China Morning Post (sold to Alibaba in 2015), and Kerry Group real estate in Hong Kong. Kuok's wealth is rooted in relationships across Malaysia, Singapore, Hong Kong, and China — he is widely regarded as one of the most connected businessmen in Asia.

Wilmar International — in which Kuok holds a significant stake — has been a central actor in the global palm oil industry, which has faced extensive documentation of: tropical deforestation, orangutan habitat destruction, and labour abuses (including child labour) in plantations across Indonesia and Malaysia.
