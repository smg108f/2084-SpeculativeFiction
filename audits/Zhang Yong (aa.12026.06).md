## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE HOTPOT EMPEROR**

Zhang Yong is the founder of Haidilao International — China's most famous restaurant chain, known for its Sichuan hotpot and its legendary customer service. Zhang started with a single hotpot restaurant in Jianyang, Sichuan in 1994, serving customers with such extreme attentiveness that Haidilao became nationally famous before social media existed. The chain now operates 1,300+ restaurants globally.

**The Haidilao service model:** Haidilao is famous for its over-the-top service — free manicures while waiting, tableside magicians, staff who remember customer preferences. This model is built on a workforce that is intensively trained, motivated through promotion pathways rather than minimum wage, and treated as a genuine career rather than a transactional job. Haidilao's employee benefits and promotion opportunities within the restaurant industry are above average.

**Food safety incident:** In 2017, a major food safety scandal hit Haidilao when an undercover reporter documented health code violations — dirty soup bases reused, rodents in kitchens. Zhang Yong issued an unusually direct and full public apology, accepting full responsibility and implementing comprehensive food safety overhaul. The crisis management response was widely praised as a model of corporate accountability.

**Singapore tax residency:** Zhang Yong holds Singapore citizenship and is tax resident in Singapore — where income tax rates are substantially lower than China. This is a common structure for Chinese billionaires but creates a disconnect between where wealth was built and where taxes are paid.

**Philanthropy:** Zhang has donated to poverty relief and educational causes in Sichuan.

### **Verdict:** MARGINAL — positive labor model for service sector; genuine crisis accountability response; Singapore tax residency creates tax contribution gap; limited philanthropy at scale.
