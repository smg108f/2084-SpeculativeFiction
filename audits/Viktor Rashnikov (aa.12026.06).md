## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE STEEL CITY OLIGARCH**

Viktor Filippovich Rashnikov is the chairman and controlling shareholder of Magnitogorsk Iron and Steel Works (MMK), one of the three largest steel producers in Russia. MMK is inseparable from the city of Magnitogorsk in the Ural region — the company is the city's primary employer and economic foundation, a relic of Stalinist industrialization that Rashnikov acquired through the 1990s privatization process. MMK produces steel used in construction, automotive, and (critically) military and defense applications in Russia.

Rashnikov acquired his MMK stake through the post-Soviet privatization era. He has been chairman since 2005 and has grown MMK into a profitable, export-oriented steelmaker. The company was listed on the London Stock Exchange (depositary receipts) until delisting following the 2022 invasion and subsequent sanctions.

The EU and UK sanctioned Rashnikov following Russia's full-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022, citing his status as a key oligarch with close ties to the Russian state and his role in providing economic support to the Russian government through steel production that supports defense and military infrastructure.

Unlike several other sanctioned oligarchs (Fridman, Prokhorov), Rashnikov has made no public statement distancing himself from the invasion. He has not publicly supported it either — his public posture is essentially silence.
