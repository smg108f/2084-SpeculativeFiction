## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE DASSAULT TRIAD**

Laurent Dassault is one of three children of Serge Dassault who inherited Groupe Industriel Marcel Dassault — the holding company for the Dassault empire founded by Marcel Dassault, who built the Mirage and Mystère jet fighters from a wartime resistance workshop into France's dominant aerospace company. The current empire spans:

**Dassault Aviation:** France's primary military aircraft manufacturer (Rafale fighter jet — the backbone of the French Air Force and a major export product) and business aviation (Falcon jets — among the most prestigious in the world). The Rafale is France's primary geopolitical leverage tool in arms export diplomacy.

**Le Figaro:** One of France's leading national newspapers — historically France's most prominent conservative daily. The conflict of interest between Dassault family ownership of Le Figaro and Dassault Aviation's dependence on French government arms contracts has been documented by French media critics for decades.

**Dassault Systèmes:** A global leader in 3D design software — CATIA and SolidWorks are standard tools in aerospace, automotive, and industrial design worldwide.

**Serge Dassault's vote-buying conviction:** In 2012, Serge Dassault was convicted of vote-buying in Corbeil-Essonnes, where he was mayor. He appealed. Laurent, Thierry, and Marie-Hélène inherited this empire after Serge's death in 2019.

Laurent Dassault is a vintner (Château Dassault, Saint-Émilion) and manages family investments.
