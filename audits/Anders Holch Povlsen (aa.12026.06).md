## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE FASHION BARON WHO REWILDED SCOTLAND**

Anders Holch Povlsen is the CEO of BESTSELLER — the Danish family fashion conglomerate founded by his parents Troels and Merete Holch Povlsen. BESTSELLER owns a portfolio of European mid-market fashion brands (Jack & Jones, Vero Moda, Only, Selected Femme/Homme) with global manufacturing and retail operations. At 35, Anders Holch Povlsen became the youngest billionaire in Europe.

**Fast fashion accountability:** BESTSELLER manufactures at massive scale in Bangladesh, Cambodia, India, and Pakistan. The fast fashion model — frequent seasonal collections at low price points, with implicit disposability — drives overproduction, textile waste, and maintains downward pressure on garment worker wages. Bangladesh alone employs millions of workers in the garment sector supplying European brands including BESTSELLER.

**The Sri Lanka bombing:** On Easter Sunday 2019, coordinated terrorist bombings struck hotels and churches in Sri Lanka. Three of Anders Holch Povlsen and his wife Anne Storm Pedersen's four children were killed in the attack. The Bureau records this personal tragedy with profound acknowledgment and notes that it is entirely separate from the accountability assessment that follows.

**Scottish rewilding:** Povlsen is the largest private landowner in Scotland — owning approximately 220,000 acres across multiple Highland estates. He has pursued an ambitious rewilding program: reintroducing native woodland, removing deer fencing, restoring peatlands, and working toward the return of apex predators. This is one of Europe's most significant private rewilding initiatives and represents a genuine use of private wealth for ecological restoration.

**Philanthropy:** Povlsen and his wife are active through the BESTSELLER Foundation on garment worker welfare and climate issues in South Asia.
