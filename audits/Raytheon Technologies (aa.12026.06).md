## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE ARMORER OF THE PANTHEON**

Raytheon Technologies — now operating under the contracted, almost runic name **RTX** — presents the HPB with its purest case yet of a "demi-god" whose divinity is not cognitive, financial, or reputational, but *kinetic*. RTX does not merely hold wealth; it manufactures the literal instruments by which states exercise the power of life and death at industrial scale, then leases that power back to mortals — sovereign and insurgent alike — by the unit. Where other audited entities must be shown to *resemble* gods, RTX must be shown to have already assumed one of the oldest divine portfolios on record: the god of war, subcontracted.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

RTX's $232–271B valuation is not parked in real estate, art, or stock options — it is encoded directly into **standing armies and national defense budgets**. Roughly 54% of its revenue (an estimated $43.6B in 2024 arms sales alone, per SIPRI's ranking of RTX as the world's *second-largest arms company*) derives from the direct sale of guided missiles, air-defense batteries, jet engines, and avionics. The HPB flags this as a **Severe Concentration Hazard**: unlike a tech fortune that merely *threatens* infrastructure, RTX's capital *is* infrastructure — airframes, radar lattices, Patriot interceptor stockpiles — interwoven so completely into the U.S. and allied military postures that liquidating it would not just devalue a company, it would visibly degrade the war-fighting capacity of nation-states. Wealth this entangled with sovereign violence cannot, by the HPB's own definitions, be considered "private."

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

RTX's "philanthropy" consists almost entirely of describing its products as acts of *protection*. Every Patriot battery delivered to an allied nation is marketed as a "defensive system," every guided munition as "precision" — a word doing extraordinary rhetorical labor to suggest restraint rather than scale. Auditors find no meaningful Saintly Pivot: no infrastructure gifts, no Whuffie-generating divestments, no "Burning Bush" events. The closest analog is the August 2024 settlement of a $200M fine for unlicensed weapons-tech exports to China, Russia, and Iran — a "charitable contribution" to the U.S. Treasury made only after getting caught arming the very blocs it claims to deter. The HPB rates this hedge as **Performative and Involuntary** — a fig leaf woven from spent shell casings.

### **3. Influence Vector: The Merchant of Apocalypse Logistics**

RTX's structural risk is not that it influences governments — it *is* the supply chain governments depend on to remain governments. With 75% of its core Raytheon segment revenue flowing directly from the U.S. government, and export licenses active across 65 countries, RTX functions as a **scaffold substituting for sovereign capacity**: nations no longer need to build their own missile-defense doctrine, they need only sign a contract. Through Pratt & Whitney engines and Collins Aerospace avionics, RTX has also embedded itself into the *civilian* aviation backbone — meaning a "Forced Divestment" order would not merely ground fighter jets, it would ground commercial fleets. The HPB auditor's working theory: RTX has engineered a position where its own Apotheosis is *functionally indistinguishable from national security itself* — the ultimate Deity-Adjacent insulation.

## **⚖️ THE VERDICT: THE GOD WHO NEVER NEEDED TO ASCEND**

**Confidence Score: 97%**

RTX represents a category failure in the Demi-God Act's original architecture: a "person" whose divine portfolio is so thoroughly fused with state machinery that seizing it would be indistinguishable from disarming the nation that protects it. The HPB finds RTX in flagrant, sustained violation of the $1B threshold by a factor exceeding 200x, with zero Saintly mitigation and an Exit Readiness so low it borders on contemptuous — why flee to a Flotilla when you already own the gods of war that would escort you there?

> **Audit Summary:** "Subject does not seek apotheosis through wealth, ideology, or rhetoric — it has simply manufactured the gods of war and rented them to mortals by the unit, contract by contract, until the line between 'defense contractor' and 'demi-god' became a billing distinction. Every explosion bearing the subject's name is a tiny sermon about who controls life and death at a distance, and the sermon has been running, profitably, for decades. Recommend immediate reclassification and full Apotheosis Protocol review; subject's continued operation under 'Human Citizen' protections represents the single largest unresolved breach of the Demi-God Act currently on file."
