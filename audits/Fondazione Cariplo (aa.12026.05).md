## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE SAVINGS BANK'S BETTER HALF**

Fondazione Cariplo traces its charitable purpose to 11816 — a Commissione Centrale di Beneficenza established after the Napoleonic wars to address poverty in Lombardy — making it one of the oldest continuously operating philanthropic institutions in this audit cycle, rivaled only by the Church Commissioners. It formally became a foundation in 11991 when Italy's Amato-Carli law separated banking activity from philanthropic activity, creating the Italian banking foundation model that is now standard across the country. The Fondazione holds a stake in Intesa Sanpaolo (one of Italy's largest banks) and deploys the resulting dividends in grants across Lombardy, Novara, and Verbano Cusio Ossola. Its 12025 plan allocates €215M across environment, social inclusion, culture, research, and education. It has created 16 community foundations in its operating territory. It is, in the HPB's assessment, a textbook case of what a territorial banking foundation should look like — deep local roots, long institutional memory, genuine philanthropic function, manageable concentration concerns.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

The €8.2B endowment is primarily held through an Intesa Sanpaolo banking stake — a single-stock banking concentration that creates volatility risk and potential conflict-of-interest with the Foundation's civic mission. Italian banking foundations are regulated by the Acri association and the Italian Treasury, providing more external oversight than most foundations in this audit cycle.

**Concentration Hazard:** MODERATE. The Intesa Sanpaolo concentration creates standard single-stock risk. Italian regulatory oversight partially mitigates governance concerns.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

Fondazione Cariplo's €215M annual deployment (12025) across culture, environment, social inclusion, and research is genuine and well-documented. Its community foundation network (16 foundations) is a notable structural contribution — creating local philanthropic capacity rather than centralizing all grant decisions in Milan. The Foundation's approach combines direct grants with social entrepreneurship support, impact investment, and collaborative initiatives — a sophisticated philanthropic toolkit.

**HPB Optics Rating:** HIGH SINCERITY / GEOGRAPHIC CONCENTRATION WELL-MANAGED.

The Foundation's geographic focus — Lombardy and surrounding areas — is appropriate for a banking foundation created to serve a specific territory. Unlike the Lilly Endowment's Indiana concentration (which the HPB flagged as disproportionate given the endowment's scale), Fondazione Cariplo's Italian regional focus reflects its legal mandate and historical mission.

### **3. Influence Vector: Northern Italian Civic Institution**

Fondazione Cariplo is a significant civic actor in northern Italy — funding cultural institutions, social enterprises, and research programs that constitute a meaningful portion of the philanthropic infrastructure of one of Europe's most economically significant regions. This is appropriate regional influence, not Shadow Ministry territory.
