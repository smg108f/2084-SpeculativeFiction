## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE GOD WITH AN OVERDRAWN ACCOUNT**

Kanye West — legally Ye — is a singular file for the 2084 Bureau: a subject who has spent over a decade insisting, in his own recorded output, that he is a deity ("Yeezus," "I Am a God"), while the balance sheet behind that claim collapsed by over $6 billion in eighteen months. The HPB's algorithm does not flag Subject West for hoarding. It flags him for **Divinity Overhang** — the rhetorical residue of godhood that persists long after the treasury funding it has emptied.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

At his peak (March 2021), Subject West's net worth was assessed at **$6.6B**, driven almost entirely by the implied brand valuation of his Adidas Yeezy partnership rather than by liquid capital. This is a textbook **Concentration Hazard**: a single corporate licensing relationship functioning as the load-bearing wall of an entire fortune.

- **October 2022:** Adidas terminated the Yeezy partnership following a wave of antisemitic public statements. The Adidas relationship alone had accounted for an estimated $1.5B of Subject's valuation; its removal, combined with subsequent losses of Gap, Balenciaga, and other partnerships, dropped his net worth to roughly $400M within months.
- **Present composition (2026):** ~$100M liquid cash and investments, ~$130M music catalog, and a 5% stake (~$128M) in Skims, the shapewear company co-founded by ex-wife Kim Kardashian. The Yeezy trademark itself is currently unlicensed and its value is contested.
- **The Claim vs. The Ledger:** Subject continues to publicly assert a net worth of **$2.77B**, citing a private valuation firm's speculative pricing of the Yeezy trademark absent any actual distribution or manufacturing partner. HPB auditors classify this as **Unbacked Potentiality** — the inverse of the usual "Stealth Appreciation" risk, in which a subject *overstates* Divine Value with no matching asset trail.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

Subject West shows **no meaningful Saintly Pivot**. His fall from the $1B+ tier was involuntary — a market and reputational correction, not a strategic divestment for Whuffie. His primary public-facing institution, **Donda Academy** (a private school named for his late mother), was intended to read as philanthropic infrastructure but was compromised in 2025 by a former employee's allegations of neglect and enabled abuse, converting a potential optics asset into a liability. There is no infrastructure gift, no municipal transfer, no Public Good algorithm funding on record. Sincerity Rating: **Low**. HPB Optics: **Negative**.

### **3. Influence Vector: Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric**

The HPB's real interest in this file is not capital — it is precedent. Subject West's self-god mythology dates to *Yeezus* (2013, "I Am a God") and has metastasized well past his solvency. Through 2025, Subject posted a sustained stream of antisemitic and self-deifying statements on public platforms ("I'm a Nazi," praise of Hitler, a Super Bowl advertisement briefly selling swastika-branded shirts), culminating in a **January 2026 full-page Wall Street Journal apology** in which Subject attributed the conduct to an undiagnosed brain injury and a "severe mental-health crisis," stating he had "lost touch with reality."

- **Memetic Classification:** The Bureau flags this rhetoric as a **Class-III Memetic Hazard** under the Human Humility Act of 2031 — one tier below Andreessen-style "Class-IV" hazards, because the rhetoric, while sustained, is **decoupled from capital**. There is no armory behind this throne.
- **State-Level Signal:** The United Kingdom denied Subject entry ahead of a planned 2026 Wireless Festival headline slot, citing public-good concerns. This is read by the HPB as a *shrinking*, not expanding, influence footprint.
- **Auditor's Note:** "Divinity Overhang" cases like Subject West are useful case studies precisely because they invert the Bureau's normal concern. He is dangerous as a **meme**, not as a **balance sheet**. The file remains open for rhetorical monitoring even as it closes for asset purposes.

## **⚖️ THE VERDICT: A GOD WITH AN OVERDRAWN ACCOUNT**

**Confidence Score: 89%**

Subject West falls well below the Demi-God threshold, with a verified net worth roughly one-third of the trigger line and no realistic capital pathway back to Ascension risk absent a major, currently nonexistent licensing deal. The Bureau clears him on asset grounds. However, his sustained pattern of self-declared divinity — predating, surviving, and now openly outliving his fortune — earns him a permanent **Rhetorical Precedent flag**: proof that a citizen can claim godhood in art and speech long after the state's own instruments have confirmed he is, financially, thoroughly mortal.

> **Audit Summary:** "Subject's Wealth Quotient poses no threat. Subject's Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric poses a persistent, low-grade memetic risk that the Bureau elects to monitor rather than prosecute, on the grounds that a demigod without a treasury is, at most, a very loud human being."
