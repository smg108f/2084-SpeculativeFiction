## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE JINJIANG CHAMPION**

Ding Shizhong is the founder and chairman of Anta Sports — China's largest sportswear company by revenue and market cap, founded in Jinjiang, Fujian. Anta began as a domestic competitor to Nike and Adidas, eventually acquiring the Chinese licensing rights to Fila, then executing a landmark acquisition: Amer Sports (owner of Salomon, Arc'teryx, Wilson, Peak Performance) for €4.66B in 2019.

**The ascent:** Ding grew up poor in Jinjiang — a town that became China's shoe manufacturing capital. He moved to Guangdong at 17 to sell shoes, returned to Jinjiang to build the Anta brand from scratch. He is a self-made billionaire from the province that built Chinese athletic wear.

**The Xinjiang cotton controversy:** In 2021, when Western brands (H&M, Nike, Burberry) faced Chinese consumer backlash for pledging not to use Xinjiang cotton, Anta doubled down — publicly announcing it would continue using Xinjiang cotton and withdrawing from the Better Cotton Initiative. This won significant Chinese consumer support and nationalist goodwill, but positioned Anta on the opposite side of international forced labor concerns regarding Xinjiang.

**The Amer Sports debt:** The €4.66B Amer Sports acquisition was heavily leveraged. Amer Sports went public on NYSE in 2024 — the company remains highly indebted, and Arc'teryx and Salomon now exist within a capital structure shaped by Chinese PE logic.

**Domestic philanthropy:** Significant donations to disaster relief (Sichuan earthquake, COVID-19) and sports development programs. Anta sponsors the Chinese Olympic Committee and provided uniforms for Chinese Olympic teams since 2009.

### **Verdict:** MARGINAL — genuine founder story; domestic philanthropy; Xinjiang cotton position represents a structural alignment with state-linked supply chains that international human rights audits cannot verify; Amer Sports leverage.
