## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE WORLD'S STRONGEST MAN (FINANCIALLY, IS FINE)**

The Bureau received Mr. Satan's name as part of the current Jump Universe audit cycle. Upon reviewing Subject's disclosed financial holdings — sports marketing revenue, peace prize receipts, and Satan City municipal operations, offset by the documented high operating costs of the latter — the Bureau's Asset Valuation Division returned a RTWI of $666 million, which is $334 million below the Demi-God Act's $1 billion Ascension threshold. Mr. Satan does not meet the threshold. Mr. Satan is not an Ascension risk.

The Bureau is filing this audit anyway, because the Bureau files audits for all subjects identified in the annual cycle, including those who clear the threshold the wrong way.

The Bureau wishes to be direct: Mr. Satan is a World Martial Arts Champion whose public fame substantially exceeds his financial holdings, who operates a city named after himself at a documented net cost to his personal finances, and who has — through what the Bureau's Intelligence Division can only classify as an extraordinary interpersonal capability — formed a genuine friendship with a being whose documented destructive capacity qualifies it for the highest Ascension classification the Bureau has on record. The Bureau does not know exactly what to do with this information. The Bureau is noting it.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

Subject's $666 million RTWI is derived from: career earnings in professional martial arts competition; personal appearance fees; branded merchandise and endorsement income; and peace prize receipts from multiple international bodies, which the Bureau notes is an unusual income source for a sports-marketing figure and reflects the scope of Subject's public credit for outcomes the Bureau's Intelligence Division attributes, in primary causal terms, to other parties.

**Concentration Hazard — None Identified:** Subject's wealth does not exceed the threshold. The Bureau's Asset Valuation Division has confirmed this calculation three times, including once after a junior analyst suggested rounding up the peace prize receipts. The Bureau did not round up. $666 million is $666 million. The threshold is $1 billion. Subject clears the threshold by $334 million in the correct direction.

**Note of Record — Satan City Operating Costs:** The Bureau's Municipal Finance Division notes that Subject funds the operation of Satan City through a combination of municipal revenue and personal subsidy, and that the personal subsidy component has, in multiple documented fiscal years, substantially reduced Subject's liquid assets. Mr. Satan appears to be the only subject in the Jump Universe audit cycle who is actively spending his own money to operate a municipality for the benefit of its residents, at a personal financial cost, because he considers this his responsibility. The Bureau is noting this because it is unusual and because the Bureau's analysts felt it should be on record.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

The Saintly Pivot analysis is, by the Act's standards, designed to assess whether a subject's philanthropic record represents genuine public benefit or strategic wealth protection. For Subject, the question is whether his public benefit activities are genuine. The Bureau's Philanthropy Review Division has conducted this review and reached the following conclusion: Mr. Satan does not have enough money to have a strategic Saintly Pivot. His charitable and municipal expenditures are funded from a personal fortune that, at $666 million, does not leave significant margin for strategic reputation management. The Satan City subsidy is, by the Bureau's assessment, a man spending his own money to take care of people he feels responsible for.

HPB Optics Rating: **GENUINE — No Strategic Motivation Identifiable; Subject Does Not Have Enough Money For Strategic Philanthropy.**

### **3. Influence Vector: The Thing About the Majin Buu Situation**

The Bureau's Intelligence Division has flagged one item from Mr. Satan's record that does not fit the standard audit framework and that the Bureau is including in a separate section because it represents the most unusual concentration of informal capability in the Jump Universe audit queue.

Mr. Satan formed a personal friendship with Majin Buu — a being the Bureau's Threat Assessment Division classifies as the highest individual destructive capability on record in the Jump Universe, exceeding Frieza's documented operational capacity at comparable mobilization levels. This friendship was not the result of Subject's martial capability, financial resources, or institutional relationships. It was the result of Subject's interpersonal warmth, his genuine care for an entity that had been designated universally hostile, and his decision to keep showing up even when showing up was dangerous.

The Bureau does not know how to classify this. The Bureau's Threat Assessment Division notes that Majin Buu's domestication from Class-Omega threat to integrated community member is, from a purely actuarial standpoint, one of the most significant risk-reduction events in the Jump Universe audit cycle — and that Subject was the primary mechanism through which it occurred. The Bureau's Intelligence Division notes that Subject did not achieve this through capability or leverage but through showing up with food and treating Buu like a person worth caring about.

The Bureau is noting this because the Bureau does not have a category for it. The Bureau is creating one: **Interpersonal Threat Mitigation (Non-Coercive) — Class One.** It will be used only for Mr. Satan, because the Bureau does not expect to encounter this again.
