The Bureau's Real Asset Registry considers Brookfield's model uniquely concerning precisely because its wealth is not paper — it is physical infrastructure that ordinary people interact with daily, often without knowing who ultimately owns it. Brookfield's aggressive push into AI-data-center infrastructure financing has only sharpened the Bureau's interest.

Brookfield's market capitalization reflects both its direct corporate holdings and its role as general partner across a sprawling network of managed funds representing trillions in assets under management — a structure that makes its true wealth reach far exceed its nominal balance sheet.

### **2. Saintly Pivot**

### **3. Influence Vector**
