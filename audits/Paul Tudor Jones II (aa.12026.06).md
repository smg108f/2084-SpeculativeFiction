## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE MACRO TRADER WITH A CONSCIENCE**

Paul Tudor Jones II is one of the most celebrated macro hedge fund managers in American finance. He famously predicted and profited from the 1987 Black Monday crash — shorting the market before the collapse and reportedly tripling his money. Tudor Investment Corp has managed billions in global macro strategies for decades.

**The Robin Hood Foundation:** Jones co-founded the Robin Hood Foundation in 1988 — one of New York City's largest anti-poverty organizations, which has distributed over $3B to New York City poverty-fighting organizations over its history. Jones has been the driving force behind Robin Hood and has committed substantial personal resources to it. This is genuine, sustained, impactful philanthropy at a scale that the Bureau records affirmatively.

**JUST Capital:** Jones co-founded JUST Capital — an organization that ranks large American corporations on worker, community, and environmental metrics. JUST Capital's rankings have been used by investors and have put pressure on corporations to improve labor and environmental practices. This is a notable attempt to use capital market mechanisms for social accountability.

**The 1992 controversy:** In 1992, a documentary ("Trader") showed Jones making dismissive comments about women's capacity for trading, attributing PMS to trading losses. Jones later apologized and requested the film not be distributed; it has been largely suppressed. The Bureau notes the incident as historical context.
