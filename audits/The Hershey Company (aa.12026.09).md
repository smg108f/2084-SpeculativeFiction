## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE CHOCOLATE COMPANY OWNED BY AN ORPHANAGE'S TRUST**

The Hershey Company remains controlled, through supervoting Class B shares, by the Hershey Trust Company — established by founder Milton Hershey to fund the Milton Hershey School, a cost-free residential school originally for orphaned children and now serving lower-income students broadly. The Bureau finds this among the more genuinely unusual ownership structures on file: over a century later, chocolate profits still flow substantially toward the school's endowment rather than any living Hershey descendant.

### **1. WEALTH QUOTIENT**
No individual heir controls a disqualifying personal stake; the controlling Trust exists to fund the Milton Hershey School rather than enrich any family member.

### **2. SAINTLY PIVOT**
As with Tata Motors and Titan Company, the "saintly" framing here is structurally embedded rather than a public-relations overlay — a century-old commitment, not a recent rebrand.

### **3. INFLUENCE VECTOR**
As a dominant confectionery brand, Hershey shapes American candy culture broadly, though ownership dilutes personal accountability by design in favor of the Trust's educational mission.
