## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE BANKER'S BANKER**

Uday Kotak founded Kotak Mahindra Finance in 1985 with a bill discounting business and grew it into Kotak Mahindra Bank — today one of India's largest and most consistently profitable private sector banks. Kotak Mahindra Bank is known for conservative lending, strong asset quality, and relatively low NPAs (non-performing assets) compared to Indian banking peers. Uday Kotak stepped down as MD & CEO in 2023 after a standoff with the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) over shareholding norms — the RBI required him to reduce his personal stake in the bank below a certain threshold, and the process of compliance was contentious. He remains a significant shareholder and non-executive director.

Kotak has been a prominent voice on Indian corporate governance — he chaired SEBI's Committee on Corporate Governance (2017-18) and produced the Kotak Committee Report, which led to significant improvements in Indian listed company governance standards.
