## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE ENTERPRISE HEIRS**

Dannine Avara is one of four children of Dan Duncan — the Texas pipeline entrepreneur who co-founded Enterprise Products Partners, one of North America's largest natural gas pipeline and midstream energy companies, with over 50,000 miles of pipeline and related infrastructure. Dan Duncan died in 2010; his estate benefited from the temporary estate tax repeal, transferring approximately $21.5B to his heirs without estate tax — the largest untaxed estate transfer in American history at that time.

**Enterprise Products accountability:** Enterprise Products Partners is midstream fossil fuel infrastructure at massive scale — natural gas pipelines, NGL fractionators, storage facilities, and marine terminals. Its infrastructure connects shale gas production (Permian Basin, Appalachian) to domestic and export markets, making it a material enabler of continued natural gas production.

**The untaxed estate:** The 2010 estate tax repeal (part of the Bush tax cuts sunset) meant that Dan Duncan's $21.5B estate transferred to his children with zero federal estate tax — depriving the US government of an estimated $4-8B in revenue depending on applicable rates. This was not illegal; it was the product of a political decision. But it is the single largest windfall from the estate tax repeal in American history.

**Philanthropic record:** The Duncan heirs have donated to Texas civic institutions. Dannine Avara specifically has donated to Houston-area causes.
