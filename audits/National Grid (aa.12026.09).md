## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE WIRES BETWEEN GODS**

National Grid plc is the literal infrastructure carrying electricity to thirty million British and American customers, currently mid-CEO-succession and mid-£60 billion grid modernization plan. It is widely held, professionally managed, and refreshingly free of a single founder's face on the letterhead. The Bureau classifies it as an Ascension Enabler: it does not ascend, but it wires the current for everyone who does.

### **2. SAINTLY PIVOT**

### **3. INFLUENCE VECTOR**
