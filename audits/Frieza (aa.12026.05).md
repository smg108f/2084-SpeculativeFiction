## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE EMPEROR WITHOUT A PLANET**

Frieza presents a challenge that the Human Protection Bureau was not, strictly speaking, designed to handle. The Demi-God Act of 2034 was written to address Earth-based wealth concentration. Frieza's wealth is Earth-*adjacent* at best — his holdings span multiple galaxies, his enforcement apparatus is an interstellar army, and his primary revenue stream is the wholesale acquisition, improvement, and resale of habitable planets. The HPB has nonetheless accepted jurisdiction on the grounds that several of Frieza's properties are within the Sol system's economic exclusion zone and that Frieza has, on multiple documented occasions, personally visited Earth and treated it as a line item in a portfolio.

The core problem is not that Frieza is wealthy beyond any precedent in HPB case history. The core problem is that Frieza is *already* something the Demi-God Act was designed to prevent: a sovereign entity with near-total control over the conditions under which other beings live, die, and reproduce. The HPB's role is to stop people from becoming gods. Frieza arrived that way.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

Frieza's net worth is catalogued at a conservative floor of **$666 quadrillion**, derived from the estimated market value of the Planet Trade Organization's known planetary portfolio at standard appraisal rates. This figure does not include: (a) planets currently under active terraforming that have not yet been listed; (b) the value of Frieza's personal military force, estimated at several hundred thousand soldiers plus capital-class warships; (c) the Frieza Force's ongoing revenue from "clearance operations," in which subject's forces exterminate native populations to improve a planet's resale condition; or (d) Frieza's personal biological assets (see Section 3).

**Concentration Hazard — Class Omega (Novel Category, First Instance):** The HPB has never previously audited an entity whose primary asset class is *real estate measured in astronomical units.* Frieza does not merely exceed the $1B Ascension threshold — he exceeds it by a factor of approximately **666,000,000.** For reference, the largest previous audit (Elon Musk, 12026.05) recorded a CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK at approximately $790B. Frieza's floor estimate is 842 million times larger. The HPB's standard comparative metrics table has required the addition of a new column.

**Concentration Hazard — Class I (Jurisdictional Ambiguity):** Frieza's legal team — operating out of a registered entity in an Ascension Haven with no extradition treaty — has filed a preliminary objection arguing that the Demi-God Act applies only to beings "whose primary domicile and primary economic activity are conducted within the territorial boundaries of signatory nations." The HPB's Office of Galactic Jurisdiction (established 12024, staff of three) is reviewing this claim. The HPB notes that it finds the argument unpersuasive and intends to proceed regardless.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

There is no Saintly Pivot. There has never been a Saintly Pivot. The concept of a Saintly Pivot requires that the subject value the goodwill of the people whose welfare they are affecting. Frieza does not. Subject has publicly and repeatedly described the populations of planets under his control as "product inventory." His only known act of public generosity on record — sparing the Saiyan population of Planet Vegeta for several decades of service — was terminated when he destroyed the planet to prevent a prophesied uprising against him.

HPB Optics Rating: **ABSOLUTE ZERO.** Subject does not perform generosity. Subject has not demonstrated awareness that generosity is a concept applicable to his behavior.

### **3. Influence Vector: Biological Transformation as Exit Mechanism**

The HPB's standard Exit Readiness metric asks whether a subject has prepared a "Flotilla" — a physical escape infrastructure allowing them to vacate Earth's legal jurisdiction. Frieza's case renders this metric almost quaint. Subject does not require a Flotilla. He is his own spacecraft. His biology includes the capacity for multiple transformation states of increasing power, survival in the vacuum of space without life support equipment, and documented recovery from near-total physical destruction. He was killed, reduced to component parts, reconstituted in a cybernetic chassis, and returned to active operation — all before the HPB's predecessor agency was founded.

**Class-V Biological Hazard (Novel Category):** The Human Protection Bureau was designed to regulate *human* wealth. Subject is not human. His capacity for physical regeneration and transformation means that even if the HPB were to successfully seize all of his assets, it would have no mechanism to prevent him from beginning the asset accumulation process again. He has done exactly this, having rebuilt the Planet Trade Organization from nothing following its destruction by Subject Son Goku (filed separately under Slayer Records). 

The HPB notes, with bureaucratic discomfort, that Subject has achieved literal immortality through a Dragon Ball wish (see *In re: Namekian Dragon Balls, HPB Advisory 12021.07*), which the HPB's Asset Valuation Division has classified as a **"Potentiality Value — Infinite"** event. An immortal galactic emperor with a self-replenishing planetary portfolio constitutes the first recorded **Class-Omega Ascension case** in HPB history.

## **⚖️ THE VERDICT: THE ACT DID NOT ANTICIPATE THIS**

**Confidence Score: 100%**

Frieza is not a Demi-God candidate. He is a Demi-God — and has been for longer than the Human Protection Bureau has existed, longer than the Demi-God Act has existed, and arguably longer than the concept of legal personhood has existed on Earth. The HPB's jurisdiction in this matter is contested, its enforcement capacity is nonexistent, and its auditors required three separate threat-level classification upgrades before a final assessment could be reached. None of this changes the audit outcome. The purpose of this document is not to suggest that the HPB can contain Frieza. The purpose of this document is to state, for the record, that the HPB has tried.

> **Audit Summary:** "Subject Frieza represents an Ascension case of a type the Act did not contemplate and the Bureau cannot resolve through standard enforcement channels. He exceeds every threshold by factors that require scientific notation. His Saintly Pivot record is not merely blank — the concept of a Saintly Pivot requires that the subject acknowledge the personhood of those beneath them, which Subject has not demonstrated in any documented interaction. The HPB formally classifies Frieza as a **Class-Omega Demi-God Entity** and refers the matter to the Office of Galactic Jurisdiction, the United Nations Extraterrestrial Sovereignty Committee, and whoever is currently capable of defeating him in direct combat."
