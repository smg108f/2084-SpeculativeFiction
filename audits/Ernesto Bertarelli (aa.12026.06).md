## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE OCEAN GUARDIAN**

Ernesto Bertarelli is the third-generation heir to Serono S.A. — the Swiss biopharmaceutical company founded by his grandfather in 1906 and built by his father Fabio into one of the world's leading biotech firms, with major products including the fertility treatment Gonal-F and MS therapy Rebif. In 2007, Bertarelli sold Serono to Merck KGaA (Germany) for $13.3B — a landmark European pharma transaction.

Since the sale, Bertarelli has deployed the proceeds through Waypoint Capital (family office) and, most significantly, the **Bertarelli Foundation**. The Foundation's signature work: ocean conservation.

**Documented ocean conservation impact:**
- Co-founded the Global Ocean Legacy project with the Pew Charitable Trusts — a partnership that has protected millions of square kilometers of ocean through marine protected area (MPA) designation globally;
- Instrumental in the designation of some of the world's largest MPAs, including Chagos/BIOT (UK), Pitcairn Islands (UK), and several Pacific Island MPAs — combined totaling millions of square kilometers;
- Bertarelli Foundation Marine Science Programme — funding scientific research at multiple universities on marine ecosystem health, plastic pollution, and fisheries management;
- Personal advocacy with UK and other governments for MPA designation.

The scale and quality of Bertarelli's ocean conservation philanthropy is among the most substantive in the world at this wealth level. The HPB identifies few billionaires who have contributed more meaningfully to a specific, measurable global public good relative to their wealth.

**Other notes:** Bertarelli won the America's Cup in 2003 and 2007 with Alinghi (representing Switzerland — the first landlocked nation to win the Cup). The 2010 America's Cup legal dispute with Larry Ellison (Oracle) was notoriously litigious and resulted in the DoG (Deed of Gift) match that Ellison won. Bertarelli is known for sailing with intensity.
