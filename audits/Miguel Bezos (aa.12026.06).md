### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

Miguel Bezos did not accumulate $30B. He received it, or something approaching it, from his son. He immigrated to the United States at 16 with very little, worked as a petroleum engineer, raised three children, and is now wealthy because his son became the richest person in the history of the world. **Concentration Hazard: NONE attributable to subject.** The HPB cannot hold a father responsible for his son's accumulation.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

Miguel and Jacklyn Bezos have donated to education, immigration advocacy, and early childhood development. The Bezos Family Foundation — which Miguel and Jacklyn run, distinct from Jeff's Bezos Earth Fund — focuses on learning science and early childhood education. **Saintly Pivot: ACTIVE AND SUBSTANTIVE.** The HPB considers the foundation's focus — early childhood education and learning equity — among the more directly meaningful charitable programs in this audit cycle.

### **3. Influence Vector: The Father's Capital**

The $250,000 seed investment Miguel provided to Amazon in 1994 is, in retrospect, one of the highest-return philanthropic acts in economic history — converting middle-class savings into the world's most transformative retail company. The HPB cannot assess whether Miguel knew what he was doing. The HPB suspects he did not. He trusted his son.
