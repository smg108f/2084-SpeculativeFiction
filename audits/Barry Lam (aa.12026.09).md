## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE MAN WHO BUILDS EVERYONE ELSE'S LAPTOPS**

Lam built Quanta Computer into the world's dominant contract manufacturer of laptops, assembling devices sold under other companies' brand names at a scale that makes Quanta functionally invisible to the consumers who use its products daily. The Bureau's concern is geopolitical as much as economic: Quanta's manufacturing base sits within Taiwan's broader technology supply chain, a region the Bureau's models flag as a potential single point of failure for global computing infrastructure.
