## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE WOMAN BEHIND THE GLASS**

Wang Laichun is the founder and chairwoman of Lens Technology — one of the world's leading manufacturers of precision glass for consumer electronics. Lens Technology makes the cover glass (sapphire and Gorilla Glass-equivalent materials) for iPhone screens, Apple Watch faces, and Samsung displays. It is Apple's primary glass supplier.

**Self-made founder:** Wang Laichun started working in a watch glass factory at age 16 to support her family, eventually founding Lens Technology in 2001 in Changsha, Hunan. She is one of the most remarkable self-made women in Chinese manufacturing history — building from factory worker to CEO of an Apple critical supplier.

**Labor conditions and the Apple supply chain:** Lens Technology has appeared in Apple supplier responsibility reports, with documented labor issues including excessive overtime hours at facilities. The pressures imposed by Apple's pricing and delivery requirements on suppliers create structural conditions where worker protections are difficult to maintain — a systemic problem in the Apple supply chain rather than uniquely Lens Technology's.

**Philanthropy:** Wang Laichun has donated to education and disaster relief in Hunan province.

**Environmental:** Glass manufacturing is energy-intensive; Lens Technology has made commitments to renewable energy at its facilities.
