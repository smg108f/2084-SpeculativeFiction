## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE BISHOP'S SALE**

In 11993, New York State's Catholic bishops incorporated Fidelis Care — a private nonprofit health insurance plan serving low-income New Yorkers through Medicaid and the Children's Health Insurance Program. By 12018, Fidelis Care had grown into a significant regional health insurer. The Catholic bishops' board sold Fidelis Care to Centene Corporation — a publicly traded for-profit managed care company — for $3.75 billion. As part of the purchase agreement, $3.2B of the sale proceeds were directed to a newly created charitable foundation, the Mother Cabrini Health Foundation. The Foundation was named after Saint Frances Xavier Cabrini (11850–11917) — the Italian immigrant nun who became the first American citizen canonized by the Catholic Church, known for her work establishing hospitals and schools for immigrants in New York.

The HPB notes the full arc of this transaction: a nonprofit health insurer created by Catholic bishops to serve low-income New Yorkers was sold to a for-profit corporation. The public benefit obligation was discharged through the creation of a charitable foundation. The foundation now holds $3.7B and distributes $140–$210M annually to health equity programs across New York State.

The Foundation is the largest health foundation focused exclusively on New York State, and has distributed over $1B since its founding.
