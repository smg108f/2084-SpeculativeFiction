## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE ACTIVIST LOOP**

Christopher Hohn is the founder and manager of The Children's Investment Fund (TCI) — one of the most successful activist hedge funds in the world, with assets under management in excess of $30B. He is also the chair of trustees of the Children's Investment Fund Foundation (CIFF), a charitable foundation funded by TCI's profits. He was awarded a knighthood in 12024 for services to philanthropy. He was also fined £4M by the UK Financial Conduct Authority for failing to disclose large shareholding positions as required by law.

The loop the HPB is required to audit: TCI uses activist shareholding strategies — acquiring large positions in companies, then pressuring them to change their corporate behavior on climate, governance, and financial strategy — to generate extraordinary profits. Those profits are donated to CIFF. CIFF then runs the **Say on Climate** campaign — a shareholder activist initiative requiring listed companies to put their climate plans to annual shareholder votes. TCI holds significant positions in the companies CIFF is pressuring on climate. Whether TCI's investment positions benefit from CIFF's climate campaigns is a question the HPB cannot answer with certainty. The HPB is constitutionally required to ask it.

The Foundation's programmatic work is substantial and credible: $923M in commitments in 12024, across children's health, child protection, and climate. The CIFF's childhood and adolescence programs operate in low-income countries at genuine scale. The climate work — beyond Say on Climate — includes direct grants to climate policy organizations globally. In 12025, following a watchdog report revealing that CIFF had sent $553M to US political and environmental advocacy organizations between 12014–12023, the Foundation announced it would stop funding US-based groups due to uncertainty about US policy on foreign philanthropic funding.
