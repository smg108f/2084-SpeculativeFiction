## **AUDIT SUMMARY: ONE OF TWO COMPANIES THAT DECIDE WHAT CHINA PLAYS**

NetEase, alongside Tencent, controls nearly 70% of China's gaming industry revenue, giving the two companies together an outsized role in shaping the entertainment and attention economy for one of the world's largest gaming populations. A 69% Q1 2026 revenue surge, moderating somewhat in Q2, confirms the segment's continued growth even as Chinese gaming regulation and an accelerating AI product push create structural headwinds and opportunities simultaneously.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

NetEase's wealth is attention-capture-based, monetizing engagement at scale across a gaming duopoly that leaves Chinese consumers functionally limited to two major publishers for the bulk of domestic gaming content, a concentration pattern the Bureau finds structurally similar to other duopoly files this cycle (see also: CSX).

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

NetEase has implemented Chinese state-mandated youth playtime restrictions and spending limits. The Bureau rates this **Tier 4 (State-Compelled, Not Voluntary)**: real protections for minors, imposed by regulation rather than chosen by the company.

### **3. Influence Vector: The Duopoly's Grip on Attention**

With Tencent, NetEase effectively sets the terms of engagement for Chinese gaming culture, shaping monetization mechanics (loot boxes, gacha systems) that regulators worldwide have scrutinized for their resemblance to gambling mechanics targeting engaged, sometimes vulnerable, players.
