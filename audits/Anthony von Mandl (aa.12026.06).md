## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE CLAW THAT BROKE THE BEER MARKET**

Anthony von Mandl built what began as a BC wine distribution business into one of the most consequential alcohol brand portfolios of the early 21st century. The Mark Anthony Group distributed wines and spirits in Canada for decades. Then, in 2016, von Mandl launched White Claw Hard Seltzer — and changed the US alcohol industry.

**White Claw:** By 2019, White Claw had outsold all craft beer brands in the United States combined. The brand popularized the hard seltzer category, pioneered RTD (ready-to-drink) alcoholic beverages as a distinct product category, and created a cultural moment ("ain't no laws when you're drinking Claws") that collapsed alcohol demographics in ways that concerned public health researchers. The product was specifically marketed and positioned to reach younger adult drinkers and to feel lighter, more approachable, and more lifestyle-adjacent than traditional alcohol.

**The RTD concern:** Hard seltzer and RTD beverages at industrial scale have been associated with: (a) lower awareness of alcohol content per unit due to the "light" branding; (b) youth-adjacent marketing aesthetics; (c) aggressive distribution that increased alcohol availability in formats designed to feel casual and low-stakes.

**Mission Hill Winery:** A genuine contribution to BC viticulture — Mission Hill in the Okanagan Valley is considered among Canada's finest wineries, and von Mandl has invested significantly in the Okanagan as a wine region. This is a legitimate credit.

**Philanthropy:** von Mandl has been a supporter of BC arts and cultural institutions, and Mission Hill hosts cultural events. The scale is modest relative to net worth.

No documented labor violations, corruption, or financial misconduct.
