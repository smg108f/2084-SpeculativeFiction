## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE FURNITURE IN EVERY LIVING ROOM**

Wanek built Ashley Furniture from a single Wisconsin factory into the world's largest furniture manufacturer, a vertically-integrated operation now run alongside his son as a private, family-controlled company. The Bureau's file follows the now-familiar "ubiquity without visibility" pattern: a product in millions of homes, a name most of those homes would not recognize.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

Held in privately-controlled Ashley Furniture Industries equity, vertically integrated from manufacturing to retail. **Concentration Hazard: MODERATE.** Genuine operating business rather than passive holding.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

Regional Wisconsin manufacturing employment and standard corporate philanthropy. **HPB Optics Rating: MODERATE.** Family has maintained domestic manufacturing employment at a scale increasingly rare in the furniture industry.

### **3. Influence Vector: The Last Domestic Manufacturer Standing**

The Bureau notes a mild positive factor rarely seen in this audit cycle: continued investment in domestic manufacturing capacity, rather than full offshoring, in an industry where most competitors have moved production abroad.
