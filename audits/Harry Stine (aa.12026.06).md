## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE SEED MAN OF IOWA**

Harry Stine is the founder of Stine Seed Company — an independent seed company that has developed some of the most widely planted soybean and corn genetic lines in North American agriculture. Based in Adel, Iowa, Stine has licensed its seed genetics to major companies including DuPont/Pioneer and has built one of the most successful independent agricultural genetics businesses in American history.

**The seed genetics economy:** Seed genetics is a critical but poorly understood part of the food system. Companies like Stine (and Monsanto/Bayer, Corteva, Syngenta) control the genetic material that determines crop yields, pest resistance, and herbicide compatibility for the crops that feed America and much of the world. Concentration in this sector raises food security and farmer choice concerns.

**Independent vs. consolidating sector:** Unlike the major seed companies that have consolidated into three or four global players, Stine has remained independent — providing farmers with a genuine alternative and applying competitive pressure on Bayer, Corteva, and Syngenta. This independence is a genuine structural positive.

**Farming community:** Stine employs thousands of seed corn detasselers (predominantly teens and young people in Iowa) in seasonal work — a traditional part of Iowa's agricultural employment landscape.

**Philanthropy:** Limited documented individual philanthropy at scale relative to wealth.

### **Verdict:** MARGINAL — agricultural genetics with seed concentration concerns; independence from the consolidating seed giants is a genuine structural positive; philanthropy below scale.
