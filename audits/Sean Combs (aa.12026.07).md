## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE MOGUL THE COURTS CAUGHT FIRST**

Sean Combs is the HPB's most unusual case file: a man who briefly crossed the Divine Threshold years before this audit was ever opened, and who was returned to Mortal Status not by an Ascension Summons, a Burning Bush Event, or a Saintly Pivot — but by a federal jury in the Southern District of New York. By the time the Bureau's retroactive review reached him, the work was already done. This audit exists mainly to confirm the obvious and to document, for the historical record, a rare case where the pre-2034 legal apparatus outran the HPB entirely.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

At his 2022 peak, Forbes assessed Combs' holdings — the Bad Boy Records catalog and publishing rights, a 50% stake in the Cîroc/DeLeón spirits partnership, Sean John apparel, Revolt Media, real estate across Holmby Hills, Star Island, and the Hamptons, and a diversified portfolio — at roughly $1B, nominally crossing the Divine Threshold. That figure has since collapsed by more than half. The January 2024 Diageo settlement stripped him of all ownership in Cîroc and DeLeón with no disclosed payout; Revolt Media was sold off in June 2024; the Holmby Hills estate was already offloaded prior to the scandal cascade. What remains is concentrated in the Bad Boy master catalog and publishing rights — an asset class the HPB flags as vulnerable to "Stealth Appreciation" (see Section 3).

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

Combs has a genuine, if complicated, philanthropic record predating his legal collapse: a $1M pledge to Howard University, a $100M pledge across HBCUs announced in 2016, and the founding of Capital Preparatory Charter Schools, a network that survives independently of his current legal status. The Bureau declines to classify any of this as a calculated Saintly Pivot in the 2084 sense — it predates the audit window and was never leveraged as an Ascension defense. It is, however, the one asset category the courts have not touched, and the HPB notes it as the closest thing to a redeeming line item in an otherwise grim file.

### **3. Influence Vector: The "Scandal Economy" Rebound Risk**

The core hazard here isn't present wealth — it's future wealth. Combs' catalog and public narrative are subject to the same "Potentiality Value" the HPB's AI flags in patent holders and IP owners: true-crime documentaries, streaming spikes, and post-incarceration media rights all represent latent appreciation vectors. Auditors project that upon his scheduled 2028 release, catalog royalties and rights licensing tied to renewed public notoriety could meaningfully rebuild his position — not to $1B, but enough to warrant a follow-up audit rather than a closed file.
