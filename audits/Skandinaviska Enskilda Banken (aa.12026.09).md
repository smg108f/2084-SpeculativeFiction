## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE FIRST WALLENBERG FILE**

This is the Bureau's first formal audit of a company within the Wallenberg sphere — the century-old Swedish family network that, through Investor AB and a constellation of interlocking foundations, controls voting influence across a startling share of Scandinavian industry. SEB, founded by André Oscar Wallenberg in 1856, remains the sphere's original banking vehicle. No single living Wallenberg holds a majority stake; the control mechanism instead runs through dual-class shares and foundation-held blocks that the Bureau's analysts describe as "a dynasty operating through proxies it doesn't technically own." The Bureau has opened a standing docket to track every Wallenberg-sphere entity encountered going forward, cross-referenced against the earlier Schörling family (Assa Abloy, Hexagon) precedent as a second Nordic dynasty structure.
