## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE CHINESE CARMAKER WHO BOUGHT VOLVO**

Li Shufu (known in international contexts as Eric Li) founded Geely Automobile in 1986 and built it into China's largest privately held automaker. He is famous for purchasing Volvo Cars from Ford in 2010 for $1.8B — a transaction widely considered one of the most improbable acquisitions in automotive history, in which a Chinese billionaire with a factory known for making motorcycles bought one of Europe's most storied safety-focused car brands. The acquisition has been considered a success: Volvo returned to profitability and expanded its lineup significantly. Geely subsequently acquired controlling stakes in: London EV Company (black cabs), Lotus Cars, Proton (Malaysian automaker), Polestar (premium EV brand), and ZEEKR (Chinese luxury EV). Li also acquired a significant stake in Daimler (parent of Mercedes-Benz), becoming its largest single shareholder, in 2018 — though this stake has been subject to scrutiny from German regulators.
