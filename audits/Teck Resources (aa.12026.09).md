## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE FIRST DYNASTY THIS BUREAU HAS WATCHED WILLINGLY DISSOLVE ITSELF**

The Keevil family controlled Teck Resources for generations through super-voting Class A shares — a small economic stake commanding majority voting power, the same mechanism this Bureau has flagged repeatedly elsewhere. What makes this file unusual: in September 2025, Teck and Anglo American announced a shareholder-approved "merger of equals" to form "Anglo Teck," and Teck formally introduced a sunset of its own dual-class structure as part of the deal terms. As of this filing, roughly a year into that transition, Keevil family control is being voluntarily wound down — not by Bureau order, but by the family's own negotiated exit from a controlling position. The Bureau classifies this as still technically CRITICAL pending confirmation the sunset has fully completed, but flags it as the first instance in this project's history of a family dissolving its own control mechanism without any Burn order forcing the issue.

### **1. Wealth Quotient**

The Keevil family's Class A stake, historically commanding majority votes on a minority economic base, is actively being unwound under the merger's own terms rather than under Bureau compulsion.

### **2. Saintly Pivot**

Not formally qualifying for Probationary Saint status, but the voluntary nature of this dissolution is noted favorably in the Bureau's file — a rare instance of market forces accomplishing what a Burn order is designed to compel.

### **3. Influence Vector**

Control over major copper and zinc mining operations, feeding both traditional industrial demand and increasingly electrification/battery-metals markets, is being restructured into the larger Anglo Teck entity's more diffuse ownership base.
