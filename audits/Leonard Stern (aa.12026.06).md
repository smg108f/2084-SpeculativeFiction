## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE PET PRODUCTS AND PROPERTY PATRIARCH**

Leonard Stern is the son of Max Stern, who built Hartz Mountain Corporation — originally a canary-importing company that became America's largest pet products company. Leonard expanded Hartz's pet products business while also building a massive New York metropolitan area real estate portfolio that became the dominant component of the family's wealth.

**The Hartz pet products controversies:** Hartz Mountain pet products have faced significant consumer safety controversies, most notably the Hartz flea and tick treatments controversy — multiple reports over years of Hartz flea and tick products (particularly for cats) causing severe neurological reactions, seizures, and deaths in pets. The EPA received thousands of adverse event reports. Hartz reformulated some products and settled lawsuits, but the products remained on market for years while reports accumulated. This is a documented consumer safety failure at scale.

**New York real estate:** Stern's Hartz Mountain Industries (rebranded from pet products) became primarily a real estate company — one of the largest commercial and industrial property owners in New Jersey and the New York metro area. Real estate development in New York's constrained market raises standard housing affordability concerns.

**New York University:** Stern is a major NYU donor — the Stern School of Business is named for him following a $30M gift. He has also donated to other New York cultural institutions.

**Political:** Stern has been a political donor with influence in New York political circles.
