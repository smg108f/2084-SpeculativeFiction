## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE GURGAON ARCHITECT**

Kushal Pal Singh — K.P. Singh — is the chairman of DLF Limited, which his father-in-law Raghvendra Singh founded and which K.P. Singh transformed into India's largest listed real estate developer. DLF built Gurgaon — the satellite city adjacent to Delhi that became India's technology and financial services hub — constructing the majority of its commercial towers, shopping malls, and residential colonies. Without DLF, there is no Gurgaon as it exists.

**The political nexus:** DLF's rapid land acquisition and development in Haryana — the state where Gurgaon is located — during periods when Congress-affiliated governments were in power attracted significant scrutiny. The most documented controversy involves Robert Vadra — son-in-law of Sonia Gandhi and therefore connected to the Congress party — who purchased DLF land in Haryana at prices that investigators and opposition politicians alleged were favorable, in a transaction that suggested DLF may have received regulatory benefits in exchange. These transactions were investigated by Haryana's state government and various bodies; no criminal conviction has resulted, but the conflict of interest was extensively documented.

**Land acquisition practices:** DLF's broader Haryana land acquisition — assembling the large tracts needed for Gurgaon's development — occurred in a period when agricultural land acquired from farmers at government-set prices was rapidly converted to commercial use at much higher valuations.
