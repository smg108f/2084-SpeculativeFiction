## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE UNDIVIDED CONGREGATION**

Subject presents the Human Protection Bureau with its first genuinely novel asset class: a following. Cristiano Ronaldo's conventional finances — a record-setting Saudi Pro League contract, a lifetime Nike deal, a hospitality-and-wellness brand empire — sit in a contested band that some trackers place just under the Demi-God threshold and others place just over it. What is not contested is that Subject commands the single largest personal, undivided audience of any living human being on Earth, and that this audience is, by documented real-world market rate, worth real money per post. This file asks a question the Bureau's founders never anticipated having to ask a professional athlete: is a billion people paying attention to one man, by itself, an asset the state should be measuring?

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

**Part A — Conventional Holdings.** Subject's documented wealth is concentrated in three places. First, footballing income: a 2025 contract extension with Al-Nassr FC reportedly worth $400M–$620M over two years — inclusive of base salary (approximately $200M–$235M annually, the highest of any footballer in history), performance bonuses, and, per multiple reports the Bureau treats as credible but unconfirmed, a stake of up to 15% in the club itself. Second, endorsement equity: a 2016 lifetime agreement with Nike reported to exceed $1B in total nominal value, including a $100M signing bonus, alongside smaller deals across other categories. Third, the **CR7 empire**: the Pestana CR7 hotel chain (New York, Madrid, Lisbon, Marrakech), CR7 Crunch Fitness gyms, the Insparya hair-restoration clinic network, and CR7-branded fragrances, apparel, and footwear. Net-worth trackers disagree sharply on how to total this: conservative estimates that count only banked salary, cashed-out endorsement income, and realized business equity land near **$800M**; more aggressive estimates that count the full multi-year value of the 2025 Al-Nassr extension — equity stake included — as already-realized wealth reach **$1.2B–$1.4B**, the basis for recent "football's first billionaire" headlines. The Bureau certifies the full **$0.8B–$1.4B** range rather than picking a winner, and notes this is precisely the kind of ambiguity the Bureau's Potentiality Value engine exists to resolve.

**Part B — The "Fair Market Value of Attention" (flagged as an experimental Bureau extrapolation, not a scholarly or financial consensus figure).** Subject holds 678,142,770 Instagram followers as of August 2026 — the largest following of any individual human on the platform, ahead of Instagram's own house account — and was the first person to cross 500 million (November 2022) and 600 million (August 2023) followers. Combined across Instagram, Facebook, X, TikTok, and YouTube, Subject was the first individual in history to surpass 1 billion combined followers. The Bureau constructs a dollar-equivalent value for this following using a four-step methodology modeled on the Wealth Quotient work already on file for Genghis Khan (tribute-economy extrapolation) and Akbar the Great (GDP-share extrapolation):

- **Step 1 — Real, documented per-unit rate.** Per Hopper HQ's 2025 Instagram Rich List, Subject commands **$3.23M per sponsored Instagram post** — the highest real-world rate of any individual on the platform, ahead of Lionel Messi ($2.6M) and every entertainment celebrity tracked.
- **Step 2 — Sustainable monetization cadence (Bureau assumption, not documented fact).** The Bureau assumes 24 monetized posts per year (twice monthly), a figure chosen conservatively to avoid the scarcity-collapse effect the influencer-marketing industry widely documents when a premium account over-monetizes. This yields a raw Instagram attention-yield of **≈$77.5M/year**.
- **Step 3 — Cross-platform scaling.** Applying a conservative 1.4x multiplier for Subject's additional reach across Facebook, X, TikTok, and YouTube (weighted below 1:1 because per-post rates on those platforms run lower than Instagram's) produces a combined theoretical annual attention-yield of **≈$108M/year**.
- **Step 4 — Capitalization at a real-world media-multiple.** Publicly traded platform and digital-media companies with large, sticky global audiences (Pinterest, Snap, and comparable peers) trade at revenue multiples roughly in the high-single to low-double-digit range. The Bureau applies an **8x–15x** band, reflecting a premium for an audience with zero content-moderation cost, zero platform infrastructure spend, and no content-acquisition overhead. This produces a **Fair Market Value of Attention of $0.9B–$1.6B**.

The Bureau states plainly: this figure describes what Subject's following would be worth if it were legally and economically treated as an appraisable, transferable asset — the same logic this Bureau's Potentiality Value engine already applies to patents and predicted earnings elsewhere in the docket. It is not a bank balance. Subject cannot liquidate his followers.

**Concentration Hazard:** Elevated and structurally unusual. Subject's conventional wealth is concentrated in a single foreign sovereign-linked employer (see Influence Vector, below) and a personally-branded lifestyle empire; his attention capital is concentrated in a single platform account with no institutional diversification whatsoever.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

Subject's philanthropic record is genuine, longstanding, and largely undertaken without HPB-style compulsion, though modest relative to the scale of the estate audited here. Subject was named the world's most charitable sports star by DoSomething.org in 2015, has donated repeatedly to children's hospitals and disaster-relief efforts, personally funded a £120,000 donation to the Madeira cancer center that treated his mother, and covered a £55,000 brain-surgery bill for a ten-month-old outside any formal campaign. Subject has also worked with UNICEF and Save the Children on an ongoing basis. **HPB Optics Assessment:** this is real "Saintly Service" in miniature — sincere, sustained, individually targeted — but it operates at the scale of a wealthy private citizen's checkbook, not the "Infrastructure Gift" tier (hospitals, power grids, server farms transferred outright) that defines a genuine pivot at this net-worth bracket. **Saintly Pivot Score: 5/10.** Credible generosity, insufficient scale.

### **3. Influence Vector: The Sovereign Paycheck & the Undivided Congregation**

Two distinct risk vectors converge in this file. The first is structural: Al-Nassr FC is majority-owned by Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund, and Subject's reported equity stake in the club makes him a direct financial stakeholder in a foreign sovereign wealth fund's soft-power apparatus — the Bureau's Sovereign Flotilla concern turned inside out, since Subject did not flee to a Flotilla but was recruited, at record compensation, by one. He holds no formal office and exercises no policy role, but his global visibility functions, whether or not intended, as a marketing instrument for the state paying him.

The second is memetic. Subject's "SIUUU" celebration and self-mythologized "GOAT" (Greatest of All Time) branding — reinforced across two decades of documented, almost monastic personal-discipline routines — function as a durable cult of individual supremacy, distinct from but structurally adjacent to the Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric this Bureau flags in other files. The Bureau notes this is sport, not sovereignty claim, and declines to classify it as a Class-IV Memetic Hazard on those grounds alone. It is flagged here because the Mortal Recertification Plan (mrp.12026.08) addresses it directly, and because a following this large attaches real commercial value to a myth of singular, unrepeatable greatness — the same myth Part B of this section just finished pricing.

**A Note on Comparative Scale:** Per the Pew Research Center's most recent comprehensive global religious-demographics report (June 2025, covering data through 2020), the world's Christian population is estimated at approximately 2.3 billion adherents (28.8% of the global population) and the world's Muslim population at approximately 2.0 billion adherents (25.6%) — each figure well in excess of Subject's estimated 1 billion-plus combined social-media following. The Bureau raises this comparison not to suggest equivalence, numerically or otherwise, but to isolate what actually makes Subject's case unprecedented in this docket: those adherence totals are distributed across centuries of denominations, sects, regional traditions, and theological schools, answering to no single living authority. Subject's following is undivided — a single, personally-branded audience attached to one living private individual, assembled within roughly two decades, subject to no doctrinal split of any kind. It is that structural singularity, not raw size relative to the world's largest religions (which it does not approach), that the Bureau's Wealth Quotient treats above as a novel and unprecedented asset class.

## **⚖️ THE VERDICT: ASCENSION BY ALGORITHM**

**Confidence Score: 74%**

The Bureau's confidence is deliberately capped below its usual CRITICAL-tier certainty for two honest reasons: Subject's conventional net worth is genuinely contested across reputable trackers, and the Fair Market Value of Attention methodology is, by this file's own admission, an experimental extrapolation with no precedent in either finance or prior Bureau casework. What the Bureau is confident of is this: under nearly every combination of conventional-estimate-plus-attention-valuation this file constructs, Subject's Combined Potentiality Value clears $1B, in several scenarios by a wide margin. This is, to the Bureau's knowledge, the first case in the active docket where a subject's ascension risk hinges not on a bank account but on a follow button.

> **Audit Summary:** "Subject may or may not be a documented billionaire, depending on which accountant you ask. He is, beyond any accountant's dispute, the single most-followed individual human being alive, and that following prices out, by real market comparables, at nine and ten figures on its own. The Bureau was built to catch people hoarding money. It was not built to catch people hoarding attention. This file is the reason it may need to be."
