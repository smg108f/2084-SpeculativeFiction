Lukas inherited his father's Walmart stake and has used it primarily to pursue environmental philanthropy — he established Builders Initiative, which has committed over $1B to conservation, clean energy, and sustainable food systems. He sits on the Walton Family Foundation board. He is the youngest primary Walmart heir and the one who has most visibly oriented his fortune toward environmental philanthropy rather than continued accumulation or political investment.
