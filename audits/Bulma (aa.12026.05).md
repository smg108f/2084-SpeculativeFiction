## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE SMARTEST PERSON IN THE ROOM (AND SHE KNOWS IT)**

Bulma Brief is the daughter of Capsule Corporation's founder, the world's foremost inventor of portable reality-compression technology, the engineer of the original Dragon Radar — without which no Dragon Ball wish in documented history would have been possible — and the woman who, at the age of sixteen, assembled the team that retrieved the Dragon Balls for the first time. She is also the spouse of the Prince of all Saiyans, a fact the Bureau's Political Relationships Division flags as an informal alliance structure that exceeds what standard kinship analysis can capture. She has, on three documented occasions, used Dragon Ball wishes to alter circumstances affecting herself and people she cares about. She will, the Bureau notes, absolutely do it again if the circumstances seem to warrant it and the Dragon Balls are available.

The HPB has already audited Capsule Corporation (HPB-12026-13-CC). This audit addresses the scientist, not the company. The distinction is not academic: Bulma Brief's personal scientific capability is the foundational IP from which Capsule Corp's technological dominance derives, and her individual wish-deployment standing — as the person who has coordinated more Dragon Ball retrievals than any other individual in documented history — is a concentration of agency that the corporate audit cannot capture.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

Subject's $70 billion RTWI represents personal holdings distinct from the Brief family's Capsule Corp controlling interest. The HPB's Asset Valuation Division notes that this figure likely understates Subject's effective wealth because it does not capture the economic value of her position as the individual most capable of assembling a Dragon Ball retrieval team, operating the Dragon Radar, and coordinating a wish deployment — a capability that, if priced commercially, would represent the most valuable personal consulting service in the DC/Marvel/Jump combined market.

**Concentration Hazard — Class I (Personal Wish Deployment Authority):** The Dragon Ball Wish Deployment Covenant (Capsule Corp audit, HPB-12026-13-CC) governs how the Dragon Balls may be used by Capsule Corporation as an entity. The Covenant does not, and cannot, address Bulma Brief's individual standing to locate, retrieve, and coordinate wish deployment independently of any corporate structure. She invented the Dragon Radar. She could build another one. She has done it before. The Bureau's Extraordinary Asset Division classifies Bulma Brief's personal wish-deployment capability as the highest-impact individual agency it has encountered outside the confirmed-deity category — including the relevant portion of the Frieza audit, where the concern was scale of territory rather than alteration of fundamental circumstances.

**Concentration Hazard — Class II (Scientific Embodied Capital):** Bulma Brief's scientific capability is, by the Bureau's Technical Assessment Division's analysis, the foundational IP of the most technologically significant company in the Jump Universe audit queue. She did not inherit this capability from Capsule Corp; Capsule Corp's commercial dominance derives from it. Her personal scientific output — documented in 47 independent patents registered to her individually, not Capsule Corp — includes advances in portable matter compression, temporal mechanics (she built a time machine), and biological enhancement technology that the Bureau classifies as the most consequential individual scientific portfolio it has audited.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

Bulma Brief's record includes: repeated participation in world-saving events for which she received no compensation and on several occasions significant personal risk; founding and leading the team that has, cumulatively, saved Earth from annihilation on multiple occasions; and making wish deployments that, on two of three documented occasions, benefited others as much or more than herself.

The Bureau does not dismiss this record. It notes that Subject's original stated motivation for assembling the first Dragon Ball retrieval was, by her own account, wishing for "the perfect boyfriend." The Bureau is aware that Subject has evolved significantly since that expedition. The Bureau is also noting the data point, because the Saintly Pivot analysis requires honesty about the full record.

Subject's current philanthropic activities are primarily conducted through Capsule Corp's Research Foundation (addressed in HPB-12026-13-CC). Her personal charitable giving is significant but not structured in a way the Bureau can independently verify. The Bureau's Philanthropy Review Division rates her record as **GENUINE — motivated by authentic interest in outcomes rather than strategic reputation management**, which it notes is, ironically, rarer among subjects of her wealth level than one would hope.

HPB Optics Rating: **COMPLEX — Genuine Capability, Genuine Motivation, Genuine Risk of Unilateral Action.**

### **3. Influence Vector: She Will Do What She Thinks Is Best**

The Bureau's most significant concern about Bulma Brief is not financial and is not the Dragon Balls specifically. It is that she is a person of extraordinary intelligence, genuine good intentions, and a documented pattern of taking decisive unilateral action when she has concluded that the situation warrants it — including, on one occasion, traveling backward in time to change outcomes she found unacceptable.

The Bureau's concern about time travel as a personal decision-making tool is addressed in the Capsule Corp audit. The Bureau's concern about Bulma Brief specifically is the combination of: the intelligence to identify when the Dragon Balls could solve a problem, the technical capability to retrieve them, the relational networks required to coordinate wish deployment, and the decisional independence to conclude that this is an appropriate response without consulting a governance structure — because no governance structure of appropriate scope has previously existed. The HPB is proposing, for the first time, to be that governance structure, at least partially. The Bureau is aware that Subject will find this proposal annoying. The Bureau is proceeding.

## **⚖️ THE VERDICT: THE WOMAN WHO FOUND THE DRAGON BALLS**

**Confidence Score: 88%**

Bulma Brief is a CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK. The 12% confidence reduction reflects the Bureau's honest acknowledgment that valuing a person whose personal scientific capability is the origin point of one company's global dominance, and whose personal Dragon Ball standing is not captured in any corporate structure, involves estimation ranges the Bureau's Asset Valuation Division flags as necessarily imprecise.

> **Audit Summary:** "Bulma Brief invented the Dragon Radar at sixteen. She built a time machine because she needed one. She has used the Dragon Balls to reshape outcomes she found unacceptable, and the Bureau notes that on the occasions she has done so, she was not wrong about the outcomes being unacceptable. The problem is not her judgment — the Bureau genuinely rates her judgment above the population mean. The problem is that 'one person's judgment, however good, without external accountability' is the definition of the concentration the Act was designed to address. The Bureau is not asking her to stop being the smartest person in the room. The Bureau is asking her to let it know, in advance, when she's planning to use the Dragon Balls."
