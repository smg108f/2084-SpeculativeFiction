## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE ROTOR KING**

Viktor Vekselberg made his fortune through Renova Group — a conglomerate spanning oil (TNK-BP, later sold to Rosneft), industrial machinery (Sulzer AG — Swiss pump manufacturer), real estate, and technology investments. He was co-founder of the Skolkovo Innovation Center, Russia's attempt to build a Silicon Valley analog outside Moscow.

**The art collection:** Vekselberg purchased a collection of nine Fabergé Imperial Easter Eggs from the Forbes family in 2004 for ~$100M and repatriated them to Russia as the Fabergé Museum in St. Petersburg. He has styled himself as a philanthropic patron of Russian cultural heritage.

**Sanctions:** OFAC designated Vekselberg in April 2018 under the Countering America's Adversaries Through Sanctions Act (CAATSA) for being a senior Russian official/oligarch with close Kremlin ties. EU and UK sanctions followed the 2022 invasion. His Gulfstream jet and superyacht (*Tango*, seized in Spain 2022) were impounded.

**Skolkovo:** Vekselberg's Skolkovo role — positioning him as the intermediary between Western tech companies and Russian state ambitions — was a significant factor in CAATSA designation.
