## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE QUEEN OF MEAN**

Leona Helmsley — born Leona Rosenthal, known as the "Queen of Mean" — was the wife of real estate developer Harry Helmsley and the public face of the Helmsley Hotels chain. She was convicted in 1989 of federal income tax evasion and other charges — sentenced to 18 months in prison (served 21 months including the 3-month medical center stay). Her trial featured testimony from a housekeeper who reported Helmsley said: *"We don't pay taxes. Only the little people pay taxes."*

**The tax conviction:** The HPB notes with some satisfaction the economy of the phrase. Leona Helmsley said out loud what many of the living subjects in these Batches believe implicitly.

**The will:** Helmsley died in 2007, leaving her estate — valued at approximately $8B — to the Leona M. and Harry B. Helmsley Charitable Trust, with instructions to focus on dogs. Her dog Trouble received $12M (later reduced by courts to $2M). Various grandchildren were specifically excluded from the will.

**The Helmsley Trust:** The trust has subsequently given broadly — to healthcare, education, and other causes — moving beyond the canine focus. The trustees have discretion.

**HPB's posthumous assessment:** The "only the little people pay taxes" conviction is the definitional statement of this entire audit project. The HPB honors it with the most direct FSS assignment in these Batches.
