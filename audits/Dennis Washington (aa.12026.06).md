## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE MONTANA INDUSTRIALIST**

Dennis Washington built the **Washington Companies** from a Montana construction business into a diversified industrial empire spanning: copper mining (Montana Resources), short-line and regional railroads (Montana Rail Link, others), marine shipping (American President Lines affiliate), and environmental services. He is Montana's wealthiest resident and a dominant figure in the state's economy.

**Mining accountability:** Montana Resources operates the **Continental Pit** copper mine in Butte, Montana — one of the largest open-pit copper mines in the US.
