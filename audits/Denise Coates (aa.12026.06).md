## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE BET365 QUEEN**

Denise Coates CBE is the founder, joint CEO, and controlling shareholder of bet365 — the world's largest online gambling company, headquartered in Stoke-on-Trent, England. She transformed a family chain of betting shops into a global online platform serving 100 million+ customers across 200 countries.

**The compensation:** Coates paid herself approximately £421M in 2022 — making her the highest-paid executive in British corporate history, year after year. This is not a salary; it is a profit extraction from a gambling company she controls. The contrast between this level of personal enrichment and bet365's location in Stoke-on-Trent — one of England's most economically deprived post-industrial cities — is stark.

**Gambling harm at scale:** Problem gambling is a serious public health issue. bet365 operates at 100M+ customer scale — a significant proportion of whom are problem gamblers by clinical definition. The gambling industry's revenue model is specifically dependent on losses from a subset of customers who cannot control their gambling. bet365 has faced regulatory action in multiple jurisdictions for insufficient responsible gambling measures.

**UKGC enforcement:** The UK Gambling Commission has fined bet365 and other major operators multiple times for failures in social responsibility and anti-money laundering controls.

**Stoke-on-Trent:** bet365 is the largest private sector employer in Stoke-on-Trent. Coates has donated to the Bet365 Foundation for community causes. The employment is real and the community investment is documented — but the source of the funds (gambling losses) creates a morally complex dependency.
