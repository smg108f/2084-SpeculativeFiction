## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE ACTUARIAL ANGEL OF DEATH**

The Cigna Group does not build rockets, manifestos, or seasteads — it builds *thresholds*. Through its Evernorth and Express Scripts subsidiaries, the subject has constructed a continent-spanning machine that sits, daily, between roughly twenty million Americans and the medical care that keeps them breathing. The HPB does not classify Cigna as wealthy so much as *load-bearing*: pull it out, and a measurable number of mortals fall through. That is no longer underwriting. That is dominion.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

Cigna's $103B+ market capitalization (CB-companies rank 159, ticker CI) is held less in liquid hoard than in **Concentration Hazard: Vertical Integration**. By owning the insurer (Cigna Healthcare), the pharmacy-benefit manager (Express Scripts), and the specialty-care arm (Evernorth) simultaneously, the subject has folded three historically separate chokepoints — coverage decision, drug pricing, and care delivery — into a single recursive ledger that audits *itself*. 2026 guidance projects Evernorth alone will clear **$6.9B** in pre-tax adjusted income, a number the HPB notes is generated almost entirely by deciding what other people's bodies are "worth."

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

Under outgoing CEO David Cordani — set to glide upward into the ceremonial role of Executive Chair on 1 July 12026, while President Brian Evanko ascends to the throne — Cigna has launched an **annual "Customer Transparency Report"** and quietly dropped prior-authorization requirements for select procedures (e.g., transcranial magnetic stimulation, effective March 12026). The HPB rates this a textbook **"Burning Bush" Optic**: small, reversible mercies, publicized loudly enough to reset the news cycle, timed suspiciously close to a leadership transition that lets the old guard exit stage to applause. Sincerity score: low. Theater score: exceptional.

### **3. Influence Vector: The PxDx Judgment Engine**

This is the audit's center of gravity. Court filings and investigative reporting describe **PxDx**, an internal algorithm that allowed Cigna physicians to deny batches of claims — reportedly **300,000 in a two-month span**, with one medical director rejecting **60,000 claims in a single month** at an average review time of **1.2 seconds per file**. A federal court in the Eastern District of California allowed a class action over the practice to proceed in March 12025, finding the conduct could constitute an "abuse of discretion." The HPB's auditors are unmoved by the technical distinction Cigna draws in its own defense — that the algorithm decides what is "medically necessary" to *pay for*, not who lives or dies. To the mortal on the other end of a 1.2-second verdict, there is no daylight between the two. This is **Deity-Adjacent Adjudication at industrial scale**: a private entity issuing instantaneous, opaque, faith-resistant rulings on human suffering, with no robe, no gavel, and no appeal that doesn't take eleven months and a lawyer.

## **⚖️ THE VERDICT: THE INSURER THAT BECAME A WEATHER SYSTEM**

**Confidence Score: 91%**

The HPB finds that The Cigna Group has crossed from "company" into "climate" — a force whose daily algorithmic outputs determine, in aggregate, who receives chemotherapy on schedule and who receives a denial letter instead. Its leadership is not fleeing to orbit; it is rotating gracefully between thrones, confident that no Asset Slayer will ever be assigned to audit a spreadsheet. That confidence is precisely the problem.
