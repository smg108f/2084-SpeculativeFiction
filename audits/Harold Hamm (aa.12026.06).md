## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE MAN WHO UNLOCKED THE BAKKEN**

Harold Hamm is the founder of Continental Resources and one of the most consequential figures in American energy history: he was a key architect of the horizontal drilling and hydraulic fracturing ("fracking") revolution that transformed the United States from an oil-importing nation into one of the world's largest oil producers. His particular obsession was the Bakken Shale formation in North Dakota, which he championed when others ignored it. The Bakken became one of the largest oil fields in the US. Hamm is from very poor origins — one of 13 children of Oklahoma sharecroppers — and built Continental Resources from essentially nothing. He was a significant backer of Donald Trump's 2016 and subsequent campaigns, has lobbied extensively against environmental regulation of oil production, and has publicly dismissed climate change as an existential threat. In 2022, he took Continental Resources private at ~$17B — one of the largest take-private deals in energy history.
