## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE DELIVERY GOD**

Wang Wei founded SF Express in 1993 in Shunde, Guangdong, starting with a delivery service between Hong Kong and the Pearl River Delta. He built it into China's leading express delivery company — the FedEx of China — with a distinctive model: SF Express employs its delivery workers directly rather than through independent contractor arrangements, which is unusual in Chinese logistics and which Wang has maintained as a quality and reliability differentiator. SF Express now handles hundreds of millions of packages and has expanded into cross-border logistics, warehousing, and supply chain services. Wang maintains a very low public profile despite his company's scale.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

SF Express is listed on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange. Wang holds a controlling stake. The company's revenue exceeds ¥200B (~$27B) annually, with a significant portion coming from e-commerce last-mile delivery. **Concentration Hazard: HIGH** — SF's market position in Chinese express delivery is dominant, and the company's labor model, while better than gig-economy alternatives, involves significant workforce welfare considerations at scale.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

Wang and SF Express have some charitable activities and have contributed to disaster relief. His decision to employ rather than contract delivery workers is itself a structural social contribution relative to industry norms. HPB rates: **MODERATE — STRUCTURAL LABOR PIVOT.** The direct employment model is the HPB's primary positive assessment.

### **3. Influence Vector: The Last Mile**

SF Express handles the last mile of an enormous proportion of Chinese e-commerce deliveries. The quality of that last mile — delivery speed, reliability, worker treatment — shapes the daily experience of hundreds of millions of Chinese consumers and the working conditions of hundreds of thousands of delivery workers. The HPB classifies this as **Critical Consumer Infrastructure** — not glamorous but consequential.
