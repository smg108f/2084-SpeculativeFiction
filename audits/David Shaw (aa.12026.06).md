## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE SCIENTIST WHO BUILT A MONEY MACHINE**

David Shaw holds a Ph.D. in computer science from Stanford. He was an early pioneer of quantitative and algorithmic trading — founding D.E. Shaw & Co. in 1988, building it into one of the world's most sophisticated hedge funds (AUM approximately $60B). He then stepped back from day-to-day management to found D.E. Shaw Research — a computational biochemistry company that uses custom supercomputers (the Anton machines) to simulate protein folding and molecular dynamics, targeting drug discovery.

**The quantitative model:** D.E. Shaw is one of the firms that pioneered high-frequency and statistical arbitrage trading. The social utility of pure quantitative finance is contested: these strategies profit primarily from information advantages and market microstructure, capturing value from other market participants rather than allocating capital to productive enterprises.

**D.E. Shaw Research:** Anton is a series of specialized supercomputers built entirely for molecular dynamics simulation. Anton 2 and 3 have enabled unprecedented simulation of protein behavior — directly contributing to drug discovery research. The work has produced peer-reviewed science and been made available to academic researchers. This is a genuine redirection of billionaire capital toward public scientific benefit.

**Government service:** Shaw served as Chair of the President's Council of Advisors on Science and Technology (PCAST) under President Obama — the most senior science advisory role in the executive branch. This is an unusual instance of financial wealth combined with sustained government public service.

**Philanthropy:** Has donated to multiple Democratic Party candidates and causes; scientific research; relatively lower-profile individual philanthropy compared to the scale of his fortune.

**Labor:** D.E. Shaw's compensation is among the highest in finance — engineers and researchers are extremely well-compensated. No documented wage theft or labor suppression.

### **Verdict:** MARGINAL — D.E. Shaw Research provides genuine public scientific benefit; government service through PCAST; quantitative finance model raises structural value-extraction concerns; philanthropy moderate but substantive research contribution partially offsets.
