## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE ORIGINAL CORPORATE RAIDER**

Carl Icahn is the original corporate raider — the archetype of the activist investor who buys significant stakes in public companies, demands management changes or asset sales, and profits from the resulting restructuring. He has made enormous bets on companies including TWA (which he broke up), Texaco, RJR Nabisco, Motorola, Netflix, Apple, Dell, and dozens of others. He has been right more often than wrong and has accumulated one of the largest personal fortunes in American finance. He has also been accused of: market manipulation (the SEC settled a case with him in 2023 related to a short-seller's report); using Icahn Enterprises as a vehicle for personal enrichment at the expense of retail investors; and being a net destroyer of industrial value through asset-stripping. He is now in his late 80s and remains active.

The HPB notes that in 2023, a short-seller report (Hindenburg Research) accused Icahn Enterprises of overvalued assets and structural self-dealing. The SEC subsequently settled enforcement charges related to undisclosed pledging of Icahn Enterprises shares.
