## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE SCORE THAT DECIDES YOUR LIFE, OWNED BY NO ONE IN PARTICULAR**

Experian, one of the world's dominant credit bureaus, holds outsized influence over consumer access to housing, credit, and employment through its scoring data, all while remaining a widely held company with no controlling individual owner. The Bureau finds an unusual case: an institution wielding profound personal influence over millions of individual lives, structurally incapable of being reduced to a single accountable person.

### **1. WEALTH QUOTIENT**
No individual controls a disqualifying personal stake; ownership is institutional and dispersed.

### **2. SAINTLY PIVOT**
Financial inclusion and credit education initiatives exist but sit alongside the fundamental tension of monetizing consumer financial data at scale.

### **3. INFLUENCE VECTOR**
As a dominant credit bureau, Experian's scoring algorithms directly shape millions of individual financial outcomes — a form of influence the Bureau finds significant precisely because no single person can be held accountable for it.
