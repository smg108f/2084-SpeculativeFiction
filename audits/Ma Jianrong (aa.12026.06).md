## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE KNITWEAR KING**

Ma Jianrong is the chairman of Shenzhou International Group — the world's largest manufacturer of knitwear sportswear, supplying Nike, Adidas, Uniqlo, and Puma. Shenzhou manufactures finished garments (T-shirts, sportswear, hoodies) at its facilities in Ningbo, China and increasingly in Cambodia and Vietnam as labor cost pressures shift production.

**The fast fashion supply chain role:** Shenzhou sits at the manufacturing base of the global sportswear and fast fashion supply chain. The company makes the actual physical garments; the brands capture the overwhelming majority of consumer value. Workers in Shenzhou's Chinese, Cambodian, and Vietnamese factories produce garments sold globally at multiples of their production cost.

**Worker conditions:** Shenzhou has generally been rated as a better-than-average manufacturer in audits by Nike and Adidas — which both conduct factory inspections. However, "better than average" in the garment industry context leaves significant room for concern about wages, working hours, and worker power.

**Xinjiang:** Shenzhou has faced scrutiny over cotton sourcing, including potential Xinjiang supply chain exposure, though the company has taken steps to audit this.

**Philanthropy:** Limited documented individual philanthropy at wealth scale.

### **Verdict:** MARGINAL — supply chain labor value extraction at scale; better-than-average industry labor standards; Xinjiang sourcing concern; limited philanthropy.
