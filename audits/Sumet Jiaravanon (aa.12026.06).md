## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE CP GROUP HEIR**

Sumet Jiaravanon is a senior member of the Jiaravanon (also written Chearavanont) family — the family that controls **Charoen Pokphand (CP) Group**, one of Asia's largest conglomerates. CP Group spans: agribusiness (world's largest shrimp and chicken producer under the CP brand), retail (7-Eleven Thailand, Lotus's supermarkets), telecommunications (True Corporation), and financial services. The family's overall wealth across all members is among the largest in Asia.

**CP's agribusiness accountability:** CP is one of the world's largest integrated animal protein producers — operating at a scale that encompasses animal feed, farming, processing, and retail. The accountability includes: deforestation for soy and feed crops, animal welfare in industrial-scale chicken and shrimp operations, and labor conditions in food processing plants. Thai seafood industry labor abuse has been documented — specifically in CP Shrimp operations, where forced labor reports in 2014-2015 caused significant reputational damage.

**True Corporation:** The merger of True Corporation and DTAC created one of Thailand's largest telecom operators — a market concentration event.

**Giving Pledge:** The CP Group family has not signed the Giving Pledge. Philanthropic activity exists but is not at a scale commensurate with family wealth.
