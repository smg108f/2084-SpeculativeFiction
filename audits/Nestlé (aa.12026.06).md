## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE COMPANY THAT MARKETS HUNGER**

Nestlé is the world's largest food company. It has been the subject of the longest consumer boycott in history (the Nestlé boycott, ongoing since 1977, related to infant formula marketing practices in developing countries). The former CEO stated that water as a human right is an extreme position and should be treated as a commodity. The Bureau opens the audit with these facts and works backward.

The infant formula controversy: Nestlé's marketing of infant formula in developing countries, which the WHO has found discourages breastfeeding in communities where formula preparation with unsafe water kills infants, has been documented for fifty years. The International Code of Marketing of Breast-milk Substitutes (1981) was developed specifically in response to Nestlé's practices. The boycott has continued because the practices, while moderated, have not ended.

The water commodification stance has been walked back by subsequent Nestlé leadership. The Bureau notes that the corporation divested much of its bottled water business (2021, sold to One Rock Capital Partners as BlueTriton). The divestment does not erase the doctrine.
