## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE AUDIT THAT CLOSES WITH A COMMENDATION**

Tony Stark is dead. The HPB audits him anyway, because the Bureau audits the estate of any individual whose RTWI exceeded the Ascension threshold at time of death, and because the way a person structures their estate is among the most revealing data points in an Ascension risk assessment. Tony Stark's estate structure is, in the Bureau's assessment, the most thoughtfully designed wealth-dissolution mechanism at this tier it has reviewed.

The Bureau is classifying Tony Stark as CLEARED (POSTHUMOUS). This is not a standard classification. The HPB has created it specifically for this audit.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

Tony Stark's estimated personal estate of $100 billion — separate from his Stark Industries equity and his Stark Resilient holdings — has been distributed through a combination of direct bequest to Pepper Potts (now Pepper Stark), establishment of the Maria Stark Foundation's endowment, direct transfer to Morgan Stark's personal trust with explicit anti-hoarding provisions, and a Stark Legacy Fund dedicated to research initiatives in clean energy, advanced materials, and defensive (non-offensive) technology.

**Note — Data File:** The HPB notes the data file annotation that "Tony's net worth tends to be as unstable as his bleeding edge tech." This is accurate. At various points in his career, Stark's RTWI ranged from near-zero (following the Stark Industries weapons shutdown and associated legal and reputational costs) to several hundred billion (following the successful commercialization of Arc Reactor technology and the Avengers-period intelligence premium). The $100 billion figure represents a stable midpoint. The instability was not strategic evasion — it was the genuine consequence of a career lived at the edge of what was technically possible.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

Tony Stark's Saintly Pivot record is the most personally costly in this audit cycle. He did not merely donate money — he physically died preventing a planetary extinction event. The HPB does not have a standard metric for this, but it is noting it in the record as a public benefit of a scale and specificity that no other subject in the cycle has approached.

Beyond the terminal act, Stark's career included: the unilateral announcement that Stark Industries would cease weapons manufacturing (genuine, if subsequently reversed by others); the development of Arc Reactor clean energy technology and its commercialization through Stark Resilient; the Iron Man program, which the HPB notes was a net public benefit despite representing the most expensive private vigilante operation in the DC/Marvel/Jump audit cycle by a significant margin; and a pattern of technology disclosure — to the Avengers, to international bodies, to SHIELD — that consistently prioritized function over commercial protection.

HPB Optics Rating: **GENUINE — Personal Cost Borne, Public Benefit Delivered.**

### **3. Estate Structure Assessment**

The Morgan Stark trust includes an explicit provision — written by Tony Stark personally, in a document the Bureau's Estate Division describes as "unusually self-aware for a posthumous legal instrument" — stating that the trust's anti-hoarding provisions are designed to prevent Morgan from crossing the Ascension threshold without choosing to do so, and that the trust's income is calibrated to provide genuine material comfort without providing the kind of wealth that, in Tony's words, "turns you into someone who needs auditing." The HPB finds this language, in a posthumous estate document, to be the most direct acknowledgment of the Act's underlying logic it has received from any subject.

Tony Stark audited himself before the Bureau could.

## **⚖️ THE VERDICT: HE STAYED.**

**Confidence Score: 100%**

Tony Stark is CLEARED (POSTHUMOUS) with commendation. His estate has been distributed in a manner consistent with the Act's underlying goals. His corporate legacy — Stark Industries (CRITICAL) and Stark Resilient (PROBATIONARY SAINT) — reflects the tension between his intentions and his institutions, a tension the HPB has documented separately. The Bureau does not give commendations. The Bureau is making an exception.
