## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE CHAIRMAN WHO GAVE IT AWAY**

Azim Premji inherited the chairmanship of Western India Vegetable Products (later renamed Wipro) from his father in 1966, dropped out of Stanford to manage the business, and over the following decades transformed it from a cooking oil company into India's third-largest IT services company — a global consulting and technology outsourcing firm. Wipro is listed on NYSE and BSE.

What distinguishes Premji in this Batch — and across the entirety of the HPB's audit cohort — is the scale and sincerity of his philanthropy:

- **~$21B committed to the Azim Premji Foundation** over his lifetime — approximately 67% of his peak estimated wealth;
- **Current net worth (~$9.2B) is less than half of what he has already given away** — a ratio unlike any subject in Batches 1-4;
- The Foundation focuses on **education quality in India** — specifically, improving the quality of India's public school system: teacher training, curriculum development, and school improvement programs in multiple Indian states. The Foundation has trained hundreds of thousands of teachers and partnered with thousands of public schools;
- **Azim Premji University** (Bangalore) — a full university focused on development studies, education, and public service, with a strong financial aid program;
- **Giving Pledge signatory** — with a personal commitment letter that described education as India's foundational need.

The HPB acknowledges: there are questions about the pace at which foundation commitments are actually transferred versus pledged. But the documented transfers of Wipro shares to the Foundation over many years are on record.
