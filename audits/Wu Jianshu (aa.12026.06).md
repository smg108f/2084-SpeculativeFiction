## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE AUTO GLASS KING**

Wu Jianshu (also written as **Cho Tak Wong** in Cantonese) founded **Fuyao Glass**, which grew to become the world's largest manufacturer of automotive glass — supplying windshields and auto glass to virtually every major automaker globally. Fuyao's facilities span China and the United States (notably the Moraine, Ohio plant featured in the documentary *American Factory*).

**American Factory context:** The Dayton, Ohio Fuyao plant became globally known through the Academy Award-winning Netflix documentary *American Factory* (2019), which documented the cultural collision between Chinese management practices and American labor expectations at the former GM plant. The film highlighted worker safety concerns, anti-union pressure, and management-labor tensions. The plant ultimately remained and became a significant Dayton employer.

**Labor practices:** Fuyao's U.S. operations faced NLRB complaints regarding anti-union activity and safety violations. These are documented, not alleged.
