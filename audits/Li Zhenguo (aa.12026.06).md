## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE SOLAR EMPEROR**

Li Zhenguo co-founded LONGi Green Energy Technology in 2000, betting on monocrystalline silicon as the superior photovoltaic technology at a time when cheaper polycrystalline alternatives dominated the market. That bet proved correct: monocrystalline silicon now dominates solar panel efficiency benchmarks, and LONGi has set multiple world records for solar cell efficiency. LONGi has at various points been the world's most valuable solar company.

**The clean energy contribution:** LONGi's manufacturing scale has contributed materially to the cost reduction of solar energy — the learning curve that makes solar the cheapest form of electricity generation in human history has been partly driven by manufacturers like LONGi pushing efficiency and production volume. This is a genuine positive externality.

**The Xinjiang polysilicon concern:** LONGi, like other Chinese solar manufacturers (see Jin Baofang / JinkoSolar, Batch 4), has sourced polysilicon from Xinjiang-based suppliers. The US Uyghur Forced Labor Prevention Act (UFLPA) has flagged Xinjiang polysilicon supply chains as implicating forced labor concerns. LONGi has stated it is moving to non-Xinjiang polysilicon sourcing, but the transition and its verification present ongoing concerns.

**Philanthropy:** Not significantly documented at scale.
