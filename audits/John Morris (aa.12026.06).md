## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE GREAT OUTDOORSMAN**

John Morris founded Bass Pro Shops in 1972 from a small fishing section inside his father's liquor store in Springfield, Missouri. He built it into the dominant American outdoor retail chain — and then in 2017 acquired Cabela's for $5.5B, creating a combined outdoor retail enterprise that approaches monopoly in the US hunting, fishing, camping, and outdoor recreation market.

**The retail empire:** Bass Pro Shops + Cabela's represent approximately 60% of US outdoor sporting goods retail at the category level for hunting and fishing. The combined company controls a significant portion of the market for firearms accessories, fishing tackle, boats (White River Marine Group — the largest boat manufacturer in North America), and camping equipment.

**Conservation philanthropy — a genuine credit:** Morris has been a significant conservation philanthropist. He founded the American National Fish and Wildlife Museum in Springfield (now the Wonders of Wildlife National Museum and Aquarium — accredited by the American Alliance of Museums). He has funded habitat restoration and conservation programs. His integration of conservation messaging into Bass Pro Shops' retail experience has introduced conservation concepts to millions of American hunters and anglers.

**Springfield civic engagement:** Morris has been a significant civic investor in Springfield, Missouri — funding entertainment, hospitality, and retail developments that have materially improved the city's economy.
