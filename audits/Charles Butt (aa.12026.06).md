## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE GROCER OF TEXAS**

Charles Butt is the third-generation chairman of H-E-B Grocery — the dominant supermarket chain in Texas, founded by his grandmother Florence Butt in 1905. H-E-B operates 340+ stores across Texas and Mexico and has become one of the most beloved regional supermarket brands in America, renowned for customer service, Texas-pride marketing, and extraordinary disaster response.

**Hurricane Harvey and COVID-19 response:** H-E-B's emergency management capabilities are genuinely exceptional. During Hurricane Harvey (2017), H-E-B deployed its mobile kitchens and supply chain to disaster-affected areas before FEMA arrived — feeding hundreds of thousands of Texans. During COVID-19, H-E-B was one of the first major retailers to implement purchase limits, mask requirements, and priority shopping hours for elderly customers. H-E-B is widely credited as having responded to both crises better than any governmental or corporate peer. This is an extraordinary case of corporate civic responsibility.

**Worker treatment:** H-E-B has historically paid above-average wages for the supermarket sector and has been recognized as a Texas employer of choice. Employee benefits exceed grocery sector norms.

**Texas community investment:** The Butt family has donated to San Antonio community institutions — the H-E-B Excellence in Education Awards fund Texas public school teachers; the Butt-Holdsworth Memorial Library is a public institution. The foundation has invested in early childhood education and Texas education reform.

**Political:** Charles Butt has made significant donations to Democrats — notably to candidates opposing Texas voting restrictions and to education advocacy. Unusual for a Texas business patriarch of this scale.

### **Verdict:** MARGINAL — affirmative disaster response record; above-average worker treatment; genuine philanthropic investment in Texas public institutions; limited individual accountability concerns.
