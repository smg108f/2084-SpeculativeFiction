## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE MEDICINE WOMAN OF LIANYUNGANG**

Zhong Huijuan is the founder and chairwoman of Hansoh Pharmaceutical Group — one of China's leading pharmaceutical companies, headquartered in Lianyungang, Jiangsu, specializing in oncology, nervous system, and anti-infective drugs. Hansoh went public in Hong Kong in 2019 in one of the year's largest IPOs.

**The enterprise:** Hansoh manufactures innovative and generic pharmaceuticals, with a growing focus on oncology biologics and small-molecule cancer treatments. The company has invested substantially in R&D, with a pipeline of innovative drugs rather than relying entirely on generic production.

**Self-made founder:** Zhong built Hansoh from a small provincial drug company into a major listed pharmaceutical group. She is one of China's most prominent female billionaire entrepreneurs and has been recognized as one of the most powerful businesswomen in Asia.

**China drug pricing context:** China has implemented aggressive drug price negotiations through the National Reimbursement Drug List, forcing pharmaceutical companies — including Hansoh — to accept steep price cuts for drugs to be reimbursed by the national health system. This context limits the standard drug pricing critique that applies to Western pharma.

**Philanthropy:** Zhong has donated to medical education and healthcare in Jiangsu province.
