## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE GREEK TANKER QUEEN**

Maria Angelicoussis inherited and leads **Angelicoussis Shipping Group**, one of Greece's and the world's largest privately-owned shipping empires, comprising **Maran Tankers** (crude oil and product tankers), **Maran Gas** (LNG carriers), and **Maran Dry** (bulk carriers). She succeeded her father John Angelicoussis and is one of the few women leading a major global shipping conglomerate.

**Fossil fuel transport accountability:** Maran Tankers moves crude oil globally. This is core fossil fuel infrastructure — the same accountability vector as Helmut Sohmen (BW Group), scaled comparably. Tanker operators enable the fossil fuel economy.

**LNG as transition fuel:** Maran Gas operates LNG carriers. Same complexity acknowledged as in Sohmen case — LNG shipping for energy transition in Asia and Europe has a legitimate argument, though it also enables new fossil fuel infrastructure lock-in.

**Greek shipping culture:** Greek shipping dynasties have long operated through favorable tax regimes (the Greek constitution exempts shipping profits from taxation). This structural tax advantage warrants acknowledgment.

**Pioneer status:** Maria Angelicoussis is a woman leading a major shipping empire in a historically male-dominated industry. This is not an exemption from accountability, but it is context.

**Environmental record:** Shipping decarbonization — Maran has invested in LNG-fueled vessels (lower emissions than HFO) as a transitional technology.

**Whuffie credit:** LNG transition investments; female leadership in male-dominated industry; no significant adverse conduct record.
