**Status:** CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK
**Primary Influence Vector:** Cognitive Infrastructure / Algorithmic Attention Control

**Total Estimated Value:** **$90B – $95B** (ByteDance private valuation; Zhang holds ~21% equity and >50% voting rights; the attention economy is the asset class)

---

## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE ARCHITECT OF INFINITE SCROLL**

Zhang Yiming did not build a product. He built a mechanism for capturing human attention and selling it in fractions of a second. TikTok has over one billion users. ByteDance had $155B in revenue in 2024. The HPB's concern is not the money — it is the *architecture*. The subject stepped down as CEO in 2021 at age 38, relocated to Singapore, and now lives quietly while the recommendation engine he designed continues to determine what approximately one-seventh of humanity looks at for an average of 90 minutes per day. He is 41 years old and has, through algorithmic design, acquired more daily access to human attention than any institution in history. He is also, by all accounts, a private person who finds this unremarkable.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

ByteDance remains private. Zhang holds approximately 21% equity with majority voting control (>50% of votes). At a conservative ByteDance valuation of ~$400–450B, this yields a personal stake of ~$85–95B. The asset is fundamentally illiquid — ByteDance has not gone public, partly due to regulatory pressure, partly due to the significant geopolitical complications of a Chinese-founded company owning the world's most-used short-video platform. **Concentration Hazard: EXTREME**. The subject's entire fortune is in one private company that is functionally the nervous system of global short-form content consumption.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

No documented philanthropy of scale. Zhang is 41 years old, has been semi-retired since 2021, and lives in Singapore. There is no public foundation, no giving announcement, no Whuffie accumulation. HPB assessment: **No Saintly Hedge Detected**. This is structurally similar to Zhong Shanshan, but more alarming — the subject controls a global cognitive infrastructure while generating zero offsetting public benefit. He has not even performed the minimum theater of philanthropy that the HPB expects of subjects at this wealth level.

### **3. Influence Vector: The Recommendation Engine as God**

This is the HPB's primary concern. TikTok's recommendation algorithm is, in practical effect, the world's largest editorial board — making real-time decisions about what 1 billion+ people see, engage with, repeat, and believe. Zhang designed the architecture of this system and retains majority voting control over the company that runs it. He does not edit individual videos. He built the system that decides which videos self-propagate. In 2084 HPB terminology, this is a **Memetic Singularity** — a single private individual's design decisions, encoded in software, shaping the daily information diet of a substantial fraction of humanity. The fact that he is no longer the CEO is irrelevant. The architecture is his. The voting rights are his. The attention harvest is his.
