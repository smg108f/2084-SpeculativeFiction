## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE CARTIER PATRIARCH**

Johann Rupert chairs Compagnie Financière Richemont, the Swiss luxury conglomerate his father Anton Rupert founded. Richemont is the world's second-largest luxury goods group (behind LVMH), best known for Cartier — one of the world's most recognized jewelry and watch brands. The Rupert family is South Africa's wealthiest dynasty. Johann has grown Richemont into a global force in hard luxury (jewelry and watches), while his brother Antonius manages Reinet Investments. Richemont controls the jewelry and watchmaking heritage of European craftsmanship but is ultimately controlled from South Africa by a family whose wealth was built during and through the apartheid era.
