## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE PIG KING**

Qin Yinglin and his wife Qian Ying co-founded and control Muyuan Foods, China's largest pig breeding company. China is the world's largest pork producer and consumer, and Muyuan is its dominant industrial-scale player — operating with a vertically integrated model covering breeding, feed production, and processing. Qin built Muyuan from a small farm starting in 1992 into a publicly listed company with an extraordinary expansion during the 2019-2021 period, when African Swine Fever devastated China's pig population and Muyuan's scale and biosecurity advantages allowed it to expand enormously while smaller producers collapsed. At peak, he and his wife were worth over $40B, making them China's richest couple. The HPB notes William Ding's pig farm (filed earlier, as a side note in his NetEase audit) — this is a different, dedicated case.

The HPB has previously processed a billionaire with a notable pig farm (William Ding — NetEase — where the farm was noted positively as a commitment to fresh food). Qin Yinglin is the actual pig king. The comparison is instructive.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

Muyuan's market capitalization at peak exceeded $50B. Even at current valuations, it is among China's most valuable agricultural companies. The scale of industrial pig farming — in terms of land use, water use, waste generation, and animal welfare — is the HPB's primary concern. **Concentration Hazard: EXTREME** in terms of food system control and environmental footprint.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

No documented philanthropy of significant scale. No Giving Pledge. For China's sometime-richest couple, the philanthropic record is minimal. HPB rates: **ABSENT.**

### **3. Influence Vector: The Food System**

Muyuan's control of China's pork supply chain means that decisions about how and where pigs are raised, what they are fed, how their waste is managed, and at what scale the industry operates are made in large part by one company — controlled by one couple. This is food system concentration at a scale that has few global equivalents.
