## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE BILLION-DOLLAR CROWN**

Subject crossed the Ascension line in December 2025 by Forbes' own accounting, becoming the fifth musician in history to reach ten figures — and unlike most subjects who arrive at the HPB's door via lawyers and spin, she arrived via a nickname the entire species already uses without irony: "Queen." Where the Human Humility Act of 2031 requires auditors to sniff out Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric, Subject's is not subtext. It is the brand.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

Subject's fortune is architecturally similar to her husband's — equity-heavy, ownership-first, allergic to leaving money on someone else's table — but built on a different chassis. She owns her masters outright for every work released since 2011 through **Parkwood Entertainment**, the vertically integrated company she founded in 2010 to house her own management, production, and licensing, retaining an estimated 70–90% of catalog revenue (catalog itself valued near **$300M**) instead of the industry-standard split. The 2023–2025 touring cycle (Renaissance + Cowboy Carter) grossed roughly **$1B combined**, with the Cowboy Carter leg alone pulling **$407.6M** across 32 dates — the highest-grossing country tour in Boxscore history, run through Parkwood's own production infrastructure rather than a third-party promoter's. Business ventures add further mass: **Cécred** (haircare, the #1 prestige launch in Ulta Beauty's history, north of a **$100M run rate**), **Sir Davis** whiskey, and a reported **$50M** NFL Christmas halftime appearance. Notably absent from the current base: **Ivy Park**, the Adidas partnership dissolved in 2023 after sales collapsed more than 50% — a rare visible failure the HPB logs as evidence Subject's empire is not immune to market correction, just resilient to it. **Concentration Hazard:** marriage to fellow demi-god Shawn Carter, plus a shared four-property real estate portfolio (Malibu, Bel Air, Hamptons, Tribeca; publicly reported purchases alone total **~$311M**) and three minor heirs (Blue Ivy, Rumi, Sir Carter), constitutes a **dynastic-track household** the HPB cannot audit as two unrelated individuals in good faith.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

**BeyGOOD**, Subject's public charity foundation, funds scholarships, disaster relief, and periodic targeted gifts — including a **$100,000** donation to the University of Houston Law Center's Criminal Justice Clinic in 2024. HPB auditors rate the sincerity real but the scale token: against a net worth that crossed $1B in the same eighteen-month window as the Cowboy Carter Tour's $400M haul, BeyGOOD's cumulative footprint is, as with the Carter household generally, a rounding error against asset appreciation. No infrastructure gifts, no municipal transfers, no "Saintly Pivot" of the kind that would meaningfully lower Subject's Real-Time Wealth Index.

### **3. Influence Vector: Deity-Adjacent Branding as Structural Camouflage**

This is the audit's central finding. Subject has spent two decades building a public identity organized around monarchy and divinity — "Queen Bey," a fanbase literally named "the Hive" that swarms in her defense, a *Renaissance* album cycle staged around glowing-horse iconography and disco-deity imagery, a *Cowboy Carter* era marketed explicitly as "Act II" of a mythic trilogy. Under the Human Humility Act of 2031, this reads as a textbook **Class-III Memetic Hazard**: language and iconography that pre-normalize demi-god status as *earned royalty* rather than a legal violation to be corrected. Compounding this, Subject entered direct political territory in October 2024, appearing at a Houston rally in support of Kamala Harris; when Subject's opponents falsely alleged an **$11 million pay-for-endorsement** scheme (debunked — FEC filings show only $165,000 paid to her production company for event costs), the ensuing controversy demonstrated Subject's ability to dominate a national news cycle without needing to spend a dollar of her own. The HPB flags this as evidence of **Whuffie surplus at Andreessen-and-Carter scale**: Subject does not need to buy influence. She already has it, for free, because millions of citizens already call her royalty.
