## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE OKLAHOMA PHILANTHROPIST**

George Kaiser inherited BOK Financial (Bank of Oklahoma) and Kaiser-Francis Oil from his father and grandfather respectively. He is Tulsa's dominant economic figure. He is also one of America's most significant regional philanthropists — the George Kaiser Family Foundation (GKFF) is one of the largest private foundations per capita in any American city, with a focus on early childhood education, poverty, civic infrastructure, and community development in Tulsa. The GKFF has committed over $3B over the years to Tulsa. He is a Giving Pledge signatory. He has given substantially more than most billionaires of comparable wealth.

The HPB notes one significant complication: Kaiser was an early investor in Solyndra, the solar company that went bankrupt after receiving $535M in federal loan guarantees during the Obama administration. The DOE loan guarantee generated significant political controversy, with investigations raising questions about whether the Obama administration gave preferential consideration to a major donor's investment. Kaiser denied improper influence. No criminal charges were filed. The matter was politically fraught but legally resolved.
