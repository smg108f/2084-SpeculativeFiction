## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE JUNCTION BOX DEMI-GOD**

Eaton Corporation plc presents the HPB with a nightmare scenario it has not previously had to formally classify: a **demi-god that did not seek apotheosis, but had it wired into the wall while no one was looking**. A 115-year-old maker of switchgear, busways, UPS units, and aerospace hydraulics has been swept, by forces entirely external to its own ambition, into the upper tier of global capital concentration — not because it built the miracle, but because the miracle cannot run without it. The HPB's working theory: Eaton is not a god. It is the conduit through which the gods draw current. That distinction may not matter to the Demi-God Act.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

Subject's value is held almost entirely in equity, manufacturing capacity, and — increasingly — in **predicted infrastructure dependency**. Eaton's PDUs, switchgear, busway, and UPS systems are now load-bearing components in nearly every hyperscale AI data center under construction; its value has risen on the strength of contracts for facilities that do not yet exist and workloads that have not yet run. The HPB flags this as a textbook **Concentration Hazard: Substrate Lock-In** — an entity whose worth derives less from what it has built than from the fact that nothing else can be built without it. Auditors note the irony that the subject's hoarding is, structurally, a form of *withholding the wires*.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

Eaton has made no meaningful gestures toward the **Infrastructure Gift** model favored by Saints — no donated grids, no Sanctuary Status negotiations, no public divestment theater. Its public-facing posture is industrial, not philanthropic: press releases about Nebraska switchgear plants and 800-volt DC reference architectures, not gifted hospitals. The HPB notes this with something approaching relief — an entity this load-bearing attempting a Saintly Pivot would functionally amount to *threatening to unplug civilization unless granted Sanctuary*. Subject has, perhaps wisely, never made that threat. It has also never needed to.

### **3. Influence Vector: The Substrate That Refuses to Be Seen**

Eaton's risk profile is unusual in that it exhibits **zero Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric**. No manifestos. No "apex predator" language. No stated theory of civilizational destiny. And yet its products now sit, quite literally, between every "breakthrough" announcement and the wall socket that powers it. The HPB classifies this as a **Substitution Hazard of the Quiet Type**: an entity that has not substituted itself for public power in *ideology*, but has, almost accidentally, substituted itself for public power in *fact*. You may thank an AI for thinking. The HPB's auditors would gently remind you that the AI was, at every step, leased its capacity to think through a junction box bearing this subject's name.

## **⚖️ THE VERDICT: THE INVISIBLE CONDUIT**

**Confidence Score: 71%**

Eaton is, by every numerical measure, a Demi-God Entity — its market capitalization sits roughly 153 times over threshold. And yet the HPB's auditors are reluctant to classify it alongside the Andreessens and Musks of the world, because Eaton has expressed no *ambition* commensurate with its scale; it has simply been picked up and carried by a wave of hyperscaler capital that it did not summon and cannot fully steer. This is not Saintly humility — it is structural blindness to its own gravitational pull. The HPB rates this **MARGINAL ASCENSION RISK**: the subject is a demi-god by net worth and an unwitting one by temperament, which makes it, if anything, more dangerous — a Force of Nature that has not yet noticed it has become one.

> **Audit Summary:** "Subject exhibits the rarest profile in our caseload: scale without self-awareness. It has not built a cathedral to itself; it has merely become the wiring inside everyone else's. We recommend close monitoring rather than immediate Summons — an entity this load-bearing, seized carelessly, risks taking the lights out for several million Human Citizens on its way down. Subject is, in the HPB's considered opinion, too plugged-in to unplug quickly."
