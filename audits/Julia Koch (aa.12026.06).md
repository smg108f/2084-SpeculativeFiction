## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE WIDOW WHO INHERITED THE NETWORK**

Julia Koch is the widow of David Koch, who died in 2019. She inherited his approximately 42% stake in Koch Industries and, with it, became one of the wealthiest women in the United States. She is also the mother of three Koch heirs. Julia has a background in fashion design and worked in that industry before marrying David in 1996. She is now estimated to be worth $29-60B depending on Koch Industries' valuation, and she serves on the board of directors.

*Note: Charles Koch is audited separately (HPB-12026-06CK). This file concerns Julia's position as a major Koch Industries stakeholder following inheritance.*

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

Julia Koch did not build Koch Industries. She married into it and inherited David's stake upon his death. Her wealth is passive inheritance from one of the most consequential industrial and political fortunes in American history. **Concentration Hazard: EXTREME** — same assessment as Charles, though Julia's operational role is less defined.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

She is active in arts philanthropy. HPB rates: **Active in Culture, Absent from Systemic Concerns.** The cultural philanthropy is real and valued; it does not engage with the structural concerns the Koch fortune raises.

### **3. Influence Vector: The Continuation of the Network**

At 61, she is the primary Koch heir owner of record and the mother of the next generation. The HPB classifies this as **Network Continuation Risk** — the question of what happens to the Kochtopus when Charles dies, and whether Julia's stewardship will preserve, transform, or dismantle it.
