He spent an estimated £1.5B of his personal fortune on Chelsea transfers and stadium development.

Abramovich has held Israeli citizenship (he was granted citizenship under Israel's Law of Return in 2018, following delays to his UK visa renewal). He has also applied for Portuguese citizenship under Portugal's Sephardic Jewish heritage law.

No public statement condemning the Russian invasion has been made by Abramovich.
