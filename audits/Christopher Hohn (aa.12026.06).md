## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE CLIMATE ACTIVIST HEDGIE**

Sir Christopher Hohn is the founder of TCI Fund Management, a London-based activist hedge fund. He is notable for two things that usually don't coexist in one person: extraordinarily aggressive activist investing that extracts maximum returns from portfolio companies, and one of the most substantial personal philanthropic commitments in British finance history.

**The Children's Investment Fund Foundation (CIFF):** Hohn co-founded CIFF with his then-wife Jamie Cooper. It has disbursed over $6B in grants focused on child health, education, and climate change mitigation — including significant funding for malaria control and child nutrition globally. After their divorce, the foundation structure became contested — a notable governance failure in what was an otherwise exemplary philanthropic vehicle.

**Climate activism:** TCI has pioneered shareholder activism on climate — filing resolutions, engaging management, and threatening divestment unless portfolio companies publish credible climate transition plans. Hohn has been one of the most vocal hedge fund managers calling for mandatory climate disclosure.

**Activist extractivism:** TCI's activist campaigns have involved aggressive cost-cutting demands, management replacement, and asset stripping at portfolio companies. The social costs of this approach — layoffs, community impacts, pension changes — are part of the accountability record.

**Pay:** Hohn has paid himself billions in carried interest and management fees — consistent with industry norms but extraordinary in absolute terms.

**Knighthood:** Hohn received a KBE for services to philanthropy (2014). The HPB notes this as a social recognition of his philanthropic contributions, reducing but not eliminating his burn obligations.
