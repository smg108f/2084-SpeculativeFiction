## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE CHICKEN CHAIRMAN**

Dan Cathy is the chairman of **Chick-fil-A**, the fast food chicken chain founded by his father S. Truett Cathy. Chick-fil-A has grown into one of the highest-revenue fast food chains in the United States — generating more per-location revenue than McDonald's — through a combination of operational excellence, exceptional customer service culture, and a distinctive Christian values identity that includes closing all locations on Sundays.

**Anti-LGBTQ+ funding:** Dan Cathy's 2012 public statement that Chick-fil-A supported "the biblical definition of the family unit" — and the revelation that the **WinShape Foundation** (the Cathy family charity funded by Chick-fil-A profits) had donated millions to organizations designated as anti-LGBTQ+ by civil rights organizations — created a national controversy. Organizations funded included the Family Research Council (FRC, designated as a hate group by the SPLC) and Exodus International (a now-defunct conversion therapy organization).

**The response:** Chick-fil-A subsequently announced it would stop funding controversial organizations and shifted WinShape's giving toward education, homelessness, and hunger. But this shift was contested — reporting in 2019 showed continued donations to organizations with anti-LGBTQ+ positions. Dan Cathy made conciliatory statements including meeting with LGBTQ+ advocates, but he has not repudiated the earlier positions with clarity.

**Employee treatment:** Chick-fil-A's franchise model is notable for its single-operator restriction (one person, one restaurant — unlike McDonald's multi-unit franchisees), creating a different labor dynamic. Operator pay is reportedly strong. Base crew pay is closer to industry standard.

**Food quality:** Chick-fil-A's product is, by fast food standards, relatively simple and consistent. Its supply chain has not attracted the same controversies as some competitors.

### **Verdict:** CRITICAL — documented institutional funding of organizations classified as hate groups; public statement creating hostile environment for LGBTQ+ employees and customers; subsequent repositioning was partial and contested; primary accountability in the family trio.
