## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE EGYPTIAN INDUSTRIALIST**

Nassef Sawiris is the youngest son of Onsi Sawiris and belongs to one of Egypt's most prominent business dynasties (siblings include Naguib Sawiris and Samih Sawiris). He built OCI NV into one of the world's largest nitrogen fertilizer and methanol producers, following the 2015 demerger from Orascom Construction. He also holds a significant personal stake in Adidas.

**OCI and nitrogen fertilizers:** OCI produces nitrogen fertilizers (ammonia, urea, UAN) — essential for global agriculture but also a significant source of greenhouse gas emissions. Nitrogen fertilizer production is one of the most energy-intensive industrial processes and is associated with downstream nitrous oxide emissions when applied to fields.

**Egyptian construction legacy:** The Orascom empire built Egypt's construction and industrial infrastructure through decades of close relationship with the Egyptian state — including during the Mubarak era. Questions about how the Sawiris family navigated Egypt's political economy over multiple regime transitions are inherent to the family story.

**Philanthropy:** Nassef Sawiris has donated to Egyptian education, healthcare, and the restoration of Notre Dame de Paris (a contribution to global cultural heritage).

**Political:** Relatively low-profile on Egyptian domestic politics compared to his brother Naguib.
