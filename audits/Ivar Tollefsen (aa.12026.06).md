## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE LANDLORD OF OSLO**

Ivar Tollefsen is Norway's richest person and the founder of Fredensborg AS — a private real estate company that has become the largest private residential landlord in Norway, owning tens of thousands of apartments across Scandinavia.

**The housing affordability crisis:** Norway, particularly Oslo, has experienced severe housing affordability deterioration — median home prices in Oslo represent among the highest price-to-income ratios in Europe. Tollefsen's Fredensborg has been a significant participant in this dynamic, acquiring rental properties at scale and benefiting from the structural shortage of affordable housing that his model does not relieve — it concentrates ownership, which tends to push prices upward.

**The nursing home controversy:** In 2020, Tollefsen purchased a chain of Norwegian nursing homes (Adleria). Norwegian media subsequently documented serious care quality problems at these facilities — understaffing, poor care conditions, and profit extraction from publicly funded eldercare contracts. The controversy sparked national debate about private equity ownership of Norwegian welfare services.

**Tax structure:** Tollefsen has structured his wealth through holding companies in ways that reduce Norwegian tax exposure — a practice legal under Norwegian law but controversial given that his wealth was built through Norwegian real estate supported by Norwegian infrastructure and the Norwegian housing market.

**Philanthropy:** Limited documented individual philanthropy at scale.

### **Verdict:** CRITICAL — housing market concentration exacerbating Norwegian affordability crisis; nursing home care quality scandal; tax structuring; low philanthropy relative to publicly-supported wealth accumulation.
