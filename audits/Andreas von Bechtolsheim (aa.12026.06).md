## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE MAN WHO WROTE THE CHECK**

Andreas "Andy" von Bechtolsheim co-founded Sun Microsystems (Stanford University Network) in 1982, helping establish the workstation era of computing. In 1998, he famously wrote a $100,000 check made out to "Google Inc." to Larry Page and Sergey Brin — before Google was incorporated, before its offices existed, before almost anyone outside Stanford knew what it was. That check is one of the most consequential investment decisions in technology history.

He co-founded Arista Networks (with David Cheriton — audited in Batch 4) to build high-performance cloud networking switches for hyperscale data centers. Arista is now one of the most important infrastructure companies in cloud computing.

**Low-profile frugal technologist:** Von Bechtolsheim is known for living simply despite his extraordinary wealth — reportedly driving modest cars, maintaining a low public profile, and focusing on engineering rather than business celebrity. He is German-born (Bavaria) and has maintained ties to German research institutions.

**The philanthropy gap:** Despite enormous wealth built across three generations of foundational computing technology (Sun, Google, Arista), von Bechtolsheim's documented philanthropy is modest relative to his net worth. He is not a Giving Pledge signatory.
