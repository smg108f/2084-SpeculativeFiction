## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE REUBEN BROTHER (DAVID)**

David Reuben and his brother Simon built **Reuben Brothers** into one of the UK's largest private fortunes through metals trading in post-Soviet Russia and Central Asia in the 1990s — specifically through Trans-World Metals/Trans-World Group, which exploited the chaos of Soviet industrial collapse to acquire control of Russian aluminum production. The Reuben brothers' role in the aluminium wars — the violent, criminally-adjacent struggle for control of Russian industrial assets in the 1990s — is a foundational accountability item.

**The aluminum wars context:** Trans-World Group operated in Russian aluminum during a period of extreme criminality and violence. Multiple killings were associated with the industry. The Reubens have consistently denied direct involvement in violence, but their wealth accumulation occurred in this environment and through the same asset acquisition methods. They exited Russia before Putin consolidated control.

**Jersey domicile:** The Reubens are domiciled in Jersey — a Channel Islands tax haven — despite their business being primarily UK-based. This is a documented tax optimization strategy.

**UK real estate:** Reuben Brothers has significant UK commercial real estate holdings, including properties in London that include public-benefit uses (data centers, commercial) alongside trophy assets.

**Philanthropy:** The Reuben Foundation funds education, medical research (particularly at Oxford and Imperial College London), and cultural institutions. Contributions are substantial but concentrated in elite institutions.
