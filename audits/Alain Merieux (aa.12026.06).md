## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE DIAGNOSTICIAN**

Alain Mérieux is the honorary chairman of **bioMérieux** and patriarch of the Mérieux family, which has been at the center of French medical diagnostics and vaccine science for over a century. His grandfather Marcel Mérieux was a student of Louis Pasteur. bioMérieux produces diagnostic systems for detecting infectious diseases — its products were central to COVID-19 testing globally.

**bioMérieux's contribution:** In vitro diagnostic testing is foundational public health infrastructure. bioMérieux's systems are used in hospitals, reference labs, and public health agencies worldwide to detect tuberculosis, HIV, sepsis, hepatitis, and — during COVID-19 — SARS-CoV-2. The company's global deployment of diagnostic capacity has saved millions of lives.

**Fondation Mérieux:** Alain Mérieux established the **Fondation Mérieux** — focused on infectious disease research and diagnostic capacity in developing countries. The foundation operates laboratories in Africa and Asia, trains scientists, and builds the health infrastructure that allows rapid response to outbreaks. This is among the most substantive infectious disease philanthropies in the world.

**Lyon and Pasteur legacy:** The Mérieux family has made Lyon a center of global biomedical excellence. Their institutional legacy — connecting Pasteurian science to modern diagnostic industry — is a genuine and historically significant contribution.

**COVID response:** bioMérieux was among the first companies to offer validated COVID-19 diagnostic tests in Europe, contributing to France's early testing capacity.
