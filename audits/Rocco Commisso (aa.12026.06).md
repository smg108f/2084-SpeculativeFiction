## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE CABLE BARON OF THE HEARTLAND**

Rocco Commisso was born in Calabria, southern Italy, and immigrated to the United States as a teenager. He founded Mediacom Communications in 1995 — building it into one of the largest cable television and internet providers in the United States, serving primarily rural and smaller markets in the Midwest and Southeast that larger operators (Comcast, Charter) declined to serve.

**The rural cable monopoly model:** Mediacom operates in markets where it is frequently the only broadband provider. This natural monopoly position generates significant profits with limited competitive pressure — the standard cable operator problem, concentrated in rural America where alternatives are fewest. Mediacom has faced customer complaints about pricing, service quality, and data caps that disproportionately affect rural households with no alternatives.

**Broadband access:** On the positive side, Mediacom has extended cable internet to rural communities that would otherwise have no broadband at all. The Bureau notes this is a mixed record: Mediacom is simultaneously the only broadband option for many rural communities and a provider that has exercised that monopoly position to charge above-market rates.

**ACF Fiorentina:** In 2019, Commisso purchased ACF Fiorentina — the storied Florentine football club — for approximately $170M. He has invested heavily in the club, funding construction of the Viola Park training facility and pursuing plans for a new Fiorentina stadium. His stewardship of Fiorentina has been broadly praised by the club's supporters and Italian football observers.

**Philanthropy:** Commisso donated $75M to Columbia University (his alma mater) — the largest gift in Columbia's history at that time. He has funded Italian-American causes and programs in Calabria.

**Immigration story:** Commisso is a prominent example of the immigrant success narrative — arriving from rural Calabria, attending Columbia on a soccer scholarship, building a cable empire. He has been an advocate for immigration as an American strength.
