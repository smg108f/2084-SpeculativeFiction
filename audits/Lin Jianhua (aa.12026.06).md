## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE SOLAR FILM KING**

Lin Jianhua is the CEO and co-founder (with his wife Zhang Hong) of **Hangzhou First PV Material** — China's leading manufacturer of **EVA (ethylene vinyl acetate) encapsulant film** used in solar photovoltaic panels. EVA film is the transparent polymer layer that protects solar cells inside panels, and Hangzhou First controls a dominant share of global supply.

**The clean energy role:** Solar panel manufacturing is a foundational enabler of the global energy transition. Hangzhou First's encapsulant film is inside solar panels on rooftops and in solar farms across every continent. The company's growth has paralleled — and enabled — the dramatic cost reduction in solar energy. This is a genuine and significant contribution to decarbonization.

**Manufacturing and labor:** Hangzhou First's operations are in the Hangzhou industrial zone. Chinese solar supply chain labor standards are subject to ongoing scrutiny — the sector has faced questions about forced labor in upstream polysilicon supply chains (Xinjiang), though encapsulant film is downstream of polysilicon and not directly implicated.

**Concentration risk:** Hangzhou First's market dominance in encapsulant film creates supply chain concentration risk for global solar manufacturing. A single company controlling a large share of a critical component in the energy transition is a systemic concern.

**Spousal co-founder:** Zhang Hong (wife) is also a billionaire through her VP role and shareholding. This is noted as a family enterprise.

### **Verdict:** MARGINAL — solar supply chain enabling role is significant Whuffie credit; manufacturing concentration risk noted; labor standard scrutiny applies; burns are reduced.
