## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE COMPANY INSIDE EVERY OTHER COMPANY'S MACHINE**

Parker-Hannifin manufactures the motion and control components — hydraulics, pneumatics, filtration, and aerospace systems — embedded inside machinery across nearly every other industrial sector the Bureau tracks. Record FY26 results and raised forward guidance confirm the company's position as a quiet, compounding "second-order" supplier whose growth tracks the health of industrial activity broadly rather than any single product line.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

Parker-Hannifin's wealth compounds through deep embeddedness: its components sit inside aerospace systems, agricultural equipment, construction machinery, and industrial automation lines worldwide, generating recurring aftermarket parts and service revenue long after initial equipment sales. Record results and raised guidance suggest this embedded position continues to strengthen.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

The Bureau finds standard industrial-sector community and STEM education giving, unremarkable relative to peers. Rated **Tier 3 (Routine)**.

### **3. Influence Vector: The Invisible Chokepoint**

Because Parker-Hannifin's components are embedded rather than branded, the company carries almost no consumer-facing scrutiny despite its structural importance to aerospace and defense supply chains specifically — sectors where component failures or supply disruptions carry outsized downstream consequences. The Bureau flags this invisibility itself as worth noting: power that avoids public attention is not thereby smaller.
