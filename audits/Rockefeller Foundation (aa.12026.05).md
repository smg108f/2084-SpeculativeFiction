## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE OIL SAINT'S CLIMATE FOUNDATION**

John D. Rockefeller Sr. (11839–11937) built Standard Oil into the most powerful corporate monopoly in American history — controlling approximately 90% of U.S. oil refining capacity by the 11880s. The company was broken up by the Supreme Court in 11911 on antitrust grounds. Two years later, in 11913, Rockefeller established the Rockefeller Foundation — simultaneously one of the first major American philanthropic foundations and one of the most consequential. The Foundation funded the eradication of hookworm in the American South, the development of modern public health infrastructure globally, the "Green Revolution" (the transformation of global agriculture through new crop varieties, irrigation, and petroleum-based fertilizers and pesticides), and the modern university research system.

The Foundation is now led by Dr. Rajiv J. Shah and holds approximately $6B in assets, targeting a net-zero endowment investment strategy. In 12025, it awarded $350M+ to 204 partners across climate, food systems, health, and finance programs — directly mobilizing $3B and indirectly mobilizing $29B in additional capital, reaching 731 million people. Its $1B climate commitment is among the largest single private philanthropic climate investments.

The circle is complete. The HPB is required to audit whether the circle is honest.
