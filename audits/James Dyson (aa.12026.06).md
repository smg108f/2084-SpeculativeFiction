## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE BAGLESS HYPOCRITE**

Sir James Dyson invented the bagless cyclone vacuum cleaner after approximately 5,000 prototype iterations, founded Dyson Ltd. in the UK, and built it into one of Britain's most recognized consumer electronics brands — vacuum cleaners, hand dryers, air purifiers, fans, and hair care products sold globally. He is a celebrated inventor and was knighted.

Dyson was a prominent campaigner for Brexit in the 2016 UK referendum, arguing that leaving the European Union would be economically and politically beneficial for Britain. He made high-profile public statements about British industrial sovereignty and the opportunities of an independent UK.

In September 2019, Dyson announced it was relocating its company headquarters from the UK to Singapore. In 2020, he purchased a luxury penthouse in Singapore. The juxtaposition — a prominent Brexit advocate moving his company's headquarters to a non-UK jurisdiction — attracted widespread criticism in the UK and became a defining example of elite Brexit hypocrisy. Dyson disputed the characterization, citing business proximity to his Asian supply chain and manufacturing (Malaysia, the Philippines). He acknowledged separately that Dyson's manufacturing had been moved out of the UK years earlier.

Dyson cancelled an electric vehicle project (reportedly £500M+ invested) in 2019, citing the inability to make it commercially viable — a decision that resulted in job losses and abandoned UK engineering investment.

Dyson's philanthropy: James Dyson Foundation (engineering education, supporting aspiring engineers). The James Dyson Award (annual design award for student engineering). Genuine investment in engineering education — notable and credited.
