## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE LAST GREAT PRIVATE FASHION HOUSE**

Giorgio Armani founded Giorgio Armani S.p.A. in 1975 with Sergio Galeotti. It has become one of the world's most recognized luxury fashion brands — spanning Armani, Emporio Armani, Armani Exchange, and other lines. Armani has resisted selling to the luxury conglomerates (LVMH, Kering) that have absorbed most of his peers. He is in his 90s and has kept the company 100% privately held, making him one of fashion's last true independent patriarch-owners. He has publicly discussed his succession planning concerns and has set up a foundation to manage the brand after his death.

Armani is one of the most recognized personal brand names in the world. He is also known for supporting charitable causes — particularly medical (he donated significantly during COVID-19 to Italian hospitals) and cultural. He has faced some criticism for the environmental footprint of fast fashion lines (Armani Exchange) and for the broader luxury industry's sustainability claims vs. practices.
