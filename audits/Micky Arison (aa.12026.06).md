## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE CRUISE SHIP EMPEROR**

Micky Arison (son of Carnival founder Ted Arison) ran **Carnival Corporation** as CEO for decades, building it into the world's largest cruise company — operating Carnival Cruise Line, Princess Cruises, Holland America, Costa, P&O Cruises, AIDA, and others across 100+ ships.

**COVID-19 pandemic accountability — severe:** Carnival Corporation's COVID response is among the most documented corporate failures of the pandemic era:
- The Diamond Princess (Princess Cruises) became one of the world's first major COVID-19 outbreak sites in February 2020, infecting 700+ passengers and crew while the ship remained at sea and in Yokohama harbor.
- Despite the Diamond Princess outbreak, Carnival continued sailing other ships for weeks, knowingly allowing COVID-positive passengers to board and disembark.
- The Grand Princess continued sailing after crew showed COVID symptoms; dozens of additional ships followed.
- Internal communications revealed Carnival leadership was aware of the risks and continued operations to protect revenue.
- Multiple class-action lawsuits and regulatory actions resulted.

**Environmental record — chronic:** Carnival has one of the worst environmental records in the cruise industry:
- 2019: Carnival pleaded guilty to criminal charges of illegally dumping oily water and falsifying records, paying $20M in fines — the largest criminal penalty in maritime history at the time. This was a repeat violation of a 2016 probation order.
- Cruise ships produce extraordinary air pollution (sulfur oxides, particulate matter) in port cities.
- Sewage dumping, food waste, and grey water discharge in sensitive marine ecosystems.

**Labor — labor exploitation at sea:** Cruise ships are registered in flags of convenience (Liberia, Panama, Bahamas), allowing Carnival to pay crew members from developing nations below U.S. minimum wage and exempt them from U.S. labor protections. This is a deliberate structural choice.

**NBA ownership:** Micky Arison owns the Miami Heat. This is not an adverse finding.

### **Verdict:** CRITICAL — COVID pandemic conduct (knowing risk continuation); criminal environmental conviction (probation violation); systematic labor exploitation through flag-of-convenience structure; FSS designated.

**Floating Sovereign Station:** FSS CARNIVAL — a decommissioned Carnival vessel, retrofitted for administrative detention, anchored in Yokohama harbor adjacent to the Diamond Princess's documented outbreak location.
