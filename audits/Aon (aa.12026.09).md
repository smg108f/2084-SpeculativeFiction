## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE COMPANY THAT PRICES EVERYONE ELSE'S RISK**

Aon is one of a small number of global insurance and reinsurance brokerages that intermediate risk placement for the majority of large corporations and institutions worldwide, giving it structural visibility into and influence over how risk is priced and allocated across the global economy without itself carrying the underlying insurance liability.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

Aon's wealth is brokerage-commission-based, extracted as a percentage of the risk placement it arranges rather than the risk itself, a fee-on-fee structure that compounds regardless of whether the underlying insurance market is hard or soft.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

Standard risk-advisory and human-capital consulting services for clients, framed as value-add rather than public benefit specifically. Rated **Tier 3 (Routine)**.

### **3. Influence Vector: The Information Asymmetry Broker**

As an intermediary sitting between insurers and the insured, Aon holds informational advantages over both sides of every transaction it arranges, a structural position the Bureau flags as worth monitoring given the brokerage sector's history of contingent-commission conflicts of interest.
