## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE PROPHET OF THE CLOUD**

Marc Benioff co-founded Salesforce in 1999 — pioneering the Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) delivery model at scale, making enterprise CRM software accessible via the cloud rather than expensive on-premise installation. Salesforce has grown into a $200B+ enterprise software company. Benioff is also one of the most vocal business leaders on social issues — climate, inequality, homelessness, and "stakeholder capitalism."

**The 1-1-1 model:** Salesforce established the 1-1-1 philanthropic model (1% of equity, 1% of product, 1% of employee time to philanthropic causes) — now adopted by thousands of companies. This is a genuine structural innovation in corporate philanthropy.

**Time magazine:** Benioff purchased Time magazine in 2018 with his wife Lynne — committing to independent journalism. Time has maintained editorial independence under his ownership, including coverage that has not been favorable to billionaires.

**The Hawaii purchase and homelessness:** Benioff has been vocal about San Francisco's homelessness crisis — simultaneously one of the few major tech CEOs to advocate for higher taxes on companies like Salesforce to fund homelessness services (Proposition C, 2018), and someone who purchased a large Hawaii estate and has homes in multiple states. The tension between advocacy and personal lifestyle is noted.

**Salesforce labor:** Salesforce conducted significant layoffs in 2023 (approximately 10% of workforce, ~8,000 people) after aggressive pandemic-era hiring, while Benioff was publicly speaking about stakeholder capitalism. The disconnect was widely noted.

**Political:** Benioff supported Biden, has donated to Democratic causes, and has been a consistent voice for progressive corporate policy.

### **Verdict:** MARGINAL — genuine philanthropic structural innovation; Time ownership maintaining journalism independence; 2023 layoffs after stakeholder capitalism advocacy is a documented tension; net positive contribution with real contradictions.
