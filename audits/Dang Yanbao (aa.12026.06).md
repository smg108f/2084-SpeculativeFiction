## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE COAL BARON OF SHANXI**

Dang Yanbao built his wealth through Jinneng Holding Group, a coal mining and energy conglomerate in Shanxi Province — China's coal heartland. Shanxi has historically been the dominant coal-producing province in China, and Jinneng is one of its major operators. Coal is China's dominant electricity source, and Shanxi's coal powers much of the country. Dang has benefited from a Chinese policy environment in the 2000s and 2010s that consolidated small-scale private coal mines into larger operators — creating significant wealth concentration among the winners of consolidation.
