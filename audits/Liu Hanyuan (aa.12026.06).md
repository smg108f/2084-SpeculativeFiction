## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE FISH FEED SOLAR BILLIONAIRE**

Liu Hanyuan founded Tongwei Group in Sichuan Province, starting in fish feed production. Tongwei is now China's largest fish feed manufacturer — producing feed for aquaculture operations across China and Southeast Asia. More unexpectedly, Liu pivoted Tongwei into solar energy — specifically solar cell manufacturing — and Tongwei Solar has become one of the world's largest producers of crystalline silicon solar cells (the key component in solar panels). Tongwei's "Fishery-Solar Symbiosis" model — building solar panels over fish ponds, allowing simultaneous aquaculture and solar power generation — is an innovative dual-use model with documented environmental and economic benefits. Liu is politically active; he is a National People's Congress delegate.
