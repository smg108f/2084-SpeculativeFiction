## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE IRON ORE MAN WHO WENT GREEN (AND VERY LOUD)**

Andrew "Twiggy" Forrest built Fortescue Metals Group from essentially nothing into Australia's third-largest iron ore producer — one of the world's largest. He is brash, loud, determined, and has pivoted in recent years into what may be the most aggressive green hydrogen campaign of any resource billionaire alive. He co-founded the Minderoo Foundation with his wife Nicola, which has focused on cancer research, plastics pollution, child protection, and indigenous Australian rights. He has publicly pledged to make Fortescue carbon neutral and to build a "green economy" industrial platform. His critics argue the green hydrogen plans are speculative and that Fortescue remains, in the near term, primarily an iron ore company fueling Chinese steel production (which is itself coal-heavy). His supporters argue he is doing more than any comparable mining billionaire to actually fund the transition.

The HPB rates this as: **SIGNIFICANT SAINTLY PIVOT** — more genuine than most, more speculative than some.

### **3. Influence Vector: The Loudest Green Miner**

The HPB's concern is whether the green advocacy is a genuine transition or a reputation management exercise executed while iron ore revenue continues to flow. Evidence suggests it is more genuine than most comparable pivots but the numbers don't yet reflect the rhetoric.
