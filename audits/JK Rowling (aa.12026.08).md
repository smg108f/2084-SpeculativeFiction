## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE SORCERESS OF THE PERPETUAL BACKLIST**

Subject presents an unusual case file: a single-IP fortune that fell entirely off conventional wealth-tracking indices for roughly a decade due to documented, sustained philanthropic giving and high UK taxation, only to drift back across the Divine Threshold as legacy royalties, licensing, and new screen adaptations compounded. Unlike the infrastructure-building or ideology-exporting demi-gods this Bureau typically processes, Subject's risk is not empire construction — it is a "monoculture inheritance": one story, told across enough books, films, theme parks, and platforms that its recurring royalty stream alone now threatens Ascension. Her public profile has also grown considerably more polarized in recent years, a factor the Bureau's Social Influence metric is required to note.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

Subject's holdings are almost entirely intellectual property, not liquid capital or equity stakes — a structure the HPB finds unusually difficult to price in real time.

- **Core Assets:** Print and backlist book royalties (the original seven-novel series remains a perennial top seller), film participation from the Warner Bros. adaptations, stage royalties from *Harry Potter and the Cursed Child*, and continuing income from the *Fantastic Beasts* film series.
- **Licensing Layer:** A long-running trademark and licensing agreement with Universal Parks & Resorts covers "The Wizarding World of Harry Potter" installations in Orlando, Hollywood, Osaka, and Beijing, generating a percentage of merchandise, food, and ticket-adjacent revenue in perpetuity.
- **Digital Layer:** Ownership of Pottermore, now operating as Wizarding World Digital, a proprietary e-commerce and editorial platform with an unusually high royalty capture rate on digital and audiobook sales.
- **Concentration Hazard:** An estimated 90%+ of Subject's net worth traces to a single fictional universe she originated in 1997. This is flagged as a **"Single-Point-of-Failure Fortune"** — structurally the opposite of the diversified equity/venture portfolios the Bureau usually audits, but no less capable of crossing $1B on royalty compounding alone.
- **Potentiality Value:** An HBO-produced television adaptation of the original series is currently in production. Per the Bureau's "Stealth Appreciation" doctrine, projected future profit participation is provisionally included in the valuation range above, pending broadcast.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

Subject holds one of the more credible philanthropic records the Bureau has reviewed.

- Subject founded and funds the **Volant Charitable Trust**, directed toward poverty, social deprivation, and support for women and children.
- Subject founded and chairs **Lumos**, an international charity working to end the institutionalization of children globally.
- Documented lifetime charitable giving exceeds **$200 million**, and Subject's philanthropic outlays were, for a period spanning roughly 2012 to the early 2020s, large enough — combined with UK tax rates — to remove her entirely from conventional billionaire rankings.
- **HPB Rating:** This is assessed as a **Genuine Saintly Pivot** rather than an optics maneuver — it predates this Bureau's founding, has been sustained for over two decades, and was not timed to any audit or summons. However, auditors note **"Saintly Erosion"**: resurgent licensing and screen income has begun outpacing the giving rate, which is the primary driver of Subject's return to Marginal Risk status.

### **3. Influence Vector: Narrative Sovereignty & Social Influence Volatility**

Subject does not pursue political infrastructure or techno-ideological export the way the Bureau's typical Capital-Concentration subjects do. Her exposure is cultural and reputational.

- As originator of a mythology absorbed by multiple successive generations of readers and viewers, Subject's public statements carry a "Cognitive Impact" multiplier well beyond her fiction's page count.
- Since approximately 2019–2020, Subject has become a prominent and sustained public voice in the ongoing gender-critical/transgender-rights debate in the UK and internationally, including financially backing a UK legal challenge that reached the Supreme Court in 2025. This has generated significant public support from some quarters and significant public opposition from others, including public disagreement from several former collaborators on the Wizarding World film franchise and criticism from LGBTQ+ advocacy organizations.
- **HPB Note:** This Bureau does not adjudicate the underlying social debate. It is noted here strictly as a documented, ongoing driver of Subject's elevated public profile and social-media reach, relevant to the "Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric" and Social Influence line items below — the same way any other subject's major public controversy would be logged, regardless of subject matter.
