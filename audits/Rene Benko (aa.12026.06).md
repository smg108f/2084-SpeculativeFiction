## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE COLLAPSE KING**

René Benko is the Austrian entrepreneur who built **Signa Holdings** into one of Europe's largest real estate and retail conglomerates — owning luxury real estate across Vienna, Berlin, Hamburg, and New York (including stakes in the Chrysler Building and other trophy properties), and controlling major European retail chains including Galeria Karstadt Kaufhof (Germany's largest department store chain) and KaDeWe.

**The collapse:** In November 2023, Signa Holdings filed for insolvency — the largest insolvency in Austrian history. The collapse affected: thousands of retail workers (Galeria Karstadt Kaufhof employees), creditors across European banking systems, and investors who had trusted Signa's opaque structure. The implosion revealed a corporate governance structure of extraordinary complexity — hundreds of holding entities designed to obscure ownership and debt.

**Criminal investigation:** Austrian prosecutors launched a criminal investigation into Benko following the collapse, focusing on suspected fraud, embezzlement, and breach of trust. The investigation was ongoing as of the HPB assessment date. Benko was declared personally insolvent.

**Retail workers:** Galeria Karstadt Kaufhof's repeated bankruptcies under Signa's ownership resulted in thousands of German retail worker layoffs across multiple rounds of restructuring. Workers were the primary victims of Signa's overleveraged structure.

**Lavish lifestyle:** While Signa collapsed, Benko had been living at extraordinary luxury — yachts, private jets, and properties — much of which is now subject to insolvency proceedings.

### **Verdict:** CRITICAL — insolvency with criminal investigation pending; thousands of retail workers harmed; German banking system exposure; governance opacity; personal insolvency means standard burn capacity is severely constrained.
