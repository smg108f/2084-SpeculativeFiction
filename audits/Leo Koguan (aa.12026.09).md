## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE SHAREHOLDER WHO WORSHIPS THE STOCK**

Koguan co-founded SHI International, a major IT reseller, but the Bureau's file is dominated by his second act: an outsized, public, ideologically-charged personal bet on Tesla stock, making him one of the company's largest individual (non-founder) shareholders. He has continued adding to the position in 2026, including a reported $180M purchase, framing the investment in explicitly devotional terms toward its CEO.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

Concentrated across SHI equity and an unusually large single-stock personal position. **Concentration Hazard: SEVERE — Single-Issuer.** The Bureau flags single-stock devotion at this scale as a distinct hazard from diversified hoarding: it ties a demi-god's fate, and voting behavior, to one company's charismatic leadership.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

Limited public philanthropic record separate from his investment activity. **HPB Optics Rating: LOW.**

### **3. Influence Vector: The Proxy Believer**

The Bureau's newest concern category: shareholders who function less as capital allocators than as ideological proxies for a company's founder, amplifying and defending leadership decisions (including executive pay packages) with personal capital and public advocacy. This converts ordinary shareholder capital into something closer to political patronage.
