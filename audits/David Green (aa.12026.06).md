## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE EVANGELICAL RETAIL PATRIARCH**

David Green founded Hobby Lobby in 1972 with a $600 loan and built it into the largest privately held arts-and-crafts retail chain in the United States, with more than 900 stores and 43,000 employees. Green is an evangelical Christian and has built his business around his faith: Hobby Lobby closes on Sundays, does not carry certain products (shot glasses, Halloween merchandise, Hanukkah items — coverage varies), and in 2014, Green was the plaintiff in *Burwell v. Hobby Lobby Stores*, the Supreme Court case where the Court ruled 5-4 that closely held corporations could be exempt from providing contraceptive coverage to employees on religious grounds under RFRA. The decision fundamentally altered the landscape of religious exemption in employment law. Green has donated heavily to evangelical Christian causes and the Museum of the Bible in Washington, D.C. — which itself was embroiled in a scandal when it was revealed that Hobby Lobby had purchased $1.6M worth of smuggled Iraqi antiquities (Dead Sea Scroll fragments later found to be forgeries; cuneiform tablets looted from Iraqi archaeological sites) — resulting in a $3M forfeiture to the U.S. government in 2020.
