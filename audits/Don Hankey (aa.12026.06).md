## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE SUBPRIME CAR LOAN KING**

Don Hankey is the founder and chairman of Westlake Financial Partners — one of America's largest subprime auto lenders. Westlake finances vehicle purchases for borrowers with poor or no credit history, at interest rates that can exceed 25% APR, secured by the vehicle being financed.

**The subprime auto lending model:** Subprime auto lending targets the most economically vulnerable consumers — those who need a car to work but cannot qualify for conventional financing. The model depends on high interest rates and frequent defaults. When a borrower defaults, the lender repossesses the car — potentially destroying the borrower's ability to commute to work, accelerating the financial spiral. Westlake has been one of the most aggressive lenders in this space.

**Regulatory actions:** The Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB) has taken enforcement action against Westlake Financial — including findings of deceptive add-on product sales (products like GAP insurance and extended warranties that were sold to borrowers without full disclosure of cost or terms). Westlake reached settlements related to these practices.

**The debt collection model:** Subprime auto lending companies typically employ aggressive debt collection practices, including GPS tracking and remote disabling devices installed in financed vehicles — allowing lenders to remotely disable a car if a payment is missed. Westlake has used these technologies. The practice is legal but raises significant consumer protection and dignity concerns.

**Philanthropy:** Hankey has donated to conservative causes and healthcare in California. Limited scale relative to wealth.

### **Verdict:** CRITICAL — predatory subprime auto lending targeting economically vulnerable consumers; CFPB enforcement for deceptive practices; remote vehicle disabling; wealth built on consumer financial distress.
