## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE DISCOUNT TOOL KING**

Eric Smidt is the owner and chairman of Harbor Freight Tools — America's largest privately held discount tool retailer. Harbor Freight sells tools, power equipment, and automotive accessories at prices dramatically below Home Depot or Lowe's equivalents, targeting price-sensitive consumers, tradespeople on tight margins, hobbyists, and DIY users. The chain has expanded from a mail-order business founded by his father Allan Smidt in 1977 to over 1,500 retail locations.

Harbor Freight's business model: manufacture or source tools primarily from Chinese factories at very low cost, sell at aggressive discount, and accept that some products will be lower quality than professional-grade alternatives. The chain attracts consumers who need a tool for occasional use and cannot justify professional tool pricing.

**Product safety:** Harbor Freight has faced documented product safety concerns over the years — CPSC recalls, some OSHA flagging of certain tool types. The chain's tools have been involved in injury incidents. The discount pricing model inherently applies cost pressure to safety margins. This is the HPB's primary concern alongside the minimal philanthropy.

No significant documented philanthropy for Eric Smidt personally.
