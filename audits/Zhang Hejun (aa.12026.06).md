## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE THIN FILM SOLAR VISIONARY (OR FRAUD?)**

Zhang Hejun is the founder of Hanergy Group — a Chinese energy company that pivoted from hydropower to become the world's largest thin-film solar manufacturer. In 2015, Hanergy Thin Film Power was one of Hong Kong's most valuable listed companies, making Zhang briefly one of China's richest people. Then, in May 2015, the stock lost half its value in minutes, trading was suspended by Hong Kong's SFC, and a years-long regulatory investigation began.

**The Hanergy controversy:** The SFC found that Hanergy Thin Film Power's listed entity derived the majority of its revenue from transactions with its private parent (also controlled by Zhang) — essentially Zhang's listed company paying Zhang's private company, inflating the listed company's revenues and share price. This circular transaction structure, combined with the opaque nature of thin-film solar sales, raised fundamental questions about whether Hanergy Thin Film Power was a real business or a share price inflation mechanism.

**The technology:** Thin-film solar is a genuine technology — lighter, flexible, and applicable to surfaces where conventional silicon panels cannot be used. Whether Hanergy's specific technology worked at scale as claimed remains contested.

**Resolution:** Zhang Hejun reached a settlement with the SFC in 2020 — paying a fine and accepting a market ban. Trading in Hanergy shares remained suspended for years.
