## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE FALL OF WANDA**

Wang Jianlin founded Dalian Wanda Group and built it into China's largest property developer and, briefly, a global entertainment empire. He was China's richest man in 2015. Then, as part of Beijing's 2017 crackdown on "irrational" overseas acquisitions by Chinese conglomerates, Wanda was forced to divest most of its international holdings at significant losses.

**The peak:** Wanda acquired AMC Theatres (largest US cinema chain), Legendary Entertainment (Hollywood studio behind Batman, Jurassic World), Sunseeker Yachts (UK luxury boats), stakes in Spanish football clubs, and other global assets in a spending spree that made Wang Jianlin a globally recognized figure.

**The fall:** In 2017, Chinese regulators restricted Wanda's credit lines and flagged its overseas acquisitions as financial stability risks. Wanda was forced to sell its hotel and tourism projects in China (to Sunac and LongFor), divest its European cinema chains, and reduce its Legendary stake. AMC was subsequently IPO'd. The forced divestiture eliminated much of the value created and damaged numerous stakeholders.

**Wanda Plazas:** The 400+ shopping centers built across Chinese cities have reshaped urban retail in China — often displacing traditional markets and street commerce with enclosed mall formats, in a dynamic similar to what Alexander Otto's ECE did in Europe.

**State relationship:** Wang Jianlin's rise and fall illustrates the defining risk of Chinese private wealth — it exists at the pleasure of the state. His fall was not criminal but politically managed.
