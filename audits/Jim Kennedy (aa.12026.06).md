## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE COX PATRIARCH**

Jim Kennedy served as chairman of Cox Enterprises for many years, stewarding the family conglomerate founded by his grandfather James Middleton Cox — a newspaper publisher who also ran for President of the United States in 1920 (losing to Warren Harding). Cox Enterprises is one of America's largest privately held companies.

**Cox Automotive:** The most significant current business — Cox owns Manheim, the world's largest automotive auction house; Autotrader and Kelley Blue Book (the dominant US used car valuation and marketplace platforms); Dealer.com and other dealer software platforms. Cox effectively has a near-monopoly position in US automotive data and wholesale vehicle transactions — the infrastructure of the used car economy.

**Cox Communications:** The sixth largest US cable and internet provider, operating primarily in the South and West. Broadband pricing practices, customer service record, and monopoly pricing in local markets are standard cable industry concerns.

**Cox Media Group:** Newspapers (Atlanta Journal-Constitution), TV stations, radio — sold several properties in recent years as print media has contracted.

**Philanthropy:** Cox family philanthropy exists (Cox Foundation, various Atlanta civic donations) but is not prominent at scale relative to enterprise value.
