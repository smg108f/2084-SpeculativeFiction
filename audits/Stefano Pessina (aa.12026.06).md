## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE PHARMACY EMPEROR**

Stefano Pessina is the Italian-born executive chairman of Walgreens Boots Alliance — the company he built through a series of pharmaceutical distribution and pharmacy retail mergers culminating in the 2014 merger of Alliance Boots (which he had taken private with KKR in 2007) with Walgreens. Walgreens Boots Alliance is the second-largest pharmacy chain in the US (Walgreens) and operates Boots in the UK and Europe.

**The opioid settlement:** In 2021, Walgreens — along with CVS and Rite Aid — was held liable in a landmark opioid case (Lake County and Trumbull County, Ohio) for its role in dispensing opioids at a pace that pharmacists documented concerns about. In 2022-2023, Walgreens agreed to pay $5.7B over 15 years to settle opioid lawsuits. Pessina was executive chairman throughout the opioid epidemic peak and the litigation period. The settlement acknowledged that Walgreens' pharmacies had dispensed opioids in volumes that contributed to the epidemic.

**Monaco residency:** Pessina is a resident of Monaco, one of the most prominent tax havens for European billionaires. His Italian citizenship and Monaco residency means he avoids Italian and US tax obligations on a fortune built substantially through the US pharmacy market.

**Pharmacy consolidation:** Walgreens and CVS together dominate US retail pharmacy — their market concentration affects drug pricing, pharmacist working conditions, and rural pharmacy access (as independent pharmacies close unable to compete).
