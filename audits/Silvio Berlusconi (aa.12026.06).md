## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE CAVALIERE**

Silvio Berlusconi was the most consequential Italian politician of the post-war era and one of the most complex figures in European political economy: media mogul, AC Milan owner, three-time Prime Minister of Italy, and subject of decades of criminal proceedings.

**Media monopoly and political conflict of interest:** Berlusconi built Mediaset into Italy's dominant private broadcaster — then entered politics as Prime Minister while retaining ownership of three commercial TV channels (Canale 5, Italia 1, Rete 4) and indirect control over RAI as head of government. This concentration — controlling both Italy's dominant private media and its public broadcaster as head of state — represented an unprecedented conflict of interest in a Western democracy.

**Criminal proceedings:** Berlusconi faced dozens of criminal trials over his career. Convictions included: 2013 tax fraud conviction (sentenced to 4 years, converted to community service due to age); multiple acquittals on other charges including bribery. He was removed from the Italian Senate in 2013 following the tax fraud conviction.

**The bunga bunga proceedings:** From 2010, Berlusconi faced charges related to payments to a minor (the "Ruby" case). He was convicted at first instance, acquitted on appeal, then reconvicted in a complex series of proceedings. These cases involved multiple women and allegations of organized prostitution at Villa Arcore.

**AC Milan:** Berlusconi owned and transformed AC Milan into one of Europe's most successful football clubs — 8 Serie A titles, 5 Champions League titles under his ownership. He later purchased Monza FC and was lifting them through Italian football's divisions before his death.
