---

## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE MAN WHO HELD THE VACCINE**

Stevanato's family business manufactures the glass vials that carried a meaningful share of the world's COVID-19 vaccine doses, converting a century-old Italian glassworks into a pandemic-era public-health chokepoint. Unlike most Ascension-risk subjects, his wealth was built by literally enabling a global "Saintly Pivot" event — the vaccine rollout — rather than resisting one.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

Wealth is held in Stevanato Group equity, publicly listed since its 2021 U.S. IPO. **Concentration Hazard: MODERATE.** Family retains majority control post-IPO, but the underlying business is transparent, audited, and produces a physically necessary good rather than abstract financial instruments.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

The strongest natural Saintly narrative in this audit cycle: the company's core product literally delivered a pandemic-ending medical intervention at global scale. **HPB Optics Rating: HIGH.** The Bureau notes, however, that the family profited substantially from a public health emergency — "Pandemic Windfall" status complicates a pure Saint reading.

### **3. Influence Vector: The Chokepoint, Not the Cure**

Stevanato Group does not make vaccines; it makes the containers that make vaccines deliverable. This is infrastructure-adjacent power of a subtle kind — control over a physical bottleneck in global pharmaceutical supply chains that becomes visible only during a crisis.
