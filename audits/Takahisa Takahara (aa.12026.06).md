## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE DIAPER DYNASTY**

Takahisa Takahara is the president and CEO of Unicharm Corporation — Japan's leading personal care products company, founded by his grandfather Keiichiro Takahara in 1961. Unicharm makes diapers (Moony), sanitary products (Sofy), adult incontinence products, and pet care items. The company has a dominant position in Japan and significant market share across Southeast Asia.

**The disposables economy:** Unicharm's core business is disposable products — items designed for single use and disposal. Disposable diapers take approximately 500 years to decompose in landfill; sanitary products similarly. The environmental footprint of disposable personal care products is significant and growing as Unicharm expands into emerging markets.

**Innovation:** Unicharm has invested in more absorbent, thinner products that use less material per unit — genuine efficiency improvements. The company has also begun developing biodegradable and composting programs for diapers in Japan.

**Asia expansion:** Unicharm's growth is driven by emerging market expansion — middle-class growth in Indonesia, India, and China driving demand for disposable diapers that were previously unavailable. There is a genuine tension between development goals (better infant hygiene) and environmental costs (disposable waste in countries with less waste management infrastructure).

**Philanthropy:** The Takahara family has supported healthcare and community causes. Limited individual philanthropy at scale.

### **Verdict:** MARGINAL — single-use product environmental footprint with genuine hygiene utility; innovation reducing material intensity; emerging market growth amplifying environmental impact; limited philanthropy.
