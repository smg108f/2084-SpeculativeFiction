## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE RAVEN KING**

Steve Bisciotti co-founded **Allegis Group** in 1983 — which grew into the largest privately-held staffing and recruitment company in the United States, with revenues exceeding $15B. He purchased a minority stake in the Baltimore Ravens in 2000 and became majority owner in 2004.

**Allegis Group:** Staffing companies occupy a complex accountability position — they provide employment access and workforce flexibility, but their business model depends on the existence of a contingent workforce that lacks the protections of direct employment. Allegis places workers in temporary, contract, and direct-hire positions across industries. The structural shift toward contingent work — which staffing companies facilitate and profit from — has reduced job security, benefits access, and career stability for many American workers.

**Baltimore Ravens:** The Ravens have been a significant Baltimore institution. Bisciotti has generally been regarded as a committed Baltimore owner. The team received public stadium subsidies (M&T Bank Stadium) — standard for NFL franchises.

**Ray Rice incident:** In 2014, Ravens running back Ray Rice was initially given a 2-game suspension for domestic violence after an elevator assault. When the full video surfaced, he was released and suspended indefinitely. Bisciotti and the Ravens were criticized for the inadequacy of the initial response and for whether the organization had seen the full video earlier than publicly stated. An independent investigation found no evidence the Ravens had seen the full video, but the handling of the incident reflected poorly on NFL and team culture around domestic violence.

**Philanthropy:** Bisciotti has given substantially to Maryland charities, youth sports, and education. The Steve and René Bisciotti Foundation is active in Maryland community giving.
