## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE CRANE KING**

Liang Wengen is the founder and chairman of Sany Group — China's largest and the world's third-largest construction machinery manufacturer, producing cranes, concrete pumps, excavators, and road machinery. Sany competes directly with Caterpillar, Liebherr, and Komatsu globally.

**Belt and Road:** Sany has been a major beneficiary and equipment supplier for China's Belt and Road Initiative (BRI) — infrastructure projects across Africa, Southeast Asia, and Central Asia. BRI infrastructure creates complex accountability questions: genuinely needed infrastructure in underserved regions, but financed with Chinese debt that has drawn criticism for sovereignty risk and conditions.

**Self-made founder:** Liang Wengen and three friends started Sany in 1989 in Hunan province with 60,000 yuan. The company's rise from provincial workshop to global construction equipment leader is one of China's most compelling entrepreneurial stories.

**Political:** Liang was elected to the National People's Congress in 2008. He was reportedly considered for the Politburo Standing Committee (China's top 7 leaders) in 2012 — an elevation that did not occur.

**Philanthropy:** Sany Foundation has donated to disaster relief, education, and poverty alleviation in China.

### **Verdict:** MARGINAL — self-made founder with genuine Sany enterprise; BRI supply chain accountability complex; political alignment with Chinese state structures; philanthropy substantive.
