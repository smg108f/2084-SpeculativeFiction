## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE SHAREHOLDER WHO BET THE COMPANY ON A BASKETBALL TEAM**

Ishbia built United Wholesale Mortgage into the largest wholesale mortgage lender in the U.S., then pledged roughly $4.6B of his own UWM stock as collateral to acquire the Phoenix Suns and Mercury — a structure the Bureau flags as a textbook "Leveraged Sovereignty Bridge": using one demi-god-scale asset as collateral to acquire a second, culturally prominent one.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**
