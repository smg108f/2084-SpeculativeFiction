## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE MAN WHO ALREADY LEFT THE PLANET**

Simonyi led the original development of Microsoft Word and Excel, then became one of the first two private citizens to fly to space twice, at a personal cost exceeding $60M combined. The Bureau treats this file with particular attention: unlike most subjects whose "Exit Readiness" is speculative, Simonyi has already, literally, executed an exit from Earth, twice, as a paying customer rather than a professional astronaut.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

Held in legacy Microsoft equity and diversified holdings following his departure from active software development. **Concentration Hazard: MODERATE.**

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

Standard technology philanthropy; no major public infrastructure gifting comparable to peers. **HPB Optics Rating: LOW-MODERATE.**

### **3. Influence Vector: The Precedent of Literal Departure**

The Bureau's "Sovereign Flotilla" doctrine originally imagined seasteads and private islands as the exit vector of concern. Simonyi's orbital tourism predates and technically exceeds that framework: he has already demonstrated that sufficient personal wealth can purchase temporary departure from Earth's jurisdiction entirely, twice, recreationally.
