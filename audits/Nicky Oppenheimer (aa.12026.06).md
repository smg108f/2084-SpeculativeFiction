## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE DIAMOND DYNASTY**

Nicky Oppenheimer is the third generation of the Oppenheimer dynasty that built De Beers into the world's dominant diamond cartel. His grandfather Ernest Oppenheimer founded the Oppenheimer empire; his father Harry Oppenheimer expanded it. Under Nicky Oppenheimer's chairmanship, De Beers controlled approximately 40-45% of global rough diamond production. In 2012, the Oppenheimer family sold their 40% De Beers stake to Anglo American for $5.1B.

**The De Beers legacy:**
- *The cartel:* De Beers built and operated the most successful commodity cartel in history — at its peak controlling 80-90% of global rough diamond supply through its Central Selling Organisation, fixing prices globally for decades. The US Department of Justice pursued De Beers for decades over this price-fixing; De Beers eventually pleaded guilty in 2004 and paid $10M.
- *Apartheid labor:* De Beers' South African mines operated under the apartheid system, including the compound/hostel system that separated Black miners from their families, paid racially differentiated wages, and maintained racially segregated facilities. Nicky Oppenheimer inherited this structure and presided over its post-apartheid transition.
- *Blood diamonds:* De Beers' operations historically intersected with conflict diamond supply chains in Angola, Sierra Leone, and the DRC. De Beers was a founding supporter of the Kimberley Process Certification Scheme, which addressed — imperfectly — conflict diamond trade.

**Post-De Beers:** Oppenheimer now manages Tswalu Kalahari — a significant private game reserve in the Northern Cape — and Fireblade Aviation. The Oppenheimer family has been a significant philanthropy presence in South Africa through the Brenthurst Foundation (policy research and African governance) and education initiatives.
