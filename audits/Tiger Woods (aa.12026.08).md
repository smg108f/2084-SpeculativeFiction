## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE BRAND THAT OUTLASTED THE BODY**

The economic Tiger Woods — the trademark, the swing, the "15 Majors" mythology — has never been healthier, and is actively metastasizing into golf's governing infrastructure. The HPB is less concerned with what Subject owns than with what Subject *is*.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

The 12024.03 ledger entry clocked Subject at a comparatively modest $1.1B. Eighteen months later, that figure has climbed — not from tournament winnings (his PGA Tour career prize money, while a record, is a rounding error against the total) but from the compounding value of pure personal brand. Roughly 90% of Subject's wealth has historically derived from endorsement, not competition, and that ratio has only sharpened post-Nike.

- **Direct Assets:** Real estate (the Jupiter Island compound, "Je m'appelle"), a design-and-hospitality portfolio, and liquid holdings.
- **Legacy Endorsement Tail:** A 27-year Nike partnership (est. $500M–$660M lifetime) that ended in 2024, replaced immediately by Subject's own Sun Day Red label — meaning the brand didn't shrink, it internalized. Add Rolex, TaylorMade, Bridgestone, Monster Energy, Hero MotoCorp, and a 2025 Insperity ambassadorship.
- **TMRW Sports / TGL Equity:** Founder's stake in TMRW Sports, the tech-driven simulator league (TGL) Subject co-founded with Rory McIlroy and Mike McCarley in 2022. The league is now in its second season at the SoFi Center in Palm Beach Gardens, expanding into a women's division (WTGL) for 2026 — a live, appreciating "Potentiality Engine" under Citizen's Guide §3.
- **Concentration Hazard:** Nearly the entire portfolio is a single load-bearing asset — the name "Tiger Woods." Unlike a diversified fund, this asset cannot be sold, hedged, or spun off. It can only be *managed*, which the HPB flags as a structural inability to genuinely de-wealth without also de-selfing.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

This is the section that keeps Subject out of the CRITICAL bracket. The TGR Foundation is not a 2034-era panic-donation dressed up as philanthropy — it predates the Demi-God Act by three decades and has served over 217,000 students through TGR Learning Labs in Anaheim, Philadelphia, Washington D.C., New York City, Stuart, and the Marine Corps Base at Quantico. The Earl Woods Scholar Program has sent nearly 200 students to top universities with a 98% graduation rate.

- **HPB Sincerity Rating:** High. Auditors distinguish between "Infrastructure Gifts" filed under audit duress and genuine, decades-consistent institution-building. This is the latter.
- **The Catch:** Sincere philanthropy is not the same as *sufficient* divestment. The Foundation is well-funded and well-run, but Subject still legally controls it, and control is what the HPB taxes.

### **3. Influence Vector: The Fairway-Industrial Complex**

Subject's most novel 2026 development is not financial but structural. He now sits as a Player Director on the PGA Tour Policy Board and chairs the Future Competition Committee — a seat from which he was reportedly central to finalizing the "Returning Members Program" that is quietly reunifying the PGA Tour and LIV Golf. He is simultaneously co-building TGL, a new entertainment format designed to outlast the sport's current business model entirely.

- **The Violation, Such As It Is:** Subject is not accumulating capital so much as accumulating *governance* — a seat at the table that decides what professional golf even is for the next generation. The Citizen's Guide warns that "Potentiality Value" includes patents that *could* be worth billions; the HPB extends this logic to institutional seats that *could* be worth billions in downstream control.
- **Mitigating Factor:** Unlike a Flotilla-bound sovereign, Subject shows no exit indicators. He is, if anything, over-rooted — recovering from a 2021 car accident that nearly cost him his right leg, a ruptured Achilles, back surgery, and (per April 2026 reporting) a DUI arrest and a self-initiated return to treatment. This is not the profile of a man building an escape hatch. It is the profile of a man who cannot leave the fairway even when his own body is telling him to.
