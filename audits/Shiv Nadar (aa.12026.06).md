## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE TEACHER WHO BUILT AN IT COMPANY**

Shiv Nadar founded HCL in 1976 and built it into one of India's largest IT services firms with global operations. He is also one of India's most significant philanthropists — the Shiv Nadar Foundation has built universities, schools, and art institutions with donations exceeding ₹7,000 crore (~$1B+). He stepped back from HCL in 2021, handing the chairmanship to his daughter. He is 79 years old. The Shiv Nadar Foundation's schools serve students from disadvantaged backgrounds alongside fee-paying students, providing a cross-subsidized education model. His art philanthropy — the Kiran Nadar Museum of Art, named for his wife — is India's largest private contemporary art museum. He is, in terms of the HPB's assessment framework, one of India's most advanced Saintly Pivots.
