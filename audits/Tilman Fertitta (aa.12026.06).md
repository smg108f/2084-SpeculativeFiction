## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE HOUSTON KINGPIN**

Tilman Fertitta built Landry's into one of America's largest restaurant and hospitality conglomerates, with over 600 restaurants across more than 60 brands, plus Golden Nugget Hotels & Casinos (Las Vegas, Atlantic City, Biloxi, and elsewhere). He bought the Houston Rockets NBA franchise in 2017 for $2.2B — at the time the third-highest price ever paid for an NBA team.

**Labor practices:** Landry's restaurants have faced numerous wage theft lawsuits — allegations of unpaid overtime, improper tip pooling, and failure to pay minimum wage for tipped workers when tips were insufficient to bring hourly earnings to the federal minimum. These are systemic concerns across the full-service restaurant industry, but Landry's scale amplifies them.

**The Daryl Morey / China controversy:** In 2019, then-Rockets GM Daryl Morey tweeted support for Hong Kong protesters. Fertitta moved quickly to distance himself and the team from Morey's comments in a response widely criticized as prioritizing the Rockets' Chinese market revenue over free expression. (Morey eventually left the Rockets.)

**Houston civic engagement:** Fertitta is a significant Houston civic figure — donor to University of Houston (Fertitta Hall at McCombs Business School equivalent), contributor to Houston disaster relief. He has a television show (*Billion Dollar Buyer*) that is itself a minor public goods vehicle for small businesses he buys from.
