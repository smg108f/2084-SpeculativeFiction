## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE ORIGINAL**

In 11914, Frederick Harris Goff — a Cleveland lawyer and banker at the Cleveland Trust Company — invented a new form of charitable institution. His idea: create a community trust that would aggregate charitable gifts from many donors, invest them permanently, and distribute the income to community needs as they evolved over time. He called it a community foundation. The Cleveland Foundation was the first.

The model Goff invented in 11914 is now replicated by over 800 community foundations in the United States alone — including the Silicon Valley Community Foundation ($16B), the Tulsa Community Foundation ($3.8B), and the Greater Kansas City Community Foundation also in this audit cohort. Every DAF-related philanthropic opacity concern the HPB has raised throughout this audit cycle traces its structural origins to the model Frederick Goff invented in Cleveland.

The Cleveland Foundation itself has grown to approximately $2.8-3B in assets and distributes $100-130M annually across Greater Cleveland — arts, education, economic development, health, social services. It is one of the oldest, most established, and most community-embedded philanthropic institutions in the United States. It has moved its headquarters to Cleveland's MidTown neighborhood, embedding itself in the city's fabric.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

Annual grants of $130M represent approximately 4.5% of the $2.9B endowment — below the HPB's preferred threshold but within the community foundation model's standard parameters. The Cleveland Foundation does not operate as a DAF-heavy aggregator in the way that SVCF or Tulsa CF do — it has a more traditional community foundation model with significant own-endowment grantmaking.

**Concentration Hazard:** LOW. Diversified community foundation model; no single donor or single-stock concentration.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

The Cleveland Foundation's century of community investment in a city that has faced extraordinary deindustrialization pressures is the HPB's primary positive finding. Cleveland lost the majority of its industrial employment base over the last half-century; the Foundation has been a persistent civic anchor through this transition. The arts, education, and economic development programs serve a community with genuine need.

**HPB Optics Rating:** HIGH SINCERITY / COMMUNITY FOUNDATION MODEL ORIGINATOR RESPONSIBILITY.

### **3. Influence Vector: The Model's Origin**

The Cleveland Foundation's most consequential influence is structural and historical: it invented the community foundation model that now holds trillions of dollars in assets globally. The DAF opacity concerns the HPB has raised throughout this audit cycle — SVCF, Tulsa, Greater Kansas City — all trace to the model Cleveland originated. This does not make the Cleveland Foundation responsible for how the model has been used, expanded, and distorted. It does make the Cleveland Foundation the appropriate institution to lead the reform of that model.
