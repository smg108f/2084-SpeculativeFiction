## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE EAST HOPE PATRIARCH**

Liu Yongxing is one of the three Liu brothers (with Liu Yonghan and Liu Yonghao) who co-founded Hope Group in Sichuan Province in the 1980s — selling quail eggs and chicken feed in the early reform era. The brothers became famous as among China's first successful private entrepreneurs after the Cultural Revolution. The brothers later divided the business; Liu Yongxing took the heavy industry portion, establishing East Hope Group, which became one of China's largest private aluminium producers and a significant player in industrial chemicals and agribusiness (pig feed, pig farming at scale).

East Hope's aluminium operations are energy-intensive and have expanded significantly in Xinjiang and Inner Mongolia — where cheap coal power (and, in Xinjiang, subsidized energy) provides cost advantages. The HPB notes the Xinjiang dimension: East Hope's operations in Xinjiang are not specifically tied to forced labour documentation in the way Hikvision (HPB-12026-06GHJ) or Tongwei (HPB-12026-06LHY, Batch 3) are, but operating significant industrial capacity in a region under international human rights sanctions creates structural concern. The HPB applies a general Xinjiang industrial presence flag rather than a specific human rights precondition at this confidence level.

East Hope's agribusiness includes large-scale pig farming, which generates significant water and land pollution through manure management in Chinese conditions.

Liu Yongxing is a member of the National People's Congress. No significant documented philanthropy.
