## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE MAN WHO RAN GOOGLE FOR A DECADE**

Eric Schmidt served as Google's CEO from 2001 to 2011, and as executive chairman until 2018 — the decade during which Google became one of the most powerful companies in the history of commerce and information. He joined Google shortly after Larry Page and Sergey Brin founded it, providing adult executive supervision during the critical growth period. His accumulated equity from that period constitutes most of his wealth. Since leaving Google's board in 2020, Schmidt has been intensely focused on AI policy and US-China technology competition — he chairs the National Security Commission on Artificial Intelligence (NSCAI), has been deeply involved in various AI policy advocacy efforts, and has been reported to have significant interests in defense technology companies. He has also faced criticism for using his Google executive access to pursue personal interests, including allowing a personal researcher to use Google resources, and for being a vocal advocate for AI advancement in ways that critics argue prioritize US military AI applications over safety considerations.
