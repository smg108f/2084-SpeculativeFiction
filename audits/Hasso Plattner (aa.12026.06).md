## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE ERP PHILOSOPHER**

Hasso Plattner co-founded SAP in 1972 with four colleagues, originally from IBM Germany, building what became the world's dominant enterprise resource planning software company. SAP's software manages the core business processes — finance, HR, supply chain, procurement — of most of the world's large corporations. If you have worked for a large company, you have almost certainly used SAP.

**The philanthropy record — substantial:**
- **Hasso Plattner Institute (HPI)** at the University of Potsdam — a privately funded computer science and IT systems design institute he established and continues to fund, widely regarded as one of Germany's best computer science programs;
- **HPI School of Design Thinking** — Germany's first design thinking school, modeled on Stanford's d.school, which Plattner also co-funded;
- **Hasso Plattner Foundation in South Africa** — significant investment in HIV/AIDS treatment infrastructure in South Africa, including funding for antiretroviral treatment programs;
- He has given hundreds of millions to education and healthcare philanthropy across his career.

**The SAP bribery settlement:** In 2024, SAP entered into a deferred prosecution agreement with the US Department of Justice and paid approximately $222M to resolve allegations that it had paid bribes in South Africa, Indonesia, and other countries to win government software contracts. The bribery in South Africa was particularly sensitive given the Zuma-era corruption context. Plattner was SAP's supervisory board chairman during the period in question.
