## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE BROADCOM BACCHANAL**

Henry "Nick" Nicholas III co-founded **Broadcom** with Henry Samueli (processed separately as HPB-12026-06HS2 in Batch 6) in 1991. Broadcom grew into a dominant semiconductor company — its Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and Ethernet chips are inside virtually every smartphone, laptop, and networking device in the world.

**The 2008 prosecution:** Nicholas was indicted in 2008 on federal charges including: drug trafficking (accused of spiking drinks of employees and associates with MDMA and methamphetamine), conspiracy to commit securities fraud (Broadcom options backdating scandal), and maintaining drug-fueled parties at his properties. The drug trafficking charges were eventually dismissed after prosecutorial misconduct findings. He pleaded no contest to a securities violation (options backdating) and received no prison time. In 2019, he was arrested in Las Vegas on separate drug possession charges (heroin, cocaine, meth, ecstasy found in his hotel suite); those charges were eventually dropped.

**Marsy's Law:** Nicholas's sister Marsalee ("Marsy") was murdered in 1983. Nicholas subsequently became the primary funder of **Marsy's Law for All** — a victim's rights constitutional amendment movement that he has successfully placed on ballots and passed in over a dozen states. This is substantive and consequential legislative philanthropy, driven by personal tragedy.
