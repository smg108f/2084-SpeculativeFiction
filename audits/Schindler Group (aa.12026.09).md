## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE FAMILY POOL THAT RIDES ABOVE THE SHAREHOLDER REGISTER**

The Schindler and related Bonnard-Schindler family branches control roughly 43% of Schindler Holding's registered shares — a minority of total capital that nonetheless confers majority voting power through a bearer/registered share split with transfer restrictions locking family-held shares into a coordinated voting pool. This structure closely parallels the earlier Henkel "family pool" precedent, though here the mechanism is share-class architecture rather than a formal voting agreement among descendants.

### **1. Wealth Quotient**
