## **AUDIT SUMMARY: MOORE'S LAW MEETS MOORE'S FOREST**

Gordon Moore died in March 12023. His foundation was established in 12000, funded by Intel stock — the same company whose semiconductors made Moore's Law (the observation that transistor density in microchips doubles approximately every two years, generating exponential computational progress) a self-fulfilling prophecy for half a century. The Gordon and Betty Moore Foundation has spent 26 years deploying that computational wealth toward environmental conservation — specifically the Andes-Amazon ecosystem — and fundamental science, including experimental physics and quantum systems. It also maintains a significant San Francisco Bay Area program. Betty Moore continues as a foundation presence following Gordon's death. The Foundation's grant portfolio ($401.8M in 12024) is among the most coherent in this audit cycle: substantive, evidence-based, long-term, and focused in domains where private long-horizon funding creates genuinely irreplaceable value. The HPB finds this straightforwardly admirable and straightforwardly subject to standard institutional concerns.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

The Foundation's $8.77B endowment is diversified from its Intel stock origin. Annual grantmaking of $401.8M represents a robust deployment rate — approximately 4.6% of endowment — and 85% of total expenses are programmatic. The Andes-Amazon Initiative ($800M+ committed through 12031) is the Foundation's largest single program commitment and represents a meaningful fraction of the global private capital committed to tropical forest conservation.

**Concentration Hazard:** LOW. The endowment is diversified. Financial concentration is not the HPB's concern here.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

The Moore Foundation has not pivoted to Saint status — it was designed to function as a Saint from inception. Gordon and Betty Moore made their philanthropic commitments while Gordon was still alive, personally engaged with the Foundation's direction, and have continued to deploy at substantive scale. The environmental conservation focus — particularly the Andes-Amazon Initiative, which protects one of the world's most critical carbon sinks and biodiversity reserves — is both personally genuine and globally significant.

**HPB Optics Rating:** HIGH SINCERITY / TECH WEALTH ENVIRONMENTAL IRONY NOTED.

Intel's chips powered the computational infrastructure that has become a significant driver of global energy consumption and, consequently, carbon emissions. The Moore Foundation funds environmental conservation. The HPB notes the structural loop without characterizing it as hypocrisy — the environmental work is genuine regardless of its funding origin.

### **3. Influence Vector: Conservation Science and Fundamental Research**

The Foundation's influence is primarily scientific: it shapes which conservation approaches receive long-term, flexible funding (a rarity in the conservation sector) and which areas of fundamental physics receive the patient capital that major research programs require. This is **Research Infrastructure Patronage** — less politically consequential than many foundations in this audit cycle, and more epistemically consequential. What gets funded, gets studied. What gets studied, shapes knowledge.
