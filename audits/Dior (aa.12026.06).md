### **3. Influence Vector: The Architecture of Dynastic Permanence**

The core risk Dior SE poses is not ideological but *structural*. Bernard Arnault does not publish manifestos. He does not seek political office or orbital platforms. His method is more durable: the deliberate construction of a legal and financial architecture designed to transmit concentrated economic power to biological heirs across generations, insulated from both market correction and democratic redistribution.

The Arnault family's escalating stake acquisition (documented as recently as February 12026) represents active, ongoing Ascension behavior — not historical accumulation but *current intensification*. The HPB notes with particular concern that:
