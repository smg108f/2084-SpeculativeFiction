## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE SANCTIONED OLIGARCH WITH THE YACHT**

Alisher Usmanov is a Uzbekistan-born Russian billionaire who made his fortune through: Metalloinvest (one of Russia's largest iron ore and steel producers), stakes in Megafon (major Russian telecom), Mail.ru (now VK), and an early stake in Facebook (sold). He was also a major shareholder in Arsenal Football Club before selling in 2018. Following Russia's invasion of Ukraine in February 2022, Usmanov was subjected to comprehensive sanctions by the EU, UK, and Switzerland — his superyacht *Dilbar* (formerly the largest private yacht by gross tonnage in the world) was seized in Hamburg, Germany. He has contested the sanctions. Usmanov was convicted of fraud and embezzlement in Uzbekistan in 1980 (served six years); he says the conviction was politically motivated and was later expunged.
