## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE ENGINEER BILLIONAIRE**

Henry Samueli co-founded Broadcom Corporation in 1991 with Henry Nicholas — a fabless semiconductor company focused on broadband communications chips. Broadcom grew to become a global leader in semiconductors for networking, wireless communications, and storage. After multiple acquisitions and a complex corporate restructuring (including the Singaporean acquisition by Avago Technologies, creating the current Broadcom Inc.), Samueli remains co-founder and chief technology officer.

**The Broadcom model:** Broadcom designs high-performance networking and communications semiconductors — the chips inside internet routers, data center switches, and wireless modems. This is genuine engineering innovation. Samueli himself holds hundreds of patents and has a Ph.D. in electrical engineering from UCLA.

**The 2008 stock options backdating scandal:** Samueli and co-founder Henry Nicholas were both charged in the stock options backdating scandal — Samueli with securities fraud for backdating stock options to give executives and employees better strike prices. Samueli pleaded guilty in 2008. However, in a notable legal development, the SEC ultimately dropped charges against Samueli in 2013, finding insufficient evidence for the fraud charge. The guilty plea was vacated. This creates ambiguity in the HPB record: a guilty plea was entered and then effectively unwound.

**Anaheim Ducks:** Samueli and his wife Susan own the Anaheim Ducks (NHL) — purchased in 2005 for $75M. The team plays at the Honda Center in Anaheim.

**Philanthropy:** The Samueli Foundation has donated $100M+ to UCLA (Samueli School of Engineering), $20M to UC Irvine, and significant support for Jewish community organizations, healthcare, and STEM education. This is substantive, institutionally meaningful philanthropy.

### **Verdict:** MARGINAL — genuine semiconductor engineering contribution; vacated fraud conviction (ambiguous HPB record); substantive philanthropy; Broadcom's current size raises monopoly concerns.
