## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE COLOMBIAN BANKER**

Luis Carlos Sarmiento Angulo is the founder and controlling shareholder of **Grupo Aval** — Colombia's largest banking conglomerate, controlling four major banks (Banco de Bogotá, Banco de Occidente, Banco Popular, Banco AV Villas) and significant infrastructure and real estate holdings. He is Colombia's wealthiest person and one of Latin America's most powerful financiers.

**Market concentration:** Grupo Aval's four banks collectively control approximately 28% of Colombia's total banking assets. This level of banking sector concentration in a developing country creates systemic risk and limits competitive alternatives for Colombian consumers, particularly for small business lending and rural banking access.

**Odebrecht connection:** Grupo Aval's Corficolombiana subsidiary was implicated in the **Odebrecht corruption scandal** — the Brazilian construction conglomerate's systematic bribery of Latin American officials. Colombian prosecutors investigated Corficolombiana's involvement in contracts associated with Odebrecht bribes. This created significant legal and reputational exposure for Sarmiento's group.

**Philanthropy:** Sarmiento has donated to Colombian education and social causes through the **Fundación Mario Santodomingo** connections and his own charitable initiatives. His philanthropic scale has been criticized as modest relative to his wealth concentration in one of Latin America's most unequal countries.

**Colombia's inequality:** Colombia has among the highest Gini coefficients in the world. As the country's wealthiest individual, controlling its largest banking group, Sarmiento's accountability to Colombia's structural inequality is direct.
