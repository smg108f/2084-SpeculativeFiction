## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE TCHIBO HEIR (MICHAEL)**

Michael Herz co-controls **Maxingvest** — the Hamburg-based holding company that owns **Tchibo** (Germany's largest coffee retail chain and mail-order company, famous for its rotating weekly non-coffee product ranges) and a significant stake in **Beiersdorf AG** (the consumer goods company behind Nivea, Eucerin, and Hansaplast). The brothers inherited the business from their father Günter Herz and uncle Alec Herz.

**Tchibo:** The Tchibo model — coffee retail combined with a weekly rotating range of consumer goods (clothing, electronics, tools) at middle-market prices — is a distinctive German retail format. Tchibo's coffee supply chain spans multiple producing countries.

**Coffee supply chain:** Coffee supply chain accountability includes: commodity pricing that often fails to provide living wages for coffee farmers in producing countries (Ethiopia, Vietnam, Brazil, Honduras); deforestation associated with coffee cultivation; and pesticide use. Tchibo has been a leader in sustainability certification in the German market — a partial Whuffie credit.

**Beiersdorf:** Nivea and Beiersdorf's consumer skin care brands have operated globally for over a century with a generally neutral-to-positive consumer safety record.

### **Verdict:** MARGINAL — coffee supply chain accountability balanced by Tchibo sustainability leadership; Beiersdorf consumer goods neutral; Hamburg family industrial legacy; standard burns.
