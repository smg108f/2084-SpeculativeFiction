## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE CHRONOPHAGE OF THE PILBARA**

Rio Tinto does not merely mine the earth; it mines the *evidence that the earth was ever inhabited*. Where other extractive demi-gods are audited for hoarding capital, the HPB has opened a novel docket for this subject: hoarding *deep time* — converting 40,000-plus years of continuous human memory into shareholder dividends, one detonation at a time. The 2020 destruction of the Juukan Gorge rock shelters — a cultural sequence stretching back 46,000 years, vaporized in seconds for the sake of ~8 million tonnes of incremental ore — remains the HPB's reference case for what auditors now formally term "Provenance Erasure." The subject's current pivot into lithium and "critical minerals" only confirms the pattern: having proven it can erase the past to feed the present, it now positions itself to own the future as well.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

Rio Tinto's value (~$104.7B market capitalization per most recent CapitalismBureau intake, ticker RIO, London/Melbourne dual-listed) is held overwhelmingly in fixed geological assets — Pilbara iron ore tenements, aluminium smelters, copper concessions, and, as of the 2025 Arcadium Lithium absorption, seven new lithium brine and spodumene deposits spanning Argentina, Quebec, and Western Australia. FY2025 underlying EBITDA reached $25.4B against a $6.5B shareholder dividend — the tenth consecutive year the payout sat at the top of its range. **Concentration Hazard flagged:** the subject's holdings are not liquid wealth but *literal claims on the substrate of the planet*, a category the HPB's actuaries struggle to price because, unlike a portfolio, it cannot be divested without first being un-mined — a physical impossibility the subject has weaponized as a defense ("you cannot make us give back what no longer exists").

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

Following the Juukan Gorge scandal, three executives (including CEO Jean-Sébastien Jacques) and chairman Simon Thompson resigned in a rare "Accountability Cascade." The subject has since funded the Juukan Gorge Legacy Foundation, established an Australian Advisory Group for Indigenous relations, and pledged to construct a full-scale physical *replica* of the rock shelters it destroyed — an act the HPB classifies as a **Grade-3 Simulacrum Offering**: penance rendered not by restoring what was lost, but by manufacturing a convincing forgery of it for tourists. Auditors note this is the architectural equivalent of returning a borrowed heirloom as a 3D-printed replica and calling it restitution.

### **3. Influence Vector: The Substrate Monopoly**

Rio Tinto's emerging risk is not political lobbying in the conventional sense — it is **infrastructural inevitability**. By controlling the iron ore that frames cities, the aluminium that skins aircraft, and now the lithium that will power every battery in the Humanist Bloc's transition economy, the subject is positioning itself as a substrate no government can audit out of existence without halting civilization itself. This is not a "scaffold" for human flourishing; it is a foundation poured over the bones of the people who lived there first, with a "net zero by 2050" plaque bolted on top.
