## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE LANDLORD OF ORANGE COUNTY**

Donald Bren is the sole owner of The Irvine Company, one of the largest privately held real estate companies in the United States. The Irvine Company owns approximately one-fifth of Orange County, California — including the entire city of Irvine, which it developed from scratch — along with significant office, retail, and apartment holdings in Los Angeles, San Diego, and other markets. The company also owns approximately 40,000 apartment units, making Bren one of the largest residential landlords in the United States. The Irvine Company traces its origins to the Irvine Ranch, a 19th-century land grant covering approximately 93,000 acres of what is now some of the most valuable real estate in California. Bren acquired the company in 1977 and has built it into a multi-billion-dollar institution. He is one of the largest donors to environmental causes in California history, with significant gifts to conservation, climate science, and the UC Irvine and UC Santa Barbara systems.
