## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE HYDRAULIC KING**

Wang Liping founded Hengli Hydraulic in Changzhou, Jiangsu Province, building it from a small hydraulic cylinder manufacturer into one of China's and the world's largest producers of hydraulic components — the core technology inside excavators, cranes, agricultural machinery, and industrial equipment. Hengli Hydraulic is listed on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange and has achieved significant market share against foreign competitors (Parker Hannifin, Bosch Rexroth, Kawasaki) that previously dominated China's hydraulic component market.

**The business:** Hydraulic components are not glamorous or publicly visible, but they are foundational to the entire global construction and agricultural equipment industry. Hengli supplies major Chinese construction equipment manufacturers including XCMG, Sany, Zoomlion, and Lonking — which in turn export to global markets.

**The supply chain concern:** Hengli's customers supply equipment used in infrastructure construction throughout China and across Belt and Road projects globally. Chinese construction machinery has been documented at Xinjiang construction sites. Hengli is several steps removed from end-use decisions, but its components are inside the machines. The HPB notes this as an indirect rather than direct concern — Wang Liping did not design infrastructure projects; he makes cylinders that move excavator arms.

**Industrial environmental impact:** Hydraulic fluid manufacturing and metalworking at scale involves industrial pollution risk. Hengli's manufacturing base in Changzhou has faced some environmental scrutiny, but no major documented violations at the scale of other Batch 4 subjects.

**Philanthropy:** Not significantly documented. Common pattern for mainland Chinese industrial founders of this profile.
