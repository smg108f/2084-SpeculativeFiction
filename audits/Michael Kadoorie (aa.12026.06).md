## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE PENINSULA PATRIARCH**

Sir Michael Kadoorie is the third-generation patriarch of Hong Kong's Kadoorie family — one of the founding merchant dynasties of colonial Hong Kong. The family controls **CLP Holdings** (China Light and Power — Hong Kong's largest electricity utility serving Kowloon and the New Territories), **HK Electric** (the electricity utility for Hong Kong Island and Lantau), and the **Peninsula Hotels** luxury hotel chain.

**Power utility monopoly:** CLP and HK Electric together supply essentially all electricity in Hong Kong — a natural monopoly position regulated by the HK Government through Scheme of Control Agreements. The regulated utility model provides stability but limits the pressure on utilities to decarbonize rapidly. CLP has made significant renewable energy investments in Asia, including solar and wind across India, Australia, and Southeast Asia — a genuine clean energy commitment.

**Peninsula Hotels:** The Peninsula brand is among the world's most prestigious luxury hotel chains. The flagship Peninsula Hong Kong (opened 1928) is a colonial-era landmark. Luxury hospitality is neutral-to-positive in accountability terms.

**Colonial-era wealth origin:** The Kadoorie family's fortune was built in colonial Hong Kong — participating in and benefiting from the colonial system that shaped the city's inequality structure. This is historical context rather than active accountability.

**China engagement:** CLP's power generation operations extend into mainland China through joint ventures with the Chinese state power sector — a complex and politically sensitive business relationship.
