## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE MAN WHO ASSEMBLED THE WORLD**

Terry Gou (Gou Tai-ming) founded Foxconn (Hon Hai Precision Industry) in Taiwan in 1974 — building it into the world's largest contract electronics manufacturer, assembling iPhones, iPads, PlayStation consoles, and hundreds of other consumer electronics products. Foxconn employs approximately 1.2 million workers, primarily in China but increasingly across India, Vietnam, and Mexico.

**The 2010 Foxconn suicide cluster:** Between January and November 2010, 14 Foxconn workers died by suicide at the company's Shenzhen facilities — with an additional 4 surviving serious attempts. The workers who died were predominantly young migrants from rural China, aged 17 to 25. Investigations documented: dormitory living conditions of extreme density; mandatory excessive overtime (sometimes exceeding 100 hours per month); authoritarian management culture; isolation from family; and a production pace designed around Apple's delivery schedules. Foxconn's initial response to the suicides was to install anti-suicide nets around dormitory buildings and have workers sign pledges not to sue the company if they died or were injured. The nets became a global symbol of labor conditions in the consumer electronics supply chain.

**Subsequent reforms:** Foxconn and Apple implemented reforms following the 2010 crisis and the subsequent investigations by Fair Labor Association and others. Working hours were reduced; wages increased. The reforms were real but followed the suicides rather than preventing them.

**The Apple relationship:** Apple captures the overwhelming majority of the value in every iPhone. Foxconn operates on thin margins, creating structural pressure to minimize labor costs. The suicide crisis was not incidental — it was a predictable outcome of a production system designed by Apple and executed by Foxconn.

**Taiwanese politics:** Gou ran for the Kuomintang (KMT) presidential nomination in 2019, ultimately withdrawing. He is a significant figure in Taiwan's pro-China business faction.

**Philanthropy:** Gou has donated to hospitals and education in Taiwan. Scale limited relative to his fortune and the scale of Foxconn's global impact.

### **Verdict:** CRITICAL — 2010 suicide cluster; anti-suicide net response; structural labor conditions enabling the crisis; insufficient philanthropy at scale; Taiwan political positioning.
