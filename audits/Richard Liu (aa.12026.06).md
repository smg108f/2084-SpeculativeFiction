## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE DELIVERY EMPEROR**

Richard Liu (Liu Qiangdong) founded JD.com (Jingdong) in 1998, originally as a physical electronics retailer, and built it into China's second-largest e-commerce platform. Unlike Alibaba's marketplace model, JD.com operates its own logistics network — a major competitive differentiator that has allowed it to deliver in same-day or next-day windows across most of China. Liu grew up in poverty in rural Jiangsu province and built JD from nothing.

**The civil settlement:** In August 2018, Liu was arrested in Minneapolis on suspicion of rape following a dinner connected to his executive MBA program at the University of Minnesota. US prosecutors declined to file criminal charges, citing insufficient evidence to prove guilt beyond a reasonable doubt. A civil lawsuit was subsequently filed by the complainant. In 2022, Liu settled the civil lawsuit for an undisclosed sum with no admission of liability.

The HPB notes: no criminal conviction, no guilty plea, no admission of liability. The HPB's position is not to adjudicate the allegation in the absence of a criminal finding. This distinguishes JD.com from Alibaba's logistics partners, Meituan's gig economy model, and most comparable Western platforms. The HPB acknowledges this as a meaningful positive.
