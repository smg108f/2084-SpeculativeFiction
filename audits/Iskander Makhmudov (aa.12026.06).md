## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE URAL METALS OLIGARCH**

Iskander Makhmudov is the controlling shareholder of UMMC-Holding (Ural Mining and Metallurgical Company) — one of Russia's largest metals companies, producing copper, zinc, gold, coal, and associated downstream engineering products. UMMC is one of the world's largest copper producers. Makhmudov is Uzbek-born and built his position in Russian metals through the turbulent privatization era of the 1990s.

**The 1990s metals acquisition context:** Russian metals wealth of this scale was largely accumulated through the chaotic privatization process of the 1990s, when Soviet-era industrial assets were transferred to private hands through processes of disputed legitimacy. The Bureau records this as structural context, not individual criminal conduct per se — though the structural context is not neutral.

**Environmental record:** UMMC's copper smelting operations in the Urals have been associated with significant air and soil pollution. The Karabash copper smelter — one of the most polluted industrial sites in Russia — is in the UMMC production geography. Remediation has been ongoing but incomplete.

**Ukraine:** Makhmudov has not been placed on the main US or EU sanctions lists as of this audit (unlike Skoch or other oligarchs). However, his status as a major Russian industrial asset holder and his continued operation within the Russian economic system following the February 2022 invasion raises standard accountability questions. No documented public statement opposing the invasion.

**Philanthropy:** UMMC funds social infrastructure in the Urals region where it operates — schools, sports facilities, and community programs in Ekaterinburg and surrounding cities.
