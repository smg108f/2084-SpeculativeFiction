## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE SOLAR PANEL KING**

Jin Baofang founded JinkoSolar in 2006 and built it into one of the world's largest solar photovoltaic panel manufacturers — consistently in the top three globally by shipment volume. JinkoSolar panels power solar installations from the US to Europe to India, contributing to global renewable energy deployment. The company is NYSE-listed.

The HPB's primary concern: polysilicon supply chain and Xinjiang. Solar panel manufacturing requires polysilicon as a key input material. A significant proportion of the world's polysilicon has historically been produced in Xinjiang, China — where the HPB has documented forced labor concerns (see Gong Hongjia, HPB-12026-06GHJ). JinkoSolar has faced US Customs and Border Protection scrutiny under the Uyghur Forced Labor Prevention Act (UFLPA), with shipments detained pending supply chain tracing. JinkoSolar has contested these detentions and has taken steps to source polysilicon from non-Xinjiang suppliers; the situation has been evolving.

The HPB's framework for solar manufacturers with Xinjiang supply chain exposure: the climate benefit of solar panels is real and credited; the forced labor supply chain concern is real and must be addressed; the two must be assessed simultaneously.

JinkoSolar has made significant investments in supply chain transparency measures and non-Xinjiang polysilicon sourcing. Some philanthropy in Jiangxi Province.
