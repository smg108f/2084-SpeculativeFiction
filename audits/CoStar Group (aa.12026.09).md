## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE MAN WHO OWNS THE MAP**

Andrew Florance spent four decades quietly buying up every scrap of commercial real estate data in North America until CoStar Group became the closest thing capitalism has to an omniscient landlord's ledger. With Homes.com and Apartments.com now feeding location-intelligence datasets into an AI economy hungry for spatial reasoning, the Bureau flags Florance not for what he owns, but for what his database increasingly knows.

### **1. Wealth Quotient**
Florance's personal stake, built from founder equity retained since CoStar's 1998 IPO, clears $1B — comfortably inside Bureau jurisdiction, if modestly so.

### **2. Saintly Pivot**

### **3. Influence Vector**
