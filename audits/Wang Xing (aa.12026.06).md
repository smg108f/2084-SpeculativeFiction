## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE DELIVERY EMPEROR**

Wang Xing co-founded Meituan in 2010, building it into China's dominant food delivery and local services super-app through a series of mergers and acquisitions — most significantly the 2015 merger with Dianping. Meituan processes hundreds of millions of food delivery orders, serves hundreds of thousands of restaurants, and operates a network of gig economy delivery riders across Chinese cities. It has expanded into grocery delivery, ride-hailing, travel bookings, and other local commerce.

**Antitrust:** In 2021, China's State Administration for Market Regulation (SAMR) fined Meituan ¥3.44B (~$530M) for monopolistic practices — specifically "choose one from two" (二选一) merchant exclusivity arrangements forcing restaurants to work exclusively with Meituan or face penalties. This is a documented antitrust violation adjudicated by Chinese regulators.

**Gig worker labor:** Meituan's delivery operation depends on hundreds of thousands of gig delivery riders — workers who are algorithmically managed, have no employment benefits, and face intense performance pressure from Meituan's routing algorithm. Multiple reports have documented: rising accident and fatality rates among delivery riders; the algorithm's role in creating impossible delivery time windows; regulatory pressure on riders to accept dangerous conditions to maintain ratings. A landmark 2020 Chinese investigative piece titled "The System Is Crushing Riders" documented this in detail.

Wang Xing has a reputation as an intellectually serious founder. In 2021, he posted a poem on Weibo that was interpreted as critical of the Chinese government; his account was suspended. This suggests some independent intellectual stance.
