## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE DESIGNER OF DISPLACEMENT**

Joe Gebbia co-founded Airbnb in 2008 with Brian Chesky and Nathan Blecharczyk, contributing the industrial design expertise and product aesthetic that made Airbnb's host experience distinctive. As CPO, Gebbia was responsible for the design systems that made it easy, appealing, and rewarding to list a home on Airbnb — including the gamification elements that encouraged hosts to grow from occasional room-renters to multi-property operators.

**Design accountability:** The Airbnb product — its host dashboard, pricing tools, Superhost status, occupancy optimization features — was designed to maximize listing frequency and host revenue. These design choices contributed structurally to the conversion of long-term residential units to short-term rentals. Gebbia's contribution was the aesthetic and UX layer that made this conversion feel natural, desirable, and status-conferring.

**Departure and Samara:** Gebbia stepped back from day-to-day Airbnb operations in 2022 and launched Samara — a company focused on designing backyard homes (ADUs) that can be used as either personal space or Airbnb rentals. The HPB notes the irony: a co-founder of the platform that reduced housing supply is now selling backyard housing units partially marketed as Airbnb-compatible.

**Philanthropy:** Gebbia has been involved in design-focused philanthropic initiatives and is a supporter of design education.

**Individual accountability relative to Chesky:** Gebbia's accountability is assessed as derivative of Chesky's primary accountability but substantive in its own right. The Airbnb product could not have scaled as it did without compelling design; that design contributed to the housing crisis. Scale assessed at ~60% of Chesky's obligation.

### **Verdict:** CRITICAL — design contribution to Airbnb's housing displacement effect; Samara product continues Airbnb ecosystem; coordinated accountability with Chesky (HPB-12026-06BC) and Blecharczyk (HPB-12026-06NB).
