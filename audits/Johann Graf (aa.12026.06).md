## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE SLOT MACHINE KING**

Johann Graf founded Novomatic in 1980 in Baden, Austria — building it into the world's largest gaming machine manufacturer and one of the largest gambling operators globally. Novomatic produces slot machines, video lottery terminals (VLTs), casino management systems, and online gambling platforms, operating across 50+ countries.

**The gambling machine model:** Novomatic's slot machines and VLTs are specifically engineered for maximum addictive engagement — variable ratio reinforcement schedules, near-miss programming, rapid play rates, and loss-chasing facilitation. These are not neutral entertainment products; they are addiction delivery mechanisms. Problem gambling research consistently identifies electronic gaming machines as the most addictive form of gambling, with the highest rates of problem gambling among users.

**Austrian political influence:** Novomatic has been at the center of Austrian political corruption investigations. In 2019, the "Ibiza affair" — a sting video showing Austrian Vice Chancellor Heinz-Christian Strache apparently offering public contracts to a fake Russian oligarch — included references to Novomatic. Strache was recorded appearing to suggest that Novomatic had made illegal campaign donations to his party (FPÖ). Novomatic and Graf denied wrongdoing; investigations produced mixed outcomes, but the episode illustrated the company's deep entanglement with Austrian political financing.

**Opacity:** Novomatic is private with limited public financial disclosure.

**Philanthropy:** Graf has made donations to Austrian cultural and sports institutions. Scale limited relative to wealth.

### **Verdict:** CRITICAL — gambling machine engineering for addiction; Austrian political corruption implication; gambling monopoly profits from problem gamblers; limited philanthropy; private company opacity.
