## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE EYE HOSPITAL TYCOON**

Chen Bang founded Aier Eye Hospital Group in 2003. Aier has grown into China's largest privately operated ophthalmic hospital chain, with hundreds of hospitals across China and international operations in Southeast Asia and Europe. The company listed on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange in 2009 and has been one of China's most consistently high-performing healthcare stocks. Aier's business model is controversial: it operates in a sector (ophthalmology) where treatment for conditions like cataracts, myopia correction, and retinal diseases is medically essential, and where private-sector operators in China have been accused of pressuring patients toward unnecessary or premium-priced procedures, exploiting the gap between China's public hospital capacity and patient demand.

The HPB notes the COVID-19 nexus: Wuhan, where Aier has operations, was the epicenter of the pandemic. A prominent Wuhan Aier hospital was publicly accused in early 2020 of continuing to perform elective eye surgeries during the lockdown — including on patients who subsequently tested positive for COVID — contributing to early spread. Aier denied wrongdoing; the incident generated significant public controversy in China.
