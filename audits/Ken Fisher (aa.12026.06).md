## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE CONFERENCE INCIDENT**

Ken Fisher is the founder and executive chairman of Fisher Investments, which manages approximately $275B in assets, primarily for retail investors and their retirement savings. Fisher Investments is famous for its aggressive direct-mail marketing and for Fisher's long-running column in Forbes.

**October 2019:** At an investment industry conference (Tiburon CEO Summit), Fisher made a series of comments comparing winning client trust to "trying to get into a girl's pants" and made other sexually degrading remarks to an audience of financial industry professionals. The comments were reported by Bloomberg.

**The consequence:** Within weeks, institutional clients withdrew more than $3B in assets from Fisher Investments — including major pension funds in Michigan, Boston, Philadelphia, and Wisconsin. Fisher initially gave a non-apology ("I regret that it has caused consternation"), then gave a fuller apology. He remains executive chairman.

**The institutional trust dimension:** Fisher Investments manages retirement savings for hundreds of thousands of individuals. The 2019 incident revealed the culture at the top of a firm entrusted with people's financial security. Fisher's response — initial minimization, subsequent fuller apology but no structural change — is the accountability record.

**Investment management:** Fisher Investments has been criticized for high fees relative to passive index alternatives and for aggressive marketing to retirees and near-retirees who might be better served by lower-cost products.

**Philanthropy:** The Fisher Center for Alzheimer's Research Foundation (named for his father) is his primary philanthropic focus — $150M+ donated for Alzheimer's research. This is genuine and significant.
