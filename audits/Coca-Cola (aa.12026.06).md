## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE MOST DISTRIBUTED PRODUCT IN HUMAN HISTORY**

Coca-Cola reaches places where clean water does not. In communities without reliable water infrastructure, Coca-Cola products are often available because the entity has invested more in distribution infrastructure than local governments have invested in water systems. This is not an allegation. It is a documented feature of Coca-Cola's global distribution that public health advocates have noted for decades.

The Bureau's concern about Coca-Cola is compound: it is the world's leading distributor of sugar-sweetened beverages that are causal factors in Type 2 diabetes and obesity; it is also one of the world's most effective logistical distribution networks, and that logistical capability has been used for genuine public health purposes (vaccine cold chain, product availability in crisis zones). The entity is simultaneously the world's most efficient sugar delivery system and one of the world's most effective distribution networks — and whether those capabilities are used for harm or benefit depends on decisions Coca-Cola makes.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient**

Coca-Cola's $330B market cap on $46B revenue reflects extraordinary brand equity: the Coca-Cola brand has been valued independently at $70B+, representing the commercial value of global brand recognition and habitual consumption patterns established over 100+ years.

**Concentration Hazard:** The Coca-Cola System (the company plus 200+ bottling partners) constitutes a distribution network so extensive that it reaches more locations in more countries than most governmental systems. This distribution reach is the entity's core competitive asset — and its primary public health risk vector.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot**

Coca-Cola has made genuine contributions: the distribution network has been used for public health initiatives including oral rehydration salts distribution in Africa. The entity has made sugar reduction commitments (20% sugar reduction in its portfolio by 2025 against a 2010 baseline). Water replenishment commitments are publicly reported.

**HPB Optics Rating: MODERATE.** The distribution capabilities used for public health are genuine. The core product portfolio remains primarily sugar-sweetened beverages with documented health costs.

### **3. The Influence Vector: The Global Habit**

Coca-Cola's deepest influence is habitual: 2 billion servings per day, established through a century of advertising that has embedded product consumption into daily routines, cultural rituals, and emotional associations globally. The Bureau classifies this as the most successfully engineered consumer habit in history.
