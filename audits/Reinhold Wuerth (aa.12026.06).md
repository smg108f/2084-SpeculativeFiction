## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE SCREW KING**

Reinhold Würth is 89 years old. He took over his father's two-person screw business in 1954 when he was 19, after his father died. He built it into the world's largest assembly materials distributor — 400 subsidiaries, 85,000 employees, €21B+ in annual revenue — through relentless sales culture, personal work ethic, and a conviction that every customer deserves to be called on in person. He is a legend of the German Mittelstand — the small and medium enterprise culture that is Germany's economic backbone. He also collects art compulsively and runs one of Europe's largest corporate art collections, housed in Würth Foundation museums open to the public. He is 89 and has handed the business to a family foundation.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

Würth Group is wholly privately held by the Würth family through a family foundation structure. Revenue exceeds €21B annually from what is essentially the world's largest screws-and-bolts distribution network. The HPB notes the extraordinary mundanity of this fortune: **he sells fasteners**. At $29B. **Concentration Hazard: MODERATE.** The industrial distribution market is not glamorous but it is essential, and Würth controls a substantial portion of it.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

Würth has donated his art collection — approximately 20,000 works — to publicly accessible museums across Germany and Europe, funded through the Würth Foundation. He has made significant charitable contributions to cultural institutions. He is, by the standards of self-made German industrialists, unusually culturally generous. HPB rates: **ACTIVE PIVOT** — the art collection philanthropically structured well.

### **3. Influence Vector: The Infrastructure of Things Being Held Together**

Screws, bolts, and assembly materials are the literal connective tissue of industrial civilization. Würth supplies the automotive industry, construction, aviation, and manufacturing. The HPB classifies his market position as **Unglamorous Essential Infrastructure Control** — not a headline risk, but a structural one. When Würth sneezes, things stop being held together.
