## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE ENVIRONMENTALIST RETAILER**

Michael Otto is the son of Werner Otto, who founded the Otto Group as a mail-order shoe catalogue in Hamburg in 1949. Michael Otto transformed it into a global retail and e-commerce conglomerate — operating in over 30 countries with more than 50 companies, including Crate & Barrel (partial stake), Bonprix, Witt Group, and numerous European retail brands. He stepped back from active management in 2007 but remains majority owner.

**The environmental record — a genuine credit:** Michael Otto is one of Germany's most significant environmental philanthropists. He has funded and chaired the WWF Germany advisory board; he established the Michael Otto Foundation, which focuses on sustainable water management and environmental education; he funded the reintroduction of beavers to Germany (beavers were extinct in most of Germany by the 20th century — Otto funded their return to the Elbe); and Otto Group was among the first German companies to publish a comprehensive environmental strategy and commit to sustainable supply chains in textiles.

**The global supply chain question:** Otto Group sources textiles and products from global supply chains, including Bangladesh, China, and South Asian manufacturers. Textile supply chain labor conditions are a systemic industry concern. Otto Group has invested in sustainability programs (Fair Wear Foundation membership, OECD due diligence adoption) but the challenge of global fashion supply chain labor remains industry-wide and not fully resolved.
