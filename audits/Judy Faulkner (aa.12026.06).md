## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE KEEPER OF HEALTH RECORDS**

Judy Faulkner founded Epic Systems Corporation in 1979 in Madison, Wisconsin — building it into the dominant electronic health records (EHR) system in the United States, used by approximately 250 million Americans' health records. Epic is used by more than half of the US population's healthcare providers. Faulkner has maintained Epic as a private company and has taken the Giving Pledge, committing to give the majority of her wealth to charity.

**The EHR monopoly:** Epic's market dominance in US hospital EHR systems creates genuine lock-in — hospitals that adopt Epic face extremely high switching costs, giving Epic significant pricing power. The healthcare IT market has become substantially a duopoly (Epic and Oracle Health/Cerner). Epic's interoperability with other health systems has been a recurring regulatory concern — the company has historically resisted sharing data across systems, though recent federal interoperability rules have forced progress.

**Data and privacy:** Epic holds the health records of approximately 250 million Americans — an extraordinary concentration of sensitive personal health information in a private company. The governance of this data, the terms of its use, and the security of its storage are thus not merely commercial questions.

**MyChart:** Epic's patient portal (MyChart) is used by tens of millions of patients to access their own health records. This democratization of health information access is a genuine public good.

**The Giving Pledge:** Faulkner has committed to give the majority of her wealth to charity. She has donated to UW-Madison and healthcare research. The Giving Pledge commitment is noted affirmatively.

**Labor:** Epic is known as a demanding employer — long hours, intense culture, but competitive compensation. No documented systemic labor violations.

### **Verdict:** MARGINAL — healthcare data concentration; EHR interoperability obstruction history; Giving Pledge commitment; genuine health information democratization through MyChart; private company structure limits accountability.
