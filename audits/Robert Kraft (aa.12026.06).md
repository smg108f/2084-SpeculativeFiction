## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE PATRIOTS PATRIARCH**

Robert Kraft built the Kraft Group from a packaging and forest products company and then purchased the New England Patriots in 1994 for $172M — saving the team from a potential move to St. Louis. The Patriots under Kraft's ownership and with Bill Belichick and Tom Brady became one of the most successful franchises in professional sports history (6 Super Bowl championships between 2001 and 2019). Kraft is known for his philanthropic work, his friendship with world leaders (Vladimir Putin notably gave him a Super Bowl ring that Kraft alleged was taken from him), and his role in AFC championship controversies (Spygate, Deflategate). In 2019, Kraft was charged with soliciting prostitution at a spa in Jupiter, Florida, during the Super Bowl weekend — charges that were later dropped after a legal challenge to the evidence collection method.
