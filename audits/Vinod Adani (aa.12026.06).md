## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE OFFSHORE ADANI**

Vinod Adani is the elder brother of Gautam Adani (separately audited; Gautam's audit is in an earlier HPB batch given his substantially higher wealth position). Vinod Adani is a Dubai and Cyprus-based businessman who controls a network of offshore investment entities connected to the Adani Group conglomerate — the Indian infrastructure, energy, ports, and media empire.

The defining concern: the January 2023 Hindenburg Research report on Adani Group alleged, among other things, that Vinod Adani controls offshore entities in Mauritius, the Caribbean, and elsewhere that were used to: (a) hold undisclosed Adani Group stakes in violation of India's minimum public shareholding requirements; (b) route funds into Adani Group entities in ways that may have artificially inflated stock prices; (c) obscure the true concentration of Adani Group ownership within the Adani family. Hindenburg's report was a short-seller report and is not a legal adjudication; the allegations remain disputed. However, the Adani Group's response did not specifically refute the claims about Vinod Adani's offshore entity structure.

India's Supreme Court ordered an investigation by SEBI (Securities and Exchange Board of India). SEBI's subsequent report was criticized by some independent analysts for lacking depth on the offshore entity question. The HPB's audit notes the contested nature of the allegations while observing that the offshore structure itself — regardless of its legality — represents the type of wealth opacity the Demi-God Act was designed to address.

Vinod Adani has no documented independent philanthropic activity.
