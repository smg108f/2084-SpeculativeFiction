## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE KING BENEATH THE AUDIT**

Arthur Curry presents the HPB with a sovereignty problem it has managed to avoid in every prior audit: the subject is not merely wealthy — he is the head of state of a nation whose territorial claim covers the majority of the planet. Atlantis has never ratified the Demi-God Act. It has, in fact, never ratified anything involving surface-world governance, a posture it maintains with the serene indifference of a civilization that has been underwater since before Rome. The HPB does not concede that this exempts Subject from review. The HPB does, however, note that its enforcement agents are not rated for depths below 200 meters, and that Atlantis is considerably deeper than that.

The core issue is not Arthur Curry's personal fortune. It is the structural reality that one individual holds sovereign authority over an ecosystem that contains roughly half of Earth's unmined mineral wealth, 97% of its liquid water, and the atmospheric oxygen supply chain for every living thing on the surface. He is not merely rich. He is the landlord of the world's life-support system.

### **1. The Wealth Quotient (The "Hoarding" Metric)**

Curry's estimated net worth of $150 trillion represents a conservative floor based on publicly estimable assets: Atlantean sovereign wealth funds, deep-sea rare earth and polymetallic nodule deposits (estimated at $750 trillion in untapped value, of which Atlantis controls access to the majority), undersea thermal energy infrastructure, and the cultural and military assets of the Atlantean kingdom. The HPB's Asset Valuation Division notes that this estimate deliberately excludes the **Speculative Territorial Premium** — the implied value of Atlantis's ability to unilaterally restrict or permit surface-world access to deep-ocean resources. If that leverage were priced as an asset, Subject's RTWI would exceed $400 trillion.

**Concentration Hazard — Class I (Sovereign Wealth Ambiguity):** Atlantean sovereign wealth is legally held by the Crown, not Subject personally. Curry's legal representatives have argued that auditing his net worth is equivalent to auditing the U.S. Treasury and attributing it to the President. The HPB disagrees. The President cannot breathe underwater, has no biological claim to the throne, and does not personally command a standing army of marine life. Subject's relationship to Atlantean wealth is not analogous to a head of state — it is the relationship of a hereditary absolute monarch to a treasury that has had ten thousand years to compound.

**Concentration Hazard — Class II (Biological Enhancement):** Subject is a hybrid human-Atlantean, granting him superhuman strength, deep-sea pressure tolerance, marine telepathy, and lifespan that significantly exceeds the human standard. The HPB's Division of Biological Asset Assessment classifies his hydrokinetic and telepathic capabilities as **Embodied Capital** with direct economic implications: his ability to summon and direct marine life constitutes, in effect, an unregulated biological workforce. The shrimp, the whales, the sharks — they are all, in a legally uncomfortable sense, employees.

### **2. The Saintly Pivot (The "Public Good" Hedge)**

Subject's record here is genuinely mixed, which the HPB acknowledges is more than it can say for most subjects at this valuation tier. Curry has, on documented occasions, intervened to prevent ecological disasters that would have harmed both surface and subsurface populations. He has cooperated with surface-world governments on maritime security and has participated in the Justice League's humanitarian operations without apparent personal financial motive.

However, the HPB is required to note that Subject's "public good" record is inseparable from his interest in protecting the ocean — which is also his sovereign territory, military domain, and primary asset. Defending the sea from pollution is not a Saintly Pivot. It is asset management. The HPB cannot accept environmental stewardship as a philanthropic act when the environment in question is the subject's own kingdom.

HPB Optics Rating: **ELEVATED RISK — Conflict of Interest on all "Public Good" filings.** The ocean is not a charity. It is Subject's home, his inheritance, and his primary power base. Doing good things for it does not constitute disinterested generosity.

### **3. Influence Vector: The 71% Problem**

The core structural risk Curry poses has nothing to do with his personal temperament, which the HPB's review finds to be generally reasonable by the standards of this audit tier. The risk is architectural. Atlantis controls access to more of Earth's surface — and more of Earth's undiscovered resources — than any surface nation has ever controlled. The Demi-God Act was designed to prevent individuals from accumulating enough wealth to hold sovereign power over others. Subject Curry inherited a kingdom that already had that power ten thousand years before the Act was drafted.

The HPB notes that Curry is, simultaneously, a U.S. citizen (born Curry, Arthur, in Amnesty Bay, Massachusetts) and the absolute monarch of a civilization that does not recognize U.S. law. This dual status creates a jurisdictional paradox that the HPB's attorneys have been reviewing for eighteen months without resolution. Subject is legally subject to the Demi-God Act as a U.S. citizen. Subject is legally exempt from the Demi-God Act as a foreign sovereign. Both of these things are true. The HPB has assigned this paradox its own case number: **HPB-JURISDICTIONAL-VOID-001.**

## **⚖️ THE VERDICT: THE OCEAN IS NOT A LOOPHOLE (BUT IT MIGHT BE)**

**Confidence Score: 91%**

Arthur Curry exceeds every Ascension threshold by factors that would qualify him for CRITICAL status on wealth alone, before any biological or sovereign multipliers are applied. The HPB's 9% confidence reduction reflects not doubt about Subject's Demi-God classification — he clearly qualifies — but about the Bureau's ability to do anything about it. Subject lives, by definition, in international waters. His kingdom does not recognize the Demi-God Act. His enforcement of surface-world agreements is voluntary. The HPB cannot compel a sovereign nation to comply with domestic legislation by droning a gold-bordered envelope to the Atlantean throne room at a depth of four thousand meters.

The HPB nonetheless issues this audit on the record, classifies Subject as a CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK, and formally requests that Subject voluntarily engage with the Mortal Recertification process as a gesture of goodwill to the surface-world communities whose oxygen he technically controls.

> **Audit Summary:** "Subject Curry is not approaching Ascension. Subject Curry has been the sovereign monarch of a civilization so wealthy that it voluntarily hid itself from the global economy for millennia to avoid diplomatic entanglement. The HPB does not face the question of whether to classify him as a Demi-God. The HPB faces the question of whether the Demi-God Act was designed to handle a situation where the subject's 'escape vehicle' is the entire ocean floor. The answer, after eighteen months of legal review, is: no."
