## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE MAN WHO BUILDS THE SERVERS THAT BUILD THE GODS**

Barry Lam founded Quanta Computer as a laptop contract manufacturer decades ago; the company has since become one of the world's largest original-design manufacturers of AI servers, assembling the physical racks that house the GPU clusters training the era's largest models. The AI infrastructure boom has made Lam Taiwan's richest person — a fortune the Bureau finds sits at an unusually direct intersection of ascension-adjacent hardware and individual concentrated wealth.
