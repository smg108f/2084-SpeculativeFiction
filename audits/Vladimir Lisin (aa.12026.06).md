## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE STEEL TSAR**

He is also a competitive sport shooter who has competed at Olympic level. He has not been sanctioned by the EU or US — a distinction that sets him apart from many Russian billionaires and is partly attributable to NLMK's significant European industrial footprint and Lisin's more careful distance from the political circle around Putin than many oligarch peers. He nonetheless operates one of Russia's largest industrial enterprises during a period of Russian military aggression.
