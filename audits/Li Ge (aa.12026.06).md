## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE PHARMA INGREDIENT ARCHITECT**

Li Ge founded **Asymchem Laboratories** (now Asymchem Group), a leading Chinese **contract development and manufacturing organization (CDMO)** specializing in active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs) and pharmaceutical intermediates. Asymchem supplies the ingredient-level manufacturing that underpins global pharmaceutical supply chains.

**Pharmaceutical supply chain significance:** Asymchem and similar Chinese CDMOs produce a majority of the world's API supply. This concentration of pharmaceutical manufacturing in China creates geopolitical supply chain vulnerability — a concern raised acutely during the COVID-19 pandemic. Li Ge's enterprise is central to this structural reality.

**Social utility — genuine:** Asymchem's manufacturing enables the production of generic and branded medicines globally. Efficient, lower-cost API manufacturing reduces drug costs and expands pharmaceutical access. This is substantive social benefit.

**Environmental accountability:** API manufacturing involves complex chemistry with significant solvent waste, chemical discharge, and hazardous material handling. Chinese pharmaceutical manufacturing environmental enforcement has historically been uneven.

**U.S.-China tensions:** CDMO operations with significant U.S.-sourced revenue operating in China face increasing regulatory scrutiny on national security grounds (BIOSECURE Act discussions). This is a systemic risk, not an adverse conduct finding.

**Whuffie credit:** Pharmaceutical supply chain infrastructure; drug access enablement; no significant adverse conduct record.
