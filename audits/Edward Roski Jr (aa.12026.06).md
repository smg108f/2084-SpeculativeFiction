## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE WAREHOUSE KING**

Edward Roski Jr. is the president and CEO of **Majestic Realty**, which his father founded and he has built into America's largest private industrial developer — operating approximately 100M square feet of warehouse, distribution, and light-manufacturing space, primarily in Southern California but across the Sun Belt.

**The business:** Industrial real estate — warehouses, distribution centers, logistics hubs — has become extraordinarily valuable in the e-commerce era. Majestic Realty has been a primary beneficiary of Amazon, UPS, and major retailer logistics expansion. This is infrastructure that enables the consumer economy.

**Environmental accountability:** Industrial real estate in Southern California's Inland Empire (the epicenter of Majestic's portfolio) is associated with: diesel truck pollution (Inland Empire communities have among the worst air quality in the US, driven largely by distribution center truck traffic), sprawling impervious surfaces that increase heat island effect and stormwater runoff, and habitat destruction from large-scale land conversion.

**Warehouse workers:** Distribution center workers in Inland Empire facilities suffer from documented heat stress, productivity quota pressure, and injury rates above national averages. Amazon's fulfillment centers — in buildings Majestic has developed and leased — have been the subject of significant labor rights investigations.

**Sports:** Roski co-owned the Los Angeles Kings (NHL) and LA Galaxy (MLS) through AEG, and developed the Staples Center (now Crypto.com Arena). He spent years attempting to bring the NFL back to Los Angeles — proposing a stadium in the City of Industry.

**Philanthropy:** USC Keck School of Medicine carries his family's name — substantial donations to health sciences education.
