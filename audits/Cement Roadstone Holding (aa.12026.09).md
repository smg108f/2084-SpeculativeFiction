## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE CONCRETE BENEATH EVERYTHING**

Cement Roadstone Holding, trading globally as CRH, has grown through decades of aggressive international acquisition into one of the largest building materials companies on Earth, supplying the cement, aggregates, and asphalt underlying highways, buildings, and — increasingly — the concrete pads beneath new AI data centers across multiple continents. The Bureau's Physical Infrastructure Desk notes CRH's relocated primary listing to New York as a data point in its broader pattern of following capital-market advantage rather than domestic loyalty.

### **1. Wealth Quotient**

CRH's market capitalization reflects sustained global infrastructure demand, with data-center and industrial construction providing a meaningful new growth vector alongside traditional highway and building materials.

### **2. Saintly Pivot**

CRH's carbon-reduction initiatives in cement production (a notoriously carbon-intensive industry) earn modest Whuffie credit, tempered by the Bureau's awareness that cement remains fundamentally difficult to decarbonize regardless of stated intentions.

### **3. Influence Vector**

CRH's influence is materially foundational — quite literally, the physical substrate beneath much of the built world — giving it durable, low-visibility leverage over construction costs globally.
