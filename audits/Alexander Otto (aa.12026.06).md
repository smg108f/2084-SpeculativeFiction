## **AUDIT SUMMARY: THE MALL KING**

Alexander Otto is the CEO of ECE Group, one of Europe's largest commercial real estate companies — specializing in the development, operation, and management of shopping centers. ECE manages over 200 shopping centers across Europe, primarily in Germany and Central/Eastern Europe, with a total retail footprint of roughly 7 million square meters. He is the son of Michael Otto, who built the Otto Group (one of the world's largest catalogue and e-commerce retail companies).

**The shopping center question:** ECE's dominance in German and Central European retail real estate gives it enormous leverage over which retailers enter which cities, what rents look like for smaller businesses, and the shape of urban commercial life. Shopping center concentration — particularly the ECE model of out-of-town or mixed-use malls anchoring regional retail — has been associated with the decline of city center independent retail in many European markets.

**The broader Otto Group context:** The Otto family's commercial legacy includes Otto Group's transition from catalogue to e-commerce — one of the more successful European retail adaptations of the digital era.
