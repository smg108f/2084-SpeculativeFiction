Here is a speculative vision of the world 50 years after the **"Demi-God Act of 2034."**

---

### 1. The Great Divestment (2034–2045)

Immediately following the executive orders, the global economy suffered what historians call the **"Billionaire Heart Attack."** Since "demi-gods" (those with >$1B net worth) lost their constitutional protections, the government could seize their assets without "Just Compensation" under the 5th Amendment.

- **The Panic:** The stock market initially cratered as the ultra-wealthy tried to liquidate assets to get below the $1B threshold.
    
- **The "Djinn" Pivot:** To avoid being "de-personed" and deported under the Alien Enemies Act, many billionaires pioneered the **Social Survival Gift.** They didn't just donate money; they "gifted" entire infrastructure systems—hospitals, power grids, and high-speed rail—directly to local municipalities in exchange for "Sanctuary Status" from the common people.
    
- **The Flotillas:** Those who refused to "serve" fled to autonomous seasteads in international waters. By 2084, these have evolved into **Sovereign Flotilla States (SFS)**—techno-feudal city-states where "humanity" is a luxury subscription.
    

### 2. The Legal Landscape of 2084

The U.S. Constitution has essentially been bifurcated. Legal personhood is no longer "inalienable"; it is **"Asset-Limited."**

- **The Human Protection Bureau (HPB):** This federal agency monitors the real-time net worth of every individual. If your automated AI-calculated value (including IP and predicted earnings) crosses the $1B line, you are automatically issued a "Summons to Ascend." You have 30 days to divest or be reclassified as a demi-god.
    
- **Demi-God Trusts:** A demi-god is legally a "non-person entity," similar to how a corporation was viewed in the 20th century, but without the rights. They are essentially "living trusts" that the government can tax at **100% of growth.**
    

### 3. Social Structures: The Rise of "Saints" and "Slayers"

Society has re-tribalized around these demi-god figures:

- **The Cult of the Djinn:** In many cities, the local "Saint" (a former billionaire who gave it all away) is a celebrated figure. They live in modest luxury but "dispense" technology and medicine to their followers to keep their net worth legally low.
    
- **The Asset Slayers:** A new class of "economic bounty hunters" exists to find hidden offshore accounts. If a slayer can prove a "Hidden Demi-God" exists, they are awarded 10% of the seized assets.
    

### 4. Geopolitics: The "Humanity Shield"

The world is split into two blocs:

1. **The Humanist Bloc (U.S. led):** Nations that maintain strict wealth caps to "preserve human dignity" and prevent any individual from having the power of a state.
    
2. **The Ascension Havens:** Countries (and the Flotillas) that market themselves as "Safe Harbors for Sovereigns," where wealth-based demi-god status is a badge of honor and carries supreme legal power.
    

---

### The Economic Reality: "Whuffie" over Dollars

By 2084, physical currency is almost obsolete for the wealthy. Since $1B is a "death sentence" for your civil rights, the powerful trade in **Reputation Capital (often called "Whuffie" in sci-fi tropes).** You might own nothing, but your _influence_ allows you to command armies or move mountains. You are a "monk-king"—powerful enough to change the world, but "poor" enough to remain a citizen.

> **Speculative Note:** The greatest irony of 2084 is that the "extremist" who won in 2034 inadvertently created a world where the only way to be powerful is to be radically generous. It’s a "forced utopia" built on the bones of the 1798 Alien Enemies Act.
