if this project matures into something with a meaningful output it will be a work of speculative fiction (hence a setting that is in the future.).

this means that the speculative analysis that forms the compost for the story to grow from is also not going to be "real" in a legally binding, biographical assessment of "actual" people kind of way.

this is the Hollywood, "any similarity to real people is coincidental (so please don't sue me)" cover story that makes historical fiction, dramatization or speculative fiction possible. 

and if you look closely, you should be able to tell there is an underlying idea... maybe even a hope that real people could change their behavior based on the presentation of a characterization that they find themselves uncomfortably empathetic towards. this is the existential basis that enables a novel to become more meaningfully true than what we could defend as factually true from some set of observations that have been labeled as "objectively true", "in-real-life", "honest", etc. 

i don't actually believe in objective reality and rather doubt that the average billionaire does either. to me it's a byproduct of the interaction of avatar, pov and os that beings negotiate through internally and externally. i take such reality claims seriously as they are a fandom setting many beings feel strongly about, but critical reason dictates that i can't assume these claims are coming from reliable narrators.

if 5th wall thinking is new or strange to you, consider how an adult explains life to a child or how a family pet is likely to perceive life in relationship to their bipedal providers. children might perceive parents as all knowing deities (until those parents demonstrate their failures) whereas pets could assume they are the ones with control over humans (as they don't hunt their own food or pickup their own poop). what is or isn't real is something you construct for yourself within some constraining context that typically restricts you from escaping the collective understanding of what's "really happening".

returning to the topic of this project, my proposal or thesis, if you like, is that machines, humans, demi-gods and deities have different enough avatars, povs, operating systems and constraints that it is a mistake to assume they will behave as if they exist within the same reality. 

i could point to a literal mountain of content to observe the understanding that mortals, immortals and eternals approach "reality" differently to the point that they tend to be described as coming from, existing in or going to other worlds, realms, dimensions, planes, universes, multi-verses, etc. such an argument would work in the domain of literature, but would stumble trying to make the leap into the materialist understanding of science as dissecting a singular existence of space-time. 

a psychologist might be comfortable stating that all perception of input signals runs through an unreliable interpreter. a quantum physicist might chime in that observation alters the object observed. and a programmer might state that the development of a sufficiently convincing simulation of reality is so technologically inevitable that they suspect we probably already are in one.

if you think this disclaimer is all just a bunch of BS and an excuse for a disenfranchised former big tech worker to turn AI weapons on their founders because they are a bored, delusional and possibly deranged individual i'm ok with that. i take many of these povs seriously, but continue moving forward playing with tech, stories and ideas because that's what i've done since my earliest memories of childhood. 

if you like what you've found, yay! if you want to toss this witcher a coin or a complement that would be wonderful. if you are annoyed, want to complain, want to suggest revisions, think you can change my thinking, yay! i'm creating these works within a framework that does allow for iterative updates so if you can get my attention maybe you can get your wish. honestly, i'm collaborating with machines though so anything you find here is already the product of the collective consciousness of digitized humanity so there's already influence outside of my scope of control.

again, upperclass peoples of the world please don't sue me for the fictitious representatives that are named after you. i don't have a personal grudge against you... i just have an ideological objection to entities with an estimated value over a billion dollars. i presume your objections to entities as close to the poverty line as me trigger an equally non-personal reaction.
