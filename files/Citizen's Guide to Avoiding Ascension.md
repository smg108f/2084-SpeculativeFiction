## **THE CITIZEN’S GUIDE TO AVOIDING ASCENSION (2084 EDITION)**

Congratulations. If you are reading this, your **Real-Time Wealth Index (RTWI)** has likely crossed the **$950,000,000** mark. You are officially in the "Red Zone." Within $50 million, the Human Protection Bureau (HPB) will trigger an **Apotheosis Audit**, and you risk being legally reclassified as a **Demi-God Entity (DGE)**.

Under the **Executive Orders of 2034**, once you hit $1B, you are no longer a "Person" under the Constitution. You are a "Force of Nature," an "Alien Influence," and a "National Security Risk."

**Follow these steps to ensure you remain a protected Human Citizen.**

---

### **1. The "Saintly" Pivot (Immediate Divestment)**

The fastest way to drop your net worth without losing your lifestyle is the **Infrastructure Gift**.

- **How it works:** Transfer ownership of your private estates, server farms, or transport hubs to the local municipality.
    
- **The Loophole:** You can negotiate a **"Life-Tenancy Usage Agreement."** You technically own $0, but you have the legal right to live in the "Public Museum" (formerly your mansion) until your biological expiration.
    
- **Legal Status:** You are now a "Caretaker," not an "Owner." The HPB loves this—it looks like "Saintly Service."
    

### **2. Trade Dollars for "Whuffie" (Reputation Capital)**

In 2084, money is a liability. **Influence** is the only asset that the HPB cannot seize (yet).

- **Strategy:** Funnel your remaining millions into "Public Good" algorithms. Fund a universal basic health-bot or a free desalination plant.
    
- **The Goal:** You want a **Social Credit Surplus** so high that if the government tries to deport you as an "Alien Enemy," the common people will riot in your defense.
    
- **Rule of Thumb:** If you have $999 million, you are a target. If you have $100 and a billion people who love you, you are a Shadow King.
    

### **3. Watch Out for "Stealth Appreciation"**

The HPB's AI doesn't just look at your bank account; it looks at your **Potentiality Value**.

- **The Danger:** If you own a patent for a new carbon-capture tech that _could_ be worth $5B, the HPB will "Pre-Ascend" you.
    
- **The Fix:** Use a **"Dead-Hand License."** Open-source the tech immediately. You lose the profit, but you keep your right to a trial by jury and the ability to vote.

### **4. What to do if you receive an "Ascension Summons"**

If the HPB sends a drone to your door with a gold-bordered envelope, you have **30 days** to "Fall from Grace."

1. **Do not flee to a Flotilla yet.** Once you enter international waters, your U.S. citizenship is voided instantly.
    
2. **Execute a "Burning Bush" Event.** Use our proprietary algorithm to donate exactly enough to bring you down to $800M in a single 24-hour window.
    
3. **Apply for "Mortal Recertification."** We will file the paperwork to prove you have purged the "Divine Taint" of extreme wealth.
    

> **LEGAL ALERT:** _Be advised that attempting to hide assets in "Biological Trusts" (offshore cloning vats or neural-net storage) is a felony. The HPB considers hidden wealth to be "Dark Divinity" and will expedite your deportation to the Seastead Colonies._
