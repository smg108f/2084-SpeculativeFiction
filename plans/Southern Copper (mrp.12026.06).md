### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [SOUTHERN COPPER CORPORATION]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-11SCCO
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice

Southern Copper Corporation's controlling 88.9% ownership by the Larrea family through Grupo México, combined with its hold on the world's largest private copper reserve base, constitutes a **Critical Mineral Sovereignty** classification under the Demi-God Act's amended Section 12 (Resource Chokepoints). Recertification requires structural dissolution of the family control concentration and reorientation of reserve benefits toward affected communities.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Reserve Democratization (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies Southern Copper's 75 million ton copper reserve base as a **Planetary Transition Asset** — resources whose economic value derives from global decarbonization policy rather than the Larrea family's investment acumen.

- **The Action:** Transfer operating control of the Peruvian mining operations (Cuajone, Toquepala, Tía María) to a **Binational Public Trust** jointly administered by Peru and Chile's governments, with 30% representation for affected Indigenous and agricultural communities.
- **The Sacrifice:** Larrea family loses direct operational control over the hemisphere's most strategic copper deposits.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Critical Mineral Sovereignty designation is lifted. The resource serves the energy transition rather than a single family's wealth accumulation.

### **2. The Community Compensation Fund (The Reparations Burn)**

The HPB's Social Impact Division classifies Southern Copper's Peruvian community relations record — particularly the Tía María permit controversy involving agricultural water fears — as a **Documented Community Harm Archive**.

- **The Action:** Establish a $5B irrevocable Community Harm Reparations Fund, administered by elected representatives from communities within 50km of all Peruvian operations, with no Grupo México representatives on the governance board.
- **The Sacrifice:** $5B drawn from retained earnings and future dividends.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Southern Copper's Social Hostility Index drops below the threshold that triggers automatic Ascension acceleration.

### **3. The Grupo México Separation (The Control Burn)**

The HPB classifies the 88.9% Grupo México ownership stake as a **Concentration of Critical Resource Control** incompatible with the Demi-God Act's prohibition on single-entity control of energy transition inputs.

- **The Action:** Grupo México must reduce its stake to below 25% within 24 months through a public offering program, with shares offered at discounted rates to Peruvian and Mexican citizens first.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Larrea family loses effective control over the world's largest copper company.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Southern Copper becomes an institution owned by the communities whose geology it extracts.

> _"The copper we extracted built our wealth on ground that was not ours, using water that belongs to communities who did not consent. We return the operational control. We pay the bill. We are no longer the owners of a continent's transition metals. We are custodians with a diminishing stake."_
