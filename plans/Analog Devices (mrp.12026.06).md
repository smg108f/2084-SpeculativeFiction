### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ANALOG DEVICES]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-05AD
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification — Probationary Saint Pathway

Analog Devices' Probationary Saint classification requires the establishment of dual-use technology governance structures that match the societal stakes of its embedded sensory infrastructure. The HPB does not dispute ADI's operational legitimacy; it requires governance commensurate with the technology's reach.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Dual-Use Ethics Board (The Sensor Sovereignty Transfer)**

ADI's product decisions about sensing technologies affect civilians, patients, soldiers, and systems they will never see. The HPB requires that these decisions not be made unilaterally by a commercial entity optimizing for revenue.

- **The Action:** ADI must establish an independent **Sensing Ethics Board** with legal authority to delay any product deployment — for a maximum of 90 days — involving sensing applications in surveillance, autonomous weapons, or population-scale monitoring. The board must include HPB representatives, civil liberties advocates, and medical ethicists. ADI funds the board; ADI does not control it.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of unilateral product timeline control for a subset of high-sensitivity applications; potential competitive disadvantage if delay affects time-to-market.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Reclassification from "Sensory Infrastructure Monopoly Risk" to "Governed Dual-Use Actor" — a category the HPB considers both more accurate and more comfortable to supervise.

### **2. The Vulnerability Disclosure Commitment (The Transparency Burn)**

ADI's embedded position in critical industrial and military infrastructure means that security vulnerabilities in its chips — whether exploitable for espionage, sabotage, or manipulation of autonomous systems — constitute national-scale risks. The HPB requires that ADI's vulnerability disclosure timelines be governed by public interest rather than commercial convenience.

- **The Action:** ADI must commit to a binding **72-Hour Critical Infrastructure Disclosure** protocol: any identified vulnerability in chips deployed in medical devices, water systems, power grids, or military platforms must be reported to the HPB within 72 hours of internal confirmation, with no commercial embargo period.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of ability to quietly patch vulnerabilities before public disclosure; potential reputational exposure.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** ADI's security posture becomes a public asset rather than a commercial one. The HPB will designate this as a **Civic Technology Commitment**, which generates Whuffie credit sufficient to offset the Sensory Infrastructure Monopoly Risk flag.

### **3. The Defense Revenue Disclosure (The Dual-Use Accounting)**

ADI does not fully disclose the proportion of its revenue derived from defense and surveillance applications. The HPB requires this information to accurately assess the organization's dual-use risk profile.

- **The Action:** ADI must disclose, in its annual HPB filing, the proportion of revenue from defense, intelligence, and law enforcement applications, broken down by application type (weapons guidance, surveillance, logistics, medical). This disclosure is public.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of commercial opacity around defense revenue; potential pressure from civil society on specific application categories.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Conversion from "Opacity Risk" to "Disclosed Actor" — a status that permits the HPB to reduce monitoring intensity proportional to the disclosed risk profile.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 ICU Nurses and Factory Safety Inspectors** and recite the following:

> *"The chips in the patient monitor and the chips in the targeting system come from the same wafers. We are not responsible for who pulls the trigger or who reads the readout. We are responsible for what the chips are allowed to sense, and who decided that. We will not hide those decisions in a revenue line. The machines we teach to feel will feel in the open."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the Apotheosis Protocol. ADI's dual-use product lines will be placed under direct HPB licensing review, with each defense application requiring individual approval from a citizen ethics panel. The company will discover that the bureaucratic overhead of operating without an internal ethics board is substantially greater than the overhead of operating with one.
