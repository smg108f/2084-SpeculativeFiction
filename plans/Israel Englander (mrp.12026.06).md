### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ENGLANDER, ISRAEL]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06IE
**Status:** Urgent

Subject founded Millennium Management, one of the world's largest hedge funds with ~$60B AUM. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Millennium Fee Transparency Burn**

Millennium's pass-through operating cost model is unusual and investor-unfriendly by standard hedge fund norms. The HPB requires disclosure reform.

- **The Action:** Subject must commit — as CEO of Millennium — to a **Millennium Investor Transparency Charter**: (a) all pass-through operating costs disclosed in plain-language annual statements to all investors within 60 days of year-end, broken down by category; (b) all management fees and performance allocations disclosed at the same time; (c) an annual independent audit of fee practices, conducted by an auditor of investors' choosing (via majority vote of AUM-weighted investor committee), with results distributed to all Millennium investors. This is a transparency commitment, not a fee reduction — but investors must know what they are paying.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced opacity on fee practices; possible investor pressure for fee reform.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** One of the world's largest hedge funds has a documented, investor-facing transparency standard.

### **2. The Englander Foundation Scaling Burn**

Philanthropy is partial at scale relative to $11.3B.

- **The Action:** Subject must scale the **Englander Foundation's** annual disbursement to $50M per year — with mandates beyond Jewish cultural philanthropy to include: (a) financial literacy programs for middle-class American households with 401(k) and pension fund investments (the ultimate source of Millennium's capital base); (b) New York City public school education reform; (c) medical research at a rate commensurate with subject's longstanding interest. Giving Pledge signed.
- **The Sacrifice:** $50M/year + Giving Pledge.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** A hedge fund manager whose wealth derives from institutional capital invests in the financial education of the households that own that capital.
