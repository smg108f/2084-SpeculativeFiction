### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [AGRICULTURAL BANK OF CHINA]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-58ABC
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Agricultural Bank of China holds approximately $7 trillion in total assets and serves as the designated financial instrument of the CCP's rural social contract, creating an Agrarian Sovereignty Lock over 590+ million rural Chinese citizens who have no viable alternative provider. Recertification requires dismantling the captive dependency structure before AgBank can be assessed as a Mortal institution rather than a state instrument of agrarian control.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Sannong Severance (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies AgBank's rural mandate as a **Monopsony Finance Hazard**. An institution that simultaneously lends to, takes deposits from, disburses government subsidies to, and processes all payment activity for hundreds of millions of people with no alternative is not a bank — it is infrastructure that has been granted a bank charter. The "Three Rural Issues" mission is not philanthropy. It is the architecture of dependency rebranded as service.

- **The Action:** AgBank must spin off its entire rural branch network — approximately 23,000 county and township branches — into a portfolio of independently governed **Rural Credit Cooperatives**, each chartered at the provincial level with boards elected by account-holders and subject to the Bureau's Competitive Access Standard (no single institution may hold >15% of a rural county's deposits and loans simultaneously).
- **The Sacrifice:** AgBank loses its rural monopoly, its captive subsidy disbursement role, and the structural loyalty infrastructure it administers on the CCP's behalf. Its balance sheet contracts by an estimated $1.8–2.4T in rural assets.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The 590 million rural accounts become customers rather than captive accounts. AgBank becomes a commercial bank. The Bureau downgrades its Political Agency classification from CRITICAL to ELEVATED.

---

### **2. The New York Penance (The AML Accountability Burn)**

In 2016 — well before the Demi-God Act — the New York Department of Financial Services fined AgBank $215 million for falsifying dollar transaction documents, obscuring payments to sanctioned countries, and silencing the internal compliance officer who attempted to report the violations. The Bureau classifies this not as a historical footnote but as **Documented Institutional Character**: the demonstrated reflex of the institution is concealment when confronted with external accountability.

- **The Action:** AgBank must establish an independent **Compliance Sovereignty Commission** staffed entirely by personnel selected by lottery from the bank's own low-level rural branch tellers and compliance officers — not by headquarters, not by the Ministry of Finance, not by Central Huijin. The Commission holds binding review authority over all overseas transactions and reports quarterly to the Bureau, not to the Party Committee.
- **The Sacrifice:** AgBank surrenders institutional control of its own compliance function to the employees it has historically treated as bureaucratic infrastructure. The Party Committee's organizational appointment power over compliance is dissolved.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Bureau reclassifies AgBank's **Institutional Obstruction** flag from active to historical-pending, contingent on 24 months of clean reporting.

---

### **3. The Inclusive Finance Inversion (The Ideological Burn)**

AgBank's public communications describe its rural lending program as "inclusive finance" — a term suggesting voluntary outreach to underserved communities. The Bureau notes that "inclusive finance" directed at a population with no alternative provider is not inclusion. It is enclosure. The institution's self-mythology as a benefactor to the rural poor must be confronted directly.

- **The Action:** AgBank must publish, in Mandarin, Cantonese, and the twelve most widely spoken regional languages of its rural service area, a **Plain-Language Dependency Disclosure** to be distributed to every account-holder: a document that states, clearly, which financial services the account-holder can currently obtain from no other provider, what alternatives the Rural Credit Cooperative spinoff will make available, and what the account-holder's rights are under the Bureau's Competitive Access Standard.
- **The Sacrifice:** AgBank's "inclusive finance" brand is replaced by accurate language. The institution must acknowledge, in writing, the structure of the dependency it administers.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Bureau's **Philanthropic Capture Doctrine** flag is cleared. AgBank's Saintly Pivot rating improves from LOW to MARGINAL.

---

### **4. The Belt and Road Portfolio Audit (The Geopolitical Burn)**

AgBank's BRI financing — $12.6 billion in loans to projects in BRI countries between 2014 and mid-2018, with continued activity through the present — creates the same class of Sovereign Debt Dependency the Bureau has flagged in the ICBC audit. AgBank's BRI exposure is smaller than ICBC's but represents a secondary Financial Sovereignty Extension vector that must be addressed in recertification.

- **The Action:** All AgBank BRI loans to foreign governments must be transferred to a newly established **Multilateral Agricultural Development Trust**, governed by a board with seats allocated to the recipient countries' agricultural ministries, the UN Food and Agriculture Organization, and two seats selected by lottery from the rural farming communities whose agricultural exports the financed infrastructure serves.
- **The Sacrifice:** AgBank surrenders bilateral leverage in its BRI portfolio. The loans still exist; the leverage relationship shifts from China-AgBank to a multilateral structure the Bureau can assess.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Bureau's **Financial Sovereignty Extension** classification is reduced from active to residual.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must — through its Chairman, Party Secretary, and all eleven members of its Board of Directors — stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Rice Farmers, Village Schoolteachers, and Rural Postal Workers from AgBank's lowest-income service counties** and recite the following:

> *"We are not the field. We do not own the harvest. The farmer who borrowed from us was not helped by us; she was offered terms she could not refuse because we made certain she had nowhere else to go. We hold trillions because we were given a monopoly over poverty. We will not call this service again."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All AgBank assets will be placed under Bureau receivership and administered by a rotating panel of subsistence farmers drawn by lottery from the 832 counties the bank claims to have lifted from poverty. The Party Committee will be dissolved. The Chairman will be reassigned to the rural branch he least recently visited.
