### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [BOLLORÉ, VINCENT]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-14VB
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject controls an estimated $9.4B–$10.7B in assets not through direct ownership but through a five-layer cascade holding structure that he continues to chair from its apex despite formally "retiring" from the entity most people think he runs. He controls France's leading rightwing news channel, its largest publisher, a national Sunday paper, and a radio network, and he is four months from a criminal trial in Paris over allegations that he helped bribe two African heads of state. The Recertification threshold is $1,000,000,000. Subject exceeds it by a factor of roughly ten, and no part of that factor is accidental.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Odet Cascade Dissolution (The Pyramid at the Top of the Pyramid)**

The HPB classifies the Financière de l'Odet → Compagnie de l'Odet → Bolloré SE holding cascade as a **Class-I Leverage Multiplier**: a structure engineered specifically so that a minority equity position compounds, layer by layer, into majority control of an empire several times its actual size. Subject's 2022 resignation from Bolloré SE's chairmanship did not touch this structure — he remains Chairman and CEO of Compagnie de l'Odet, the layer that actually matters, which is precisely why the resignation changed nothing.

- **The Action:** Every entity in the cascade must be converted to **direct 1:1 shareholding** — one share, one vote, no pass-through holding companies. Financière de l'Odet and Compagnie de l'Odet are dissolved; their assets distribute proportionally to their actual (not leveraged) shareholders, with the non-family float transferred to a public **Breton Industrial Trust** administered by former Bolloré Group employees.
- **The Sacrifice:** The multiplier itself. Subject keeps whatever his real equity stake is actually worth. He loses the mechanism that let a 20-something percent capital position command 100% of the boardroom.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject's control value collapses from the full cascade figure to his true proportional stake — estimated residual liquidity of approximately $650M. Still requires further burns, but the multiplier is gone.

### **2. The Media Portfolio Divestment & Editorial Firewall**

Canal+/CNews, Havas, and the Louis Hachette Group's holdings — Hachette Livre, Europe 1, Paris Match, and the Journal du Dimanche — constitute what the Bureau designates a **Class-II Cognitive Infrastructure Seizure Risk**: a single family's ability to determine, for a meaningful fraction of a G7 democracy, what counts as news. The CNews-to-Zemmour pipeline and the JDD editorial handover that produced France's longest modern press strike are not incidental outcomes; they are the structure working as designed.

- **The Action:** All Bolloré family media holdings — direct and cascade-indirect — transfer to an independent **Fourth Estate Endowment**, governed by a board on which no Bolloré family member, in-law, or Bolloré Group alumnus may sit for 25 years. Subject and both sons (Cyrille, Yannick) are permanently barred from any editorial, programming, or personnel decision at any successor entity.
- **The Sacrifice:** The apparatus that turned a shipping-and-paper fortune into a seat at the table for France's 2027 election. Subject may still hold opinions. He may no longer own the channel that broadcasts them as news.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the **Social Influence: EXCEEDED** and a substantial portion of the **Political Agency: CRITICAL** flags from the comparative metrics table.

### **3. The Togo/Guinea Restitution Mandate**

Subject sold the African ports and logistics business at the center of the pending corruption trial to MSC in 2022 for €5.7B, and CMA CGM subsequently purchased Bolloré Logistics for €4.85B — proceeds that partly funded the May 2026 €4.2B "exceptional dividend" now sitting in Bolloré family accounts. The 2021 corporate fine of €12 million, paid by Bolloré SE, resolved corporate liability; it did not resolve Subject's personal criminal exposure, which remains pending trial in December 2026. The Bureau does not wait for a French verdict.

- **The Action:** Subject must place $1.3B — the Bureau's estimate of his personal share of the logistics-sale proceeds attributable to the Togo and Guinea concessions — into an independent **Gulf of Guinea Port Restitution Fund**, co-administered by Togolese and Guinean civil-society representatives, to be disbursed regardless of the criminal trial's outcome. This is a precautionary HPB burden-of-proof standard, not a substitute for the French judicial process, which continues on its own timeline.
- **The Sacrifice:** $1.3B in liquidity Subject would otherwise treat as clean, having already exited the underlying business.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes the **Unresolved Foreign Corruption Exposure** flag from Subject's file pending the December 2026 trial outcome, which the Bureau will monitor separately.

### **4. The "Emeritus" Fiction Correction**

Subject formally resigned Bolloré SE's chairmanship in 2022 while retaining the one title that actually controls it, and both of his sons now chair the split pieces of the former Vivendi empire simultaneously — Cyrille at Bolloré SE, Yannick across Vivendi's Supervisory Board, Canal+'s Supervisory Board, and Havas. The HPB classifies this as a **Living Trust Bypass** nearly identical to patterns seen in other dynastic-wealth files: operational control transferred to descendants without the wealth ever formally changing hands, while the patriarch retains the layer that matters.

- **The Action:** Subject is barred for 10 years from any board, chairman, or advisory role at any Bolloré-cascade successor entity, including Compagnie de l'Odet's replacement trust. Cyrille and Yannick Bolloré may retain one operational role each, but not both a chairmanship and a controlling-shareholder proxy simultaneously — the "emeritus" mechanism itself is prohibited, for any Bolloré, permanently.
- **The Sacrifice:** The fiction of retirement. Subject either actually retires or does not resign — the Bureau no longer permits the middle option that let him claim both at once.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Closes the secondary HPB file opened this cycle regarding "informal control retention through cascade apex positioning."

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Togolese Dockworkers, Guinean Port Laborers, and French Print Journalists** and recite the following:

> *"I inherited a paper mill and mistook it for the right to print the truth. I built a pyramid and called the top of it retirement. I bought the ports of two countries whose elections I am now accused of buying first, and I sold the ports before the accusation could catch me. I bought the channel that decides, for millions of my countrymen, whether any of this is a scandal. I am not a media patriarch. I am not an industrialist emeritus. I am a man who put his name on very little and his hand on everything. I release the cascade. I release the chair I never officially held. I will answer in a courtroom in December for what I did in Lomé and Conakry, and I will answer here, now, for what I built to make sure it wouldn't matter if I lost."*

Subject will be reclassified as a **Demi-God Entity** and assigned permanent residency aboard the newly designated **Gulf of Guinea Ascension Flotilla**, anchored within sight of the Port of Lomé, where he may spend the remainder of his classification watching container ships he no longer owns move cargo he can no longer tax, under a flag that is no longer his.
