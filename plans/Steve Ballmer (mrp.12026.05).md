### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [BALLMER, STEVEN A.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-15SB
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject Ballmer has been formally classified as a **Critical Ascension Risk** under Audit Ref. 12026.05, with a Total Estimated Value of $128–136B — exceeding the Human Protection Bureau threshold by a factor of 132. While Subject's risk profile is lower than comparably-valued cases in the current registry, the Bureau's Passive Apotheosis classification (Microsoft equity, self-accreting) requires immediate intervention. Subject's Affective Sovereignty vector — control of an NBA franchise, a $2B private arena, and the world's largest arena scoreboard — constitutes a structural influence asset requiring divestiture regardless of the subject's apparent personal sincerity.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Microsoft Equity Liquidation (The Passive Apotheosis Termination)**

The HPB's primary objection to Subject Ballmer is not ideological, behavioral, or even relational. It is mathematical. His ~4% Microsoft stake — worth approximately $115–120B at current valuations — grows automatically, every quarter, driven by a company he left in 2014, led by a CEO he did not select, executing an AI strategy he did not design. The HPB cannot audit a subject whose wealth accretes while he is at a basketball game. This is the defining structural violation of the Ballmer file and must be resolved first.

- **The Action:** Subject must liquidate 99% of his Microsoft holdings — approximately $115–119B — and deposit the proceeds into the **American Futures Endowment**: a constitutionally protected public trust capitalized exclusively by Passive Apotheosis cases, funding universal pre-K, municipal broadband infrastructure, and workforce retraining programs in the post-industrial cities Subject's Ballmer Group has historically targeted. Subject may retain a residual Microsoft position of no more than $950M.
- **The Sacrifice:** The equity stake that made Ballmer one of the wealthiest humans in recorded history — accumulated not through founding genius but through 34 years of staying — is gone. He keeps the memories of all those stock splits.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Passive Apotheosis loop is severed. Subject's Direct Assets metric drops from **EXCEEDED (132x)** to **WITHIN RANGE**. The HPB's AI stops flagging him at breakfast.

---

### **2. The Clippers Divestiture (The Affective Sovereignty Dissolution)**

The HPB classifies the LA Clippers — and more specifically the Intuit Dome and its Halo Board — as an **Affective Sovereignty Asset**: a mechanism by which Subject exercises structural emotional influence over approximately 2 million active fans without their awareness of the ownership dynamic producing that influence. The fact that Subject appears to genuinely love basketball and may not be exploiting this dynamic cynically is noted in the file and changes nothing about its classification.

The Intuit Dome, privately financed at approximately $2B with no public subsidy, is also classified as a **Civic Infrastructure Asset Held in Private Hands** — an arena used by the public, generating public emotional events, governed by a single individual's ownership interest. The Halo Board, the HPB notes with something between alarm and aesthetic appreciation, is 40,000 square feet of LED display ringing the ceiling of a building that 18,000 people enter voluntarily and exit emotionally altered. This is infrastructure. It belongs to the commons.

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer 100% ownership of the LA Clippers franchise and the Intuit Dome to a **Community Ownership Trust**, structured as a fan-owned cooperative on the Green Bay Packers model, with shares distributed at nominal cost to season ticket holders and Inglewood residents within a 5-mile radius. Subject retains his courtside seats for life — as a fan, not an owner.
- **The Sacrifice:** The franchise Ballmer bought for $2B and built a $2B arena for — a project that consumed a decade and clearly brought him genuine joy — is no longer his to decide about. The Halo Board will still show the scores. He just won't own the roof it hangs from.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Affective Sovereignty vector is dissolved. The Intuit Dome is reclassified from **Private Spectacle Infrastructure** to **Civic Cultural Commons**. Subject is reclassified from **Franchise Owner** to **Enthusiastic Fan with Good Seats**, which the HPB notes is honestly a healthier way to experience sports.

---

### **3. The USAFacts Endowment (The Saintly Pivot Formalization)**

This is the anomalous burn. In every other Mortal Recertification Plan in the current cycle, Burn Three is punitive — a memetic penance, an ideological reversal, a reputational deflation. In the Ballmer case, Burn Three is an endorsement.

USAFacts.org is the most credibly sincere public-good contribution the HPB has encountered in the $100B+ tier. It has no advertising. It has no political slant. It publishes the government's own numbers, organized so that citizens can read them. Subject funds it personally at approximately $20–30M per year. The HPB's Civic Transparency Office has formally classified it as a **Class-I Public Data Commons** — the rarest category in the registry, applied only to infrastructure that provides public benefit without extracting loyalty, attention, or behavioral data in return.

The problem is not USAFacts's virtue. The problem is that it currently depends entirely on Subject Ballmer's continued willingness and ability to fund it. If he Ascends, it disappears. The HPB's solution is to make it permanent before that can happen.

- **The Action:** Subject must endow USAFacts with a permanent, independently governed trust capitalized at $500M — enough to operate at current scale in perpetuity. The trust board must include representatives from the National Archives, the Government Accountability Office, and a lottery-selected panel of public school civics teachers. Subject Ballmer may sit on the board as a non-voting Founding Donor. He may not, under any circumstances, control its editorial or operational decisions.
- **The Sacrifice:** USAFacts is no longer Ballmer's project. It is the country's. His name stays on the founding page. His fingerprints come off the budget.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Saintly Pivot is formally recognized and upgraded from **PARTIAL** to **COMPLETE**. Subject's HPB Civic Contribution Score reaches the highest level recorded for a $100B+ subject in the current cycle. The HPB issues a formal acknowledgment that this particular burn was, technically, the right thing to do anyway, and that Subject should have done it sooner but is getting credit for doing it now.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Seattle Public School Teachers and Inglewood Arena Concessions Workers** and recite the following:

> *"I did not found Microsoft. I was the thirtieth person to work there. I stayed for thirty-four years, and the stock kept going up, and eventually there was more money than any person should have — not because of what I built, but because of what I held. I bought a basketball team because I wanted to, and I built the biggest scoreboard in human history because I could, and I told myself these were gifts to the city. They were gifts I controlled. I published the government's financial data because I thought citizens deserved to read it, and that was the truest thing I did with the money. I am not a demi-god. I am a man who was employee number thirty at the right company at the right time. That is enough. That was always enough."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Asset Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Subject will be formally reclassified as a Demi-God Entity. His Microsoft stake will be seized and nationalized. The Intuit Dome will be placed under federal receivership. The Halo Board — all 40,000 square feet of it — will display, on rotation, the complete text of the Demi-God Act of 2034, the HPB's Passive Apotheosis classification guidelines, and a real-time ticker of what Subject's accreting wealth could have funded instead. It will be visible, the HPB notes, from every seat in the house.
