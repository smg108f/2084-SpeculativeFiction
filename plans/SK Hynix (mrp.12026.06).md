### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [SK HYNIX]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-12SH
**Status:** Precautionary — Supply Diversification Obligation Active

SK Hynix's Marginal Ascension Risk classification stems from systemic AI infrastructure concentration rather than corporate misconduct. Recertification requires the organization to actively reduce the global AI ecosystem's dependency on its single-vendor HBM position.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Competitor Investment Mandate (The Chokepoint Dilution Burn)**

An organization that holds 50–55% market share in a critical AI infrastructure component must not be permitted to sustain that dominance through market dynamics alone. The HPB requires SK Hynix to actively invest in reducing its own chokepoint position.

- **The Action:** SK Hynix must establish a **$2B HBM Ecosystem Fund** — investing in HBM development programs at Micron, Samsung, and emerging Asian semiconductor manufacturers — with the explicit mandate of ensuring that no single HBM vendor exceeds 40% global market share by 2035. Fund investments are subject to HPB review to ensure they constitute genuine capability-building rather than acquisition of potential competitors.
- **The Sacrifice:** $2B in direct investment that benefits competitors; deliberate sacrifice of market share dominance; potential reduction in HBM premium pricing power.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Reclassification from "AI Infrastructure Chokepoint" to "AI Ecosystem Architect" — a status the HPB considers more appropriate for the world's most important memory company.

### **2. The U.S. Listing Governance Requirement (The Transparency Burn)**

SK Hynix's planned $29.4B U.S. listing — the largest Korean equity offering in American market history — represents a deliberate capital concentration event at global scale. The HPB requires that this listing be accompanied by governance commitments commensurate with its size.

- **The Action:** As a condition of its U.S. listing, SK Hynix must commit to: (a) maintaining an independent board committee for technology licensing decisions; (b) establishing a public **Supply Chain Resilience Report** disclosing geographic concentration risks in its production facilities; (c) creating a **Customer Dependency Registry** identifying the AI systems that would be disrupted by a major SK Hynix production outage, with contingency plans for each.
- **The Sacrifice:** Enhanced disclosure requirements; board governance overhead; potential investor concern about disclosed concentration risks.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Conversion from "Opacity Risk" to "Publicly Accountable Infrastructure Provider" — a status required for any organization seeking $29B in public capital, in the HPB's view.

### **3. The Korean Tax Windfall Commitment (The National Wealth Sharing Burn)**

South Korea's government is publicly debating how to allocate the tax windfall from SK Hynix's surging semiconductor profits. The HPB notes that the workers who generated this wealth — and the communities surrounding the Icheon production facilities — have a legitimate claim to a portion of the prosperity.

- **The Action:** SK Hynix must commit 5% of annual net profit (above a baseline of $3B) to a **Korean Technology Commons Fund**, supporting science education, worker upskilling, and community development in semiconductor manufacturing regions. This commitment is binding for 15 years and independent of government direction.
- **The Sacrifice:** Estimated $500M–$1B annually at current profit levels.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Reclassification from "National Champion Without Community Commitment" to "Embedded Social Contributor" — a status that generates significant Whuffie credit in the HPB's Korean regional framework.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Korean Semiconductor Factory Workers and Global AI Researchers** and recite the following:

> *"We make the memory that AI thinks with. We hold 55% of the market because we were right about HBM before anyone else. We will not use that rightness to become unchallengeable. The fund is established. The competitors will grow. The workers in Icheon will share the profit. We are the most important memory company in the world, and we acknowledge that this makes us the most important memory company the world cannot afford to be dependent on. We intend to solve that problem ourselves, before the HPB is required to solve it for us."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the Apotheosis Protocol. SK Hynix's HBM patents will be placed under a **Critical Infrastructure Compulsory License**, making them available to qualified manufacturers worldwide at regulated royalty rates. The company will discover that the cost of licensing its technology voluntarily through an ecosystem fund is substantially less than licensing it involuntarily through HPB compulsion.
