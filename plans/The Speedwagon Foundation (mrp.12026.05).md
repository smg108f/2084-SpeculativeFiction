### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [THE SPEEDWAGON FOUNDATION]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-20-SF
**Status:** Precautionary / PROBATIONARY SAINT Monitoring Protocol

The Speedwagon Foundation has been classified PROBATIONARY SAINT. No immediate Asset Burn is required. The Foundation's $333 billion endowment exceeds the $1 billion threshold but is held in a genuine nonprofit trust structure with a mission charter that reflects the Act's intended purpose. The HPB's monitoring requirements for this entity are lighter than standard PROBATIONARY SAINT protocols, in recognition of the Foundation's century-long demonstrated mission integrity.

There is no 30-day burn schedule. There is a permanent compliance framework and one institutional resilience requirement.

---

### **PROBATIONARY SAINT MONITORING REQUIREMENTS**

**Annual Compliance Reporting:**
The Speedwagon Foundation must file an annual PROBATIONARY SAINT Compliance Report covering: (1) endowment balance and allocation — confirming mission-aligned deployment of all investment returns; (2) research output summary — a classified annex covering supernatural threat research findings may be submitted to a sealed HPB registry in lieu of public disclosure, where the nature of the research requires it; (3) governance integrity confirmation — annual board composition and independence certification.

**Mission Drift Early Warning Indicators:**
The following events trigger an emergency HPB review: (a) any change to the Foundation's nonprofit status or trust structure; (b) any endowment investment strategy change that prioritizes returns over mission alignment; (c) any for-profit subsidiary formation.

---

### **INSTITUTIONAL RESILIENCE REQUIREMENT**

The HPB's primary concern with the Speedwagon Foundation is not its current operations — it is the fragility created by its research monopoly. The Bureau requires that the Foundation address this fragility through a structured knowledge-sharing program.

- **The Action:** The Foundation must establish a Supernatural Threat Research Commons — a structured program for sharing non-classified research findings and training methodologies with at least three independent research institutions globally, such that the Foundation's core knowledge base is no longer held exclusively within a single institution. The Commons must be operational within 18 months. Classified materials remain sealed; the Commons addresses the institutional knowledge fragility in non-classified research domains.
- **The Purpose:** Ensures that if the Foundation were ever compromised, defunded, or structurally disrupted, its century of research would not be lost with it. Robert E.O. Speedwagon built the Foundation to outlast him. The Foundation must now be built to outlast itself.

---

### **THE PROBATIONARY SAINT ACKNOWLEDGMENT**

The Speedwagon Foundation's board must file an annual PROBATIONARY SAINT Acknowledgment including the following:

> _"Robert E.O. Speedwagon gave this Foundation everything he had and asked only that the work continue. The work has continued. We acknowledge that the work is not yet finished, that the threats the Foundation was built to address have not been fully resolved, and that our obligation is not merely to the endowment but to the mission it was built to serve. We accept the Bureau's monitoring as consistent with the transparency that genuine public benefit requires. We accept the Institutional Resilience Requirement as consistent with what Mr. Speedwagon would have wanted: a Foundation that outlasts any single institution, including itself."_

**PATHWAY TO FULL CLEARANCE:** Available after five consecutive years of full compliance, completion of the Supernatural Threat Research Commons, and an independent governance audit. The HPB anticipates no obstacles to clearance and has pre-assigned a senior analyst to facilitate the process.
