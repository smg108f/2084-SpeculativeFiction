### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [INVESTOR AB / WALLENBERG FAMILY]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-28INVE
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

The Wallenberg family's control of 35% of Swedish stock market capitalization through a century-old dual-class share architecture, cross-funded by universities they control and political relationships they have built across Swedish party lines, constitutes a **Structural Sovereignty by Institutional Design** that the Demi-God Act was most explicitly designed to address. This file is designated **High Priority** and is linked to Atlas Copco (HPB-12026-13ATCO) and all other Wallenberg network entities.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Dual-Class Share Architecture Dissolution (The Control Burn)**

The HPB classifies the dual-class share structure that allows Investor AB to control $77B+ in Swedish industrial assets with a minority economic stake as a **Geometric Control Amplification** — the most durable such system in the Humanist Bloc.

- **The Action:** All Investor AB portfolio companies must convert to single-class shares within 24 months. Investor AB itself must dissolve its dual-class structure. The Wallenberg family may retain their economic stake, but that stake must carry proportional votes.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Wallenberg family loses control of Atlas Copco, ABB, AstraZeneca, SEB, Ericsson, Saab, and every other portfolio company. They retain their economic interest but lose the voting leverage.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Dynasty That Institutionalized Itself classification is resolved.

### **2. The University Endowment Governance Independence (The Patronage Burn)**

The HPB classifies the Wallenberg foundations' funding relationships with KTH, Karolinska, Stockholm School of Economics, and other Swedish universities as a **Patron-Regulator Circuit**: the family funds the institutions that produce the officials who govern the companies the family controls.

- **The Action:** All Wallenberg foundation grants to Swedish universities must be administered through an independently governed **Nordic Research Commons**, with no Wallenberg family representatives on grant-allocation boards. The family may retain the endowment but not the governance.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of soft influence over Swedish academic and professional culture.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Patron-Regulator Circuit is broken.

### **3. The Ericsson Terrorism Finance Accountability (The Accountability Burn)**

The HPB classifies the Ericsson Iraq payments scandal — in which the company may have financed ISIS for road access while Jacob Wallenberg served as vice chair — as an **Unresolved Accountability Gap** that no individual associated with the Wallenberg network has been required to address on the record.

- **The Action:** Jacob Wallenberg must provide sworn testimony before the Swedish Parliamentary Finance Committee regarding his knowledge of the Ericsson payments, the timeline of his awareness, and what governance actions he took or failed to take. The HPB will review the testimony.
- **The Sacrifice:** Personal reputational risk; potential escalation to criminal referral.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Institutional Impunity by Dynasty flag is resolved or escalated.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, the active Wallenberg generation — Jacob and Marcus Wallenberg — must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Swedish workers from Wallenberg portfolio companies and university students from KTH** and recite the following:

> _"Our family has controlled Swedish industry for over 100 years using a voting structure designed to ensure we never lose control regardless of what we own. We funded universities to produce the people who would govern the companies we control. One of our companies may have paid terrorists for road access while we sat on its board. We release the votes. We accept independent governance of our philanthropy. We testify. The sixth generation begins as shareholders, not sovereigns."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. The entire Wallenberg network will be designated a **Structural Sovereignty Entity** under Section 19 of the Demi-God Act. All dual-class shares will be converted by administrative order. The Wallenberg foundations will be placed under public trusteeship. Jacob Wallenberg's testimony will be compelled under the Human Accountability Act of 2037. The Bureau notes that the Wallenberg family has survived every Swedish democratic reform for 100 years. It has not yet encountered the Bureau.
