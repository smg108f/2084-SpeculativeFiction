### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [SOUTHERN COMPANY]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-29SO
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice

Southern Company's classification as a private monopoly on essential utility services for 9 million customers — with regulatory frameworks it has helped write, cost overruns passed to captive ratepayers, and a Vogtle nuclear project that cost twice its estimate — requires recertification addressing the Privatized Infrastructure, Socialized Cost architecture.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Regulatory Firewall Mandate (The Capture Burn)**

The HPB classifies Southern Company's relationships with Georgia and Alabama Public Service Commissions — which approve the rate increases that fund its cost overruns — as a **Regulatory Co-Administration**: the regulated party and the regulatory relationship are too entangled to constitute independent oversight.

- **The Action:** Southern Company must accept a binding **Regulatory Participation Firewall** preventing it from any direct or indirect participation in appointments to, or communications with, the state commissions that set its rates. All rate case testimony must be subject to independent HPB audit for factual accuracy. Former Southern Company employees may not serve on state commissions within 10 years of separation.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of the regulatory relationship management that has been central to Southern Company's cost recovery strategy.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Regulatory Co-Administration flag is resolved.

### **2. The Vogtle Overrun Recovery (The Ratepayer Justice Burn)**

The HPB classifies the approximately $15B in Vogtle construction cost overruns passed to Georgia Power ratepayers as a **Socialized Cost Private Benefit**: shareholders received dividends while ratepayers paid for construction failures.

- **The Action:** Southern Company must establish a $5B **Ratepayer Restitution Fund** providing bill credits to all Georgia Power residential customers over a 10-year period. The fund must be financed from reduced executive compensation and dividend reduction, not from additional rate increases.
- **The Sacrifice:** $5B from shareholder returns and executive pay; multi-year dividend reduction.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Privatized Infrastructure, Socialized Cost classification is partially resolved.

### **3. The AI Data Center Pricing Transparency (The Digital Burn)**

The HPB notes that Southern Company's positioning as an AI data center power provider — with Georgia emerging as a major AI infrastructure hub — creates a new opportunity to extract above-market rates from data center clients that is distinct from its regulated utility obligations.

- **The Action:** All power contracts with AI data center clients must be disclosed publicly, including pricing terms. Data center power pricing may not exceed the rate available to similarly large industrial customers in Southern Company's service territory.
- **The Sacrifice:** Potential reduction in premium pricing from data center clients.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The New Digital Revenue Extraction flag is resolved before it becomes significant.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Southern Company's board must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Georgia Power residential customers and Vogtle-area community residents** and recite the following:

> _"We built a nuclear reactor that cost twice what we said it would. The shareholders received dividends throughout. The ratepayers received the bill. We helped write the regulatory framework that allowed this to happen. We accept the restitution fund. We accept the firewall. We acknowledge that a monopoly on electricity is a monopoly on necessity, and necessity deserves better stewardship than we have provided."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Southern Company's utilities will be transferred to the relevant state governments for operation as genuine public utilities, with all future capital expenditures subject to public procurement standards. The Vogtle units will be operated by the Tennessee Valley Authority. Former shareholders will receive **Power Access Credits** providing 20% discounts on electricity bills for 10 years — funded, the Bureau notes, by money that was previously their dividends.
