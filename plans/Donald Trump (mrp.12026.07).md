### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[TRUMP, DONALD J.]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-07DT
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice *(Enforcement Deferred — see Apotheosis Audit REF. 12026.07)*

Subject's asset profile exceeds the Demi-God threshold by a factor of seven, compounded by an unresolved Constitutional Enforcement Paradox: Subject currently commands the agencies tasked with enforcing his own recertification. This plan is filed as a binding record, to take effect the moment Subject next holds civilian status only.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days of that trigger.

---

### **1. The "Trump Organization" Sovereign Wealth Severance (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies Subject's foreign-licensed real estate empire — the golf resorts, towers, and Gulf development deals bearing his name — as a **"Franchise of the Throne."** Since re-taking office, licensing revenue has grown roughly 900%, meaning the office itself has become a marketing asset. That is not permitted to a Mortal.

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer all Trump-branded real estate holdings and foreign licensing contracts to a blind, non-family-administered "American Heritage Trust."
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of the Trump name as a licensable brand. Existing towers and resorts must be renamed within 12 months.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject becomes a former developer with a pension, not a landlord of four continents.

### **2. The "Truth Social" Decoupling (DJT Stock)**

Subject's ~52% stake in Trump Media & Technology Group is not merely an equity position — it is a **direct financial stake in a media platform that also functions as his personal state broadcaster.** The HPB classifies this as "Potentiality Fused With Propaganda," a Class-III hazard.

- **The Action:** Convert Trump Media & Technology Group into a nonpartisan, editorially independent "Public Square" nonprofit. Subject's shares are surrendered, not sold.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of the platform that functions as his direct, unmediated channel to 100M+ followers.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject regains status as "one voice among many," rather than a broadcaster with a government behind him.

### **3. The Crypto Purge (World Liberty Financial & $TRUMP Token)**

The Bureau's ledger shows Subject's family netted an estimated $2.3B from crypto ventures while retail investors — many of them his own supporters — lost an equivalent $2.3B on the same instruments. The HPB classifies this as **"Reverse Whuffie": extraction disguised as populism.**

- **The Action:** Dissolve personal and family holdings in World Liberty Financial and the $TRUMP token. Remaining treasury funds are redirected to a restitution pool for retail token holders who lost money.
- **The Sacrifice:** Approximately $1.8B in disclosed crypto-linked wealth, plus the public admission that the "populist" coin enriched the house, not the players.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the Bureau's "Reverse Whuffie" flag — the single most damaging entry in Subject's Saintly Pivot column.

### **4. The "Winner" Penance (Brand & Succession Freeze)**

Subject's self-mythology rests on a lifelong claim to be uniquely, categorically a "winner" — a status he has already begun transmitting to his adult children as heirs to the Organization, Trump Media, and World Liberty Financial alike. The HPB classifies inherited exceptionalism as a **Dynastic Ascension Risk**, a loophole by which Demi-God status simply skips a generation.

- **The Action:** Subject must publicly open-source his own name and likeness for commercial use (no further "Trump-branded" licensing, living or posthumous). Donald Jr. and Eric Trump are separately referred for their own Apotheosis Audits, disqualifying either from inheriting the family's pre-Recertification asset base.
- **The Sacrifice:** The "winner" brand — the core product Subject has sold for fifty years — becomes public domain.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Closes the dynastic loophole. The family fortune cannot simply re-Ascend under a different first name.
