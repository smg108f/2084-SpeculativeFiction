### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [LISIN, VLADIMIR]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06VL

---

### **1. The European Operations Independence Burn**

NLMK's European operations (NLMK Belgium, NLMK Denmark, NLMK France, etc.) employ thousands of European workers and supply European markets. The HPB asks for formal operational and governance separation of these entities from NLMK Russia, to protect European employees and supply chains from Russian war economy contamination.

- **The Action:** Formally separate NLMK's European subsidiaries into an independent holding structure with European-domiciled governance, independent board, and no active operational profit flow to Russian parent during the period of Russian military aggression in Ukraine. This is an operational firewall, not divestiture.
- **The Sacrifice:** Integrated management efficiency; consolidated profit flow to Russia.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a dual-use war economy concern into a separated European operation with clear accountability.

### **2. The War Position Burn**

Unlike sanctioned Russian peers, Lisin has not made a public position on the war in Ukraine. The HPB asks for one.

- **The Action:** Subject to publicly call for a ceasefire in Ukraine and diplomatic resolution. The HPB requires a statement, not material support for either side — a position consistent with the business case for ending a war that is damaging European markets his company depends on.
- **The Sacrifice:** Political comfort within the Russian business establishment.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Creates a public record of position. Distinguishes Lisin from peers who have remained entirely silent.

### **3. The Foundation Burn**

At $22B with minimal philanthropy, the HPB requires a substantive philanthropic commitment.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Lisin Foundation** with $500M endowment, focused on industrial worker welfare in NLMK's European and Russian operating communities, and sport access programs (consistent with Lisin's personal interest in competitive sport).
- **The Sacrifice:** $500M.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Creates a genuine philanthropic identity and connects it to both the subject's personal values (sport) and his industrial footprint (workers).
