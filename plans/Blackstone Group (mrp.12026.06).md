### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [BLACKSTONE GROUP]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-07BX
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Blackstone Group's Critical Ascension Risk classification and its simultaneous breach of the HPB's Capital Governance Reclassification threshold require immediate remediation. This plan addresses both the organizational capital concentration and CEO Schwarzman's individual Demi-God status, which are treated as structurally linked.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Housing Portfolio Divestiture (The Governance Relinquishment)**

Blackstone's residential real estate holdings — including its BREIT vehicle, which holds tens of billions in apartment complexes and single-family homes — constitute the HPB's most immediate concern. A private investment firm that sets rental prices for hundreds of thousands of Americans exercises housing governance without democratic accountability.

- **The Action:** Blackstone must transfer all residential real estate holdings to a **National Housing Commons Trust** within 18 months, receiving 65% of appraised value. The Trust will operate properties on a cost-recovery basis, with rents set at HPB-certified affordability thresholds.
- **The Sacrifice:** Estimated $80B–$120B in residential real estate value, accepted at a 35% discount as the **Capital Governance Penalty** for operating housing as an extraction vehicle.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removal from the "Capital Governance Sovereign" classification. Blackstone retains commercial real estate, infrastructure, and credit operations — a significant firm, no longer a parallel government.

### **2. The AUM Reduction Plan (The Scale Burn)**

Blackstone's $1.3 trillion AUM exceeds the HPB's Systemic Control Threshold by 160%. The organization must present a credible 24-month plan to reduce AUM below $500B through managed investor redemptions and subsidiary spin-offs.

- **The Action:** Blackstone must file an **AUM Reduction Roadmap** with the HPB within 30 days, identifying which asset classes will be wound down, spun off to independent management, or returned to limited partners. The plan requires HPB approval before implementation.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of management fee revenue estimated at $4B–$6B annually, carried interest revenue, and the organizational prestige of being "the world's largest alternative asset manager."
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Reclassification from "Capital Governance Sovereign" to "Large Investment Manager" — a category with regulatory precedent, oversight frameworks, and a comprehensible public role.

### **3. The Schwarzman AI Foundation Audit (The Philanthropic Transparency Burn)**

Schwarzman's announced philanthropic pivot toward an AI-focused foundation triggers the HPB's **Elite Reproduction Philanthropy Protocol**: a framework for scrutinizing whether charitable vehicles are genuine public goods or instruments for maintaining influence while appearing to divest.

- **The Action:** The Schwarzman AI Foundation must submit its charter, governance structure, and proposed grant-making priorities to the HPB's **Philanthropic Integrity Review Board** before any assets are transferred. Grant recipients must be demonstrably public-benefit institutions with no commercial relationship to Blackstone portfolio companies.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of ability to use philanthropy as a soft influence channel. The foundation may do genuine good; it may not do Blackstone good.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** If the review board approves the foundation's structure, Schwarzman receives significant Whuffie credit — enough to partially offset his individual Demi-God audit penalties and potentially avoid the full individual Burn Order.
