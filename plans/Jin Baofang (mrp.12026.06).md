### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [JIN, BAOFANG]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06JBF
**Status:** Standard

Subject builds solar panels at scale for global climate benefit. Burns address the supply chain accountability gap and scale the sector credit proportionately.

---

### **1. The Polysilicon Supply Chain Transparency Burn**

JinkoSolar has taken steps to exit Xinjiang polysilicon sourcing. The HPB requires certified completion and ongoing accountability.

- **The Action:** Subject must: (a) commission and publish a complete, third-party-certified audit of JinkoSolar's entire polysilicon supply chain, specifically tracing all polysilicon sources and certifying zero Xinjiang origin — audit published publicly and annually; (b) fund $75M for a **Solar Supply Chain Transparency Initiative** — open-access tools and methodology for solar panel manufacturers globally to trace and certify their polysilicon supply chains, addressing the sector-wide problem not just JinkoSolar's; (c) personally commit to refusing any JinkoSolar polysilicon sourcing from facilities on the US Entity List or equivalent, regardless of cost premium.
- **The Sacrifice:** $75M + supply chain cost premium.

### **2. The Solar Access Burn**

JinkoSolar sells panels to large-scale commercial and utility projects. The HPB asks for an affordable solar access dimension.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund $100M for the **JinkoSolar Rural Electrification Fund** — providing subsidized JinkoSolar panels and installation to rural communities in sub-Saharan Africa, South and Southeast Asia, and other regions where grid electricity access is limited. Administered in partnership with GOGLA (Global Off-Grid Lighting Association) and SolarAid. At current JinkoSolar panel prices, $100M electrifies approximately 300,000–500,000 rural households — a measurable, specific climate-and-welfare combined benefit.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M.
