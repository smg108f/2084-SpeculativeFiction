### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ZURICH INSURANCE GROUP AG]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-32ZURN
**Status:** Precautionary

Zurich Insurance Group's MARGINAL ASCENSION RISK classification reflects a prospective rather than immediate concern: as climate change accelerates, Zurich's actuarial models will increasingly determine which human communities are viable, making the company's risk-pricing decisions a form of Climate Triage Sovereignty the Bureau must establish oversight protocols for before the decisions become consequential at scale.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Actuarial Model Public Disclosure (The Transparency Burn)**

The HPB classifies Zurich's proprietary climate risk models — which predict community viability under various climate scenarios — as **Private Triage Intelligence**: the company knows which communities will become uninsurable before those communities do.

- **The Action:** Zurich must make available, on a 3-year delayed basis, its complete property-level risk assessments for all communities where it is a major insurer, to an **HPB Climate Triage Observatory** accessible to the affected communities, their governments, and independent researchers.
- **The Sacrifice:** Competitive disadvantage from information sharing; potential regulatory pressure from disclosed risk models.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Information Asymmetry of Concern designation is resolved.

### **2. The Market Withdrawal Community Notice Mandate (The Exit Governance Burn)**

The HPB classifies Zurich's authority to unilaterally withdraw insurance coverage from communities or markets as a **Governance Decision Without Democratic Process**: a private Swiss corporation can effectively sentence a community to economic non-viability by deciding it is no longer insurable.

- **The Action:** Before withdrawing from any residential or small commercial market affecting more than 10,000 households, Zurich must provide 5-year advance public notice and participate in a government-convened transition panel to develop alternative insurance or public safety net mechanisms.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of operational flexibility in market withdrawals; potential continuation of unprofitable market exposure during transition periods.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Climate Triage Sovereignty flag is resolved.

### **3. The NZIA Re-Commitment (The Climate Burn)**

The HPB classifies Zurich's 2023 withdrawal from the Net-Zero Insurance Alliance — citing antitrust concerns — as an **Antitrust-Laundered Climate Retreat**: using a legal technicality to exit a climate commitment that was becoming commercially inconvenient.

- **The Action:** Zurich must publicly commit to the substantive climate underwriting restrictions it adopted as a NZIA member, through unilateral corporate policy rather than industry collective action, making the antitrust concern moot while preserving the climate commitment.
- **The Sacrifice:** Commercially constrained fossil fuel underwriting portfolio.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Antitrust-Laundered Climate Retreat flag is resolved.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, CEO Mario Greco and the board must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Florida homeowners who have been dropped from coastal coverage and Swiss alpine community representatives facing glacier retreat** and recite the following:

> _"We know which communities will be uninsurable before they know. We left the industry climate alliance because commitment had become legally complex. We withdraw from markets when they stop being profitable without obligation to those left behind. We accept the disclosure. We accept the notice period. We accept that knowing who will be abandoned and not telling them is not a defensible position for an institution of our scale."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Zurich's climate risk models will be seized under the **Public Interest Information Act of 2038** and made immediately available to the HPB Climate Triage Observatory. Zurich's market withdrawal rights in Humanist Bloc territories will be suspended pending review. The Bureau notes this outcome would have the unintended consequence of making Zurich's actuarial data public four decades before the voluntary disclosure timeline — an outcome Zurich's shareholders will not prefer.
