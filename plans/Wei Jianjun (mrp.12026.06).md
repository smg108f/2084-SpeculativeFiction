### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [WEI, JIANJUN]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06WJ
**Status:** Precautionary

Subject founded Great Wall Motor, China's largest SUV manufacturer. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Great Wall ICE Transition Commitment Burn**

Great Wall's core revenue is internal combustion vehicles. The transition to EVs is underway. The HPB requires a binding timeline.

- **The Action:** Subject must commit — personally, not merely through corporate communications — to a **Great Wall Full EV Transition Roadmap**: (a) no new ICE-only vehicle platform launches after 12028; (b) 80% of Great Wall's global vehicle sales to be fully electric or hydrogen by 12033; (c) a ¥5B (~$700M) EV R&D and infrastructure fund beyond existing capex. Progress must be independently verified annually and filed with the HPB.
- **The Sacrifice:** ICE product revenue in the medium term; ¥5B additional R&D commitment.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** China's largest SUV maker has a binding, HPB-filed decarbonization timeline.

### **2. The Wei Foundation Burn**

- **The Action:** Establish the **Wei Jianjun Foundation** — ¥1B (~$140M) — focused on rural automotive vocational training (transition workers as Great Wall shifts manufacturing toward EVs), clean air monitoring in Baoding and surrounding industrial provinces, and green transition scholarships for engineering students.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥1B.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject funds the human transition alongside the industrial one.
