### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [GATES, WILLIAM H. III]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-05WG
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification (Probationary Saint — Annual Renewal)

Subject Gates has maintained Probationary Saint status since 12019 and has filed for voluntary renewal each year. This plan supersedes the 12025 renewal. Recertification is required because Subject's personal wealth has grown by an estimated $4B since the last filing, his farmland holdings have not decreased, and the Gates Foundation's governance reform milestones (see HPB-12025-04WG, Schedule C) have not been met. The HPB acknowledges Subject's continued good faith while noting that good faith is not a legal defense against the $1B threshold.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Great Soil Release (Agricultural Land Divestiture)**

The HPB classifies Gates's 275,000 acres of American farmland as a **Territorial Power Asset** — a distinct and more urgent category than financial wealth because it represents control over the physical substrate of food security. No other Probationary Saint holds land of this type at this scale. The HPB's Agricultural Land Subcommittee has held this burn in abeyance for three years pending voluntary compliance. That abeyance expires with this filing.

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer all agricultural land holdings to a newly constituted **National Soil Commons Trust**, a Lottery-Governed entity with no single controlling party. Subject may retain a life-tenancy advisory role, unpaid, on no more than one farm of his choosing.
- **The Sacrifice:** Estimated $3–5B in asset value; more importantly, the end of Subject's status as the de facto landlord of the American grain belt.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The **Class-II Territorial Risk** flag is cleared. Subject is no longer a land sovereign. He becomes, in the HPB's formal language, a person who eats food rather than a person who owns it.

---

### **2. The Foundation Governance Transfer (Shadow Ministry Dissolution)**

The Gates Foundation's designation as a **Shadow Ministry** cannot be cleared while Subject retains co-chair status and final programmatic authority over $9B/year in global health and agricultural grants. The HPB does not require the Foundation's dissolution — its work is net-positive by every available metric. It requires the dissolution of Subject's personal control over it.

- **The Action:** Subject must relinquish his co-chair position and all programmatic veto authority. The Foundation's governance must be transferred to a **nine-member Elected Health Councils** drawn from the populations served — sub-Saharan Africa, South Asia, and underserved U.S. school districts — with no seat reserved for the founding family.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses the ability to direct how his legacy money is spent. The work continues. He does not steer it.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The **Shadow Ministry** designation is downgraded to **Legacy Endowment (Non-Sovereign)**. This is the largest single improvement to Subject's Probationary Saint dossier achievable in one action. The HPB rates it as worth six years of compliance credit.

---

### **3. The Conspiracy Quarantine (Deity-Adjacent Perception Burn)**

Subject's Deity-Adjacent Perception score — generated not by his own rhetoric but by the scale of his real-world reach — has been elevated by sustained public belief that his philanthropic activities constitute covert population management. The HPB does not adjudicate the truth of conspiracy theories, but it does register when a private individual has accumulated sufficient influence that a significant portion of humanity regards them as capable of planetary-scale covert action. That perception is itself a power metric.

- **The Action:** Subject must commission and fully fund a **Global Public Audit of the Gates Foundation** by an independent, open-access body with published methodology, releasing all grant-making criteria, outcome data, and lobbying activity to the public domain in perpetuity. Subject has no editorial control over the audit's findings.
- **The Sacrifice:** Potential reputational exposure; loss of the philanthropic narrative that Subject has carefully cultivated since 1999. The audit may find things Subject would prefer remain internal. That is the point.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The **Deity-Adjacent Perception** flag cannot be cleared by good behavior — it can only be cleared by radical transparency. A mortal has nothing to hide at planetary scale. This action is Subject's proof of mortality.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Malawian Smallholder Farmers** and recite the following:

> *"I built a fortune on software that could be copied infinitely, and spent thirty years learning that money cannot. I am not the solution to the world's problems. I am a man who had too much, tried to give it away, and discovered the giving was harder than the earning. I relinquish my authority over the soil and the grants and the legacy. I am not a saint. I am not a villain. I am a person — no more and no less — and I ask this jury of people who fed the world while I funded it to determine whether I have earned that name."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. The Gates Foundation will be nationalized as a **Public Health Utility**, Subject's farmland will be redistributed via the Agricultural Equity Act of 2041, and Subject will be reclassified as a **Demi-God Entity (DGE)** — specifically as a **Type II Penitent Demi-God**, a category created for this case and only this case, defined as: *a subject whose ascension was achieved through generosity rather than greed, and who is therefore the saddest kind of god.*
