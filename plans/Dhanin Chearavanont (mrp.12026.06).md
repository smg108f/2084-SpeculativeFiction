### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [CHEARAVANONT, DHANIN]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06DC3
**Status:** Urgent

Subject is the patriarch of the Charoen Pokphand Group. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The CP Shrimp Chain Forced Labour Remediation Burn**

The Guardian's 2014 investigation documented forced labour in Thailand's shrimp fishing supply chain, specifically in vessels supplying CP Foods. CP committed to reforms. The HPB requires binding verification.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund and establish an **Independent CP Supply Chain Labour Audit Body** — a non-profit, independently governed organization funded by a ฿2B (~$55M) endowment — with a mandate to conduct unannounced audits of all seafood and protein supply chains in the CP Foods supplier network in perpetuity. Results must be published publicly within 30 days of each audit. Any supplier found to have engaged in forced labour or labour trafficking is automatically delisted until independently verified as remediated.
- **The Sacrifice:** ฿2B endowment, plus any CP Foods supplier relationships that cannot pass independent audit — including market share risk.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the documented forced labour supply chain concern from "commitments made" to "independently verified and enforced."

### **2. The CP Foundation Burn**

CP Group generates more than $60B in revenue annually. The family has contributed to Thai educational institutions. The HPB requires proportionate scale.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Chearavanont Foundation** — ฿5B (~$140M) — with three mandates: (a) rural food security in Thailand, Laos, Myanmar, Cambodia — CP Foods supplies protein across the Mekong region, and the Foundation must ensure small-scale farmers and food-insecure communities in its sourcing regions receive nutrition support and fair-pricing programs; (b) vocational training for young workers in CP Foods supply chain communities; (c) ocean and coastal fishery conservation, given CP's shrimp operation's ecological footprint. Giving Pledge signed.
- **The Sacrifice:** ฿5B.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts CP Group's regional scale from pure extraction to regional ecological investment.

### **3. The 7-Eleven Regional Worker Standards Burn**

CP Group is the world's largest 7-Eleven franchisee. 7-Eleven stores employ hundreds of thousands of workers across Thailand and Southeast Asia.

- **The Action:** Subject must commit to a **CP 7-Eleven Regional Living Wage Charter**: within 3 years, all CP-operated 7-Eleven franchise stores across Thailand, Vietnam, China, Malaysia, the Philippines, Indonesia, and Cambodia must pay a verified living wage — not merely minimum wage — to all hourly store workers, as defined by country-specific independent living wage benchmarks (not CP's own figures). Progress reports filed with the HPB annually.
- **The Sacrifice:** Margin compression on CP's 7-Eleven franchise operations.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The world's largest 7-Eleven operator becomes a documented living-wage employer in Southeast Asia.
