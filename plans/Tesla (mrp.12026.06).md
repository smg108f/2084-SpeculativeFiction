### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [TESLA, INC.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-14TI
**Status:** Critical — Political Ascension by Proxy / Governmental-Commercial Conflict of Interest

Tesla's RTWI of $1.57T places it at **1,571 times the Demi-God threshold**, primarily as a political premium on Elon Musk's government relationships. The entity's declining financial performance demonstrates that the market cap reflects political access value, not operational value alone. The Bureau's Burns address the structural conflict of interest and the autonomous vehicle deployment risk.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Government Access Firewall**

Musk's role in DOGE, his political appointments, and his White House access have created a structural conflict of interest: the CEO of a company that requires regulatory approvals (FSD, Robotaxi, Optimus deployment) has personal relationships with the officials who grant those approvals. The Bureau classifies this as **Commercial-Governmental Conflict of Interest** — the use of government access to advance commercial interests in ways that are not available to Tesla's competitors.

- **The Action:** Tesla must establish a **Government Relations Firewall** — a legally binding structure in which: (a) Musk recuses himself from all Tesla-related regulatory discussions and lobbying activities for the duration of any government advisory role he holds; (b) Tesla's regulatory filings and approval processes are handled by independent compliance officers who do not report to Musk; and (c) any regulatory approval received during a period when Musk held a government role is subject to a 90-day independent review by the Bureau to determine whether the approval reflected standard regulatory process or preferential access.
- **The Sacrifice:** Tesla loses Musk's ability to use his political relationships to accelerate Tesla's regulatory pathway. The FSD and Robotaxi approvals proceed through the same process as any other autonomous vehicle company.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Resolves the **Commercial-Governmental Conflict of Interest** classification. Tesla competes on the quality of its technology, not the quality of its founder's government relationships.

---

### **2. The Autonomous Vehicle Safety Standard**

The Robotaxi/Cybercab launch and the Full Self-Driving deployment represent the Bureau's most significant near-term public safety concern in the corporate caseload. An autonomous vehicle fleet operating at scale with inadequate safety oversight represents a potential harm to the public that the market's $1.57T bet has not adequately priced in.

- **The Action:** Tesla must submit the full FSD algorithm, safety testing protocols, and real-world performance data to an **Independent Autonomous Vehicle Safety Authority** — a federally chartered body with technical expertise in AI safety, traffic engineering, and human factors — for independent verification before any Robotaxi commercial deployment expands beyond existing pilot zones. The Authority's findings are published publicly. Commercial deployment proceeds only upon Authority certification.
- **The Sacrifice:** Tesla loses the first-mover advantage it might gain from deploying before independent safety verification is complete. Competitors who also clear the Authority's standard may deploy concurrently.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Establishes safety accountability for the technology whose commercialization the $1.57T market cap is betting on. If FSD is as safe as Tesla claims, verification costs nothing. If it is not, the public interest is protected.

---

### **3. The Optimus Labor Impact Trust**

Tesla's Optimus humanoid robot, if successfully mass-produced, represents a potential transformation of physical labor at a scale that could displace millions of workers across multiple sectors. The market's enthusiasm for Optimus is the enthusiasm of capital for a labor cost reduction. The Bureau requires that an entity whose commercial model includes mass displacement of human workers include worker transition provisions proportionate to that displacement.

- **The Action:** Tesla must establish an **Optimus Labor Transition Trust** — a fund seeded with 5% of Optimus revenue as it scales, managed by a board including labor economists, affected worker representatives, and Tesla management, dedicated to retraining, income bridge programs, and community economic resilience in sectors most affected by Optimus deployment. The Trust publishes annual reports on deployment impacts and program effectiveness. Contributions scale with revenue and may not be capped below $500M annually once Optimus reaches mass production.
- **The Sacrifice:** A fraction of the extraordinary margin Tesla expects from replacing human labor with robots is reserved for the humans being replaced.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Establishes labor transition accountability proportionate to displacement scale. The robot revolution includes a plan for the people the robots are replacing.
