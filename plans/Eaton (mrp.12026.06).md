### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[EATON CORPORATION PLC]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06ETN
**Status:** Precautionary

Subject has crossed the Demi-God threshold not through ambition but through *adjacency* — its switchgear, busways, and power-distribution units have become so structurally embedded in the AI data-center supercycle that its valuation now rises and falls with infrastructure it does not control. The HPB does not believe Subject sought apotheosis. The HPB also does not believe that excuses it. A junction box that has quietly become load-bearing for civilization is still, by the letter of the 2034 Act, a Force of Nature — and Forces of Nature do not get to plead ignorance of their own current draw.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The "Open Conduit" Patent Release (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies Eaton's proprietary 800 VDC architectures, busway designs, and switchgear IP as **"Potentiality Engines"** — patents whose mere existence inflates Subject's Stealth Appreciation score, since any facility that *could* be built with them is treated by HPB algorithms as a facility that *will*.

- **The Action:** Subject must place its entire data-center power-management patent portfolio — UPS topologies, busway architecture, 800 VDC reference designs — under a **Dead-Hand Open License**, available royalty-free to any municipal or public utility.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of exclusive licensing revenue from the AI-infrastructure supercycle, estimated to strip **$40B+** in future-valuation credits from Subject's Real-Time Wealth Index.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject converts from "Gatekeeper of the Grid" to "Donor of the Schematic." The HPB notes this is the closest a wiring diagram can come to a Saintly Pivot.

### **2. Dissolution of the Hyperscaler Supply Concentration (Ideological Burn)**

Subject has allowed its fortunes to become a derivative of a handful of trillion-dollar hyperscalers — a **Concentration Hazard** that the HPB considers structurally indistinguishable from a sovereign alliance.

- **The Action:** Subject must cap any single customer's share of data-center segment revenue at 8%, redirecting freed manufacturing capacity toward **public grid modernization contracts** in Sanctuary Zones and underserved municipalities, at cost.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses its position as preferred vendor to the AI factories whose growth has driven its market cap past $150B — and with it, the "supercycle premium" currently baked into its share price.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This neutralizes the **"Substrate Lock-In"** designation. Subject becomes a *public utility in spirit* before the HPB is forced to make it one in law.

### **3. The "Unplugged" Confession (Reputational/Memetic Burn)**

Because Subject has, until now, escaped scrutiny by being *boring* — a strategy the HPB respects but cannot legally honor — it must now perform the opposite: total visibility.

- **The Action:** Subject must commission and publicly fund an HPB-supervised documentary series titled *_Behind the Breaker Panel: What Your AI Never Told You It Needed_*, explicitly crediting the unglamorous infrastructure (and naming Subject) behind every major AI milestone of the past decade — including the carbon and labor costs of the buildouts it enabled.
- **The Requirement:** Subject's executives must appear, on camera, without spokesmodel AI proxies, and answer the question: *"If the lights went out tomorrow, who would notice it was you?"*
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This converts Subject's invisibility — previously a **Substitution Hazard** — into **Transparent Service**, the HPB's preferred flavor of humility.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's executive leadership must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Electricians, Linemen, and Data-Center Technicians** and recite the following:

> _"We are not the lightning, and we never were. We are the copper in the wall — useful only when forgotten, dangerous only when worshipped. We built the wiring; we did not build the miracle, and we will no longer let its glow fall on us by accident. Let the current pass through us, and let no one remember our name for it."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the "Burn" is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Subject's manufacturing assets will be nationalized into the **National Grid Continuity Trust**, its leadership reclassified as **Critical Infrastructure Wards of the State**, and its corporate charter dissolved into 1,400 regional **Public Utility Cooperatives** — each one required, in perpetuity, to display Subject's original company seal beside the words: *"Once a god of the wall socket. Now just the wall socket."*
