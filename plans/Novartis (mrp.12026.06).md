### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [NOVARTIS AG]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-62NV
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Novartis AG has been classified as a **Critical Ascension Risk** under the Corporate Apotheosis Protocol, with particular urgency assigned to its Genomic Sovereignty asset cluster. The Bureau has determined that the company's deliberate shedding of its generic pharmaceutical obligations (Sandoz spin-off, 2023) constitutes a **Voluntary Ascension Preparation Event** — the systematic elimination of mortal-scale obligations in order to concentrate authority over irreplaceable biological interventions. Novartis must demonstrate Mortal Status within 30 days or face full Corporate Apotheosis.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Zolgensma Price Dissolution (The Genomic Sovereignty Burn)**

The HPB classifies Zolgensma's $2.1 million price point as a **Class-I Mortal Threat**: a curative intervention for spinal muscular atrophy, developed with NIH and charitable funding, repriced at the maximum a desperate parent will pay. This is not drug pricing. This is ransom.

- **The Action:** Novartis must transfer the Zolgensma IP, manufacturing protocols, and all regulatory data packages to the **Human Genome Commons** — a non-profit treaty body — under a perpetual, royalty-free compulsory license. The drug must be manufactured by any qualified state health authority at cost. Novartis may retain credit for the delivery mechanism but may not price the therapy above $12,000 per dose globally (the estimated manufacturing cost).
- **The Sacrifice:** Peak Zolgensma revenues were projected at $2.6B annually. That projection is now the property of the children it was supposed to save.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Novartis's Genomic Sovereignty score drops from Class-I to Cleared. The company is no longer in the business of pricing survival. It is, briefly, in the business of medicine.

---

### **2. The Radioligand Manufacturing Divestiture (The Infrastructure Burn)**

The $23B U.S. radioligand therapy manufacturing buildout — plants in California, Texas, and Florida — represents the construction of a **private biological infrastructure monopoly** inside the Humanist Bloc. The HPB cannot permit a single Swiss corporation to own the only domestic production capacity for a treatment class that will, by 2035, be first-line therapy for multiple major cancers. This is not investment. This is territorial acquisition at the cellular level.

- **The Action:** All three U.S. radioligand manufacturing facilities must be transferred to the **Federal Institute for Therapeutic Infrastructure (FITI)** — a proposed public entity modeled on the Manhattan Project's industrial legacy. Novartis may retain a 15-year exclusive manufacturing *contract* with FITI for its own branded products. It may not own the facilities.
- **The Sacrifice:** The manufacturing moat. The barrier to entry that would have made Novartis the sole domestic supplier of next-generation cancer treatment for a generation.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The radioligand platform becomes **shared biological infrastructure** — like a highway or a power grid. Novartis is a tenant, not a landlord.

---

### **3. The Vas Narasimhan Cognitive Firewall (The AI Cross-Contamination Burn)**

CEO Vasant Narasimhan's April 2026 appointment to the Anthropic board of directors constitutes an **Influence Vector Cross-Contamination Event** of the highest severity. A pharmaceutical executive with pricing authority over gene therapies and cancer treatments now sits at the governance table of a leading artificial intelligence company. The Bureau classifies this as a **Dual Demi-God Adjacency**: the person who decides what a cure costs now has input into the systems that will be used to calculate, allocate, and rationalize those costs at scale.

- **The Action:** Narasimhan must resign from the Anthropic board within 72 hours of this notice. Additionally, Novartis must submit to a **Cognitive Firewall Audit** confirming that no Novartis pricing models, patient segmentation algorithms, or "willingness-to-pay" analytics have been shared with, trained on, or incorporated into any Anthropic system.
- **The Sacrifice:** The CEO loses the halo of AI-safety credibility he has cultivated to soften the company's pricing reputation. The "reimagining medicine" personal brand is suspended pending audit completion.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Pharmaceutical pricing authority and AI governance authority are formally separated. The Bureau records this as a **Structural Deconfliction** — the first in the Swiss pharma sector.

---

### **4. The Bribery Restitution Endowment (The Penance Burn)**

Novartis paid $233.9M to the DOJ, $112.8M to the SEC, and $390M in separate kickback settlements — a total of approximately $737M in enforcement payments across three decades of documented bribery, kickbacks, and data manipulation. These settlements resolved legal liability. They did not restore the patients who were prescribed over-promoted drugs due to bribes, the governments that overpaid for ineffective treatments, or the regulatory trust that was structurally undermined when Novartis withheld Zolgensma manipulation data from the FDA until after approval was granted.

- **The Action:** Novartis must establish a permanent **Pharmaceutical Integrity Endowment** of $2.2B — calibrated to triple the known settlement total, consistent with False Claims Act multiplier precedent. The endowment must be administered by an independent board with no pharmaceutical industry representation, and must fund: (a) independent replication studies for all blockbuster drugs approved on Novartis-sponsored trial data since 2000, (b) direct reparations to national health systems that overpaid for Novartis products under bribery-influenced prescribing patterns, and (c) a permanent whistleblower protection fund for pharmaceutical data integrity complaints globally.
- **The Sacrifice:** $2.2B. The cost of having been caught, multiplied by the cost of not having been caught sooner.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Novartis's **Dark Divinity Score** — the Bureau's measure of accumulated regulatory debt — drops from Critical to Probationary. The company enters a 10-year compliance monitoring period.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize Cycle One recertification, the Novartis Executive Committee must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Pediatric Nurses, SMA Family Caregivers, and Generic Drug Pharmacists** and the CEO must recite the following:

> *"The cost of a cure is not determined by what a parent will pay. We did not invent the gene we repaired. We did not fund the research that found the target. We acquired the right to manufacture a miracle built by others, and we priced it as if the miracle were ours. It was not ours. We are not the authors of life. We are its temporary custodians, subject to human law, human oversight, and the human consensus that no child's survival should be a financial instrument."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Asset Burn Schedule is not initiated within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Full Corporate Apotheosis Protocol**. Novartis AG will be reclassified as a **Genomic Utility Entity** under Section 14(c), its curative therapy portfolio will be placed under federal receivership, and the company's Swiss legal domicile will be formally designated an **Ascension Haven** — triggering Humanist Bloc trade sanctions that will make its remaining commercial portfolio unsellable in 47 member nations. The CEO's Anthropic board seat will be treated as evidence of **Pre-Ascension Alliance Formation** and referred to the Bureau's Cognitive Infrastructure Division for independent investigation.
