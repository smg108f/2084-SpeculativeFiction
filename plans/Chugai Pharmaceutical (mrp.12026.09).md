### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT CHUGAI PHARMACEUTICAL**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09CHG
**Status:** Precautionary Monitoring — Voluntary Compliance Encouraged

### **1. The Burn: Antibody Technology-Transfer Consortium**

**The Action:** Chugai licenses its proprietary bispecific-antibody engineering platform (the technology underlying Hemlibra) to a WHO-supervised generic manufacturing consortium for use in low-income hemophilia treatment programs, at cost.
**The Sacrifice:** Estimated ¥400B in foregone licensing revenue over a decade.
**Mortal Status Gain:** -8% RTWI; reclassification from "Quiet-Vector Risk" to "Cooperative Technical Partner."

### **2. The Burn: Reverse-Royalty Transparency Accord**

**The Action:** Chugai publishes, for the first time, the full terms of its reverse-royalty arrangement with parent Roche, subjecting the Basel-Tokyo wealth flow to Bureau and Humanist Bloc audit.
**The Sacrifice:** Loss of negotiating leverage in future Roche alliance renewals; estimated ¥50B in strategic ambiguity value.
**Mortal Status Gain:** -3% RTWI; removes "structural opacity" flag from Bureau file.
