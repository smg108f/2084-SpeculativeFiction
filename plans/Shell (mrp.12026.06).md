### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [SHELL PLC]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-52SH
**Status:** Critical — Transition Delay by LNG Infrastructure / Climate Lawsuit Appeal Precedent

Shell's RTWI of ~$261B places it at **261 times the Demi-God threshold**. Burns mirror Exxon (HPB-12026-17EM) and Chevron (HPB-12026-36CV) with Shell-specific LNG infrastructure additions.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Production Decline Schedule (Mirror of Exxon Burn 2)**

Shell must publish a binding **Managed Production Decline Schedule** aligned with the Humanist Bloc Climate Authority's 1.5°C pathway — declining new oil and gas exploration investment, no new long-term LNG supply contracts after 12028, and phasing out existing LNG contracts at the earliest commercially viable opportunity. Shell's Energy Transition Strategy is acknowledged as the most serious in the fossil fuel major caseload; the Bureau requires it to be binding and auditable.

---

### **2. The LNG Phase-Out Commitment**

Shell's LNG infrastructure — tankers, terminals, 20-30 year supply contracts — creates structural lock-in that will delay global decarbonization beyond what climate science requires.

- **The Action:** Shell must commit to a **LNG Phase-Out Timeline** — no new LNG supply contracts with terms extending beyond 12040; all existing contracts that can be restructured to shorter terms or renewable energy alternatives are offered renegotiation by 12028. Shell's LNG shipping fleet transitions to ammonia or hydrogen propulsion by 12035. LNG terminal investments are conditioned on compatibility with green hydrogen or renewable energy gas conversion.
- **The Sacrifice:** Shell's highest-growth fossil fuel business line is constrained by a phase-out timeline that prioritizes climate over revenue extension.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Shell's LNG position transitions from transition-delay infrastructure to transition-compatible infrastructure.

---

### **3. The Humanist Bloc Climate Authority Acceptance**

The Dutch climate lawsuit established the principle that courts can require emission reductions from private companies. The appeal reversal established that national courts cannot enforce this principle globally. The Bureau requires Shell to accept Humanist Bloc Climate Authority jurisdiction as the multinational enforcement framework that national courts cannot provide.

- **The Action:** Shell must formally accept **Humanist Bloc Climate Authority jurisdiction** — a binding submission to the Authority's 1.5°C production pathway requirements, with compliance monitored by the Authority and violations subject to Humanist Bloc trade sanctions. Shell may continue to operate in non-Humanist Bloc markets; its Humanist Bloc market access is conditional on Authority compliance.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The jurisdictional gap that allowed Shell to appeal out of national court enforcement is resolved by supranational authority acceptance.

---

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Shell's CEO must stand before a **Jury of 12 Dutch Climate Activists, LNG-Dependent Energy Workers, and Climate Scientists** and recite:

> _"A Dutch court ordered us to reduce emissions by 45% by 2030. We appealed. We won. The market cap went up. We understand why the Bureau finds this sequence instructive. The Humanist Bloc Climate Authority jurisdiction is accepted. The LNG phase-out timeline is binding. The production decline is scheduled. We have the most serious transition strategy of the fossil fuel majors. We commit to being bound by something stronger than our own strategy."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** Humanist Bloc market access for Shell energy products suspended. LNG contracts in Humanist Bloc markets subject to mandatory phase-out review. Humanist Bloc Climate Authority sanctions activated.
