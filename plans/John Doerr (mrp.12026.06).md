### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [DOERR, JOHN]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06JD2
**Status:** Moderate

Subject's philanthropic record is the most creditable VC profile in Batch 4. Burns are calibrated accordingly — targeting the downstream labor concerns of his portfolio companies and scaling the climate work.

---

### **1. The Portfolio Labor Accountability Burn**

Kleiner Perkins' portfolio includes companies (Amazon, in particular) whose labor practices have been the subject of HPB scrutiny in other audits. Doerr did not design those practices, but his capital scaled them.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish a **Kleiner Perkins Labor Standards Commitment**: (a) going forward, all new Kleiner Perkins investments must meet independently verified minimum labor standards as a condition of investment — living wage, safe working conditions, no worker financial penalty systems; (b) fund $100M for the **Tech Industry Worker Transition Fund** — specifically supporting the warehouse and gig workers displaced by automation at companies in which Doerr's firms have invested.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M.

### **2. The Climate Scale-Up Invitation**

The $1.1B Stanford Sustainability School is real. The HPB's invitation is to extend the climate OKR framework from a book and a school into a funded action program.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund $150M for the **Speed & Scale Implementation Fund** — directly seeding the specific climate interventions documented in his book: electrifying transportation, decarbonizing industry, fixing food, protecting nature, cleaning up carbon, advancing equity. Not just advocacy — funded action. The fund should be independently governed and report annually against the OKR framework he proposed.
- **The Sacrifice:** $150M.
