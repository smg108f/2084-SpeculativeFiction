### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [TOTALENERGIES, SE]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-81TE
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

TotalEnergies SE has been flagged under the **Corporate Entity Ascension Protocol** following its formal withdrawal of net zero commitments in 12026.03 and its continued LNG portfolio expansion targeting 25 Mt/year by 2030. The entity has crossed the Substitute Sovereignty threshold in at least four nations and must reduce its jurisdictional footprint and direct asset valuation below the HPB's **Corporate Demi-God Ceiling** ($50B adjusted market cap for multi-sovereign infrastructure entities) or face mandatory dissolution into public-trust utility structures.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The EACOP Abandonment and Reparative Land Return (The Largest Burn)**

The East African Crude Oil Pipeline — 1,443 kilometers, 100,000+ displaced persons, 30+ activists detained — is the HPB's primary evidence for TotalEnergies' Substitute Sovereignty classification in Uganda and Tanzania. It is also the most direct embodiment of the company's operating doctrine: infrastructure as coercion, compensation as theater.

- **The Action:** TotalEnergies must immediately halt EACOP construction, transfer all pipeline easements and physical infrastructure completed to date to a jointly administered Uganda-Tanzania Public Lands Trust, and fund a 20-year land remediation and community reparation program valued at no less than $4B from current assets. All compensation agreements signed in English by non-English-literate landholders must be voided and renegotiated under internationally supervised community consent protocols.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeit the entire EACOP investment sunk cost (estimated $3–5B), all future Uganda/Tanzania crude revenue, and the production-sharing agreements with both governments.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the **Substitute Sovereignty violation** in Uganda and Tanzania. Removes TotalEnergies from the HPB's Forced Displacement Registry. Demonstrates that the entity can surrender a profitable asset rather than subordinate a population.

### **2. The LNG Expansion Freeze and Open-Source Infrastructure Transfer**

TotalEnergies' 40% LNG production growth target — to be achieved through new terminals in Qatar, the United States, and Oman — represents the single largest carbon lock-in commitment by any non-state actor in the current audit cycle. Under the HPB's **Potentiality Clause**, the projected future emissions of these assets are counted against the entity's present Ascension score.

- **The Action:** All LNG projects not yet in production must be frozen. Completed LNG terminal infrastructure must be transferred under a **Dead-Hand License** to the International Energy Commons Authority (IECA) as public utility assets, operated at cost with no profit extraction. TotalEnergies may retain technical management contracts at flat fee — no equity, no upside.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of projected LNG revenue stream estimated at $40–60B over the 2026–2040 window. Relinquishment of the company's primary growth thesis.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Reduces the entity's **Temporal Concentration Hazard** score from Class III to Class I. Converts TotalEnergies from a growth-stage resource extractor into a regulated infrastructure operator — closer in HPB classification to a public utility than a sovereign actor.

### **3. The Net Zero Recantation and Atmospheric Debt Acknowledgment**

In 12026.03, TotalEnergies formally withdrew its net zero 2050 commitment, citing the "pace of the energy transition." The HPB classifies this statement as **Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric** under the Human Humility Act of 12031 (Retroactive Application): specifically, the implicit claim that the entity's operational timeline supersedes the collective atmospheric timeline of the human species. A corporation does not get to determine that humanity has moved too slowly for the corporation to act responsibly.

- **The Action:** TotalEnergies' board and CEO must issue a formal **Atmospheric Debt Declaration** — a public document, filed with the HPB and entered into the Paris Agreement successor archives — quantifying the company's cumulative scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions since 1954, expressing this figure in plain language (e.g., equivalent to X years of total human respiration), and committing to a binding emissions reduction schedule with automatic asset-seizure triggers if milestones are missed. The declaration must be read aloud by the CEO at a ceremony attended by representatives of low-lying coastal communities in Bangladesh, Kiribati, and Louisiana.
- **The Sacrifice:** Exposure to binding legal liability indexed to a specific tonnage figure the company has historically refused to calculate publicly. Reputational cost of the ceremony itself.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the **Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric** violation. Converts the company's previously unquantified atmospheric footprint into a legally legible debt — making TotalEnergies a debtor rather than an authority.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, the TotalEnergies board of directors must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 displaced Ugandan farmers, Bangladeshi climate refugees, and Louisiana coastal fisherfolk** and collectively recite the following:

> _"We are not the energy transition. We are a company. The atmosphere was here before us and will outlast us. We have no right to set the pace of survival for others, and our worth is not measured in barrels but in the land we return and the air we leave breathable."_
