### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT MARVELL TECHNOLOGY GROUP**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09MRV
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification

### **1. The Burn: Custom Silicon Access Equity**

**The Action:** Marvell commits a fixed percentage of custom AI silicon design capacity to non-hyperscaler academic and public-sector research computing initiatives at cost.
**The Sacrifice:** Estimated $1.5B annually in foregone premium design-contract revenue.
**Mortal Status Gain:** -9% RTWI.

**The Action:** Marvell publishes full energy-efficiency benchmarks for its custom AI silicon, enabling public comparison of per-watt AI compute costs across the industry.
**The Sacrifice:** Negligible direct cost; competitive transparency exposure.
**Mortal Status Gain:** -3% RTWI.
