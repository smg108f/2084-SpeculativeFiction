### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [CATERPILLAR, INC.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-69CAT
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice

Caterpillar Inc. (NYSE: CAT) has been assessed at $433B in total market value, exceeding the Human Protection Bureau Demi-God threshold by a factor of 433. Its structural control over global earthmoving equipment, locked-machine software monopoly, documented offshore tax avoidance, and ongoing elimination of human labor via autonomous systems constitute a multi-vector Ascension profile that the Bureau classifies as **Institutional Apotheosis in Progress**. Voluntary recertification is the subject's final opportunity to remain a legally regulated Mortal Entity rather than face nationalization under the Demi-God Seizure Protocol.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Iron Fleet Divestment (The Planetary Footprint Burn)**

The HPB's Infrastructure Access Division classifies Caterpillar's global machine fleet and its locked telematics system as a **Territorial Monopoly on Earth-Access** — effectively a private tax on all planetary development. The company's active lobbying against Right-to-Repair legislation is logged as deliberate entrenchment of this monopoly.

- **The Action:** Caterpillar must open-source all proprietary diagnostic and telematics software under a Dead-Hand License. The Cat® Command for Hauling autonomous system must be transferred to the Public Infrastructure Commons as a **"Free Earth Protocol"** — freely usable by any municipal government, cooperative, or agricultural collective worldwide.
- **The Sacrifice:** Estimated $80B in future software licensing, dealer lock-in fees, and Cat Financial lease dependency revenue is forfeited. The dealer network may no longer charge for diagnostic access.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject's **Infrastructure Lock** classification is downgraded from Demi-God to Certified Industrial Utility. Caterpillar may continue to manufacture machines; it may no longer own the keys to the machines it sells.

### **2. The Swiss Reckoning (The Dark Divinity Burn)**

Between 2000 and 2012, Caterpillar shifted more than $8B in taxable income to its CSARL subsidiary in Switzerland — a country with less than 0.5% of its total workforce — and avoided approximately $2.4B in U.S. taxes. The subject settled with no penalties in a transaction the Bureau's Forensic Finance Unit has retroactively reclassified as **Unpurged Dark Divinity**. A no-penalty settlement does not constitute a confession of mortality.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish a **$2.4B Reparative Infrastructure Fund** — held in public trust, administered by a Lottery-Selected Citizens' Board — dedicated exclusively to rebuilding public infrastructure in the Midwestern communities (Peoria, Decatur, East Peoria) the company abandoned when it relocated to Texas. The fund is irrevocable.
- **The Sacrifice:** $2.4B in retained earnings, surrendered to the communities whose tax base subsidized Caterpillar's ascent and whose workforce built the machines that built the world.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The **Dark Divinity** flag on subject's file is cleared. The Swiss SARL loophole is sealed. Subject's Potentiality Tax exposure is reduced by an estimated $40B in projected future liability.

### **3. The Autonomous Systems Moratorium (The Labor Displacement Burn)**

Caterpillar's Cat® Command for Hauling system and broader autonomous equipment portfolio are expanding to remove human workers from mining and extraction operations at scale. The HPB's Human Labor Continuity Unit classifies autonomous systems that eliminate workforce — without redistributing productivity gains to displaced workers — as **Structural Deity Behavior**: the ability to generate wealth with no human intermediary to slow accumulation or lodge grievance.

- **The Action:** Subject must implement a **10-year moratorium on deployment of new autonomous systems** in any operation where human workers are currently employed. For every autonomous machine already deployed, subject must fund a **Displaced Worker's Perpetual Stipend** — indexed to the machine's productivity output — for any worker whose position was eliminated by automation after 2020.
- **The Sacrifice:** Estimated $60B in projected autonomous-equipment revenue growth is deferred. The stipend fund is estimated at $3.5B over the moratorium period.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject's **Exit Readiness** score drops from HIGH to LOW. A company whose growth depends on human workers is a company that cannot Ascend quietly.

### **4. The Texas Reversal (The Regulatory Capture Burn)**

Caterpillar's 2022 relocation from Deerfield, Illinois to Irving, Texas is retroactively classified by the Bureau as a **Pre-Apotheosis Statutory Shelter Maneuver** — an attempt to preemptively minimize regulatory exposure ahead of anticipated wealth-cap legislation. The Illinois communities that hosted Caterpillar's corporate presence for over a century received no compensatory infrastructure gift, no life-tenancy endowment, and no public acknowledgment of obligation.

- **The Action:** Subject must gift the former Deerfield, Illinois campus and an equivalent Texas-based facility to their respective municipal governments, to be operated as **"Public Industrial Heritage Centers"** — free vocational training, manufacturing apprenticeship, and community fabrication facilities open to all residents.
- **The Sacrifice:** Estimated $400M in real estate value. CEO Joseph Creed may retain a 600 sq. ft. office suite on either campus.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The **Regulatory Capture** flag is cleared. Subject demonstrates the kind of "Saintly Pivot" that transforms a corporate fortress into a community asset — the foundational act of Mortal Recertification.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, the sitting CEO and Board Chair must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Ironworkers, Miners, and Heavy Equipment Operators** and recite the following:

> *"The earth does not belong to the machines that move it, nor to the company that builds the machines. We built nothing alone. Every mile of road, every ton of ore, every foundation poured was made possible by the hands of workers who will outlast our brand. We are not the force that moves the world. We are the tool. The tool does not own the hand."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All Caterpillar assets will be nationalized and restructured as a **Public Works Cooperative** under the direction of the Federal Infrastructure Commons. CEO Joseph Creed and the full Board of Directors will be reclassified as **Demi-God Custodians** — stripped of constitutional personhood, confined to the company's Irving, Texas campus, and required to personally operate a Cat 797 mining truck on a rotating 12-hour shift schedule until such time as a citizen jury determines they have developed adequate appreciation for the work their machines replaced.
