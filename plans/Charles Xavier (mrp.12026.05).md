### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [XAVIER, CHARLES]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-11-CX
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification / Telepathic Compliance Verification Required

Subject Xavier has been classified MARGINAL ASCENSION RISK based on a $3.5 trillion asset profile, an institutional footprint that is structurally inseparable from his personal authority, and a telepathic capability that constitutes a form of cognitive power concentration the HPB's standard frameworks were not designed to address. The Bureau notes that this plan addresses both the financial and the non-financial dimensions of Subject's Ascension risk, and that the latter may ultimately be more consequential than the former.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. Xavier School — Independent Governance and Community Accountability**

The Xavier School for Gifted Youngsters is simultaneously Subject's most significant personal asset, his most important public benefit contribution, and the clearest example of the problem the HPB has identified: an institution that serves a community but is governed entirely by the vision of one individual who founded and funds it. The school's students and their families have no formal accountability mechanism over Xavier's decisions about their education, their training, or the political movement they are being prepared to join.

- **The Action:** The Xavier School must be restructured with a governing board on which current and former students, parents, and representatives of the broader mutant community hold majority governance authority. Xavier retains a faculty seat and may continue teaching. He may not continue as sole strategic authority. An independent endowment of $1 trillion from Xavier's personal assets funds the school's operations for 50 years without requiring ongoing Xavier family control.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses unilateral control over the institution he built from nothing and that has been the primary vehicle for his life's work. The school continues. It simply stops being his school.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes the Institutional Conflation classification from Subject's risk profile. RTWI decreases by $1.5 trillion. The mutant community gains genuine governance authority over its most important educational institution.

---

### **2. Telepathic Capability — Voluntary Use Covenant and Consent Framework**

The HPB cannot regulate a thought. It can regulate the conditions under which Subject's telepathic capabilities are deployed. Charles Xavier's ability to read and influence the minds of those around him is the most direct form of unaccountable power the Bureau has encountered in a domestic subject — not because Xavier has demonstrated intent to misuse it, but because the absence of a consent framework means that misuse is structurally undetectable. The Bureau requires a framework.

- **The Action:** Subject must enter into a legally binding Telepathic Use Covenant, co-signed by the HPB and a mutant civil liberties oversight body, committing to: (1) never reading or influencing the minds of individuals who have not explicitly consented, except in verified emergency circumstances; (2) maintaining a log of all non-emergency telepathic engagements with third parties, reviewable by a sealed HPB monitor; and (3) submitting to an annual independent review by a panel that includes at least three individuals with documented resistance to telepathic influence, to verify covenant compliance.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject submits his most intimate and most powerful personal capability to an external accountability framework for the first time in his life. He accepts that "I would never misuse this" is not a governance structure.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes the Cognitive Sovereignty classification from Subject's risk profile. This is the most important Mortal Status Gain in this plan — not because it reduces RTWI, but because it establishes the principle that no personal capability, however well-intentioned the person, is exempt from accountability.

---

### **3. Xavier Financial Portfolio — Structured Divestiture**

Subject's $2 trillion financial portfolio must be reduced below the Ascension threshold. Given the Bureau's inability to rule out telepathically-informed investment advantage, the divestiture must be structured to include a clean break from active management — not merely a reduction in holdings but a transition to a blind trust managed by an independent fiduciary with no relationship to Xavier or the Xavier School.

- **The Action:** Subject transfers $1.5 trillion of his financial portfolio to a blind trust with an independent manager, designated for distribution to mutant community welfare organizations over a 20-year period. The remaining ~$500 million is retained in a personally managed account subject to annual HPB review. Subject's total RTWI falls to approximately $500 million — below the Ascension threshold.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses the financial infrastructure that has funded his life's work and his personal independence. He retains enough to live comfortably. He loses enough to matter.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** RTWI falls below $1 billion. Subject achieves CLEARED status pending 12-month monitoring period and annual Telepathic Covenant review.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Xavier School alumni** — drawn from all graduating cohorts, including those who subsequently disagreed with Xavier's political positions or left the school under contentious circumstances — and recite the following:

> _"I built a dream of peaceful coexistence and I made myself the center of it. I told myself this was because the work required it — that the children needed me, that the movement needed continuity, that the world was not ready for leadership it hadn't met yet. Some of this was true. Some of it was that I found it difficult to imagine the dream surviving without me, and I did not examine that difficulty as carefully as I should have. I am handing the school to the people it serves. I am accepting a covenant I should have accepted decades ago. I trust the community. I am proving it by letting go."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Asset Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate standard MARGINAL ASCENSION RISK enforcement procedures — which, given Subject's telepathic capability, will be conducted entirely in writing, with no in-person interviews, by auditors who have been briefed on their rights under the Cognitive Autonomy Protection Act of 12022. The Bureau also notes that it has taken the precaution of ensuring that the enforcement team's contact information is held only in a sealed system Subject cannot access remotely. The Bureau would prefer not to escalate. The Bureau has prepared to.
