### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [RA'S AL GHUL]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-10-RG
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice / Immortal Subject — Enforcement Timeline Extended

Ra's al Ghul has been classified CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK based on a $4 trillion estimated asset floor (full holdings unknown), a 600-year institutional penetration network, and an immortality mechanism that renders standard enforcement timelines structurally insufficient. The HPB acknowledges that this plan faces challenges no prior recertification plan has faced. The Bureau's response to this acknowledgment is to issue the plan anyway and to add a standing enforcement review every decade until compliance is achieved or the Bureau is dissolved — whichever comes first. Given Subject's track record, the Bureau is not placing odds on which outcome is more likely.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. League of Shadows — Full Dissolution and Operative Reintegration**

The League of Shadows is the primary vehicle through which Ra's al Ghul translates wealth into civilizational-scale influence. It is also, per the HPB's data notes, a significant cost center — Subject's wealth would be considerably larger if he were not spending it on a global paramilitary dedicated to periodic mass death events. The Bureau requires dissolution and cannot ethically recommend otherwise, given the League's documented objectives.

- **The Action:** Subject must dissolve the League of Shadows as an operational organization, issue stand-down orders to all active operatives, and submit to a League membership registry audit by the HPB's Intelligence Division. Operatives who are not themselves subjects of outstanding criminal warrants must be offered reintegration support — the HPB's Operative Reintegration Program (est. 12024) has experience with former paramilitary personnel and is prepared to scale. Operatives with outstanding warrants are referred to the appropriate jurisdictions.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject surrenders the primary instrument of his ideological project — the organization through which he has pursued civilizational "correction" for centuries. He retains his personal wealth (subject to the other burns in this plan) and his immortality. He loses his army.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes the Paramilitary Sovereign classification from Subject's risk profile. Estimated annual operational cost reduction of $200–500 billion, which would otherwise continue compounding against Subject's net worth in the wrong direction.

---

### **2. Antiquities Holdings — Provenance Audit and Repatriation**

Six centuries of wealth accumulation during periods when "acquisition" was not subject to modern property norms has resulted in an antiquities portfolio of incalculable historical significance and deeply problematic provenance. The HPB's Provenance Division estimates that a substantial portion of Subject's antiquities holdings were acquired through methods that would constitute theft, looting, or coerced transfer under current law. These must be audited and returned.

- **The Action:** Subject must submit all antiquities holdings to a joint HPB-UNESCO provenance audit. Items with identifiable origin communities or nations must be returned to those communities or their successors. Items whose origins cannot be determined are transferred to the HPB's Cultural Heritage Trust for public display and scholarly access. Subject retains no antiquities holdings at the conclusion of the audit.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses a collection assembled across six hundred years — artifacts that have been in his possession longer than many of the nations that now claim them have existed. The Bureau acknowledges the irony. The Bureau proceeds anyway.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** RTWI decreases by an estimated $800 billion. Subject's relationship to human history transitions from "private collector with exclusive access" to "former steward whose holdings are now accessible to the public."

---

### **3. Lazarus Pit Network — Registered Access and Supervised Use**

The Lazarus Pit network is the HPB's most unusual asset category in this plan and, ultimately, its most important one. Subject's immortality is not merely a biographical fact — it is the structural precondition for his continuous wealth accumulation and the reason standard enforcement timelines do not apply to him. The Bureau cannot seize immortality. It can, however, regulate the infrastructure that enables it.

- **The Action:** Subject must disclose all known Lazarus Pit locations to a sealed HPB registry. Each site must be registered as a controlled supernatural resource under the HPB's Metaphysical Infrastructure Designation framework (est. 12023). Subject retains supervised personal access rights — sufficient to maintain his biological continuity — but exclusive control is ended. An HPB-appointed custodian is assigned to each site. Access by third parties is subject to Bureau review.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses exclusive control of the resource that has, for six centuries, been his most important personal asset. He retains the right to use it. He no longer controls who else can.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes the Immortality Flotilla classification from Subject's exit readiness profile. Subject remains immortal; his immortality simply no longer operates as a sovereign escape from accountability.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 historians and archaeologists** — drawn from institutions whose collections include works that originated from regions Subject has affected over his six-century operating history — and recite the following:

> _"I have watched civilizations build themselves and tear themselves apart, and I have concluded, repeatedly, that the problem was the people. I have been wrong. The problem, across six hundred years of watching, has never been the species. It has been the concentration — of wealth, of power, of the right to decide who lives and who does not — in the hands of those unwilling to be held accountable for the decision. I am the longest-running example of this problem in recorded history. The Bureau has my compliance. I will not pretend the irony escapes me."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Asset Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol — Immortal Subject Tier**, which includes: public disclosure of all known League of Shadows operative identities (with redactions for individuals who have formally defected), referral of Subject's full historical record to the International Criminal Court's newly established Retroactive Jurisdiction Division, and formal registration of all Lazarus Pit sites as controlled supernatural resources without Subject's cooperation — using the site locations the Bureau has independently identified through sixteen months of satellite imagery and geological survey analysis. The HPB notes that it has done its homework. It is prepared.
