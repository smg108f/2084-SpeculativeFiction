### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [T'CHALLA]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-04-TC
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice / Strategic Subject — Caution Advised

The HPB has reason to believe Subject T'Challa has read this plan before it was written. This is not a joke. Subject's intelligence apparatus is the most sophisticated on Earth, his diplomatic preparation for every engagement is documented to be exhaustive, and the Bureau's lead analyst notes that T'Challa arrived at the audit intake interview with a folder. The HPB has nonetheless prepared the following plan in good faith, acknowledging that Subject may already have a counter-proposal, and that his counter-proposal is probably better than ours.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. Vibranium Synthesis Research — Open-Source Release**

The HPB's Strategic Materials Division has determined that Wakandan scientists have, with high probability, achieved meaningful progress toward vibranium synthesis — a process that would, if shared, eliminate Wakanda's monopoly on the world's most strategically important material. The continued non-disclosure of this research is the single largest structural source of Subject's RTWI premium. If vibranium can be synthesized, it becomes a commodity. If it becomes a commodity, it stops being a $100 trillion monopoly.

- **The Action:** Subject must commission an independent audit of Wakandan vibranium research by a joint panel of Wakandan and international scientists, and release any synthesis pathway discoveries under a Dead-Hand License — open-sourced immediately, with no Wakandan IP claim retained. If no synthesis pathway exists, Subject must publish the full negative results, enabling the international community to stop pursuing a dead end and Wakanda to stop bearing the suspicion that it is hoarding the solution.
- **The Sacrifice:** If synthesis is possible, Subject sacrifices the single largest source of Wakanda's economic leverage. The vibranium monopoly ends. Wakanda remains the world's most technically advanced nation, but it stops being the world's only source of the most important material in advanced manufacturing.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** RTWI drops by an estimated $40–200 trillion depending on market response to synthesis availability. Wakanda transitions from monopolist to innovator — a status that is, arguably, more sustainable and more aligned with Subject's stated values.

---

### **2. Wakandan Outreach Centers — Full Technology Transfer**

Subject's post-isolation Outreach Center network — currently operating in Oakland, London, Lagos, and several other sites — has been praised globally as a model of equitable development. The HPB's concern is that these centers operate on Wakandan terms, funded by Wakandan resources, governed by Wakandan priorities. They are generous. They are also an extension of Wakandan soft power into communities that have no reciprocal influence over Wakanda's governance. The Saintly Pivot, to be credible, must involve genuine release of control — not the appearance of generosity while retaining the architecture of dependency.

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer full governance of all Outreach Centers to independent community boards drawn from the populations they serve, with a 10-year endowment from the Wakandan sovereign wealth fund sufficient to sustain operations without requiring ongoing Wakandan approval or oversight. Wakanda may maintain a liaison seat on each board. It may not hold veto power.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses direct control of Wakanda's most visible international presence. The communities the centers serve become their own advocates rather than Wakanda's beneficiaries.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes the soft-power premium from Subject's RTWI calculation. More importantly, converts the Outreach network from a Wakandan asset to a genuine public good — which is what Subject has always said it was.

---

### **3. The Black Panther Mantle — Succession Framework and Bast Covenant Review**

The Heart-Shaped Herb and the Black Panther mantle represent a Dynastic Biological Asset that the HPB cannot seize, cannot value, and cannot ignore. The succession mechanism — in which the Herb's enhancement passes to each new Black Panther — creates a perpetual biological wealth concentration event. Each new monarch inherits not merely a treasury but a physical capability advantage over all unenhanced humans. The HPB is not asking Subject to give up the mantle. The HPB is asking Subject to ensure that it does not become a vehicle for hereditary biological exceptionalism.

- **The Action:** Subject must convene a Wakandan Constitutional Assembly to codify, for the first time in writing, the conditions under which the Black Panther mantle is awarded, the obligations it carries, and the mechanisms by which it can be revoked by the Wakandan people rather than solely by the Panther lineage or Bast's divine discretion. The resulting document must be ratified by a popular vote of Wakandan citizens.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject subjects the most sacred institution of Wakandan culture to democratic oversight. The Panther mantle — which has always been simultaneously divine gift and royal privilege — becomes, in part, a public trust.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes the Embodied Capital designation from the Bast mantle line. HPB Optics Rating upgrades to MARGINAL CONCERN.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 citizens of the Wakandan border communities** — the farmers, traders, and families who lived on the edges of Wakanda's isolation for generations, who never had access to the technology inside the walls — and recite the following:

> _"Wakanda's strength was never the vibranium. It was the people who protected it, refined it, built with it, and kept its secret for centuries — people who never held it and never profited from it, but gave their lives to guard what they were told was a sacred trust. I have benefited from their sacrifice. I have called it heritage. The Bureau calls it a monopoly. They are both right. What I share now, I share not as a gift from a king, but as a debt that cannot be fully repaid — only acknowledged."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Asset Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol — Sovereign Tier** and formally request that the United Nations Security Council convene a Vibranium Access Emergency Session. The HPB notes that this action will almost certainly be vetoed by three permanent members who have signed bilateral vibranium supply agreements with Wakanda, and that Subject probably anticipated this contingency as well. The Bureau is running out of leverage. The Bureau is proceeding anyway.
