### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [OFER, IDAN]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06IO
**Status:** Precautionary

*Coordinated with Eyal Ofer (HPB-12026-06EO). Burns are complementary — the Ofer family shipping empire is assessed as a thematic unit. Idan's burns focus on EPS emissions, IDE desalination access, and philanthropy scaling. Eyal's burns (Batch 2) focus on Zodiac Maritime and the Green Shipping Charter.*

Subject controls Eastern Pacific Shipping (LNG/tanker/bulk fleet) and Kenon Holdings (IDE Technologies desalination). The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The EPS LNG Fleet Transition Burn**

Eastern Pacific Shipping operates a major LNG carrier fleet. The HPB credits LNG as a bridge fuel but requires a transition timeline.

- **The Action:** Subject must commit — in coordination with Eyal Ofer's Green Shipping Charter (HPB-12026-06EO) but as a separate EPS commitment — to an **EPS Clean Shipping Roadmap**: (a) all new EPS vessel orders after 12027 to be dual-fuel or alternative fuel ready (green ammonia, hydrogen, or methanol); (b) a $100M EPS Clean Shipping Innovation Fund for green fuel bunkering R&D; (c) annual emissions disclosures filed with the HPB independently from any Zodiac Maritime filings.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M + higher newbuild costs.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Eastern Pacific Shipping has a binding, independently reported transition timeline, coordinated with but separate from Eyal's Zodiac Maritime commitments.

### **2. The IDE Desalination Access Burn**

IDE Technologies is among the world's leading desalination companies. The technology has genuine public utility — freshwater access is an escalating global crisis. The HPB asks that this technology serves the most water-insecure populations, not only those who can pay.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund an **IDE Desalination Access Fund** — $50M — to subsidize IDE technology deployment in water-stressed, low-income communities globally: priority to sub-Saharan Africa, South Asia, and the Middle East, in partnership with the UNDP and WHO water access programs. This is not a corporate marketing exercise — it is a separate philanthropic fund using IDE technology for non-commercial deployment.
- **The Sacrifice:** $50M.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The world's leading desalination technology company is documented as serving communities that cannot pay commercial rates.

### **3. The Ofer Foundation Scaling Burn**

Subject is an active Israeli philanthropist. The HPB credits this but asks for scale.

- **The Action:** Subject must increase the **Idan Ofer Foundation's** annual disbursement to $50M per year, with an expanded mandate beyond Israeli cultural institutions to include: (a) water access programs in Middle Eastern and Mediterranean water-insecure regions (coordinated with the IDE Fund above); (b) Israeli-Palestinian educational exchange programs, as a constructive use of his regional positioning.
- **The Sacrifice:** Scaled philanthropic commitment; political exposure of the Israeli-Palestinian education mandate.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** A major Israeli philanthropist's giving reflects the breadth of his regional impact.
