### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [HILDEBRAND, JEFFERY]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06JH2
**Status:** Urgent

Subject owns Hilcorp Energy and operates aging oil and gas infrastructure in ecologically sensitive Alaskan waters with a documented violation record. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Alaska Environmental Remediation Burn**

Hilcorp's Cook Inlet operations and documented violation history require proportionate environmental investment.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish the **Hilcorp Alaska Environmental Trust** — $250M — independently administered, with a mandate to: (a) fund comprehensive environmental monitoring of Cook Inlet, including methane and oil pollution tracking, marine ecosystem health, and Beluga whale population monitoring (Cook Inlet Beluga whale is an endangered species directly affected by Cook Inlet operations); (b) fund Alaskan First Nations (particularly Dena'ina and Alutiiq communities) who depend on Cook Inlet's fisheries and ecosystems and who bear disproportionate harm from oil and gas operations; (c) fund the decommissioning and site remediation of aging Cook Inlet platforms that Hilcorp has acquired; (d) commission an independent third-party audit of all current Hilcorp methane leak rates across its full portfolio, results to be published publicly.
- **The Sacrifice:** $250M.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The fines ($3M) were regulatory minimum; this is proportionate to actual environmental exposure.

### **2. The Worker Profit-Sharing Expansion Burn**

The $100,000 employee bonus model is credited. The HPB asks for formalization and extension.

- **The Action:** Subject must: (a) formally codify Hilcorp's profit-sharing program in the company's governance documents, making it legally binding rather than discretionary; (b) expand the program to include a **Hilcorp Community Dividend** — for every employee bonus cycle, an equal per-employee amount is donated to community funds in the counties and boroughs where Hilcorp operates (Houston, Kenai Peninsula Borough, North Slope Borough, and other operational areas); (c) fund $50M to a **Hilcorp Renewable Transition Apprenticeship** for current Hilcorp oil-and-gas workers to gain qualifications for renewable energy jobs before those transitions occur.
- **The Sacrifice:** $50M (apprenticeship) + ongoing community dividend.
