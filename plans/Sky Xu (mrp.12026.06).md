### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [XU, YANGTIAN "SKY"]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06SX
**Status:** Urgent

Subject founded SHEIN and derives his fortune from a business model built on sub-living-wage garment labour and algorithmic design appropriation. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Garment Worker Remediation Burn**

SHEIN's supplier model has been documented producing garments for £0.03 per unit. The HPB does not accept that SHEIN's self-commissioned audits resolve this.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish the **SHEIN Supply Chain Justice Fund** — $500M — administered by an independent board of garment worker unions, NGOs (including Clean Clothes Campaign and the Worker Rights Consortium), and supply chain transparency experts. The Fund must: (a) pay back-wage supplements to all current and former workers in documented SHEIN supplier factories who were paid below the local living wage in the past decade, verified by third-party auditors; (b) transition all SHEIN active supplier contracts to independently verified living-wage standards within 36 months, with SHEIN absorbing the cost increase rather than passing it to suppliers; (c) fund a permanent garment worker ombudsman for SHEIN's supply chain, reporting publicly.
- **The Sacrifice:** $500M + ongoing supply chain cost increases.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The fund matches the scale of the harm, not the scale of the PR budget.

### **2. The Designer Restitution Burn**

SHEIN has copied independent designers at industrial scale. The HPB treats this as systematic theft.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish the **SHEIN Creator Restitution Fund** — $100M — to compensate independent designers whose work was appropriated by SHEIN. Administered through a transparent claims process run by an independent IP arbitration panel. Claims of $500–$50,000 per documented theft instance; expedited process for small independent designers. Subject must also publicly commit SHEIN to an independently enforced IP compliance policy with automatic settlement for future infringement.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The victims of the design-theft model are compensated at scale.

### **3. The Textile Waste and Microplastics Burn**

SHEIN's business model generates textile waste globally. The HPB requires proportionate environmental accountability.

- **The Action:** Subject must: (a) fund $150M for global textile waste collection and processing programs, prioritizing the markets where SHEIN has highest disposal rates; (b) commit SHEIN to a binding 50% reduction in polyester content by 12031, independently verified; (c) fund $50M for microplastics research and ocean remediation in partnership with existing scientific programs.
- **The Sacrifice:** $200M.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 SHEIN Supplier Factory Workers (from Guangzhou factories), Independent Fashion Designers Whose Work Was Appropriated, and Ocean Microplastics Researchers**:

> *"SHEIN made fashion cheap. It made it cheap in ways that were not always visible to the customer, but were very visible to the worker earning three pence per garment and to the designer who saw their work appear on my platform without credit or compensation. The Supply Chain Justice Fund is not a PR initiative — it is a wage restoration program run by the people who can verify the harm. The Creator Restitution Fund pays designers whose work built SHEIN's catalog. The environmental program addresses what 6,000 new polyester styles per day does to the ocean. These are the costs I did not pay when I should have."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** Subject will be assigned to **FSS SHEIN** — a vessel that produces 6,000 new styles per day, entirely designed by the independent creators who sued SHEIN, manufactured on board by a crew paid a verified living wage per garment (not per item — hourly living wage regardless of output), and sold at prices that honestly reflect all labour and environmental costs. Every garment is labeled with the name of the designer who created it and the wage paid to make it.
