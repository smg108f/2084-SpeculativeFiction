### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ALPHABET INC. / GOOGLE LLC]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-05AG
**Status:** Critical — Epistemic Monopoly / Pre-Seizure Notice

Alphabet's RTWI of $4.5T places it at **4,500 times the Demi-God threshold** — the highest in the HPB's current active caseload for any entity. The entity has been found to hold an illegal search monopoly by federal court. Standard Asset Burn methodology applies, with special attention to the **Epistemic Infrastructure** dimension, which the Bureau classifies as a distinct category of demi-god risk beyond conventional wealth concentration.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Search Index Public Commons**

Google's search index — the map of the internet's contents that underlies both the search engine and the AI answer machine — was built by crawling publicly available information at vast computational expense. The Bureau classifies this index as a **Captured Public Commons**: the collective output of billions of humans publishing to the public internet, indexed, organized, and monetized by a single private entity that neither created the content nor sought permission to build a commercial monopoly on top of it.

- **The Action:** Google must publish its full search index as an open data commons under a perpetual public license — not the algorithm, not the ranking, but the raw index of what the internet contains. A Federally chartered **Open Web Index Authority** receives and maintains this index, making it available to any search provider, researcher, or public institution on equal terms. Google may continue to operate its own search engine using its own algorithms over this open index; it may not use its exclusive possession of the world's most comprehensive index as a barrier to competition.
- **The Sacrifice:** The structural moat created by fifteen years of index superiority is eliminated. Competitors no longer face the billion-dollar crawling cost of building a comparable index from scratch. The search market is opened to genuine competition.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Resolves the **Captured Public Commons** classification. Converts the index from a private moat into the public infrastructure it was always built from.

---

### **2. The Gemini Answer Transparency Mandate**

Google Gemini's evolution from search results to synthesized answers constitutes the most serious Epistemic Concentration risk in the Bureau's current caseload. When Gemini provides an answer, it does not link to sources in a manner that users naturally evaluate — it provides a verdict that users receive as authoritative. The commercial advertising architecture of Google's business creates structural incentives to favor answers that serve advertiser interests, demote answers that compete with Google's own products, and present as neutral syntheses that reflect algorithmic choices.

- **The Action:** All Gemini AI answers provided in the search context must be accompanied by: (a) full source citations with equal visual prominence to the answer text; (b) a publicly auditable "answer reasoning trace" — a machine-readable log of what sources were consulted, what weights were applied, and what alternative syntheses were generated but not selected; and (c) an independent **AI Answer Auditor** — a federally chartered body with full access to Gemini's answer generation infrastructure — that publishes quarterly reports on systematic biases, advertiser-correlated answer patterns, and factual accuracy. Google's right to maintain proprietary ranking algorithms is preserved; its right to use those algorithms to shape the world's epistemic environment without accountability is not.
- **The Sacrifice:** Google loses the ability to present Gemini's commercial judgments as neutral factual syntheses. The advertising architecture of the search business is rendered transparent alongside the answers it funds.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Resolves the **Epistemic Concentration** classification at the Gemini layer. The answer machine remains; it is now accountable.

---

### **3. The Advertising Structural Separation**

Google's advertising business — the monetization of the epistemological monopoly — has been the subject of a simultaneous antitrust case focused on Google's domination of the ad technology stack. Google owns the buy-side tools (DV360), the sell-side tools (Google Ad Manager), the exchange (AdX), and the largest publisher in the system (YouTube). The DOJ identified this as a structural conflict of interest that extracts maximum value from every advertising transaction while foreclosing competition.

- **The Action:** Google must divest either its buy-side advertising tools (DV360) or its sell-side tools (Google Ad Manager + AdX), as determined by the Federal Antitrust Authority. The divested entity must be fully independent of Alphabet ownership and governance. Google may retain YouTube as a content platform; it may not retain simultaneous ownership of both the buying and selling infrastructure of the digital advertising market.
- **The Sacrifice:** Google loses the vertical integration premium that allows it to simultaneously represent advertisers, publishers, and operate the exchange, setting prices at every layer. The most lucrative structural position in digital advertising is unwound.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Resolves the **Advertising Vertical Integration Monopoly** classification. The digital advertising market gains structural separation that the Bureau calculates will return approximately $30–50B annually to publishers who currently receive a smaller share of ad revenue than a competitive market would produce.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Sundar Pichai and the Alphabet Board must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Journalists Whose Publications Were De-Ranked by the Algorithm, Small Business Owners Whose Traffic Collapsed in a Core Update, and Librarians Who Watch Their Users Ask Google Instead of Them** and recite the following:

> _"We did not write the internet. We did not create the knowledge. We crawled it, indexed it, ranked it, and called the ranking 'relevance.' We built an answer machine and told the world it was a search engine until the moment we decided it wasn't. The index was built from the public's creation and will now belong to the public. Gemini will show its work. The advertising exchange will be separated from the advertising buyer and the advertising seller. We remain a company. We no longer remain the oracle. The world has always known more than we have shown it."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. The search index will be placed under federal receivership and transferred to the Open Web Index Authority by executive order. Gemini's answer generation logs will be subpoenaed under the Epistemic Accountability Act of 12027 and published in full. The ad tech vertical integration will be unwound by federal antitrust order, with Google required to divest Ad Manager and AdX within 180 days. Sundar Pichai will be required to answer a specific and binding set of questions from a federal court about the algorithm's ranking criteria, under oath, on the record, in public, without the words "relevant," "quality," or "user experience" as substitutes for specificity. The Bureau anticipates this will take several days.
