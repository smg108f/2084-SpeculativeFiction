## The Three Burns

**Sacrifice:** Loss of the pricing opacity that has historically advantaged large-volume buyers over smaller downstream manufacturers.

**Mortal Status Gain:** Small manufacturers gain visibility into whether they are paying fair, comparable prices for critical inputs.

### Burn 2: Environmental and Safety Disclosure

**Sacrifice:** Increased compliance costs and public exposure of facility conditions previously undisclosed.

**Mortal Status Gain:** Workers and surrounding communities gain verified safety and environmental accountability.

### Burn 3: Supply Diversification Support

**Action:** Support development of alternative domestic suppliers for critical intermediate compounds, reducing single-source dependency risk for downstream manufacturers.

**Sacrifice:** Deliberate reduction of market concentration advantage.

**Mortal Status Gain:** Downstream manufacturing ecosystems become less vulnerable to single-supplier disruption or pricing power.
