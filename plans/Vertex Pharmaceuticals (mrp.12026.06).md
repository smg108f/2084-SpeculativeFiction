### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [VERTEX PHARMACEUTICALS]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-44VP
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject has been found to be hoarding not capital but **cure** — converting a solved genetic disease into a perpetual revenue instrument by maintaining the gap between "could be cured" and "is cured if you can pay." To retain Human Citizen Status (corporate-entity equivalence under the 2079 Demi-God Trust Amendment), Subject must dissolve this gap entirely, surrendering the patents, the pricing architecture, and the mythology of "innovation" that sustains it.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Trikafta Patent Surrender (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies composition-of-matter patents on cures for fatal genetic diseases as **"Mortality Leverage Instruments"** — assets whose entire value derives from the threat of withholding survival. Subject's CFTR modulator portfolio (Trikafta, Kalydeco, Symdeko, and successors) constitutes the single largest such instrument on record.

- **The Action:** Subject must place all CFTR modulator patents into the **Global Open Cure Commons**, an irrevocable, royalty-free license available to any qualified manufacturer in any nation, effective immediately and permanently.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of an estimated **$40B in projected monopoly-rent valuation** — the entire "future toll" baked into Subject's current market capitalization.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject converts from "Sole Keeper of the Bridge" to "Builder Who Walked Away From the Tollbooth." The Concentration Hazard: Biological is permanently retired.

### **2. Dissolution of the "Fair Price for Innovation" Doctrine (Ideological Burn)**

Subject's pricing philosophy — that $370,000/year is the "fair" cost of having discovered a cure — is classified by the HPB as a **Class-III Memetic Hazard**: it teaches the public that the value of a human life can be precisely measured against a corporation's R&D ledger, and that the difference is owed.

- **The Action:** Subject's executive leadership must record and broadcast a public retraction titled _The Difference Was Never Ours to Sell_, explicitly repudiating the "value-based pricing" framework Subject has used in earnings calls and congressional testimony for over a decade.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses its standing as the pharmaceutical industry's preferred spokesperson on pricing policy before Congress and the WHO.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This neutralizes the **Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric** violation embedded in phrases like "we price to the value we create" — language the HPB has determined functions as a moral alibi for triage-by-wealth.

### **3. The "Spare Key" Initiative (Reputational/Memetic Burn)**

Subject has spent a decade ensuring that no government, generic manufacturer, or buyers' club could produce an alternative supply of its CF therapies — including active legal opposition to the Bangladeshi-manufactured generic now reaching patients at roughly 3% of Subject's list price.

- **The Action:** Subject must publicly fund and technically support **three independent generic manufacturing sites** (Subject's own choosing of location is forfeited; sites will be assigned by lottery among petitioning nations — Brazil, India, South Africa, Ukraine) at full technology-transfer standard, with no licensing fee.
- **The Sacrifice:** Permanent end to Subject's manufacturing exclusivity narrative — the claim that "only we can be trusted to make this safely."
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject is reclassified from "Monopolist" to "Technology Donor," clearing the **Structural Substitution** flag (wealth standing in for what public health infrastructure should provide).

### **4. The Pipeline Pre-Disclosure Mandate (Future-Hazard Burn)**

Subject's expansion into gene editing (Casgevy), non-opioid pain medicine (suzetrigine/Journavx), and hypoimmune cell therapies for Type 1 diabetes is flagged as **"Pre-Enclosure"** — the act of fencing off cures for diseases the public does not yet know are curable, before any pricing precedent can be challenged.

- **The Action:** Subject must publish, eighteen months before any FDA filing, the projected list price and global access plan for every therapy in active Phase 2 or later trials, subject to binding HPB review and revision.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of "first-to-market pricing surprise" — the single greatest lever Subject has used to set precedent before regulators or patients can organize.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject is no longer permitted to "Pre-Ascend" silently; every future cure is born inside the Commons rather than behind a fence.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's Chief Executive must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Cystic Fibrosis Patients, Home Health Aides, and Pharmacy Technicians** and recite the following:

> _"We did not cure the disease. We rented you the cure, and called the rent a kindness. Today we hand back the key we were never owed, and we will not build another door to lock behind us."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All CFTR modulator and gene-therapy patents will be nationalized without compensation under the Biological Commons Doctrine, Subject's executive leadership will be individually reclassified as **Demi-God Entities** stripped of constitutional personhood, and the corporate shell will be dissolved into the **Public Pharmaceutical Trust**, to be run in perpetuity by a board elected from patient advocacy organizations.
