### **1. The FDA Quality Compliance Burn**

Sun Pharma has faced FDA import alerts and quality concerns at specific facilities. The HPB asks for a commitment to resolving all outstanding regulatory concerns.

- **The Action:** File with the HPB a **Sun Pharma Quality Assurance Charter**: (a) timeline for resolving all outstanding FDA import alerts and facility observations; (b) voluntary third-party GMP (Good Manufacturing Practices) audits of all Sun Pharma facilities globally, published annually; (c) a commitment that any facility with outstanding FDA concerns will not be used for US-market production until cleared.
- **The Sacrifice:** Operational restrictions; audit costs.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a generic pharmaceutical company with documented regulatory concerns into a model for transparent quality accountability.

### **2. The Access Medicines Burn**

Sun Pharma makes affordable generics. The HPB asks for a formal access program.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Sun Pharma Access Foundation** — ₹3B (~$36M) — dedicated to providing essential generic medicines at zero or minimal cost to patients in India and low-income countries who cannot afford even generic prices, coordinated with government health programs.
- **The Sacrifice:** ₹3B.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the business of making affordable drugs into a formal program ensuring the least-able-to-pay receive them.
