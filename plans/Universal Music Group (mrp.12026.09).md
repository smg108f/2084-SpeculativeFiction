### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT UNIVERSAL MUSIC GROUP**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09UMG
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification

### **1. The Burn: Artist AI Licensing Revenue Floor**

**The Action:** UMG establishes a binding minimum revenue-share floor guaranteeing individual artists a fixed percentage of any AI licensing settlement or partnership revenue generated from their specific recordings, published transparently rather than absorbed into general label revenue.
**The Sacrifice:** Estimated $200M annually in redirected AI licensing revenue.
**Mortal Status Gain:** -7% RTWI.

### **2. The Burn: Independent Artist Voice Council**

**The Action:** UMG establishes an independent artist advisory council with genuine input rights on future AI licensing negotiations, rather than negotiating unilaterally on artists' behalf.
**The Sacrifice:** Reduced unilateral negotiating flexibility.
**Mortal Status Gain:** -3% RTWI.
