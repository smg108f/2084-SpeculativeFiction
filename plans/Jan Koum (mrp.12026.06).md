### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [KOUM, JAN]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06JK2
**Status:** Precautionary — Values-Coherent Classification Applied

Subject founded WhatsApp, sold it to Meta/Facebook for $22B, and resigned from Meta's board citing privacy concerns. He funds the Signal Foundation and Freedom of the Press Foundation. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The WhatsApp Privacy Remediation Burn**

The sale of WhatsApp to Meta resulted in the degradation of WhatsApp's original privacy architecture through data-sharing policy changes. Koum resigned from the board when this began. The HPB notes that the sale itself enabled this outcome.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund, independently of Meta or Signal Foundation, a **Global Encrypted Messaging Access Fund** — $300M — to: (a) fund the development, maintenance, and hosting of open-source end-to-end encrypted messaging alternatives (Signal Protocol derivatives, Matrix, and others) globally, with specific priority on access in countries where end-to-end encryption is under government threat; (b) fund litigation support through organizations like EFF and Privacy International defending end-to-end encryption in countries attempting to mandate backdoors; (c) fund technical education — teaching end-to-end encryption and digital privacy to journalists, activists, and civil society organizations in high-risk countries.
- **The Sacrifice:** $300M.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The man who sold private messaging to Facebook funds an independent ecosystem of private messaging as a structural counterweight.

### **2. The Koum Foundation Immigration and Access Burn**

Subject emigrated from Ukraine at 16, lived on food stamps, and built a $22B company. The HPB asks that this origin story be reflected in philanthropic priorities.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Koum Foundation** — $200M — with three mandates: (a) youth coding and technology education for recent immigrants to the United States and Western Europe, targeting communities similar to Koum's own origin (low-income, Eastern European, Ukrainian); (b) Ukrainian humanitarian aid — technology access (devices, connectivity) for displaced Ukrainians; (c) food security in Mountain View and San Jose for low-income residents — a direct acknowledgment of Koum's own food stamps experience.
- **The Sacrifice:** $200M.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject's personal origin story is mirrored in his philanthropy.
