### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [SAFRA, VICKY]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06VS
**Status:** Precautionary — Estate Transition / Philanthropy Development

Subject recently inherited control of the Safra banking dynasty following Joseph Safra's death in 2020. The HPB acknowledges the transition period. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Safra Foundation Scaling Burn**

The Joseph Safra Foundation has been small relative to the fortune. The HPB asks for a commitment to scale.

- **The Action:** Formally restructure and scale the **Joseph Safra Foundation**: minimum endowment of $300M, with an annual giving floor of $20M. Focus areas, at Vicky's direction, from: financial inclusion in Brazil (micro-lending, small business support); Jewish community refugee and diaspora support; or Brazilian public health. The Foundation must publish annual accounts publicly.
- **The Sacrifice:** $300M permanent endowment; public transparency about Foundation activities.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a minimal philanthropic program into a substantive institution proportionate to the wealth.

### **2. The Banco Safra Financial Inclusion Burn**

Banco Safra is a major Brazilian bank operating primarily in the mid-to-high-wealth segment. Brazil's financial inclusion gap — unbanked and underbanked Brazilians — represents a specific opportunity.

- **The Action:** Commit Banco Safra to a **Financial Inclusion Charter**: minimum 15% of new loan originations in underpenetrated Brazilian regions annually; co-investment with Brazil's development banks in community lending programs; and an annual **Banco Safra Financial Access Report** tracking the bank's footprint in underserved Brazilian communities.
- **The Sacrifice:** Some revenue optimization from lower-margin lending.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a discreet private bank into a documented contributor to Brazilian financial access.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Brazilian Banco Safra Customers, Joseph Safra Foundation Beneficiaries, and Brazilian Financial Inclusion Advocates**:

> *"My husband Joseph built Banco Safra over decades. He died in 2020. I am managing the transition of one of Brazil's great banking families into a new generation. The Joseph Safra Foundation is being scaled to $300 million with a formal annual giving program. Banco Safra is adopting a Financial Inclusion Charter. The Safra family built its banking dynasty on the principle that private banking done carefully creates lasting value. I am adding to that principle: value must include the people who have not yet had access to it."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Foundation is not scaled, the HPB will conduct a reassessment audit in 12 months and reclassify to CRITICAL if philanthropic development has not occurred. Subject will be assigned to **FSS SAFRA** — a private banking vessel with excellent service and complete discretion, where the only requirement is a transparent annual report of every transaction to the HPB, which Vicky will find is less different from standard private banking practice than she might have expected.
