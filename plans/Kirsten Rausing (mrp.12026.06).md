### **SHARED BURN (ONE-THIRD SHARE): The Tetra Pak Packaging Burn**

200 billion aseptic cartons per year. The multi-layer construction (paper + LDPE + aluminum foil) creates recycling difficulty that the company has not yet resolved at global scale.

- **The Action (Kirsten Rausing's one-third share):** Subject must contribute her proportionate share to the **Rausing Packaging Remediation Fund** — total family commitment $300M (each sibling $100M): (a) investment in aseptic carton recycling infrastructure globally — specifically in the middle-income countries where carton recycling rates are lowest and where Tetra Pak penetration is highest; (b) funding for open-source packaging research to develop next-generation aseptic packaging with mono-material recyclability; (c) retroactive community investment in the communities hosting current Tetra Pak manufacturing facilities, proportionate to historical environmental impact.
- **Kirsten Rausing's Sacrifice:** $100M (shared obligation).

### **INDIVIDUAL BURN: The Horse Welfare and Medical Research Scale-Up**

The existing philanthropic record is the most creditable of the three siblings. The HPB's individual burn is calibrated accordingly — it extends and formalizes what is already present.

- **The Action:** Subject must: (a) endow $75M to formally establish the **Lanwades Foundation** — expanding existing medical research giving into a permanent, independent, publicly transparent grant-making body focused on rare disease research and cancer biology, with an open application process and published grant decisions; (b) fund a **Thoroughbred Welfare Initiative** — $25M — focused on the welfare of racehorses after their racing careers end (re-homing, retraining, and sanctuary programs for ex-racehorses in the UK, Ireland, and France), because the industry that Lanwades Stud profits from produces horses that require care the industry does not adequately fund.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M.
