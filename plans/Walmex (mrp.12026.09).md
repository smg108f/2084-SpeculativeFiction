### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [WAL-MART DE MÉXICO (WALMEX)]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09WMX
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification

Walmex's Ascension classification stems from its regional monopsony position and the labor-displacement timing of its 2026 automation push during a soft-spending period. Recertification targets both the supplier-side leverage and the worker-side transition.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Supplier Payment Terms Reform**

- **The Action:** Publish standardized, accelerated payment terms for small and mid-sized regional suppliers, independently audited annually.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced working-capital advantage currently gained from extended supplier payment cycles.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Addresses the monopsony-leverage concern directly.

### **2. The Automation Transition Guarantee**

- **The Action:** Commit to retraining and internal redeployment guarantees for any worker displaced by the 2026 automation push, for a minimum of 18 months.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced near-term cost savings from the automation initiative.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Decouples the automation strategy from unmitigated worker harm during a soft-spending downturn.

### **3. The Regional Price Impact Disclosure**

- **The Action:** Publish an annual report on Walmex's estimated impact on regional consumer prices and supplier margins in markets where it holds dominant share.
- **The Sacrifice:** Increased public and regulatory scrutiny of market concentration effects.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts an ambiguous "low prices help everyone" narrative into a transparent, checkable account.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Walmex's leadership must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Regional Suppliers and Store Workers** and recite the following:

> _"We built our growth on your patience with our payment terms and your acceptance of automation you did not ask for. We will now pay you faster and catch you when the machines take your shift."_
