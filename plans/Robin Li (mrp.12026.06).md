### **1. The Medical Advertising Accountability Burn** *(activates upon AC-1)*

- **The Action:** Subject must fund ¥2B for the **Wei Zexi Medical Accuracy Foundation** — providing: (a) compensation to families harmed by fraudulent medical providers who purchased Baidu search prominence; (b) an independent Chinese Medical Information Standards Board that audits Baidu's medical advertising against evidence-based medicine criteria, with authority to remove non-compliant ads; (c) free evidence-based medical information search results permanently prioritized above paid placement in all health-related queries.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥2B.

### **2. The Information Access Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must fund $150M for a **Global Chinese Information Access Fund** — supporting: (a) internet freedom tools (VPNs, Tor, circumvention software) for Chinese users; (b) journalism and free information organizations serving Chinese-language audiences outside Chinese government jurisdiction; (c) digital literacy programs helping Chinese users understand the nature and scope of internet filtering they experience.
- **The Sacrifice:** $150M (to be administered outside Chinese government jurisdiction).

### **3. The Search Accountability Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must fund ¥500M for a **Baidu Search Transparency Initiative** — publishing quarterly reports on: (a) all categories of content filtered from Baidu search results; (b) all government requests for content removal or query filtering received and complied with; (c) the methodology by which commercial content is ranked relative to organic results. Reports published on a server outside Chinese government jurisdiction and accessible globally.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥500M.
