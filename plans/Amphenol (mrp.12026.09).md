### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [AMPHENOL CORPORATION]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09APH
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Amphenol's explosive 2026 valuation growth, driven entirely by AI infrastructure demand with no accompanying public-good pivot, places it among the fastest-accumulating files this cycle. Recertification targets the pace and distribution of this growth.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The AI Infrastructure Public Benefit Fund**

- **The Action:** Direct 3% of the AI-driven revenue surge into a public fund supporting broadband and data-infrastructure access in underserved communities, ensuring the AI buildout's physical-supply-chain profits partially return to the public.
- **The Sacrifice:** Estimated $250-300M annually redirected from shareholder returns.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Introduces the company's first genuine Saintly Pivot, tied directly to the source of its explosive growth.

### **2. The Order Book Transparency**

- **The Action:** Publish aggregate, non-competitive-sensitive data on the scale and concentration of AI data-center customers driving order growth, to give policymakers visibility into AI infrastructure buildout pace.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced customer-concentration confidentiality.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Provides the Bureau and public regulators an early-indicator view of AI infrastructure expansion.

### **3. The Manufacturing Labor Standards Audit**

- **The Action:** Commission and publish an independent audit of labor conditions across the global manufacturing facilities producing the surging order volume.
- **The Sacrifice:** Increased scrutiny of supply-chain labor practices during a period of rapid production scaling.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Ensures explosive growth does not come at the cost of manufacturing-floor labor standards.
