### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[BLACKROCK, INC.]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06BLK
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject is not a person and cannot be marched to a Spaceport — but the entity *behaves* as a demi-god, votes as a demi-god, and sees as a demi-god, and the HPB will not permit a "structural loophole" to shield an Aggregation Singularity from recertification. The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days, or the Bureau will proceed under the **Corporate Personhood Reversal Statute of 2061**.

---

### **1. The Aladdin Blinding (The Largest and Most Symbolic Burn)**

The HPB classifies Aladdin as a **Privatized Oracle** — a single proprietary lens through which an estimated $20 trillion of the world's capital now squints before it dares to move. No entity may own the eye the market sees through.

- **The Action:** Subject must spin Aladdin off into a **Public Risk Commons**, governed by a rotating, lottery-selected board of credit-union analysts, actuaries, and retired pension administrators, with its source code and risk models published in full.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of all licensing revenue, proprietary-model advantage, and — most critically — the unique vantage point of seeing every competitor's exposure before they do.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** BlackRock becomes a **mere participant** in the market it formerly surveilled from above. Estimated reduction in "Cognitive Impact" Potentiality Value: **$4.2 trillion**.

### **2. The Great Unbraiding (Cross-Holding Divestment)**

The HPB's auditors flagged a uniquely disturbing pattern: Subject is simultaneously a top shareholder of companies that compete head-to-head — Coca-Cola and PepsiCo, Ford and GM, every major airline and every major bank — meaning Subject's "vote" is never truly aligned with any single enterprise's success, only with the aggregate float. This is **Distributed Omnipresence**, and it is the purest form of Hoarding the Bureau has yet documented: not owning one castle, but a brick in every castle on the board.

- **The Action:** Subject must divest down to a **single-sector cap of 2%** in any index or actively managed product, selecting one industry in which to hold meaningful positions and fully exiting the rest — converting remaining holdings into **non-voting, donor-advised trusts** distributed to the public pension systems of the municipalities where its data centers are sited.
- **The Sacrifice:** The end of "coordinated voting strategy" across the so-called Big Three; Subject can no longer cast a unified quarter-share of every shareholder vote in the S&P 500.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject is reclassified from **Silent Universal Shareholder** to **Sector Participant**. This neutralizes the "Shadow Cabinet" violation outright.

### **3. The Stakeholder-Capitalism Penance (Memetic Rebuttal)**

Subject has spent a decade publishing letters describing itself as a "fiduciary for the common saver" and a force "democratizing" market access — language the HPB's Memetics Office classifies as **Custodial Mythology**: framing trillions in privately-directed capital as merely "other people's money," even while wielding it as a singular, coordinated bloc.

- **The Action:** Subject must publish, under its own letterhead and with no ghostwriting permitted by senior leadership, a manifesto titled *We Were Never Just Holding It For You: An Apology From the Aggregator*, openly itemizing every instance in the past decade where its proxy votes shaped a corporate outcome in ways individual shareholders never requested or were informed of.
- **The Requirement:** Fund a **$2 billion "Glass Ledger" Initiative** — a real-time, public-facing dashboard showing, for any citizen, exactly how their retirement fund's shares were voted and why, on every single ballot, in plain language.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the **Custodial Camouflage** rating; converts "Saintly Camouflage" into genuine "Saintly Transparency."

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's Chief Executive and entire Board must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Retired Pension-Fund Clerks, Credit Union Tellers, and Public Librarians** and recite the following:

> _"We do not see the whole board. We never did — we only ever held up a mirror and called the reflection ours. From this day forward we own one thing, in one place, in plain sight, and we vote it the way our neighbor votes: alone, and without knowing what anyone else will do."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol** under the Corporate Personhood Reversal Statute. Aladdin's servers will be physically seized and relocated to the **National Ledger Vault in Sioux Falls**, where its risk models will be open-sourced by federal decree; all cross-holdings will be auctioned in randomized, sealed-bid lots to prevent any successor entity from reassembling the aggregate view; and BlackRock's corporate charter will be dissolved into forty regional stewardship cooperatives, none permitted to communicate with one another for a period of no less than twenty years.
