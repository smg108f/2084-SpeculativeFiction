### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ROYAL BANK OF CANADA / IDE-RBC]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-01RBC
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice / Institutional Demi-God Classification

Royal Bank of Canada has been formally classified as an **Institutional Demi-God Entity (IDE)** under the 2041 Supplement to the Demi-God Act of 2034. Its market capitalization of $240–265B USD, structural control of Canadian mortgage and lending markets, and $263B+ in cumulative fossil fuel financing since 2016 constitute an Ascension profile of the highest order. Recertification is required before the HPB activates the **Institutional Apotheosis Protocol**, which would transfer the bank's Canadian retail assets directly to a network of federally chartered credit unions.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Mortgage Commons Transfer (The Structural Burn)**

The HPB classifies RBC's mortgage book as a **Chokepoint Asset** — a private holding whose scale gives the institution effective policy-making power over Canadian housing. A single lender holding 25–30% of a nation's residential mortgage debt is not a bank; it is a landlord of the financial system.

- **The Action:** Transfer all residential mortgage holdings below $1.5M CAD to a new federally chartered entity: the **Canadian Mortgage Commons (CMC)**. RBC retains commercial lending only.
- **The Sacrifice:** Approximately $400–500B CAD in mortgage assets exit RBC's balance sheet. The bank loses its primary instrument of retail dependency — the mechanism by which it ensures that millions of citizens cannot afford to bank elsewhere.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** RBC ceases to be a Chokepoint Entity for housing. Its Structural Sovereignty score drops from CRITICAL to ELEVATED. The CMC becomes a public-benefit corporation; RBC becomes, merely, a large commercial bank.

### **2. The Fossil Finance Reckoning (The Thermodynamic Burn)**

Since the Paris Agreement, RBC has financed more fossil fuel expansion than any other bank on Earth in peak years — a distinction it has held while simultaneously publishing Net-Zero pledges timed to outlast every current executive's tenure. The HPB classifies this pattern as **Temporal Fraud**: commitments made in a time horizon the institution has no intention of inhabiting.

- **The Action:** Full divestment from all fossil fuel expansion financing within 18 months. Existing fossil fuel loans may not be renewed. RBC must additionally transfer $26.3B — 10% of its cumulative Paris-era fossil financing, calculated at USD — to the **HPB Climate Debt Registry**, to be disbursed as adaptation grants to communities in the Global South most affected by emissions enabled by RBC lending.
- **The Sacrifice:** RBC exits the most profitable segment of its corporate lending portfolio. The $26.3B transfer reduces its equity buffer significantly and will require dilutive capital raises.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Thermodynamic Hostage-Taking violation is cleared. RBC's climate pledge becomes, for the first time, a statement about the present rather than an alibi for the future.

### **3. The HSBC Reversal (The Oligopoly Burn)**

The 2024 acquisition of HSBC Canada was the defining act of RBC's Ascension — the moment at which the institution chose scale over sufficiency. The Competition Bureau's approval, obtained under conditions, satisfied the letter of 2020s regulatory law while violating its spirit. The HPB is not bound by the Competition Bureau's findings.

- **The Action:** RBC must divest the HSBC Canada retail branch network — all former HSBC locations, client accounts, and associated mortgage portfolios — to a new independent institution. This entity must be structured as a **B-Corp cooperative** with no single shareholder holding more than 5% of voting shares.
- **The Sacrifice:** RBC reverses its most significant strategic acquisition. The $13.5B purchase price is not recovered. The approximately 130 former HSBC branches, 780,000 client relationships, and associated deposits exit RBC's balance sheet permanently.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Canada regains a structurally independent banking alternative. RBC's market share returns to pre-2024 levels. The IDE classification is downgraded to Marginal Ascension Risk pending the completion of Burns 1 and 2.

### **4. The CEO Testimony Requirement (The Memetic Burn)**

The institution has no face in the way a human demi-god does, but it has spokespeople. The "responsible banking" ideology that has insulated RBC from accountability — the careful language of climate commitment, the community investment press releases, the testimony before parliamentary committees about the bank's essential role in Canadian prosperity — constitutes what the HPB terms **Institutional Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric**. The bank has claimed to be indispensable. Indispensability is a divine attribute.

- **The Action:** The sitting CEO of RBC must appear before a public session of the HPB's **Citizen Review Panel** — not Parliament, not a bank committee — and answer unscripted questions from a jury of 12 lottery-selected Canadians: renters, gig workers, farmers, and First Nations members from communities crossed by pipelines financed by RBC. The session is broadcast. No prepared remarks are permitted.
- **The Sacrifice:** The institution loses the protection of mediated language. Its human representative must answer, in public, to the people whose lives are structured by its decisions.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Institutional Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric score is cleared. The bank is reclassified from "Force of Nature" to "Large Regulated Entity." This is the correct classification.

To finalize recertification, the sitting CEO of Royal Bank of Canada must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Canadian Renters, Pipeline-Adjacent Farmers, and First Nations Housing Advocates** and recite the following:

> _"We are not the foundation of Canada. We are a lender. We exist because Canadians permit us to exist, not because Canada cannot exist without us. Our size is not stability. Our profits are not prosperity. We have confused our own indispensability with public good, and we will spend the next decade proving that we understand the difference."_

Dave McKay's portrait in the RBC boardroom will be replaced with a laminated copy of this recertification plan.
