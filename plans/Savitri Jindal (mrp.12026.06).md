### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [JINDAL, SAVITRI]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06SJ
**Status:** Precautionary — Industrial-Political Conflict / Steel Legacy

Subject is chairperson emeritus of the Jindal Group and founding matriarch of India's largest private steel family. Age accommodation applied (73). The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Political Firewall Burn**

Savitri Jindal has held political office while formally chairing a major industrial group with state-level interests in the same jurisdiction. The HPB asks for a formal separation.

- **The Action:** File a binding **Jindal Political-Industrial Firewall Charter**: (a) formal resignation from any active political candidacy or party position while retaining any formal role in Jindal Group entities; (b) OR transfer of all Jindal Group chairperson roles to an independent trustee while she holds political office; (c) annual disclosure of any state government contracts received by Jindal Group entities during any period of her political office. Subject may choose (a) or (b), but must select one.
- **The Sacrifice:** Either political office or formal industrial control — but not both simultaneously without full disclosure.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts India's most prominent industrial-political dual-role into a documented accountability structure.

### **2. The Steel Worker Community Burn**

India's steel industry — including Jindal operations — employs large workforces in conditions that warrant investment. The HPB asks for a community program proportionate to the group's scale.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Jindal Steel Worker Community Trust** — ₹3B (~$36M) — funded jointly with family members who control operating entities, supporting: (a) healthcare access for steel workers and their families across Jindal operations; (b) children's education programs for steel worker families in plant regions; (c) environmental health monitoring in communities adjacent to Jindal steel facilities.
- **The Sacrifice:** ₹3B; family coordination.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts India's largest private steel family into documented investors in their own workforce's wellbeing.

### **3. The OPJGU Scaling Burn**

The O.P. Jindal Global University is genuinely excellent. The HPB asks for a scholarships commitment that specifically reaches the communities most affected by the steel industry.

- **The Action:** Establish the **O.P. Jindal Steel Community Scholarship Fund** — ₹1B (~$12M) annually — providing fully funded university places at OPJGU to students from Haryana and Chhattisgarh communities where Jindal Group has its major steel operations, prioritizing students from families of steel workers and adjacent communities.
- **The Sacrifice:** ₹1B/year; directed scholarship rather than selective prestige admissions.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts an elite private university into a documented pipeline from steel-worker communities into higher education.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Jindal Steel Workers from Haryana, OPJGU Students, and Haryana State Election Observers**:

> *"My husband Om Prakash Jindal built the Jindal Group. When he died in 2005, I became its chairperson and I was elected to the Haryana assembly. I understand that the HPB is asking about this combination. The political firewall charter is filed. The Steel Worker Community Trust is established. The OPJGU Scholarship Fund is funded annually. Om Prakash Jindal built schools everywhere he built steel plants because he believed they were the same kind of investment. I am continuing that belief at a scale proportionate to what we have built."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the political firewall charter is not filed, the HPB will refer the Jindal Group's state government contract history to the India Accountability Bureau for independent review. Subject will be assigned to **FSS JINDAL**, a steel-hulled vessel where every decision requires unanimous agreement among the four Jindal sons and their mother — the HPB considers this the most realistic possible consequence because the Jindal family decision-making process already functions this way.
