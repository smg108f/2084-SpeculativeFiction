### **1. The Kraft Heinz Worker Legacy Burn**

The Kraft Heinz zero-based budgeting experiment destroyed $15B in equity value and eliminated thousands of jobs. Sicupira's individual obligation addresses the Kraft Heinz worker dimension.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund $150M for the **Kraft Heinz Worker Restitution Fund** — directed to workers displaced in Kraft Heinz facilities closed under the zero-based budgeting regime: severance gap-filling, retraining programs, and community investment in the US and Canadian towns where Kraft Heinz plant closures were concentrated.
- **The Sacrifice:** $150M.

### **2. The Brazilian Business Education Scale-Up**

The Dom Cabral Foundation support is genuine. The HPB invites its extension toward a more direct community impact.

- **The Action:** Subject must endow $75M to expand the **Dom Cabral Foundation** — specifically its programs reaching SME owners and entrepreneurs in lower-income Brazilian states, moving business education from the executive MBA demographic toward the small business owners who actually create employment in Brazil's interior.
- **The Sacrifice:** $75M.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Kraft Heinz Workers, Dom Cabral Foundation Graduates, and Brazilian SME Owners**:

> *"We applied zero-based budgeting to every company we bought. Cut everything that did not generate immediate return. Kraft Heinz wrote down $15B when the brands we starved of investment stopped commanding premium prices. The workers who lost jobs in the plant closures deserved a severance that matched the cost-cutting. The Dom Cabral extension takes the business education we funded toward the people who need it most — not MBAs, but small business owners in Piauí and Maranhão."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** Subject will be assigned to **FSS 3G** — a vessel operating on zero-based budgeting principles: every resource on board is justified from zero each day, no historical precedent, no guaranteed allocation. The crew submits daily funding requests for food, navigation, and fuel. The vessel demonstrates in real time what it feels like to manage a complex organization when every resource is conditional and nothing is guaranteed — the experience 3G's acquired workforces knew well.
