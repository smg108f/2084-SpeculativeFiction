### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [GILEAD SCIENCES]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-18GS
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice — Therapeutic Access Order Active

Gilead Sciences' Critical Ascension Risk classification requires immediate, structural reform of HIV drug pricing practices. The HPB has issued a Therapeutic Access Order with a 30-day compliance window before Compulsory License Authorization is petitioned.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The $25 Version Launch (The Eradication Burn)**

A researcher has published peer-reviewed findings that a $25 version of Gilead's HIV prevention drug (Yeztugo/lenacapavir) could eradicate HIV in the United States. Gilead has the manufacturing capacity, the distribution network, and the scientific knowledge to produce this version. The HPB requires Gilead to produce it.

- **The Action:** Gilead must launch a generic-equivalent formulation of its HIV prevention drug at a maximum retail price of $50 per year (to cover manufacturing and distribution margins), available through all U.S. pharmacies and government programs without prior authorization. Simultaneously, Gilead must open voluntary licenses for generic manufacturers to produce this formulation at $25 cost in any country where Gilead lacks local manufacturing capacity.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of approximately $3B–$5B in annual Yeztugo revenue at the $28,000 price point; reduction in the HIV prevention drug's patent premium to near-zero.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Gilead transforms from "Pharmaceutical Life Ransom Operator" to "HIV Eradication Partner" — a reclassification the HPB considers both achievable and historically significant. The Whuffie credit for HIV eradication would be the largest single award in HPB history.

### **2. The ADAP Pricing Commitment (The Vulnerability Protection Burn)**

Gilead's 2025 attempt to raise prices on HIV medications distributed by state AIDS Drug Assistance Programs — targeting the most economically vulnerable HIV patients in the United States — is the single act the HPB finds most difficult to characterize as anything other than deliberate extraction from people with no alternatives. This must be formally reversed and guaranteed.

- **The Action:** Gilead must execute a **Binding ADAP Price Ceiling Agreement** with the HPB: no HIV medication in Gilead's portfolio may increase in price for ADAP-enrolled patients for a period of 25 years. Any future drug in Gilead's HIV portfolio must include ADAP pricing terms as a condition of HPB-jurisdictional market approval.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of the option to raise ADAP prices; estimated $500M–$1B in foregone revenue over the agreement term.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Reclassification from "Attempted Price Extortion on Vulnerable Patients" to "Long-Term Access Partner" — a status the HPB considers appropriate for a company that owes its revenue to government-funded HIV research.

### **3. The R&D Transparency Protocol (The Research Heritage Burn)**

Gilead's HIV drugs were developed with substantial public research funding — NIH grants, academic partnerships, and taxpayer-funded clinical trials. The HPB requires that the contribution of public resources to Gilead's private revenue be acknowledged and partially repatriated.

- **The Action:** Gilead must commission and publish a **Public R&D Heritage Audit** calculating the proportion of its HIV drug portfolio's development cost attributable to publicly funded research. For each drug where public funding exceeded 20% of total development costs, Gilead must contribute 5% of annual revenues to a **Public Pharmaceutical Research Commons Fund**, which grants royalty-free licenses for any publicly funded research to any generic manufacturer.
- **The Sacrifice:** Estimated $300M–$600M in annual research commons contributions; acknowledgment of the public's stake in privately-held pharmaceutical IP.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Conversion from "Private Capture of Public Research" to "Research Heritage Contributor" — a status the HPB considers minimum compliance with the social contract under which pharmaceutical research operates.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 HIV-Positive ADAP Recipients and NIH Research Scientists** and recite the following:

> *"We found the drug. Taxpayers funded the research. We found the price. The price was $28,000. A researcher found the version that costs $25. We found reasons not to make it. The HPB has found reasons why we must. The $50 version launches this year. The ADAP ceiling is signed. The research commons is funded. We developed these drugs to end HIV. We are going back to that purpose. It turns out it was always compatible with running a company. We just forgot."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will petition Congress for Compulsory License Authorization for Gilead's full HIV drug portfolio. Generic manufacturers will be authorized to produce and distribute all Gilead HIV drugs at cost. The HPB will note, for the historical record, that Gilead was given 30 days to choose to provide affordable HIV medication voluntarily — and declined.
