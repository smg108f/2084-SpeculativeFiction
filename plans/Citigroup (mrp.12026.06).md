### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[CITIGROUP, INC.]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06CG
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject was clinically resurrected once, in 2008, through an act of forced public sacrifice it neither earned nor has ever fully repaid in spirit. The HPB will not authorize a second resurrection. Subject must reduce its "Divine Footprint" below the $1B threshold and permanently sever its role as a substitute for sovereign power, or be escorted into final reclassification.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The "Lazarus Ledger" Disclosure (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies Subject's continued operation as a **Resurrection Debt** — a sum owed not to shareholders but to the public that funded its survival in 2008–2009. Subject has spent eighteen years treating that bailout as a closed chapter rather than an open account.

- **The Action:** Subject must publish, in full and in plain language, the **Lazarus Ledger** — an itemized public accounting of every dollar of taxpayer guarantee, injection, and emergency facility extended to it in 2008–2009, indexed against every dollar of profit, dividend, and executive compensation generated since. The ledger must remain permanently displayed on the doors of every branch, in the manner of a debt that has not yet been forgiven.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses the comfortable fiction that 2008 is "history." The Resurrection becomes a permanent, visible condition of its existence — not a closed wound but an open one, displayed daily to the people who funded the stitches.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** By converting its survival into a publicly acknowledged debt rather than a privately celebrated triumph, Subject sheds an estimated **$40B** in "Too-Big-To-Fail Premium" — the portion of its valuation that exists only because markets assume it will always be saved again.

### **2. Severance of the Sovereign Lending Apparatus (Ideological Burn)**

Subject's deepest violation is its role as an unelected co-author of sovereign fiscal policy across more than ninety markets — a private institution writing terms that bind the citizens of nations who never voted for it.

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer its entire emerging-market sovereign debt and global depositary note operation to a **Multilateral Public Lending Commons**, governed jointly by the borrowing nations themselves rather than by Subject's shareholders.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses its single largest source of quiet structural power — the ability to set, in practice, the borrowing terms of governments too indebted to refuse them.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This neutralizes the **"Substitution for Sovereign Power" Violation** in its entirety. Subject becomes a *vendor* to nations rather than their *landlord*.

### **3. The "We Heard a Compliment" Penance (Reputational/Memetic Burn)**

Subject's internal culture has, for nearly two decades, treated the phrase "too big to fail" as a badge rather than an indictment — a verdict on its danger that it chose to hear as a tribute to its importance.

- **The Action:** Subject's senior leadership must co-author and publish, under their own names, a manifesto titled *We Heard a Compliment Where They Meant a Warning: An Apology for Misreading Our Own Resurrection*. The document must explicitly renounce the "too big to fail" framing as a doctrine and commit Subject's lobbying apparatus to actively campaigning for binding size caps on financial institutions — including itself.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses its most cherished piece of self-mythology: the belief that its scale is proof of its virtue rather than evidence of a danger that should never be allowed to recur.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This neutralizes Subject's **Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric** score and converts its most dangerous narrative — *we are too important to lose* — into its most humbling one: *we were too dangerous to keep, and we knew it, and we said so ourselves.*

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's board and CEO must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Laid-Off Mortgage Clerks, Foreclosed Homeowners, and Municipal Pension Administrators** and recite the following:

> _"We were declared too big to fail, and we mistook the warning for a crown. We did not earn our second life — it was given to us, against the better judgment of the people who paid for it, and we have spent every year since pretending otherwise. We are not too important to lose. We are only as important as the trust we have spent eighteen years failing to deserve. We give the keys back."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the "Burn" is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Subject's charter will be dissolved, its sovereign-lending book seized and redistributed to the borrowing nations themselves, and its remaining executive leadership will be permanently barred from financial institutions and reassigned — under the **Mortal Labor Reallocation Act** — to ten years of service processing small-dollar microloans at a single rural credit union in the American heartland, the better to relearn, one modest transaction at a time, what money is actually for.
