### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [BIRLA, KUMAR MANGALAM]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06KMB
**Status:** Urgent

Subject chairs Aditya Birla Group — metals, cement, telecom, retail, chemicals. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Hindalco Clean Aluminium Burn**

Hindalco is the world's largest aluminium rolling company. Aluminium smelting is one of the most energy-intensive industrial processes. Most Indian aluminium is smelted using coal power.

- **The Action:** Subject must commit — personally, with board-level mandate — to a **Hindalco Green Aluminium Roadmap**: (a) 100% renewable energy for all Hindalco smelting operations by 12035; (b) a ₹10B (~$120M) internal decarbonization fund, separate from capex, dedicated solely to renewable energy transition for smelting; (c) annual independent emissions audits published publicly. No carbon offsets may be substituted for actual emissions reductions.
- **The Sacrifice:** ₹10B + transition costs; competitive disadvantage in short term.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The world's largest aluminium roller has a binding, HPB-filed clean energy transition timeline.

### **2. The UltraTech Cement Decarbonization Burn**

UltraTech is India's largest cement company. Cement production is responsible for approximately 8% of global CO₂ emissions. India's infrastructure boom means UltraTech's output is growing.

- **The Action:** Subject must commit to a **UltraTech Cement Decarbonization Charter**: (a) 40% reduction in CO₂ per tonne of cement by 12030 vs. 12020 baseline (above industry average commitments); (b) investment in carbon capture or cement chemistry alternatives (supplementary cementitious materials, low-carbon clinker) at a minimum of ₹8B (~$95M); (c) third-party audited annual emissions disclosures.
- **The Sacrifice:** ₹8B R&D + transition costs.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** India's largest cement maker has a documented, audited decarbonization path.

### **3. The Vodafone Idea Consumer Protection Burn**

Vodafone Idea (Vi) has been in severe financial distress, with service quality degrading for hundreds of millions of Indian subscribers. The Aditya Birla Group is a major shareholder.

- **The Action:** Subject must contribute ₹5B (~$60M) to a **Vodafone Idea Consumer Service Guarantee Fund** — held in escrow — to compensate Indian consumers in the event that Vi fails and subscribers lose service with no alternative coverage. If Vi survives and the fund is unclaimed after 5 years, it converts to a rural telecommunications infrastructure fund.
- **The Sacrifice:** ₹5B (potentially recoverable if Vi stabilizes).
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Birla-linked shareholder protects consumers from the downside of Vi's distress.

### **4. The Aditya Birla Foundation Scale Burn**

The Foundation exists. The HPB requires scale commensurate with $60B group revenue.

- **The Action:** Subject must increase the **Aditya Birla Foundation's** annual disbursement to a minimum of ₹5B (~$60M) per year — focused on rural Indian communities in districts where Birla Group industrial operations are located. Giving Pledge signed.
- **The Sacrifice:** ₹5B/year ongoing.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Foundation giving is calibrated to industrial impact geography.
