### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [SIRIVADHANABHAKDI, CHAROEN]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06CS2
**Status:** Urgent

Subject controls ThaiBev, F&N, and Frasers Property. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Thailand Alcohol Harm Reduction Burn**

ThaiBev is one of Thailand's largest alcohol producers. The HPB requires the subject to fund proportionate harm reduction.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish the **ThaiBev Alcohol Harm Reduction Fund** — ฿2B (~$55M) — administered by an independent public health board (not ThaiBev-affiliated) — with mandates to fund: (a) Thai drunk-driving prevention and law enforcement; (b) domestic violence intervention programs in provinces with highest alcohol-associated incident rates; (c) addiction treatment and recovery services. ThaiBev marketing must include independently verified harm-reduction messaging — not self-authored.
- **The Sacrifice:** ฿2B + marketing constraints.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The largest contributor to Thai alcohol revenue becomes the largest funder of Thai alcohol harm reduction.

### **2. The Charoen Foundation Burn**

- **The Action:** Establish the **Charoen Sirivadhanabhakdi Foundation** — ฿3B (~$85M) — focused on Thai rural education and youth vocational training in provinces where ThaiBev sourcing (rice, sugarcane, cassava) originates. Giving Pledge signed.
- **The Sacrifice:** ฿3B.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject's agricultural supply chain communities receive direct investment.

### **3. The F&N / Frasers Affordable Housing Burn**

Frasers Property operates across Singapore, Australia, Thailand, and Europe. The HPB asks that some of that property serves housing affordability.

- **The Action:** Frasers Property must commit to a **Regional Affordable Housing Reserve**: 15% of all new residential developments in Singapore, Thailand, and Australia over the next 5 years must be designated affordable units — priced at or below 30% of local area median income for eligible buyers. Subject personally underwrites any shortfall from market pricing with a ฿1B (~$28M) Affordable Housing Development Grant.
- **The Sacrifice:** ฿1B + margin concession on 15% of new residential.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Frasers Property becomes a documented contributor to housing affordability in three countries.
