### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [POONAWALLA, CYRUS]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06CP
**Status:** Precautionary (Minimal Requirements)

Subject is classified PROBATIONARY SAINT. Serum Institute's existing public health mission substantially satisfies HPB requirements. The Bureau's primary ask is governance succession security and formal institutionalization of the public health mission. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Vaccine Access Mission Charter Burn**

Serum Institute's public health mission — affordable vaccines for developing countries — should be formally codified in the company's governance structure to be binding regardless of ownership succession.

- **The Action:** Serum Institute to file a Mission Charter committing to: (a) maintaining affordable vaccine pricing for GAVI-eligible countries as a primary business obligation, (b) reserving minimum 60% of production capacity for developing country supply, (c) independent public health advisory oversight of pricing decisions. Charter binding regardless of future ownership changes.
- **The Sacrifice:** Governance flexibility to change pricing strategy in future; potential higher-revenue alternative uses of production capacity.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts Serum Institute from a family company with excellent values into an institutionally mission-locked public health infrastructure. The HPB considers this the most important single governance action available for this subject.

### **2. The Pandemic Preparedness Endowment Burn**

COVID-19 demonstrated that Serum Institute's capacity was the world's primary pandemic vaccine manufacturing option. This capacity should be formally reserved for future pandemic response.

- **The Action:** Establish a **Serum Pandemic Preparedness Reserve** — a dedicated manufacturing capacity reserve and ₹5,000 crore (~$600M) endowment capable of rapidly scaling production of novel pathogen vaccines within 100 days of emergency declaration, in partnership with WHO and CEPI.
- **The Sacrifice:** ₹5,000 crore and some manufacturing flexibility.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the world's largest vaccine manufacturer into a formally resourced global pandemic first responder.
