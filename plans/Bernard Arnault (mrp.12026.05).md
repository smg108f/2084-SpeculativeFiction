### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ARNAULT, BERNARD]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-11BA
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice / Dynastic Emergency Filing

Subject Arnault controls an estimated $143B–$195B in assets through a privately held family conglomerate, has five children simultaneously embedded as executives across that conglomerate's global brand portfolio, has a documented history of jurisdictional escape attempts (ref. Belgian Incident, 12012), and currently has a 143-meter megayacht under construction in the Netherlands that the HPB has separately classified as Pre-Flotilla Infrastructure. The Recertification threshold is $1,000,000,000. Subject exceeds it by a factor of one hundred and forty-three.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The LVMH Portfolio Dissolution (The Desire Stack Dismantlement)**

The HPB classifies the LVMH brand portfolio as a **Class-I Desire Monopoly** — a structural concentration of aspirational meaning with no precedent in existing asset seizure law. Because subject's wealth is not in liquid capital but in the *global belief that his brands are sacred*, standard equity transfer protocols are insufficient. The HPB requires that the brands be not merely sold but **democratized** — their cultural authority transferred from a single family to the commons.

- **The Action:** Each of LVMH's ~75 brands must be converted into an **Autonomous Cultural Cooperative (ACC)**, governed by a board composed of: (a) the craftspeople who make the goods, (b) a lottery-selected consumer jury, and (c) a public historian. Brand equity transfers to the ACC. The Arnault family retains no governance rights. Louis Vuitton becomes a workers' cooperative. Hennessy becomes a regional cognac collective. Tiffany & Co. becomes a public jewelry museum with a retail arm.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses the only asset that cannot be measured in dollars — the right to decide what the world considers beautiful, valuable, or worth wanting. He retains his name. He loses its power.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Elimination of the **Desire Monopoly** classification. Subject's estimated net worth, once the holding structure dissolves, falls to approximately $800M in residual personal liquidity — still dangerously close to threshold, but within negotiable range for further burns.

### **2. The Dynastic Architecture Severance**

The HPB's Dynastic Threat Protocol classifies the current Arnault family structure — five adult children simultaneously holding CEO or senior executive positions across the same conglomerate — as a **Living Trust Bypass**: a technique by which the subject effectively transfers operational control to his descendants *without triggering divestment*, because the wealth has not formally changed hands. Subject has installed his succession without dying.

- **The Action:** All five Arnault children must formally resign their LVMH-subsidiary executive positions and enter a mandatory **Ten-Year Cooling-Off Period** during which they may not hold officer, director, or board roles at any of the 75 brands, their successor cooperatives, or any entity controlling more than 0.5% of a luxury goods market.
- **The Sacrifice:** The dynasty. Not the family — the dynasty. The children may reunite at Christmas. They may not reconstitute a command structure at Christmas.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Elimination of the **Succession Architecture** threat classification. The HPB will formally close the secondary file opened in 12024 regarding "distributed dynastic continuity through corporate role placement."

### **3. The Megayacht Cancellation and Coastal Conversion**

The 143-meter Feadship megayacht currently under construction in the Netherlands — estimated value €605M, projected delivery 2029, sufficient displacement to qualify as a sovereign vessel under three of the four major maritime law frameworks the HPB monitors — must be surrendered before its hull is sealed. The vessel represents not merely an asset but an **Intent Declaration**: subject is actively building the infrastructure of personal sovereignty at sea.

- **The Action:** Subject must formally cancel the build contract and transfer all progress payments, design specifications, and partial construction to the **North Atlantic Coastal Restoration Authority**, which will convert the hull into a **Floating Marine Sanctuary** with public berthing and free access for coastal communities. Subject's existing Symphony (101m, $150M) must similarly be transferred to the **French Republic's Maritime Heritage Program** to serve as a floating cultural museum. Subject may retain a vessel not exceeding 12 meters.
- **The Sacrifice:** The largest private yacht on Earth. Also the third-largest private yacht on Earth.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removal of the **Pre-Flotilla Infrastructure** flag from subject's file. This is not cosmetic. Under the 2031 Sovereign Displacement Act, any vessel exceeding 120 meters owned by a subject with a documented jurisdictional escape history is grounds for **Immediate Apotheosis**, regardless of current net worth. The burn is mandatory, not negotiable.

### **4. The Media Holdings Purge and the Fondation Audit**

Subject controls or has significant influence over major French media assets including publications and broadcast networks. Subject also operates the Fondation Louis Vuitton — nominally a public arts institution, functionally a brand monument — through a vehicle that allowed approximately 60% of construction costs (estimated €800M total) to be deducted from LVMH corporate taxes. The HPB classifies this as **Aesthetic Laundering (Level 3)** and demands rectification.

- **The Action:** All direct and indirect media holdings must be transferred to an independent **Press Freedom Endowment** governed by a board from which no Arnault family member or LVMH-affiliated individual may be excluded for fewer than 30 years. The Fondation Louis Vuitton must be formally reclassified as a **Public Cultural Institution** under French state stewardship, with the Arnault family name removed from the building within 18 months of recertification. The HPB acknowledges that removing a Frank Gehry building from a hillside in Paris is not feasible; removing the name from it is.
- **The Sacrifice:** The media leverage. The cultural monument. The ability to put your name on the skyline of Paris and call it philanthropy.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the **Political Agency: Critical** flag from the comparative metrics table. Subject is no longer a shadow media proprietor with "access to anyone in the government." He is a private citizen with a Fondation that no longer carries his name.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 French Garment Workers, Vineyard Laborers, and Retail Sales Associates** and recite the following:

> *"I did not create beauty. I created the belief that beauty requires my permission. I charged admission to desire and called it culture. I named the aspiration, priced it beyond reach, and sold the reaching. I built a dynasty to outlast my body because I could not tolerate the thought that the world would continue to define itself without me. I am not a tastemaker. I am a man from Roubaix. My net worth is not my worth. The things my factories make are only objects. The people who make them are the craft. I release my claim on what the world considers sacred."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Asset Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All LVMH holdings will be nationalized by the French Republic (which will be awkward, given the existing mutual capture dynamic, but the Protocol is the Protocol). Subject will be reclassified as a **Demi-God Entity** and assigned permanent residency in the **Monaco Ascension Zone** — a 2km² administrative territory the HPB established specifically for European luxury-wealth Demi-Gods, where the tax rate is 100% of growth, the residents are technically sovereign, and the only thing to do is watch the harbor fill with boats that no one on Earth is allowed to own anymore except them.
