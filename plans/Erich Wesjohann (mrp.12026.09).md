### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[WESJOHANN, ERICH]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09EW
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

The Genetic Chokepoint finding requires structural resilience measures, not simple asset transfer — the underlying concern is food-system fragility, not personal enrichment alone.

---

### **1. The Genetic Diversity Mandate (The Largest Burn)**

- **The Action:** Open-license a portion of EW Group's proprietary breeding-line genetics to an independent, publicly-accessible poultry genetics repository, reducing single-company control of global breeding diversity.
- **The Sacrifice:** Surrenders exclusive commercial control of decades of proprietary breeding research.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Directly reduces the "Genetic Chokepoint" hazard.

### **2. The Supply Resilience Fund**
 
- **The Action:** Commit $500M to establishing redundant, geographically-distributed breeding stock reserves outside EW Group's direct control, as an insurance policy against disease outbreak.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Builds structural resilience the current concentration lacks.

### **3. The Transparency Mandate**

- **The Action:** Publish annual, independently-audited reporting on global market share, pricing influence, and biosecurity practices.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts an invisible chokepoint into a monitored one.
