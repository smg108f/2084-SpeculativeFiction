### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [WU, YAJUN]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06WYJ
**Status:** Standard

Subject's stewardship of Longfor through the Chinese property crisis is credited. Burns are accordingly modest and targeted.

---

### **1. The Affordable Rental Housing Burn**

Longfor has built primarily for homeowners at market prices. Its rental apartment business is growing but still market-rate. The HPB asks for affordable rental investment.

- **The Action:** Subject must commit $150M to the **Longfor Affordable Rental Initiative** — establishing a ring-fenced portfolio of below-market-rate rental apartments in Chongqing, Chengdu, Xi'an, and other Western China cities where Longfor operates, targeting young workers and migrant families who cannot access homeownership. Minimum 5,000 units. Below-market pricing independently verified. Administered separately from Longfor's commercial operations with a community governance board.
- **The Sacrifice:** $150M.

### **2. The Sector Accountability Burn**

Longfor maintained discipline when peers did not. The HPB credits this and asks that this discipline be shared — through advocacy and public knowledge.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish the **Longfor Developer Governance Institute** — $50M — a think tank and training organization that publishes Longfor's financial discipline and governance model openly, provides training to smaller Chinese property developers on sustainable balance sheet management, and advocates for regulatory reforms that prevent the over-leveraging that caused the Evergrande crisis from recurring.
- **The Sacrifice:** $50M.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Longfor Rental Tenants, Chongqing Migrant Workers, and Chinese Property Sector Analysts**:

> *"I started Longfor in Chongqing. We built for people who wanted to own something — a home, a future. When the rest of the sector borrowed beyond what it could pay back, we didn't.
