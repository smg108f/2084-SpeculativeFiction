### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [GATES FOUNDATION]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-02GF
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice / Institutional Governance Reform Order

The Gates Foundation has been classified PROBATIONARY SAINT. This status requires active compliance with governance reform conditions to prevent reclassification as a **Perpetual Shadow Ministry** — an institutional designation that triggers automatic HPB receivership. Recertification targets the Foundation's structural permanence, not its grant programs, which the HPB acknowledges as legitimate and beneficial.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Sunset Clause Burn (The Permanence Reckoning)**

The HPB's Institutional Mortality Doctrine requires that any private institution operating at Shadow Ministry scale must have a documented lifespan. The Gates Foundation has no mandatory dissolution date. It is, in the HPB's terminology, **structurally immortal** — a private choice-making entity with no death on the calendar.

- **The Action:** The Foundation's governing documents must be amended to include a mandatory **50-Year Sunset Clause**, under which the Foundation must either (a) transfer all remaining endowment assets to a jointly-governed public trust with democratic oversight, or (b) submit to HPB reclassification and receivership at the end of the period. The clock begins at recertification.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Foundation concedes that it cannot outlast the democratic accountability question indefinitely. Its permanence becomes conditional.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The institution acquires an expiration date. It becomes, in HPB terms, **a very large mortal organization** rather than a perpetual one.

---

### **2. The Governance Democratization Burn**

The Foundation's board is small, private, and self-selecting — appropriate for a family foundation, insufficient for an institution that functions as the global health ministry of 40+ nations. The HPB classifies single-board control at this scale as a **Democratic Deficit Class II**.

- **The Action:** The Foundation must establish a **Global Health Accountability Council** — a 24-member elected body representing grantee nations, patient advocacy groups, and civil society organizations from the countries where Foundation programs operate. The Council holds veto power over grant allocations above $500M and annual strategic priorities.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Gates Foundation's board surrenders unilateral control over its largest programmatic decisions. The preferences of the countries receiving aid become structurally audible.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Foundation transitions from a **Philanthropic Sovereign** to a **Collaborative Distributor**. Shadow Ministry classification downgraded.

---

### **3. The Dependency Audit and Transition Fund Burn**

The HPB has documented that at least 40 national health systems have become structurally dependent on Gates Foundation programming following the USAID defunding of 12025. This dependency was not designed maliciously. It is nonetheless dangerous. The Foundation must now address it.

- **The Action:** Commission an independent **Structural Dependency Audit** of all active grant programs. For any country where Gates Foundation funding constitutes more than 20% of public health infrastructure, the Foundation must establish a **5-year Transition Fund** with matched contributions from the recipient country's government and HPB-designated multilateral partners, designed to eliminate single-source dependency.
- **The Sacrifice:** Approximately $3–5B in endowment capital committed to transition funding over the next decade. The Foundation's flexibility decreases.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Global health infrastructure becomes less vulnerable to the Foundation's choices — and to the Foundation's eventual end.

---

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, the Foundation's Board of Trustees must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Community Health Workers from Gates-Funded Programs in Sub-Saharan Africa** and recite the following:

> *"We are not the solution. We are the bridge. We acknowledge that our choices — which diseases we fund, which crops we prioritize, which schools receive our software — are political choices made without political accountability. We accept the Sunset. We accept the Council. We accept that the world we are trying to help must eventually govern itself without us."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If governance reforms are not initiated within 30 days, the HPB will reclassify the Gates Foundation as a **Perpetual Shadow Ministry** and place its endowment under a jointly-administered public trust governed by the UN Development Programme, the African Union Commission, and the HPB's Civil Society Division. The name will be retired. The grants will continue, better governed.
