### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [MONDELEZ]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-08MD
**Status:** Precautionary — Child Marketing Prohibition and Supply Chain Audit Orders Active

Mondelēz's Marginal Ascension Risk classification is driven by neurological marketing practices targeting children and undisclosed labor violations in the cocoa supply chain. Recertification requires structural reform of both, not merely enhanced disclosure.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Child Marketing Prohibition (The Neurological Targeting Burn)**

The HPB has classified Mondelēz's advertising of ultra-processed products to children under 14 as a **Class-II Memetic Hazard**: the use of cartoon characters, playful narratives, and behavioral targeting algorithms to create brand associations in pre-decisional minds. Children cannot consent to neurological brand conditioning. The marketing must stop.

- **The Action:** All advertising of Oreo, Chips Ahoy!, Cadbury, and equivalent ultra-processed products in media consumed primarily by under-14 audiences must cease immediately. This includes digital platforms with algorithmic age-targeting capabilities. A $200M fund must be established to support independent nutrition education programs in elementary schools across Mondelēz's 150 operating markets.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of estimated $2B–$4B in brand-building investment effectiveness as the under-14 acquisition pipeline closes; reduced long-term brand loyalty curves.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Conversion from "Neurological Extraction Operator" to "Adult Consumer Brand" — a classification that is both more honest and substantially less alarming to the HPB.

### **2. The Cocoa Supply Chain Audit (The Labor Extraction Penance)**

Mondelēz's West African cocoa supply chain — which feeds Cadbury Dairy Milk, Milka, and Toblerone — has been documented to include child agricultural labor. The HPB classifies this as a **Supply Chain Labor Violation** whose relationship to the brand's premium positioning is particularly incoherent: charging consumers premium prices for chocolate produced by child labor is a business model that requires both premium buyers and exploited children to function simultaneously.

- **The Action:** Mondelēz must commission and publicly release an independent child labor audit of its full Tier 1 and Tier 2 cocoa supply chain within 90 days. Any supplier found using child labor must be placed on a remediation plan with binding timelines; any supplier refusing remediation must be terminated and replaced. A $150M **Cocoa Community Investment Fund** must be established to support school attendance, adult farmer income support, and alternative livelihood programs in affected communities.
- **The Sacrifice:** Estimated $500M–$1B in supply chain restructuring costs; potential short-term cocoa sourcing disruptions; premium brand narrative requires reconciling with premium production standards.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Reclassification from "Supply Chain Labor Violator" to "Active Remediation Partner" — a status that earns Whuffie credit proportional to the speed and completeness of the remediation.

### **3. The Ultra-Processing Transparency Protocol (The Ingredient Honesty Burn)**

Mondelēz's products are engineered at molecular level to maximize consumption. The HPB considers this a form of behavioral influence that consumers have the right to understand. Current nutritional labeling does not convey the behavioral engineering embedded in the product design.

- **The Action:** All Mondelēz products sold in HPB-jurisdictional markets must carry a **Behavioral Engineering Disclosure**: a standardized label indicating the product has been formulated to encourage repeated consumption, with a plain-language explanation of the specific mechanisms (salt, sugar, fat ratios; texture engineering; flavor layering). This is not a health warning; it is a consent tool.
- **The Sacrifice:** Brand desirability impact estimated at 5–15% volume reduction as consumers encounter honest framing; reputational cost of being the first major food company to acknowledge product engineering publicly.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The HPB will issue a **Consumer Transparency Commendation** — a designation no food company currently holds and which the HPB considers sufficient Whuffie to achieve Probationary Saint status if the child marketing and cocoa audits are also completed.
