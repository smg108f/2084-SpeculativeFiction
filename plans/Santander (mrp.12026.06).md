### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [BANCO SANTANDER SA]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-35SAN
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice

Banco Santander's Dynastic Continental Banking Architecture classification — Botín family effective control through a 2% stake, university funding networks, and post-colonial Latin American expansion — requires recertification addressing the structural family influence and the geographic concentration in formerly colonized markets.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Botín Family Governance Separation (The Dynasty Burn)**

The HPB classifies Ana Botín's effective control of a €1.7T bank through a 2% personal stake — supplemented by institutional loyalty culture, foundation relationships, and the Botín family's four-generation institutional grip — as a **Cultural Ownership Without Proportional Economic Stake**: the family controls the institution without owning a proportionate share of it.

- **The Action:** The Botín family must formally accept a governance structure in which the executive chairman is an independent appointment subject to shareholder vote, with no preference given to Botín family candidates. The 2% economic stake confers 2% influence, no more.
- **The Sacrifice:** Ana Botín's chairmanship is subject to shareholder ratification without family preference; future family succession is not guaranteed.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Cultural Ownership classification is resolved.

### **2. The Latin American Rate Transparency Mandate (The Post-Colonial Burn)**

The HPB classifies Santander's interest rate differential across markets — charging significantly higher rates in Latin American markets than in Spain or the UK for equivalent credit products — as a **Post-Colonial Financial Extraction**: applying different pricing standards to markets that have less regulatory protection and lower political leverage.

- **The Action:** Santander must publish annual cross-market pricing reports comparing equivalent credit products across all operating countries. In any market where rates exceed twice the Spanish equivalent rate for equivalent risk products, Santander must justify the differential to the HPB within 90 days or reduce rates to the justified level.
- **The Sacrifice:** Estimated €500M-€1B annual margin reduction in Latin American consumer lending.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Post-Colonial Financial Architecture flag is resolved.

### **3. The Fundación Botín Independence Mandate (The Patronage Burn)**

The HPB classifies the Fundación Botín's funding relationships with Spanish universities and cultural institutions as a **Dynastic Soft Power Network**: the family funds the institutions that produce the professionals who view the Botín name with cultural gratitude rather than regulatory scrutiny.

- **The Action:** The Fundación Botín must be reorganized with a fully independent board of trustees elected by an open competitive process, with no Botín family members on the governance board for a minimum of 10 years. The family may retain the endowment; they may not govern its distribution.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Botín family loses the philanthropic network's political and cultural protection function.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Dynastic Soft Power Network classification is resolved.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Ana Botín must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Santander retail customers from Brazil and Spain** and recite the following:

> _"My family has run this bank since my great-grandfather's time. I chair it on the strength of a 2% stake and a century of institutional culture. We expanded into Latin America during privatizations that restructured those countries for capital markets rather than their citizens. We charge more there because we can. We fund universities because it is good, and because it protects us. I accept the independent chairmanship process for my successors. I accept the rate comparison. I accept the independent foundation. The Botín family's contribution to this institution is its history. Its future belongs to its shareholders and its customers equally."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. The Bank of Spain will be requested to administer a compulsory governance review under its systemically important bank powers. Santander's Latin American subsidiaries will be referred to the respective national banking regulators for independent rate reviews. The Fundación Botín will be referred to Spain's cultural heritage authority for assessment as a public cultural trust. The Bureau notes that this outcome would, in practical terms, transfer the Botín family's institutional legacy from their control to their country's. They may find this acceptable. They may not.
