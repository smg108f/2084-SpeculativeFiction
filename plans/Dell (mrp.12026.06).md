### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [DELL TECHNOLOGIES INC]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-17DELL
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice

Dell Technologies' classification reflects the convergence of Michael Dell's 46% voting control over an AI infrastructure provider with an emerging role supplying server hardware to government and institutional AI systems. Recertification targets the governance concentration and the governance AI hardware dependency simultaneously.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Voting Control Dissolution (The Governance Burn)**

The HPB classifies Michael Dell's 46% voting control over Dell Technologies as a **Founding Deity Retention**: a governance structure designed to ensure that a public company remains personally controlled by its founder indefinitely.

- **The Action:** Michael Dell must reduce his voting stake to below 15% within 36 months through a structured share conversion program. No new dual-class shares may be issued. The Michael & Susan Dell Foundation's Dell stock holdings may not substitute for personal voting power.
- **The Sacrifice:** Michael Dell loses effective control over Dell Technologies.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Dell Technologies becomes a normally governed public company. Michael Dell's individual HPB file is updated with a Reduced Control Indicator.

### **2. The Government AI Hardware Transparency Mandate (The Influence Burn)**

The HPB classifies Dell's position as a primary AI server supplier to government and institutional AI systems as an **Unaudited Infrastructure Dependency** — the government is buying the servers that run the AI that governs citizens from a company controlled by one person.

- **The Action:** All Dell government AI infrastructure contracts must be subject to public tender with full technical specification disclosure. Dell must accept HPB oversight representatives on all federal AI infrastructure projects. Contracts exceeding $500M must include source code escrow provisions.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of preferred vendor status with some government clients; increased contract competition.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Physical Substrate for Governing AI flag is resolved.

### **3. The JUUL-Adjacent Governance Reform (The Accountability Burn)**

The HPB notes that Michael Dell's investment record includes a $12.8B JUUL investment that was largely written off — representing a governance failure at scale with downstream implications for youth nicotine addiction. The Bureau requires prospective governance standards.

- **The Action:** Dell must adopt binding ESG investment screening for all corporate capital allocation above $100M, with independent board approval required for investments in addiction-adjacent industries. Michael Dell personally must sign a Conflict of Interest certification reviewed annually by the HPB.
- **The Sacrifice:** Limited corporate investment flexibility.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Dell exits the Governance Failure Risk register.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Michael Dell and the board must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Dell Technologies factory workers and federal government IT procurement officers** and recite the following:

> _"I built this company, took it private, brought it back, and retained control because I could. I supplied AI servers to governments without disclosing the governance risk. I accept that the machines governments use to make decisions about citizens should not be purchased from a company a single person controls. I release the votes. The infrastructure is public.
