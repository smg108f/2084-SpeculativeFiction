### **1. The Water Rights Divestiture (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies private water-banking rights as a "Captured Commons Asset."

- **The Action:** Transfer a controlling majority of Kern Water Bank and associated water-banking rights to a public California water authority, retaining only agricultural-use allocation at standard public rates.
- **The Sacrifice:** Surrenders the single most valuable and controversial asset underlying the fortune.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Ends private control of a public commons resource.

### **2. The Orchard Transition Fund**

- **The Action:** Commit $800M to transitioning a portion of pistachio/almond acreage to lower-water-intensity crops, with results published annually.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Directly reduces the water-intensity footprint driving the concentration hazard.

### **3. The Water Policy Lobbying Freeze**

- **The Action:** A 10-year moratorium on all direct and indirect lobbying related to California water rights and allocation policy.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the Critical-tier Political Agency flag tied to water policy influence.
