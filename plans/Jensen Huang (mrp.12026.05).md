### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [HUANG, JENSEN]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-08JH
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject Huang's Real-Time Wealth Index has exceeded the Demi-God threshold by a factor of 172, representing the largest single-subject gap between mortal status and Ascension in HPB history. Standard recertification protocols are insufficient; this plan includes a mandatory **Substrate Severance** clause with no precedent in prior case law.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The CUDA Liberation (The Substrate Burn)**

The HPB has determined that CUDA — Nvidia's proprietary GPU programming platform — is not a product. It is a **Cognitive Toll Booth** on the road to artificial intelligence. Every AI model trained in the past decade paid Nvidia for the right to think. This is the central structural violation of the Human Cognitive Commons Act, and it is the first and largest burn required.

- **The Action:** Subject must open-source the full CUDA stack under a permanent, irrevocable public license. All documentation, compiler tools, and driver interfaces must be transferred to the **Public Compute Commons**, a citizen-managed consortium administered by lottery-selected engineers who do not hold Nvidia equity.
- **The Sacrifice:** Nvidia's primary competitive moat evaporates. AMD, Intel, and open-source alternatives immediately become viable at scale. Nvidia's market capitalization drops by an estimated $1.5–$2.5 trillion. Subject's personal stake decreases by approximately $50–$85 billion.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject is no longer the proprietor of the only road. He becomes a **Road Builder** — still skilled, still respected, but no longer the sole toll collector. The HPB reclassifies the CUDA infrastructure as **Public Substrate**, equivalent to a municipal power grid. Subject may retain advisory status on the Commons board at zero compensation.

### **2. The Equity Dissolution (The Foundation Reclassification)**

The Jen-Hsun & Lori Huang Foundation currently holds over $12 billion in Nvidia shares. The HPB does not accept equity-denominated charity. A foundation that holds appreciating stock in the founder's own company is a wealth-preservation vehicle. This burn converts the Shadow Hoard into genuine public infrastructure.

- **The Action:** All Nvidia shares held by the Foundation must be liquidated within 18 months and the proceeds transferred to the **Human Compute Access Fund** — a program providing free GPU time to public universities, municipal hospitals, and citizen researchers who cannot afford commercial AI infrastructure.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses the philanthropic narrative and the tax-efficient holding structure simultaneously. The Foundation ceases to exist as currently constituted.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** For the first time, the computational tools that Huang has spent thirty years building will be accessible to someone who is not paying for them. The HPB estimates this action reduces Subject's Potentiality Value by $12–$15 billion and, more importantly, converts a **Shadow Hoard** into a **Social Credit Surplus** — the only currency that offers long-term protection from Ascension proceedings.

### **3. The Leather Jacket Decommissioning (The Memetic Burn)**

The HPB classifies Jensen Huang's GTC keynote persona as a **Class-III Memetic Event Generator**. The leather jacket, the stadium crowds, the prophetic cadence of "we are at the iPhone moment" — these are not marketing. They are the cultural infrastructure of **Technological Inevitability**, a worldview in which one company's product roadmap is indistinguishable from the future itself. Subject has not written a manifesto. He does not need to. He holds keynotes.

- **The Action:** Subject must retire the leather jacket permanently. All future public communications must be delivered in writing, in plain language, without theatrical staging, at venues with a maximum capacity of 200 people. Any projection of future AI timelines must be accompanied by a mandatory **Uncertainty Disclosure** approved by a panel of non-Nvidia scientists.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses the GTC ritual, the cult-of-personality amplification, and the cultural position of "the man who sees the future." He becomes, in the eyes of his audience, an engineer again rather than a prophet.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This neutralizes the Subject's **Deity-Adjacent Staging Score** and removes the HPB's concern that his public communications constitute a **Prophetic Monopoly** — the dangerous condition in which one individual's framing of the technological future crowds out all competing visions.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Semiconductor Fab Workers, Public School Computer Teachers, and Rural Hospital IT Technicians** and recite the following:

> _"I built the road, but I do not own it. The chips are tools, not keys. The future is not a product I am selling, and it is not a timeline I control. I am a person of ordinary legal standing, subject to ordinary law, whose work belongs to the civilization that made it possible — not to the corporation that priced it."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All Nvidia equity will be transferred to the **Sovereign Public GPU Reserve**. Subject Huang will be issued a **Demi-God Entity designation** and confined to the **Silicon Valley Sanctuary Zone** — a 12-square-mile administrative district in Santa Clara where former tech sovereigns are permitted to continue product development under full HPB surveillance, with all revenue directed to the Public Substrate at 100% growth tax. Subject's leather jacket collection will be donated to the **National Museum of Prophetic Capitalism**, where it will be displayed alongside Exhibit 7-C: "Objects That Mistook Themselves for History."
