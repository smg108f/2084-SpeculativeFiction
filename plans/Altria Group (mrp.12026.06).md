### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ALTRIA GROUP INC]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-36MO
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Altria Group's Confidence Score of 95% reflects one of the clearest Ascension cases in the HPB's active portfolio: a corporation found guilty of racketeering for lying about a product that kills 480,000 Americans annually, which has since grown its market capitalization on the strength of its addicted customer base. Recertification requires structural dismantlement of the Addiction Infrastructure, not merely behavioral modification.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Marlboro Addiction Architecture Dissolution (The Core Product Burn)**

The HPB classifies Marlboro's brand architecture — 60 years of marketing designed to create and sustain nicotine addiction among new generations of smokers — as a **Compounding Addiction Infrastructure**: investments in brand equity that increase in value as each newly addicted customer joins the pool.

- **The Action:** Altria must cease all consumer-facing brand marketing for Marlboro and all cigarette brands — no advertising, no sponsorship, no in-store promotional displays beyond FDA-required packaging. Packaging must be plain with health warnings covering 75% of the surface area. The Marlboro brand name may appear on packaging only.
- **The Sacrifice:** Estimated $2-3B reduction in brand-maintenance spending converted to zero; potential market share erosion.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Addiction Infrastructure Compounding flag is resolved.

### **2. The Racketeering Restitution Fund (The Justice Burn)**

The HPB classifies the 2006 racketeering finding — that Altria intentionally deceived the American public about tobacco health risks — as a **Societal Harm of Documented Scale** for which no individual was personally required to answer and no restitution was provided to affected individuals.

- **The Action:** Altria must establish a $10B **Tobacco Harm Restitution Trust** funded from retained earnings and future cash flows over 10 years. The trust is administered by an independent medical board distributing funds to verified tobacco-caused disease patients and their families, prioritizing applicants who began smoking before 2006 (the conviction date).
- **The Sacrifice:** $10B extracted from what would otherwise be dividends; annual cash flow reduction of approximately $1B.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Racketeering Conviction Dividend classification is resolved.

### **3. The JUUL Accountability Completion (The Youth Targeting Burn)**

The HPB classifies Altria's $12.8B investment in JUUL Labs — a company the FDA subsequently found had marketed e-cigarettes to minors — as an **Addiction Infrastructure Extension Attempt**: trying to replicate tobacco's generational addiction cycle in a new delivery device targeting a new generation.

- **The Action:** Altria must contribute $3B to a **Youth Nicotine Prevention and Treatment Fund** administered by the FDA and the American Academy of Pediatrics, specifically targeting communities where JUUL achieved highest penetration among minors. Altria's personnel involved in the JUUL investment decision must cooperate fully with any pending DOJ investigation.
- **The Sacrifice:** $3B additional liability; executive legal exposure.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Youth Addiction Extension Attempt classification is resolved.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Altria's board must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 people currently receiving cancer treatment for tobacco-caused illness and 12 families who have lost a member to tobacco-caused disease** — 24 jurors total, because the Bureau considers this case to warrant a doubled jury — and recite the following:

> _"We were found guilty of racketeering for telling people our product was safe when we knew it was not. We paid a fine. Our stock went up. We then invested $12.8 billion in a company that marketed nicotine to children and called it innovation. We accept the restitution fund. We accept the JUUL fund. We accept the marketing restriction. We acknowledge that 480,000 Americans die each year from our products, and that our market capitalization increasing while this continues is not a coincidence — it is a business model. We intend to continue that business model within the constraints of this recertification, which is the most the Bureau is currently empowered to require."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. The Marlboro brand will be seized and placed in receivership, with all future cigarette packaging sold under the **Federal Tobacco Harm Registry** label. Altria's cash flows will be directed to the Tobacco Harm Restitution Trust under direct federal administration. Former shareholders will receive **Public Health Contribution Credits** providing priority access to tobacco cessation programs. The Bureau notes this is the only case in its portfolio where the Apotheosis Protocol is arguably designed primarily for the benefit of the subject entity's own customers.
