### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [GENERAL DYNAMICS CORPORATION]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-30GD
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice

General Dynamics' classification as a Private Manufacturer of Public Force — with CEO Phebe Novakovic's biography spanning CIA, Office of the Secretary of Defense, and Gulfstream-to-submarine corporate leadership — requires recertification addressing the revolving door architecture and the structural privatization of sovereign warfare capability.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Revolving Door Governance Mandate (The Accountability Burn)**

The HPB classifies the executive appointment pathway from national security agencies to defense contractors as a **Institutional Self-Reproduction Circuit** — public officials who build relationships and knowledge in government service monetize them in private defense contracting.

- **The Action:** General Dynamics must accept a binding governance requirement: no new executive or board appointments may come from individuals who held positions at the DOD, CIA, or NSA within the past 10 years (extended from the existing 2-year cooling-off period). Existing appointments remain for their current terms; successors must comply.
- **The Sacrifice:** Significant restriction on the talent pipeline that has defined GD's leadership culture.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Revolving Door Principal classification for new appointments is resolved.

### **2. The Arms Export Accountability Mandate (The Weapons Burn)**

The HPB classifies General Dynamics' February 2025 $2.05B arms sale to Israel as a transaction that generated significant public protest and raised accountability questions about the end use of weapon systems provided through U.S. export approvals.

- **The Action:** For all future arms export contracts, GD must publish annual End Use Monitoring Reports for each transaction, documenting documented civilian casualty incidents involving GD-supplied systems, independently verified. Reports must be provided to the HPB and published publicly.
- **The Sacrifice:** Transparency about weapon system use that was previously available only through classified channels.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Sovereign Warfare Opacity flag is resolved.

### **3. The Nuclear Submarine Oversight Structure (The Sovereignty Burn)**

The HPB classifies GD's Electric Boat division — the sole manufacturer of nuclear submarines for the U.S. Navy — as a **Private Custodian of Nuclear Deterrence Infrastructure**, an arrangement where the operational continuity of American nuclear deterrence depends on the private management decisions of a single corporation.

- **The Action:** Electric Boat must accept a **Dual-Governance Structure**: an HPB-appointed board of directors with equal authority to GD's corporate board over all strategic decisions affecting the submarine program (timeline, technology, workforce). Electric Boat's nuclear-classified operations must be audited jointly by HPB and the Navy IG.
- **The Sacrifice:** GD loses sole operational control over its most strategically sensitive division.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Private Manufacturer of Public Force flag is replaced with Supervised Defense Infrastructure.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, CEO Phebe Novakovic — whose career trajectory from CIA to OSD to GD the HPB regards as the clearest single biography of the revolving door — must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 submarine shipyard workers and Palestinian civilians documented in GD weapons end-use reports** and recite the following:

> _"I came from government intelligence and moved to a company that sells weapons to the government I came from. I built relationships in service and monetized them in commerce. I sold arms into conflicts where civilians died. I accept the governance overlay. I accept the transparency reports. I accept that the machines of sovereign power should not be manufactured by entities that answer only to shareholders."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Electric Boat will be transferred to direct Navy ownership and operated as a federal shipyard. Gulfstream will be divested to a civilian aerospace consortium. General Dynamics' remaining assets will be assessed individually for defense utility designation. The Bureau notes that defending national security by nationalizing one of its primary contractors is a paradox. It has prepared for this paradox.
