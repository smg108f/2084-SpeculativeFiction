### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [PETERFFY, THOMAS]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-21TP
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject Peterffy's Real-Time Wealth Index has exceeded the CB-class floor ($100,000,000,000) with no active divestment record and no Saintly Pivot on file. His controlling stake in Interactive Brokers Group constitutes a single-asset sovereignty risk that the HPB classifies as a **Market Infrastructure Hostage Situation** — the subject's legal status and a major retail trading platform are currently the same balance sheet entry.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The IBKR Decoupling (The Controlling Stake Dissolution)**

The HPB classifies Peterffy's ~75% stake in IBG LLC as a **Category-I Systemic Asset** — wealth so structurally embedded in a public-facing market platform that its continued private ownership constitutes a threat to the HPB's mandate independent of the dollar figure. Interactive Brokers processes millions of retail trades daily. The subject owning the majority of that infrastructure is the 2084 equivalent of a private citizen owning the post office.

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer controlling interest (minimum 74% of IBG LLC stake) to a **Public Markets Trust**, governed by a citizen board selected by lottery from the IBKR retail customer base — the same ordinary investors Peterffy originally built the platform to serve.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject relinquishes all voting rights, dividend income, and governance authority over Interactive Brokers. He may retain a symbolic 1% non-voting stake as a **Founder's Memorial Share** — a classification that carries no income and no power, only a plaque.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** RTWI drops from ~$100B to an estimated $700M–$800M. Subject crosses below the $1B threshold. CB-class monitoring terminates. The platform is reclassified as a **Public Market Utility**, joining the 2084 roster of citizen-owned financial infrastructure.

---

### **2. The Palm Beach Property Pivot (The Fortress Liquidation)**

Subject's Palm Beach residence is classified by the HPB as a **Sovereign Enclave Risk** — a high-security private estate in a Florida jurisdiction historically associated with wealth-class clustering and HPB evasion. The bureau notes that Palm Beach has the highest per-capita concentration of Apotheosis-adjacent subjects in the continental United States.

- **The Action:** Transfer the Palm Beach residence to Palm Beach County to be operated as a **New American Welcome Center** — a public resource for newly arrived immigrants with no English, no savings, and no network. The center's mission statement will reference Peterffy's own 1965 arrival in New York with no English and $100 in cash.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject vacates the primary residence. Per the Life-Tenancy Loophole, he may retain occupancy of a **500 sq. ft. guest cottage** on the property, reclassified as a Caretaker Unit.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Real estate holdings removed from RTWI. Subject's **Saintly Pivot Score** increases from NEGLIGIBLE to PROVISIONAL — the first recorded entry. The HPB notes the particular fitness of this conversion: a man who arrived with nothing builds a shelter for people arriving with nothing.

---

### **3. The 2012 Ad Inversion (The Ideological Penance)**

The HPB's Memetic Compliance Division has reviewed Peterffy's 2012 self-funded television advertisement, in which Subject directly addressed the American public to warn that electing Democrats would put the country "on the road to socialism." The ad is classified as **Class-III Retrograde Ideology** — not because its content violated any law at the time of broadcast, but because its explicit argument (that wealth redistribution is a civilizational threat) is precisely the argument the Demi-God Act of 2034 was designed to answer. Subject spent personal wealth to argue against the regulatory framework that now governs him. The bureau requires a rebuttal.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund and personally appear in a new public broadcast — equivalent airtime and budget to the 2012 ad — titled **"I Was Wrong About the Threshold."** The ad will not require Subject to recant his biography or his beliefs about Communism. It will require him to acknowledge, on camera, that a man with $100 billion and a man with $100 are not political equals, and that the HPB's CB-class monitoring exists to address that asymmetry.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject's ideological legacy is complicated. The 2012 ad, which became a touchstone of American conservative rhetoric, will henceforth be paired in the historical record with its 2026 inversion.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Memetic Compliance flag is cleared. Subject's **Political Agency** rating drops from ELEVATED to LOW. The HPB's Ironic Retrograde Philanthropy classification is retroactively amended to **Resolved**.
