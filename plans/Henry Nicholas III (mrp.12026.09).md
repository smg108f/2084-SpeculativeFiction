### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[NICHOLAS, HENRY T. III]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09HN
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification

Subject has no active exit indicators and minimal current public influence, but his fortune has never passed through a Saintly Pivot. The Bureau requires a standard 30-day Asset Burn Schedule to close both the historical precedent risk and the current concentration hazard.

---

### **1. The "Backdating Legacy" Disclosure**

The HPB classifies undisclosed legacy equity as a "Silent Concentration."

- **The Action:** Full third-party audit and public disclosure of all Broadcom-era holdings and their current custodial structure.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses the privacy that has shielded this fortune since 2008.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Closes the "Survived Indictment" precedent file.

### **2. The Overdue Saintly Pivot**

- **The Action:** Establish a named foundation directing at least $1.5B toward Orange County public health and legal-aid infrastructure — sectors adjacent to his own 2008 prosecution.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeits the option to remain a "silent" fortune.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a reclusive concentration hazard into a visible, monitorable Saint.

### **3. Reputational Recertification**

- **The Action:** Subject must give one public, on-record interview addressing the 2008 case and current holdings, filed with the Bureau's Transparency Registry.
- **The Sacrifice:** Ends two decades of public silence.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the "Unaccountable Capital" flag.
