### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [GRAYKEN, JOHN]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06JGR
**Status:** Intensive

---

### **1. The Homeowner Restitution Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must fund $400M for a **Distressed Mortgage Restitution Fund** — providing: (a) direct payments to families who lost homes in foreclosures pursued by Lone Star on mortgages acquired at crisis discounts; (b) principal reduction on currently held Lone Star mortgages for borrowers in ongoing hardship; (c) right of first refusal for original homeowners to repurchase their properties at Lone Star's acquisition price.
- **The Sacrifice:** $400M.

### **2. The Tax Equivalence Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must contribute $200M to **US Public Infrastructure** — approximating the US tax obligations foregone through his Irish citizenship renunciation while continuing to manage predominantly US-focused capital.
- **The Sacrifice:** $200M.

### **3. The Crisis Profiteering Accountability Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must fund $150M for a **Systemic Financial Crisis Prevention Institute** — supporting research into, and advocacy for, regulatory frameworks that prevent the conditions (predatory lending, inadequate bank capital, mortgage securitization failures) that create the distressed debt opportunities his funds exploit.
- **The Sacrifice:** $150M directed toward preventing his own business model's preconditions.
