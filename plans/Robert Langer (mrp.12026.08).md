### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[LANGER, ROBERT]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-08RL
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification / Precautionary

Subject's Apotheosis Audit (REF. 12026.08) found the most favorable conduct profile this office has processed: no rhetoric, no PAC, no exit prep, and a licensing-and-mentorship practice that predates the Demi-God Act by decades. None of it, however, was ever filed as a legal structure, and Subject's diffuse 40+ company portfolio conservatively clears the threshold regardless. This plan converts existing informal good conduct into binding recertification terms rather than imposing punitive seizure.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Patent Commons Endowment (The Largest Burn)**

Subject's 1,500-patent portfolio, licensed to more than 400 companies worldwide, generates the single hardest-to-price line on his balance sheet — precisely the kind of forward-looking "Potentiality Value" the HPB's Stealth Appreciation algorithm was built to catch. It is also, not coincidentally, the asset Subject has already spent forty years licensing as broadly as possible rather than hoarding.

- **The Action:** All *future* royalty rights across the patent portfolio must be irrevocably assigned to a newly chartered **Patent Commons Trust**, which will continue licensing the technology on the same broad, non-exclusive terms Subject already favors — but with proceeds redirected to public research infrastructure instead of Subject's personal accounts. Existing signed licenses are grandfathered on a ten-year declining schedule.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject forfeits essentially all forward royalty income — the largest source of ongoing "Potentiality Value" the algorithm flagged — retaining only the tail of already-executed agreements.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Formally converts a lifetime habit of open licensing into the "Dead-Hand License" the Citizen's Guide recommends, closing the Stealth Appreciation flag for good.

### **2. The Moderna Equity Untangling (Second Burn)**

Subject's single largest and most liquid asset is his 4.0% Moderna stake (~$945M at current market cap), a position he has already begun structurally distancing himself from by stepping off the company's board in 2024 without incident or dispute.

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer 75% of his Moderna holding into a newly established **mRNA Access Trust**, administered jointly by MIT and an HPB-approved public health board, which will fund royalty-free licensing of platform improvements to neglected-disease research programs (tuberculosis, malaria, and other conditions with insufficient commercial incentive).
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject retains a capped 25% residual stake for his lifetime, after which it reverts to the Trust. He gives up the bulk of his most liquid, most easily-seized asset.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes the one cleanly-priced line item on the balance sheet, and does so in a way that extends rather than interrupts his existing pattern of directing science toward underserved problems.

### **3. The Founders' Diffusion Registry (Third Burn — structural)**

The Wealth Quotient finding that most concerns the Bureau is not any single holding — it is that Subject's forty-plus lab spinouts have never been consolidated into one legible ledger. A wealth-generation machine this productive cannot be allowed to keep operating off the books, however benign its operator.

- **The Action:** Subject must submit a complete, annually updated disclosure ledger of every equity, board, and advisory position held across all current and future Langer Lab spinout companies, filed with the HPB's Asset Slayer Division. Going forward, Subject's personal equity retention in any new lab spinout is capped at 2%, with the remainder directed into a **Langer Lab Founders' Trust** that funds the next generation of student founders directly.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses the ability to keep compounding personal wealth through future spinouts — the mechanism that quietly built this fortune in the first place.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Permanently closes the "diffusion hazard" pattern. Subject becomes the rare demi-god candidate who is audited into transparency rather than out of a fortune.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Graduate Students and Adjunct Lab Technicians** and recite the following:

> _"I never set out to be rich. I set out to make something that worked, and then I taught five hundred people to do the same, and somewhere in that teaching a fortune accumulated that I never priced and never chased. That is not innocence. A machine that makes billionaires by accident is still a machine that makes billionaires, and I built it, bench by bench, without ever once checking what it was worth. I will check now. I will keep checking. My worth was never the royalty. It was the reaction — and I forgot, for a while, that the two are not the same thing."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the "Burn" is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Subject's PI status will be revoked, the Langer Lab will be dissolved and its active grants reassigned, and the entire 1,500-patent portfolio will be entered into the public domain effective immediately — not as an act of generosity, but as a seizure. Subject will be permitted to continue teaching, without laboratory privileges, at an HPB-monitored community bench for the remainder of his tenure.
