### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [PERA, ROBERT]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06RP
**Status:** Precautionary

Subject is the founder of Ubiquiti Inc. and owner of the Memphis Grizzlies. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Memphis Grizzlies Community Burn**

Pera has been criticized as a disengaged Grizzlies owner. Memphis is one of the poorest major cities in the United States.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Grizzlies Memphis Community Foundation** — 2% of Grizzlies annual revenue permanently — funding: youth basketball access in Memphis neighborhoods; educational programs for Memphis youth in the FedExForum district; and community health initiatives in the city's most underserved areas. Pera must personally attend one Foundation community event per year.
- **The Sacrifice:** 2% revenue; personal engagement requirement.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the most disengaged ownership style in the NBA into a documented community anchor.

### **2. The Ubiquiti Global Access Burn**

Ubiquiti's products are affordable. The HPB asks for a formal access program.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Ubiquiti Connectivity Access Fund** — $100M — providing subsidized or donated Ubiquiti equipment to community internet access programs in the bottom 30 countries by internet penetration. Partner with local ISPs and community networks.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M; some commercial opportunity in these markets.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the most affordable commercial WiFi company into a documented digital inclusion contributor.

### **3. The Pera Foundation Burn**

- **The Action:** Establish the **Pera Foundation** — $200M endowment — focused on STEM education in Memphis and digital connectivity. Giving Pledge signed.
- **The Sacrifice:** $200M.
