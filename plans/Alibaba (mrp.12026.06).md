### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ALIBABA GROUP HOLDING LIMITED]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-72AL
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject Alibaba has been classified CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK following audit finding that the entity constitutes a **Commerce Substrate Capture** spanning domestic Chinese e-commerce, cross-border logistics in 200+ countries, cloud computing, digital payments (via 33% Ant Group stake), and declared AGI development — with a $53B AI infrastructure commitment in progress. The entity is too embedded in the mechanics of global commerce to be audited without first being disembedded from it.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Cainiao Divestiture (The Logistics Liberation)**

The HPB classifies Cainiao Smart Logistics as the entity's most acute Commerce Infrastructure threat. A private company that operates bonded warehouses, cross-border fulfillment centers, and last-mile delivery networks across SE Asia, Europe, the US, and the Middle East is not a logistics business. It is a privately held postal system with no treaty obligations and no civilian oversight.

- **The Action:** Transfer full ownership of Cainiao Smart Logistics Network to a newly constituted **International Commerce Commons Authority (ICCA)**, a multilateral public body jointly governed by all nations in which Cainiao operates. Subject retains no equity, board seats, or data access rights.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses the supply-chain intelligence layer — all shipment data, customs pre-clearance relationships, and bonded warehouse infrastructure reverts to public commons. Cainiao's RMB 101.3B annual revenue exits the consolidated entity.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject is reclassified from **Commerce Infrastructure Entity** to **Commerce Platform Entity** — still dangerous, but no longer operating its own post office. The ICCA framework also severs the covert intelligence-collection function that the Bureau cannot currently audit.

---

### **2. The AGI Pledge Reversal (The Ambition Forfeit)**

CEO Eddie Wu declared in 2025 that AGI is Alibaba's "primary objective." The entity backed this declaration with a RMB 380B ($53B) AI infrastructure commitment. The HPB classifies this as a **Class-III Scaffolding Ascension Declaration**: an explicit announcement that the entity intends to build the cognitive substrate on which the next generation of economic activity will run. This is not a product roadmap. It is an Apotheosis filing without the paperwork.

- **The Action:** The $53B AI infrastructure commitment must be re-directed in full to the **Global Compute Commons (GCC)** — an open-access, multilaterally governed AI infrastructure entity modeled on CERN. All AI research conducted by Alibaba Cloud Intelligence must be open-sourced under a **Dead-Hand License** (per the HPB's 2031 Potentiality Suppression Guidelines). The entity may use GCC infrastructure commercially but may not own it.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject surrenders its bid to become the foundational AI layer of the Chinese and global economy. Alibaba Cloud becomes a tenant of a public utility rather than the utility itself. AGI, if achieved under GCC governance, is owned by no one.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The **Scaffolding Ascension** designation is cleared. Subject remains a large cloud customer. The cognitive substrate is no longer privately held.

---

### **3. The Ant Group Divestiture (The Payment Rail Surrender)**

Alibaba holds a 33% stake in Ant Group, the world's most-used digital payment processor operating through Alipay. The 2023 restructuring reduced founder Jack Ma's voting power but did not reduce the systemic risk of a private entity processing payments at this scale. The HPB notes: a Universal Hidden Toll levied on commerce is a tax. Private entities are not permitted to levy taxes.

- **The Action:** Alibaba's 33% Ant Group stake must be transferred to a **Public Financial Commons Trust (PFCT)** co-governed by China, the ASEAN bloc, and the European Union. Subject receives no compensation — the stake is classified as a **Retroactive Infrastructure Debt** for the years Alipay functioned as unregulated payment infrastructure.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject severs its structural connection to the payment rail that underpins its own marketplace. Taobao/Tmall merchants will now pay through a public utility rather than through the entity's vertically integrated system. The cross-subsidy between commerce and payments is broken.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject drops from **Commerce-Payments-Logistics Triad** to a standalone commerce platform. The Bureau estimates this removes approximately $40–60B from the entity's systemic valuation premium.

---

### **4. The "Bazaar of the People" Pivot (The Marketplace Demystification)**

Subject's founding mythology — Jack Ma, a former English teacher with no technical background, built the world's largest commerce platform from a Hangzhou apartment — is one of the most potent pieces of Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric in the HPB's archive. The "ordinary man who became a god of commerce" narrative has been used to justify the entity's accumulation in China and abroad. The Bureau requires this mythology to be structurally inverted, not merely disclaimed.

- **The Action:** Taobao and Tmall must transition to a **Cooperative Merchant Model** within 12 months: all sellers on the platform collectively own 30% of the entity's domestic commerce revenue through a mandatory profit-sharing registry. The entity functions as infrastructure for the merchant community rather than as a private tollbooth above it. Jack Ma must deliver a public address — broadcast on Xinwen Lianbo, name included — titled *"I Was the Merchant, Not the Market."*
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses the merchant lock-in that drove the "choose one from two" antitrust violation in the first place. The $2.8B antitrust fine bought the entity time; the Cooperative Merchant Model discharges the underlying structural debt.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The entity's **Commerce Infrastructure** classification is downgraded to **Commerce Facilitator**. The founding mythology shifts from "god of commerce" to "servant of merchants." The Bureau considers this theologically appropriate.

> *"We are not the bazaar. We are a stall in someone else's bazaar. We did not build commerce; we built a road, and we charged too much to use it. The road now belongs to everyone who walks on it. We remain. We serve. We do not own the market; we participate in it. My name is not on the market's name. The market has no name."*
