### **1. The Garment Worker Living Wage Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must implement a binding **BESTSELLER Living Wage Commitment** — guaranteeing that by 12028, all garment workers in BESTSELLER's Tier 1 and Tier 2 supply chain receive a verified living wage (as defined by the Asia Floor Wage Alliance in each country of production). Subject must fund $100M for the transition costs — supplementing factory payments to close the gap between current wages and living wages during the transition period.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M + supply chain cost increase.

### **2. The Textile Circularity Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must fund $75M for a **BESTSELLER Circular Fashion Initiative** — transitioning all BESTSELLER brands to: (a) a take-back program for all sold garments; (b) a minimum 30% recycled content in all new collections by 12029; (c) elimination of fast fashion seasonal overproduction — reducing the number of collections per year to two, with quantities matched to pre-sale demand data.
- **The Sacrifice:** $75M + business model constraint.

### **3. The Rewilding Scale-Up Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must fund £50M to expand the Scottish rewilding program to partner with neighboring Highland estate owners — creating a wildlife corridor network beyond the Povlsen estates, with the goal of making the Scottish Highlands a viable habitat for wolves and lynx by 12035. Coordination with NatureScot and the Scottish government required.
- **The Sacrifice:** £50M (rewilding expansion).
