### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [NIKE, INC. / KNIGHT FAMILY TRUST]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-99NK
**Status:** Precautionary / Pre-Ascension Monitoring

Nike, Inc. does not currently hold individual DGE status, but the Knight family trust's Potentiality Value, the Jordan Brand's semi-autonomous cultural equity, and the Elliott Hill turnaround's projected RTWI recovery trajectory place this entity in the **Precautionary Recertification Queue**. The following Asset Burn Schedule is issued as a preventive instrument. Failure to execute within 30 days of formal Ascension Summons — projected to trigger within 24–36 months if recovery proceeds on schedule — will result in full CB-Lion seizure proceedings.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days of Summons.

---

### **1. The Swoosh Severance (The Supply Chain Accountability Burn)**

The HPB classifies Nike's manufacturing outsourcing model as a **Distributed Moral Hazard Engine**: a structure in which the entity accumulates aspirational-identity capital domestically while externalizing labor exploitation to Ascension-Haven-adjacent jurisdictions. This is the foundational architecture of Nike's Ascension risk and must be dismantled before any other recertification step is viable.

- **The Action:** Nike must transfer legal ownership of its top-tier Vietnamese and Indonesian supplier factories — currently producing 74% of global footwear — to worker-managed cooperative trusts administered under the **HPB's Southeast Asia Labor Sovereignty Protocol**. Nike retains design and quality oversight contracts at zero margin for a period of no less than ten years.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses the margin differential between offshore labor cost and retail price point — approximately $4.2B annually in operating income attributable to this structure. The "Just Do It" premium can no longer be funded by the labor of workers who cannot afford the product.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This action eliminates the **Distributed Moral Hazard** classification and converts Nike from a labor-extraction entity to a design-licensing cooperative partner. RTWI impact: estimated -$18B in Potentiality Value credits.

---

### **2. The Jordan Brand Emancipation (The Cultural Sovereignty Burn)**

The HPB's Split-Entity review has determined that Jordan Brand operates with sufficient cultural autonomy — separate creative direction, distinct consumer identity, ~$7B annual revenue, and a personal mythology built on a living individual's legacy — to constitute a **Nested Identity Empire**. This cannot remain a wholly-owned subsidiary of a CB-Lion-tier corporate entity.

- **The Action:** Jordan Brand must be spun out as an **independent public benefit corporation**, with 51% of equity transferred to a lottery-selected board of 100 inner-city basketball program participants from Chicago, Los Angeles, and Portland — the cities most directly shaped by the Jordan mythology. Michael Jordan's personal estate retains a ceremonial 5% stake, classified as **Legacy Whuffie** and non-monetizable.
- **The Sacrifice:** Nike loses its single most profitable cultural asset and the halo effect it casts over the parent brand. The "we are Jordan" brand association — the most aggressive aspiration-identity anchor in the consumer portfolio — is severed.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Jordan Brand becomes a **Community Cultural Trust**, exempt from HPB monitoring. Nike's RTWI drops by an estimated $22B in brand-equity Potentiality credits. The split also extinguishes the Split-Entity Audit flag.

---

### **3. The "Just Do It" Inversion (The Memetic Detoxification Burn)**

The HPB's Behavioral Sovereignty Division has classified "Just Do It" as a **Class-III Identity Scaffold** — a commercial belief system that substitutes product acquisition for genuine human agency. Subjects who have been exposed to this scaffold for more than twenty years (the majority of consumers born after 1975) show measurable conflation between athletic self-improvement and brand loyalty. This is the consumer-sector equivalent of Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric: aspirational inflation delivered at scale.

- **The Action:** Nike must retire the "Just Do It" trademark globally and commission, at its own expense, a ten-year **Public Agency Campaign** — produced by non-Nike creative teams, distributed through public media channels, and explicitly uncoupled from any product promotion — titled *"You Already Are."* The campaign must center on the proposition that human physical potential requires no commercial scaffold.
- **The Sacrifice:** Nike loses its most valuable and longest-running brand asset. The Swoosh may be retained as a product identifier but may no longer appear in aspirational or identity-adjacent contexts — only as a manufacturer's mark, like a laundry tag.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This action clears the **Class-III Identity Scaffold** violation. Nike becomes a shoe company. This is considered a significant demotion and is treated as such.

---

### **4. The Phil Knight Dynastic Trust Dissolution (The Bloodline Burn)**

The Knight Family Trust is the latent Potentiality Engine that would survive any corporate restructuring and re-concentrate wealth along dynastic vectors. The HPB does not audit the dead, but it does audit what the dead leave behind.

- **The Action:** The Knight Family Trust must be restructured as a **Public Endowment for Athletic Infrastructure**, administered by a seven-member board with no Knight family representation. Assets are directed exclusively toward public school athletic programs in Oregon, with a funding floor of $500M annually. Phil Knight may retain a **Life-Tenancy Usage Agreement** on his primary residence; all other real property transfers to municipal ownership.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Knight family loses dynastic control over the wealth Phil Knight spent a lifetime building. Travis Knight's film production activities are unaffected provided his individual RTWI remains sub-threshold.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The dynastic Potentiality Engine is extinguished. Phil Knight becomes, in HPB classification, a **Caretaker Saint** — a former accumulator who has surrendered the mechanism of perpetuation. This is the highest honor available to a subject of his profile.

> *"We did not make you faster. We made you afraid you were slow without us. The shoe is not the self. The self was never for sale. We will not do it again."*
