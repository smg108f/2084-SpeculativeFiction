### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ARNAULT FAMILY / CHRISTIAN DIOR SE]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-91CD
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice / Composite Entity Classification Pending

Christian Dior SE has been flagged under the **Distributed Ascension Pattern (DAP)** protocol. The entity functions as a dynastic sovereignty vehicle disguised as a publicly traded holding company. Standard individual Ascension thresholds do not apply; this plan is filed under the 2041 Amendment provisions for **Composite Demi-God Entities**, treating the Arnault family unit and its associated holding cascade as a single organism for divestment purposes. The Bureau notes that the subject has had fifty years to complete this voluntarily.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. Dissolution of the Shell: Unwinding Christian Dior SE**

The HPB classifies Christian Dior SE not as a company but as a **Sovereignty Instrument** — a legal wrapper whose sole function is to concentrate LVMH voting control within a family bloodline. The shell must be broken before any meaningful divestment can proceed.

- **The Action:** Christian Dior SE must distribute its LVMH shares into a **Public Luxury Endowment Trust** governed by an independent board of 50 elected representatives drawn from LVMH's global workforce — artisans, seamstresses, cellar workers, perfumers, and retail staff.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Arnault family relinquishes all voting control over LVMH. They may retain economic participation in the endowment (as beneficiaries, not governors) capped at $800M aggregate family value. The recursive holding chain — Groupe Arnault → Agache → Dior SE → LVMH — is legally severed.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Composite Demi-God classification is dissolved. Each Arnault heir is recertified as an individual with standard Human Citizen wealth monitoring. The 75 LVMH brands pass into stewardship governance — still operating, still producing, but no longer instruments of dynastic accumulation.

### **2. Repatriation of the Cultural Legitimacy Buffer**

The HPB classifies the Fondation Louis Vuitton, the LVMH arts patronage portfolio, and the Notre-Dame restoration pledge as **Prestige Assets** — philanthropic gestures whose primary function is to purchase social immunity from redistribution, not to generate public goods.

- **The Action:** Full and unconditional transfer of the Fondation Louis Vuitton building and collection to the French public domain, administered by the Ministry of Culture. No admission fees. No naming rights. No family board seats. The Notre-Dame pledge, if not yet fully disbursed, is paid out immediately to the restoration trust without condition or PR event.
- **The Sacrifice:** The family loses its most visible "Saintly" credential — the one asset that allowed them to claim cultural stewardship rather than mere accumulation. They become, in public perception, what they always were: very wealthy shareholders.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The **Cultural Legitimacy Buffer** is cleared from the HPB's evasion-tactics register. The Bureau's Prestige Index for the Arnault family drops from HIGH to MODERATE, bringing them within the standard audit cycle.

### **3. Dismantling the Succession Architecture**

The five-heir executive embedding program — Antoine at communications, Delphine at Dior Couture, Alexandre at Loro Piana and Rimowa, Frédéric at TAG Heuer, Jean at Berluti — constitutes a **Pre-Distributed Succession Apparatus**, a technique the 2041 Amendments specifically flag as evasion of single-entity Ascension thresholds.

- **The Action:** All five Arnault children must resign executive positions at LVMH subsidiaries within the 30-day window. They may seek employment at LVMH entities as individual candidates through standard merit-based hiring processes, subject to a 5-year cooling-off period, under the same terms as any other applicant.
- **The Sacrifice:** The deliberate decades-long project of dynastic preparation — the grooming, the brand assignments, the carefully differentiated portfolios — is voided. The Arnault name becomes a liability in LVMH hiring, not an asset, for the duration of the cooling-off.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The HPB's **Dynastic Succession Hazard** flag is cleared. The subject ceases to constitute an organism and becomes, legally, five individual people and one divested elder.

### **4. The Heritage Rhetoric Recantation**

LVMH's brand narratives — "savoir-faire," "maison," "heritage," "timelessness" — constitute what the Bureau's Memetic Division classifies as **Luxury Ideology**: a systematic cultural argument that the accumulation of taste, craft, and beauty by one family is equivalent to, and deserving of the same protections as, the common inheritance of humanity. This ideology has provided political cover for the Arnault structure across five decades.

- **The Action:** Bernard Arnault must deliver a public address — broadcast without editing rights across all LVMH-affiliated media platforms — acknowledging that "heritage" is not a property right, that craft belongs to the craftspeople who practice it, and that the luxury economy's cultural legitimacy was borrowed, not owned.
- **The Sacrifice:** The narrative justification for the entire enterprise — the idea that the Arnaults were *custodians* of beauty rather than owners of a $300B legal structure — is surrendered publicly.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The **Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric** violation (specifically: the implicit claim that one family's taste constitutes civilization's guardian) is cleared.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, each adult member of the Arnault family must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 LVMH Production Workers** — one leatherworker, one champagne cellar hand, one perfumer, one seamstress, one retail associate, one logistics driver, one vineyard laborer, one glassblower, one watchmaker's apprentice, one fragrance tester, one warehouse picker, and one cleaning staff member from a Dior flagship — and recite the following:

> *"I did not make any of this. The hands that made it are in this room. I held the structure that held the structure that held the company that paid their wages, and I mistook that distance for greatness. Beauty is not inherited. Craft is not a bloodline. I release my claim on both."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Asset Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Christian Dior SE will be reclassified as a **Composite Demi-God Entity** in perpetuity. All Arnault family members will be issued individual Ascension Summons, and the LVMH holding cascade will be placed under Bureau-appointed receivership. The family will be offered one of two options: voluntary relocation to the **Monaco Sanctuary Zone** (designated Ascension Haven, EU sector) or reassignment as permanent cultural heritage docents at the Fondation Louis Vuitton — which will by then belong to everyone else.
