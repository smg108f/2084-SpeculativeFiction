### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[NIPPON TELEGRAPH & TELEPHONE, NTT]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06NTT
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice

NTT has been classified a **Sovereign Substrate Entity** — a demi-god so thoroughly woven into national infrastructure that seizing it outright risks seizing the nation along with it. Recertification therefore proceeds by *de-fusion*: the Bureau will surgically separate "the company" from "the medium," and return each to its proper, mortal size.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The IOWN Open Surrender (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies the Innovative Optical and Wireless Network as a **Class-V Substrate Hazard**: a private proposal to replace the physical layer of the internet itself. No entity may own the medium through which a civilization thinks.

- **The Action:** All IOWN patents, photonic-network specifications, and All-Photonics Connect infrastructure must be surrendered to an international, open-license **Public Substrate Commons**, administered jointly by the ITU and a Lottery-Selected Citizens' Council.
- **The Sacrifice:** NTT loses first-mover advantage on the next forty years of global networking — the very thing IOWN was engineered to secure.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** NTT becomes a *contributor* to the substrate rather than its landlord. Estimated **$31B** in "Potentiality Value" is struck from the Real-Time Wealth Index.

### **2. The Government Decoupling (Severing the State's Hand)**

A demi-god wearing a government ID badge is still a demi-god — and a state that co-owns its own nervous system cannot be trusted to regulate it. The 2031 Hybrid Sovereignty Amendment forbids any entity from being simultaneously "instrument of the state" and "private accumulator of capital."

- **The Action:** The Japanese government must either (a) fully nationalize NTT as a public utility with zero shareholder upside, or (b) divest its remaining stake entirely into a **Citizens' Connectivity Trust** with one-share-one-household voting rights. NTT's executives must publicly choose which arm is amputated.
- **The Sacrifice:** NTT loses the ambiguity that has shielded it from antitrust scrutiny for over a century — the comfortable fog of "are we a company or are we Japan?"
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the **"Shadow Cabinet" Violation.** The entity becomes legible — and legible things can be governed.

### **3. The Atonement Tour (Reputational Burn)**

NTT's 2025 data breach — exposing records tied to roughly 18,000 partner organizations — was followed by a president's "early retirement," a gesture the Bureau has logged as **Performative Penance, Grade A**. Genuine atonement requires discomfort, not a tasteful exit.

- **The Action:** Surviving leadership must conduct a televised, unscripted **Public Reckoning Tour**, visiting all 18,000 affected organizations in person to personally restore their trust, with no PR staff, translators-of-convenience, or pre-approved questions permitted.
- **The Sacrifice:** The mythology of NTT as a quietly competent, untouchable institution — replaced by the spectacle of its executives apologizing, badly, in real time, on camera, for a year.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts "Structural Sainthood" (a halo issued at incorporation) into *earned* Whuffie — the only kind the HPB recognizes as real.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's executive board must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Switchboard Technicians, Cable-Vessel Crew, and Rural Postal Carriers** and recite the following:

> _"We did not become the space between people. We were only ever the wire — and a wire that forgets it is a wire becomes a wall. We return the medium to the message, and the message to the people who speak it."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. NTT's networks will be placed under emergency international administration, its executives reclassified as a **Demi-God Trust** subject to 100% growth taxation, and relocated — fittingly — to a windowless data-center module aboard the **Pacific Relay Flotilla**, where they will spend their remaining years experiencing, firsthand, what it is like to be infrastructure.
