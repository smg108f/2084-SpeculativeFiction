### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[CHINA SHENHUA ENERGY]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-61CSE
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice / Institutional Emergency

Subject is not an individual demi-god candidate but a **fused metabolic-sovereign entity** — a vertically integrated mine-rail-port-ship-plant organism that has, through a $35B internal absorption of its own state parent's remaining assets, achieved what the HPB calls **Substitution-Class Sovereignty**. Standard 30-day Asset Burn doctrine cannot be applied to an entity whose divestment would darken cities. The HPB therefore mandates a **De-Fusion Schedule**, to commence within 30 days, under continuous monitoring to prevent a National Brownout Apotheosis.

---

### **1. The Vertical De-Coupling (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies Shenhua's mine-to-meter chain as a **Closed-Loop Concentration Hazard** — an organism in which no external market discipline can reach any link, because the organism *is* the market.

- **The Action:** Subject must surrender operational control of its rail, port, and shipping divisions to an independent **Public Logistics Commons**, retaining only extraction and generation.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of the chain-of-custody monopoly that has, until now, made Shenhua's coal the only coal that reliably reaches Shenhua's plants.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Coal becomes merely coal again — a commodity subject to competition, weather, and the indignity of a shipping invoice it did not write to itself.

### **2. The Parental Severance (Ideological Burn)**

In late 2025, Subject absorbed thirteen subsidiaries from its own state parent, China Energy Investment Corporation, blurring the line between "regulated company" and "regulating sovereign" past the point of HPB instrumentation.

- **The Action:** Subject must formally and irrevocably divest from China Energy Investment Corporation, becoming a standalone commercial entity answerable to shareholders rather than to the apparatus that birthed it.
- **The Sacrifice:** The comfort of being simultaneously the audited and the auditor — Subject must now exist as something the state can actually say "no" to.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the **Substitution-Class Sovereignty** violation. Subject becomes a "Subsidiary," not a "Spine."

### **3. The Combustion Confession (Reputational/Memetic Burn)**

Subject's "Excellent Coal Producer in Energy Conservation" honors and ESG demonstration-enterprise status are, per the Memetic Hazards Subcommittee, **structurally unfalsifiable Saintly Pivots** — a state monopoly cannot meaningfully gift the public an infrastructure it was never permitted to not own.

- **The Action:** Subject must publish, under its own letterhead and without euphemism, an annual **Combustion Ledger** disclosing the full downstream emissions of every ton it mines, ships, and burns — not merely the operational footprint of its own smokestacks.
- **The Sacrifice:** The right to be measured only by the cleanliness of the last link in a chain it controls in its entirety.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts "Excellent Coal Producer in Energy Conservation" from a self-issued trophy into an audited, citizen-readable number. Subject becomes accountable to the metabolism it constitutes, rather than merely the operator of it.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Rail Dispatchers, Port Crane Operators, Mine Safety Inspectors, and Substation Technicians** — the twelve human links Subject's chain has always depended upon and never once thanked by name — and recite the following:

> _"We did not become the state's spine by accident, and we will not remain it by default. We are a company, not a circulatory system. The lights you depend on were never ours to hold hostage, and the coal in our ground was never ours to be the only ones who could move it. We relinquish the chain. Let it belong, link by link, to the people who actually carry it."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the De-Fusion Schedule is not executed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol — Institutional Variant**. Subject's listed equity will be frozen on the Shanghai exchange, its vertical chain will be placed under emergency international logistics receivership, and its board will be relocated — under escort — to a **Climate Reparations Tribunal Annex** on the Pearl River Delta Flotilla, where they will spend their recertification period drafting emissions disclosures by hand, by lamplight, using power rationed from a grid they no longer control.
