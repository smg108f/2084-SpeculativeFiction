### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [MENARD JR., JOHN]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06JMn
**Status:** Urgent — Zero Philanthropy / Labour Practices / Hazardous Waste

Subject is the founder and president of Menards. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 60 days.

---

### **1. The Employee Profit-Sharing Acceleration Burn**

Menards' profit-sharing structure defers employee profit distributions significantly. The HPB asks for structural reform.

- **The Action:** File with the HPB a **Menards Employee Profit Sharing Charter** reforming the profit-sharing structure: (a) all accrued profit-sharing balances older than 3 years must be paid out to current employees within 90 days of this charter filing; (b) the future profit-sharing vesting schedule may not exceed a 3-year total vest; (c) the employee fine system (for email misuse and other minor infractions) is suspended pending an independent review that determines which, if any, fine categories are lawful and proportionate. Results of review published.
- **The Sacrifice:** Accelerated payouts; loss of the deferred compensation system's float.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the most criticized aspect of Menards' employer practices into a documented reform.

### **2. The Environmental Remediation Burn**

Menards settled an EPA hazardous waste disposal case for $1.1M. The HPB asks for a proportionate remediation program.

- **The Action:** Establish a **Menards Environmental Remediation Fund** — $50M — dedicated to: (a) full cleanup of any remaining contamination linked to the EPA settlement; (b) environmental audits of all current Menards store sites for any legacy contamination; (c) a permanent Menards Environmental Compliance Program with independent auditor, published annually.
- **The Sacrifice:** $50M; ongoing public transparency about environmental compliance.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts an EPA settlement fine into a documented, permanent environmental accountability program.

### **3. The Menard Foundation Establishment Burn**

$17B in wealth; no philanthropy. The HPB requires a correction.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Menard Foundation** with a minimum $500M endowment, focused on Wisconsin workforce development and community infrastructure in Midwest cities where Menards stores are the primary employer — specifically: vocational training programs, community college partnerships, and local infrastructure grants in underserved Midwest communities. Giving Pledge must be signed.
- **The Sacrifice:** $500M; public philanthropic identity; Giving Pledge commitment.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the most unphilanthropic major retailer in America into a documented Midwest community investor.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Menards Employees from Wisconsin, EPA Region 5 Environmental Officers, and Midwest Community College Instructors**:

> *"I started Menards in 1960 with one store in Wisconsin. I built it into the third-largest home improvement chain in America. I run it privately because private companies can take a long view. The HPB is asking me to take a long view of my employees and my environmental record. The profit-sharing reform is filed. The Environmental Fund is established. The Menard Foundation is funded with $500 million and the Giving Pledge is signed. I have built something that the Midwest depends on for building supplies. I am trying to make sure it also invests in the Midwest's people."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the profit-sharing reform is not filed, the HPB will refer Menards' employee compensation structure to the Department of Labour for a full wage and hour audit. If the Foundation is not established, Menard will be classified as the highest wealth-to-philanthropy gap in the registry — a designation published annually. Subject will be assigned to **FSS MENARDS** — a vessel where every crew member is subject to the same deferred compensation structure and fine system as Menards employees, where Menard discovers that sailing a ship where the crew hasn't been fully paid yet is its own form of accountability, and where the ship's internal email policy is posted prominently in the galley.
