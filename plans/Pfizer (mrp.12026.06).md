### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [PFIZER INC.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-83PFE
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice / Mandatory Institutional Recertification

Pfizer Inc. is a corporate entity in excess of the HPB's Institutional Demi-God threshold, having exceeded $1B in assets by a factor of 153. Recertification is mandated under the 2041 Revised Charter, Section 9 ("Non-Person Entities Exercising Biological Sovereignty"), on the grounds that the subject controls tiered access to human health outcomes at scale, has a documented history of regulatory capture, and continues to operate a patent lattice that functions as private infrastructure over public survival.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Patent Lattice Dissolution (The Biological Tollbooth Burn)**

The HPB classifies Pfizer's IP portfolio — particularly orphan drug exclusivities applied to mass-market indications, and settlement agreements that delay generic competition by years — as a **Class-I Mortality Instrument**. The tafamidis franchise is the clearest example: a drug granted rare-disease status now generating billions annually from a large cardiac patient population, with generics contractually blocked until 2031 while patients pay $225,000+ per year in pre-2084 pricing.

- **The Action:** Full open-licensing of all compounds whose initial development received any government funding, NIH research, or emergency-use public subsidy. This includes Comirnaty, Paxlovid, all tafamidis formulations, and any pipeline asset receiving taxpayer-funded clinical infrastructure. Patent lattice must be converted to a **Public Commons License** with royalty-free generic manufacturing rights granted globally.
- **The Sacrifice:** Estimated $40–60B in future exclusivity-protected revenue. The Vyndamax "tollbooth" opens immediately. Generic manufacturers enter the tafamidis market within 18 months.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Pfizer transitions from **Biological Sovereign** to **Research and Manufacturing Servicer** — a company that makes things, rather than one that controls who may access them. The HPB reclassifies the entity from Class-I Mortality Instrument to **Provisional Mortal Corporation**.

### **2. The Pfizergate Accountability Procedure (The Regulatory Fusion Burn)**

The HPB cannot permit an entity to hold Mortal status while maintaining active infrastructure for the capture of sovereign regulatory bodies. Pfizer's documented history — the $2.3B off-label marketing settlement (2009), the "Pfizergate" EU text-message contract (2021), and the 2025 House Judiciary inquiry into clinical trial timing — constitutes a pattern the Bureau classifies as **Systematic Democracy Erosion**.

- **The Action:** Mandatory dissolution of all pharmaceutical lobbying operations in the United States and European Union. All PAC contributions frozen and transferred to a **Public Pharmaceutical Oversight Fund** administered by a lottery-selected citizen board. CEO Albert Bourla, or his successor, must submit to a public testimony session before a randomly selected panel of 50 patients who paid out-of-pocket for Pfizer drugs in the preceding decade.
- **The Sacrifice:** Pfizer loses its seat at the regulatory table. Drug approval timelines may lengthen. Competitors gain equal access to the same regulatory architecture Pfizer has spent 40 years cultivating. Estimated loss of competitive advantage: $15–20B in net present value of favorable regulatory outcomes.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The **Shadow Cabinet Violation** is cleared. Pfizer may continue to engage regulators through public-record channels only, visible to all competitors and citizens equally.

### **3. The COVID Windfall Redistribution (The Pandemic Profit Penance)**

Pfizer extracted approximately $56B in net profit from the COVID-19 pandemic between 2021 and 2022, using vaccines developed in part on publicly funded mRNA research, sold through government contracts, to a captive market created by a global emergency. The HPB classifies this revenue as **Emergency Infrastructure Rent** — profit taken from a public catastrophe that could not, by definition, involve a functioning competitive market.

- **The Action:** 50% of all COVID-era net profits (estimated $28B) must be transferred to a **Global Pandemic Preparedness Commons** — a non-Pfizer-controlled, internationally governed fund dedicated to open-source vaccine development, accessible manufacturing infrastructure in low-income nations, and stockpile capacity for future pandemics. Pfizer retains governance representation proportional to a $0 contribution (i.e., none).
- **The Sacrifice:** $28B in retained earnings. CEO Bourla must publicly acknowledge, in a statement read into the Congressional Record, that the emergency use authorization framework created a non-competitive market and that Pfizer's pricing within that market was a choice, not a necessity.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The **Pandemic Extraction Flag** is removed from Pfizer's audit record. The HPB reduces its Whuffie Deficit classification from **Severe** to **Marginal**.

### **4. The Pipeline Transparency Protocol (The Cure-Timing Burn)**

The 2025 House Judiciary Committee inquiry into whether Pfizer withheld positive COVID-19 vaccine trial results to avoid influencing a presidential election — if substantiated — would constitute the single most consequential act of **Cure-Timing** in pharmaceutical history. The HPB treats the potential deliberate withholding of life-saving clinical data as a **Class-II Human Rights Violation**, regardless of whether it affected electoral outcomes.

- **The Action:** All clinical trial data — including trial timing decisions, internal communications about release schedules, and records of any discussion linking data release to political calendars — must be transferred in full to a publicly accessible archive administered by the National Institutes of Health. Any future clinical trial whose data is withheld for more than 14 days beyond standard regulatory review timelines triggers automatic open-licensing of the compound in question.
- **The Sacrifice:** Pfizer loses the ability to manage the *timing* of its own scientific reputation. The competitive advantage of information asymmetry — knowing your drug works before your competitors, your regulators, and the public — is permanently neutralized.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The **Cure-Timing Flag** is conditionally cleared pending full archive compliance. The HPB removes Pfizer from its **Biological Sovereignty Watchlist** upon 24 months of verified transparent disclosure.
