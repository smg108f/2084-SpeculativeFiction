### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[NEXTERA ENERGY, CORPORATE PERSONHOOD]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-66NE
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject is hereby notified that "doing good while owning the meter" does not constitute a valid Saintly Pivot under the 2034 Code. Because Subject's clean-energy halo and its monopoly chokehold are structurally inseparable, the HPB has determined that the only path to Mortal Recertification is to **sever the halo from the leash** — surgically, publicly, and within 30 days.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Florida Power & Light Public Devolution (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies FPL not as an asset but as a **Captured Survival Organ** — a function of the public commons that Subject has been permitted to wear as a private appendage for far too long.

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer full operational and regulatory control of Florida Power & Light to a **Floridian Grid Commons Trust**, governed by a rotating citizen board, with NextEra retaining only a non-voting "Generation Contractor" role.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses the franchise monopoly that anchors roughly half its valuation — and with it, the ability to set the price of staying alive during a Category 5 hurricane.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The "Substitution Hazard" is closed. NextEra becomes a vendor to the public, not the public's landlord.

### **2. The Net-Metering Reparations Act (Ideological Burn)**

Subject's lobbying record shows a sustained campaign to prevent ordinary citizens from generating their own rooftop power — the single clearest betrayal of its "clean energy savior" branding on file.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund, at full cost, a **"Sun on Every Roof" Program**, installing and connecting rooftop solar with full net-metering rights for every household currently inside its former Florida service territory, at zero charge to the homeowner.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject permanently forfeits the regulatory advantage it spent over a decade and millions of dollars purchasing — and actively funds the technology that lets its former captive customers leave it.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the "Saintly Camouflage" finding; converts a documented harm into a documented (and irreversible) act of restitution.

### **3. The "Ghost Candidate" Disclosure & Memetic Quarantine (Reputational Burn)**

The HPB classifies Subject's dark-money election interference — funding spoiler campaigns to confuse Florida voters — as a **Class-III Democratic Hazard**, a quieter cousin of "Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric": the conviction that an entity may rightfully author the choices of the electorate it powers.

- **The Action:** Subject must publish a complete, unredacted ledger of every dollar spent on political consulting, "ghost" candidates, and dark-money operations since 2018, hand-delivered to the Florida Elections Archive and read aloud at a televised hearing by a sitting NextEra board member.
- **The Sacrifice:** Total loss of "plausible deniability" — and of any future capacity to quietly shape the regulators who oversee it.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the "Regulatory Capture" violation; demonstrates the entity can survive sunlight, not just generate it.

### **4. The Dominion Renunciation (Scale Burn)**

Subject's 12026 maneuver to acquire Dominion Energy's grid is, per HPB modeling, an attempt to replicate the Florida chokehold across Virginia and the Carolinas — precisely the kind of "Structural Sovereignty" expansion the Demi-God Act was written to prevent.

- **The Action:** Subject must publicly and irrevocably withdraw from the Dominion acquisition, and sign a **Non-Expansion Covenant** barring any future bid for additional state-level utility monopolies.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forecloses the entity's primary growth vector — the data-center power gold rush — permanently capping its scale at current territory.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes the "Exit Readiness" growth pathway from future audits; confines Subject to its existing (and now de-fanged) footprint.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's board of directors must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Florida Linemen, Hurricane Shelter Volunteers, and Rooftop Solar Installers** and recite the following:

> _"We are not the sun, and we are not the storm. We do not get to charge you for the right to survive either one. The light was never ours to sell — only ours, for a while, to carry."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the "Burn" is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. FPL's monopoly charter will be revoked by emergency federal order, all generation assets will be nationalized into the Public Grid Authority, and Subject's executive leadership will be permanently relocated to **staffed rotation duty inside the very substations and storm-recovery crews they once commanded from boardrooms** — to learn, at last, what it costs to keep the lights on.
