## The Three Burns

**Mortal Status Gain:** Hundreds of millions of device users gain visibility into how their data is collected and used across the ecosystem.

### Burn 2: Device Longevity Commitment

**Action:** Commit to binding minimum software update support periods for all device categories, ending planned obsolescence patterns common to the smartphone industry.

**Sacrifice:** Reduced incentive for accelerated device replacement cycles.

**Mortal Status Gain:** Consumers gain longer usable device lifespans and reduced electronic waste.

### Burn 3: Founder Visibility and Accountability Parity

**Action:** Publish standard governance and equity disclosures at a level of transparency matching the company's more publicly visible co-founder, ending the reputational insulation of lower personal visibility.

**Sacrifice:** Loss of the reduced scrutiny that comes with lower public profile.

**Mortal Status Gain:** Accountability commensurate with actual equity stake and decision-making power, not with public name recognition.
