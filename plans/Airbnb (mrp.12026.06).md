### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[AIRBNB, INC.]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06ABNB
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject is a non-biological Demi-God Entity that achieved Ascension not through hoarding buildings but through hoarding the *authority to define what a building is for* — a more dangerous and more deniable form of divinity. Recertification requires Subject to surrender its toll-booth position and physically re-embed itself within the housing markets it has spent two decades quietly annexing.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Listing Reversion Mandate (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies Airbnb's algorithmic booking layer as a **"Substitution Engine"** — a private system that has replaced public housing-allocation functions across thousands of municipalities without ever being deputized to do so.

- **The Action:** Subject must permanently delist all properties located in any ZIP code where the long-term rental vacancy rate falls below 5%, and transfer the resulting tenancy-matching function to municipal housing authorities, royalty-free, in perpetuity.
- **The Sacrifice:** An estimated 40% reduction in active global inventory and the surrender of all proprietary "demand-pricing" algorithms tied to housing scarcity.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject ceases to be a *de facto* zoning authority and becomes, at last, merely a booking website — which is what it always claimed to be.

### **2. The "Belonging" Rebrand Forfeiture**

Subject's central mythology — that opening your home to strangers constitutes "belonging" and "empowerment" — is classified by the HPB as a **Class-III Memetic Hazard**: it launders housing-market extraction through the language of community.

- **The Action:** Subject must retire the "Belong Anywhere" trademark entirely and fund, at $250M, an independent "Housing Truth Index" that publishes — unredacted, city by city — exactly how many long-term residences each Airbnb market has converted to short-term yield.
- **The Sacrifice:** The loss of two decades of brand equity built on the premise that commodifying your spare room is a form of generosity.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This neutralizes the **Whuffie Cosplay** violation and forces Subject to compete on price and service alone, like a mortal company.

### **3. The Host Liability Transfer**

Subject has spent its corporate life insisting it is a "platform," not a landlord, hotelier, or employer — a structural dodge the HPB calls **"Plausible Absenteeism."**

- **The Action:** Subject must reclassify itself, in every jurisdiction where it operates, as a co-employer and co-landlord of record alongside its hosts, accepting joint legal and tax liability for every listing on its platform.
- **The Sacrifice:** Immediate exposure to the full weight of municipal housing codes, hotel taxation, and tenant-protection statutes Subject has spent a decade lobbying against.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the **Infrastructure Capture** charge — Subject becomes accountable for the housing it touches, rather than merely profiting from its absence.

### **4. The Founder's Penance (Chesky Clause)**

Brian Chesky's personal stake (~$9.2B, ~32% voting control) constitutes a **Sovereign Voting Bloc** inside a company already flagged for Ascension.

- **The Action:** Chesky must convert his Class B supervoting shares to standard Class A shares and personally relocate, for one calendar year, into a rent-controlled unit secured through an Airbnb long-term-stay listing — at market rate, under his own name, reviewed publicly by his hosts.
- **The Sacrifice:** Surrender of founder-level control and the comfort of never having to be a "guest" in the world he built.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Demonstrates "Saintly Empathy" in the one currency the HPB actually trusts: lived experience of the system you designed.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject (represented by its CEO and Board) must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Long-Term Renters Displaced by Short-Term Listings** and recite the following:

> _"We did not build belonging. We built a meter and called it a welcome mat. We mistook your address for our inventory, and your neighborhood for our growth chart. From this day, a door is a door — not a unit, not a node, not a yield. We will knock before we profit."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Subject's corporate charter will be dissolved, its booking infrastructure nationalized into the **Public Tenancy Commons**, and its executive leadership relocated — under standard "guest" terms, with average review scores — to a **Sanctuary Zone micro-unit in the very neighborhoods their platform helped hollow out**, there to remain until the Commons' citizen-reviewers certify them "a good host to have had."
