### **1. The Medical Device Access Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must commit SEK 500M (≈$50M) to a **Getinge Global Health Access Program** — providing sterilization equipment and critical care infrastructure at cost to hospitals in lower-middle-income countries, where hospital infection rates from inadequate sterilization remain a leading cause of preventable death.
- **The Sacrifice:** SEK 500M.

### **2. The Manufacturing Quality Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must ensure Getinge achieves and maintains full FDA compliance across all US manufacturing facilities — funding whatever operational investment is required — and must publish an annual independent **Product Quality and Safety Report** covering all jurisdictions, not just those with regulatory enforcement.
- **The Sacrifice:** Compliance investment and transparency.

### **3. The Industrial Ecosystem Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must fund SEK 300M (≈$30M) for **Swedish Industrial Apprenticeship Expansion** — increasing manufacturing apprenticeships in the Swedish companies he controls, specifically targeting youth employment in post-industrial regions where Getinge and Lifco facilities operate.
- **The Sacrifice:** SEK 300M.
