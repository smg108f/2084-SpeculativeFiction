### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [TRANE TECHNOLOGIES PLC]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09TT
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Trane's explosive AI-buildout-driven growth, without a proportional public-good pivot, places it among this cycle's Critical Ascension Enabler files. Recertification targets redistribution of the windfall.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The AI Infrastructure Public Benefit Fund**

- **The Action:** Direct 3% of the AI-driven backlog revenue into a public fund supporting energy-efficient cooling retrofits for public schools and hospitals in regions hosting major data-center developments.
- **The Sacrifice:** Estimated $300M+ annually redirected from shareholder returns.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Introduces a genuine Saintly Pivot tied directly to the source of the company's explosive growth.

### **2. The Data Center Community Impact Disclosure**

- **The Action:** Publish aggregate data on energy and water consumption enabled by Trane's cooling systems across major data-center deployments, alongside community impact assessments for host regions.
- **The Sacrifice:** Increased scrutiny of the environmental footprint enabled by the company's core growth driver.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Provides transparency into the resource consumption the AI cooling boom represents.
