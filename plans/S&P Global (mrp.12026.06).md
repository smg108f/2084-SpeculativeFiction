### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[S&P GLOBAL, INC.]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06SPG
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject has been found to be exercising **Privatized Judgment** at planetary scale — a function indistinguishable from sovereign authority — while holding the legal form of a publicly traded company. To retain Human-Adjacent Operating status and avoid Utility Reclassification, Subject must surrender its adjudicative monopoly and convert its grading apparatus into a public commons.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The "Verdict Engine" Decommission (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies proprietary credit-rating methodologies as **"Sovereign Liturgy"** — closed-door rituals by which Subject pronounces nations and corporations worthy or unworthy of capital. No private entity may retain sole authorship of a formula that moves national borrowing costs.

- **The Action:** Subject must open-source its complete sovereign and corporate ratings methodology under a **Dead-Hand License**, transferring stewardship to a rotating, lottery-selected **Public Ratings Commons**.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of all proprietary methodology IP and the licensing revenue it generates — an estimated **$40B** in future-valuation credits.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject ceases to be the sole author of "creditworthiness" and becomes one contributor among many. The "Verdict Engine" stops being a throne.

### **2. Dissolution of the Moody's Duopoly Accord**

The HPB has determined that Subject and its chief rival have, through decades of regulatory inertia, constructed a **closed adjudicative cartel** controlling roughly 80% of the world's credit verdicts — an arrangement that functions as a shadow government of borrowing costs.

- **The Action:** Subject must formally petition global regulators to **strip mandatory dual-rating requirements**, actively subsidizing the entry of at least five new community-governed rating cooperatives into the market within 18 months.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses its guaranteed market position and the "natural moat" that has made grading a near-zero-competition business for a century.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the **"Shadow Cabinet" Violation** — Subject is no longer a structural gatekeeper of sovereign debt, merely a participant in a crowded, mortal field.

### **3. The Kensho Surrender (Cognitive Infrastructure Penance)**

Subject's 2018 acquisition of Kensho — at the time billed as "the largest A.I. deal in history" — fused its judgment apparatus to a generative-AI engine, creating a closed loop in which Subject's own AI now drafts the narratives that justify Subject's own grades. The HPB classifies this self-referential loop as a **Class-III Memetic Hazard: Self-Validating Omniscience**.

- **The Action:** Subject must spin off Kensho into an **independent, non-profit Public Risk Literacy Institute**, with its models, training data, and outputs released under open license to any municipality, union, or citizen collective that requests them.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses its AI-narrative advantage and the ability to model — and thereby pre-author — the world's understanding of "risk."
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Breaks the closed loop. Subject can no longer grade a future it also wrote the script for.

### **4. The "Sustainability Score" Recantation**

Subject currently sells ESG and sustainability ratings back to the very institutions it grades — monetizing virtue itself as a subscription product. The HPB considers this the most cynical form of **Saintly Pivot Counterfeiting**.

- **The Action:** Subject must discontinue all paid ESG/sustainability scoring products and instead fund, at no charge, a **Citizen Sustainability Audit Corps** — staffed by lottery-selected community members who publish their own plain-language assessments of corporate behavior.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of an entire high-margin product line and the moral high ground it purported to occupy.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject stops selling the public a subscription to judgments about the public. The verdict returns to the people it was rendered upon.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's executive leadership must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Small-Business Owners, Municipal Bond Clerks, and Credit Union Tellers** and recite the following:

> _"I am not the scale upon which the world is weighed. I am one voice among many, and my opinion of your worth carries no more weight than your neighbor's. I relinquish the pen that wrote 'AAA' and 'junk' over the lives of strangers. Grade me now, as I once graded you."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the "Burn" is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Subject's ratings charter will be nationalized and dissolved, its executive leadership will be individually reclassified as **Demi-God Entities** under the doctrine of "Aggregated Adjudicative Personhood," and the company's headquarters will be converted into a **Public Ledger Museum** — a permanent exhibit on the institutions of 2026 that were, briefly and absurdly, allowed to grade the planet for profit.
