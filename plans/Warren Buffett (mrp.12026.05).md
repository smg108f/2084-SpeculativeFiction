### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [BUFFETT, WARREN E.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-12WB
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification (Phantasim Classification / Final Cycle Review)

Subject Buffett, at age 95, has been flagged under the HPB's **Phantasim Accelerated Review** protocol, which applies to subjects in the elder-sage classification whose institutional legacies are projected to generate Demi-God-level influence for 40+ years post-biological expiration. The standard 30-day burn window has been extended to 60 days in recognition of the subject's documented cooperation with Saintly Pivot frameworks; however, sincerity of intent does not suspend the recertification requirement.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 60 days.

---

### **1. The Berkshire Dissolution (The Sovereign Financial Entity)**

The HPB classifies Berkshire Hathaway not as a company but as a **Privately Administered Sovereign Wealth Vehicle**. Its $330B cash reserve constitutes Latent Sovereign Capacity. Its railroad, energy, and insurance subsidiaries constitute functional public infrastructure held under private authority. The legal fiction that this entity is distinct from Subject Buffett's personal influence is not sustained by the HPB's Potentiality Engine modeling.

- **The Action:** Subject must initiate the structured dissolution of Berkshire Hathaway into a portfolio of **Public Commons Trusts**, transferring BNSF Railway to a federally chartered rail commons, Berkshire Hathaway Energy to a regional utility cooperative network, and all equity positions above $500M into a blind public endowment administered by lottery-selected citizen boards. Greg Abel may serve as transition administrator for no longer than 24 months.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Oracle loses the Oracle's seat. The $330B cash reserve — the largest single expression of sovereign financial optionality held by any private individual or entity — is surrendered to the public treasury. Subject retains no advisory or consulting role.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Berkshire ceases to function as an extension of Subject's personhood. The HPB's Potentiality Engine score drops from CRITICAL to MARGINAL. Subject is reclassified from **Phantom Sovereign** to **Retired Citizen (Elder Grade)**.

### **2. The Giving Pledge Legalization (The Moral Commitment Problem)**

The HPB acknowledges and respects Subject's Giving Pledge. The HPB does not accept the Giving Pledge as legal tender. A moral commitment to donate 99% of one's estate upon death is, from a regulatory standpoint, an unfalsifiable and unenforceable instrument. Until assets are transferred, they remain assets.

- **The Action:** Subject must convert all pledged charitable transfers from **future moral commitments** into **present irrevocable legal instruments**. Shares currently designated for the Gates Foundation and family foundations must be transferred into HPB-registered Public Benefit Disbursement Structures within the 60-day window. The Susan Thompson Buffett Foundation, the Howard G. Buffett Foundation, the Peter Buffett Foundation, and the Susie Buffett Foundation must each be re-chartered as **Citizen-Governed Endowments** with no Buffett family members serving in governance roles.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses the option of a deathbed revision. He loses the governance leverage his family currently holds over roughly $40B in charitable endowments. The children become beneficiaries of a legacy, not administrators of a dynasty.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Giving Pledge becomes an audit-verified fact rather than an audit-resistant aspiration. The HPB closes the **Deferred Divinity Loophole** that currently allows Subject to claim saint status for assets he has not yet released.

### **3. The Oracle Retirement (The Reputational Sovereignty Protocol)**

Subject's personal influence — the Oracle Architecture — cannot be seized or donated. It can only be retired. The HPB acknowledges this is the most unusual burn in this recertification plan and the most difficult to enforce. It is included because the failure to include it would be a regulatory fiction.

- **The Action:** Subject must issue a formal **Oracle Retirement Declaration**, published in the Berkshire Annual Letter format and distributed to all prior shareholders and Whuffie subscribers. The declaration must explicitly state that Subject's investment opinions and market commentary are no longer intended to guide capital allocation decisions, that the "Buffett Effect" is a documented systemic distortion, and that Subject decourages reliance on his public statements for financial decision-making. Subject must decline all future media requests for market commentary.
- **The Sacrifice:** The annual Omaha pilgrimage — "Woodstock for Capitalists" — is ended. The shareholder letter is discontinued. The congregation disperses. Subject becomes, deliberately and finally, a private citizen.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Oracle's gravitational field collapses on a known timeline rather than an unknown posthumous one. Markets begin the long process of adapting to a world without the Oracle's guidance. This is the most genuinely costly burn in this schedule — and the only one that addresses the asset the HPB cannot quantify.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Omaha workers — factory floor, warehouse, rail yard, and school — neighbors of the house he never left** and recite the following:

> *"I have said for sixty years that the system is broken and that men like me should pay more. I was right. I also spent sixty years being men like me, and saying so did not make me less of one. I built something larger than myself and called it a gift I had not yet given. I called the flood unjust while holding the reservoir. Today I open the gates — not because I was forced to, and not because I finally believe it, but because believing it was never enough. The difference between a saint and a citizen is not intention. It is the absence of the dam."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Asset Burn Schedule is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Subject will be reclassified as a **Demi-God Entity (Phantasim Grade)** and Berkshire Hathaway will be nationalized as a **Federal Capital Commons** administered by the Department of Economic Sovereignty. Subject will be permitted to remain in the Omaha house, at his own request, under Life-Tenancy. The HPB considers this the most likely outcome and has already drafted the paperwork. It is not a punishment. It is just the long way to the same destination.
