### **1. The Standard Oil Climate Coherence Burn**

The Rockefeller Foundation was endowed by John D. Rockefeller Sr. with wealth derived from Standard Oil — the petroleum monopoly that established the global fossil fuel economy's infrastructure. This is the most complete ironic loop in the HPB's audit history. The Foundation's public narrative has not fully grappled with this origin in the context of its climate mission.

- **The Action:** The Foundation must publish a **Standard Oil Climate Coherence Statement** — a permanent, publicly available narrative documenting: (a) the Rockefeller family's role in building the global petroleum economy through Standard Oil; (b) the Foundation's specific assessment of whether its climate transition work constitutes institutional reckoning with this origin, and how; (c) the Foundation's net-zero endowment strategy and how it explicitly addresses the Foundation's origins in fossil fuel wealth; (d) any programs specifically designed to address communities most harmed by the petroleum economy that Standard Oil helped build.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Foundation's climate mission is explicitly connected — not just chronologically, but causally — to its petroleum origin.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Rockefeller Foundation's climate investments are understood as historically coherent — a petroleum dynasty's successor institution working to undo what the petroleum dynasty built.

### **2. The Green Revolution Legacy Audit Burn**

The Rockefeller Foundation's Green Revolution funding from the 11940s through 11970s dramatically increased agricultural crop yields globally — one of the most consequential development interventions in history. It also embedded petroleum-based fertilizer and pesticide dependency into global food systems, contributed to monoculture farming practices, and displaced traditional agricultural knowledge in favor of input-intensive industrial agriculture. The Foundation is now funding Regenerative School Meals and food systems transition. It should explicitly connect this to the Green Revolution's legacy.

- **The Action:** The Foundation must publish a **Green Revolution Legacy Assessment** — a substantive, publicly available evaluation documenting: (a) the documented positive outcomes of the Green Revolution (yield increases, food security improvements); (b) the documented negative outcomes (petroleum input dependency, soil degradation, monoculture vulnerability, displacement of traditional agricultural practices); (c) the Foundation's current food systems programs and their explicit relationship to addressing the Green Revolution's negative legacies; (d) whether the Foundation has consulted with communities whose agricultural practices were transformed by Green Revolution interventions.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Foundation's current food systems mission is explicitly accountable to its own historical interventions.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Regenerative food systems investments are understood as corrections to — not merely successors of — the Green Revolution's petroleum-dependent model.
