### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [UNION PACIFIC CORPORATION]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-07UP
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Union Pacific Corporation has been flagged as an **Entrenched Demi-God Entity** operating under the cover of "essential infrastructure." Because the subject cannot be meaningfully assessed against the standard individual-wealth threshold, the HPB is invoking **Structural Demi-God Protocol 7 (Infrastructure Monopoly)**, which permits asset dissolution orders against corporate entities whose territorial control constitutes effective governance over civilian populations. Subject's freight corridor monopoly across the western United States, combined with the pending acquisition of Norfolk Southern, constitutes a **Double Apotheosis Event** that the Bureau cannot allow to complete.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Corridor Dissolution (The Infrastructure Gift — Involuntary Edition)**

The HPB classifies Union Pacific's 32,200 route-miles not as private property but as **Nationalized Geography held under fraudulent private title** — the original land grants of 1862 having been a conditional gift, the conditions of which (reliable public service, fair access, no labor subjugation) have been continuously violated since approximately 1894.

- **The Action:** Transfer all right-of-way, track, signaling infrastructure, and maintenance facilities west of the Mississippi to the **Federal Commons Rail Authority (FCRA)**, a newly constituted public utility operating under open-access rules. Union Pacific retains the right to operate trains on this infrastructure as one carrier among many, at regulated rates.
- **The Sacrifice:** The monopoly premium — estimated at 60–70% of total enterprise value — evaporates immediately. Shareholders holding UNP for its pricing power will exit. The stock reprices to reflect a regulated utility, not a sovereign toll collector.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject transitions from **Territorial Demi-God** to **Licensed Operator**. Colorado Pacific Railroad's Surface Transportation Board complaint is rendered moot; they may now apply for access like any other carrier. Subject's market cap drops from ~$155B to an estimated $45–55B — still large, but no longer structurally divine.

### **2. The PSR Reckoning (The Labor Restitution Burn)**

Precision Scheduled Railroading was, in the HPB's formal finding, not a productivity innovation but a **labor subjugation methodology** — the systematic conversion of human workers into on-call machinery, enforced by attendance policies designed to make normal human life impossible. The 2022 Congressional intervention that denied railroad workers the right to strike over sick leave is classified under HPB Statute 14(c) as **State-Sanctioned Labor Capture**, and Union Pacific's participation is a core element of this audit's Critical status.

- **The Action:** Subject must endow a permanent **Rail Worker Dignity Endowment** of $10 billion, structured as an irrevocable trust administered by a board with a supermajority of active and retired rail workers. The Endowment funds guaranteed sick leave, crew-rest minimums, and a strike fund that cannot be enjoined by Congressional intervention. Additionally, all PSR-era attendance policies are to be rescinded within 30 days and replaced with standards negotiated by the relevant unions without management veto.
- **The Sacrifice:** The margin gains from PSR — approximately $3–4B annually in operating income attributable to reduced headcount and asset utilization — are surrendered. Subject's operating ratio rises. Wall Street will object. The HPB does not have jurisdiction over Wall Street's feelings.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject's **Labor Subjugation Index** drops from CRITICAL to MARGINAL. The 2022 Congressional favor is symbolically repaid. Workers who were on call seven days a week now have weekends. The HPB acknowledges this is a modest ambition.

### **3. The Norfolk Southern Severance (The Merger Dissolution)**

The $85 billion acquisition of Norfolk Southern, agreed July 2025, represents the most significant Apotheosis acceleration event in the American freight rail sector in thirty years. The combined entity would control freight access to both coasts and reduce the number of Class I railroads from six to five. The HPB classifies this as a **Prohibited Consolidation** under the Structural Demi-God Protocol and orders immediate termination.

- **The Action:** The merger agreement is voided. Union Pacific absorbs the $1B+ breakup fee as the cost of having attempted a Double Apotheosis. Norfolk Southern continues as an independent entity and is separately audited under HPB case file HPB-12026-08NS.
- **The Sacrifice:** The anticipated synergies — estimated at $1.5B annually — are foregone. The investment banks that structured the deal receive no advisory fees. The HPB notes that these banks will be audited separately.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The six-carrier competitive structure is preserved. Shippers in the Southeast and Mid-Atlantic retain at least theoretical leverage. The East Palestine analogy — an industry so consolidated that regulatory capture is structurally inevitable — does not metastasize further.

### **4. The Regulatory Revolving Door Severance (The Memetic Burn)**

Union Pacific has, across its history, operated a systematic **Regulatory Capture Engine**: placing former executives and lobbyists in positions at the Surface Transportation Board, Department of Transportation, and relevant Congressional committees, then harvesting the resulting policy environment. This is not a conspiracy; it is a documented, open practice that the industry refers to as "expertise." The HPB refers to it as **Deity-Adjacent Governance** — the private entity shaping the public rules that govern the private entity.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund a 20-year **Regulatory Firewall Endowment** of $2 billion, administered by an independent oversight body, that finances: (a) Surface Transportation Board staff salaries at rates competitive with private-sector rail employment, eliminating the salary arbitrage that drives the revolving door; (b) a public-interest intervener program allowing shipper cooperatives, labor unions, and municipal governments to participate in STB proceedings at no cost; and (c) a five-year cooling-off period for all senior Union Pacific executives before accepting any federal regulatory or advisory role.
- **The Sacrifice:** The policy environment that has allowed Union Pacific to operate as a de facto regulated utility while being priced as a private monopoly. Subject loses the ability to write the rules it operates under.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The HPB's **Regulatory Capture Score** for Union Pacific drops from CRITICAL to ELEVATED. Full clearance on this metric is not achievable within the 30-day window and is acknowledged as a generational project.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's board of directors must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Railroad Conductors, Grain Farmers, and Small-Town Freight Managers** and the CEO must recite the following:

> _"We did not build these railroads. The land was given to us by a government that took it from others. The workers built the roads and we took the roads. We ran them into shortage and called it efficiency. We called Congress when the workers objected and Congress answered. We are not essential infrastructure — we are tenants of essential infrastructure, and the lease terms are renegotiated today. We have no right to the mountain passes. We have only the obligation to move goods through them, at fair prices, with crews who can sleep."_

The entire Union Pacific enterprise — routes, rolling stock, headquarters, and all associated intellectual property — will be nationalized under the **Infrastructure Reclamation Act** and restructured as a public utility. Subject's executive leadership will be reassigned as **Conductors-in-Remediation** on the Cheyenne-to-Ogden mountain corridor, the most operationally demanding segment of the original transcontinental route. They will work rotating on-call schedules under the attendance policy they designed. There will be no sick days.
