### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [WANG, WENYIN]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06WW
**Status:** Urgent — Critical Infrastructure / Zero Philanthropy / Extreme Opacity

Subject controls China's dominant copper wire manufacturer. Documented public footprint is near-zero. The HPB proceeds with best available information. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 60 days.

---

### **1. The Transparency Burn (Prerequisite)**

Before substantive Burns can be designed, the HPB needs to know who Wang Wenyin is.

- **The Action:** Subject must file with the HPB a **Personal Accountability Disclosure**: (a) full beneficial ownership structure of Amer International Group and affiliated entities; (b) a personal statement of philanthropic intentions and values; (c) identification of any existing charitable giving not publicly documented; (d) confirmation of residence and primary jurisdictions. This is not voluntary — it is a precondition for recertification.
- **The Sacrifice:** The extraordinary privacy that $19B has purchased.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The HPB cannot assess, audit, or certify a subject it cannot find.

### **2. The Copper Infrastructure Commons Burn**

Copper wire is critical to the global energy transition. Wang's company makes a significant portion of the world's supply. The HPB asks for a public benefit commitment.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Amer Electrification Foundation** — a $500M endowment, independently administered — focused on: (a) expanding affordable copper wire access for electrification projects in Global South countries undertaking rural electrification; (b) funding worker safety research in copper mining communities globally; (c) supporting the development of copper recycling infrastructure to reduce primary mining dependency.
- **The Sacrifice:** $500M; some public visibility about Amer's supply chain positioning.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts control of a critical green transition supply chain into an active contribution to the transition's equity and sustainability.

### **3. The Amer Worker Welfare Burn**

Amer employs a very large manufacturing workforce. Nothing is publicly known about the company's labor practices.

- **The Action:** Publish an **Amer International Worker Welfare Report** — independently reviewed — covering wages relative to Chinese manufacturing sector averages, workplace safety incident rates, supply chain labor standards for raw copper sourcing, and a five-year worker welfare improvement plan.
- **The Sacrifice:** Transparency about a manufacturing company's labor practices.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts China's most invisible major employer into a documented labor accountability case.
