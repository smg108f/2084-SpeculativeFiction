- **The Action:** Publish a binding **US Aldi Supply Chain Ethics Charter**: (a) annual supplier audit covering wages, working conditions, and environmental practices at all private-label suppliers; (b) minimum price floors for produce suppliers protecting against race-to-bottom pricing; (c) a $50M **Aldi Farmer Partnership Fund** supporting small-scale US farmers who supply Aldi with technical assistance, fair pricing practices, and market access.
