### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[LOCKHEED MARTIN, CORPORATION]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-66LM
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject has been classified as the manufacturer of record for the literal hardware of planetary annihilation — fighter jets, intercontinental ballistic missiles, submarine-launched nuclear deterrents, and orbital missile-defense architecture — sustained almost entirely by a single sovereign patron. The HPB finds that no entity this structurally fused to a nation's capacity for civilization-ending violence may continue to exist as a "person" under any legal theory. Recertification is not optional; it is the only alternative to nationalization.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Decommissioning of the F-35 "Sword Vault" (The Largest Burn)**

The F-35 Lightning II program — the single most expensive weapons system ever constructed, with a projected lifetime cost north of $1.7 trillion — is the load-bearing pillar of Subject's Divine Footprint. The HPB classifies it as a **"Potentiality Engine of Apocalypse Scale"**: an asset whose value derives not from what it has done, but from what it could, someday, against someone, be ordered to do.

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer all F-35 design IP, sustainment databases, and proprietary supply-chain access to an **Open Defense Commons**, jointly administered by the Pentagon and a rotating citizen oversight board. Subject retains zero exclusive control.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of an estimated **$40B** in locked-in future sustainment revenue and "predicted dominance" valuation — the single largest Burn in HPB recorded history.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject is reclassified from "Sole Armorer" to "Contracted Maintenance Vendor," ending its co-sovereignty arrangement with the state and severing the "Captured State" violation.

### **2. The Strategic Deterrence Renunciation (Ideological Burn)**

Subject's Missiles and Fire Control division — manufacturer of ICBMs, Trident II D5 submarine-launched nuclear missiles, and reentry vehicles — represents the HPB's gravest concern: a corporation holding, in its own product catalog, the literal means to end the world, euphemized as "deterrence."

- **The Action:** Subject must permanently exit the strategic nuclear weapons and reentry-vehicle business lines, transferring all related contracts, tooling, and personnel to a **state-owned, non-profit Strategic Stewardship Authority** with no shareholder structure of any kind.
- **The Sacrifice:** Total divestment from all nuclear-adjacent revenue streams — an estimated **$28B** in contract-backlog valuation — permanently and without buy-back rights.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject is no longer the entity for whom "the end of the world" is a recurring line item on a quarterly earnings call. This neutralizes the **Apocalypse-Scale Manufacturing** designation entirely.

### **3. The "Deterrence Is Not a Brand Name" Penance (Memetic / Reputational Burn)**

The HPB has determined that Subject's communications strategy — describing weapons of mass destruction in the bureaucratic language of "layered architecture," "strategic stability," and "the Free World's defense umbrella" — constitutes a **Class-III Linguistic Laundering Hazard**, designed to make the unthinkable sound procurement-routine.

- **The Action:** Subject must publish, under its own corporate letterhead and at its own expense, a public **Plain-Language Catalog**, listing every product it manufactures using only terms a fifth-grader would recognize ("a missile that can destroy a city," "a jet that drops bombs," "a satellite that helps aim weapons"). No euphemisms permitted; HPB linguists will audit drafts.
- **The Sacrifice:** The permanent loss of the "deterrence" vocabulary in all future shareholder communications, marketing, and Congressional testimony — Subject may no longer describe its products in terms gentler than their function.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This collapses the **Memetic Insulation Layer** the HPB identified in the Audit, forcing Subject's continued existence (if any) to be evaluated on what it actually makes, not on what it calls what it makes.

### **4. The Single-Customer Dependency Severance (Structural Burn)**

Subject's entire valuation rises and falls with a single government's appropriations calendar — an arrangement the HPB considers indistinguishable from being a state organ wearing a stock ticker as a costume.

- **The Action:** Subject must cap any single national government's share of total revenue at 20%, diversifying remaining capacity toward civil aerospace, disaster-response logistics, and non-weaponized satellite infrastructure, all subject to HPB "Dead-Hand License" review to prevent quiet militarization.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of "Prime Contractor of Record" status across its largest and most lucrative defense programs — an estimated additional **$15B** in annual contract value.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Severs the "co-sovereignty" relationship the Audit identified; Subject becomes a vendor among vendors, not a second branch of government.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's Board of Directors and Chief Executive must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Veterans, Munitions-Plant Line Workers, and Survivors of Conflict Zones Where Subject's Hardware Was Deployed** and recite the following:

> _"We called it deterrence because we were the ones being paid to build it. We called it stability because the order forms came from a government and not a grievance. We see now that the sword does not become a plowshare merely because the hand that forged it wore a contractor's badge. We will make smaller things, for smaller reasons, answerable to people who can refuse them."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All facilities, contracts, and intellectual property will be nationalized without compensation under the post-2034 doctrine, and Subject's executive leadership will be permanently barred from defense, aerospace, or any "Apocalypse-Adjacent Industry" — and personally relocated, under escort, to the **Trinity Site Decommissioning Camp (New Mexico)**, to spend the remainder of their certified lives in the literal shadow of the place where this entire industry's logic was first proven correct.
