### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [WATARI, L.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-16-LW
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification / Epistemic Asymmetry Disclosure Required

L. Watari has been classified MARGINAL ASCENSION RISK based on a $1.8 trillion RTWI, prediction market operations that the HPB believes derive in part from information asymmetries generated by the Wammy's House intelligence network, and his structural position as the operational backbone of the world's most consequential investigative capability. The HPB approaches this plan with the unusual acknowledgment that Subject likely already knows the contents of it, having information advantages the Bureau cannot match. The Bureau is proceeding on the basis that this does not exempt Subject from compliance and that Watari, based on his documented values, will understand why.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. Prediction Market Operations — Information Source Disclosure and Firewall**

The HPB requires that Subject disclose, under a sealed confidential protocol, the information sources that underlie his prediction market operations — specifically, any sources that derive from the Wammy's House network or L's investigative activities. If these sources constitute a material information advantage in the markets Subject participates in, a structural firewall must be established between the network's intelligence output and Subject's personal financial operations.

- **The Action:** Subject must submit a confidential Information Source Disclosure to the HPB's Financial Intelligence Division, documenting all information channels that inform his prediction market positions. Any channel that derives from the Wammy's House network or L's investigative work must be firewalled from his personal investment decisions, with compliance verified by an independent financial monitor with appropriate clearances. Subject may continue prediction market operations using only information available to general market participants.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject forfeits his structural information advantage in financial markets — the advantage that has, the Bureau believes, been the primary driver of his $1.8 trillion RTWI. His prediction accuracy, unassisted by proprietary intelligence, will decline. His returns will decline with it.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Epistemic Asymmetry Risk classification removed. RTWI is expected to decrease significantly over time as the information advantage is eliminated from Subject's financial operations.

---

### **2. Wammy's House Network — Formal Separation of Humanitarian and Intelligence Functions**

The Wammy's House network's dual function — genuine humanitarian institution and intelligence-gathering apparatus — creates a structural conflict of interest that the Bureau requires be formally separated. The humanitarian work must stand on its own, governed by its own accountability structure, without the intelligence function operating within the same institutional shell.

- **The Action:** Subject must formally separate the Wammy's House humanitarian operations — child welfare, education, and support services — from any intelligence-gathering activities, with the humanitarian operations placed under independent nonprofit governance with a public board, transparent financial reporting, and no financial relationship to Subject's prediction market activities. The humanitarian endowment must be funded at $500 billion from Subject's liquid assets, operating independently of Subject's personal financial performance.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses the integrated structure that has made the Wammy's House network simultaneously a humanitarian mission and a strategic asset. The orphanages continue. The intelligence pipeline is formally severed from them.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Wammy's House Infrastructure classified as Genuine Public Good (humanitarian only). RTWI decreases by $500 billion in endowment transfer.

---

### **3. L Support Operations — Public Interest Declaration and HPB Liaison**

Subject's role as L's operational backbone gives him indirect influence over the outcomes of investigations that affect global governance, corporate accountability, and individual fates. The Bureau requires that this role be disclosed to a sealed HPB liaison and that a formal conflict-of-interest protocol be established to ensure that L's investigation priorities are not influenced by Subject's financial positions.

- **The Action:** Subject must file a formal declaration of his role in L's operational support with a sealed HPB liaison, including a description of the nature and scope of his support, the financial relationship between his prediction market operations and L's investigative schedule, and any cases in which Subject's financial interests intersect with L's active investigations. The HPB liaison will review this declaration annually and flag any conflicts for L's independent review.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject discloses a relationship and a potential conflict of interest that he has kept private for decades. L, who values Subject's judgment and partnership, will have access to an independent review of whether that judgment has ever been influenced by Subject's financial interests.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** L Support Influence classification removed from risk profile. The Bureau's confidence in the independence of L's investigations is, for the first time, supported by a structural accountability mechanism rather than Subject's personal integrity alone.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Wammy's House alumni** — drawn from all cohorts, including those who did not become detectives and whose subsequent lives the network did not continue to support — and recite the following:

> _"I built the houses because the children needed them. I also built them because I needed what they would become. Both of these things were true, and I chose not to examine how tightly they were connected. I told myself that since the children benefited, the structure was justified. The Bureau has asked me to separate the two, and I have found that I cannot do it easily — which tells me something I should have examined sooner. The houses will stand. They will stand independently, without my financial interests as the foundation. I hope that makes them stronger. I believe it will."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** The HPB acknowledges that Subject has likely modeled the enforcement escalation path and determined its probability of success. The Bureau notes, for Subject's consideration, that the sealed confidential disclosure in the enforcement scenario includes information that the Bureau's Intelligence Division has independently gathered about Subject's prediction market positions and their correlation with Wammy's House intelligence output — information that Subject may find it preferable to address through the voluntary disclosure process rather than the enforcement one. The Bureau is not making a threat. It is sharing information, which Subject will appreciate as an appropriate method of communication.
