### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [MARRIOTT INTERNATIONAL, INC.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09MAR
**Status:** Precautionary

Marriott's classification reflects structural, not behavioral, risk — its role as an unwitting barometer of travel-class bifurcation rather than any deliberate accumulation strategy. Recertification here is light-touch, focused on transparency rather than asset seizure.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Booking Data Transparency Note**

- **The Action:** Publish an anonymized, aggregate annual report on booking-segment trends (luxury vs. standard vs. government/business) to give policymakers visibility into the travel-economy bifurcation Marriott's earnings calls already describe informally.
- **The Sacrifice:** Minor competitive disclosure of segment performance.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts an incidental social-barometer role into a formally useful public data contribution.

### **2. The Franchise Worker Wage Floor**

- **The Action:** Establish a published minimum wage floor for franchisee-employed housekeeping and service staff across the Marriott brand network, above local statutory minimums in high-cost markets.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced franchisee margin flexibility; estimated $150–200M system-wide cost.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Addresses the labor side of the "two-tier" narrative the luxury-resilience story otherwise obscures.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Marriott's board must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Franchise-Employed Housekeeping Staff** and recite the following:

> _"We noticed, out loud and on an earnings call, that the wealthy still travel freely while the rest travel less. We will not just notice this. We will pay the people who make the traveling possible enough to travel themselves, someday."_
