### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [LORD, MAXWELL]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-28-ML
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice / Telepathic Influence Risk / Enhanced Procedural Protocols Active

Maxwell Lord has been classified CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK. Subject has, in all three preliminary contacts initiated by the Bureau, responded warmly, cooperatively, and with apparent enthusiasm for the recertification process. The Bureau's analysts have reviewed all three responses for signs of telepathic influence. They found none they could prove. The Bureau has accordingly implemented **Enhanced Procedural Protocols** for this plan's drafting: all requirements were finalized before any contact with Subject; no HPB personnel who have met Subject in person reviewed this draft; and the final sign-off was conducted by analysts who have never been in the same room as Maxwell Lord and who were briefed on the above before reading anything Subject has said about the Bureau. The Bureau is not being paranoid. The Bureau is being appropriately cautious about a telepath whose specific capability is making people feel good about agreeing with him.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. Media Empire — Structural Independence Mandate**

Maxwell Lord's $100 billion media empire spans broadcast, digital, and content production. The HPB's Financial Intelligence Division has flagged a statistically anomalous pattern in Lord's acquisition negotiations: target companies have consistently accepted terms that subsequent analysis indicates were unfavorable relative to available alternatives. The Bureau cannot prove telepathic influence was the mechanism. The Bureau can require that future commercial operations occur under structural conditions that do not depend on Subject's goodwill.

- **The Action:** Subject must divest controlling interest (above 20%) in all media properties whose acquisition negotiations have been flagged by the HPB's Financial Intelligence Division — a list of seven specific entities delivered under separate cover — within 30 days, through a court-supervised competitive sale process conducted without Subject's participation in negotiations. Subject may retain minority, non-controlling stakes. Subject may not participate in, attend, or be briefed on negotiation proceedings as they occur.
- **The Sacrifice:** Lord loses control of the acquisitions most likely to reflect commercial advantage derived from his telepathic capability. He retains significant media holdings. He loses the ones the Bureau cannot explain.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** RTWI drops from $100 billion to approximately $65 billion. Flagged-acquisition media holdings removed from personal control. Post-burn media positions subject to quarterly review by an Independent Commercial Conduct Monitor whose appointment Lord does not approve.

---

### **2. Telepathic Will Influence — Cognitive Commerce Prohibition**

The Bureau's analysts have identified that Maxwell Lord's telepathic capability — specifically, the ability to bend others' will toward outcomes of Subject's preference, experienced by targets as their own decision — is categorically distinct from surveillance-class telepathy (see Charles Xavier, HPB-12026-11-CX) and requires a distinct governance framework. Xavier's covenant addresses what Subject may *know*. Lord's covenant addresses what Subject may *cause*.

- **The Action:** Subject must execute a **Cognitive Commerce Prohibition Covenant** — a legally binding instrument, witnessed by a non-Lord-adjacent telepath approved by the HPB, stipulating that Subject will not deploy will-influencing capability in any commercial negotiation, employment decision, media editorial decision, regulatory interaction, or civic proceeding. Subject must submit to annual telepathic verification of Covenant compliance by an HPB-designated independent telepath. Results are filed with the Bureau and are not published; the Bureau is aware of the chilling effect of public disclosure and is balancing it against the audit's requirements.
- **The Sacrifice:** Lord accepts, for the first time, formal prohibition on the use of his most operationally significant capability in the commercial contexts where it is most valuable to him. He also accepts that his compliance is verified by someone else's telepathic access — a condition the Bureau notes Subject may find more discomfiting than it sounds.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Telepathic Embodied Capital classification moved from Unregulated to Governed. The Bureau acknowledges this does not eliminate the capability. The Bureau is governing what it can govern.

---

### **3. JLI Intelligence Archive — Disclosure and Destruction**

Maxwell Lord's founding of the Justice League International generated an operational intelligence asset: detailed knowledge of the capabilities, vulnerabilities, personal relationships, and psychological profiles of the most powerful superhero team of its era, gathered through a relationship premised on shared values. The Bureau's Intelligence Division has concluded that this intelligence has been used in Lord's subsequent activities in ways the heroes involved would not have consented to. The archive is not personal memory — the Bureau cannot require Subject to forget. The archive is, however, documented: files, recordings, operational assessments.

- **The Action:** Subject must disclose the full contents of any JLI-derived intelligence archive — documents, recordings, or digital files — to a joint review panel comprising one HPB analyst and one JLI-era member selected by the Justice League. The panel will determine which materials were gathered through legitimate shared operations (exempt from destruction) and which represent intelligence extracted from trust relationships without consent (subject to destruction with Subject's cooperation in the destruction process). Subject has 30 days to disclose and 60 days to complete panel review.
- **The Sacrifice:** Lord surrenders the operational intelligence advantage his JLI relationship generated — a resource he has held for decades and which the heroes who trusted him do not know he has. He also sits in a room with a JLI-era member who will be reading the archive. The Bureau notes this is not a punishment it designed. It is a consequence of the audit's requirements.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** JLI Legacy Intelligence Exploitation classification resolved. Heroes who trusted Subject receive an accounting of what that trust produced.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Citizens** — the Bureau has specifically requested that the jury be drawn from people who have not previously met, worked with, or been in extended social contact with Maxwell Lord. The HPB's jury selection team will verify this. Subject must recite the following without elaboration, amendment, or, the Bureau notes, eye contact with individual jurors:

> *"I founded the Justice League International because I believed in what it could be, and also because I understood what standing next to it would be worth. Both of those things were true at the same time. I have spent a long time telling the first story and not thinking too hard about the second. The Bureau has thought about the second. The Bureau is correct to have done so. I have an ability that I have used, at times, in ways that people around me did not know about and would not have agreed to. I am acknowledging this because the covenant requires it and because I have concluded, in a room with no one I have ever influenced, that it is also true. I am not asking for credit for the acknowledgment. The Bureau has advised me not to."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Asset Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate standard CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK enforcement procedures. The Bureau notes, for the record, that all enforcement personnel assigned to this case were selected specifically for having no prior contact with Subject; that briefing materials explicitly flagged Maxwell Lord's telepathic capability and its operational characteristics; and that the Bureau has taken the unusual step of placing this note in the public case summary rather than the internal enforcement file, so that it is on record that the HPB was aware of the risk and structured its enforcement accordingly. The Bureau is not accusing Subject of planning anything. The Bureau is, as it has been throughout this audit, being appropriately cautious about a man whose capability is making people feel like they arrived at their own conclusions.
