### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [LOWE'S COMPANIES, INC.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-97LC
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice / Phantom Public Utility Designation

Lowe's Companies has been flagged under the **Phantom Public Utility Protocol** for maintaining a private capital structure over infrastructure that 285,000 workers and the entire American contractor class depend upon for their economic survival. The company's $43B buyback program — executed while median worker pay remained below $33,000 — constitutes a **Temporal Wealth Warp** that must be reversed before the substrate of American domestic life calcifies further into private hands.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Buyback Reversal (The Extraction Engine Burn)**

The HPB classifies Lowe's $43B buyback program (2019–2023) as the defining act of Ascension: capital extracted from worker wages and public-subsidy-dependent suburban infrastructure, compressed into shareholder value for the already-wealthy. This is the engine. It must be dismantled first.

- **The Action:** Lowe's must dissolve its share repurchase authorization and transfer an amount equal to five years of buyback expenditure — indexed at $43B — into a **Home Trades Sovereign Wealth Fund**, administered jointly by an elected board of unionized construction workers, municipal housing authorities, and regional small-business contractors.
- **The Sacrifice:** Institutional shareholders lose the mechanism by which they have extracted the majority of the company's free cash flow. The stock price normalizes to reflect the company's actual productive value rather than its financial engineering value.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The company's capital structure is decoupled from pure shareholder extraction. The Home Trades Fund becomes a genuine Public Good asset, providing grants to independent tradespeople, community repair programs, and affordable housing maintenance. Lowe's RTWI drops by an estimated $40B–$60B in potentiality credits.

---

### **2. The Duopoly Dissolution (The Infrastructure Unlock)**

The HPB cannot permit a private entity to control 28.8% of the U.S. home improvement market in perpetuity — not when the alternative is that ordinary people cannot maintain their homes without enriching a Demi-God entity. Antitrust was the law of the 20th century. Divestiture is the law of 2084.

- **The Action:** Lowe's must divest no fewer than 40% of its retail locations to **Municipal Home Supply Cooperatives** — non-profit entities governed by local residents and contractor-members, with Lowe's retaining supply-chain relationships but not ownership or pricing control. Priority markets: rural and exurban communities where no alternative supplier exists.
- **The Sacrifice:** Lowe's ceases to function as a unitary retail empire and becomes a partial *wholesaler* to its own former stores. The contractor-capture credit system is dissolved; Pro accounts are transferred to the cooperatives, which compete on service rather than lock-in.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Phantom Public Utility designation is lifted in divested markets. The HPB reclassifies Lowe's from **Infrastructural Demi-God** to **Regulated Supply Partner**. Market cap impact: estimated $30B–$50B reduction.

---

### **3. The Marvin Ellison Reckoning (The Narrative Inoculation Burn)**

CEO Marvin Ellison has deployed his biography — poverty in Memphis, corporate ascent against the odds — as a **Narrative Inoculation** against critique of the extraction model he has implemented. The HPB does not dismiss the biography. It takes it seriously enough to hold the subject to it.

- **The Action:** Ellison must establish and personally endow a **$500M Trades Equity Fund** — financed by clawback of his compensation during the buyback years — to provide no-interest capital loans to Black and minority tradespeople seeking to establish independent contracting businesses. The fund must be governed by its recipients, not by Lowe's, Ellison, or any entity he controls. He must step down as CEO and serve a mandatory five-year term as the fund's non-voting Advisor, with no compensation.
- **The Sacrifice:** Ellison loses his platform as corporate executive. His personal narrative is no longer available as a shield for the corporation's labor practices — because it has been converted into a material obligation.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The **Narrative Inoculation Attempt** flag is cleared. The HPB records Ellison as a **Provisional Saint** pending fund performance review in 12031.

---

### **4. The Suburban Dependency Severance (The Sprawl Tithe)**

Lowe's has a structural financial interest in the continued expansion of low-density suburban development — every new subdivision is a captive customer base. This constitutes **Territorial Rent-Seeking at Civilizational Scale** and must be severed.

- **The Action:** Lowe's must tithe 2% of gross annual revenue into a **Compact Development Transition Fund**, earmarked for urban infill housing, walkable neighborhood retrofit, and public transit-adjacent community repair programs — specifically the kinds of housing patterns that reduce per-household demand for Lowe's product categories (smaller homes require less lumber, fewer appliances, less landscaping equipment).
- **The Sacrifice:** The company actively funds the erosion of its own most profitable growth vector: suburban sprawl. This is the point.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The HPB's **Sprawl Feedback Loop** flag is cleared. Lowe's is no longer classified as a *beneficiary of public land-use policy failures*.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Lowe's Companies must convene a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Home Depot Associates, Independent Plumbers, and Rural Hardware Store Owners** — the precise constituencies most harmed by the duopoly — and its CEO must recite the following:

> *"I did not build this. The people who frame the walls and lay the pipes built this. The suburb did not ask for me; I positioned myself between the suburb and its own maintenance. I am not the American Dream. I am the markup on the materials it requires. My company's growth is not evidence of its value — it is evidence of how thoroughly we made ourselves impossible to avoid. I relinquish the toll booth. The road belongs to the people who travel it."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All Lowe's retail locations will be nationalized under the **Phantom Public Utility Seizure Act**, converted into **Federal Home Supply Commons**, and operated at cost. Marvin Ellison will be remanded to a mandatory five-year residency in a rural county with no Lowe's, no Home Depot, and a single independent hardware store — where he will be required to source materials for his own home repairs using the supply chain ecosystem his buyback program helped destroy.
