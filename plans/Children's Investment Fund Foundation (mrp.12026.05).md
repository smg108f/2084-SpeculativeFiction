### **1. The Activist Loop Conflict Disclosure Burn**

TCI Fund Management acquires activist shareholding positions in companies and pressures them to change corporate behavior, generating profits that fund CIFF. CIFF's Say on Climate campaign pressures listed companies to adopt and submit climate plans to shareholder votes. TCI holds positions in companies subject to Say on Climate pressure. Chris Hohn chairs both TCI and CIFF's trustee board. The HPB's Activist Loop Protocol requires that this structural relationship be formally disclosed, assessed for conflicts of interest, and subject to an independent review.

- **The Action:** CIFF must commission and publish an independent **Activist Loop Conflict Assessment** — evaluating: (a) whether TCI's investment positions have materially benefited from CIFF's Say on Climate campaigns, or vice versa; (b) whether CIFF's grant-making decisions in climate advocacy have been influenced, directly or indirectly, by TCI's investment strategy; (c) what formal conflict-of-interest policies exist between TCI and CIFF and whether they are adequate; (d) whether the current governance structure — with Hohn chairing both — is appropriate given the structural overlap.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Foundation publicly subjects its most consequential structural relationship to independent scrutiny. The assessment may find conflicts of interest that require restructuring.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Say on Climate campaign's charitable legitimacy is affirmed or corrected based on independent evidence. The loop becomes disclosed, not assumed benign.

### **2. The Governance Separation Burn**

Chris Hohn serves as Chair of CIFF's trustee board while simultaneously managing TCI Fund Management, the hedge fund that funds CIFF. This is not prohibited by UK charity law. It is, in the HPB's assessment, structurally inappropriate for a foundation whose programmatic activities intersect with its funder's commercial strategy.

- **The Action:** CIFF must implement a **Governance Separation Protocol** — within 12 months of this order: (a) Chris Hohn must either step down as Chair of CIFF trustees or formally recuse himself from all CIFF decisions related to climate advocacy, Say on Climate, and any grantmaking that could reasonably affect TCI's investment positions; (b) the Foundation must appoint a majority-independent trustee board (currently undefined); (c) an independent Chair must be appointed to lead CIFF's climate program governance.
- **The Sacrifice:** Hohn's direct control over the Foundation that bears his philanthropic vision is constrained. He may continue as a trustee and funder; he may not simultaneously chair the institution and manage its funding source.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** CIFF's climate programs become structurally independent from TCI's investment strategy, allowing the charitable work to be evaluated on its own merits.

### **3. The US Advocacy Accountability Burn**

Between 12014 and 12023, CIFF sent $553M to US political and environmental advocacy organizations. A 12025 watchdog report revealed this spending; CIFF subsequently announced it would withdraw from US grantmaking. The HPB requires a complete public accounting of this spending before CIFF's US advocacy record is closed.

- **The Action:** CIFF must publish a **US Advocacy Expenditure Accounting** — documenting: (a) the full list of US organizations that received CIFF funding between 12014–12023; (b) the categorization of each grant as charitable advocacy, political expenditure, or civic/educational; (c) the Foundation's assessment of whether any grants exceeded the boundaries of charitable activity under UK charity law; (d) the findings of any internal or external review commissioned in response to the watchdog report.
- **The Sacrifice:** The $553M in US spending becomes publicly legible rather than acknowledged only in response to investigative reporting.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Foundation's US withdrawal decision is documented as a principled response to legal and reputational concerns, not an avoidance of accountability.
