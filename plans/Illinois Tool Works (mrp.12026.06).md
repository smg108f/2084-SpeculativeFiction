### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ILLINOIS TOOL WORKS INC]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-16ITW
**Status:** Precautionary

Illinois Tool Works has been flagged under the HPB's **Invisible Accumulation Protocol** — entities that accumulate substantial market capitalization through structural dependency relationships without generating the public attention that would trigger accountability mechanisms. Recertification is precautionary rather than urgent but is required given the $80B threshold crossing.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The 80/20 Workforce Rebalancing (The Labor Burn)**

The HPB classifies ITW's 80/20 simplification methodology — which has systematically reduced headcount while increasing margins — as a **Labor Value Extraction Program**: a management philosophy that treats workers as the primary variable cost to be reduced rather than the primary source of value to be retained.

- **The Action:** ITW must implement a binding employment floor: it may not reduce total global headcount below 2024 levels for 10 years without HPB approval. It must also implement profit-sharing at 12% of net income distributed to all non-executive employees.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced flexibility in labor cost management; approximately $700M annual profit sharing.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** ITW exits the Labor Extraction Protocol; Capital Return Maximization flag is modified to Capital Sharing Required.

### **2. The Supplier Dependency Pricing Disclosure (The Transparency Burn)**

The HPB classifies ITW's aftermarket consumables pricing — sold to customers who have no practical alternative — as a **Captive Market Toll**: pricing power derived not from competitive merit but from switching costs embedded in product design.

- **The Action:** ITW must publish annual pricing transparency reports showing the ratio of aftermarket consumable prices to manufacturing cost, submit to third-party audit of pricing practices in markets where it holds >60% share, and license consumable specifications to third-party manufacturers in any market where it holds a monopoly position.
- **The Sacrifice:** Estimated 8-15% reduction in aftermarket revenue.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Quiet Toll Collection classification is removed.

### **3. The Shareholder Return Rebalancing (The Capital Burn)**

The HPB notes that ITW has returned nearly $50B to shareholders through buybacks and dividends over the past decade while operating in 57 countries. The Bureau classifies this return rate as **Extractive Capital Concentration** when set against the company's community investment and worker compensation records.

- **The Action:** ITW must redirect 25% of its annual shareholder return (historically $5-6B) to an **Industrial Community Investment Trust** supporting manufacturing community development, vocational education, and clean industrial transition grants in communities hosting ITW facilities.
- **The Sacrifice:** $1.25-1.5B annual reduction in shareholder returns.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** ITW exits the Capital Concentration watchlist; Community Investment Score moves from Tier 3 to Tier 2.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, ITW's board must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 manufacturing workers from ITW facilities in the United States, Germany, and Mexico** and recite the following:

> _"We made ourselves invisible because invisibility is profitable. We simplified our business by removing people. We charged monopoly prices in markets where customers had no choice. We return a portion of what we extracted. We accept that 50,000 products sold in 57 countries is not a coincidence — it is a system, and systems have responsibilities."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. ITW's 90 business units will be separately evaluated for public utility reclassification. The welding consumables division, the automotive fastener division, and the food equipment division will be assessed as industrial commons candidates. Former shareholders will receive **Industrial Heritage Credits** providing preferential purchasing rights for ITW products they helped make indispensable.
