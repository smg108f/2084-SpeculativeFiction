### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [PLATTNER, HASSO]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06HP
**Status:** Moderate

Subject's philanthropic record is among the better in Batch 5. Burns are calibrated to address the SAP bribery settlement's affected jurisdictions and scale up existing work.

---

### **1. The SAP Bribery Restitution Burn**

SAP paid $222M in bribery fines for payments made in South Africa, Indonesia, and other countries. The fines went to regulators. The countries whose governance was corrupted received nothing specific. Subject's personal obligation addresses this gap.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund $100M for the **SAP Bribery Restitution Fund** — directed personally (separate from SAP's corporate settlement) to: (a) anti-corruption institutional capacity building in South Africa and Indonesia specifically — funding independent anti-corruption agencies, civil society organizations, and investigative journalism in both countries; (b) a public acknowledgment, from Plattner personally as supervisory board chairman during the bribery period, of what SAP did in those countries and what his individual responsibility is.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M.

### **2. The South Africa Healthcare Scale-Up**

The existing HIV/AIDS philanthropy in South Africa is the most creditable element of this audit. The HPB invites its formalization.

- **The Action:** Subject must permanently endow $75M for the **Plattner Foundation South Africa** — ensuring the HIV/AIDS and healthcare programs outlast subject's active management, with an independent board and published annual reporting on health outcomes.
- **The Sacrifice:** $75M (additional to existing commitments).
