### **2. The Conservation Endowment Burn**

Christy Walton's conservation giving in Wyoming and Montana is genuine and earns credit. The HPB asks for endowment-level commitment.

- **The Action:** Subject must permanently endow her conservation work — $100M dedicated to the **Greater Yellowstone Conservation Endowment** (administered by existing conservation partners in the Greater Yellowstone ecosystem), ensuring land protection and wildlife corridor preservation in the Wyoming/Montana region she has supported throughout her philanthropic life.

### **3. The Public Education Balance Burn**

Charter school advocacy has diverted philanthropic attention from public school systems. The HPB requires balance.

- **The Action:** Subject must commit $75M to **Wyoming and Montana public school systems** — specifically rural public schools in the states where her conservation focus lies, funding: (a) technology infrastructure in rural public schools; (b) teacher salary supplements in under-resourced school districts; (c) school library programs. - **The Sacrifice:** $75M.
