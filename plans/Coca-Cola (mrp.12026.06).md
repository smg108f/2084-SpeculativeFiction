### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [THE COCA-COLA COMPANY]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-40CC
**Status:** Precautionary — Global Sugar Distribution Infrastructure / Public Health Obligation

Coca-Cola's RTWI of ~$330B places it at **330 times the Demi-God threshold**. MARGINAL classification — precautionary Burns calibrated to leverage Coca-Cola's distribution capabilities for public health rather than against it.

The following **Voluntary Commitment Schedule** must be confirmed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Distribution Network Public Health Commitment**

Coca-Cola's distribution network reaches more locations in more countries than any other private entity on Earth. The Bureau requests a formal commitment to use this network for public health purposes at scale.

- **The Action:** Coca-Cola commits to dedicating 5% of its annual distribution network capacity to **Public Health Priority Products** — oral rehydration salts, water purification tablets, essential nutrition supplements, and vaccines requiring cold chain logistics — at cost in markets where governmental distribution infrastructure is insufficient. The program is co-designed with WHO and UNICEF and independently audited.
- **Voluntary Sacrifice:** 5% of distribution capacity is reallocated from commercial products to public health products.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The most effective private distribution network on Earth formally accepts a public health mandate proportionate to its reach.

---

### **2. The Portfolio Reformulation Commitment**

Coca-Cola has made sugar reduction commitments. The Bureau requests they be bound and extended.

- **The Action:** Coca-Cola commits to a binding **Portfolio Reformulation Standard** — by 12030, a minimum of 50% of Coca-Cola's global beverage volume by serving meets WHO sugar guidelines (less than 25g added sugar per serving). New product launches in markets with high Type 2 diabetes prevalence must meet the WHO guideline as a mandatory condition, not a voluntary choice.
- **Voluntary Sacrifice:** Core revenue is partially shifted toward lower-margin reduced-sugar products.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The entity that delivers 2 billion servings daily moves its portfolio toward global health standards.

---

### **3. The Sugar Tax Neutrality Commitment**

Coca-Cola's lobbying against sugar taxes in multiple markets has delayed public health interventions that peer-reviewed evidence supports. The Bureau requires neutrality.

- **The Action:** Coca-Cola commits to **Sugar Tax Neutrality** — it will not fund, directly or through trade associations, political or regulatory campaigns opposing evidence-based sugar taxes or front-of-pack nutrition labeling requirements in any Humanist Bloc market.
- **Voluntary Sacrifice:** Coca-Cola loses a lobbying tool that has protected its core revenue from public health regulation.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Public health policy on sugar-sweetened beverages is made on scientific evidence rather than corporate lobbying.

---

### **THE "MORTAL" CONFIRMATION**

To finalize recertification, Coca-Cola's CEO must stand before a **Jury of 12 Public Health Nurses in Countries With High Diabetes Rates, Community Water Access Advocates, and Distribution Workers** and confirm:

> _"We have 2 billion servings per day in 200 countries. The distribution network is extraordinary and it has been used to sell sugar. The 5% commitment goes to oral rehydration salts and vaccines and clean water. The portfolio moves toward the WHO guidelines. The lobbying against sugar taxes ends. We are the most effective distribution infrastructure in private hands. We accept that this comes with obligations beyond our shareholders."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** Sugar Tax Neutrality imposed by Humanist Bloc trade condition. Portfolio Reformulation Standard imposed by FDA nutrition disclosure requirement. Distribution Network Public Health commitment required as condition of bottler licensing in markets receiving international development assistance.
