### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[WOLFF, TOTO]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-08TW
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Per Apotheosis Audit REF. 12026.08, Subject Wolff's fortune (~$2.7B) sits nearly three times the Ascension threshold with no proportionate "Saintly Pivot" on file, and his household holds concurrent governance influence over both Formula 1's dominant works team and its official developmental pipeline. To retain **Human Citizen Status**, Subject must execute the following **Asset Burn Schedule** within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Mercedes-AMG Petronas Equity Divestment (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies controlling and near-controlling equity in a $6B global sporting franchise as a **"Podium Concentration Hazard"** — a single asset whose valuation is driven by streaming-era narrative economics rather than fundamentals, and which grants its holder disproportionate influence over one of the world's most-watched sports.

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer his voting equity in the Mercedes-AMG Petronas F1 Team into the **Silverstone Commons Trust**, a fan- and employee-governed vehicle. He may retain his operational title and day-to-day authority as **Team Principal Custodian**, but forfeits all capital appreciation rights, dividends, and future sale proceeds.
- **The Sacrifice:** An estimated $1.6B–$2.0B in equity value, plus any future upside from further stake sales at rising team valuations.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject becomes a **paid operator**, not an **owner** — the HPB's preferred "Caretaker" classification. His name stays on the pit wall; his name comes off the cap table.

### **2. The Satellite Portfolio Liquidation**

Subject's holdings outside the team — Marchfifteen and Marchsixteen Investments, the 0.95% Aston Martin Lagonda stake, HWA AG, BRR Rallye Racing, and the Häkkinen-partnered sports management agency — constitute a diversified private hedge against exactly the kind of forced divestment this Plan requires.

- **The Action:** Full liquidation, proceeds directed into a new **Grassroots Motorsport Access Fund**, providing karting scholarships and mechanic apprenticeships to applicants with no prior industry connections.
- **The Sacrifice:** An estimated $350M–$450M in private holdings.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Closes the "quiet reinvestment" loophole — Subject cannot simply rebuild a demi-god-scale position through side vehicles while his primary asset is held in trust.

### **3. The Wolff-Wolff Firewall (Household Governance Burn)**

The HPB does not audit marriages, but it does audit **combined household influence**, and the 2023–2024 FIA confidentiality dispute demonstrated that this household treats "team business" and "governance business" as porous categories.

- **The Action:** F1 Academy's governance, funding, and personnel decisions must be placed under an irrevocable, independently audited firewall — legally and financially separated from any Mercedes team interest, with Subject barred from any advisory or financial role in F1 Academy for the duration of his Mercedes tenure.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses any claim to F1 Academy as part of the "family portfolio"; the household's combined influence over the sport's top tier and its development pipeline is formally severed.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the **Household Governance Capture** flag. Susie Wolff's work stands on its own record, unentangled from her husband's equity position.

### **4. The Horner Amnesty (Reputational Burn)**

Subject's public persona — built on marginal-gains ruthlessness and a decade of open hostility toward rival principals, most notably Christian Horner — is logged as a **Class-III Competitive Escalation Hazard**, incompatible with the humility standard expected of Human Citizens.

- **The Action:** Subject must issue a public, unconditional reconciliation statement to Horner and all rival team principals, retire all "rival-diminishment" rhetoric from public commentary, and fund a **$50M Grassroots Karting Access Initiative** in lieu of further feuding.
- **The Sacrifice:** The competitive-antagonist public image that has defined his career; $50M in additional philanthropic commitment.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the Deity-Adjacent "I decide who wins" rhetoric pattern flagged in the Audit.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Karting Instructors and Volunteer Grid Marshals** and recite the following:

> _"I have spent my life shaving tenths from a lap. I now surrender the stopwatch. I am not the fastest car on the grid, the richest man on the pit wall, or the most ruthless in the room — and for the first time, I do not need to be. My worth is not a delta to be minimized.
