### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [WAYNE, BRUCE]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-26-BW
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice / Coordinated with HPB-12026-07-WE / Personal Arsenal Disclosure Required

Bruce Wayne has been classified CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK. This plan is issued in coordination with the Wayne Enterprises corporate recertification plan (HPB-12026-07-WE). Together, the two plans address the full scope of Subject's wealth concentration — the company and the man, the Batcave and the boardroom. The Bureau notes that this plan has been prepared with the expectation that Subject already knows it is coming, has read a preliminary version, and has prepared a response. The Bureau has prepared a response to his response.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. Batman Arsenal — Registered Nonprofit Transfer with Operational Continuity**

Bruce Wayne's personal military arsenal — vehicles, suits, weapons systems, and surveillance infrastructure — constitutes a private military capability the HPB cannot allow to remain as personal property at this scale. The Bureau does not require that the Batman operation cease. It requires that the infrastructure supporting it be structured as a public safety institution rather than a personal possession.

- **The Action:** All Batman operational assets must be transferred to the Gotham Public Safety Technology Foundation (established under HPB-12026-07-WE), which will hold them in trust for use in public safety operations under a governance framework that includes Batman as the designated operational director — but not as the asset owner. Batman continues operating. Batman's equipment is no longer Bruce Wayne's personal property.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses personal ownership of the most distinctive personal military collection in the DC Universe. He retains operational control as director of the GPSTF. He loses the ability to use, modify, or expand the arsenal without GPSTF board oversight.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Personal Military Arsenal classification removed from personal RTWI. Asset transfers approximately $8 billion out of personal holdings. Batman becomes, legally, a public safety institution.

---

### **2. Personal Real Estate Portfolio — Partial Community Land Trust Transfer**

Subject's personal real estate holdings — Wayne Manor and associated properties, plus holdings in twelve cities — represent a dynastic concentration that the Bureau requires be partially redistributed. The Manor may be retained as Subject's personal residence under a Life Tenancy arrangement; the broader portfolio must be reduced.

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer 50% of his non-residential real estate holdings (by value) to the Wayne Foundation's newly independent Community Land Trust (established under HPB-12026-07-WE), retaining Wayne Manor and personal residences under a Life Tenancy Agreement with the Foundation. The remaining commercial holdings are retained pending continued review.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject reduces his real estate footprint by approximately $25 billion. Wayne Manor remains his home. His city-spanning property portfolio does not remain his investment.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** RTWI drops from $100 billion to approximately $67 billion. Continued burns required.

---

### **3. End Condition — The Promise He Hasn't Made**

The Bureau's final requirement is not financial. It is the one structural gap in twenty-plus years of Batman's operations: there is no defined end condition. No measurable goal whose achievement would permit Bruce Wayne to stop. No framework for determining when the work is done. The Bureau cannot audit an indefinite personal project and classify it as compliant — not because the project is wrong, but because an indefinitely ongoing deployment of personal wealth toward a personal objective, without external accountability or a completion framework, is exactly what the Act was designed to address.

- **The Action:** Subject must file a Batman Mission Charter with the HPB — a document defining: (1) the measurable outcomes that would constitute success sufficient to trigger a formal review of whether the Batman operation should continue, be restructured, or transition to a successor; (2) the succession framework if Subject is incapacitated; and (3) a 10-year strategic review process in which an independent panel — appointed jointly by the HPB and Gotham City's elected government — assesses progress against the Mission Charter's goals and recommends continuation, restructuring, or transition.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject commits, for the first time in two decades, to the possibility that the Batman might be done. Not the certainty — the possibility. He accepts that an external body might look at his work and say: this has accomplished what it set out to accomplish, and it is time to build something else.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Indefinite Personal Mission classification resolved. The Batman operation becomes, for the first time, a project with a completion framework rather than a permanent personal commitment funded by private wealth.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Gotham City residents** — specifically drawn from people who have never met Batman and whose understanding of him is entirely secondhand — and recite the following:

> _"I made a promise to my parents when I was eight years old. I have kept that promise for thirty years. I am proud of that. I am also aware that the boy who made it did not understand what it would cost — not just me, but the city I was promising to save. I made Gotham dependent on one man's willingness to suffer for it. That is not a safe city. That is a city that has outsourced its safety to a grief. I cannot end the grief. I can end the outsourcing. The Mission Charter is my acknowledgment that Gotham deserves an institution, not a wound. I am working on building one. The Bureau is helping me. I would not have asked for the help. I needed it anyway."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Asset Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate standard CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK enforcement procedures, including forced personal asset seizure above the $1 billion threshold, coordinated with the Wayne Enterprises enforcement action under HPB-12026-07-WE. The Bureau notes that it has full knowledge of the Batcave's location and has chosen not to include this fact in the enforcement planning documents as a show of good faith. The Bureau would prefer to keep it that way.
