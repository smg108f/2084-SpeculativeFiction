### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [WANG, LIPING]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06WL2
**Status:** Moderate

Subject's industrial manufacturing wealth lacks commensurate philanthropic or community investment. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 180 days.

---

### **1. The Industrial Community Burn**

Hengli Hydraulic's manufacturing base in Changzhou has generated significant industrial activity and associated environmental and community impacts.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund ¥500M (~$70M) for the **Hengli Industrial Community Fund**: (a) ¥200M for environmental remediation and air quality monitoring around Hengli's Changzhou manufacturing facilities, with results published publicly; (b) ¥200M for worker welfare — specifically skills upgrading and retraining for factory workers, living wage auditing, and occupational health programs for hydraulic fluid and metalworking exposure; (c) ¥100M for Changzhou community infrastructure (public parks, healthcare access, affordable housing) adjacent to Hengli's manufacturing sites.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥500M.

### **2. The Supply Chain Transparency Burn**

Hengli's hydraulic components reach global markets through Chinese OEM customers. The end-use contexts include some that require transparency.

- **The Action:** Subject must implement and fund a **Hengli Supply Chain Transparency Program**: (a) annual public reporting on all major OEM customers and their end-use markets, with sufficient detail to allow assessment of where Hengli equipment ultimately operates; (b) ¥100M for an **Industrial Ethics Institute** (based in China, internationally partnered) focused on supply chain due diligence standards for industrial component manufacturers; (c) adoption of an explicit policy that Hengli will not accept orders from customers seeking equipment confirmed to be used in forced labor facilities.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥100M.
