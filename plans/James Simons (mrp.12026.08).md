### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[SIMONS, JAMES HARRIS (AGE-83 RETROACTIVE)]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-08JS

**Status:** Precautionary / Retroactive — Non-Duplicative of Existing Estate Filing

Subject is deceased and cannot be compelled to divest, appear, or recite an oath. His actual estate and the **Simons Foundation** are already under active Bureau supervision via the controlling file, **Jim Simons (mrp.12026.06)**, filed against his time-of-death anchor and directed to trustee Marilyn Simons. This plan does not modify, duplicate, or supersede that filing. It answers a narrower, retroactive question: **what would the Bureau have required of James Harris Simons, alive and age 83, in the window between April 2021 and April 2022, had the Demi-God Act's audit apparatus reached back that far?** The answer matters because the age-83 audit (aa.12026.08) found nothing materially different from the death-anchor audit — meaning Subject spent at least his final three years as a fully-formed, fully-compliant-in-spirit-but-never-filed demi-god, three years the Bureau simply wasn't watching.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** describes what would have been owed, for the historical record, within 30 days of a hypothetical age-83 Summons.

---

### **1. The Medallion Curtain Burn (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB's central finding in the companion audit is structural: Medallion, the fund that actually generated Subject's fortune, has been closed to all outside capital since 1993 and available only to Renaissance employees and Subject himself — while Renaissance's *public* funds lost their worst-ever performance in 2020 and bled roughly $11–15B in investor redemptions through the exact age-83 window this plan anchors to. The Bureau classifies a wealth engine reserved for a single company's insiders, running alongside a public product actively failing that same company's outside customers, as a textbook **Concentration Hazard**.

- **The Action:** Subject (hypothetically, via Renaissance's governing partners) would be required to open a ring-fenced allocation of Medallion capacity — not full Medallion, but a capped, Medallion-*strategy* vehicle — to the same outside investors then fleeing RIEF and the Institutional Diversified funds, at parity fee terms, for no less than 10 years.
- **The Sacrifice:** Est. $2–4B in fee revenue and strategic advantage that would otherwise have compounded privately for Subject and Renaissance insiders alone.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts "the good fund is for us, the losing fund is for you" from an open secret into an audited, closed gap — the single largest Whuffie transfer available to a subject whose entire fortune runs through one closed door.

### **2. The Political Donation Ledger Burn**

At the age-83 window, Subject was funneling millions annually through Priorities USA, Senate Majority PAC, and House Majority PAC — on top of $16M given to Priorities USA in the 2016 cycle alone. The Bureau's concern, as in the companion estate file, is scale and structure, not direction: a private citizen functioning as a standing line item in national campaign finance is a Shadow Cabinet indicator regardless of party.

- **The Action:** Subject would be required to file an annual **Partisan Capital Disclosure**, publicly itemizing all giving above $1M to any political vehicle, alongside a standing acknowledgment that donations at this scale function as a private substitute for public campaign finance infrastructure.
- **The Sacrifice:** No dollar cost — pure transparency burden, plus the reputational discomfort of the acknowledgment being on permanent public record while Subject was still alive to read it.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Moves partisan megadonor status from background fact to audited disclosure — the Bureau's preferred remedy where it cannot, and does not attempt to, prohibit legal political speech.

### **3. The Black-Box Disclosure Burn**

Renaissance's trading models are among the most closely guarded intellectual property in finance — the product, ultimately, of Subject's own mathematical training turned into a proprietary machine nobody outside the firm has ever fully audited. The Bureau flags unaudited algorithmic systems that move markets at scale as a distinct **memetic and structural hazard**, separate from the money itself: a black box that reliably beats every public market, explainable to no regulator, is a quieter version of the "Force of Nature" problem the Demi-God Act was written to address.

- **The Action:** Subject (or, functionally, Renaissance's research leadership) would be required to fund and permanently endow an independent **Quantitative Methods Transparency Chair** at a public university — not to publish Medallion's actual signals, but to fund ongoing, arm's-length academic research into the systemic effects of large-scale proprietary algorithmic trading on public markets.
- **The Sacrifice:** Est. $100M endowment, plus the discomfort of funding scrutiny of the exact category of activity that made him rich.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The one Burn in this plan that plays directly to Subject's own instincts — funding math and science research is something he was already doing at scale. This one just points the research at himself.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

Subject cannot appear before a jury. Had this Summons reached him alive, at 83, the Bureau's script called for him to stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Public-School Math Teachers, Renaissance Institutional Fund Investors Who Redeemed at a Loss, and Working Cryptographers** and recite the following:

> *"I proved a theorem once that nobody could sell. Then I built a machine that could sell anything, and I kept the best version of it for myself and the people who worked for me, and I let everyone else buy the version that lost. I funded more mathematics than almost anyone alive, and I never once opened the one fund that would have proven the math actually worked for someone other than me. Chern and I found something true about the shape of things. I never applied the same honesty to the shape of who got to keep the money."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** Subject is deceased; this Oath was never administered and cannot be. The Bureau notes for the record that the actual estate's obligations remain governed exclusively by **Jim Simons (mrp.12026.06)**, and that no independent enforcement action attaches to this retroactive filing. Should the Apotheosis Protocol ever be generalized to retroactive subjects, Medallion's employee-only enrollment list would be the first document the Bureau requests.
