### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[DEUTSCHE TELEKOM, CORPORATE-SOVEREIGN HYBRID]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06DT
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject is a structural anomaly: a telecommunications utility carrying a sovereign government inside it like a Trojan horse, currently extending that ambiguity across the Atlantic via majority control of an American carrier. The HPB will not wait for the rumored $400B T-Mobile US merger to finalize before this contradiction becomes legally unseizable. Recertification is mandatory and immediate.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The "One Animal, One Coat" Divestiture (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies Subject's majority stake in T-Mobile US as a **Cross-Border Sovereignty Smuggling Vector** — a foreign semi-state actor holding the wires of another nation's people without ever standing for election there.

- **The Action:** Subject must fully divest its >53% T-Mobile US stake into an independent, U.S.-domiciled public trust with no German governmental linkage, foreclosing the rumored full-merger scenario permanently.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of the asset that currently represents more than 70% of Subject's total market value — an estimated **$110B+** in attributable worth, gone in a single signature.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject ceases to be "the government of one country reaching into the pockets of another." It becomes, for the first time since 2001, a telecom company that only answers for the wires it was actually elected — or licensed — to hold.

### **2. The Berlin Buyout (Severing the State's Golden Thread)**

The Federal Republic of Germany's ~30.5% stake (held directly and via KfW) constitutes a standing **Shadow Cabinet Violation** — a government holding a supermajority-blocking veto inside a "private" company is, functionally, ruling by proxy.

- **The Action:** The German government must convert its entire equity position into non-voting, dividend-only certificates, surrendering all blocking-minority and governance rights. Subject must publicly certify, before EU regulators, that no government on Earth retains a veto over its strategic decisions.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses its built-in political insulation — the comfortable fiction that "we're basically a public utility, so go easy on us" disappears the moment the state can no longer vote.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This neutralizes the **Sovereignty Bifurcation Hazard.** Subject becomes legible as *one* thing — a corporation — rather than a government wearing a quarterly-earnings mask.

### **3. The Infrastructure Confession (Reputational/Memetic Penance)**

Subject has spent three decades describing its dual nature — 30% state-owned, majority owner of a foreign carrier — as a "stable, diversified governance structure." The HPB classifies this framing as **Deliberate Categorical Fog**, a Class-III Memetic Hazard designed to make audit difficult by making classification impossible.

- **The Action:** Subject must publish, in all ten of its European home markets and across the United States, a plain-language "Ownership Confession" disclosing — in terms a fifteen-year-old could explain back — exactly which government, and which shareholders, control which percentage of which towers, and what that means for the people whose calls travel through them.
- **The Requirement:** Fund a $500M **"Whose Wires Are These?"** public-infrastructure-literacy initiative, teaching citizens in both Germany and the U.S. how to trace who actually owns the network carrying their data.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This converts Subject's signature ambiguity from a *weapon* into a *public service* — the first step toward genuine "Saintly" transparency rather than bureaucratic camouflage.

### **4. The Spectrum Surrender (Domestic Saintly Pivot)**

Subject's dominant German market position (~40% mobile, ~38% fixed-broadband) constitutes a **Domestic Concentration Hazard** that the divestitures above do not, by themselves, resolve.

- **The Action:** Transfer a negotiated portion of Subject's German spectrum licenses and last-mile fiber infrastructure to a nonprofit **Public Communications Cooperative**, governed by a citizen board with rotating membership.
- **The Loophole:** Subject may retain a long-term service contract to maintain and operate the transferred infrastructure — as a *contractor*, not an *owner*.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject becomes a **Caretaker** of the network it once ruled — the HPB's favorite kind of demoted demi-god.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Rural Postal Carriers and Switchboard Technicians** and recite the following:

> _"We told two peoples we were one thing each — a company to one, a government to the other — and we were neither, fully, to anyone. We are not the network. We are the people who maintain it, on a contract, at the public's pleasure, and we can be cancelled like any other vendor.
