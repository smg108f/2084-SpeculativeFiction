### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [SINOPEC]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-02SI
**Status:** Deferred — Sovereign Referral Pending

Sinopec's classification as a State-Embedded Carbon Sovereign places it outside the standard Mortal Recertification pathway. However, the HPB requires a contingency Burn Schedule for the scenario in which the International Human Dignity Tribunal declines jurisdiction — at which point Sinopec's Deferred Ascension status converts automatically to an active Pre-Seizure Notice.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days of Tribunal declination.

---

### **1. The Refinery Relinquishment (The Carbon Infrastructure Transfer)**

The HPB classifies Sinopec's refining capacity as **Legacy Combustion Infrastructure** — physical plant whose continued operation imposes costs on future generations without their consent. The 252 million metric tons of annual crude processing represents the HPB's largest single Class-III Temporal Hazard on record.

- **The Action:** Sinopec must transfer operational control of all refineries processing greater than 10 million metric tons annually to a **Global Energy Transition Trust**, a multilateral body with a charter mandate to wind down capacity by 2.5% annually. The Trust pays Sinopec 40% of appraised value over 20 years — the temporal deferral reflecting the temporal nature of the harm.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of approximately $45B in refining asset value and the strategic control that makes Sinopec indispensable to Chinese state energy policy.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Reclassification from "Carbon Sovereign" to "Managed Wind-Down Partner" — a status that earns significant HPB credit while preserving near-term energy security.

### **2. The Petrol Station Conversion (The Retail Energy Pivot)**

Sinopec's 30,000 petrol stations represent the most geographically distributed piece of fossil infrastructure in the world. The HPB classifies this network as both a threat and an opportunity: converted to hydrogen, charging, and food access nodes, the same infrastructure that created carbon lock-in could facilitate decarbonization.

- **The Action:** Sinopec must commit 35% of annual CapEx to retrofitting petrol stations as multi-energy hubs within 10 years, with community ownership stakes offered to the neighborhoods each station serves.
- **The Sacrifice:** Diversion of capital from upstream petroleum projects; acceptance of lower per-unit margins from multi-energy retail.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The HPB will reclassify the station network from "Withdrawal Infrastructure" to "Transition Infrastructure" — a designation that removes the Class-III Temporal Hazard from Sinopec's audit record.

### **3. The Climate Pledge Formalization (The Paris Penance)**

Sinopec's failure to meet minimum Paris Agreement alignment standards — documented by Carbon Tracker and cited in this audit — represents a specific, measurable, and correctable HPB violation. This burn requires no asset transfer, only an enforceable commitment.

- **The Action:** Sinopec must submit a binding climate roadmap to the HPB's **Temporal Harm Registry**, setting hard emissions reduction targets verified by an independent international auditor, with financial penalties payable to a **Future Citizens' Climate Fund** for any year of non-compliance.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of capital allocation flexibility; exposure to binding financial penalties.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removal from the HPB's "Planetary Negligence" watchlist. The HPB notes this burn is the minimum required; it earns no Whuffie — only the cessation of active demerit accumulation.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Coal Workers' Children and Delta Farmers** and recite the following:

> *"The oil belonged to the earth before it belonged to the state. The carbon belongs to the atmosphere, and the atmosphere belongs to everyone who breathes. We burned the future to fuel the present, and we acknowledge the debt. We will spend the remainder of our operational life repaying it — not because we were forced, but because the children standing in the flooded fields have names."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed within 30 days of Tribunal declination, all Sinopec assets will be placed under **Planetary Emergency Receivership** and administered by the HPB's **Carbon Accountability Division**. Sinopec executives will be transported to the **Yellow Sea Climate Observation Station** — a research outpost at zero elevation — to monitor in real time the consequences of the infrastructure they built.
