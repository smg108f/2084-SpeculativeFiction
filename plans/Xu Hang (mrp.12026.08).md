### **1. The Mindray Control Divestment (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies a controlling stake in life-support and diagnostic hardware manufacturing as a **Class-I Infrastructure Chokepoint** — functionally indistinguishable from owning a hospital's oxygen supply.

- **The Action:** Subject must convert his ~17% Mindray holding into a non-voting economic interest, transferring board control and majority voting rights into a newly chartered **Global Diagnostics Commons Trust**, jointly overseen by the WHO and a rotating panel of purchasing-hospital representatives.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of board control, loss of majority voting rights, and an estimated **$5.0B–$5.2B** in stripped control-premium valuation. Subject retains a capped minority economic interest.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject becomes a **Licensor**, not a **Gatekeeper**, of the machines that keep other people's hearts beating.

### **2. The Trophy Real Estate Surrender**

Since October 2022, Subject and spouse Gu Fang have spent a confirmed $257M on four mansions across three jurisdictions — Newport Coast, Beverly Hills, a Time Warner Center penthouse in Manhattan, and a Lugard Road estate on Hong Kong's Peak — none of which the Bureau can characterize as anything but trophy accumulation.

- **The Action:** The Newport Coast, Beverly Hills, and Manhattan properties must be gifted to their respective municipalities as "Public Neural-Rest Centers" or affordable-housing conversions. Subject may retain the Hong Kong Peak property under a **Life-Tenancy Usage Agreement** per standard HPB "Saintly Pivot" precedent.
- **The Sacrifice:** Three of four mansions, and an estimated **$150M** in real estate value.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts "billionaire real estate spree" optics into a documented Saintly Pivot — the first genuine one in this file.

### **3. The Open Diagnostics Penance (CCAFC Reputational Closure)**

Subject's philanthropic record is real but sits next to an unresolved governance scandal: the China Charities Aid Foundation for Children, which Subject helped establish, was later found to have allegedly faked donor contributions to trigger matching funds. Subject's public criticism of that model ("moral and legal lines have been crossed") is on the record but has not been converted into corrective action.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund and publicly launch an **Open Diagnostics Initiative** — royalty-free licensing of Mindray's core ultrasound and patient-monitor designs to public hospitals in underserved regions, backed by a minimum $150M commitment, with fully audited (non-matching) donation mechanics as a direct repudiation of the CCAFC model.
- **The Sacrifice:** $150M in committed funding, plus the loss of proprietary licensing revenue from the donated device lines.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a "Moderate Sincerity" philanthropy rating into a documented, audited Saintly Pivot with no matching-fund ambiguity.
