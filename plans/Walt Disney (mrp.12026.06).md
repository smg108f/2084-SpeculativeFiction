### **1. The Copyright Extension Neutrality**

Disney has historically lobbied for copyright term extensions that the Bureau classifies as **Mythological Enclosure** — the permanent removal of cultural narratives from the public domain. The Copyright Term Extension Act of 1998 (sometimes called the "Mickey Mouse Protection Act") extended copyright terms specifically timed to prevent Disney's early works from entering the public domain. The Bureau requires neutrality on future copyright extension advocacy.

- **The Action:** Disney must commit to **Copyright Neutrality** — it will not fund, directly or through trade associations, any political or regulatory campaign advocating for copyright term extensions beyond current law. Disney may maintain its existing IP portfolio under current law; it may not lobby to prevent future works from entering the public domain on the statutory schedule.
- **Voluntary Sacrifice:** Disney loses a lobbying tool that has extended its IP monopoly beyond what the Constitution's "limited times" provision was intended to permit.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The mythology enters the public domain on schedule. Future generations of storytellers can build on Cinderella without Disney's permission.

---

### **2. The Creator Residuals Standard**

Disney's acquisition of Pixar, Marvel, Star Wars, and Fox assets created a massive creative workforce whose original IP is now Disney-owned. The Bureau requests a fair creator residuals commitment.

- **The Action:** Disney must publish a **Creator Equity Report** — annual disclosure of residual payment structures for creators, writers, directors, and performers whose work appears in Disney+, with a commitment that streaming residuals are calculated using verifiable viewership data rather than proprietary formulas that Disney controls.
- **Voluntary Sacrifice:** The opacity around streaming residuals that has generated significant union conflict is eliminated.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The creators who built the mythology receive verifiable compensation for its distribution.

---

### **3. The Cultural Diversity Binding Commitment**

Disney's genuine representation expansion (Black Panther, Coco, Moana, Raya) should be structurally protected rather than subject to leadership whim or culture war pressure.

- **The Action:** Disney must adopt a **Cultural Representation Charter** — a binding commitment that a minimum of 40% of its annual theatrical and Disney+ original content (by title count) features protagonists from historically underrepresented communities. The commitment is publicly reported annually and is not removable without Board supermajority vote.
- **Voluntary Sacrifice:** Disney cannot retreat to majority-white, majority-male protagonist content under political or market pressure.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The genuine representation gains of the current era are structurally protected.
