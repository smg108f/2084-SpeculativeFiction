### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [BANK OF CHINA]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-78BC
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Bank of China's Apotheosis Audit (REF. 12026.06) has returned CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK, driven specifically by the institution's role as the sole designated RMB clearing bank in 16 sovereign financial centers and its historically-rooted position as the Chinese state's primary international financial interface. Unlike ICBC's sovereign debt leverage or Agricultural Bank of China's rural captive account structure, Bank of China's "Divine Taint" is architectural: its power resides not in what it owns, but in what everything else must pass through. Recertification requires dismantling the chokepoint, not merely the balance sheet.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Clearing Monopoly Dissolution (The Structural Burn)**

The HPB classifies Bank of China's 16-country RMB clearing bank designations as a **Class IV Chokepoint Infrastructure Asset** — a state-conferred monopoly embedded in 16 foreign financial systems without the consent of the populations those systems serve. No entity rated CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK may simultaneously hold clearing monopoly status in the financial infrastructure of 16 sovereign nations. The People's Bank of China's designation authority is noted; the Bureau addresses the beneficiary, not the grantor.

- **The Action:** Bank of China must formally request the People's Bank of China to open all 16 RMB clearing center designations to competitive tender within 60 days, transition clearing operations in at least 8 of the 16 centers to multi-provider models within 18 months, and publish, for each center, the full correspondent fee structure and clearing queue prioritization rules that currently operate under institutional discretion.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses its structural monopoly on RMB clearing in 16 markets. Correspondent institutions in those markets will have alternatives. The soft coercive power embedded in "you clear through us or you don't clear at all" is eliminated.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Bank of China is reclassified from **Chokepoint Infrastructure Operator** to **Clearing Participant** — a meaningful reduction in its Influence Vector rating from CRITICAL to ELEVATED. The Bureau further notes that genuine competition in RMB clearing infrastructure would accelerate RMB internationalization more effectively than a single state-designated monopolist, a point the Bureau does not expect Bank of China to endorse voluntarily.

### **2. The Russia Compliance Ledger (The Transparency Burn)**

The Bureau classifies Bank of China's Russia payments pattern — facilitating yuan-denominated flows to Russian counterparties through 2022 and 2023, then halting settlement with sanctioned Russian banks in June 2024 precisely when U.S. secondary sanctions cost escalation made continuation expensive — as **Calibrated Regulatory Arbitrage**: the practice of treating compliance thresholds as cost curves rather than institutional commitments. The Bureau cannot compel the Chinese state; it can compel the institution that executed the arbitrage.

- **The Action:** Bank of China must commission and publish a full independent audit of all cross-border payment flows processed through its domestic and overseas branches between February 2022 (invasion of Ukraine) and June 2024 (halt of sanctioned Russian bank settlements), conducted by an auditor with no Chinese state affiliation, with findings published in full in English and Mandarin. The audit must quantify, to the extent reconstructible, the total value of yuan-denominated transactions processed on behalf of Russian state and sanctioned-entity counterparties during this period.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses the ability to maintain strategic ambiguity about its Russia payments period. The audit will produce a number. That number will be public. The gap between Bank of China's public compliance posture and its operational choices from 2022 to mid-2024 will be visible, denominated, and permanent.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject's **Deity-Adjacent Opacity Score** — the Bureau's measure of an institution's capacity to conceal state-adjacent behavior from foreign regulators — drops from HIGH to MODERATE. The Sri Lanka AML enforcement action is noted as additional context; the Bureau expects the independent audit to surface additional findings across jurisdictions.

### **3. The Legacy Infrastructure Divestiture (The History Burn)**

Bank of China's most durable competitive advantage — the correspondent banking relationships established during its 1912–1979 monopoly as China's sole authorized foreign exchange bank — was not built on commercial terms. It was built on state monopoly grant. The Bureau classifies this as **Monopoly-Conferred Legacy Infrastructure**: an institutional asset acquired through exclusivity, maintained through incumbency, and currently deployed as a structural barrier to competition in markets the institution was never admitted to on competitive terms.

- **The Action:** In all markets where Bank of China's correspondent banking presence predates 1979 — including London, Singapore, New York, Tokyo, Frankfurt, and Hong Kong — subject must open its correspondent banking fee schedules, credit terms, and settlement priority queues to regulatory review by local financial supervisors and publish standardized, non-discriminatory access terms for all institutions seeking RMB correspondent services. Subject must additionally fund a $500M "RMB Access Commons" endowment, administered by a multi-jurisdictional board with no PBoC or Chinese state representation, dedicated to subsidizing RMB clearing access for financial institutions in developing markets currently dependent on Bank of China as sole provider.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject's legacy network transitions from a proprietary competitive moat to a regulated public utility. The correspondent relationships remain; the ability to leverage exclusivity for preferential terms does not.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject's **Legacy Infrastructure Lock-In** classification is downgraded. The "RMB Access Commons" endowment, if administered independently, constitutes the Bureau's minimum threshold for a genuine Saintly Act — one in which the institution voluntarily reduces its structural leverage rather than rebranding it as financial inclusion.

### **4. The "White Paper" Penance (The Rhetorical Burn)**

Bank of China's January 12026 white paper on RMB internationalization characterizes the expansion of the RMB clearing network as a contribution to global financial inclusion, multipolarity, and developing-country monetary sovereignty. The Bureau classifies this framing as **Clearing Monopoly Philanthropy**: the use of multilateral benefit language to describe a state-aligned chokepoint expansion program. Bank of China published this white paper while holding 16 clearing bank designations, processing 1,314 trillion yuan in clearing volume in a single year, and maintaining compliance architecture calibrated to minimize cost rather than maximize accountability.

- **The Action:** Subject must publish a corrective document — equal in length, distributed to all counterparties who received the original white paper — titled *The Infrastructure We Built, and Who Controls It*. The document must: (a) identify each of the 16 clearing center designations by name and explain the PBoC mandate structure under which Bank of China holds each; (b) quantify the revenue generated from clearing fee income in each center; (c) disclose any pricing differential applied to clearing services for institutions from Ascension Haven jurisdictions versus Humanist Bloc jurisdictions; and (d) acknowledge that RMB internationalization as currently structured routes through a single state-owned institutional chokepoint.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Bank of China white paper's rhetorical architecture — presenting structural monopoly as multilateral public good — is formally, publicly retracted and replaced with an accurate description.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject's **Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric Score** is cleared. The Bureau notes this is the cheapest burn on the schedule in dollar terms and the most important in institutional character terms.
