### **1. The Problem Gambling Accountability Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must fund £300M for the **bet365 Problem Gambling Remediation Fund** — (a) comprehensive treatment and counseling for problem gamblers in every country where bet365 operates, free at point of access; (b) an independent problem gambling research institute studying the neurological and socioeconomic impacts of online gambling platforms; (c) binding maximum daily loss limits (£500/day, £2,000/month) implemented across bet365, removing design features that enable loss-chasing.
- **The Sacrifice:** £300M + revenue reduction from design changes.

### **2. The Stoke-on-Trent Community Transition Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must fund £150M for a **Stoke-on-Trent Economic Diversification Fund** — investing in: (a) manufacturing skills training and SME development so that the city's economy is not dependent on bet365 employment; (b) affordable housing for Stoke workers; (c) healthcare infrastructure for a city with above-average health deprivation statistics.
- **The Sacrifice:** £150M.

### **3. The Compensation Restructure Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must cap personal annual compensation extraction from bet365 at £50M (still extraordinary by any normal standard) — directing the difference between current extraction levels and the cap to the **Bet365 Employee Profit Share Fund**, distributing profits to bet365's 9,000+ employees.
- **The Sacrifice:** £370M+/year in foregone personal extraction.
