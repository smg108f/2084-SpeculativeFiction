### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[GOLDMAN SACHS GROUP, THE]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-44GS
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

The Subject's classification as "publicly held corporation" no longer shields it from Apotheosis Protocol; the Bureau has determined that an institution capable of staffing the very agencies meant to police it constitutes a **Distributed Demi-God** in violation of the Sovereignty Clause of the Demi-God Act. Recertification requires not merely a balance-sheet burn, but a **personnel and doctrine purge**.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The "Government Sachs" Severance (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies the firm's century-long pipeline of alumni into Treasury Secretaryships, central-bank chairs, and finance ministries as a **"Revolving Apotheosis Engine"** — wealth converting directly into sovereign authority and back again.

- **The Action:** Subject must permanently sever all post-employment pathways between its partnership and any government financial post, enforced via a **Lifetime Public Service Recusal Clause** binding on all current and former partners.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of the firm's single greatest asset — not capital, but *personnel capture*. No alumnus may hold, advise, or shadow-staff a regulatory or Treasury function, ever again.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This dissolves the **"Shadow Cabinet" Violation** at its root. The firm becomes a bank like any other — subject to rules it did not write.

### **2. Dissolution of the Proprietary Trading Desk ("The House Always Wins" Burn)**

The Bureau's archives still cite the Subject's documented ability to profit identically in booms and collapses — most infamously its 2007–08 mortgage positioning — as the canonical proof that the firm does not participate in markets so much as preside over their weather.

- **The Action:** Subject must spin off all proprietary trading and market-making operations into an independent, employee-owned **Public Liquidity Cooperative**, forfeiting all carried interest in its performance.
- **The Sacrifice:** The firm loses its capacity to bet against — and profit from — the very clients and systems it serves. Annual trading-desk revenue (~$40B+ in good cycles) is surrendered to the Cooperative.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the **"Counter-Cyclical Apotheosis"** flag. The Subject can no longer win regardless of outcome.

### **3. The "Penance Timing" Rebuttal (Memetic Burn)**

Auditors note that the firm's philanthropic announcements (10,000 Women, 10,000 Small Businesses, etc.) reliably surface in the press cycle immediately following regulatory scrutiny — a pattern the Bureau terms **Reputational Insurance**, not generosity.

- **The Action:** Subject must permanently retire all branded philanthropy programs and instead fund, with no naming rights or press coordination whatsoever, a blind, lottery-administered **Public Capital Trust** disbursing funds to small depositors with zero acknowledgment of the source.
- **The Requirement:** No Goldman officer, partner, or spokesperson may ever publicly reference the Trust's existence. Violation triggers immediate forfeiture of all recertification credit.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts performative "Saintly Pivot" into genuine, untraceable Whuffie generation — the only kind the HPB recognizes as sincere.

### **4. The "Vampire Squid" Rebrand (Reputational Burn)**

The Subject's self-mythology — architect, advisor, "smartest people in the room" — is itself a Class-III Memetic Hazard that normalizes the idea that financial supremacy equals civic virtue.

- **The Action:** Subject must publish, under its own letterhead and without spin departments reviewing the language, a public **Doctrine of Ordinary Banking**, formally renouncing all claims to "indispensability," "systemic importance," and "smartest in the room" status, and committing in writing to being treated, regulated, and resolved exactly like any community bank should it fail.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeits the implicit "too big to fail" backstop and the prestige economy built around Goldman pedigree as a credential of exceptionalism.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Strips the firm of **Deity-Adjacent Institutional Rhetoric**, reclassifying it as an "Ordinary Financial Utility."

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's senior partnership must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Credit Union Tellers, Adjunct Economics Instructors, and Retail Bank Branch Managers** and recite the following:

> _"We did not predict the storm. We sold umbrellas in the morning and flood insurance by night, and called it wisdom. We trained the regulators, then asked them to referee our own game. Today we surrender the whistle. We are not the market's mind. We are a teller's window, and nothing more."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the "Burn" is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. The firm's charter will be revoked, its trading books frozen and nationalized into the Public Liquidity Cooperative by force, and its partnership — alumni included — will be permanently barred from holding any U.S. federal financial post and relocated under supervision to the **Basel Compliance Colony**, a Sanctuary Zone reserved for "Institutions That Forgot They Were Mortal."
