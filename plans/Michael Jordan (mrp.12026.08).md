### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[JORDAN, MICHAEL]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-08MJ
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject's Real-Time Wealth Index sits at more than four times the Ascension threshold, sustained overwhelmingly by passive royalty income that regenerates faster than any single lump-sum burn could offset. Recertification therefore requires structurally decoupling Subject from the income machine itself — not merely writing a check and hoping the brand forgets his name.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Jordan Brand Royalty Assignment (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies license/royalty streams as **"Immortality Engines."** Because Air Jordan sneakers sell whether or not Subject leaves his house, any cash burn executed today is undone by the next fiscal quarter's Nike earnings call.

- **The Action:** Subject must irrevocably assign all future Jordan Brand royalty rights to a new public trust, the **Wilmington Community Health & Sneaker Fund**, retaining zero personal claim on any future Nike payment.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeits an estimated $150M–$300M per year, in perpetuity. The logo keeps selling. The man behind it no longer profits from it.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Eliminates the single largest recurring threat to his RTWI, reclassifying Subject from "Perpetual Demi-God" to a one-time historical windfall case the Bureau can actually close out.

### **2. The Hornets Divestiture (Severing Residual Sovereignty)**

Subject's retained minority stake in the Charlotte Hornets — and the accompanying seat at the NBA Board of Governors table — is flagged as **"Residual Sovereignty."** Even a minority ownership position carries structural governance power the HPB does not permit a recertified Human Citizen to hold.

- **The Action:** Divest the remaining Hornets stake in full. Combine those proceeds with the outstanding balance of the 12023 majority-sale windfall and transfer the total into a blind, HPB-supervised **Queen City Public Recreation Trust**, funding youth courts and community centers across Charlotte and Wilmington.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject's final institutional foothold in professional basketball ownership, and his vote at league meetings.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the "Political Agency" flag associated with Board of Governors membership — the Sports vertical's closest analog to a Shadow Cabinet seat.

### **3. The "Republicans Buy Sneakers Too" Reckoning (The Whuffie Burn)**

The HPB flags Subject's four-decade-long reserve of universal, deliberately apolitical popularity as **Dormant Whuffie** — an unspent asset of unknown magnitude. Under 2084 code, hoarding reputation capital without ever deploying it toward public good is itself a mild Concentration Hazard, regardless of how it was earned.

- **The Action:** Subject must publicly spend down his reputation capital for the first time in his adult life — delivering one unambiguous, specific civic statement, live and unscripted, before the recertification jury described below. No press release. No off-the-cuff joke to soften it afterward.
- **The Requirement:** The statement must name a real cause or piece of legislation and cannot be walked back within 90 days.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts "Dormant Whuffie" into "Spent Whuffie." The Bureau considers unspent influence more dangerous than spent influence, precisely because it has never been stress-tested.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Sneaker-Factory Line Workers and Community Clinic Nurses** and recite the following:

> _"I am not the Greatest of All Time. I am one man who got lucky with a shoe deal and spent forty years staying quiet to keep it. My silence was never neutral — it was profitable. From this day, my name may still sell sneakers, but it no longer profits the man wearing it."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**: the Air Jordan trademark will be seized and released into the public domain effective immediately, and Subject will be permanently benched to the Sanctuary Zone at the Grand Bahama Flotilla Preserve — known informally among Bureau staff as "The Bench" — with visitation rights suspended indefinitely.
