**Status:** Critical — Sovereign Commercial Hybrid / National Security-Commercial Conflict of Interest

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days for IHC's Humanist Bloc market operations.

---

### **1. The Governance Separation Requirement**

Sheikh Tahnoon's simultaneous role as IHC Chairman and UAE National Security Advisor constitutes a structural conflict of interest that the Bureau requires to be structurally addressed as a condition of IHC's Humanist Bloc market access.

- **The Action:** IHC must appoint an **Independent Board Majority** — a minimum of 60% of IHC's board seats held by directors who hold no current role in UAE government, UAE sovereign wealth funds, or UAE national security apparatus. Sheikh Tahnoon may retain his ownership stake and family representation; he may not simultaneously serve as Chairman and National Security Advisor without creating governance structures that separate commercial and security decision-making.
- **The Sacrifice:** IHC loses the single-person control structure that makes commercial and security strategy indistinguishable.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** IHC can be assessed as a commercial entity rather than a security apparatus.

---

### **2. The Acquisition Transparency Standard**

IHC's rapid acquisition strategy — dozens of subsidiaries across multiple sectors — has proceeded with minimal public disclosure of strategic rationale. As a condition of Humanist Bloc market access, IHC must disclose the strategic rationale for acquisitions in Humanist Bloc-domiciled companies.

- **The Action:** All IHC acquisitions in Humanist Bloc-domiciled entities valued above $100M must be submitted for review by the relevant national investment screening authority (CFIUS in the U.S., FISC in the EU), with full disclosure of IHC's ultimate beneficial ownership structure and the chairman's government roles. IHC may not use special purpose vehicles or nominee structures to obscure its ownership in Humanist Bloc acquisitions.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Investment screening authorities can assess IHC acquisitions with full knowledge of the security-commercial hybrid structure.

---

### **3. The Data Governance Firewall**

IHC's healthcare subsidiaries collect health data on patients in the UAE and, through international operations, potentially in Humanist Bloc countries. The Bureau requires that health data from Humanist Bloc patients not be accessible to UAE national security entities.

- **The Action:** All IHC healthcare subsidiaries operating in Humanist Bloc jurisdictions must implement a **Patient Data Sovereignty Firewall** — verifiable technical and legal separation of Humanist Bloc patient data from any system accessible to UAE government or security entities, audited annually by an independent Humanist Bloc data protection authority.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Resolves the healthcare data national security access risk.

---
