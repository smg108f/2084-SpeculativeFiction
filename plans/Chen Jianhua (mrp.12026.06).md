### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [CHEN, JIANHUA]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06CJ
**Status:** Moderate

Subject's chemicals manufacturing wealth lacks environmental transparency and commensurate community investment. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 180 days.

---

### **1. The Industrial Environmental Accountability Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must fund ¥600M (~$85M) for the **Industrial Environmental Accountability Fund**: (a) independent air and water quality monitoring around all manufacturing facilities, with results published publicly; (b) environmental remediation for documented pollution around existing facilities; (c) adoption of international chemical safety standards (REACH-equivalent) across all production lines.

- **The Sacrifice:** ¥300M (~$42M).
