### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [FONDAZIONE CARIPLO]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-20FC
**Status:** Precautionary / Portfolio Diversification and Transparency Order

Fondazione Cariplo has been classified PROBATIONARY SAINT. Recertification requirements are minimal — the Foundation's conduct is sound and its community foundation model is commendable. The primary structural concern is banking stock concentration.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Intesa Sanpaolo Diversification Burn**

The Foundation's endowment is substantially concentrated in Intesa Sanpaolo banking stock — a legacy of the Amato-Carli law's separation of banking from philanthropy. This concentration creates both volatility risk and a potential conflict of interest: the Foundation is a major shareholder of a bank operating in its primary grant territory.

- **The Action:** Fondazione Cariplo must adopt a binding **10-Year Portfolio Diversification Plan** reducing its Intesa Sanpaolo equity concentration to no more than 30% of total endowment assets within 5 years and no more than 20% within 10 years.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Foundation may forego Intesa Sanpaolo dividend income on divested shares, partially offset by diversified portfolio returns.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Foundation's charitable capacity becomes stable and independent of a single bank's performance. The conflict-of-interest between major banking shareholder and civic grantmaker is reduced.

---

### **2. The Community Foundation Expansion Burn**

The 16 community foundations created by Fondazione Cariplo represent the Foundation's most structurally distinctive contribution. The HPB recommends — but does not require — that this model be formally documented and its impact evaluated, with publication of findings for other banking foundations across Europe.

- **The Action:** Commission and publish a **Community Foundation Impact Assessment** — a decade-long evaluation of the 16 community foundations' effectiveness in building local philanthropic capacity, and a published framework for scaling this model to other Italian banking foundations.
- **The Sacrifice:** Minimal — primarily time and evaluation resources.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Fondazione Cariplo's most distinctive structural innovation becomes a documented, exportable model for European territorial philanthropy.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Fondazione Cariplo's Board of Directors must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Community Foundation Leaders from the 16 Cariplo-Created Foundations** and recite the following:

> *"We have been doing this since 11816. We have seen Napoleon, two world wars, and the Amato-Carli law. We have €8 billion and we deploy it for Lombardy. We acknowledge that our banking stake creates an entanglement we should resolve, and that the community foundations we have built are the best thing we have done. We diversify the portfolio. We document the model. We continue for another two centuries."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If diversification is not initiated within 30 days, the HPB will note the unresolved banking concentration risk in its annual institutional watch list and recommend that the Italian Treasury's regulatory division review the Foundation's compliance with Italian banking foundation diversification requirements. The HPB notes this is largely a formality — Fondazione Cariplo's record is strong. Consider diversification an overdue administrative correction to an otherwise well-run institution.
