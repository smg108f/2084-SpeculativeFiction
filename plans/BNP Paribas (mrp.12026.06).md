### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [BNP PARIBAS SA]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-14BNP
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

BNP Paribas's 2014 conviction for facilitating financial transactions with Sudan, Iran, and Cuba — combined with its systemic designation as a G-SIB and its balance sheet exceeding French GDP — places it in the HPB's highest-priority institutional audit tier. Recertification must address both the historical sanctions violations and the structural impunity that G-SIB designation has normalized.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The G-SIB Protection Dissolution (The Structural Burn)**

The HPB classifies BNP Paribas's G-SIB "too-big-to-fail" designation as an **Institutionalized State Guarantee** — a form of public subsidy that reduces BNP's cost of funding while privatizing its profits.

- **The Action:** BNP Paribas must divest sufficient assets to reduce its balance sheet below €1.5T — removing the basis for systemic designation. Divestiture must include its investment banking division and at least one major international retail banking subsidiary.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of approximately 40% of balance sheet; investment banking revenues estimated at €8-10B annually.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** BNP exits G-SIB status; the implicit state guarantee is removed; it becomes an entity that can actually fail without triggering a financial crisis.

### **2. The Sanctions Accountability Restitution (The Justice Burn)**

The HPB's Justice Division classifies the $8.9B fine for Sudan/Iran/Cuba sanctions violations — paid without criminal conviction of any individual — as an **Accountability Deficit** that the Bureau is not empowered to remedy retroactively but is required to address prospectively.

- **The Action:** BNP must establish a $2B irrevocable Sanctions Harm Reparations Fund, administered by an independent humanitarian tribunal, distributing funds to individuals and communities damaged by the financial facilitation that the 2014 plea agreement documented.
- **The Sacrifice:** $2B additional cost beyond what was paid in 2014.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Sanctions Harm Acknowledgment Protocol is satisfied; BNP's Institutional Impunity Score is reduced from Critical to Elevated.

### **3. The State Influence Decoupling (The Sovereignty Burn)**

The HPB classifies BNP's partial French state ownership, ECB supervision integration, and role as a primary French government debt underwriter as an **Entangled Sovereignty** — the private and public financial interests are structurally indistinguishable.

- **The Action:** French state institutional shareholding must be publicly disclosed in full; BNP must accept independent HPB oversight representatives on its compliance board; all government debt underwriting relationships must be subject to public tender rather than relationship banking.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of preferred access to French state business; potential reduction in sovereign advisory fees.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Parallel Sovereign classification is removed.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, BNP Paribas's board must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Sudanese economic refugees and French retail banking customers** and recite the following:

> _"We processed transactions for regimes under international sanction. We paid a fine that no individual in our organization was personally required to pay. We accept that 'too big to fail' is not a defense. We accept that banking is a public good, not a privilege. We become smaller."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. BNP Paribas will be nationalized by the French Republic and reconstituted as a **Public Banking Infrastructure Authority** — which, the HPB notes, is essentially what it already is, only without the pretense of private ownership.
