### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [EQUINOR ASA]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-20EQNR
**Status:** Precautionary / Structural Complexity Flag

Equinor's 67% Norwegian government ownership creates a recertification paradox: the entity that would oversee compliance is itself the majority shareholder. The HPB refers this file to its **State-Owned Enterprise Division** while initiating standard recertification proceedings against the minority public float.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days (subject to Norwegian government coordination protocol).

---

### **1. The Sovereign Wealth Fund Decoupling (The Structural Burn)**

The HPB classifies the arrangement in which Norway's Government Pension Fund Global (GPFG) — the world's largest sovereign wealth fund — is funded by Equinor's oil production, creating a structural incentive for Norway to continue oil production indefinitely, as a **Self-Funding Carbon Lock-In**.

- **The Action:** Norway must legally separate the GPFG's investment mandate from Equinor's production decisions. A binding phase-out schedule for Equinor's North Sea oil production — reducing output by 10% annually from 2027 — must be enacted by the Storting, with GPFG drawdown authorized to compensate for reduced oil revenues.
- **The Sacrifice:** Norway accepts reduced oil revenue; GPFG begins drawdown decades earlier than planned.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Self-Funding Carbon Lock-In classification is resolved. Norway's climate commitments become structurally credible.

### **2. The European Energy Geopolitical Dependency Resolution (The Sovereignty Burn)**

The HPB classifies Equinor's role as Europe's primary post-Russia natural gas supplier as an **Accidental Sovereign Energy Authority** that should be governed by international treaty rather than commercial contract.

- **The Action:** Equinor's gas supply agreements with European nations must be transferred from commercial contracts to a **Nordic-European Energy Security Treaty** governed by the relevant governments, not by Equinor's commercial terms.
- **The Sacrifice:** Equinor loses the pricing leverage of bilateral commercial negotiations.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Geopolitical Leverage classification is resolved; Equinor becomes a supply operator rather than a policy actor.

### **3. The Offshore Wind Acceleration (The Transition Burn)**

The HPB notes that Equinor's offshore wind investments — Empire Wind, Dogger Bank — are moving at the pace of profitable commercial development rather than the pace of climate necessity.

- **The Action:** Equinor must accelerate its renewable energy capital allocation to equal 50% of total annual capex by 2028, with the Norwegian government committing to waive regulatory approval timelines for Equinor renewable projects.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced oil production investment; potential short-term earnings impact.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Equinor's Accidental Fossil Fuel Champion classification is modified to Transitional Energy Authority.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Equinor's board and the Norwegian Minister of Petroleum and Energy must stand jointly before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Norwegian and Dutch citizens** and recite the following:

> _"We built the world's largest sovereign wealth fund by selling oil. We committed to climate leadership and continued selling oil to fund it. We acknowledge that funding sustainability with extraction is not sustainable. We accept the phase-out. The fund will provide for Norway. The oil will stop providing for the fund."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. The Bureau acknowledges it cannot nationalize a company that is already 67% nationalized. It will instead refer the matter to the International Climate Court under the Human Futures Act, which Norway ratified in 2031. The irony will be noted in the public record.
