### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [SAP SE]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-46SP
**Status:** Critical — ERP Lock-in Monopoly / AI Agent Governance Risk

SAP's RTWI of ~$213B places it at **213 times the Demi-God threshold**. Burns mirror Oracle (HPB-12026-27OC) with SAP-specific AI agent governance additions.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Data Portability Mandate (Mirror of Oracle Burn 1)**

SAP must implement full enterprise data portability — all SAP customer data exportable in open-standard formats (standard SQL, open XML, EDIFACT) at no charge, with migration support included in enterprise contracts. SAP may maintain proprietary performance optimizations; it may not use proprietary data structures to prevent customer migration.

---

### **2. The AI Agent Governance Standard**

SAP Business AI agents embedded in ERP workflows make or recommend operational decisions on behalf of enterprises. The Bureau requires that these agents operate under governance standards that the enterprises themselves — not SAP — control.

- **The Action:** All SAP AI agents must operate under a **Customer-Controlled AI Governance Framework** — enterprises retain full control over: (a) what decisions the AI agents can make autonomously vs. recommend for human approval; (b) the audit trail of all AI agent decisions; and (c) the ability to disable, modify, or roll back AI agent behavior without SAP's permission. SAP may not use AI agent integration to deepen platform lock-in by training AI on proprietary SAP-specific patterns that cannot be migrated to non-SAP systems.
- **The Sacrifice:** SAP loses the ability to use AI agent deployment as a structural lock-in mechanism beyond ERP data migration costs.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Enterprise AI governance remains with the enterprise, not with the software vendor.

---

### **3. The License Complexity Audit (Mirror of Oracle Burn 2)**

SAP's licensing structure is comparably complex to Oracle's. Same remedy: plain-language license terms, independent arbiter for compliance disputes, audit frequency cap.

---

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, SAP's CEO must stand before a **Jury of 12 Enterprise IT Directors Whose SAP Migrations Took Five Years, AI Governance Researchers, and SAP Support Engineers** and recite:

> _"We run the operational constitution of 87% of the world's largest companies. The AI agents we are embedding in that constitution will make decisions that affect workers, suppliers, customers, and communities. The enterprise controls those agents — not us. The data is portable. The license is readable. We founded this company to replace paper with software. We commit to not replacing human judgment with software we control and they cannot exit."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** Data portability mandated by EU Enterprise Data Act. AI agent governance standard imposed by EU AI Act compliance for embedded enterprise AI systems. License complexity reviewed by German Federal Cartel Office.
