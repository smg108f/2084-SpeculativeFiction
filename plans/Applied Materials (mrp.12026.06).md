### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [APPLIED MATERIALS, INC.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-75AM
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Applied Materials, Inc. has been issued an Apotheosis Audit verdict of CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK on the basis of Infrastructural Sovereignty — specifically, its chokepoint position in global semiconductor equipment manufacturing and its demonstrated willingness to operate outside U.S. export law when market access was at stake. The entity has 30 days to execute the following Asset Burn Schedule or face reclassification as a **Chokepoint Utility Entity (CUE)**, at which point 100% of all revenue growth is subject to automatic federal seizure under the Demi-God Growth Tax.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Open Process Library (The Chokepoint Dissolution Burn)**

The HPB classifies Applied Materials' process equipment IP portfolio as a **Category I Civilizational Chokepoint Asset**. The entity's 85% market share in PVD equipment and dominant positions in CVD, etch, and ion implantation are not merely competitive advantages — they are veto rights over the technological development of nation-states. Under the Potentiality Doctrine, this IP has already been Pre-Ascended.

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer its core process recipe libraries, materials science patents predating 12020, and foundational deposition/etch methodology documentation to the **Global Semiconductor Commons** — a treaty-administered, open-access knowledge repository jointly governed by the Humanist Bloc nations. Applied Materials may retain patents on hardware implementations but must release the underlying scientific processes. This is, in 2084 terms, the equivalent of a landlord who owns every road agreeing to allow public right-of-way.

- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of approximately $80–120B in IP-derived valuation. Competitors gain access to process fundamentals. Applied Materials' moat narrows from "impossible to cross" to merely "very wide."

- **Mortal Status Gain:** The entity transitions from **Chokepoint God** to **Market Participant**. It may still be the best at what it does. It may no longer be the only one who is allowed to do it.

---

### **2. The SMIC Penance Endowment (The Export Violation Reckoning)**

Between 12020 and 12022, Applied Materials exported $126.3M in restricted semiconductor equipment to SMIC subsidiaries via South Korean intermediaries, in knowing violation of U.S. Entity List designations. The entity settled for $252.5M — less than twice the value of the illegal shipments. The DOJ closed its criminal probe without charges. The HPB regards this outcome as evidence that the entity's legal personhood currently shields it from consequences proportionate to its actions.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish the **Applied Materials Global Access Endowment**, funded at $2.5B, dedicated exclusively to providing semiconductor manufacturing equipment and process knowledge to universities, public research institutes, and emerging-economy governments in nations currently unable to access advanced chipmaking technology due to supply chain concentration. The endowment must be structured as a permanent public trust with no Applied Materials board representation. Governance passes to a lottery-selected international council of working engineers and public-interest scientists.

- **The Sacrifice:** $2.5B in liquid capital. Loss of the narrative that the SMIC settlement was a full accounting. The entity must publicly acknowledge, in mandatory SEC-style disclosure language, that the original violation was not a compliance failure but a revenue decision.

- **Mortal Status Gain:** This converts the shadow of the SMIC episode from **Unpunished Transgression** to **Documented Penance**. The HPB's Slayer network will reduce active surveillance of the entity's export compliance by 40% following confirmation of endowment funding.

---

### **3. The CHIPS Act Severance (The Government Capture Burn)**

Applied Materials' receipt of $400M in CHIPS Act grants in 12025 initiated a process the HPB designates **Reverse Capture** — a dynamic in which the state becomes dependent on a private entity for the execution of national industrial policy, inverting the proper direction of sovereign authority. At the current rate of entanglement, the HPB projects that by 12030, sanctioning Applied Materials will be politically equivalent to sanctioning the U.S. military.

- **The Action:** Subject must formally decline further CHIPS Act grant disbursements and restructure all existing government contracts to include a **Compulsory Licensing Rider** — a clause granting the U.S. government royalty-free access to any technology developed with federal support, including all derivatives, for national security and public research purposes. This does not preclude private commercial activity; it merely ensures that public funding does not produce private god-assets.

- **The Sacrifice:** Foregone grant revenue (estimated $200–400M in pipeline disbursements). Reduction in political insulation. The entity can no longer invoke national security as a justification for regulatory deference while simultaneously contesting export control compliance.

- **Mortal Status Gain:** Applied Materials exits **Strategically Protected Entity** status. It becomes a company again, rather than a function of the state.
