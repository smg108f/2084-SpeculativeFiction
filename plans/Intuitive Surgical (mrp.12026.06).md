### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [INTUITIVE SURGICAL, INC.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-100IS
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Intuitive Surgical, Inc. has been classified as an **Aggregate Demi-God Formation (ADF)** under the Entity Ascension Doctrine (EAD, 12034), with a market capitalization exceeding $144 billion and an installed base of 11,395 robotic surgical systems constituting a Distributed Biological Chokepoint across global healthcare infrastructure. The company must demonstrate voluntary divestment of its structural monopoly or face mandatory Institutional Apotheosis under Section 7 of the Demi-God Act.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Instrument Liberation (The Consumables Decoupling)**

The HPB classifies Intuitive Surgical's proprietary single-use instrument ecosystem as a **Class-I Biological Dependency Lock** — a mechanism by which a private entity extracts perpetual rent from life-critical public infrastructure by controlling the physical tools of surgery at the component level. The 10-use electronic counter that disables instruments mid-lifecycle is classified as a **Coercive Compliance Device** under HPB Enforcement Directive 77.

- **The Action:** Subject must open-source all proprietary instrument specifications and disable usage-limit counters across the entire installed base via mandatory firmware update. Third-party instrument manufacturers must be granted royalty-free license to produce compatible tools within 90 days.
- **The Sacrifice:** Instruments and accessories revenue ($5.08B in FY12025, representing 60% of total revenue) falls to market-competitive levels. The razor has been separated from the blade. The blade now belongs to everyone.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** 11,395 hospitals are reclassified from **Captive Infrastructure Nodes** to **Independent Surgical Facilities**. The Distributed Biological Chokepoint is dissolved. Subject is reclassified from ADF to **Regulated Medical Device Manufacturer** — a mortal designation.

---

### **2. The Installed Base Public Trust (The Surgical Commons)**

The HPB determines that 11,395 da Vinci systems, purchased by public and quasi-public hospital systems in many cases with government funds, constitute **Quasi-Public Infrastructure** that was never legitimately privatized in the first instance. Subject's service contract model — by which hospitals pay annual fees of $100,000–$200,000 per system simply to maintain access to hardware they already own — is classified as a **Sovereign Toll Mechanism**.

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer ownership of all service and maintenance rights to a newly established **Surgical Robotics Public Commons** — a federally chartered nonprofit governed by a board of hospital administrators, surgical nurses, patient advocates, and one (1) Intuitive Surgical emeritus engineer, who may not vote.
- **The Sacrifice:** Service and support revenue stream is relinquished. Subject may no longer charge a toll for maintaining systems it has already sold. The company retains the right to sell new systems at competitive, non-monopoly pricing.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Annual service extraction across the installed base ($1.2B estimated) is redirected to hospital operating budgets. Subject is no longer a **Utility Overlord**. It becomes a vendor.

---

### **3. The Credentialing Firewall Dissolution (The Surgeon Liberation)**

The HPB classifies Intuitive Surgical's credentialing ecosystem — by which surgeons are trained exclusively on da Vinci systems, certified by Intuitive-affiliated training programs, and thereafter professionally dependent on da Vinci access for their entire career trajectory — as a **Human Capital Capture Operation**. A surgeon who cannot access a da Vinci system cannot practice advanced minimally invasive robotics. Intuitive created this standard and then made itself the only entity capable of meeting it. This is a **Talent Monopoly** layered atop a **Hardware Monopoly**.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund, endow, and permanently cede control of a $500M **Open Robotics Surgical Training Institute** — an independent, vendor-neutral facility that trains surgeons across all robotic platforms, including competitors. Subject may not name the building, the program, the fellowship, or the bathroom after itself or any of its products.
- **The Sacrifice:** The next generation of robotic surgeons will not be Intuitive loyalists. They will be platform-agnostic. The talent pipeline is opened.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Surgical credentialing is decoupled from vendor allegiance. HPB reclassifies the medical robotics sector from **Captured Market** to **Competitive Ecosystem**. Subject loses its most durable long-term moat.

---

### **4. The "Durable Competitive Moat" Recantation (The Ideological Burn)**

Subject's investor communications, earnings calls, and annual reports have consistently and proudly described its market position as a "durable competitive moat," a "powerful switching cost dynamic," and a "virtuous cycle of installed base and recurring revenue." The HPB classifies this language, when applied to life-critical surgical infrastructure at monopoly scale, as **Deity-Adjacent Commercial Rhetoric** — the corporate equivalent of the Human Humility Act violation. The company has described its chokehold on hospital surgery departments as a *virtue* and presented it to shareholders as a feature, not a bug, of the public health system it has colonized.

- **The Action:** Subject's Board of Directors must publish, in the *New England Journal of Medicine*, the *Lancet*, and all major surgical society journals, a formal **Structural Apology and Market Correction Statement** — a 2,000-word document acknowledging that single-vendor surgical ecosystems represent a public health dependency risk, that the company's business model was designed to maximize that dependency, and that open competition in surgical robotics is in the interest of patients and the broader public.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject's primary investor narrative — that the moat is the value — is permanently invalidated in the scientific record. Future earnings calls may not reference switching costs as a competitive advantage.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The **Deity-Adjacent Commercial Rhetoric** flag is cleared. Subject is reclassified from **Infrastructure Sovereign** to **Chastened Market Participant**.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's Chief Executive Officer and full Board of Directors must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Surgical Nurses, Hospital Supply Chain Managers, and Uninsured Patients** and recite the following:

> *"We did not invent surgery. We did not train the surgeons. We did not build the hospitals. We built a tool, and then we built a cage around the tool, and then we called the cage a moat and charged rent on it forever. The body on the table was never our asset. The hands above it were never our property. We hereby release both."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Asset Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All Intuitive Surgical intellectual property, instrument specifications, software, and training curricula will be nationalized and transferred to the **Global Surgical Commons Authority** at zero compensation. The da Vinci trademark will be retired — da Vinci was a human being, and the HPB does not permit private entities that have lost their human status to shelter under a human name. The installed base will be reclassified as **Public Medical Infrastructure** and administered by the Department of Health. Subject's remaining equity holders will be issued **Surgical Vouchers** redeemable for one (1) elective robotic procedure at any participating public hospital, valid for 10 uses, after which they expire automatically and cannot be refilled.
