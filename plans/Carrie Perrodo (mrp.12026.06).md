### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [PERRODO, CARRIE]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06CP2
**Status:** Urgent

Subject controls an oil company that extracts resources from some of the world's poorest countries with minimal documented benefit to those communities. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The DRC Oil Revenue Justice Burn**

Perenco is one of the DRC's largest oil producers. The DRC contains vast natural resources and has one of the world's lowest per-capita incomes. The gap between what Perenco extracts and what the Congolese people receive from that extraction is the HPB's primary concern.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund $200M for the **Perenco Congo Community Fund**: (a) direct investment in the Congolese communities adjacent to Perenco's oilfields — healthcare, school construction, clean water, and electricity access for communities that live above the oil being extracted; (b) a **Revenue Transparency Commitment** — Perenco to publish its full tax and royalty payments to the Congolese state annually, meeting EITI (Extractive Industries Transparency Initiative) standards, so Congolese civil society can track whether the government is receiving and deploying the revenue; (c) funding for an independent environmental monitoring program for Perenco's DRC infrastructure, with results published.
- **The Sacrifice:** $200M.

### **2. The African Operations Community Burn**

Perenco operates in Gabon, Cameroon, and other African nations with similar dynamics — oil extraction, limited local benefit, aging infrastructure.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund $100M for the **Perenco Africa Development Fund** — covering Gabon and Cameroon community investment on the same model as the DRC fund, plus a commitment that Perenco will meet EITI disclosure standards in all African jurisdictions by 12027.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M.

### **3. The Hydrocarbon Wind-Down Obligation**

Perenco's business model depends on continued oil extraction. The HPB requires a credible transition commitment.

- **The Action:** Subject must publish a binding **Perenco 12035 Transition Plan** — with a specific timeline for reducing Perenco's oil production to zero by 12035, with investment in renewable energy projects in the same countries where Perenco currently extracts oil, converting Perenco's operational presence from extraction to energy transition.
- **The Sacrifice:** $50M (transition plan development and initial renewable energy scoping).
