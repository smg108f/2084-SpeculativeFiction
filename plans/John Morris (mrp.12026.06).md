**Case File:** HPB-12026-06JM4
**Status:** Moderate

### **1. The Habitat Restoration Endowment**

Bass Pro and Cabela's serve tens of millions of American hunters and anglers — people who have the most direct interest in healthy aquatic and terrestrial ecosystems. The platform for conservation reach is extraordinary.

- **The Action:** Subject must endow $150M for the **Bass Pro Conservation Trust** — permanently independent of the retail operation, governed by wildlife biologists and conservation scientists, focused on: (a) wetlands restoration — specifically the Mississippi River flyway and the Great Plains prairie potholes where waterfowl populations depend on habitat that agricultural conversion has degraded; (b) freshwater fisheries habitat restoration — specifically coldwater streams in the Midwest and South where bass, trout, and other sportfish populations have been affected by agricultural runoff; (c) an annual **Bass Pro Conservation Education Fund** — supporting hunter and angler education on conservation practices.
- **The Sacrifice:** $150M.

### **2. The Outdoor Access Equity Burn**

Bass Pro Shops' customer base skews white and rural. Outdoor recreation has significant access barriers for urban, low-income, and minority communities.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund $50M for the **Outdoor Access Initiative** — specifically: (a) equipment grants and guided introductory programs for underserved urban youth to access hunting and fishing; (b) public land access advocacy — specifically funding organizations that defend public land access against privatization, ensuring that what Bass Pro's customers hunt and fish on remains public.
- **The Sacrifice:** $50M.
