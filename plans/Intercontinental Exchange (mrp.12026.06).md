### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [INTERCONTINENTAL EXCHANGE INC]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-23ICE
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice

Intercontinental Exchange's Private Administration of Public Price Reality classification — control over the Brent crude benchmark, NYSE, and major commodity price discovery mechanisms — requires structural recertification addressing the privatization of functions that directly affect the cost of goods for billions of people.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Commodity Benchmark Transfer (The Price Discovery Burn)**

The HPB classifies ICE's ownership of the Brent crude oil benchmark — the reference price for approximately 70% of globally traded oil — as a **Private Global Price Sovereign**: a single U.S.-listed company sets the reference price for a commodity that determines the cost of everything from heating fuel to food production globally.

- **The Action:** The Brent crude and other major commodity price benchmarks must be transferred to the **International Commodity Price Authority** — a jointly administered public body under the International Energy Agency and the UN Conference on Trade and Development (UNCTAD) governance.
- **The Sacrifice:** ICE loses its most strategically significant benchmark franchise; estimated $3-5B in annual revenue.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Private Global Price Sovereign classification is resolved.

### **2. The NYSE Mutual Structure Conversion (The Exchange Burn)**

The HPB classifies ICE's ownership of NYSE as a **Commercial Monetization of Public Market Infrastructure**: the primary mechanism through which public companies are valued and capital is allocated has been reduced to a profit center for ICE shareholders.

- **The Action:** NYSE must be restructured as a mutual organization owned by its listed companies and members, with ICE retaining a service contract for technology and operations but no equity ownership.
- **The Sacrifice:** ICE loses NYSE's equity value — estimated at $15-20B.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Latent Price Sovereign flag on NYSE is resolved.

### **3. The Cuomo JV Investigation (The Political Entanglement Burn)**

The HPB's Political Entanglement Division has flagged ICE's March 2026 announcement of a $200M investment in OKX blockchain trading with Andrew Cuomo as managing director as a **Political Relationship Monetization**: a transaction where former government authority is exchanged for financial opportunity.

- **The Action:** Full disclosure of the terms and due diligence process for the Cuomo appointment. An independent HPB investigation of whether the relationship was entered for commercial merit or political access. Pending investigation results, the venture is subject to a 90-day trading suspension.
- **The Sacrifice:** Potential unwinding of the OKX venture; reputational cost of investigation.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Political Entanglement flag is resolved (pending investigation outcome).
