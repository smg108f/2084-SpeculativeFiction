### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[TORONTO DOMINION BANK]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-44TD
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject is a chartered demi-god entity whose "humanity" is a marketing department, not a legal status. Having confessed to laundering criminal capital at civilizational scale while branding itself as the bank that "feels human," Subject must now prove — structurally, publicly, and irreversibly — that it can survive without either the oligopoly or the disguise.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Oligopoly Severance (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies Canada's "Big Five" cartel structure as a **Sovereign Substitution Engine** — a private arrangement that performs the function of a central bank without any of the accountability. Subject's ~85–90% share of that arrangement is its single largest source of "Potentiality Value."

- **The Action:** Subject must spin off one-third of its Canadian retail operations into an independent, depositor-owned cooperative, forfeiting all cross-institutional pricing coordination with BMO, Scotiabank, CIBC, and RBC.
- **The Sacrifice:** Permanent loss of "Big Five" membership and an estimated **$58B** in future-monopoly valuation credits.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject is reclassified from "Oligopoly Pillar" to "Regional Lender" — a Human Citizen-grade institution that must now *compete* for trust rather than presume it.

### **2. The $18.3 Trillion Reckoning (Ideological Burn)**

Between 12018 and 12024, Subject's own automated systems left some 92% of US transaction volume unmonitored — not through incapacity, but through a documented "flat cost paradigm" that prized convenience and shareholder comfort over the basic duty of knowing where money comes from. The HPB does not consider this an accident deserving of a fine; it considers it a confession deserving of restitution.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund and fully staff, in perpetuity, an independent **Public Ledger Trust** — a citizen-auditable transparency system that publishes aggregated transaction-monitoring data for Subject's North American operations in real time, reviewed by a rotating board of community auditors with full subpoena power over Subject's compliance division.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject permanently surrenders the right to internally police its own anti-laundering systems. An estimated **$1.3B annually** is redirected from shareholder returns to the Trust's operating budget.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This neutralizes the **"Willful Blindness" Violation** and converts Subject's greatest scandal into its most heavily surveilled virtue.

### **3. The "Feels Human" Penance (Reputational/Memetic Burn)**

Subject spent decades and untold marketing budgets cultivating the persona of the warm, trustworthy, "human" bank — ATMs that say thank you, tellers trained to ask about your day — while quietly becoming, in the HPB's words, "a corridor through which criminal fortunes moved with negligible friction." This is a textbook **Brand-Shielded Negligence** pattern, and it must be publicly inverted.

- **The Action:** Subject must retire the "feels human" campaign entirely and replace it, for no less than five years, with a mandatory disclosure campaign titled _We Let It Through. Here's What We're Doing About It_, airing in the same media slots and at the same budget level as the abandoned campaign, detailing — branch by branch — how the laundering networks operated and which internal decisions enabled them.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses its single most valuable intangible asset: the *appearance* of innocence. Brand-trust valuation drops by an estimated **$22B**.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This neutralizes Subject's **Deity-Adjacent Marketing** score and forces a rare event in corporate history — a demi-god telling the truth about itself, unprompted, on a loop, for years.

### **4. The Branch-Floor Divestiture (Structural Burn)**

Subject's executive compensation and shareholder-return structure is built atop the very "flat cost paradigm" that starved its compliance systems. The HPB will not certify an institution whose internal incentives still reward the conditions that produced the scandal.

- **The Action:** Subject must convert 15% of senior executive equity compensation into a **Frontline Worker Trust**, distributed annually to branch-level staff — the same employees whose 12017 testimonies about aggressive sales pressure the bank dismissed for years.
- **The Loophole:** Executives may retain base salary and a modest, capped bonus structure tied solely to compliance-audit outcomes, not growth metrics.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject becomes, for the first time, an institution whose leadership profits only when its frontline succeeds — not when its balance sheet grows regardless of how.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize Phase I recertification, Subject's Board of Directors and CEO must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Branch Tellers, Night-Shift Call Center Operators, and Small Business Loan Applicants** and recite the following:

> _"We called ourselves the bank that feels human, and for nine years we let billions in poison move through our hands and called it a cost of doing business. We were not a neighbor. We were a door left open on purpose. From this day, our ledgers are yours to read, our profits are shared with the floor, and our name means nothing until our hands are clean enough to shake."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not executed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Subject's charter will be suspended, its Canadian retail network nationalized into the **Dominion Public Banking Trust**, and its executive leadership will be relocated — under permanent compliance escort — to the **Atlantic Compliance Colony**, a remote financial-monitoring facility off the coast of Nova Scotia, where they will spend no fewer than ten years personally reviewing flagged transactions by hand.
