### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[SANOFI S.A.]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-77SY
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject Sanofi has been classified a Demi-God Entity under the Biological Sovereignty clause. Because its "Divine Footprint" is encoded directly into the molecules that determine whether millions of human beings live, suffer, or die, the Bureau finds ordinary asset seizure insufficient — the cure itself must be returned to the commons before the corporation can be returned to mortality.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Insulin Commons Transfer (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies Sanofi's insulin patent portfolio (Lantus, Apidra, and all associated analogue formulations) as a **"Pancreas Cartel Instrument"** — intellectual property whose entire value derives from the involuntary nature of its customer base. No person "chooses" insulin; they simply pay or they die. Wealth extracted under such conditions cannot be legally classified as "earned."

- **The Action:** Subject must release all insulin-analogue patents into the **Global Open Formulary**, a public, royalty-free manufacturing license available to any nation, cooperative, or generic producer on Earth.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of all insulin licensing revenue, in perpetuity, plus dissolution of Sanofi's seat in the three-company pricing axis informally known as "the Pancreas Cartel."
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject's net worth drops by an estimated **$31B** in foreclosed future-pricing power. Subject is reclassified from "Apothecary-Sovereign" to "Licensed Compounder."

### **2. Dissolution of the Orange Book Armory**

Sanofi's pattern of strategic patent-listing — using legal tripwires to delay generic competition long after a molecule's true innovation window has closed — is classified by the HPB as a **Class-III Memetic Hazard: "Manufactured Scarcity."**

- **The Action:** Immediate, unconditional withdrawal of all Orange Book listings currently the subject of FTC or comparable regulatory scrutiny, with a public, machine-readable registry of every molecule whose generic release was delayed and by how many days.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses its primary legal mechanism for extending monopoly pricing beyond the original patent term — its "Kingmaker" leverage over which generics reach which markets, and when.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This clears the **"Manufactured Scarcity" Violation** and ends Subject's status as a State-Level Actor in international drug-access negotiations.

### **3. The Global Health Unit Penance ("Mercy Is Not a Luxury Good")**

Because Subject's philanthropic "Saintly Pivot" has been found by auditors to activate exclusively in markets where profit was already mathematically impossible, the HPB requires a public Rebuttal Act that cannot be quietly walked back once cameras are gone.

- **The Action:** Subject must convert its entire "Global Health Unit" into an independent, externally-governed nonprofit — the **Apothecary's Restitution Trust** — funded by a permanent 15% levy on all Sanofi vaccine and insulin revenue worldwide, governed by a board majority-composed of patients and clinicians from low- and middle-income nations.
- **The Requirement:** Subject's executive leadership must personally fund and narrate a public testimonial series titled *We Held the Cure in One Hand*, documenting — by name, by face — patients who died waiting for medicines Sanofi could have priced within reach.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This neutralizes Subject's **"Strategic Generosity, Post-Hoc"** designation and converts its public-relations infrastructure into a permanently audited instrument of actual restitution.

### **4. The Vaccine Sovereignty Surrender**

Subject's vaccine manufacturing and distribution network — capable of reaching hundreds of millions of bodies per year — constitutes, in HPB terms, a **"Private Public-Health Ministry"** answerable to no electorate.

- **The Action:** Transfer all vaccine manufacturing IP and a controlling technology-transfer stake to the **World Health Organization's Open Vaccine Consortium**, with binding technology-transfer agreements to regional manufacturing hubs in Africa, South Asia, and Latin America.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject relinquishes first-mover pricing advantage on all future pandemic-response products — the single most lucrative category in its forward pipeline.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject is recognized as a "Co-Manufacturer" of global health rather than its private gatekeeper, and is cleared of the **Sovereign Ideology** flag entirely.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's executive leadership must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Pharmacists, Diabetes Patients, and Rural Clinic Nurses** and recite the following:

> _"We held the cure in one hand and the price tag in the other, and we let the tag decide who lived. We called mercy a luxury good and sold it back to the desperate at a markup we set ourselves. We were wrong. The molecule was never ours to ration — it was only ever ours to deliver. We return it now, freely, to the bodies it was always meant for."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All molecular patents will be nationalized without compensation, Subject's executive leadership will be permanently barred from pharmaceutical governance anywhere in the Humanist Bloc, and the corporate entity will be dissolved into the **Open Formulary Custodial Trust**, its name retired and its archives sealed as a teaching case in the HPB's permanent exhibit, *The Apothecary Who Forgot the Patient.*
