### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [IBERDROLA SA]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-19IBE
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice

Iberdrola SA's classification as a **Policy Co-Creation Entity** — a corporation that shapes the regulatory framework governing its own operations across five major jurisdictions — requires recertification that addresses the structural merger between private commercial interest and public energy policy implementation.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Regulatory Participation Firewall (The Sovereignty Burn)**

The HPB classifies Iberdrola's pattern of active participation in drafting renewable energy permitting frameworks that it then benefits from commercially as a **Policy Self-Authorization**: the same entity that lobbies for expedited offshore wind permitting is the entity that wins the offshore wind contracts.

- **The Action:** Iberdrola must accept a binding **Regulatory Participation Firewall**: it may not participate, directly or through industry associations where it holds a leadership position, in any regulatory drafting process for which it has active commercial applications pending. An independent HPB monitor will enforce the firewall across all five primary jurisdictions.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of the policy influence that has been its most durable competitive advantage.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Policy Co-Creation classification is removed.

### **2. The Subsidy Recapture Agreement (The Public Good Burn)**

The HPB calculates that Iberdrola has received approximately €20B in renewable energy subsidies, preferential permitting, and grid access guarantees across Spain, the UK, and the US since 2010 — support provided by public institutions on the basis of public interest.

- **The Action:** Iberdrola must agree to a **Subsidy Recapture Structure**: for each year in which its EBITDA margin exceeds 30%, it will transfer 20% of the margin above 30% to the relevant national energy transition authorities, capped at €2B annually globally.
- **The Sacrifice:** Estimated €1-1.5B annual recapture in high-margin years.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Saint-and-Beneficiary Coupling flag is resolved.

### **3. The Galán Governance Transparency Mandate (The Relationship Burn)**

The HPB classifies Chairman Ignacio Galán's cross-jurisdictional political relationships — maintained with governments of multiple ideological orientations across Spain, UK, US, and EU — as an **Unregistered Influence Network** operating outside standard lobbying disclosure requirements.

- **The Action:** Full public disclosure of all Galán meetings with government officials in the past five years; mandatory lobbying registration for all Iberdrola executive-level political contacts; no post-employment cooling-off period waivers for former regulators joining Iberdrola.
- **The Sacrifice:** Transparency about relationships whose value lies partly in their discretion.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Unregistered Influence Network classification is resolved.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Chairman Galán and the board must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Spanish and British electricity ratepayers** and recite the following:

> _"We built the wind farms because the world needs them. We also wrote the permits for the wind farms, lobbied the governments that approved them, and bid on contracts under frameworks we helped design. We acknowledge this is not the same as doing it because the world needs it. We accept the firewall. We accept the recapture. We remain committed to the wind."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Iberdrola's renewable energy assets will be transferred to a **Multi-National Energy Commons** administered jointly by the governments of Spain, UK, USA, and Brazil. Chairman Galán's political contacts will be evaluated individually for their own HPB filing requirements.
