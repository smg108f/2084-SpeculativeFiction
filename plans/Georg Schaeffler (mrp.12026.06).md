### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [SCHAEFFLER, GEORG]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06GS2
**Status:** Urgent

Subject controls a company whose capital foundation includes unacknowledged forced labour from the Second World War. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Historical Labour Acknowledgment Burn**

The Industriewerk Herzogenaurach used forced and slave labour during the Second World War. Schaeffler AG has not established a victim compensation program equivalent to those created by peer German industrial companies.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish the **Schaeffler Historical Responsibility Foundation** — €150M — with the following mandate: (a) commission a full, independent historical audit of the use of forced and slave labour in Industriewerk Herzogenaurach and its predecessor facilities, 1939–1945, with findings published publicly in German and English; (b) fund compensation for documented victims and their descendants — working through the existing "Remembrance, Responsibility and Future" (EVZ) Foundation framework or an equivalent German-government-overseen mechanism; (c) fund a permanent education program in the Herzogenaurach region (including at Schaeffler's headquarters facilities) about the use of forced labour in German industry. Named the Schaeffler Historical Responsibility Foundation — the company name on the foundation acknowledges institutional, not merely personal, responsibility.
- **The Sacrifice:** €150M (~$165M).
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Schaeffler joins the German industrial companies that have formally reckoned with their wartime labour history.

### **2. The Continental Debt Rescue Accountability Burn**

The 2008–2009 debt rescue of Schaeffler preserved the family's equity at the cost of systemic banking risk. The HPB requires proportionate reinvestment.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund €75M ($82M) to the **Schaeffler Supplier Resilience Fund** — specifically for Schaeffler's small and medium-sized automotive supplier partners, who face existential risk during the ICE-to-EV transition while Schaeffler's own transition is better resourced. The Fund provides grants and transition support for suppliers to retool for EV component manufacturing.
- **The Sacrifice:** €75M.

### **3. The EV Transition Commitment Burn**

Schaeffler AG's bearing technology is compatible with both ICE and EV drivetrains. The HPB requires a binding commitment.

- **The Action:** Subject must commit Schaeffler AG — through binding supervisory board resolution — to a published, independently verified timeline for achieving 80% of revenue from EV-compatible and renewable energy components by 12033.
- **The Sacrifice:** Governance and strategic constraint (capital allocation required).

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Documented Descendants of Forced Labour Workers from Herzogenaurach, Schaeffler Supplier Workers from Bavaria, and Automotive Industry Researchers**:

> *"My grandfather built Industriewerk Herzogenaurach. The historical record of that period is what it is. I did not choose the capital I inherited. I did choose what to do with it. The Historical Responsibility Foundation is my acknowledgment — as the current owner — that the company's capital base includes value that was extracted from forced labour, and that this creates an obligation that passes with the ownership, not only with the generation that committed the act. The supplier fund protects the smaller companies that depend on Schaeffler for their survival while we transition. The bearings will be the same. The accountability will be new."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** Subject will be assigned to **FSS SCHAEFFLER** — a vessel whose entire drivetrain is built from Schaeffler bearings and EV components (all fully renewable-energy-compatible), powered by electricity, operating as a floating archive of German industrial history, carrying the published findings of the historical labour audit to ports in Poland, Ukraine, and Russia — the countries from which the majority of forced labour workers in German industry were taken — where it will be docked and open to the public free of charge.
