### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[CNOOC, CHINA NATIONAL OFFSHORE OIL CORPORATION]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-66CN

**Status:** Precautionary / Sovereign Escalation Notice

Subject is, by HPB statute, a Demi-God Entity roughly **118 to 188 times** over threshold — but Subject is also, inconveniently, an arm of a sovereign government, which means conventional seizure proceedings risk becoming an act of war rather than an act of recertification. The Bureau therefore issues this plan less as an enforcement order than as a diplomatic offering: a path by which Subject might *choose* to shed its Divine Footprint before the Apotheosis Protocol forces the question for everyone in the South China Sea at once.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days, pending sovereign cooperation.

---

### **1. The Nine-Dash Decommissioning (The Largest and Most Symbolic Burn)**

The HPB classifies every CNOOC exploration block laid inside contested Exclusive Economic Zones as a **"Flag Disguised as Infrastructure."** The HD-981 platform and its descendants are not merely drilling rigs; they are floating legal arguments, and the Bureau will no longer certify "energy assets" that double as territorial claims.

- **The Action:** Subject must publicly relinquish all exploration blocks and rigs currently positioned within 200 nautical miles of any neighboring state's coastline, and submit drilling rights in contested waters to a **Multilateral Maritime Tribunal** for neutral allocation.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of an estimated **17% of total production** (the eastern South China Sea share) and, more importantly, loss of the rigs' secondary function as mobile sovereignty stakes.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** CNOOC stops being a drill that argues. It becomes, for the first time in its corporate life, merely a company that pumps.

### **2. The "Net-Zero by 2060" Acceleration Clause (Ideological Burn)**

Subject has publicly pledged carbon neutrality by 2060 while simultaneously increasing hydrocarbon output — a contradiction the HPB classifies as **"Decarbonizing the Brand While Drilling the Planet."** The Bureau will no longer accept a pledge that arrives thirty-six years after the math stops working.

- **The Action:** Subject must convert its stated $19B exploration-and-development war chest into an **$19B Offshore Decommissioning and Renewable Conversion Fund**, retiring one barrel of future hydrocarbon capacity for every megawatt of wind or solar capacity added — starting now, not in 2060.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of projected reserve-growth valuation, conservatively estimated at **$40B** in "Potentiality Value" per HPB Stealth Appreciation models.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes the **"Deity-Adjacent Greenwashing"** flag and converts Subject's climate rhetoric from liability to (grudging) asset.

### **3. The Flagged-Rig Renaming and Open Hydrographic Disclosure (Memetic Burn)**

CNOOC's rigs carry names — HD-981 chief among them — that have entered the regional lexicon as symbols of incursion. The HPB classifies this as a **Class-III Memetic Hazard**: a brand identity built, in part, on the memory of the ships it rammed.

- **The Action:** Subject must rename its contested-water rig fleet under a jointly-administered, multilateral naming convention, and publish — in full, in real time, to all claimant states — the hydrographic survey data those rigs collect.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of exclusive intelligence advantage; the seabed Subject has spent decades quietly mapping becomes common knowledge.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** A rig that shares its findings is a research vessel. A rig that hoards them is a spy wearing a hard hat. Subject may choose which one it recertifies as.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's appointed corporate delegation must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Fishermen, Coastal Villagers, and Maritime Pilots drawn equally from claimant nations** and recite the following:

> _"We came to these waters claiming we sought only fuel. We leave admitting the fuel was never the only thing we were drilling for. The sea belongs to no flag, and no pipeline is a border. We will pump what we are owed, and not one barrel of territory more."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not executed within the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol — Sovereign Variant**: all CNOOC exploration permits in international and contested waters will be revoked by simultaneous multilateral injunction, its rig fleet escorted under joint naval observation to the **Decommissioned Platform Reef Zone (the "Iron Atoll")** off the Paracels, and converted — flag by flag, rig by rig — into the largest artificial coral sanctuary on Earth: a monument built from the very steel it once used to claim the sea.
