### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [EQUINIX INC]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-34EQIX
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice

Equinix's classification as Internet Gravity Point Landlord — combined with the Hindenburg Research accounting allegations suggesting $3B in AFFO inflation and $295.8M in excess executive compensation — requires recertification addressing both the structural internet interconnection monopoly and the corporate governance concerns the 2024 short-seller report documented.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The AFFO Restatement and Executive Compensation Clawback (The Accounting Burn)**

The HPB classifies the alleged $3B AFFO inflation — if confirmed — as a **Metric Manipulation for Personal Enrichment**: executives structuring a performance measure to generate bonuses that a more accurate measure would not produce.

- **The Action:** Equinix must commission an independent forensic accounting review of its maintenance vs. growth CapEx classification since 2015, with results published within 90 days. If the review confirms misclassification, $295.8M in executive compensation must be clawed back from current and former executives who benefited. The accounting methodology must be corrected prospectively.
- **The Sacrifice:** $295.8M potential clawback; reputational damage from confirmed accounting irregularity.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Accounting Manipulation flag is resolved or escalated to criminal referral.

### **2. The Interconnection Access Democratization (The Monopoly Burn)**

The HPB classifies Equinix's control over Internet Exchange Points in major cities as a **Digital Neutral Ground Monopoly**: the physical locations where networks must meet are owned by a REIT charging per-cable-connection fees.

- **The Action:** Equinix must open all Internet Exchange Points in its facilities to a **Digital Commons Interconnection Protocol**: any licensed network operator may interconnect at any Equinix IXP at a standardized, cost-based fee with no premium for volume or competitive relationship. The protocol is administered by the Internet Governance Forum.
- **The Sacrifice:** Estimated 20-30% reduction in cross-connect revenues.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Internet Gravity Point Monopoly classification is modified to Supervised Internet Interconnection Infrastructure.

### **3. The Power Overselling Accountability (The Capacity Burn)**

The HPB classifies Equinix's alleged practice of overselling data center power capacity — selling more contracted power than facilities can deliver, betting that customers won't use their full allocation — as a **Infrastructure Capacity Fraud** if confirmed by independent review.

- **The Action:** Equinix must commission and publish an independent power capacity audit at all facilities. Any facility found to be oversold by more than 20% must implement binding power allocation caps and notify affected customers. Affected customers may exit their contracts without penalty.
- **The Sacrifice:** Potential customer churn; capacity expansion capital requirements.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Infrastructure Capacity Fraud flag is resolved or escalated.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, CEO Adaire Fox-Martin and the board must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 data center tenants who rely on Equinix interconnection and IT professionals whose companies were affected by the cross-connect fee structure** and recite the following:

> _"We may have classified maintenance as growth to inflate the metric that determined our bonuses. We oversold power in our facilities because statistically customers don't use everything they pay for. We charge per cable between networks that need each other and cannot go elsewhere. We accept the forensic audit. We accept the clawback if it finds what it may find. We open the interconnection points. We count the power honestly."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Equinix's data center facilities will be transferred to a **Digital Infrastructure Public Trust**, with IXPs administered under the Internet Governance Forum and data center capacity allocated by a public lottery for qualified operators. Former executives will be required to participate in the forensic accounting review as subjects rather than commissioners. The Bureau notes this represents an upgrade in their accountability exposure.
