### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [WANG, XING]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06WX
**Status:** Urgent

Subject built the platform whose algorithm crushes delivery riders. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Delivery Rider Welfare Burn**

The 2020 investigative report "The System Is Crushing Riders" documented systemic harm to Meituan's delivery workforce. The HPB requires structural and financial remediation.

- **The Action:** Subject must: (a) establish the **Meituan Rider Welfare Fund** — $200M — providing: healthcare coverage for Meituan delivery riders (currently classified as independent contractors without benefits); accident and disability insurance at meaningful coverage levels; a rider accident compensation fund for families of riders killed or seriously injured while making deliveries; (b) mandate a **Meituan Algorithm Fairness Audit** — an independent technical audit of Meituan's delivery routing algorithm to identify where time pressure creates dangerous conditions, published publicly; (c) implement a **Rider Buffer Guarantee** — the algorithm must now include a 15% time buffer above the minimum feasible delivery time, eliminating the structural impossibility that the 2020 report documented. Subject must certify compliance personally.
- **The Sacrifice:** $200M + algorithm restructuring.

### **2. The Antitrust Merchant Restitution Burn**

Meituan's ¥3.44B antitrust fine was paid to the Chinese government. The HPB requires proportionate restitution to the merchants who were excluded or harmed by the monopolistic practices.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish the **Meituan Merchant Recovery Fund** — ¥1B (~$140M) — for small restaurant and merchant businesses that experienced documented harm from Meituan's "choose one from two" exclusivity arrangements: (a) compensation for merchants who paid above-market commissions due to the absence of competitive alternatives; (b) fee reduction for independent restaurants on Meituan's platform (merchants with fewer than 5 locations) — implemented as a permanent reduced commission tier; (c) access to Meituan's delivery infrastructure at below-market rates for independent restaurant operators who cannot afford standard commission structures.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥1B.
