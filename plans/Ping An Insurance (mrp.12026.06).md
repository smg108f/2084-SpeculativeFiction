### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [PING AN INSURANCE]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-15PA
**Status:** Urgent — Data Sovereignty Audit Order Active

Ping An Insurance's Critical Ascension Risk classification requires immediate disclosure of data sharing arrangements with Chinese government entities, biometric data governance, and beneficial ownership structure. The HPB acknowledges that this audit operates at the boundary of its jurisdictional authority given Ping An's Chinese domicile, but proceeds on the basis that any organization whose data systems affect global financial markets and biometric databases maintained in multiple jurisdictions falls within HPB review scope.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Biometric Data Governance Protocol (The Surveillance Architecture Burn)**

Ping An's facial and voice recognition systems — deployed to 1.4 million agents and tens of millions of policyholders — constitute a biometric database of extraordinary scale. The HPB requires that any organization maintaining this scale of biometric data demonstrate that it is governed in the interests of the data subjects, not the data holder.

- **The Action:** Ping An must publish a **Biometric Data Governance Charter** covering: (a) all biometric data types collected; (b) retention periods; (c) access controls and audit logs; (d) explicit disclosure of all government access requests received in the past five years and their outcomes; (e) a binding commitment to notify data subjects within 30 days of any government data access request relating to their biometrics.
- **The Sacrifice:** Exposure of government data access patterns; potential diplomatic consequences; customer concern upon receiving notifications.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Conversion from "Biometric Financial Panopticon" to "Disclosed Biometric Operator" — a status the HPB considers the minimum acceptable for any organization holding facial recognition data on hundreds of millions of people.

### **2. The Ownership Disclosure Order (The Murky Structure Burn)**

The HPB cannot assess Ping An's Ascension Risk without understanding who owns it. The "murky structure" characterized in published analysis — including reported CCP-adjacent family ownership worth billions — must be fully disclosed.

- **The Action:** Ping An must submit to the HPB a complete **Beneficial Ownership Disclosure**: all shareholders controlling more than 0.5% of equity or voting rights, including trusts, holding companies, and family structures, with CCP affiliation status for each disclosed entity. This disclosure is submitted to the HPB; the HPB will publish a summary, redacting personal addresses but not names or ownership percentages.
- **The Sacrifice:** Full exposure of political ownership affiliations; potential market disruption if ownership is more concentrated or politically affiliated than currently understood.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Reclassification from "Unauditable" to "Disclosed" — enabling the HPB to assess actual political risk rather than speculating about it.

### **3. The Data-Finance Structural Separation (The Convergence Burn)**

The convergence of insurance, banking, and biometrics in a single corporate structure with CCP-adjacent ownership is the HPB's primary structural concern. This convergence must be architecturally limited.

- **The Action:** Ping An must implement **Data Firewall Protocols** ensuring that biometric data collected for insurance purposes cannot be shared with or accessed by Ping An's banking, investment, or technology subsidiaries without explicit customer consent for each specific use. A neutral third-party technical auditor must certify the firewall's effectiveness annually.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduction in cross-selling effectiveness that depends on unified data profiles; competitive disadvantage versus integrated competitors.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Conversion from "Surveillance-Finance Fusion" to "Segmented Financial Services Provider" — a category that substantially reduces the HPB's Critical Ascension Risk classification.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Chinese Rural Insurance Policyholders and International Data Privacy Advocates** and recite the following:

> *"We know your face. We know your voice. We know your medical history. We know your bank account. We know these things because you gave them to us for insurance purposes. We acknowledge that knowing these things creates obligations we have not fully honored. The government access requests will be disclosed. The ownership will be revealed. The data will stay where you put it. We are an insurer. We are not a surveillance service. The distinction matters, and we will demonstrate it."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the Apotheosis Protocol. All Ping An biometric databases will be placed under the supervision of the **International Biometric Data Accountability Tribunal**, with all data access — including by Ping An itself — requiring Tribunal approval. The company will discover that operating a biometric surveillance architecture without governance is possible; operating it under full Tribunal oversight is substantially less efficient.
