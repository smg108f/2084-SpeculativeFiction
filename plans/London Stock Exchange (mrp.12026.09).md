### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [LONDON STOCK EXCHANGE GROUP PLC]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09LSEG
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

LSEG's Critical classification stems from its structural combination of trading-venue infrastructure and financial data provision. Recertification targets this vertical integration directly.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Data Access Neutrality Mandate (The Largest Burn)**

- **The Action:** Guarantee equal-terms access to reference data products for all market participants, including competing exchanges and data providers, independent of trading-venue relationship.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of preferential data-bundling advantages currently available to LSEG's own exchange clients.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Directly addresses the vertical-integration concentration concern.

### **2. The Switching Cost Reduction**

- **The Action:** Publish standardized data-migration protocols to reduce the multi-year switching costs currently locking institutional clients into LSEG's data infrastructure.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced client retention advantage from switching-cost lock-in.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Restores genuine competitive pressure in the financial data market.

### **3. The Market Structure Transparency**

- **The Action:** Publish annual disclosure of any preferential arrangements between the exchange and data divisions that could advantage LSEG-affiliated trading activity.
- **The Sacrifice:** Increased regulatory scrutiny of internal cross-divisional arrangements.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Ensures the dual market-and-data role does not create undisclosed structural advantages.
