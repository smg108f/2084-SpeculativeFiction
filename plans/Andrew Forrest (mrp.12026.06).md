### **1. The Green Hydrogen Accountability Burn**

Andrew Forrest has made more public statements about green hydrogen than almost anyone alive. The HPB now asks for accountability.

- **The Action:** Publish a binding **Fortescue Green Transition Accountability Charter**: (a) specific, time-bound milestones for green hydrogen revenue as a percentage of Fortescue total revenue; (b) explicit sunset date for Fortescue's fossil energy operations; (c) annual independent auditor assessment of progress against these milestones, published publicly. Forrest must also personally commit to a **Green Hydrogen Revenue Floor** — the point at which he will publicly acknowledge if the pivot has failed and recalibrate strategy accordingly.
- **The Sacrifice:** The ability to keep the green pivot as perpetual rhetoric without metrics.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts Australia's loudest green mining billionaire from advocacy into accountability.

### **2. The Pilbara Environmental Remediation Burn**

Fortescue's Pilbara iron ore operations have significant environmental footprint — land disturbance, water use, dust pollution, and impact on the ecosystems and communities of the Pilbara region.

- **The Action:** Fund a **Pilbara Environmental Legacy Trust** with a A$500M ($330M) endowment — independently administered — to fund environmental rehabilitation of mined land, water system restoration, and long-term monitoring of Pilbara ecosystems. This is separate from existing Minderoo commitments.
- **The Sacrifice:** A$500M.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Creates a permanent remediation fund proportionate to the environmental cost of iron ore extraction.

### **3. The Minderoo Scaling Burn**

The Minderoo Foundation is substantive but the HPB asks for one specific addition: a formal, binding commitment to indigenous land rights advocacy at a scale commensurate with Forrest's wealth and his stated commitments.

- **The Action:** Forrest must make a formal legal commitment via Minderoo to: fund indigenous land rights legal advocacy at A$50M/year minimum; support free, prior, and informed consent (FPIC) processes for any Fortescue operations on or near indigenous land; and establish an **Indigenous Advisory Council** with formal veto power over Fortescue operations affecting native title lands.
- **The Sacrifice:** Corporate flexibility on indigenous land development.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts stated indigenous rights commitments into binding institutional structures.
