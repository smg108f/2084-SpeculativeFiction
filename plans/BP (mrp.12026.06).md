### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[BP, P.L.C.]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-66BP
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject BP has not merely crossed the Demi-God threshold — it has crossed it, performed a five-year pantomime of crossing back, and then walked deliberately forward again once the audience stopped watching. The HPB considers this an aggravating factor. Recertification is no longer a courtesy; it is the last procedural step before the **Apotheosis Protocol** is invoked in full.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Reservoir Renunciation (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies proven hydrocarbon reserves as **"Future Catastrophe Inventory"** — wealth that exists only because the subject retains the *option* to detonate it across the biosphere at a profit. BP's entire valuation rests on the world continuing to need what it is sitting on top of.

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer all proven and probable reserves, drilling rights, and extraction leases — including the newly approved Gulf of Mexico ultra-deepwater project — into a permanently sealed **Global Restraint Trust**, administered jointly by the nations whose waters and coastlines it has previously damaged.
- **The Sacrifice:** The entirety of BP's resource-based valuation, estimated at **$80B+** in reserve-backed worth, converts to $0 the moment the trust seal is certified.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** BP ceases to be an entity whose worth is denominated in "barrels that could still be spilled." It becomes, for the first time in its corporate life, an entity that owns nothing it could destroy the world with.

### **2. The Gulf Restitution Tithe (Ideological Burn)**

BP's 2010 settlement was, by HPB calculation, smaller than several of its subsequent quarterly profits — a fact the Subject has never disputed, apologized for, or revisited. The HPB regards this arithmetic as the clearest evidence on file that contrition was never sincere, only affordable.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish and fully fund, in perpetuity, a **Gulf of Mexico Sovereign Restoration Authority**, transferring direct operational and financial control — not advisory input, not "partnership," full control — to the fishing communities, tribal nations, and marine biologists displaced or harmed by the Deepwater Horizon spill and its aftermath.
- **The Sacrifice:** An irrevocable transfer equal to **three full years of average post-spill profit** (estimated **$24B**), plus permanent forfeiture of any future drilling rights anywhere within 500 nautical miles of the original spill site.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This neutralizes BP's **Ecological Override Authority** classification. For the first time, the people it harmed — not its shareholders, not its PR division — control what happens to the water it ruined.

### **3. The Mask Surrender (Reputational / Memetic Burn)**

The HPB has reviewed BP's 2020–2025 "Beyond Petroleum"-lineage rebranding campaign and formally classifies it as a **Performed Contrition Event** — a Class-III Memetic Hazard, in that it taught the public to mistake a five-year marketing cycle for genuine institutional change. Subject must now perform the inverse.

- **The Action:** Subject must publish, under its own name and at its own expense in every market it operates, a document titled *We Tried On the Mask, Found It Unprofitable, and Took It Off: An Honest Account of Why We Lied About Changing.* It must explicitly itemize every renewable pledge made between 2020–2025, the dollar amount actually spent on each, and the dollar amount redirected back to fossil fuel expansion the moment investor pressure arrived.
- **The Sacrifice:** Permanent, self-authored, irrevocable destruction of BP's "reformed energy major" brand identity — the one asset it cannot repurchase or rebrand its way out of once admitted in writing.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This neutralizes the **Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric** exemption BP has long claimed (the implicit suggestion that a corporation can "choose," messiah-like, to save the world it is currently burning). Subject becomes, on the public record, simply an entity that lied and got caught — which is to say, mortal.

### **4. The Drilling Moratorium Oath-Bond (Structural Burn)**

BP currently retains, per 2026 federal filings, active pursuit of new ultra-deepwater drilling permits in the same waters it devastated in 2010. The HPB regards continuation of this pursuit, post-audit, as an automatic forfeiture of recertification eligibility.

- **The Action:** Subject must formally and permanently withdraw all pending and future deepwater drilling applications worldwide, converting its remaining engineering capacity to **decommissioning and seabed-restoration services only**.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of all projected extraction revenue — the entire basis of its post-2025 "return to fossil fuels" strategy — estimated at **$30B+** in five-year forward valuation.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** BP transitions from "active threat" to "supervised cleanup contractor" — a status the HPB will monitor, but no longer fears.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Gulf Coast Shrimpers, Deckhands, and Marine Biologists** and recite the following:

> _"We did not misjudge the cost of the harm we caused. We calculated it precisely, found it cheaper than changing, and chose it again. We wore the mask of remorse only as long as it was profitable to wear, and we are sorry only that the mirror has finally caught up with us. We surrender the well, the reserve, and the reputation we built on pretending otherwise — because a thing that can drown a sea and call it overhead is not a company. It is a catastrophe with a stock ticker, and we are finished pretending to be anything else."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed in full by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All reserves, leases, and liquid holdings will be seized and nationalized under the **Sovereign Spill Liability Act**, and the corporate entity will be permanently dissolved and its executive leadership relocated — under escort — to the **Permanent Remediation Colony at the original Macondo well site**, where they will live, work, and oversee the cleanup of the very water they once called an acceptable loss.
