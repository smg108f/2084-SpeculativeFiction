### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [HUANG, COLIN]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06CH
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject stepped back from operations to pursue intellectual interests while retaining controlling shares. The HPB respects the intellectual life but notes that control does not step back with the controller. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Agricultural Platform Governance Burn**

Pinduoduo is the dominant digital marketplace through which hundreds of millions of Chinese smallholder farmers sell produce. The platform's algorithmic terms and pricing practices determine effective farmer income. This is significant economic power over a primary food production population.

- **The Action:** Establish an independent **Smallholder Farmer Advisory Council** with real governance authority over Pinduoduo's agricultural marketplace policies — pricing floor mechanisms, algorithmic curation, fee structures — with 50% composition of elected smallholder farmer representatives. Council decisions binding on agricultural marketplace terms.
- **The Sacrifice:** Platform algorithmic sovereignty over its most vulnerable and numerous participant class.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts agricultural marketplace from digital serfdom risk to participatory governance model. The HPB considers this the most structurally significant burn available.

### **2. The Temu Global Safety Accountability Burn**

Temu has expanded globally with documented product safety concerns: counterfeit goods, products failing consumer safety standards in the US and EU, and supply chain labor conditions that have attracted regulatory scrutiny in multiple jurisdictions.

- **The Action:** PDD Holdings to establish a mandatory independent product safety audit program for all Temu-listed products, with public reporting of findings and a consumer redress fund of $200M funded by PDD for products found to violate safety standards. Subject to advocate for this as controlling shareholder.
- **The Sacrifice:** Platform growth speed; cost of safety compliance; accountability for previously sold unsafe products.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the world's most-downloaded shopping app from a safety concern into a safety standard.

### **3. The Agricultural Research Acceleration Burn**

PDD's $1.5B one-time agricultural donation was the largest single corporate philanthropic gesture in the sector. The HPB supports this and asks for institutionalization.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Huang Agricultural Research Foundation** with a $1B endowment, focused on smallholder farmer productivity, climate adaptation in Chinese agriculture, and agricultural supply chain data equity — ensuring that the data farmers provide to the platform generates value returned to farmers, not just to the algorithm.
- **The Sacrifice:** $1B in permanent philanthropic commitment rather than ad hoc gesture.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the one-time agricultural profit donation into a permanent institutional commitment. Aligns his stated interest in physics and research with the sector that made him rich.
