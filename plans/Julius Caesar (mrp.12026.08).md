### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[CAESAR, JULIUS]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-08JCA
**Status:** Precautionary

Subject is deceased (44 BC) and cannot be compelled to divest, appear, or recite an oath. There is, however, unlike a private fortune, a genuine ongoing "estate": the political-religious title Subject purchased in life (Pontifex Maximus, later absorbed into the imperial office), and the personal name itself, which became the working word for "absolute ruler" in at least three language families and is still doing that job in 2084. The HPB's Office of Posthumous Franchises treats this diffuse, effectively ownerless brand — now claimed by no living heir, though borrowed by two millennia of monarchs who never asked permission — as the de facto estate for recertification purposes.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days by whichever institutions, states, and cultural entities currently profit from the brand, or their court-appointed equivalents.

---

### **1. The Gallic Plunder Restitution (The Largest Burn)**

Per audit aa.12026.08, Subject's fortune at the age-50 anchor rests on a documented seizure of the Roman state treasury (Pliny, *Natural History* 33.56) plus a capitalized annual tribute extracted from conquered Gaul (Suetonius) plus a conservative allowance for uncatalogued temple and battlefield plunder — real wealth taken from real places, chief among them regions now within modern France, Belgium, and Switzerland.

- **The Action:** The capitalized value of the Gallic tribute stream and the documented 49 BC treasury seizure is converted into a **Gallic Reparations Whuffie Fund**, administered jointly by the HPB's Office of Posthumous Franchises and the heritage-preservation authorities of the modern nations occupying former Gallic territory. No living claimant may draw against it directly; it funds cultural and infrastructure restitution projects in the historically conquered regions in perpetuity.
- **The Sacrifice:** The single largest documented line item in Subject's fortune — a treasury withdrawal made over a tribune's formal objection — is permanently extinguished as personal or dynastic property.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the audit's most concrete, primary-sourced asset from "conqueror's spoils" to "restitution instrument" — the closest thing this file has to a genuine Saintly Pivot.

### **2. The Pontifex Maximus Decommission**

Subject fused Rome's highest religious office with personal political ambition, reportedly winning the priesthood in 63 BC through decisive bribery, and leaned publicly on his family's claimed divine descent from Venus — a lineage claim formalized architecturally within two years of the age-50 anchor via the Temple of Venus Genetrix, built with war spoils. This is a textbook Deity-Adjacent Franchise: metaphysical sanction purchased, then deployed to justify accumulation and conquest.

- **The Action:** The office and its divine-genealogy claim are stripped of any residual legitimizing authority in the Bureau's registry. Any modern political or commercial use of "descended from the gods," "chosen by destiny," or equivalent framing tied to Subject's lineage claim must carry a mandatory rider identifying it as a purchased, self-serving religious-political fusion — not neutral mythology.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loses the unearned "sanctioned by heaven" framing that has flattered every subsequent ruler who claimed descent, mandate, or destiny.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Formally separates church from throne, twenty centuries after the fact, and denies the "Genus Veneris" claim any further free promotional use.

### **3. The "Caesar" Trademark Dissolution**

Subject's name did not merely survive him — it became the generic term for supreme ruler across multiple empires and languages that never touched Rome: Kaiser in German, Tsar in Russian and the broader Slavic world, Qaysar in Arabic. Two thousand and sixty-nine years after Subject's death, "Caesar" remains arguably the single most commercially and politically successful personal brand in human history, still lending unearned gravitas to every "salad," "cipher," and self-styled strongman that borrows it.

- **The Action:** All state, commercial, and cultural entities currently using "Caesar," "Kaiser," "Tsar," or direct derivatives as a title, honorific, or brand of authority must register the usage with the HPB and attach a mandatory **"Dictator Perpetuo Context Disclosure"** — a plain-language note that the original bearer of the name seized a state treasury, commanded personally loyal legions, and was assassinated by his own governing body for accumulating exactly the kind of power the title now romanticizes.
- **The Sacrifice:** The name loses its two-millennia frictionless association with legitimate, glorious, or inevitable rule.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the most durable strongman brand in recorded history from unexamined honorific to flagged cautionary label.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Discharged Legionary Veterans and Indebted Roman Shopkeepers** — the two constituencies Subject's populist programs most directly courted, and the two constituencies best positioned to judge whether that courtship was generosity or good politics — and recite the following:

> _"I crossed a shallow river once, and called it fate because I had already decided to cross it. The die was never cast — I threw it myself, and blamed the throw. I held an office bought with bribes and called it the will of the gods. I forgave a fraction of what was owed and let the grateful forget it was only a fraction. I will not be dictator for a year, for a decade, or forever. I will not let my name become another man's excuse to rule without asking. Let 'Caesar' be a name in a history book again — not a throne."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** This is, per Bureau posthumous-franchise precedent, the permanent default state — Subject cannot burn, testify, or comply. The Apotheosis Protocol therefore activates automatically at the 30-day mark regardless: the "Caesar"/"Kaiser"/"Tsar" trademark class is nationalized into public-cautionary-utility status, usable but never again without the Context Disclosure, and the case is filed under the Bureau's **"Franchise Without Heir"** doctrine.

The Bureau notes for the record, with no small discomfort, that a recertification of sorts already occurred on 15 March 44 BC: roughly sixty senators, twenty-three blades, zero burn schedule, zero due process, and a body left at the foot of a statue of the man Subject had once defeated in this same civil war. It worked, in the narrowest possible sense — Subject's personal Ascension trajectory ended that afternoon. The Bureau wishes to be unambiguous that this is not a model it endorses, recommends, or would ever authorize. A conspiracy of dagger-wielding colleagues is not a Lottery-Selected Jury. Stabbing a man sixty times in a hallway is not a substitute for thirty days and an asset schedule. History reached the outcome this Bureau exists to prevent by getting there first, illegally, and at the cost of a civil war that outlasted the assassins by more than a decade. The Bureau records the precedent and closes the file.
