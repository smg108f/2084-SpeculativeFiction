### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [CROWDSTRIKE HOLDINGS INC]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-25CRWD
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

CrowdStrike's classification as the proof case for **Structural Impunity** in cybersecurity infrastructure — having caused the largest IT outage in history, retained all major government contracts, and increased lobbying spend in the aftermath — requires urgent recertification with specific accountability mechanisms that the July 2024 incident demonstrated were entirely absent.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Liability Cap Dissolution (The Accountability Burn)**

The HPB classifies CrowdStrike's contractual limitation of liability provisions — which capped its legal exposure from the July 2024 outage at subscription fees paid, despite Fortune 500 losses of $5.4B — as a **Structural Impunity Architecture**: a commercial contract designed to ensure the company bears no meaningful portion of the harm it causes.

- **The Action:** CrowdStrike must accept statutory minimum liability for any outage affecting more than 500,000 endpoints simultaneously, set at 30% of total documented losses up to $20B. This liability must be held in a regulated escrow fund of $2B, replenished annually from CrowdStrike revenues.
- **The Sacrifice:** Existential balance sheet risk if another outage occurs at July 2024 scale; $2B in escrowed capital.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** CrowdStrike's Structural Impunity classification is resolved.

### **2. The Kernel Access Governance Mandate (The Deep Access Burn)**

The HPB classifies CrowdStrike's kernel-level operating system access on millions of critical infrastructure devices as a **Deep Infrastructure Presence** without corresponding public oversight.

- **The Action:** CrowdStrike must accept an HPB-appointed technical oversight board with full read access to Falcon agent code, all content configuration updates before deployment, and veto authority on any update affecting more than 1M government or critical infrastructure endpoints simultaneously.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of operational autonomy over its core product deployment; competitive intelligence exposure to oversight board.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Deep Infrastructure Presence flag is replaced with Supervised Infrastructure Access.

### **3. The CEO Accountability Provision (The Personal Burn)**

The HPB classifies the decision to send a deputy rather than CEO George Kurtz to testify before Congress after the July 2024 outage as a **Accountability Displacement** — structuring a corporation's relationship with democratic oversight to minimize personal accountability for those most responsible.

- **The Action:** Effective immediately, CrowdStrike's CEO is personally required to testify before any legislative body requesting testimony related to outages affecting more than 250,000 endpoints. Failure to appear constitutes a violation of the Demi-God Act's Personal Accountability Provision.
- **The Sacrifice:** CEO faces personal accountability for company actions — an arrangement the Bureau considers standard.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Accountability Displacement flag is resolved.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, CEO George Kurtz must stand — personally, not via a deputy — before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 hospital IT administrators and airline operations managers affected by the July 2024 outage** and recite the following:

> _"My company's software update grounded planes, cancelled surgeries, and shut down emergency services. I sent someone else to explain this to Congress. I accepted contracts that capped my liability at the value of the subscription fee while the losses I caused ran to billions. I accept the escrow. I accept the oversight board. I accept personal accountability. I will appear when asked."_

All government and critical infrastructure contracts will be transferred to a federal cybersecurity authority. George Kurtz will be required to appear before Congress. The Bureau notes he will find the experience familiar, if belated.
