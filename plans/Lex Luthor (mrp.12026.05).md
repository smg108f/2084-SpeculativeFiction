### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [LUTHOR, LEX]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-23-LL
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice / Personal Audit Coordinated with HPB-12026-05-LC / Subject Attendance Required

This plan is issued in coordination with the LexCorp corporate recertification plan (HPB-12026-05-LC). Together, the two plans address Lex Luthor's full Ascension risk profile — the corporate vehicle and the individual who operates it. Subject has attended zero of five prior HPB interview requests. Subject's attendance at the compliance meeting for this plan is not optional. The Bureau has noted the legal arguments against mandatory attendance. The Bureau disagrees with them and will be proceeding accordingly.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. Anti-Superman Strategic Intelligence — Public Domain Release**

Subject's personal intellectual investment in understanding Kryptonian vulnerabilities has produced the most comprehensive privately held anti-Kryptonian intelligence database in existence. This database constitutes both an asset — governments and private parties have paid for access — and a risk: a private individual holding exclusive knowledge of how to harm or kill the world's most powerful hero is a concentration of anti-superhuman capability that the HPB cannot ignore regardless of whether Luthor has ever deployed it with lethal intent.

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer all anti-Kryptonian research, tactical analysis, and vulnerability documentation to a sealed HPB-UN joint registry, with access restricted to governments with legitimate planetary defense obligations and prohibited to private parties, criminal organizations, and Subject himself post-transfer. Subject may retain his own recollection of the knowledge — the Bureau cannot compel forgetting — but may not use it commercially, may not share it, and loses the asset value it currently represents.
- **The Sacrifice:** Luthor loses the most distinctive personal intellectual asset he has built across a lifetime of obsessive study. He remains smarter than almost anyone else. He simply stops being the exclusive curator of a Superman kill database.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Anti-Superhuman Strategic Intelligence classification removed from personal RTWI. Estimated asset reduction of $30–40 billion.

---

### **2. Presidential Asset Conversion — Registered Conflict Disclosure**

Subject's use of the US presidency to cultivate regulatory relationships and access classified intelligence that subsequently informed private business decisions constitutes Public Office Conversion — an asset accumulation the HPB's Political Asset Division cannot leave unaddressed. The Bureau cannot retroactively undo the presidency. It can require full disclosure of the relationships and access it generated, and it can restrict their continued commercial use.

- **The Action:** Subject must file a comprehensive Conflict Disclosure with the HPB and the Office of Government Ethics covering all regulatory relationships, classified intelligence access, and government contracting positions cultivated during his presidential term and continuing to generate financial benefit post-term. All commercially exploited relationships must either be terminated or, where termination is not possible, placed under ongoing disclosure obligations with annual HPB review.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses the ability to monetize his presidential relationships without disclosure. The relationships may continue to exist. They may not continue to operate as undisclosed assets.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Public Office Conversion classification resolved. Political capital converted from undisclosed asset to disclosed and monitored relationship portfolio.

---

### **3. Personal Attendance — The Interview**

The HPB requires that Lex Luthor attend a compliance meeting in person. Not by attorney. Not by written submission. Not by video link managed by counsel. In person, in a Bureau facility, with Bureau auditors, for a minimum of four hours. Subject may have one attorney present in a non-speaking advisory capacity.

- **The Action:** Subject schedules and attends the compliance meeting within the 30-day window. The meeting covers: (a) review of both the personal and LexCorp audit findings; (b) Subject's response to the specific findings, on the record; (c) Subject's commitment to the Asset Burn schedule in both plans. The meeting is recorded. The transcript is filed in the sealed HPB registry.
- **The Sacrifice:** Lex Luthor, who is the smartest man in most rooms, must sit in a room where the agenda is set by other people and where his primary defense — not being present — is unavailable. He must answer questions. He must do so without knowing in advance exactly what will be asked.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Bureau's five-session interview backlog is resolved. Subject's cooperation is documented. The audit record becomes, for the first time, bilateral.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 citizens of Metropolis** — specifically drawn from individuals who have been present during Superman's documented interventions, people whose lives he has saved — and recite the following:

> _"I am the smartest human being alive. I have spent that intelligence, for decades, trying to prove that a man who flies is the real threat to human potential. I built weapons. I ran for office. I sent lawyers to five audit interviews. I have been, by every available metric, the most prepared person in every room I have ever entered — and I have used that preparation to avoid every accountability structure that was designed to contain people like me. The Bureau is not asking me to be less intelligent. It is asking me to be accountable. Those are not the same thing. I have spent my life pretending they were. I am attending the interview."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If Subject fails to attend the compliance meeting or complete the Asset Burn Schedule within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol — Individual Criminal Tier**, which includes: immediate forced divestiture of all personal assets above $1 billion; referral of the full LexCorp and personal audit records to the DOJ, the SEC, and the International Criminal Court's DC Universe Division; and public release of the Bureau's full findings including the Presidential Asset Conversion documentation. The HPB additionally notes that it has, as a procedural matter, already shared the audit's existence and general findings with Clark Kent of the Daily Planet — not as a leak, but as a notification to a journalist of public interest in a matter of documented public concern. Mr. Kent expressed that he would be following the story closely. The Bureau found his interest unsurprising.
