**Whuffie Credit Applied:** Self-made from factory floor in rural poverty — exceptional entrepreneurial trajectory. Burns partially reduced.
**Status:** Standard (reduced)

---

### **1. The Worker Welfare Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must fund RMB 500M (≈$70M) for a **Lens Technology Worker Excellence Program** — improving dormitory conditions, providing on-site healthcare, subsidizing education for workers' children in their home provinces, and implementing a profit-sharing program distributing 3% of Lens annual profits to frontline production workers.
- **The Sacrifice:** RMB 500M + ongoing profit share.

### **2. The Hunan Hometown Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must fund RMB 300M (≈$42M) for **Hunan Rural Education Access** — building and endowing schools in the rural Hunan counties where Lens Technology recruits most of its workforce, ensuring that the next generation of children from these communities have access to quality education rather than only factory employment.
- **The Sacrifice:** RMB 300M.

### **3. The Women in Manufacturing Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must establish a **Zhou Qunfei Women's Manufacturing Leadership Fund** — funding RMB 200M in programs promoting women's advancement from factory floor to technical and managerial roles in Chinese manufacturing, specifically in Hunan and other central provinces where female factory workers predominate but female management is rare.
- **The Sacrifice:** RMB 200M.
