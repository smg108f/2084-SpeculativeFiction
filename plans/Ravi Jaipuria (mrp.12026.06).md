### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [JAIPURIA, RAVI]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06RJ
**Status:** Moderate

Subject's beverage and fast food empire operates at scale in a market with a significant and growing diabetes epidemic. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 180 days.

---

### **1. The Public Health Proportionality Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must fund ₹5B (~$60M) for the **RJ Corp Public Health Fund**: (a) ₹2B for India's National Programme for Prevention and Control of Cancer, Diabetes, Cardiovascular Diseases and Stroke (NPCDCS) — specifically the diabetes prevention component in states where Devyani and RJ Corp have the highest market penetration; (b) ₹2B for **Nutrition Literacy Programs** — funding school-based nutrition education in India, teaching children and families to understand the nutritional content of the products RJ Corp sells; (c) ₹1B for a **Healthier Menu Initiative** — R&D investment in lower-sugar, lower-calorie alternatives across Devyani's KFC and Pizza Hut menus in India, with a binding commitment to offer at least one low-sugar Pepsi alternative in every RJ Corp outlet by 12028.
- **The Sacrifice:** ₹5B (~$60M).

### **2. The Community Development Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must fund ₹3B (~$36M) for the **RJ Corp Foundation** focused on livelihoods and small business development in the communities where franchises operate — supporting independent food vendors and small restaurant operators who face competition from RJ Corp's franchised operations.
- **The Sacrifice:** ₹3B (~$36M).

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Indian Diabetes Patients, South Asian Nutrition Researchers, and Independent Food Vendors**:

> *"We sell Pepsi and fried chicken across South Asia. More than a billion servings a year. India's diabetes rate is among the fastest growing in the world. The public health fund goes to prevention programs in the markets where we operate. The nutrition literacy program means our customers understand what they are buying. The community fund supports the independent vendors who compete with our franchises — because their livelihoods matter too."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** Subject will be assigned to **FSS RJ CORP** — a vessel that serves exclusively the healthiest versions of every product in the RJ Corp and Devyani portfolio — zero-sugar Pepsi, grilled chicken only, salad in place of fries — at the same price as the standard menu, demonstrating what the franchise looks like when public health is the design constraint rather than the afterthought.
