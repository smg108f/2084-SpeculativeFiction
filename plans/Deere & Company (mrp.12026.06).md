### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[DEERE & COMPANY]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06DC
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject has been classified a **CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK** on grounds that its market position constitutes not wealth concentration but **subsistence capture** — direct, revocable control over the machinery that plants and harvests a measurable share of the human food supply. Recertification is not optional; it is the only mechanism by which the Bureau can guarantee that no future dispute, glitch, or boardroom decision can be used to starve a continent.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The "Service ADVISOR" Decoupling (The Largest and Most Symbolic Burn)**

The HPB classifies proprietary repair-authorization software as a **"Divine Kill-Switch"** — an asset whose entire value derives from the Bureau's and the public's *inability to remove it*. Subject's continued ownership of Service ADVISOR™ constitutes the single clearest act of god-behavior on file: the quiet reservation of a button that could, at subject's sole discretion, brick a harvest mid-season.

- **The Action:** Subject must release **all diagnostic, repair, and firmware-authorization tools** — including Service ADVISOR™ — into an open, irrevocable public commons license, administered by a farmer-elected **Repair Commons Trust**, with no subscription, dealership-exclusivity, or kill-switch capability of any kind, ever, for any machine bearing the subject's name.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of all aftermarket service revenue, dealership-network leverage, and the structural ability to ever again "remotely disable" a customer's equipment.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject converts from **Toll-Keeper of the Harvest** to **Tool-Maker Among Equals** — an estimated **$40B** reduction in "potentiality value" tied to monopolized aftermarket control.

### **2. The "Granary God" Renunciation (Ideological Burn)**

Subject's marketing self-mythology — "we run so the world can eat" — is, per HPB review, a **Class-III Memetic Hazard**: it recasts dependency as benevolence and positions a publicly traded corporation as a quasi-sacred guarantor of survival.

- **The Action:** Subject must publicly retire all "feeding the world" branding and instead publish, in full, an internal accounting titled *Who We Could Have Starved, And When*: a transparent ledger of every documented instance in which a software lock, license dispute, or remote disablement capability *could have* prevented a farmer from completing planting or harvest — whether or not it was used.
- **The Sacrifice:** The carefully cultivated image of the benevolent feeder of nations; replaced with a public record of **leverage it chose to hold**.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the **Subsistence-Adjacent Rhetoric** flag and reclassifies subject's public messaging from "Saintly" to merely "honest."

### **3. The Dealer-Network Tithe (Relational Burn)**

Subject's exclusive dealership architecture — the wall between farmer and the right to fix one's own machine — is the connective tissue of its monopoly profile.

- **The Action:** Subject must convert no fewer than **40% of authorized dealership service contracts** into farmer-owned repair cooperatives, fully equipped, fully licensed, and permanently free of exclusivity clauses, funded by a one-time **$2.5B Cooperative Endowment** drawn directly from corporate reserves.
- **The Sacrifice:** Permanent loss of the aftermarket chokehold that the FTC's $99M settlement only dented.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject is reclassified from **Sole Authorizer** to **Equipment Donor**, shedding the "Shadow Monopolist" designation entirely.

### **4. The Harvest-Season Ceasefire Clause (Reputational Burn)**

To close the loophole the Bureau finds most chilling — the *theoretical* ability to disable equipment during planting or harvest windows — subject must make that theoretical impossibility a matter of permanent public record.

- **The Action:** Subject must engineer, publish, and submit to independent third-party audit a **hard-coded, irreversible prohibition** against any remote disablement, geofencing, or feature-lock capability on any unit, to be verified annually by the Repair Commons Trust in perpetuity.
- **The Sacrifice:** The quiet leverage subject has held — and never had to use — over every customer who ever signed a lease.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Closes the **"Kill-Switch Pastoral"** finding permanently; subject may be considered for **Probationary Saint** review no sooner than five harvest cycles hence.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**
