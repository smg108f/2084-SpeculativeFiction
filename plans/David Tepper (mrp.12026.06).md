### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [TEPPER, DAVID]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06DT
**Status:** Urgent — Distressed Debt Profits / Insufficient Philanthropic Offset

Subject is the founder of Appaloosa Management and owner of the Carolina Panthers. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 60 days.

---

### **1. The 2009 Reckoning Burn**

David Tepper earned approximately $7B personally in 2009 — the worst year of the global financial crisis for most workers and homeowners. The money was earned legally through distressed bank debt positions. The HPB asks for a proportionate reckoning.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Appaloosa Community Recovery Fund** — a $500M contribution — dedicated to: (a) housing stability programs in the Carolinas (where Tepper's NFL franchise gives him regional presence and responsibility) for families who lost homes in the 2008-2009 foreclosure crisis; (b) financial resilience education programs targeting the populations most harmed by the crisis; (c) predatory lending reform advocacy funded at $25M/year for 10 years.
- **The Sacrifice:** $500M; public acknowledgment of the 2009 profit's context.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the largest single-year crisis profit in hedge fund history into a documented investment in the communities that lived through the other side of those returns.

### **2. The Carolina Panthers Community Accountability Burn**

NFL franchise ownership gives Tepper significant influence over one of the largest cultural institutions in the Carolinas. The HPB asks for accountability commensurate with this influence.

- **The Action:** Establish a **Panthers Community Foundation** with a minimum $50M endowment — permanently funded from 1% of Panthers annual revenue — dedicated to: (a) youth sports access in lower-income Carolina communities; (b) player mental health and post-career transition support; (c) Carolina stadium community benefit agreements ensuring surrounding neighborhood investment from team development.
- **The Sacrifice:** 1% of annual revenue; some stadium development flexibility.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts an NFL franchise from a private asset into a documented community benefit entity.

### **3. The Giving Pledge Burn**

Tepper has not signed the Giving Pledge. At $18B, this is notable.

- **The Action:** Sign the Giving Pledge and commit publicly to giving the majority of wealth to philanthropy, with an annual giving floor of 3% of net worth.
- **The Sacrifice:** The optionality of keeping all of it.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Joins 200+ peers who have made the same commitment; converts Appaloosa's returns from purely private into partially public.
