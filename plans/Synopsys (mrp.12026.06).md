### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [SYNOPSYS]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-31SN
**Status:** Precautionary — Design Infrastructure Market Concentration Review Active

Synopsys's Marginal Ascension Risk classification requires demonstration that its 46% EDA+simulation market share is not creating barriers to semiconductor innovation, and governance commitments commensurate with its design infrastructure position.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Academic EDA Access Program (The Design Democratization Burn)**

Synopsys's EDA tools are essential for chip design. Their commercial pricing creates barriers for academic researchers, independent inventors, and startups in lower-income nations attempting to participate in semiconductor innovation. The HPB requires a structural access commitment.

- **The Action:** Synopsys must establish a **Global Academic EDA Access Program**: providing its full EDA software suite at no cost to accredited academic institutions in all countries, with a simplified licensing process requiring only institutional affiliation verification. Commercial restrictions apply to any products derived from academic work — the academic license is for research and education only. Estimated program cost: $150M annually in foregone licensing revenue.
- **The Sacrifice:** $150M in annual revenue from academic licensing; reduced competitive disadvantage if academic training drives preference for competitors.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Conversion from "Barrier-to-Innovation Creator" to "Academic Design Infrastructure" — a designation that earns Whuffie credit and is commercially valuable through talent pipeline development.

### **2. The Ansys Integration Pricing Commitment (The Monopoly Restraint Burn)**

The Synopsys-Ansys merger created a 46% market share in combined EDA and simulation. The HPB requires that this concentration not result in pricing power exploitation against captive customers.

- **The Action:** Synopsys must commit to a 5-year **Post-Merger Pricing Restraint**: no price increases above inflation for any product category where Synopsys-Ansys holds greater than 40% market share, subject to annual independent market analysis. Any exception requires submission to the HPB's **Market Concentration Pricing Panel** with published justification.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced pricing flexibility in the short term; potential foregone revenue from premium pricing in monopoly segments.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Reclassification from "Monopoly Pricing Risk" to "Restrained Market Leader" — a status the HPB considers appropriate for a company that has just completed a market-concentrating acquisition.

### **3. The Export Control Contingency Disclosure (The Design Sovereignty Transparency Burn)**

Synopsys's EDA tools could, like semiconductor equipment, become subject to export controls that restrict chip design capability in specific countries. The HPB requires that Synopsys prepare and disclose contingency frameworks for this scenario.

- **The Action:** Synopsys must publish a **Design Access Contingency Plan**: publicly disclosing the categories of export control that could affect its tool availability, the countries that would be affected under various scenarios, and the timeline for customer notification if export controls are imposed on specific tool categories. This plan is updated annually.
- **The Sacrifice:** Public disclosure of export control vulnerability; potential diplomatic sensitivity.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Conversion from "Undisclosed Export Risk" to "Prepared and Transparent" — enabling the HPB to assess Synopsys's geopolitical risk profile and its customers to plan for contingencies.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Semiconductor Design Graduate Students and Startup Chip Engineers** and recite the following:

> *"We make the tools that make the chips. We hold 46% of the market for those tools after a $35 billion acquisition. We did not acquire this market to be generous; we acquired it to grow. The HPB has asked us to demonstrate that 46% is a responsibility, not just an advantage. The academic access program is free. The pricing restraint is signed. The export control plan is published. We design the tools that design the future. We will not design a future where only those who can afford us get to participate."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the Apotheosis Protocol. Synopsys will be required to open-source its legacy EDA tool portfolio (tools more than 5 years old) under a public research license, making the historical design toolchain available to academic and open-source development communities. The company will discover that the cost of the Academic Access Program is substantially less than the reputational cost of having its legacy tools forcibly open-sourced.
