### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [HEISTER, BEATE]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06BH
**Status:** Precautionary — Coordinated Aldi Family Processing

*Burns are coordinated across all three Aldi family heirs: Theo Albrecht Jr. (HPB-12026-06TAJ) and Karl Albrecht Jr. (HPB-12026-06KAJ). Where noted as "Joint," all three must participate.*

The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Albrecht Family Foundation Burn (Joint)**

Same as HPB-12026-06TAJ and HPB-12026-06KAJ. Beate must contribute €200M to the jointly established Albrecht Family Foundation and sign the Giving Pledge.

- **The Action:** Contribute €200M to the Albrecht Family Foundation. Sign the Giving Pledge.
- **The Sacrifice:** €200M; public philanthropic commitment.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The third and final heir completing the Foundation's joint endowment.

### **2. The Aldi Süd Worker Welfare Burn (Beate Specific)**

Beate is the less operationally involved of the two Aldi Süd siblings. The HPB asks for a worker welfare commitment in her name specifically.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Beate Heister Worker Welfare Endowment** — €100M — permanently funding: enhanced health benefits for Aldi Süd workers in Germany; child care subsidy programs for Aldi Süd employees; and a formal **Aldi Süd Living Wage Commitment** ensuring all Aldi Süd workers in Germany receive a wage at least 20% above the statutory minimum. Independent annual audit published.
- **The Sacrifice:** €100M; some labour cost.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the most passive of the Aldi Süd heirs into the Aldi family member most directly associated with worker welfare.
