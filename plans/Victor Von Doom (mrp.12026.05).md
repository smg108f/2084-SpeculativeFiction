### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [VON DOOM, VICTOR]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-27-VD
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice / Non-Compliant Subject / Diplomatic Complexity — Sovereign Nation

Victor Von Doom has been classified CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK. Subject has communicated, through a written message delivered by Doom-bot to the HPB's front desk, that he "does not recognize the Bureau's jurisdiction over sovereign nations, does not intend to comply with any recertification plan, and finds the entire enterprise beneath the dignity of his attention." The Bureau has filed this communication in Subject's case file. The Bureau is proceeding.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. Latverian Constitutional Amendment — Independent Oversight Body**

The HPB cannot require that Doom redistribute Latverian sovereign wealth — it would be auditing a foreign nation's treasury, which exceeds the Bureau's domestic mandate. The Bureau can, however, require that Subject — as a US-adjacent actor whose capabilities and activities routinely affect US persons and interests — submit to an accountability framework governing his personal use of Latverian sovereign resources for extra-national activities.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish, by royal decree, a Latverian Independent Oversight Council — a body of seven advisors drawn from Latverian civil society, with a mandate to review any use of Latverian sovereign resources for activities outside Latveria's borders. The Council's decisions are advisory, not binding — Doom retains absolute authority. The Council's findings are, however, published publicly on an annual basis.
- **The Sacrifice:** Doom accepts, for the first time, that someone other than himself will formally comment on his decisions. The comments are not binding. They are public. For a man of Doom's pride, this is a meaningful sacrifice.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Absolute Monarchic Control classification partially mitigated. Latverian governance gains its first formal external accountability mechanism, however limited.

---

### **2. Doom-Bot Military Production — Registered International Disclosure**

KaibaCorp's autonomous systems were flagged as a Category-A Proliferation Risk. Doom's Doom-bot production exceeds KaibaCorp's autonomous weapons output in both scale and capability. The Bureau requires disclosure of the Doom-bot program's production capacity, deployment locations, and operational parameters to the UN's Autonomous Systems Registry — not for regulation of Latverian domestic security, which is sovereign, but for transparency regarding Doom-bot deployments outside Latverian territory.

- **The Action:** Subject must register all Doom-bot deployments outside Latverian territorial borders with the UN Autonomous Systems Registry within 30 days of each deployment. Domestic deployments are exempt. Extra-national deployments are not.
- **The Sacrifice:** Doom's most operationally useful autonomous capability — the ability to project force globally through Doom-bots without attribution — becomes, to a degree, attributable. The deployments continue. The secrecy does not.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Extra-national Doom-bot operations become governed under international disclosure framework. Embodied Capital classification partially mitigated.

---

### **3. Science-Magic Research — Open Publication of Non-Weapons Findings**

Doom's synthesis of advanced technology and mystical practice has produced significant scientific advances that are currently held as personal intellectual property, accessible only to those Doom chooses to share them with. The Bureau requires that non-weapons-application findings be published through standard scientific channels.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish a publication program — "Doom's Contributions to Human Knowledge," a title the Bureau notes Subject will likely find acceptable — through which all non-weapons scientific and mystical research findings are published annually in peer-reviewed journals. Weapons applications are exempted. Clean energy, medical, and fundamental science findings are not.
- **The Sacrifice:** Doom shares what he has learned, with people he has not chosen, who will receive credit alongside him in the citation record.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Science-Magic IP hoarding classification partially resolved. The world gains access to advances it did not have. Doom gains citations, which the Bureau suspects he will find more satisfying than he will admit.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Latverian citizens** — standard procedure, non-negotiable — and recite the following:

> *"Doom rules Latveria. Doom does not require instruction on what Doom owes his people. Doom's people are fed, protected, educated, and governed by someone who is better at governing than the alternatives the Bureau might prefer. Doom acknowledges this and does not expect gratitude. Doom also acknowledges — and this will not be repeated outside this room — that the Council was not Doom's idea, and that Doom has, on three occasions in the past decade, made decisions he subsequently reconsidered. The Council is for those occasions. Doom accepts the Council. The Bureau will not make this a larger moment than it is. Doom has spoken."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If Subject does not comply within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol — Sovereign Tier**, coordinating with the UN Security Council, the Avengers Council, and — as a last resort — Reed Richards, whose willingness to address Doom-related non-compliance the Bureau considers the most credible enforcement option available to it. The Bureau notes that this last option is deeply unsatisfying to everyone involved, including the Bureau. The Bureau is prepared to deploy it regardless. Doom presumably already knows this. The Bureau has nothing to add.
