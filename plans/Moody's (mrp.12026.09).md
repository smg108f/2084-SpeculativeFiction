### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [MOODY'S CORPORATION]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09MCO
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Moody's Critical classification stems from its structural conflict of interest, rating the creditworthiness of the entities that pay its fees, with direct influence over sovereign fiscal capacity worldwide. Recertification targets this conflict at its root.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Issuer-Pays Decoupling (The Largest Burn)**

- **The Action:** Transition sovereign and major public-entity credit ratings to a subscriber-pays or independently-funded model, removing direct fee payment from the governments and institutions being rated.
- **The Sacrifice:** Significant revenue restructuring; estimated loss of a substantial share of sovereign-rating fee income.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Directly resolves the Critical Political Agency flag by eliminating the structural conflict of interest.

### **2. The Sovereign Rating Transparency Mandate**

- **The Action:** Publish full, plain-language methodology and all material inputs behind every sovereign credit rating decision, reviewable by an independent public oversight panel.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of proprietary methodology opacity currently protecting the rating process from external challenge.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Makes visible the basis for decisions that materially affect national borrowing costs.

### **3. The Revolving Door Cooling-Off Period**

- **The Action:** Implement a binding multi-year cooling-off period preventing former Moody's sovereign-ratings staff from immediately joining the finance ministries or treasury departments of nations they rated, and vice versa.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced talent-pipeline flexibility between public finance roles and ratings work.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Reduces the risk of undue influence flowing through personnel movement rather than published methodology alone.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Moody's board must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Public Sector Workers from Nations It Has Rated** and recite the following:

> _"We were paid by the very governments whose credit we judged, and we called this independence. We will now be paid by no one with a stake in the grade, so the grade can finally mean what it claims to mean."_
