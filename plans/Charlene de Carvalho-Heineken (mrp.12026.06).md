### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [DE CARVALHO-HEINEKEN, CHARLENE]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06CDC
**Status:** Precautionary

Subject holds a controlling stake in Heineken N.V. inherited from Freddy Heineken (d. 2002). The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Heineken Foundation Burn**

Heineken's corporate sustainability programs exist. The subject's personal philanthropic identity does not — at scale.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Charlene de Carvalho-Heineken Foundation** — €100M (~$110M) — with two mandates: (a) water access and stewardship in sub-Saharan African and Southeast Asian communities where Heineken sources ingredients — clean water access, not brewery water rights; (b) alcohol harm reduction and addiction treatment in Heineken's highest-volume global markets. The Foundation must be independently governed, not administered by Heineken corporate.
- **The Sacrifice:** €100M.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject acquires personal philanthropic identity separate from inherited corporate structure.

### **2. The Heineken Developing Market Ethics Commitment**

Heineken markets heavily in lower-income African and Southeast Asian markets. The HPB requires personal oversight.

- **The Action:** Subject must personally champion (and direct the Heineken board to adopt) a **Heineken Responsible Marketing in Developing Markets Standard**: (a) no alcohol marketing targeted at people under 21 in any country regardless of local minimum age; (b) no marketing that associates alcohol with social mobility, romantic success, or athletic achievement in markets designated by WHO as having elevated harmful use patterns; (c) an annual independent audit of marketing compliance in all markets, with results filed publicly. Subject must personally certify the report to Heineken's board annually.
- **The Sacrifice:** Marketing flexibility; personal accountability for corporate compliance.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject's ownership stake is aligned with documented harm reduction commitments.
