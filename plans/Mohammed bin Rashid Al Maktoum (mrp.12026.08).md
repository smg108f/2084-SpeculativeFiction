### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[AL MAKTOUM, MOHAMMED BIN RASHID]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-08MBRAM
**Status:** Precautionary / Filed In Perpetuity *(Structurally Non-Actionable — see Apotheosis Audit REF. 12026.08)*

Subject's fortune clears the Demi-God threshold by a factor of at least fourteen, compounded by a **Hereditary Sovereignty Paradox** more acute than any prior case: Subject's authority as Ruler of Dubai is not a term of office but a birthright, with no election to lose, no legislature to override him, and no domestic court positioned above his own. This plan is filed as a binding record on the theoretical understanding that recertification requires a body capable of enforcing it — a body that, at present, does not exist within Subject's jurisdiction and has only ever appeared, briefly and from abroad, in the London Family Division.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** is entered into the permanent file, to be executed within 30 days of any future circumstance in which enforcement becomes possible.

---

### **1. The "Dubai Holding" Sovereign Severance (The Largest Burn)**

Subject's majority stake in Dubai Holding — hotels, real estate, telecom free zones, spanning 13 countries — is not a passive investment portfolio. It is the balance sheet of a city-state that Subject also happens to govern, meaning every planning decision, every free-zone license, and every tourism initiative Subject signs as Ruler directly inflates an asset Subject also personally owns. The HPB classifies this as a **"Self-Regulating Fortune."**

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer his majority stake in Dubai Holding to a blind, non-family-administered "Emirate Heritage Trust," with an enforceable firewall between the Ruler's Office and the Trust's commercial decisions.
- **The Sacrifice:** Operational control of Jumeirah, Dubai Properties, Meraas, and TECOM — the visible skyline of the city Subject built in his own image.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject becomes a Ruler with a government, not a Ruler with a government *and* a controlling shareholder's stake in everything the government approves.

### **2. The Godolphin & Darley Bloodstock Decoupling**

Subject's global thoroughbred racing and breeding empire is among the largest in the world and functions as a soft-power prestige asset as much as a business — a personal passion the HPB classifies as **"Luxury Concentration with Diplomatic Spillover,"** since Godolphin's presence at every major international racecourse doubles as ambient statecraft.

- **The Action:** Godolphin and Darley's bloodstock, training facilities, and racing operations are transferred to an independent international equine trust, operated without Subject's naming rights or involvement.
- **The Sacrifice:** Personal ownership of one of the world's great racing dynasties, built over four decades.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes the single largest "pure luxury" line item from Subject's asset sheet and severs a quiet but real channel of personal soft power.

### **3. The "My Vision" Mythology Purge (Reputational Burn)**

Subject is a published poet and the author of *My Vision: Challenges in the Race for Excellence*, a leadership memoir that frames Dubai's transformation as the product of his singular foresight. His name is affixed, in his own lifetime, to Mohammed Bin Rashid City, Mohammed Bin Rashid Library, Mohammed Bin Rashid University, the Mohammed Bin Rashid Space Centre, and the Global Initiatives foundation itself. The HPB classifies this as **"Self-Mythologized Infrastructure"** — a Demi-God pattern in which the ruler's personal brand becomes indistinguishable from the public works themselves.

- **The Action:** Subject must open-source *My Vision* and his published poetry into the public domain, and every publicly funded institution bearing his given name must be renamed for a place, not a person, within 24 months.
- **The Sacrifice:** The "Visionary Ruler" persona — the intangible asset that makes the other two burns politically survivable.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Dubai's institutions become the city's, not the dynasty's, on paper if nowhere else.

### **4. The Sovereign Accountability Reckoning (Non-Financial Burn)**

No divestment in this plan addresses the Bureau's most serious finding: the 2019–2021 rulings of the High Court of England and Wales (Sir Andrew McFarlane, President of the Family Division), which found on the balance of probabilities that Subject orchestrated the abductions of Sheikha Shamsa (2000) and Sheikha Latifa (2002, 2018), and conducted a campaign of intimidation against Princess Haya bint Hussein. This burn cannot be paid in equity, and the HPB does not pretend otherwise.

- **The Action:** Full, verified, ongoing compliance with the December 2021 £554 million High Court financial order, plus an independent, UN-facilitated welfare check confirming Sheikha Latifa's unrestricted freedom of movement, renewed annually for as long as concerns persist.
- **The Sacrifice:** None that a balance sheet can measure. The Bureau notes this explicitly rather than pretending a burn schedule can convert judicial findings into "Mortal Status."
- **Mortal Status Gain:** None automatically granted. This item exists in the file as a standing condition, not a completable transaction — the Bureau will not certify Subject's "Saintly Pivot" as sincere while it remains open.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Migrant Construction Workers who built the Burj Khalifa and Palm Jumeirah** and recite the following:

> _"I did not build this city. Sand does not remember whose name is stamped on the tower, and a Vision is not a Vision until it belongs to the nine million who live inside it and not to the one who narrated it. My name comes off the library, the university, and the skyline. What is left standing was never mine to sign."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** The HPB acknowledges, in the record, that it has no mechanism to compel compliance. Subject's Apotheosis Protocol cannot proceed by the standard route — there is no Sanctuary Zone or Flotilla to deport Subject to, because Subject already rules the closest real-world approximation of one. In lieu of enforcement, the Bureau enters this file as a permanent, unexecuted judgment, to be revisited only in the event Subject's sovereign immunity ever lapses.
