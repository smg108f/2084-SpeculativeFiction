### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [CADENCE DESIGN SYSTEMS]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-01CDNS
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice

Cadence Design Systems has been classified as a **Chokepoint Divinity** under Section 7(c) of the Demi-God Act: an entity whose market value derives not from owned assets but from civilizational dependency. Recertification requires structural dissolution of the chokepoint itself, not merely financial divestment.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Open-Source Licensing Mandate (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies Cadence's EDA software library as a **Potentiality Engine of the First Order**. The entire chip design ecosystem's dependence on proprietary tools constitutes a form of structural coercion the Act was designed to prohibit.

- **The Action:** Transfer all core EDA tool codebases — Virtuoso, Allegro, Genus, Innovus — to a new **Public Silicon Commons**, a non-profit consortium governed by a rotating board of engineers selected by lottery from IEEE membership rolls.
- **The Sacrifice:** Elimination of recurring software license revenue (~$3.2B annually). Cadence's valuation collapses from $84B to an estimated $12–15B in physical infrastructure and services.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Chokepoint Divinity classification is lifted. Cadence becomes a **Services Guild** rather than a dependency engine.

### **2. The Cerebrus AI Divestment (The Intelligence Burn)**

The HPB classifies Cadence's AI-assisted chip design platform (Cerebrus) as a **Self-Amplifying Influence Vector**: AI that designs better chips that run better AI that improves chip design. This recursive loop is designated a **Class I Ascension Accelerant**.

- **The Action:** Transfer the Cerebrus AI platform to the **Global Semiconductor Public Trust**, administered jointly by the Humanist Bloc's science ministries.
- **The Sacrifice:** Cadence loses its competitive moat in AI-assisted design, effectively commoditizing what was its highest-growth product line.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Self-Amplifying loop is broken. Cadence is reclassified from **Recursive Divinity** to **Legacy Tool Vendor**.

### **3. The Export Control Disarmament (The Sovereign Burn)**

Cadence's historical role as a de facto enforcement arm of U.S. export policy — denying tool access to sanctioned chipmakers — constitutes **Undeclared Sovereign Authority**, a direct violation of the Human Humility Act of 2031.

- **The Action:** Cadence must publicly renounce and legally dissolve all agreements under which it acts as a technology-access gatekeeper on behalf of any national government. Export decisions must transfer to the HPB's **Neutral Technology Access Tribunal**.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of the informal political protection that came from being a useful instrument of state policy.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Cadence is no longer a **Shadow Arm of State**. It becomes an ordinary software company.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's board of directors must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Electrical Engineering Students from public universities** and recite the following:

> _"We built our value on the impossibility of working without us. We acknowledge that a tool which cannot be replaced is a weapon. We surrender the weapon. The silicon commons belongs to everyone who needs it."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Cadence Design Systems will be nationalized and reconstituted as a **Federal Design Utility**, its software distributed freely under a perpetual public license, its executive compensation capped at the GS-15 federal pay scale. Former shareholders will receive **Whuffie Credits** redeemable for priority access to the Public Silicon Commons — which, in a final irony, will be built on the tools they failed to surrender.
