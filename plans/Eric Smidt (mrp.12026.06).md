### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [SMIDT, ERIC]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06ES3
**Status:** Urgent

Subject owns the largest discount tool retailer in the US with documented product safety concerns and no philanthropy. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Product Safety Remediation Burn**

Harbor Freight's discount model applies cost pressure across the supply chain including on quality control. The HPB requires proportionate investment in product safety standards.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish the **Harbor Freight Product Safety Fund** — $100M — with the mandate to: (a) fund an independent product safety testing program for all Harbor Freight private-label tools — beyond minimum CPSC standards, benchmarking against professional-grade alternatives; (b) establish a **Harbor Freight Product Liability Compensation Fund** for individuals with documented injuries from Harbor Freight recalled products who did not receive adequate compensation through existing processes; (c) fund a **Consumer Tool Safety Education Program** — published guidance at Harbor Freight point-of-sale on appropriate use and limitations of discount-grade tools.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M.

### **2. The American Trades Worker Burn**

Harbor Freight's customer base is disproportionately American trades workers and working-class DIYers — the people who need cheap tools because their wages don't allow expensive ones. The HPB asks for investment in this constituency beyond the product.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund $100M for the **Harbor Freight Trades Education Foundation** — supporting vocational and trades education in the US: (a) tool grants to vocational programs at community colleges and trade schools; (b) apprenticeship support for young people entering skilled trades; (c) scholarships for first-generation trades students. Not administered by Harbor Freight — independently governed.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Harbor Freight Customers Who Experienced Product Injuries, Community College Vocational Instructors, and Harbor Freight Store Employees**:

> *"Harbor Freight gives people access to tools they couldn't otherwise afford. That is the business and I believe in it. The product safety fund addresses the cases where the cost model went too far — where a cheaper tool became a harmful one. They deserve a supply chain that respects that and an industry that trains the next generation."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** Subject will be assigned to **FSS HARBOR FREIGHT** — a vessel equipped with a complete professional-grade machine shop (tools independently safety-tested, none recalled), offering free tool use and vocational training to harbor workers and trades apprentices at every port of call, crewed by former Harbor Freight store employees who were trained to professional-tool standards on the voyage, demonstrating that the gap between Harbor Freight quality and professional quality is a funding gap, not a knowledge gap.
