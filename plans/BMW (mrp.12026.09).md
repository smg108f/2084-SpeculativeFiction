### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [BAYERISCHE MOTOREN WERKE AG]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09BMW
**Status:** Precautionary

BMW's Ascension classification is driven by legacy valuation rather than active accumulation behavior, and the company's own margin collapse is doing much of the Bureau's work already. Recertification here is precautionary — designed to lock in the current trajectory toward Mortal status rather than let a market rebound re-inflate the threat.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Combustion Sunset Commitment**

The Bureau classifies BMW's continued combustion-engine production as a **Deferred Externality** — profitable today, costly to the climate ledger tomorrow.

- **The Action:** Publish a binding, dated combustion-production sunset schedule tied to regional infrastructure readiness, rather than aspirational targets.
- **The Sacrifice:** Near-term revenue certainty in the profitable ICE/hybrid segment.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the "Strategic Straddle" ambiguity the Bureau currently rates as unresolved risk.

### **2. The Net Cash Redistribution**

With net cash reportedly rivaling the company's own market capitalization, the Bureau classifies BMW as **Capital-Hoarding Under Distress** — sitting on liquidity while margins erode and workers absorb restructuring risk.

- **The Action:** Direct a portion of the cash reserve into a binding no-layoff guarantee for German manufacturing employees through the current downturn, rather than shareholder buybacks.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced buyback support for the depressed share price.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts "hoarded" capital into a Saintly Pivot the Bureau can actually credit.

### **3. The Leadership Transparency Burn**

- **The Action:** The incoming CEO must publish a public, numbers-based account of the margin collapse — not investor-relations language — including which strategic bets (EV timing, combustion retention) are being revised and why.
- **The Sacrifice:** Short-term reputational discomfort; loss of ambiguity as a hedge.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Restores Bureau confidence that the company's decline is honest strategy correction, not concealment.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, BMW's board must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Bavarian Line Workers** and recite the following:

> _"We built engines for a world that is ending and hedged our bets instead of choosing. We will stop hedging. We will tell you, plainly, what comes next for your jobs before it happens, not after."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will place BMW under **Enhanced Monitoring** rather than the full Apotheosis Protocol, given its already-declining threat profile — but any reversal toward EV-timeline concealment will trigger immediate escalation.
