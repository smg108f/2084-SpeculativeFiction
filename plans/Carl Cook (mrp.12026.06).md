### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [COOK, CARL]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06CC2
**Status:** Urgent

Subject controls Cook Medical, whose IVC filters injured thousands of patients. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The IVC Filter Patient Remediation Burn**

Cook Medical's litigation settlements resolved the immediate legal liability. The HPB is not bound by litigation settlements in its assessment of ongoing moral accountability.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish an **IVC Filter Patient Remediation Trust** — $250M — independent of Cook Medical, administered by a board including patient advocates, bioethicists, and physicians with no Cook Group ties. The Trust's mandate: (a) additional compensation for any IVC filter patients whose injuries were not fully addressed by the litigation settlement — particularly those who experienced ongoing medical complications post-settlement; (b) medical monitoring and follow-up care for living IVC filter patients who received Cook devices and remain at risk of delayed fracture events; (c) a **Medical Device Failure Public Registry** — a permanent, independently maintained registry of IVC filter failure cases (Cook and others), publicly accessible, designed to ensure that the knowledge of device failure risk is never again withheld from physicians and patients.
- **The Sacrifice:** $250M.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The legal settlement's scope is expanded; the public registry addresses the information failure that made the harm worse.

### **2. The Cook Medical Patient Safety Commitment Burn**

The HPB requires that Cook Medical be restructured to prevent future instances of known-risk suppression.

- **The Action:** Subject must implement — as controlling shareholder — a mandatory **Cook Medical Patient Safety Transparency Protocol**: (a) any Cook Medical internal analysis identifying a patient safety risk above a defined threshold must be disclosed to the FDA within 30 days; (b) Cook Medical must establish a permanent **Independent Patient Safety Board** with authority to compel disclosure; (c) subject must personally commit $50M to fund the Board and its external advisory network for the first decade. The Board reports publicly, annually.
- **The Sacrifice:** $50M + structural governance change.

### **3. The Indiana Community Health Burn**

The Cook family's Indiana philanthropy is genuine. The HPB asks for a health-specific dimension.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund $75M for the **Cook Indiana Rural Health Initiative** — expanding primary care access in rural Indiana counties where Cook Medical employees and their families live. Indiana ranks poorly on several rural health access metrics; a company of Cook's regional presence has disproportionate capacity to address this.
- **The Sacrifice:** $75M.
