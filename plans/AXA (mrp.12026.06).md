### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [AXA]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-02AXA
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

AXA has been designated a **Risk Oracle Entity** under the Demi-God Act's Section 9(b) provisions governing entities whose private actuarial decisions carry the effective force of public policy. Recertification requires the surrender of catastrophe modeling infrastructure and the dissolution of sovereign advisory relationships.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Actuarial Commons Transfer (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies AXA's proprietary catastrophe databases — climate risk models, pandemic actuarial tables, infrastructure failure probability matrices — as **Public Necessity Assets** that have been improperly held in private hands.

- **The Action:** Transfer all proprietary risk modeling infrastructure to the **Humanist Bloc Climate and Risk Commons**, a publicly administered repository accessible to all member-state governments and academic institutions.
- **The Sacrifice:** AXA loses its informational edge — the primary source of its underwriting advantage over state and mutual insurers.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** AXA is reclassified from **Risk Oracle** to **Risk Processor** — a company that applies models it no longer owns.

### **2. The Sovereign Advisory Withdrawal (The Political Burn)**

AXA's simultaneous role as private insurer and government climate finance advisor constitutes **Dual Sovereignty**, which the Act forbids.

- **The Action:** Immediate resignation from all formal advisory positions with G7, EU, and ASEAN governmental bodies. All former advisory staff must be placed on a 5-year cooling-off period from AXA employment.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of regulatory access and the informal influence that comes from being invited into policy rooms.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** AXA is no longer a **Shadow Policy Setter**. It is a regulated insurer, subject to — rather than participant in — the rules it once helped write.

### **3. The Uninsurable Regions Remedy (The Geographic Burn)**

AXA's practice of designating regions "uninsurable" has accelerated population displacement in ways that advantage AXA's remaining portfolio. The HPB classifies this as **Actuarial Terraforming**.

- **The Action:** AXA must fund a €50B **Universal Baseline Coverage Pool**, providing minimum-level insurance coverage to all communities currently classified as uninsurable due to climate risk, at no premium to residents.
- **The Sacrifice:** Guaranteed underwriting losses, funded from AXA's investment portfolio liquidation.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Actuarial Terraforming classification is lifted. AXA demonstrates that it can absorb risk rather than merely redistribute it to those least able to bear it.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, AXA's Group Executive Committee must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Flood-Displaced Homeowners from currently-uninsurable coastal zones** and recite the following:

> _"We priced risk without bearing it. We modeled catastrophe without living in it. We advised governments that then regulated our competitors. We surrender the models. We take the losses. We are an insurer, not an oracle, and we will be judged by what we cover, not by what we exclude."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. AXA will be reconstituted as a **Federal Mutual Insurance Authority** under direct democratic governance, its actuarial databases published in full, and its investment arm dissolved. Former executives will be assigned as claims adjusters in the coastal displacement zones their models helped create — positions from which they will gain, at minimum, perspective.
