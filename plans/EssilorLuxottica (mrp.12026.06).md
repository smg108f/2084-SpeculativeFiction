### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[ESSILORLUXOTTICA, CORPORATE ENTITY]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06EL
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject has achieved what the HPB terms "Total Ocular Capture" — vertical control of frames, lenses, retail, prescriptions, and now the augmented-reality layer that will mediate human sight itself. A corporation that controls how billions of people *see* cannot also be permitted to own the means of seeing. Recertification is not optional; it is corrective surgery.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Vertical Chain Severance (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies subject's end-to-end control — factory, frame, lens, optometrist, retail counter, and now AR display — as a **"Total Capture Engine."** No single entity may simultaneously manufacture the correction, diagnose the need for it, and sell the cure.

- **The Action:** Subject must spin off manufacturing (Essilor lens plants, the 600+ factories), retail (LensCrafters, Sunglass Hut, GrandVision, Ray-Ban stores), and prescribing services (in-store optometry) into three independently governed, separately regulated entities with no shared ownership above 5%.
- **The Sacrifice:** The "vertical integration premium" — the very thing that let a $4 frame retail for $230 — evaporates. Estimated valuation loss: **$38B**.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject is reclassified from "Monopoly Organism" to "Component Supplier," shedding the bulk of its Concentration Hazard rating.

### **2. The Meta Optic Recall (Ideological Burn)**

Subject's smart-glasses partnership represents the HPB's gravest concern: a corporation that already controls *what people see* now positioning itself to control *how they interpret what they see*, frame by AI-annotated frame.

- **The Action:** Immediate suspension of the Ray-Ban × Meta AI/Display glasses program. All existing display-layer patents and neural-interface research must be open-sourced into the **Public Vision Commons**, available royalty-free to any manufacturer.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject forfeits its projected dominance of the augmented-reality eyewear category — by HPB estimate, over **$20B** in future "Perceptual Real Estate" valuation — and its status as Meta's exclusive optical hardware partner.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the "Substitution Risk" finding; subject is no longer positioned to become humanity's default reality-filter.

### **3. The Cost-of-Sight Confession (Reputational/Memetic Burn)**

For decades subject benefited from consumers never learning that a $4 frame and a $1.25 lens were sold to them, bundled, for up to $800.

- **The Action:** Subject must publish, under its own letterhead and at its own expense in every market it serves, a full itemized **"True Cost of Sight" disclosure** — manufacturing cost, markup, and margin — for every SKU it has ever sold, alongside a public registry of all "restrictive agreements" used to suppress competing opticians.
- **The Requirement:** Subject's executive leadership must personally staff a "Vision Equity Hotline" for 90 days, fielding complaints from citizens who paid the markup.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the "Cosmetic Saintliness" finding into a verified Transparency Pivot — the first step toward genuine Whuffie accumulation.

### **4. The Optometry Commons Endowment**

Subject's charitable "OneSight" initiative is, per the audit, a loyalty program dressed as philanthropy.

- **The Action:** Convert OneSight into an independently governed **Global Vision Commons Trust**, endowed with **$15B** in subject's reserves, mandated to provide free corrective lenses and exams in regions where subject's own pricing structure has historically priced out access.
- **The Sacrifice:** Permanent, irrevocable transfer of governance — subject retains no seat, no veto, no naming rights.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Replaces "Cosmetic Saintliness" with a structurally enforced public benefit obligation — the only kind the HPB recognizes as sincere.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's board and controlling shareholders (the Del Vecchio family trusts, collectively) must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Opticians, Frame-Factory Line Workers, and Public-School Vision-Screening Nurses** and recite the following:

> _"We mistook the eye for a market and the customer for a captive. We will no longer stand between any person and the world they are owed the right to see clearly — nor charge them admission to their own eyesight. The lens is not ours. It never was."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the "Burn" is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All retail storefronts will be seized and converted into free public Vision Clinics overnight, subject's executive leadership will be fitted — permanently, and without an off switch — with first-generation prototype smart-glasses running on a public, ad-supported operating system, and relocated to the **Greenland Ocular Compliance Colony**, where they will spend their remaining years seeing the world exactly as they once sold it back to everyone else.
