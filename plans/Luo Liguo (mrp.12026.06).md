### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [LUO, LIGUO]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06LL2
**Status:** Urgent

Subject controls 30% of global MDI capacity — the world's dominant isocyanate chemistry operation. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Industrial Hazard Safety Burn**

The isocyanate class of chemicals carries documented industrial catastrophe risk. The HPB requires that Wanhua's dominance in this class come with proportionate safety investment that goes beyond regulatory compliance.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish the **Wanhua Industrial Safety Foundation** — $150M — with the following mandate: (a) independent safety audit of Wanhua's Yantai and BorsodChem facilities, conducted by third-party industrial safety experts with Bhopal-precedent awareness, results published publicly; (b) funding for a **Global Isocyanate Industry Safety Institute** — sharing Wanhua's safety protocols openly with all isocyanate manufacturers globally, as a public good — the HPB notes that Bhopal occurred at a facility with inadequate safety protocols; if Wanhua holds 30% of global MDI capacity, its safety standards becoming the global standard is a public good; (c) community emergency response fund for communities adjacent to Wanhua's major manufacturing sites — Yantai (China) and Kazincbarcika (Hungary).
- **The Sacrifice:** $150M.

### **2. The Wanhua Community Benefit Burn**

Yantai and the BorsodChem community in Hungary host major isocyanate manufacturing complexes with associated environmental and risk burdens.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund $100M in community benefit programs: (a) $60M for Yantai environmental monitoring, air and water quality improvement, and healthcare access for Wanhua workers and adjacent communities; (b) $40M for the BorsodChem/Kazincbarcika community in Hungary — historically a post-industrial town with significant economic dependence on the chemical complex, including worker welfare, local healthcare, and economic diversification.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Wanhua Chemical Workers in Yantai, Kazincbarcika BorsodChem Community Members, and Industrial Safety Regulators**:

> *"MDI is in the foam in the seat you are sitting on. Wanhua makes 30% of the world's supply. That is a significant responsibility. The isocyanate chemistry is hazardous; the safety protocols must be proportionate to that hazard. The Safety Foundation makes our protocols public — what we know about safe MDI production, every other producer should also know, because a disaster at any isocyanate facility affects the reputation and regulation of all of them. The community fund addresses Yantai and Kazincbarcika — the places that host the facilities and bear the proximity risk. I have not given much to charity. This is where I start."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** Subject will be assigned to **FSS WANHUA** — a vessel whose insulation panels are made entirely from Wanhua polyurethane foam (certified non-hazardous, fully processed end-product), serving as a floating industrial safety training center for chemical plant workers from Yantai, Hungary, and globally, where the entire curriculum is about the isocyanate class of chemicals — what they do, what Bhopal demonstrated, and what Wanhua's 30% global market position means for the standard of care that the entire industry owes to the workers and communities that live alongside it.
