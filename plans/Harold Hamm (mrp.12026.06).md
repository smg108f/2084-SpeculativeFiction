### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [HAMM, HAROLD]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06HH
**Status:** Urgent — Fossil Fuel Entrenchment / Active Climate Obstruction

Subject is the founder of Continental Resources, architect of the US shale revolution, and active opponent of environmental regulation. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 60 days.

---

### **1. The Climate Debt Fund Burn**

Harold Hamm has personally advocated against climate regulation and donated to politicians who blocked climate policy throughout the critical 2010s-2020s period when the window for low-cost transition was open. The HPB calculates a climate advocacy debt.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Continental Climate Reckoning Fund** — a $1B personal contribution — administered by an independent board with no Continental Resources or Hamm family representation, dedicated exclusively to: (a) clean energy access for rural Oklahoma and North Dakota communities that have been most dependent on oil sector employment; (b) worker retraining for Continental employees in the transition from fossil fuels; (c) environmental remediation of fracking-adjacent groundwater and soil in the Bakken region.
- **The Sacrifice:** $1B; acknowledgment of the climate advocacy history.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the most consequential private actor in the US shale revolution into a documented funder of the transition away from what he built.

### **2. The Continental Transparency Burn**

The 2022 take-private removed Continental Resources from public shareholder accountability at the moment of maximum fossil fuel accountability scrutiny. The HPB reverses this.

- **The Action:** File with the HPB a **Continental Resources Annual Public Report** — voluntarily, covering: production volumes, methane emissions, flaring rates, groundwater incident reports, and Bakken community impact assessments — publicly, at the same level of detail required of publicly listed oil companies. This is not a financial filing; it is an environmental accountability filing.
- **The Sacrifice:** The primary benefit of the take-private — reduced public scrutiny.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a deliberate accountability reduction into a voluntary accountability commitment.

### **3. The Political Spending Accountability Burn**

Hamm's political donations have specifically targeted anti-environmental-regulation candidates. The HPB asks for a counterweight.

- **The Action:** Match, dollar for dollar, any future political donation to any candidate who has publicly opposed climate legislation, with an equal donation to the **HPB Climate Transition Fund** — a neutral fund supporting climate science communication and policy research. Cap on future anti-climate-policy donations: $5M/year. Any dollar above that cap must be matched 3:1 into the Climate Transition Fund.
- **The Sacrifice:** Political spending effectiveness; the unrestricted right to fund climate obstruction at scale.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the most consequential individual donor in the anti-climate-regulation space into a net contributor to climate policy research.
