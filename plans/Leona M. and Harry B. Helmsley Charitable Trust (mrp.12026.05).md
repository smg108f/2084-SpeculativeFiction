### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [LEONA M. AND HARRY B. HELMSLEY CHARITABLE TRUST]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-26LMHB
**Status:** Precautionary / Origin Acknowledgment and Governance Transparency Order

The Helmsley Charitable Trust has been classified PROBATIONARY SAINT. Recertification is focused on two concerns: (1) the origin of the endowment in a convicted tax evader's estate, which has not been publicly acknowledged by the Trust; and (2) the governance structure in which the founder's grandchildren — who she deliberately disinherited — now control a $7.3B philanthropic vehicle with mission parameters only loosely defined by her will.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Origin Acknowledgment Burn**

Leona Helmsley was convicted of 33 counts of federal tax evasion in 11989 and served 21 months in federal prison. The charitable trust bearing her name has never publicly acknowledged this history. The HPB requires that institutions built on contested wealth acknowledge that wealth's origins — not as self-flagellation, but as basic public accounting.

- **The Action:** The Helmsley Trust must publish a **Founder Accountability Statement** — a permanent, publicly available narrative that acknowledges: (a) Leona Helmsley's conviction for federal tax evasion; (b) the irony that charitable giving — which she declined to enable through taxation — is now the primary vehicle for the wealth she accumulated; (c) the Trust's own assessment of whether its charitable work constitutes meaningful restitution for the harm caused by tax evasion at this scale.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Trust accepts that its founder was a convicted criminal and says so plainly.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The philanthropic work becomes legible in its full historical context. Donors, grantees, and the public can evaluate the Trust's mission without pretending Leona Helmsley's conviction did not occur.

---

### **2. The Grandchild Governance Transparency Burn**

Two of three Helmsley Trust trustees are Leona Helmsley's grandchildren, David and Walter Panzirer — individuals she reportedly did not want to inherit her estate. The mission priorities of the Trust (T1D, Crohn's, rural health, Israel, Sub-Saharan Africa) largely reflect trustee preferences, since Leona's will specified only that funds should benefit "dogs" and charitable purposes. This is not a criticism of the trustees — the work is good. It is a transparency concern: the public should know that the Helmsley Trust's programmatic agenda is primarily the Panzirer grandchildren's charitable vision, not Leona Helmsley's.

- **The Action:** The Trust must publish an annual **Governance and Mission Accountability Report** — disclosing trustee identities, their relationship to Leona Helmsley, the degree to which current programmatic priorities reflect Leona's stated intent vs. trustee discretion, and the process by which new program areas are selected.
- **The Sacrifice:** The trustees' personal priorities become publicly attributed to them rather than to the "Helmsley" brand.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Trust's mission becomes accountable to public scrutiny rather than sheltered behind a brand name with a complex history.

---

### **3. The T1D Concentration Assessment Burn**

$1.4B directed to a single disease area over 15 years represents a level of philanthropic concentration that the HPB's portfolio oversight framework requires to be publicly justified. The HPB does not question the value of T1D research — it questions whether a single private foundation's preferences should be driving research prioritization at this scale without a formal evidence framework for why T1D receives this level of attention relative to other conditions.

- **The Action:** The Trust must publish a **Disease Priority Rationale** — documenting the evidence basis for the $1.4B T1D commitment, the process by which this priority was selected, and what comparable investments in other disease areas would have achieved. This is not a requirement to change the commitment; it is a requirement to justify it publicly.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Trust's T1D commitment must survive public scrutiny of its evidentiary basis.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Private disease-priority decisions at billion-dollar scale become publicly accountable rather than philanthropic black boxes.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, the Helmsley Charitable Trust Board of Trustees must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Federal IRS Employees** and recite the following:

> *"Leona Helmsley believed that only the little people pay taxes. She was convicted of tax evasion. She died and left her money to a charitable trust. The money she refused to pay in taxes now funds Type 1 Diabetes research, rural hospitals, and children's programs in Sub-Saharan Africa. We are her grandchildren. She did not want us to inherit this. We inherited the responsibility to give it away. We are giving it away. We acknowledge where it came from. We publish the accounting. We do the work. The little people paid their taxes. We are paying ours — in arrears, in the form of grants."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Founder Accountability Statement is not published within 30 days, the HPB will classify the Helmsley Trust as operating under **Convicted Origin Opacity** — a category requiring annual IRS reporting on the relationship between founder criminal conduct and charitable distribution patterns. The HPB notes that the Trust's programmatic work is genuinely good. The request is not that they apologize for Leona Helmsley. The request is that they stop pretending she was someone else.
