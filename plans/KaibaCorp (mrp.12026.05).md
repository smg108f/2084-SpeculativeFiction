### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [KAIBACORP / KAIBA, SETO]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-24-KC
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice / Founder-Governance Concentration

KaibaCorp has been classified MARGINAL ASCENSION RISK based on a $108 billion valuation, a holographic technology standards monopoly, and a governance structure the HPB's Corporate Accountability Division describes as "indistinguishable from one person's executive preferences rendered in corporate form." The Bureau anticipates that Seto Kaiba will view this plan as a challenge. The Bureau is comfortable with that framing. The Bureau has its own competitive drive.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. Holographic Platform — Open Standards Protocol**

KaibaCorp's holographic platform's market dominance has been maintained, in part, through the company's control of proprietary protocols that create switching costs for users who have adopted the standard. The Bureau's Technology Assessment Division requires that the core platform protocols be opened — not the company's proprietary implementations, which may remain competitive advantages, but the underlying standards that allow competing products to interoperate.

- **The Action:** KaibaCorp must publish open specifications for the holographic interface protocols used in all sectors where its market share exceeds 40%, enabling third-party products and services to achieve platform interoperability. The company retains all proprietary implementation advantages — its hardware, its content partnerships, its technical execution. It loses the ability to use protocol secrecy as a switching cost.
- **The Sacrifice:** KaibaCorp loses market position in sectors where its advantage derived from lock-in rather than quality. In sectors where its advantage is genuine, it retains it.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Network Effect Monopoly classification replaced by Open Standards Leader. RTWI impact estimated at $15 billion reduction in lock-in premium.

---

### **2. Corporate Governance — Independent Board with Real Authority**

KaibaCorp's board has, in practice, served as a formality. Seto Kaiba's decisions have not been materially constrained by board oversight at any point in the company's history. The Bureau requires that the board be reconstituted with genuine independence and genuine authority.

- **The Action:** KaibaCorp must reconstitute its board with a majority of independent directors selected through a process not controlled by Seto Kaiba, empowered to act on strategic decisions affecting the company's market position, technology licensing, and space program without requiring Kaiba's personal approval for decisions below $500 million. An HPB-appointed governance monitor attends board meetings for 24 months following reconstitution.
- **The Sacrifice:** Seto Kaiba operates a company with people who can, for the first time, say no to him in a legally binding capacity. The Bureau notes that Kaiba has faced stronger opponents than a board of directors and that the skill set required to accept legitimate challenge is not one he lacks — it is one he has chosen not to apply to governance.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Single-Individual Governance classification resolved. KaibaCorp becomes, for the first time, a company rather than a decision.

---

### **3. KaibaCorp Space Program — Public Interest Partnership**

Subject's space travel development represents a significant forward investment with both personal and commercial motivations. The Bureau does not require that Subject abandon the space program. It requires that a program of this scale and public implication be developed with public interest partnerships rather than as a purely private capability.

- **The Action:** KaibaCorp must enter into a Space Program Public Interest Agreement with the applicable international space authority, committing to: (1) shared technical findings from the space program under a research commons framework; (2) priority access to space travel capability for designated public interest missions (disaster response, scientific research, international communication infrastructure); and (3) annual disclosure of the program's development status and safety record to a public registry.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject's space program stops being exclusively his. The capability he is building becomes, in part, a public asset while remaining primarily a commercial enterprise.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Prospective Concentration Risk (space) reclassified to Governed Development. Kaiba retains first-mover commercial advantages. He accepts first-mover public obligations.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 KaibaCorp employees** — specifically drawn from the engineering teams that built the holographic platform and the space program — and recite the following:

> _"I built this company because I decided to, and I built it the way I wanted, and it worked. The Bureau is telling me that 'it worked' is not sufficient governance. I disagree with that assessment. I am complying with this plan anyway — not because the Bureau convinced me, but because the only opponents worth engaging are the ones who can actually make a move. The board will have authority. The protocols will be opened. The space program will have public partnerships. None of this will make KaibaCorp weaker. Anyone who thinks accountability and capability are in conflict has not thought carefully enough about either. I have. This is what thinking carefully looks like."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Asset Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate standard MARGINAL ASCENSION RISK enforcement procedures, including forced open-sourcing of the holographic platform protocols under mandatory licensing at regulated rates. The Bureau notes that Seto Kaiba has, in documented history, never lost a contest he took seriously. The Bureau is asking him to take this one seriously. The Bureau believes he is capable of winning it. The Bureau is counting on it.
