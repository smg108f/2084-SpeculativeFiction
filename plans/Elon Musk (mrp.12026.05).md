### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [MUSK, E.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-01EM
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject Musk's Real-Time Wealth Index has exceeded the $1B Demi-God Threshold by a factor of 790, representing the largest individual Ascension overage in HPB history; failure to comply will trigger the full Apotheosis Protocol, including permanent reclassification as a Non-Person Sovereign Entity and immediate forfeiture of voting rights, freedom of movement, and biological legal standing.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The "Ark-Mind" Dissolution (The Civilizational Infrastructure Burn)**

The HPB classifies the SpaceX-xAI merged entity as a **Class-I Sovereign Infrastructure Threat**: a single private structure that controls humanity's primary off-world escape vehicle and its most advanced private AI system. No citizen may hold a Flotilla and a mind-replacement tool in the same hand.

- **The Action:** Subject must structurally separate SpaceX and xAI back into independent entities and transfer majority operational control of SpaceX's Mars program to a **Public Interplanetary Authority (PIA)** — a jointly-governed multinational body. Subject may retain a non-voting, 5% "Founding Contributor" stake in each entity, capped at $1B combined value.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject relinquishes the unified "Ark-Mind" structure and his sole claim on the Mars corridor. The rockets still fly. They fly for everyone now.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The HPB reclassifies Subject from **Sovereign Infrastructure Holder** to **Public Contributor**. His Flotilla Risk score drops from CRITICAL to ELEVATED.

### **2. The DOGE Penance (The State Capture Reversal)**

Subject spent 12025 embedding allies inside the regulatory agencies legally responsible for overseeing his own companies, and dismantling consumer protection infrastructure that covered his own financial products. The HPB classifies this as **"Regulatory Cannibalism"** — the most severe form of Shadow Cabinet violation on record.

- **The Action:** Subject must publicly fund and endow the **Independent Regulatory Restoration Fund** at a minimum of $50B, disbursed to rebuild the CFPB, SEC enforcement division, and Federal Aviation Administration oversight capacity — specifically the units responsible for Tesla, X Money, and SpaceX launch approvals.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses the institutional "moat" built by DOGE. The agencies watching him will be better funded than they were before he arrived.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This resolves the **Shadow Cabinet Critical** flag. Subject is no longer classified as a State-Level Actor.

### **3. The "Last Man" Recantation (The Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric Burn)**

Subject's public theology — in which he is the singular agent preventing human extinction, and in which empathy itself is reframed as a civilizational pathogen — constitutes the most sustained Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric violation in the HPB's database. The **"civilizational suicidal empathy"** formulation alone carries a Class-V Memetic Hazard designation.

- **The Action:** Subject must deliver a public address — no shorter than 60 minutes, broadcast globally — titled **"I Am Not the Answer."** The address must specifically affirm that (1) no single person's survival infrastructure constitutes humanity's survival infrastructure, (2) the multi-planetary future belongs to the species, not its funders, and (3) his children hold no preferential claim on the off-world future.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject's entire public mythology is premised on the opposite of each of these points. This is not a press release. It will cost him the congregation.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric score cleared. Subject's memetic hazard classification drops from Class-V to Class-II (residual).

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Federal Workers, Automotive Assembly Line Workers, and Public School Teachers** and recite the following:

> *"I am not the last man. I am not the first. The stars do not belong to me because I built the ship. I will not save civilization — civilization will save itself, with or without my permission. My children are citizens, not heirs. My companies are tools, not temples. I am, at most, one vote."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All assets will be nationalized under the Demi-God Entity seizure provisions. Subject will be reclassified as a **Non-Person Sovereign Force**, stripped of U.S. citizenship, and escorted — via the very Starship fleet he built — to the **Mars Autonomous Zone**, where he will be the first permanent resident of the colony he designed but no longer owns. The HPB notes, without irony, that this is precisely what he said he wanted.
