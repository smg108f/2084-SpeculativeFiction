---

### **1. The AI Policy Conflict-of-Interest Register Burn**

Schmidt holds government advisory roles while having financial interests in defense technology companies. The HPB requires disclosure.

- **The Action:** File a public **Schmidt AI Policy Conflict Register**: a full disclosure of all personal financial interests (equity, advisory roles, board seats, consulting arrangements) in AI and defense technology companies, cross-referenced with any government advisory role Schmidt holds or has held since 2019. Updated quarterly. Must include any indirect interests through Schmidt Futures or related investment vehicles.
- **The Sacrifice:** Financial privacy about technology investment portfolio.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the most active private-citizen AI policy advocate into the most transparent one — allowing the public to assess his advocacy in light of his interests.

### **2. The AI Safety Investment Rebalancing Burn**

Schmidt's AI policy advocacy has been weighted toward military AI applications and US technological competition with China. The HPB asks for a rebalancing toward AI safety.

- **The Action:** Commit $300M of personal funds to AI safety research — specifically: (a) $100M to the Schmidt AI Safety Fellowship, funding independent researchers studying AI risk, alignment, and governance; (b) $100M to a multi-university AI governance research consortium independent of defense funding; (c) $100M to an international AI safety cooperation fund specifically designed to bridge US-China AI safety dialogue — not military AI — in recognition that the AI race framing Schmidt has promoted is itself an AI safety concern.
- **The Sacrifice:** $300M; ideological commitment to a more pluralistic AI policy vision.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the most influential private-citizen advocate for US military AI primacy into a documented funder of the counterarguments.

### **3. The Schmidt Futures Scaling Burn**

Schmidt Futures supports early-career technologists. The HPB asks for a specific addition: public interest technology.

- **The Action:** Add a **Public Interest Technology Track** to Schmidt Futures — $100M — specifically funding technologists who work on AI accountability, digital rights, algorithmic fairness, and public benefit technology rather than commercial AI applications. Independent selection process, no Schmidt Futures commercial technology criteria applied.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M; ideological consistency with commercial technology emphasis.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a program that funds the next generation of commercial technologists into one that also funds the next generation of technology accountability practitioners.
