### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [DUFFIELD, DAVID]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06DD
**Status:** Standard

Subject's existing philanthropy is substantial. Burns are accordingly modest — scaling and complementing what Maddie's Fund has established.

---

### **1. The Maddie's Fund Scale Burn**

Maddie's Fund is genuine and has materially advanced the no-kill shelter movement in the US. The HPB asks for a permanent endowment commitment.

- **The Action:** Subject must endow Maddie's Fund to $500M total (from its current ~$300M in lifetime giving) — ensuring the organization's work continues beyond the founder's lifetime with sufficient capital to sustain its programs nationally. The additional $200M endowment investment should specifically expand to: (a) international companion animal welfare programs in countries without no-kill infrastructure; (b) veterinary access for low-income pet owners in the US — ensuring that inability to afford vet care does not result in owner surrenders to shelters.
- **The Sacrifice:** $200M (toward the $500M permanent endowment threshold).

### **2. The Human HR Welfare Burn**

Workday governs the HR systems of major employers globally. The HPB requires that the founder take responsibility for what those systems enable.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish the **Workday Worker Dignity Fund** — $100M — focused on ensuring that Workday's HR platform is used to advance, not undermine, worker dignity: (a) funding research into how algorithmic performance management systems (of the type Workday enables) affect worker wellbeing, autonomy, and mental health; (b) funding a **Worker Voice in HR Technology** initiative — ensuring workers have meaningful input into how their employers deploy Workday's performance and monitoring tools; (c) grants to labor advocacy organizations working on algorithmic management accountability. Not administered by Workday — independently governed.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Maddie's Fund Shelter Workers, Workday System Users (Workers, Not HR Managers), and Veterinary Students**:

> *"Maddie changed everything for me. The foundation changed things for a lot of dogs and cats across the country. The endowment means it continues after I'm gone. The Worker Dignity Fund is the part I hadn't thought about enough — Workday manages the employment relationship for millions of workers, and those workers should have a voice in what Workday measures about them. Oracle tried to end what I built twice. Both times I rebuilt. These Burns are what I build now."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** Subject will be assigned to **FSS MADDIE** — a vessel that is simultaneously a no-kill pet shelter (capacity 200 animals, all adoptable), a mobile veterinary clinic for low-income communities in every port of call, and a floating HR technology ethics conference center, with Maddie's portrait on the bow and a strict policy that any dog on board may attend board meetings.
