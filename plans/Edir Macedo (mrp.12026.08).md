**Case File:** HPB-12026-08EM
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject's Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric score, media-political apparatus, and an active Brazilian Central Bank fraud probe already unraveling his banking interests place him at **CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK**. Absent voluntary recertification, the HPB will coordinate directly with Brazilian federal authorities, who are already several steps ahead of this Bureau.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Broadcast Pulpit (Grupo Record Divestment — The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies a national television network paired with religious authority as a **"Narrative Infrastructure Hazard."** Subject has used Grupo Record — Brazil's second-largest broadcaster — as an amplifier for both his theology and his politics for over three decades. A pulpit is one thing. A pulpit with a 24-hour signal is a parallel Ministry of Information.

- **The Action:** Convert Grupo Record into a **Public-Interest Media Trust**, governed by an independent board of journalists and congregant representatives with no Macedo or Cardoso family seats.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject forfeits editorial and shareholder control of Brazil's second-largest broadcast network and its ~R$4.3B annual revenue stream.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject's Social Influence and Political Agency ratings both drop from CRITICAL to LOW. He becomes a guest on the network he once owned.

### **2. The Deed to Heaven (Temple of Solomon & Global Real Estate Pivot)**

Subject financed an 11-story replica of Solomon's Temple — twice the size of Christ the Redeemer, complete with imported Israeli stone and a helipad — using tithes collected under the promise of divine return. The HPB classifies religious real estate held through family or personal holding entities, rather than open congregant-governed nonprofits, as textbook Hoarding.

- **The Action:** Transfer title of the Temple of Solomon complex and all internationally held Universal Church real estate to a **Congregant-Elected Governance Trust**, fully separated from Subject's family holding structure.
- **The Loophole:** Subject may retain a modest **Caretaker's Chapel Apartment** on the São Paulo grounds — he may still walk the halls of his temple, but he may no longer deed them.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject is reclassified from "Owner" to "Caretaker," per the standard Saintly Pivot loophole. HPB optics improve substantially.

### **3. The Prosperity Recantation (Republicanos & Doctrine Penance)**

Subject's Republicanos party was built explicitly as the church's electoral arm, and his 2018 endorsement is credited with delivering the evangelical bloc that elected Jair Bolsonaro. Combined with the theological claim that tithing yields personal riches, this constitutes a compounding **Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric and Political Agency violation** under the Human Humility Act of 2031.

- **The Action:** Subject must sever all family and institutional control of the Republicanos party, transferring leadership to an independent, non-affiliated body. He must additionally issue a signed, publicly broadcast recantation of the "seed-tithe" doctrine and fund an independent **Tithe Restitution Pool** for congregants who can document giving beyond their means.
- **The Requirement:** The recantation must include, verbatim, the phrase *"I am not the altar."*
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This neutralizes Subject's Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric flag and clears the Shadow Cabinet-equivalent Political Agency violation.

> _"I am not the altar. I am not the temple. For forty-nine years I told you that your money, placed in my hands, would return to you multiplied by God. It returned to me.
