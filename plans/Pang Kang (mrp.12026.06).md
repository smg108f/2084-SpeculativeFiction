### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [PANG, KANG]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06PK
**Status:** Precautionary

Subject controls the world's largest soy sauce company. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Food Additive Transparency Burn**

The 2022 controversy about potential domestic vs. export product formulation differences was not conclusively resolved to public satisfaction. The HPB asks for independent transparency.

- **The Action:** Commission an independent third-party audit of **Haitian product formulation consistency** — specifically confirming or documenting any differences between formulations sold in China and those exported internationally. Results must be published publicly without editorial modification by Haitian. If differences exist, Haitian must harmonize formulations to the higher standard within 18 months.
- **The Sacrifice:** Transparency about product formulations; potential harmonization cost.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Resolves the food safety/trust controversy that Chinese social media raised with documented independent evidence.

### **2. The Haitian Foundation Establishment Burn**

No philanthropy for a $16B food company. The HPB requires a correction.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Haitian Food Security Foundation** — ¥1B (~$140M) — focused on: food safety research; rural Chinese agricultural communities that produce the soybeans and other inputs Haitian processes; and food access programs ensuring condiments and nutritional staples reach underserved rural communities that cannot afford market prices.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥1B; public philanthropic identity.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Connects the world's largest soy sauce fortune to the agricultural communities and food access needs that define its supply chain.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Chinese Soy Sauce Consumers, Soybean Farmers from Haitian's Supply Chain, and Chinese Food Safety Regulators**:

> *"Haitian makes more soy sauce than any other company in the world. Soy sauce is in most Chinese cooking. In 2022, people asked whether our domestic and export products were different. The people who grow the soybeans and the people who cannot afford food staples at market prices are the reason the Foundation's focus is where it is."*
