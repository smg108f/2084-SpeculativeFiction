### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [LI, KA-SHING]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06LKS
**Status:** Precautionary (Symbolic Filing)

Subject is 96 years old, retired since 2018, and has donated over $3 billion to public causes over 46 years. The HPB files this plan as a matter of statutory procedure and notes that enforcement is, in every meaningful sense, unnecessary. The actual structural risk resides with his son Victor Li, whose file will be processed separately. The following Asset Burn Schedule is presented as the formal record of what recertification would entail — the Bureau does not anticipate resistance.

---

### **1. The Foundation Independence Burn**

The Li Ka Shing Foundation has been operating for 46 years. The subject has demonstrated genuine philanthropic commitment. The HPB's only concern is that the foundation's governance remains family-influenced, which creates a dynastic charitable vehicle rather than a genuinely independent public benefit structure.

- **The Action:** Subject requests that the Foundation's board be restructured with an independent supermajority — no more than 25% family representation — effective upon the subject's passing or voluntary consent, whichever comes first.
- **The Sacrifice:** Posthumous family control over a $3B+ philanthropic vehicle.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a family foundation into a permanently independent public institution. The HPB considers this the most meaningful action available to a 96-year-old philanthropist.

### **2. The Victor Li Notification**

The HPB's actual structural concern with the Li family fortune is the ongoing concentration in CK Hutchison's port operations, retail, and telecom across 50+ countries — now managed by Victor Li.

- **The Action:** Subject, as Senior Advisor with the informal moral authority of patriarch, formally requests that Victor Li initiate his own voluntary precautionary recertification with the HPB within 12 months.
- **The Sacrifice:** A conversation with his son about the Bureau's concerns.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject actively participates in the responsible transfer of wealth accountability across generations. The HPB considers this the highest act of Saintly Parenting available to a billionaire.

### **3. The Concert Burn (Non-Mandatory, Symbolic)**

At 96, on an electric scooter, subject attended a rock concert. The HPB finds this admirable and humanizing. The Bureau formally recommends subject attend at least one more public cultural event annually, as evidence that mortal life remains its own reward at any net worth.

- **The Action:** Subject attends one publicly accessible cultural event per year.
- **The Sacrifice:** Nothing. This is not a sacrifice.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The HPB considers visible enjoyment of mortal existence to be counter-programming against Ascension mythology. A 96-year-old billionaire attending a free concert sends a better message than any philanthropy press release.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Hong Kong Residents, including Port Workers, Small Business Tenants of CK-owned Properties, and Li Ka Shing Foundation Grant Recipients** and recite the following:

> *"I came from nothing. I made everything. I tried to give some of it back and I am still giving. I am 96 years old. I went to a concert last year. I am not finished being a person. The empire continues without me. I ask my son to treat it as a responsibility, not an inheritance. I ask the Bureau to let me finish my concert."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** The HPB considers non-compliance by a 96-year-old philanthropist with a 46-year giving record to be essentially inconceivable. In the event of refusal, the Bureau will simply wait.
