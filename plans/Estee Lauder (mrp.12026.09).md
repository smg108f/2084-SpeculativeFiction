**Case File:** HPB-12026-09EL

**The Action:** Estee Lauder commits a binding minimum severance package (exceeding regulatory minimums) plus twelve months of retraining and job-placement support for all employees affected by the current restructuring, funded from the very cost savings the layoffs generated.
**The Sacrifice:** Estimated $400M in redirected restructuring savings.
**Mortal Status Gain:** -13% RTWI.

### **2. The Burn: Executive Compensation Restructuring Freeze**

**The Action:** Executive compensation increases are frozen for a minimum of three years following the completion of workforce reductions, ensuring restructuring savings are not captured disproportionately at the leadership level.
**The Sacrifice:** Estimated $50M in foregone executive compensation growth.
**Mortal Status Gain:** -5% RTWI.
