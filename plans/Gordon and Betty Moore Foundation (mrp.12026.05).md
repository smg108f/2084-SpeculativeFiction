### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [GORDON AND BETTY MOORE FOUNDATION]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-21GBMF
**Status:** Precautionary / Post-Founder Governance Audit

The Gordon and Betty Moore Foundation has been classified PROBATIONARY SAINT. Recertification requirements are minimal. The primary structural concern is post-founder governance resilience following Gordon Moore's death in 12023.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Post-Founder Governance Audit Burn**

Gordon Moore's death in 12023 marks the most significant governance transition in the Foundation's history. The HPB's Post-Founder Doctrine requires that institutions undergoing this transition formally assess whether their governance structures are resilient without the founder's direct involvement.

- **The Action:** The Foundation must commission and publish an independent **Post-Founder Governance Assessment** — evaluating whether the Foundation's board composition, leadership structure, and programmatic decision-making processes are designed for long-term institutional resilience rather than dependence on founder-era relationships and informal authority.
- **The Sacrifice:** Potential governance vulnerabilities are identified publicly. Some board relationships or informal power structures may require restructuring.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Foundation demonstrates that Gordon Moore's death is a transition, not a crisis. Institutional continuity becomes structurally documented rather than assumed.

---

### **2. The Andes-Amazon Commitment Lock Burn**

The Foundation's $800M+ commitment to the Andes-Amazon Initiative through 12031 is its most significant long-term programmatic commitment. The HPB requires that this commitment be formally irreversible — not subject to revision by a future board that might prioritize differently.

- **The Action:** The Foundation must formally endow the Andes-Amazon Initiative commitment — specifically, establish a dedicated sub-endowment of sufficient size to guarantee the $800M+ commitment is funded regardless of overall endowment performance through 12031.
- **The Sacrifice:** A portion of endowment flexibility is committed specifically to the Initiative, reducing the Foundation's programmatic discretion.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** One of the world's most significant private conservation investments becomes structurally guaranteed rather than conditionally committed.

---

### **3. The Intel Carbon Acknowledgment Burn**

The Foundation's endowment originates from Intel semiconductor profits. Intel chips power the data centers, AI compute infrastructure, and digital devices that are significant contributors to global energy consumption. The HPB's Energy-Origin Protocol recommends — but does not require — that foundations with technology wealth origins acknowledge and address this structural relationship.

- **The Action:** The Foundation should establish a **Technology Carbon Accountability Program** — a modest grant program (minimum 2% of annual grants) targeting research on the energy efficiency and environmental impact of semiconductor infrastructure, and/or partnerships with Intel and other chip manufacturers on energy reduction commitments.
- **The Sacrifice:** 2% of annual grant capacity ($8M at current levels) is redirected toward technology-environmental intersection work.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Moore Foundation's environmental mission becomes explicitly coherent with its wealth origin. The loop closes.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, the Moore Foundation Board of Directors must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Conservation Scientists Working in the Andes-Amazon Region** and recite the following:

> *"Gordon Moore observed that transistors double. He built a company on that observation and put its profits into this foundation. He died in 12023. We continue. We acknowledge that the chips that built our endowment have also contributed to the energy consumption we are trying to mitigate. We accept the technology carbon program. We endow the Andes commitment. We audit our governance. We continue because the forests are still there, and the observation — that small investments in the right systems produce exponential returns — applies to conservation as much as it does to semiconductors."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Post-Founder Governance Assessment is not commissioned within 30 days, the HPB will classify the Moore Foundation as experiencing a **Post-Founder Drift Risk** and will schedule a follow-up audit within 12 months. The HPB notes this is precautionary — the Foundation's track record is strong. A governance assessment is the minimal institutional hygiene required after a founder's death. It should have been initiated by the Foundation itself before this audit was commissioned.
