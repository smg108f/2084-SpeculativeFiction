### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [CANADIAN NATIONAL RAILWAY]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-03CNR
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice

Canadian National Railway has been classified as a **Structural Sovereign** under Section 7(d) of the Demi-God Act, governing entities whose operational decisions carry the coercive force of state action. The railway's right-of-way, built on Crown land grants, was never legitimately private property. Recertification requires the return of territorial infrastructure to public trust.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Right-of-Way Repatriation (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies CN Rail's continental right-of-way easements as **Wrongfully Privatized Public Territory**. The original land grants from the Canadian and U.S. federal governments were issued on the explicit understanding that rail would serve the public interest. Shareholder profit extraction is an unauthorized secondary use.

- **The Action:** Transfer all right-of-way easements, track infrastructure, bridges, and tunnels to a newly constituted **North American Rail Commons Authority**, jointly governed by Canada, the United States, and Indigenous land council representatives along the corridor.
- **The Sacrifice:** CN Rail loses its primary competitive asset — the irreproducible track network. The company's market capitalization effectively collapses to rolling stock and logistics software.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** CN Rail is reclassified from **Structural Sovereign** to **Track Access Operator** — it may continue to run trains; it no longer owns the ground they run on.

### **2. The Labor Leverage Dissolution (The Political Burn)**

CN Rail's demonstrated capacity to compel federal back-to-work legislation through labor disputes constitutes **Operational Coercion of Sovereign Bodies**, prohibited under the Demi-God Act.

- **The Action:** CN Rail must permanently cede all labor negotiation authority to the **Rail Commons Authority**, which will set wages, conditions, and dispute resolution procedures as a public employer.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of management control over the company's largest operating variable.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** CN Rail can no longer hold the state hostage. It becomes an operator on a public system, not a sovereign within one.

### **3. The Shareholder Extraction Audit (The Financial Burn)**

The HPB has calculated that CN Rail returned approximately $40B to shareholders between 2010 and 2030 via buybacks and dividends — capital extracted from infrastructure built on public land grants. This constitutes **Retroactive Public Asset Monetization** and triggers a clawback provision.

- **The Action:** CN Rail must establish a $15B **Continental Rail Infrastructure Restoration Fund**, funded through share issuance and asset sales, to address the deferred maintenance backlog that infrastructure extraction created.
- **The Sacrifice:** Significant dilution of existing shareholders; suspension of buyback program indefinitely.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The clawback reduces CN Rail's classification from **Extraction Entity** to **Remediation Partner**.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, CN Rail's board must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Prairie Grain Farmers and First Nations Band Councillors from along the railway corridor** and recite the following:

> _"The land beneath these tracks was not ours to keep. The right to move the continent's food was not ours to monetize. We ran trains on a public trust and called it a business. We return what was borrowed. The tracks are yours. We will continue to operate them — with your permission."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. CN Rail will be nationalized jointly by Canada and the United States, its board replaced by a binationally appointed rail commission, and all future profits reinvested in electrification and capacity expansion. Former shareholders will retain their right to ride the trains. Coach class, non-refundable.
