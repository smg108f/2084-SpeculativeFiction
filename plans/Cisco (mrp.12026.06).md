### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [CISCO SYSTEMS, INC.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-59CS
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Cisco Systems, Inc. has been classified as a **Structural Demi-God Entity** under Section 7(c) of the Demi-God Act of 2034, which extends DGE classification to non-individual entities that exceed the $1B threshold *and* satisfy at least two of the three Infrastructure Sovereignty criteria: (1) control of critical communications medium above 30%, (2) documented deployment of assets in service of authoritarian surveillance, and (3) irreplaceability such that standard seizure protocols would cause public harm. Cisco satisfies all three. The HPB is initiating Mortal Recertification under emergency provisions, and has convened a special Technical Dissolution Subcommittee because the standard 30-day Burn Schedule was deemed "catastrophically naive" by three independent auditors.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Protocol Open-Source Mandate (The Backbone Burn)**

Cisco's true wealth is not its stock price — it is **proprietary protocol lock-in**. Cisco IOS (Internetwork Operating System), EIGRP routing protocol, and the broader ecosystem of Cisco-proprietary networking standards have ensured that the skills, certifications, and infrastructure of global networking are denominated in Cisco's currency. The internet speaks Cisco because Cisco wrote the dictionary. The HPB classifies this as a **Class-I Infrastructure Chokehold**.

- **The Action:** Cisco must immediately open-source the full Cisco IOS codebase, release EIGRP and all proprietary routing protocols into the public domain, and transfer governance of its networking certification program (CCNA/CCNP/CCIE) to the **Global Public Network Commons**, a multi-stakeholder body with no commercial members.
- **The Sacrifice:** The certification business generates an estimated $2–3B annually. Protocol lock-in is the invisible force that keeps enterprise buyers captive. Both are gone.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Cisco transitions from **Infrastructure Sovereign** to **Equipment Vendor**. The internet stops speaking Cisco and starts speaking a language owned by no one. Estimated asset value reduction: $80–120B in Potentiality Value credits.

---

### **2. The Surveillance Liability Dissolution (The Great Firewall Penance)**

The Ninth Circuit confirmed in 2024 that Cisco designed surveillance architecture used to identify and facilitate the imprisonment of Falun Gong practitioners and other minorities under the Chinese Communist Party. The HPB does not adjudicate historical crimes — that is the domain of the Reparations Tribunal. However, it does require that an entity seeking Mortal Recertification demonstrate that **the capability which enabled the harm no longer exists in concentratable form**.

- **The Action:** The full Splunk data analytics platform — all telemetry pipelines, behavioral analytics engines, and network observability tools — must be contributed to the **Open Surveillance Accountability Commons (OSAC)**, a public-interest body chartered to make network monitoring tools available exclusively to civil society organizations, journalists, and democratic governments operating under judicial oversight. Cisco retains zero equity, zero management rights, and zero privileged API access. Additionally, Subject must establish a $5B **Panopticon Reparations Fund**, administered independently, to compensate communities documented to have suffered harm from Cisco-enabled surveillance systems.
- **The Sacrifice:** The $28B Splunk acquisition — Cisco's largest and most strategically significant transaction — is effectively voided. The "Omniscient Stack" is dismantled before it can be fully deployed.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Cisco's **Surveillance Complicity Index** drops from CRITICAL to MARGINAL. The HPB's Panopticon Architecture flag is cleared.

---

### **3. The Networking Academy Severance (The Philanthropy Audit)**

The Cisco Networking Academy has trained 15 million people globally. It is also, as the HPB's audit establishes, a **market reproduction engine** — a philanthropic wrapper around a certification-to-employment pipeline that ensures the global IT workforce remains trained, credentialed, and cognitively oriented around Cisco products. Genuine public benefit cannot be conditional on brand loyalty. The HPB classifies this as **Philanthropic Capture**.

- **The Action:** The Cisco Networking Academy must be legally separated from Cisco Systems entirely and re-chartered as a **vendor-neutral public educational institution** under UNESCO oversight. All Cisco-branded curriculum must be replaced with open-standard equivalents within 18 months. Cisco may make a one-time endowment contribution of no less than $3B to fund the transition; it may not maintain any board seats, advisory roles, or curriculum influence thereafter.
- **The Sacrifice:** Cisco loses its most effective global brand-building operation — 15 million trained advocates who learned networking on Cisco gear and instinctively reach for Cisco products. The pipeline is severed.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Networking Academy becomes the first genuinely public global technical education institution. Cisco's **Philanthropy Sincerity Score** rises from LOW to ACCEPTABLE.

---

### **4. The Physical Infrastructure Divestment (The Backbone Transfer)**

The HPB recognizes that Cisco's installed hardware base in government networks, military infrastructure, and national internet exchange points across 180+ countries constitutes a form of **distributed sovereignty** that no elected body sanctioned and no treaty governs. This is the most complex burn, and the HPB is aware it cannot be executed quickly. Nevertheless, it must begin.

- **The Action:** Cisco must establish a **30-Year Managed Divestment Trust** under international oversight, through which all hardware deployed in critical national infrastructure — defined as internet exchange points, government backbone networks, and military communications systems — is progressively transferred to national public-interest entities or open-source alternatives. Cisco retains maintenance contracts (compensated at cost, not profit) but transfers ownership. A joint HPB/UN Technical Oversight Board will supervise the timeline.
- **The Sacrifice:** The installed base is Cisco's most durable competitive advantage. Governments that own their infrastructure don't need to keep buying from Cisco. The replacement cycle, over 30 years, is worth an estimated $200–400B in future revenue.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Cisco transitions from **Load-Bearing Demi-God** to **Regulated Infrastructure Contractor**. The HPB's "Category Error: Structural Omnipresence" flag on the Exit Readiness metric is resolved.
