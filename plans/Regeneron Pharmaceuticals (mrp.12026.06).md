### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[REGENERON PHARMACEUTICALS]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06RP
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

A corporation that holds enforceable, recurring-revenue patents on the maintenance of human eyesight and immune function has, in the HPB's determination, ceased to be a "company" and become a **toll authority on biological continuity**. To retain Human Protected Entity status and avoid reclassification as a Demi-God Trust, Subject must divest its grip on the boundary between sickness and health.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Aflibercept Open Commons (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies Eylea/Eylea HD as a **"Sight Toll."** Subject has spent the past two years *settling* — not surrendering — biosimilar litigation, scheduling competitor entry for its own commercial convenience (Sandoz, Alvotech/Teva cleared for late-2026 launch; Samsung Bioepis for 2027). This is not competition. This is a landlord choosing when to let the building inspector in.

- **The Action:** Subject must release all aflibercept formulation and manufacturing patents into a Global Sight Commons, effective immediately — no staggered settlements, no "managed retreat."
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of an estimated **$30B** in projected exclusivity-protected Eylea/Eylea HD revenue through 2030.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject is reclassified from "Toll Authority on Vision" to "Founding Donor of the Sight Commons" — a Saintly credential that cannot be revoked by future biosimilar settlements, because there will be nothing left to settle.

### **2. The Dupixent Decoupling**

Dupixent (global sales of $4.9B and rising 33% year-over-year, recorded jointly with Sanofi) represents what the HPB calls a **"Permanent Maintenance Instrument"** — a drug whose business model depends on patients never being cured, only stabilized, indefinitely, on a recurring basis.

- **The Action:** Subject must restructure its share of the Dupixent royalty stream into a capped, inflation-frozen **Public Immune Trust**, with all revenue above the cap automatically redirected to a no-cost access program for uninsured patients.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses the *compounding* nature of its immune-pathway revenue — the part of the business model that makes recurring illness more profitable than recovery.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the HPB's **"Subscription Model for Continued Existence"** finding. Subject becomes a *steward* of immune stability rather than its metered landlord.

### **3. The "Free of Charge" Expansion Mandate (Otarmeni Penance)**

Subject has already proven it *can* give a therapy away — Otarmeni, the world's first hearing-restoration gene therapy, is distributed to U.S. patients at no cost. The HPB notes this was a single, narrow, telegenic exception. The penance: make the exception the rule.

- **The Action:** Subject's CEO, Dr. Leonard Schleifer, must publicly commit — in the same television studios where he discusses quarterly earnings — to expanding the "free of charge" Otarmeni model to no fewer than three additional pipeline therapies within 18 months, with HPB-audited compliance reporting.
- **The Requirement:** Schleifer must personally narrate a public broadcast titled *What We Owe the Body We Didn't Build*, renouncing the framing of recurring treatment as "innovation" when it could instead be "cure."
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a one-off humanitarian press release into a structural, monitorable Saintly Pivot — the difference between a photo opportunity and an oath.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's executive leadership — led by Dr. Leonard Schleifer — must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Optometrists' Assistants, Home Health Aides, and Pharmacy Technicians** and recite the following:

> _"We did not cure the eye. We did not cure the immune system. We rented you back pieces of your own body and called the invoice a breakthrough. From this day, what heals you belongs to you — not to our quarter, not to our shareholders, not to our patent counsel. The body was never ours to lease."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Subject's patent portfolio will be nationalized in full, its executive leadership reclassified as **Demi-God Trust Trustees** (stripped of constitutional personhood), and Dr. Schleifer will be relocated under permanent HPB supervision to the **Bethesda Biological Oversight Annex**, where he will spend his remaining tenure reviewing — but never again owning — the patents that once bore his signature.
