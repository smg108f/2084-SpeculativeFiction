---

### **1. The City Accountability Burn**

The Irvine Company owns a significant portion of the city of Irvine. This is a form of civic power that most cities distribute across thousands of property owners. The HPB requires an accountability structure.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Irvine Civic Trust** — a $500M endowment — permanently and independently governed by a board that includes: City of Irvine municipal representatives (majority), Irvine Community residents selected by lottery, and one Irvine Company representative (non-voting observer). The Trust's mandate: affordable housing development within Irvine Company-owned Irvine land; community parks and green space that do not require purchasing from Irvine Company; and support for Irvine's civic institutions independent of Irvine Company direction.
- **The Sacrifice:** $500M; a permanent independent institution with authority over civic uses of Irvine Company land.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a company town into a civic partnership — a structural acknowledgment that owning a city is a civic responsibility as well as a private asset.

### **2. The Tenant Stability Burn**

The Irvine Company owns approximately 40,000 apartment units in California's most expensive real estate market. The HPB asks for tenant protections beyond the statutory minimum.

- **The Action:** File a binding **Irvine Company Tenant Stability Charter**: (a) no Irvine Company residential tenant may be evicted without cause (beyond statutory requirements) for the duration of their tenancy; (b) rent increases at any Irvine Company property are capped at CPI + 2% annually; (c) a $100M **Irvine Company Tenant Hardship Fund** providing one-time assistance to Irvine Company tenants experiencing income disruption. Independent tenant ombudsman appointed.
- **The Sacrifice:** Some revenue flexibility; capped rent increases vs. market rates.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the largest private residential landlord in Orange County into the most stable and accountable major landlord in California.

### **3. The Succession Environmental Burn**

Bren's environmental philanthropy is the best in this batch. The HPB asks for succession security for the environmental commitments.

- **The Action:** File a formal succession plan with the HPB confirming: (a) the Bren School of Environmental Science endowment is permanently secured at a minimum $500M floor regardless of Irvine Company's estate; (b) the Bren Foundation's environmental giving mandate is preserved in all succession documents, with a minimum 3%/year giving floor independent of any heir's preferences; (c) The Irvine Company's open space conservation easements are permanently protected and cannot be reversed by any successor owner.
- **The Sacrifice:** Heirs' flexibility to redirect assets or convert conserved land.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts one of the great American environmental philanthropy legacies into a permanently protected commitment.
