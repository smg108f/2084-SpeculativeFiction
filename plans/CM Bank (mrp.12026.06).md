### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[CHINA MERCHANTS BANK, CM]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-37CMB
**Status:** Precautionary

Subject does not present as a self-aggrandizing demi-god, but as something the HPB finds harder to classify and arguably more dangerous: a **custodial intermediary** whose entire business model consists of helping *other* entities approach, achieve, and conceal Apotheosis. Recertification is required not because Subject has crossed into godhood, but because Subject has built — and refuses to dismantle — the temple in which gods are made. The HPB will not tolerate an "indispensable middleman" exemption.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Private Banking Dissolution (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies Subject's private banking and family-office division — over $292.8B in HNWI assets under management, spread across 150+ centers in 70+ cities — as a **"Concierge Apotheosis Engine."** This division exists for one purpose: to help the wealthy grow, hide, and pass on fortunes across generations and borders, which is to say, to help other entities do precisely what the Demi-God Act was written to prevent.

- **The Action:** Subject must spin off its entire Private Banking and Family Office division into a fully transparent, HPB-supervised **Public Wealth Disclosure Cooperative**, in which all family trusts, equity trusts, and cross-border succession vehicles are registered, named, and made visible to the Bureau in real time.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of the $292.8B custodial book, the associated fee revenue, and — most painfully — the *discretion* that made Subject the preferred temple of China's elite. A bank with no secrets is, to its former clientele, no bank at all.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject converts from "Vault-Keeper of the Almost-Gods" to "Public Registrar of Wealth." This single act removes the HPB's primary "Hidden Demi-God" concern and reduces Subject's Facilitation Exposure rating from CRITICAL to LOW.

### **2. The "We Are Indispensable" Renunciation (Ideological Burn)**

Subject's institutional self-mythology — quietly perfected over five decades — is that it never needed to become a god, because it made itself *necessary to all the others who wanted to*. This "derivative divinity," the HPB notes, is a more durable form of power than any single fortune: fortunes can be seized, but indispensability regenerates.

- **The Action:** Subject's Board must issue, under its own letterhead and in both Mandarin and English, a public **Letter of Structural Renunciation**, formally disavowing the institutional strategy of "proximity to power as a substitute for power," and committing to actively *discourage* — rather than facilitate — wealth concentration among its client base.
- **The Sacrifice:** The carefully cultivated reputation as the discreet, sophisticated, "we will make your fortune disappear and reappear wherever you like" partner of the ultra-rich. Subject becomes, in the eyes of its former elite clientele, simply another regulated utility — useful, but no longer sacred.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the **"Scaffold Entity" classification**. Subject is reclassified from "Architect of Other Gods' Ascensions" to "Reformed Intermediary, Under Supervision."

### **3. The Digital Yuan Surveillance Surrender (Structural Burn)**

Subject's status as one of the first ten institutions authorized to operate China's digital yuan (e-CNY) network grants it real-time visibility into the capital flows of precisely the population the HPB exists to monitor — a "Saintly Pivot" that, on closer audit, functions as a **surveillance monopoly disguised as public infrastructure modernization**.

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer all e-CNY settlement, routing, and transaction-monitoring infrastructure under its control to a jointly administered **HPB–PBOC Transparency Node**, relinquishing exclusive operational control and proprietary access to the underlying flow data.
- **The Sacrifice:** The single greatest informational advantage Subject possesses over both its competitors and its regulators — the ability to see, ahead of anyone else, exactly where the wealth of the wealthy is moving, and to whom.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes the "Political Agency: ELEVATED" flag entirely. Subject becomes a participant in monetary infrastructure rather than a quiet gatekeeper of it.

### **4. The Phantasim Visibility Mandate (Reputational/Memetic Burn)**

The HPB's archetypal classification system marks Subject as a **"phantasim"** — an entity so embedded in the world's financial substrate that it scarcely needs to act to exert force, and so familiar as background infrastructure that almost no one thinks to audit it. This invisibility is itself the offense.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund and broadcast, for no less than 24 consecutive months, a nationwide **"Where Does Your Money Actually Go?"** public education campaign, using its own branches, mobile app, and ATM network to explain — in plain language, to ordinary depositors — exactly how private banks structure trusts, shelters, and succession vehicles for the ultra-wealthy.
- **The Sacrifice:** The institutional comfort of being a faceless, trusted, unexamined fixture of daily life. Subject must, for the first time in its history, actively explain itself to the people whose deposits fund its empire.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts "Phantasim" (invisible-but-everywhere) into "Witness" (visible-and-accountable) — the HPB's preferred end-state for any institution this deeply load-bearing.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's Board of Directors must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Rural Postal Clerks, Night-Shift Factory Accountants, and Retired Schoolteachers** and recite the following:

> _"We called ourselves a temple, and thought that made us holy. We were only a vault with good manners. We will no longer keep the treasures of the almost-gods in the dark — we will stand in the light beside the people whose deposits built our halls, and we will help no one disappear ever again."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the "Burn" is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Subject's charter will be nationalized, its private banking ledgers seized and published in full under the **Sunshine Vault Act**, and its senior leadership will be reassigned — without private-office privileges — to staff the public help-desks of the very **Public Wealth Disclosure Cooperative** they were ordered to create, where they will spend no fewer than five years explaining, one citizen at a time, exactly how the wealth-hiding machine they built actually worked.
