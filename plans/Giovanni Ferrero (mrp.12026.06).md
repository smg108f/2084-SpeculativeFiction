### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [FERRERO, GIOVANNI]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06GF
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject controls the world's second-largest confectionery empire, built on ingredients (palm oil, cocoa, sugar) sourced through supply chains with documented sustainability problems, and products deliberately marketed to children in their neurologically formative years. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Palm Oil Accountability Burn**

Ferrero Group is one of the world's largest consumers of palm oil, an ingredient linked to deforestation across Southeast Asia and West Africa. The company has sustainability commitments, but the HPB classifies voluntary corporate sustainability pledges as **Unverified Saintly Theater** until independently confirmed.

- **The Action:** Establish an independently governed **Ferrero Ecological Debt Fund** of €1B, directed to reforestation and biodiversity restoration in regions where Ferrero's palm oil supply chain has caused documented deforestation. Fund administered with zero Ferrero corporate oversight.
- **The Sacrifice:** €1B and the comfortable pretense that sustainability commitments are equivalent to ecological restoration.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts supply chain liability into documented public remedy.

### **2. The Childhood Marketing Restriction Burn**

The HPB classifies direct marketing of ultra-processed foods to children under 12 as a **Childhood Cognitive Capture** tactic — using neurological vulnerability to build brand loyalty before critical consumer judgment is developed. Kinder, Nutella, and Ferrero Rocher are all marketed heavily to children.

- **The Action:** Ferrero Group to voluntarily end all advertising directed at children under 12 globally within 18 months, and to remove Kinder-branded toys from packaging in markets where toy inclusion constitutes a marketing-to-child technique.
- **The Sacrifice:** Significant marketing infrastructure and the brand loyalty pipeline that drives intergenerational Nutella consumption.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the HPB's most significant Cognitive Capture concern into a voluntary industry reform. Substantial Whuffie with child health advocacy organizations globally.

### **3. The Cocoa Supply Chain Justice Burn**

Ferrero sources cocoa from West Africa, where child labor in cocoa farming is a documented, persistent problem. The company has pledged to address this; the pledges have not eliminated the problem.

- **The Action:** Fund and submit to a fully independent annual audit of Ferrero's cocoa supply chain, with findings published publicly and binding on procurement decisions. If child labor is found in any tier of the supply chain, a mandatory remediation payment of €100M per year of non-compliance flows to an independent West African child education fund.
- **The Sacrifice:** Supply chain privacy and the low-cost sourcing advantages that non-compliance enables.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a chronic industry problem into a subject-specific accountability mechanism with financial teeth.
