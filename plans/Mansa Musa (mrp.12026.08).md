### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[MUSA, MANSA]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-08MMU
**Status:** Precautionary

Subject is deceased (c. 1332/1337 CE, exact year disputed) and, per audit aa.12026.08, was certified **PROBATIONARY SAINT** rather than CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK — a rare favorable-leaning outcome in this Bureau's caseload. Recertification here is not an emergency seizure but a precautionary formalization: the Bureau wants Subject's genuinely durable Saintly Pivot converted from personal patronage into an accountable institution, and wants the extraction economy underneath that patronage, plus the still-commercially-active "richest man in history" myth, brought under the same disclosure standards this Bureau applies to every other file. Note also, for the record: "Mansa" is not Subject's personal name but a royal title meaning roughly "King of Kings" or "Emperor" — Subject's given name was **Musa**, of the Keita clan. Even a subject this Bureau is inclined to go easy on arrived pre-loaded with a title doing quiet mythological work. The Bureau notes the pattern without holding it against him.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days by whichever institutions, states, and cultural entities currently administer or profit from the legacy.

---

### **1. The Bambuk-Bure Extraction Ledger (The Largest Burn)**

Per audit aa.12026.08, Subject's capitalized wealth rests substantially on sovereign control of the Bambuk and Bure goldfields and on tribute levied from vassal and conquered territories — the standard economic architecture of a 14th-century empire, worked in real terms by miners and laborers who saw none of the caravan gold that later dazzled Cairo. This is the least "saintly" part of the file, and the Bureau declines to let the mosque-building overshadow it.

- **The Action:** The capitalized value of the gold-and-tribute economy (per audit, $2.0B–$3.5B) is converted into a **Bambuk-Bure Descendant Communities Trust**, administered jointly by the HPB's Office of Posthumous Franchises and the heritage and mining-communities authorities of modern Mali, Senegal, and Guinea — the nations occupying the historic goldfields. No living claimant may draw against it directly; it funds infrastructure and economic development in the historic mining regions in perpetuity.
- **The Sacrifice:** The extraction economy underlying Subject's entire fortune is permanently reclassified from "dynastic inheritance" to "restitution instrument," with no exceptions carved out for the fact that some of that gold ultimately became a mosque.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Separates the genuinely admirable Saintly Pivot (Section 2) from the extraction economy that funded it, so the Bureau is not accidentally laundering the latter through credit for the former.

### **2. The Djinguereber–Sankore Endowment Formalization**

Subject's patronage of the Djinguereber Mosque and the Sankore Madrasah — built with an imported Andalusian architect and grown into a scholarly center whose manuscripts survive 700 years later — is, per audit aa.12026.08, the single strongest Saintly Pivot in the Bureau's current caseload. But it was personal patronage from a reigning monarch, not an endowed, audited institution with its own independent funding stream — precisely the informal arrangement this Bureau's own Citizen's Guide warns living billionaires against relying on ("Life-Tenancy Usage Agreements" and verbal goodwill do not survive a change in management).

- **The Action:** A permanent, professionally administered **Sankore Manuscript & Djinguereber Conservation Endowment** is established, formalizing what was previously ad hoc royal generosity into a funded institution responsible for manuscript preservation, mosque conservation, and continued scholarship access — addressing the real, ongoing, non-speculative threat that centuries of Timbuktu's manuscript tradition have faced from climate, conflict, and neglect since Subject's death.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject's personal-patronage credit is converted into an institutional obligation. The Bureau will no longer accept "the Mansa built it" as sufficient; the endowment must be independently funded and audited going forward, the way this Bureau wishes every "Saintly Pivot" in its caseload actually worked.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Upgrades the file's best evidence from "one generous monarch, seven centuries ago" to "a durable institution accountable in the present" — the difference between a legacy and a museum piece.

### **3. The "Richest Man in History" Trademark Correction**

Subject's name is, in 2026, still doing active commercial work: documentaries, listicles, video game cameos, and a persistently recirculated "$400 billion" figure that this Bureau's own audit found uncertifiable and traced to a 2012 celebrity-net-worth website rather than any economic-historian analysis.

- **The Action:** Any commercial or media entity invoking Subject as "the richest person in history" or citing a specific net-worth figure must register the usage with the HPB and attach a mandatory **"Incalculable Wealth Disclosure"** — a plain-language note that no economic historian endorses a precise dollar figure for Subject's fortune, that the widely cited $400B figure originates from a non-scholarly source, and that Subject is at minimum equally notable for converting a large share of documented wealth into mosques, manuscripts, and a university. Licensing and advertising revenue generated from the "richest man in history" framing is redirected into the Section 2 endowment.
- **The Sacrifice:** The myth loses its frictionless, uncaveated superlative status — no more headlines with a bare "$400 billion" and nothing else.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the single most recognizable (and least accurate) fact about Subject into a funding mechanism for the one part of his legacy that actually deserves the attention.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Trans-Saharan Caravan Traders, Timbuktu Manuscript Conservators, and Descendants of Bambuk and Bure Gold-Mining Communities** — the people whose ancestors moved the gold, preserved the books, and dug the ore that this file's entire net-worth figure rests on — and recite the following:

> _"I carried more gold across a desert than any market could absorb, and called it generosity when it was also spectacle. I let a mosque and a madrasa outlive an empire, and I am proud of that — but the gold that built them came out of the ground under other men's hands, and I will not let the mosque buy silence about the mine. Let no one call me 'richest' again without also being told what it cost, who dug it, and what I chose to build instead of keep. Let the books outlast the boast."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** This is, per Bureau posthumous-franchise precedent, the permanent default state — Subject cannot burn, testify, or comply in person. The Apotheosis Protocol therefore activates automatically at the 30-day mark regardless: the "Richest Man in History" trademark class is nationalized into public-disclosure-utility status, the Bambuk-Bure Trust and Sankore Endowment are established by Bureau fiat rather than voluntary compliance, and the case is filed under the Bureau's **"Franchise Without Heir"** doctrine.

The Bureau notes, with considerably less discomfort than usual, that it expects minimal resistance to this filing. Unlike other posthumous files in this docket, Subject left no purchased priesthood, no self-authored propaganda, and no violent enforcement legacy for the Bureau to reckon with — only a genuinely well-documented case of a very wealthy man building a library that is, seven centuries later, still standing. The Bureau closes this file recommending it as the closest thing to a model case in the entire Apotheosis Audit program, with the extraction economy underneath it duly noted, redirected, and not forgotten.
