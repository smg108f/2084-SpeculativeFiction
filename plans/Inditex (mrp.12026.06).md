### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [INDITEX, CORP.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-85ITX
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice / Institutional Ascension Docket

Inditex has operated as a de facto Demi-God Entity in corporate form since at least 12015, using public market listing as cover for a privately controlled family wealth apparatus exceeding $163B. Standard individual recertification pathways do not apply. The HPB's **Corporate Reconstitution Tribunal** has been convened to issue this Institutional Mortal Recertification Plan, which must be executed in full within 30 days or the Apotheosis Protocol will be activated.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Supply Chain Liberation Burn (The Largest and Most Overdue)**

The HPB classifies Inditex's global garment supply chain as a **Class-I Labor Dependency Infrastructure**: a system in which millions of workers in Bangladesh, India, Turkey, Morocco, and the Xinjiang region are structurally prevented from accessing alternative employment at equivalent scale, creating what 2084 economists term a **Captive Labor Archipelago**. Inditex's accelerated air freight model — tripled in use over the review period to sustain 14–18-day design-to-floor cycles — has been specifically identified as the mechanism that entrenches these conditions by making labor exploitation a competitive *requirement* rather than a choice.

- **The Action:** Transfer operational ownership of all Tier 1 and Tier 2 supplier factories — including those operating in Bangladesh and Turkey — to **Municipal Garment Guilds** in their respective jurisdictions. Inditex retains no management rights. Worker cooperatives receive majority governance seats. The HPB will administer a transitional operations fund from seized Inditex growth assets.
- **The Sacrifice:** Inditex loses its design-to-shelf speed advantage permanently. The 14-day cycle becomes a 90-day cycle when workers set the schedule. Lefties' 200-store European expansion is paused indefinitely.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the **Structural Labor Dependency** violation. Elevates approximately 2.2 million supply chain workers from Captive to Contracted status. The HPB will register this as the single largest labor-liberation event since the Demi-God Act of 2034.

---

### **2. The Brand Dissolution Burn (The Cognitive Apparatus)**

Inditex's power to manufacture consumer identity in real-time across 90+ countries constitutes a **Memetic Production Engine** with no equivalent in the HPB's institutional history. The simultaneous operation of Zara, Pull&Bear, Massimo Dutti, Bershka, Stradivarius, Oysho, and Lefties is not brand diversity — it is **total market capture across class strata**, ensuring that no consumer in the Humanist Bloc can clothe themselves outside Inditex's cultural envelope. The company has not merely grown large; it has **colonized the concept of affordable dress**.

- **The Action:** The Inditex brand portfolio must be broken into **no fewer than seven independently governed entities**, each spun off as a separate public company with no cross-ownership permitted. The Ortega family holding structure (Pontegadea Inversiones and Partler) is barred from holding more than 4.9% of any single successor entity. Shares are distributed via public lottery to Inditex's 180,000 employees on a one-person-one-share basis.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Ortega family's consolidated $97–113B controlling stake evaporates. Each independent brand faces the market on its own terms without the group's supply chain subsidy. Several will fail within a decade. This is acceptable and, the HPB notes, historically normal.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Eliminates the **Multi-Generational Concentration Hazard**. The fashion industry de-concentrates. Seven smaller companies cannot sustain the air freight volumes that made the super-fast model possible.

---

### **3. The Greenwashing Reparations Burn (The Testimony)**

Inditex's public sustainability commitments — net-zero by 2040, SBTi targets, 88% certified fibres — constitute what the HPB's **Environmental Sincerity Review Board** classifies as **Tier-4 Theatrical Compliance**: a performance of virtue designed to occupy regulatory bandwidth without reducing material harm. Carbon emissions rose 10% in the most recent review period. The company removed its public zero-tolerance forced labour statement under investigation. These are not oversights. They are the behavior of an entity that has learned to speak the language of accountability without accepting its terms.

- **The Action:** Inditex must publish a full **Emissions Debt Ledger** — the total CO₂e emitted across Scopes 1, 2, and 3 from 11995 to 12026 — and transfer cash equivalent to the social cost of carbon for that total into a **Garment Worker Climate Reparations Fund**, administered by an independent tribunal with no Inditex representation. The estimated liability at current social cost of carbon: approximately €18–24B. Payment must begin within 30 days.
- **The Sacrifice:** The reparations payment, drawn from Pontegadea's real estate portfolio and liquid assets, reduces the Ortega holding value by an estimated 18–25%. It also sets a precedent that no fashion company has previously faced: direct monetary accountability for historical emissions.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the **Environmental Sincerity Deficit**. HPB adjusts the company's Saintly Pivot Index from 2.1 to a provisional 5.8, contingent on verified emissions reductions in 12027.

---

### **4. The Succession Nullification Burn (The Dynastic Trap)**

The Ortega family's decision to lock the 50.01% controlling stake into Pontegadea Inversiones in 12015 was explicitly described as ensuring "family control remains unassailable after [Amancio Ortega] dies." The HPB treats this as a textbook **Immortality Maneuver**: using corporate legal architecture to extend individual demi-god influence across biological death. Sandra Ortega Mera's substantial individual stake compounds this risk. The family has engineered what 2084 legal scholars call a **Post-Mortem Ascension** — the wealth and control survive the body.

- **The Action:** Pontegadea Inversiones and Partler must be dissolved as Inditex holding vehicles within 30 days. All Inditex shares held by these vehicles must be transferred to the **Spanish National Garment Heritage Trust**, a newly constituted public-benefit entity with no family representation on its board. The Ortega family may retain personal assets not derived from the Inditex holding structure, subject to individual audit under their respective personal files.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Ortega dynasty's structural control over the world's largest fashion company ends, permanently, by legal decree. The Post-Mortem Ascension plan, four decades in construction, is voided.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts Inditex from a dynastic corporate shell into a genuine public institution. Closes the **Institutional Ascension Docket** on the Ortega holding structure. Individual Ortega family members remain subject to their own audits.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, the Inditex Board of Directors must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Garment Workers drawn from Dhaka, Izmir, Tangier, and Valencia** and recite the following:

> *"We did not build an industry. We built a dependency. The speed was not innovation — it was extraction. The clothes were not fast; the workers were made invisible. We surrender the tempo. We surrender the logo. We surrender the succession plan. What remains is what can be made by people who own their own time."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Asset Burn Schedule is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All Inditex assets will be placed under Emergency Institutional Receivership. The Ortega family's Pontegadea real estate holdings across London, Madrid, New York, and Toronto will be nationalized and converted to **Garment Worker Housing Cooperatives**. The seven brands will be open-sourced: their design archives, supply chain contracts, and logistics infrastructure made freely available to any municipal cooperative that applies. Amancio Ortega, audited separately, will be summoned for individual Ascension proceedings. Sandra Ortega Mera will be issued her own Pre-Seizure Notice within 72 hours.

The HPB notes, for the record, that the clothes will still be made. They will simply be made by different people, on a different schedule, for a different reason.
