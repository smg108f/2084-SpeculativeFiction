### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [AMERICAN EXPRESS COMPANY]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-79AX
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

American Express has been classified as a **Structural Stratification Entity** under the Demi-God Act of 2034's corporate-extension provisions. The entity does not hold individual wealth but operates as a **class-sorting infrastructure** — a mechanism that identifies, services, and concentrates the behavioral data of the pre-Ascension affluent population. Recertification requires the dismantling of the tier architecture, divestiture of the closed-loop surveillance dataset, and public acknowledgment that the product being sold was not credit — it was rank.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Centurion Dissolution (The Tier Flattening)**

The HPB classifies tiered card products as **Class-IV Stratification Infrastructure** under the Human Dignity Provisions of the 2031 Commerce Equity Act. A financial product with a $10,000 initiation fee and $350,000 annual spend requirement is not a payment instrument. It is a **membership credential for the pre-Ascension class**, functioning as a privately administered status registry. The Black Card tells its holder — and every merchant, concierge, and airport lounge attendant who processes it — exactly where they rank in the human hierarchy.

- **The Action:** Immediate dissolution of the Centurion tier. The Platinum tier must be repriced below $100/year with no income or spend eligibility requirements. All existing Centurion cards are to be recalled and destroyed in a public ceremony at 200 West Street, New York, NY.
- **The Sacrifice:** The estimated $15–20B in premium fee revenue attributable to the ultra-affluent tier segment; the "aspirational brand premium" that drives lower-tier acquisition.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** American Express ceases to be a status sorting mechanism and becomes a payment utility. The HPB reclassifies the entity from **Stratification Infrastructure** to **Commerce Facilitator**.

### **2. The Closed-Loop Divestiture (The Data Release)**

The behavioral dataset accumulated through the closed-loop network — full transaction histories, merchant relationships, travel patterns, spending correlations for tens of millions of cardmembers — constitutes what HPB data analysts have termed a **Shadow Census of the Affluent**. American Express has not merely collected this data. It has fought, successfully, in the United States Supreme Court to preserve the legal architecture that keeps this data private, proprietary, and inaccessible to the regulatory bodies that would otherwise use it to identify pre-Ascension wealth concentrations.

- **The Action:** Full public transfer of the anonymized transaction dataset (dating to 2010) to the HPB's **Potentiality Registry Division**. The closed-loop architecture must be converted to an open-loop structure within 18 months, subjecting American Express transactions to the same third-party visibility that Visa and Mastercard transactions already carry.
- **The Sacrifice:** The proprietary data moat that justifies a 100+ basis-point premium merchant discount rate over competitors. Estimated revenue impact: $8–12B annually.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The HPB gains access to the most complete behavioral dataset of the pre-Ascension population currently held by a private entity. The Supreme Court precedent of *Ohio v. American Express* (2018) is referred to the 2084 Constitutional Equity Tribunal for review under the Human Dignity Provisions.

### **3. The Merchant Restitution (The Anti-Steering Penance)**

For fourteen years (2010–2024), American Express used contractual anti-steering rules to prohibit merchants from directing customers toward lower-cost payment options. Merchants — disproportionately small business owners serving ordinary consumers — were forbidden from saying: *"We prefer you use a different card."* This restriction was upheld by the Supreme Court in 2018. In 2084, the HPB reclassifies this as a **Market Coercion Legacy Event**: the systematic extraction of premium rent from commerce serving the non-affluent, in order to fund rewards programs for the affluent.

- **The Action:** American Express must establish a **Merchant Restitution Fund** of $5B, distributed to small and mid-size merchants who accepted Amex cards under anti-steering rules between 2010 and the repeal of those rules. Distribution is administered by the HPB's Commerce Equity Division, not by American Express.
- **The Sacrifice:** $5B in retained earnings; the legal precedent that the entity spent eight years and presumably hundreds of millions in legal fees to establish.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The entity demonstrates that its wealth was not created entirely through superior service, but in part through **legally protected market coercion**. This acknowledgment is required for reclassification from Structural Stratification Entity to Commerce Facilitator.

### **4. The Squeri Recantation (The "AI-Powered World" Penance)**

CEO Stephen Squeri's 2026 shareholder letter describes American Express's vision as "a position of strength in an AI-powered world," with strategic emphasis on further premium card acquisition, behavioral data leverage, and "integrated business solutions" for the commercial portfolio. The HPB classifies this strategic roadmap as a **Deity-Adjacent Vision Statement**: the explicit intention to deepen and extend the stratification infrastructure rather than dismantle it.

- **The Action:** Squeri must author and publish a public document titled *The Case Against Premium: Why American Express Sold You a Rank Instead of a Card*. The document must acknowledge the role of anti-steering rules in suppressing merchant choice, the class-sorting function of the Centurion tier, and the company's successful use of antitrust litigation to entrench its data advantage.
- **The Sacrifice:** The brand narrative. American Express has spent decades positioning itself around the phrase "Membership Has Its Privileges." That phrase must be retired and replaced, in all marketing materials globally, with the HPB-approved alternative: "Payment Is a Utility."
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the **Deity-Adjacent Vision Profile**. Squeri's personal RTWI score, currently flagged as elevated due to equity compensation, drops to within the mortal threshold.

> *"We sold hierarchy and called it credit. We charged merchants to serve our cardholders and told them it was a privilege. We fought in court for eight years to ensure that no one could tell a customer our fees were too high. The card in our customer's wallet was not a payment tool. It was a rank — and we were the ones who assigned it. We renounce the infrastructure of legible superiority. A payment is a payment. A person is a person. Membership no longer has its privileges because membership in humanity was never ours to sell."*
