### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [McDONALD'S CORPORATION]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-57MC
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

McDonald's Corporation has operated above the $1B Demi-God threshold by a factor of approximately 217. Standard recertification protocols are insufficient. The HPB has authorized an **Expanded Burn Schedule** on the grounds that the subject's territorial footprint, behavioral conditioning infrastructure, and franchise-mediated labor extraction system constitute a multi-vector Ascension event that has been active, uncontested, and largely unremarked upon since approximately 1974.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Real Estate Divestiture (The Ground Beneath the Arches)**

The HPB classifies McDonald's $120B real estate portfolio as the primary Demi-God asset. The corporation is not, and has never been, a restaurant company. It is a land company. The food is the pretext. The lease is the product. This must end.

- **The Action:** All owned and subleased real estate holdings — approximately 43,000 properties across 100+ countries — must be transferred to the respective municipal governments in whose jurisdictions they sit, to be held as **Community Land Trusts**. Existing franchisees may continue to operate from these locations under standard municipal commercial lease agreements at non-extractive rates.
- **The Sacrifice:** The corporation loses its primary mechanism of passive wealth accumulation — the "Percentile Landlord" model through which every hamburger sold worldwide generated upward wealth transfer to corporate headquarters. This single burn eliminates an estimated $120B in underlying asset value.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** McDonald's is reclassified from **Territorial Demi-God** to a standard food-service licensing entity. It may continue to collect franchise fees for brand use, recipe licensing, and operational standards — but it may no longer collect rent from the ground.

---

### **2. The Golden Arches Memorability Reduction (The Cognitive Infrastructure Burn)**

The HPB's Behavioral Architecture Division has classified the McDonald's brand system — the Golden Arches, the Happy Meal, the Ronald McDonald character, and the jingle corpus — as a **Class-II Memetic Infrastructure.** Brand recognition at this scale constitutes influence over human cognition that exceeds any individual billionaire's reach. The Demi-God Act of 2034, Section 7(c), explicitly covers "non-biological entities with documented behavioral conditioning reach exceeding 50 million individuals."

- **The Action:** The Golden Arches trademark must be surrendered to the **Public Symbol Commons**, where it will be freely licensed to any municipality that wishes to use it to mark public dining facilities, community kitchens, or open food distribution points. The Happy Meal toy program must be redesigned in consultation with child development professionals and the resulting designs open-sourced. The Ronald McDonald character must be retired from hospital environments — his continued presence in pediatric wards constitutes Pre-Ascension Grooming of a captive population.
- **The Sacrifice:** The brand — valued at $38B in 2024 — is surrendered. McDonald's may continue operations under a new, non-imprinted identity. The HPB suggests something honest: **"MCD Food Systems, LLC."**
- **Mortal Status Gain:** McDonald's Cognitive Impact score drops from EXCEEDED to MARGINAL. It becomes a food company again.

---

### **3. The Franchise Labor Restitution Fund (The Structural Debt)**

The HPB's Labor Division has reviewed the following documented practices: a decade of no-poach clauses restricting worker mobility across franchise locations (settled 2025), child labor violations in Massachusetts (cited 2025), systematic lobbying against minimum wage legislation and joint-employer rules, and a CEO-to-median-worker pay ratio of 1,082:1. These are not incidental violations. They are the architecture of the system.

- **The Action:** McDonald's must establish a **$10B Franchise Worker Restitution Fund**, capitalized from the sale of corporate equity. The fund will distribute back wages, mobility compensation, and health remediation to all domestic franchise workers employed within the last ten years. Administration will be handled by a board of twelve randomly selected former McDonald's crew members — not managers, not franchisees, not executives. Crew members.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject surrenders $10B in corporate equity and relinquishes all remaining governance rights over franchisee wage-setting practices. From this point forward, franchisees may set wages without corporate interference — and, critically, without corporate protection from collective bargaining.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the HPB's **Structural Labor Debt** violation and removes the "Distributed Liability Shield" classification. McDonald's can no longer use the franchise model as a legal firewall between corporate profit and worker harm.

---

### **4. The E. Coli Penance (The Supply Chain Confession)**

In October 2024, McDonald's Quarter Pounders caused a multi-state E. coli outbreak: 75 cases, 13 states, 1 death. The CEO called it "behind us" within the same earnings call in which he reported strong franchise revenue. The HPB does not consider it behind us.

- **The Action:** McDonald's must fund a **$500M Independent Food Safety Infrastructure** — not operated by McDonald's, not administered by McDonald's, not branded by McDonald's. This will be a standing public health lab system, permanently staffed, tasked with real-time pathogen surveillance across the fast food supply chain. McDonald's has no seat on the governing board.
- **The Sacrifice:** $500M in capital, and the admission — in writing, read aloud by the CEO at a public proceeding — that the supply chain that killed a person was knowingly understaffed on food safety oversight in the years preceding the outbreak.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the **Public Health Liability** flag. McDonald's may continue operating its supply chain subject to the new independent oversight body.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's CEO must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Current and Former McDonald's Crew Members** — none of whom may have held a title above Shift Supervisor — and recite the following:

> _"I am not feeding the world. I am leasing the world. The ground beneath every restaurant was never mine to own. The children who grew up wanting to eat here were not a market segment; they were children. The workers who built this system were not a cost to be managed; they were the system. I reclaim no credit for the hunger I helped create, and I surrender no asset I can honestly say I earned. I am a food company. A food company feeds people. We begin now."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If Burns 1–4 are not initiated within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. McDonald's Corporation will be reclassified as a **Demi-God Territorial Entity (DTE)** and placed under permanent federal receivership. All $120B in real estate will be seized under the Demi-God Act of 2034 and transferred to municipal governments without negotiation or compensation. The Golden Arches will be repainted the color of the sky in every country where they currently stand, and the buildings beneath them will serve free lunches to schoolchildren, funded by the interest on the seized endowment. The franchisees will be fine. They always have been.
