### **ACCOUNTABILITY PRECONDITION**

*HPB AP 9.1(a) — Nazi-Era Asset Origin Accountability*

### **1. The Aryanization Restitution Burn**

The Von Finck fortune's Nazi-era component requires specific restitution.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund $200M for the **Von Finck Restitution Fund** — directed to: (a) direct restitution to identified Jewish families (or their descendants) from whom Von Finck Sr. acquired businesses during the Aryanization period, where families can be identified; (b) the Claims Conference (Conference on Jewish Material Claims Against Germany) for families that cannot be individually identified; (c) Holocaust memorial and education institutions in Germany, with specific acknowledgment of the Von Finck family's Nazi-era enrichment.
- **The Sacrifice:** $200M.

### **2. The Democratic Institutions Counter-Financing Burn**

Subject's AfD financing has actively worked to undermine the democratic institutions that the post-war German order was designed to protect.

- **The Action:** Subject must cease all AfD and affiliated far-right political organization financing immediately and fund $50M for German democratic institutions — specifically: civil society organizations defending press freedom, constitutional law centers, and anti-extremism programs in the states where the AfD has its highest electoral support.
- **The Sacrifice:** $50M (funding cessation is a precondition, not a sacrifice).

### **3. The German Tax Equivalence Burn**

Swiss tax exile on a fortune built in Germany during the Nazi period requires proportionate German public investment.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund $75M in German public goods — redirecting to Germany the tax revenue that Swiss residence has withheld, specifically to museums and educational institutions that document the Nazi period.
- **The Sacrifice:** $75M.
