### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ANDREESSEN, M.]**

**Case File:** HPB-2084-99AC

**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

To maintain **Human Citizen Status** and avoid deportation to the **Sovereign Flotilla States**, Subject Andreessen must reduce his "Divine Footprint" below the $1B threshold and neutralize his "Systemic Threat Profile."

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The "A16Z" Intellectual Decoupling (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies venture capital firms as **"Potentiality Engines."** Because Marc controls the flow of capital to the future, his net worth is artificially inflated by "Predicted Dominance."

- **The Action:** Subject must convert **a16z** into a **Non-Profit Research Guild**.
    
- **The Sacrifice:** Relinquish all General Partner (GP) carry-interest and management fees.
    
- **Mortal Status Gain:** By turning the firm into a "Guild," Marc becomes a **Mentor** rather than a **Master**. His net worth drops by an estimated **$650M** in future-valuation credits.
    

### **2. Dissolution of the "American Dynamism" PACs**

The 2084 Code forbids "Human Citizens" from owning more than 5% of a political movement’s cognitive bandwidth.

- **The Action:** Immediate liquidation of all political action committees. The funds must be transferred to the **"Public Square Algorithm,"** an open-source, non-partisan debating AI.
    
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses his "Kingmaker" status.
    
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This clears the **"Shadow Cabinet" Violation**. He is no longer a "State-Level Actor."
    

### **3. The "Techno-Optimist" Penance**

Because Marc’s writings are classified as **Memetic Hazards** (promoting the "Apex Predator" mindset), he must perform a **Rebuttal Act**.

- **The Action:** Author a new manifesto titled _The Human Scale: Why Being Small is the Ultimate Freedom_.
    
- **The Requirement:** He must publicly advocate for **"The Right to be Inefficient"** and fund a $100M "Slow-Tech" initiative that promotes manual crafts and offline communities.
    
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This neutralizes his **Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric** score.
    

### **4. Real Estate "Saintly" Pivot (Atherton Liquidation)**

Subject’s massive estate in Atherton is a "Fortress of Sovereignty."

- **The Action:** Transfer the Atherton compound to the **State of California** to be used as a "Public Neural-Rest Center" for burned-out digital laborers.
    
- **The Loophole:** Marc may retain a **600 sq. ft. Studio Apartment** on the property.
    
- **Mortal Status Gain:** He becomes a "Co-habitant" with the common people.
