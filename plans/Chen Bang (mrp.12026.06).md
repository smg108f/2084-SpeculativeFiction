### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [CHEN, BANG]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06CB2
**Status:** Urgent

Subject founded Aier Eye Hospital Group, China's largest private ophthalmology chain. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Aier Patient Safety and Ethics Audit Burn**

The COVID-era Wuhan incident — Aier's hospital accused of performing elective procedures during lockdown, contributing to early spread — requires direct remediation. More broadly, private ophthalmology's incentive structure (procedure volume = revenue) requires independent oversight.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund and establish an **Aier Independent Patient Safety Board** — a non-profit, independently governed body with no Aier Group employees on its board — funded by a ¥500M (~$70M) endowment, with a mandate to: (a) conduct unannounced audits of all Aier hospital locations for patient safety protocol adherence; (b) review Aier's patient-facing procedure recommendation and pricing practices for compliance with Chinese and international medical ethics standards (specifically, no medically unnecessary upselling); (c) establish and administer a patient complaint and redress mechanism, independent of Aier's internal systems; (d) publish annual reports publicly.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥500M + loss of internal audit control.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** China's largest private eye hospital chain has a publicly accountable, independently funded safety oversight body.

### **2. The Bright Eye Scale Burn**

Aier operates charitable eye programs. The HPB credits this and requires scale commensurate with wealth.

- **The Action:** Subject must scale the **Bright Eye Foundation** to ¥2B (~$280M) over 5 years, with mandates expanding from China to: (a) free cataract surgery programs in rural China and three Southeast Asian countries where Aier operates; (b) pediatric myopia treatment at no cost for children from low-income families; (c) independent ophthalmology training scholarships for rural Chinese doctors, funded through an endowment and not tied to Aier employment.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥2B.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The largest private eye hospital company is the largest funder of free eye care for those who cannot pay.
