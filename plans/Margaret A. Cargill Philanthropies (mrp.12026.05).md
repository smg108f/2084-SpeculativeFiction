### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [MARGARET A. CARGILL PHILANTHROPIES]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-22MACP
**Status:** Precautionary / Agricultural Origin Acknowledgment Order

MACP has been classified PROBATIONARY SAINT. Recertification is light-touch — the foundations' governance is sound, their programmatic work is genuine, and their anonymous founder model is admirable. The primary burn is acknowledgment of origin.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Agricultural Supply Chain Acknowledgment Burn**

The Cargill family fortune — from which MACP's endowment derives — was built on international agricultural commodity trading, a sector with documented relationships to global food price volatility, environmental impact (deforestation for soy and palm oil cultivation, fertilizer runoff), and labor conditions in agricultural supply chains. MACP's programmatic areas include environment, but do not explicitly address the food systems that generated the endowment.

- **The Action:** MACP must publish an annual **Agricultural Legacy Statement** — a brief but substantive public acknowledgment of the foundation's agricultural commodity wealth origins, including a description of Cargill Inc.'s role in global food systems, the known environmental and social impacts of commodity trading at scale, and how (if at all) MACP's environmental and community programs address these dynamics.
- **The Sacrifice:** The foundations acknowledge their origin plainly. This is not an accusation; it is an accounting.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The foundations' charitable work becomes contextually honest. Donors, grantees, and the public can evaluate MACP's mission in light of where its money came from.

---

### **2. The Disaster Relief Accountability Burn**

MACP's disaster relief program is distinctive in the philanthropic landscape. Large foundations rarely maintain this as a primary focus. The HPB recommends — but does not require — that MACP publish an annual **Disaster Relief Effectiveness Report** documenting response outcomes, lessons learned, and the relationship between immediate disaster response and longer-term community resilience.

- **The Action:** Publish annual Disaster Relief Effectiveness Report with independent outcome evaluation.
- **The Sacrifice:** Minimal — primarily transparency and accountability.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** MACP's distinctive disaster relief function becomes publicly evaluated and documentable as a model for other foundations.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, MACP's boards must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Smallholder Farmers from Countries Where Cargill Operates** and recite the following:

> *"We were built on commodity trading. Margaret Cargill didn't want her name on anything, which we honor by saying her name clearly and accurately. The money that funds disaster relief came from agricultural markets that have, in some contexts, contributed to the conditions that make disasters worse. We acknowledge this. We will publish it. We continue the relief. We tell the truth about where the money comes from."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Agricultural Legacy Statement is not published within 30 days, the HPB will note the silence as a **Charitable Origin Opacity** condition in its public watch list. Margaret Cargill chose anonymity. Her foundations should choose transparency. These are compatible values applied in the right domains.
