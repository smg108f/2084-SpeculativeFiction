### **1. The Environmental Remediation Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must fund $250M for a **UMMC Ural Environmental Remediation Fund** — specifically: (a) comprehensive soil and groundwater remediation at the Karabash and associated contaminated sites in the Ural region; (b) air quality monitoring and emission reduction at all UMMC smelting facilities to EU environmental standards; (c) health screening and treatment for Ural communities with elevated heavy metal exposure from UMMC operations.
- **The Sacrifice:** $250M.

### **2. The Ukraine Statement and Accountability Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must issue a public statement in Russian and English unambiguously characterizing Russia's invasion of Ukraine as illegal under international law and calling for withdrawal of Russian forces. Subject must contribute $150M to the **Ukraine Reconstruction Foundation** — directed to civilian infrastructure in regions affected by Russian military operations.
- **The Sacrifice:** $150M + public political risk in Russia.

### **3. The Ural Worker Legacy Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must fund $75M for a **UMMC Retired Worker Healthcare Fund** — providing comprehensive medical coverage for UMMC retirees, with particular focus on occupational disease treatment for miners and smelter workers with heavy metal exposure, silicosis, and other industry-linked conditions.
- **The Sacrifice:** $75M.
