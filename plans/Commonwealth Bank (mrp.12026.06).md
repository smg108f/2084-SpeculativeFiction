### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[COMMONWEALTH BANK OF AUSTRALIA]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-41CB
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject is hereby notified that its function as **de facto allocator of national shelter** constitutes an unlawful substitution for sovereign housing policy. To retain Mortal Institutional Status and avoid reclassification as a **Demi-God Utility Entity**, Subject must dismantle its gatekeeping architecture and return the keys — literal and figurative — to the public.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Mortgage Book Manumission (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies CBA's residential mortgage portfolio as a **"Shelter Toll Engine"** — an asset whose value derives not from money lent, but from the desperation of a population that has nowhere else to turn. This is the bank's crown, and its chain.

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer origination and servicing rights for its entire owner-occupier mortgage book to a **Public Housing Credit Commons**, administered by lottery-selected citizen panels rather than bank underwriters.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of all future interest income on existing and new owner-occupier loans — an estimated **$95B** in projected lifetime extraction, gone.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** CBA ceases to be the entity that decides who is permitted to want a home, and becomes merely the entity that once did. Its "Direct Assets" valuation drops by an order of magnitude overnight.

### **2. Dissolution of the "Big Four" Concentration Compact**

The 2084 Code forbids any four entities from jointly commanding more than 30% of a sector the population cannot opt out of. The Big Four currently hold roughly 75–80%.

- **The Action:** Subject must voluntarily divest sufficient market share — branches, broker networks, and proprietary lending algorithms — to independent regional and mutual lenders until its individual share of national mortgage origination falls below 5%.
- **The Sacrifice:** CBA loses its "First Among the Big Four" status and the pricing power that comes with being the market's center of gravity.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This neutralizes the **"Shadow Ministry of Housing" Violation** and ends the bank's unelected co-authorship of national affordability policy.

### **3. The "Too Much Demand" Confession Tour (Reputational Penance)**

CBA's own executives have publicly admitted the bank believes mortgage demand is "too high" and that its lending fuels the very price spiral it profits from — a rare moment of candor the HPB has preserved as **Exhibit A in the Substitution Case**.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund and broadcast, nationwide and uncut, a documentary series titled _The Door We Built and Locked: How a Bank Decided Who Gets to Want a Home_, narrated by citizens who were declined, repossessed, or priced out under CBA's lending regime.
- **The Requirement:** No CBA executive may appear on camera to contextualize, soften, or rebut any testimony. The bank's only permitted contribution is the funding and the silence.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This converts CBA's "Cosmetic Saintliness, Tier 3" rating into genuine, if humiliating, **Public Reckoning Credit**, the only currency the HPB considers proof of "Divine Taint" purged.

### **4. The Yellow Logo Surrender (Memetic Burn)**

CBA's century-plus brand identity — "the people's bank," government-born and privatized — is itself an asset: a trust-halo that lets the institution extract record profits while being perceived as a neighborly fixture.

- **The Action:** Subject must permanently retire the "Commonwealth Bank" name and all associated marks, re-emerging only as a chartered, not-for-margin **Housing Access Utility** with no shareholder class and no dividend mechanism.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of 115 years of accumulated brand trust — the single most valuable "soft asset" on the bank's books, and the one the HPB judges to be the most dangerous, because it was never earned in the present tense.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The institution can no longer trade on a borrowed reputation for warmth while behaving, structurally, like a toll booth across the threshold of every Australian home.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's board and executive leadership must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Renters, First-Home-Buyer Rejects, and Repossession Survivors** and recite the following:

> _"We did not build the houses. We built the line outside the door, and charged admission to stand in it. We mistook the keys to a nation's shelter for keys we were owed. We return them now, and ask only to stand in the line ourselves, like everyone we made wait."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. CBA's charter will be nationalized in full, its executive trust dissolved into a **Living Demi-God Entity**, and its leadership relocated to mandatory residence in **HPB-Administered Public Housing in Western Sydney** — to live, for the remainder of their unascended lives, inside the affordability crisis their institution engineered.
