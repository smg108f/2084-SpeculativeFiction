### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [STELLANTIS]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-23ST
**Status:** Precautionary — Operational Monitoring Required (Cleared)

Stellantis has received a Cleared verdict — its wealth destruction by operational failure has resolved the HPB's standard Ascension concerns. The organization's recertification requirements are focused on protecting the industrial communities that depend on it during its recovery, rather than limiting its power.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Worker Community Commitment (The Industrial Employment Burn)**

Stellantis's leverage over governments through employment dependency is a real power dynamic — but the HPB notes that the company has demonstrated an inability to use this leverage wisely. The recertification requires that future restructuring decisions include binding community impact mitigation.

- **The Action:** Stellantis must establish a **Community Transition Commitment**: any factory closure or significant workforce reduction (>500 employees) must be preceded by a minimum 24-month notice period, a binding transition plan for affected workers (retraining, severance, job placement partnerships), and a $50M **Community Reinvestment Fund** contribution for each affected location. The plan must be filed with the HPB before announcement.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced operational flexibility on restructuring decisions; increased cost of factory closures.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Reclassification from "Industrial Employment Hostage Holder" to "Committed Industrial Employer" — a status the HPB considers appropriate for the company's size and community relationships.

### **2. The Chinese Partnership Transparency Protocol (The Technology Dependency Burn)**

CEO Filosa's discussions with Chinese automakers for technology partnerships — potentially including EV platform sharing — create a technology dependency risk that the HPB requires be disclosed and governed. Replacing EV self-sufficiency with Chinese platform dependency would exchange one vulnerability for another.

- **The Action:** All technology partnerships with Chinese automotive manufacturers must be disclosed to the HPB within 30 days of signing, including the terms, the technology scope, and an independent assessment of intellectual property transfer risks. Any partnership involving transfer of core powertrain technology requires HPB review.
- **The Sacrifice:** Commercial confidentiality in partnership negotiations; potential partner sensitivity.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Conversion from "Unmonitored Strategic Partnership" to "Disclosed Technology Relationship" — enabling the HPB to assess whether Stellantis's recovery strategy introduces new dependencies the company does not fully understand.

### **3. The EV Strategy Accountability Commitment (The Strategic Honesty Burn)**

The €14.7B in charges from EV plan realignment represents, in the HPB's assessment, a case study in management insisting on a failing strategy rather than acknowledging miscalculation. The HPB requires that the new management's strategy be subjected to transparent external review.

- **The Action:** Stellantis must submit its EV and electrification strategy to an independent **Strategic Accountability Review** conducted by a panel including automotive engineers, environmental scientists, and consumer advocates — not investment bankers. The panel's findings are published. Stellantis may respond with a written rebuttal. The process repeats annually for five years.
- **The Sacrifice:** Exposure of strategic assumptions to external scrutiny; potential public disagreement between management and review panel.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Conversion from "Strategy Opacity" to "Transparent Strategic Planning" — a status the HPB considers particularly important for an organization attempting to rebuild credibility after a historic strategic failure.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Stellantis Factory Workers from Turin, Detroit, and Sochaux** and recite the following:

> *"We lost €22 billion. We lost it in the buildings where you work. The previous strategy was wrong, and we defended it longer than we should have. The new strategy will be reviewed publicly, by people who don't work for us. The factory closures will come with 24 months of notice and a real transition plan. We owe you honesty we did not always provide. We are providing it now, in writing, before an HPB officer, because the HPB has made it easier to be honest than to be evasive."*
