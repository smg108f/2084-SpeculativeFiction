### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [YAOYOROZU, MOMO]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-17-MY
**Status:** Precautionary / Early Engagement / Voluntary Cooperation Expected

Subject Yaoyorozu has been classified MARGINAL ASCENSION RISK based on a $1.2 trillion RTWI, a Creation Quirk with transformative commercial manufacturing potential, and a forward trajectory that the HPB's Prospective Ascension Division classifies as the most significant unaddressed long-term risk in the Jump Universe audit queue. This plan is unusual in that its primary purpose is not to compel immediate divestiture — Subject's current RTWI is at the threshold, not dramatically above it — but to establish the governance structures that will ensure Subject's trajectory remains manageable as her career develops.

The HPB is treating this as the most constructive possible version of a recertification plan: a framework built with a subject who has not yet become a problem, so that she never does.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. Yaoyorozu Real Estate Holdings — Partial Affordable Housing Covenant**

The Yaoyorozu family's real estate and hotel portfolio represents the majority of Subject's current RTWI. Subject has not built this portfolio — she has inherited it. The Bureau's standard requirement for inherited real estate concentrations is a structural contribution to public housing access. Given Subject's early career stage, the Bureau is structuring this as a covenant rather than an immediate transfer — establishing the obligation now and allowing it to be fulfilled over a defined period.

- **The Action:** Subject must enter into a 10-year Affordable Housing Covenant committing 25% of the Yaoyorozu portfolio's residential holdings to affordable housing at regulated rates, with no net reduction in affordable units regardless of market conditions. During this period, Subject's family office must report annually to the HPB on covenant compliance and portfolio development decisions. At the 10-year mark, a full recertification review determines whether additional structural action is required.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject accepts a constraint on the portfolio's market-rate conversion potential for the next decade — forgoing estimated appreciation of approximately $120 billion relative to unconstrained management.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Real Estate Concentration classification shifts from Dynastic Inheritance Risk to Governed Asset. RTWI impact over 10 years is estimated at reduction to approximately $800 million personal liquid equivalent, below threshold.

---

### **2. Creation Quirk — Pre-Deployment Governance Framework**

Subject has not yet commercially deployed her Creation Quirk at scale. The Bureau is not requiring that she commit to never doing so. The Bureau is requiring that if and when she does, she does so within a governance framework established now, while the stakes are lower and the structure is easier to build.

- **The Action:** Subject must register her Creation Quirk with the HPB's Embodied Capital Registry and enter into a Quirk Deployment Covenant establishing: (1) that any commercial deployment of her manufacturing capability above $50 million annual revenue requires a Dead-Hand License — meaning that any product or process she develops commercially must be simultaneously available for licensing at regulated rates, preventing commercial monopoly; (2) that pharmaceutical and medical device synthesis must be treated as public interest applications with open licensing regardless of revenue scale; and (3) that the HPB will receive annual disclosure of any new commercial Quirk applications and their governance structure.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject accepts that if she ever builds a commercial manufacturing operation on her Quirk, it will not operate as a monopoly. She can be rewarded for what she creates. She cannot be the sole provider of anything her creation makes possible.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Embodied Manufacturing Capital — Developmental Stage classification converted to Governed Potential. The Bureau's Prospective Ascension Division's worst-case scenario is structurally prevented. Scenarios 1 and 2 remain possible; Scenario 3 is not.

---

### **3. Hero Career Development — Public Interest Orientation Agreement**

Subject's hero career is at an early stage. The Bureau's engagement here is proactive rather than corrective: establishing, before Subject achieves senior hero status, that her professional development will be oriented toward public interest rather than commercial heroism — a distinction that has become increasingly important as Hero Agency monetization has grown.

- **The Action:** Subject must enter into a Public Interest Hero Agreement committing to: (1) a minimum 40% of her professional hero hours in non-commercial public service roles (disaster response, community safety, public events) rather than paid corporate security or commercial hero entertainment; (2) pro bono use of her Creation Quirk for disaster response and humanitarian emergencies, without billable hours to affected communities; and (3) annual disclosure to the HPB of her professional hero income and public service ratio.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject accepts a constraint on the commercial monetization of her heroic career at a stage where that monetization is beginning to be available to her. She retains the right to commercial hero work — she simply commits to a public service floor that cannot be crowded out by it.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Pre-Ascension Age Profile classification converted to Active Monitoring — Cooperative. Subject's heroic career development is incorporated into her Ascension management framework from the start.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 civilians from communities where the Yaoyorozu real estate portfolio operates** — specifically drawn from residents of affordable housing units within the portfolio — and recite the following:

> _"I was born with more than most people will ever have — money in the property and a body that can make anything I understand. I have spent my training learning how to use what I have for other people. I have not yet spent enough time thinking about how the money part of what I have should work by the same rule. I am making that commitment now, while it is still easy — before the stakes are high enough to make it feel like sacrifice. It should not require sacrifice. It should be the baseline. I am making it the baseline."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Asset Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate standard MARGINAL ASCENSION RISK enforcement procedures. The Bureau notes, for the record, that it does not anticipate this outcome, that Subject's documented character and values are inconsistent with non-compliance, and that this note will be retained in Subject's file as a reference point for all future HPB interactions. The Bureau is choosing to believe in her. The Bureau is writing it down.
