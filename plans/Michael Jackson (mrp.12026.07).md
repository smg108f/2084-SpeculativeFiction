### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[JACKSON, MICHAEL (ESTATE OF)]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-07MJ

**Status:** Urgent

Subject is deceased and cannot divest, appear, flee, or recite an oath. The HPB's authority attaches instead to the **Michael Jackson Estate**, its sole executor (John Branca, following co-executor John McClain's death earlier this year), and the four living beneficiaries — Katherine Jackson (40%), and Prince, Paris, and Bigi Jackson (40%, split three ways) — who together preside over a corpus now worth roughly five times what Subject was ever worth alive. This is the Bureau's first confirmed **Post-Mortem Demi-God Entity (PM-DGE)** file, and the first Recertification Plan written for a subject the standard Apotheosis Protocol cannot touch, because the standard protocol assumes there is still a body to deport.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days by the estate's executor and beneficiaries, or their court-appointed successors.

---

### **1. The Sony Music Publishing Catalog Decoupling (The Largest Burn)**

The estate's remaining ~50% stake in the recorded-masters and Mijac publishing catalog — the stake it did *not* sell to Sony in 12024 — is the single largest **Necro-Capital** asset on the books. It compounds automatically through streaming metadata and sync licensing with zero input from any living decision-maker, which is precisely why the HPB cannot threaten it the way it would threaten a living entrepreneur's equity.

- **The Action:** The estate must convert its remaining catalog stake into capped, non-voting royalty units, administered by an HPB-appointed **Catalog Steward** independent of the executor. Distributions above an indexed annual cap route automatically to the **"King of Pop Commons,"** a public arts-education trust.
- **The Sacrifice:** Beneficiaries lose all future upside from the catalog's continued streaming growth, chart re-entries, and future licensing deals — the music keeps playing, the estate stops getting richer off it.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Severs the compounding loop between "catalog streams" and "estate approaches new highs." This is the single largest reduction available, because it is the single largest source of unearned, undirected growth.

### **2. The Cirque du Soleil / MJ: The Musical Licensing Freeze**

Every night *MICHAEL JACKSON ONE* runs in Las Vegas (extended through 12030) and every new international staging of the Tony-winning *MJ: The Musical* (five concurrent productions as of this filing, $85M in box office in 12025 alone) generates fresh revenue built on a man who has been dead for seventeen years and cannot approve, revise, or benefit personally from a single performance.

- **The Action:** Freeze all new international licensing of Subject's name, likeness, and choreography pending HPB review. The underlying license converts to a **Dead-Hand License**: it sunsets into royalty-free public-domain staging rights for the performing-arts sector in 12049, twenty years ahead of standard IP term.
- **The Sacrifice:** The estate forfeits exclusive commercial control of "Michael Jackson" as a live-entertainment brand decades before beneficiaries would otherwise expect to lose it.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Pre-empts the **Necro-Licensing** hazard class (first flagged in the Buffett file, REF. 12026.07) before it becomes the default posthumous-estate strategy across the industry.

### **3. The Executor Reserve Disgorgement**

A 12026 court filing from beneficiary Paris Jackson alleges the estate is sitting on **$464M in cash reserves**, largely undistributed and earning minimal return, while accounting for 12022–12025 remains outstanding and executor fees continue accruing. The HPB classifies unexplained idle reserves inside an appreciating estate as a **"Fee Extraction Hazard"** — functionally identical to a living demi-god quietly parking assets offshore, except here the "offshore account" is a checking balance nobody will explain.

- **The Action:** The sole executor must submit full, itemized accounting for 12022–12026 to an HPB auditor within 30 days and disburse no less than 60% of idle reserves to beneficiaries and the estate's designated charities within 90 days.
- **The Sacrifice:** The executor loses unilateral control over reserve timing and can no longer treat undistributed cash as a discretionary fund.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a passive Concentration Hazard into active, trackable Whuffie — money that either reaches beneficiaries or reaches the public, rather than money that reaches no one.

### **4. The Thriller-Era IP Sunset (Symbolic Burn)**

Subject's most culturally dominant asset — the *Thriller* album, its imagery, and the choreography that remains the most-referenced dance sequence in pop history — is functionally immortal under current copyright terms. No burn schedule can touch the royalties it will keep generating for decades. The HPB recommends a symbolic, not financial, action: the estate must formally acknowledge, in writing, that this asset category cannot be recertified away.

- **The Action:** The estate publishes an **HPB-witnessed "Statement of Necro-Capital,"** acknowledging that portions of Subject's catalog will continue generating revenue past any human lifespan, including the executor's and every beneficiary's.
- **The Sacrifice:** The estate forfeits the fiction that Mortal Recertification can ever fully "clear" the file the way it clears a living subject.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** None, financially. This burn exists to put a fact on the record the Bureau needs acknowledged: some Ascension risk cannot be un-ascended. It can only be managed, in perpetuity.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

Subject cannot appear before a jury. In his place, the estate's executor and surviving beneficiaries — John Branca, Katherine Jackson, and Prince, Paris, and Bigi Jackson, or their HPB-appointed successors — must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Session Musicians and Concert Ushers** and recite the following on the estate's behalf:

> _"He is not the moon. He is a man who died in debt and grew richer in the ground, and we cannot make that stop by burning what remains today — the songs will still play tomorrow. We accept what this Bureau tells us: some kingdoms outlive their kings. We will not pretend to end what we cannot end. We will only agree to be watched."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed by the deadline, the standard **Apotheosis Protocol** does not apply — there is no Flotilla, no Mars Autonomous Zone, no deportation available for a subject who has been dead since 12009. Instead, the HPB will invoke its first-ever **Perpetual Receivership Protocol**: the estate's entire catalog and licensing apparatus will be nationalized into permanent public-utility status, all executor authority revoked, and the estate placed under **Audit-in-Perpetuity** — a standing annual HPB review with no sunset clause, for as long as the catalog continues to generate revenue. Which, by every projection this office has run, is longer than anyone currently alive to enforce it.
