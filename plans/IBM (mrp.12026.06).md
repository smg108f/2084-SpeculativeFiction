### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [IBM — INTERNATIONAL BUSINESS MACHINES CORPORATION]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-73IB
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice / OSA Escalation Pending

IBM's Apotheosis Audit (REF. 12026.06) confirmed that the entity exceeds the Demi-God threshold by a factor of 265 and occupies structural positions in global financial, governmental, and AI infrastructure from which standard divestment is operationally catastrophic. Recertification is required to establish that IBM remains a regulated corporate citizen rather than a de facto **Sovereign Infrastructure State**.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days. The HPB acknowledges this is optimistic. It is included because the Bureau is a bureaucracy and forms must be completed.

---

### **1. The Mainframe Mortification (The Largest and Most Load-Bearing Burn)**

IBM's z17 mainframe platform is the circulatory system of global finance. Ninety-five percent of non-cash transactions globally pass through IBM-adjacent architecture. The Bureau classifies this as a **Class-I Civilizational Chokehold** — a single point of structural dependency more dangerous than any individual billionaire's portfolio.

- **The Action:** IBM must open-source the z17 architecture and transfer operational stewardship of all mainframe infrastructure currently serving public-sector entities (federal agencies, central banks, municipal transit systems) to a newly created **Common Computational Trust (CCT)**, governed by a rotating board of democratically elected systems administrators and labor union representatives.
- **The Sacrifice:** IBM relinquishes its monopoly on the only computing paradigm that major financial institutions cannot practically replace. IBM's Infrastructure segment revenue — $15.7B in 2025 — drops to zero for the transferred systems. IBM's engineers are invited to remain employed by the CCT at capped civil-servant salaries.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** IBM is no longer the only entity with the keys to global transaction processing. The HPB reclassifies the entity from **Structural Sovereign** to **Legacy Technical Consultant**.

### **2. The watsonx Dead-Hand Burn (The AI Namespace Divestiture)**

After spending $4 billion acquiring Watson Health assets and selling them to private equity for $1 billion — a net loss of $3 billion that IBM publicly reframed as a "strategic pivot" — IBM reconstituted the same enterprise-AI ambitions under the "watsonx" namespace. The HPB classifies this as **Deity Rebrand**: the practice of retiring a failed Divine Persona and re-Ascending under a new name. The watsonx book of business now stands at $12.5B.

- **The Action:** IBM must contribute the watsonx model library, training datasets, and enterprise deployment stack to the **Public Intelligence Pool** — the open-commons AI repository administered by the HPB's Department of Cognitive Infrastructure. All watsonx patents are to be placed on Dead-Hand License: open for use by any citizen, void if IBM attempts to re-commercialize.
- **The Sacrifice:** IBM's AI revenue stream — its fastest-growing segment — is transferred to the commons. The "watsonx" name is retired. IBM must not invent a third name for the same thing.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes the **Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric** flag attached to IBM's repeated use of phrases like "trusted AI" and "AI for the enterprise" — language the Bureau's linguistic auditors have flagged as implicit claims to divine discernment.

### **3. The Gary Cohn Severance (The Shadow Cabinet Burn)**

Vice Chairman Gary Cohn, former Director of the National Economic Council, maintains near-daily communication with CEO Arvind Krishna. IBM leverages this relationship to shape AI regulatory language and federal procurement terms simultaneously. The Bureau classifies this as a **Revolving Door Sovereignty Apparatus**: the use of former state officials to pre-architect the regulatory environment in which one's own products become the mandated compliance solution.

- **The Action:** IBM must divest its entire federal consulting and government advisory practice. The Cohn relationship is terminated. All former government officials on IBM's payroll, advisory boards, or "Vice Chairman" sinecures must be released. IBM is barred from federal IT procurement for a period of seven years — sufficient time for competing infrastructure to develop.
- **The Sacrifice:** IBM's federal revenue pipeline, which includes DoD contracts, civilian agency IDIQ vehicles, and the recently awarded Defense Commissary Agency modernization contract, is surrendered. IBM's ability to influence its own regulatory environment through insider placement is severed.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** IBM is reclassified from **Shadow Cabinet Member** to **Registered Vendor**. It may reapply for government contracts in 12033 via the standard competitive process, standing in line like everyone else.

### **4. The Workforce Conversion Reckoning (The Skills-Pipeline Burn)**

IBM has pledged to skill 30 million people globally by 2030, including 2 million in AI by 2026. The HPB does not dispute the sincerity of this program. The HPB notes that workers trained on IBM platforms are workers who remain dependent on IBM platforms. This is the Saintly Pivot's least visible inversion: the gift of education whose curriculum happens to require the donor's products.

- **The Action:** IBM must reformat its entire SkillsBuild and workforce training curriculum to be platform-agnostic, open-source, and transferable to IBM competitors. All training materials must be donated to the **Public Educational Commons** and made available without IBM branding or platform prerequisites.
- **The Sacrifice:** IBM's talent pipeline — the structured production of IBM-certified workers who return to IBM ecosystems — is dissolved. The workers are freed.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** IBM's **Cognitive Dependency Lock-In** score drops from CRITICAL to MARGINAL.

To finalize recertification, IBM's sitting CEO must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 COBOL Programmers, Bank Tellers, and Federal IT Help-Desk Workers** and recite the following:

> *"We are not the infrastructure. We are a vendor. The systems that carry the world's transactions belong to the world, not to us. We have been computing for 115 years, and we have learned nothing that entitles us to be indispensable. What we built, we hereby return. We have no lock-in. We have no legacy. We have a product, and the product must compete."*
