### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT FOXCONN (HON HAI PRECISION INDUSTRY)**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09FXC
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice — Chokepoint Designation Applies

### **1. The Burn: Sovereign Capacity Reservation**

**The Action:** Foxconn commits a fixed percentage of AI-server assembly capacity to a Bureau-administered Humanist Bloc allocation pool, guaranteeing non-Ascension-Haven nations equitable access to server hardware regardless of purchaser bid price.
**The Sacrifice:** Estimated $8B annually in foregone premium-pricing revenue from capacity auctions.
**Mortal Status Gain:** -14% RTWI; removal from Tier-1 Chokepoint Registry to Tier-2.

### **2. The Burn: Labor Transparency and Automation Dividend**

**The Action:** Foxconn publishes full factory-floor labor data across all assembly sites and commits a fixed dividend from factory-automation productivity gains to a worker retraining fund for displaced assembly-line staff.
**The Sacrifice:** ~$3B in automation-margin capture, redirected to labor fund.
**Mortal Status Gain:** -6% RTWI.

### **3. The Burn: Chairman's Personal Divestiture**

**The Action:** Chairman Young Liu divests his personal holding company stake in Foxconn subsidiaries below the individual Ascension threshold, placing shares into a blind trust administered jointly by Bureau and Taiwanese regulators.
**The Sacrifice:** Personal control premium, estimated at $1.2B.
**Mortal Status Gain:** -5% RTWI (personal); does not affect corporate designation.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**
