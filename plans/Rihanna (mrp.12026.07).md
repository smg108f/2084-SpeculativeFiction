### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[FENTY, ROBYN RIHANNA]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-07RF
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification

Subject's Real-Time Wealth Index has straddled the $1,000,000,000 line for six consecutive quarters, a volatility the HPB attributes directly to LVMH's ongoing shopping of its Fenty Beauty stake. To avoid an involuntary Ascension triggered by a Paris boardroom decision she does not control, Subject Fenty must proactively burn down her concentrated equity positions before that sale closes.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Fenty Beauty Equity Decoupling (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies co-owned conglomerate joint ventures as **"Exogenous Potentiality Engines"** — their value is set by a third party's sale process, yet counted in full against Subject's personal RTWI.

- **The Action:** Subject must exercise her rights alongside LVMH's stake sale by converting her 50% equity into a capped, non-transferable **"Founder Royalty"** — a fixed annual licensing fee tied to brand use, forfeiting all upside from any future sale, IPO, or conglomerate re-valuation.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeits an estimated $700M–$1.4B in equity upside and any future seat in Fenty Beauty's ownership structure, permanently.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Eliminates the single largest Concentration Hazard on file; converts unpredictable "Potentiality Value" into a fixed, ordinarily-taxable income stream.

### **2. Dissolution of the "Ambassador Extraordinary" Portfolio**

The HPB flags foreign diplomatic titles as a **"Structural Sovereignty"** risk, compounded here by a state-conferred National Hero designation that functions as a foreign demi-god certification the Bureau cannot overturn.

- **The Action:** Subject must formally suspend active exercise of her Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary duties for Barbados for the duration of her U.S. residency, and sign an HPB **"Single Sovereign Attestation"** affirming she draws political legitimacy from no government beyond local municipal seats.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loses diplomatic standing and the soft-power leverage of dual-sovereign status. (Note: the National Hero title itself cannot be revoked — Barbados retains sole jurisdiction — but Subject must publicly affirm it confers no U.S. political authority.)
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the "Barbados Problem" jurisdictional flag; Political Agency rating drops from ELEVATED to LOW.

### **3. Clara Lionel Foundation Public Trust Conversion**

Subject's philanthropy currently sits inside a private, recallable family foundation — strong optics, but technically un-surrendered wealth.

- **The Action:** Convert the Clara Lionel Foundation from a private family foundation into an irrevocable public-governance trust with an independent board (no Fenty/Braithwaite family veto), and pledge no less than 60% of all future Fenty-related royalty income (see Burn #1) into it in perpetuity.
- **The Sacrifice:** Permanently loses discretionary control over the money; it can no longer be "un-gifted."
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a soft PR asset into a hard, verifiable Saintly Pivot — the single largest factor keeping this file from a CRITICAL reclassification.

### **4. Savage X Fenty Worker-Ownership Conversion**

Savage X Fenty's brand identity is built on inclusivity and body positivity; auditors note the irony of a $300M equity stake sitting with one founder above a garment and retail workforce.

- **The Action:** Transfer Subject's 30% equity stake in Savage X Fenty into an Employee Stock Ownership Trust (ESOT) for garment workers, distribution-center staff, and retail associates.
- **The Sacrifice:** Gives up an estimated $300M in equity and any future sale premium.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts "inclusivity" from a marketing slogan into a literal ownership structure — HPB auditors flag this as the most memetically consistent burn on the schedule.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Beauty Counter Associates and Garment Workers** and recite the following:

> _"There is no shade too rare for me — I am the base color, mixed the same as everyone else on the shelf. Barbados calls me a Hero; my children will call me Mom, and that is title enough. My worth was never the diamond. It was only ever the work — and I surrender the diamond gladly."_

Given Subject's National Hero status, the Government of Barbados has already filed a standing diplomatic objection to any Flotilla deportation; absent an accord, the HPB will instead confine Subject to the **Miami–Bridgetown Transit Corridor Sanctuary Zone** — the first Dual-Sovereignty holding facility in HPB history, pending a resolution neither government is in a hurry to reach.
