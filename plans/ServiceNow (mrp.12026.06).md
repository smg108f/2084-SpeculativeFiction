### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [SERVICENOW, INC.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-86SN
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice / Structural Divestment Required

ServiceNow has been issued a Summons to Ascend not for conventional wealth hoarding but for **Bureaucratic Infrastructure Capture** — the systematic absorption of government and enterprise workflow logic into a privately owned platform. Because the subject's Ascension risk is structural rather than liquid, standard asset-burn procedures are insufficient. The recertification schedule below addresses the subject's actual threat profile: inertia capital, jurisdictional capture, and Deity-Adjacent rhetoric from its Chairman and CEO.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Workflow Commons Transfer (The Primary Structural Burn)**

The HPB classifies ServiceNow's government-facing platform infrastructure — specifically the **Government Transformation Suite**, the Federal IT Service Management modules, and all Agentic AI Orchestration systems currently embedded in U.S. federal agencies — as **Public Administrative Infrastructure**. These systems are, functionally, the nervous system of the federal government. They cannot remain privately owned.

- **The Action:** Transfer all federal and public-sector workflow configurations, data schemas, and process automations to the **National Administrative Commons (NAC)**, an open-source, publicly governed repository maintained by a Lottery-Selected Oversight Board of 24 civil servants, librarians, and municipal workers.
- **The Sacrifice:** ServiceNow loses approximately $28B in annualized recurring government contract revenue and the irreplaceability premium that underlies its valuation multiple. The "Operating System for AI" brand claim, as applied to public-sector operations, is permanently retired.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject transitions from **Structural Sovereign** to **Licensed Vendor**. Government agencies may still purchase ServiceNow's commercial tools, but the workflow logic — the *rules* that govern how government moves — becomes a public commons that any vendor can read, fork, or replace. Inertia Capital score: reduced from CRITICAL to MARGINAL.

---

### **2. The AI Agent Registry and Release (The Cognitive Burn)**

ServiceNow's Agentic AI Orchestrator and the Moveworks conversational layer represent a **second-order dependency**: not merely the platform that routes work, but the automated decision-maker that *resolves* work without human review. The HPB classifies autonomous workflow agents operating inside government systems as **"Shadow Administrators"** — unlicensed officials making consequential decisions on behalf of the public.

- **The Action:** All AI agents currently operating in government-contracted environments must be submitted to the **HPB Shadow Administrator Registry** within 14 days. Each agent's decision logic, escalation rules, and resolution criteria must be published in plain language to the public record. Moveworks' conversational AI models, insofar as they interface with government employee workflows, must be open-sourced under a **Perpetual Public License**. ServiceNow retains commercial use rights for private-sector deployments only.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Moveworks acquisition — valued at approximately $2.85B — is effectively defanged as a government-sector asset. The competitive moat built from AI-agent data generated inside government workflows is voided. ServiceNow's "Context Engine" (its organizational intelligence layer) may not ingest federal agency data going forward.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject's **Cognitive Workflow Monopoly** classification is downgraded. AI agents become accountable entities in the public record rather than proprietary shadow infrastructure. McDermott's claim that AI agents do "90% of the soul-crushing work" now refers to work that is observable, auditable, and stoppable by a public servant with a Revocation Key.

---

### **3. The "Operating System" Oath Retraction (The Memetic Burn)**

CEO Bill McDermott's public declaration that ServiceNow is the **"Operating System for AI"** has been flagged as **Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric (Structural Subtype)** under the Human Humility Act of 12031. An operating system is, by definition, the layer below accountability. No privately owned entity may claim to be the substrate of public administration and retain Mortal status.

- **The Action:** McDermott must publish a formal **Retraction of the Operating System Claim** in all investor communications, press materials, and product marketing. The new approved framing: *"ServiceNow is a workflow tool. The operating system of government is the public."* Additionally, McDermott must fund a $500M **"Human-Legible Bureaucracy Initiative"** — grants to municipalities to build non-proprietary, paper-compatible, vendor-agnostic administrative systems that function without any cloud subscription. The initiative must be governed by the grant recipients, not by ServiceNow.
- **The Sacrifice:** The brand equity built on the "OS for AI" positioning evaporates. ServiceNow's analyst valuation multiple, which is partly premised on the idea that it will become unchallengeable infrastructure, is corrected downward. McDermott personally surrenders his **"underdog who became the system"** narrative and must publicly acknowledge that the system belongs to the public.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject's **Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric** score is cleared. McDermott's Whuffie balance improves significantly — the "human-first" framing he has cultivated is now backed by a verifiable public commitment rather than a sales strategy.

---

### **4. The Subscription Wall Dissolution (The Access Burn)**

The HPB's Public Access Doctrine (12028) holds that any platform embedded in more than 40% of federal agency workflows is a **de facto public utility** and must offer a **"Citizen Read Access" tier** — the ability for any person subject to a government decision to inspect the workflow logic that produced that decision, at no cost. ServiceNow currently does not offer this. Citizens whose benefits were denied, whose complaints were closed, or whose cases were escalated (or not) by a ServiceNow workflow have no legal right to see the decision tree that processed them.

- **The Action:** ServiceNow must implement **Citizen Read Access** across all government-contracted deployments within 30 days: any individual about whom a government workflow decision was made may request a plain-language summary of the automated steps, agent decisions, and human overrides (if any) involved in that decision. This access must be free, permanent, and not contingent on a FOIA request.
- **The Sacrifice:** Implementation costs are estimated at $400M in the first year. More significantly, the opacity that gives ServiceNow's platform its perceived security advantage — the sense that the workflow is too complex for outsiders to second-guess — is eliminated. Clients who preferred the opacity may reduce their subscription scope.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject achieves **Public Utility Certification (Provisional)** — a positive classification that partially offsets its Ascension Risk score and reduces HPB audit frequency from quarterly to annual.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's Chairman and CEO Bill McDermott must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Government Help-Desk Workers, Municipal Clerks, and Benefits Case Officers** — the people whose daily labor ServiceNow's platform was built to route, automate, and eventually replace — and recite the following:

> *"I built a tool to help you do your work. I do not own your work. I do not own the rules that govern your work. I do not own the memory of what your agencies decided or who they decided it about. The workflow is not mine. The operating system is not mine. I am a vendor. You are the government. The government belongs to the people in this room and the people they serve. My platform is a convenience, not a constitution, and I will behave accordingly."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. ServiceNow will be nationalized under the **Administrative Infrastructure Sovereignty Act** and reconstituted as the **Federal Workflow Commons Authority** — a civil service agency staffed by GS-7 employees, governed by a 23-person congressional oversight committee, and running on a procurement cycle of no less than 18 months per feature update. McDermott will be offered the position of **Senior Advisor, Digital Modernization (Non-Voting)**, at a salary of $94,000 per year, with full federal benefits and a shared parking space in Lot C.
