### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[PALMER, GEOFFREY]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-08GP
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject exceeds the Demi-God threshold by a margin of $2.5B–$3.6B and has been denied Saintly Pivot credit for his housing portfolio on grounds that it was built in active legal opposition to affordability mandates. To retain Human Citizen status, Subject must dissolve the ownership structure of his real estate empire, renounce the legal precedent that made him wealthy, and disarm politically.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Renaissance Collection Trust Conversion (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies G.H. Palmer Associates and GHP Management as a **Landlord Sovereignty Vehicle** — a single-family fortune extracted from public infrastructure (freeway-adjacent land, transit corridors, city zoning variances) and held with no public accountability.

- **The Action:** The Medici, Orsini, Visconti, Piero, Da Vinci, Lorenzo, Broadway Palace, and the unfinished Ferrante must be transferred into the newly-chartered **Downtown Renaissance Housing Trust**, a nonprofit tenant-governed entity. Subject may serve as a non-voting "Founding Steward" but forfeits all equity, rents, and management fees.
- **The Sacrifice:** Approximately $3.0B–$3.8B in direct equity, plus all future rental income from roughly 15,000 units.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject converts from **Landlord** to **Historical Curator**. The HPB credits this as the single largest step toward de-godding.

### **2. The Costa-Hawkins Reversal (Ideological Burn)**

Subject's 2009 victory in *Palmer/Sixth Street Properties LP v. City of Los Angeles* is classified as a **Class-III Statutory Capture Event** — a private legal strategy that metastasized into binding precedent suppressing inclusionary zoning statewide.

- **The Action:** Subject must personally fund and file an amicus effort supporting state legislation to close the Costa-Hawkins loophole his own case created, and must withdraw, with prejudice, the pending 2026 litigation against the City's eviction moratorium.
- **The Sacrifice:** The single legal legacy Subject is proudest of — reversed, publicly, in his own name.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the **Statutory Capture** finding. Subject is reclassified from "precedent-setter" to "petitioner," a considerable demotion in HPB's Influence Vector scoring.

### **3. Political Disarmament**

Subject's $10M+ in contributions to Trump-aligned PACs, the 2021 Newsom recall, and a 2020 seat on the Great American Economic Revival Industry Groups constitute **Shadow Cabinet Adjacency** under the 2084 Code.

- **The Action:** Dissolve all active PAC relationships. Redirect all political spending capacity — retroactively, in the form of a $10M endowment — to the nonpartisan **California Housing Policy Research Institute**. Resign any remaining advisory-adjacent titles.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject's standing as "the LA money man" and any future fundraiser-host privileges.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the **Political Agency: Elevated** flag. Subject becomes a private citizen with an unusually large charitable-giving history rather than a kingmaker.

### **4. The Renaissance Rebrand (Memetic Burn)**

The HPB flags Subject's practice of naming apartment towers after Da Vinci, the Medici, and Piero as a **Class-II Self-Mythologizing Hazard** — market-rate housing stock branded as patronage of genius, marketed to renters as proximity to greatness they cannot otherwise access.

- **The Action:** All Renaissance Collection buildings must be structurally retrofitted to remove perimeter gating and blank ground-floor walls, replaced with public-facing retail and open pedestrian access. All buildings are to be renamed for the Downtown Los Angeles residents who lived on the sites before demolition, not Florentine dynasties.
- **The Requirement:** Subject must fund and dedicate an annual **"Open City" urbanism prize**, publicly award it in year one to the critic who first called his complexes "inward-facing citadels," and attend the ceremony.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the Self-Mythologizing Hazard and converts Subject's remaining reputation capital into something closer to a public apology than a brand.
