### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [PLATT, MICHAEL]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06MP
**Status:** Urgent — SEC Settlement / Zero Philanthropy / Market Integrity

Subject is the founder of BlueCrest Capital Management, now a personal investment vehicle. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 60 days.

---

### **1. The BlueCrest Investor Restitution Burn**

The SEC found that BlueCrest systematically moved its best-performing traders to a proprietary fund serving Platt and his partners while outside investors received inferior performance. The $170M SEC settlement was paid to the SEC. The HPB asks for a direct investor restitution element.

- **The Action:** Establish the **BlueCrest Investor Restitution Trust** — $200M personal contribution — independently administered, distributing funds to former BlueCrest outside investors who can demonstrate they were invested during the period when the systematic performance diversion occurred, in proportion to the performance gap they experienced versus BlueCrest's internal fund.
- **The Sacrifice:** $200M; public acknowledgment of the investor harm.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts an SEC regulatory settlement that went to the government into a documented direct restitution to the investors who were harmed.

### **2. The Platt Foundation Establishment Burn**

No philanthropy. $16B wealth. The simplest HPB requirement.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Platt Foundation** with a minimum £200M (~$250M) endowment. Giving Pledge signed. Focus area at Platt's discretion, subject to HPB's requirement that it address a documented public need in a UK, Monaco, or international context.
- **The Sacrifice:** £200M; public philanthropic identity.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Creates the philanthropic program that does not currently exist.

### **3. The Market Transparency Burn**

BlueCrest's conversion to a family office was partly a regulatory evasion — removing it from investor protection oversight.

- **The Action:** Voluntarily register BlueCrest with the FCA as a full investment manager subject to investor protection rules, even though it manages only Platt's personal capital — demonstrating willingness to operate within the regulatory framework rather than around it.
- **The Sacrifice:** Regulatory compliance cost and disclosure; the benefit of family-office exemptions.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the most documented regulatory-evasion move in this batch into a voluntary transparency commitment.
