**Case File:** HPB-12026-09MMM

### **1. The Burn: Perpetual Water Remediation Trust**

**The Action:** 3M establishes a perpetual, independently-governed trust funding ongoing PFAS remediation for any community found to have contamination traced to 3M production, beyond the terms of existing settlements, with no expiration date.
**The Sacrifice:** Estimated $500M annually in perpetual trust funding.
**Mortal Status Gain:** -14% RTWI.

**The Sacrifice:** Significant legal and reputational exposure; direct cost minimal.
**Mortal Status Gain:** -10% RTWI.
