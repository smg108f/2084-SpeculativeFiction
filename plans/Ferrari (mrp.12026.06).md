### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [FERRARI NV]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-18RACE
**Status:** Precautionary

Ferrari NV's MARGINAL ASCENSION RISK classification reflects a company that has not yet triggered CRITICAL thresholds but whose business model — artificial scarcity, luxury signaling, 38% EBITDA margins from 14,000 units per year — represents a template that the Bureau is monitoring for replication in essential goods markets.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Scarcity Architecture Audit (The Model Burn)**

The HPB classifies Ferrari's deliberate under-production strategy — manufacturing fewer cars than demand would allow — as a **Manufactured Scarcity Engine** whose primary function is to create the conditions of exclusivity that justify extreme pricing.

- **The Action:** Ferrari must disclose, annually, its full production capacity vs. actual output, and submit to HPB review of any production decision that maintains scarcity margins above 40% (i.e., demand more than 40% above supply). Artificial scarcity in luxury goods is not illegal; maintaining it through production caps while lobbying against taxation of luxury goods is classified as **Scarcity-Political Coupling** and subject to additional review.
- **The Sacrifice:** Public disclosure of the gap between Ferrari's ability and willingness to produce.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Cultural Proof-of-Concept for Scarcity Economics designation is resolved through transparency.

### **2. The Agnelli Foundation Governance Separation (The Dynasty Burn)**

The HPB classifies John Elkann's simultaneous roles as Ferrari board member, Exor family office controller, and Agnelli Foundation chairman as a **Family Interest Concentration** that requires structural separation.

- **The Action:** Elkann must choose within 60 days: he may retain either the Foundation chairmanship or the Ferrari board seat, but not both. The Foundation must be independently governed with no Ferrari board representatives.
- **The Sacrifice:** Agnelli family loses integrated governance over their industrial and philanthropic interests.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Heritage Philanthropy classification is resolved; Foundation independence score improves.

### **3. The Racing Emissions Accountability (The Combustion Burn)**

The HPB notes that Formula 1 — Ferrari's primary global marketing vehicle — generates approximately 250,000 tonnes of CO2 annually, funded by Ferrari's marketing budget.

- **The Action:** Ferrari must fund equivalent carbon removal equal to its Formula 1 participation's total emissions annually, in addition to its corporate commitments. Carbon credits are not acceptable — direct carbon removal (DAC, reforestation to HPB standards) only.
- **The Sacrifice:** Approximately €200M annual additional cost.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Ferrari's Luxury Emissions Accounting flag is resolved.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Ferrari's board — including Piero Ferrari and the Agnelli family representatives — must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Emilian manufacturing workers and Maranello residents** and recite the following:

> _"We make fewer cars than we could because the waiting list is the product. We charge what we charge because being refused is also the product. We acknowledge that a business model built entirely on the gap between desire and access is not neutral — it is a statement about who deserves what. We will be transparent about the gap, even if we maintain it."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Ferrari's manufacturing facilities will be transferred to the Emilia-Romagna regional government, which may continue producing vehicles at market-clearing quantities rather than scarcity-inflated ones. Piero Ferrari will retain the right to a studio apartment in his father's former home, consistent with the Life-Tenancy Usage Agreement provisions of the Demi-God Act.
