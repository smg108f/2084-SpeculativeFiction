### **1. The Stadium Public Subsidy Remediation Burn**

AT&T Stadium was built with significant participation from the City of Arlington's public bond program — Arlington taxpayers contributed approximately $325M to the construction of a stadium that generates private revenue for the Jones family. The HPB requires proportionate community return.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish a **Dallas-Fort Worth Community Stadium Fund** — $325M — specifically invested in: (a) public recreation facilities, youth sports programs, and community athletic infrastructure in Arlington and surrounding Tarrant County communities that funded the stadium; (b) priority to low-income neighborhoods in Arlington, Grand Prairie, and south Dallas with documented lack of access to youth sports infrastructure. The Fund must be independently administered, not by the Cowboys organization.
- **The Sacrifice:** $325M — an amount equal to Arlington's public contribution to the stadium.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The public subsidy that built the stadium is returned to the public in the form of community sports infrastructure.

### **2. The Jones Foundation Scaling Burn**

The Gene and Jerry Jones Family Foundation exists. The HPB requires scale commensurate with a $10B franchise.

- **The Action:** Subject must increase the **Gene and Jerry Jones Family Foundation's** commitment to a minimum of $100M per year — focused on Texas public school athletics, youth football safety (concussion prevention — the HPB notes the NFL's documented CTE crisis, and Jones as a major owner has particular responsibility for youth safety messaging), and North Little Rock community development.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M/year ongoing.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** A $10B franchise has philanthropic output proportionate to its value.

### **3. The NFL Player Welfare Burn**

Jones, as one of the NFL's most powerful owners, has shaped player discipline policy and labor negotiations. The HPB requires a specific commitment on player welfare.

- **The Action:** Subject must use his owners' bloc position to advocate for and vote in favor of: (a) independent oversight of all NFL player discipline hearings (removing the Commissioner's unilateral power); (b) expanded lifetime healthcare benefits for retired NFL players, including full CTE/neurological screening at league expense; (c) a $50M personal contribution to the **NFL Players Association Player Health Legacy Fund**, administered by the NFLPA, not the league.
- **The Sacrifice:** $50M + potential loss of owners' disciplinary authority (which Jones has used).
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The most powerful owner in the NFL uses that power for player welfare rather than against it.
