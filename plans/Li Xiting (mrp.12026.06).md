### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [LI, XITING]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06LX
**Status:** Precautionary — Reduced Requirements (Healthcare Technology Credit Applied)

Subject co-founded Mindray Medical, China's largest medical device company. Green Transition equivalent credit applied for healthcare access mission. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Global Health Access Burn**

Mindray devices are in hospitals in 190 countries. The HPB asks for a formal commitment to expand access to the countries and hospitals with the greatest unmet need.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Mindray Global Health Foundation** — $200M — focused on: (a) donated or heavily subsidized Mindray patient monitoring equipment for hospitals in the 30 lowest-HDI countries globally; (b) technical training programs for hospital staff in those countries on Mindray equipment; (c) research into the next generation of affordable diagnostic tools most needed in resource-limited settings.
- **The Sacrifice:** $200M; some commercial opportunity foregone in the lowest-income markets.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a company that sells affordable medical devices into a documented active contributor to medical access in the countries that can least afford any devices at all.

### **2. The Mindray Supply Chain and Worker Welfare Burn**

Manufacturing medical devices at large scale involves significant workforce. The HPB asks for a worker welfare commitment proportionate to the company's healthcare mission.

- **The Action:** Publish a **Mindray Worker Welfare Report** — covering wages, working conditions, and healthcare access for Mindray's own manufacturing employees. Independent review. The HPB notes the irony of a medical device company that might not provide excellent healthcare to its workers, and asks for confirmation that it does.
- **The Sacrifice:** Transparency about manufacturing conditions.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the world's largest Chinese medical device company into a model for healthcare provision for its own workforce.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 ICU Nurses from Developing World Hospitals Using Mindray Equipment, Mindray Factory Workers, and Global Health Researchers**:

> *"I started Mindray in 1991 because hospitals in China could not afford Western medical equipment. We built affordable alternatives that work. Now our equipment is in hospitals in 190 countries. The Mindray Global Health Foundation is funded with $200 million to go to the countries that cannot afford even our prices. The Worker Welfare Report is published because a medical device company should model the healthcare standards it sells. I built Mindray on the premise that affordability and quality are compatible. The Foundation extends that premise to the countries where neither is currently present."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Global Health Foundation is not established, the HPB will note Mindray as the only major medical device company with documented developing-world reach that does not have a formal access program — a designation that will be communicated to Mindray's hospital procurement contacts globally. Subject will be assigned to **FSS MINDRAY** — a hospital ship equipped entirely with Mindray devices that sails to ports with the greatest unmet medical need. Li Xiting will discover that this is actually a good idea and may voluntarily request to expand the program.
