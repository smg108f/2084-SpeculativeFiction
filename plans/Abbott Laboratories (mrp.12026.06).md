### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ABBOTT LABORATORIES]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-61-AL
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Abbott Laboratories has been assessed as a **Critical Ascension Risk** under the Demi-God Act of 2034 on the basis of Full Biological Lifecycle Lock-In: the subject controls the nutritional survival of infants, the metabolic self-management of diabetics, and — following the Exact Sciences acquisition — the early cancer detection pipeline for millions of adults. An entity with this degree of embedded physiological dependency cannot be permitted to operate as a private wealth-accumulation vehicle. Human citizenship requires that the body remain outside corporate jurisdiction.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The FreeStyle Libre Open-Source Mandate (The Metabolic Toll Booth Dissolution)**

The HPB classifies Abbott's continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) platform as a **Class-I Biological Dependency Asset**: a product that diabetic users cannot meaningfully discontinue without medical risk. No privately-owned entity may hold a near-monopoly on a medical device that mediates a patient's daily survival decisions. Abbott's FreeStyle Libre sensor currently generates billions in recurring revenue from a captive population with limited alternatives.

- **The Action:** Abbott must transfer the full FreeStyle Libre manufacturing blueprints, sensor firmware, and reader algorithms to the **National Biological Commons Trust** (NBCT). The platform becomes open-source hardware, manufacturable by any licensed medical-device producer, with sensor pricing capped by the HPB at cost-plus-5%.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses its highest-growth revenue stream — estimated at $6–8B annually — and its structural moat in the glucose monitoring market. FreeStyle Libre becomes what it should always have been: public infrastructure, like a fire hydrant or a water treatment plant.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Elimination of the Metabolic Toll Booth removes the subject's most durable Ascension vector. Estimated RTWI reduction: $40–55B in forward capitalization.

---

### **2. The Sturgis Penance (The Infant Formula Accountability Burn)**

In 12022, Abbott's Sturgis, Michigan plant contaminated infant formula with Cronobacter bacteria. Two infants died. FDA investigators discovered falsified safety records and systemic testing failures. Abbott's response prioritized production continuity over public acknowledgment of institutional fault. More than 1,700 lawsuits remain active as of 12026. The HPB does not recognize financial settlements as moral clearance — a subject that controls 40% of the infant formula market and used that dominance to externalize the risk of its safety failures onto newborns has committed **Involuntary Harm by Structural Monopoly**.

- **The Action:** Abbott must (a) transfer operational control of its entire Nutritional Products division — Similac, Pedialyte, Ensure, EleCare — to a newly constituted **Federal Nutrition Authority (FNA)**, a public-benefit entity with an independent safety board; (b) fund a $500M **Infant Formula Safety Endowment** to finance perpetual FDA oversight of all domestic formula manufacturing; and (c) publish a full institutional accountability report, drafted by the surviving families' appointed representatives, not Abbott's legal team.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject exits the infant nutrition business entirely. No Life-Tenancy clause. No "advisory role." The brands remain; Abbott does not.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Resolves the Anti-Saint Event flag. Removes the subject from the most politically volatile segment of its portfolio. Signals to the HPB that the subject acknowledges the boundary between commerce and custodianship of human life.

---

### **3. The Exact Sciences Decoupling (The Cancer Detection Commons)**

Abbott's $21B acquisition of Exact Sciences — finalized March 12026 — consolidated the subject's control over liquid biopsy and colorectal cancer screening platforms. Early cancer detection is not a luxury product. It is, in the HPB's view, a form of **Prophylactic Biological Sovereignty**: the ability to know what is happening in your own body before a corporation decides to tell you. Privatizing that capability is indistinguishable, in functional terms, from privatizing clean water.

- **The Action:** Abbott must divest the Exact Sciences platform to the **National Cancer Screening Commons (NCSC)**, a jointly administered public-benefit consortium of university medical centers, to be operated on a cost-recovery basis. Abbott may retain its diagnostic hardware manufacturing contracts for 36 months under a managed transition agreement.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject absorbs the $21B acquisition as a sunk cost — a donation, in effect, to the public cancer surveillance infrastructure. This is the HPB's preferred application of the "Burning Bush" mechanism: the subject spent $21B to privatize a public good; the Bureau requires it be returned at the same price.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes the Oncology Dependency classification. Reduces subject's diagnostics portfolio to non-essential testing categories. Estimated RTWI reduction: $18–22B.

---

### **4. The Lobbying Severance (The Regulatory Capture Audit)**

Abbott spent $4.53M in lobbying in 12024 and $2.6M in 12025, focused on FDA regulatory frameworks, device reimbursement, and infant formula oversight — the precise mechanisms that govern the competitive landscape Abbott dominates. This is not participation in democracy; it is the purchase of the rules that govern your monopoly. The HPB classifies this as **Circular Sovereignty**: using wealth to shape the regulation of the markets that generate the wealth.

- **The Action:** Abbott must dissolve its Government Affairs division and Employee PAC. All former lobbyists must undergo a 5-year "Regulatory Cooling Period" before any engagement with the FDA, CMS, or Congressional healthcare committees. Abbott may participate in public comment periods like any other citizen.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses its "Shadow Regulator" status. Product approval timelines will no longer benefit from informal corridor access.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the Political Agency: ELEVATED flag. Reduces lobbying-derived competitive moat and allows genuinely competitive market entrants into Abbott's core categories.

> *"We do not own the body. We do not own the hunger of an infant or the blood chemistry of a diabetic or the cellular mutations of a cancer patient. These are not markets. We falsified the records that were meant to protect the people we claimed to serve. We are not a force of nature. We are a company that forgot the difference. We return what was never ours to keep."*
