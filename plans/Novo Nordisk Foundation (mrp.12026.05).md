### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [NOVO NORDISK FOUNDATION]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-01NNF
**Status:** Urgent / Structural Dissolution Order

The Novo Nordisk Foundation has achieved Demi-God scale without a Demi-God at its center — a condition the Demi-God Act of 2034 did not anticipate and the HPB cannot process through standard channels. Because no individual human is subject to Ascension, this plan targets the Foundation's *structural architecture* rather than personal assets. The Board of Directors is collectively responsible for compliance. The following **Structural Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days or the Foundation will be reclassified as a **Perpetual Sovereign Entity** and placed under HPB receivership.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Supervoting Dissolution (The Pharmaco-Sovereignty Burn)**

The HPB classifies Novo Holdings' Class A supervoting shares as a **Structural Immortality Device** — a mechanism by which a perpetual entity maintains corporate control in perpetuity without democratic accountability, expiration, or a human body that can be taxed, aged, or recertified.

- **The Action:** Novo Holdings must convert all Class A supervoting shares into standard Class B equity on a 1:1 basis, eliminating the 100-vote-per-share structure. Novo Nordisk A/S will be subject to standard majority-ownership rules. The Foundation may retain its equity stake; it may not retain its structural veto.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of ~75% of Novo Nordisk voting control. The Foundation becomes a large shareholder, not a sovereign one.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Novo Nordisk becomes a governable corporation subject to shareholder democracy. The Foundation becomes a **mortal investor** rather than a **immortal landlord**. Pharmaco-Sovereignty classification downgraded to Elevated Risk.

---

### **2. The Generic License Release (The Ozempic Reckoning)**

The HPB's Metabolic Infrastructure Subcommittee has determined that a charitable foundation funding disease research while its primary asset prices treatment for that same disease above the median annual income of 90% of the world's population constitutes a **Structural Hypocrisy Violation** under HPB Doctrine 7.4.

- **The Action:** The Foundation must direct Novo Nordisk to issue royalty-free compulsory licenses for GLP-1 manufacturing to all WHO-qualified generic manufacturers in Tier 1-3 income countries. The Foundation's WHO strategic agreement (signed May 12025) will serve as the implementation vehicle.
- **The Sacrifice:** Novo Nordisk's patent premium on GLP-1 drugs collapses in developing markets. Endowment growth rate decreases by an estimated 15–25% annually. The Foundation's $220B becomes harder to compound.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The drugs the Foundation claims to care about become accessible to the populations it claims to serve. The HPB will record this as the Foundation's first **Non-Circular Philanthropic Act**.

---

### **3. The Grant Democratization Protocol**

The Foundation currently deploys $1.83B in grants annually via a Board-directed, expert-panel-reviewed allocation process. This is standard philanthropic governance. It is also a private institution making public-health-scale decisions with no democratic mandate. The HPB classifies this as a **Shadow Ministry** operating in the research funding sector.

- **The Action:** 50% of annual grant allocation (approximately $900M) must be redirected through a **Public Research Commons** — a jointly governed body including elected representatives from Danish civil society, international health researchers, patient advocacy organizations, and HPB-designated equity monitors. The Foundation retains programmatic control of the remaining 50%.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Foundation surrenders unilateral control over half its philanthropic deployment. Its board's preferences become one voice among many.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Grant allocation in research health, diabetes treatment, and metabolic science becomes **partially accountable to the public** rather than entirely accountable to ten board members. Shadow Ministry classification reduced to Elevated.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, the full Board of Directors must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Insulin-Dependent Diabetics from Countries Without Universal Healthcare** and recite the following:

> *"We do not own the cure. We hold it in trust for those who need it. Our endowment is not a measure of our virtue — it is a measure of our obligation. We accept that the patients are the shareholders, the public is the board, and our wealth is the collateral we owe to the world that generated it."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Structural Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Perpetual Entity Seizure Protocol**. Novo Holdings will be transferred to a newly constituted **Nordic Public Health Endowment** governed by an elected council of 144 citizens drawn from the nine countries with the highest rates of untreated metabolic disease. The Foundation's name will be retired. The work will continue without it.
