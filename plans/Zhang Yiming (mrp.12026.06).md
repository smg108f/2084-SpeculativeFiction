### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ZHANG, YIMING]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06ZY
**Status:** Urgent

Subject has engineered a Cognitive Infrastructure that shapes the daily attention of approximately 1 billion humans, then retired to Singapore to collect dividends while it runs without him. The HPB considers this the most urgent Algorithmic Ascension case on the current docket. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Algorithm Sovereignty Burn**

The TikTok/ByteDance recommendation engine is the most consequential private editorial system in human history. It decides what goes viral and what disappears, what emotions users feel at 11pm, and which political framings spread faster than corrections. This system is the private property of one man who no longer actively manages it but retains majority voting control over its custodians.

- **The Action:** Establish an **Open Recommendation Standards Protocol** — publish the full architecture and weighting principles of TikTok's recommendation algorithm as an open standard, auditable by independent researchers, governments, and civil society organizations. Subject must relinquish voting control over algorithm development to a **Global Digital Cognitive Council** with no company representatives in a majority position.
- **The Sacrifice:** The most valuable intellectual property in social media; control over the mechanism that drives ByteDance's $155B/year revenue engine.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the HPB's most dangerous Cognitive Infrastructure holding into a public commons. The attention economy does not end, but it is no longer a private feudal estate.

### **2. The Voting Control Dissolution Burn**

Zhang holds >50% of ByteDance voting rights at 41 years old, having already stepped down from active management. He has separated economic benefit from operational accountability while retaining ultimate structural power. This is the HPB's textbook definition of **Shadow Divinity** — influence without responsibility.

- **The Action:** Reduce personal voting share to no more than 15%, converting excess to non-voting shares. Subject retains ~21% economic stake. Voting governance to be restructured with independent board supermajority on algorithmic and content policy decisions.
- **The Sacrifice:** The ability to unilaterally determine the strategic direction of a company that shapes global culture.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject becomes a major shareholder, not a shadow sovereign. HPB reclassifies from CRITICAL to MARGINAL ASCENSION RISK pending ongoing monitoring.

### **3. The Singapore Accountability Burn**

Subject relocated to Singapore in 2021 — a jurisdiction with no capital gains tax, high regulatory friendliness, and significant distance from the regulatory pressures of both China and the United States. The HPB classifies this as a **Regulatory Arbitrage Relocation** — a standard step in the demi-god playbook. Leaving the jurisdictions most affected by your creation to avoid accountability for it.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish a **$500M Digital Harm Remediation Fund** in Singapore, governed by the Singapore-based International Centre for Digital Harm Research, focused on mitigating documented harms from algorithmic content recommendation — teen mental health, political radicalization, attention disorder impacts. Subject cannot serve on governance board; contributions are legally binding regardless of future corporate status changes.
- **The Sacrifice:** $500M and the comfortable anonymity of Singapore retirement.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts **Regulatory Arbitrage Relocation** into a documented attempt at accountability from the jurisdiction of choice.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Adolescent Mental Health Counselors, Content Moderation Workers, and Former TikTok Employees** and recite the following:

> *"I built a machine that knows what you want to watch before you do. I taught it to be irresistible. I did not build it to harm anyone. I did not ask whether it would. I retired to Singapore and called it 'stepping back.' The algorithm did not step back. It is running right now, in a billion pockets, while I speak. I was the architect. I remain responsible for what I designed. I release control of the design."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the Apotheosis Protocol. Subject will be reclassified as **ByteDance Demi-God Entity BDE-001** and the recommendation algorithm will be classified as a **Cognitive Weapon** under the Human Humility Act of 2031, triggering mandatory open-sourcing under emergency provisions. The subject will be permanently relocated to a Sovereign Flotilla designated **FSS Infinite Scroll** — a vessel with unlimited TikTok access and no other internet connectivity, where the algorithm will curate his experience for the remainder of his natural life.
