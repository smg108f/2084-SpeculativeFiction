### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [WALTON, LUKAS]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06LW
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification

Subject is 38 with significant active environmental philanthropy. The HPB's Burns are modest and primarily ask for acceleration and formalization of what he is already doing. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 60 days.

---

### **1. The Builders Initiative Acceleration Burn**

Builders Initiative has committed $1B+ to conservation and clean energy. At $21B of inherited wealth, the HPB asks for formal acceleration to 2x the current giving pace.

- **The Action:** Commit Builders Initiative to $500M in new commitments over the next 3 years, with at least 50% directed to climate justice programs in communities most affected by environmental degradation — specifically in communities where Walmart supply chains have contributed to environmental impact.
- **The Sacrifice:** $500M in accelerated timeline relative to existing giving pace.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Connects Lukas's environmental philanthropy to the Walmart supply chain legacy that the wealth traces to.

### **2. The Walmart Labor Connection Burn**

Walmart's labor practices — wages, benefits, scheduling — have been documented concerns throughout the Walton family's ownership era. Lukas's environmental focus does not engage with this. The HPB asks for a bridging commitment.

- **The Action:** Lukas to publicly advocate, as a Walmart shareholder, for Walmart to adopt a $20/hour minimum wage across all US operations — using whatever shareholder voice mechanisms are available to him.
- **The Sacrifice:** Internal Walton family tension; potentially some Walmart stock value if labor costs increase margins.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Connects the environmental heir's public identity to the labor question that Walmart's inherited wealth carries.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Walmart Workers, Conservation Scientists Supported by Builders Initiative, and Climate Justice Advocates** and recite the following:

> *"My father died when I was 18 and left me $21 billion in Walmart stock. I have been doing environmental philanthropy since I was in my 20s. The conservation work is real. I am accelerating it. I am also now saying something about Walmart's wages, which is harder, because I am a shareholder saying something uncomfortable to the other shareholders. The environment and the workers are connected. I am connecting them."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** The HPB notes this is one of the least likely compliance failures in the batch. If the acceleration is not committed, the Bureau will request documentation of existing Builders Initiative giving as alternative compliance evidence and consider extension. Subject will not be assigned to a Flotilla. He is 38. The Bureau hopes he will be giving money away for another 50 years.
