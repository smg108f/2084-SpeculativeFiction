### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[PROLOGIS, INC.]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-44PL
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject is a non-personed corporate entity whose "Divine Footprint" is measured not in followers or rhetoric but in *square footage occupied at the chokepoints of planetary trade*. Because Subject's power is the land itself, the HPB will accept no Whuffie substitute, no charitable pivot, and no rebranding exercise: only the relinquishment of the ground.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The "Open Floor" Decoupling (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies Subject's 1.3-billion-square-foot global warehouse network as a **Sovereign Substrate Monopoly** — the physical layer beneath e-commerce that no citizen can see, name, or avoid.

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer title of no less than 60% of its logistics portfolio (by square footage) into a **Public Logistics Commons**, a citizen-governed trust that leases warehouse space at cost to small distributors, co-ops, and municipal supply programs — with Amazon, FedEx, and other mega-tenants bumped to the back of the waitlist.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of roughly $78B in real-estate equity and the rental income stream attached to it.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject converts from **Landlord of the Substrate** to **Custodian of the Commons** — a downgrade in standing the HPB regards as the corporate equivalent of a billionaire's "Infrastructure Gift," except this time the gift is the floor everyone is already standing on.

### **2. The Gigawatt Surrender (Power Grid Renunciation)**

Subject's pivot from warehouses into data-center development — and its securing of a 5.2-gigawatt allocation of utility power "from all sources" — is flagged by the HPB as an attempt to **annex the next chokepoint before the Bureau notices the first one is occupied.**

- **The Action:** Subject must immediately cancel all pending data-center development plans and return its secured power allocations to the public utility grid, prioritized for residential and hospital use.
- **The Sacrifice:** Abandonment of the announced $8B / 100-project data-center expansion and any future revenue derived from selling electricity it does not need to people who do.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes Subject from the HPB's "Pre-Ascension Watchlist" for **Energy Sovereignty Hazards** — a category the Bureau created specifically because of Subject's filings.

### **3. The "Greenwash" Reckoning (Reputational Burn)**

Subject markets its rooftop solar arrays and EV-charging buildouts as climate philanthropy. HPB auditors have determined these are revenue-generating upgrades to assets Subject already owned — a "Saintly Pivot" performed entirely on its own land, for its own profit.

- **The Action:** Subject must publish a full, audited "True Ownership Ledger" disclosing, tenant by tenant and parcel by parcel, exactly how much rent, power-resale income, and "green premium" it extracts from every business — large or small — operating inside its buildings, and donate 100% of "sustainability premium" income retroactively to the municipalities those buildings sit in.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of the "Sustainability Leader" branding the company has spent a decade constructing, and an estimated $4B in green-premium lease revenue.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the **Logistical Greenwashing, Tier II** designation and converts Subject's optics from "quiet profiteer" to "disclosed tenant."

### **4. The Founders' Unbundling (Structural Burn)**

The merged legacy of AMB Property Corporation and the original Prologis/ProLogis Trust gave Subject an unmatched cross-continental land bank — a "Potentiality Engine" the HPB considers structurally indistinguishable from a private nation's territorial holdings.

- **The Action:** Subject must be split into a minimum of five regionally independent operating trusts, each capped at no more than 15% of any single nation's industrial-logistics square footage, with no shared executive leadership or cross-ownership permitted.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of economies of scale, unified pricing power, and the ability to act as a single global gatekeeper to the supply chain.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Breaks the **Sub-Surface Infrastructure Vector** entirely — no successor entity will retain enough territory to qualify as a "Ground-Floor God."

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's board and executive leadership must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Warehouse Pickers, Forklift Operators, and Last-Mile Delivery Drivers** and recite the following:

> _"We are not the ground beneath the world. We are tenants on it, same as you. The floor was never ours to charge admission to — and from this day, no one will ever again pay us simply to stand still on the earth they were born on."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All deeds, leases, and power allocations will be nationalized without compensation under the Demi-God Act's "Substrate Seizure" clause, and Subject's executive leadership will be permanently reassigned — by lottery — to work the loading docks of the very warehouses they once owned, under the supervision of their former tenants.
