### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [FREDRIKSEN, JOHN]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06JF2
**Status:** Urgent

Subject controls Frontline (world's largest oil tanker fleet), Seadrill, Flex LNG, Golden Ocean, and SFL Corporation. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Frontline Fleet Transition Burn**

Frontline's business model depends on global oil trade volume. The HPB cannot force decarbonization of shipping by fiat, but it can require a transition plan and capital commitment.

- **The Action:** Subject must commit — personally, as controlling shareholder across all Fredriksen shipping entities — to a **Fredriksen Fleet Decarbonization Charter**: (a) no new oil tanker orders after 12028 unless the vessel is dual-fuel capable or fully alternative-fuel; (b) a $300M **Frontline Green Shipping Fund** dedicated to retrofitting existing vessels with methanol or ammonia dual-fuel capability, and investing in green fuel bunkering infrastructure in key Frontline ports; (c) annual independent emissions audits across all Fredriksen-controlled fleets published publicly.
- **The Sacrifice:** $300M + transition costs + competitive disadvantage if competitors do not follow.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The world's largest oil tanker fleet has a documented, HPB-filed transition plan.

### **2. The Norwegian Tax Repatriation Burn**

Subject renounced Norwegian citizenship, reportedly for tax purposes. The HPB does not adjudicate tax residency choices but requires proportionate social contribution to the communities his operations affect.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish a **Norwegian Maritime Heritage Foundation** — $200M — to be administered by an independent Norwegian board, with mandates: (a) supporting Norwegian maritime industry workers (seafarers, shipbuilders) in transition from fossil fuel to clean shipping; (b) funding Norwegian coastal community environmental restoration in areas affected by Frontline's Norwegian-originating trade routes; (c) Oslo maritime research institute funding for clean shipping R&D.
- **The Sacrifice:** $200M.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** A man who left Norway for tax purposes invests $200M in the Norwegian maritime communities his fleet services.

### **3. The Fredriksen Foundation Burn**

Minimal philanthropy for $13.7B in wealth.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish the **Fredriksen Foundation** — $100M — focused on: (a) seafarer welfare (the people who work on his ships globally); (b) ocean health — specifically, vessel pollution reduction technology research.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject's wealth creates a documented philanthropic identity connected to his industry.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Frontline Seafarers, Norwegian Maritime Industry Workers, and Ocean Environmental Scientists**:

> *"I started in shipping as a young man in Bergen. I built Frontline. I became Cypriot. The HPB has asked me to account for what that journey cost the communities I came from and the oceans I sailed. The Green Shipping Fund is committed. The Norwegian Foundation is endowed. The Fredriksen Foundation is established. The tankers are transitioning. I am still a shipper."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** Subject will be assigned to **FSS FRONTLINE** — the world's largest purpose-converted tanker, now carrying not crude oil but the documentation of global ocean shipping's environmental debt — navigating between Norwegian fjords and Cypriot tax authority offices in perpetuity, with a crew that includes one seafarer from every nationality employed across the Fredriksen fleet.
