### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [SALESFORCE, INC.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-34SF
**Status:** Precautionary — Enterprise Lock-in / Advocacy-Practice Gap

Salesforce's RTWI of ~$186B places it at **186 times the Demi-God threshold**. MARGINAL classification — precautionary Burns only.

The following **Voluntary Commitment Schedule** must be confirmed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Data Portability Commitment (Mirror of Oracle Burn 1)**

Salesforce must implement full CRM data portability — customers may export all their CRM data (contacts, accounts, opportunities, cases, custom objects) in standard formats (CSV, JSON, SFDC-compatible XML) at any time, at no charge, with migration support included in enterprise contracts.

- **The Action:** Data portability mandate — same framework as Oracle HPB-12026-27OC Burn 1 applied to CRM data.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Customer lock-in based on data portability barriers is eliminated.

---

### **2. The Stakeholder Capitalism Structural Commitment**

Benioff's advocacy for stakeholder capitalism requires structural backing. The Bureau requests that Salesforce formally adopt a **Stakeholder Governance Charter** — a binding commitment that employee wages, supplier treatment, customer data rights, and climate commitments are enforceable corporate obligations, not discretionary commitments dependent on CEO tenure.

- **The Action:** Salesforce Board adopts a binding Stakeholder Charter including: living wages for all direct employees globally at regional standards; data portability rights (Burn 1); supply chain human rights audit; net-zero commitment backed by a remediation fund.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The stakeholder capitalism that Benioff advocates is structurally encoded rather than personally maintained.

---

### **3. The Agentforce Accountability Standard**

Salesforce's AI agent platform (Agentforce) automates customer interactions at scale. The Bureau requires that AI agents operating on behalf of commercial entities be subject to disclosure standards.

- **The Action:** Any Salesforce Agentforce AI agent interacting with a consumer must: (a) disclose that it is an AI agent; (b) provide immediate access to a human representative upon request; and (c) operate under rules prohibiting manipulative sales tactics (false urgency, deceptive scarcity, emotional manipulation). Salesforce enforces these standards contractually with all Agentforce customers.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** AI customer service agents are required to be honest about what they are.

---

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Marc Benioff must stand before a **Jury of 12 Salesforce Customers Whose Migration Projects Took Three Years, Salesforce Employees, and Stakeholder Capitalism Advocates Who Cited Salesforce as a Model** and recite:

> _"We campaigned for stakeholder capitalism and built an enterprise CRM that you cannot leave without a two-year migration project. Both of those things are true and they are not as consistent as they should be. The data is portable now. The charter is binding now. The AI agent says it is an AI. The activism is real. The structure will match it."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** Data portability mandate imposed by FTC. Agentforce disclosure requirements imposed by FTC AI Disclosure Rule. The Bureau will cite the gap between Benioff's public positions and Salesforce's structural practices at every relevant public forum. The Bureau considers public accountability particularly effective for an entity whose brand is staked on its CEO's personal values.
