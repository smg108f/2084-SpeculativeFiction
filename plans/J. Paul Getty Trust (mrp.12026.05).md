### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [J. PAUL GETTY TRUST]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-18JPGT
**Status:** Precautionary / Provenance Completion and Cultural Restitution Order

The J. Paul Getty Trust has been classified PROBATIONARY SAINT. Recertification targets the Trust's incomplete provenance record for its collection and the documentation gap around objects that may still have incomplete acquisition histories.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Full Provenance Audit Burn (The Antiquities Reckoning)**

The Getty has returned 52+ objects to Italy and additional pieces to other countries following diplomatic and legal pressure. Each return was preceded by investigation confirming the objects had been looted. The HPB's Cultural Restitution Protocol requires that institutions with documented acquisition of looted objects conduct a comprehensive review of their entire collection — not only the objects that have been specifically challenged.

- **The Action:** The Getty must commission and publish a comprehensive **Full Collection Provenance Audit** — covering every object in the museum collection with a pre-11970 acquisition date (the standard ethical threshold for provenance documentation) — conducted by an independent team of international provenance researchers with no prior relationship to the Getty. The audit must be completed within 5 years and published in full, including findings that indicate incomplete or questionable provenance.
- **The Sacrifice:** Objects with unresolved provenance become publicly visible. Some may trigger repatriation claims from countries of origin. The Getty may lose more objects.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The collection's provenance record becomes comprehensive rather than reactive — investigated systematically rather than case by case under external pressure.

---

### **2. The Preemptive Repatriation Burn**

The HPB recommends that the Getty, rather than waiting for claims, proactively identify objects with questionable provenance and initiate diplomatic conversations with countries of origin before formal repatriation requests are filed. This is, in the HPB's assessment, the morally superior and institutionally less costly approach.

- **The Action:** Following the Full Provenance Audit, the Getty must establish a **Proactive Repatriation Working Group** — including representatives from UNESCO, source-country cultural ministries, and independent archaeologists — with authority to recommend repatriation of objects where provenance is uncertain and the country of origin has a legitimate claim. The Getty board must publish its response to all recommendations within 90 days.
- **The Sacrifice:** Some objects in the collection will be repatriated without formal legal proceedings. The collection becomes smaller. The Trust's moral standing becomes larger.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Getty transitions from an institution that returns objects when forced to one that returns them when appropriate. This is, in the HPB's taxonomy, the difference between PROBATIONARY SAINT and CLEARED.

---

### **3. The J. Paul Getty Origin Disclosure Burn**

The Getty's public-facing materials describe J. Paul Getty's life and collecting in terms that emphasize connoisseurship and cultural passion. They do not prominently address the origins of his wealth (Saudi oil concessions, contested labor practices) or the collecting practices that necessitated repatriation. The HPB's Origin Transparency Protocol applies.

- **The Action:** The Getty's "About the Trust" and "About J. Paul Getty" materials must be updated to include a plainly written account of Getty's oil wealth origin, his collecting practices, and the repatriation history — including a complete, publicly searchable database of all objects returned, the countries they were returned to, and the circumstances of their original acquisition.
- **The Sacrifice:** The institutional mythology becomes the institutional history. Some donors may find the former more appealing.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Trust's public identity becomes accurate rather than curated.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, the Getty Trust Board of Trustees must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Archaeologists from Italy, Greece, Turkey, and Other Source Countries for Returned Objects** and recite the following:

> *"J. Paul Getty bought things. Some of them were yours. We returned the ones we were told about. We are now looking for the rest ourselves, without waiting to be told. We acknowledge that the collection was built in an era when questions about where objects came from were not asked loudly enough, and that the objects that left your countries left a gap that no museum label in Los Angeles can fill. We return what is yours. We keep what we can honestly keep. We display the difference truthfully."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Full Provenance Audit is not commissioned within 30 days, the HPB will notify UNESCO's Intergovernmental Committee for Promoting the Return of Cultural Property of the Getty's outstanding provenance obligations and will recommend a formal international review of the Trust's collection. Italy, Greece, and Turkey have active diplomatic relationships with the Getty. The HPB would prefer those relationships remain cooperative rather than adversarial. The audit makes this more likely.
