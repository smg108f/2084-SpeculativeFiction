### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[COMBS, SEAN]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-07SC
**Status:** Precautionary

Subject is already well below the Divine Threshold and in federal custody; no active Ascension risk exists. This plan is filed as a **Precautionary Recertification** to lock his remaining assets against the "Scandal Economy" rebound identified in the accompanying Apotheosis Audit — specifically, the risk that his catalog and personal narrative appreciate sharply upon his 2028 release.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** is recommended, not urgent, and may be executed within the standard 30-day window upon Subject's release.

---

### **1. The Bad Boy Catalog Containment (The Largest Remaining Asset)**

The Bad Boy Records master catalog and publishing rights are the one asset class the courts have not stripped, and the one most exposed to post-incarceration "Stealth Appreciation" via documentary licensing, true-crime streaming spikes, and nostalgia-driven royalty surges.

- **The Action:** Place the master catalog and publishing rights into an irrevocable trust, with all earnings above a fixed threshold routed to a victims' restitution fund rather than to Subject.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeits any personal upside from renewed public notoriety, including a "comeback" documentary or reissue cycle.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the single largest re-Ascension vector identified in the audit.

### **2. Residual Real Estate Consolidation**

Subject's remaining real estate — including the Star Island, Miami compound and any Hamptons or Los Angeles holdings not already liquidated during the litigation period — represents a lingering "Fortress of Sovereignty" the Bureau prefers dissolved rather than inherited or re-leveraged.

- **The Action:** Transfer remaining non-primary residences to a court-supervised liquidation trust, proceeds applied against outstanding civil judgments before any return to Subject.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loses the physical monuments to the Combs Global empire.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Confirms "Caretaker," not "Owner," status upon release.

### **3. Brand Dissolution (Sean John, Combs Wines & Spirits Residuals, AQUAhydrate)**

The dormant consumer brands under the former Combs Global umbrella retain nominal trademark value that could be revived opportunistically as a rehabilitation vehicle.

- **The Action:** Formally dissolve or place remaining trademarks into non-recoupable public license, foreclosing any "redemption arc" product relaunch.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loses the option to rebuild a commercial empire post-release.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Closes the "Comeback Vector" the Bureau flags as the primary reason this file remains open rather than fully archived.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Sound Engineers and Tour Crew** and recite the following:

> _"I built a throne out of gold records and a name that meant 'the last one standing.' It did not save me from twelve strangers in a jury box. I am not Bad Boy. I am not the industry. I am a citizen who answers to the law like anyone else, and I will not build another empire to hide behind."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days of release, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol.** In Subject's case, the Bureau notes this consequence is largely symbolic: Subject has already spent longer in a federal correctional facility than most Flotilla exile sentences run, and no seastead in the Ascension Havens has expressed interest in receiving him.
