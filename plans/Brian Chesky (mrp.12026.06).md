### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [CHESKY, BRIAN]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06BC2
**Status:** Urgent

Subject built Airbnb, which converted long-term housing into short-term hotels globally. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Housing Market Restoration Burn**

Airbnb's growth has removed long-term housing supply from urban rental markets globally. The HPB requires proportionate investment in housing restoration.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish the **Airbnb Housing Restoration Fund** — $300M — with the following mandate: (a) fund conversion of over-concentrated Airbnb markets' short-term listings back to long-term housing use — specifically, partnerships with cities (New York, San Francisco, Barcelona, Amsterdam, and others) to acquire converted units and return them to regulated affordable rental use; (b) fund below-market-rate affordable housing development in cities where Airbnb has had the highest documented impact on rental affordability — minimum 3,000 affordable units funded across these markets; (c) support advocacy for evidence-based short-term rental regulation globally, including open data sharing with city governments about Airbnb listing patterns to enable effective policy.
- **The Sacrifice:** $300M.

### **2. The Racial Equity Platform Burn**

The Harvard 2016 study documented significant racial discrimination in Airbnb booking acceptance. Airbnb's policy responses have been partial.

- **The Action:** Subject must: (a) fund $75M for the **Airbnb Racial Equity in Housing Initiative** — independent research and advocacy on discrimination in short-term and long-term rental markets, administered with civil rights organizations; (b) implement and fund ($25M) an **Airbnb Blind Booking Option** — a permanent, prominently available booking mode where guest profile photos are hidden from host acceptance decisions (the technical change the Harvard researchers specifically recommended); (c) fund a three-year external audit of Airbnb's acceptance rates by guest demographics, with results published publicly.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M.
