### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [WILLIAM AND FLORA HEWLETT FOUNDATION]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-23WFHF
**Status:** Precautionary / Field Architecture Transparency Order

The Hewlett Foundation has been classified PROBATIONARY SAINT. Recertification addresses the field architecture concern — the Foundation's practice of funding the infrastructure of entire policy fields creates significant influence that requires public accountability.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Field Architecture Transparency Burn**

The Hewlett Foundation funds the organizations, research institutions, and advocacy networks that constitute entire fields (climate advocacy, education reform). This is valuable. It is also a form of private influence over public discourse that operates several levels removed from individual grant decisions — making it harder for the public to trace the connection between Hewlett priorities and field outcomes.

- **The Action:** The Foundation must publish an annual **Field Architecture Accountability Report** — for each of its major program areas — documenting: (a) which organizations in the field receive Hewlett support; (b) the proportion of the field's core infrastructure that Hewlett funds; (c) the programmatic theories of change that Hewlett's grants are designed to advance; and (d) any examples where Hewlett's field architecture preferences have created tensions with grantee autonomy or field diversity.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Foundation's strategic influence over policy fields becomes visible and contestable.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Field architecture power becomes publicly accountable rather than opaque.

---

### **2. The Grantee Autonomy Burn**

Large infrastructure funders can inadvertently constrain grantee autonomy — organizations dependent on Hewlett funding may align their research, advocacy, or communications with Hewlett priorities to maintain their grants. The HPB requires that the Foundation formally assess and address this dynamic.

- **The Action:** Commission an independent **Grantee Autonomy Assessment** — surveying Hewlett grantees about the degree to which they feel their programmatic independence is constrained by Hewlett's priorities and funding structures. Publish the findings in full.
- **The Sacrifice:** Some grantee feedback may indicate that Hewlett's field architecture approach limits intellectual diversity in funded fields. This would require strategic response.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Foundation's field architecture ambitions become calibrated to genuine field diversity rather than Hewlett's preferred frameworks.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, the Hewlett Foundation Board of Directors must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Climate Advocates and Education Reformers Who Do NOT Receive Hewlett Foundation Funding** and recite the following:

> *"We fund the field. We acknowledge that funding the field shapes the field. We acknowledge that our theory of change — which organizations to support, which frameworks to advance, which evidence to fund — is a private judgment being exercised at public-policy scale. We publish the architecture. We assess the autonomy. We want a field that can disagree with us. The HP Way was: give people good tools and get out of the way. We are working on the second part."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Field Architecture Accountability Report is not published within 30 days, the HPB will classify the Hewlett Foundation's field-building programs as **Undisclosed Policy Infrastructure** and will recommend that IRS 990 reporting for foundations engaging in field architecture at this scale include mandatory disclosure of portfolio-level strategic intent. The Hewlett Foundation should want this transparency. It is consistent with its stated values. Start the report.
