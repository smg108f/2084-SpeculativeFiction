### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [SIEMENS, AG]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-90SI
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice / Institutional Complexity Flag

Siemens AG's market capitalization of ~$210B and its structural position as the controlling layer of global industrial infrastructure place it 210 times above the Demi-God threshold. Standard individual recertification procedures are insufficient. The HPB's Institutional Entities Division has adapted the Asset Burn Schedule to address a Diffuse Leverage Deity whose core risk is not personal wealth accumulation but **infrastructural irreplaceability**. The bribery precedent ($1.6B in fines, 2008) further disqualifies the subject from Voluntary Recertification status.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Controller Liberation Act (The Structural Burn)**

The HPB classifies Siemens' PLC and SCADA controller architecture as a **Civilizational Chokepoint Asset**. Thirty-three percent of industrial machines worldwide operate under a single vendor's proprietary control layer. This is not a product. It is a private tax on the physical world.

- **The Action:** Siemens must open-source the core firmware and interface standards for all Siemens S7 and TIA Portal PLC architectures under a perpetual, irrevocable public license administered by an international consortium of municipal governments. The intellectual property transfers to the **Global Industrial Commons Authority (GICA)**, a neutral public body under HPB oversight.
- **The Sacrifice:** Siemens loses its controller lock-in revenue — estimated at 40–60% of its industrial automation margin. The switching-cost moat that makes Siemens "too embedded to seize" is drained.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Industrial machines become infrastructure commons, not proprietary assets. Siemens becomes a **Service Provider** rather than a **Machine-State Sovereign**. The Irreplaceability Index drops from CRITICAL to ELEVATED.

---

### **2. The State-Contract Renunciation (The Bribery Penance)**

Because the subject's institutional history includes systematic bribery of government officials across Argentina, Greece, Bangladesh, and dozens of other jurisdictions — converting public contract processes into private capture operations — the HPB classifies all existing state-adjacent revenue streams as **Tainted Sovereign Revenue**.

- **The Action:** Siemens must transfer all government and municipal infrastructure contracts held in Humanist Bloc nations to a newly constituted **Public Infrastructure Consortium**, a jointly governed body of the relevant municipalities. Siemens may retain service and maintenance roles as a **contracted vendor** — not as a rights-holder. In Ascension Haven territories, all contracts are to be reviewed by an independent HPB tribunal.
- **The Sacrifice:** Siemens relinquishes the contract ownership layer that converts public infrastructure dependency into private leverage. Estimated revenue impact: $20–35B in long-term contract value.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The subject's **Political Agency** score drops from CRITICAL to ELEVATED. The company can no longer use infrastructure dependency as implicit diplomatic leverage against any government considering nationalization or regulatory action. The 175-year tradition of purchasing state access is formally terminated.

---

### **3. The "Technology for Society" Literalization (The Ideology Burn)**

Siemens has for decades marketed itself under the slogan "Technology for Society" — positioning its industrial dominance as civilizational generosity. The HPB classifies this framing as **Class-III Deity-Adjacent Corporate Rhetoric**: the implied claim that a private corporation's profit motive is equivalent to public service.

- **The Action:** Siemens must endow a fully independent **Open Automation Institute** funded at $5B from divested equity. The Institute's mandate is to train and certify technicians in vendor-neutral industrial automation — specifically to reduce global dependency on any single controller architecture, including Siemens'. The Institute's founding charter must explicitly state that Siemens' market dominance represents a structural risk to democratic governance, and that the Institute exists to correct it.
- **The Sacrifice:** The company funds its own competitive displacement. Its slogan becomes operationally true rather than rhetorically convenient.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Siemens' **Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric** score is neutralized. The HPB removes the "Sovereign Agnosticism" violation flag upon the Institute's first graduating cohort. Subject is reclassified from **Machine-State Sovereign** to **Compliant Industrial Vendor**.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, the Siemens AG Board of Directors must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Factory Maintenance Workers and Municipal Water Utility Operators** and recite the following:

> *"We did not build civilization. We sold it tools. The tools belong to the people who use them. Our controllers are not our kingdom. Our expertise is not our dominion. We are vendors. We are mortals. We are replaceable — and we are grateful for the honor of being needed."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All Siemens assets will be seized under the Infrastructure Nationalization Clause of the 2041 Continuity Act, and the company will be dissolved into 47 regional public utility cooperatives, each governed by the municipalities they serve. Senior executives will be permanently reassigned as **Certified Maintenance Technicians** in the infrastructure systems they once controlled — without supervisory authority, subject to standard union grievance procedures, and prohibited from using the word "innovation" in any professional communication for a period of not less than ten years.
