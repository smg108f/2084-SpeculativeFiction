### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ENBRIDGE INC.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09ENB
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification

Enbridge's valuation and secured-backlog growth place it well past the Ascension threshold, and its own leadership has done the Bureau's messaging work by publicly acknowledging the "risk downstream, profit at home" structure. Recertification is required to convert the company from a toll-collecting Demi-God back into an accountable public-facing utility partner.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Backlog Transparency Burn**

The Bureau classifies Enbridge's $41B secured project backlog as a **Potentiality Engine** — future toll revenue booked today as present valuation.

- **The Action:** Publish full rate-payer impact statements for every project in the backlog, and cap allowable rate-base returns on new pipeline and LNG capacity at a Bureau-reviewed ceiling.
- **The Sacrifice:** An estimated $8–10B in forward valuation tied to uncapped rate-of-return assumptions.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The "public risk, private profit" flag is cleared; Enbridge's Toll Booth classification drops one tier.

### **2. The Cross-Border Chokepoint Divestiture**

A single private entity controlling both Canadian export capacity and U.S. delivery infrastructure is, per the Bureau's charter, a **Continental Sovereignty Hazard**.

- **The Action:** Spin off either the U.S. gas-distribution utility segment or the Canadian mainline export system into an independently governed, rate-regulated public trust.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of vertically integrated pricing power across the full oil-to-doorstep chain.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** No single subject retains chokepoint control over an entire continent's energy circulation.

### **3. The Transition-Washing Correction**

Enbridge's "energy transition partner" branding is rated Cosmetic Pivot — real dollars, disproportionate messaging.

- **The Action:** Either triple committed renewable/hydrogen capital expenditure to match the scale of fossil transmission spend, or drop the transition branding from investor and public materials entirely.
- **The Sacrifice:** Continued reputational exposure (Option B) or several billion in redirected capex (Option A).
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Saintly Pivot rating becomes honest, which the Bureau values more than optimistic.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Enbridge's board must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Ratepayers from Border-State Utility Districts** and recite the following:

> _"We built the only road and then charged a toll to everyone who had no other way to travel. We called the risk yours and the profit ours. We will now build roads that belong to the people who walk them."_

The mainline pipeline and gas-distribution network will be transferred to a **Bi-National Energy Commons Trust**, jointly governed by U.S. and Canadian public utility boards, with former shareholders issued **Delivery Credits** redeemable against their own energy bills.
