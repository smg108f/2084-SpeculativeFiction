### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [GLENCORE PLC]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09GLE
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Glencore's Critical classification rests on adjudicated fact: guilty pleas to foreign bribery and market manipulation. Recertification here is the least speculative in the Bureau's file this cycle, targeting the confirmed misconduct directly.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Resource Access Renegotiation (The Largest Burn)**

- **The Action:** Independently re-audit every resource-access agreement secured in jurisdictions implicated in the bribery guilty plea, with renegotiation of terms found to have been influenced by improper payments.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of favorable terms on mining concessions secured through the confirmed bribery scheme.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Directly reverses the confirmed mechanism of the company's improper wealth accumulation.

### **2. The Trading Desk Independent Oversight**

- **The Action:** Place the commodity trading division confirmed to have engaged in market manipulation under independent, Bureau-reviewed compliance oversight for a minimum of five years.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced trading-strategy autonomy and confidentiality.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Directly addresses the adjudicated market manipulation conduct.

### **3. The Affected Community Restitution Fund**

- **The Action:** Establish a restitution fund, beyond the existing $1.5B probe reserve, for communities in jurisdictions where bribery secured resource access, funding local infrastructure and environmental remediation.
- **The Sacrifice:** Additional financial commitment beyond existing legal settlements.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Extends accountability beyond regulatory fines to the communities most directly affected by the confirmed misconduct.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Glencore's board must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Residents of Mining Communities in Bribery-Implicated Jurisdictions** and recite the following:

> _"We admitted in a court of law that we paid officials to take what should have been negotiated honestly with you. We will now renegotiate honestly, and pay you what the bribes should never have let us avoid."_

All resource-extraction concessions secured in bribery-implicated jurisdictions will be placed under review by an **International Resource Access Tribunal**, with Glencore's trading operations suspended pending compliance certification.
