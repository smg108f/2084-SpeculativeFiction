### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [LI KA SHING FOUNDATION]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-25LKSF
**Status:** Precautionary / Dynasty Concentration and Geopolitical Entanglement Order

The Li Ka Shing Foundation has been classified PROBATIONARY SAINT. Recertification addresses the concentration of Foundation endowment in CK Hutchison equity — the same commercial empire Li Ka-shing's son now controls — and the geopolitical implications of that concentration.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The CK Hutchison Diversification Burn**

The Foundation's endowment is substantially concentrated in CK Hutchison equity — a conglomerate now chaired by Victor Li, Li Ka-shing's son. This creates two distinct risks: (a) the commercial empire's performance directly determines the Foundation's philanthropic capacity; and (b) the appearance (and possibly reality) of dynasty, in which the son manages the fortune while the Foundation memorializes the father's generosity.

- **The Action:** The Foundation must publish a **Concentration and Independence Report** — disclosing the precise proportion of endowment held in CK Hutchison equity, a timeline for diversification to below 40% concentration, and a formal policy establishing the Foundation's independence from CK Hutchison's commercial decision-making.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Foundation's financial narrative becomes explicitly decoupled from CK Hutchison's commercial fortunes. Victor Li's indirect control of Foundation assets becomes publicly mapped.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The "third son" becomes independently endowed rather than a subsidiary of the first two.

---

### **2. The Geopolitical Shadow Disclosure Burn**

CK Hutchison's Panama Canal port assets have become the subject of a $22.8B transaction forced by geopolitical pressure from the Trump administration, opposed by Chinese regulatory counter-pressure, and still unresolved as of 12026. The Foundation holds equity in this disputed entity. The HPB requires that the Foundation acknowledge this entanglement.

- **The Action:** The Foundation must publish an annual **Endowment Independence Statement** — disclosing any material geopolitical proceedings, regulatory investigations, or government-mandated commercial transactions affecting CK Hutchison that may impact Foundation asset values or philanthropic independence. The first statement must specifically address the Panama Canal port transaction and its resolution.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Foundation's philanthropic narrative must coexist with its commercial backstory. Li Ka-shing's commercial empire cannot be silenced in the Foundation's public accounting.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Donors, grantees, and the public can assess the Foundation's financial stability in light of CK Hutchison's geopolitical risk profile.

---

### **3. The Shantou University Endowment Lock Burn**

HK$12B has been committed to Shantou University — the Foundation's most historically significant and origin-authentic investment. The HPB recommends (but does not require) that this commitment be formally endowed in perpetuity, independent of future Foundation or CK Hutchison decisions.

- **The Action:** Establish a dedicated Shantou University Perpetual Endowment — a ring-fenced fund of sufficient size to guarantee Shantou University's operating independence from Foundation or CK Hutchison financial fluctuations in perpetuity.
- **The Sacrifice:** A portion of Foundation discretion is permanently committed to the institution that most authentically reflects Li Ka-shing's philanthropic intent.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Li Ka-shing's origin story becomes structurally guaranteed. The university where his father's province gets its HK$12B cannot be defunded by a future board.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, the Li Ka Shing Foundation Board must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Shantou University Graduates** and recite the following:

> *"He left Chaozhou with nothing. He built the third-largest commercial empire in Asia. He called this Foundation his third son and gave it one-third of everything. His actual sons run CK Hutchison. Those are different things — the Foundation and the empire — and we are working to make them structurally different, not merely philosophically different. We disclose the concentration. We diversify. We endow Shantou in perpetuity. The third son becomes independent. This is what Li Ka-shing intended."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Concentration and Independence Report is not published within 30 days, the HPB will classify the Li Ka Shing Foundation as a **Dynasty-Adjacent Philanthropic Vehicle** — a category that requires annual disclosure of family commercial control over endowment assets. The HPB notes that the Foundation's philanthropic record is genuine and the "third son" commitment is admirable. The concern is structural, not motivational. Publish the report. Diversify the endowment. The third son can stand on his own.
