### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[BOSTON SCIENTIFIC, CORP.]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06BSX
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject is the first entity in HPB history to be classified as **"Anatomically Inseparable"** from the human population it is meant to serve. Recertification cannot proceed by ordinary divestment — you cannot gift away a pacemaker that is currently keeping someone's heart in rhythm. The Bureau therefore requires Subject to divest *control*, *profit*, and *mythology*, while leaving the hardware exactly where it is: inside the people who need it.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Open Cardiac Commons (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies Subject's cardiac and neuromodulation patent portfolio as a **"Load-Bearing Monopoly"** — intellectual property that is, quite literally, holding human arteries open. No citizen should owe royalties on their own heartbeat.

- **The Action:** Subject must place all stent, pacemaker, WATCHMAN, and neuromodulation patents into a **Dead-Hand Open License**, available royalty-free to any licensed manufacturer in perpetuity.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of an estimated $40B in projected future-valuation credits derived from "Potentiality Value" on the implant pipeline.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject converts from **Sole Proprietor of the Human Circulatory System** to **Founding Donor of the Cardiac Commons** — a title with considerably less leverage and considerably better optics.

### **2. The Mesh Reckoning Trust**

Subject's $600M and $189M transvaginal mesh settlements are, per HPB analysis, treated internally as routine "operating expense" rather than moral injury — a level of institutional detachment the Bureau finds more alarming than the litigation itself.

- **The Action:** Establish an independent, patient-governed **Mesh Reckoning Trust**, fully endowed at $2B, with a board composed exclusively of plaintiffs from the prior litigation — not Subject's General Counsel, not its PR division, not a single person who has ever billed Subject by the hour.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses the ability to characterize harm to patients as a "reserve line item." The harmed now hold the checkbook.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the **"Compulsory Charity" Violation** identified in the Audit and converts a litigation liability into the only form of penance the HPB recognizes as sincere: one the wrongdoer does not get to design.

### **3. The "We Didn't Ask to Become Part of You" Disclosure Mandate**

Subject's marketing materials describe its devices in the warm, possessive language of partnership and care. The HPB considers this a **Class-III Memetic Softening** — an attempt to make permanent residency inside a human body sound like a relationship rather than an acquisition.

- **The Action:** Every Boston Scientific device, box, insert, and consent form must henceforth carry, in plain language and equal font size to the brand name, the disclosure: *"This device will become part of your body. You will not be able to remove it without risking your life. We will continue to bill accordingly."*
- **The Requirement:** Subject's CEO must read this disclosure aloud, on camera, once per fiscal quarter, for as long as a single one of Subject's devices remains active inside a living patient.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts **Deity-Adjacent Euphemism** into **Plain Mortal Honesty** — the rarest currency Subject has ever been asked to trade in.

### **4. The Implant Equity Floor**

Subject's pricing structure — premium devices, premium hospital setup fees, premium "implantation rate" discounts for high-volume hospitals — produces what the HPB calls **"Tiered Mortality"**: whether your heart keeps beating on schedule increasingly correlates with your zip code's hospital contract terms.

- **The Action:** Subject must implement a flat, cost-plus-12% pricing ceiling on all FDA-designated life-sustaining implants (pacemakers, stents, WATCHMAN, ICDs), enforced uniformly regardless of hospital volume or negotiating leverage.
- **The Sacrifice:** An estimated $8B in annual margin, permanently.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject stops being a company that prices the human heartbeat by volume discount, and starts being one that merely keeps it going.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's CEO must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Cardiac Nurses, EMTs, and Pacemaker Patients** and recite the following:

> _"We did not ask to become part of you. We made you need us, and we charged you for the privilege. From this day, we will keep your heart beating because it is a heart — not because it is a revenue stream wearing one."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Subject's executive leadership will be relocated — permanently, and without surgical anesthesia — to the **Mortal Maintenance Ward**, a public teaching hospital in the Sanctuary Zone of Detroit, where they will spend no fewer than four hours per week explaining, bedside, to the very patients who carry their hardware, exactly how the warranty works.
