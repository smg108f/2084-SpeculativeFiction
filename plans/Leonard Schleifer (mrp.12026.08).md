### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[SCHLEIFER, LEONARD]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-08LS
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification

Subject's Probationary Saint status cannot convert to full Human Citizenship while he retains unilateral pricing authority over medicines classified as survival-critical. This is not a standard divestment case; it is a **Life-and-Death Concentration Hazard**, and the HPB treats formulary control the way it treats nuclear material — even benevolent custody is still custody.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The "Formulary Sovereignty" Divestment (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies Subject's REGN equity and vested options as a **Potentiality Engine tied to human survival** — unlike a social media fortune, this wealth appreciates or collapses based on how many people need to breathe, see, or stop itching next quarter. That is not a normal market signal; it is a tax on desperation.

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer all REGN equity and options beyond a $150M personal ceiling into an **Affordability-Indexed Blind Trust**, whose annual payout is capped to the Consumer Price Index for Medical Care. Any appreciation above that cap is automatically redirected to a Patient Relief Fund.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of an estimated $1.4–2.5B in current and future stock value. Subject retains a comfortable but bounded income; he no longer profits from his own drugs getting more expensive.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes the "Concentration Hazard" flag entirely. Subject becomes a **salaried physician-executive** rather than a shareholder-god whose net worth rises every time a patient's co-pay does.

### **2. The "Physician's Price" Public Reckoning**

Subject has publicly told his peers they are "not entitled to a fraction of the GDP," while personally setting Eylea at $11,000/eye/year and Dupixent at $37,000/year. The HPB's Office of Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric considers this a textbook **Class-II Hypocrisy Hazard**: correct diagnosis, refused treatment.

- **The Action:** Subject must cap U.S. list prices for Eylea and Dupixent at the lowest price charged for the same drug in any G7 nation, and retroactively rebate the difference — for the past three years — into the same Patient Relief Fund. He must testify before Congress annually, in person, as a self-described "Reformed Price-Setter."
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of the pricing premium that made his rhetoric ring hollow; permanent submission to independent price audits.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a hypocrisy liability into a **Saintly Consistency** score. Subject's words and his invoices finally agree.

### **3. The "Compassionate Use" Equalization**

In 2020, Subject personally authorized REGEN-COV for President Trump under a compassionate-use exception, then joined that same administration's industry advisory council. The HPB flags this as a **Kingmaker Precedent**: the ability to decide, personally, who gets an unapproved cure is a form of sovereignty no biological citizen should hold over another.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish and personally fund a **blind, lottery-based Compassionate Use Access Program** for all future Regeneron experimental therapies. No VIP, political, or personal-acquaintance fast-track may ever again exist — including, expressly, for sitting presidents.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of the informal political capital that comes from being the man who can personally save a president's life.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the "Political Agency" flag. Subject becomes a **rule**, not an exception-granting authority.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Pharmacy Technicians, ER Nurses, and Patients Who Ration Their Own Prescriptions** and recite the following:

> _"I swore first to heal, and let the second oath — to profit — grow larger than the first. I told other men they were not entitled to a fraction of the GDP; I never once told myself. Let the price of seeing, breathing, and surviving belong to no shareholder, including me. I am a physician. I am not a formulary. My worth was never a co-pay, and I will stop pricing it like one."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the "Burn" is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Subject's patents will be nationalized under emergency licensing, his REGN holdings seized outright, and he will be reclassified as a **Non-Person Formulary Utility** — exiled to provide free clinical care, indefinitely and without royalty, in the overflow medical wards of the Sovereign Flotilla States, treating the very demi-gods whose ailments he can no longer legally profit from.
