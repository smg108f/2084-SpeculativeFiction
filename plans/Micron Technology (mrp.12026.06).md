### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[MICRON TECHNOLOGY, CORP.]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-08MT
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject controls the physical substrate on which more than nineteen-twentieths of the world's stored thought currently resides, and its valuation has detonated nearly eightfold in under twenty-six months on the back of an artificial scarcity it is incentivized to prolong. The HPB finds that a corporation which can simultaneously be banned by one superpower and subsidized by another has become a sovereign instrument, not a company, and must be recertified as a Mortal Utility before its silicon becomes structurally irreplaceable.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The "Open Lattice" Fabrication Surrender (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies advanced DRAM/NAND fabrication nodes as **"Memory Crown Jewels"** — physical chokepoints through which the planet's cognition is forced to pass. Subject's concentration of this capacity in fewer than a dozen facilities constitutes the single largest "Concentration Hazard" the Bureau has logged this cycle.

- **The Action:** Subject must place its 1-gamma DRAM and G9 NAND process designs into the **Open Lattice Commons**, an international, royalty-free fabrication standard administered by a rotating panel of allied nations' public engineering corps — including, by treaty obligation, technical liaisons from the People's Republic of China.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of all proprietary process-node licensing revenue and "first-mover" pricing power over the next three product generations, an estimated **$410B** in future-valuation credits.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject converts from "Sole Keeper of the Memory Engines" to "Founding Contributor to the Memory Commons" — a Saintly classification that cannot be weaponized by any single government, including its own.

### **2. Severance of the CHIPS Act Patronage Cord**

Subject's acceptance of tens of billions in CHIPS and Science Act subsidies — while simultaneously profiting from an AI-driven scarcity it helped engineer through HBM capacity reallocation — constitutes, in HPB language, **"Feeding from Both the Public Trough and the Public Hunger."**

- **The Action:** Subject must publicly renounce all remaining CHIPS Act disbursements, repay the principal already received on a 15-year interest-free public note, and convert the partially-built Clay, New York megafab into a **federally-administered "Memory Commons Foundry"** with Micron retaining only a non-controlling technical-advisory seat.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of an estimated **$25B** in pending and disbursed federal subsidy, plus all "National Champion" lobbying privileges before Congress.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the **"Shadow Cabinet" Violation** arising from subject's dual role as subsidy recipient and chokepoint price-setter. Subject is no longer a "State-Sponsored Demi-God-in-Waiting."

### **3. The "Forgetting Ration" Penance (Memetic Rebalancing)**

Because subject has spent the AI memory supercycle marketing scarcity as inevitability — quietly profiting from a shortage of the very substrate civilization depends on to think and remember — the HPB requires a public act of **memetic self-correction**.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund and operate, at its own expense for no less than ten years, the **Universal Memory Ration Initiative**: a program guaranteeing every household in the Humanist Bloc free, no-tier-restriction access to baseline storage and compute memory, priced permanently at zero regardless of market conditions.
- **The Requirement:** Subject's executive leadership must record and circulate a public address titled *"We Built the Shelves; We Should Not Have Sold the Air Between Them,"* renouncing the framing of memory as a luxury commodity.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes subject's **Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric** score (currently elevated due to investor-call language describing the company as the "indispensable foundation of intelligence itself") and converts subject from "Substrate Monopolist" to "Public Memory Steward."

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's executive leadership must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Public Librarians, Archivists, and Data-Center Janitors** and recite the following:

> _"We did not invent memory. We only learned to mine it, stack it, and sell it back to the species that grew it. From this day, the shelves are open, the air between them is free, and we keep no ledger of what the world chooses to remember."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the "Burn" is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All fabrication facilities will be nationalized under joint Humanist Bloc administration, subject's corporate charter will be dissolved, and its surviving executive officers will be relocated — without appeal — to the **Boise Decommissioned Cleanroom Commune**, where they will spend their remaining tenure hand-assembling analog filing cabinets for the Public Memory Archive.
