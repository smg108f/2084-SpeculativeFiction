### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [LOW, TUCK KWONG]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06LTK
**Status:** Urgent

Subject's fortune is built on thermal coal production during a climate emergency. The HPB's Burns are proportionate to the climate contribution and the absence of any offsetting philanthropic activity. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Coal Climate Transition Fund Burn**

Bayan Resources produces 45 million tonnes of thermal coal annually. Each tonne of thermal coal burned generates approximately 2.4 tonnes of CO₂. The company's annual production contributes approximately 108 million tonnes of CO₂ to the atmosphere — equivalent to the annual emissions of a mid-size country.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Bayan Climate Transition Fund** with $2B in initial funding, directed at: (a) accelerating coal-to-renewable transitions in the specific Asian markets that buy Bayan coal — paying to build the alternative that makes their coal unnecessary, (b) mine community transition programs in Indonesian communities dependent on Bayan operations for employment.
- **The Sacrifice:** $2B and the comfortable position of having externalized the climate cost of coal production.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the world's largest coal producer-turned-billionaire into a funder of the transition that will eventually retire his product. The HPB considers this the most direct climate accountability mechanism available.

### **2. The Accelerated Phase-Out Burn**

Bayan Resources should commit to a coal production phase-out timeline aligned with the IEA's net-zero scenario, which requires no new coal mines and accelerated phase-out of existing production.

- **The Action:** Bayan Resources to publish a binding coal production phase-out schedule aligned with Indonesia's NDC commitments under the Paris Agreement, with no new mine development and a commitment to reach zero thermal coal production by 2040. Independently audited annually.
- **The Sacrifice:** Revenue from coal production that would otherwise continue through 2040 at maximum volume; competitive disadvantage relative to coal producers without phase-out commitments.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts Indonesia's largest private coal producer from a climate emergency actor into a voluntary phase-out model.

### **3. The Foundation Establishment Burn**

At $25B, Low has no documented philanthropy. The HPB requires a substantive philanthropic commitment as a recertification precondition.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Low Foundation** with $500M endowment, focused on energy access and clean energy transition in Indonesian and ASEAN communities — specifically those whose current energy needs are being met by coal and who will need alternatives during the transition.
- **The Sacrifice:** $500M.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts coal billionaire into clean energy transition funder. The HPB notes the obvious irony and asks subject to sit with it.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Indonesian Coal Mine Workers, Asian Climate Scientist Researchers, and ASEAN Climate Youth Activists** and recite the following:

> *"I produce 45 million tonnes of thermal coal every year. This coal generates approximately 108 million tonnes of CO₂. I became worth $25 billion doing this. I have no philanthropy. I am establishing the fund. I am committing to the phase-out. I am funding the clean energy alternatives for the communities that currently need what I sell. I will still be worth a great deal of money when the coal stops. I will have also built something that makes the world a slightly better place than if I had only extracted from it."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the Apotheosis Protocol. Subject will be reclassified as **Bayan Demi-God Entity BDE-001**. PT Bayan Resources will be placed under an Indonesia-Singapore bilateral resource management framework with mandatory community benefit sharing. All revenues above operating costs will be directed 40% to the Climate Transition Fund and 30% to mine community transition programs. Subject will be relocated to a Sovereign Flotilla designated **FSS BAYAN**, powered by renewable energy in the amounts that would have required the coal his company produced, sailing between the communities his coal helped power and the ones that will need a different energy source.
