### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [LU, XIANGYANG]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06LX
**Status:** Precautionary — Supply Chain Ethics Precondition Applied

*Subject's cobalt processing operations have been cited in Amnesty International and other investigations of DRC child labour in artisanal mining supply chains. A specific supply chain remediation burn is required as a primary action.*

---

### **1. The DRC Cobalt Child Labour Remediation Burn**

This is the HPB's primary concern. Huayou's sourcing from DRC artisanal and small-scale mining (ASM) has been linked to documented child labour.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund and establish an **Independent Huayou DRC Supply Chain Child Labour Audit Body** — a ¥1B (~$140M) endowment — independent of Huayou corporate governance, staffed by child rights specialists, mining engineers, and DRC community representatives, with a mandate to: (a) conduct unannounced audits of all Huayou DRC supplier sites; (b) immediately delist any supplier site found to use child labour, until independently verified as remediated; (c) fund remediation: every child found in Huayou's supply chain receives school enrollment funding, healthcare, and family economic support through a Huayou-funded but independently administered DRC Children's Education and Welfare Program; (d) publish full audit results publicly within 60 days of each audit. No carbon credits, no offset schemes — this is a direct accountability mechanism.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥1B endowment; potential supply chain disruption from delisting.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** China's largest cobalt processor has independently verified, publicly reported child labour remediation — not corporate commitments, actual audits.

### **2. The Huayou DRC Community Investment Burn**

Huayou has DRC community programs. The HPB requires scale.

- **The Action:** Subject must scale the **Huayou DRC Community Investment Program** to ¥500M (~$70M) over 5 years, with independent governance (not Huayou marketing or CSR department), focused on: (a) DRC mining community clean water access; (b) DRC community healthcare at Huayou-proximate mining sites; (c) DRC artisanal miner economic alternatives — vocational training and non-mining livelihood programs for ASM miners and their families.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥500M.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The cobalt processor becomes the DRC's largest private investor in mining community alternatives.

### **3. The Lu Foundation Burn**

- **The Action:** Establish the **Lu Xiangyang Foundation** — ¥300M (~$42M) — focused on Chinese battery industry ethics research and scholarship for DRC students in Chinese universities.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥300M.
