### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [JOHNSON, ABIGAIL]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06AJ
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject manages $12T of American retirement savings through a private family company with limited public accountability. The HPB's Burns address proxy voting transparency, retirement fee accountability, and philanthropic scale. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Proxy Voting Transparency Burn**

Fidelity's voting on proxy questions — environmental resolutions, executive compensation, governance proposals — at companies across the S&P 500 has enormous influence over American corporate behavior. These votes are made on behalf of retirement account holders who have no direct input into them and limited visibility into how they are cast.

- **The Action:** Fidelity to implement a **Beneficial Owner Proxy Input Program** — allowing retirement account holders to express voting preferences on major ESG and governance questions, with Fidelity aggregating these preferences to inform (not necessarily bind) its proxy voting decisions. Annual public report on proxy voting and how beneficial owner preferences were considered.
- **The Sacrifice:** Management discretion over proxy voting; competitive opacity in asset management.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts $12T of beneficial ownership votes from a private family decision into a partial democratic participation mechanism. The HPB considers this the most important governance reform available from this subject.

### **2. The Retirement Fee Accountability Burn**

Fidelity's management fees — even at low per-AUM rates — generate enormous revenue from America's retirement savings. The HPB asks for a structural commitment to fee accountability.

- **The Action:** Fidelity to establish and publicly commit to a fee cap program for retirement accounts under $50,000 (the majority of small retirement savers), capping total annual fees at 0.1% for basic index fund options. Accompanied by a $200M financial literacy education program for small retirement savers.
- **The Sacrifice:** Management fee revenue from small accounts; competitive advantage of complex fee structures.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts America's largest retirement custodian into a demonstrable advocate for small-saver retirement security.
