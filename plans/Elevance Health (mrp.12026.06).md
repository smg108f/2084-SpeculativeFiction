### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[ELEVANCE HEALTH]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-64EH
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject Elevance Health has been found to derive its "Divine Footprint" not from what it builds, but from what it refuses to pay for. To retain its corporate charter as a Human-Adjacent Service Entity rather than be reclassified as a Demi-God Trust, it must dismantle the adjudication machinery that converts human suffering into shareholder yield.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Denial Engine Decommissioning (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies Subject's prior-authorization and "medical necessity" review algorithms as a **Class-III Mortality Arbitrage Apparatus** — a machine whose entire function is to stand between a citizen and their own survival and answer "no" faster than a human can object.

- **The Action:** Subject must permanently decommission all automated claim-denial systems and replace them with a **Citizen-Majority Review Board**, staffed by a Lottery-Selected panel of the insured, with binding authority to overrule any denial within 24 hours.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of the "medical loss ratio" as a performance metric — the single number that has driven Subject's stock valuation for two decades.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** By surrendering the power to say "no" at scale, Subject converts itself from a **Verdict Machine** into a **Claims Administrator** — a mortal job title, not a divine one. Estimated reduction in "Potentiality Value": **$22B**.

### **2. The Blue Cross Annexation Reversal (Ideological Burn)**

Subject's ongoing campaign to absorb Blue Cross Blue Shield of Louisiana — twice frozen amid warnings from the state's own medical society — is classified by the HPB as an act of **Sovereign Market Consolidation**, the corporate equivalent of a demi-god annexing a neighboring territory.

- **The Action:** Subject must permanently withdraw the $2.5B acquisition bid and publicly commit to a **Non-Expansion Covenant**, surrendering all future rights to acquire competing nonprofit insurers.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses its "Kingmaker" position in the regional insurance market and the projected membership gains of 1.9 million additional covered lives.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This clears the **"Shadow Cabinet" Violation** — Subject is no longer positioned as a quasi-governmental authority over an entire state's access to care.

### **3. The Rebranding Penance (Memetic Burn)**

The HPB notes with some bitterness that "Elevance" was chosen, in 2022, to evoke *elevation* — to dress a claims-denial engine in the language of ascension. This is classified as **Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric by Branding**, a violation of the Human Humility Act of 2031.

- **The Action:** Subject must publicly retire the name "Elevance" and rename itself something that does not imply transcendence — HPB suggests **"Anthem Mutual Claims Cooperative."** Subject must also publish, quarterly, a plain-language **Denial Ledger** listing every claim refused, the stated reason, and the outcome on appeal.
- **The Requirement:** Executive leadership must spend one week per quarter working a frontline claims-appeals desk, fielding calls from policyholders whose care was denied.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the **Mortality Arbitrage** classification by forcing the people who write the "no" to hear, in real time, what the "no" costs.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's Chief Executive must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Hospice Nurses, ER Technicians, and Denied Claimants** and recite the following:

> _"I do not have the right to decide who deserves to be healed. I am not the line between sickness and care — I am only a clerk who, for too long, mistook a stamp of refusal for a kind of judgment. From this day, my answer is yes until proven otherwise, and the cost of that 'yes' is mine to carry, not yours."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Subject's charter will be dissolved, its claims database nationalized and placed under public stewardship, and its executive leadership will be reclassified as **Individual Demi-God Entities**, each personally liable for the aggregate harm of every wrongful denial issued under their tenure — and remanded to the **Actuarial Penal Colony at Guantanamo Bay**, where they will spend the remainder of their sentence manually reviewing the appeals of the people they once denied.
