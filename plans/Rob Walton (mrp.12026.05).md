### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [WALTON, S. ROBSON]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-81RW
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject Walton's RTWI of $149B places him at **149 times the Demi-God threshold** — the highest passive-accumulation multiplier in the Bureau's current active caseload. Recertification is complicated by the dynastic nature of the estate: any individual divestment must be coordinated with the parallel **Walton PSE Protocol** to prevent asset migration within the family network.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Walmart Equity Dissolution (The Great Unholding)**

The HPB classifies inherited controlling equity in a corporation generating $650B in annual revenue as **"Bloodline Capital"** — the most dangerous class of Demi-God asset, because it compounds across generations without requiring the heir to do anything. Subject Walton has held Walmart stock as his primary asset since inheriting it from Sam Walton in 1992. For over three decades, this stock has appreciated on its own momentum. The Bureau is unimpressed by passivity at this scale.

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer all Walmart equity holdings into a **Worker Sovereignty Trust** — a new class of instrument under the 2031 Economic Democracy Act in which Walmart's frontline workforce holds beneficial interest. Subject retains no voting rights and no dividend claim.
- **The Sacrifice:** Approximately $130B in direct and indirect Walmart holdings. Subject's annual dividend income — estimated at $450M+ — is extinguished entirely.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Bureau's most powerful Recertification tool. Worker Sovereignty Transfer converts a Demi-God's foundational asset into a public utility, generating massive Social Credit Surplus. Subject becomes, overnight, the patron of 1.6 million Walmart workers. The irony is intentional: the family that resisted a $15 minimum wage must now gift the entire enterprise to its workforce.

---

### **2. The Rocky Mountain Auto Collection — Public Museum Transfer**

The HPB classifies the Rocky Mountain Auto Collection as a **Class-II Wealth Crystal** — a hoard of physical objects whose sole function is to store value extracted from productive circulation. Two Ferrari 250 GTOs. A 1936 Bugatti worth $50M+. A McLaren F1. A Le Mans-winning Ford GT. These are not transportation. They are **steel reliquaries of accumulated capital**, and Senator Sanders was right to name them. The collection is valued conservatively at $200–$500M; the Bureau uses the upper bound for Recertification purposes.

- **The Action:** The entire Rocky Mountain Auto Collection must be transferred to a **National Automotive Heritage Trust**, with permanent public access, free of charge, at a facility to be constructed in Bentonville, Arkansas — the town that Walmart built and the Walton family transformed. The cars stay. They become a monument not to Rob Walton's taste, but to the labor that made them purchasable.
- **The Sacrifice:** $500M in Wealth Crystals. Subject loses all private access except as a member of the public.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This single act resolves the Bureau's **Physical Hoarding Violation** and simultaneously completes the Bentonville Djinn Pivot the family began with Crystal Bridges. Bentonville becomes a genuine commons — art, music, cycling, and now the greatest automotive collection in American public life.

---

### **3. The Political Donation Moratorium and PAC Dissolution**

The HPB's 2031 Influence Containment Act prohibits individuals in the Red Zone (RTWI >$950M) from making political contributions exceeding $1,000 per cycle. Subject Walton donated $16.5M to Republican causes between 12023 and 12024 — a **Shadow Cabinet-level violation**. His NFL ownership further amplifies political reach through the Sports Sovereignty rubric.

- **The Action:** Immediate dissolution of all political giving vehicles. Subject must submit to a **five-year Political Silence Order** — no donations, no endorsements, no bundling. The $17.6M deployed in 12024 must be matched dollar-for-dollar into the **Public Square Algorithm Fund** (non-partisan civic AI infrastructure).
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses his status as the single largest political donor among NFL owners. He loses his kingmaker role in conservative coalition building. Greg Penner and Carrie Walton Penner's political activities are separately audited under the PSE Protocol.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the **Political Agency Violation**. Subject drops from CRITICAL to LOW on the Shadow Cabinet metric.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Walmart Cashiers, Stockroom Associates, and Distribution Center Workers** and recite the following:

> _"I did not build this. My father built this. His workers built this. I was a passenger in a vehicle I did not drive, and I have been mistaken for its owner for thirty years. I return what was never mine to hold. I am not the heir of Walmart. I am a citizen of the United States of America, and my worth is not what I inherited but what I choose to give back."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All assets will be seized and transferred to the Worker Sovereignty Trust by federal order. Subject will be reclassified as a **Passive Demi-God Entity** — a new and untested legal category — and remanded to the Bentonville Sanctuary Zone, where he will live under Life-Tenancy in the Crystal Bridges Museum guest quarters, surrounded by public art his family paid for, free to walk the cycling trails his family built, stripped of everything except the town he inadvertently made beautiful.
