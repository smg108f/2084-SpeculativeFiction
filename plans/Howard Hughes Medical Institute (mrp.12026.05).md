### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [HOWARD HUGHES MEDICAL INSTITUTE]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06HHMI
**Status:** Precautionary / Origin Transparency and Investigator Selection Reform

HHMI has been classified PROBATIONARY SAINT. Recertification is precautionary and focused on two specific structural concerns: the institution's founding as a tax avoidance vehicle (which must be publicly acknowledged rather than airbrushed) and the opacity of its Investigator selection process (which shapes scientific careers at institutional scale without adequate transparency). The Institute's actual research work is not in question.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Origin Disclosure Burn (The Howard Hughes Tax Shelter Reckoning)**

The HPB's Institutional Mythology Doctrine requires that charitable institutions created for purposes other than charity acknowledge the circumstances of their founding in their public communications. HHMI's founding was a tax avoidance strategy by a man who had no demonstrable interest in medicine. This fact has been largely sanitized from HHMI's public self-presentation over seven decades.

- **The Action:** HHMI must amend its annual report and "About Us" materials to include a plainly written, historically accurate account of the Institute's founding by Howard Hughes in 11953, including the IRS investigation of its charitable status, the Congressional scrutiny of its structure, and Hughes's own disengagement from the institution during his lifetime. This disclosure must appear in a prominent location, not a footnote.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Institute's mythology — the story of a great visionary who built science — is replaced by the more complicated and accurate story of a tax shelter that became science through institutional persistence. Some donors may find this less appealing.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** HHMI acknowledges that great institutions can emerge from imperfect origins without pretending the origins were something else. The public record is corrected.

---

### **2. The Investigator Selection Transparency Burn**

HHMI's Investigator appointment process is one of the most consequential talent selection mechanisms in American science, and one of the least transparent. The criteria, the review panel compositions, the decision rationales — none of this is publicly disclosed. The HPB classifies this as a **Career-Shaping Opacity** condition: private decisions with public scientific consequences.

- **The Action:** HHMI must publish, for each Investigator competition cycle, the selection criteria in detail, the composition of review panels (by field expertise, institution, and demographic), aggregate statistics on applicant pools and selection rates by field and institution type, and — within appropriate confidentiality limits — the general reasoning categories used in selection decisions.
- **The Sacrifice:** HHMI's selection process becomes subject to public scrutiny. Selection patterns that favor certain institution types, career stages, or research paradigms will be visible.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Investigator selection process becomes auditable by the scientific community it serves. Career-Shaping Opacity classification resolved.

---

### **3. The Janelia Open-Science Burn**

The Janelia Research Campus produces world-class neuroscience and imaging science. Some of its most impactful work — particularly in connectomics and imaging tool development — has global significance. The HPB requires that institutions at this scale adopt binding open-science commitments commensurate with their public subsidy (via charitable tax status).

- **The Action:** HHMI must adopt a binding open-science policy requiring that all Janelia-generated research data, imaging tools, and computational methods be made freely available within 12 months of publication, with no commercial licensing restrictions on tools developed with HHMI funds.
- **The Sacrifice:** Some commercial partnerships or technology licensing revenues may be foreclosed.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The scientific outputs of a $22B public-benefit institution become public goods without qualification.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, the HHMI Board of Trustees must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Early-Career Biomedical Researchers Who Applied for HHMI Investigator Status and Were Not Selected** and recite the following:

> *"Howard Hughes built this to avoid taxes. We inherited it and turned it into something worth defending. We acknowledge both things. We acknowledge that we select scientists in private, with enormous consequences for their careers, and that this has not been adequately accountable to the scientific community we claim to serve. We open the selection process. We open the science. We correct the history. The Institute survives this transparency. The IRS would be pleased."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If origin disclosures and selection transparency reforms are not initiated within 30 days, the HPB will petition the IRS to reopen the question of whether HHMI's charitable status — granted under circumstances that were legally contested and publicly debated — continues to meet the standards for a genuine public benefit organization. The HPB notes that Howard Hughes himself would have found this threat deeply irritating. The HPB considers this appropriate.
