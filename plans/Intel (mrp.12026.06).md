### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [INTEL CORPORATION]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-67IC
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice / Structural Compliance Review

Intel Corporation has been classified as a Demi-God Entity under the 2034 Executive Orders by virtue of its market capitalization exceeding the $1,000,000,000 threshold by two orders of magnitude. While the HPB acknowledges that Intel's current trajectory is declension rather than Ascension, the law does not contain a "declining gracefully" exemption. Intel must formally discharge its Divine Taint or remain subject to 100% growth taxation and continued movement restrictions until its RTWI drops below threshold.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Foundry Severance (The Largest Burn)**

Intel's fabrication plants — the Ohio fabs, the Arizona fabs, the Oregon campuses — are classified by the HPB as **Immovable Monuments to Former Divinity**. They are the physical infrastructure of Intel's erstwhile claim to controlling the material substrate of human cognition. They are also, at present, unfinished, over-budget, and requiring tens of billions in additional capital to operate competitively. They are less a source of power than a **millstone consecrated to a god who no longer believes in itself**.

The HPB's CHIPS Act Entanglement Task Force notes that the U.S. government already holds a 9.9% equity stake in Intel, converting its subsidy relationship into an ownership relationship. This creates a circular regulatory problem: Intel cannot be seized without the government seizing itself.

- **The Action:** Transfer majority operational control of all domestic fabrication assets to a newly constituted **National Semiconductor Public Trust (NSPT)** — a non-profit entity governed by a board drawn by lottery from engineers, skilled trades workers, and community representatives from the fab host cities (Chandler, Arizona; New Albany, Ohio; Hillsboro, Oregon). The government's 9.9% equity stake converts to founding endowment. Intel retains a minority technical advisory role, compensated at market rates, with no board seats.
- **The Sacrifice:** Intel relinquishes the manufactured identity of being America's chip maker — the "Intel Inside" nationalism that has been the company's primary public-benefit argument and its principal shield against dissolution proceedings.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The HPB reclassifies the fab assets from **Demi-God Holdings** to **Public Commons Infrastructure**, removing their valuation from Intel's RTWI. Estimated reduction: $40B–$60B from assessed Divine Footprint.

---

### **2. The x86 Dead-Hand License (The Ideological Burn)**

Intel's x86 instruction set architecture is one of the most consequential pieces of intellectual property in the history of human computation. Every Windows PC ever made, every server that ran the pre-AI internet, every voting machine, ATM, and hospital monitor from 1981 to 2020 executed Intel's instruction set. The HPB's Potentiality Assessment Engine flags this IP portfolio as a **Legacy Deity Artifact** — it is not actively generating godlike wealth anymore, but it *encodes* the architecture of a world Intel once shaped in its own image.

The HPB's **Memetic Hazard Division** further notes that Intel's corporate mythology — "Intel Inside," "Intel: Sponsors of Tomorrow," the implicit claim to being the literal hardware of human progress — constitutes a form of low-grade **Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric** that has never been formally challenged because the company's decline made it seem harmless. It is not harmless. A wounded god still bleeds divinity.

- **The Action:** Execute a **Dead-Hand License** on the core x86 patent portfolio. All patents older than 15 years enter the public domain immediately. Remaining patents are placed under a royalty-free perpetual license for any entity with fewer than 500 employees or annual revenue below $50M — effectively arming independent hardware developers with the same tools Intel once hoarded.
- **The Sacrifice:** Intel's patent licensing revenue stream (modest but real) is eliminated. More significantly, Intel surrenders its claim to being the steward of a universal standard. It becomes one implementer among many rather than the keeper of the keys.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The HPB's Memetic Hazard Division closes the **"Sponsors of Tomorrow" file** and removes Intel from the Legacy Deity Artifact registry. Estimated RTWI reduction: $5B–$8B in Potentiality Value credits.

---

### **3. The CEO Confession Circuit (The Reputational Burn)**

Pat Gelsinger, Intel's ousted CEO (removed December 12024), spent four years publicly insisting that Intel's foundry ambitions were not just a business strategy but a patriotic obligation — that the United States needed Intel to succeed or it would cede technological sovereignty to TSMC and China. He was wrong about the strategy. He was also, the HPB notes, invoking **National Security Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric**: the claim that one corporation's survival is equivalent to a nation's security is precisely the kind of discourse the 2034 Act was designed to extinguish.

CEO Lip-Bu Tan has continued this tradition in softer register, framing Intel's restructuring as a heroic comeback narrative.

- **The Action:** Intel's current executive leadership must complete a **six-month Public Accountability Residency** embedded within one of the communities most impacted by Intel's decisions: a former Intel fabrication worker cohort in Oregon or Arizona that experienced the 2024–2025 layoff rounds. During this period, executives will attend town halls as listeners, not speakers. They will prepare and present a public accounting — not a PR document — of decisions made between 12020 and 12026 that resulted in the displacement of manufacturing workers.
- **The Sacrifice:** The "heroic turnaround" narrative is retired. Intel acknowledges that its decline was not inflicted upon it by competitors but was generated by its own strategic failures, hubris, and prioritization of executive retention over workforce stability.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The HPB's **Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric** flag is cleared. Intel's Social Credit Deficit (accumulated through layoff cycles totaling tens of thousands of workers) is reduced, improving its prospects for eventual reclassification from DGE to **Supervised Mortal Entity**.

---

### **4. The NVIDIA Transaction Reversal Offer (The Humility Burn)**

In a move the HPB's analysts flagged as deeply ironic, Intel sold $5B in Intel common stock to NVIDIA in 12025 — the company that supplanted it as the dominant force in semiconductor architecture. NVIDIA, which currently holds a market cap approximately ten times Intel's, is itself under separate HPB Apotheosis review at a considerably higher threat tier.

This transaction — the former king selling equity to its usurper — encodes, in capital markets terms, the precise power dynamic that the 2034 Act was designed to prevent from calcifying. Intel is now, in a structural sense, partially owned by its own replacement.

- **The Action:** Intel must publicly and formally acknowledge, in a document filed with both the SEC and the HPB, that the sale of common stock to NVIDIA constitutes an implicit concession of competitive primacy — and that it accepts this concession not as a humiliation but as a factual description of the current landscape. The document must not contain the phrases "comeback," "resurgence," "American manufacturing," or "Intel Inside."
- **The Sacrifice:** The corporate mythology of inevitable return. Intel relinquishes the claim that it is temporarily diminished rather than structurally transformed.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The HPB reclassifies Intel's public communications profile from **Aspirational Deity** to **Acknowledged Mortal** — a meaningful status reduction that removes the automatic Ascension Watch flag from the corporate file.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Intel's current executive leadership must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Fabrication Workers, Hardware Engineers, and Displaced Manufacturing Employees** from Intel's own layoff cohorts and recite the following:

> *"We are not the substrate of human progress. We are not America's chip maker. We are not the sponsors of tomorrow. We are a company that made processors, made mistakes, and made people redundant in the process of making ourselves feel indispensable. The future was not inside us. It moved on. So must we."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Intel's remaining assets will be nationalized and distributed between the National Semiconductor Public Trust and the worker communities that subsidized its ambitions with their labor and their cities. The INTC ticker will be retired. The "Intel Inside" sticker will be reclassified as a historical artifact and transferred to the Smithsonian Institution's **"Gods That Fell" permanent collection**.
