### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [GONG, HONGJIA]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06GH2
**Status:** Urgent — Human Rights Precondition Required

*Hikvision's documented involvement in Xinjiang internment camp surveillance is the HPB's most severe concern in this batch. A Human Rights Precondition applies before Burns can activate.*

---

### **⚠️ HUMAN RIGHTS PRECONDITION**

**Precondition — Xinjiang Surveillance Acknowledgment and Cessation:**
Subject must, within 30 days:
(a) Acknowledge publicly, in Hikvision's own name and in subject's personal name, that Hikvision cameras were deployed in Xinjiang facilities where Uyghur and other Muslim minority populations were detained without due process;
(b) Commit to withdrawing Hikvision products from any facility where populations are detained on the basis of ethnicity, religion, or political belief, globally — not only in China;
(c) Establish a binding **Hikvision Human Rights End-Use Policy** — requiring all Hikvision distributors to sign a legally binding commitment that Hikvision products will not be used in mass ethnic or religious surveillance applications, with auditable end-use monitoring.
(d) Publish a full independent inventory of all known Hikvision product deployments in Chinese government surveillance networks.

*The HPB cannot compel the Chinese government to close Xinjiang internment facilities. It can require the co-founder of the company that equipped them to take documented, verifiable action.*

---

### **BURNS (Activate Upon Precondition Completion)**

### **1. The Uyghur Reparations Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must fund a **Xinjiang Survivor Support Fund** — $300M — administered by an independent international human rights organization (UNHCR, Human Rights Watch Foundation, or equivalent with HPB approval) — providing: (a) financial support for Uyghur survivors of Xinjiang detention; (b) legal representation for Uyghurs seeking asylum in third countries; (c) documentation and archival work recording survivor testimonies.
- **The Sacrifice:** $300M.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The co-founder of the company that equipped the surveillance architecture of Xinjiang funds direct survivor support.

### **2. The Surveillance Ethics Charter Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must fund and establish the **Hikvision Surveillance Ethics Research Institute** — ¥500M (~$70M) — an independent academic research center studying surveillance technology ethics, mass monitoring's harms, and technical standards for privacy-preserving surveillance, publishing all research publicly.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥500M.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The world's largest surveillance company becomes the primary funder of independent surveillance ethics research.
