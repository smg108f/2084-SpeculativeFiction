### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [OTTO, MICHAEL]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06MO
**Status:** Moderate

Subject's environmental philanthropy is among the more creditable records in Batch 5. Burns are calibrated accordingly — scaling up the existing work and addressing the supply chain gap.

---

### **1. The Textile Supply Chain Justice Burn**

Otto Group's global textile sourcing requires specific labor accountability beyond existing voluntary commitments.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund €100M for the **Otto Supply Chain Justice Fund**: (a) binding living wage commitments for all first-tier Otto Group suppliers — verified by independent third parties, with results published annually; (b) a fund to pay the wage gap between current supplier wages and a living wage in Bangladesh, Vietnam, and China for Otto Group suppliers during the transition period; (c) an **Otto Supplier Development Program** — supporting smaller suppliers in meeting environmental and labor standards rather than dropping them.
- **The Sacrifice:** €100M.

### **2. The Environmental Legacy Endowment**

The WWF work and Foundation are genuine. The HPB invites their formalization and scale-up.

- **The Action:** Subject must endow €75M for the **Michael Otto Environmental Legacy Fund** — permanently independent of Otto Group corporate interests, governed by an external board, focused on rewilding and water systems in Germany and Northern Europe. The beaver reintroduction is the template: species restoration, watershed rehabilitation, and public access to nature.
- **The Sacrifice:** €75M.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Bangladesh Garment Workers, German Rewilding Scientists, and Otto Group Sustainability Officers**:

> *"My father sold shoes from a catalogue. I built a company that sells everything, everywhere. The supply chain touches millions of people sewing things in Asia. The living wage commitment is overdue. The supplier development program acknowledges that dropping them is not the answer — raising them is. The environmental fund is the beaver project, scaled. Germany can have its rivers back. Europe can have its wetlands back. These are not impossible things."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** Subject will be assigned to **FSS OTTO** — a vessel crewed by Otto Group textile suppliers from Bangladesh and Vietnam, sailing to the ports of Hamburg and Bremen where Otto Group's logistics hubs are located, anchoring in the harbor and operating as a floating garment factory with living wages and transparent working conditions — demonstrating what the supply chain looks like when the worker is in the same harbor as the consumer.
