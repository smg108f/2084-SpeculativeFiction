### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [RELX]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09RELX
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

RELX has been classified as a **Knowledge Tollgate** and **Academic Enclosure** entity under Sections 7(c) and 11(a) of the Demi-God Act. The company's primary business model — charging for access to publicly funded research — constitutes an ongoing extraction from the academic commons that has no legitimate analog in 2084's knowledge economy. Recertification requires structural dismantling of the enclosure mechanism.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Open Science Mandate (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies Elsevier's journal portfolio — specifically the paywalled access to publicly funded research — as a **Public Knowledge Appropriation** that must be permanently reversed.

- **The Action:** All Elsevier journal archives must be transferred to the **Global Open Science Repository**, a publicly governed, freely accessible database. RELX may continue to operate editorial and review management services for journals as a fee-for-service platform — it simply may no longer charge readers for access to content it did not produce.
- **The Sacrifice:** Elimination of approximately £3.5B in annual subscription revenue — roughly 50% of total revenue.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** RELX transitions from **Academic Encloser** to **Science Infrastructure Operator** — still viable; no longer extractive.

### **2. The LexisNexis Public Access Protocol (The Legal Burn)**

LexisNexis's role as the primary legal research database in common-law jurisdictions means that access to legal knowledge — understanding what the law says — is gated behind a subscription paywall. The HPB classifies this as **Judicial Knowledge Privatization**: citizens have a right to understand the law; RELX should not profit from that right.

- **The Action:** All statutory text, case law, and regulatory materials in LexisNexis must be published via a free-tier Public Legal Access portal. RELX may charge for premium analytics, citation tracking, and AI-assisted research tools — it may not charge for access to raw legal text.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of approximately £800M in institutional subscription revenue from law firms and courts.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** LexisNexis becomes a **Legal Analytics Service** rather than a **Law Access Gatekeeper**.

### **3. The Open-Access Lobbying Reversal (The Political Burn)**

RELX has spent decades lobbying against mandatory open-access requirements that would eliminate its subscription model, effectively using private capital to maintain an information enclosure against the public interest.

- **The Action:** RELX must publicly withdraw all lobbying positions opposing open-access mandates, dissolve its academic publishing lobbying infrastructure, and fund a £500M **Open Science Transition Endowment** to support institutions migrating to open-access models.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of political influence over the regulatory environment that sustains its business model.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The **Regulatory Capture** classification is lifted.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, RELX's board and CEO must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Graduate Researchers from institutions that cannot afford Elsevier subscriptions** and recite the following:

> _"The science was not ours. The law was not ours. We built a toll road through the public's own knowledge and charged the public to pass. We acknowledge that access to truth should not require a subscription. We open the gates. We remain as maintenance workers for the infrastructure that humanity built — compensated fairly, entitled to nothing more."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. RELX's journal and legal archives will be nationalized and published in their entirety under permanent Creative Commons licensing. RELX executives will be assigned as content moderators for the newly public repository — a role that requires reading, in full, every paper that was previously locked behind the paywall they maintained. The HPB estimates this will take approximately 400 years, which is more time than the journals have existed.
