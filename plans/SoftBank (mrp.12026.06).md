### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [SOFTBANK]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-30SB
**Status:** Urgent — Civilizational Investment Governance Order Active

SoftBank's Critical Ascension Risk classification requires governance frameworks commensurate with the civilizational scale of its AI investment decisions. Masayoshi Son's individual wealth and influence independently approach HPB threshold; the organizational Burn Schedule addresses both.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The AI Impact Assessment Board (The Civilizational Bet Accountability Burn)**

Son's $65B OpenAI investment and Stargate chairmanship make him the primary private patron of potentially transformative AI. Decisions of this consequence require governance structures that extend beyond SoftBank's board of directors.

- **The Action:** SoftBank must establish an independent **AI Societal Impact Assessment Board**: a 9-member body including AI safety researchers, social scientists, ethicists, international development economists, and public representatives (selected by lottery from jurisdictions where SoftBank operates). The board reviews all proposed AI investments above $1B for anticipated societal consequences and publishes its assessment before SoftBank's investment decision is finalized. SoftBank may proceed with board-reviewed investments even against the board's recommendation — but must publish a written response to board concerns.
- **The Sacrifice:** Delay on large AI investment decisions pending board review; public accountability for investment rationale; potential public opposition to specific investments.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Conversion from "Civilizational Bet Sovereign" to "Accountable AI Patron" — a status the HPB considers commensurate with the stakes of Son's investment thesis.

### **2. The WeWork Accountability Protocol (The Track Record Humility Burn)**

Son's $47B WeWork valuation — which ultimately resulted in a $40B loss — demonstrates that confident conviction about transformative futures can be expensively wrong. The HPB requires that SoftBank's AI investment thesis be subject to ongoing external reality-checking.

- **The Action:** SoftBank must establish a **Vision Fund Reality Audit**: an annual independent review of SoftBank's AI investment portfolio by a panel that includes AI researchers not affiliated with SoftBank portfolio companies. The review assesses whether portfolio companies are achieving their stated technological and societal impact milestones. The findings are published. Son must respond publicly to any finding that a major portfolio company is failing to achieve its stated civilizational impact.
- **The Sacrifice:** Public accountability for portfolio underperformance; potential reputational cost of published critical assessments.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Conversion from "Unaccountable Vision Fund" to "Monitored AI Investment Portfolio" — a status that enables the HPB to reduce monitoring intensity as the Vision Fund Reality Audit establishes a track record.

### **3. The Stargate Public Benefit Commitment (The Infrastructure Sovereignty Burn)**

Son's chairmanship of the $500B Stargate AI infrastructure project creates a governance conflict: a private individual chairs the buildout of national AI infrastructure. The HPB requires that this governance gap be structurally addressed.

- **The Action:** Son must recuse himself from Stargate governance decisions relating to procurement from SoftBank portfolio companies, and must commit to publishing a **Stargate Conflict of Interest Registry** — a real-time disclosure of any Stargate decisions that benefit or could benefit SoftBank's investment portfolio. An independent ethics monitor must review all such decisions before they are finalized.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced influence over Stargate procurement decisions that affect SoftBank portfolio value; public accountability for conflict-of-interest situations.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Conversion from "Conflicted Infrastructure Chairman" to "Disclosed Conflict Actor" — a status the HPB considers appropriate for the chairman of a $500B national AI infrastructure project.
