### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [DANG, YANBAO]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06DY
**Status:** Urgent

Subject controls Jinneng Holding Group, a Shanxi coal mining and energy conglomerate. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Jinneng Coal Transition Burn**

Coal is the wealth source. The HPB requires a binding transition plan and a climate reparations fund.

- **The Action:** Subject must commit — personally, as controlling shareholder — to a **Jinneng Clean Energy Transition Roadmap**: (a) no new greenfield coal mining projects after 12026; (b) Jinneng will close or convert 30% of active coal mine capacity to alternative uses (methane capture, geothermal, or coal mine solar) by 12030; (c) a ¥5B (~$700M) **Shanxi Clean Energy Transition Fund** — directed at renewable energy development in Shanxi Province, funded personally by subject, not from Jinneng corporate — dedicated to solar, wind, and pumped hydro development in Shanxi. Additionally, subject must fund a ¥1B (~$140M) **Shanxi Coal Miner Transition Fund** providing retraining, income support, and early retirement packages for Jinneng miners whose jobs are eliminated by the transition.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥5B + ¥1B; foregoing future coal mine development.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Shanxi's coal wealth is redirected into Shanxi's clean energy future.

### **2. The Dang Foundation Burn**

Zero documented philanthropy is the HPB's secondary concern.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Dang Yanbao Foundation** — ¥2B (~$280M) — focused on: (a) Shanxi air quality monitoring and health programs (coalfield communities face elevated rates of respiratory disease); (b) Shanxi miner occupational health programs including black lung treatment and prevention; (c) Chinese clean energy research scholarships.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥2B.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The coal baron is the primary funder of Shanxi's coal-related public health remediation.
