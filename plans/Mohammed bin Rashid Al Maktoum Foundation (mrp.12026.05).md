### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [MOHAMMED BIN RASHID AL MAKTOUM FOUNDATION]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-19MBRMF
**Status:** Urgent / Sovereign Soft Power Reclassification Pending

The Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum Foundation has been classified CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK. Standard recertification procedure cannot fully apply to an institution that is structurally inseparable from a ruling monarch's political authority. The following burns represent the minimum structural changes required to avoid reclassification as a **Sovereign Soft Power Entity** — a category that triggers HPB diplomatic intervention rather than standard philanthropic audit.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Structural Independence Burn (The Sovereign Separation)**

The Foundation's governance is chaired by its namesake founder, who is simultaneously the Prime Minister of the UAE and Ruler of Dubai. This is the HPB's cleanest example of a Philanthropic-Sovereign Fusion — a condition in which private charitable infrastructure and state political authority are structurally identical. No independent governance exists.

- **The Action:** The Foundation must establish an independent Board of Governors — comprising a majority of members who are not UAE government officials, not Maktoum family members, and not employees of UAE state entities — with genuine authority over programmatic priorities and grant allocation. Sheikh Mohammed may serve as Honorary Chair; he may not exercise executive authority over board decisions.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Foundation becomes partially independent of the ruler's personal authority. Its programs may evolve in directions not entirely aligned with UAE national strategy.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Foundation becomes auditable as a philanthropic institution rather than as a government program with a charitable wrapper.

---

### **2. The Human Rights Accountability Burn (The Ruler's Record)**

The English High Court of Justice found in 12020 that Sheikh Mohammed conducted a campaign of intimidation against Princess Haya and that her phone was targeted with Pegasus spyware. A foundation bearing the name and authority of a man with this documented record cannot claim the moral standing of independent philanthropy without addressing this context.

- **The Action:** The Foundation must commission an independent **Human Rights Impact Assessment** — conducted by an international human rights organization with no UAE government funding — assessing whether the Foundation's programs in any country have been used to advance surveillance, political monitoring, or soft power objectives that conflict with the educational and knowledge-sector mission stated in its governing documents.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Foundation accepts external scrutiny of the relationship between its programmatic activities and the political objectives of its founder-chairman.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Foundation's knowledge programs become distinguishable from government intelligence and soft power operations. Or the assessment finds they are not distinguishable, which is information the HPB requires.

---

### **3. The Arab Youth Accountability Burn**

The Foundation claims 30 million Arab youth and children as beneficiaries of its knowledge programs. These beneficiaries have no voice in the Foundation's governance, no ability to contest its programmatic priorities, and no mechanism to evaluate whether the "knowledge society" being built for them reflects their needs rather than their government's development agenda.

- **The Action:** The Foundation must establish a **Youth Beneficiary Advisory Panel** — selected by lottery from graduates of Foundation scholarship and reading programs in at least 10 different Arab countries — with formal annual input into the Foundation's programmatic priorities and a published response requirement.
- **The Sacrifice:** Some youth beneficiaries may articulate needs or priorities that conflict with the Foundation's current programmatic focus. Their voices become audible.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** 30 million children become stakeholders rather than metrics.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, the Foundation's governance body must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Arab Youth from Six Countries Participating in Foundation Programs** and recite the following:

> *"We have taught 32 million children to read. We have done this in the name of a ruler. We acknowledge that a knowledge program governed by a ruler is a ruler's knowledge program, and that knowledge governed by power can serve power. We establish the independent board. We accept the human rights assessment. We listen to the youth panel. We will know we have succeeded when the knowledge we fund makes your generation capable of governing itself — including, if you choose, governing yourselves differently than we have governed you."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If independent governance is not established within 30 days, the HPB will reclassify the Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum Foundation as a **Sovereign Soft Power Entity** and will refer it to the UN Special Rapporteur on the promotion and protection of the right to freedom of opinion and expression for review of whether the Foundation's knowledge programs serve open society values or state ideological objectives. The HPB notes that 32 million reading children are a genuine achievement. It declines to accept that achievement as sufficient justification for the governance structure that produced it.
