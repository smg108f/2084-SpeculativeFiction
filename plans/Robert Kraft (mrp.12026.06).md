- **The Action:** Subject must establish the **Patriots Community Equity Fund** — $150M — dedicated to: (a) youth sports access in Massachusetts, New Hampshire, Maine, Rhode Island, Vermont, and Connecticut — specifically, full-cost youth football, soccer, and athletics programs for low-income youth in New England; (b) Foxborough and surrounding towns community infrastructure — the Gillette Stadium footprint's host communities; (c) anti-human trafficking programs in Massachusetts and Florida, with focus on supporting survivors of trafficking at spas and massage parlors — the HPB requires this specifically given the 2019 incident context, not as an admission of guilt but as proportionate acknowledgment of the issue.
- **The Sacrifice:** $150M.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Patriots' community investment is documented, proportionate, and specifically addresses the harm category raised by the 2019 incident.
