### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [VON BECHTOLSHEIM, ANDREAS]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06AvB
**Status:** Moderate

Subject co-built foundational internet infrastructure and accumulated $8.6B without commensurate public contribution. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 180 days.

---

### **1. The Open Computing Infrastructure Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must fund $150M for the **Von Bechtolsheim Open Computing Fund**: (a) open-source networking and computing infrastructure — funding the projects and organizations that maintain the open-source foundations (Linux, OpenStack, RISC-V, FreeBSD) that Sun and Arista's proprietary products were built upon; (b) university computing infrastructure grants — high-performance networking equipment for universities in low-income countries that cannot afford Arista-level infrastructure; (c) an annual **SUN Prize** (named for his first company) — $10M to the team that makes the most significant open-source contribution to computing infrastructure in a given year.
- **The Sacrifice:** $150M.

### **2. The German Technical Education Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must endow $75M for German technical university computing programs — specifically the TU Munich and related institutions near his Bavarian origins — building a **Von Bechtolsheim Computer Engineering Institute** that trains the next generation of hardware and networking engineers.
- **The Sacrifice:** $75M.
