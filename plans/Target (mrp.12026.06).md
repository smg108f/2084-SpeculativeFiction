### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [TARGET CORPORATION]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-12TGT
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification

Target Corporation's classification as MARGINAL ASCENSION RISK reflects market capitalization rather than acute behavioral risk. Recertification is nonetheless required given the $80B+ threshold crossing and the company's demonstrated instability as a cultural battleground entity. The HPB recommends Voluntary Recertification to stabilize the company's Influence Vector before external actors use it to manufacture civil unrest.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Worker Compensation Rectification (The Labor Burn)**

The HPB classifies Target's Fair Workweek violations — for which it paid a $20M New York City settlement — as a **Systematic Labor Extraction** pattern affecting its most economically vulnerable employees.

- **The Action:** Implement binding fair scheduling guarantees at all 1,900 stores, provide a retroactive $500M wage justice fund for all hourly employees employed over the past five years, and transfer 5% of annual free cash flow to an Employee Ownership Trust.
- **The Sacrifice:** Approximately $500M immediate cost; $300M+ annual ongoing through ownership trust.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Target's Labor Exploitation Index drops to Tier 3 (Acceptable); HPB Social Stability Score improves by 18 points.

### **2. The DEI Commitment Resolution (The Cultural Burn)**

The HPB classifies Target's partial retreat from its Pride merchandise commitments under boycott pressure as a **Failed Incomplete Pivot** — which satisfies no constituency and demonstrates that the company's values are contingent on commercial pressure.

- **The Action:** Target must either (a) fully commit to its original $2B Black business support pledge and LGBTQ+ inclusive merchandising on a permanent, publicly verifiable basis, OR (b) fully withdraw all identity-based branding commitments and stop using marginalized communities as marketing segments. The Bureau is indifferent to which choice is made. It is not indifferent to continued inconsistency.
- **The Sacrifice:** Either continued boycott risk (option a) or alienation of progressive consumers (option b). The Bureau notes there is no comfortable path.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Cultural Battlefield classification is resolved. Target becomes legible to consumers and the HPB can close the Influence Vector file.

### **3. The Supply Chain Transparency Mandate (The Structural Burn)**

The HPB classifies Target's private label supply chain, which manufactures goods in jurisdictions with limited labor oversight, as an **Externalized Labor Cost** that inflates margins while obscuring human cost.

- **The Action:** Full Tier 1-3 supplier transparency by 2025, binding living wage requirements for all direct suppliers, and an annual third-party audit published in full.
- **The Sacrifice:** Estimated 2-4% margin compression as supply chain costs normalize.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Target exits the HPB's Supply Chain Exploitation watchlist.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Target's board must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Target store hourly workers** and recite the following:

> _"We used your identities to sell products and retreated when it cost us. We used your labor to build margins and scheduled you without consideration for your lives. We accept that a corporation's values are measured by what it does when it is uncomfortable, not when it is convenient."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Target's store network will be transferred to a **National Retail Public Trust**, with each location governed by its local municipal council and staffed under federal employment standards. Former shareholders will receive **Retail Access Credits** redeemable for one (1) complimentary shopping visit to what was previously their investment.
