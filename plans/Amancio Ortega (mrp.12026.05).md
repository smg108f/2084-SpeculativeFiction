### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ORTEGA GAONA, AMANCIO]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-14AO
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject Ortega's combined Inditex equity and Pontegadea real estate holdings exceed the Demi-God threshold by a factor of 134, and his Phantasim classification (age 90) does not reduce the Bureau's obligation to pursue recertification — the Ortega System will outlive its founder, and wealth that transfers intact is wealth that ascends. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Pontegadea Dissolution (The Hidden Empire Burn)**

The HPB classifies Pontegadea Inversiones as a **Class-I Opacity Hazard**: a private wealth vehicle of $15–18B that owns prime commercial real estate across multiple sovereign nations, generates passive rental income from major corporations, and publishes no accounts, no valuations, and no public-facing documentation of any kind. It is, in HPB terms, a **Shadow Landlordship** — the extraction of rent from the economies of London, New York, and Madrid by a single private entity with no democratic accountability.

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer the full Pontegadea portfolio — all commercial real estate holdings across all jurisdictions — to a newly constituted **Public Urban Commons Trust**, governed by elected representatives from the municipalities in which the properties sit. Tenants' lease agreements remain intact; rental income flows to city infrastructure funds rather than Partex.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses approximately $15–18B in assets and all future rental income streams. Pontegadea ceases to exist as a private entity.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** By converting the shadow landlordship into a democratic commons, Subject removes the HPB's most intractable audit obstacle. The properties remain economically productive; the extraction is simply redirected from a single Phantasim subject to the millions of citizens who live and work in proximity to them. Subject's **Opacity Score** drops from Class-I to Baseline Mortal.

### **2. The Inditex Control Surrender (The Fabric Burn)**

Ortega's 59% controlling stake in Inditex is the engine of the Demi-God wealth loop. At current valuations it exceeds $110B. More critically, it is the structural mechanism that produces 840 million garments per year — a planetary extraction rate that the HPB's Environmental Potentiality Index classifies as a **Compounding Biosphere Debt**. The HPB does not merely object to the concentration of this wealth; it objects to the incentive structure that the concentration creates. A controlling shareholder who maximizes dividend yield maximizes production volume. Maximizing production volume maximizes planetary harm. The Ortega stake is not a passive financial instrument. It is a vote, cast every quarter, for more garments and more extraction.

- **The Action:** Subject must divest the full Ortega/Partex controlling stake in Inditex into a **Planetary Fabric Trust**, a new legal entity whose mandate is to wind down Inditex's annual garment production by a minimum of 5% per year for twenty years, redirect supply chain relationships toward cooperative ownership structures for garment workers in Bangladesh, Vietnam, Morocco, and Turkey, and fund textile recycling infrastructure at scale. Subject retains no governance rights. Marta Ortega Pérez is similarly prohibited from assuming the divested stake.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject surrenders control of the company he founded, the company that is the source of all primary wealth, and the dynasty's dominant inheritance vehicle. The Ortega name remains on the founding story; it no longer appears on the shareholder register.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Eliminates the primary wealth engine. Reduces Subject's estimated net worth from $134B to residual non-controlling holdings. Converts the Inditex system from a Demi-God Dividend Machine to a **Managed Descent Entity** accountable to planetary rather than shareholder interests.

### **3. The Visibility Oath (The Phantom Burns Last)**

The HPB has no mechanism to audit what it cannot see. Ortega's radical personal invisibility — no interviews, no public appearances, no photographs since retirement, no social presence — is not merely a lifestyle choice. At Demi-God wealth levels, invisibility is a **Structural Defense**. You cannot negotiate with, regulate, or hold accountable a subject who does not publicly exist. The Bureau classifies this as **Deliberate Audit Evasion by Absence**, a Class-II Opacity violation distinct from active concealment but functionally identical in its effects.

- **The Action:** Subject must submit to a minimum of four **HPB Public Accountability Sessions** per year — open, recorded, broadcast via Public Commons Media — in which Subject answers questions from a panel of randomly selected citizens regarding the operations of the Ortega wealth system, the environmental record of Inditex's supply chain, and the disposition of assets under recertification. Sessions are not optional. They are not mediated by lawyers. They are conducted in Galician, Spanish, and English.
- **The Sacrifice:** The invisibility. The privacy that has been the subject's most jealously guarded asset for six decades. The HPB recognizes this may be the most painful burn on the schedule.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** A subject who can be seen, questioned, and answered is a subject who is accountable. Accountability is the foundational condition of Mortal status. This burn does not reduce wealth — it reduces the **protective function** of invisibility, making all other burns enforceable rather than theoretical.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Garment Workers, Textile Recyclers, and Galician Fishermen** and recite the following:

> *"I left school at thirteen because the world told me I had no other choice. I spent sixty years proving the world wrong by building the largest fast-fashion system in human history. I was not wrong to want more than poverty. I was wrong to build a machine that required the poverty of others to run. A delivery boy who becomes a billionaire is not a miracle. It is a diagnosis. I am returning what was never only mine."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Asset Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All Inditex equity and Pontegadea holdings will be seized under the **Planetary Extraction Emergency Powers Act**, Partex will be dissolved as a family holding structure, and Subject will be reclassified as a **Demi-God Entity: Passive Extraction Class**. Given Subject's Phantasim designation and extreme preference for anonymity, the Bureau will — with considerable irony — decline to announce this reclassification publicly. He will simply become, legally, what he has always appeared to be: invisible.
