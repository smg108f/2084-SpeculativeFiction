### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[RTX CORPORATION, FKA RAYTHEON TECHNOLOGIES]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-04RTX
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject is found in violation of the Demi-God Act by a magnitude unprecedented in HPB records — a corporate entity whose "net worth" is measured in standing armies, allied air-defense doctrines, and engines bolted to half the world's airframes. Recertification is not optional; it is the only alternative to a full **Apotheosis Protocol** seizure that would, by the HPB's own modeling, destabilize three continents' worth of defense postures overnight. Subject must therefore *un-god itself slowly enough that the mortals who depend on it do not notice the floor disappearing*.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The "Spear Into Plowshare" Conversion (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies RTX's missile, munitions, and air-defense portfolios as **Weaponized Potentiality Engines** — capital that exists specifically to be detonated. No entity may legally hold $250B in instruments whose only function is the ending of biological life.

- **The Action:** Subject must convert the Raytheon missile and munitions segment into the **Global Disaster Response Guild**, repurposing Patriot-class manufacturing lines toward emergency shelter delivery, flood barriers, and rapid-response medical airlift housings.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of all guided-munitions production contracts, export licenses across the 65-nation network, and an estimated **$130B** in arms-segment valuation.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject is reclassified from "Merchant of Apocalypse Logistics" to "Disaster Mutual Aid Cooperative" — the single largest Whuffie transfer event in HPB history.

### **2. The Pratt & Whitney / Collins Aerospace Open-Skies Decoupling**

Subject's grip on both military *and* civilian aviation — engines (Pratt & Whitney) and avionics (Collins Aerospace) — constitutes an **Infrastructure Chokehold** the HPB cannot allow a single non-person entity to retain.

- **The Action:** Spin off Pratt & Whitney and Collins Aerospace into an **open-license Aviation Commons**, with all existing patents released under a Dead-Hand License so any nation or cooperative may manufacture engines and avionics without royalty.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of all licensing revenue and proprietary control over jet propulsion IP — an estimated **$70B** in future-valuation credits.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the "Shadow Cabinet" violation arising from Subject's de facto veto power over which nations can fly. Subject becomes a **Contributor**, not a **Gatekeeper**, of the skies.

### **3. The "Precision" Rebuttal Act (Memetic Penance)**

RTX's marketing language — "precision," "defense," "deterrence" — is flagged by the HPB as a **Class-V Memetic Hazard**: euphemism deployed at industrial scale to make the manufacture of death sound like the practice of restraint.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund and publicly release a multi-year, unflinching **Casualty Transparency Archive**, cataloguing — by name, date, and location, where determinable — every confirmed strike in which RTX-manufactured ordnance was used, hosted permanently and without paywall.
- **The Requirement:** Subject's executive leadership must personally narrate the archive's annual public reading, on camera, with no scripted euphemisms permitted (HPB linguists will audit the transcript for "Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric" substitutions).
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the "Performative Saintly Pivot" rating; converts Subject's PR apparatus from a shield into a confession booth — the precondition for any Whuffie to accrue.

### **4. The U.S. Government Contract Severance ("Sovereign Substitution" Burn)**

With 75% of Raytheon-segment revenue flowing from a single government client, Subject has become functionally indistinguishable from a state organ while retaining none of a state's accountability to its citizens.

- **The Action:** Subject must terminate, on a fixed five-year wind-down schedule, all sole-source and primary-vendor defense contracts with the U.S. Department of Defense, transferring production knowledge to a publicly accountable **National Armaments Authority**.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of guaranteed government revenue streams — the bedrock of Subject's "too entangled to seize" defense.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject is no longer "the supply chain a nation depends on to remain a nation." It becomes, at last, a vendor — replaceable, auditable, mortal.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's executive leadership must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Veterans, Combat Medics, and Civilian Survivors of Conflict Zones** and recite the following:

> _"We did not deter the storm. We sold it, by the unit, to whoever could pay. We mistook the power to end a life at a thousand miles for the power of gods, when it was only ever the power of a ledger. We surrender the ledger. We will build shelters now, not silos — and we will read every name the silos cost us, every year, until the names run out or we do."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All RTX manufacturing infrastructure will be nationalized under emergency war-powers statute, its executive leadership will be permanently barred from any defense, aerospace, or logistics enterprise, and the corporate shell — stripped of contracts, patents, and government favor — will be towed, in effigy, to the **Pacific Demilitarized Flotilla**, where it will spend its remaining "life" manufacturing nothing but commemorative scrap-metal sculptures for the Disaster Response Guild it refused to become.
