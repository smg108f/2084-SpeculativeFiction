### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT PALANTIR**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09PLTR
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

### **1. The Burn: Independent Civil Liberties Oversight Board**

**The Action:** Palantir funds and submits to a fully independent civil liberties oversight board with binding authority to review and publicly report on all government surveillance and law enforcement analytics deployments using its platforms, across every allied government contract.
**The Sacrifice:** Estimated $2B annually in contract-relationship friction and reduced government sales-cycle speed.
**Mortal Status Gain:** -15% RTWI.

### **2. The Burn: Domestic Surveillance Use Prohibition**

**The Action:** Palantir adopts a binding, publicly-verifiable contractual prohibition against any use of its platforms for domestic surveillance of citizens exercising lawful protest, assembly, or speech rights, with violations subject to independent audit and contract termination.
**The Sacrifice:** Estimated $500M in potential lost domestic contract revenue.
**Mortal Status Gain:** -12% RTWI.
