### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ARISTA NETWORKS]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-20AN
**Status:** Precautionary — AI Infrastructure Disclosure Required

Arista Networks' Probationary Saint classification reflects a clean record and a forward-looking monitoring requirement. Recertification is straightforward and requires minimal operational sacrifice relative to Arista's scale.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The AI Infrastructure Deployment Disclosure (The Transparency Burn)**

Arista's revenue is increasingly derived from AI hyperscaler data center networking. The HPB requires transparency about the scale and nature of this AI infrastructure contribution.

- **The Action:** Arista must publish an annual **AI Infrastructure Deployment Report** disclosing: (a) proportion of revenue from AI vs. general enterprise networking; (b) proportion of AI revenue from hyperscalers vs. other applications; (c) a qualitative assessment of the primary applications its networking equipment enables — training, inference, surveillance, research, consumer services. This report is submitted to the HPB and publicly released.
- **The Sacrifice:** Competitive intelligence exposure regarding revenue mix and client concentration.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Conversion from "Opaque AI Infrastructure Provider" to "Transparent AI Infrastructure Contributor" — enabling the HPB to assess Arista's proxy infrastructure risk accurately.

### **2. The Public Institution Networking Program (The Equity Burn)**

Arista's technology is deployed in some of the world's most advanced commercial data centers. Its technology is less accessible to hospitals, universities, research institutions, and public sector organizations with lower infrastructure budgets. The HPB requires a commitment to public-benefit networking access.

- **The Action:** Arista must establish a **Public Institution Networking Fund**: providing its enterprise networking products at 40% discount to qualifying public research universities, public hospitals, and government research agencies. The fund is capped at $100M annually in discount value.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M annually in foregone revenue; administrative complexity of qualifying and managing a nonprofit-tier program.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Reclassification from "Hyperscaler-Only Infrastructure" to "Public Benefit Infrastructure Partner" — a designation that earns Whuffie credit and provides a narrative counterweight to Arista's hyperscaler client concentration.

### **3. The EOS Security Vulnerability Protocol (The Software Safety Burn)**

Arista's EOS network operating system is deployed in thousands of enterprises and AI data centers. Its vulnerabilities are not merely commercial risks — they are infrastructure risks with public consequences. The HPB requires a public-interest vulnerability disclosure framework.

- **The Action:** Arista must publish a **Critical Infrastructure EOS Vulnerability Policy**: any vulnerability in EOS affecting networks used in critical infrastructure (hospitals, utilities, emergency services, financial systems) must be disclosed to those operators and to relevant national CERTs within 48 hours of internal confirmation, without commercial embargo. A dedicated EOS Security Hotline must be established for critical infrastructure operators.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of commercial patch release exclusivity for critical infrastructure operators; potential reputational exposure from disclosed vulnerabilities.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Conversion from "Commercial Software Vendor" to "Critical Infrastructure Security Partner" — a category that earns HPB commendation and reduces monitoring intensity.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 University IT Directors and Public Hospital Network Engineers** and recite the following:

> *"We build the switches that connect the world's most powerful computers. We do this well. We have not thought carefully about the responsibility that comes with this. The disclosure report will be published annually. The public institution discount is established. The vulnerability protocol is active. We make infrastructure. Infrastructure has obligations to the public it serves, not only to the customers who paid for it."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the Apotheosis Protocol. All Arista networking equipment deployed in HPB-critical infrastructure categories will be subject to mandatory open-source firmware requirements, enabling public sector operators to audit and maintain the systems themselves. Arista will discover that the cost of maintaining closed-source EOS in the face of mandatory openness requirements is substantially greater than the disclosure protocol it declined.
