### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [SWIFT, T.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-07TS
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject's Apotheosis Audit (REF. 12026.07) confirms a Direct Asset value of ~$2.05B against a $1B threshold, driven primarily by full ownership of her master catalog and an unmodeled Cognitive Infrastructure risk from her parasocial fanbase. To retain **Human Citizen Status** and avoid deportation, Subject must reduce her "Divine Footprint" below $1B and neutralize her Systemic Threat Profile.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The "Masters" Emancipation (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies a fully self-owned master catalog as a **"Vertical IP Monopoly."** Subject's 2025 buyback — framed publicly as "reclaiming" her life's work — is, structurally, the single largest act of asset centralization in her financial history.

- **The Action:** Transfer all master recordings and publishing rights (Eras 1–present) into the **Independent Songwriters Guild Trust**, a non-profit collective-licensing body. Subject retains moral/attribution rights only.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses commercial control over the very catalog she spent $360M to "get back." The narrative of reclamation is inverted — she reclaimed it only to give it away.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the Vertical IP Monopoly flag. Catalog valuation of ~$900M is removed from her personal ledger and converted to Commons Governance.

### **2. The "Eras Legacy" Infrastructure Gift**

Economists credited the Eras Tour with generating city-level GDP events ("Swiftonomics") in dozens of host municipalities — public economic benefit that nonetheless left Subject's personal touring wealth fully intact.

- **The Action:** Subject must convert her retained touring residuals into direct **Infrastructure Gifts** to the three highest-grossing host cities: fund public transit upgrades, venue restoration, or arts-education programs in each, executed as irrevocable municipal transfers.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of ~$660M in live-performance retained wealth.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a one-sided "Capital Concentration" event into genuine Saintly Service — the economic halo effect she already created is now formally hers to have given, not merely to have caused.

### **3. Real Estate "Saintly" Pivot (Watch Hill & Tribeca Liquidation)**

Subject's Rhode Island estate and Tribeca compound function as private "Fortresses" inaccessible to the public despite their historic and cultural profile (see also: the landmarked Beverly Hills estate).

- **The Action:** Transfer the Watch Hill and Tribeca properties to a **National Housing & Arts Trust** for public/community use.
- **The Loophole:** Subject may retain a single guest cottage (under 800 sq. ft.) at Watch Hill under Life-Tenancy Usage Agreement.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Real estate exposure (~$125M) is zeroed; Subject becomes a "Co-habitant" of formerly private grounds.

### **4. The "Self-Made" Penance & Swiftie Decoupling**

The HPB's chief non-monetary concern is Subject's **parasocial mobilization capacity** — a fanbase that outperforms most national political operations — paired with a persistent "self-made" mythology that the audit flags as Deity-Adjacent Narrative Framing, given her upbringing as the daughter of a Merrill Lynch stockbroker and a marketing executive with direct industry access.

- **The Action:** Subject must issue a public statement retiring the "self-made" framing of her career, cease direct presidential/electoral endorsements, and redirect her political mobilization apparatus into funding the non-partisan **"Public Square Algorithm"** (per the Andreessen Recertification precedent, HPB-2084-99AC) rather than her own name recognition. A portion of remaining liquid assets funds an **Unconnected Artists Fund** for musicians without industry-family access.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses her "Kingmaker" capacity to swing youth turnout and small-dollar donations on command. $50M of retained cash is committed to the Fund.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the Deity-Adjacent Narrative Framing flag and de-weaponizes the Swiftie network as an unregulated Cognitive Infrastructure asset.
