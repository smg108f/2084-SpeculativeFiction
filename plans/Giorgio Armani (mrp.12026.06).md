### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ARMANI, GIORGIO]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06GA2
**Status:** Urgent — Age Accommodation Applied (Advanced Age; Armani Foundation as Authorized Representative for Structural Burns)

*Given subject's advanced age, the HPB authorizes the Giorgio Armani Foundation to execute structural Burns on subject's behalf. Subject must personally sign off on the plan within 30 days.*

---

### **1. The Armani Supply Chain Sustainability Burn**

The Giorgio Armani brand family — including Armani Exchange — has supply chain sustainability gaps. Fur-free since 2016 is credited. The broader fashion supply chain requires binding commitments.

- **The Action:** Subject must commission and publish a **Full Giorgio Armani S.p.A. Supply Chain Sustainability Audit** — €50M — conducted by an independent environmental and labour audit firm, covering: (a) all Armani Exchange manufacturing facilities for living wage compliance, working conditions, and environmental standards; (b) all fabric and material supply chains for environmental impact (including water use, chemical discharge, and carbon footprint); (c) all packaging across all Armani lines for recyclability and waste targets. Results published publicly within 18 months. Any supplier found to be below international best-practice standards must remediate or be delisted.
- **The Sacrifice:** €50M audit cost + potential supplier disruption.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The last independent luxury house has a fully transparent, publicly disclosed supply chain baseline.

### **2. The Armani Foundation Legacy Burn**

The Armani Foundation is the vehicle for succession. The HPB asks for clarity on its public-purpose mandate.

- **The Action:** Subject must file — as part of the Foundation's governance documents — a **Armani Foundation Public Purpose Charter**: (a) a minimum of 25% of the Foundation's annual revenues from Armani S.p.A. dividends or assets must be disbursed to: Italian fashion industry worker welfare (including pensions and healthcare for retired Armani and Italian luxury supply chain workers), Italian cultural preservation, and global medical research; (b) annual disbursement reports published publicly; (c) an Independent Ethics Committee (majority of external members) overseeing all Foundation grants.
- **The Sacrifice:** 25% of Foundation revenue to public purposes in perpetuity.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The foundation that will preserve the Armani brand has a documented and enforceable public-purpose mandate — not merely a brand preservation vehicle.

### **3. The Armani Milan Community Burn**

Armani has been a defining force in Milan — culturally, commercially, and aesthetically. The HPB asks for direct Milan community investment.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund the **Armani Milan Community Fund** — €30M — for: (a) Milan public school arts and design education in underserved neighborhoods; (b) Milan fashion industry vocational training scholarships for first-generation students; (c) restoration of one significant Milan historic public space or building, agreed with the City of Milan.
- **The Sacrifice:** €30M.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The man who made Milan's reputation invests in the Milan that allowed him to do so.
