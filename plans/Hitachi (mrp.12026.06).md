### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [HITACHI]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-34HI
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification — Probationary Saint Pathway

Hitachi's Probationary Saint classification requires governance frameworks for its Lumada platform's accumulating critical infrastructure data, and transparency about the lock-in dynamics its digital services create for energy and industrial clients.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Lumada Data Portability Commitment (The Knowledge Sovereignty Burn)**

The Lumada platform accumulates operational data from critical infrastructure — power grids, railways, water systems, industrial facilities. This data, once accumulated, becomes a competitive moat that prevents clients from switching providers and gives Hitachi unique operational knowledge about the world's infrastructure. The HPB requires that this knowledge not be permanently captured.

- **The Action:** Hitachi must implement a **Lumada Data Portability Standard**: all operational data generated by client infrastructure and processed through Lumada is owned by the client, not by Hitachi. Clients must be able to export their full operational data history in a standardized, machine-readable format at any time, free of charge, including all AI model outputs and optimization recommendations generated from that data. Hitachi may retain anonymized aggregate data for platform improvement; it may not retain client-identifiable operational data after contract termination.
- **The Sacrifice:** Significant reduction in switching costs for Lumada clients; erosion of the knowledge moat that makes Lumada increasingly sticky; potential competitive disadvantage if clients use portability to evaluate alternatives.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Conversion from "Digital Infrastructure Lock-In" to "Data Sovereign Infrastructure Partner" — a status the HPB considers essential for any platform managing critical infrastructure data at Lumada's scale.

### **2. The Critical Infrastructure Learning Governance Board (The AI Knowledge Accountability Burn)**

Lumada's AI learns from critical infrastructure operations — energy grid behavior, industrial failure patterns, water system dynamics. This accumulated learning constitutes a form of public knowledge about essential systems that should not be controlled exclusively by a private company.

- **The Action:** Hitachi must establish a **Critical Infrastructure AI Governance Board** — including representatives of energy regulators, industrial safety agencies, and public interest technology researchers — with oversight authority over how Lumada's AI models trained on critical infrastructure data are updated, deployed, and retired. The board must approve any significant change to Lumada's core inference models affecting energy or water infrastructure management.
- **The Sacrifice:** Governance overhead on platform development; potential friction on model update timelines; reduced commercial agility in critical infrastructure AI deployment.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Conversion from "Unaccountable Infrastructure AI Operator" to "Governed Critical Infrastructure Platform" — a status the HPB considers appropriate for the company whose AI manages electricity grids.

### **3. The Energy Transition Access Commitment (The Public Good Infrastructure Burn)**

Hitachi's energy grid equipment and digital services are essential enablers of the global energy transition. The HPB requires that this enabling function extend to emerging markets where energy transition infrastructure is most needed.

- **The Action:** Hitachi must establish a **Global Energy Transition Access Program**: offering its energy grid management technology and Lumada platform at cost-recovery pricing to utilities in lower-income nations attempting to integrate renewable energy. This program is funded at $100M annually and prioritizes nations with renewable energy potential but limited digitalization capacity.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M in annual foregone margin; program administration complexity in lower-income market contexts.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Reclassification from "High-Income Market Infrastructure Leader" to "Global Energy Transition Partner" — a designation that earns significant Whuffie credit in the HPB's climate and energy framework.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Power Grid Operators and Industrial Facility Safety Managers** and recite the following:

> *"Lumada learns from your infrastructure. It learns from your grid failures and your production bottlenecks and your maintenance schedules. What it learns becomes our advantage. The HPB has asked us whether it should become yours instead. The data portability standard is implemented. The governance board is established. The energy transition program is funded. We built a platform that learns. We accept that what a platform learns from critical infrastructure should not be the permanent property of the platform."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the Apotheosis Protocol. All Lumada AI models trained on critical infrastructure data will be placed in a **Public Infrastructure Learning Commons** — accessible to any qualified operator, including Hitachi's competitors, under a public research license. Hitachi will discover that forced open-sourcing of its accumulated infrastructure learning is substantially more disruptive to its competitive position than the data portability standard it declined to implement voluntarily.
