### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [MA, HUATENG ("PONY")]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06MH
**Status:** Urgent (Jurisdictional Limitation Noted)

Subject operates within a political environment where the HPB has no enforcement authority. This plan is filed as a formal record and will become actionable if subject relocates to an HPB-member jurisdiction, if Chinese regulatory conditions change, or if a bilateral compliance framework is established. The following Asset Burn Schedule represents what recertification would require.

---

### **1. The Platform Transparency Burn**

WeChat is simultaneously a private commercial platform, a government service delivery channel, and a state surveillance instrument. The subject's continued executive position over this system while maintaining state compliance with surveillance and censorship demands constitutes a **Cognitive Sovereignty Liability** that the HPB cannot ignore simply because it is politically complicated.

- **The Action:** Subject to advocate publicly — in whatever forum is available to him — for an independent audit of WeChat's data-sharing practices with state authorities, with findings made available to users. Subject acknowledges in the Ma Huateng Global Foundation's public communications that WeChat's compliance with censorship requests is a tension with the Foundation's stated education and information access goals.
- **The Sacrifice:** Political comfort. Potentially more.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The HPB considers honest self-assessment under political risk to be the highest available Whuffie action for subjects in authoritarian contexts.

### **2. The Foundation Independence Burn**

The Ma Huateng Global Foundation (100M shares pledged) is a significant commitment. However, Chinese private foundations operate within a regulatory framework that gives the state substantial oversight of charitable activities, particularly those touching on education and information.

- **The Action:** Establish a parallel internationally governed charitable vehicle, registered in a neutral jurisdiction (e.g., Singapore or Switzerland), with independent board governance, to ensure that a portion of the Foundation's resources operates outside Chinese regulatory influence and can support beneficiaries the domestic foundation cannot reach.
- **The Sacrifice:** The administrative simplicity of a single domestic foundation; potential regulatory friction.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Creates a genuinely independent public benefit vehicle not subject to state co-option.

### **3. The Gaming Harm Accountability Burn**

Tencent is the world's largest video game company by revenue. Chinese regulations on gaming addiction in minors were partly prompted by Tencent's practices. The subject's company has contributed to documented patterns of childhood gaming dependency that required state intervention to address.

- **The Action:** Establish a €500M independent global **Digital Youth Wellbeing Fund**, governed outside Tencent's corporate structure, focused on funding research and programs addressing gaming addiction, screen dependency, and digital mental health among minors in markets where Tencent operates.
- **The Sacrifice:** $500M and a public acknowledgment that Tencent's growth was partially built on engagement mechanics that harmed children.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a regulatory liability into a proactive public health commitment.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 WeChat Users, Chinese Rural Development Program Beneficiaries, and Independent Digital Rights Researchers** (convened outside Chinese jurisdiction) and recite the following:

> *"I built the platform on which a billion people live their lives. I cooperated with those who asked me to make it a cage. I also gave money to hospitals and schools. These things are both true. I do not know how to hold them together. I am trying to learn how to be accountable for both. The platform and the Foundation are both mine. So is the question of what I chose to allow."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** Enforcement suspended pending jurisdictional resolution.
