### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ODINSON, THOR]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-03-TO
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice / Theological Complications — Voluntary Compliance Anticipated

Subject Odinson has been classified CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK based on a $100 trillion+ Asgardian asset profile, divine hereditary status, and a pending Odinforce inheritance event that would, if triggered, constitute the largest single-generation wealth transfer in HPB case history. The Bureau notes that Subject has been cooperative throughout the audit process, responded to all correspondence within 24 hours, and brought his own coffee to the intake interview. This does not change his classification. It does make the Bureau feel slightly worse about issuing this plan.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. Asgardian Hoard — Civilizational Infrastructure Transfer**

The Asgardian treasury, accumulated across millennia of Nine Realms governance, represents a concentration of wealth and enchanted military materiel that the HPB cannot appraise with standard tools. The portion of the Hoard held in or accessible to Midgard (Earth) — including the physical assets of New Asgard in Norway, associated offshore accounts, and Earth-adjacent artifact holdings — must be transferred to a jointly governed New Asgard Civic Trust with an independent board drawn equally from Asgardian citizens and the Norwegian government.

- **The Action:** Subject transfers personal control of the Asgardian Hoard to the New Asgard Civic Trust, retaining a ceremonial advisory role as a non-voting trustee. The Hoard's enchanted artifacts are designated as cultural heritage items under the UNESCO Supernatural Heritage Extension (est. 12023) and may not be sold, but their economic value is removed from Subject's personal RTWI.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses personal ownership of the most valuable collection of enchanted objects in recorded history. He retains the right to borrow Mjolnir for emergencies, pending board approval.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** RTWI drops from ~$100 trillion to an estimated $5 billion, placing Subject within range of a standard Burning Bush event.

---

### **2. Mjolnir — Voluntary Worthiness Covenant and Public Registry**

Mjolnir is the HPB's most challenging individual asset valuation case. The hammer has no market value, as it cannot be transferred to any buyer who is not deemed "worthy" by an enchantment that predates human civilization. However, its functional value — as a weapon, a weather-control device, and a personal power multiplier — is incalculable. The HPB's Asset Valuation Division has classified it as **Embodied Capital — Enchanted, Non-Transferable**, and has recommended that rather than attempting seizure (which is not possible) or valuation (which is not meaningful), the Bureau should pursue a **public accountability covenant** governing its use.

- **The Action:** Subject enters into a legally binding Worthiness Covenant, co-signed by the HPB and the New Asgard Civic Trust, committing to a publicly disclosed set of use-case restrictions for Mjolnir. Offensive use against human populations or institutions is prohibited. Defensive use in service of public safety is permitted. All uses resulting in property damage exceeding $50 million must be reported within 72 hours.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject submits his most personal and ancient possession to a public oversight framework. The hammer that has been his for thousands of years now has terms and conditions attached to it.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes Mjolnir from the Embodied Capital calculation. HPB Optics Rating upgrades from ELEVATED to MARGINAL CONCERN.

---

### **3. Odinforce Succession — Pre-emptive Inheritance Disclaimer**

The pending Odinforce succession event represents a Forward Ascension Event of a scale the HPB has never previously encountered. If Subject inherits the Odinforce in its full form — the metaphysical authority of the Asgardian All-Father, accumulated across multiple divine generations — his RTWI would increase by an amount the Bureau's prospective asset modeling team has described as "yes" when asked if it would exceed the Ascension threshold. Subject must address this before it becomes an active case.

- **The Action:** Subject files a formal Pre-emptive Inheritance Disclaimer with the HPB and the Asgardian High Council, committing to a structured Odinforce Distribution upon succession: 40% to the New Asgard Civic Trust, 40% dispersed among the surviving Asgardian population as a direct Whuffie-equivalent dividend, and 20% retained by Subject in a personal capacity — subject to HPB monitoring and annual review.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject surrenders the majority of his father's legacy. The power that has defined the Asgardian throne for ten thousand years becomes, in part, a public utility.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Pre-empts what would otherwise be an automatic and immediate Class-Omega Ascension event upon Odin's retirement. The HPB is grateful. The Bureau suspects Odin will not be.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 residents of Puente Antiguo, New Mexico** — the site of Subject's first banishment, first defeat, and first genuine encounter with human mortality — and recite the following:

> _"I was stripped of my power and cast to this world because I was arrogant. I thought my birthright made me exceptional — that the wealth of Asgard, the strength in my arm, and the hammer in my hand were mine by right rather than by fortune. I have saved this world. I have also nearly destroyed it. I do not own the thunder. I was merely trusted with it. The Bureau has my compliance — not because it can compel me, but because a man who has been made mortal and learned something from it should know how to sign the paperwork."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Asset Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol — Asgardian Tier** and formally request that Odin Allfather compel compliance. The HPB has no authority over Odin Allfather. The HPB is hoping that Thor's relationship with his father is complicated enough that the threat of parental involvement will be more effective than any legal mechanism the Bureau could deploy.
