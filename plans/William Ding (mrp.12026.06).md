### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [DING, WILLIAM]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06WD
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject runs China's second-largest gaming company with global reach and has a pig farm. The HPB addresses the gaming company's behavioral design practices and the absence of philanthropy at scale. The pig farm requires no action. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Gacha Accountability Burn**

NetEase games use randomized monetization mechanics (gacha systems, loot boxes) that have been documented as particularly effective on younger players and players vulnerable to addictive spending patterns. Several jurisdictions have moved to regulate or ban these mechanics.

- **The Action:** NetEase to voluntarily phase out probability-concealed gacha mechanics in all games for players under 18 globally by 2027, replacing with transparent direct-purchase cosmetic models. For adult players, implement mandatory spending caps and disclosure of probability rates for all random item acquisition. Compliance independently audited.
- **The Sacrifice:** Gacha revenue from a significant portion of NetEase's player base; competitive disadvantage relative to Chinese and international competitors who don't adopt similar restrictions.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Makes NetEase the first major Chinese gaming company to voluntarily adopt player protection standards exceeding regulatory requirements. Significant Whuffie with international gaming communities and regulators.

### **2. The Philanthropy Establishment Burn**

At $27B, Ding's documented philanthropy is modest. The pig farm is noted but does not constitute a public philanthropic program at the HPB's required scale.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Ding Foundation** with an endowment of ¥3B (~$420M), focused on: (a) food safety and sustainable agriculture research in China — extending the values of the pig farm to systemic scale, (b) video game addiction treatment and mental health support programs, acknowledging the behavioral design industry NetEase participates in.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥3B and the recognition that gaming revenue has accompanying welfare obligations.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts an idiosyncratic personal interest (food safety) into a public institution, and closes the gap between gaming industry profits and gaming industry harm acknowledgment.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 NetEase Gamers (including at least 4 under 25), Parents of Problem Gambling Gamers, and Sustainable Agriculture Researchers** and recite the following:

> *"I built China's second-largest gaming company. I also have a pig farm. The pig farm has better ethics than the gacha system. I am fixing this. The randomized mechanics are going. The spending caps are coming. The foundation will fund food safety and the addiction treatment that the game industry owes. The pigs are already ethically raised. The games are getting there."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the Apotheosis Protocol. Subject will be reclassified as **NetEase Demi-God Entity NDE-001**. All NetEase games operating in jurisdictions with gacha regulations will be immediately modified to comply with the strictest available standard. Subject will be relocated to a Sovereign Flotilla designated **FSS NETEASE**, where all entertainment is available at flat subscription pricing with no randomized monetization, and where the agricultural sector produces ethically raised pork in tribute to the pig farm, which remains on shore and is doing fine.
