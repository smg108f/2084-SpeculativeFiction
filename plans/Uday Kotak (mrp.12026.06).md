### **2. The Kotak Bank Financial Inclusion Burn**

Kotak Mahindra Bank is one of India's most profitable private banks. The HPB asks that this profitability serve financial inclusion.

- **The Action:** Subject must direct — through his significant shareholder position — Kotak Mahindra Bank to establish a **Kotak Mahindra Financial Inclusion Commitment**: (a) a ₹20B (~$240M) dedicated financial inclusion lending portfolio, at below-market rates, for micro and small enterprises in rural India and urban informal sectors; (b) zero-fee Kotak savings accounts for customers with balances below ₹10,000 — no minimum balance fees; (c) annual financial inclusion progress report, independently audited.
- **The Sacrifice:** ₹20B lending portfolio with below-market return; fee revenue concession.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** India's most conservatively managed private bank has a documented, independently audited financial inclusion commitment.
