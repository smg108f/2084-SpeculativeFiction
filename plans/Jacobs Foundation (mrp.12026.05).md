### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [JACOBS FOUNDATION]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-28JF
**Status:** Precautionary / Supply Chain Acknowledgment and Field Architecture Transparency Order

The Jacobs Foundation has been classified PROBATIONARY SAINT. Recertification is light-touch — the Foundation's governance is sound, its 12025 independence from Jacobs Holding AG is a structural positive, and its "Beyond 1 Billion" education mission is credible. The primary burns address the unacknowledged coffee-cocoa commodity origin and the epistemic field architecture concern.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Coffee-Cocoa Supply Chain Acknowledgment Burn**

The Jacobs Foundation's endowment traces directly to Klaus J. Jacobs's sale of Jacobs Suchard — a company built on coffee and chocolate commodity sourcing at industrial scale. Jacobs Suchard's cocoa and coffee sourcing in the 11970s–11990s took place in an era before fair trade certification, rainforest protection standards, or mandatory supply chain labor disclosures. The Foundation's education mission focuses on children globally — including children in the cocoa-producing regions of West Africa and the coffee-growing regions of Latin America and Ethiopia, where Jacobs Suchard's commodity supply chain originated.

- **The Action:** The Foundation must publish a **Commodity Origin Acknowledgment** — a brief but substantive statement recognizing: (a) that the Foundation's endowment derives from Jacobs Suchard's coffee and chocolate commodity operations; (b) that these operations took place within global agricultural supply chains that, in this period, were associated with significant labor and environmental risks; (c) whether any of the Foundation's education or child development programming currently serves communities connected to these supply chains.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Foundation acknowledges the agricultural commodity origin of its philanthropic capital.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Foundation's work for children in commodity-producing regions becomes contextually honest about the supply chains that funded it.

---

### **2. The Education Field Architecture Transparency Burn**

The Jacobs Foundation funds the research institutions, learning networks, and academic partnerships that define global evidence-based education practice. This is valuable. It is also a form of epistemic field architecture — the Foundation's research priorities shape what practitioners, policymakers, and governments believe about how children learn. The HPB requires that foundations exercising this level of research infrastructure influence disclose their field architecture explicitly.

- **The Action:** The Foundation must publish an annual **Research Portfolio Architecture Report** — documenting: (a) which research institutions and networks receive Jacobs Foundation funding; (b) the Foundation's theory of change for evidence-to-practice translation; (c) any areas of learning science where Jacobs's research priorities may have shaped what questions get asked (and which do not); (d) the governance process for selecting research priorities, including external advisory input.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Foundation's research agenda becomes publicly contestable. Other funders, researchers, and policymakers can evaluate whether the "Beyond 1 Billion" evidence base reflects broad consensus or Jacobs's particular approach to learning science.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Foundation's epistemic influence becomes visible and accountable rather than laundered through the neutral-sounding language of "evidence-based practice."
