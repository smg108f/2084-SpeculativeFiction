### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [BRIN, SERGEY]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-03SB
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject Brin has been issued an Apotheosis Summons following confirmation of a ~$270B Alphabet equity stake, active AGI directorship at Google DeepMind, and documented possession of sovereign-grade mobile infrastructure (one ocean-going vessel; one intercontinental airship). He has 30 days to execute the following Asset Burn Schedule or face reclassification as a Demi-God Entity under the Executive Orders of 2034.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Alphabet Divestiture (The "You Are Not the Search Bar" Burn)**

Subject Brin's ~6% Alphabet stake is the HPB's single largest documented Cognitive Infrastructure Concentration Hazard. The equity gives him a governance claim — through Class B voting shares — over the search, email, mapping, video, and AI systems used by more than three billion people daily. This is not wealth. This is jurisdiction.

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer all Class B voting shares to the **Public Cognitive Commons Trust**, a federally administered non-profit operating under HPB oversight. Class C non-voting shares may be gifted to the Brin Family Foundation, provided foundation grantmaking authority is transferred to a lottery-selected board of 15 citizens earning less than $100,000 annually.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses all voting governance rights over Alphabet and effectively cedes control over AGI development direction at Google DeepMind.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Net worth drops to an estimated $0–$40B in retained non-voting equity (pending further divestiture schedule); voting power returns to the public. Subject is reclassified from "Cognitive Infrastructure Controller" to "Interested Observer."

---

### **2. The Flotilla Surrender (The "You Already Left Once" Burn)**

The HPB classifies the *Dragonfly* superyacht (466 ft., estimated value $450M, intercontinental range) and the LTA Research Pathfinder 1 airship (124m, world's largest aircraft) as a paired sovereign mobility platform. Together, they constitute a functional Sovereign Flotilla State capable of operating indefinitely outside U.S. territorial waters. Subject Brin fled the Soviet Union at age six. The HPB acknowledges the historical resonance. It nonetheless notes that the Bureau does not grant exemptions for biography.

- **The Action:** The *Dragonfly* must be donated to the **U.S. Coastal Emergency Response Fleet** and repurposed as a mobile disaster-relief hospital. LTA Research must be restructured as a **Public Infrastructure Cooperative**, with Pathfinder operations open-sourced and Pathfinder 3 construction in Akron transferred to a municipal trust.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses the only asset in his portfolio capable of physically extracting him from U.S. jurisdiction in under 72 hours. He becomes, for the first time since childhood, genuinely stationary.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Exit Readiness classification drops from HIGH to LOW. Subject is reclassified from "Flotilla-Adjacent" to "Earthbound Citizen." The HPB considers this poetic.

---

### **3. The AGI Resignation (The "You Are Not Prometheus" Burn)**

In early 2026, Subject Brin personally assembled a Google DeepMind strike team to accelerate AGI development and publicly declared Google's intent to build the world's first Artificial General Intelligence. The HPB classifies this as an active Class I Cognitive Infrastructure Event. No private individual may direct the architectural development of a system intended to supersede human cognition. The mandate is jurisdictional, not personal.

- **The Action:** Subject must formally resign from all active research and product direction roles at Google DeepMind. A **Public AGI Oversight Charter** must be established with $5B in seed funding drawn from Subject's retained Alphabet holdings, administered by a board drawn equally from the scientific community, civil society, and lottery-selected public representatives.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject Brin surrenders the project he returned from semi-retirement to complete. He will not see whether Gemini becomes the first AGI. He will watch from the same seat as everyone else.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Political Agency classification drops from ELEVATED to LOW. Subject exits "Reality-Shaping Vector" classification and returns to standard citizen status.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Public School Teachers, Warehouse Workers, and Municipal Bus Drivers** and recite the following:

> _"I came to this country with nothing. I built something so large it cast a shadow over the country itself. I understand now that a shadow is not a gift. I return what I borrowed from the attention and labor of three billion people who never consented to be infrastructure. I am not the search. I am not the algorithm. I am not the AGI. I am Sergey. I am six years old. I am starting over."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the Apotheosis Protocol. All Alphabet voting shares will be nationalized and placed under the Public Cognitive Commons Trust without Subject's participation. Subject Brin will be reclassified as a Demi-God Entity and offered his choice of two relocation options: the **North Atlantic Flotilla Collective** (coordinates classified), or a permanent research fellowship at the **Antarctic Neutral Science Reserve** — where the internet exists, but there is no one to search.
