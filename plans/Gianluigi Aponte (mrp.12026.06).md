### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [APONTE, GIANLUIGI]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06GA
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject controls 20% of global container shipping through a fully private, non-disclosing family company. At 83, his succession plan has implications for global trade infrastructure. The HPB treats this as its most urgent physical infrastructure recertification case of this cycle. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 30 days.

*Note: Rafaela Aponte-Diamant has a parallel recertification plan (HPB-12026-06RAD). The family is required to coordinate all burns.*

---

### **1. The Transparency Infrastructure Burn**

MSC is the world's largest container shipping company and discloses essentially nothing — no public financials, no public sustainability reporting of substance, no ownership structure disclosure. For a company that constitutes 20% of global trade infrastructure, this is a systemic governance failure.

- **The Action:** MSC to publish annual public financial disclosures — revenue, profit, fleet utilization, carbon emissions, and labor conditions — reviewed by an independent auditor and publicly accessible. Subject and family to establish a public-facing governance board with at least 3 independent members, including labor and environmental representation.
- **The Sacrifice:** The opacity that has been MSC's operational signature for 50 years.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts 20% of global trade infrastructure from a private black box into a publicly accountable entity. The HPB considers this the single most important Burn on this entire audit cycle.

### **2. The Succession Plan Burn**

Aponte is 83. MSC is a family company with no documented public succession structure. The risk that 20% of global container shipping transitions to the next generation — or fractures — without any governance framework is an HPB-classified **Global Trade Continuity Risk**.

- **The Action:** File with the HPB a documented succession framework for MSC governance, including provisions for independent board oversight, labor continuity protections, and regulatory notification requirements — to be publicly disclosed within 90 days.
- **The Sacrifice:** Family sovereignty over succession planning; the comfort of keeping plans private.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a personal business succession into a public governance event, commensurate with the company's global infrastructure significance.

### **3. The Maritime Workers Endowment Burn**

MSC employs tens of thousands of seafarers under flag-of-convenience arrangements that allow the company to access labor markets with minimal regulatory constraint. The HPB requires a structural giving commitment to these workers.

- **The Action:** Establish the **MSC Maritime Workers Endowment** with a $500M founding commitment, focused on seafarer healthcare, mental health support, port community investment, and retirement provision for workers across MSC's global fleet.
- **The Sacrifice:** $500M and the tacit assumption that flag-of-convenience labor structures have no outstanding obligations.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the world's largest fleet from an extractive labor arrangement to one with active worker investment. Substantial Whuffie with international labor organizations.
