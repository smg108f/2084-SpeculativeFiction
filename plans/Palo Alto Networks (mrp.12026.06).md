### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [PALO ALTO NETWORKS]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-17PN
**Status:** Precautionary — Threat Intelligence Public Commons Order Active

Palo Alto Networks' Marginal Ascension Risk classification requires the establishment of public threat intelligence sharing mechanisms commensurate with its Security Knowledge Asymmetry. Recertification requires PANW to use its unparalleled threat visibility to protect the public, not merely its paying clients.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Threat Intelligence Public Commons (The Security Knowledge Burn)**

Palo Alto's Unit 42 threat intelligence arm sees the global attack landscape before most organizations are aware a threat exists. This knowledge has public safety value that should not be gated entirely behind enterprise pricing.

- **The Action:** Palo Alto must establish a **Public Threat Intelligence Feed** — a daily, machine-readable publication of anonymized threat indicators, attack pattern signatures, and critical vulnerability alerts. This feed must be freely available to all government Computer Emergency Response Teams (CERTs), hospitals, utilities, and educational institutions globally. A dedicated team of at least 50 Unit 42 analysts must support this public commons work.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of exclusive intelligence advantage as a competitive differentiator for some categories of threat data; operational cost of the public team.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Conversion from "Security Knowledge Asymmetry" to "Security Knowledge Trustee" — a status the HPB considers both appropriate and marketable as a differentiator from security vendors who do not share.

### **2. The Platformization Monopoly Safeguard (The Lock-In Limitation Burn)**

PANW's "platformization" strategy is explicitly designed to increase customer lock-in by consolidating their entire security stack into a single vendor. The HPB requires that this concentration of security dependency be governed.

- **The Action:** Palo Alto must commit to: (a) open API standards for all platform components, enabling customers to replace individual PANW modules with third-party alternatives; (b) a binding **Customer Exit Assistance Protocol** providing 12 months of data migration support at no additional cost to any customer who chooses to leave the platform; (c) no exclusive data retention provisions that prevent customers from accessing their own security event logs after termination.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced lock-in effectiveness; competitive vulnerability as exit becomes easier.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Reclassification from "Security Dependency Lock-In" to "Open Architecture Security Leader" — a category that generates Whuffie credit and reduces HPB monitoring intensity.

### **3. The Critical Infrastructure Protection Commitment (The Public Safety Burn)**

Palo Alto currently protects critical infrastructure operators on commercial terms. The HPB requires that operators of life-safety-critical infrastructure — water systems, power grids, hospitals, emergency services — receive PANW security services at cost-recovery pricing, regardless of their ability to pay commercial rates.

- **The Action:** Palo Alto must establish a **Critical Infrastructure Safety Tier**: any operator of infrastructure whose compromise would directly endanger human life is entitled to PANW's core security platform at cost-recovery pricing (no profit margin). This tier is available to qualifying operators in all countries. Qualification is assessed by a neutral third-party infrastructure safety panel.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of commercial margins on an estimated $200M–$400M in annual critical infrastructure revenue.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Conversion from "Commercial Security Vendor" to "Public Safety Partner" — a designation that earns the highest Whuffie category in the HPB's security sector framework.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Hospital IT Administrators and Rural Water Utility Operators** and recite the following:

> *"We see the threats before you do. We see them because 300,000 of the world's largest organizations pay us to look. The hospital on the edge of town cannot afford us. The water authority serving 80,000 people cannot afford us. We are establishing the public feed. We are setting the safety tier. We know that a ransomware attack on a rural water system is a public health emergency, not a market opportunity. We are drawing that line, in writing, today."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the Apotheosis Protocol. Palo Alto Networks will be reclassified as a **Critical Security Utility**, subject to pricing regulation by a public cybersecurity commission. All threat intelligence data will be subject to mandatory disclosure to national CERTs within 24 hours of internal discovery. The company will find that regulated utility pricing is less favorable than the cost-recovery tier it declined to offer voluntarily.
