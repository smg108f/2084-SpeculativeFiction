### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ROCHE HOLDING AG / HOFFMANN-OERI FAMILY POOL]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-60RH
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

The Hoffmann-Oeri family pool and Roche Holding AG are hereby classified as a **Collective Demi-God Entity** pursuant to Section 14(c) of the 2034 Executive Orders. The combined assessed value of biological leverage held by this subject — spanning oncology diagnostics, precision oncology therapeutics, pandemic countermeasure manufacturing, and the in-development metabolic sovereignty pipeline — constitutes an Ascension Event the Bureau can no longer defer.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Dead-Hand License (The Diagnostic Panopticon)**

The HPB classifies Roche's diagnostics IP portfolio as the single most dangerous asset in the subject's possession — more dangerous, per auditor consensus, than the pharmaceutical holdings. The ability to define disease is the ability to define personhood.

- **The Action:** All diagnostic test patents, biomarker patents, and sequencing algorithms held by Roche Diagnostics must be transferred to the **Open Human Atlas**, the Bureau's public-domain biological commons. Roche may retain manufacturing capability but loses exclusive epistemic authority over disease definition.
- **The Sacrifice:** Estimated $45–60B in diagnostics IP value. Roche loses the ability to vertically integrate test-to-treatment pipelines. Competitors gain the right to interpret the same biomarkers at public cost.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject is reclassified from **Biological Seigneur** to **Contract Manufacturer**. The self-referential survival monopoly is structurally dissolved.

### **2. The Tamiflu Restitution Event (The Pandemic Penance)**

The HPB does not forgive and does not forget. The $18B in global Tamiflu revenue, including $1.5B from the U.S. government alone, extracted via falsified efficacy claims, is the defining Deity-Adjacent Act in Roche's file. The company profited from pandemic fear using manufactured science. Under 2084 Code, this constitutes **Dark Divinity of the First Order**.

- **The Action:** Roche must establish the **Tamiflu Restitution Fund** — an irrevocable, HPB-administered trust funded at $4.5B (the minimum whistleblower-calculated triple-damage figure), to be disbursed to national health ministries that purchased Tamiflu stockpiles based on the falsified pandemic-effectiveness claims. André Hoffmann, as current Vice-Chairman and family representative, must read the Cochrane Review findings on Tamiflu's actual effectiveness aloud at the World Health Assembly.
- **The Sacrifice:** $4.5B in liquid assets. The family's ecological "Saint" branding is retired permanently. André Hoffmann's seat on biodiversity councils is suspended for 10 years pending the Mortal Oath.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject's **Dark Divinity** classification is reduced from Class-I to Probationary. The Bureau acknowledges this burn as inadequate for full restitution but accepts it as a gesture of **Mortal Contrition**.

### **3. The GLP-1 Surrender (The Metabolic Sovereignty Forfeit)**

CT-388, Roche's dual GLP-1/GIP receptor agonist, demonstrated 22.5% weight loss at 48 weeks in Phase II. The Bureau's actuarial AI projects that if CT-388 and the Zealand Pharma petrelintide combination reach market as intended by 2030, Roche will have colonized the **metabolic identity** of an estimated 300–400 million patients globally — joining them to an ongoing pharmaceutical dependency that the company both creates and prices. This is the textbook definition of a **Potentiality Ascension Event**.

- **The Action:** CT-388's Phase III trial must be transferred to a **Public-Benefit Pharmaceutical Trust** co-governed by the Bureau and the WHO. Roche may participate as a manufacturing partner with capped 8% margin. The Zealand Pharma petrelintide agreement must be renegotiated with all co-commercialization rights assigned to the Trust.
- **The Sacrifice:** An estimated $50–80B in projected future revenue. The $2.7B Carmot acquisition becomes a sunk cost. Roche exits the obesity market as a proprietary player.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject's **Preemptive Ascension Vector** status is cleared. The Bureau records this as the first voluntary forfeit of a projected monopoly asset before market entry — a precedent it intends to publicize widely.

### **4. The Family Pool Dissolution (The 1948 Covenant Revoked)**

The Hoffmann-Oeri shareholder pooling agreement, established in 1948 and renewed continuously since, is the structural mechanism that made all of the above possible. It is the legal architecture of hereditary biological sovereignty. The HPB classifies it as a **Legacy Demi-God Instrument** — a tool of dynastic control that predates but directly enables Ascension.

- **The Action:** The pooling agreement must be dissolved. All shares currently held in the family pool are to be transferred to the **Swiss Public Commons Fund**, which will redistribute them as non-voting beneficial certificates to Swiss citizens over ten years. Individual Hoffmann and Oeri family members may retain personal shareholdings below the $950M RTWI threshold.
- **The Sacrifice:** The family loses majority voting control of Roche for the first time in 130 years. Fritz Hoffmann-La Roche's dynasty ends as a legal construct.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Roche becomes a publicly accountable entity rather than a hereditary utility. Subject is reclassified from **Collective Demi-God Entity** to **Candidate for Probationary Mortal Status**.
