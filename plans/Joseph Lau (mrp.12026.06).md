### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [LAU, JOSEPH (LAU LUEN-HUNG)]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06JL2
**Status:** Urgent — Criminal Conviction Precondition Required

*Subject was convicted in absentia in Macau (2014) for bribery and money laundering. A criminal precondition applies before standard Burns activate.*

---

### **⚠️ CRIMINAL CONVICTION PRECONDITION**

**Precondition — Macau Sentence Accountability Statement:**
The HPB is not a criminal jurisdiction and cannot compel extradition. However, subject must file a formal **Criminal Accountability Statement** acknowledging the Macau conviction and committing to: (a) not pursuing any further legal challenge to the Macau verdict in Hong Kong courts; (b) contributing HK$100M (~$13M) to the **Macau Anti-Corruption and Rule of Law Fund** — administered by Macau's CCAC (anti-corruption body) and restricted to funding anticorruption education and prosecution capacity, not to benefit the subject; (c) acknowledging publicly, in writing, that the land bribery for which he was convicted caused public harm.

*The HPB does not require physical return to serve the sentence — this is outside the HPB's jurisdiction. It does require the above as a condition of Burns activating.*

---

### **BURNS (Activate Upon Precondition Completion)**

### **1. The Chinese Estates Affordable Housing Burn**

Chinese Estates is a major Hong Kong developer. Hong Kong has one of the least affordable housing markets in the world.

- **The Action:** Subject must commit — through his controlling stake in Chinese Estates — to a **Chinese Estates Affordable Housing Program**: (a) 20% of all new Chinese Estates residential units developed in Hong Kong over the next 10 years designated as affordable units at or below 30% of area median income; (b) HK$1B (~$130M) **Hong Kong First-Home Fund** — administered independently — subsidizing first-home purchases for median-income earners in Hong Kong.
- **The Sacrifice:** HK$1B + affordable unit margin concession.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** A developer convicted of bribery in a land deal invests in affordable housing access.

### **2. The Lau Foundation Hong Kong Burn**

- **The Action:** Establish the **Joseph Lau Foundation** — HK$500M (~$65M) — focused on Hong Kong hospital infrastructure (expanding on subject's existing hospital donations, at scale) and youth arts education (connecting to subject's art collection).
- **The Sacrifice:** HK$500M.
