### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [WERTHEIMER, GÉRARD]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06GW
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification

Subject is classified MARGINAL — lowest intensity recertification available. The HPB asks very little of Gérard Wertheimer, partly because he has done very little. That is both the problem and the solution: the subject must begin to do something. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 60 days.

*Note: Subject's brother Alain Wertheimer has a parallel recertification plan (HPB-12026-06AW). Coordination is strongly encouraged.*

---

### **1. The "Show Up" Burn**

The HPB's primary concern with Gérard is not what he has done. It is the total absence of public civic presence from someone who controls half of a $70B global brand. Passive inherited wealth at this scale requires at minimum a public identity.

- **The Action:** Gérard is to develop and announce a personal philanthropic focus area — distinct from the Wertheimer family foundation — and commit a minimum of $500M over 10 years to it. Subject chooses the cause area. The Bureau requires only that he choose, commit, and be publicly associated with the choice.
- **The Sacrifice:** The comfort of complete public anonymity.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the "quietest billionaire" from a pure balance sheet entry into a civic participant. The HPB considers simply showing up the most meaningful Burn available for this subject.

### **2. The Chanel Governance Burn**

As a 50% owner of a privately held global brand, Gérard participates in Chanel's governance without public accountability. At minimum, his co-ownership should carry co-responsibility for Chanel's supply chain and sustainability obligations.

- **The Action:** Formally, and publicly, co-endorse Alain's supply chain transparency commitment (HPB-12026-06AW) as a co-owner, establishing joint family accountability for Chanel's corporate social responsibility obligations.
- **The Sacrifice:** The ability to disclaim responsibility for Chanel's practices by virtue of having no operational role.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts passive co-ownership into active co-accountability.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Chanel Artisans, Luxury Industry Workers, and French Citizens** and recite the following:

> *"My name is Gérard Wertheimer. I am worth approximately $33 billion. Most people do not know my name. I am not sure this is ideal. My grandfather made a deal. My brother runs the company. I attend meetings. I am now also doing something else. I am choosing a cause. I am becoming visible. This is the beginning of my story. I apologize it took this long to start."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the Apotheosis Protocol. Subject will be reclassified as **Chanel Passive Demi-God Entity CPDE-001** — a newly created classification for billionaires who have accumulated divine thresholds through inaction alone. Subject will be required to attend a mandatory 6-month HPB Civic Visibility Residency, during which he will give one public interview per month. Subject will be relocated to a Sovereign Flotilla designated **FSS INVISIBLE**, where all residents are known but choose not to be seen, and where Gérard will find this deeply familiar.
