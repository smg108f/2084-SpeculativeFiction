### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [GLASENBERG, IVAN]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06IG
**Status:** Urgent

Subject was Glencore's CEO during a period when the company bribed officials in seven countries. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Bribery Restitution Burn**

The $1.5B corporate fine went primarily to US, UK, and Brazilian regulators. The countries whose governance was corrupted — Nigeria, Cameroon, Ivory Coast, Equatorial Guinea, South Sudan, Venezuela — received little or nothing.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund $200M (personal, separate from Glencore's corporate settlement) for the **Glencore Governance Restoration Fund** directed to the seven bribery jurisdictions: (a) anti-corruption institutional capacity building in each of the seven countries — specifically, funding of independent anti-corruption agencies, investigative journalism in those countries, and civil society organizations working on resource governance; (b) direct community investment in the specific communities where Glencore operated in those countries; (c) transparency measures — publishing a full timeline of Glencore's bribery payments in each country, for each year, as a permanent public record, so the countries' citizens understand exactly what was done in their name.
- **The Sacrifice:** $200M.

### **2. The DRC Cobalt Child Labor Burn**

The relationship between Glencore's Katanga operations and artisanal child mining in the region requires specific remediation.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund $100M for the **DRC Katanga Community Development Fund**: (a) alternative livelihood programs for artisanal miners in Katanga to reduce economic dependence on the artisanal mining that puts children at risk; (b) school construction and teacher support in Katanga mining communities; (c) independent child labor monitoring around all Glencore DRC concessions, with results published publicly.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M.
