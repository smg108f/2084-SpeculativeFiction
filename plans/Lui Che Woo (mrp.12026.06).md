---

### **1. The Galaxy Entertainment Responsible Gambling Burn**

Galaxy Entertainment's casino revenues depend on gambling volume. Macau's gaming market draws heavily from mainland Chinese visitors, where gambling addiction has been documented as a social and family harm.

- **The Action:** Subject must direct Galaxy Entertainment — through his controlling family stake — to establish a **Galaxy Responsible Gaming Foundation**: (a) HK$500M (~$64M) independent endowment, governed by addiction medicine specialists and social workers, not Galaxy corporate employees; (b) mandatory self-exclusion program with biometric enrollment available at all Galaxy properties; (c) annual addiction rate monitoring with publicly disclosed results; (d) dedicated funding for Macau and Guangdong Province gambling addiction treatment centers.
- **The Sacrifice:** HK$500M + marketing constraint (responsible gambling not merely checkbox compliance).
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The largest casino operator on the former Portuguese colony has a documented, independently funded addiction remediation program.

### **2. The LUI Che Woo Prize Scaling Burn**

The LUI Che Woo Prize exists and is substantive. The HPB asks for an endowment commitment commensurate with wealth.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish a permanent **LUI Che Woo Prize Endowment** of HK$2B (~$260M) — ensuring the Prize continues for at least 50 years independently of the family's corporate holdings. Prize criteria must include a specific new category for gambling harm reduction and addiction medicine research.
- **The Sacrifice:** HK$2B endowment.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Prize — which is the subject's most meaningful philanthropic contribution — is given permanence beyond his lifetime.
