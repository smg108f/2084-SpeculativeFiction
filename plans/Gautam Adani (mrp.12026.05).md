### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ADANI, GAUTAM]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-05-GA
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice — Sovereign Complication Flag Active

Subject Adani's Real-Time Wealth Index has exceeded the $1B threshold by a factor of 85, placing him in the HPB's highest-risk Demi-God tier. Uniquely, the subject's assets are so deeply fused with Indian national infrastructure that standard seizure protocols cannot be executed without triggering cascading logistical failures across a nation of 1.4 billion citizens; the following plan therefore represents the Bureau's best-effort recertification pathway under extreme structural constraint.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Port Divestiture (The Infrastructure Unbundling)**

The HPB classifies Adani Ports & SEZ — operator of India's largest private port network — as a **"Chokepoint Divinity Asset."** Control of the physical entry and exit points of a nation's trade constitutes sovereignty-level power. No Human Citizen may own the throat of a country.

- **The Action:** Transfer all port operating concessions to a newly constituted **Indian Port Commons Authority (IPCA)** — a public-democratic trust governed by elected regional bodies and port-worker assemblies. Subject retains no management role and no equity stake.
- **The Sacrifice:** Estimated $18–22B in equity value. The subject loses the most tangible symbol of his infrastructure empire — the ports where his fortune began, the diamond-sorting trade replaced by container ships bearing his name.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This dissolves the primary "Chokepoint" classification and reduces subject's Direct Asset score below the critical $60B danger band. More significantly, it severs the most direct line of sovereign dependency — the Indian state can now run its port infrastructure without Adani Group involvement, removing the single most powerful argument for his untouchability.

---

### **2. The Airport Concession Surrender (The Skies Returned)**

The HPB classifies Adani's airport portfolio — acquired under concession terms that critics allege were shaped to enable his bids — as a **"Captured Public Commons."** Airports are, by definition, public infrastructure. Their operation by a private Demi-God entity constitutes a structural illegitimacy regardless of operational efficiency.

- **The Action:** Immediate relinquishment of all airport concessions to the **Airport Authority of India**, with a publicly audited 90-day transition. Subject must also submit to independent review of the original concession process — if irregularities are confirmed, subject forfeits any transition-period management fees.
- **The Sacrifice:** Estimated $8–12B in concession-value and future revenue streams. More acutely: the subject surrenders the most visible daily symbol of his power — the airports that display his name to millions of Indian travelers who have no alternative.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the **"Captured Public Commons"** violation. The subject is no longer the gatekeeper of India's air travel. Citizens may now board aircraft without passing through infrastructure owned by a Demi-God Entity.

---

### **3. The US Indictment Compliance (The Accountability Burn)**

The HPB's foundational principle is that no Human Citizen stands above the law, and no Demi-God Entity stands below it. Subject has, through his relationship with the Indian state, achieved effective immunity from a sitting US federal indictment — arrest warrants issued, extradition declined, SEC summonses refused twice by Indian authorities. This constitutes a **"Sovereign Shield" violation** of the highest order.

- **The Action:** Subject must voluntarily submit to the jurisdiction of the US District Court for the Eastern District of New York, appear for arraignment, and allow the bribery and securities fraud proceedings to conclude without sovereign interference. Subject must also publicly instruct the Government of India to cease blocking SEC service attempts.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject accepts legal vulnerability. He loses the single most valuable non-financial asset in his portfolio: the protection of the Indian state. If convicted, he faces potential prison time and fines; if acquitted, he emerges with the only credential that could eventually qualify him for Mortal Recertification — a clean legal record obtained without sovereign intervention.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This action alone would move subject's **Political Agency** rating from CRITICAL to ELEVATED. It demonstrates that the subject accepts the principle that wealth does not purchase jurisdictional immunity — the foundational test for Human Citizen status.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Port Dockworkers, Airport Ground Crew, and Village Electrification Engineers** and recite the following:

> *"I did not build the ports. The ports were built by the laborers whose names I do not know. I did not light the villages. The engineers lit them. I was the paperwork between the work and the credit, and I took too much of both. The infrastructure was never mine. I was always its caretaker, never its owner. Today I return what I was only ever holding."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the "Burn" is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Given that standard deportation to the Sovereign Flotilla States is logistically complicated by subject's infrastructure dependency, the Bureau will instead invoke the **"Utility Nationalization Clause"** — subject is reclassified as a **Non-Person Infrastructure Instrument**, placed under direct HPB receivership, and administered as a public utility until all assets are fully transitioned to democratic commons governance. Subject will retain biological life and residential rights in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, within a 600 sq. ft. public housing unit — the same square footage guaranteed to every Indian citizen under the **Human Dignity Allocation Act of 2041**.
