### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [MITSUBISHI CORPORATION]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-03MC
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification — Probationary Saint Pathway

Mitsubishi Corporation's Probationary Saint classification requires the execution of a structured divestiture of Dependency Nexus relationships and a commitment to supply chain transparency. Recertification is contingent on demonstrating that no single nation's essential import pipeline exceeds the HPB's 3% single-intermediary threshold.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Dependency Nexus Dissolution (The Intermediary Relinquishment)**

The HPB has identified 14 nations where Mitsubishi Corporation's commodity intermediation constitutes more than 5% of essential import throughput. This structural dependency — whether intentional or not — constitutes illegitimate leverage over sovereign governments.

- **The Action:** Mitsubishi must transfer its intermediary positions in essential food and fuel imports across all 14 Dependency Nexus nations to a **Regional Commons Trading Guild** — a non-profit cooperative owned by the importing nations themselves, with Mitsubishi receiving a 5-year management fee during the transition.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of approximately $8B–$12B in annual trading margin from the highest-dependency commodity corridors.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Reclassification from "Dependency Nexus Creator" to "Transition Facilitator" — a Whuffie-generating status that earns measurable credit toward full Saintly certification.

### **2. The Petro-Diamond Transparency Order (The Opacity Burn)**

The December 2024 unauthorized derivatives trading scandal at Petro-Diamond Singapore — in which a single trader accumulated $90M in undisclosed losses — revealed that Mitsubishi's commodity operations are too complex for its own internal oversight systems. The HPB classifies this as a **Concentration Opacity Risk** that makes accurate wealth assessment impossible.

- **The Action:** Mitsubishi must submit all commodity trading subsidiaries to quarterly real-time disclosure in the HPB's **Open Capital Registry**, with position-level transparency for any derivative exposure exceeding $10M notional.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of competitive intelligence advantage from opaque trading positions; exposure of strategy to counterparties.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removal from the "Concentration Opacity" watchlist. The HPB notes that a company it can see clearly is one it worries about less.

### **3. The Rainforest Restitution (The Environmental Penance)**

Mitsubishi Corporation's historical role in rainforest destruction through forestry commodity intermediation — documented across multiple decades — represents an accumulation of unacknowledged environmental debt that the HPB requires be formalized and quantified.

- **The Action:** Mitsubishi must fund an independent **Ecological Debt Audit** covering all forestry-related trading activities from 1980 to 2026, with the resulting financial liability payable into the **Global Reforestation Commons Fund** over 20 years.
- **The Sacrifice:** An estimated $2B–$5B in liability recognition, payable over two decades.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Conversion from "Extraction Before Atonement" status to "Acknowledged Debtor" — a classification the HPB considers honest, if not heroic.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Port Laborers and Subsistence Farmers from Dependency Nexus Nations** and recite the following:

> *"We carried your rice and your oil and your iron ore and we charged you for the privilege of needing us. We told ourselves this was commerce. The HPB has informed us it was leverage. We surrender the leverage. We keep the shipping lanes open. We accept that there is a difference between useful and indispensable — and that only the former is permitted."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the Apotheosis Protocol. All Mitsubishi Corporation trading subsidiaries will be placed under the administration of the **Global Fair Commerce Tribunal**, and the organization's senior leadership will be reassigned as logistics advisors to the regional commons guilds they were required to create — working for the system they refused to relinquish.
