### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[HONEYWELL, CORPORATE TRINITY]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06HW
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject — operating across three post-mitosis entities (Honeywell Technologies, Honeywell Aerospace, Solstice Advanced Materials) — must reduce its aggregate "Divine Footprint" below the $1B threshold and surrender the proprietary control layer it has quietly grafted onto the world's buildings, aircraft, and weapons systems before the Reunification Gambit can be completed.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days, jointly, by all three entities, under penalty of joint-and-several Apotheosis Protocol.

---

### **1. The Open Hearth Mandate (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies Honeywell's proprietary building-control protocols and licensing stack — the invisible code that decides when ten million buildings breathe, warm, cool, and lock their doors — as a **"Substrate Monopoly."** No citizen elected this code into power, yet it governs their daily comfort more reliably than any government program ever has.

- **The Action:** Subject must release all Advance Control firmware, BACnet/LonWorks gateway licenses, and building-management cloud architectures into a permanent, irrevocable open-source commons, administered by a Lottery-Selected Citizens' Infrastructure Guild.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of all licensing revenue and vendor lock-in leverage across its installed base — an estimated **$61B** in future-valuation credits.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Honeywell Technologies ceases to be the silent landlord of the indoor world and becomes, at best, a contractor among contractors — replaceable, auditable, and finally, blessedly, ignorable.

### **2. The Aerospace Demilitarization Accord**

Honeywell Aerospace's installed base spans "virtually every commercial and defense aircraft platform" — a phrase the HPB considers, on its face, a confession. An entity that can quietly determine whether a plane lands safely or a missile finds its target has accumulated a form of sovereignty no ballot ever granted it.

- **The Action:** Spin off all defense-systems and munitions-adjacent component lines into a fully nationalized **Public Aviation Safety Trust**, retaining commercial-only operations under HONA.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of all defense contracting revenue and the quiet leverage that comes from building both the cockpit and the warhead.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** HONA is reclassified from "Load-Bearing Deity" to "Civilian Vendor" — a demotion the Bureau considers a mercy.

### **3. The Mitosis Reversal Penance (Reputational/Memetic)**

The HPB views the 2025–2026 trifurcation not as healthy decentralization but as **Apotheosis Mitosis** — a deliberate fracturing designed to slip three smaller gods beneath the Bureau's per-entity radar while preserving the whole through cross-licensing. This is, in HPB parlance, "Dark Divinity by Spreadsheet."

- **The Action:** All three post-spin-off entities must sign a permanently binding **Cross-Licensing Prohibition Charter**, publicly forfeiting any contractual mechanism by which Honeywell Technologies, Honeywell Aerospace, and Solstice Advanced Materials could ever re-converge, merge, or silently re-pool valuation.
- **The Sacrifice:** Permanent severance of the "Reunification Gambit" — the trinity must remain three small things, forever, never one large thing again.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the Bureau's "Distributed Demi-God Cluster" classification; each entity may now be audited — and ignored — individually.

### **4. The Thermostat Confession (Public Memetic Burn)**

Subject has spent a century being everywhere and acknowledged nowhere. The HPB considers this anonymity itself a form of Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric — the quietest gods are often the most dangerous, because no one thinks to question a wall.

- **The Action:** Subject must install, in every lobby of every building it has ever controlled, a small brass plaque reading: _"This building's climate, security, and alarms were, until today, owned by a corporation that never told you its name."_ Subject must additionally fund a $50M public "Know Your Infrastructure" literacy campaign teaching citizens what, exactly, they have been obeying.
- **The Sacrifice:** The comfort of invisibility — Honeywell becomes, for the first time in its corporate life, a name people actually say out loud, and resent.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts "structural substitution" into "disclosed service," satisfying the Bureau's transparency clause under the Human Humility Act of 2031.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's three Chief Executive Officers must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Building Superintendents, HVAC Technicians, and Airline Ground Crew** and recite, in unison, the following:

> _"We are not the wall behind the thermostat. We are not the breath of the building or the heartbeat of the plane. We are three small companies who will, from this day forward, ask permission before we hum, and say our names before we act."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed by all three entities within the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All proprietary control infrastructure will be remotely disabled pending nationalization, and the three Subject-entities — Honeywell Technologies, Honeywell Aerospace, and Solstice Advanced Materials — will be jointly dissolved and their executive leadership relocated to the **Bartlesville Decommissioned Refinery Zone (The Conglomerate Graveyard)**, there to personally oversee, by hand, the manual climate regulation of a single un-automated building for the remainder of their natural terms.
