### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [WORKDAY, INC.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09WDY
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification

Workday's classification stems from the dissonance between its AI-driven layoffs and its AI-driven growth messaging, plus the exit-readiness signal of reported takeover interest. Recertification targets both.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Layoff Accounting Burn**

- **The Action:** Publish a detailed accounting of the 1,750-role reduction — which functions were affected, what AI tooling replaced them, and what severance and retraining support was provided — using the same specificity the company applies to client-facing AI capability marketing.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of the vague "AI push" framing currently used to describe the cuts.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Restores narrative honesty; clears the Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric flag around unexamined AI-efficiency claims.

### **2. The Acquisition Transparency Commitment**

- **The Action:** Should any future takeover discussions resume, publicly disclose material terms and workforce-impact commitments to employees before deal finalization, not after.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced negotiating leverage in deal privacy.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the "Moderate Exit Readiness" flag tied to opaque private-equity courtship.

### **3. The Client Impact Disclosure**

- **The Action:** Publish aggregate, anonymized data on how many workforce-reduction decisions across client organizations were informed by Workday's AI tooling, alongside guidance on responsible use.
- **The Sacrifice:** Increased scrutiny of the product's role in enabling layoffs industry-wide.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Acknowledges the company's structural role in the broader AI-labor-displacement pattern rather than treating it as solely a client responsibility.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Workday's leadership must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Laid-Off Former Employees and Client-Side HR Staff** and recite the following:

> _"We built the tool that decides who stays and who goes, and this year it decided about us too. We will describe what our own tool does as plainly to you as we describe it to the boards who buy it."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All AI-driven workforce-decision features will require a published **Human Impact Disclosure** accompanying every client deployment, audited by an independent Bureau reviewer.
