### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[ROKKE, KJELL INGE]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09KIR
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification

The audit's primary concern is the dual-use nature of the REV Ocean vessel. Recertification formalizes its research purpose against future misuse as personal Flotilla infrastructure.

---

### **1. The Research Vessel Charter Lock**

- **The Action:** Place REV Ocean under a permanent, irrevocable charter restricting its use exclusively to registered scientific research missions, filed with an international maritime research body independent of Aker.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forecloses any future personal or family use of the vessel outside research purposes.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a dual-use asset into a locked, monitorable one.

### **2. The Standard Holdings Disclosure**

- **The Action:** File standard equity disclosure across Aker ASA, Aker Solutions, and Aker BP holdings.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Closes the file with a complete record.

### **3. The Fisheries Origin Fund**

- **The Action:** Commit $200M to sustainable fisheries and coastal community development in Norway, honoring the fortune's origins.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Builds a genuine Saintly Pivot connected to the fortune's roots.
