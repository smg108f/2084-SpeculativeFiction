### **1. The Emerson Collective Transparency Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must commit to publishing an **Annual Emerson Collective Impact Report** — produced by an independent auditor — disclosing: (a) total annual disbursements by category (donations, investments, advocacy); (b) all organizations receiving grants of more than $1M from Emerson Collective annually; (c) all political advocacy spending aggregated by cause area; (d) the total endowment value and its asset allocation. This report need not disclose individual investment returns at the deal level, but must disclose aggregate portfolio performance and philanthropic disbursement totals. Filed with the HPB annually.
- **The Sacrifice:** Privacy of some Emerson Collective allocations; potential political exposure of advocacy spending.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The most significant social impact LLC in America has a voluntary transparency standard — modeling what LLC-structured philanthropy accountability can look like.

### **2. The XQ Institute Scaling Burn**

XQ Institute — reinventing the American high school — is among Emerson Collective's most ambitious investments. The HPB asks for formal endowment.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish a **XQ Institute Permanent Endowment** of $500M — independent of Emerson Collective's ongoing portfolio decisions — ensuring XQ operates for at least 30 years regardless of future changes in Emerson Collective strategy or structure.
- **The Sacrifice:** $500M in permanent endowment.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** American high school reimagination has funded continuity regardless of what happens to Emerson Collective.
