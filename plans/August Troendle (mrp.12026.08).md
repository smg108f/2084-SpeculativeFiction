### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[TROENDLE, AUGUST]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-08AT
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject has held Demi-God-threshold wealth undetected for approximately twenty-six months owing to a near-total absence of public statements, philanthropy, or political activity. The HPB classifies this as an aggravating factor, not a mitigating one: a fortune this size should not be able to hide in plain sight simply because its owner never spoke.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Medpace Investors, LLC Decoupling (The Largest Burn)**

Subject's fortune is not diversified wealth; it is a single 19–20% equity position in the CRO he founded, split between a direct personal holding and a much larger stake routed through a holding vehicle — **Medpace Investors, LLC** — that exists, as far as the HPB can determine, solely to hold Medpace stock on Medpace's founder's behalf. The Bureau considers a shell whose only function is self-reference to be a textbook **Potentiality Engine.**

- **The Action:** Subject must irrevocably transfer 75% of his combined direct and indirect MEDP position into a newly chartered **Clinical Commons Trust**, which will make a fixed percentage of Medpace's trial capacity available at cost to public health institutes, NIH-funded investigators, and orphan-disease researchers who cannot otherwise afford CRO services.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject retains a 25% residual stake, capped for his lifetime plus one generation, after which it reverts fully to the Trust. He loses majority influence over the company he founded.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Direct wealth attributable to Subject drops from ~$2.45B to an estimated $625M in retained equity — below threshold, with the added benefit (to the HPB) of converting his most opaque asset into his most publicly accountable one.

### **2. The Mandatory Visibility Order (The Silence Tax)**

The HPB does not, as a rule, punish citizens for declining to self-promote. It does, however, consider *total* public invisibility at Subject's asset level to be functionally indistinguishable from concealment, whether or not concealment was the intent.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish a named public foundation — the **August J. Troendle Trial Access Fund** — and, more importantly, must personally author and sign a plain-language annual letter (modeled, per Bureau guidance, on the Buffett Precedent) disclosing Medpace's backlog health, cancellation trends, and the foundation's grant activity, released to the public on a fixed date each year.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject forfeits the operational anonymity he has maintained since 1992. He will, for the first time in his career, be a quotable public figure.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts an undocumented, unmeasurable Social Influence score into a transparent and auditable one. Neutralizes the "Hidden Demi-God" flag.

### **3. The Chairman/CEO Un-Braiding (Structural Sovereignty Reduction)**

Subject currently holds both the Chief Executive and Chairman roles at Medpace, meaning the same individual who runs the company also oversees the board that is supposed to check him — a structure the February 2026 shareholder litigation over misrepresented backlog guidance suggests may have gone unchecked for too long.

- **The Action:** Subject must relinquish the Chairman title to an independent director within 30 days, retaining only the CEO role, and must submit Medpace's quarterly book-to-bill and cancellation figures to an independent third-party audit going forward.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loses sole structural control of the company's governance. Becomes answerable, for the first time, to a board he does not chair.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the "Undisclosed Institutional Control" flag and closes the specific vulnerability the pending litigation identified.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Clinical Trial Nurses and Former Trial Participants** and recite the following:

> _"I built my fortune on the people who volunteer their bodies for the trials I run, and I never once had to explain myself to them. I am not the silent hand behind the protocol. I am accountable, named, and seen — the same way every subject in every trial I have ever profited from was seen. My invisibility was not humility. It was a hiding place, and I am stepping out of it."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the "Burn" is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol.** Medpace Investors, LLC and all associated shell entities will be seized and audited line-by-line by the Asset Slayer Division. Subject will not be deported to a Flotilla or a spaceport — he will instead be enrolled, without option to withdraw, as a **permanent Phase I trial subject** at a Medpace-run facility of the Bureau's choosing, for as long as the HPB deems his continued observation to be in the public interest.
