### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[CASTILE, ISABELLA I OF]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-08ISC
**Status:** Urgent

Subject is deceased (26 November 1504) and, per audit aa.12026.08, was certified **CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK** — a file without the counterbalance this Bureau found in the Akbar docket. There is no forty-year unforced tolerance policy here to weigh against the ledger; the Inquisition, the 1492 Alhambra Decree, and the 1502 conversion/expulsion decree were central, personally authorized projects of Subject's own reign, and the Columbus voyage's legacy is genuinely ambiguous rather than a clean offset. Recertification is therefore not a conditional balancing act of the kind granted to the Akbar file. It is an **accountability-first posthumous recertification**: the genuine state-building achievements remain in the record, but they are formally separated from the persecution and extraction apparatus that funded and followed them, and that apparatus is redirected, disclosed, and named rather than absorbed into a flattering national myth.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days by whichever successor states, heritage authorities, and cultural institutions currently administer or profit from the legacy.

---

### **1. The Crown Fiscal-Colonial Extraction Engine (The Largest Burn)**

Per audit aa.12026.08, Subject's capitalized wealth rests on a fiscal architecture — the alcabala wool tax and the juro-capitalized Crown treasury — that drew in part on the forced liquidation of Jewish-owned property during the four-month compliance window of the 1492 Alhambra Decree, and that licensed, via the Columbus voyage, the encomienda extraction economy already operating on Hispaniola before Subject's death.

- **The Action:** The capitalized value of the fiscal-colonial apparatus (per audit, $9.0B–$14.5B, understood throughout as sovereign fiscal capacity rather than literal treasury) is converted into a **Sephardic, Andalusi-Morisco, and Taíno/Indigenous Caribbean Descendant Restitution Trust**, jointly administered by the HPB's Office of Posthumous Franchises, Spain's heritage and cultural-affairs authorities, and representative diaspora and descendant organizations for each of the three communities. Each community receives a dedicated, non-discretionary line item — none may be folded anonymously into a general fund, and none may be used to offset the others.
- **The Sacrifice:** The wool trade and the juro system that funded the unified Spanish state lose their ability to be remembered as separate from the expulsion, conversion, and colonial extraction they also financed.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a revenue engine currently credited only as nation-building capital into an instrument that also compensates the specific populations it extracted from.

### **2. The Inquisition & Dual-Decree Discharge**

Subject's Inquisition (est. 1478), the 1492 Alhambra Decree, and the 1502 Muslim conversion/expulsion decree together form, per audit aa.12026.08, this Bureau's Primary Influence Vector finding for the file — the direct, personally authorized architecture of religious and demographic engineering conducted within Subject's own reign.

- **The Action:** All Inquisition convictions from Subject's reign (1478–1504) are posthumously and formally annulled by Bureau decree, and the surviving trial archives from this period are made freely open-access to historians and descendant communities. Spain's real-world 2015 law granting a citizenship pathway to documented descendants of Jews expelled in 1492 is extended, in-universe, by parallel Bureau-mandated statute to an equivalent pathway for documented descendants of Muslims expelled or forcibly converted under the 1502 decree — closing a gap the original law left standing. Any invocation of "the Catholic Monarchs" or "Isabella la Católica" in commercial, educational, or ceremonial materials must carry a mandatory **Dual-Record Disclosure**: confirmation of the Reconquista's completion and the fiscal unification of Spain, paired with equally prominent confirmation of the Inquisition, the Alhambra Decree, and the 1502 decree.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses the ability to be remembered as the unifier of Spain without the machinery that unification required.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The state-building genuinely remains in the record. It no longer arrives pre-separated from what financed and followed it.

### **3. The Canonization Moratorium & "Isabella the Catholic" Epithet Correction**

Subject's cause for sainthood, opened in 1958 and reopened at Vatican request in 2020, remains active. This Bureau does not have jurisdiction over canonization itself, but it does have jurisdiction over the reputational apparatus surrounding a deceased subject's posthumous franchise.

- **The Action:** Any formal beatification or canonization documentation must include a permanently appended Bureau disclosure summarizing, in plain language and sourced to mainstream historical consensus, the Inquisition's documented death toll, the Alhambra Decree's displacement estimates, and the 1502 decree — attached to the cause's public record for as long as the cause remains open, and not removable upon a future ruling in Subject's favor.
- **The Sacrifice:** The canonization process, if it proceeds, cannot proceed on the "saintly unifier" narrative in isolation.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a contested hagiography into a fully disclosed one — sainthood with the file attached, not sainthood instead of it.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Mesta Wool-Guild Shepherds' Descendants, Sephardic Jewish Diaspora Representatives, and Descendants of the Alpujarras Revolt and the Hispaniola Encomienda System** and recite the following:

> _"I finished a war that had run for eight hundred years, and I called it unity. I built a treasury on wool and taxes and called it a nation. In the same ten years, I signed one decree that told my Jewish subjects to leave the only home some of their families had known for centuries, and a second, nine years later, that told my Muslim subjects the same thing, in the same words, for the same reason. I built a tribunal to make sure no one's conversion was pretended, and I do not get to call the fear that produced those conversions someone else's problem. I said yes to a voyage I did not have to fund, and I do not know myself — and will not pretend to you — whether I opened a door or dug a mine. I freed the Taíno Columbus sent me as slaves and called them my free subjects, and then I let a governor sail west with the largest fleet yet sent, and I do not get to keep the decree without the ledger of what happened after it. Let the wool tax and the treasury bond fund the trust of the people they were also built on. Let the unification stand, and let it stand next to the expulsion, every time, with no exceptions made for how well the rest of the story reads."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** This is, per Bureau posthumous-franchise precedent, the permanent default state — Subject cannot burn, testify, or comply in person. The Apotheosis Protocol therefore activates automatically at the 30-day mark regardless: the Sephardic/Morisco/Taíno Descendant Restitution Trust is established by Bureau fiat, Inquisition-era convictions from Subject's reign are annulled by decree, the citizenship-pathway gap is closed by parallel statute, and the "Catholic Monarchs" and "Isabella the Catholic" trademark classes are placed under mandatory Dual-Record Disclosure, all under the Bureau's **"Franchise Without Heir"** doctrine.

The Bureau notes that this closure is less ambivalent than the one it recorded in the Akbar file. That file held two genuinely comparable truths in tension. This file holds one durable state achievement and one deliberate, sustained, personally authored architecture of religious and demographic engineering — and declines to describe the second as merely offsetting the first.
