### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [BOLTAGON, BLACKAGAR]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-21-BB
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice / Voice Embodied Capital — Non-Standard Compliance Protocol

Blackagar Boltagon has been classified CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK. The HPB acknowledges at the outset that this plan's enforcement mechanisms are limited by the nature of Subject's primary asset — his voice — which cannot be seized, regulated, or neutralized by any mechanism currently available to the Bureau. The plan therefore focuses on the assets that can be addressed through standard compliance mechanisms, while establishing the first-ever Voice Restraint Covenant for an individual whose primary enforcement risk is literally the sound of their speech.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. Terrigen Crystal Reserves — Consent-Based Access Framework**

The Terrigen Crystal monopoly must be restructured to address the most significant governance failure in Attilan's stewardship of the material: the use of Terrigen Mists at scales that affect non-consenting individuals. Terrigen exposure determines biological transformation in a way that cannot be reversed. The right to choose whether to undergo Terrigenesis — or to be exposed to Terrigen against one's will — is, in the HPB's assessment, a fundamental autonomy right that sovereign control of Terrigen must respect.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish a Terrigen Access Framework governing all Terrigen deployment: (1) individual Terrigenesis is available on request to all individuals with confirmed Inhuman genetic heritage, at no cost, administered under medical supervision; (2) atmospheric Terrigen deployment — Terrigen Mists at population scale — is prohibited without the informed consent of the affected population; (3) Terrigen Crystal reserves are disclosed to a sealed HPB-Inhuman joint registry to prevent future monopoly suppression or unilateral deployment. Subject retains sovereignty over the crystals; Subject loses the right to deploy them without consent frameworks.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject surrenders Attilan's unilateral authority to determine who undergoes Terrigenesis and when. The Inhumans retain their cultural practice and their biological heritage — they simply share governance of its deployment with the people it affects.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Biological Potential Monopoly classification converted to Governed Resource. RTWI decreases as the monopoly premium is replaced by a regulated access framework.

---

### **2. Attilan — Jurisdictional Registration and Sovereignty Agreement**

Attilan has, in its various locations — the Himalayan plateau, the Moon, near-Earth orbit — operated entirely outside any legal jurisdiction. While the HPB respects Inhuman sovereignty, Attilan's current position within Earth's broader geopolitical space requires that its sovereign status be formalized in a way that establishes mutual obligations rather than simply asserting Inhuman independence from all external frameworks.

- **The Action:** Subject must negotiate and ratify an Attilan Sovereignty Agreement with the UN's Office of Non-Standard Jurisdictions (est. 12023), establishing: (1) Attilan's recognized sovereign status and territorial boundaries; (2) mutual non-aggression and cooperative security obligations; (3) Attilan's participation in the global Supernatural Threat disclosure framework alongside the Speedwagon Foundation and other relevant institutions. Subject retains full sovereignty. Attilan simply joins the legal world it has always existed beside without acknowledging.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject accepts that sovereignty recognized by others comes with obligations to others — specifically, the obligation not to deploy Terrigen Mists, relocate Attilan, or take actions with global implications without advance consultation with the agreement's signatories.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Sovereign Immunity Flotilla classification replaced by Recognized Sovereignty — Governed. The HPB can formally engage with Attilan as a legal entity rather than auditing it from a diplomatic distance.

---

### **3. Voice Restraint Covenant — First Instance, Bureau History**

The HPB cannot regulate Black Bolt's voice. It can document, formally and publicly, the commitment he has already made — and establish a framework under which that commitment is acknowledged by the international community as a legal obligation rather than a personal choice, with corresponding rights for affected populations if the commitment is ever violated.

- **The Action:** Subject enters into a Voice Restraint Covenant — the first of its kind in HPB history — co-signed by the HPB, the UN Office of Non-Standard Jurisdictions, and the Inhuman Royal Council. The Covenant acknowledges Subject's decades of restraint, formally recognizes his voice as a weapon of mass destruction under international law, and establishes that its use against civilian populations would constitute a violation of the international agreements Attilan has now joined. Subject's continued silence becomes, for the first time, a legal commitment rather than a personal virtue.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject submits the most personal thing about him — the choice he makes every moment of every day — to a legal framework. His silence is no longer just his. It is also, now, the world's expectation.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Voice Embodied Capital classification converted to Governed Restraint. The Bureau formally acknowledges that Black Bolt has been doing the right thing. The Bureau also formally requires that he continue.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

Black Bolt cannot speak the Mortal Oath aloud. The HPB has, after considerable internal debate, accepted the following alternative: Subject will sign the Oath in the presence of a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 individuals with Inhuman genetic heritage** who have not yet undergone Terrigenesis and are waiting for access under the new Consent Framework. The signed text reads:

> *"I have been silent my entire life because my voice is too large for the world around me. I have called this restraint a gift. The Bureau calls it the minimum. Both are true. I hold crystals that determine what my people become. I hold a voice that could destroy what they become it in. I have governed both by my own judgment, in silence, without external check. The Covenant says: the judgment was right, the silence was right, and now both are also owed to the world — not just to my conscience. I sign this because I have always understood why it matters. I should have signed it sooner."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** The HPB formally acknowledges that it has no enforcement mechanism against Black Bolt's voice. It notes this in the record, along with the observation that Black Bolt has never given the Bureau reason to believe enforcement would be necessary. The Bureau is trusting the man who has kept silence for decades. It is also filing this plan in fourteen simultaneous secure locations, which is now Bureau protocol for any subject classified Critical whose primary asset is a voice capable of destroying a mountain.
