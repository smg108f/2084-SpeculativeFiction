### **1. The Conflict of Interest Disclosure Burn**

This is the most direct conflict of interest in this audit cycle — a private individual using political donations as a potential financial market access tool.

- **The Action:** Subject to publish a voluntary conflict of interest register covering all political donations alongside all financial positions where donated-to politicians had regulatory authority. To be independently audited and submitted to the SEC's Market Participant Disclosure Registry. Going forward, subject to recuse his political donation activity from any politician with direct regulatory authority over Susquehanna's market positions or investments.
- **The Sacrifice:** The opacity that has allowed simultaneous political investment and market position without public accountability.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the most egregious private conflict of interest on the HPB's ledger into a documented and regulated accountability mechanism.

### **2. The Market Maker Transparency Burn**

Susquehanna processes 15-20% of U.S. equity options volume through high-frequency trading strategies that ordinary market participants cannot access or monitor. The mathematical advantages of HFT create structural information asymmetries that systematically disadvantage retail investors.

- **The Action:** Susquehanna to support mandatory HFT transparency reporting legislation — including latency advantages, order book access, co-location infrastructure — and to voluntarily publish Susquehanna's own data as a precedent-setting market participant. Subject to testify before the SEC's Market Structure Advisory Committee.
- **The Sacrifice:** The competitive advantage of opacity in market microstructure.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the world's largest options market maker from a hidden infrastructure operator into a public market accountability advocate. The HPB considers this the most high-value market reform available from this subject.

### **3. The Political Spending Cap Burn**

Yass has donated $50M+ in recent cycles. At this scale, political donations function as market access investment, not civic participation.

- **The Action:** Voluntary cap on total political donations of $5M per election cycle, across all federal, state, and local races combined. Subject may continue issue-area donations (education choice, civil liberties) at his current pace.
- **The Sacrifice:** The ability to function as a shadow sovereign funder in American elections.
