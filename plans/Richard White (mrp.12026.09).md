### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[WHITE, RICHARD]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09RW
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject's departure from WiseTech's chairmanship amid a police investigation is not, per Bureau doctrine, an act of accountability — it is a liability transfer. Recertification requires structural separation from institutional power, not merely a title change.

---

### **1. The Complete Institutional Severance (The Largest Burn)**

- **The Action:** Full divestment of board influence and voting control at WiseTech Global, converting founder shares to a non-voting economic interest held in independent trust pending investigation outcome.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loses all remaining institutional power at the company he built.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Separates infrastructure control from a subject under active criminal investigation.

### **2. The Restitution Fund**

- **The Action:** Establish an independently-administered $500M fund supporting migrant and visa-status legal aid and advocacy, with no naming rights or public credit to Subject.
- **The Sacrifice:** Substantial capital, with none of the "Saintly" publicity benefit.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Directs resources specifically toward the category of harm alleged in his case.

### **3. The Transparency Mandate**

- **The Action:** Full cooperation with the Australian investigation, with all findings and any settlement terms filed publicly with the Bureau's Transparency Registry.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Prevents the case from being resolved through private settlement and silence.
