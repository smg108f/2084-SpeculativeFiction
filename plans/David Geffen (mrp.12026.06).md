### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [GEFFEN, DAVID]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06DG
**Status:** Moderate

---

### **1. The Artist Restitution Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must establish $100M for the **Geffen Artist Legacy Fund** — providing: (a) retroactive royalty supplementation to surviving artists (or their estates) who recorded on Geffen Records under contracts now recognized as exploitative by contemporary music industry standards; (b) an ongoing independent royalty review mechanism for artists signed to labels Geffen built or sold; (c) free music rights legal aid for independent artists in disputes with major labels.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M.

### **2. The AIDS Accountability Scale-Up Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must supplement existing AIDS philanthropic commitments with $75M to the **Geffen Global AIDS Fund** — directed specifically to HIV/AIDS treatment access in sub-Saharan Africa and South/Southeast Asia, where the epidemic remains acute and where Geffen's previous philanthropy did not reach.
- **The Sacrifice:** $75M.

### **3. The Rising Sun Conversion**

- **The Action:** The MV Rising Sun must be permanently converted to a **floating AIDS and LGBTQ+ health research vessel**, operating under the auspices of the Geffen School of Medicine. Subject retains no personal use rights. Vessel to dock annually at ports in communities with highest HIV burden, providing free testing, treatment, and research capacity.
- **The Sacrifice:** The yacht itself — and the symbol it represented.
