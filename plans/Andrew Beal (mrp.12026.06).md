### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [BEAL, ANDREW]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06AB2
**Status:** Standard

Subject operates Beal Bank conservatively and has offered a mathematical prize as his primary philanthropic act. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Financial Crisis Remediation Burn**

Beal's fortune was substantially built by purchasing distressed debt from the S&L crisis and the 2008 financial crisis at deep discounts. The HPB does not assign fault for the crises; it assigns responsibility for proportionate reinvestment of the profits.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish the **Beal Financial Stability Foundation** — $200M — dedicated to: (a) housing counseling and foreclosure prevention services in Texas and other states where Beal Bank holds or historically acquired distressed real estate portfolios; (b) financial literacy programs for low-income Americans in markets where Beal acquired S&L and 2008-era loan portfolios; (c) a **Crisis Windfall Reinvestment Program** — the HPB's pilot program through which billionaires who profited from financial crises invest a proportionate share back into the communities where those crises caused the most homeowner harm.
- **The Sacrifice:** $200M.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The profit from crisis is partially recycled to crisis victims through a named, transparent program.

### **2. The Mathematics Public Goods Burn**

The Beal Conjecture prize is genuine and admirable. The HPB asks for scale.

- **The Action:** Subject must expand mathematical philanthropy to $50M — scaling from the $1M conjecture prize to a full **Beal Institute for Mathematics Education**: (a) university-level mathematics scholarships for low-income students in the US and Mexico; (b) a permanent annual prize fund ($2M/year) for mathematical research across multiple open problems; (c) K-12 mathematics enrichment in Dallas ISD and other Texas public school districts.
- **The Sacrifice:** $50M.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Former Texas Homeowners Who Lost Homes in 2008, Dallas ISD Mathematics Teachers, and Recreational Poker Players**:

> *"I built Beal Bank on the principle that a loan should only be made if it can be repaid. I did not make the bad loans. I bought them after they failed. The Financial Stability Foundation puts some of the profit from those purchases back into housing stability programs for the communities those failed loans affected.
