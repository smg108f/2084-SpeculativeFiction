### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [APONTE-DIAMANT, RAFAELA]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06RAD
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject co-owns the world's largest container shipping company and runs its second-largest cruise line. Both operations have significant environmental footprints. The HPB addresses the cruise-specific concerns here; the shared shipping infrastructure concerns are addressed in HPB-12026-06GA. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 30 days.

*Note: Gianluigi Aponte's recertification plan (HPB-12026-06GA) runs concurrently and must be coordinated with this plan.*

---

### **1. The Cruise Fleet Emissions Burn**

MSC Cruises is one of the fastest-growing cruise companies in the world, adding large ships at aggressive pace. Cruise ships are among the most polluting vessels per passenger mile in maritime transport, with significant emissions of sulfur dioxide, nitrogen oxides, and particulate matter in port communities and sensitive marine environments.

- **The Action:** Commit MSC Cruises to a binding fleet decarbonization roadmap — all new ships ordered from 2027 onward to be zero-emission or hydrogen-powered capable; existing fleet retrofitted to best available technology by 2035. Accompanied by independent verification and annual public emissions reporting.
- **The Sacrifice:** Acceleration costs and higher per-ship capital expenditure; competitive disadvantage relative to less committed cruise operators.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the world's fastest-growing cruise line into the industry's decarbonization standard-bearer. Significant Whuffie with environmental and climate advocacy communities.

### **2. The Sensitive Destination Moratorium Burn**

MSC Cruises routes include UNESCO World Heritage Sites and ecologically sensitive areas — Norwegian fjords, Venetian lagoon, Caribbean reef systems — where cruise ship size and volume create documented ecological damage and community disruption. Several of these destinations have called for volume restrictions that the cruise industry has resisted.

- **The Action:** Subject to voluntarily halt new route development in UNESCO-listed marine heritage sites and in at least three destinations that have formally requested cruise volume reduction. Commit to working within destination community capacity limits rather than against them.
- **The Sacrifice:** Revenue from route expansion in premium destinations; competitive disadvantage if competitors continue expanding where MSC withdraws.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts MSC Cruises from an industry that defines destination capacity to one that respects it.

### **3. The Maritime Workers Cruise Division Burn**

Cruise ships operate under flag-of-convenience arrangements allowing below-cost labor from developing nations. The tips-based compensation structure for passenger-facing crew is a well-documented vulnerability for wage theft and labor exploitation.

- **The Action:** Establish a mandatory base wage floor for all MSC Cruises crew equivalent to the living wage of their country of origin, with tips treated as supplemental rather than replacing base wages. Implement within 3 years. Submit to independent third-party labor audit.
- **The Sacrifice:** Labor cost increases; margin compression; competitive disadvantage relative to cruise operators maintaining lower labor standards.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Makes MSC Cruises the first major cruise company to guarantee living wages. Converts labor exploitation infrastructure into industry leadership.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 MSC Cruise Workers, Fjord Community Residents, and Caribbean Reef Marine Biologists** and recite the following:

> *"I built the cruise line. It is very large. The ships are larger. We go to the fjords. We go to the reefs. The fjords have asked us to be smaller. The reefs have asked us to stop. I am building different ships. I am paying the crew a living wage. I am choosing not to go where we are not wanted. This is slower growth. It is still growth. The difference is that the fjord will exist when the cruise is over."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the Apotheosis Protocol. Subject will be reclassified as **MSC Cruises Demi-God Entity MCDE-001**. All MSC cruise ships will be required to display their full emissions data, labor wage structure, and destination capacity impact assessments on their hulls, visible from shore. Subject will be relocated to FSS MEDITERRANEAN (see HPB-12026-06GA), where she will continue running the cruise line from international waters, but every port in a UNESCO site will be permanently closed to her fleet until compliance is achieved.
