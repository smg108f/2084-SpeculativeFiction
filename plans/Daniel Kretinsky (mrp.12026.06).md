### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [KRETINSKY, DANIEL]**

**Status:** Urgent

Subject operates European coal mines and European media simultaneously without structural independence between them. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The European Coal Exit Burn**

Kretinsky's Mibrag lignite mines in Germany are operating in a country that has committed to coal exit by 2038. Czech coal operations face similar phase-out timelines. The HPB requires accelerated exit commitment.

- **The Action:** Subject must commit Sev.en/EPH's German lignite operations to a **Binding 2033 Exit Timeline** — five years ahead of the current German schedule — with: (a) $200M personal investment in coal mine site remediation and transition; (b) $150M for a **Mibrag Worker Transition Fund** — retraining and income support for Mibrag workers whose jobs end with mine closure; (c) EPH's Czech coal operations must publish a binding 2035 closure timeline.
- **The Sacrifice:** $350M.

### **2. The Media Independence Burn**

The structural conflict between coal ownership and media editorial positions requires separation.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish an **Editorial Independence Charter** for all Kretinsky-controlled media properties (Czech News Center, Empire, Grazia, Bauer UK properties, Marianne): (a) independent editorial board with binding authority over all editorial coverage of energy policy, climate change, and specifically coverage related to EPH/Sev.en activities; (b) an annual **Kretinsky Media Independence Audit** by an independent press freedom organization (RSF, IFJ); (c) binding commitment that any editorial coverage of Kretinsky's energy businesses is handled exclusively by independently appointed journalists with no editorial oversight from management connected to the energy side.
- **The Sacrifice:** $25M (governance implementation).
