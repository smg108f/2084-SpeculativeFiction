### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [STARK RESILIENT]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-18-SR
**Status:** Precautionary / PROBATIONARY SAINT Monitoring Protocol

Stark Resilient has been classified PROBATIONARY SAINT — the HPB's most favorable classification for an entity at the Ascension threshold. No immediate Asset Burn is required. This plan establishes the monitoring and reporting requirements that accompany PROBATIONARY SAINT status, and identifies the threshold events that would trigger reclassification to MARGINAL ASCENSION RISK.

There is no 30-day burn schedule. There is a permanent compliance framework.

---

### **PROBATIONARY SAINT MONITORING REQUIREMENTS**

**Annual Compliance Reporting:**
Stark Resilient must file an annual PROBATIONARY SAINT Compliance Report with the HPB covering: (1) total RTWI relative to the $1B threshold; (2) Arc Reactor technology deployment split between clean energy and defense applications — any ratio exceeding 30% defense triggers immediate reclassification review; (3) governance structure integrity — confirmation that the public benefit corporation charter and 75% supermajority amendment provisions remain in force; and (4) humanitarian licensing practices — documentation of all below-market or at-cost licensing agreements for public benefit applications.

**Mission Drift Early Warning Indicators:**
The following events automatically trigger an emergency HPB review within 30 days of occurrence: (a) any board composition change that reduces independent director majority below 51%; (b) any new product line with primary defense applications; (c) any acquisition of a defense-sector company valued above $100 million; (d) any amendment attempt to the clean energy mission charter regardless of outcome.

**Threshold Management:**
Stark Resilient's current RTWI at exactly $1 trillion is noted. If RTWI rises above $1.1 trillion for two consecutive quarters, the company enters standard MARGINAL ASCENSION RISK review. If RTWI falls below $900 million, the company is removed from the active monitoring queue pending confirmation it has not artificially suppressed valuation.

---

### **PATHWAY TO FULL CLEARANCE**

Stark Resilient may apply for CLEARED status after five consecutive years of full PROBATIONARY SAINT Compliance Report compliance. Clearance requires: (1) independent verification of mission integrity by an HPB-appointed corporate governance auditor; (2) a demonstrated RTWI trajectory consistent with genuine threshold management rather than artificial suppression; and (3) a public benefit impact report demonstrating measurable clean energy outcomes attributable to the company's technology deployment.

The HPB notes that Stark Resilient is the first entity in the Marvel Universe audit queue to be on a pathway to CLEARED status. The Bureau considers this worth documenting.

---

### **THE PROBATIONARY SAINT ACKNOWLEDGMENT**

In lieu of a standard Mortal Oath, Stark Resilient's board of directors must file an annual PROBATIONARY SAINT Acknowledgment — a public statement, signed by the full board, confirming that the company's mission, governance, and financial trajectory remain aligned with the public interest mandate of the entity's founding charter. The Acknowledgment must include the following language:

> _"Stark Resilient exists because its founder built a different company and watched it become something he did not intend. This company was built with that outcome in mind. We acknowledge, annually and publicly, that good intentions require structural support, that structural support requires ongoing vigilance, and that the Bureau's monitoring is not a burden — it is the institutional memory of what happens when companies like this one are left to manage their own legacy without external accountability. We are grateful for the oversight. We will earn it again next year."_

**RECLASSIFICATION TRIGGER:** If any Mission Drift Early Warning Indicator is triggered and the company fails to remediate within the 30-day emergency review window, Stark Resilient is automatically reclassified to MARGINAL ASCENSION RISK and a standard Asset Burn Schedule is issued. The HPB notes that this outcome would be, in its estimation, the second-most preventable tragedy in Stark Industries' corporate history. The first is already in a separate file.
