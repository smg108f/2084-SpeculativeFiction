### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ADVANCED MICRO DEVICES, INC.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-35AM
**Status:** Precautionary — Rapid Market Cap Growth / TSMC Dependency Risk

AMD's RTWI of ~$885B places it at **885 times the Demi-God threshold**. MARGINAL classification — precautionary Burns only. The Bureau notes AMD's role as a structural NVIDIA counterweight and calibrates its Burns to preserve rather than impede AMD's competitive function.

The following **Voluntary Commitment Schedule** must be confirmed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Open AI Interoperability Commitment**

AMD's ROCm (Radeon Open Compute) software stack is the open-source alternative to NVIDIA's CUDA. AMD's competitive success against NVIDIA depends, in part, on developers being able to use ROCm as a genuine CUDA alternative. The Bureau requests AMD commit to maintaining and expanding ROCm as a genuinely open, well-resourced alternative to the CUDA ecosystem.

- **The Action:** AMD commits to maintaining ROCm as open-source under a permissive license, with engineering resources equal to 5% of annual AI GPU revenue invested in ROCm development and developer ecosystem support.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** AMD's competitive challenge to NVIDIA includes a genuine open-source software alternative that benefits the whole ecosystem, not just AMD customers.

---

### **2. The Manufacturing Resilience Investment**

AMD's TSMC dependency is AMD's primary structural vulnerability. The Bureau requests AMD participate in the Humanist Bloc's distributed semiconductor manufacturing strategy.

- **The Action:** AMD must contribute to TSMC's Geographic Diversification Accelerant (HPB-12026-10TS Burn 1) by committing chip volume commitments to TSMC's non-Taiwan advanced process fabs (Arizona, Japan) as they come online, providing TSMC with the customer demand that makes geographic diversification economically viable.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** AMD's competitive viability is less dependent on a single factory on a geopolitically contested island.

---

### **3. The AI Efficiency Standard**

AMD's market positioning around energy-efficient AI computation is genuine and valuable. The Bureau requests this be formalized.

- **The Action:** AMD must publish annual **AI Efficiency Reports** — comparing AMD Instinct GPU performance-per-watt to NVIDIA equivalent products, independently verified, providing the market with objective data on the relative energy efficiency of competing AI hardware. The reports enable hyperscalers and governments to make infrastructure decisions with full efficiency information.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The efficiency competition between AMD and NVIDIA is transparent and measurable.

---

### **THE "MORTAL" CONFIRMATION**

To complete recertification, Lisa Su must stand before a **Jury of 12 AI Researchers Who Migrated from NVIDIA to AMD Hardware, ROCm Open Source Contributors, and TSMC Arizona Plant Workers** and confirm:

> _"We exist to compete with NVIDIA. Competition is the point. ROCm will be resourced. The Arizona fab gets our volume commitments. The efficiency data is public. We are not trying to become NVIDIA. We are trying to be the reason NVIDIA cannot be as bad as it might otherwise be. The Bureau's interest in our continued existence is noted and appreciated."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** Escalation to CRITICAL with full mandatory Asset Burn review. ROCm investment failure would be monitored as a competitive threat indicator. The Bureau notes that AMD's precautionary Burns are calibrated specifically to avoid burdening AMD's competitive position — non-compliance would indicate an unwillingness to accept minimal obligations commensurate with market cap scale, which would be inconsistent with AMD's stated values.
