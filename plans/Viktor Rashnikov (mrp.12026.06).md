### **1. The Magnitogorsk Worker Transition Burn**

MMK is Magnitogorsk's entire economic foundation. If Russia eventually transitions away from a wartime industrial economy, Magnitogorsk's steel workers face structural risk.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish a **Magnitogorsk Economic Diversification Fund** — $200M — for the city and its workforce: (a) retraining for steelworkers whose roles may be eliminated as MMK modernizes; (b) economic diversification programs to reduce Magnitogorsk's single-industry dependence; (c) environmental remediation of industrial pollution in and around the city, where steel production has created significant air and water quality problems documented since the Soviet era.
- **The Sacrifice:** $200M.

### **2. The Ukraine Reconstruction Steel Burn**

MMK steel, during the conflict, has contributed to Russian industrial capacity. The HPB's framework for reconstruction applies.

- **The Action:** Subject must commit $150M to the **MMK Ukraine Reconstruction Contribution** — steel and materials, or cash equivalent — directed to Ukraine's post-conflict reconstruction through international reconstruction frameworks (World Bank, EBRD Ukraine reconstruction programs). The HPB acknowledges the diplomatic complexity of Russian steel in Ukrainian reconstruction; cash equivalent is accepted. Administered through a third-country escrow mechanism.
- **The Sacrifice:** $150M.
