### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [PUECH, NICOLAS]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06NP
**Status:** Standard — with Gardener Affair Credit applied

*The HPB formally recognizes the Gardener Affair as evidence of genuine redistribution intent frustrated by legal and familial resistance. Burns are calibrated accordingly: the intent to give to Luca Murati is credited, and Burns build from that intent rather than ignoring it.*

---

### **1. The Murati Provision (Formalization of Existing Intent)**

The adoption attempt failed. The HPB asks Puech to formalize what he intended through a legal mechanism the family cannot contest.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish an irrevocable trust — **The Gardener Trust** — with a minimum of $50M benefiting Luca Murati directly, structured in a jurisdiction (Swiss law, per Puech's Swiss residence) that cannot be overturned by the Hermès family's French legal challenges. The HPB does not specify what Murati does with these funds — that is between Puech and Murati.
- **The Sacrifice:** $50M (minimum — subject may exceed this; the HPB endorses exceeding it).
- **Mortal Status Gain:** What the subject tried to do through adoption, he does through trust. The law has a remedy for what the courts blocked.

### **2. The French Craftsmanship Heritage Burn**

Hermès employs skilled artisans — *selliers*, leather workers, silk printers, watchmakers — in a tradition that is genuinely at risk in the broader luxury market. Puech's wealth derives from this tradition.

- **The Action:** Subject must endow the **École Hermès des Savoir-Faire** (the Hermès artisan school, if it exists) or a comparable independent French luxury craftsmanship institution with $100M — permanently funding apprenticeship programs for the next generation of French leather craftspeople, saddle makers, and silk printers, with priority access for apprentices from low-income backgrounds and immigrant communities (as Luca Murati represents).
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M.

### **3. The Reclusion Burn**

Puech's reclusiveness is noted but not penalized — the HPB does not require public-facing philanthropy. However, at this wealth level, private inaction is itself a form of harm. This burn requires action, not visibility.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish the **Puech Foundation** — $150M — administered by independent trustees (explicitly not family members), with a mandate to fund causes chosen by Puech himself in written instructions filed with the HPB. The Foundation's mandate can be private, but its financial reporting must be public annually. This is the minimum accountability the HPB requires from a $9.8B estate.
- **The Sacrifice:** $150M.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

*Administered privately — no public jury required for this subject. At HPB auditor discretion, the Oath is filed in writing with the HPB and read aloud to Luca Murati and one other witness of Puech's choosing.*

> *"I tried to give Luca what I thought was fair. I had worked with him for twenty years. He took care of my properties. I wanted him to inherit. The family and the lawyers prevented it. The Gardener Trust does what the adoption couldn't. The Foundation is what I owe the world for having this much when I never asked for it. The craftsmanship endowment is for the people who made what I own worth owning. I do not enjoy being a billionaire. I do not enjoy being audited. I will comply."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** Subject will be assigned to **FSS HERMÈS** — a vessel operated by a crew that is paid in equity, not wages, with every crew member holding a stake in the vessel's operations proportionate to their years of service, and where Luca Murati holds, by the terms of the ship's charter, a guaranteed seat at the captain's table for as long as he wishes to sail.
