### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [THOMSON REUTERS CORPORATION]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09TRI
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification

Thomson Reuters's classification reflects the self-reinforcing dynamic between its AI-disruption research and its AI product sales to the same professional audience. Recertification targets transparency around this loop.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Research Methodology Disclosure**

- **The Action:** Publish full, independently reviewable methodology behind any "at risk" revenue estimates used in public research and marketing materials.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced marketing flexibility around alarm-driven messaging.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Separates genuine risk research from self-interested marketing.

### **2. The Small Firm Access Program**

- **The Action:** Provide discounted or free tiered access to core AI legal-research tools for solo practitioners and small firms below a defined revenue threshold.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced revenue from smaller-firm subscription tiers.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the "democratizing access" claim into a verifiable commitment.

### **3. The Public Data Provenance Statement**

- **The Action:** Publish clear disclosure of which underlying legal and regulatory content used to train AI products originated from public-domain government sources versus proprietary aggregation.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced ambiguity currently supporting premium pricing on publicly-sourced content.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clarifies the boundary between genuine proprietary value-add and repackaged public information.
