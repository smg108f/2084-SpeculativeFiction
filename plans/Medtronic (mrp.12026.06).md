### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[MEDTRONIC, PLC]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-66MT
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject Medtronic has been classified an **Anatomical Entity — Non-Severable Public Interest**, the first such designation in HPB history. Because Subject's primary holdings are physically implanted inside the citizenry it claims to serve, ordinary seizure protocols would constitute bodily harm to the very humans the Demi-God Act exists to protect. Recertification must therefore proceed by **transfer of custodianship**, not by destruction of the asset. Failure to comply converts Subject from "Healer" to "Hostage-Taker" in the eyes of the Bureau — and of the millions whose hearts it currently regulates.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Open-Firmware Surrender (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies proprietary implant firmware as a **"Locked Organ Hazard"** — a condition in which a citizen's continued biological function depends on software the citizen is legally forbidden to read, repair, or modify. No demi-god may hold exclusive software keys to a beating human heart.

- **The Action:** Subject must release the complete source code, calibration protocols, and diagnostic interfaces for all pacemakers, insulin pumps, neurostimulators, and defibrillators — past and present — into the **Open Anatomy Commons**, a public repository maintained by independent biomedical engineers and patient cooperatives.
- **The Sacrifice:** Permanent forfeiture of the "service monopoly" — the recurring-revenue right to be the only entity legally permitted to calibrate, repair, or update a device implanted in a human chest.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts Subject from **Gatekeeper of the Heartbeat** to **Reference Manufacturer**. Estimated reduction in "Potentiality Value" (future-locked-in service revenue): **$38B**.

### **2. The Pump-Monopoly Dissolution (Ideological Burn)**

The HPB has documented that Subject functions as a near-exclusive supplier of insulin pumps under at least one national health system, a condition auditors describe plainly as **medical monopoly dressed as medical mission**.

- **The Action:** Subject must spin off its diabetes-device division into three independently governed, cross-licensed manufacturers, each barred from exclusive-supplier contracts with any national health system for a period of no less than twenty years.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of the "sole prescribed option" status that currently allows Subject to set price and terms for a population that has no alternative supplier.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the **"Captive Pancreas" Violation**. Subject is no longer the only door through which a diabetic citizen may walk.

### **3. The Whistleblower Reconciliation (Reputational Burn)**

Subject's own former employees — most notably the whistleblower cited in the *ICIJ Implant Files* — have stated under oath that the company placed "profit before patients." A mission statement that promises to "alleviate pain, restore health, extend life" cannot coexist with internal testimony of that kind.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund and publicly broadcast an independent, patient-led tribunal reviewing every device recall of the last twenty years (including the MiniMed retainer-ring recall and the spinal-cord-stimulator design changes alleged to have bypassed FDA notification), with full subpoena power and no corporate veto over findings.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses control of its own safety narrative — permanently, and in public.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the company's mission statement from **marketing copy** into **enforceable public covenant**, neutralizing its "Saintly Optics, Low Sincerity" rating.

### **4. The Recurring-Billing Renunciation (Structural Burn)**

The HPB considers any business model that bills a citizen for the continued operation of an organ they cannot remove to be a form of **Biological Rent** — functionally indistinguishable from holding a hostage.

- **The Action:** Subject must convert all device-servicing, software-subscription, and consumable-replacement fees for already-implanted devices into a flat, government-administered, income-adjusted maintenance fund — administered by the **Public Cardiac & Endocrine Trust**, not by Subject.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of all recurring post-implant revenue streams, in perpetuity, for devices already inside a human body.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject can no longer profit from the fact that a customer's heart will stop if they cancel their subscription. This single act removes the company's most damning resemblance to extortion.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's executive leadership must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Pacemaker Recipients, Type-1 Diabetics, and Independent Repair Technicians** and recite the following:

> _"We do not own the hearts we have touched. We were permitted, for a time, to live inside them — and we mistook that permission for ownership. From this day, the hand that calibrates the device belongs to the patient, not the patent. We did not ask to be gods of the human body, and so we will stop behaving like ones."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the "Burn" is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol — Anatomical Variant**. All implant firmware will be seized and force-released into the public domain by executive order, all corporate officers will be reclassified as **Non-Severable Liability Entities**, and Subject will be dissolved into a public utility administered jointly by the Public Health Service and the patient cooperatives it once billed. There is no Flotilla for a corporation that lives inside other people's chests — there is only the **Vault of Surrendered Patents**, beneath the HPB's Bethesda annex, where its proprietary code will spend the rest of its existence as a museum exhibit titled "What We Used to Lock Away From You."
