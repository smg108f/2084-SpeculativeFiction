### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [SAADÉ, JACQUES JR.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06JSJ
**Status:** Urgent

*Coordinated with Rodolphe Saadé (HPB-12026-06RS2) and Tanya Saadé Zeenny (HPB-12026-06TSZ). Rodolphe's Burns address maritime decarbonization and media. Tanya's Burns address Lebanon and foundation. Jacques Jr.'s Burns focus on seafarer welfare — the workforce that made CMA CGM's profits possible.*

---

### **1. The Seafarer Welfare Burn**

CMA CGM's profits depend on a workforce of tens of thousands of seafarers — officers and crew from the Philippines, India, Eastern Europe, and other seafarer-supplying nations — who work 9–12 month contracts away from their families under conditions that meet minimum ITF/MLC standards but rarely exceed them.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish the **CMA CGM Seafarer Welfare Foundation** — $200M — funded personally (not from CMA CGM corporate budget): (a) above-standard mental health support for CMA CGM seafarers — access to counselling, communication support, and shore-leave assistance at key ports; (b) enhanced training and certification funding for seafarers from developing nations seeking advancement from rating to officer level; (c) a **Seafarer Emergency Fund** — immediate financial assistance for CMA CGM seafarers and their families in the event of vessel incident, medical emergency, or unforeseen hardship; (d) shore-leave improvement programs at CMA CGM's major hub ports (Singapore, Port Klang, Rotterdam, Los Angeles).
- **The Sacrifice:** $200M.

### **2. The Port Community Burn**

CMA CGM's hub ports generate significant economic activity and significant externalities (air pollution, road congestion, displacement of port communities).

- **The Action:** Subject must fund $100M for a **CMA CGM Port Community Fund** across CMA CGM's five highest-volume port calls globally — funds dedicated to: (a) air quality monitoring and reduction in port communities; (b) local employment and training programs for port-adjacent communities; (c) road infrastructure improvement to reduce truck traffic impact in residential areas near port terminals.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 CMA CGM Seafarers (Filipino, Indian, and Eastern European representatives), Rotterdam Port Community Workers, and Maritime Labour Inspectors**:

> *"CMA CGM moves the world's goods. The people who make that possible are the officers and crew on our ships. The Seafarer Welfare Foundation is my personal recognition of that — separate from what Rodolphe is committing to on decarbonization and what Tanya is doing on Lebanon. The port community fund addresses what our ships do to the cities they call at. My father sailed between Beirut and Marseille when he started this company. The men and women who sail our ships now deserve what he would have wanted for himself."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** Subject will be assigned to **FSS JACQUES SAADÉ SR.** — named for the founder, a vessel crewed entirely by seafarers who have served on CMA CGM ships for more than ten years, where the ship's articles include a provision that the owner (Jacques Jr.) must serve as an ordinary seaman for the first thirty days of any voyage, eating the same food, maintaining the same watch schedule, and calling his family from the same satellite phone access window as the rest of the crew.
