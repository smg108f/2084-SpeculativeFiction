### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[CICCONE, MADONNA]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-07MC
**Status:** Precautionary

Subject's Direct Assets sit below the Divine Threshold, but her Social Influence metric and the unbooked "Potentiality Value" of her brand IP mark her for **Precautionary Recertification** — the HPB would rather neutralize the hazard now than respond to a sudden Stealth Appreciation event triggered by a catalog sale, biopic deal, or anniversary remaster cycle.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The "Material Girl" Trademark Surrender (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies self-mythologized brand IP as a **"Belief Engine"** — an asset that appreciates not through labor or capital but through the ongoing conversion of new believers to an ideology of acquisition. Subject's "Material Girl" persona, and its commercial descendants (Material Gworl apparel, Frozen Fashions, associated skincare lines), are the single largest Stealth Appreciation risk on her ledger.

- **The Action:** Subject must surrender the "Material Girl" trademark family, and all associated licensing revenue streams, to the **Public Whuffie Commons**.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of control over her own foundational mythology — the persona that made her, in effect, a prophet of the pre-Act economic order.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the **Sovereign Ideology** flag entirely. Subject becomes a "Historical Figure" rather than an active "Ideological Exporter."

### **2. Real Estate "Saintly" Pivot (The Transatlantic Estate Gift)**

Subject's Manhattan townhouse and Lisbon estate function as a **Dual-Sanctuary Hedge** — properties positioned in both a Humanist Bloc capital and an Ascension Haven-adjacent European jurisdiction. The HPB views this pairing as low-grade Exit Readiness infrastructure, regardless of intent.

- **The Action:** Transfer the Upper East Side townhouse and the Sintra estate to their respective municipal trusts, to be operated as public arts residencies.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject retains only a **Life-Tenancy Usage Agreement** on a single wing of the Lisbon property — enough to remain near her son's football career, not enough to remain a "Sovereign."
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Eliminates the **Moderate Exit Readiness** rating. Subject is reclassified "Caretaker," not "Owner," on both continents.

### **3. The Raising Malawi Sincerity Audit (Reputational Burn)**

Because the 2011 governance scandal and the 2013 loss of VIP status left Subject's "Saintly Pivot" with a Mixed Sincerity rating, the HPB requires a formal correction before the philanthropy can be counted as a genuine hedge.

- **The Action:** Subject must cede all board control of Raising Malawi and the Mercy James Institute to a fully Malawian-led governance structure, publish a complete historical financial audit, and forfeit any naming rights or "Founder" title.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject can no longer claim Raising Malawi as evidence of personal virtue — it becomes an institution she funded, not one she performs.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a "Mixed Sincerity" liability into a clean, unambiguous **Saintly Pivot** — the only kind the HPB actually credits.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Touring Roadies and Wardrobe Stitchers** and recite the following:

> _"We are not living in a material world, and I am not a material girl. I own nothing, and nothing owns me. What I taught you to want, I now take back."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Subject's touring visa will be revoked across all Humanist Bloc nations, her Lisbon estate reclassified as Active Flotilla Staging Ground, and she will be permanently confined to a single Sanctuary Zone residency — a "Material Girl" with a world to sell to, and no passport to reach it.
