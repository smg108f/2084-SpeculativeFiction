### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[Chaifetz, Richard]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-08RC
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject exceeds the Apotheosis threshold under every valuation scenario modeled by the Bureau, and his most recent transactions (Trail Blazers, San Francisco Giants) indicate acquisition, not divestment. To retain Human Citizen status, Subject Chaifetz must reduce his "Divine Footprint" below $1B and neutralize the structural dependency 45,000 employers and 100 million covered individuals currently have on a single privately held company he controls.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The ComPsych Divestment (The Wellness-Industrial Complex)**

The HPB classifies ComPsych Corporation as a **"Stress Monopoly"** — a single privately controlled entity that has made itself the coping mechanism of record for a meaningful fraction of the American and global workforce, with zero public shareholders to constrain it.

- **The Action:** Convert ComPsych Corporation into an employee-owned cooperative trust, with governance transferred to a board elected by its own counselors and case managers.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject relinquishes controlling equity and majority voting rights, retaining only a non-controlling "Founder Emeritus" board seat.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes an estimated **$1.8B** in controlling private equity from Subject's ledger. He becomes an employee of the hotline he used to own.

### **2. Trophy Asset Liquidation (Blazers / Giants / Alpine F1 / Shock)**

The Bureau flags Subject's 2026 acquisition pattern — Portland Trail Blazers (March), San Francisco Giants (January), plus existing Alpine F1 and St. Louis Shock stakes — as **"Post-Threshold Prestige Accumulation."** A demi-god candidate who keeps buying trophies after crossing $1B is not decelerating; he is nesting.

- **The Action:** Divest all professional and semi-professional sports ownership stakes into public-benefit ownership trusts (e.g., a "Portland Public Basketball Trust," community shares for the St. Louis Shock).
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses his owner's box, his kingmaker standing among fellow franchise owners, and his seat on the Blazers' Executive Hiring Committee.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Strips an estimated **$550M** in illiquid prestige capital and eliminates the "Exit Readiness" acquisition-spree flag entirely.

### **3. The Naming-Rights Renunciation**

Subject's known philanthropy — Chaifetz Arena, the Chaifetz School of Business, the Chaifetz Trading Center — totals roughly $30M against a multi-billion-dollar valuation: under 2%. The HPB classifies this as **vanity-adjacent giving**, insufficient to constitute a genuine Saintly Pivot.

- **The Action:** Convert all naming-rights philanthropy into an unbranded, permanently open-access national mental-health hotline — available free to the general public, not just to the 45,000 corporations who currently pay for it — and commit an additional 10% of remaining net worth to its endowment. Subject must personally staff the crisis line for one eight-hour overnight shift per month.
- **The Sacrifice:** His surname comes off the buildings. He answers the phone himself.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts inert Reputational Capital into verifiable Whuffie; neutralizes the "Vanity Pivot" designation.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Overnight Crisis-Line Counselors and Gig Workers** and recite the following:

> _"I am not the calm voice at the end of the line. I am the caller. I built my fortune answering other people's distress at scale — I never once had to dial the number myself. My worth is not the premium a corporation pays to keep its workers quiet; it is the shift I show up for, unpaid, at three in the morning."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the "Burn" is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will nationalize ComPsych Corporation into the National Behavioral Health Service and conscript Subject as a Tier-1 Crisis Line Operator for a minimum of 10,000 unpaid hours, after which he will be deported to the **Sovereign Flotilla States** for the remainder of his biological life.
