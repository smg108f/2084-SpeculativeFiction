### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[LARREA MOTA VELASCO, GERMÁN]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-08GL
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject exceeds the Demi-God threshold by a factor of roughly seventy and carries two unresolved mass-casualty and mass-contamination events tied directly to assets he controls. Recertification cannot proceed on net-worth reduction alone; the HPB requires simultaneous structural divestment and remediation.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Southern Copper Sovereignty Transfer**

The HPB classifies vertically integrated mining conglomerates as **"Ground-to-Vault" hazards** — entities that control a resource from extraction through processing through the capital it generates. Larrea's combined stake and voting control across Grupo México and Southern Copper Corporation constitute the single largest component of his Divine Footprint.

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer all personal and family voting control of Grupo México and Southern Copper Corporation into a binding **Extraction Commons Trust**, governed by a rotating board that includes seats reserved for representatives of Sonoran and Coahuilan communities affected by mine operations.
- **The Sacrifice:** Relinquish the chairmanship, all board voting rights, and dividend command. Subject retains a fixed, taxable salary as a non-voting technical advisor only.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Eliminates the largest single line item of the Concentration Hazard. Larrea becomes an employee of the company his father built, not its sovereign.

### **2. The GMexico Transportes Rail Devolution**

Ferromex and Ferrosur, operating under GMexico Transportes, give Subject private toll-gate control over more than 11,000 kilometers of Mexican freight rail — infrastructure the HPB considers a **public logistics artery**, not a private asset, regardless of who financed its construction.

- **The Action:** Cede majority operational and beneficial control of GMXT to a binational public rail authority, ending Subject's ability to negotiate ad hoc passage fees with the Mexican state on a case-by-case basis.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loses his single largest point of leverage over the federal government and the recurring toll revenue that comes with it.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the **"Political Agency: Elevated"** flag from his audit file. He becomes a rail customer like everyone else, rather than its gatekeeper.

### **3. The Sonora & Coahuila Reckoning**

This is not a reputational gesture. The HPB treats Pasta de Conchos (65 miners killed, 2006) and Buenavista del Cobre (roughly 22,000 people directly affected by contaminated water, 2014) as open files, not history. No recertification proceeds while either remains substantively unaddressed.

- **The Action:** Subject must personally and irrevocably fund (a) completion of body recovery operations for the remaining 38 of 65 Pasta de Conchos miners under independent international mine-safety oversight, and (b) a re-endowed Fideicomiso Río Sonora at ten times its original 2-billion-peso value, permanently administered by affected-community representatives with no reversion clause and no closure date. Subject must additionally issue a formal, public, non-legally-hedged acknowledgment of the negligence findings in both cases.
- **The Sacrifice:** An estimated $1.4 billion in escrowed, non-recoverable remediation funding, plus the reputational cost of an admission he has avoided for twenty years.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This is the HPB's mandatory **Blood Debt Clearance.** Regardless of how far his net worth falls, Subject does not requalify as a Human Citizen while these files remain open.

### **4. The Banamex Withdrawal & Family Trust Dissolution**

Subject's pending bid to acquire full control of Banamex would complete a "mine-to-bank" vertical stack the HPB will not permit any individual to hold.

- **The Action:** Formally and publicly withdraw from the Banamex acquisition. Redirect the earmarked capital into a **Public Credit Union Seed Fund** serving Sonoran and Coahuilan communities located near Grupo México operations.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loses entry into Mexican retail banking and the political capital that would have come with it.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Prevents a new Concentration Hazard from forming before it starts.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Miners, Rail Conductors, and Sonoran Well-Water Technicians** and recite the following:

> _"I built a fortune from the ground up, and left men in the ground to do it. I own no mine, no rail, no vault. I am dust like the sixty-five who never came home, and my worth from this day is measured only in what I give back, not what I was permitted to take."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol.** Grupo México and Southern Copper will be nationalized outright under the Strategic Minerals Emergency Powers Act, and Subject will be escorted to the **Baja Sovereign Flotilla** — a seastead built, fittingly, from decommissioned mining vessels — for permanent Demi-God residency.
