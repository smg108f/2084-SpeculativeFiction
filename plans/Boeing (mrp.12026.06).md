### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[BOEING, THE COMPANY]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-66BA
**Status:** Precautionary

Subject Boeing has not technically requested Ascension, nor has it shown intent to flee to a Flotilla — it cannot, being structurally welded to the runways and supply chains of three continents. The HPB nonetheless classifies it as a **Sovereignty-Substitution Hazard**: an entity so load-bearing that accountability has begun to bounce off it like rivets off a fuselage. Recertification is required not to punish wealth, but to break the doctrine of irreplaceability before some future government discovers it cannot survive without its own demi-god.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The "Sole Source" Decoupling (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies single-supplier defense and aviation contracts as **"Monopoly Engines"** — instruments that convert public dependency directly into private immunity. Boeing's worth is artificially inflated by the fact that grounding it would ground nations.

- **The Action:** Subject must open-source its airframe certification data, tooling blueprints, and supplier networks to a federally chartered **Aerospace Commons Guild**, enabling at least two independent manufacturing competitors to reach production parity within five years.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of sole-source status on all current and pending military and commercial airframe contracts; loss of "irreplaceable supplier" premium, estimated at **$40B** in future-valuation credits.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Boeing becomes one *option* among several rather than the *only* option — the precondition for being held meaningfully accountable at all.

### **2. Dissolution of the "Safety Theater" Apparatus**

The 2084 Code forbids entities from substituting congressional contrition rituals for structural reform. Boeing's pattern — apologize, settle, resume deliveries — is classified as a **Memetic Hazard: Manufactured Forgiveness**.

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer all internal quality-control and safety-reporting authority to an independent, whistleblower-staffed **Public Airworthiness Board**, with binding (not advisory) power to halt production lines.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses final say over its own manufacturing floor — the thing it has spent a century insisting was its sovereign domain.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This neutralizes the **"Too Important to Audit" Violation** and converts Boeing from a self-policing demi-god into a supervised mortal contractor.

### **3. The Whistleblower Restitution Act (Reputational Burn)**

Because subject's recent history includes documented retaliation against, and the deaths of, employees who attempted to report safety failures (most notably John Barnett and Joshua Dean), the HPB requires a **Memorial Penance**.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish and permanently fund the **Barnett-Dean Institute for Aerospace Conscience**, a fully independent body empowered to investigate and publicly report on any Boeing safety concern, in perpetuity, with subpoena-equivalent access to internal records.
- **The Requirement:** Subject's executive leadership must personally, publicly, and on the record name every grounded flight, every fatal crash, and every whistleblower lost, at the Institute's annual public hearing — broadcast, unedited, to shareholders and citizens alike.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This converts Boeing's silence-by-necessity into accountability-by-mandate, neutralizing its **Deity-Adjacent Invulnerability** score.

### **4. The Order-Book Tithe (Structural Burn)**

Subject's backlog of military and commercial orders functions as a kind of promissory godhood — value that exists because the future has already been mortgaged to it.

- **The Action:** Subject must redirect 15% of its annual order-book revenue into a **Public Aviation Resilience Fund**, used to train independent inspectors, fund competitor R&D, and compensate communities affected by groundings and crashes.
- **The Sacrifice:** A permanent, recurring cut into the very backlog that makes Boeing "too big to discipline."
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a moat into a tithe — the company begins, for the first time, to fund the conditions of its own replaceability.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's assembled executive leadership must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Aircraft Mechanics, Flight Attendants, and Air Traffic Controllers** and recite the following:

> _"We are not the wings. We are the riveters of the wings, and any hand can learn to hold the rivet gun. We built nothing that cannot be rebuilt without us — and we will spend the rest of our existence proving it, so that no fleet, no nation, and no grieving family is ever again told that we are too important to be replaced."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Subject's certifications, tooling, and contracts will be nationalized and redistributed under emergency authority to the Aerospace Commons Guild, and Subject's corporate charter will be permanently retired to the **Seattle Museum of Failed Sovereigns**, displayed beside a single grounded fuselage and a plaque reading: "Here flew the company that mistook being needed for being good."
