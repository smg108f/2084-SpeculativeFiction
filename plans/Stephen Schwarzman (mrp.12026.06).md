### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [SCHWARZMAN, STEPHEN]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06SS
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject has philanthropized into every elite institution in the country while his primary business systematically extracts returns from housing and healthcare infrastructure. The HPB requires the philanthropy to address the harm. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Housing Remediation Burn**

Blackstone's residential real estate portfolio — one of the largest private landlords in the United States — has been documented raising rents at rates exceeding local market averages in multiple cities. Private equity landlordship at institutional scale structurally requires rent increases to generate targeted returns.

- **The Action:** Blackstone Real Estate to establish a mandatory **Resident Stability Pledge** — no more than CPI+2% annual rent increases for all single-family and multifamily residential units in its portfolio, backed by a $500M resident support fund to provide emergency housing assistance to Blackstone tenants facing displacement or financial distress. Independently audited; Schwarzman to advocate as CEO.
- **The Sacrifice:** Rent increase flexibility that private equity return models depend on; approximately $500M.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the world's largest private landlord from a displacement accelerant into a housing stability mechanism. Largest possible Whuffie gain available from this subject.

### **2. The Healthcare Returns Cap Burn**

Blackstone has owned nursing homes, hospitals, and healthcare infrastructure. Private equity ownership of healthcare creates structural conflict between mandated investment returns and patient care investment. The HPB requires a commitment from Schwarzman to advocate for return structure reform in healthcare PE.

- **The Action:** Schwarzman to publicly advocate for — and Blackstone to voluntarily adopt — a healthcare portfolio return cap: maximum 10% IRR on healthcare infrastructure investments, with excess returns above that threshold directed to a Blackstone Healthcare Access Fund supporting uninsured and underinsured patient care. To apply to all current and future Blackstone healthcare holdings.
- **The Sacrifice:** Returns above 10% on healthcare assets; the principle of maximizing returns without sector-specific ethical constraints.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts private equity healthcare ownership from a public interest conflict into a model with built-in public benefit mechanism.

### **3. The Essential Services Philanthropy Burn**

Schwarzman's philanthropy has been directed overwhelmingly at elite educational institutions. The HPB asks him to direct at least a portion of it toward the sectors his business operates in.

- **The Action:** Establish a $500M **Schwarzman Community Infrastructure Fund**, focused on affordable housing preservation, community healthcare clinic support, and public infrastructure maintenance in communities where Blackstone holds significant real estate or infrastructure positions. Independent governance.
- **The Sacrifice:** $500M directed to communities affected by Blackstone's operations rather than universities.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Connects the philanthropy to the footprint. The HPB considers this the most symbolically important Burn on this ledger.
