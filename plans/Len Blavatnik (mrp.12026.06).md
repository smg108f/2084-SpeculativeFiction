### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [BLAVATNIK, LEONARD]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06LB
**Status:** Urgent

Subject has built one of the most comprehensive institutional legitimacy portfolios of any oligarch-era wealth accumulator. The HPB's concern is the foundation beneath it. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Provenance Reckoning Burn**

Blavatnik's original wealth came from Soviet-era asset acquisition, including aluminum and oil, during a privatization process widely documented to have disadvantaged ordinary citizens of the former Soviet Union. The subsequent philanthropy, however sincere, does not settle the HPB's provenance concern.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Post-Soviet Restitution Fund** with an endowment of $500M, governed by an independent board including Ukrainian and Russian civil society representatives, focused on communities in former Soviet states whose industrial assets were privatized during the 1990s with documented harm to local populations. The fund is not Blavatnik-branded and subject has no programming control.
- **The Sacrifice:** $500M and the fiction that knighthoods and university donations fully resolve extraction-era provenance questions.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** First direct acknowledgment by subject that original accumulation has outstanding debts to specific communities. The HPB considers this the most important Burn on this ledger.

### **2. The Warner Music Independence Burn**

WMG majority control by a single private individual is a cultural infrastructure concentration concern. Three companies already control ~80% of recorded music globally. Blavatnik's ownership concentrates one-third of that structure in private, non-transparent hands.

- **The Action:** Commit to transitioning WMG majority ownership to a publicly listed structure with independent board governance within five years, reducing Blavatnik family control to a minority stake of not more than 25%.
- **The Sacrifice:** Control of one of the world's three major music gatekeepers.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts cultural infrastructure from private ownership to public market accountability. Significant Whuffie with artists' rights and music access advocates.

### **3. The Institutional Disclosure Burn**

Blavatnik's philanthropic network — Oxford, Harvard, Yale, Tel Aviv University, Carnegie Hall — represents the single most comprehensive institutional influence network of any HPB subject to date. The HPB does not object to philanthropy; it requires transparency about what the philanthropy buys.

- **The Action:** Publish a voluntary comprehensive Institutional Influence Register — all donations over $1M, to all institutions, since 1990, with stated purpose and any donor influence conditions attached. To be independently audited and publicly accessible.
- **The Sacrifice:** The opacity that currently makes it impossible to distinguish purely philanthropic from access-purchasing donations.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts institutional philanthropy from softpower into transparent civic participation.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Former Soviet Industrial Workers (Ukraine, Russia), Emerging Artists on WMG Label Contracts, and Oxford Graduate Students in Government** and recite the following:

> *"I came from Odessa. I made money in aluminum and oil when the Soviet Union fell and the assets were available. I am not sure all of those assets were available in a way that was fair to the people who worked in them. I donated to Oxford. I donated to Harvard. I donated to Carnegie Hall. These things are true and good. They do not fully pay the debt. I am establishing the fund. I am releasing the music company. I am registering the donations. I am trying to become legible."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the Apotheosis Protocol. Subject will be reclassified as **Access Industries Demi-God Entity AIDE-001**. The Blavatnik School of Government will be required to offer a mandatory module on Soviet-era privatization and wealth provenance. Warner Music Group will be placed under a cultural stewardship trust. Subject will be relocated to a Sovereign Flotilla designated **FSS ODESSA**, where his complete asset history will be publicly displayed alongside all music currently licensed through WMG — accessible free of charge to all former Soviet citizens.
