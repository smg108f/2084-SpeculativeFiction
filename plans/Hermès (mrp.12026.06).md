### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [HERMÈS INTERNATIONAL S.C.A.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-38HI
**Status:** Precautionary — Managed Scarcity / Luxury Cultural Currency

Hermès' RTWI of ~$196B places it at **196 times the Demi-God threshold**. MARGINAL classification — light precautionary Burns only.

The following **Voluntary Commitment Schedule** must be confirmed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Artisan Living Wage Covenant**

Hermès' artisanal employment model is its best attribute. The Bureau requests it be formalized.

- **The Action:** Hermès commits to a binding **Artisan Living Wage Covenant** — all Hermès artisans and craft workers in France and globally receive wages at minimum 150% of local living wage, updated annually, published in the annual report.
- **Voluntary Sacrifice:** None. The entity already pays premium wages; binding them formalize the commitment.

---

### **2. The Scarcity Transparency Standard**

The Birkin's managed scarcity creates an informal purchase qualification system — customers must buy other products to earn the right to buy the primary product. The Bureau requests transparency on the criteria.

- **The Action:** Hermès must publish (without identifying individual customers) the general criteria by which Birkin and Kelly access is extended — what purchase history is considered, what relationship factors matter, and whether any criteria result in disparate access by race, nationality, or other protected characteristics. The Bureau is not requiring Hermès to end scarcity management; it requires that the admission criteria be transparent and non-discriminatory.
- **Voluntary Sacrifice:** The mystique of the Birkin allocation process is partially reduced by transparency.

---

### **3. The Craft Heritage Open Archive**

Hermès holds deep knowledge of traditional saddlery, leatherwork, and silk production that constitutes cultural heritage. The Bureau requests that this knowledge be partially preserved in an open form.

- **The Action:** Hermès commits to contributing documentation of its historical craft techniques (predating 12040 designs) to the French National Crafts Archive, available to artisans, design schools, and craft preservation programs.

---

### **THE "MORTAL" CONFIRMATION**

To finalize recertification, the Hermès Board must stand before a **Jury of 12 Hermès Artisans, Craft School Students, and Luxury Market Researchers** and confirm:

> _"The bag cannot be purchased by money alone. The artisan who makes it earns enough to live with dignity. The criteria by which we decide who can buy it will be transparent and non-discriminatory. The knowledge of how we make it will outlast us in the archive. We make beautiful things slowly and well. We intend to continue."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** Escalation to CRITICAL. Artisan wage compliance reviewed by French labor ministry. Birkin allocation practices reviewed by anti-discrimination authority for evidence of discriminatory criteria.
