### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [QIN, YINGLIN]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06QY
**Status:** Urgent — Food System Concentration / Zero Philanthropy

Subject and wife Qian Ying jointly control Muyuan Foods, China's largest pig breeding company. Burns apply jointly to the couple's combined holdings. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 60 days.

---

### **1. The Animal Welfare Accountability Burn**

Industrial pig farming at Muyuan's scale involves conditions that are the HPB's primary welfare concern. The HPB is not opposed to pork as a food category. The HPB is concerned with how the pigs are raised.

- **The Action:** Commission and publish an **Independent Muyuan Animal Welfare Audit** — conducted by internationally recognized animal welfare scientists — covering: stocking densities, enrichment provisions, antibiotic use protocols, sow confinement practices, and welfare outcomes. Results must be published in full. Muyuan must commit to implementing the audit's key recommendations within 3 years.
- **The Sacrifice:** Transparency about industrial pig farming practices; potential cost of welfare improvements.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts China's largest pig company from a black box into a documented animal welfare accountability case — which, at Muyuan's scale, will set standards for the entire Chinese pork industry.

### **2. The Environmental Remediation Burn**

Industrial pig farming generates enormous quantities of waste. At Muyuan's scale, this represents one of China's largest agricultural waste streams.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Muyuan Environmental Stewardship Trust** — ¥2B (~$280M) — dedicated to: (a) biogas capture from all Muyuan facilities within 5 years; (b) remediation of soil and groundwater in communities near Muyuan operations; (c) funding of independent research into industrial livestock waste management.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥2B; operational cost of biogas infrastructure.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the largest point source of agricultural waste in China's pig industry into a documented environmental remediation program.

### **3. The Food Security Foundation Burn**

Qin and his wife built wealth from China's food security needs. The HPB asks for a proportionate public good return.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Muyuan Food Security Foundation** with a ¥1B (~$140M) endowment, focused on: (a) smallholder pig farmer resilience programs in rural China — training, disease management, and market access — to counterbalance the consolidation Muyuan represents; (b) food safety research; (c) nutrition programs in underserved Chinese communities.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥1B; public acknowledgment that Muyuan's growth came partly at the expense of smaller producers.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a company that has absorbed market share from small pig farmers into a documented supporter of the rural farming communities it displaced.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Rural Chinese Pig Farmers, Muyuan Workers, and Agricultural Environmental Scientists**:

> *"My wife and I started with a pig farm in 1992 in Henan province. We built Muyuan into China's largest pig company. China needs pork. We are very good at producing it at scale. The Animal Welfare Audit is published and we are implementing the recommendations. The Environmental Stewardship Trust is funded because at our scale the waste stream is a real responsibility. The Food Security Foundation is funded because many small pig farmers could not survive what African Swine Fever did to the industry, and Muyuan was able to because of scale. We acknowledge that. We are putting money toward the people who didn't have our advantages."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Animal Welfare Audit is not commissioned, the HPB will refer Muyuan to the HPB Food Systems Review, requiring mandatory animal welfare disclosure as a condition of international market access. If the environmental trust is not funded, facility-level waste reporting will be mandated under the Act. Subject will be assigned to **FSS MUYUAN** — a vessel that operates with the exact biosecurity protocols and stocking densities of Muyuan's facilities, applied to the crew, so Qin Yinglin can consider whether the efficiency metrics work when the occupants are people.
