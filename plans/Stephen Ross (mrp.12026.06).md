### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ROSS, STEPHEN]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06SR3
**Status:** Urgent

Subject founded Related Companies, built Hudson Yards, and owns the Miami Dolphins. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Hudson Yards Community Equity Burn**

Hudson Yards is a $25B luxury development on Manhattan's far west side. The surrounding communities — Hell's Kitchen, Chelsea, the former meatpacking district — have seen rising costs and displacement partly attributable to Hudson Yards' development pressure. The HPB requires a community equity contribution.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish a **Hudson Yards Community Equity Fund** — $300M — independently administered by a board of Hell's Kitchen, Chelsea, and far west side community organizations (not Related Companies or Ross affiliates) — dedicated to: (a) affordable housing development in Hudson Yards' shadow (west side Manhattan, below 59th Street); (b) displacement prevention — direct rental assistance for long-term residents priced out by Hudson Yards development; (c) small business support grants for community businesses displaced or at risk from Hudson Yards development pressure.
- **The Sacrifice:** $300M.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The developer of New York's largest luxury enclave invests $300M in the communities that bore the cost of that development.

### **2. The Related Companies Affordable Housing Scale Burn**

Related Companies has built affordable housing as part of its business model. The HPB asks subject to scale this beyond commercial necessity.

- **The Action:** Subject must commit — through Related Companies — to a **Related Affordable Housing Commitment**: 30% of all future Related Companies residential development (not just tax-credit-eligible development) must be designated affordable units in perpetuity, not merely in compliance periods. This applies to all Related Companies projects in all markets over the next 10 years.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced margin on 30% of residential development.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The builder of America's most expensive real estate is a documented affordable housing developer at scale.

### **3. The Miami Dolphins Community Burn**

The Miami Dolphins serve the most diverse major American metro. Hard Rock Stadium receives public infrastructure subsidy.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish a **Dolphins Community Foundation** — $50M — dedicated to Miami-Dade youth sports, education, and arts programs in Liberty City, Overtown, and other historically underserved Miami communities. Independently administered.
- **The Sacrifice:** $50M.
