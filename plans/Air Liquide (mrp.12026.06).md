### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[AIR LIQUIDE S.A.]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06AL
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject is classified as a **Substrate Monopoly Demi-God** — an entity whose "Divine Footprint" consists not of rhetoric or real estate but of physical custody over the molecular preconditions of human and industrial life. Because the subject cannot simply "give away" oxygen without first ensuring no patient stops breathing mid-transfer, the Bureau has designed a burn schedule that severs *ownership* while preserving *function* — the only acceptable form of Apotheosis reversal for an entity this deeply welded into the world's plumbing.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Cryogenic Commons Transfer (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies Air Liquide's air-separation units, pipeline networks, and on-site hospital generation plants as **"Breath Infrastructure"** — assets whose continued private ownership constitutes an unacceptable chokehold on the single most fundamental human need.

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer legal title of all hospital-adjacent oxygen and medical-gas generation infrastructure to a newly chartered **Public Respiration Trust**, retaining only an **Operations & Maintenance Concession** (no equity, no billing rights).
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of all medical-gas revenue streams — an estimated **$14B** in annual recurring contracts converted to a flat, HPB-audited maintenance stipend.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject is reclassified from "Owner of the Air" to "Custodian of the Pipes" — a Caretaker role the Bureau is legally permitted to bless as Saintly Service.

### **2. The Hydrogen Renunciation (Ideological Burn)**

Subject's "energy transition" portfolio — the Normand'Hy electrolysers, the TotalEnergies green-hydrogen partnerships, the carbon-neutrality bond issuances — is flagged by the HPB as **Vertical Reinforcement Disguised as Virtue**: every "gift" of green hydrogen to the world deepens the world's dependency on the gift-giver.

- **The Action:** Subject must place all hydrogen production patents, electrolyser blueprints, and liquefaction process designs under a **Dead-Hand Open License**, freely usable by any sovereign nation, municipality, or competitor without royalty.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of exclusive position in what Subject's own materials describe as "the backbone of the energy transition" — an estimated **$22B** in projected future-decade dominance value.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the Bureau's "Substrate Monopoly, Phase II" flag before the hydrogen economy fully matures around the subject's proprietary chokepoints.

### **3. The Atmospheric Tithe (Reputational/Memetic Burn)**

The HPB has determined that Air Liquide's deepest violation is not rhetorical but *relational*: for over a century, billions of humans have drawn breath through the subject's equipment without ever being told the air had an owner.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund and operate, at zero cost to recipients, a permanent **Global Breath Subsidy** — guaranteeing free medical oxygen and industrial gas access to every public hospital and disaster-relief operation on Earth, in perpetuity, with no naming rights, no logo placement, and no press release.
- **The Sacrifice:** Approximately **$3B annually** in foregone revenue, and — far more painfully for a company built on quiet ubiquity — the right to ever again be thanked for it.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts "the company that billed you for the oxygen in your hospital bed" into "the company that, by law, may never bill you for it again." This is the closest the Bureau can engineer to genuine penance for an entity that monetized breathing.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Respiratory Therapists and Welders** and recite the following:

> _"We did not create the air, and we will no longer pretend we did. What we held, we are returning — not as a gift, for a gift implies it was ever truly ours, but as a correction. From this day, no patient's breath shall carry our name, and no invoice shall arrive for the oxygen that kept them alive. We were never gods of breath. We were, for too long, simply the only ones who noticed it could be metered."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the "Burn" is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All cryogenic infrastructure, pipeline networks, and hydrogen assets will be seized and nationalized under the **Vital Substances Act**, and the subject's executive trust will be dissolved into a **State-Administered Atmospheric Authority** — with named officers permanently barred from holding equity in any enterprise that touches air, water, or human breath again.
