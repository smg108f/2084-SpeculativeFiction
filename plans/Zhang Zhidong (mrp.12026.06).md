### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ZHANG, ZHIDONG]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06ZZ
**Status:** Precautionary

Subject co-founded Tencent and served as CTO, building WeChat's technical architecture. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The WeChat Privacy Research Burn**

WeChat is a dual-use platform: essential communications infrastructure and mandatory Chinese state surveillance tool. Zhang built the architecture.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund a **WeChat Privacy and Digital Rights Research Institute** — ¥500M (~$70M) — housed at an independent Hong Kong or international university (not Tencent-affiliated), with a mandate to: (a) independently document WeChat's censorship and surveillance capabilities and their effects on Chinese and international users; (b) research technical mitigations that could provide WeChat users with greater privacy while complying with applicable law; (c) publish all research publicly. The Institute's independence from Tencent must be structurally guaranteed in its charter.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥500M + reputational risk of independent research findings.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The architect of WeChat funds independent research into what WeChat has become.

### **2. The Zhang Foundation Scaling Burn**

- **The Action:** Scale subject's personal foundation to ¥2B (~$280M) total committed, focused on: (a) Chinese medical research — rare diseases and conditions underserved by commercial pharmaceutical investment; (b) Chinese digital literacy and open-source software education; (c) international computing research scholarships.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥2B.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 WeChat Users in China, Hong Kong Digital Rights Researchers, and Chinese Rare Disease Patients Receiving Foundation Support**:

> *"I built QQ and then WeChat. WeChat is how China communicates. It is also how China is monitored.
