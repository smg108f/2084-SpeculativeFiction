### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [VON MANDL, ANTHONY]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06AvM
**Status:** Moderate

Subject built the hard seltzer category from nothing and captured a significant share of US alcohol consumption. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 180 days.

---

### **1. The Alcohol Harm Reduction Burn**

White Claw and the hard seltzer category grew in part by lowering the perceived barrier to casual alcohol consumption. The public health obligation is proportionate to the market penetration.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund $150M for the **Mark Anthony Harm Reduction Fund**: (a) $75M for **responsible consumption programs** embedded in the hard seltzer marketing ecosystem — not token label disclaimers, but active public health campaigns funded at a scale proportionate to the marketing spend that built the category; (b) $50M for **alcohol use disorder treatment access** in the US communities where hard seltzer penetration is highest, focusing on the demographic (young adult, 21-28) that White Claw most successfully reached; (c) $25M for **independent RTD health research** — including funding epidemiological research on whether RTD beverage growth has increased population-level alcohol consumption or merely shifted it.
- **The Sacrifice:** $150M.

### **2. The Okanagan Commons Burn**

Mission Hill and the Okanagan wine investment represent genuine regional stewardship. The HPB invites its institutionalization.

- **The Action:** Subject must endow $75M for the **Okanagan Valley Agricultural and Cultural Endowment**: (a) support for small and independent Okanagan wineries who cannot match the marketing budgets of large players; (b) Indigenous land stewardship programs for Syilx Okanagan Nation members in the wine-growing valley (the Okanagan wine industry has grown on Syilx territory); (c) arts and cultural programming at Mission Hill and across the BC interior.
- **The Sacrifice:** $75M.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Okanagan Syilx Nation Members, BC Addiction Medicine Physicians, and Small Okanagan Winery Owners**:

> *"White Claw made alcohol feel like sparkling water. That was the point. It worked. By 2019, we were outselling craft beer. I built a category. The harm reduction fund addresses what a category does at scale: you reach people who weren't drinking, or weren't drinking as much, and you make it easier to drink more than you intended. That is the obligation of anyone who industrializes a vice. The Okanagan fund is the other side — the land we work, the people whose territory it is, the small wineries that cannot compete with our distribution."*
