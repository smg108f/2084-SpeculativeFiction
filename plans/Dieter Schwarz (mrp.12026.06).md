### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [SCHWARZ, DIETER]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06DS
**Status:** Precautionary

The HPB acknowledges this is a difficult case. Subject has no jet. No yacht. Three photographs. A functioning charitable foundation. The Bureau has auditors who find this file emotionally conflicting. Nevertheless, Europe's largest food retailer is privately controlled by one 85-year-old man, and the Demi-God Act does not have a modesty exemption. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Foundation Independence Burn**

The Dieter Schwarz Foundation structure gives subject de facto control over charitable profit allocation. He determines how much is charitable and how much is reinvested. This is not independence; it is managed generosity. The HPB requires genuine structural separation between the wealth-generator and the charitable vehicle.

- **The Action:** Convert the Dieter Schwarz Foundation to a fully independent charitable governance structure with an independent board, removing all Schwarz family members from governance roles. Subject may retain honorary status. The board must publish an annual report on allocation decisions and must commit a minimum of 5% of Schwarz Group annual profit to public benefit (approx. €9.5B/year).
- **The Sacrifice:** Control over how much of the Schwarz Group's profit serves the public. The foundation becomes genuinely independent.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts managed philanthropy into genuine public obligation. HPB reclassifies the foundation structure from Concentration Hazard to Partial Mitigation.

### **2. The Schwarz Group Competitive Transparency Burn**

Europe's largest food retailer sets prices and supply chain terms that affect what hundreds of millions of people eat. The Schwarz Group is privately held, with no obligation to publish the kind of supplier practice, pricing policy, or labor condition data that public companies must disclose. Private ownership of essential retail infrastructure without public accountability is, in the HPB's classification, a **Democratic Deficiency**.

- **The Action:** Submit to a voluntary annual public audit of Schwarz Group supply chain labor conditions, pricing practices, and environmental impact — equivalent to the reporting required of publicly traded European retailers — published and freely accessible.
- **The Sacrifice:** Competitive transparency. Lidl's pricing strategy and supplier margins become public knowledge.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts invisible essential infrastructure into accountable public service. Not a financial sacrifice; a transparency sacrifice, which the HPB considers proportionate.

### **3. The Regional Anchor Burn**

Subject's philanthropy is concentrated in Heilbronn, Germany — a small city by global standards. The wealth was generated across 31 countries and hundreds of millions of consumers. The HPB requires that the giving have some proportionality to the earning geography.

- **The Action:** Expand the Dieter Schwarz Foundation's scope to fund food security and vocational training programs across the 31 countries in which Lidl operates, with a mandatory allocation of €500M over five years to programs in the five lowest-income markets where Schwarz Group sources products.
- **The Sacrifice:** €500M over five years; the comfort of local-only philanthropy.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Connects the wealth's geographic origin (supply chains across multiple continents) to the giving's geographic distribution. The HPB considers this both ethical and narratively coherent.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Lidl Checkout Workers, Schwarz Group Supplier Farmers, and Heilbronn Vocational School Graduates** and recite the following:

> *"I sold groceries cheaply. I built the stores everywhere. I kept the shares and gave some of the profit to the city I was born in. The workers in the stores are not from Heilbronn. The farmers who grow what Lidl sells are not from Germany. My foundation did not reach them. I have enough modesty to not own a yacht. I need enough modesty to not own a continent's food supply. I release control of the foundation. I release the privacy of the supply chain. The grocery belongs to everyone who shops in it."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the Apotheosis Protocol. Subject will be reclassified as **Schwarz Group Demi-God Entity SGDE-001**. The Bureau notes that enforcing Flotilla relocation for an 85-year-old man who does not own a yacht presents logistical challenges. Subject will instead be designated a **National Grocery Heritage Monument** and required to open a single Lidl store in his Heilbronn home, at which he will serve as unpaid cashier for a minimum of 20 hours per week. The HPB considers this appropriate and expects the local community would be gracious about it.
