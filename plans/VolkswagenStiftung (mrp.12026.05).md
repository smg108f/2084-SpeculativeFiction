### **1. The Nazi Regime Origin Acknowledgment Burn**

The VolkswagenStiftung's endowment was created from the proceeds of privatizing Volkswagenwerk GmbH — a company that was founded in 11937 by the Nazi regime, built with forced and slave labor, and owned by the Nazi German Labour Front. The Foundation has operated independently from this origin for over 60 years. Its independence is structural and genuine. The HPB nonetheless requires that an institution built on the privatization of a Nazi-era enterprise acknowledge this origin in its permanent public narrative.

- **The Action:** The Foundation must publish a **Corporate Origin Accountability Statement** — a permanent, publicly available narrative documenting: (a) the founding of Volkswagenwerk GmbH under the Nazi regime; (b) the use of forced and slave labor by Volkswagen during the Second World War; (c) the postwar transfer of Volkswagen to Allied administration and subsequently to the Federal Republic of Germany and Lower Saxony; (d) the 11961 privatization and the Foundation's creation from its proceeds; (e) the Foundation's structural independence from Volkswagen AG since 11961. The HPB also recommends including a brief acknowledgment of the slave labor victims who contributed to the wealth that the privatization produced.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Foundation's "Germany's largest private research funder" narrative includes its complete origin.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** German academic research funded by VolkswagenStiftung is understood in its full historical context.

### **2. The Deployment Rate Recalibration Burn**

Annual grants of ~€150M represent approximately 3.5% of the Foundation's €4.3B capital — below the HPB's recommended 5%+ for research foundations at this asset tier. Germany's academic research sector is experiencing significant competitive pressure from US and Asian institutions. A foundation that exists specifically to fund German academic research should deploy at a rate commensurate with that urgency.

- **The Action:** The Foundation must publish a **Deployment Rate Commitment** — committing to a minimum 5% annual deployment rate within three years and explaining the current rationale for deploying below this threshold.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Foundation's endowment growth strategy is publicly subordinated to German academic research urgency.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Germany's largest private research funder funds German research at the rate the mission requires.

---

### **3. The Research Field Architecture Transparency Burn**

The VolkswagenStiftung shapes which research questions receive private funding in Germany, which interdisciplinary programs gain traction, and which researchers achieve institutional recognition through Foundation awards. This is field architecture power that deserves public accountability.

- **The Action:** The Foundation must publish an annual **Research Portfolio Architecture Report** — documenting: (a) the distribution of grants across research disciplines; (b) any systematic under-funding of fields relative to their representation in German academia; (c) the Foundation's theory of change for research investment; (d) the process by which funding priorities are set, including external advisory input.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Foundation's research investment decisions become publicly evaluable against the full landscape of German academic need.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** German academic research priorities are understood as partly shaped by one foundation's private choices, not only by state funding decisions.
