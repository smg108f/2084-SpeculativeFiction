### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [DUKE ENERGY CORPORATION]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09DUK
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification

Duke Energy's classification stems from its dual role as monopoly ratepayer extractor and emerging AI-infrastructure power supplier. Recertification targets the alignment between its rate-hike requests and its data-center-driven capital expansion.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Data Center Cost Allocation Burn**

- **The Action:** Establish a separate rate class for data-center and large industrial AI customers that fully funds the new gas-generation capacity built to serve them, ring-fenced from residential rate increases.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced flexibility to blend infrastructure costs across the full customer base.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the "Ascension Enabler subsidized by households" flag entirely.

### **2. The Residential Rate Freeze**

- **The Action:** Freeze residential rate increases for 24 months while the data-center cost allocation above is implemented and verified.
- **The Sacrifice:** Approximately $1.2B in deferred revenue recognition.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Restores basic fairness between captive residential customers and the AI infrastructure boom the company is now built to serve.

### **3. The Regulatory Transparency Mandate**

- **The Action:** Publish all utility-commission filings, rate-case testimony, and lobbying expenditures in a single public dashboard updated quarterly.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced ability to manage rate-case strategy outside public scrutiny.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the "Moderate Political Agency" flag.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Duke Energy's board must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Residential Ratepayers from Its Service Territories** and recite the following:

> _"We asked you to pay more for power in the same year we asked investors for billions to build power for machines. We will now make the machines pay their own way before we ask you for another cent."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Rate-setting authority will be transferred fully to an independent **Regional Ratepayer Trust**, with data-center and AI infrastructure customers billed exclusively through a separate, cost-isolated tariff structure.
