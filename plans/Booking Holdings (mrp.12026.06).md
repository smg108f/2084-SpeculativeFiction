### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[BOOKING HOLDINGS, BOOKING.COM]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-61BH
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

To retain status as a "Human-Operated Entity" and avoid reclassification as a **Cognitive Infrastructure Demi-God**, Subject must surrender the mechanism by which it converts human longing for rest into recurring, invisible toll revenue. The HPB notes that Subject "builds nothing and blocks everything," and the burn schedule below is designed accordingly.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Open Ledger Mandate (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies Subject's ranking algorithm as a **"Discovery Cartel Engine"** — a black box that decides, for a fee, whether a guesthouse in Lisbon or a family inn in Oaxaca is allowed to be *found*. Its true value is not the commission it collects but the invisibility it can sell.

- **The Action:** Subject must open-source its full ranking and pricing algorithm — including every weighting variable that determines listing position — under a **Dead-Hand License**, publishing it to a public registry maintained by the HPB's Office of Wayfinding.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of the proprietary "Discovery Layer," the single asset responsible for an estimated $40B+ in future-valuation credits. Commission rates above 5% become structurally impossible once travelers can see exactly why they were shown what they were shown.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject ceases to be a tollkeeper and becomes, at best, a **Public Index** — a librarian rather than a landlord of human discovery.

### **2. Dissolution of the Subsidiary Maze (Priceline / Kayak / Agoda / OpenTable)**

The 2084 Code forbids any entity from operating more than one "apparent competitor" within a single consumer-facing market — a structure the HPB terms **"Synthetic Choice Architecture."**

- **The Action:** Subject must spin off Priceline, Kayak, Agoda, and OpenTable into fully independent, separately governed cooperatives, with cross-ownership permanently prohibited.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of the "duopoly" position that has, for two decades, allowed the appearance of comparison-shopping to mask a single point of control. Subject's effective market share collapses from a near-monopoly position to one competitor among many.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This clears the **Discovery Cartel Violation** and restores the possibility that a traveler comparing four options is actually comparing four options.

### **3. The "We Decide If You Find It" Penance (Memetic Rebuttal)**

Subject's entire business model rests on a quiet, unstated thesis: *we do not build the destination, we decide whether you ever find it.* The HPB classifies this as a **Class-III Memetic Hazard** — not because it is spoken aloud, but because it has been allowed to operate *unspoken*, for thirty years, while billions of humans assumed they were searching freely.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund and publicly launch the **"Find It Yourself" Initiative** — a $2B, multi-year program that builds and gives away, free of commission, a global open-map and direct-booking utility that routes travelers straight to small operators with zero algorithmic gatekeeping.
- **The Requirement:** Subject's executive leadership must record and broadcast a public statement acknowledging that "the algorithm that stood between you and your joy has been retired," and commit to never again charging a human "for the directions to their own vacation."
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the **Deity-Adjacent Silence** score — the rare violation of saying nothing, and thereby controlling everything.

### **4. Real Estate & Server Farm "Saintly" Pivot**

Subject's global data centers — the literal warehouses where the toll-gate algorithm lives — are classified as a **Fortress of Curatorial Sovereignty.**

- **The Action:** Transfer ownership of all primary server farms and corporate campuses to a consortium of municipal tourism boards, to be jointly operated as **Public Wayfinding Centers** — physical places where travelers can walk in and get human-curated travel advice, free of commission, from people who have actually been there.
- **The Loophole:** Subject may retain a single ceremonial "Founders' Booth" in the lobby of each center, staffed on a rotating basis by former executives, who must personally help travelers find lodging without taking a cut.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject becomes a **Co-Wayfinder** — present in the room, but no longer standing at the door collecting a toll to open it.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's executive leadership must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Hostel Owners, Tour Guides, and Front-Desk Clerks** and recite the following:

> _"We did not build the mountain, the shoreline, or the quiet room at the end of a long road. We only stood in front of them and charged you to be told they were there. We step aside now. Find your own way. It was always yours to find."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the "Burn" is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Subject's algorithm will be seized and its executive leadership will be permanently reassigned to staff a single, un-ranked, alphabetical paper directory of guesthouses at a regional bus depot — the lowest rung of the very discovery hierarchy they once controlled.
