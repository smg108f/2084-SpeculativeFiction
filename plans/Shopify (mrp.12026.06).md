### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[SHOPIFY INC.]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06SHP
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification

Subject is a corporate entity rather than a single biological actor, but the Demi-God Act of 2034 extends "Asset-Limited Personhood" review to any infrastructure provider whose chokepoint exceeds 1% of national commercial throughput. Shopify's toll sits beneath an estimated 5.7 million storefronts; left uncorrected, this "Distributed Hoarding" configuration will harden into a permanent Substitution Vector. Recertification is offered now, voluntarily, while the entity can still credibly claim to be on the rebels' side.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The "Toll Booth" Decoupling (The Largest Burn)**

Shopify Payments and Shop Pay represent the entity's true crown jewels — the rails beneath the rails, historically responsible for roughly half of total revenue and the lever by which a single 1% chargeback ratio can freeze a merchant out of their own business overnight.

- **The Action:** Spin off Shopify Payments into an independently governed **Merchant Mutual Clearinghouse**, owned in cooperative shares by the merchants who transact through it, with suspension and freeze decisions subject to a jury of affected sellers rather than an automated risk model.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of payment-processing revenue and the unilateral right to suspend accounts without merchant-side review.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The chokepoint becomes a commons. Subject converts from "Toll-Keeper" to "Co-Op Member," shedding an estimated **$48B** in future-valuation credit tied to processing-fee dominance.

### **2. Open-Sourcing the "Universal Commerce Protocol"**

The HPB classifies emerging agentic-commerce protocols as **"Potentiality Engines"** — infrastructure that doesn't generate revenue today but positions its owner to meter every transaction an AI agent will ever make on a merchant's behalf. Subject's quiet bid to author the standard beneath the next economy is its single largest "Stealth Appreciation" exposure.

- **The Action:** Submit the Universal Commerce Protocol to a neutral, multi-stakeholder open standards body (no Shopify board seats, no veto rights) under a Dead-Hand License.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of first-mover control over the rails of agentic commerce — the thing that would have made Subject indispensable for the next fifty years.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the **Pre-Ascension** flag entirely. Subject becomes a contributor to the future rather than its landlord.

### **3. The "Arming the Rebels" Penance (Memetic Correction)**

Subject's foundational mythology — "Amazon is building an empire, Shopify is arming the rebels" — is now rated by the Bureau as a **Class-III Memetic Comfort Hazard**: a story so good it lets millions of dependent business owners mistake their tenancy for sovereignty.

- **The Action:** Lütke must personally author and publish a follow-up memo, internally circulated as gospel, titled *We Were Never the Rebellion: A Correction*, plainly stating that Shopify is now load-bearing infrastructure and that "your own business" running on its rails is a tenancy, not a kingdom.
- **The Requirement:** Fund a $75M **"Exit Ramp" Initiative** — open documentation, data-portability tools, and migration grants that make it *genuinely* possible for merchants to leave the platform without losing their customer relationships.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the mythology from comforting fiction into honest disclosure. This is the rarest kind of Saintly Pivot: one that costs the Subject real customers.

### **4. The Suspension Reform Burn**

The 1% chargeback threshold and automated account-freeze protocols are flagged as **"Silent Seizure"** mechanisms — the corporate equivalent of a demi-god deploying private security against citizens with no due process.

- **The Action:** Replace automated suspension with a 72-hour human-reviewed appeals window and public quarterly disclosure of all merchant account actions, including reasons and outcomes.
- **The Sacrifice:** Slower fraud-response times and reduced "protective" margin on Shopify Payments revenue.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the **"Shadow Enforcement" Violation.** Subject is no longer adjudicating commercial life-and-death in private.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's CEO must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Independent Shop Owners, Warehouse Pickers, and Customer-Service Reps** and recite the following:

> _"We told you that you were building your own kingdom. We did not mention that we owned the land beneath it, the roads to it, and the gate at its entrance. We were never arming a rebellion — we were drafting one into permanent service. From this day, the land is held in common, the gate has no lock, and the rebels may finally leave, if they still wish to stay, by choice and not by toll."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the "Burn" is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Shopify Inc. will be reclassified as a **Public Commercial Utility**, its leadership will be placed under a Bureau-appointed Board of Mortal Stewardship, and Tobi Lütke will be relocated — under Life-Tenancy Usage Agreement — to a **600 sq. ft. unit above a single small storefront in Ottawa**, where he will be required to personally operate a Shopify-powered shop for no less than five years, subject to the same chargeback thresholds, suspension algorithms, and app-store fees as every other merchant he once promised to arm.
