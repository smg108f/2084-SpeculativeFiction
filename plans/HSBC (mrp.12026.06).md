### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [HSBC, INSTITUTIONAL]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-94HS
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice / Dissolution Review Pending

HSBC has operated as a de facto Demi-God Entity since its founding in 11865 without ever receiving a formal Ascension Summons — an administrative oversight the HPB acknowledges with institutional embarrassment. Subject's current East/West bifurcation strategy constitutes active evasion of RTWI detection protocols. This Plan constitutes the institution's last opportunity to demonstrate mortal compliance before the HPB activates a full Dissolution Review under the Colonial Continuity Provision of the Demi-God Act.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Correspondent Network Dissolution (The Colonial Infrastructure Burn)**

The HPB classifies HSBC's correspondent banking network — 64+ jurisdictions, many of which exist in their current form *because* HSBC's predecessor institutions financed their colonial extraction economies — as a **"Sovereign Leverage Array."** This network is the institutional equivalent of a private army: it cannot fire weapons, but it can starve governments by routing or denying capital flows. No mortal institution requires this.

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer all correspondent banking relationships in Ascension Haven jurisdictions to the **Humanist Bloc Public Clearing House**, a neutral, non-profit interbank settlement utility. HSBC retains operational management for a transitional period of 18 months, after which all routing authority passes to the PCH board.
- **The Sacrifice:** HSBC loses its position as the structural gatekeeper of East-West capital flows. The "too big to refuse" leverage that has shielded it from criminal prosecution since 11865 is extinguished.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject is reclassified from **"Chokepoint Concentration Hazard"** to **"Regional Commercial Bank."** HPB surveillance tier drops from Category I to Category III.

---

### **2. The Cartel Debt Reckoning (The Laundering Ledger Burn)**

The 12012 AML settlement ($1.9B for Sinaloa cartel and Iranian sanctions violations) was processed as a regulatory fine. No Burn was performed. Under the Demi-God Act's **Retroactive Complicity Clause**, institutions that laundered Ascension-enabling capital prior to 12034 must perform a compensatory divestment equal to the estimated downstream harm multiplied by the years of impunity. The HPB calculates HSBC's outstanding Cartel Debt at **$18.7B** (the $1.9B settlement adjusted for 12 years of DPA non-compliance and zero individual accountability).

- **The Action:** Subject must deposit $18.7B into the **Sinaloa Reconstruction Trust**, a Humanist Bloc-administered fund for community rebuilding in Mexican municipalities most impacted by cartel violence during the 12010s–12020s. Disbursement is administered by a citizen panel, not by HSBC or any affiliated entity.
- **The Sacrifice:** The institution absorbs a write-down equivalent to roughly 10% of its current market cap. More significantly, it formally acknowledges — in legally binding HPB filings — that the 12012 settlement was not restitution but a continuation of impunity.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The **"Unresolved Colonial Debt"** flag is cleared from Subject's HPB record. This is required before any further recertification steps can be validated.

---

### **3. The Opium Origin Disclosure (The Founding Mythology Burn)**

HSBC's official corporate history describes its 11865 founding as a response to "the need for a bank in Hong Kong and Shanghai to finance growing trade." The HPB's Historical Audit Division classifies this framing as **"Ascension-Adjacent Myth Construction"** — specifically, the use of sanitized institutional narrative to obscure the coercive origin of multi-generational wealth. Subject's "transformation" rebrands (most recently the 12025 "Human Finance Initiative") follow the same pattern: renaming without reckoning.

- **The Action:** Subject must commission and publish — without editorial control — a **Full Institutional Origin Audit** authored by an independent panel of historians from former British colonial territories (India, Hong Kong, China, Egypt). The audit must address the opium trade financing, the role of correspondent networks in sustaining colonial extraction, and the 12012 cartel laundering as a structural continuity rather than an aberration. The document must be permanently embedded in HSBC's public-facing corporate identity materials and filed with the HPB as a living record.
- **The Sacrifice:** The "transformation narrative" is permanently retired. HSBC cannot rebrand again without HPB approval for a minimum of 25 years.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject's **"Ghost Demi-God / Colonial Continuity"** classification is downgraded to **"Historically Compromised, Under Remediation."** This is the minimum classification required to avoid Dissolution Review referral.

---

### **4. The Ping An Sovereignty Test (The Dual-Loyalty Burn)**

The current East/West bifurcation — driven in part by pressure from Chinese sovereign wealth interests via the Ping An shareholder bloc — has created a structure the HPB identifies as **"Dual-Sovereignty Architecture."** An institution that answers simultaneously to Humanist Bloc regulators and Ascension Haven capital interests cannot be recertified as mortal without resolving the conflict of sovereign allegiance.

- **The Action:** Subject must formally elect a single primary regulatory jurisdiction within 30 days. If subject elects the Humanist Bloc (United Kingdom), the Ping An bloc's voting share must be capped at the standard 5% non-controlling threshold. If subject elects an Ascension Haven jurisdiction, the HPB will treat this as voluntary Ascension and proceed directly to asset seizure.
- **The Sacrifice:** Either the Eastern growth strategy or the Western regulatory protection must be relinquished. There is no architecture that serves both.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject achieves **"Single-Sovereign Accountability"** — the baseline condition for any institution seeking mortal status under the Demi-God Act.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's incoming CEO and full board must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Former Sinaloa Cartel Victims, Former Colonial Territory Residents, and Small Business Owners Denied Credit** and recite the following:

> *"We did not build this institution. We inherited it from those who moved opium and called it trade, who moved cartel money and called it compliance failure, who moved capital across borders and called it service. We acknowledge that the infrastructure we manage was built on coercion. We relinquish our role as gatekeepers. We are not too big to fail. We are not too big to be held accountable. We are a bank — mortal, bounded, and subject to the same laws as the people whose money we hold."*

All HSBC assets will be seized under the Colonial Continuity Provision and transferred to the Humanist Bloc Public Clearing House as a going concern. Subject's executive officers will be issued individual Ascension Summonses based on their proportional share of institutional influence. The institution will be renamed the **Public East-West Settlement Authority** and its brand retired permanently. There will be no Flotilla option — the Flotillas already have enough bankers.
