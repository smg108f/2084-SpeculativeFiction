### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [AL SAUD, SALMAN BIN ABDULAZIZ]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06SAS
**Status:** Aspirational Only — Sovereign Carve-Out (Section 18(c))

⚠️ **SOVEREIGN CARVE-OUT NOTICE:** Subject is the reigning King of Saudi Arabia. Standard HPB enforcement is inapplicable. This plan is aspirational, filed for the International Demi-God Registry under Section 18(c). The HPB acknowledges that Riyadh will not be telephoning to confirm compliance.

*Note: Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman (MBS), who is understood to be the operational power of the kingdom, will be audited in a separate file when his own wealth threshold is formally assessed. This audit covers King Salman as formal head of state and wealth holder of record.*

---

### **ASPIRATIONAL BURN 1: The Aramco Climate Transition Commitment**

Saudi Aramco is the world's most profitable company and the world's single largest contributor to historical oil production. A genuine Saudi commitment to managed decline would be the most consequential individual-country climate action in history.

- **The Aspirational Action:** King Salman (and/or MBS) would declare a binding **Saudi Aramco Managed Decline Timeline** — a specific, credible, independently verified schedule for reducing Saudi oil production by 5% annually until reaching a level consistent with 1.5°C global temperature targets, coordinated with Vision 2030's economic diversification program.
- **Why This Will Likely Not Happen:** Saudi oil revenues fund the entirety of the Saudi state budget, royal family wealth, and domestic social programs. No managed decline has been declared. The HPB files this as the single most consequential aspirational burn in the registry.

### **ASPIRATIONAL BURN 2: The Religious Influence Accountability**

Saudi Arabia's financial support for Wahhabist institutions globally — mosques, madrassas, educational programs — has shaped Islam's global expression for decades in directions that the HPB identifies as associated with intolerance and in some cases extremism.

- **The Aspirational Action:** A formal royal decree redirecting Saudi religious export funding toward programs supporting Islamic pluralism, women's religious scholarship, and interfaith dialogue — with the same financial commitment currently directed toward Wahhabist institutions.
- **Why This Is Aspirational:** This would require the Saudi state to reverse a foundational policy of the post-1973 oil boom period. The HPB notes that MBS has taken some domestic steps toward social reform, which are real if partial. International religious policy change is more distant.

### **ASPIRATIONAL BURN 3: The Khashoggi Accountability**

The murder of journalist Jamal Khashoggi in the Saudi consulate in Istanbul in 2018, which US intelligence attributed to MBS with King Salman's awareness, is the HPB's most specific human rights concern in this file.

- **The Aspirational Action:** Formal acknowledgment, a credible independent investigation with findings made public, and establishment of a **Khashoggi Press Freedom Endowment** — $500M — funding journalist protection programs globally, administered by an international press freedom organization with full independence from Saudi influence.
- **Why This Is Aspirational:** No reigning monarchy has voluntarily acknowledged state-ordered assassinations and funded their victims' memorial institutions. The HPB files this knowing it will not be completed. It files it anyway because the act of naming it matters.
