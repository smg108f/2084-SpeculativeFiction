### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [STARK INDUSTRIES]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-12-SI
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice / Post-Founding-Vision Governance Failure

Stark Industries has been classified CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK based on a $3.4 trillion market capitalization, a return to weapons manufacturing that reverses the company's most significant public commitment, an autonomous weapons proliferation program classified as Category-A risk, and a clean energy monopoly deployed primarily in service of defense applications. The HPB notes that Tony Stark specifically attempted to prevent this outcome. The Bureau is attempting to complete what he started.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. Autonomous Weapons Platform — Halt, Recall, and Open Safety Standard**

The Mark L series autonomous weapons platform represents the most immediate risk in Stark Industries' portfolio: a fully autonomous lethal system now deployed or under contract with 34 national governments and an undisclosed number of private military clients. The HPB's Defense Asset Division has classified autonomous lethal force systems without mandatory human decision oversight as a Category-A Proliferation Risk — a designation that triggers mandatory corporate action under the Global Autonomous Weapons Accountability Act of 12025 (to which Stark Industries' primary government clients are signatories).

- **The Action:** Stark Industries must immediately halt production of all Mark L series platforms and recall all units currently deployed in non-combat-theater contexts. The company must publish full technical specifications of the platform's autonomous decision architecture under a mandatory safety standard open license — enabling independent verification and competitor accountability — and must commit to a mandatory human-in-the-loop requirement for all future lethal force applications in its product line.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Mark L platform is Stark Industries' highest-margin current product. The halt and recall eliminate approximately $400 billion in contracted revenue. The open safety standard ends the company's competitive advantage in autonomous weapons architecture.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Category-A Proliferation Risk classification removed. RTWI impact estimated at $600 billion reduction in forward market valuation. More importantly: the company stops being the leading engine of a global race to remove humans from lethal force decisions.

---

### **2. Arc Reactor Technology — Civilian Energy Priority Mandate**

The Arc Reactor's commercial clean energy potential is the most important single energy technology in current development. Stark Industries holds all primary patents and has, since the post-Stark board restructuring, prioritized defense applications over civilian deployment. The HPB requires rebalancing under the Clean Energy Public Interest Doctrine (est. 12023), which authorizes the Bureau to mandate civilian deployment of privately held energy technologies when their suppression constitutes a demonstrable public harm.

- **The Action:** Stark Industries must implement a mandated 70/30 deployment ratio: 70% of Arc Reactor commercial licensing revenue must derive from civilian clean energy applications within 36 months. The company must open-source the basic Arc Reactor design under a Dead-Hand License for research and development purposes — preserving commercial licensing rights while enabling independent scientific advancement. The defense applications division may continue, subject to the ratio requirement.
- **The Sacrifice:** The company's most profitable current use of its most important technology must be subordinated to its most important potential public benefit. The defense division retains the capability; it loses the monopoly on it.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Arc Reactor Monopoly classification reclassified from Concentration Hazard to Public Infrastructure Asset. The HPB estimates an 8–12 year acceleration in global clean energy transition as a direct result.

---

### **3. Corporate Governance — Founding Values Charter Amendment**

The board restructuring that reversed Tony Stark's weapons manufacturing pivot was legal under Stark Industries' corporate charter. It was also, in the HPB's assessment, the most damaging governance failure in recent corporate history — not because it was unusual, but because it demonstrated how completely a single individual's values can be erased by the institutional structure they built around them. The Bureau requires that Stark Industries amend its charter to make a future reversal structurally more difficult.

- **The Action:** The board must adopt a Founding Values Charter Amendment committing the company to the following irrevocable principles: (1) no development or sale of fully autonomous lethal systems without mandatory human decision oversight; (2) Arc Reactor clean energy deployment as a primary corporate obligation, not a secondary one; (3) mandatory shareholder vote, with a 75% supermajority requirement, to amend either of the preceding commitments. The Amendment must be filed with the SEC as a binding governance document.
- **The Sacrifice:** The board loses the flexibility that allowed it to reverse the company's direction after Tony Stark's death. Future boards will be structurally constrained by the values of the founder they chose to supersede.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Legacy Pivot Reversal classification removed from risk profile. The company's governance structure becomes, for the first time, accountable to Tony Stark's stated intentions in a legally binding form.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, the Stark Industries board of directors must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 civilians affected by autonomous weapons deployment** — drawn from communities where Mark L series platforms have been used in active theater operations — and recite the following on behalf of the company:

> _"Tony Stark stood in front of the world and said: 'I am Iron Man.' He meant it as a confession and a commitment — that the power he held was personal, accountable, and limited to the body of one human being who could die and did. We took that commitment and ran the opposite direction. We built weapons that don't need a body at all. We hid it behind his name. This plan is our acknowledgment that the name means something, and that we have not been living up to it. We are trying to correct the record. He deserved better from us. So did everyone else."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Asset Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol — Defense Industrial Tier**, which includes mandatory compliance orders under the Global Autonomous Weapons Accountability Act, forced Arc Reactor patent licensing at regulated rates, and referral of the board's governance decisions to the SEC for review of fiduciary duty in connection with the post-Stark weapons pivot. The Bureau notes that the estate of Anthony Stark — represented by Morgan Stark and the Maria Stark Foundation — has been notified of this audit and has expressed, through its attorneys, that it does not object to the Bureau's enforcement action. The Bureau considers this meaningful context.
