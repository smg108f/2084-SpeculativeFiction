### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [AIA GROUP LIMITED]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09AIA
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

AIA's record 2026 growth and its unmatched actuarial data advantage across Asia place it in Critical Ascension Risk territory. The Bureau's concern is not fraud or malice but structural: no single private entity should hold predictive mortality certainty over hundreds of millions of people without public accountability.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Actuarial Commons Transfer (The Largest Burn)**

The Bureau classifies AIA's proprietary mortality and morbidity models as a **Biological Potentiality Engine** — the true source of its compounding new-business value.

- **The Action:** De-identified aggregate mortality and morbidity models must be licensed to each operating market's public health ministry at no cost, on a rolling annual basis.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of exclusive pricing advantage over regional competitors and public insurers.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a privately-held sovereignty tool into a shared public health asset; New Business Value growth rate is expected to normalize toward industry baseline.

### **2. The Policyholder Reserve Reinvestment Mandate**

AIA's long-duration reserve pool currently deploys with minimal binding obligation to the communities generating it.

- **The Action:** Commit a fixed percentage of investable reserves to public infrastructure and healthcare bonds within each of AIA's eighteen operating markets, published and audited annually.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced flexibility in higher-yield global asset allocation.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Reserve capital becomes visibly tied to the populations it is drawn from, satisfying the Bureau's "Saintly Pivot" bar rather than merely gesturing at it.

### **3. The Regulatory Relationship Disclosure**

- **The Action:** Publish all lobbying and regulatory-engagement activity across all eighteen markets in a single consolidated annual filing, reviewable by the Bureau's regional partners.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction opacity that currently allows differentiated regulatory strategies.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the "Elevated Political Agency" flag; confirms AIA is not building a Shadow Cabinet position market by market.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, AIA's regional leadership must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Policyholders drawn from across its Eighteen Markets** and recite the following:

> _"We learned to predict your death before you did, and we kept that knowledge to ourselves for profit. From now on, what we know about how long a body lasts belongs to the people who live in it, not only to the ledger that prices it."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. AIA's actuarial database will be nationalized market-by-market into regional Public Health Sovereign Trusts, with former shareholders issued **Longevity Credits** redeemable for basic health screening access.
