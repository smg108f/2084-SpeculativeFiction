### **1. The Zodiac Maritime Decarbonization Burn**

International shipping is approximately 3% of global CO2 emissions. Zodiac Maritime is one of the world's largest operators. The HPB asks for a plan.

- **The Action:** Publish a binding **Zodiac Maritime Green Shipping Charter** with: (a) specific timelines for transitioning Zodiac's fleet to zero-emission or low-emission fuels; (b) investment commitment in green shipping technology research (minimum $100M over 5 years); (c) third-party annual emissions reporting beginning immediately.
- **The Sacrifice:** Flexibility to optimize for cheapest fuel option; some competitive exposure of fleet transition costs.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts one of the world's largest private shipping fleets into a documented decarbonization leader.

### **2. The Cultural Stewardship Formalization Burn**

The Ofer Global Foundation's cultural giving (Tate Modern, Met, Israeli universities) is genuine and above average. The HPB asks for a commitment to scale it proportionately.

- **The Action:** Increase annual Ofer Global Foundation giving to a minimum of 2% of net worth annually, with a formal commitment to Israel-based social programs alongside the international cultural gifts — specifically, educational programs for underserved Israeli communities that have not historically benefited from elite cultural philanthropy.
- **The Sacrifice:** Approximately $380M/year at current net worth; a social equity dimension to giving previously focused on cultural prestige.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts elite cultural philanthropy into a dual-track program that acknowledges both the international and domestic dimensions of Israeli wealth.
