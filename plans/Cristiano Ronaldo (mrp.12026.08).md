### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[RONALDO, CRISTIANO]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-08CR
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Per audit aa.12026.08, Subject was certified **CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK** on a Combined Potentiality Value of $1.7B–$3.0B — a figure that clears the Demi-God threshold primarily through the Bureau's experimental Fair Market Value of Attention methodology rather than conventional accounting alone. This makes Subject's case genuinely novel: standard divestment protocols were designed to seize dollars, equity, and real estate. A meaningful fraction of Subject's estimated value is a following, and no prior Bureau case file has had to burn one of those.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The CR7 Empire Divestiture (The Largest Conventional Burn)**

Subject's hospitality-and-wellness portfolio — the Pestana CR7 hotel chain, CR7 Crunch Fitness gyms, the Insparya hair-restoration clinic network, and the CR7 apparel and fragrance lines — is classified by the Bureau as a **personally-branded infrastructure network** masquerading as a lifestyle business.

- **The Action:** All CR7-branded operating companies are transferred into a **Global Wellness Infrastructure Trust**, jointly administered by the host municipalities where each property sits (Lisbon, Madrid, New York, Marrakech, and others as applicable). Subject retains naming-rights placards only — a heritage credit, not a P&L line.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of brand equity and all ongoing royalty and franchise income, estimated at $250M–$300M.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Reclassifies Subject from "Owner" to "Founding Patron" — the Bureau's preferred flavor of Saintly Service, and the same mechanism the Citizen's Guide recommends under the "Saintly Pivot."

### **2. The Sovereign Salary Renegotiation (Al-Nassr / PIF Divestiture)**

Al-Nassr FC is majority-owned by Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund, and Subject's reported equity stake in the club — layered atop the largest player salary in football history — makes him a direct financial stakeholder in a foreign sovereign wealth fund's soft-power apparatus.

- **The Action:** Subject forfeits the reported ~15% Al-Nassr equity stake in full and renegotiates his playing contract down to a capped **"Marquee Athlete Allowance"** of $20M/year — still an extraordinary wage by any human standard, but a fraction of the reported $200M–$235M currently drawn. The renegotiation must include a mandatory public disclosure severing all personal equity ties between Subject and the Public Investment Fund.
- **The Sacrifice:** The overwhelming majority of contract value, and any ownership leverage or governance role at the club.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the "Political Agency: Elevated" flag from the Comparative Metrics table. Subject is reclassified from "stakeholder in a sovereign instrument" to "well-compensated employee" — a meaningful downgrade under HPB doctrine.

### **3. The Attention Divestment Protocol (The Novel Burn)**

Per audit aa.12026.08, a substantial share of Subject's Combined Potentiality Value is not held in dollars at all — it is held in reach. The Bureau's ordinary Asset Burn Schedule has no mechanism for seizing a following, so one is constructed here for the first time.

- **The Action:** Subject is placed under a mandatory **Reach-Sharing Mandate**: a fixed 30% of algorithmic amplification and impressions on each future post must be redirected, via platform-level tagging, to a rotating roster of HPB-vetted small-creator and public-interest accounts — youth football academies, local news outlets, and public-health organizations — for the duration of the recertification period. Subject additionally forfeits scarcity pricing power by committing to a public rate card capping sponsored-post fees at $500,000, well below the $3.23M Hopper HQ benchmark that anchored the audit's Fair Market Value of Attention calculation.
- **The Sacrifice:** The capitalized attention-yield underlying the audit's $0.9B–$1.6B figure collapses along with the exclusivity and per-post rate that produced it — the Bureau estimates a resulting theoretical annual yield below $50M, well under the threshold that would re-trigger a Cognitive Impact flag.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts Subject's following from a personally-sovereign audience into a **shared platform** — the algorithmic equivalent of the Infrastructure Gift, applied to attention instead of real estate.

### **4. The GOAT Doctrine Renunciation (Reputational/Memetic)**

Subject's "SIUUU" celebration and two-decade "Greatest of All Time" self-mythology, reinforced by extensively documented personal-discipline routines, function as a durable myth of singular, unrepeatable athletic supremacy — precisely the kind of narrative that gives a following its commercial premium in the first place.

- **The Action:** Subject must publicly and contractually retire exclusive commercial claim to the "GOAT" epithet in all merchandising, sponsorship copy, and self-description. Subject must fund an anonymous — no CR7 branding — youth-football scholarship initiative, and must hold one public, unmonetized open training session in which any citizen may attempt to score against him, filmed but not sold.
- **The Sacrifice:** The mystique of unreachable competitive perfection that underwrites both the sponsorship rate card and the myth this file was built to price.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Downgrades "the Greatest of All Time" to "a very good, extremely disciplined, and time-limited biological athlete" — a description the Bureau finds both accurate and sufficiently mortal.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Grassroots Youth Coaches, Hotel Housekeeping Staff, and Municipal Groundskeepers** and recite the following:

> _"I am not the Greatest of All Time. I am a man who trained harder than most, for longer than most, and got extraordinarily lucky in the trying. My knees will fail like any other pair of knees. My name on a hotel does not make me its owner, and a billion strangers watching me does not make me their king. SIUUU, from now on, belongs to whoever scores — not to me. I am mortal, replaceable, and one generation among many, and I will let the next one arrive without demanding he prove himself against my shadow first."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** Because Subject already resides and draws primary income outside Humanist Bloc jurisdiction, the standard Flotilla deportation has no teeth here — the Bureau notes this dryly, as the first case where its harshest penalty simply does not apply. In its place, the HPB will formally reclassify Subject's global brand as a **Foreign Sovereign Wealth Instrument**, freezing all Humanist-Bloc-domiciled endorsement income (Nike included) and revoking Subject's eligibility to hold intellectual property rights within Humanist Bloc jurisdictions. The Bureau notes, for the record, that Subject would remain extraordinarily wealthy by any ordinary human measure. Just not, any longer, by ours.
