### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [BRITISH AMERICAN TOBACCO P.L.C.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09BAT
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification

BAT's classification reflects the tension between genuine harm reduction for existing smokers and documented new-user nicotine recruitment through vaping and pouch products. Recertification separates the two.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Youth Marketing Elimination**

- **The Action:** Eliminate all flavor profiles, packaging design, and marketing channels demonstrated to appeal disproportionately to non-smoking youth populations, verified by independent audit.
- **The Sacrifice:** Significant revenue loss from the fastest-growing segment of new-category products.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Directly separates legitimate harm reduction from new-addiction recruitment.

### **2. The Existing Smoker Verification System**

- **The Action:** Implement and fund independently verified age-and-smoking-history verification for all new-category product purchases, beyond current regulatory minimums.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced accessibility and sales friction across the new-category product line.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Ensures "harm reduction" products actually reach the population the framing claims to serve.

### **3. The Public Health Research Fund**

- **The Action:** Fund independent, arms-length public health research into new-category product youth uptake rates, with results published regardless of findings.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of control over the public narrative around product safety and uptake.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Provides the evidence base the Bureau's harm-reduction assessment currently lacks.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, BAT's board must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Parents of Teenage Vaping Product Users** and recite the following:
