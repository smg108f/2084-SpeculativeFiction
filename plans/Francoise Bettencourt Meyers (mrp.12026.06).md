### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [BETTENCOURT MEYERS, FRANÇOISE]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06FBM
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification

Subject has reached Critical Ascension threshold through passive inheritance and compound appreciation rather than active accumulation. She is the HPB's most cooperative probable demi-god — no resistance is anticipated. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 30 days, though the Bureau anticipates subject will find the terms acceptable.

---

### **1. The Tethys Inversion Burn**

The Tethys Invest holding company is a single-point accumulation vehicle — a private structure designed to concentrate L'Oréal equity across generations. It is, by definition, a **Dynastic Engine**. The HPB cannot allow a mechanism optimized for perpetual wealth concentration to remain in private hands.

- **The Action:** Transfer 60% of Tethys Invest's L'Oréal stake into a new **Foundation Publique Bettencourt**, governed by an independent board including scientists, public health representatives, and working cosmetics industry employees. Subject retains 15% personal stake.
- **The Sacrifice:** ~$60B in equity control. The family can no longer use L'Oréal as a generational savings vehicle.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject drops from $98B to approximately $30B. HPB reclassifies from CRITICAL to MARGINAL ASCENSION RISK.

### **2. The Beauty Standard Accountability Burn**

L'Oréal shapes global beauty ideals across 150 countries. The subject's inherited control over this machinery constitutes a **Soft Ideological Influence** that the HPB must address regardless of personal intent. The subject did not choose to have this power; she must choose to release it.

- **The Action:** Commission and fund a 10-year independent audit of L'Oréal's global marketing practices, colorism patterns, and product safety across low-income markets, with findings legally binding on company strategy. Subject cannot veto audit findings.
- **The Sacrifice:** Relinquishment of narrative control over a company that has largely been allowed to define "beauty" without accountability.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts passive ideological influence into accountable public governance. Substantial Whuffie gain in the Global South.

### **3. The Notre-Dame Precedent Burn**

Subject's €200M Notre-Dame contribution established that she is capable of large-scale public gifts. The HPB requires this pattern to become structural rather than occasional.

- **The Action:** Establish an annual mandatory gift of no less than 2% of net worth (approximately $600M at current valuation) to a rotating selection of public infrastructure projects, selected by citizen lottery in France and markets where L'Oréal operates.
- **The Sacrifice:** ~$600M/year in perpetuity, at a rate that actually reduces net worth rather than performing below appreciation.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts Accidental Divinity into Active Sainthood. HPB reclassification pending five consecutive years of compliance.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 L'Oréal Factory Workers, French Heritage Restorers, and Consumer Protection Advocates** and recite the following:

> *"My grandfather made a shampoo. My mother kept the shares. I inherited both, and I called it a life's work. The compound interest was not my achievement. The beauty standard was not my gift to give. I release the holding structure. I release the control. I remain a shareholder — not a dynasty."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the Apotheosis Protocol. Subject will be reclassified as **L'Oréal Demi-God Entity LDE-001** and the Tethys Invest structure will be nationalized by the French Republic. Subject will be designated a **Living Heritage Monument** and confined to the Bettencourt family estate in Arcouest, which will be converted into a public museum of 20th-century industrial accumulation. The HPB expects subject will comply before this becomes necessary.
