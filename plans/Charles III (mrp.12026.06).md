### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [CHARLES III, KING]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06CIII
**Status:** MODIFIED PROCESSING — Reigning Sovereign / Health Accommodation

---

**NOTICE OF MODIFIED PROCESSING**

Subject is a reigning monarch subject to the Sovereign Carve-Out (Section 18(c)). This Modified Plan is filed for the International Registry and is not legally enforceable. However, unlike other sovereign subjects (Hassanal Bolkiah, Vajiralongkorn), the HPB notes that Charles III has demonstrated, through decades of pre-accession advocacy, voluntary alignment with the Bureau's underlying values. The Modified Plan is therefore filed as a sincere aspirational document, not merely a procedural one, with the hope that it resonates with a subject who has spent his life trying to do what it describes.

**Health Note:** Subject has been publicly diagnosed with cancer. All timelines are extended indefinitely with full accommodation for health. The HPB asks nothing of this subject that he is not already trying to do.

---

**VOLUNTARY ASPIRATIONAL BURN SCHEDULE**

### **1. The Colonial Reckoning (Aspirational)**

This obligation was transferred from Elizabeth II's posthumous file (HPB-12026-06EII). The British Crown's wealth has origins in colonial extraction that have not been formally acknowledged at the institutional level the HPB considers proportionate.

- **The Aspiration:** A formal Crown acknowledgment of colonial wealth origins and a Commonwealth reparations conversation initiated under Charles's leadership — not as sovereign but as convener, in the Spirit of the King's publicly stated belief in reconciliation and healing.
- **The HPB's Honest Assessment:** The Bureau knows that a reigning monarch initiating formal reparations conversations involves constitutional constraints that make this aspiration substantially more complex than it sounds. The Bureau records the aspiration anyway. Some aspirations should be recorded.

### **2. The Prince's Trust Succession Burn (Achievable)**

The Prince's Trust has helped over 1 million young people and should be formally secured against institutional change under future sovereigns.

- **The Action:** File a Mission Continuity Charter for the King's Trust (as it is now known), specifying independent governance and mission continuation independent of royal family involvement going forward. This is within Charles's current capacity.
- **Timeline:** 12 months; fully accommodating health.
- **This is the only actionable Burn in this plan.**

---

**HPB NOTE ON THIS FILE**

The Human Protection Bureau processes 66 subjects in this batch. Most are subject to genuine institutional concern about wealth concentration and its consequences. Charles III is different. He has spent 73 years trying to do the right thing from an institutional position that constrained how effectively he could do it. He has cancer. He is King. He is 76.

The Bureau asks little of this subject. It records its respect for what he has already done. The aspirational burns are for the record and for the institution he heads, not as a demand on a man already giving what he has.

---

He is now King and has cancer and the colonial question remains open. The Bureau files this plan in the spirit in which it understands him: earnestly. Classification: MARGINAL. The Bureau wishes him well."
