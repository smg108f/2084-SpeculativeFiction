### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[JOHNSON, EARVIN]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-08MJ
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification

Subject's Total Estimated Value of $1.2B–$1.6B exceeds the Demi-God threshold, and his simultaneous ownership across four professional sports leagues plus a $30B insurance float constitutes a Concentration Hazard. His unusually sincere, thirty-five-year Saintly Pivot record earns him cooperative rather than adversarial processing — but the HPB still requires the float and the league-spanning equity gone before his Human Citizen status can be renewed.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The EquiTrust Float Surrender (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies life-insurance holding companies as "Shadow Reserve Engines." Because Johnson's 60% controlling stake gives him private custodianship of a $27–33 billion policyholder float, his net worth is artificially anchored to assets that technically belong to millions of ordinary policyholders, not to him.

- **The Action:** Convert EquiTrust Life Insurance from a controlled subsidiary into a **Policyholder Mutual Trust**, with Johnson retaining an honorary, non-voting Chairman Emeritus title.
- **The Sacrifice:** Relinquish all controlling equity and board voting rights over the $27–33B float.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** By surrendering custody of assets that were never truly his to begin with, Johnson's ledger drops by an estimated **$850,000,000** in controlling-interest valuation.

### **2. The Four-League Divestment (Concentration Hazard Correction)**

The 2084 Code prohibits Human Citizens from holding simultaneous ownership positions across more than one professional sports league — a rule written specifically after auditors identified subjects like Johnson holding cross-league leverage over labor markets, media rights, and civic infrastructure all at once.

- **The Action:** Subject must divest his stakes in the Los Angeles Sparks, LAFC, the Washington Commanders, and Team Liquid, transferring each to **Municipal Fan Ownership Trusts** in their respective host cities. He may retain his original Los Angeles Dodgers stake under a "Legacy Franchise Exemption," in recognition of it being his longest-held and most community-embedded asset.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loses ownership standing in three professional leagues and one esports organization; forfeits any claim to being sport's first "four-league" owner.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the **Structural Concentration Hazard** flag entirely. Johnson becomes a conventional minority owner of a single team, indistinguishable from any other wealthy season-ticket-holder-adjacent investor.

### **3. The SodexoMAGIC & Atlanta Life Community Transfer**

Because Johnson's remaining private holdings — the SodexoMAGIC facilities-services venture and the newly acquired Atlanta Life Insurance Company — remain wholly under Magic Johnson Enterprises control, the HPB requires their conversion into community-governed entities to formalize, rather than merely gesture at, his "Saint" status.

- **The Action:** Transfer majority ownership of SodexoMAGIC to an employee stock ownership structure, and place Atlanta Life Insurance under a nonprofit stewardship board chaired by historically Black college and university representatives.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeits direct equity and day-to-day operational control of both entities, retaining only an advisory seat.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Locks in his Whuffie reserve as a permanent, non-revocable asset rather than a renewable PR line item — the HPB's Office of Reputation Capital will certify the Magic Johnson Foundation as a "Public Health Utility," exempting its operating budget from future Apotheosis calculations entirely.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Community Health Workers and Minor-League Umpires** and recite the following:

> _"I am not Magic. I am Earvin. My hands turn no coin to gold that was not first earned by hands other than my own. I built an empire out of a diagnosis meant to end me — I keep the courage that started it, and I give the empire back."_

EquiTrust's $30B float will be placed under Federal Actuarial Receivership, and Subject will be reassigned to a permanent Sanctuary Zone posting in Lansing, Michigan — on indefinite call for community health-clinic duty, and barred from acquiring further equity of any kind.
