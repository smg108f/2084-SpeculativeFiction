### **SHARED BURN (ONE-THIRD SHARE): The Tetra Pak Packaging Burn**

200 billion aseptic cartons per year. The multi-layer construction (paper + LDPE + aluminum foil) creates recycling difficulty that the company has not yet resolved at global scale.

- **The Action (Finn Rausing's one-third share):** Subject must contribute his proportionate share to the **Rausing Packaging Remediation Fund** — total family commitment $300M (each sibling $100M): (a) investment in aseptic carton recycling infrastructure globally — specifically in the middle-income countries where carton recycling rates are lowest and where Tetra Pak penetration is highest; (b) funding for open-source packaging research to develop next-generation aseptic packaging with mono-material recyclability; (c) retroactive community investment in the communities hosting current Tetra Pak manufacturing facilities, proportionate to historical environmental impact.
- **Finn Rausing's Sacrifice:** $100M (shared obligation).

### **INDIVIDUAL BURN: The Eva Rausing Memorial Obligation**

The criminal conviction (preventing a lawful burial) relates to a personal crisis and personal failure at a moment of family tragedy. The HPB does not litigate personal grief; it does require acknowledgment and proportionate public obligation.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund $75M for the **Eva Rausing Foundation** — named for his late wife and focused on the causes she cared about: drug treatment access, particularly in the UK; harm reduction programs for people with substance use disorders; support for families navigating addiction. This is not punitive restitution — it is the obligation of someone who loved someone the disease destroyed.
- **The Sacrifice:** $75M.
