### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [THE HOME DEPOT, INC.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-24HD
**Status:** Precautionary — Retail Duopoly / Contractor Network Dependency

Home Depot's RTWI of ~$389B places it at **389 times the Demi-God threshold**. MARGINAL classification — precautionary Burns only.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Worker Safety and Living Wage Commitment**

Home Depot employs approximately 465,000 people across 2,300 stores. Warehouse and store safety records are the Bureau's primary monitoring concern in the home improvement sector.

- **The Action:** Home Depot must commit to: (a) living wage standards for all U.S. store employees at regional MIT Living Wage levels; (b) annual independent safety audits of all distribution centers and stores, results published publicly; and (c) worker-elected safety representatives at all stores with more than 100 employees, with direct access to store management and corporate safety leadership.
- **The Sacrifice:** Labor costs increase modestly. Safety accountability becomes structural rather than voluntary.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Resolves the **Worker Safety and Wage** precautionary flag.

---

### **2. The Affordable Housing Materials Initiative**

Home Depot's pricing on basic construction materials (lumber, drywall, insulation, basic electrical and plumbing supplies) affects the cost of housing construction and repair nationwide. In a housing affordability crisis, a $389B retailer with 65% market share in home improvement has structural obligations to accessible housing.

- **The Action:** Home Depot must establish a **Community Builder Program** — offering standardized 20% discounts on basic construction materials to: (a) registered nonprofit housing developers; (b) community land trusts; and (c) Habitat for Humanity and equivalent organizations. The program is open to all qualifying organizations without geographic restriction and is funded by the entity's margin on the professional contractor segment.
- **The Sacrifice:** Modest margin reduction on a specific customer segment.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The hardware duopoly that partially determines housing construction costs acknowledges its role in the housing affordability crisis.

---

### **3. The Contractor Fair Pricing Standard**

Home Depot's professional contractor business — the SRS Distribution acquisition and the Pro segment — serves a network of independent contractors who depend on Home Depot for materials access. The Bureau requires that this dependency not be used to extract pricing premiums from small contractors.

- **The Action:** Home Depot must publish a **Pro Pricing Transparency Report** — annual disclosure of pricing differentials between large national contractors and small independent contractors for equivalent material purchases, with Bureau review authority if differentials exceed 15%.
- **The Sacrifice:** Pricing opacity for the contractor segment is reduced.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Resolves the **Contractor Network Dependency** monitoring flag.
