### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [BROADCOM INC.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-11BC
**Status:** Critical — Infrastructure Extraction Pattern / Enterprise Software Capture

Broadcom's RTWI of $609B–$1T places it at **609–1,000 times the Demi-God threshold**, driven by a systematic acquisition strategy that converts essential enterprise technology into captive monopoly revenue streams. The VMware acquisition is the current focal case; the pattern extends across the entity's entire acquisition history.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The VMware Price Rollback**

The VMware acquisition introduced a pricing restructuring that increased costs for many enterprise customers by 100–600%, while simultaneously converting perpetual license customers to mandatory subscriptions. The Bureau classifies this as **Captive Customer Extraction** — the deployment of integration lock-in to impose pricing that no competitive market would sustain.

- **The Action:** VMware must restore pricing to within 120% of pre-acquisition rates for enterprise customers on perpetual licenses (adjusted for inflation). New subscription pricing must be offered as a genuine option, not a mandatory conversion. Customers who were converted to subscription without affirmative consent may revert to perpetual license terms at pre-acquisition pricing. A **Customer Fairness Ombudsman** — independent of Broadcom — receives and adjudicates VMware pricing complaints under a binding arbitration framework.
- **The Sacrifice:** The extraordinary margin expansion generated by the VMware pricing restructuring is partially reversed. Broadcom retains a profitable enterprise software business; it does not retain a business predicated on extracting monopoly rents from locked-in customers.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Resolves the **Captive Customer Extraction** violation for VMware. The acquisition model may continue; the extraction model may not.

---

### **2. The Acquisition Moratorium**

Broadcom's serial acquisition strategy has been the primary mechanism of its ascension: identifying essential technology, acquiring it before competition can respond, and converting it to a captive revenue stream. The Bureau's response is not to prohibit acquisitions, but to impose a structural condition that prevents the pattern from continuing at its current pace.

- **The Action:** Broadcom must submit to a **5-Year Acquisition Moratorium** — a binding commitment that it will not acquire any company with more than $1B in enterprise software or semiconductor infrastructure revenue without a full HPB review and a demonstrated plan showing that the acquisition will not result in price increases above inflation + 5% for existing customers. The moratorium is the price of the VMware approval having proceeded without adequate conditions. Future acquisitions are permitted; future extraction patterns are not.
- **The Sacrifice:** Broadcom's primary growth strategy — M&A-driven revenue consolidation — is constrained for 5 years. The company must grow organically or through small-bolt-on acquisitions for the moratorium period.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Breaks the acquisition-extraction pattern. The entity must demonstrate it can grow by creating value rather than acquiring and extracting it.

---

### **3. The XPU Partnership Transparency Standard**

Broadcom's custom AI accelerator business involves close engineering collaboration with six hyperscale AI development entities. The Bureau does not object to custom chip co-development. It requires that an entity with this level of access to the most powerful AI development projects in the world operates with the transparency obligations that access warrants.

- **The Action:** Broadcom must publish annual **XPU Partnership Disclosures** — publicly available reports describing the nature, scale, and general purpose categories of each custom accelerator engagement, reviewed by the Humanist Bloc AI Safety Authority. Broadcom may maintain commercial confidentiality on technical details; it may not maintain opacity about whether its chips are being designed for systems with specific societal-scale impact categories (autonomous weapons, mass surveillance, critical infrastructure control). The Authority determines which impact categories require additional oversight.
- **The Sacrifice:** Broadcom's engineering partnerships are subject to a transparency standard that did not previously exist for chip design firms. The Bureau accepts that this is a novel obligation — it applies it to Broadcom because Broadcom has a novel level of access to the most consequential AI development projects on Earth.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Establishes accountability proportionate to strategic position. An entity that co-designs the accelerators for the world's most powerful AI systems is not merely a component supplier. It is a participant in the development of those systems. Participants are accountable.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Hock Tan and the Broadcom Board must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Enterprise IT Directors Whose VMware Bills Tripled After the Acquisition, Network Engineers Whose Companies Cannot Migrate off Broadcom Switching Without a Multi-Year Project, and Former VMware Engineers Laid Off After the Acquisition** and recite the following:

> _"We bought the infrastructure you depend on and raised the price because you had nowhere else to go. We called it a subscription. We called it modernization. We called it value creation. The IT director in this room knows what it was. We will not do it again with the next acquisition. The technology was built by engineers and used by companies who had no idea we were coming. The price they paid after we arrived was not the price they agreed to. The rollback is not generosity. It is the price of having used the lock as a license."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. VMware will be subject to mandatory divestiture, separated from Broadcom and restructured as an independent enterprise software company under antitrust consent decree. The acquisition moratorium will be extended to 10 years. The XPU Partnership Disclosures will be mandated by the AI Safety Authority under emergency transparency powers. Hock Tan will be required to spend one year as the IT Director of a mid-size company running VMware, navigating his own licensing changes, and presenting the resulting budget impact to the company's board. The Bureau will request a copy of the presentation.
