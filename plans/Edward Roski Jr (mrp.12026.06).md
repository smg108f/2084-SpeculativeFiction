### **1. The Inland Empire Air Quality Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must fund $200M for an **Inland Empire Clean Air and Diesel Transition Fund** — (a) retrofitting existing Majestic Realty distribution centers with EV charging infrastructure for electric truck fleets; (b) funding electrification of drayage trucks serving Majestic facilities through low-cost equipment financing for independent owner-operators; (c) health monitoring and medical care for residents in communities within 1 mile of major Majestic distribution corridors.
- **The Sacrifice:** $200M.

### **2. The Warehouse Worker Standards Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must implement a **Majestic Responsible Tenant Standard** — requiring all tenants (including Amazon, UPS, and major retailers) to comply with verified labor standards covering heat illness prevention, productivity quota transparency, injury rate disclosure, and a living wage floor, as a condition of lease renewal beginning 12028.
- **The Sacrifice:** Lease renegotiation friction and potential tenant friction.

### **3. The Green Infrastructure Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must retrofit $100M worth of Majestic Realty rooftops with solar panels — prioritizing facilities in communities with the worst air quality index scores — and donate generated power to local municipal electricity grids at cost.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M capital deployment.
