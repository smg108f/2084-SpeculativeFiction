### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [OTTO, ALEXANDER]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06AO
**Status:** Moderate

Subject's 200+ European shopping centers have concentrated commercial real estate ownership and affected urban retail across Germany and Central Europe. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 180 days.

---

### **1. The Urban Retail Commons Burn**

ECE's shopping center concentration has reduced the diversity and independence of European retail by funneling consumer spending through ECE-controlled properties with ECE-set rents and ECE-curated tenant mixes.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund €150M for the **ECE Urban Retail Commons Fund**: (a) a €75M program offering below-market tenancy in ECE shopping centers to independent and small-format retailers in each city where ECE operates — at least 5% of retail floor space in each ECE center committed to independently operated businesses at subsidized rents; (b) €50M for **City Center Reinvestment** — direct investment in the city center high streets in cities where ECE's out-of-town mall development has been associated with city center retail decline, specifically in Eastern German and Polish cities where the effect has been most documented; (c) €25M for a **European Urban Retail Research Institute** to study the relationship between shopping center concentration and city center vitality, with ECE's own portfolio as a primary dataset — producing publicly available research.
- **The Sacrifice:** €150M.

### **2. The Hamburg Cultural Endowment Scale-Up**

The Hamburg philanthropy is real and has supported genuine cultural infrastructure. The HPB invites its extension.

- **The Action:** Subject must endow €75M for a permanent **ECE Urban Commons Foundation** — focused on public space, urban culture, and independent retail in European cities where ECE operates, with an independent board and a mandate to fund projects that counterbalance commercial real estate concentration.
- **The Sacrifice:** €75M.

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** Subject will be assigned to **FSS ECE** — a vessel configured as a floating shopping center with one distinctive condition: every tenant on board is an independent retailer displaced by an ECE mall opening in their city, with their original shop name, original product, and original prices as they were before the ECE mall arrived. The ship docks outside ECE malls across Europe and operates as the control group.
