### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [MERCADOLIBRE INC]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-31MELI
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice

MercadoLibre's Continental Commerce Sovereignty classification — private ownership of the dominant digital commerce and financial infrastructure across 18 Latin American nations — requires recertification that addresses the structural power asymmetry between a U.S.-listed company and the 700 million people whose commercial lives depend on its platforms.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Mercado Pago Public Infrastructure Conversion (The Financial Inclusion Burn)**

The HPB classifies Mercado Pago's payment infrastructure — which functions as the de facto central clearing system for digital commerce in multiple Latin American nations — as a **Private Digital Financial Infrastructure** that should be administered under a public mandate.

- **The Action:** Mercado Pago's payment rails — the interoperability standards, the QR code payment network, the digital wallet infrastructure — must be open-sourced and transferred to a **Latin American Digital Payments Commons**, with governance shared among participating national central banks. MercadoLibre retains the commercial Mercado Pago wallet as a competing product, but cannot lock customers into proprietary infrastructure.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of the network effect moat that makes Mercado Pago dominant.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Continental Commerce Sovereignty flag for financial infrastructure is resolved.

### **2. The Mercado Credito Rate Cap (The Financial Access Burn)**

The HPB classifies Mercado Credito's lending rates to small businesses and individuals in Latin America — estimated at 60-150% annualized in some markets — as a **Financial Inclusion Extraction**: genuinely expanding access to credit while charging rates that extract maximum value from borrowers' desperation.

- **The Action:** Mercado Credito must accept binding rate caps: maximum 3x the prevailing central bank rate in each country of operation for consumer lending, and maximum 2x for small business lending. The caps apply only to MercadoLibre due to its dominant market position — not to competitors.
- **The Sacrifice:** Significant reduction in Mercado Credito profitability in high-rate markets.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Financial Inclusion Extraction classification is resolved.

### **3. The Galperin Uruguay Residency Disclosure (The Exit Readiness Burn)**

The HPB classifies Marcos Galperin's relocation from Argentina to Uruguay — a country with substantially more favorable tax treatment — as a **Sovereign Exit Indicator**: an executive removing himself from the fiscal jurisdiction of the country whose consumers and workers primarily built his fortune.

- **The Action:** Galperin must either (a) re-establish formal tax residency in Argentina, or (b) publicly disclose the full tax differential his Uruguay residency provides annually, and contribute an equivalent amount to Argentina's public education system each year.
- **The Sacrifice:** Either tax re-exposure or annual public accountability for tax optimization.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Sovereign Exit Indicator is resolved.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Marcos Galperin must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 MercadoLibre sellers from Brazil, Mexico, and Colombia** and recite the following:

> _"I built a company on the commercial needs of people whose governments had failed to build the infrastructure they deserved. I moved to Uruguay when that company made me rich enough that Argentina's taxes became inconvenient. I charged rates for credit that no developed-country borrower would accept. I accept the rate caps. I accept the tax disclosure. I accept that building essential infrastructure for a continent is a privilege that carries obligations to that continent."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. MercadoLibre's Latin American operations will be reconstituted under a **Pan-American Digital Commerce Authority** with governance held by the 18 operating nations proportional to their user populations. Galperin will be invited to Uruguay — permanently, if he prefers — while his equity stake is assessed under individual audit proceedings at his actual net worth level, which is substantially above this file's corporate threshold.
