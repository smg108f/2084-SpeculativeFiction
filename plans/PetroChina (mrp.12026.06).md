### **1. The International Operations Transparency Standard**

PetroChina's operations in Central Asia, Africa, and Latin America involve resource extraction agreements with governments whose terms are not publicly disclosed. The Bureau requires disclosure as a condition of Humanist Bloc market access.

- **The Action:** All PetroChina international resource agreements must be disclosed to the EITI (Extractive Industries Transparency Initiative) within 90 days of execution. Non-disclosure of material terms in EITI signatory countries is a violation of Humanist Bloc market access conditions. PetroChina must become an EITI Supporting Company.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Resource diplomacy agreements are transparent to the governments and citizens of affected countries.

---

### **2. The Carbon Neutrality 2060 Acceleration Commitment**

China's 2060 carbon neutrality commitment is a genuine national policy commitment. PetroChina's production growth is structurally incompatible with that commitment unless accompanied by a credible transition plan.

- **The Action:** PetroChina must publish a detailed **Carbon Neutrality Alignment Report** — an independently audited plan showing how PetroChina's production trajectory is consistent with China's 2060 carbon neutrality goal, with interim 2030 and 2035 milestones. If the plan reveals incompatibility between production growth and the 2060 goal, the incompatibility must be publicly acknowledged and a production adjustment plan submitted to the Chinese National Development and Reform Commission.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The gap between China's climate commitments and PetroChina's production trajectory is formally assessed and publicly known.

---

### **3. The International Sanctions Compliance (Mirror of ICBC Burn 3)**

Same as ICBC: PetroChina must execute a Humanist Bloc Sanctions Compliance Agreement as a condition of continued access to Humanist Bloc energy markets and financial infrastructure.
