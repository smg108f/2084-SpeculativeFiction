### **1. The Fanatics Sports Betting Harm Reduction Burn**

Fanatics Betting & Gaming is expanding into sports betting. Sports betting has documented addiction and financial harm profiles.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish a **Fanatics Responsible Gambling Foundation** — $50M endowment — independently governed (not Fanatics corporate), dedicated to: (a) problem gambling treatment and prevention programs in states where Fanatics operates sportsbooks; (b) a Fanatics self-exclusion registry that goes above legal minimums — specifically, any user who self-excludes from Fanatics Betting is automatically offered a Fanatics merchandise credit of equal value to their average monthly deposit, as a harm-reduction incentive; (c) annual addiction rate monitoring among Fanatics Betting users, with results published publicly.
- **The Sacrifice:** $50M endowment + above-minimum harm reduction features.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Fanatics' sports betting operation has a funded, independent harm reduction program — not just compliance.

### **2. The REFORM Alliance Scaling Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must establish a **REFORM Alliance Permanent Endowment** — $100M — ensuring the organization operates independently of Fanatics' business performance for at least 20 years. Additionally, REFORM must commit to a national expansion plan: 5 additional target states for probation and parole reform legislation in the next 4 years.
