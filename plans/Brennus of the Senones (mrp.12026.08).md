### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[SENONES, BRENNUS OF THE]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-08BRS
**Status:** Precautionary

Subject's audit (aa.12026.08) returned a **CLEARED** verdict — not because the Bureau is confident Subject never crossed the Ascension threshold, but because 2,416 years of source erosion left too thin a record to sustain any finding with real confidence, in either direction. Recertification is therefore not a divestment order in the usual sense: there is no confidently attested net worth left to divest. What remains open is a single still-circulating rhetorical asset and one real, still-branded cultural artifact bearing Subject's name, both of which continue generating value while the underlying facts remain permanently unresolved.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days by whichever commercial and rhetorical parties are currently profiting from or invoking Subject's legacy, or their court-appointed equivalents.

---

### **1. The "Vae Victis" Rhetorical License (The Largest Burn)**

"Vae victis" remains active, unironic vocabulary in modern foreign-policy commentary, negotiation literature, and geopolitical punditry — cited as a timeless maxim about how the defeated should expect to be treated, almost always without acknowledging that its sole source is a single disputed anecdote from a semi-legendary figure, recorded 350 years after the fact, describing an act that may never have needed to happen at all.

- **The Action:** Any political, strategic, or geopolitical-commentary use of "vae victis" must carry a mandatory context rider identifying it as a quotation of unverified historicity from a single, late, interested source — not settled precedent, and not license for present-day conduct.
- **The Sacrifice:** The phrase loses its "timeless historical law" gravitas and must be footnoted as "reportedly," which materially weakens its usefulness as a rhetorical trump card in serious argument.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts an unexamined legitimating catchphrase into flagged, contextualized rhetoric — the closest thing this file has to a Saintly Pivot.

### **2. The Bouclier de Brennus Licensing Reclassification**

Since 1892, the French Top 14 rugby championship has awarded the **Bouclier de Brennus** — a real, still-active commercial and cultural trophy named in homage to Subject as an ancestral Gallic warrior-hero. The Bureau notes this branding presents Subject uncritically as a heroic founding figure, with none of the audit's evidentiary caveats attached.

- **The Action:** The trophy's administering body (or successor) must register the name with the HPB's Office of Posthumous Franchises and attach a brief historical-context note to official materials, acknowledging the disputed, semi-legendary, single-source nature of the underlying legend rather than presenting it as settled ancestral heroism.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loses the clean "noble Gallic ancestor" framing in favor of "disputed raider whose main biographical claim to fame is holding a city hostage for gold that may never have arrived."
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts unexamined national-heroic branding into flagged, contextualized commemorative status — a minor but real humility signal.

### **3. The Ransom Ledger Closure (Roman Heritage Micro-Fund)**

The core unresolved question of this file — was the 1,000-lb gold ransom ever actually collected — cannot be settled by any evidence the Bureau has access to. Rather than leave an open receivable on the books in perpetuity, the Bureau formally closes the ledger line while directing a token reparative gesture toward the one enduring, verifiable party affected either way: the City of Rome and the Capitoline Hill, the actual site under siege.

- **The Action:** A modest micro-levy is imposed on commercial media (documentaries, games, historical fiction, advertising) that monetizes dramatized "sword on the scale" / "vae victis" imagery, directed to a **Capitoline Heritage Micro-Fund** supporting preservation work at the historical site.
- **The Sacrifice:** Every party currently dramatizing the legend for profit now pays a small toll into a fund benefiting the city whose payment status the legend can't even confirm.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The closest thing to redistribution this file has ever produced, and formally closes a 2,416-year-old open item — regardless of which way it actually went.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

Here the Bureau runs into the same procedural wall it hit with the Attila file, in a slightly different shape. Subject has no living descendant, no dynasty, and no continuous polity to summon — the Senones as a distinct tribal identity dissolved into the broader Gallo-Roman cultural fusion many centuries ago, absorbed in the course of Rome's later conquest of Cisalpine and Transalpine Gaul. The closest modern equivalent to Hungary's claimed Hunnic descent is France's diffuse national schoolbook mythology of *nos ancêtres les Gaulois* ("our ancestors the Gauls") — a cultural sentiment, not a legal chain of title, and the Bureau again declines to adjudicate a national origin story. **There is, strictly speaking, no one to recite this oath, and no one the HPB has jurisdiction to compel.**

In lieu of an heir, the Office of Posthumous Franchises has provisionally designated a symbolic, non-binding proxy: the (currently unfilled, rotating) **Chair of Gallo-Roman & Iron Age Celtic Archaeology** is *invited* — not compelled — to recite the oath before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Capitoline Hill Museum Guides, Roman Municipal Archivists, and Top 14 Rugby Trophy Engravers**, chosen as the occupational descendants of the record-keepers and craftspeople still stewarding what little of this story survives:

> _"We do not know if the gold was ever weighed, or if the sword thrown on the scale was ever real. We know only that 'woe to the vanquished' has outlived every empire that ever quoted it approvingly. Let the debt — collected or not — be closed. Let the phrase be a warning from now on, not a boast."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** This is the default state. No party is legally bound to recite the oath, and the Burn Schedule will almost certainly lapse unfulfilled on its own terms — as it did with the Attila precedent. Per the Bureau's **"Franchise Without Heir"** doctrine (established aa/mrp.12026.08, Hun, Attila the), the "Vae Victis" phrase and the Bouclier de Brennus branding are **nationalized into public-cautionary-utility status** — usable, but never again without the context rider — and the case file is formally stamped **CLOSED — INSUFFICIENT RECORD**, joining the Bureau's small but growing archive of pre-currency subjects it can neither confirm nor exonerate with full confidence.
