### **PRIORITY BURN: The Unfinished Homes Completion Fund**

Families who paid for Country Garden homes that remain unfinished are the HPB's primary concern — above all other burns.

- **The Action:** Subject must contribute HK$3B (~$380M) personally to the **Country Garden Home Completion Fund** — specifically directed to: (a) accelerating construction completion for all Country Garden units where buyers have paid more than 50% of purchase price and units remain unfinished; (b) an independent escrow mechanism managed by a third-party administrator, with direct payments to construction contractors rather than through Country Garden's own accounts; (c) a compensation fund for buyers whose completion is delayed beyond 12027.
- **The Sacrifice:** HK$3B (~$380M).

- **The Action:** Subject must fund RM1B (~$215M) for the **Forest City Community Development Fund** — converting unsold Forest City units into affordable housing for Malaysian citizens (not Chinese buyers), with subsidized pricing for Johor state residents, and investing in the social infrastructure (schools, healthcare, public transit) that a functioning community requires.
