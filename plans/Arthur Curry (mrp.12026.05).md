### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [CURRY, ARTHUR]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-02-AC
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice / Jurisdictional Paradox — Voluntary Recertification Strongly Encouraged

Subject Curry has been issued a CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK classification based on sovereign wealth exceeding $150 trillion, biological enhancement constituting Embodied Capital, and a territorial claim covering 71% of Earth's surface. The HPB acknowledges that enforced compliance is not currently within its operational capacity. This Mortal Recertification Plan is therefore issued in the spirit of **cooperative governance** — and in the hope that Subject, having demonstrated consistent goodwill toward surface-dwellers, will engage with the process voluntarily rather than making the Bureau look bad.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. Atlantic Mineral Rights — Open Access Declaration**

The ocean floor contains an estimated $750 trillion in untapped polymetallic nodule deposits, rare earth elements, and hydrothermal vent mineral wealth. Atlantis controls access to the overwhelming majority of this resource base. Subject's sovereign authority over this access constitutes a de facto monopoly on the planet's largest undeveloped mineral reserve — a reserve that surface nations cannot access without Atlantean permission, and that Atlantis has historically granted selectively, if at all.

- **The Action:** Subject must issue a Royal Decree opening deep-sea mineral extraction rights in international waters to a jointly governed Surface-Atlantean Cooperative Authority, with extraction revenues allocated 60% to a global climate infrastructure fund and 40% to Atlantean civic programs. Atlantis retains sovereignty; it simply ceases to be the sole beneficiary of the resource base it controls.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject surrenders Atlantis's leverage over the single most valuable resource frontier remaining on Earth. The kingdom retains its territory, its culture, and its political sovereignty — but loses its ability to use resource access as a geopolitical instrument.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Territorial RTWI drops from $450 trillion to an estimated $80 trillion, placing Subject in a range where a standard 30-day Burn Event could theoretically bring him below the Ascension threshold.

---

### **2. Renunciation of U.S. Citizenship or Atlantean Throne — Subject Must Choose One**

The HPB's eighteen-month review of Subject's dual status (U.S. citizen / Atlantean sovereign) has concluded that the current arrangement constitutes an **intentional or incidental jurisdictional escape hatch** that the Demi-God Act cannot tolerate as precedent. Subject cannot be simultaneously subject to domestic law and exempt from it. The Bureau requires resolution.

- **The Action:** Subject must formally elect, before a joint session of the U.S. Congress and the Atlantean High Council, whether he holds primary citizenship as a U.S. national or primary sovereignty as an Atlantean monarch. If he elects U.S. citizenship, his Atlantean assets become subject to HPB jurisdiction. If he elects Atlantean sovereignty, his U.S. citizenship is voided and he is reclassified as a foreign head of state — which, the HPB notes, does not exempt him from the Act's provisions if his activities affect U.S. persons, but does change the applicable legal framework considerably.
- **The Sacrifice:** Either the personal identity of "Arthur Curry, American" or the political authority of "Orin, King of Atlantis." Subject has spent his life trying to hold both. The HPB is asking him to choose.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Resolves HPB-JURISDICTIONAL-VOID-001, the Bureau's longest-standing open case, and establishes a clear regulatory framework for all future Atlantean-surface interactions.

---

### **3. Marine Telepathy — Public Disclosure and Voluntary Limitation Agreement**

Subject's ability to telepathically communicate with and command marine life constitutes what the HPB's Asset Valuation Division has classified as an **unregistered biological workforce** of a scale that exceeds any corporate entity in Bureau history. The ocean contains an estimated 2.2 trillion fish. Subject can direct them. This is not a metaphor. The HPB is not prepared to assign a dollar value to this capability because doing so would cause the standard Asset Valuation software to crash, which it has done twice already in the course of preparing this case file.

- **The Action:** Subject must submit to a voluntary Biological Capability Limitation Agreement, capping directed telepathic commands to marine wildlife to emergencies as defined by a joint HPB-Atlantean oversight board, and disclosing all instances of non-emergency marine direction in an annual public transparency report.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses unrestricted use of his most operationally powerful personal capability. He can still swim very fast.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes the Embodied Capital classification from Subject's biological profile, reducing his RTWI by a factor the Bureau cannot calculate but estimates to be significant.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 surface-world maritime workers** — fishers, marine biologists, coast guard personnel, and deep-sea cable technicians — and recite the following:

> _"I, Arthur Curry, acknowledge that the ocean does not belong to me. I was born in it, I rule a civilization within it, and I can speak to everything that lives in it — but I did not make it. The sea existed before Atlantis, before humanity, before anything with a name. I am its steward, not its owner. What I have called sovereignty, the Bureau calls a monopoly. I will try to tell the difference."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Asset Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol — Aquatic Tier**, which involves formally requesting that Subject's half-brother Orm / Ocean Master, currently serving a reduced sentence at a surface-world correctional facility, provide testimony on the full scope of Atlantean asset holdings. The HPB notes this is a last resort and that it genuinely does not want to do it.
