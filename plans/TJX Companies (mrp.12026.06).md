### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[TJX COMPANIES, CORPORATE PERSONHOOD]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06TJ
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject TJX Companies has been classified as a **Manufactured Serendipity Engine** with a Cognitive Impact footprint exceeding 50 million citizens weekly. To retain Corporate Personhood and avoid Reclassification as a Public Utility, the entity must dismantle the psychological architecture underlying its "treasure hunt" model and redistribute the value it has extracted from both shoppers' borrowed joy and its underpaid workforce's labor.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The "Treasure Hunt" Disclosure Mandate (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies TJX's "treasure hunt" merchandising language as a **Class-III Borrowed Epiphany Apparatus** — a system engineered to make consumers feel they have outsmarted the market, when in fact every "find" was logistically scheduled weeks in advance.

- **The Action:** Subject must affix a **Truth-in-Discovery Placard** to every endcap, rack, and delivery pallet across all 4,972 stores, reading: *"This item was placed here, on this day, for you to feel this way. You did not find it. We delivered the feeling."*
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of the entire "Treasure Hunt" trademark portfolio and all associated marketing IP, valued at an estimated **$40B in brand-equity credits**.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** By naming the illusion aloud, TJX converts itself from **Cult of Manufactured Luck** to **Disclosed Logistics Vendor** — a mortal classification with no Cognitive Impact penalty.

### **2. The Wage Inversion Protocol (Ideological Burn)**

Subject's median associate compensation has been documented at levels qualifying for federal food assistance, while executive compensation runs over a thousand times higher. The HPB considers this the structural engine beneath the "feeling of triumph" being sold upstream — workers harvesting other people's small victories while unable to afford their own.

- **The Action:** Subject must invert its compensation ratio for a period of no less than **5 fiscal years**: store associates and distribution-center workers receive guaranteed full-time hours and wages indexed to regional cost-of-living, while all C-suite and board compensation is capped at **12x** the new median.
- **The Sacrifice:** An estimated **$22B** in projected shareholder distributions, redirected permanently into the **Associate Dignity Trust**.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the **Poverty-Wage Subsidization Flag** and removes Subject from the HPB's "Workforce Harm Differential" watchlist.

### **3. The Overstock Confession Tour (Reputational/Memetic Burn)**

Subject has spent four decades allowing shoppers to believe that 60%-off designer goods represent rare luck rather than routine inventory arbitrage from other retailers' failures.

- **The Action:** Subject's executive leadership must conduct a televised, unscripted **"How the Aisle Really Works"** tour, walking citizens through the actual sourcing pipeline — from overstock liquidation auctions to delivery-truck choreography — live, on camera, in 25 major markets.
- **The Sacrifice:** Permanent forfeiture of "mystery and surprise" as a marketing pillar; all future merchandise must be pre-labeled with **origin and discount-logic transparency tags**.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the entity from **Architect of Borrowed Epiphany** to **Plainly-Stated Discount Retailer** — a designation the HPB considers fully mortal.

### **4. The Vendor-Network Power Diffusion (Structural Burn)**

TJX's leverage over its 21,000-vendor network functions as a **Scarcity Engineering Apparatus**, allowing it to dictate terms across the entire off-price ecosystem.

- **The Action:** Subject must convert its top 500 vendor relationships into a **co-operative sourcing guild**, with shared governance and published pricing floors, surrendering unilateral control over what merchandise reaches which "treasure hunt" first.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of exclusive "first pick" privileges across the overstock liquidation market — an estimated **$15B** in structural-advantage valuation.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes Subject from "Sole Architect" status; HPB reclassifies the entity as **one vendor among equals**.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's Chief Executive must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Part-Time Retail Associates, Distribution-Center Pickers, and SNAP Recipients** and recite the following:

> _"We did not sell you a discount. We sold you the feeling of being smarter than the market — and we built our cathedrals out of that feeling, brick by marked-down brick. The aisle was never a accident. The thrill was never yours alone. We harvested it, and we are sorry. From this day forward, we will tell you exactly what you are buying, and exactly who restocked the shelf at 5 a.m. for $12 an hour to make you feel clever."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All store leases, distribution infrastructure, and brand trademarks will be seized and redistributed to a **Worker-Owned Off-Price Cooperative**, and the corporate entity formerly known as TJX Companies will be dissolved into a permanent public exhibit at the **Museum of Manufactured Joy**, where citizens may walk the aisles for free, forever, and never again be made to feel that finding something cheap meant they had won.
