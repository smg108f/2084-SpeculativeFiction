---

### **1. The Electoral Investment Cap Burn**

The subject has contributed $100M+ to a single presidential campaign, making her one of the largest single-donor electoral investors in U.S. history. The HPB classifies unlimited electoral political donation by a demi-god-threshold individual as a **Democracy Bypass Mechanism** — a private purchase of public governmental power.

- **The Action:** Subject voluntarily pledges — and establishes legal mechanisms to enforce — a lifetime cap of $5M on all future electoral political contributions in any jurisdiction. Existing contributions are grandfathered; future electoral spending is capped at a level the HPB considers within the bounds of vigorous citizenship rather than sovereignty purchase.
- **The Sacrifice:** The ability to single-handedly finance presidential campaigns. The ability to functionally determine the outcome of elections through checkbook.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts electoral sovereignty into electoral participation. HPB reclassifies from CRITICAL to MARGINAL ASCENSION RISK on Political Agency metric.

### **2. The Israel Hayom Governance Burn**

Subject owns Israel Hayom, the largest-circulation daily newspaper in Israel, distributed free of charge, which critics and researchers have documented as functioning as a political advocacy vehicle for specific Israeli political positions. A private foreign national owning the dominant print media outlet in a foreign democratic country is a **Cross-Border Cognitive Sovereignty** violation.

- **The Action:** Transfer ownership of Israel Hayom to an independently governed Israeli media trust, with editorial independence guaranteed by charter and no shareholder influence over editorial decisions. Subject may maintain a minority economic stake of up to 10%.
- **The Sacrifice:** Ownership of the most-read newspaper in Israel.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a privately controlled political media asset into an editorially independent public information institution.

### **3. The Casino Harm Accountability Burn**

Las Vegas Sands' business model systematically extracts value from human psychological vulnerability to gambling. The company has faced problem gambling criticism across its markets. A subject whose political influence is founded on a problem-gambling revenue stream has an additional HPB obligation.

- **The Action:** Establish a $300M **Global Problem Gambling Recovery Fund**, administered independently of Las Vegas Sands, with programs in each jurisdiction where Sands operates, focused on treatment, prevention, and economic recovery for individuals harmed by problem gambling.
- **The Sacrifice:** $300M and public acknowledgment that the casino industry profits from addiction.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Connects the source of wealth to the harm it causes and funds remedy.
