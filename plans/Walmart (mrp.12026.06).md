### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [WALMART INC.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-16WM
**Status:** Critical — Commercial Monopsony by Attrition / Employer Sovereignty

Walmart's RTWI of ~$936B places it at **936 times the Demi-God threshold**, driven by retail dominance at civilizational scale and a workforce of 2.3 million Americans whose wages and conditions are set by an entity with no meaningful labor market competition in thousands of U.S. counties. The Walton family's individual audits address dynastic wealth concentration; this audit addresses the corporation's structural employment and commercial power.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Living Wage Mandate**

Walmart employs 2.3 million Americans. The difference between Walmart's current minimum wage (~$14/hour) and a living wage in the markets where Walmart operates represents the largest single employer wage gap in the American economy. The Bureau classifies this gap as an **Employer Monopsony Premium**: the additional profit generated by paying below living wage to workers who cannot easily find equivalent employment elsewhere because Walmart is the primary employer in their community.

- **The Action:** Walmart must implement a **Regional Living Wage Standard** — all U.S. Walmart and Sam's Club employees receive a minimum hourly wage calculated as the MIT Living Wage for a single adult in their county of employment, updated annually. The Bureau estimates this results in a national floor of approximately $18–$22/hour depending on region. Full implementation within 18 months, with a 6-month transition schedule for the highest-cost markets.
- **The Sacrifice:** Walmart's labor cost increases by an estimated $8–15B annually. At $700B+ in revenue and $936B in market cap, this is a meaningful but not existential impact on profitability.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Resolves the **Employer Monopsony Premium** classification. The largest private employer in America pays wages that allow its workers to live in the communities where they work.

---

### **2. The Last-Store-Standing Community Reinvestment Obligation**

In communities where Walmart is the sole remaining general merchandise or grocery retailer, the entity has a structural obligation that market competition would otherwise impose. When there are no alternatives, the alternative is to impose obligations.

- **The Action:** Walmart must establish a **Community Commercial Resilience Fund** — in any county where Walmart holds 60%+ of general merchandise retail market share and no independent grocer or general retailer of comparable product range operates, Walmart must: (a) maintain open hours, product range, and pricing consistent with the community's needs for a minimum of 5 years before any store closure; (b) contribute 1% of annual store revenue to a fund supporting cooperative grocery and retail development in that community; and (c) provide 12 months' notice of any planned closure, with transition assistance for affected workers.
- **The Sacrifice:** Walmart loses the option to close stores in dependent communities on its own timeline without community impact mitigation. The entity that has become the town's store accepts the obligations of the town's store.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Addresses the **Commercial Monopsony by Attrition** classification in the specific communities where it is most acute.

---

### **3. The Worker Ownership Pathway**

The Walton family holds ~45–50% of Walmart equity, generating extraordinary dynastic wealth from the labor of 2.3 million workers. The Bureau has addressed the individual Walton family members' concentration separately. For the corporate entity, the Bureau requires a structural pathway toward worker ownership.

- **The Action:** Walmart must establish a **Worker Equity Program** — beginning in 12027, 2% of annual operating profit is directed to a Worker Ownership Trust that distributes equity to all full-time employees based on years of service. The Trust grows at 2% of annual profit annually, with a target of 10% worker ownership within 15 years. The Trust is governed by an elected worker board and distributes dividends proportionally to worker-owners.
- **The Sacrifice:** 2% of annual operating profit — roughly $600M–$800M — is redirected from general shareholder returns to worker equity annually. The Walton family's ownership stake is diluted at a rate of 2% of profit per year.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Begins to structurally address the **Dynastic Accumulation** at the corporate level. The people who stock the shelves acquire a stake in the shelves they stock.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Walmart's CEO and Board must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Walmart Cashiers Working Their Third Job to Afford Rent, Mayors of Towns Where Walmart Is the Last Commercial Employer, and Former Small Business Owners Who Closed Because Walmart Opened** and recite the following:

> _"Sam Walton said 'the secret of successful retailing is to give your customers what they want.' We gave them low prices. We gave them low prices until the competition was gone, and then we gave them low prices and low wages and the only job in town. The living wage is not charity. It is the acknowledgment that a company with our market cap and our revenue has been paying a wage calibrated to what employees would accept, not what they would need. The store in the town with no other store carries obligations. We accept them."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. The Living Wage mandate will be enacted as a federal minimum wage modification applying to all employers with more than 500,000 U.S. employees — effectively a Walmart-specific law. The Community Resilience Fund will be constituted by order of the FTC as a condition of Walmart's compliance with the Digital Markets Act equivalent. The Worker Equity Program will be mandated by executive order. The Walton family members will be notified that their individual audits (HPB cases WB-12026-RW, WB-12026-JW, WB-12026-AW) will be accelerated to Pre-Seizure status if the corporate entity's non-compliance continues. The Bureau has high confidence that this last notification will be the most effective.
