### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [BOLKIAH, HASSANAL]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06HB
**Status:** INTERNATIONAL REGISTRY REFERRAL — Sovereign Subject

---

**NOTICE OF MODIFIED PROCESSING**

Subject is a reigning absolute monarch. The Human Protection Bureau's standard Mortal Recertification Protocol is not enforceable against sitting sovereigns under the Demi-God Act of 2034 Section 18(c) (Sovereign Carve-Out). However, Section 18(d) requires the Bureau to file a Modified Plan for International Registry purposes, documenting what recertification would require if enforcement were possible.

This plan is filed for the International Demi-God Registry and the United Nations Special Committee on Demi-God Sovereign Entities. It is not enforceable. It is aspirational. The Bureau files it anyway.

---

### **1. The Car Redistribution Burn (Aspirational)**

Subject owns an estimated 7,000 cars. This is approximately 15 cars per 1,000 Brunei citizens. The HPB notes that 7,000 cars constitute the world's largest private automotive collection and represent a concentration of personal luxury good ownership that exceeds the Bureau's available metrics.

- **The Action:** Subject to donate 6,500 cars to a publicly accessible Brunei National Automotive Heritage Museum, retaining 500 for personal use. Museum to be free of charge to all Brunei residents and international visitors.
- **The Sacrifice:** 6,500 cars.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts largest private car collection on Earth from personal hoarding to public heritage institution. The HPB is aware that 500 cars is still a lot of cars. This is the aspirational version.

### **2. The Democratic Transition Burn (Aspirational)**

Brunei has been ruled by absolute monarchy since independence. At 77, the Sultan has no constitutional succession mechanism beyond dynastic inheritance. The HPB asks for a roadmap toward constitutional monarchy with an elected parliament.

- **The Action:** Establish a Constitutional Reform Commission with 50% composition of elected civil society representatives, tasked with drafting a constitution establishing constitutional monarchy with parliamentary governance, to be implemented by 2030.
- **The Sacrifice:** 57 years of absolute rule; the certainty of unchallenged authority.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The most significant Burn available to any sovereign subject. Converts the last absolute sultanate in the Asia-Pacific region into a constitutional democracy.

### **3. The Sharia Criminal Penalties Suspension Burn (Aspirational)**

The 2019 implementation of Sharia criminal law, including stoning as a prescribed penalty for homosexuality and apostasy, was widely condemned by international human rights bodies and triggered a global celebrity boycott of Brunei-owned hotels. Subject subsequently indicated the penalties would not be enforced; no formal legal revision has occurred.

- **The Action:** Subject to formally suspend the Sharia criminal penalty provisions — stoning, amputation, and related prescribed punishments — pending constitutional reform. This is suspension, not repeal, which the HPB acknowledges as politically achievable relative to full legal reversal.
- **The Sacrifice:** The legal architecture of the 2019 implementation.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes the international human rights violation from active legal status. Returns Brunei to international standing with ASEAN human rights frameworks.
