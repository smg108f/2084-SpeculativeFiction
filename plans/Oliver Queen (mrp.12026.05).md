### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [QUEEN, OLIVER]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-08-OQ
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification / Structural Assistance Required

Subject Queen has been classified MARGINAL ASCENSION RISK — a designation that reflects his documented genuine commitment to wealth redistribution and his multiple prior divestiture attempts. This plan differs from standard recertification plans in one important respect: the HPB believes Subject wants to comply. The Bureau's role here is not to compel action but to provide the structural scaffolding that Subject's previous divestiture attempts have lacked. He has tried to give it away six times. This time, the Bureau is helping him do it in a way that holds.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. Queen Industries — Irrevocable Worker-Ownership Transfer**

Subject's previous divestiture attempts have failed for a consistent structural reason: he has transferred operational control without transferring ownership in a legally irrevocable form, allowing reconsolidation when circumstances changed. This plan requires an irrevocable transfer mechanism that cannot be reversed by market events, corporate governance crises, or Subject's own subsequent decisions — including decisions he will make during future periods of financial distress, which the HPB's actuarial models suggest are statistically likely given Subject's history.

- **The Action:** Subject must execute an irrevocable transfer of 70% of his Queen Industries equity stake to an Employee Stock Ownership Plan (ESOP) structured with an independent trustee and legally binding restrictions preventing any single party — including Subject — from acquiring more than 5% of shares without HPB approval. The remaining 30% is transferred to the Queen Foundation (see Burn #2). Subject retains no equity stake.
- **The Sacrifice:** This is a permanent transfer. There is no mechanism for reconsolidation. When Subject loses Queen Industries this time, he does not get it back. The Bureau notes that Subject has stated, on multiple occasions, that this is what he wants. The Bureau is taking him at his word and structuring it so that his word is binding.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** RTWI drops from $7 trillion to $0 (equity). Subject's remaining wealth is tied to the Queen Foundation endowment and his personal liquid assets, which the Bureau estimates at approximately $400 million — below the Ascension threshold.

---

### **2. Queen Foundation — Community Endowment with Irrevocable Charter**

The Queen Foundation has existed in various forms across Subject's multiple wealth cycles, serving as his primary philanthropic vehicle and, not coincidentally, a reconsolidation mechanism — assets transferred to the Foundation have, in two prior cycles, been retrieved by Subject during periods of corporate crisis. This plan structures the Foundation in a form that prevents retrieval.

- **The Action:** The Queen Foundation is restructured with an irrevocable charter prohibiting the return of assets to any Queen family member under any circumstances. The endowment is set at $2 trillion (the 30% equity transfer from Burn #1 plus Subject's remaining liquid holdings). The Foundation's board is restructured to give majority control to representatives of the Star City communities the Foundation serves, with particular emphasis on the Glades neighborhood that Subject has prioritized in his philanthropy.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses his last financial exit ramp. The Foundation cannot be raided. The money is gone in the most legally durable sense the HPB's attorneys can construct.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Completes the divestiture that Subject has been attempting for two decades. The HPB formally acknowledges this as the longest-running Voluntary Recertification process in Bureau history.

---

### **3. Green Arrow Operation — Registered Nonprofit Status**

Subject's vigilante operation must be brought into a legal framework — not because the Bureau requires vigilantes to register (it does not, and the relevant legislation remains contested), but because the continued funding of the Green Arrow operation through Queen Industries resources represents an ongoing governance violation at a company that is about to become employee-owned. The new ESOP-governed Queen Industries cannot be asked to fund its former owner's personal activities without disclosure and consent.

- **The Action:** Subject establishes the Star City Public Safety Initiative as a registered nonprofit, funded by a $50 million annual endowment from the Queen Foundation, with full public disclosure of operations, expenditures, and outcomes. The Green Arrow's activities become, for the first time, subject to a public accountability framework — not one that requires unmasking Subject, but one that documents what the operation does, how much it costs, and what outcomes it produces.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject accepts public accountability for a project he has operated in personal shadow for years. The Green Arrow stops being one man's private war and becomes a community institution with a budget and a board.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Covert Operations Subsidy classification removed. Subject's personal RTWI reaches $0 in equity. Estimated personal liquid assets of ~$400 million remain — below the threshold by a comfortable margin.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 residents of the Glades, Star City** — the neighborhood most devastated by the Undertaking and most directly served by Subject's subsequent philanthropic efforts — and recite the following:

> _"I spent years fighting for the city with a bow and calling it justice. I spent years trying to give away a company and calling it politics. I did both badly because I was still the one holding the bow and signing the checks. Justice that depends on one man's aim isn't justice — it's charity with arrows. What I'm signing today I can't take back. That's the point. I've been trying to let go for twenty years. The Bureau helped me build a knot I can't untie. Thank you. I mean that."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** In the event Subject does not execute the irrevocable transfer documents within 30 days, the HPB will activate standard CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK enforcement procedures — which, the Bureau notes, would result in a forced divestiture with less favorable terms than the voluntary plan. The HPB strongly prefers the voluntary path. Subject, based on his stated values and two decades of attempted compliance, almost certainly does too. The Bureau is confident he will sign.
