### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [WÜRTH, REINHOLD]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06RW
**Status:** Precautionary

Subject is classified MARGINAL and is 89 years old with company already in family foundation structure. The HPB requires minimal additional action beyond what is already in place. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days, with full accommodation for subject's age and existing philanthropic commitments.

---

### **1. The Foundation Succession Burn**

The Würth family foundation structure is an appropriate vehicle for managing industrial wealth at this scale. The HPB's concern is succession clarity: at 89, the question of how the foundation's governance transitions to the next generation — and whether that transition preserves the public cultural mission of the art collection — requires formal documentation.

- **The Action:** Publish a documented Foundation Succession Charter specifying governance transition terms, art collection public access guarantees, and employee welfare protections, to remain binding on family heirs regardless of operational changes. Charter to be independently reviewed and filed with the German Foundation Authority.
- **The Sacrifice:** Family flexibility in post-succession governance.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the admirable existing structure into a legally binding permanent commitment, not dependent on Reinhold's personal values persisting in successors.

### **2. The Workforce Acknowledgment Burn**

Würth's 85,000 employees represent one of the largest workforces in German industry. The company's success is built on a genuine sales culture, but also on the specific labor conditions of industrial distribution workers — warehouse staff, delivery drivers, field sales representatives — many of whom work physically demanding roles.

- **The Action:** Establish a **Würth Employee Legacy Fund** with a €300M endowment, dedicated to long-term Würth employee welfare: retirement supplementation for long-tenure workers, skills transition support, and healthcare. Structured as independently governed and accessible to all employees with more than 10 years of service.
- **The Sacrifice:** €300M — modest relative to the $29B valuation.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the sales culture legend into a visible commitment to the workers who made it real.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Würth Assembly Workers, Long-Tenure Sales Representatives, and Würth Museum Visitors** and recite the following:

> *"My father died when I was 19. He left me a two-person screw business. I did not know what a screw was worth. Now I know: everything that is held together. I have 85,000 people who hold it together with me. I am 89. I have written down what happens next. The art stays public. The employees are provided for. The screws will continue to be sold. Something will be held together after I am gone."*

Subject will be relocated to a Sovereign Flotilla designated **FSS FASTENER**, held together entirely with Würth-brand screws, where he will continue to personally call on customers as he has done for 70 years.
