### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [KIM, MICHAEL]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06MK2
**Status:** Urgent

Subject built MBK Partners through leveraged buyouts that generated substantial personal returns and documented worker harm, particularly at Homeplus. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Homeplus Worker Remediation Burn**

Homeplus workers — particularly older women in part-time retail positions — experienced job losses during MBK's ownership. These were not MBK's only workforce impacts in Korea but they are the most documented.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish the **MBK Worker Legacy Fund** — $200M — specifically for workers in companies previously owned by MBK Partners who experienced job loss or wage reduction during MBK's ownership period (Homeplus, and other portfolio companies as documented by independent audit): (a) severance supplementation for workers whose packages did not meet the HPB's minimum adequacy threshold; (b) retraining and job placement for former Homeplus and MBK portfolio company workers; (c) pension gap supplement for older workers (particularly Homeplus employees over 50) whose retirement planning was disrupted by job loss.
- **The Sacrifice:** $200M.

### **2. The Asian Business Ecosystem Burn**

MBK Partners' LBO model has extracted value from Korean, Japanese, and Chinese businesses that were built by local entrepreneurs and local workers. The HPB requires proportionate reinvestment.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund $150M for the **MBK Northeast Asia Entrepreneurship Fund** — supporting first-generation entrepreneurs in Korea, Japan, and China who cannot access private equity-scale capital without an LBO structure: (a) patient capital at below-market terms for Korean SME businesses with genuine growth potential; (b) Japanese manufacturing SME succession programs (Japan faces a significant SME succession crisis as founders retire); (c) Chinese social enterprise funding.
- **The Sacrifice:** $150M.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Former Homeplus Workers, Korean Small Business Owners, and MBK Portfolio Company Employees**:

> *"I built MBK Partners on the principle that Northeast Asian markets had undervalued businesses that could be improved with patient capital and operational discipline. That is true. It is also true that Homeplus workers lost jobs during our ownership. The Worker Legacy Fund addresses those workers specifically — it does not pretend the restructuring didn't happen. The Entrepreneurship Fund is the other side of what private equity can be: not extraction, but growth. I came from Korea. I made a fortune buying and restructuring Korean companies. This is what I owe back."*
