### **1. The Plastic Transition Burn**

Reynolds and Pactiv Evergreen produce billions of units of single-use plastic and foam annually. The HPB requires a binding transition commitment.

- **The Action:** Subject must: (a) commit Reynolds Consumer Products and Pactiv Evergreen to a **12033 Single-Use Plastic Reduction Plan** — binding 50% reduction in single-use plastic by volume by 12033, with annual published progress reports; (b) fund $250M for the **Hart Packaging Transition Fund**: development of compostable, recyclable, or reusable alternatives to current single-use products — specifically targeting foam (non-recyclable), LDPE bags, and disposable cups; (c) fund $100M for **Plastic Waste Remediation** — directed to ocean plastic cleanup and community recycling infrastructure in markets where Hart's products are predominantly sold.
- **The Sacrifice:** $350M + binding business transformation.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund $75M for the **Rank New Zealand Foundation** — focused on Māori economic development, conservation of New Zealand's unique ecosystems, and affordable housing in Auckland (where packaging workers and support staff are concentrated).
- **The Sacrifice:** $75M.
