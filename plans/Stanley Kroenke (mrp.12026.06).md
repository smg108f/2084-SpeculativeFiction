### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [KROENKE, STANLEY]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06SK2
**Status:** Urgent

Subject controls six major professional sports franchises across NFL, Premier League, NBA, NHL, MLB, and MLS. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The St. Louis Community Remediation Burn**

The $790M settlement with the City of St. Louis for the Rams relocation compensated institutional parties. It did not compensate St. Louis fans, the broader St. Louis community, or the workers and businesses affected by the departure of an NFL franchise.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish the **St. Louis Community Legacy Fund** — $200M — independently administered by a board including St. Louis community representatives (not Kroenke or NFL affiliates) — dedicated to: (a) St. Louis sports and recreation infrastructure for underserved communities; (b) economic development grants in the neighborhoods most affected by the Rams departure; (c) funding a St. Louis youth football program that provides free access to hundreds of low-income youth annually. This is separate from and additional to the $790M settlement.
- **The Sacrifice:** $200M.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The community that lost the Rams receives a documented, independently administered community legacy investment.

### **2. The Arsenal Fan Governance Burn**

Arsenal's participation in the European Super League proposal — which would have removed promotion/relegation from English football — was withdrawn only after massive fan protest. The HPB requires structural fan governance reform.

- **The Action:** Subject must direct Arsenal FC — through his controlling shareholder position — to establish a **Arsenal Fan Trust Governance Structure**: (a) a Fan Advisory Board with genuine powers: the ability to delay (by 90 days) any decision to join a closed competition, alter the club's league membership, or relocate; (b) the Fan Advisory Board must be elected by verified season ticket holders and members, not appointed by Kroenke; (c) a £50M (~$64M) **Arsenal Community Foundation Endowment** — dedicated to north London community programs, youth football, and fan affordability (ticket price ceilings for the most affordable seat categories).
- **The Sacrifice:** £50M + structural reduction in owner's unilateral authority at Arsenal.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The club that tried the Super League is the first Premier League club with genuine fan-elected governance powers.

### **3. The Kroenke Sports Foundation Burn**

Six franchises. Minimal documented philanthropy.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Kroenke Sports and Entertainment Foundation** — $100M — providing: (a) youth sports access grants in every city where Kroenke owns a franchise (Los Angeles, Denver, Aurora-Colorado Springs, and London); (b) community athletic facility grants for underserved schools.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject's sports empire has a documented community footprint.
