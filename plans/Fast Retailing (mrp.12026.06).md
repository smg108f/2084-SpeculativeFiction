### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [FAST RETAILING]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06FR
**Status:** Urgent — Dual Audit (Organization + Founder Individual Track)

Fast Retailing's Marginal Ascension Risk classification, combined with founder Tadashi Yanai's independent breach of the $1B personal threshold, requires simultaneous organizational and individual remediation. The Labor Integrity Order issued under this audit must be resolved before any succession planning proceeds.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Indonesian Severance Payment (The Labor Debt Discharge)**

The HPB classifies unpaid severance owed to garment workers as a **Specific Quantified Moral Liability** — a debt that is not abstract or disputed but documented, named, and owed. The $5.5M owed to Indonesian workers represents money already earned by human beings who are waiting for it. The HPB has limited tolerance for supply chain complexity as an explanation for specific, audited, owed wages.

- **The Action:** Fast Retailing must discharge the full $5.5M in outstanding Indonesian worker severance within 30 days, with an additional 15% interest penalty payable to a **Supply Chain Worker Justice Fund**, for the period of delay.
- **The Sacrifice:** $6.3M in direct cash outlay — a rounding error on the company's $24B revenue, which is precisely what makes the delay inexcusable.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removal from the HPB's **Specific Labor Debt Registry** — a public database of organizations with documented, unresolved wage theft. The HPB notes that no amount of brand messaging about democratic fashion offsets an entry in this registry.

### **2. The Living Wage Commitment (The Supply Chain Accountability Burn)**

Fast Retailing's supply chain operates on labor cost arbitrage as a structural feature, not an oversight. The HPB requires that this model either be acknowledged and reformed, or defended publicly before a citizen panel.

- **The Action:** Fast Retailing must publish a binding **Global Living Wage Roadmap** covering all Tier 1 and Tier 2 suppliers, with independently verified wage floor increases of at least 25% above local minimum wage by 2029. Progress is reported annually to the HPB's **Worker Dignity Registry** and is publicly accessible.
- **The Sacrifice:** Estimated $800M–$1.5B in annual supply chain cost increases; potential reduction in gross margins; competitive disadvantage versus rivals who maintain labor arbitrage.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Reclassification from "Labor Arbitrage Operator" to "Transitioning Ethical Manufacturer" — a status that generates significant Whuffie credit and may offset Yanai's individual audit penalties.

### **3. The Dynastic Succession Freeze (The Inheritance Block)**

Yanai's stated 10-year succession plan involves transferring control of Japan's most valuable fashion company — and one of its largest personal wealth concentrations — to the next generation of the Yanai family. The HPB classifies this as a planned **Demi-God Wealth Transfer** and will not permit it to proceed until Yanai's individual audit is resolved.

- **The Action:** All succession planning documents must be filed with the HPB. No transfer of voting control may proceed without HPB approval. Any succession structure that preserves family voting control above 40% will trigger a mandatory Potentiality Dilution Review.
- **The Sacrifice:** Yanai loses unilateral control over succession timing and structure. The next generation cannot inherit a Demi-God estate.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The HPB will issue a **Succession Compliance Certificate** upon review — which, despite its bureaucratic sound, is worth considerable commercial goodwill in a regulatory environment where inheritance of concentrated wealth is treated as a national security event.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Garment Workers from Indonesia, Bangladesh, and Vietnam** and recite the following:

> *"The clothes are made by people. The people have names. The people have children. The affordable price you pay in the store is subsidized by a wage I set below what a person needs to live. I am not selling democracy. I am selling my version of it. I will change the version. Starting with the severance I owe, and ending with a wage that does not require anyone to choose between food and rent."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the Apotheosis Protocol. All Fast Retailing retail locations will be required to display, alongside their price tags, the estimated hourly wage of the worker who made each garment. The succession freeze will become permanent until Yanai personally appears before a Citizen Audit Panel. The HPB considers this a proportionate response and is prepared to enforce it.
