### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [AX:SON JOHNSON, ANTONIA]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06AAJ
**Status:** Standard

Subject has stewarded a 150-year-old Swedish conglomerate responsibly. Burns address the philanthropy scale gap and the retail workforce dimension.

---

### **1. The Axel Johnson Worker Welfare Burn**

The Axel Johnson Group employs tens of thousands of workers across its retail, service, and distribution businesses. Retail and service sector workers are typically in the lower wage tiers.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish the **Axel Johnson Worker Investment Fund** — SEK 1B (~$100M) — dedicated to: (a) reaching verified living wage for all Axel Johnson Group employees across all Swedish operations (Åhléns, KICKS, Martin & Servera, and others); (b) a permanent **Axel Johnson Employee Development Program** — funded training and career advancement for retail and service workers who want to progress into management, specialist, or technical roles; (c) an **Axel Johnson Women in Leadership Initiative** — formalizing and expanding the family's existing tradition of women's leadership into the broader group workforce, funding mentorship and advancement programs.
- **The Sacrifice:** ~$100M.

### **2. The Foundation Scaling Burn**

The family foundation is genuine but below scale. The HPB asks for a permanent endowment at proportionate level.

- **The Action:** Subject must permanently endow the Axel Johnson family foundation at SEK 500M (~$50M), expanding its mandate from primarily Swedish cultural philanthropy to include: (a) Swedish social welfare — specifically programs for women and children in Sweden who are not served by the welfare state; (b) sustainable retail and food systems research — complementing Axel Johnson Group's position across retail and food service.
- **The Sacrifice:** ~$50M.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Åhléns Retail Workers, KICKS Beauty Consultants, and Swedish Social Welfare Researchers**:

> *"My family has run this group for 150 years. My great-great-grandfather started with oil trading. We grew into everything you see. Four generations of women have led this company — not because it was required but because it was right. The Worker Investment Fund formalizes what the company has always tried to do: treat the people who work for us well enough that they want to stay. The foundation endowment scales what the family has always believed: that a company operating in Swedish society owes something to that society beyond employment. 150 years is a long time to learn that lesson."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** Subject will be assigned to **FSS AXEL JOHNSON** — a vessel operating on the same model as the Axel Johnson Group itself: diverse, practical, long-term, and Nordic. The ship runs a department store in the forward hold, a pest control research station amidships, an IT distribution hub aft, and a cosmetics counter on the main deck, crewed entirely by Axel Johnson Group employees on rotating secondment, with the entire operation managed by a team of women at every level of the command structure, demonstrating what 150 years of careful stewardship looks like at sea.
