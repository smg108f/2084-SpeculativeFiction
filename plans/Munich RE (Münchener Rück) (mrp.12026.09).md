### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [MUNICH RE]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09MUV
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Munich Re's Critical classification reflects its dual role as public climate-risk authority and profitable pricer of that same risk. Recertification targets separating these functions.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Model Transparency Mandate (The Largest Burn)**

- **The Action:** Publish full, independently-reviewable methodology behind proprietary catastrophe and climate-risk models used to determine regional insurability and pricing.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of proprietary modeling advantage that currently underwrites premium pricing power.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a privately-held climate authority into a transparently accountable one.

### **2. The Uninsurability Impact Fund**

- **The Action:** Direct a defined percentage of reinsurance profit toward a fund supporting climate adaptation infrastructure in regions the company's own models have flagged as becoming uninsurable.
- **The Sacrifice:** Estimated several hundred million dollars annually redirected from shareholder buybacks.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Ensures profit from pricing climate risk partially funds adaptation for the communities most affected by it.

### **3. The Public Research Independence Firewall**

- **The Action:** Establish an independently governed firewall between Munich Re's public climate-research publications and its proprietary commercial pricing models.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced synergy between research investment and pricing advantage.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Restores public confidence that climate research is not merely commercial pricing intelligence in public-benefit packaging.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Munich Re's board must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Residents of Regions Flagged as Becoming Uninsurable** and recite the following:

> _"We saw the storm coming in our models before you saw it in your neighborhood, and we profited from knowing first. We will now share what we know, and help pay for what you need to survive it."_
