### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [OSBORN, NORMAN]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-31-NO
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice / Undisclosed Military Asset / Documented Harm Record / Enhanced Monitoring Protocols Active

Norman Osborn has been classified CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK. Subject has not responded to the Bureau's preliminary audit notice. The Bureau's Intelligence Division notes that non-response during a quiet period is consistent with Subject's documented pattern and does not indicate the absence of Subject's attention to this proceeding. The Bureau is proceeding on the assumption that Subject is aware of this plan's contents, has evaluated his options, and has concluded that cooperative recertification is currently in his interest. The Bureau finds this likely and is structuring the plan accordingly — with enforcement mechanisms that do not depend on Subject's continued assessment of cooperation as preferable.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. Goblin Formula — Full Disclosure and Regulatory Registration**

Norman Osborn has developed a proprietary human enhancement compound — the Goblin Formula — that has never been disclosed to any regulatory body, never filed as a military technology, and never subjected to the safety and oversight review that any pharmaceutical or military enhancement compound is required to undergo. The Bureau's Chemical Safety Division classifies this as a Category-A compliance failure. The Bureau's Defense Asset Division classifies it as an undisclosed personal military asset. The Bureau's Pharmaceutical Review Division classifies it as an unregulated human enhancement compound with documented psychological side effects. All three classifications require resolution.

- **The Action:** Subject must submit complete Goblin Formula specifications — synthesis process, documented effects, psychological impact data from Subject's personal use, and any variants developed through Oscorp's research infrastructure — to a joint review panel comprising the Bureau's Chemical Safety Division, the Defense Asset Division, and an independent neurological safety expert. Within 30 days of disclosure, the panel will determine: (1) whether the Formula qualifies as a regulated military enhancement compound; (2) what safety protocols apply to any future use; and (3) whether Oscorp's research and development infrastructure has produced additional undisclosed compounds subject to the same disclosure requirement. Subject must also submit to comprehensive medical and psychological evaluation by Bureau-designated physicians.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject discloses the one asset he has most consistently concealed. He does so to a panel that includes the Bureau's Intelligence Division representative, who will have read the complete documented harm record. The Bureau notes that Subject has operated under the assumption that the Goblin Formula's existence, while known, is not formally on record in a regulatory context. The Bureau is changing that.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Goblin Formula moves from undisclosed personal military asset to regulated compound under active monitoring. All future use subject to notification and medical monitoring requirements. The Bureau will know.

---

### **2. Oscorp Military Division — Independent Oversight and Dual-Use Technology Audit**

Oscorp's military contracting division holds relationships and technology conversations that generate strategic access significantly beyond what a $10 billion private company would normally produce. The Bureau's Defense Asset Division requires that these relationships be disclosed, audited, and governed under an independent oversight framework.

- **The Action:** Subject must appoint an Independent Military Technology Monitor — nominated by the Bureau's Defense Asset Division from a list of qualified candidates, and not subject to Osborn approval — with full access to Oscorp's military contracting relationships, active contracts, and technology development pipeline. The Monitor's annual report is filed with the Bureau and with Oscorp's board of directors simultaneously. The Monitor has authority to refer concerns to the Bureau's Defense Asset Division for further action. Subject retains operational authority over Oscorp. Subject does not retain the ability to conceal Oscorp's military activities from the Bureau.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject accepts, for the first time, that someone inside Oscorp reports to the Bureau rather than to him. This is not a minor sacrifice for Subject, whose documented pattern includes using corporate authority to conceal activities from external review. The Monitor is specifically positioned to make that concealment harder.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Oscorp military contracting moves from undisclosed access to governed framework. The Bureau gains ongoing visibility into the sector of Subject's operations most likely to generate harm.

---

### **3. Institutional Access Registry — Pattern Interruption Requirement**

The Bureau's Intelligence Division has documented a consistent pattern of Subject rebuilding institutional trust following exposure events and using that rebuilt trust in subsequent harmful activities. The Bureau cannot prevent Subject from interacting with government bodies, defense contractors, or regulatory agencies — these interactions may be entirely legitimate. The Bureau can require that they be tracked.

- **The Action:** Subject must register all formal interactions with governmental bodies, defense agencies, and regulatory entities with the Bureau's Institutional Access Registry — a non-public disclosure mechanism that creates a contemporaneous record of who Subject is building relationships with and in what context. The Registry does not restrict Subject's interactions. It creates a record the Bureau can consult if Subject's pattern re-activates. Subject must also notify the Bureau within 48 hours of any direct approach to a governmental or defense contracting entity that occurs outside Subject's ordinary Oscorp business relationship — defined as any new relationship or any relationship with an entity Subject has not interacted with in the prior 24 months.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses the opacity that has made institutional access rebuilding effective. The Bureau will know, in real time, which relationships he is developing. The Bureau notes that this requirement's value is entirely in its deterrent effect; if Subject is not planning another pattern cycle, it costs him very little. The Bureau is not assuming he is not planning another pattern cycle.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Institutional access pattern becomes visible to the Bureau in real time. The structural precondition for the pattern's next iteration is substantially more difficult to satisfy covertly.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Citizens** — selected with the specific exclusion of any individual with documented prior contact with Oscorp or its subsidiaries. Subject must recite the following, with no modifications, pauses of more than three seconds, or departures from the written text:

> *"I have harmed people. I have used positions of legitimate authority to accumulate the access required to harm more people, and then I have rebuilt my legitimacy and done it again. I am not reciting this because I have experienced a change of character. I am reciting this because the Bureau has structured an oath that requires I say it, and because I have concluded that compliance is currently the most productive option available to me. The Bureau knows I have concluded this. The Bureau has written that into the enforcement provisions. I find the Bureau's approach efficient. The Goblin Formula is disclosed. The Monitor is appointed. The Registry is active. If I do this again, the Bureau will know sooner. I am acknowledging that. The jury is not required to believe I mean it. The Bureau is not required to believe I mean it. The record will reflect what I do."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Asset Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the full suite of CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK enforcement procedures, including: immediate referral of the Goblin Formula concealment to the Bureau's Chemical Safety Division and the relevant defense oversight bodies as an undisclosed military compound; forced registration of all Oscorp military contracting relationships through compulsory disclosure authority; and publication of the Bureau's complete documented pattern analysis — including the Intelligence Division's full harm record — to all governmental and defense contracting entities identified as current or potential Osborn relationship targets. The Bureau notes that this last enforcement mechanism is the one Subject will find most consequential. The Bureau has designed the enforcement suite accordingly.
