### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[HUN, ATTILA THE]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-08ATH
**Status:** Precautionary

Subject is deceased (453 AD) and cannot be compelled to divest, appear, or recite an oath. There is also, unlike the Buffett precedent (mrp.12026.07), no trust, no widow, no corporate successor — the Hunnic confederation dissolved at the Battle of Nedao a single year after Subject's death, and no modern state holds an unbroken legal claim to his throne. What survives instead is a **still-monetizing terror-brand**: a name that continues to generate commercial and rhetorical value — video-game franchises, metalcore bands, commemorative coinage, political insults, national founding-mythology — flowing to nobody in particular and regulated by nobody at all. The HPB's Office of Posthumous Franchises is treating this diffuse, ownerless brand apparatus as the de facto "estate" for recertification purposes.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days by whichever commercial and cultural entities are currently profiting from the brand, or their court-appointed equivalents.

---

### **1. The Flagellum Dei Trademark Dissolution (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies the unregulated commercial and rhetorical circulation of "Attila the Hun" / "Scourge of God" as a **Terror-Brand Laundering Hazard** — a 1,573-year-old atrocity narrative that has been fully absorbed into casual usage (video-game titles, metal band names, sports mascots, "makes Attila the Hun look like a pushover" as a compliment to a tough negotiator) without any of the parties profiting from it acknowledging what actually funded the original brand.

- **The Action:** All commercial entities currently licensing or trading on the "Attila"/"Hun"/"Scourge of God" mark (game studios, media franchises, coin mints, merchandisers) must register with the HPB and attach a mandatory **"Scourge of God Context Disclosure"** to any new commercial use — a plain-language note on what the tribute economy actually cost the cities it was extracted from.
- **The Sacrifice:** The brand loses its frictionless, consequence-free "cool ruthless guy" packaging.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts unexamined glorification into flagged cautionary-tale status, which the HPB's Whuffie algorithm scores as a net-positive humility signal — the closest thing this file has to a Saintly Pivot.

### **2. The Sword of Mars Decommission**

Subject's court operated on a live divine-right narrative — a sword allegedly sanctioned by the war god himself, later reframed by Christian chroniclers into the "Scourge of God" title. This is the direct ancestor of every strongman "chosen by history" narrative since, and it is still doing unpaid promotional work.

- **The Action:** Any modern political, commercial, or media use of "Sword of Attila" / divine-right imagery must carry a mandatory rider identifying it as **coercive propaganda**, not neutral legend. Academic Hunnological curricula are directed to teach the sword myth as a fabricated legitimacy narrative, not as folklore presented at face value.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loses the unearned "chosen by a god" framing that has quietly flattered every subsequent strongman who invoked it.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Defangs a Class-IV Memetic Hazard at its root, fifteen centuries after the fact but better late than never.

### **3. The Danubian Reparations Levy**

The tribute economy that funded Subject's court was extracted from real, specific, still-existing places — modern Niš, Serbia (ancient Naissus) and Sofia, Bulgaria (ancient Serdica) chief among them. The brand built on that extraction still generates revenue today, none of which has ever flowed back to the descendants of the people who paid for it the first time.

- **The Action:** A mandatory micro-levy is imposed on all commercial products and media using "Attila" or "Hun" branding for profit (coins, games, band royalties, sports merchandise), funding a **Danubian Reparations Whuffie Fund** directed toward heritage and infrastructure projects in the historically sacked regions.
- **The Sacrifice:** Every party currently monetizing the terror-brand for free now pays a toll into a fund benefiting the cities that brand was built by burning.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts pure brand extraction into an actual Saintly Pivot substitute — arguably the first redistributive act this file has ever recorded in Subject's name.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

Here the Bureau runs into a procedural wall it has no precedent for. Subject has no living descendant, no dynasty, and no continuous polity to summon — the Hunnic confederation scattered at Nedao within a year of his death, and no modern nation holds an unbroken legal claim to his throne (Hungary's centuries-old cultural tradition of Hunnic descent is a mythology, not a legal chain of title, and the Bureau declines to adjudicate a national origin story). **There is, strictly speaking, no one to recite this oath, and no one the HPB has jurisdiction to compel.**

In lieu of an heir, the Office of Posthumous Franchises has provisionally designated a symbolic, non-binding proxy: the (currently unfilled, rotating) **Chair of Hunnological & Migration-Period Studies** is *invited* — not compelled — to recite the oath before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Border-Region Innkeepers and Toll Collectors**, the occupational descendants of the people most repeatedly ruined by Subject's tribute demands:

> _"We hold no sword of Mars, no scourge, no warrant from any god. What was taken from Naissus and Serdica was taken by a man, not a myth, and it can be repaid by men, not gods. Let 'Attila' be a name in a history book again — not a brand."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** This is the default state. No party is legally bound to recite the oath, which means the Burn Schedule will almost certainly lapse unfulfilled on its own terms. The HPB's adapted Apotheosis Protocol therefore triggers automatically at 30 days regardless of compliance: the "Attila"/"Scourge of God" trademark class is **nationalized into public-cautionary-utility status** — usable, but never again without the Context Disclosure — and the case is filed under the Bureau's new **"Franchise Without Heir"** doctrine, flagged for the attention of whichever future subject dies famous enough, and unclaimed enough, to need it next.
