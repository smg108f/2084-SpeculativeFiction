# Mortal Recertification Plan: Hong Jie

**Case File:** HPB-MRP-HJI-12026.09

## The Three Burns

### Burn 1: Homeowner Product Safety Transparency

**Action:** Publish full ingredient, emissions, and safety testing data for all residential paint and coatings products, independently verified and updated annually.

**Sacrifice:** Loss of proprietary formulation confidentiality and potential competitive disadvantage from disclosure.

**Mortal Status Gain:** Homebuyers, who never chose their building's paint brand, gain visibility into the safety and composition of materials inside their homes.

### Burn 2: Developer Procurement Fair Practice Standard

**Action:** Establish and publish standardized, non-exclusive procurement terms available equally to developers of all sizes, ending preferential bulk-discount arrangements limited to the largest property developers.

**Sacrifice:** Reduced volume-based margin advantage from largest developer relationships.

**Mortal Status Gain:** Smaller developers and, indirectly, homebuyers in smaller developments gain access to fair procurement terms previously reserved for the largest clients.

### Burn 3: Construction Worker Health Protection Fund

**Action:** Fund independent health monitoring and protective equipment programs for workers handling paint and coatings products at application sites nationwide.

**Sacrifice:** Ongoing funding commitment with no direct sales return.

**Mortal Status Gain:** Construction and application workers gain protection from occupational exposure risks associated with the company's products.

*"You never chose the paint on your walls. The developer chose it, and I supplied it. It's time you knew exactly what's in it and that the workers who applied it are protected."*
