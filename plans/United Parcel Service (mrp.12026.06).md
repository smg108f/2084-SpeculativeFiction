### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[UNITED PARCEL SERVICE, CORPORATE]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06UPS
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification

Subject is not accused of seeking godhood — it is accused of becoming one *by accident*, through sheer ubiquity, while quietly disassembling the human workforce that built its claim to public trust. The HPB requires Subject to prove that "the bloodstream of commerce" can still pump human blood.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The "Network of the Future" Moratorium (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies automated sortation infrastructure as a **Substitution Engine** — a system that converts decades of union-built public goodwill directly into shareholder value while erasing the people who generated that goodwill.

- **The Action:** Subject must freeze all facility automation and closure activity under the "Network of the Future" program for a period of no less than five years, and convert the capital earmarked for robotic sortation into a **Driver and Sorter Trust**, jointly administered with Teamsters Local representatives.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of an estimated **$3.2B** in projected automation-driven labor savings.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject is reclassified from "Efficiency Engine" to "Employer of Record" — the HPB's highest rating for entities that keep their promises to the people who made them indispensable.

### **2. The "Brown Truck" Divestiture (Ideological Burn)**

UPS has spent a century building an image of the friendly neighborhood driver — a mascot of trust now being quietly hollowed out from within.

- **The Action:** Subject must publicly and contractually guarantee that no last-mile delivery route within city limits may be performed by an autonomous vehicle or drone for the duration of the recertification period, and must publish quarterly, auditable headcount reports comparing union driver totals against automation deployment.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses the "first-mover" advantage in the autonomous-delivery race against rival logistics demi-gods.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This neutralizes the **Quiet Replacement Pattern** — the practice of using a beloved human face to mask the removal of the humans behind it.

### **3. The "Whose Hands Carried This" Penance (Reputational Burn)**

UPS's marketing speaks of "delivering what matters" — but never names who does the carrying.

- **The Action:** Subject must redesign its public packaging, app, and tracking interface so that every delivered parcel displays the first name and years-of-service of the driver and sorters who physically handled it — a permanent, structural acknowledgment that "the miracle of arrival" is performed by named human beings, not by "the network."
- **The Requirement:** Fund a $250M "Visible Hands" initiative documenting and broadcasting the daily labor of warehouse and route workers, to be aired in place of corporate efficiency advertising for no less than two years.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This converts UPS's invisible labor force into a *visible* one — the precise inversion of the automation strategy the HPB flagged as its core threat.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's Board of Directors must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Package Sorters, Drivers, and Warehouse Loaders** and recite the following:

> _"We are not the bloodstream. You are. We are only the vessel that carries what your hands have moved. We will stop replacing you with machines that cannot be thanked, cannot be organized, and cannot be looked in the eye — and we will put your names back on what we deliver, because the miracle was never ours. It was always yours."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the "Burn" is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Subject's logistics network will be nationalized into the **Federal Circulation Authority**, its executive leadership will be reclassified as "Non-Biological Decision Nodes," and its automated sortation hubs will be repurposed as **Public Worker Retraining Depots** — staffed, ironically, entirely by humans.
