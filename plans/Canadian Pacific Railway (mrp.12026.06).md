### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [CANADIAN PACIFIC KANSAS CITY]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06CPKC
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice

Canadian Pacific Kansas City has been classified as a **Tri-Sovereign Pressure Point** — an entity whose operational decisions carry coercive leverage over three sovereign governments simultaneously. The 2023 merger that created the first USMCA-spanning rail network was, in retrospect, an inadvertent transfer of tri-national infrastructure sovereignty to a private shareholder base.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The USMCA Rail Commons Conversion (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB designates the CPKC network a **Tri-National Infrastructure Asset** requiring tri-national governance. No private shareholder group has a legitimate claim to sovereign-infrastructure leverage over three democracies.

- **The Action:** Transfer the full CPKC track network to the **USMCA Rail Commons Authority**, a trilateral body governed by rotating representatives from Canada, the United States, and Mexico, with mandatory Indigenous corridor consultation rights.
- **The Sacrifice:** CPKC loses its track ownership — the entirety of its competitive moat. The company's market capitalization falls from ~$82B to estimated ~$15B in rolling stock and logistics operations.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** CPKC is reclassified from **Tri-Sovereign Pressure Point** to **USMCA Track Access Operator**.

### **2. The Mexico Corridor Equity Program (The Historical Burn)**

CPKC's Kansas City Southern acquisition gave it control of rail infrastructure that runs through regions of Mexico with significant Indigenous territorial claims and historical underinvestment in communities along the corridor.

- **The Action:** CPKC must establish a MXN$50B (~$3B) **Mexico Rail Corridor Community Equity Fund**, providing infrastructure investment, employment programs, and land remediation in communities within 10km of the rail line in Mexico.
- **The Sacrifice:** Significant capital commitment; loss of approximately 15–20% of accumulated retained earnings.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The **Colonial Infrastructure Legacy** classification attached to the former KCS routes is formally resolved.

### **3. The Labor Leverage Dissolution (The Operational Burn)**

CPKC's demonstrated capacity to compel back-to-work legislation in multiple countries constitutes **Multinational Coercion**, which the HPB's cross-border provisions prohibit.

- **The Action:** CPKC must accept binding tri-national arbitration for all future labor disputes, with mandatory 90-day cooling-off periods and no government back-to-work option — disputes must be resolved by arbitration, not legislation.
- **The Sacrifice:** Management loses the implicit threat of government intervention as a negotiating tool.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Multinational Coercion classification is lifted.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, CPKC's board of directors must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 4 Canadian Farmers, 4 American Autoworkers, and 4 Mexican Manufacturing Workers from along the rail corridor** and recite the following:

> _"We connected three nations for our shareholders. We will now maintain those connections for the people who live along the tracks, whose ancestors' land the tracks cross, and whose livelihoods depend on the trains running. The corridor was never ours. It was only ever theirs."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol** and jointly petition the governments of Canada, the United States, and Mexico to invoke eminent domain simultaneously. The resulting tri-national rail utility will be named the **USMCA People's Railroad**, which CPKC executives will be required to use for all official travel — in coach, subject to the same scheduling delays they spent a decade inflicting on others.
