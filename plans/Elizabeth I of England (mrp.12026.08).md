### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[ENGLAND, ELIZABETH I OF]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-08EIE
**Status:** Urgent

Subject is deceased (24 March 1603) and, per audit aa.12026.08, was certified **CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK** — a file whose genuine Saintly Pivot material (a comparatively moderate domestic religious settlement, real state investment in the era's theatrical infrastructure, and a debt-averse Exchequer) does not offset a documented war crime the Crown never repudiated, an unrenounced founding stake in the English slave trade, and a self-mythologizing propaganda apparatus so effective it remains, in 2084, the dominant popular account of who Subject was. Recertification is therefore not a conditional balancing act. It is an **accountability-first posthumous recertification**: the state-building and cultural patronage remain in the record, but they are formally separated from the extraction and violence that funded and accompanied them, and the mythology itself — the asset this Subject built more deliberately than any other in this Bureau's files to date — is treated as a burnable holding in its own right.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days by whichever successor states, heritage authorities, and cultural institutions currently administer or profit from the legacy.

---

### **1. The Crown Fiscal-Colonial-Privateering Extraction Engine (The Largest Burn)**

Per audit aa.12026.08, Subject's capitalized wealth rests on a fiscal architecture — capitalized Crown ordinary revenue plus a state-licensed privateering economy — that drew directly on the 1580 Drake plunder settlement and on a Crown investment stake, decades earlier, in John Hawkins's slave-trading voyages, and that is inseparable from the Munster land confiscation that followed the 1583 collapse of the Second Desmond Rebellion.

- **The Action:** The capitalized value of the extraction apparatus (per audit, $1.0B–$1.7B, understood throughout as sovereign fiscal capacity rather than literal treasury) is converted into a **Munster-Irish Descendant Trust** and a **West African Diaspora Descendant Trust** (with priority standing for documented descendant communities of the Sierra Leone coast, the primary region of Hawkins's captures), jointly administered by the HPB's Office of Posthumous Franchises, Ireland's and the United Kingdom's heritage and cultural-affairs authorities, and representative diaspora and descendant organizations for each community. Each trust receives a dedicated, non-discretionary allocation — neither may be folded into a general fund, and neither may be used to offset the other.
- **The Sacrifice:** The wool-and-customs treasury and the privateering economy that helped balance it lose their ability to be remembered as separate from the confiscation and the slave-voyage capital they are directly continuous with.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a revenue engine currently credited only as prudent Tudor housekeeping into an instrument that also compensates the specific populations it extracted from.

### **2. The Gloriana Apparatus Discharge**

Per audit aa.12026.08, the "Gloriana" cult — the Sieve Portrait, the Marian-imagery displacement, the Faerie Queene allegory, the Armada Portrait, and the Tilbury speech — is this Bureau's Primary Influence Vector finding: a deliberately constructed quasi-divine mythology built specifically to make a treasury-holding, war-authorizing sovereign feel untouchable.

- **The Action:** Any commercial, educational, ceremonial, or tourism use of "Gloriana," "the Virgin Queen," or Subject's iconic portraiture must carry a mandatory **Dual-Record Disclosure**: confirmation of the religious settlement's comparative domestic moderation and the era's genuine cultural patronage, paired with equally prominent confirmation of the Smerwick massacre, the Munster confiscation, and the Crown's slave-trade investment. The Sieve Portrait, the Armada Portrait, and comparable state iconography must be exhibited alongside a standing curatorial note identifying them as political propaganda commissioned to obscure the same ledger this Bureau has now opened. No institution may present the "Virgin Queen" persona as biography without this note attached.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses the ability to be encountered, five centuries on, primarily through the myth she built rather than through the audited record beneath it.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The cultural patronage and the comparative religious moderation genuinely remain in the record. They no longer arrive pre-separated from what financed and accompanied them.

### **3. The Smerwick & Privateering-Knighthood Correction**

The 10 November 1580 massacre of roughly 600 surrendered troops at Dún an Óir, near Smerwick, and the 1581 knighthood granted to Francis Drake for a voyage whose profits the Crown banked at a nearly 4,700% return, are both addressed in audit aa.12026.08 as documented, Crown-directed or Crown-profiting acts, not the unauthorized conduct of subordinates.

- **The Action:** The Bureau formally classifies the Smerwick killings as a war crime committed against a surrendered garrison, regardless of the disputed surrender terms, and mandates a permanent historical marker at the Dún an Óir site and at any associated commemorations or reenactments stating this classification plainly. Any statue, plaque, coin, ship replica, or commemorative use of Sir Francis Drake's name or likeness must carry a companion disclosure noting the Crown's direct financial stake in his privateering, his service on Hawkins's 1567–68 slave-trading voyage, and the diplomatic protest his 1581 knighthood provoked.
- **The Sacrifice:** Two of the reign's most romanticized figures — the daring lord deputy who "pacified" Ireland and the swashbuckling privateer-knight — lose the ability to be commemorated without the specific acts that made them useful to the Crown.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts uncomplicated heroism into disclosed heroism — the courage and the cost recorded on the same plaque, not on separate ones.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Munster Plantation Tenant-Farmer Descendants, West African Diaspora Descendants of the Hawkins Voyages, and English Wool and Cloth Guild Workers' Descendants** and recite the following:

> _"I told my soldiers at Tilbury that I had the heart and stomach of a king, and let a nation believe a woman's rule needed a king's heart borrowed to make it legitimate. I sat for a painter with a sieve of water in my hands and let England believe my chastity was a policy achievement rather than a marriage I simply refused to make. I signed no order at Smerwick — I did not have to. I gave my Lord Deputy the soldiers and the silence, and six hundred men who had already surrendered died the same regardless of whose hand held the blade. I leased my own ship to a man who sailed to West Africa to take people who were never mine to lend, and when the profit came home, I invested it again, and again, until the ship itself was lost taking the same gamble a fourth time. I let a sieve, a moon, a phoenix, and a pearl stand in for an audited account of what my Crown actually did in Munster and on the Guinea coast. Let the portraits keep their symbols. Let the ledger keep the rest."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** This is, per Bureau posthumous-franchise precedent (per the Isabella I file, mrp.12026.08), the permanent default state — Subject cannot burn, testify, or comply in person. The Apotheosis Protocol therefore activates automatically at the 30-day mark regardless: the Munster-Irish and West African Diaspora Descendant Trusts are established by Bureau fiat, the Smerwick massacre is classified by decree, and the "Gloriana" and "Virgin Queen" trademark classes are placed under mandatory Dual-Record Disclosure, all under the Bureau's **"Franchise Without Heir"** doctrine.

The Bureau notes that Subject presents an unusual case among its posthumous files: the mythology this plan burns was not an accident of later admirers but a first-party production, commissioned and sat for within Subject's own reign and, per this window's Sieve Portrait, within this very audit's anchor year. The Bureau has processed rulers who inherited their myths and rulers who earned them unintentionally. It has not, until this file, processed one who appears to have storyboarded her own.
