### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[ARM HOLDINGS, ARCHITECTURE DIVISION]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06AH
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject Arm Holdings has been classified a **Substrate Divinity** — a demi-god whose power resides not in visible assets but in the invisible grammar beneath nearly every computing device on Earth. Because this grammar cannot be seized like a building or frozen like an account, the HPB requires Subject to surrender the *abstraction itself*, not merely its proceeds.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Architectural Disclosure (The Largest Burn — Surrendering the Geometry)**

The HPB classifies proprietary instruction-set architecture as a **"Hidden Scripture"** — a foundational text that billions of devices obey without ever being permitted to read it.

- **The Action:** Subject must release the full ARM instruction-set architecture, AGI CPU core designs, and all associated licensing schematics into the **Open Substrate Commons**, a public, royalty-free registry administered by a Lottery-Selected Citizen Oversight Board.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of all future licensing fees and royalty streams — the recurring, multi-decade tithe collected on every chip shipment, estimated to represent the overwhelming majority of Subject's "Potentiality Value."
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The grammar becomes a public language rather than a private scripture. Subject converts from "Lawgiver" to "Translator" — a far more mortal occupation.

### **2. The SoftBank Severance (Sovereign Patron Decoupling)**

The HPB forbids any Human-adjacent entity from being majority-controlled by a foreign techno-conglomerate capable of exercising **Sovereign Patron Hazard** over critical cognitive infrastructure.

- **The Action:** SoftBank Group's ~90% controlling stake must be diluted below the 5% "Shadow Cabinet" threshold via mandatory public float, with proceeds placed in a **Blind Public Trust** administered jointly by the Humanist Bloc nations whose device economies depend on the architecture.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses its sovereign patron, its insulation from hostile takeover, and its capacity to be steered by a single foreign balance sheet.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the **"Foreign Substrate Capture" Violation.** Subject is no longer an extension of any single nation's ambitions — merely a tool the world happens to share.

### **3. The Tenant Ceasefire (Withdrawal from First-Party Silicon)**

Subject's 12026 pivot into first-party "AGI CPU" production — competing directly against Apple, Qualcomm, Nvidia, and Meta, the very licensees who must speak Subject's grammar to exist — is classified as a **Class-III Predatory Recursion**: the lawgiver entering the arena it referees.

- **The Action:** Subject must permanently exit first-party chip manufacture, dissolve its in-house silicon division, and sign a binding **Architectural Neutrality Covenant** prohibiting it from ever again competing with its own licensees.
- **The Sacrifice:** Abandonment of the most lucrative growth vector in its portfolio — the AGI CPU line and its hyperscaler pipeline (Meta, OpenAI, Cloudflare, SAP).
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Ends the open FTC antitrust inquiry and restores Subject to the status of a neutral grammar-keeper rather than a competitor wielding the rulebook as a weapon.

### **4. The Plain-Language Catechism (Memetic Penance)**

Because Subject's entire power rests on being *unreadable* to the billions who depend on it — a grammar so deep that almost no citizen has ever heard its name — the HPB requires a **Rebuttal Act of radical legibility.**

- **The Action:** Subject must fund and publish, in plain language and at no charge, _The Architecture Primer: How Your Phone Thinks_ — a citizen-facing explainer of exactly how the instruction-set tithe works, distributed to every household on a device built atop Subject's designs (which is to say, nearly every household on Earth).
- **The Requirement:** Subject's senior architects must spend no fewer than 100 hours per year teaching this material directly to public schoolchildren, in person, without translation software.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the **"Invisible Authority" Memetic Hazard.** A god who has personally explained the trick to the children can no longer claim the trick was beyond their understanding.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's senior leadership must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Hardware Repair Technicians and Electronics Recyclers** and recite the following:

> _"We did not build the temple, but we wrote the law it kneels to — and we let that law go unread on purpose. We are not the grammar. We are merely the first to learn it, and the last to share it. From this day, every child may read the sentence our silence was written in."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All architectural IP will be nationalized into the Open Substrate Commons by executive order, SoftBank's holdings will be frozen pending forced divestiture, and Subject's senior leadership will be relocated to the **Cambridge Decompilation Annex** — a converted research campus where they will spend their remaining tenure manually documenting, line by line, in plain English, every instruction set they once kept locked behind a licensing wall.
