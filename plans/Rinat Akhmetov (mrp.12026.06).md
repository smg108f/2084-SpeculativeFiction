### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [AKHMETOV, RINAT]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06RA2
**Special Note:** Subject's assets have been significantly impaired by the Russian invasion of Ukraine. Burns are calibrated to recoverable and post-reconstruction wealth.
**Status:** Intensive

---

### **1. The Ukrainian Reconstruction Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must commit $500M to **Ukrainian Industrial Reconstruction** — specifically rebuilding steel and energy infrastructure in Ukrainian communities devastated by the war, prioritizing Mariupol, Donetsk region, and eastern Ukraine where Metinvest's operations were centered and where civilian infrastructure was most destroyed.
- **The Sacrifice:** $500M — directing reconstruction toward communities, not just restoring corporate assets.

### **2. The De-Oligarchization Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must publicly support Ukraine's anti-oligarch legislation — including media ownership limits, political donation restrictions, and state enterprise privatization reforms — and divest SCM's media assets to an independent editorial trust within 24 months of peace.
- **The Sacrifice:** Political influence and media control.

### **3. The Energy Transition Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must publish a binding **DTEK Coal Phase-Out Timeline** — committing to no new coal extraction investment after 12027, with all DTEK capex redirected to renewable energy, and a $100M **Donbas Coal Worker Transition Fund** supporting retraining and income support for miners displaced by the transition.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M + revenue constraint.

---

**FSS if non-compliant:** Subject assigned to **FSS METINVEST** — a vessel made of Ukrainian steel, currently under reconstruction, flying the Ukrainian flag. It will be the finest ship in the harbor when it is done. The workers are building it.
