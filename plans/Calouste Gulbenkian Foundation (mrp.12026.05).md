### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [CALOUSTE GULBENKIAN FOUNDATION]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-39CGF
**Status:** Precautionary / Colonial Petroleum Origin Acknowledgment and Cultural Dominance Transparency Order

The Calouste Gulbenkian Foundation has been classified PROBATIONARY SAINT. Recertification requirements are modest. The Foundation's cultural mission is genuine, the Museum is a legitimate public institution, and the Portuguese anchor is admirable for a man who was stateless by choice. The primary burns address the colonial Iraq petroleum origin and the Foundation's disproportionate influence over Portuguese national cultural life.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Colonial Iraq Petroleum Acknowledgment Burn**

Calouste Gulbenkian's "Mr. Five Percent" stake in the Iraq Petroleum Company derived from his role as deal-maker in the colonial extraction of petroleum from territories that became the modern nation of Iraq. The IPC was a consortium of European oil companies operating in the British Mandate of Mesopotamia — a colonial administration that, in the HPB's assessment, requires specific acknowledgment by institutions that benefited from it. The Foundation's endowment traces directly to these oil royalties.

- **The Action:** The Foundation must publish a **Petroleum Origin Accountability Statement** — a permanent, publicly available narrative documenting: (a) Calouste Gulbenkian's role in the Iraq Petroleum Company and the origin of his 5% personal stake; (b) the colonial administrative context in which these oil agreements were negotiated; (c) the Foundation's assessment of whether any portion of its endowment or programs should acknowledge or address the communities in the Iraqi oil-producing regions where this wealth originated; (d) whether the Foundation has considered any specific programmatic relationship with contemporary Iraq.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Foundation's art museum and concert hall share a public document with the colonial oil fields that funded them.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Gulbenkian Museum's extraordinary collection is understood in its full historical context — assembled with wealth derived from the extraction of resources from a colonial territory.

---

### **2. The Portuguese Cultural Dominance Transparency Burn**

The Gulbenkian Foundation operates what is, for practical purposes, a parallel national cultural infrastructure in Portugal — museum, orchestra, choir, concert hall, and gardens that constitute some of Portugal's most significant cultural institutions. This degree of private cultural influence over a national identity requires public acknowledgment of the Foundation's decision-making power.

- **The Action:** The Foundation must publish an annual **Cultural Infrastructure Accountability Report** — documenting: (a) the proportion of Portugal's cultural institutional capacity that Gulbenkian resources represent; (b) the Foundation's governance process for decisions about the Museum's acquisitions, the Orchestra's programming, and the gardens' public access; (c) any areas of Portuguese cultural life that the Foundation's priorities have systematically under-funded or ignored; (d) the Foundation's policy on maintaining public access to Museum and concert hall programming across income levels.
- **The Sacrifice:** Portugal's most significant private cultural funder becomes accountable to the public whose cultural life it shapes.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Gulbenkian Foundation's cultural dominance is understood as a private choice bearing public responsibility.

---

### **3. The Armenian Heritage Programming Burn**

Calouste Gulbenkian was Armenian. He was born in Istanbul, worked across the Middle East and Europe, and died in Lisbon — a trajectory shaped partly by the Armenian Genocide of 11915, which disrupted Armenian communities across the Ottoman Empire. The Foundation has historically acknowledged Gulbenkian's Armenian heritage in its institutional identity. The HPB recommends — but does not require — that the Foundation establish a dedicated **Armenian Cultural Heritage Program** — a sustained commitment to Armenian arts, education, and community programs, recognizing that the Foundation's founder was an Armenian who could not return to his homeland.

- **The Action:** Establish or expand a dedicated Armenian Cultural Heritage Program with a minimum 3% of annual grants.
- **The Sacrifice:** Minimal — primarily an expansion of existing heritage acknowledgment into programmatic investment.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Calouste Gulbenkian's Armenian identity becomes a programmatic commitment, not only a biographical footnote.
