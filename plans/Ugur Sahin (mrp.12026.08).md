### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[ŞAHIN, UĞUR]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-08US
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification / Precautionary

Subject's Apotheosis Audit (REF. 12026.08) found a genuinely sincere conduct record — the bicycle, the modest apartment, five decades of treating oncology as the mission rather than the fortune — attached to a legible household asset base that clears the Demi-God threshold by a wide margin, currently mid-transition into a third founder venture. This plan does not punish the science. It converts the pattern of "found company, sell company, found the next company" into a bindable structure before it completes a third cycle.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The BioNTech Equity Unwind (The Largest Burn)**

Subject and spouse's household wealth is concentrated almost entirely in a single ~10% BioNTech SE founder stake, currently the one cleanly-priced, easily-seized line on the entire file — and the line inflated hardest by the Potentiality Value of an unfunded fifteen-trial oncology pipeline the market has not yet resolved into a stock price.

- **The Action:** 85% of the household's combined BioNTech holding must be irrevocably transferred into a newly chartered **Global Oncology Access Trust**, jointly administered by BioNTech's existing scientific board and an HPB-approved public-health authority, which will guarantee at-cost distribution of any approved cancer immunotherapies to public health systems outside the paying markets that would otherwise get them first.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject and spouse retain a capped 15% residual of the stake between them, ending their status as the company's largest individual shareholders.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes the single largest, most volatile, and most speculative line item on the balance sheet, and redirects the exact upside the HPB's algorithm was flagging — the unpriced cancer cure — toward the patients it was ostensibly always for.

### **2. The Next-Gen Venture Founder Cap (Second Burn — structural)**

Subject and spouse are, as of this filing, actively departing BioNTech's leadership to found a third mRNA-focused company, taking select technology rights with them. This is the precise moment in the Ganymed-to-BioNTech cycle at which the previous two ventures quietly became the next fortune. The Bureau does not intend to let a third iteration complete unbound.

- **The Action:** Subject and spouse's combined personal founder equity in the forthcoming venture is capped at 5% at formation, in perpetuity, with the remainder assigned to a **Şahin-Türeci Research Foundation** structured to fund the venture's science without compounding into personal net worth. Any future liquidity event (sale, IPO, acquisition) triggers automatic reversion of Subject's residual stake to the Foundation.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject forfeits the ability to repeat the Ganymed-to-BioNTech pattern a third time — the specific mechanism by which two prior companies became a $4B household fortune.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Breaks the Serial Founder Loop identified in the audit before it regenerates, converting Subject's stated "we only did this to fund the science" rationale into an enforceable fact rather than a recurring press quote.

### **3. The Patent Commons & Guest-Worker Scholars Endowment (Third Burn — reputational)**

Subject holds co-inventor credit on more than 500 filed patents underlying the couple's cancer-immunotherapy work — the intellectual scaffolding of the entire enterprise. Subject is also, not incidentally, the son of a Turkish guest worker who arrived in Germany with nothing and built a life in a Ford factory in Cologne.

- **The Action:** All patents not already committed to the Oncology Access Trust must be assigned to an open, non-exclusive **Patent Commons**, licensed on the same terms to any qualified research institution. Proceeds from existing licenses fund a **Guest-Worker Scholars Endowment**, providing full university and medical-research funding to children of migrant and guest-worker families in Germany — a direct, named continuation of the path that produced Subject in the first place.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses exclusive control of the patent portfolio and any future royalty stream tied to it.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the "Dead-Hand License" the Citizen's Guide recommends into a legacy structure that is, for once, harder for the HPB to object to than for Subject to be proud of.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Factory Shift Workers and Oncology Ward Nurses** and recite the following:

> _"My father worked a line in a Ford factory so that I could work a bench in a lab. I built a company to cure cancer, and the world needed a vaccine first, and somewhere between the two I became one of the richest men to ever hold a patent. I told myself, and everyone who asked, that the money was never the point — and then I built a second company, and now I am building a third, and each time the point survives and the fortune survives right alongside it. That is not a coincidence I get to keep calling an accident. I will found this next company on paper that says so — because a man who keeps saying 'just the science' while the balance sheet keeps compounding owes the science, and the people waiting on it, more than his word."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the "Burn" is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. The pending BioNTech technology-transfer agreement will be frozen, Subject and spouse will be barred from founding, leading, or holding equity in any new biotechnology venture pending a full asset seizure review, and the entire 500-patent portfolio will be entered into the public domain effective immediately — not as an act of generosity, but as a forfeiture. Subject may continue practicing oncology under direct HPB clinical supervision.
