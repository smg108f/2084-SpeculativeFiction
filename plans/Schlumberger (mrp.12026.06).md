### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [SLB / SCHLUMBERGER]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-21SLB
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice

SLB's classification as **Private Custodian of Planetary Geology** — accumulated through a century of proprietary data collection across 120 countries — requires recertification that addresses the privatization of irreplaceable public-interest intelligence about Earth's extractable resources.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Subsurface Data Commons Transfer (The Knowledge Burn)**

The HPB classifies SLB's proprietary seismic, formation evaluation, and reservoir modeling data library as a **Planetary Intelligence Asset** that was accumulated using technology developed on publicly funded geological science, on land largely not owned by SLB, and at costs partly externalized onto affected communities.

- **The Action:** SLB must transfer all subsurface data older than 30 years to the **Global Subsurface Commons**, a UN-administered open data repository accessible to governments, academics, and climate researchers. Data from the past 30 years must be contributed under a compulsory licensing framework within 10 years.
- **The Sacrifice:** SLB's primary competitive moat — its data library — is progressively reduced to zero.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Private Map of Earth's Extractable Resources classification is resolved.

### **2. The Sanctioned Jurisdiction Operations Disclosure (The Accountability Burn)**

The HPB classifies SLB's operations in countries under Humanist Bloc sanctions — maintained through various service contracts, data licensing arrangements, and technical support agreements — as **Shadow Sovereignty Operations** that undermine the international accountability mechanisms the Humanist Bloc is built on.

- **The Action:** SLB must publish a full public register of all current operations in Humanist Bloc-sanctioned jurisdictions, with annual updates. It must exit all operations in countries designated Tier 1 Sanctions targets within 18 months.
- **The Sacrifice:** Revenue from sanctioned jurisdiction operations estimated at $1-2B annually.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Political Opacity Conduct flag is resolved.

### **3. The New Energy Rebalancing (The Transition Burn)**

The HPB notes that SLB's "New Energy" division, while genuine, remains a token portion of revenues relative to its oilfield services core business — representing what the Bureau designates as **Transition Theater**.

- **The Action:** SLB must commit binding capex: at least 30% of annual research and development spending must be directed to non-hydrocarbon energy applications by 2028, rising to 50% by 2032. Progress must be reported quarterly to the HPB Energy Transition Monitor.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced oilfield technology R&D; potential competitive disadvantage with oil company clients.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Transition Theater classification is replaced with Genuine Transition Investment.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, SLB's CEO and board must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 geologists from developing nations whose resources SLB has surveyed** and recite the following:

> _"We know where Earth's oil is. We know this because we were paid to find it, using science funded by public universities, on land belonging to sovereign nations and their peoples. We called this knowledge our proprietary asset. We release it to the commons. The planet's geology belongs to the planet."_

SLB's operational infrastructure will be reconstituted as a **Public Geoscience Services Authority**, providing oilfield services at cost to national oil companies transitioning away from fossil fuel dependence.
