**Case File:** HPB-12026-06GR

---

### **1. The Climate Debt Fund Burn**

Rinehart's climate denial funding has contributed to Australian policy delays that scientists attribute to additional cumulative CO₂ emissions. Iron ore enables steel production, a significant source of industrial emissions. The HPB requires a climate remediation fund proportionate to this contribution.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Hancock Climate Debt Fund** with an endowment of A$3B (~$2B), independently governed, focused on: (a) Australian renewable energy transition in regional communities most affected by the fossil fuel economy, (b) climate adaptation for communities on Australia's coasts and in the Pilbara region most exposed to climate impacts, (c) international climate adaptation for Pacific Island nations where Australian emissions have contributed to sea-level rise.
- **The Sacrifice:** A$3B and the ideological comfort of 20 years of climate skepticism.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The most significant available conversion of climate harm into climate remedy. HPB reclassifies climate policy metric from CRITICAL to ELEVATED.

### **2. The Aboriginal Land Rights Reckoning Burn**

Hancock Prospecting's operations sit on land that Aboriginal communities have documented traditional connections to. Lang Hancock's public statements about Aboriginal people were among the most overtly racist made by any prominent Australian businessperson. The inherited fortune carries this history.

- **The Action:** Hancock Prospecting to enter binding native title agreements with all Aboriginal communities with traditional connections to Hancock's operating areas, including revenue sharing of 1% of annual iron ore revenue into a community-controlled fund. Subject to make a public personal apology for her father's documented statements. The HPB does not require her to renounce the inheritance; it requires her to acknowledge what came with it.
- **The Sacrifice:** 1% of iron ore revenue; the silence that has allowed her father's legacy to stand uncommented upon.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts Australia's most significant contested resource extraction operation into a model of native title revenue sharing.

### **3. The Media Editorial Independence Burn**

Rinehart co-owns Nine Entertainment (formerly Fairfax). Media ownership by the subject of climate policy advocacy creates a direct conflict of interest in coverage of climate policy.

- **The Action:** Divest Nine Entertainment stake within 3 years, or establish an independently governed Editorial Independence Charter ensuring no Rinehart family input into editorial decisions on coverage of mining, climate policy, or resources sector regulation. Charter to be binding and enforced by an independent press council.
- **The Sacrifice:** Media influence over Australian public discourse.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts media ownership by a climate policy advocate into either divestiture or structural editorial independence.
