### **1. The Ultra-Processing Transparency Standard**

---

### **2. The Plastic Accountability Commitment**

### **3. The Snack Reformulation Timeline**

Products sold in school cafeteria channels in Humanist Bloc countries must meet the WHO nutritional guidelines as a mandatory standard.
