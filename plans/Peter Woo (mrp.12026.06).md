### **1. The Hong Kong Housing Burn**

Hong Kong has among the least affordable housing globally. Large commercial real estate holders with liquidity can contribute to addressing the residential crisis.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Wheelock Housing Access Trust** — HK$1B (~$130M) — funding: (a) conversion of any identified Wheelock commercial or legacy residential properties to subsidized housing for middle-income families on a minimum 20-year affordable lease basis; (b) a report on Wheelock's land bank identifying any parcels that could be released for affordable residential development without significant commercial loss.
- **The Sacrifice:** HK$1B; some commercial flexibility on land bank.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a major Hong Kong commercial real estate holder into a documented contributor to the city's most acute social need.

### **2. The Woo Foundation Establishment Burn**

- **The Action:** Establish the **Peter Woo Foundation** — HK$500M (~$65M) — focused on Hong Kong education equity and arts heritage. Giving Pledge signed.
- **The Sacrifice:** HK$500M.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Creates a proportionate philanthropic institution.
