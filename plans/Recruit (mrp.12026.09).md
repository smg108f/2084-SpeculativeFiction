### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [RECRUIT HOLDINGS CO., LTD.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09RCT
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification

Recruit's classification reflects its dominant global job-market intermediary position combined with its own 2026 workforce reductions. Recertification targets platform neutrality and layoff transparency.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Layoff Accounting Disclosure**

- **The Action:** Publish a detailed accounting of the roughly 1,300 Indeed and Glassdoor job cuts — affected functions, severance terms, and rationale — with the same specificity applied to the company's own hiring-platform marketing claims.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of narrative control over the framing of the reductions.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Restores consistency between the company's public messaging and its own internal practice.

### **2. The Search Ranking Neutrality Audit**

- **The Action:** Commission and publish an independent audit confirming Indeed's job-listing search rankings are not systematically favoring paid employer listings over organic ones in ways that disadvantage smaller employers or job seekers.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced monetization flexibility if bias is found and must be corrected.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Confirms the "free access" Saintly Pivot claim extends to genuine search neutrality.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Recruit's leadership must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Laid-Off Indeed and Glassdoor Employees** and recite the following:

> _"We built the place the world goes to find work, and this year some of you lost yours here. We will tell you plainly why, and make sure the platform stays honest for everyone still searching."_
