### **1. The BUA Food Security Access Burn**

BUA Foods controls significant shares of Nigeria's sugar and flour markets. The pricing of staple foods affects Nigeria's poorest households.

- **The Action:** Subject must: (a) establish a **BUA Food Access Commitment** — binding annual publication of BUA Foods' commodity pricing and margin data, with an independent consumer protection monitor; (b) fund ₦15B (~$35M) for a **Nigerian Food Security Endowment** — specifically subsidizing staple food access for Nigeria's lowest-income households through partnerships with state social protection programs.
- **The Sacrifice:** ₦15B (~$35M).
