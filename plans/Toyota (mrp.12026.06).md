### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [TOYOTA MOTOR CORPORATION]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-28TM
**Status:** Precautionary — EV Transition Delay Advocacy / Industrial Scale Transition Obligation

Toyota's RTWI of ~$234B places it at **234 times the Demi-God threshold**. MARGINAL classification — precautionary Burns.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The BEV Transition Commitment**

Toyota's multi-pathway argument must be accompanied by a concrete BEV transition timeline that demonstrates the argument is principled rather than defensive. Without a binding BEV commitment, the "multi-pathway" position is indistinguishable from delay.

- **The Action:** Toyota must publish a **BEV Transition Covenant** — a binding commitment that: (a) by 12030, at least 30% of Toyota's global vehicle sales volume are battery electric vehicles; (b) by 12035, no new combustion engine-only vehicle models are introduced in Humanist Bloc markets; and (c) Toyota's lobbying in all markets is consistent with supporting binding BEV transition timelines, not opposing them.
- **The Sacrifice:** Toyota commits to a BEV acceleration timeline that requires significant investment in BEV platform development.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Resolves the **EV Transition Delay** precautionary flag.

---

### **2. The Industrial Worker Transition Fund**

Toyota's manufacturing scale — hundreds of thousands of workers in Toyota City and its global supply chain — creates worker transition obligations as combustion engine components are phased out. The Bureau requires binding commitment to workers in the transition.

- **The Action:** Toyota must establish a **Manufacturing Transition Fund** — 2% of annual operating income dedicated to worker retraining, income bridge programs, and community investment in Toyota-dependent manufacturing regions as the transition away from combustion engine components reduces demand for specific manufacturing skills.
- **The Sacrifice:** 2% of operating income annually to worker transition support.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Establishes that the world's largest automaker accepts transition obligations to the workers whose skills are made obsolete by the transition.

---

### **3. The Hydrogen Bet Transparency**

Toyota's investment in hydrogen fuel cell technology is either a genuine long-term bet on a viable pathway or a strategic defense of combustion infrastructure expertise repackaged as clean technology. The Bureau requires clarity.

- **The Action:** Toyota must publish an annual **Hydrogen Progress Report** — an independent technical assessment of Toyota's hydrogen fuel cell program against benchmarks: cost per kilometer vs. BEV, fueling infrastructure buildout, vehicle availability. If the program falls below benchmarks for three consecutive years, Toyota must commit to redirecting 50% of hydrogen program investment to BEV platform development.
- **The Sacrifice:** The hydrogen bet becomes publicly accountable against measurable benchmarks.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Hydrogen is either vindicated by results or redirected to what works.

---

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Toyota's CEO must stand before a **Jury of 12 Toyota Plant Workers, Climate Scientists, and Prius Owners** and recite:

> _"The Prius mattered. The hybrid mattered. We will make the BEV matter too, on a timeline that does not depend on our convenience. The multi-pathway argument is honest. The timeline must be honest too. The workers who made the combustion engine great will have support through the transition. The hydrogen bet will be tested against reality. The transition is not optional. We are large enough to lead it."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** Escalation to CRITICAL with full mandatory Asset Burn. Toyota's lobbying positions in Humanist Bloc markets placed under Bureau monitoring. BEV transition plan required as condition of Humanist Bloc market access.
