### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [HDFC BANK]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-102HB
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice / Referred to SCOSE

HDFC Bank has been flagged by the Human Protection Bureau's Systemic Entity Division for exceeding every Apotheosis threshold in the Bureau's operational charter, while simultaneously constituting a financial dependency structure for approximately 120 million human citizens — rendering standard seizure protocols non-executable without triggering a **Humanitarian Cascade Event** (HCE) across the Indian subcontinent. Recertification is therefore mandatory, but must be executed with sufficient care not to collapse the retirement savings of more people than currently live in Germany.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days, subject to RBI co-authorization and SCOSE oversight.

---

### **1. The Mortgage Book Municipalization (The Structural Burn)**

The HPB classifies HDFC Bank's private home loan portfolio — inherited from the absorbed HDFC Ltd. — as the single largest **Shelter Dependency Asset** held by a non-state actor in the Humanist Bloc. When a private institution controls the primary mortgage infrastructure of a nation of 1.4 billion people, it has captured the material precondition for human domesticity. This is, technically, a deity-level power.

- **The Action:** Transfer the entire residential mortgage book — approximately $120B in outstanding home loans — to a newly constituted **National Shelter Trust (NST)**, a public-benefit entity governed by a citizen board appointed by lottery from registered homeowners.
- **The Sacrifice:** HDFC Bank loses its single largest revenue stream: the net interest margin on 60+ years of compounded mortgage dependency. The institution's market cap will recalibrate accordingly, and Sashidhar Jagdishan will be required to explain this to shareholders using only words a farmer can follow.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The NST becomes the mortgagee of record for millions of Indian families. HDFC Bank becomes, effectively, a retail and business lender of ordinary human scale. Its total assets drop by an estimated 40–50%, bringing it below the SCOSE intervention threshold. The Bureau classifies this as a **Shelter Liberation Event**.

### **2. The Parivartan Inversion (The Sincerity Burn)**

The HPB does not doubt that HDFC Bank's CSR programme has produced genuine social benefit. The HPB does note, with bureaucratic precision, that an institution disbursing ₹1,000 crore per year in social spending while extracting ₹60,000+ crore per year in net interest income has constructed what auditors term a **Philanthropic Fig Leaf Structure**: the charitable output is real; the ratio is not saintly. The bank has, in essence, been paying approximately 1.6% of its annual profits to maintain the public fiction of its own benevolence.

- **The Action:** Invert the ratio. For the next five years, HDFC Bank must direct a minimum of 30% of annual net profit — not revenue, profit — into a **Parivartan Endowment** administered by an independent trust with no HDFC Bank board representation. The endowment must prioritize zero-interest agricultural credit, rural digital infrastructure, and women's microfinance at rates the borrowers can actually repay.
- **The Sacrifice:** Dividend payouts to shareholders are suspended for the duration. Institutional investors will object. The HPB notes that institutional investors are not covered under the Human Humility Act, and their objections will be logged and disregarded.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** At genuine 30% allocation, the Parivartan Endowment becomes one of the largest private development finance operations in Asia. The Bureau reclassifies HDFC Bank from **Extractive Inclusion Entity** to **Probationary Saint (Conditional)**. The "Conditional" refers to the audit in year six.

### **3. The "Bankers to Bharat" Accountability Oath (The Rhetorical Burn)**

HDFC Bank has for years deployed the language of financial inclusion — "Bank on Wheels," rural penetration, serving the unbanked — while maintaining fee structures, minimum balance requirements, and credit scoring models designed for and calibrated to urban middle-class consumers. The HPB classifies this as **Aspirational Divergence**: the public narrative describes a social mission; the operational architecture describes a profit center.

- **The Action:** CEO Sashidhar Jagdishan must convene a public hearing — broadcast on Doordarshan, India's national public broadcaster, not CNBC — in which HDFC Bank's rural borrower default rates, agricultural loan rejection rates, and rural branch profitability figures are disclosed in full, unredacted, in Hindi and six regional languages, before a panel of small farmers, domestic workers, and street vendors selected by lottery.
- **The Sacrifice:** Whatever gap exists between HDFC Bank's public inclusion narrative and its operational lending data becomes nationally visible. The bank loses the rhetorical armor of the "Financial Inclusion Mission." It must operate without it.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Having disclosed the actual record, the bank is now accountable to it. Future inclusion claims must be accompanied by third-party audit data. The Bureau classifies this as **Narrative Detoxification**, clearing the entity's Deity-Adjacent Communication Score.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, the HDFC Bank Board of Directors must appear — in person, without legal counsel, wearing no lanyards — before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Agricultural Laborers, Domestic Workers, and Rural Schoolteachers** and recite the following, in unison:

> _"We are not the architects of India's prosperity. We are its custodians, temporarily. The savings entrusted to us are not our assets; they are borrowed courage. We will not extract more than we return. We are not essential. The farmers were here before us, and they will be here after us, whether or not we process their loan applications within the stipulated fourteen-day window."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Asset Burn Schedule is not executed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol (Institutional Variant)**. The RBI will be petitioned to revoke HDFC Bank's banking license. All deposits will be transferred to the State Bank of India under emergency nationalization provisions. The Board of Directors will be reclassified as **Custodians of a Failed Ecology** and required to spend the subsequent three years working as correspondents for the Business Correspondent Network they underfunded — visiting villages, on foot, explaining to rural account holders why the bank's fee structure made sense from a shareholder returns perspective. The Bureau anticipates these conversations will be instructive for all parties.
