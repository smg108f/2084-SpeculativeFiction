### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT BROOKFIELD CORPORATION**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09BRF
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

### **1. The Burn: Public Infrastructure Ownership Registry**

**The Action:** Brookfield publishes a complete, searchable public registry of every port, toll road, power asset, and data center it or its managed funds control, ending the practice of obscuring ultimate ownership behind fund-of-funds structures.
**The Sacrifice:** Estimated $4B in strategic-ambiguity value across negotiations and regulatory relationships.
**Mortal Status Gain:** -12% RTWI.

### **2. The Burn: Infrastructure Access Guarantee**

**The Action:** Brookfield commits that essential infrastructure assets (water, power, transit) acquired by its funds maintain binding public-access and rate-fairness commitments regardless of fund exit or resale.
**The Sacrifice:** Estimated $2B in reduced asset resale flexibility.
**Mortal Status Gain:** -8% RTWI.

### **3. The Burn: Bruce Flatt Personal Divestiture**

**The Action:** CEO Bruce Flatt places personal carried-interest holdings above the individual Ascension threshold into a Bureau-monitored blind trust.
**The Sacrifice:** Personal control premium, estimated $800M.
**Mortal Status Gain:** -5% RTWI (personal).
