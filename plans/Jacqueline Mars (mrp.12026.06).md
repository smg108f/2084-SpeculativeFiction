- **The Action:** Both Jacqueline and John must file joint shareholder resolutions at Mars, Inc. board level requiring: (a) independent third-party audit of the full cocoa supply chain, with specific attention to child labor and deforestation, published publicly; (b) binding 5-year targets for fully traceable, certified sustainable cocoa in all Mars chocolate products; (c) a minimum annual investment of $50M in West African cocoa farming community development (separate from existing commitments).

- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts two of the world's largest candy company heirs into the accountability mechanism for the chocolate industry's most documented problem.
