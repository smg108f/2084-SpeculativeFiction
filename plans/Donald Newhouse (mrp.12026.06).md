### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [NEWHOUSE, DONALD]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06DN
**Status:** Urgent

Subject co-controls Advance Publications (Condé Nast, local newspapers, Reddit stake). The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Local Journalism Restoration Burn**

The gutting of the Times-Picayune is the HPB's primary example; it is not the only case. Advance's local newspaper network has undergone significant staff reduction.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish the **Advance Local Journalism Restoration Fund** — $200M — with a mandate to: (a) restore minimum viable daily journalism staffing at all Advance-owned local newspapers currently operating below 50% of their 2010 editorial headcount; (b) fund the conversion of at least 5 Advance local news properties to nonprofit local news organizations where the community requests it and can demonstrate governance capacity; (c) fund a **Times-Picayune New Orleans Journalism Endowment** — $30M specifically — restoring investigative capacity at the Times-Picayune/NOLA.com for New Orleans local journalism. Administered by an independent board of working journalists.
- **The Sacrifice:** $200M.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The family that gutted local journalism across multiple markets invests proportionately in its restoration.

### **2. The Newhouse Foundation Scaling Burn**

- **The Action:** Scale the **Newhouse family foundation** to $50M annual disbursement, with mandates including: (a) local journalism training (expanding Newhouse School Syracuse partnerships); (b) investigative journalism funding in Advance markets; (c) media diversity — funding journalists of color in Condé Nast and local newsrooms.
- **The Sacrifice:** $50M/year.
