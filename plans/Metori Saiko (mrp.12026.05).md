### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [SAIKO, METORI]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06-MS
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice / Voluntary Compliance Unlikely — Enforcement Preparation Advised

Subject Saiko has been classified CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK based on $10 trillion in Conglomerate holdings, a media influence footprint reaching approximately 800 million individuals, and a philanthropic record the Bureau's reviewers described, in internal notes, as "aggressively minimal." The HPB anticipates that Subject will respond to this plan with legal objections, media coverage framing the Bureau as overreaching, and a counter-offer involving a named building. The Bureau declines all three in advance.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. Saiko Conglomerate Media Division — Editorial Independence Mandate**

The Saiko Conglomerate's media holdings — encompassing print, broadcast, and digital platforms reaching approximately 800 million consumers — have been operated as a personal narrative infrastructure rather than a journalistic enterprise. HPB review of editorial decisions over the past decade found a statistically significant correlation between negative coverage of entities critical of the Saiko family and positive coverage of Saiko Conglomerate commercial interests. The Bureau classifies this as a **Narrative Capture Asset** and requires its structural separation from Subject's control.

- **The Action:** Subject must divest controlling interest in all Saiko Conglomerate media properties to an independent public media trust, governed by an editorial board with no financial relationship to the Saiko family. The trust must be endowed with a 20-year operational fund from Conglomerate proceeds sufficient to sustain operations without advertiser or owner pressure.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses his most powerful tool for managing public perception of himself, his family, and his business interests. For the first time in his adult life, he will be reported on by journalists he does not employ.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** RTWI decreases by estimated $2.5 trillion. Narrative Infrastructure Asset classification removed. HPB Optics Rating upgrades from POOR to MARGINAL.

---

### **2. Saiko Family Foundation — Restructure as Independent Grant-Making Body**

The Saiko Family Foundation's existing endowment and grant-making infrastructure must be restructured to remove all Saiko family involvement in grant decisions and eliminate all naming rights provisions. Philanthropy that puts the donor's name on the result is not philanthropy — it is advertising with a tax deduction. The Bureau requires that Subject's giving be genuinely anonymous in its public-facing presentation going forward.

- **The Action:** The Saiko Family Foundation is restructured as the Saiko Public Trust, governed entirely by an independent board. All existing naming rights agreements for buildings, chairs, and programs funded by the Foundation are voided. The endowment is increased to $500 billion from Conglomerate liquid assets, and all future grants must be awarded without donor attribution requirements.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject's name comes off the buildings. Every institution that has hung a Saiko plaque in exchange for funding must remove it. Subject's philanthropic legacy becomes invisible — which is, the HPB notes, what genuine philanthropy looks like.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes the Monument-Building classification from Subject's philanthropic record. Converts a self-promotional asset management program into a functional public good.

---

### **3. Conglomerate Labor Practices — Worker Governance Mandate**

The HPB's Labor Asset Division has flagged the Saiko Conglomerate's workforce — estimated at 4.2 million employees across its manufacturing, logistics, and services divisions — as a structural dependency that constitutes an exercise of sovereign-scale power over individuals' livelihoods. Subject's ability to determine the working conditions, compensation, and economic security of millions of people, without any reciprocal accountability to those people, is among the most direct expressions of Demi-God-scale influence the Bureau regularly encounters.

- **The Action:** Subject must implement worker board representation of at least 40% across all Conglomerate subsidiaries with more than 500 employees, with worker representatives holding genuine voting authority on compensation, working conditions, and strategic decisions affecting employment levels. Implementation must be complete within the 30-day window, with governance structures verified by HPB monitor.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses unilateral authority over 4.2 million people's working lives. The workers over whom he has exercised de facto sovereign power gain formal input into the conditions of their employment.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes the Labor Sovereignty classification from Subject's risk profile. RTWI impact is structural rather than direct — the Conglomerate's market value is expected to decrease as worker governance increases operating costs, which the Bureau considers an acceptable consequence.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Saiko Conglomerate employees** — drawn specifically from the manufacturing and logistics divisions, where median compensation is lowest relative to Conglomerate profitability — and recite the following:

> _"I did not build the Saiko Conglomerate. People I will never meet built it, across generations I did not live through, under conditions I have never been asked to endure. I inherited the result and called it my achievement. I put my name on things that other people paid for and called it generosity. The Bureau has shown me the accounting. My name is worth less than I believed, and the people whose names I covered with mine are worth more. I will not pretend otherwise again."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Asset Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol — Conglomerate Tier**, which includes forced divestiture of all Saiko Conglomerate holdings above $1 billion in assessed value, appointment of an HPB-designated trustee to manage the liquidation, and public disclosure of all internal communications related to the Foundation's naming-rights agreements. The HPB notes that the last item will be, in the Bureau's estimation, the one that causes Subject the most distress. The Bureau has planned accordingly.
