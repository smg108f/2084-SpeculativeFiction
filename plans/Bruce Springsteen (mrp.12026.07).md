### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[SPRINGSTEEN, BRUCE]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-07BS
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification / Precautionary

Subject's RTWI sits within the "Red Zone" ($750M–$1.2B) rather than over the $1B threshold, so no seizure order is issued. However, given the volatility of his touring and real-estate income, the Bureau requires a precautionary Asset Burn to establish a safe margin below Ascension and to resolve the reputational contradiction between Subject's "working man" persona and his actual holdings.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The "Born to Run" Public Songbook Trust**

Subject's 2021 sale of his entire recorded and published catalog to Sony converted decades of "Potentiality Value" into a $500M cash windfall — the single largest concentration of liquid, unassigned wealth on his balance sheet, and the easiest target for a clean, symbolic Saintly Pivot.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Born to Run Public Songbook Trust**, endowing free instruments, recording space, and music education in deindustrialized towns (Asbury Park, NJ; Youngstown, OH; Freehold, NJ) with $150M of catalog-sale proceeds.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loses $150M of his liquid cash reserve — the single most "hoarded" asset on record.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a passive windfall into a verified public good. RTWI drops meaningfully with zero legal ambiguity.

### **2. The Wellington Equestrian Divestment**

Auditors flagged the family's Wellington, FL show-jumping estate — home base for daughter Jessica's Gucci-sponsored Olympic career — as the clearest visible contradiction of the "working man" brand. Elite equestrian sport is, per HPB classification, a "Concentration Hazard of Leisure."

- **The Action:** Transfer the Wellington equestrian property to a nonprofit riding-access initiative (**Stable Ground**) providing free lessons and stabling to working-class youth in Palm Beach County; retain a modest life-tenancy cottage only.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loses direct ownership of the family's equestrian real estate and some of the private training infrastructure underpinning Jessica's sponsorship optics.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the "Aristocratic Recreation" flag and converts a private luxury asset into public access.

### **3. The Dynamic Pricing Penance**

The 2023–2024 backlash — fans paying up to $5,000 a seat on a tour partly framed around "defending democracy" — is the clearest documented instance of Subject's rhetoric outrunning his revenue model. Auditors classify this as low-grade Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric: claiming common cause with the citizenry while pricing them out of the room.

- **The Action:** Permanently abolish dynamic and platinum pricing on all future tours. Establish the **Nosebleed Trust**, guaranteeing a fixed 15% ticket allotment per show at inflation-adjusted 1985 prices, funded by redirecting the forfeited premium margin (est. $50–80M per tour cycle).
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeits future premium-pricing revenue and the leverage it provided over touring income projections.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Resolves the central hypocrisy driving Subject's Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric score; realigns brand and balance sheet.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Diner Waitresses, Factory Retirees, and Ticket-Line Regulars** and recite the following:

> _"I am not the voice of the working man. I am a rich man who sold his voice for five hundred million dollars and let it be re-sold back to the people who raised me at five thousand dollars a seat. My hands are soft. My name is not sacred. From here on, I play for the price of the ticket, not the size of the wallet."_

In lieu of Flotilla exile, Subject will be placed on the **E Street Manifest** — a mandatory, unpaid, Bureau-escorted busking circuit through the Rust Belt towns his catalog claims to represent, continuing until his RTWI recertifies below threshold on its own.
