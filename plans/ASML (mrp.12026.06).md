### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ASML HOLDING N.V.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-22AS
**Status:** Critical — Strategic Global Infrastructure Entity (Level 1) / Absolute Manufacturing Monopoly

ASML's RTWI of ~$665B places it at **665 times the Demi-God threshold**, with the additional **SGIE-1 designation** — the Bureau's new classification for entities whose structural irreplaceability exceeds the standard demi-god assessment framework. Standard asset divestment does not address an absolute manufacturing monopoly. The Bureau's Burns address governance obligations proportionate to ASML's unique structural position.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Public Interest Governance Charter**

An entity with 100% market share in a product essential to all advanced semiconductor manufacturing is not a private company in any meaningful sense. It is a private company with a public infrastructure function at a scale that exceeds any private company governance framework in history. The Bureau requires ASML to accept governance obligations that reflect this reality.

- **The Action:** ASML must accept classification as a **Strategic Global Infrastructure Entity** — a new legal category under the Humanist Bloc Strategic Technology Act. This classification requires: (a) an International Governance Board with representation from the Netherlands, the United States, the EU, Japan, and South Korea — the primary Humanist Bloc nations whose semiconductor industries depend on ASML; (b) ASML's production capacity allocation decisions (which customers receive machines, in what quantities, on what timeline) are subject to review by the International Governance Board for strategic alignment; (c) ASML's R&D roadmap for next-generation lithography (High-NA EUV, future nodes) is shared with the Board under appropriate security classification.
- **The Sacrifice:** ASML loses full unilateral discretion over its production allocation and R&D direction. The entity that makes the only bridge accepts that the bridge is governed by more than the bridge builder.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Establishes governance proportionate to ASML's civilizational strategic importance. The entity remains private and profitable; its strategic decisions are subject to international oversight.

---

### **2. The Technology Succession Plan**

ASML's monopoly is maintained by the accumulated expertise of its engineering workforce and its supplier relationships — particularly with Carl Zeiss SMT (optics) and Trumpf (light source). The Bureau requires ASML to invest in ensuring that its technology does not become irreplaceable in the most dangerous sense: fragile.

- **The Action:** ASML must establish a **Technology Resilience Program** — a €2B commitment over 10 years to: (a) document and open-publish (under appropriate IP protection) the fundamental physics and engineering principles of EUV lithography at a level of detail sufficient for a well-funded successor to reconstruct the capability within 10 years in the event of ASML's failure; (b) establish a second EUV assembly facility outside Veldhoven (most likely in the United States or Japan, with Humanist Bloc subsidy support) that provides geographic resilience; and (c) fund training programs for EUV engineering expertise at European, American, and Japanese research universities.
- **The Sacrifice:** ASML funds the technical foundation of its own eventual competitive challenge and geographic redundancy. The sole bridge builder funds the engineering education for the next generation of bridge builders.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Reduces the **Single-Facility Vulnerability** risk. The knowledge behind the bridge is not held in a single building.

---

### **3. The Equitable Access Standard**

ASML's production backlog means that its allocation decisions — which customers receive machines first — shape the competitive dynamics of the global semiconductor industry. Currently, the largest and wealthiest customers (TSMC, Samsung, Intel) receive preferential allocation by commercial logic. The Bureau requires a public interest dimension to allocation decisions.

- **The Action:** ASML must allocate 5% of annual EUV machine production to a **Humanist Bloc Semiconductor Resilience Fund** — a pooled ownership vehicle through which Humanist Bloc governments collectively access EUV capability for strategic semiconductor research, defense-adjacent applications, and domestic manufacturing development without commercial bidding competition. Machines in the Fund are managed by the International Governance Board and may be hosted at any Humanist Bloc facility meeting ASML's technical requirements.
- **The Sacrifice:** 5% of ASML's most in-demand product line is reserved for public interest allocation.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Establishes that the entity whose machines make strategic computing possible provides a reserved allocation for collective strategic purposes.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, ASML's CEO and Board must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Semiconductor Engineers at Companies That Cannot Get EUV Machine Allocations, EUV Facility Workers in Veldhoven, and Representatives of Nations Whose Semiconductor Sovereignty Depends on ASML Export Decisions** and recite the following:

> _"We built the only bridge over an uncrossable river, and then we charged a toll, and then we decided who could cross and who could not, and we called this a commercial decision. The International Governance Board will now be part of those decisions, because decisions about who can develop advanced computing technology are not commercial decisions. They are civilizational decisions. We are very good engineers. We accept that being the world's only manufacturer of a civilization-critical technology makes us something more than a company. We accept the governance that comes with that."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol** in coordination with the Humanist Bloc Strategic Technology Authority. The Dutch government — which already applies export controls on ASML machines as a matter of national policy — will be formally requested to condition ASML's operating license on SGIE-1 governance compliance. The Technology Resilience Program will be mandated as a condition of the €2B+ in EU CHIPS Act subsidies that ASML has received or is receiving for R&D and capacity expansion. The Humanist Bloc Semiconductor Resilience Fund will be constituted by treaty, with the first allocation drawn from the next production batch. The Bureau notes that ASML cannot exist without the Humanist Bloc's export license regime, engineering talent pipeline, and research ecosystem — and the Humanist Bloc cannot exist without ASML's machines. This is a mutual dependency. The Bureau is merely formalizing what is already true.
