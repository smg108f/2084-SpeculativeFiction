---

### **1. The Affordable Housing Burn**

GuocoLand develops premium property in markets where housing affordability is severely constrained. The HPB requires proportionate investment in affordable housing.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish the **Hong Leong Affordable Housing Trust** — $200M — with a mandate to develop or co-develop affordable housing units in Malaysia (Kuala Lumpur and major urban centres) and Singapore at below-market pricing, targeting households earning below the median income. Minimum 2,000 units. Administered independently, not as a GuocoLand commercial project. Subject must also direct GuocoLand to include an affordable housing component — minimum 15% of units at regulated pricing — in all future major Malaysian residential developments.
- **The Sacrifice:** $200M + ongoing GuocoLand policy constraint.

### **2. The Financial Access Burn**

Hong Leong Bank's mortgage and lending practices determine who accesses credit in Malaysia.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish the **Hong Leong Financial Inclusion Fund** — $100M — providing: (a) microfinance and small business loans to underserved Malaysian communities (particularly rural Sabah/Sarawak, which are chronically underserved by major Malaysian banks); (b) subsidized mortgage guarantee schemes for first-time Malaysian homebuyers below the median income threshold; (c) a financial literacy program operating through Hong Leong Bank branches for first-generation bank customers.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M.
