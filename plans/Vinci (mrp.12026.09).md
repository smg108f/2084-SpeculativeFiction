### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [VINCI SA]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09VCI
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Vinci's concession model — multi-decade, government-outlasting control over toll roads and airports — represents a structural sovereignty risk the Bureau rates Critical. Recertification targets the duration and renegotiability of these concessions directly.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Concession Renegotiation Clause (The Largest Burn)**

- **The Action:** Insert binding public-interest renegotiation windows every 15 years into all existing and future concession contracts, allowing toll and fee structures to be revisited by elected authorities without triggering penalty clauses.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of multi-decade pricing certainty that currently underwrites the concession valuation model.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a 70-year lock into a series of accountable, revisitable public partnerships.

### **2. The Order Book Publication Mandate**

- **The Action:** Publish full terms of every active and pending concession contract — toll formulas, renewal terms, penalty clauses — in a public registry searchable by citizens of the affected jurisdiction.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of negotiating leverage from contract-term opacity in future bids.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the "Elevated Political Agency" flag by making concession terms legible to the public they bind.

### **3. The Profit-Sharing Reinvestment Requirement**

- **The Action:** Direct 20% of concession operating profit above a Bureau-set baseline back into the specific infrastructure under concession, rather than shareholder distributions.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced dividend flow during peak concession-profit years.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the "structurally self-serving" Saintly Pivot into one with measurable public benefit.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Vinci's board must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Daily Toll-Road Commuters** and recite the following:

> _"We bought the road you have no choice but to drive on and wrote a contract that outlives the people who signed it. We will now write contracts that can be reopened by the people who pay the toll every single day."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All concession assets will revert to a **Public Infrastructure Commons**, governed jointly by the originating municipal and national authorities, with former shareholders issued **Transit Credits** redeemable for toll-free travel.
