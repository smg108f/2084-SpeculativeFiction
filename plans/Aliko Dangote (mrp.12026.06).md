### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [DANGOTE, ALIKO]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06AD2
**Status:** Urgent

Subject is Africa's wealthiest person and controls Dangote Cement, Dangote Industries, and the Dangote Refinery. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Dangote Cement Pricing Transparency Burn**

Dangote Cement's dominance in African markets — particularly Nigeria — has been associated with cement prices significantly above international benchmarks. The HPB cannot regulate pricing but can require transparency and mandate structural access commitments.

- **The Action:** Subject must commission an **Independent Dangote Cement Pricing Audit** — conducted by an external economics firm with no Nigerian government or Dangote affiliation — assessing whether cement prices in Dangote-dominant markets (Nigeria, Ghana, Ethiopia, Tanzania, etc.) are above competitive parity, and what the consumer welfare cost is annually. Results must be published publicly. If the audit confirms above-competitive pricing equivalent to more than $500M in annual consumer overcharge, subject must establish an **African Affordable Housing Construction Fund** — $300M — subsidizing cement costs for affordable housing construction by state and municipal authorities in affected markets.
- **The Sacrifice:** $300M (conditional on audit findings) + pricing transparency.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** If the overcharge concern is confirmed, it is remediated. If not confirmed, subject has independently cleared Dangote Cement's pricing practices.

### **2. The Dangote Foundation Scaling Burn**

The Foundation has committed $1.2B to nutrition and health. The HPB credits this. The HPB also notes that $14.2B in wealth at $1.2B total commitment is below the 10% threshold.

- **The Action:** Subject must commit to increasing the **Dangote Foundation's** total commitment to $2B within 5 years, with specific additional mandates: (a) $300M for African university science and engineering infrastructure; (b) $200M for African agricultural supply chain development (reducing dependence on fertilizer and food imports — the Dangote Fertiliser plant should serve this). Annual disbursement reports filed with the HPB.
- **The Sacrifice:** $800M in additional commitments.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Foundation commitments reach a level proportionate to continental wealth.

### **3. The Refinery Consumer Commitment Burn**

The Dangote Refinery's stated mission is to end Nigeria's paradoxical fuel import dependence. The HPB holds subject to that mission.

- **The Action:** Subject must file a binding **Refinery Domestic Supply Priority Commitment**: (a) a minimum of 60% of Dangote Refinery output must be sold domestically in Nigeria at a price no more than 10% above cost-of-refining plus reasonable margin, for the first 10 years of operations; (b) a $100M **Nigeria Fuel Access Fund** to subsidize refined fuel costs for rural Nigerian communities and small commercial operators in the first 3 years of refinery operations.
- **The Sacrifice:** Export revenue forgone; $100M fund.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The refinery fulfills its stated national purpose.
