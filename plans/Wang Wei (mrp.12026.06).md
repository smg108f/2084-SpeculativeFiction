### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [WANG, WEI (SF EXPRESS)]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06WW
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject built China's largest direct-employment delivery network. The HPB's Burns are modest, acknowledging the positive labor model, while asking for philanthropy at appropriate scale and delivery worker welfare formalization. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Delivery Worker Legacy Burn**

SF Express employs hundreds of thousands of delivery workers directly — a significantly better model than gig contracting. The HPB asks for formal worker welfare standards that lock in the benefits of this model.

- **The Action:** Publish a **SF Express Worker Welfare Standard**, covering: minimum wage floors above Chinese statutory minimums, healthcare access for all employees and immediate family, retirement supplementation for long-tenure delivery workers, and mental health support. Standard to be independently audited and publicly reported annually.
- **The Sacrifice:** Labor cost increases at modest margin; competitive disclosure.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts an informal industry-leading labor practice into a formally documented and audited standard. Establishes SF Express as China's logistics labor standard-setter.

### **2. The Foundation Establishment Burn**

At $21B with limited documented philanthropy, the HPB requires a substantive philanthropic commitment.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Wang Wei Foundation** with ¥2B (~$280M) endowment, focused on logistics worker education and vocational training programs — specifically upskilling delivery workers into higher-paying logistics management, technology, and supply chain roles. Independent governance.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥2B.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Creates a foundation whose mission is directly connected to the company's workforce and provides upward mobility for the workers who built the network.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 SF Express Delivery Workers, E-Commerce Customers, and Chinese Labor Researchers** and recite the following:

> *"I started with a bicycle and a delivery bag in Shunde in 1993. I employed people when other companies were contracting them. This was the right choice. I am now formalizing what was right into a standard that will outlast me. The workers get benefits. The workers get training. The foundation helps them move forward. I delivered a lot of packages. I want the people who delivered them to also receive something."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed, subject will be reclassified as **SF Express Demi-God Entity SFDE-001**. SF Express will be required to publish worker welfare data alongside all corporate filings. Subject will be relocated to a Sovereign Flotilla designated **FSS SF EXPRESS**, where he will personally deliver packages to all other Flotilla residents using the direct employment model he pioneered, demonstrating that it works at scale. This is not much of a punishment. The Bureau has noticed this.
