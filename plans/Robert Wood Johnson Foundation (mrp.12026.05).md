### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ROBERT WOOD JOHNSON FOUNDATION]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-17RWJF
**Status:** Precautionary / Historical Reckoning Completion Order

RWJF has been classified PROBATIONARY SAINT. The Foundation's self-initiated historical reckoning is the most significant actionable element of this recertification. The HPB's role is to ensure the reckoning produces structural change rather than institutional narrative management.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Historical Reckoning Completion Burn**

RWJF has publicly committed to "reckoning with its history." The HPB's Historical Accountability Doctrine requires that such reckonings produce: (a) a specific, documented account of the historical failures being acknowledged; (b) concrete programmatic changes resulting from that account; and (c) external verification that the changes are genuine rather than rhetorical.

- **The Action:** RWJF must publish a formal **Historical Accountability Report** — independently reviewed — documenting specific historical grant programs, research funding decisions, or institutional positions that reinforced or ignored health inequities, with a direct accounting of how those decisions were made, who made them, and what communities were affected. The report must be accompanied by a published strategic response detailing how at least $100M in annual grant programming will be redirected as a result of the findings.
- **The Sacrifice:** RWJF acknowledges specific historical failures rather than generalizing about "reckoning." Some of those failures will be attributable to specific board decisions, grant programs, or research agendas that have legacy implications.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The historical reckoning becomes a structural act rather than a communications posture.

---

### **2. The Health Equity Portfolio Burn**

RWJF's impact investment allocation ($325M toward health equity) and its goal of leveraging $1B+ from external investors — surpassed in 12024 — represent genuine innovation. The HPB requires that this commitment be formalized and expanded.

- **The Action:** RWJF must publish a binding commitment to maintain its impact investment allocation at a minimum of 5% of total endowment assets (approximately $675M at current endowment levels) and to increase the external leverage target to $2B by 12030.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Foundation commits endowment capital to mission-aligned investments rather than pure return optimization.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The endowment itself — not just grants — becomes a structural tool for health equity. J&J money pays its dues to the health system it profited from.

---

### **3. The Community Voice Burn**

RWJF shapes American health policy through research funding and advocacy. The communities most affected by health inequities are the subjects of this research but not its directors. The HPB's Community Voice Protocol requires large health funders to establish formal mechanisms for affected community input into research priorities.

- **The Action:** RWJF must establish a **Community Health Equity Advisory Council** — a 24-member body drawn from community health workers, patient advocates, and residents of high-health-burden communities — with formal quarterly input authority into RWJF's research priority agenda and a required published response when RWJF's board declines Council recommendations.
- **The Sacrifice:** RWJF's research agenda becomes formally contestable by the communities it studies.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Health policy research funded by private philanthropy becomes accountable to the people whose health it claims to improve.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, RWJF's Board of Trustees must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Community Health Workers from Communities With the Lowest Health Equity Scores in States Where RWJF Has Funded Research** and recite the following:

> *"We were built on bandage money. We have spent 54 years funding research about your communities. We have not always funded research by your communities, or accountable to your communities, or directed by your communities. We acknowledge this. Our reckoning is not a blog post. It is a portfolio redirect, a formal report, and a council with teeth. We are trying to heal the system. We start by admitting what the system — including our part in it — has done."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Historical Accountability Report is not commissioned within 30 days, the HPB will reclassify RWJF's "reckoning" as a **Communications Strategy** rather than an institutional commitment and will publish this reclassification. The HPB notes that a Foundation that announces a reckoning and then declines to complete it is doing more damage to health equity discourse than one that never announced it in the first place. Start the report.
