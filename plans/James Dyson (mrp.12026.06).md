### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [DYSON, JAMES]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06JD2
**Status:** Urgent

Subject advocated for Brexit and then relocated to Singapore. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Brexit Accountability Burn**

Dyson championed Brexit. British workers and the British economy are living with the consequences of that advocacy. Dyson is living in Singapore. The HPB requires proportionate investment in the UK industrial base that Dyson's advocacy helped reshape.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish the **Dyson British Manufacturing Investment Fund** — £200M (~$250M) — dedicated entirely to British manufacturing: (a) funding UK manufacturing startups in advanced engineering, cleantech, and precision manufacturing — the sectors where Brexit's reduction in EU single market access has hurt British industrial exporters most; (b) workforce development in engineering manufacturing skills in British regions hit hardest by manufacturing decline (West Midlands, North East England, Yorkshire); (c) restoring UK-based R&D investment: subject must invest a minimum of £50M in UK-based Dyson engineering research and development — not simply in the James Dyson Foundation's education programs but in actual UK engineering jobs at Dyson.
- **The Sacrifice:** £200M.

### **2. The Engineering Education Scaling Burn**

The James Dyson Foundation and the Dyson Award are credited. The HPB asks for scaling.

- **The Action:** Scale the James Dyson Foundation to a permanent £100M endowment, with an expanded mandate: (a) triple the Dyson Award's reach (currently 27 countries); (b) establish **Dyson Engineering Bursaries** for engineering students at UK universities from low-income backgrounds — 500 bursaries per year, full tuition equivalent; (c) fund school-level design and engineering education in British state schools in post-industrial regions.
- **The Sacrifice:** £100M (~$125M).

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 British Manufacturing Workers, UK Engineering Students from Low-Income Backgrounds, and Brexit-Affected British Exporters**:

> *"I invented a vacuum cleaner in Britain. 5,127 prototypes. I believed that Britain could compete on its own terms. I believed Brexit was good for Britain. I moved the headquarters to Singapore for business reasons. I understand how those two things look together. The engineering bursaries are what the Foundation was already doing, at the scale that £9.3B justifies. Britain made me. The UK industrial base deserves what I am committing here."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** Subject will be assigned to **FSS DYSON** — a vessel powered entirely by Dyson cyclone technology (modified to maritime scale), manufactured entirely in the United Kingdom, crewed by British engineers from the regions where Dyson's manufacturing was not located, performing engineering education demonstrations at every port of call, with a prominent display on the bridge showing the number of prototypes it took to get the ship's propulsion system to work — and the number of UK engineering jobs the project created.
