### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [SAVERIN, EDUARDO]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06ES2
**Status:** Urgent

Subject co-founded Facebook using US university infrastructure, US investors, and US users, then renounced US citizenship before the IPO to avoid an estimated $700M in taxes. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Tax Equivalence Burn**

The HPB does not pursue legal enforcement of avoided taxes. It requires proportionate philanthropic deployment as moral equivalence.

- **The Action:** Subject must deploy $700M — the HPB's estimate of avoided US exit taxes — to public goods causes, with at least 50% directed to causes that would have benefited from US public spending: (a) $300M to US public education technology — specifically bridging the digital divide in US public schools (Saverin's wealth came from a US tech platform built on US universities; this is direct reciprocity); (b) $200M to public health programs in Southeast Asia — where Saverin lives and operates, and where B Capital's portfolio companies employ workers; (c) $200M to a **B Capital Impact Fund** — a carve-out of B Capital Group's investment mandate directed to early-stage technology companies in ASEAN countries where the primary beneficiaries are local communities, not US/European venture returns. The Impact Fund's returns are reinvested, not extracted.
- **The Sacrifice:** $700M.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The tax-equivalent amount is deployed to public goods rather than private accumulation.

### **2. The Facebook Infrastructure Acknowledgment Burn**

Facebook was built on ARPANET (the US-government-funded precursor to the Internet), Harvard's infrastructure, and US investment capital. Saverin's stake reflected these inputs.

- **The Action:** Subject must donate $50M to the **Internet Archive** and to open-access digital infrastructure programs — permanently free, publicly accessible technology foundations that echo the public nature of the infrastructure that made Facebook possible.
- **The Sacrifice:** $50M.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 US Public School Technology Teachers, ASEAN Tech Startup Founders Funded by B Capital, and Harvard University Financial Aid Officers**:

> *"I co-founded Facebook at Harvard. The US created the conditions that made that possible — the university, the internet infrastructure, the investors, the users. I moved to Singapore. I renounced my citizenship before the IPO. I have said my reasons were personal. The HPB is not asking me to re-litigate my reasons. It is asking me to account for the public goods that were foregone. $700M in avoided taxes, deployed to public goods causes, is the account. The Internet Archive fund acknowledges that the infrastructure Facebook ran on was public and should stay public. I built something real. This is how I account for what that building cost."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** Subject will be assigned to **FSS FACEBOOK** — a vessel flying both the Brazilian and Singaporean flags (neither American), crewed by a team of US public school technology teachers who have never had adequate equipment, sailing to US ports and distributing refurbished computers to school districts that could have been funded by the $700M in exit taxes, with a plaque on the bridge noting that the Senate once proposed naming a bill after the vessel's captain.
