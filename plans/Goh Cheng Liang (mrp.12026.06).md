### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [GOH, CHENG LIANG]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06GCL
**Status:** Precautionary — Giving Pledge Credit Applied

Subject holds a major stake in Nippon Paint through Wuthelam Holdings. He is a Giving Pledge signatory. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Nippon Paint Environmental Commitment Burn**

Nippon Paint is a global chemicals and coatings company. VOCs, lead remediation in legacy products, and supply chain environmental impact require binding commitments.

- **The Action:** Subject must direct the Wuthelam stake's board influence to achieve (and personally underwrite via a S$50M grant) a **Nippon Paint Independent Environmental Audit** — conducted by an external environmental engineering firm — assessing: (a) VOC emissions across all manufacturing sites; (b) lead content in all current product formulations globally (with mandatory reformulation of any above international best-practice thresholds); (c) supply chain environmental impact from raw material sourcing. Results must be published publicly within 18 months.
- **The Sacrifice:** S$50M + any product reformulation costs revealed by the audit.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The world's second-largest paint maker has a publicly verifiable environmental baseline, funded by its largest shareholder.

### **2. The Goh Foundation Scale Burn**

The Goh Cheng Liang Foundation exists. The Giving Pledge is signed. The HPB asks for scale commensurate with wealth.

- **The Action:** Subject must increase the **Goh Cheng Liang Foundation's** annual disbursement to a minimum of 1% of total wealth per year — at current valuation, approximately S$190M (~$140M) annually — sustained for a minimum of 10 years. Priority areas: Singapore public healthcare; Southeast Asian educational access; environmental science research at Singapore universities.
- **The Sacrifice:** ~$140M/year in philanthropic disbursement.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** A Giving Pledge signatory whose giving is calibrated to his actual wealth.
