### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [FOXCONN INDUSTRIAL INTERNET]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09FII
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Foxconn Industrial Internet's explosive AI-server-driven growth, without any accompanying public-good pivot, places it among this cycle's most urgent Ascension Enabler files. Recertification targets redistribution of the manufacturing windfall.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Manufacturing Labor Standards Verification (The Largest Burn)**

- **The Action:** Commission and publish an independent audit of labor conditions, working hours, and compensation across all facilities producing the surging AI server volume, given the historical scrutiny of large-scale electronics manufacturing labor practices.
- **The Sacrifice:** Increased scrutiny and potential cost of labor-standard improvements during rapid production scaling.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Ensures explosive growth does not come at the cost of manufacturing-floor labor conditions.

### **2. The AI Infrastructure Public Benefit Fund**

- **The Action:** Direct 2% of AI-server-related revenue into a public fund supporting technology-manufacturing workforce training in regions hosting production facilities.
- **The Sacrifice:** Estimated significant sum annually redirected from shareholder returns given the scale of AI server revenue.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Introduces a genuine Saintly Pivot tied directly to the source of explosive growth.

### **3. The Production Capacity Transparency**

- **The Action:** Publish aggregate data on AI server production capacity and client concentration to give policymakers visibility into the physical bottleneck shaping global AI infrastructure expansion.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced strategic opacity around production capacity and client relationships.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Provides early-indicator transparency into the pace of the broader AI buildout.
