### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [SUMITOMO MITSUI FINANCIAL GROUP]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-26SMFG
**Status:** Precautionary

SMFG's Historical Institutional Sovereignty classification — a banking network with 400-year institutional lineage, keiretsu cross-shareholding architecture, and structural integration with Japanese industrial policy — requires recertification that the Demi-God Act's architects did not anticipate needing to apply to institutions predating the American republic.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Keiretsu Cross-Shareholding Dissolution (The Dynasty Burn)**

The HPB classifies the Sumitomo-Mitsui keiretsu cross-shareholding network as a **Mutual Sovereignty Web**: a structure in which SMFG holds equity in its corporate clients and those clients hold equity in SMFG, creating a closed-circuit financial ecosystem that operates partly outside market discipline.

- **The Action:** SMFG must divest all cross-shareholding equity positions in keiretsu member companies over a 10-year period. Keiretsu members holding SMFG equity must similarly divest. The entire cross-shareholding network must dissolve into genuinely independent market relationships.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of the financial stability provided by the mutual equity cushion; SMFG faces genuine market discipline for the first time in its modern history.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Dynastic Industrial Sovereignty flag is removed; SMFG becomes an ordinary bank.

### **2. The Patient Capital Formalization (The Public Good Burn)**

The HPB acknowledges that SMFG's relationship banking model — providing patient long-term capital to manufacturers who cannot access short-term capital markets — is a genuine public benefit that should be preserved as Japan transitions away from the keiretsu structure.

- **The Action:** SMFG must establish a formal **Japanese Industrial Patient Capital Fund** of ¥10T, independently governed with government co-investment, providing 10-year minimum term lending to Japanese manufacturers at guaranteed rates. This replaces the keiretsu's informal function with a transparent, accountable structure.
- **The Sacrifice:** Formalization replaces the informal relationship advantages that the keiretsu provided.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Patient Capital Institutional Legacy is preserved in a form compatible with the Demi-God Act.

### **3. The Interlock Board Separation (The Governance Burn)**

The HPB classifies the interlocking directorate structure of the keiretsu — SMFG board members sitting on client company boards, client executives sitting on SMFG's board — as a **Governance Circuit Closure**: decisions that should reflect market accountability reflect relationship obligation instead.

- **The Action:** No SMFG executive or board member may simultaneously hold a director, officer, or advisory position at any SMFG corporate lending client. A 5-year cooling-off period applies post-separation.
- **The Sacrifice:** The relationship banking culture that has defined SMFG for a century.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Board independence score moves from inadequate to compliant.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, SMFG's board must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Japanese manufacturing workers and retail banking depositors** and recite the following:

> _"Our family of companies has shared equity, shared directors, and shared assumptions since before Japan was modern. We financed each other through every crisis because we are each other. We accept that the Demi-God Act sees this as a problem. We dissolve the web. We formalize the patience. We become a bank that is only a bank."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will request that the Bank of Japan activate the **Financial System Structural Reform Authority** established under the Demi-God Act's Annex VII (Financial Institutions with Historical Antecedents). The Bureau notes that applying 2034 legislation to a 1590 institution has not previously been attempted. The Bureau is prepared to try.
