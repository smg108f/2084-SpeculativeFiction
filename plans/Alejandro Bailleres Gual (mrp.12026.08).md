### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[BAILLÈRES GUAL, ALEJANDRO]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09ABG
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject exceeds the Demi-God threshold by a factor of roughly nineteen through a single inherited control trust spanning silver mining, life insurance, pension administration, and luxury retail — with an unresolved $630 million land-restitution dispute and an active labor strike sitting inside the same corporate umbrella. Recertification cannot proceed on net-worth reduction alone; the HPB requires structural divestment of the social-safety-net holdings in particular, given their quasi-public function in Mexican civil life.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The GNP–Profuturo Social Safety Net Transfer**

The HPB classifies privately-held insurance and mandatory pension administration at national scale as a **Class-I Social Infrastructure Hazard** — instruments that function as public utilities for the citizens who depend on them, regardless of who holds the equity. Subject's family trust sits atop Grupo Nacional Provincial (GNP), one of Mexico's largest insurers, and Profuturo, a major Afore pension-fund administrator, giving Subject an unaccountable private claim on the retirement savings and health coverage of millions of Mexican citizens who never consented to being his balance sheet.

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer all family-trust voting control of GNP and Profuturo to a newly constituted **Mexican Social Trust Commons (MSTC)**, governed by a board with seats reserved for policyholder and Afore-account-holder representatives, elected by lottery from the affected customer base. Subject may retain no board seat, advisory role, or dividend preference.
- **The Sacrifice:** An estimated $7.0 billion in equity value and voting control — the largest single line item in Subject's Divine Footprint. Subject loses the ability to set premiums, investment allocation for pension funds, or claims policy for either institution.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Eliminates the Political Agency: Elevated flag tied to social-safety-net capture. Subject becomes a private citizen with no leverage over other people's old age.

### **2. The Peñoles–Fresnillo Extraction Reckoning**

This is not a reputational gesture. The HPB treats the **Soledad-Dipolos / Ejido El Bajío** land-restitution dispute and the **Tizapa mine** labor strike as open files, not history. No recertification proceeds while either remains substantively unaddressed.

- **The Action:** Subject must (a) unconditionally accept the independent 2026 valuation and fund the full $630 million restitution owed to Ejido El Bajío for gold extracted at Soledad-Dipolos, with no further legal challenge to the valuation; (b) fully settle the Tizapa mine labor dispute on terms acceptable to the national mining union, with independent labor-standards monitoring for five years; and (c) transfer majority voting control of Industrias Peñoles and Fresnillo plc to an **Extraction Commons Trust**, governed by a rotating board including seats for affected Sonoran communities and mine-worker representatives. Subject retains a fixed, taxable salary as a non-voting technical advisor only.
- **The Sacrifice:** An estimated $9.0 billion in mining equity and voting control, plus $630 million in escrowed, non-recoverable restitution funding. Subject loses the chairmanship of Fresnillo and the vice-chairmanship of Peñoles, along with the right to dispute the El Bajío valuation any further.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The HPB's **Restitution Recognition Protocol** allows for a formal reduction in Subject's Concentration Hazard score upon completion. Subject's file will be annotated: "Acknowledged extractive harm. Restitution executed in full." Regardless of net worth, this file does not close without it.

### **3. The Palacio de Hierro & ITAM Decoupling**

Grupo Palacio de Hierro and the ITAM Board of Governors present a shared problem for the Bureau: both are prestige instruments of the Baillères name, one commercial and one academic, and both currently sit under Subject's personal chairmanship. The HPB cannot certify a genuine Saintly Pivot at ITAM while the same hand that chairs its board also chairs the luxury retailer that trades on the family name three blocks away.

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer full operational control of Grupo Palacio de Hierro to an employee-ownership cooperative structure, retaining no board seat or veto. Separately, Subject must resign the chairmanship of the ITAM Board of Governors in favor of an independently nominated academic chair, with the Baillères family permitted only a single non-controlling honorary seat in recognition of the university's founding.
- **The Sacrifice:** An estimated $2.5 billion in retail equity, and — more personally costly to Subject — the third-generation family chairmanship of the institution his grandfather founded and his father led for over fifty years.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Palacio de Hierro becomes genuinely employee-governed rather than family-branded. ITAM's philanthropic legitimacy is no longer bundled with a living demi-god candidate's personal prestige. The HPB's Saintly Score for the ITAM half of this action alone is **81/100** — high marks for institutional durability, docked for how long it took a summons to force the separation.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Mexican Citizens — specifically: three Ejido El Bajío farmers, three Tizapa mine workers, three GNP policyholders, and three ITAM scholarship students** and recite the following:

> *"My family built a palace and called it iron, and a mine and pulled silver from it, and I inherited both before I earned either. I collected premiums from people who feared dying and pensions from people who feared growing old, and I called this diversification. Land was taken in Sonora and a court said the taking was illegal, and I let lawyers argue about the number for over a decade. I chaired a university my grandfather founded so that my name would keep meaning something even after the ore ran out. I am releasing the mine, the vault, and the chair. What is left is a man with the same last name as a department store, and nothing else."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Asset Burn Schedule is not completed within the 30-day window, the HPB will activate the **Full Apotheosis Protocol**. Voting control of Industrias Peñoles, Fresnillo plc, GNP, and Profuturo will be transferred by federal order to the Extraction Commons Trust and the Mexican Social Trust Commons respectively. Subject will be issued a **Structural Beneficiary Notice**, restricting his ability to hold corporate governance positions for 15 years, and will be escorted to a **Designated Observation Residence** — specifically, a converted wing of the original Palacio de Hierro flagship store in Mexico City's Centro Histórico, now a public heritage site, where Subject may live as a caretaker and greet the public who once shopped beneath his family's name.
