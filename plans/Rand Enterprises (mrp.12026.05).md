### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [RAND ENTERPRISES / RAND, DANNY]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-15-RE
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice / Intermittent Subject Availability — Accelerated Governance Mandate

Rand Enterprises has been classified MARGINAL ASCENSION RISK based on a $3 trillion valuation, a global shipping infrastructure that functions as an economic chokepoint, and a governance structure characterized by the controlling individual's documented pattern of multi-year extradimensional absences. The HPB is issuing this plan with an explicit provision: compliance must be structured to function whether Danny Rand is present or not, because the Bureau cannot guarantee his presence.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. Rand Enterprises Governance — Absence-Proof Independent Structure**

The fundamental problem with Rand Enterprises is not its asset base — it is the absence of governance infrastructure that does not require Danny Rand's personal presence to function responsibly. The Board has historically been unable to provide consistent leadership during Subject's absences, and the company's strategic direction has drifted in ways that conflict with its controlling shareholder's stated values on multiple occasions. This must be structurally corrected.

- **The Action:** Rand Enterprises must restructure its governance with: (1) a fully independent board majority with no financial relationship to the Rand family, empowered to act on all strategic matters without requiring shareholder approval for decisions below $500 million; (2) a formally documented Values Charter that constrains board action during periods of controlling shareholder absence; and (3) a legally registered Absence Protocol — co-signed by Danny Rand, the HPB, and the new board — that triggers enhanced independent oversight whenever Subject's absence from Earth's physical plane exceeds 90 consecutive days.
- **The Sacrifice:** Danny Rand loses the ability to return from K'un-Lun and find the company arranged according to his personal preferences, because the independent board will have been making decisions in his absence within a constrained framework rather than improvising. He gains a company that hasn't been looted or redirected by the time he comes back.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Governance Instability classification removed. The company's structural vulnerability — which has been, paradoxically, a protection — is replaced by genuine accountability infrastructure.

---

### **2. Global Shipping Division — Infrastructure Commons Contribution**

Rand Enterprises' shipping network controls a systemically significant fraction of global cargo movement. Like Capsule Corp's compression technology, this position creates an infrastructure dependency risk that the HPB cannot address through asset seizure without causing disproportionate economic harm. The Bureau requires a structural contribution to the global shipping commons.

- **The Action:** Rand Enterprises must transfer operational management of all port facilities in developing-nation contexts (approximately 40% of its global port portfolio by volume) to a jointly governed International Shipping Commons Authority, with Rand Enterprises retaining a 30% non-controlling stake. The company continues to benefit from the network — it simply stops being the sole governing authority over infrastructure that dozens of nations' economies depend on. Revenue from the transferred facilities is directed to port community development programs.
- **The Sacrifice:** Rand Enterprises loses control of a significant portion of its most systemically important assets. The company's market valuation is expected to decrease by approximately $600 billion as the monopoly premium on developing-nation port control is eliminated.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Infrastructure Dependency classification downgraded from Systemic Risk to Managed Participation. RTWI decreases by $600 billion.

---

### **3. K'un-Lun Obligations — Formal Disclosure and Scheduling Framework**

The HPB cannot regulate the Iron Fist's obligations to K'un-Lun. It can require that those obligations be formally disclosed and incorporated into Rand Enterprises' governance documents so that the company can plan accordingly, rather than experiencing each extradimensional departure as an unexpected governance crisis.

- **The Action:** Danny Rand must file a formal Extradimensional Absence Disclosure with the HPB, documenting the known schedule and expected duration of K'un-Lun access windows, his expected absence periods, and the governance protocols that will govern the company during each absence. This disclosure must be updated whenever Subject's extradimensional schedule changes materially. Rand Enterprises' annual shareholder report must include an "Absence Readiness" section confirming that the Absence Protocol is current and the independent board is functioning.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject discloses, to a regulatory body, the existence and approximate schedule of a mystical extradimensional city that has existed in secret for centuries. K'un-Lun's masters may have opinions about this. Subject is invited to manage that conversation.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Extradimensional Exit classification removed from risk profile. The HPB gains the ability to plan enforcement timelines around Subject's actual availability.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Rand Enterprises shipping employees** — specifically drawn from port facilities in developing nations that experienced the most governance instability during Subject's prior absences — and recite the following:

> _"K'un-Lun taught me that a weapon without discipline is just danger with a handle. I have been a weapon with a company attached, and I have not always applied that lesson to the company. I left it behind when I left. The people who worked for it did not get to leave — they had to manage the consequences of my absence without my guidance and without any framework I built for them before I went. I am building that framework now. The Iron Fist is a responsibility. The $3 trillion company is also a responsibility. I am accepting both of them at the same time, which I should have done from the start."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Asset Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate standard MARGINAL ASCENSION RISK enforcement procedures. The Bureau notes that it is prepared to serve the compliance notice by any means necessary, including delivery to K'un-Lun during its next physical appearance window — which the Bureau has, following Subject's Extradimensional Disclosure obligation, formally requested be communicated to the HPB's scheduling office at least 60 days in advance.
