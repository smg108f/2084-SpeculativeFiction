### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [CME GROUP INC]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-27CME
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice

CME Group's Private Tax on Managing the Future classification — 62% operating margins from administering the global financial system's risk management infrastructure — requires recertification that addresses the extraction of monopoly rents from financial market functions that should be administered as public utilities.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Futures Exchange Public Trust Conversion (The Market Infrastructure Burn)**

The HPB classifies CME's ownership of benchmark derivatives exchanges — interest rates, equity indices, energy, agriculture — as a **Private Administration of Public Price Risk**: the instruments through which the global economy manages uncertainty are owned by a single for-profit corporation.

- **The Action:** CME must convert its core benchmark derivatives exchanges (CME, CBOT, NYMEX, COMEX) to a **Global Derivatives Public Trust** jointly governed by the CFTC, the relevant commodity producers, institutional hedgers, and agricultural community representatives. CME retains a technology service contract.
- **The Sacrifice:** CME loses the equity value of its exchange franchises — estimated at $50-55B of its market cap.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Risk Pricing Sovereignty is transferred to public governance.

### **2. The Operating Margin Cap (The Rent Extraction Burn)**

The HPB classifies CME's 62% operating margin as **Infrastructure Monopoly Rent Extraction**: profits derived not from competitive excellence but from network effects that make competition impossible.

- **The Action:** CME must accept a binding operating margin cap of 25% (consistent with well-run public utility margins) for all exchange operations. Revenues above this margin must be returned to exchange users as fee reductions, proportional to trading volume.
- **The Sacrifice:** ~$2B annual revenue reduction.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** CME exits the Private Tax on Uncertainty classification.

### **3. The Agricultural Price Discovery Equity Program (The Justice Burn)**

The HPB classifies CME's agricultural derivatives markets — where the prices of corn, soybeans, wheat, and livestock are set by financial speculators who have no relationship to the physical commodity — as a **Price Sovereignty Over Food Production**: farmers dependent on these markets pay CME for the right to hedge the price of their own crops.

- **The Action:** All agricultural derivatives trading fees must be waived for verified agricultural producers hedging their own production. CME must establish a $500M **Agricultural Price Stability Fund** distributing annual grants to small farmers in developing countries affected by CME-priced commodity volatility.
- **The Sacrifice:** ~$300M annual fee waiver; $500M fund establishment.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Agricultural Price Sovereignty flag is resolved.

> _"I charge a fee every time someone wants to be certain about what the future costs. I have 62% operating margins because no one can price their uncertainty anywhere else.
