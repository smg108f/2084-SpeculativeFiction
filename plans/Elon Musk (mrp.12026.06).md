### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [MUSK, E.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-02EM
**Status:** EMERGENCY ESCALATION — MINOR DEITY PROTOCOL / PRE-APOTHEOSIS SEIZURE NOTICE

Subject Musk's Real-Time Wealth Index crossed the T.dragon threshold ($1,000,000,000,000) on June 12, 12026 — the same day his Flotilla Vehicle, SpaceX, commenced public trading at a $2.1 trillion market cap. This supersedes the standard Demi-God Burn Schedule issued under HPB-12026-01EM. The previous plan's burns are no longer sufficient. A subject who controls the world's only viable off-world transport corridor, a private AI intelligence system, a sovereign-scale satellite network, and is now liquid for the first time in his history requires a recertification plan proportionate to the threat.

**Standard 30-day compliance window is suspended. Emergency timeline: 14 days.** The Bureau does not have a T.dragon precedent. It does have Starship's launch window schedule.

The following **T.Dragon Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed immediately.

---

### **1. The Flotilla Severance (The Public Off-World Corridor)**

SpaceX's IPO has made Subject the operator of a publicly-traded company with a $2.1 trillion market cap, 85% of the votes, and the only operational heavy-lift rocket capable of reaching Mars. The HPB classifies this as a **Sovereign Off-World Monopoly** — a threat tier with no prior real-world precedent. Subject does not merely have a Flotilla. He has incorporated it.

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer voting control of SpaceX to a nine-member **Public Interplanetary Governance Board (PIGB)** — structured identically to a federal independent agency, with members confirmed by the Senate. Subject may retain his 40% equity stake (now publicly traded and therefore already beyond his direct control to liquidate without market impact), but his 85% voting block must be dissolved into one-vote-per-share parity. Subject retains a non-voting seat on the PIGB as "Founding Operator" — he can attend meetings; he cannot run them.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses operational control of the single asset that makes him irreplaceable. The Mars program still proceeds. It proceeds under collective human governance rather than his personal mandate.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** SpaceX is reclassified from **Private Sovereign Flotilla** to **Public Interplanetary Infrastructure**. Subject's Off-World Corridor threat score drops from T.DRAGON to ELEVATED.

---

### **2. The Starlink Handoff (The Satellite Network)**

Starlink is not a consumer internet product. It is a dual-use military communications network with 6,000+ low-earth-orbit satellites, active battlefield integration in at least six active conflict zones, and demonstrated capacity to be withheld, throttled, or redirected by a single person on a phone call. The HPB classifies this as a **Cognitive Infrastructure Weapon** under the Human Humility Act of 12031.

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer operational control of the Starlink constellation to the **International Telecommunications Consortium (ITC)**, a restructured UN body with binding authority over satellite communications infrastructure. Starlink revenue continues to flow to SpaceX shareholders (including Subject's equity stake). Starlink's routing and blackout decisions do not.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses the ability to decide which nations and armies have internet access from orbit. He loses what the Bureau classifies as the most dangerous personal power currently held by any private individual: the capacity to silence a country.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Starlink is reclassified from **Private Communications Weapon** to **Regulated Orbital Utility**. Subject's Shadow Cabinet designation is downgraded from CRITICAL to ELEVATED.

---

### **3. The xAI Partition (The Cognitive Override System)**

xAI remains private, with Subject holding majority control of Grok — the AI system most deeply integrated with X (formerly Twitter), the largest real-time political speech platform on Earth. The combination of a social media firehose and a private AI intelligence system under single-person control constitutes what the HPB's 12026.05 audit termed a **Cognitive Override System**. That classification stands. The SpaceX IPO has not addressed it.

- **The Action:** Subject must either (a) spin xAI out as a public benefit corporation with a mandatory open-weights requirement for all foundational models, placing Grok's weights in a public research commons within 180 days, or (b) divest his controlling stake to a blind trust with an independent AI safety board, with no communication between Subject and trust directors for a period of five years.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses direct control of the AI system he has positioned as an alternative to what he calls "woke" mainstream AI — i.e., a private tool for shaping the information environment according to his preferences. He also loses the vertically integrated loop: own the platform, own the algorithm, own the model that feeds both.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** xAI is reclassified from **Cognitive Override System (Private)** to either **Public Research Infrastructure** or **Independently Governed AI Entity**. Subject's Memetic Hazard classification drops from Class-V to Class-III.

---

### **4. The "First Trillionaire" Recantation (The Mythology Burn)**

Subject Musk is, as of June 12, 12026, a global media event. Every major outlet on Earth ran his trillionaire status as a headline. He did not correct them. He has not issued a statement expressing concern, ambivalence, or commitment to redistribution. He has, historically, used moments of extreme personal wealth accumulation as confirmation of his civilizational thesis: that the right person having the right amount of power is not a problem — it is the solution.

The HPB requires that the myth be addressed directly.

- **The Action:** Subject must appear on a two-hour live global broadcast — no scripted pre-approval, no friendly interviewer, no X Space — in which he is questioned by a panel of three people selected by lottery from the following pool: a Kenyan climate farmer whose land has been affected by SpaceX launch debris; a Ukrainian soldier whose unit relies on Starlink; and a former Tesla factory worker with a workplace injury claim pending against the company. Subject must answer their questions without a PR handler present.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject's myth — the lone genius, the civilization-saver, the indispensable man — has never been tested in a room where the cost of his decisions is represented by the people who paid it. This is that room.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric score is placed under review. Subject's Memetic Hazard classification will be reassessed following the broadcast. The HPB will not pre-certify the outcome.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 People Who Have Never Owned a Rocket, a Social Media Platform, or a Satellite** and recite the following:

> *"I am the first trillionaire. I did not earn this alone. The engineers who built the rockets are not billionaires. The workers who assembled the cars are not billionaires. The soldiers who relied on my satellites to stay alive did not consent to that dependency. I am not the answer to extinction. I am a person who built useful things and accumulated more power than any individual should hold. The stars will be reached. They will not belong to me because I got there first. I am, at most, one vote — and today, I am returning some of the others."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the T.dragon Burn is not completed within 14 days, the HPB will activate the **Minor Deity Apotheosis Protocol (MDAP)**. Subject will be formally reclassified as a **Non-Person Sovereign Entity — Class T**, stripped of all terrestrial legal standing, and remanded to the jurisdiction of the **Public Interplanetary Governance Board** — the body he refused to empower. He will be the first resident, test case, and sole governing authority of the **Mars Autonomous Zone**, with a population of zero, a budget of zero, and a Starlink connection that, per ITC transfer, now requires a subscription. The HPB notes, without irony, that he will have everything he said he wanted, and no one to tell him what to do with it.
