### **1. The Lebanon Reconstruction Burn**

The Saadé family are Franco-Lebanese. Lebanon's economic collapse and the 2020 Beirut port explosion represent a humanitarian crisis in the family's country of origin. The Beirut port blast occurred at Lebanon's main port — a facility through which CMA CGM ships regularly passed.

- **The Action:** Subject must commit $200M to the **Saadé Lebanon Reconstruction Fund** — administered independently, not through existing Saadé family foundations, with Lebanese civil society and international NGO co-governance: (a) $75M for Beirut port district reconstruction and community restoration (the neighborhoods most severely damaged in the 2020 explosion); (b) $75M for Lebanese public education — specifically, public school infrastructure and teacher salaries in a system that has collapsed under economic crisis; (c) $50M for Lebanese healthcare access — rural clinics and hospital supply chains in regions outside Beirut.
- **The Sacrifice:** $200M.

### **2. The Foundation Independence Burn**

The Fondation CMA CGM and Saadé Foundation exist but are structurally tied to the family's commercial interests. The HPB asks for a genuinely independent philanthropic vehicle.

- **The Action:** Subject must endow an independent **Fondation Tanya Saadé Zeenny** — $100M — governed by a fully independent board (no CMA CGM employees, no family employees in governance roles), with a mandate chosen by Tanya herself but published publicly and subject to annual independent audit. This foundation must be permanent — endowed, not annual-giving-dependent — and must continue to operate beyond any CMA CGM ownership transition.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M.
