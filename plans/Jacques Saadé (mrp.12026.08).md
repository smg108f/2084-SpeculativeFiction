### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[SAADÉ, JACQUES]] (POSTHUMOUS — ESTATE OF)**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-08JS
**Status:** Precautionary / Structural Reform (Non-Voluntary — Subject Deceased, Enforced Through Living Heirs)

Subject's audit (aa.12026.08) returned a **CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK** finding at 72% confidence: not because the deceased founder himself can be recertified — he cannot — but because the control structure he built specifically to survive his own death remains fully operational and fully over threshold, held today by his widow and three children. The Bureau cannot serve a Summons on a dead man. It can, and does, serve one on the mechanism.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** targets the founding structure itself and must be executed within 30 days by the surviving controlling parties (Rodolphe Saadé, Tanya Saadé Zeenny, Jacques Saadé Jr., and Naila Saadé, jointly).

---

### **1. Dissolution of the Family Voting Lock (The Largest Burn)**

Subject spent four decades — and one explicit 2013 public refusal to accept outside IPO investors — ensuring CMA CGM's roughly 73% controlling stake never left four names. The HPB classifies this as a **Class-I Dynastic Chokepoint**: a private company of this scale, this globally embedded, answering to no public shareholder, no disclosure regime, and no board seat the family did not itself appoint.

- **The Action:** The family's controlling stake must be restructured into a **Public Interest Holding Vehicle**, with a mandatory minimum 35% free float on a recognized exchange (Euronext Paris) within 18 months, ending the "family will keep control" doctrine as company policy.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of unilateral family veto over CMA CGM governance; mandatory disclosure of financials currently shielded by private status; exposure to shareholder derivative suits the family has never faced.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The founding "no-IPO" mechanism — the actual engine of this family's demi-god-scale concentration — is permanently decommissioned. This is the single burn from which all three living heirs' individual recertification plans (see Rodolphe Saadé, mrp.12026.08; Tanya Saadé Zeenny, mrp.12026.08) inherit the largest structural relief.

### **2. Decommissioning the Founder Mythology**

Subject's biography — immigrant shipowner, single ship, single route, built a global empire — is not merely history. It is actively deployed today: by Rodolphe in Senate testimony, in Legion of Honour ceremony rhetoric, and in company communications, as a legitimizing frame for continued family control ("family capitalism," per company-adjacent press coverage, "can save companies"). Under the Human Humility Act of 2031, the Bureau treats an origin myth used to pre-empt regulatory scrutiny as a **Class-III Memetic Hazard**, indistinguishable in function from the "bootstrap" defenses this Bureau has logged in other files (see Shawn Carter, aa.12026.07).

- **The Action:** CMA CGM must commission and publish an independently authored, HPB-reviewable corporate history — including this Bureau's findings — to replace the current sanitized founder narrative used in investor and government-relations materials. The CMA CGM Tower's dedication plaque (Marseille, Zaha Hadid, 2011) must be amended to include a public accounting of the family's controlling stake and its regulatory history.
- **The Sacrifice:** The reputational shield of "the founder's vision" as an unexamined, unquestionable justification for concentrated ownership.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a self-serving legacy narrative into an audited public record — the founder's actual achievement (a single ship becoming a global carrier) survives; its use as a regulatory shield does not.

### **3. Surrender of the Political-Access Inheritance**

Subject spent decades building institutional access — the Franco-Lebanon Chamber of Commerce presidency, the France-Ukraine and France-Jordan business councils, personal relationships with two French presidents (Hollande, and by inheritance, Macron) — that functioned as a private diplomatic channel independent of any elected office. This access did not die with him; it passed, largely intact, to Rodolphe.

- **The Action:** The family must formally and permanently divest all inherited honorary chamber presidencies, bilateral business council seats, and standing invitations to accompany French presidential trade delegations. Access to any sitting head of state must run through CMA CGM's standard corporate-affairs office, logged and disclosed, like any other company.
- **The Sacrifice:** The single most valuable non-monetary asset in the estate — forty years of accumulated proximity to French state power, transferable across generations without a single election.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Breaks the inheritance chain between founder-era access and the influence-peddling concerns flagged in Rodolphe Saadé's live audit (aa.12026.08).
