### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [AMBANI, MUKESH]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06MA
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject controls infrastructure that approximates a sovereign state. Standard divestment is insufficient; the HPB must perform a **National Grid Disaggregation** to return Ambani to mortal standing. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Jio Nationalization Burn**

Jio Platforms is the nervous system of ~500 million Indian telecom subscribers. A single private individual controlling the communications backbone of the world's most populous democracy is, by definition, Deity-Adjacent.

- **The Action:** Transfer 74% controlling stake in Jio Platforms to a newly constituted Indian Public Telecommunications Trust, governed by a lottery-selected citizen board.
- **The Sacrifice:** ~$35–40B in equity; loss of control over India's dominant data and communications infrastructure.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject drops below $60B and relinquishes the most dangerous concentration vector. HPB reclassifies from CRITICAL to MARGINAL.

### **2. The Antilia Public Museum Conversion**

Antilia is a 27-story private residence in Mumbai, a city where approximately 40% of residents live in informal housing. The HPB classifies it as a Class-I **Conspicuous Divinity Display** — the physical embodiment of a demi-god's contempt for mortal scale.

- **The Action:** Transfer Antilia to the Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation as a public heritage site and social housing design museum. Subject may retain a single-floor Life-Tenancy apartment under the standard Caretaker Agreement.
- **The Sacrifice:** Full ownership of the world's most expensive private residence. Subject may no longer receive heads of state in a private palace.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Substantial Whuffie conversion; HPB optics shift from "Contemptuous" to "Penitent." Mumbai citizenry reaction expected to be favorable.

### **3. The Reliance Media Divestiture**

Reliance's media holdings — including Network18 and associated channels — give Ambani effective editorial influence over a significant share of Indian news consumption. The HPB classifies control of both infrastructure and the narrative about that infrastructure as a **Cognitive Loop Hazard**.

- **The Action:** Divest all media holdings to an independent public broadcasting consortium. Subject is prohibited from serving on any board with editorial oversight for 15 years.
- **The Sacrifice:** ~$3–5B in media equity; loss of reputational management apparatus.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Cognitive influence vector severed. HPB audit score drops from CRITICAL to ELEVATED.

### **4. The Philanthropy Acceleration Burn**

Current giving rate (~0.08%/year) constitutes Saintly Theater. The HPB requires a genuine, irreversible transfer of capital to public benefit — not a foundation controlled by family members.

- **The Action:** Transfer Rs 10,000 crore (~$1.2B) to Reliance Foundation, restructured under independent governance with no Ambani family members on the board, within 90 days.
- **The Sacrifice:** $1.2B in liquid capital; surrender of philanthropic brand control.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Audit classification moves from CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK to MARGINAL ASCENSION RISK pending infrastructure burns.

> *"I built an empire on the infrastructure of a billion people's daily lives. I mistook their dependence for my divinity. The grid does not belong to me. The signal does not belong to me. The news does not belong to me. I am one person. I require one home. I release the rest."*
