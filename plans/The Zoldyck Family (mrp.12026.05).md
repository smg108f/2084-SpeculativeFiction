### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [THE ZOLDYCK FAMILY]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09-ZF
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice / Extreme Enforcement Hazard — Caution Advised

The Zoldyck Family has been classified CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK based on a $6 trillion privately held asset base, a client network providing structural immunity from standard enforcement mechanisms, and a multi-generational conditioning program that optimizes each family member for lethality and evasion. The HPB's General Counsel has reviewed this plan. The HPB's General Counsel has requested combat pay. The request is under review.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. Cessation of Assassination Services and Business Dissolution**

The Zoldyck Family's primary revenue stream is the commission of homicide. The HPB's recertification framework does not include a mechanism for licensing or restructuring a murder-for-hire enterprise as a public benefit organization. It includes a mechanism for dissolution. The Bureau requires that the family's assassination service operations cease permanently, that all outstanding contracts be voided, and that all contractual relationships with clients be terminated.

- **The Action:** The Zoldyck Family must formally dissolve its assassination service operations, issue written notification of contract termination to all active clients, and submit to a 24-month operational monitoring period during which any resumption of services would trigger immediate Apotheosis Protocol activation. The family retains all non-operational assets (real estate, financial holdings, personal property). It may no longer operate as a for-profit assassination enterprise.
- **The Sacrifice:** The family's primary income stream — the only business they have operated for generations — is permanently closed. The Zoldycks must find something else to do. The HPB notes, without judgment, that their particular skill set has significant applications in security, physical training, and consulting that do not involve homicide.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Revenue-generating capacity classified as below-threshold pending 24-month verification. RTWI drops by an estimated $800 billion in annual revenue capitalization.

---

### **2. Kukuroo Mountain — Partial Demilitarization and Jurisdictional Recognition**

Kukuroo Mountain's physical infrastructure — which includes weapons systems, defensive architecture, trained security personnel, and what the HPB's Military Assessment Division describes as "a functional fortress operating outside any national legal framework" — constitutes a Sovereign Flotilla State equivalent in everything but the legal designation. The Bureau requires that the compound's military infrastructure be reduced to a level consistent with private residential security rather than small-nation defense.

- **The Action:** The Zoldyck Family must decommission all offensive weapons systems on Kukuroo Mountain, reduce security personnel to a ratio consistent with HPB residential security guidelines (maximum 1 armed guard per 500 square meters of residential property), and formally register the compound's territorial boundaries with the relevant national jurisdiction — acknowledging that Kukuroo Mountain is subject to that jurisdiction's laws, including tax obligations and building codes.
- **The Sacrifice:** The family loses its most important physical protection: the operational invulnerability of a compound that no external authority has previously been willing to challenge. They retain their home. They lose their fortress.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes the Sovereign Flotilla equivalent classification from the risk profile. Kukuroo Mountain becomes, in legal terms, a very large private residence.

---

### **3. Client Disclosure — Confidential HPB Registry**

The Zoldyck Family's client network is the most significant obstacle to accountability not only for the family itself but for the power structures that have used their services. The Bureau does not require public disclosure of client identities — the HPB's Intelligence Division has made a pragmatic determination that a public list would create instability disproportionate to any enforcement benefit. The Bureau does require confidential disclosure to a sealed HPB registry, which will be used to inform the Bureau's ongoing monitoring of other subjects.

- **The Action:** The Zoldyck Family must provide a full confidential accounting of all clients served in the past 20 years to a sealed HPB registry accessible only to the Director and two designated senior auditors. The registry will not be used for prosecution of clients except in cases involving ongoing crimes against HPB personnel or the democratic process. It will be used to inform the Bureau's risk assessments of other subjects.
- **The Sacrifice:** The family surrenders the fundamental promise it has made to every client: absolute secrecy. Even sealed, the existence of the registry removes the guarantee of perfect discretion that has been the family's most important commercial asset.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes the Clientele Shield classification from the risk profile. The Bureau gains intelligence. The family gains a compliance record that can support a future CLEARED determination.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, the Zoldyck Family patriarch must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 individuals with no prior knowledge of or relationship to the Zoldyck Family** — ordinary civilians selected from across the Jump Universe, with identities protected by the HPB — and recite the following:

> _"The Zoldyck name has meant one thing for generations: we will do what others cannot bring themselves to do, and we will do it perfectly, and we will never speak of it. We called this professionalism. We called it discipline. We called it family. The Bureau calls it a $6 trillion enterprise built on the premise that some lives are worth ending for the right price. We do not dispute the accounting. We dispute only the idea that our skill obligated us to this use of it. It did not. We chose it. We are choosing differently now."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Asset Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol — Assassination Enterprise Tier**, which involves the simultaneous public release of the sealed client registry (with appropriate redactions for national security), the referral of all documented contract activity to international criminal prosecution authorities, and the formal designation of Kukuroo Mountain as a Hostile Non-State Actor compound under the UN Security Council's Jump Universe Jurisdiction Extension (Resolution 12024-7). The HPB notes that this outcome is bad for the Zoldyck Family. It is also, candidly, bad for approximately three hundred of the most powerful entities in the world, which is why the Bureau is offering the sealed registry option as an alternative. The HPB prefers the quiet path. So, the Bureau suspects, does everyone involved.
