### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [WULIANGYE YIBIN]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-08WLY
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification

Wuliangye Yibin has been classified as a **Marginal Ascension Risk** with a **Passive Political Infrastructure** designation. As a Marginal case, voluntary recertification is the preferred path. Wuliangye's cultural significance warrants preservation; its role as a political lubricant for concentrated power requires restructuring.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The State Procurement Disentanglement (The Political Burn)**

The HPB classifies Wuliangye's state institution sales channel — government agencies, state-owned enterprises, and party event procurement — as a **Fiscal-Political Entanglement** that converts public funds into relationship capital for power-holders.

- **The Action:** Wuliangye must refuse all direct state procurement orders and government entertainment budget purchases, redirecting those sales channels to independent commercial distributors with no government institutional clients.
- **The Sacrifice:** Estimated 15–20% revenue reduction in the premium tier that derives from state procurement.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Fiscal-Political Entanglement classification is lifted. Wuliangye becomes a commercial beverage company that happens to be consumed by powerful people, rather than a supplier embedded in the state's social machinery.

### **2. The Yibin Heritage Commons (The Cultural Burn)**

The 3,000-year-old fermentation heritage of Yibin — the complex of ancient cellars, cultivation relationships, and traditional knowledge that underlies Wuliangye's product — is a cultural asset that Wuliangye has privatized.

- **The Action:** Transfer the historic Yibin distillery complex and all traditional fermentation knowledge documentation to a **Sichuan Cultural Heritage Commons Trust**, with Wuliangye retaining exclusive commercial rights under a 99-year lease — but the underlying heritage belonging to the public.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of outright ownership of irreplaceable cultural assets; ongoing lease obligations.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Wuliangye becomes a **Steward** of public heritage rather than its owner.

### **3. The Sichuan Agricultural Equity Program (The Community Burn)**

Wuliangye's premium pricing depends on sorghum varieties grown by smallholder farmers in Sichuan Province, who receive minimal share of the final product value.

- **The Action:** Establish a ¥20B **Sichuan Grain Farmers Profit-Share Trust**, entitling Wuliangye's contracted agricultural suppliers to 10% of premium product revenue on a permanent basis.
- **The Sacrifice:** Ongoing margin compression in the premium tier.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The value chain exploitation that underwrites Wuliangye's premium pricing is acknowledged and partially remediated.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Wuliangye's board must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Sichuan Sorghum Farmers** and recite the following:

> _"The earth of Yibin made this spirit before we did. The farmers who grew the grain made this spirit with us. The ancient cellars beneath our distillery belong to a civilization, not a corporation. We sell a drink. We do not own a culture. We return what was never ours to keep."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol** and recommend to the Humanist Bloc's China Policy Division that Wuliangye be reclassified as a **State Ritual Infrastructure Asset** — which, the HPB notes with some dryness, is what Wuliangye effectively already is, and which would result in the Chinese government acquiring full ownership without compensation. The current shareholders may find this outcome less desirable than voluntary recertification.
