### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[DROGO, KHAL]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-08-KD
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice — Executor Compliance Required

Subject's audit (aa.12026.08) returned a **CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK** finding, driven almost entirely by a single marital asset line: three dragon eggs, gifted at Subject's own wedding, that have since — verifiably, not hypothetically — hatched into three living dragons. Subject is deceased, which under normal HPB procedure would close this file the way it closed the Brennus and Attila files. It does not close this one, because Subject's principal heir and widow, **Daenerys Stormborn Targaryen**, inherited both his remaining khalasar and direct physical custody of the asset in question, and is very much alive, airborne-asset-adjacent, and not currently below the threshold. This plan is therefore executed against Subject's estate, with Subject's widow standing as executor and, the Bureau notes for the record, principal remaining party of interest.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days by Subject's estate, or by Subject's widow acting in her capacity as executor.

---

### **1. The Khalasar Dispersal Order (The Largest Burn, Already Mostly Completed by Circumstance)**

Subject's khalasar of 40,000 riders — the largest assembled in a generation — represented a private military-logistics concentration the Bureau would ordinarily order broken up under armed HPB escort. The Bureau's field notes indicate this has, in practical terms, already happened: per available accounts, the bulk of the khalasar dispersed and scattered to rival khals within days of Subject's death, as is customary Dothraki practice for a khal who "cannot ride." Only a small remnant — several dozen riders, three bloodriders, and assorted household dependents — remained loyal to the widow.

- **The Action:** The Bureau formally ratifies the dispersal that already occurred, issues retroactive dissolution paperwork for the record, and closes the "40,000 riders" line item as a Bureau-recognized, non-recoverable asset loss rather than a live concentration hazard.
- **The Sacrifice:** None, technically — the khalasar dispersed on its own, following its own laws, faster than the Bureau's paperwork could ever have moved. The Bureau notes this is the first Burn in its history that completed itself before the Bureau finished drafting it.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes ~$0.9B–$1.5B in horse-and-plunder logistics value from the estate's ledger. This alone would have been sufficient for a CLEARED finding, had it been the only line item.

### **2. The Dragon Egg Forfeiture Order (The Burn That Arrived Too Late)**

This is, by a wide margin, the largest single line item this office has ever attempted to burn, and the Bureau must report, with some professional embarrassment, that it cannot be executed as written. The Asset Burn Schedule calls for forfeiture of the three dragon eggs to the HPB's Office of Extinct & Mythological Megafauna prior to any hatching event, on the standard theory that a Class-I strategic asset should be surrendered before it activates, not after. By the time this plan reached drafting, the eggs had already been placed on Subject's own funeral pyre by his widow, alongside the body of the healer responsible for his death, and had hatched into three live dragons in the resulting fire — a ritual the Bureau's Weapons of Mythological Destruction Desk did not anticipate, was not consulted on, and would very much like it on the record that it did not authorize.

- **The Action:** Forfeiture of the eggs to Bureau custody, as originally scheduled.
- **The Sacrifice:** Cannot be assessed. The asset in question no longer exists in a forfeitable form — it exists in three forms, all of which can fly and one of which reportedly does not like strangers.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** None achievable under this filing. The Bureau formally notes that Subject's own cremation was the proximate cause of the exact asset-concentration event this Burn was drafted to prevent, and refers the now-independent dragon asset class to a forthcoming, separate file under Subject's widow's own name.

### **3. Renunciation of the "Stallion Who Mounts the World" Succession Prophecy**

Subject's unborn son, Rhaego, was the subject of an active in-universe prophecy naming him the eventual uniter of every khalasar on the Dothraki Sea and conqueror of everything beyond it. Rhaego was stillborn, a casualty of the same ritual that killed Subject, meaning the Bureau is in the unusual position of needing to formally retire a succession threat whose principal never drew breath.

- **The Action:** The estate issues a formal renunciation of the Rhaego succession prophecy, filed with the Dothraki oral record (such as it is) via the Dosh Khaleen at Vaes Dothrak, stating plainly that no heir uniting the khalasars under Subject's bloodline exists, was born, or is expected.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject's household loses its single largest piece of political mythology — the promise that this bloodline was destined to rule the world was, by a wide margin, the most valuable thing Subject's name was ever attached to, and it is being formally struck from the record on a technicality of biology.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Retires a Class-II Dormant Succession Hazard that, left unaddressed, would have remained available for any future claimant wishing to invoke it on Subject's behalf.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's widow and executor must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 members of the Dosh Khaleen** (the crone-council of widowed khaleesis at Vaes Dothrak, the customary body for exactly this kind of proceeding) and recite, on Subject's behalf, the following:

> _"A khal who cannot ride is no khal. He could not ride. He is no khal. He owned no gold, no house, no ship, and no throne beyond the black salt sea — only horses, and a wife, and a wedding gift he never understood the weight of. Let the stallion who mounts the world stay unborn. Let the herds go to whichever khal can hold them. Let the fire that took him keep whatever it made of him, and let the rest of us walk back down out of the fire on two legs, and call that enough."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If this Burn Schedule is not executed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol** and formally merge this file into the pending audit for Subject's widow, Daenerys Stormborn Targaryen — a merger the Bureau expects would happen automatically regardless of compliance, since she is, as of this filing, in physical possession of the only asset in this entire file that actually matters. The Bureau notes this is likely the correct outcome. It rarely gets to say that about one of its own Failure clauses.
