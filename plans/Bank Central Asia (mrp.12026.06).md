### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [BANK CENTRAL ASIA]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-24BBCA
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice

Bank Central Asia's Private Financial Sovereign classification — 54% Hartono family control of Indonesia's primary commercial banking franchise, constituting a national financial emergency risk if it fails — requires recertification that addresses dynastic ownership concentration over critical financial infrastructure.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Ownership Dilution Mandate (The Dynasty Burn)**

The HPB classifies the Hartono family's 54% stake in BCA as a **Family Financial Sovereign**: a single family controls the private financial backbone of the world's fourth most populous nation.

- **The Action:** The Hartono family must reduce their BCA stake to below 25% within 36 months through a structured public offering, with preferential share allocations to Indonesian citizens, Indonesian pension funds, and provincial government investment entities — not foreign institutional investors.
- **The Sacrifice:** Hartonos lose majority control of BCA and the financial leverage that control provides to their other Djarum Group businesses.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** BCA's Private Financial Sovereign classification is removed; it becomes an institution owned substantially by the people whose transactions it processes.

### **2. The Financial Inclusion Acceleration (The Public Good Burn)**

The HPB notes that BCA's superior digital banking infrastructure serves Indonesia's urban commercial sector disproportionately well while rural and eastern Indonesian populations remain relatively underserved.

- **The Action:** BCA must implement a binding **Eastern Indonesia Financial Inclusion Mandate**: 20% of annual net lending growth must be directed to provinces in Sulawesi, Maluku, Papua, and Nusa Tenggara at interest rates not exceeding 3% above the most favorable rates available in Jakarta. A $1B rural banking infrastructure fund must be established.
- **The Sacrifice:** Approximately $200M annual margin dilution from below-market rural lending.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** BCA's Financial Archipelago classification is broadened from commercial backbone to genuine national financial infrastructure.

### **3. The Djarum Cross-Subsidy Audit (The Relationship Burn)**

The HPB classifies the financial relationship between BCA and the Hartono family's Djarum cigarette and other business interests as a potential **Captive Bank Arrangement**: using control of a public deposit-taking institution to preferentially finance family businesses.

- **The Action:** Full public disclosure of all BCA lending relationships with Djarum Group entities, with a mandatory 10-year public register going back from audit date. All future Djarum Group lending from BCA must be subject to external credit committee approval with no Hartono-related directors participating.
- **The Sacrifice:** Transparency about family business financing; potential exposure of preferential terms.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Captive Bank flag is resolved or escalated to criminal referral, depending on audit findings.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Robert and Michael Hartono must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Papuan subsistence farmers and Jakarta factory workers** and recite the following:

> _"We own the bank that processes most of Indonesia's money. We also make cigarettes. We fund badminton. The Hartono family's remaining stake will be acquired at assessed fair value. The Bureau notes that "fair value" will be calculated after the Djarum cross-subsidy audit results are published.
