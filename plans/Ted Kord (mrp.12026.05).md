### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [KORD, TED]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-14-TK
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification / Cooperative Engagement Expected

Subject Kord has been classified MARGINAL ASCENSION RISK based on a $3 trillion Kord Industries valuation, a defense R&D concentration the Bureau believes is more the result of market structure than personal preference, and a philanthropic record that is genuine but insufficient in scale. The HPB anticipates voluntary cooperation and has designed this plan accordingly — it is structured as a facilitation rather than a compulsion, on the premise that Subject has the values and the capability to complete recertification without enforcement escalation. The Bureau is, for the second time in this audit cycle, trying to help a subject do what he already wants to do.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. Kord Industries R&D Portfolio — Civilian Application Priority Mandate**

The HPB's Technology Assessment Division has reviewed Subject's personal patent record and concludes that his inventive output has been systematically commercialized toward defense applications at a ratio that does not reflect his stated preferences or his humanitarian engineering track record. Market incentives have driven defense weighting. The Bureau requires that Subject restructure Kord Industries' commercialization priorities to give civilian applications genuine first-consideration status.

- **The Action:** Subject must restructure Kord Industries' R&D commercialization process to implement a Civilian First Review: all new technologies must be evaluated for civilian humanitarian application before defense commercialization is pursued. Any technology with significant potential civilian humanitarian use must be offered for Dead-Hand licensing at publicly accessible rates before defense-exclusive contracts are signed. Subject may maintain defense contracting — he must simply ensure that civilian access comes first.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject foregoes first-mover defense exclusivity on any technology with meaningful civilian applications. Revenue per innovation decreases as exclusivity is reduced. The benefit delivered per innovation increases substantially.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Dual-Use R&D classification reclassified from Concentration Hazard to Managed Risk. HPB Optics Rating upgrades from MARGINAL to CLEAR on the technology dimension.

---

### **2. Kord Foundation — Scale-Up and Independence**

Subject's philanthropic operation is genuine but undersized. The Kord Foundation's annual disbursements represent approximately 0.5% of Kord Industries' revenue. Subject has stated publicly that he believes this is insufficient. The HPB agrees and is formalizing that agreement as a compliance requirement.

- **The Action:** Subject must endow the Kord Foundation with $1 trillion from Kord Industries liquid assets, restructure it with an independent board (majority community representatives, no Kord family financial ties), and commit to a minimum 5% annual revenue philanthropy target going forward. The Foundation's focus areas — STEM education, disaster response technology, and community engineering — are appropriate and may be maintained; the scale is what must change.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses $1 trillion in corporate liquid reserves and accepts an ongoing giving commitment that constrains future capital allocation. The Foundation stops being a modest expression of good intentions and becomes a major institution.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Philanthropy classification upgrades from Insufficient to Genuine Structural Commitment. RTWI decreases by $1 trillion. Subject's personal profile moves from MARGINAL to approaching CLEARED.

---

### **3. Blue Beetle Operation — Transparent Nonprofit Structure**

Subject's vigilante operation must be brought into an accountable legal framework. Unlike the Wayne case, this is a relatively modest ask — the Blue Beetle operation is smaller in scale and, based on Subject's demonstrated values, likely to welcome rather than resist public accountability.

- **The Action:** Subject establishes the Kord Public Safety Initiative as a registered nonprofit, funded by a $25 million annual endowment from the Kord Foundation, with public disclosure of operations, expenditures, and outcomes. Subject retains operational discretion on individual missions; aggregate resource use and safety records become publicly reported on an annual basis.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject accepts public accountability for a personal project. The Blue Beetle operation stops being a purely private decision and becomes a community institution with a transparent track record.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Covert Operations Subsidy classification removed. Subject's personal RTWI with the Foundation endowment reduction falls to approximately $800 million — below threshold pending verification.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 STEM students** from schools in communities where Kord Industries has defense manufacturing facilities — students whose educational path may lead them to work on the same technologies Subject has been wondering about the uses of — and recite the following:

> _"I have spent my career inventing things and watching what they become. Some of what I made helped people. Some of it helped people hurt other people. I told myself I couldn't control what the market did with my work once it left my hands. That was partly true and partly convenient. I had more control than I used. I am restructuring how this company decides what to build for, so that the next generation of engineers who come up through Kord Industries learns to ask 'who does this help' before 'who will pay for this.' I should have built that question in from the start. I'm building it in now."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** The HPB does not anticipate needing to detail enforcement escalation for Subject Kord. In the event of unexpected non-compliance, standard MARGINAL ASCENSION RISK enforcement procedures apply. The Bureau notes, for the record, that it would be genuinely surprised by this outcome and would update its behavioral models accordingly.
