### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ZHONG, SHANSHAN]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06ZS
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject controls essential biological infrastructure — drinking water and biological diagnostics — at a scale that constitutes a public health dependency. The HPB has classified this as an **Essential Services Monopoly** requiring immediate disaggregation. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Nongfu Spring Public Water Trust Burn**

An 84.5% stake in a nation's dominant bottled water brand is, in the HPB's classification, indistinguishable from owning the rain. Drinking water is not a product. It is a human right that the subject has, without particular malice, turned into a quarterly earnings event.

- **The Action:** Divest 69.5% of the Nongfu Spring stake to a **China Public Water Utility Trust**, retaining 15% as a personal holding. The Trust to be governed by provincial water authorities and citizen health advocates.
- **The Sacrifice:** ~$47B in equity. Control of China's most recognizable beverage brand passes to public governance.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject drops below $30B. HPB reclassifies from CRITICAL to MARGINAL ASCENSION RISK. The Bureau acknowledges this will be structurally disruptive to the Chinese bottled water market; this is the point.

### **2. The Wantai Biologics Public Health Burn**

A 73% stake in a vaccine and hepatitis diagnostics company means the subject's continued wellbeing is structurally aligned with public health outcomes — an alignment that the HPB finds philosophically inappropriate. Public health infrastructure should not be a private profit center.

- **The Action:** Transfer 58% of the Wantai stake to a **National Biological Security Trust** under joint public health administration, retaining 15% personal stake. Subject to have no operational control over vaccine pricing or distribution decisions.
- **The Sacrifice:** ~$5–8B in equity and operational control of a diagnostic and vaccine infrastructure company.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes the **Biological Dependency Hazard** classification. Public access to hepatitis diagnostics is no longer a private shareholder decision.

### **3. The Whuffie Emergency Burn**

Subject has accumulated zero philanthropic capital over a career spanning three decades of extraordinary wealth creation. In the HPB's Whuffie accounting, this is a **Social Credit Debt** — an absence so conspicuous it constitutes a risk factor. A demi-god with no community goodwill has no buffer when the Bureau comes calling.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Zhong Foundation for Rural Water Infrastructure** with an endowment of no less than ¥10 billion (~$1.4B), focused on clean water access in Chinese provinces underserved by municipal systems. Independent governance; no Zhong family members on the board.
- **The Sacrifice:** $1.4B in liquid capital; mandatory public profile. The Lone Wolf must be named on something.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts zero Whuffie into a measurable social credit surplus. HPB optics shift from "Invisible Utility Owner" to "Reluctant Public Benefactor."

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Rural Farmers, Municipal Water Workers, and Village Health Clinic Staff** and recite the following:

> *"I found a spring. I put it in a bottle. I sold it to people who were thirsty and called it innovation. The spring was not mine to keep. The thirst was not mine to monetize. I return the water to the water. I remain a man who once sold it — nothing more."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the Apotheosis Protocol. Subject will be reclassified as **Nongfu Demi-God Entity NDE-001** and relocated to a Sovereign Flotilla designated **FSS Lone Wolf** — a single-occupancy research vessel in the South China Sea stocked with an unlimited supply of Nongfu Spring water, with no one else aboard to sell it to.
