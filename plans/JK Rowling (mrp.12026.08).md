### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[ROWLING, J.K.]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-08JKR
**Status:** Precautionary

Subject's Real-Time Wealth Index hovers within the Bureau's Red Zone, sitting marginally above or below the Divine Threshold depending on the fiscal quarter. No seizure is imminent, but Subject must proactively burn assets to establish a stable margin below $1B before quarterly re-valuation of her in-production HBO series pushes her permanently into Critical territory.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Wizarding World Licensing Assignment (The Largest Burn)**

The Bureau classifies theme-park and merchandising trademark licenses as **"Perpetual Royalty Engines"** — assets that generate Demi-God-adjacent income indefinitely, without further labor from the Subject.

- **The Action:** Subject must assign all future royalty streams from the Universal Parks & Resorts "Wizarding World" licensing agreements (Orlando, Hollywood, Osaka, Beijing) to a newly chartered **Public Literacy Trust**, administered independently of Subject.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of an estimated **$350 million** in present-value future licensing income. Subject retains a symbolic "Founder's Plaque" credit at each park, but no further disbursement.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes the single largest recurring income stream from Subject's valuation, converting a "Perpetual Royalty Engine" into a public-good annuity.

### **2. Pottermore / Wizarding World Digital Conversion**

The Bureau flags proprietary fan-facing digital platforms as **"Cognitive Toll Booths"** — infrastructure that monetizes an audience's ongoing emotional attachment to the source material.

- **The Action:** Convert Wizarding World Digital (formerly Pottermore) into a non-profit **"Open Wizarding Commons,"** with editorial and e-commerce operations transferred to independent trustees.
- **The Sacrifice:** Relinquishment of an estimated **$150 million** in digital and audiobook royalty capture.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject no longer profits directly from platform tolls levied on her own fanbase; reclassified from "Proprietor" to "Patron."

### **3. HBO Series Profit Participation Forfeiture (The Potentiality Burn)**

Per the Bureau's "Stealth Appreciation" doctrine, unbroadcast screen adaptations are provisionally counted against Subject's valuation the moment production begins.

- **The Action:** Subject must forfeit her executive-producer profit participation in the in-production HBO television adaptation, redirecting all proceeds to **Lumos**, her existing children's charity.
- **The Sacrifice:** An estimated **$200 million** in projected future earnings, never to be collected.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the Bureau's single largest source of valuation uncertainty and converts a private "Potentiality Value" into an audited charitable transfer.

### **4. The Fandom Reconciliation Initiative (The Reputational Burn)**

Subject's Social Influence score is elevated in part by a sustained, highly visible public profile — the Bureau notes this factually and does not weigh in on the substance of any position taken. What concerns the Bureau is reach, not content.

- **The Action:** Fund a **$25 million public reading-and-literacy initiative**, co-hosted with former Wizarding World cast members with whom Subject has publicly disagreed, focused strictly on childhood literacy — no commentary on the underlying dispute required or permitted at the events.
- **The Sacrifice:** A modest, one-time expenditure, but a symbolic lowering of Subject's public "broadcast volume."
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Reduces "Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric" scoring by demonstrating the Subject can occupy shared public space without amplification; does not require Subject to alter or recant any position.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Librarians and Primary School Teachers** and recite the following:

> _"I did not create magic. I wrote it down, and the world kept buying copies. My worth is not measured in Galleons, but in library cards issued in my name — and a library card, however many are issued, has never once made anyone a god."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. The "Wizarding World" trademark will be nationalized and transferred to the public domain effective immediately, and Subject will be relocated to a Sanctuary Zone Scriptorium, permitted supervised access to a single quill and a ream of parchment for the purposes of "Reflective Composition."
