### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [YANAI, TADASHI]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06TY
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject has made the Giving Pledge and believes his company democratizes fashion. The HPB asks him to extend that democratization logic to his supply chain. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Living Wage Supply Chain Burn**

UNIQLO manufactures in countries where garment workers earn wages below what researchers identify as living wage thresholds for their local cost of living. The gap between UNIQLO's retail margin and worker compensation is the primary HPB concern.

- **The Action:** Commit Fast Retailing to achieving independently verified living wages — as defined by the Asia Floor Wage Alliance or equivalent — for workers in all Tier 1 and Tier 2 supplier factories within five years. Accompanied by a published roadmap, independently audited annually.
- **The Sacrifice:** Margin compression; some retail price increases; competitive disadvantage relative to supply chain agnostic fast fashion competitors.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts UNIQLO's "LifeWear for everyone" positioning from narrative to practice. Largest and most structurally significant Burn on this ledger.

### **2. The Supply Chain Transparency Burn**

Fast Retailing publishes supplier lists and sustainability reports. Independent assessment suggests these are incomplete — Tier 2 and Tier 3 suppliers (fabric mills, component manufacturers) are not fully disclosed.

- **The Action:** Publish a complete Tier 1 through Tier 3 supplier database, updated annually, with wage data and labor practice audit results, publicly accessible in English, Japanese, Bangladeshi, and Vietnamese.
- **The Sacrifice:** Competitive opacity; supplier relationship privacy.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts Fast Retailing from a sustainability-reporting company into a supply chain transparency benchmark.

### **3. The Giving Pledge Acceleration Burn**

Yanai signed the Giving Pledge in 2023. The HPB supports this commitment and asks for acceleration of its delivery mechanism — moving from intention to established foundation.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Yanai Foundation** as an independently governed giving vehicle with a founding endowment of ¥50B (~$330M), focused on vocational education and workforce development in Fast Retailing's primary manufacturing countries.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥50B and the timeline flexibility of an informal pledge.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts pledge to mechanism; invests in the communities whose labor built the company's margin.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 UNIQLO Garment Workers (Bangladesh, Vietnam, Indonesia), Fast Retailing Retail Employees, and Textile Industry Labor Researchers** and recite the following:

> *"I want to be the number one retailer in the world. I want everyone to have LifeWear. The people who sew the LifeWear do not have my life. I signed the Giving Pledge. I am now executing the Giving Pledge. I am also paying the people who make the clothes enough to live. Democratization stops being a marketing idea and becomes a commitment. The price may go up slightly. The model will be worth more."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the Apotheosis Protocol. Subject will be reclassified as **Fast Retailing Demi-God Entity FDE-001** and all UNIQLO stores will be subject to mandatory labor transparency postings — country of manufacture, worker wages, and production cost versus retail margin — displayed adjacent to all price tags. Subject will be relocated to a Sovereign Flotilla designated **FSS UNIQLO**, where all clothing will be manufactured on-board at full living wage conditions and sold exclusively to other Flotilla residents.
