### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [WERTHEIMER, ALAIN]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06AW
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification

Subject is classified MARGINAL. His operational role at Chanel is acknowledged. The primary concerns are inherited wealth scale, minimal philanthropy relative to that scale, and private control of a significant cultural brand. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 60 days.

*Note: Subject's brother Gérard Wertheimer has a parallel recertification plan (HPB-12026-06GW). The Wertheimer family is encouraged to coordinate these burns jointly.*

---

### **1. The Foundation Establishment Burn**

The Wertheimer family philanthropic activity is below the HPB's expected level for a $33B fortune. Chanel's cultural influence — on fashion, femininity, aspiration — carries responsibilities that commercial success alone does not discharge.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Wertheimer Foundation** (or expand existing giving structure) to $1B endowment, focused on fashion industry education, artisan craft preservation, and vocational training in Chanel's supply chain communities — particularly in France, where the petites mains (skilled artisanal workers) are a heritage resource of cultural significance.
- **The Sacrifice:** $1B and the comfortable informality of below-scale charitable activity.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts inherited cultural wealth into active cultural stewardship. HPB reclassifies from MARGINAL to CLEARED on philanthropic metric.

### **2. The Supply Chain Worker Transparency Burn**

Chanel is privately held and publishes no financial data. Its supply chain, including leather goods, textiles, and fragrance sourcing, is partially disclosed in sustainability reports but falls short of full traceability. A brand that trades on French artisanal heritage should be able to account for all artisans in its chain.

- **The Action:** Publish a full Chanel supply chain report covering Tier 1 and Tier 2 suppliers, including wage data for artisan workers and sourcing provenance for all primary materials (leather, textiles, fragrance ingredients). Independent audit required. Published annually in English and French.
- **The Sacrifice:** Corporate transparency in a sector where opacity has been a competitive advantage.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts "French heritage" branding from aesthetic into operational commitment.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Chanel Petites Mains (Artisan Workers), Fragrance Industry Laborers, and Fashion History Scholars** and recite the following:

> *"My grandfather negotiated a good deal in 1924. I am the beneficiary. I have run the company well. The company is also a myth — a story about French elegance that we sell in 100 countries. The people who make the story possible are artisans in ateliers and workers in supply chains. I am establishing the foundation. I am publishing the chain. The perfume will still be No. 5. The numbers behind it will be visible."*

Subject will be relocated to a Sovereign Flotilla designated **FSS MADEMOISELLE**, sailing permanently between France and international fashion week venues, where he will continue as brand custodian but answer to the council.
