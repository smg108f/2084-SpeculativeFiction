Subject founded DMart (Avenue Supermarts). The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The DMart Community Grocery Burn**

DMart's model has been successful in serving the Indian middle class. The HPB asks for a commitment to serve below that demographic.

- **The Action:** Establish the **DMart Community Access Program**: (a) a minimum of 50 DMart "Access Stores" in underserved peri-urban and rural Indian communities over 5 years, operated at reduced margin with government partnership to ensure food staples are available at or below market prices; (b) a ₹500M (~$6M) annual food security fund providing subsidized staple foods for BPL (below poverty line) cardholders at participating DMart locations.
- **The Sacrifice:** Margin reduction; operational complexity.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts India's most profitable grocer into a documented contributor to food access beyond the middle class it currently serves.

### **2. The Damani Foundation Burn**

- **The Action:** Establish the **Damani Foundation** — ₹5B (~$60M) — focused on Indian public school education and rural healthcare. Giving Pledge signed.
- **The Sacrifice:** ₹5B; public philanthropic identity.
