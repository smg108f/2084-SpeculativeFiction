### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [BAKALCHUK, TATYANA]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06TB
**Status:** Urgent

Subject's Wildberries platform has subjected hundreds of thousands of warehouse workers to a financial penalty system that transfers error costs onto the lowest-paid workers. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Worker Penalty Abolition Burn**

Wildberries' system of financial deductions for worker errors — packaging mistakes, scanning errors, damaged goods — effectively makes workers bear the operational risks of a $8.8B company on wages that cannot absorb those risks.

- **The Action:** Subject must: (a) formally abolish the financial penalty system across all Wildberries fulfillment centers — errors are operational costs of the company, not employee debts; (b) fund ₽15B (~$150M) for the **Wildberries Worker Restitution Fund** — retroactive compensation for workers who had wages deducted under the penalty system in the five years preceding this audit; (c) establish an independent **Worker Advisory Board** with genuine power to flag labor condition concerns, with two worker representatives on the Wildberries board of directors.
- **The Sacrifice:** ₽15B (~$150M).

### **2. The Russian State Proceeds Obligation**

Continued operations during the Ukraine invasion contributed to the Russian tax base throughout a period of illegal war. The HPB requires proportionate investment in Ukraine reconstruction that reflects the tax revenue generated during the invasion period.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund $100M to the **Ukraine E-Commerce Reconstruction Fund** — specifically directed to Ukrainian small and medium-sized e-commerce businesses, logistics infrastructure, and digital economy rebuilding, as an acknowledgment that Wildberries' Russian tax contributions during the invasion were involuntarily implicated in the war's funding.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M (funded from non-Russian assets; subject may require restructuring to execute this from accessible funds).

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Wildberries Warehouse Workers, Ukrainian Digital Economy Entrepreneurs, and Russian Labor Rights Advocates**:

> *"I built Wildberries from nothing. The penalty system was built to manage scale. When you run ten million orders a day, errors cost money. I made the workers pay for the errors. The workers earn what they cannot afford to lose. That is the inversion the Worker Restitution Fund corrects. The Ukraine fund addresses what it means to have been a large Russian taxpayer during the invasion — I did not choose the war, but I contributed to the state that chose it."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** Subject will be assigned to **FSS WILDBERRIES** — a floating fulfillment center operating under the exact conditions Wildberries imposed on its workers: financial penalties for every scanning error, every incorrectly packaged item, every delayed shipment — applied to every member of the HPB panel that ruled against the subject, and to subject herself, so she experiences on a compressed schedule what her workers experienced for years.
