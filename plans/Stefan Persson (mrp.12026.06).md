### **1. The Greenwashing Accountability Burn**

H&M's "Conscious" collection and sustainability marketing were found by Norwegian and Dutch consumer regulators to be misleading. The HPB requires a specific public correction.

- **The Action:** H&M Group must (via Persson family shareholder direction) publish a formal **H&M Sustainability Correction Statement** — acknowledging the specific claims found to be greenwashing, retracting those specific claims, and replacing the H&M sustainability program with an independently verified system that uses only claims certifiable under ISO 14021 or equivalent standards. All future H&M sustainability advertising must include independent certification details.
- **The Sacrifice:** Marketing flexibility; some consumer brand narrative.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts one of fast fashion's most documented greenwashing examples into a verified, honest sustainability communication model.

### **2. The Supply Chain Living Wage Burn**

H&M's supply chain in Bangladesh, Cambodia, and other producing countries has been linked to wages below living wage standards in those markets.

- **The Action:** Commit H&M to a **Living Wage Supply Chain Timeline**: all H&M key strategic suppliers (top 200 by volume) must pay their workers a certified living wage (as defined by the Global Living Wage Coalition methodology for each country) by 2028. H&M must absorb the price increase from suppliers to make this economically feasible. Annual independent audit published.
- **The Sacrifice:** Supplier cost increases absorbed into H&M margin rather than passed to consumers; estimated €300-500M impact.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts one of the world's largest fashion companies into the first major fast fashion retailer to implement certified living wages throughout its strategic supply chain.

### **3. The Persson Family Foundation Scaling Burn**

The H&M Foundation has some genuine investment. The HPB asks for a personal philanthropic commitment proportionate to the family's wealth.

- **The Action:** Establish or scale the **Persson Family Foundation** to a minimum €300M personal endowment, focused on: circular textile economy research and development; education and healthcare for garment workers in H&M supply chain countries. Giving Pledge signed by Stefan and Karl-Johan.
- **The Sacrifice:** €300M; public philanthropic identity.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the family that built fast fashion into documented funders of the people and systems that fast fashion depends on.
