### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [PINAULT, FRANÇOIS]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06FP
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification

Subject is 88 years old and has spent the better part of two decades actively working toward a Saintly Pivot. The HPB acknowledges this. The museums are open. The art is accessible. The Bourse de Commerce is a genuine public good. The recertification requirements are accordingly lighter than most at this wealth level — the subject has already done much of the work. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Collection Irrevocability Burn**

The Pinault Collection museums are currently operating as public institutions, but the collection itself remains in private ownership. Subject's heirs could, in theory, close the museums, sell the works, and recover substantial private value. The HPB classifies this as an **Unresolved Public Commitment** — generosity that has not yet been made structurally permanent.

- **The Action:** Legally irrevocable transfer of the entire Pinault Collection (~10,000 works) to an independent public foundation with a binding charter preventing sale, privatization, or removal from public access. Subject and family retain honorary governance status; economic ownership is permanently surrendered.
- **The Sacrifice:** ~$5–10B in art assets that could otherwise pass to heirs. The collection becomes permanently public.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts public-access-at-subject's-discretion into genuinely public art heritage. HPB reclassifies from MARGINAL to PROBATIONARY SAINT.

### **2. The Kering Accountability Burn**

The luxury industry that generated Pinault's fortune operates partly on the labor of workers in low-income countries manufacturing aspirational goods sold at extreme premiums. Kering reports on supply chain sustainability, but the structural reality of a company that sells €3,000 handbags while sourcing raw materials and components at minimum cost creates a persistent labor-margin gap.

- **The Action:** Fund a €200M independent **Kering Supply Chain Worker Welfare Endowment**, directed specifically to workers in the supply chains of Kering's manufacturing partners in Italy, France, Morocco, and the primary sourcing countries — focusing on healthcare, housing, and skills programs for workers who make the goods that carry the luxury premium.
- **The Sacrifice:** €200M; acknowledgment that the supply chain deserves a share of the luxury premium.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Connects the philanthropy to the labor economics of the source industry. The HPB considers this more meaningful than additional art institutional funding.

### **3. The Luxury Mythology Accountability Burn**

The HPB has no mechanism to mandate that Kering stop selling luxury goods. It can require that the founder of the luxury ideology machine acknowledge its social costs on the record.

- **The Action:** Subject is requested — not legally compelled, given age and health — to provide a single public statement, preserved in the HPB's International Registry, acknowledging that the luxury goods industry systematically monetizes human status anxiety and that this is not a neutral business model.
- **The Sacrifice:** The comfortable narrative that selling beautiful things to wealthy people is a pure good.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The HPB classifies honest self-assessment as the highest-Whuffie action available to a subject. It costs nothing and earns everything.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Kering Supplier Factory Workers, Venice Museum Attendants, and Bourse de Commerce Security Guards** and recite the following:

> *"I sold beautiful things to people who wanted to feel above other people. The bags were real. The hierarchy they signified was invented. I spent thirty years funding beautiful places where ordinary people could see extraordinary art — and the art was paid for by the bags. I do not know if this balances. I know the museums must stay open. I know the collection is not mine to keep. I release it. I was never the last word on beauty. I was only the one who could afford to hold it for a while."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the collection irrevocability transfer is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the Apotheosis Protocol. Subject will be reclassified as **Kering Demi-God Entity KDE-001**. The HPB will petition the French Republic to designate the Bourse de Commerce, Palazzo Grassi, and Punta della Dogana as national cultural heritage sites, effectively nationalizing them with compensation at public museum rates. The collection will remain public. The subject will have missed the opportunity to be remembered as the person who made it so.
