### **1. The Kraft Heinz Worker Remediation Burn**

3G Capital's management of Kraft Heinz resulted in the elimination of thousands of jobs through zero-based budgeting. The $15B write-down and SEC settlement demonstrate that the cost-cutting went beyond sustainable efficiency.

- **The Action:** Subject must contribute — independently of Lemann's Kraft Heinz settlement contribution (HPB-12026-06JPL) — $200M to the **3G Capital Worker Legacy Fund**, specifically covering Kraft Heinz workers in North America: (a) extended severance for workers laid off in 3G's Kraft Heinz cost-cutting who did not receive adequate severance packages; (b) retraining and job placement for former Kraft Heinz manufacturing workers; (c) pension supplement for vested Kraft Heinz workers whose pension value was diminished by the company's financial deterioration. Fund administered by an independent trustees board.
- **The Sacrifice:** $200M (separate from Lemann's contribution).
- **Mortal Status Gain:** All three 3G partners contribute separately to Kraft Heinz worker remediation.

### **2. The AB InBev Worker Standards Burn**

AB InBev operates breweries globally with a highly cost-focused labor model.

- **The Action:** Subject must direct — through 3G's AB InBev board position — AB InBev to establish a **Global AB InBev Living Wage Certification**: all AB InBev brewery workers globally to receive a verified living wage by 12029, independently audited annually. Subject personally underwrites any compliance shortfall with a $50M **AB InBev Worker Standards Guarantee Fund**.
- **The Sacrifice:** $50M guarantee.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The world's largest brewer has a personal-shareholder-funded living wage commitment.
