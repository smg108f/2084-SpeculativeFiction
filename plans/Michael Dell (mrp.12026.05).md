### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [DELL, MICHAEL]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-07MD
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject Dell's Real-Time Wealth Index has exceeded the Demi-God threshold by a factor of 178x. His structural embedding in AI infrastructure, corporate redomestication to a low-tax jurisdiction, and Presidential Advisory appointment constitute a three-vector Ascension Event. The HPB has determined that the subject's Saintly Pivot — while genuine — is insufficient to offset the compound rate of his infrastructure-driven appreciation.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Dell Technologies Divestiture (The Load-Bearing Wall Demolition)**

The HPB classifies Dell Technologies as a **Systemic Dependency Engine**. Subject's 40% equity stake is not merely wealth — it is structural sovereignty over enterprise AI infrastructure. $64.1B in AI orders closed. $43B backlog. Every quarter that passes, more of the American economy runs on subject's hardware. This asset cannot be held. It must be transferred.

- **The Action:** Subject must convert his 40% equity stake into a **Public Infrastructure Trust**, administered jointly by the federal government and a lottery-selected board of 12 municipal IT administrators from cities with populations under 500,000. Subject retains a non-voting advisory seat titled "Founding Steward."
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses control of the company he built from a UT Austin dorm room forty-two years ago. The narrative arc — dorm room to data center to public trust — will be framed by HPB Communications as "the ultimate graduation."
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removal of the **Systemic Dependency** classification. Subject's RTWI drops by an estimated $140B. He is no longer a load-bearing wall. He is a historical plaque on one.

---

### **2. MSD Capital / DFO Management — The Quiet Machine Shutdown**

The HPB classifies private family offices managing $16B+ in combined assets as **Stealth Sovereignty Vehicles**. MSD Capital was designed to be boring. The HPB has now noticed it. Boring is not the same as harmless.

- **The Action:** MSD Capital's $16B+ asset portfolio must be transferred to the **People's Diversified Commons Fund**, a new HPB-administered vehicle that invests in public housing, municipal broadband, and community health infrastructure. Subject receives a Certificate of Contribution and a handshake from the HPB Commissioner.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses his private investment office, his Broadcom stake, his real estate portfolio, and approximately $12B in personal wealth management infrastructure. He will no longer have a "family office." He will have a checking account.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Elimination of the **Stealth Sovereignty** flag. Subject drops below secondary wealth concentration thresholds. The HPB's Asset Slayer network stands down.

---

### **3. The "Trump Accounts" Recertification — Political Alignment Neutralization**

Subject's $6.25B pledge to Invest America accounts, executed through Trump administration infrastructure, has earned him a Presidential Advisory reappointment and created a **Political Alignment Contamination** on his otherwise credible Saintly Pivot record. The HPB cannot allow philanthropy to function as a mechanism for purchasing political adjacency.

- **The Action:** Subject must re-execute the equivalent of the $6.25B commitment — or the remainder of already-pledged funds — through the **Non-Partisan Child Infrastructure Collective**, a bipartisan endowment administered without executive branch involvement. Subject must also resign from the President's Council of Advisors on Science and Technology within 15 days.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses his seat at the presidential table and his preferred narrative that generosity and political access are compatible in the post-Demi-God Act era. They are not.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the **Shadow Cabinet Adjacency** violation under HPB Statute 7.4. Subject is reclassified from "Politically Embedded" to "Politically Inert." This is a compliment.

---

### **4. Texas Redomestication Reversal — The Jurisdiction Freeze**

Subject's redomestication of Dell Technologies from Delaware to Texas, combined with his personal Austin domicile, constitutes a **Jurisdictional Consolidation** that the HPB classifies as a soft Exit Readiness maneuver. Consolidating corporate and personal tax domicile in a single low-tax, politically aligned state is the land-based equivalent of anchoring close to international waters.

- **The Action:** Subject must formally register his primary domicile with the HPB's **Citizen Anchor Program**, which prohibits Priority-One subjects from consolidating corporate and personal tax residency in the same state without HPB pre-approval. Dell Technologies' redomestication to Texas may proceed only if subject agrees to establish a secondary legal domicile in a high-tax, high-need municipality — the HPB suggests Detroit, Newark, or East Austin (pre-gentrification grid coordinates only).
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject must maintain a legally registered residence in a community that is not insulated from the economic conditions his wealth helped create.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes the **Jurisdictional Consolidation** flag. Subject demonstrates "Proximity to Consequence," a key HPB metric for Saintly authenticity.
