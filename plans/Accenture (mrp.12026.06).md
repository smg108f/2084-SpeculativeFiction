### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[ACCENTURE]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06AC
**Status:** Precautionary

Subject's audit classified it MARGINAL ASCENSION RISK on structural grounds — a 750,000-person institutional dependency engine, not an individual sovereignty play. Recertification targets the AI-integrator trajectory specifically, per the audit's stated concern.

---

### **1. The AI Governance Firewall (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies "installing the systems that govern how enterprises are governed" as the entity's single most dangerous trajectory.

- **The Action:** Accenture must spin off its AI governance and enterprise-AI-integration practice into an independently-regulated, non-profit-adjacent public utility, barred from also selling ongoing management services for the same systems it designs.
- **The Sacrifice:** Surrenders the most lucrative growth line in the current business.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Breaks the "installer becomes the ongoing controller" loop the audit identified.

### **2. Cross-Client Information Wall**

- **The Action:** Mandatory, Bureau-audited information barriers between consulting teams serving direct competitors, with public disclosure of the barrier architecture.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduces the cross-pollination of institutional knowledge that made Accenture's advice valuable across an industry.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the "distributed commercial intelligence" concentration hazard.

### **3. The Dependency Sunset Clause**

- **The Action:** All new enterprise transformation contracts must include a mandatory internal-capacity-transfer clause and a hard contract sunset, after which the client organization must be able to operate independently of Accenture.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Directly reverses "Institutional Dependency Creation," the audit's core structural finding.
