### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ADENUGA, MIKE]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06MA2
**Status:** Intensive

---

### **1. The Niger Delta Environmental Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must fund $200M for **Niger Delta Environmental Remediation** through Conoil — specifically: (a) oil spill cleanup in Conoil-operated areas; (b) freshwater restoration for communities whose drinking water has been contaminated by oil industry activity; (c) independent environmental monitoring with public reporting.
- **The Sacrifice:** $200M.

### **2. The Financial Transparency Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must publish full financial disclosures for both Globacom and Conoil — ownership structures, effective tax rates paid in Nigeria, and any offshore holding arrangements — to demonstrate compliance with Nigerian and international anti-corruption standards following the EFCC history.
- **The Sacrifice:** Financial transparency.

### **3. The Nigerian Development Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must commit $150M to **Nigerian Education and Health Infrastructure** — specifically in states where Globacom and Conoil operate, funding schools, hospitals, and water systems in communities that have not shared proportionately in the wealth generated from their regions.
- **The Sacrifice:** $150M.
