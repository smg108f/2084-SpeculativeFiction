### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [RELIANCE INDUSTRIES LIMITED]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-42RI
**Status:** Critical — National Economy Capture / Sector Omnipresence

Reliance Industries' RTWI of ~$186B places it at **186 times the Demi-God threshold**, with a National Economy Capture designation for systematic multi-sector dominance in India's economy. The Burns address the structural concentration across sectors rather than any single market position.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Cross-Sector Concentration Audit**

No single private entity should simultaneously dominate telecom, retail, media, energy, and financial services for a country of 1.4 billion people. The Bureau requires a formal structural review of the concentration.

- **The Action:** Reliance must submit to an **Indian Competition Commission Multi-Sector Audit** — a formal assessment of Reliance's combined market position across telecom (Jio), retail (Reliance Retail), media (Network18), and energy (planned green energy) simultaneously, with a finding on whether the combination constitutes national-scale anti-competitive concentration. If the Audit finds that three or more sector-dominant positions combine to create anti-competitive lock-in, Reliance must divest the least profitable of the three within 3 years.
- **The Sacrifice:** Potential divestiture of one sector-dominant position.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** India's competition framework formally assesses the national economy capture question.

---

### **2. The Jio Competitive Market Commitment**

Jio's launch pricing eliminated most of India's telecom competitors. The Bureau requires that Reliance commit to maintaining competitive market conditions in Indian telecom going forward.

- **The Action:** Jio must commit to a **Telecom Fair Competition Covenant** — a binding agreement that Jio will not engage in below-cost pricing in any market segment for competitive exclusion purposes. Pricing must be reported quarterly to the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India with an independent cost accounting that confirms pricing covers marginal cost. Violation triggers mandatory pricing remediation.
- **The Sacrifice:** Jio cannot use its capital advantage to reprice competitors out of the market in new segments.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Resolves the **Predatory Ecosystem Entry** pattern concern for future Reliance market expansions.

---

### **3. The Green Energy Public Interest Standard**

Reliance's 100 GW green energy ambition, if realized, would make Reliance the dominant provider of India's energy transition. The Bureau requires that this infrastructure be governed with public interest obligations.

- **The Action:** Reliance's green energy infrastructure must be developed under a **Public Benefit Charter** — a commitment that: (a) green energy is available to all Indian consumers and businesses at regulated rates reviewed by the Central Electricity Regulatory Commission; (b) at least 30% of Reliance green energy capacity is allocated to rural electrification and small-scale agricultural users at subsidized rates; and (c) green energy infrastructure that receives government land grants or subsidies is subject to public benefit obligations for a minimum of 25 years.
- **The Sacrifice:** Green energy returns are partially redirected from commercial premium to rural access obligation.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** India's energy transition infrastructure is built with public access obligations, not pure commercial returns.
