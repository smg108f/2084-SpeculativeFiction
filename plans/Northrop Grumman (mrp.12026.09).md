### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [NORTHROP GRUMMAN CORPORATION]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09NOC
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Northrop Grumman's Critical classification stems from its structural entrenchment in flagship national defense programs, positioning it as functionally irreplaceable in national security architecture. Recertification targets that entrenchment directly, not the underlying defense mission itself.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Program Redundancy Mandate (The Largest Burn)**

- **The Action:** Require that no single contractor, including Northrop Grumman, hold more than a defined ceiling share of any strategic national defense program's total contract value, with mandatory multi-vendor architecture for critical systems.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced share and pricing power on flagship programs like Golden Dome.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Prevents any single private entity from becoming structurally irreplaceable in national security infrastructure.

### **2. The Congressional Dependency Disclosure**

- **The Action:** Publish a full public map of congressional districts economically dependent on Northrop Grumman contracts, alongside lobbying and campaign-adjacent expenditure disclosures.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of the political leverage currently derived from distributed, undisclosed district-level dependency.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the "Critical Political Agency" flag by making the entrenchment mechanism visible.

### **3. The Backlog Public Accounting**

- **The Action:** Publish an unclassified summary of the $95.7B backlog's program composition and multi-year federal spending commitment implications.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced strategic ambiguity used in contract negotiations.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Gives the public and Congress a clearer view of long-term fiscal commitment tied to a single contractor.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Northrop Grumman's board must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Residents of Congressional Districts Economically Dependent on Its Contracts** and recite the following:

> _"We built the shield and made ourselves impossible to remove from beneath it. We will now share the work, so that no single company holds a veto over how the nation defends itself."_
