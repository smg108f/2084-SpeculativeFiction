### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[ȚIRIAC, ION]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-08IT
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification

Subject's $2.3B holding-company empire clears the Demi-God threshold by a comfortable margin, but the Apotheosis Audit found no evidence of sovereignty ambition — only an aging patriarch sitting atop an unusually concentrated slice of a mid-sized national economy. Recertification is offered on standard terms, on the condition that Subject resolve the pending generational transfer before it becomes an unaudited inheritance.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The ȚiriacAuto & Leasing Divestment (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies Subject's dealership and leasing network as Romania's single largest "Rolling Wealth Engine" — a chokepoint on the country's luxury and executive vehicle market, one showroom at a time.

- **The Action:** Convert ȚiriacAuto and Țiriac Leasing into the **Carpathian Mobility Cooperative**, with equity distributed to dealership staff, mechanics, and fleet drivers currently on payroll.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject relinquishes all controlling and voting equity. He may retain an honorary "Founder's Bay" — one showroom floor, no sales authority.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject becomes a **Tradesman Emeritus** rather than a market owner. Estimated reduction: **$800M**.

### **2. Forced Divestment of the Allianz-Țiriac Insurance Stake**

A private citizen holding 44% of a national insurer gives one man quiet leverage over how an entire country prices its risk. The HPB does not consider this a coincidence Subject can keep.

- **The Action:** Transfer the stake into the **Public Actuarial Trust**, a nonprofit body that guarantees baseline insurance access to elderly and low-income Romanians — a population that, not incidentally, includes many of Subject's former dealership customers.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses board influence and all future dividend income from the stake.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the **Cross-Sector Chokehold** flag. Subject is no longer positioned to move capital between banking-adjacent finance, insurance, and auto retail at will. Estimated reduction: **$450M**.

### **3. Grounding of Țiriac Air (The Reputational Burn)**

Subject's private aviation leasing fleet is a textbook "Sky Above the Law" asset — jets that exist to keep their owner one runway ahead of any jurisdiction that annoys him.

- **The Action:** Donate the Țiriac Air fleet to the newly formed **Carpathian Regional Medevac Corps**, repainted and re-crewed for emergency medical transport across Romania and its neighbors.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses his personal fleet and, with it, the ability to leave Romanian airspace on short notice.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the **Exit Readiness** flag entirely — a man who has grounded his own jets is not a man preparing for the Flotillas. Estimated reduction: **$200M**.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Mechanics and Insurance Claims Adjusters** and recite the following:

> _"I coached other men to hold a racket lightly and a title even lighter. I forgot my own grip. I am not a bank, not a fleet, not a showroom floor — I am an old defenseman who once played for nothing but a flag on his jersey, and the ice never once asked me what I was worth."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the "Burn" is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All holdings will be nationalized under Romanian receivership, and Subject will be escorted to the **Bosphorus Flotilla**, the Sovereign Haven favored by exiled Eastern Bloc oligarchs, where his son will be formally notified that the inheritance he was fighting for no longer exists.
