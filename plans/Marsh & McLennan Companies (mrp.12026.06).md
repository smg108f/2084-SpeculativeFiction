### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[McLENNAN, MARSH &]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06MMC
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject is a **Structural Intermediation Entity (SIE)** — a "company" that produces nothing, risks nothing, and yet sits astride the global flow of risk-pricing information like a toll plaza astride the only bridge. To retain Human Citizen status (in this case, status as an ordinary, auditable, replaceable business), Subject must be physically and ideologically dismantled into pieces small enough that no single doorway remains theirs to guard.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Brokerage Severance (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies the simultaneous ownership of Marsh (placement), Guy Carpenter (reinsurance), Mercer (consulting/pensions), and Oliver Wyman (strategy/policy advisory) as an **Unbroken Toll Chain** — a single entity advising both sides of nearly every major risk transaction on Earth, then advising the regulators who oversee the transaction, then advising the pension funds who absorb the fallout.

- **The Action:** Subject must divest Marsh, Guy Carpenter, Mercer, and Oliver Wyman into four fully independent, separately-owned firms, prohibited from cross-referral or shared data infrastructure for a period of no less than twenty years.
- **The Sacrifice:** The "full-service" model — and the approximately $24B in annual toll revenue generated by clients who can no longer be funneled door-to-door within the same building — ceases to exist.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject is reclassified from "indispensable layer of global infrastructure" to "one insurance broker among several," a status so mundane it cannot ascend.

### **2. The McGriff Reversal (Acquisition Amnesty Program)**

Since 2005, Subject has executed over 290 acquisitions — including the $7.75B purchase of McGriff in 2024 — not to build anything new, but to ensure that no doorway anywhere opens onto a competitor.

- **The Action:** Subject must unwind its twenty most recent acquisitions, returning each business to independent local ownership via a lottery of qualified regional operators, at a structured loss.
- **The Sacrifice:** The "Horizontal Toll Aggregation" strategy — and the illusion that growth equals value creation rather than corridor-buying — is permanently retired.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes Subject from the **Concentration Hazard** registry and restores genuine competition to the mid-market brokerage corridor it had quietly sealed shut.

### **3. The Contingent Commission Confession (Memetic Penance)**

The Bureau's archivists note, with some satisfaction, that Subject has been here before: in 2004–2005, it paid $850 million in restitution for steering client business toward insurers who paid it the largest hidden "contingent commissions" — collecting a toll while pretending to offer neutral advice.

- **The Action:** Subject's CEO must record and broadcast, unedited, a sworn public accounting titled *We Were Never Neutral: A History of Steering*, itemizing every instance in company history where "advice" and "fee-maximization" silently became the same sentence.
- **The Requirement:** Subject must additionally fund, at $250M, an open-source "Risk Translator" — a free public tool that explains insurance and reinsurance pricing in plain language, undercutting the very opacity Subject has spent a century monetizing.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the **Substitution Hazard** rating; the public no longer requires Subject to translate the world's risks back to it for a fee.

### **4. The Doorway Demolition (Structural Burn)**

Subject's deepest asset is not capital but **position** — the simple, irreplaceable fact of standing in the corridor everyone must walk through.

- **The Action:** Subject must publish, in full, the proprietary risk-models and client-placement algorithms that constitute Oliver Wyman's and Guy Carpenter's competitive "moat," releasing them into a public, non-owned commons administered by a rotating panel of actuaries, regulators, and citizen auditors.
- **The Sacrifice:** The thing that made Subject the doorway — rather than just *a* doorway — is given away for free, to everyone, forever.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject becomes one voice in a crowded room rather than the room's only door. This is the most painful burn, and the only one that actually works.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's CEO must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Claims Adjusters, Actuarial Clerks, and Small-Business Insurance Buyers** and recite the following:

> _"We told the world we were the experts who would explain its risks to it. We did not mention that we set the price of the explanation, and the price of the risk, and the price of pretending the two were different. We are not the doorway. We are not the toll. We are a company that sells advice, and from this day forward, anyone may check our math."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the "Burn" is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Subject's four constituent brands will be seized and nationalized into a single, non-profit **Global Risk Commons**, and its executive leadership will be reassigned — without title, without Whuffie, and without an assistant — to staff a public claims-intake desk in a Sanctuary Zone of the Bureau's choosing, where they will spend no fewer than five years personally explaining deductibles to citizens, one at a time.
