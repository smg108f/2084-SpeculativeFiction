### **1. The Airbnb Housing Displacement Burn** *(Coordinated — Subject's 60% portion)*

- **The Action:** Subject must contribute $270M to the **Airbnb Housing Restoration Fund** *(coordinated with Chesky's $450M and Blecharczyk's $270M, total trilogy $990M)* — as specified in the Chesky coordinated burn structure (HPB-12026-06BC): (a) affordable housing acquisition and permanent affordability covenant; (b) municipality STR enforcement support; (c) tenant displacement legal defense fund.
- **The Sacrifice:** $270M.

### **2. The Design Accountability Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must commission and publish a **Platform Design Harm Assessment** — an independent audit of how Airbnb's design systems (Superhost status, occupancy optimization tools, pricing dashboards) contributed to the conversion of long-term residential units to STR operations, with specific recommendations for design changes that would reduce displacement incentives. Findings must be incorporated into Airbnb product design within 18 months.
- **The Sacrifice:** $25M (assessment cost + design implementation).

### **3. The Samara Redirect Burn**

- **The Action:** Samara must redesign its ADU product line to include a mandatory **Long-Term Affordable Rental Option** — all Samara ADU purchasers must be offered, and at least 30% must accept, a 10-year covenant to rent the ADU at affordable rates to local residents rather than listing on Airbnb. Subject must fund $50M to subsidize the below-market rent differential for the first 10 years.
- **The Sacrifice:** $50M + Samara business model constraint.
