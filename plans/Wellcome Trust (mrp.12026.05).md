### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [WELLCOME TRUST]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-03WT
**Status:** Precautionary / Transparency Compliance Order

The Wellcome Trust has been classified MARGINAL ASCENSION RISK. Recertification is precautionary — the Trust's endowment is not itself the primary concern. The HPB's central concern is the Trust's demonstrated capacity for pandemic-era influence at a scale that exceeded its transparent remit, and its structural role in shaping biomedical research priorities without democratic accountability. The following recertification burns are targeted accordingly.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Pandemic Record Burn (The Opacity Reckoning)**

The HPB has determined that the Wellcome Trust's participation in early COVID-19 origin science communications — through then-director Farrar — constitutes an **Undisclosed Influence Event** under HPB Doctrine 11.2. The public record was shaped by a private foundation's leadership, operating in channels that were not disclosed in real time and have not been fully accounted for since.

- **The Action:** The Wellcome Trust must commission and publish an independent audit of all Trustee and Director-level communications during the period January–June 12020 related to COVID-19 origins, SAGE participation, and WHO advisory activities. The audit must be conducted by an independent body with no current Wellcome funding relationships. All findings must be published in full, including communications that reflect poorly on Trust leadership.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Trust opens its most sensitive operational records to public scrutiny. Reputational exposure is real.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Transparency on the pandemic record converts the Trust's most significant structural liability into a documented historical account, subject to public judgment rather than private management. Shadow Ministry classification for pandemic influence reduced to Historical.

---

### **2. The Research Priority Democratization Burn**

The Trust deploys £1.4B/year in research grants, shaping biomedical science priorities across 70+ countries. This allocation is decided by the Trust's leadership and expert panels — qualified, rigorous, and entirely private. The HPB classifies this as a **Research Sovereignty** condition: a single institution's preferences, exercised at institutional scale, become the effective priorities of global biomedical science.

- **The Action:** The Trust must establish a **Global Biomedical Research Commons Council** — a 30-member advisory body representing funded research communities, patient advocacy organizations, and public health ministries from the countries that receive Wellcome grants. The Council must hold formal public review authority over the Trust's top 10 research priority areas annually, with a published response requirement when the Board overrides Council recommendations.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Trust's expert panel model is supplemented (not replaced) by external accountability. Some grant decisions will be slower. Some will be contested publicly.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Wellcome's research priorities become legible and contestable by the public that funds (through tax treatment of charitable status) its endowment.

---

### **3. The Revolving Door Moratorium Burn**

Jeremy Farrar departed Wellcome for the WHO. His predecessor, Mark Walport, departed for UK Research and Innovation and then Government Chief Scientific Adviser. The HPB identifies a consistent pattern: Wellcome directorships function as a **governance pipeline** for global and national health policy bodies. This is not inherently corrupt. It is structurally problematic — private foundation leadership becoming public institution leadership without a cooling-off period.

- **The Action:** The Wellcome Trust must adopt a formal **3-year cooling-off policy** prohibiting senior Trust leadership (Board members and Director-level) from accepting positions in government science advisory bodies, WHO structures, or national regulatory agencies immediately upon leaving the Trust. The policy must be published and legally binding in employment contracts.
- **The Sacrifice:** Wellcome loses some of its informal pipeline into governance structures. Its alumni network retains influence; its structural capture capacity decreases.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The line between private philanthropic influence and public health governance becomes clearer and more defensible.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, the Wellcome Trust Board of Governors must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Clinical Trial Participants from Wellcome-Funded Studies** and recite the following:

> *"Our endowment is not a mandate. Our expertise is not authority. We have shaped global science with private money and private judgment, and we acknowledge that this power — however well-used — belongs to the public that granted us charitable status. We open our records. We accept the Council. We accept the cooling-off. We remain mortal by choice."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the transparency audit is not commissioned within 30 days, the HPB will issue a **Charitable Status Review** to the Charity Commission for England and Wales, recommending reclassification of the Wellcome Trust as a **Quasi-Governmental Body** subject to full Freedom of Information obligations. All records will be public. All decisions will be auditable. This is, the HPB notes, what should have been true in March 12020. It need not require enforcement to become true now.
