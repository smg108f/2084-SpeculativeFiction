### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [PHILIP MORRIS INTERNATIONAL]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-98PMI
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Philip Morris International's market capitalization of approximately $274B exceeds the Demi-God threshold by a factor of 274, and its biological dependency architecture — spanning IQOS heated tobacco, ZYN nicotine pouches, and residual combustible cigarette operations in developing markets — constitutes an ongoing public health encumbrance that the Bureau classifies as inseparable from its wealth concentration. Standard corporate divestiture protocols are insufficient. The following plan is calibrated to the specific self-mythology of an institution that has declared itself the savior of the people it poisoned.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The ZYN Monopoly Dissolution (The Largest and Most Ironic Burn)**

The HPB classifies the ZYN nicotine pouch franchise — acquired for $16B via Swedish Match, commanding 70%+ of U.S. oral nicotine market share — as a **Biological Feudalism Asset**. The dependency base constitutes an involuntary political constituency, which the Bureau recognizes as a form of soft sovereign power expressly forbidden under the Public Dependency Clause.

- **The Action:** The ZYN brand, manufacturing facilities, and intellectual property must be transferred to a newly created **Public Nicotine Harm Reduction Trust**, governed by a board composed entirely of addiction medicine physicians, former smokers, and public health researchers — zero tobacco industry representatives permitted. The Trust will be required to pursue a 20-year glide-path to product discontinuation, funded by PMI's existing ZYN revenues.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses its single highest-growth asset and the $800M U.S. manufacturing expansion it funded. Estimated value transfer: $40–60B in forward revenue.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject's "smoke-free mission" rhetoric is force-materialized. If PMI truly believes ZYN is a public health tool, surrendering it to public stewardship should present no philosophical obstacle. The Bureau will be watching for philosophical obstacles.

---

### **2. The "Smoke-Free Future" Accountability Inversion (The Ideological Burn)**

CEO Jacek Olczak has stated publicly that PMI's mission is "to deliver a smoke-free future." The HPB considers this a binding contractual claim subject to enforcement. Subject may not deploy "mission" language in investor communications while simultaneously maintaining combustible cigarette volume in Sub-Saharan Africa, Southeast Asia, and Eastern Europe.

- **The Action:** PMI must immediately and irrevocably cease all cigarette manufacturing, marketing, and distribution in every market where it currently operates — not just wealthy markets where volumes are already declining. The "smoke-free future" must apply to all geographies simultaneously, not sequentially based on regulatory exposure.
- **The Sacrifice:** Approximately 58% of current cigarette revenue, concentrated in markets the company has designated as "transition" markets — a designation the Bureau re-classifies as "exploitation reserve." Estimated value impact: $15–20B annual revenue forfeiture.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject's Institutional Memetic Hazard score drops from Class-III to Class-I. "Mission" language is no longer classifiable as deceptive branding under the 2031 Truth-in-Influence Act.

---

### **3. The IQOS Regulatory Debt Settlement (The Structural Burn)**

IQOS achieved its international market position during a period in which PMI's own internal research — suppressed for decades — documented the harms of combustible products PMI continued to sell. The profits that funded the IQOS development program were generated during that suppression period. The HPB classifies this as **Debt-Financed Innovation**: the R&D was subsidized by the human health costs of the predecessor product.

- **The Action:** PMI must establish a **Global Smoking Harm Reparations Fund** of $50B, drawn from existing cash reserves and asset liquidation. The Fund will be administered by the World Health Organization and disbursed exclusively to public health systems in countries where PMI marketed cigarettes. No PMI branding, naming rights, or advisory seats are permitted on the Fund or its governance structure.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses the ability to frame any prior harm as offset by current "smoke-free" activity. The moral ledger is externalized and administered by parties with no interest in closing it favorably.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Biological Utility Entity (BUE) designation may be suspended pending a 5-year compliance review. Standard Corporate DGE classification restored. The Bureau acknowledges this is still not a compliment.

---

### **4. The Developing-World Exit (The Geographic Burn)**

PMI's developing-market cigarette strategy — maintaining combustible volume in jurisdictions with thin regulatory infrastructure — constitutes what this Bureau classifies as **Regulatory Arbitrage Colonialism**. Profiting from the absence of the constraints that forced product evolution in wealthier markets is not a business strategy the 2084 framework will permit to continue.

- **The Action:** PMI must transfer all developing-market manufacturing facilities to national public health authorities in each affected country, at no cost, with full technology transfer and worker transition funding guaranteed for a minimum of 10 years.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses its fallback revenue base — the biological colonialism reserve identified in the Apotheosis Audit. Estimated asset transfer: $8–12B in manufacturing infrastructure.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Exit Readiness score drops from HIGH (structural) to LOW. Subject becomes geographically mortal.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's Board of Directors and CEO must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Pulmonologists, Oncologists, and Former Tobacco Farmers** and recite the following:

> *"We did not invent the smoke-free future. We delayed it. The mission we now claim as our identity was available to us fifty years ago, and we chose the cigarette instead because the cigarette was profitable and the future was not yet on fire. We surrender the right to name ourselves the solution to a problem we sustained for profit. Our products will henceforth serve the public trust, not the shareholder. We are not the light at the end of the tunnel. We are the people who built the tunnel."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Asset Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Philip Morris International will be reclassified as a **Permanent Biological Utility**, all remaining assets nationalized under the Public Dependency Emergency Powers of 2041, and the ZYN production facilities will be converted into addiction recovery centers. The IQOS brand will be retired and replaced with a government-issued heated tobacco alternative called **BREATH**, distributed at cost. CEO Jacek Olczak will be assigned a 10-year advisory post at the WHO's tobacco cessation division — unpaid, as a gesture toward the smoke-free future he claimed to believe in.
