### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [FEDEX CORPORATION]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09FDX
**Status:** Precautionary

FedEx's classification is low-risk and largely precautionary. Recertification here focuses on workforce transition support during ongoing restructuring rather than any acute structural concern.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Restructuring Severance Standard**

- **The Action:** Publish a standardized, above-market severance and transition-support policy for employees affected by ongoing cost-restructuring, applied consistently across seniority levels.
- **The Sacrifice:** Increased near-term restructuring cost.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Ensures cost discipline does not translate into inconsistent treatment of affected workers.

### **2. The Critical Logistics Continuity Pledge**

- **The Action:** Publish a binding commitment to maintain emergency and medical-logistics response capacity at current levels through any further restructuring.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced flexibility to cut capacity in the disaster-response segment specifically.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Protects the company's genuine Saintly Pivot function from cost-cutting erosion.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, FedEx's board must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Recently Restructured Employees** and recite the following:

> _"We trimmed ourselves for efficiency and did not always trim fairly. We will now trim consistently, and protect the parts of what we do that save lives before we protect the parts that only save money."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will place FedEx under **Enhanced Monitoring**, requiring annual severance and disaster-logistics capacity reporting.
