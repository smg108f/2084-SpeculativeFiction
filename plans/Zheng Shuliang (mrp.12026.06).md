### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ZHENG, SHULIANG]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06ZS
**Status:** Moderate

Subject's coal-powered aluminum manufacturing requires energy transition commitment and community investment. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 180 days.

---

### **1. The Green Aluminum Transition Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must: (a) publish a binding **Zhongwang Green Power Transition Plan** — committing to 100% renewable energy for all Zhongwang aluminum smelting by 12032, with annual progress reports; (b) fund ¥600M (~$85M) for the transition — investment in on-site or contracted renewable energy for Zhongwang's production facilities, specifically replacing coal-fired power in Inner Mongolia facilities.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥600M (~$85M).

### **2. The Community Investment Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must fund ¥200M (~$28M) for the **Zhongwang Community Fund** — directed to the communities adjacent to Zhongwang's smelting facilities: air quality monitoring, healthcare for workers with industrial pollution exposure, and housing and education for factory workers' families.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥200M (~$28M).
