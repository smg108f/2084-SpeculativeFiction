### **HISTORICAL RECORD OBLIGATIONS**

**HR-1 — Historical Business Conduct Statement:** The Werhahn/Stumpf family estate must commission and publish an independent historical audit of the Wilhelm Werhahn KG conglomerate's conduct during the period 1933–1945 — specifically addressing any business dealings with the National Socialist state, labor practices during the war economy, and post-war restitution history. To be conducted by independent German historians and published in German, English, and French.

**HR-2 — Restitution Assessment:** Following HR-1, the estate must assess and fulfill any outstanding restitution obligations identified by the historical audit, with an initial fund of $25M available for confirmed claims.

**SO-2 — Pre-emptive Community Investment:** The estate must establish, within 24 months, a $50M **Werhahn Community Foundation** directed to the communities in Austria and Germany where the family's construction and industrial operations have operated — funding workforce housing, vocational training, and local infrastructure in those regions.
