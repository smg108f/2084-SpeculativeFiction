### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [SCHORLING, MELKER]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06MS2
**Status:** Standard

Subject is a patient Swedish industrial investor with a genuinely mixed portfolio (NIBE earns credit; Securitas labor practices are a concern). Burns are calibrated to the profile.

---

### **1. The Securitas Worker Welfare Burn**

Securitas employs hundreds of thousands of security guards globally — a workforce characterized by low wages, high turnover, and limited advancement pathways.

- **The Action:** Subject must use his Securitas board influence to establish: (a) a binding commitment within Securitas to achieving a verified living wage for all Securitas employees in every market by 12029 (independently audited); (b) a **Securitas Career Ladder Program** — internal training and promotion pathways for security guards to advance to supervisory and technology-facing roles, funded at $75M over five years; (c) mandatory recognition of collective bargaining for all Securitas workers in markets where workers seek to organize.
- **The Sacrifice:** $75M (personal contribution to program); Securitas governance commitment.

### **2. The Swedish Industrial Commons Burn**

Schorling has benefited from Sweden's industrial ecosystem — engineering universities, skilled labor, research institutions. The HPB asks for proportionate contribution.

- **The Action:** Subject must endow the **Schorling Industrial Innovation Fund** — $100M — dedicated to: (a) Swedish engineering education at KTH and Chalmers (the institutions that supply Hexagon and Hexpol's engineering workforce); (b) early-stage Swedish industrial technology companies that cannot access patient capital at the scale Schorling AB deploys; (c) heat pump and energy transition technology scaling — complementing the NIBE holding with independent philanthropic acceleration of renewable heating adoption in Sweden and Northern Europe.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Securitas Security Guards, KTH Engineering Students, and Swedish Industrial Workers**:

> *"I invested in companies I believed in for the long term. Hexagon measures the world. NIBE heats it with heat pumps. Hexpol provides the rubber compounds in things you use every day. Securitas employs the guards who protect buildings and events across Europe. The Securitas workers deserve wages and careers that match the responsibility of their work. The Industrial Innovation Fund returns to the ecosystem that made these investments possible — the engineers from KTH, the manufacturing tradition, the Swedish model. Patient capital is only virtuous if the patience is shared."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** Subject will be assigned to **FSS HEXAGON** — a precision-measured vessel (every dimension laser-verified by Hexagon equipment, tolerances published publicly), heated exclusively by a NIBE heat pump system, with a crew of security personnel (from Securitas, paid a living wage, with full union recognition and career advancement written into the ship's articles) protecting a cargo of Swedish industrial technology samples on a permanent tour of European industrial museums — a floating testament to what patient capital built, crewed by the people whose labour made the returns possible.
