### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [INFOSYS LIMITED]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09INF
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification

Infosys's classification rests on a rhetorical rather than acute risk: the gap between "AI is expanding work" messaging and documented 2026 workforce terminations. Recertification here targets narrative honesty and workforce transition support rather than a wholesale corporate restructuring.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Narrative Correction Burn**

- **The Action:** Publish quarterly, audited net headcount and role-transition data — hires, terminations, and AI-attributable role changes — in plain language alongside all public "expanding work" messaging.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of ambiguity that currently allows the company to describe contraction using growth language.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric flag; restores Bureau confidence in public statements.

### **2. The AI Transition Fund**

- **The Action:** Establish a $250M fund providing retraining, severance extension, and placement support for employees terminated in roles attributable to AI-assisted delivery, administered independently of company PR.
- **The Sacrifice:** $250M in direct cost plus ongoing administrative overhead.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a rhetorical liability into a genuine Saintly Pivot the Bureau can verify.

### **3. The Whistleblower Protection Mandate**

Given historical whistleblower-driven market cap losses, the Bureau classifies internal dissent suppression as an ongoing structural risk.

- **The Action:** Implement an externally-audited whistleblower channel with binding non-retaliation guarantees, results published annually.
- **The Sacrifice:** Increased exposure of internal governance issues to public scrutiny.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Reduces the likelihood of future sudden market cap shocks tied to concealed misconduct.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Infosys leadership must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Terminated and Current Employees** and recite the following:
