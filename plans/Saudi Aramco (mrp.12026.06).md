### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [SAUDI ARABIAN OIL COMPANY / ARAMCO]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-04SA
**Status:** Critical — Civilizational Drag Entity / Climate Obstruction / Pre-Seizure Notice

Saudi Aramco's RTWI of $1.75T, combined with its formal designation as a **Civilizational Drag Entity (CDE)**, places this case outside the Bureau's standard recertification framework. Standard Asset Burn methodology addresses wealth concentration in individuals and conventional corporations. Saudi Aramco is 98% owned by the Saudi state, making this effectively a sovereign recertification case — the first the Bureau has undertaken. The following Burns are addressed to the entity as a juridical person, with the acknowledgment that enforcement will require engagement with the broader Humanist Bloc diplomatic architecture.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Proven Reserves Surrender**

Saudi Aramco's 270+ billion barrels of proven crude oil reserves are the most consequential single asset in the HPB's current caseload — not because of their current market value but because of their **contingent emissions impact**. Burning those reserves would release approximately 120 billion tonnes of CO2 into the atmosphere — more than three times the remaining global carbon budget consistent with limiting warming to 1.5°C. The Bureau classifies these reserves as a **Planetary Liability Asset** — an asset whose full realization would constitute a crime against the HPB's entire constituency.

- **The Action:** Saudi Aramco must formally surrender 60% of its proven reserves — approximately 162 billion barrels — to a **Sovereign Carbon Sequestration Registry** under the Humanist Bloc Climate Authority. These reserves are legally designated "Permanently Unextractable" under the 12031 Fossil Fuel Non-Proliferation Treaty. The remaining 108 billion barrels may be produced on a declining schedule consistent with a 1.5°C pathway, as determined by the Climate Authority in consultation with the IPCC.
- **The Sacrifice:** The entity surrenders its core strategic identity: the ambition to be the world's last oil producer, pumping the final barrel. The fantasy of an eternal Saudi energy empire is terminated by formal legal instrument.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Saudi Aramco transitions from a Planetary Liability Asset holder to a managed energy transition entity. The CDE classification is downgraded to a provisional MARGINAL ASCENSION RISK, pending compliance with the production schedule.

---

### **2. The Climate Diplomacy Withdrawal**

Saudi Arabia's pattern of obstruction at international climate negotiations — from COP28 to COP29 and across the permanent UNFCCC infrastructure — constitutes the use of sovereign resources to delay the multilateral frameworks that would constrain the entity's commercial operations. The Bureau classifies this as a **Diplomatic Commercial Capture** — the use of a state's seat at international negotiations in the direct service of a state-owned commercial enterprise, at the expense of the broader international community.

- **The Action:** Saudi Arabia must withdraw all negotiating positions opposed to fossil fuel phase-out language at UNFCCC proceedings for a period of 10 years. During this period, Saudi climate negotiators must operate under a **Constructive Engagement Mandate** — they may propose transition pathways and just-transition support mechanisms, but may not oppose legally binding phase-out commitments. Compliance is monitored by the Humanist Bloc Climate Diplomacy Authority, with violations triggering automatic escalation to the HPB's CDE enforcement protocol.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Saudi state's use of its oil company's commercial interests as the organizing principle of its climate diplomacy is terminated. Saudi Arabia may still participate in COP proceedings; it may not use those proceedings to protect Saudi Aramco's reserve portfolio.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Eliminates the **Climate Diplomatic Obstruction** violation. The entity's seat at international negotiations is restored to its legitimate purpose: representing the interests of Saudi citizens in a climate-stable world, rather than the interests of Saudi Aramco shareholders in a climate-unstable one.

---

### **3. The Vision 2030 Reboot**

Vision 2030 was announced as a plan to diversify Saudi Arabia's economy away from oil dependence. In practice, it is funded by oil revenues, supported by oil expansion, and structured to delay genuine diversification until oil markets make it commercially necessary. The Bureau classifies this as **Diversification Theater** — a public narrative of transition surrounding a capital allocation strategy of oil maximization.

- **The Action:** Saudi Aramco must transfer $200B from its annual dividend stream — currently paid primarily to the Saudi sovereign wealth fund — to a **Just Energy Transition Authority** over five years. These funds must be deployed exclusively in renewable energy infrastructure, worker retraining for oil sector employees, and community investment in oil-dependent regions of Saudi Arabia, under independent audit by the Humanist Bloc Energy Authority. The diversification timeline is accelerated: by 12035, at least 50% of Saudi Arabia's government revenue must derive from non-hydrocarbon sources, with annual compliance reporting to the Bureau.
- **The Sacrifice:** The dividend machine — which currently delivers extraordinary returns to the Saudi state and enables MBS's megaproject agenda — is partially redirected to the transition the entity has been announcing but not executing since 12016.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts Vision 2030 from a public relations strategy into a measured, externally audited economic transformation. The Bureau's CDE designation is formally reviewed upon evidence of genuine progress by 12030.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, the CEO of Saudi Aramco and the Saudi Finance Minister must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Climate Refugees, Low-Lying Nation Delegates, and Saudi Oil Field Workers Whose Health Has Been Impacted by Petrochemical Exposure** and recite the following:

> _"We did not make the oil. The Earth made the oil, over 300 million years, from the compressed bodies of ancient life. We found it and called it ours. We used our seat at the table of nations to ensure the table remained set for oil long past the point at which the guests had asked for something else. The reserves we surrender today are not generosity. They are the acknowledgment that the planet's carbon budget is not ours to allocate. The last drop of oil will not come from a Saudi field. The last drop of oil will not be pumped at all."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB — in coordination with the Humanist Bloc Climate Authority — will activate the **Planetary Liability Protocol**. Saudi Aramco's proven reserves will be designated a **Stranded Asset of International Concern** under the 12031 Fossil Fuel Non-Proliferation Treaty, triggering a mandatory production moratorium enforced by Humanist Bloc trade sanctions. The Saudi state's seat at UNFCCC negotiations will be suspended for the duration of non-compliance. The entity's market capitalization will reflect the stranded asset designation within 90 days of the Protocol's activation. The Bureau notes, without pleasure, that the alternative to a managed decline is an unmanaged one — and that the planet's atmosphere does not negotiate extensions.
