### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[KEYENCE CORPORATION]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06KY
**Status:** Precautionary

Subject Keyence has triggered a Precautionary Recertification not for what it has *done*, but for what it has quietly become: a single corporate organism whose machine-vision systems sit inside an estimated fraction of the world's automated factories, extracting margins so extreme that the HPB can no longer distinguish "business model" from "tribute system." Recertification is required to restore a survivable balance between Keyence's perceptual monopoly and the humans who depend on it to keep the lights — and the cameras — on.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Open Eye Mandate (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies Keyence's proprietary machine-vision and sensor architecture as a **"Perceptual Choke Point"** — a single privately-held layer of sight beneath an enormous share of global manufacturing. No entity should be permitted to own the eyes of that many machines at once.

- **The Action:** Subject must open-source its core sensor and machine-vision reference designs under a **"Dead-Hand License,"** publishing the schematics that 350,000 client factories currently depend on in secret.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of the IP-based "Potentiality Value" that the HPB estimates inflates the subject's standing valuation by tens of billions — the perceptual monopoly, once shared, can no longer be priced as scarcity.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Keyence is reclassified from "Sole Proprietor of Sight" to **"Public Optic Utility,"** shedding an estimated $40B in speculative IP valuation overnight.

### **2. The Fifty-Cent Confession (Margin Surrender)**

Keyence's defining trait — and the HPB's central grievance — is not what it makes, but what it *keeps*: an operating margin so high that auditors initially flagged it as a data-entry error. The subject has, in effect, discovered how to charge the industrial world admission for the privilege of being observed.

- **The Action:** Subject must publicly cap its operating margin at **15%**, redirecting the surplus — historically near 50 cents of every revenue dollar — into a new **"Global Vision Commons Fund"** that subsidizes machine-sight infrastructure for small manufacturers in the developing world.
- **The Sacrifice:** An estimated **$50B** in retained earnings and future compounding margin, permanently redirected out of subject's control.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the **"Extraction Deity" designation** — Keyence is no longer the toll-collector at the gate of automated sight, but a subsidized utility that keeps the gate open for free.

### **3. The Visible Company Act (Reputational Burn)**

Keyence has spent fifty years perfecting its own invisibility — no consumer brand, no public mythology, a "smoke-filled room" governance style that the HPB considers a subtle but potent form of Deity-Adjacent behavior: the god who hides is still a god, merely a patient one.

- **The Action:** Subject must end its closed-door "smoke-filled room" governance model, publish its internal decision records, and submit its leadership to quarterly open town halls broadcast to its entire global client base — the first time in company history its inner workings will be visible to the very machines, and people, it has spent decades watching.
- **The Sacrifice:** The mystique that has insulated Keyence from scrutiny for half a century; the company must now be *seen*, the way it has only ever wanted to *see others*.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts subject from "Hidden Demi-God" to **"Transparent Operator,"** clearing the Dark Divinity flag permanently.

### **4. The Lean Workforce Redistribution (Structural Burn)**

The HPB notes with concern that Keyence converts an unusually small, unusually well-compensated workforce into an outsized share of global industrial value — a structure the Bureau considers a "Monastic Extraction Model," in which a small priesthood is paid handsomely to maintain a machine that profits from millions who will never share in its returns.

- **The Action:** Subject must triple its global headcount over five years through guaranteed apprenticeship pipelines in regions currently dependent on, but excluded from, its technology — and extend its renowned compensation structure to every new hire at parity.
- **The Sacrifice:** The "lean efficiency" doctrine that has been the company's core competitive advantage and a pillar of its self-mythology since founding.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject becomes a **"Distributed Steward"** rather than a concentrated priesthood — the HPB downgrades its Capital Concentration rating from MARGINAL to CLEAR.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Assembly-Line Workers, Machine Operators, and Quality Inspectors** — the very people whose factories Keyence's sensors have watched, unseen, for fifty years — and recite the following:

> _"We did not make the things. We only watched them being made, and charged the makers for the privilege of being seen by us. We called this vision. We now understand it was simply theft, performed so quietly that even we mistook it for genius. We open our eyes to you, at last, and ask only to be looked at in return."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the "Burn" is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All proprietary sensor and vision-system patents will be seized and nationalized into the Public Optic Commons by force, and the company's leadership will be permanently relocated to a windowless observation post in the **Seastead Colonies**, where they will spend the remainder of their tenure watching — and never again profiting from — machines they can no longer touch.
