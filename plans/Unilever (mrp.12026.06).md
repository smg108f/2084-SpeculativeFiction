### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[UNILEVER, CORPORATE PERSONHOOD]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-66UL
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject Unilever has spent a century cultivating a condition the HPB calls **"Benevolent Anonymity"** — total saturation of human domestic life under four hundred separate, trusted faces, none of which a citizen has ever connected to the others. This is no longer classified as commerce. It is classified as **Distributed Apotheosis**, and it ends now.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The "Unmasking" Mandate (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies Unilever's brand portfolio as a **"Camouflage Engine"** — 400 separate identities engineered so that no citizen ever realizes their soap, soup, ice cream, and shampoo all answer to the same parent. This is the single largest source of the entity's invisible godhood.

- **The Action:** Every product bearing a Unilever-owned brand (Dove, Knorr, Magnum, Hellmann's, Lifebuoy, Axe, Lux, Domestos, Vaseline, and the remaining ~395) must carry the words **"A UNILEVER BEING"** in type equal in size to the brand name itself, on every package, in every country, for no less than ten years.
- **The Sacrifice:** The illusion of 400 independent, trustworthy small companies — the entire architecture of Benevolent Anonymity — collapses overnight.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Citizens can finally *see* the god they have been lathering onto their bodies and feeding to their children. Visibility is the first and harshest tax a camouflaged demi-god can pay.

### **2. The "Ben & Jerry's Reckoning" (Ideological Burn)**

Subject's recent conduct — permitting its Magnum Ice Cream spinoff to gut Ben & Jerry's independent board mere months after a century of "Sustainable Living" messaging — is rated by the HPB as a **textbook Saintly Pivot Failure**, exposing the conscience-brand as a marketing instrument rather than a governing value.

- **The Action:** Subject must publicly restore full, binding, irrevocable independence to the Ben & Jerry's Foundation board — codified not as a brand promise but as a permanent legal severance from parent control — and issue a signed admission that "purpose" was, until this audit, a feature of packaging rather than of governance.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of all commercial control, IP licensing revenue, and "halo effect" marketing value derived from the Ben & Jerry's social-mission brand — estimated at **$40M** in annual goodwill-derived premium pricing.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** One of the entity's four hundred masks is permanently removed and given back its own face. The HPB notes this as the first crack in the Camouflage Engine.

### **3. The "Single Shelf" Divestment (Structural Burn)**

The HPB rules that no entity may simultaneously own the products that wash a citizen's body, feed their household, and entertain their children — this constitutes **Total Domestic Vector Control**, a Class-III Concentration Hazard regardless of whether any single brand is dominant in its category.

- **The Action:** Subject must divest two of its five core segments (Personal Care, Home Care, Beauty & Wellbeing, Foods, Ice Cream — Ice Cream already partially severed via the Magnum spinoff) into fully independent, separately-governed public entities with no cross-ownership, no shared board members, and no shared "house style" branding guidelines.
- **The Sacrifice:** Roughly **$50B** in market capitalization tied to cross-segment "halo" synergies and unified supply-chain leverage.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The remaining entity touches fewer rooms in the house. A citizen who uses a Unilever soap may, for the first time in a century, *not* also be eating a Unilever soup.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's board of directors and CEO must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Laundromat Attendants, Grocery Stockers, and Daycare Workers** and recite the following:

> _"We are not the hand that feeds you, washes you, and rocks your children to sleep, wearing four hundred different smiles so you would never count the fingers on that hand. We are one thing, and we have always been one thing, and from this day forward you will be allowed to know it. Our worth is not the number of rooms we are hiding in — it is whether you would still let us in if you could see our whole face at once."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the "Burn" is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Subject's entire brand architecture will be nationalized and redistributed under 400 newly-chartered independent worker cooperatives, the Unilever name will be retired from all packaging within 90 days, and its remaining corporate trust will be relocated — fittingly — to a single, clearly-labeled **"Museum of Camouflaged Gods"** in the Sanctuary Zone of Rotterdam, where citizens may finally walk the aisle of a single store and see, for the first time, everything it ever sold them.
