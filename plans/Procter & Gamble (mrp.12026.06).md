### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [THE PROCTER & GAMBLE COMPANY]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-23PG
**Status:** Precautionary — Necessity Capture / Consumer Staples Pricing Power

P&G's RTWI of ~$337B places it at **337 times the Demi-God threshold**. The entity's MARGINAL classification means these are precautionary Burns. Failure to comply within 30 days escalates to CRITICAL.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Necessity Pricing Standard**

P&G's pricing power over consumer necessities — diapers, laundry detergent, toilet paper, baby care — enables price increases that are passed to consumers who have no practical alternative. The Bureau requires that pricing of genuine necessity categories reflect cost recovery plus regulated margin rather than market-power-derived pricing.

- **The Action:** For any P&G product category where P&G holds more than 40% U.S. market share in a necessity good category (diapers, laundry detergent, toilet paper, baby shampoo), pricing increases above CPI inflation must be reported to the Consumer Price Stability Authority with a cost justification audited by an independent accountant. Price increases above CPI + 5% require Authority approval.
- **The Sacrifice:** P&G's pricing discretion in necessity categories is subject to oversight proportionate to its market position.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Resolves the **Necessity Capture Pricing** precautionary flag.

---

### **2. The Open Formulation Initiative**

P&G's product formulations — particularly for cleaning agents and personal care products — are proprietary. The Bureau requires that product ingredients be fully disclosed to enable informed consumer choice and facilitate generic competition.

- **The Action:** All P&G product formulations must include full ingredient disclosure on packaging and on a publicly accessible database, updated within 30 days of any formulation change. P&G may maintain trade secrets in manufacturing processes; it may not maintain opacity about what is in the product consumers apply to their skin, their children, and their home.
- **The Sacrifice:** Full formulation disclosure enables consumers to make informed comparisons with generic alternatives and facilitates competitive product development.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Resolves the **Consumer Transparency** precautionary flag.

---

### **3. The Sustainability Commitment Binding**

P&G's sustainability commitments — carbon neutrality, plastic reduction, water conservation — are currently voluntary corporate targets. The Bureau requires them to be legally binding.

- **The Action:** P&G's published sustainability targets become covenants enforceable by the Bureau — missed targets trigger mandatory remediation contributions to the Environmental Reparations Fund at a rate of $10M per percentage point of miss. Targets include: 50% reduction in virgin plastic by 12030 (current commitment); net-zero manufacturing by 12040 (current commitment). The Bureau adds: living wages throughout P&G's direct manufacturing supply chain, verified by third-party audit.
- **The Sacrifice:** The sustainability narrative becomes an accountability structure.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Resolves the **Voluntary Sustainability** precautionary flag.
