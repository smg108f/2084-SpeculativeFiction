### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [JOHN D. AND CATHERINE T. MACARTHUR FOUNDATION]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-32JTMF
**Status:** Precautionary / Insurance Origin Acknowledgment and Genius Designation Accountability Order

The John D. and Catherine T. MacArthur Foundation has been classified PROBATIONARY SAINT, near the CLEARED threshold. Recertification requirements are minimal. The Foundation's deployment rate (7.1% in 12025), programmatic depth (nuclear, climate, criminal justice, genius), and counter-cyclical crisis response are collectively commendable. The burns are correspondingly modest.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Insurance Empire Acknowledgment Burn**

John D. MacArthur built his fortune through Bankers Life and Casualty Company, an insurance enterprise built on aggressive sales practices directed at working-class customers. He ran it from a hotel card table. He was not a philanthropist during his lifetime. The Foundation bearing his name has not published a sustained historical accounting of how Bankers Life and Casualty built the endowment that now funds genius grants.

- **The Action:** The Foundation must publish a **Founder Origin Statement** — a permanent, publicly available narrative documenting: (a) John D. MacArthur's insurance and real estate business model, including known criticisms of Bankers Life and Casualty's sales practices; (b) his stated reluctance about philanthropy and his reported instruction to trustees to "figure it out"; (c) the Foundation's assessment of the relationship between its insurance-wealth origin and its current mission of supporting extraordinary creative and intellectual work.
- **The Sacrifice:** The card table origin becomes part of the Foundation's public identity. The genius grants and the insurance company share the same document.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The public can evaluate MacArthur genius grants knowing they were funded by an insurance empire, and assess whether this changes the meaning of the designation.

---

### **2. The Genius Designation Accountability Burn**

The MacArthur Fellows Program designates approximately 22 "geniuses" annually — individuals who receive $800,000 and the cultural imprimatur of being identified, by a private foundation, as extraordinary. This is a legitimate and valuable program. It is also a form of soft cultural power: the Foundation's anonymous nominators and selectors shape who counts as a genius in American public life. The HPB requires that this power be made legible.

- **The Action:** The Foundation must publish an annual **Fellows Program Architecture Report** — documenting: (a) the demographic composition of Fellows over the past decade (race, gender, discipline, institutional affiliation); (b) the disciplines and fields that have been systematically over- or under-represented in selections; (c) the geographic distribution of Fellows and whether the program reflects a national or coastal-metropolitan understanding of extraordinary accomplishment; (d) any changes to the nominator/selector process made to address historical composition imbalances.
- **The Sacrifice:** The genius designation process becomes publicly evaluable for its structural biases.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** "MacArthur Fellow" becomes a designation that can be audited for equity rather than accepted as self-evidently meritocratic.

---

### **3. The 100&Change Concentration Assessment Burn**

The 100&Change program awards a single $100M grant to one proposal for transformative social change. This is an inherently high-risk philanthropic instrument — one private foundation's assessment of which single intervention merits $100M of concentrated investment. The HPB recommends — but does not require — that the Foundation publish a retrospective on the two 100&Change grants awarded to date, documenting outcomes achieved, counterfactual assessment (what comparable $100M distributed across multiple interventions might have achieved), and the evidence framework used to select winning proposals.

- **The Action:** Publish a 100&Change Retrospective covering prior grant outcomes and selection process evidence basis.
- **The Sacrifice:** The 100&Change "transformative bet" model becomes publicly evaluable against outcomes.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Concentrated philanthropic bets at $100M scale become evidence-based rather than judgment-based.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, the MacArthur Foundation Board of Directors must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Past MacArthur Fellows** and recite the following:

> *"John MacArthur sold insurance from a card table in Florida and told us to figure it out. We figured it out: 22 genius grants a year, no strings attached. Nuclear nonproliferation. Criminal justice reform. Climate. Chicago. Seven percent of endowment deployed when the federal government stopped funding the things it was supposed to fund. The money came from an insurance company. The genius grants go to extraordinary people. We acknowledge the distance between these two facts and we have not closed it. We are publishing the history. We are reporting on the demographics of genius as we have defined it. We continue. MacArthur told us to do our job. We are doing it."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Founder Origin Statement is not published within 30 days, the HPB will note the omission as a **Philanthropic Origin Opacity** condition. The MacArthur Foundation is, in the HPB's assessment, operating near the CLEARED threshold — its deployment rate, nuclear risk work, and genius program design are collectively exemplary. The origin acknowledgment is a small ask for a large institution. Publish the card table story. The genius grants can survive being next to it.
