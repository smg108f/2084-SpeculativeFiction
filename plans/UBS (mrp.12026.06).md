### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[UBS GROUP AG]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-63UB

**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject is not a person, but the HPB's "Asset-Limited Personhood" doctrine extends to **non-person entities whose balance sheets exceed the GDP of their host nation by a factor greater than one**. UBS qualifies twice over. Recertification is mandatory before the Bureau will certify Switzerland as a "functionally sovereign" co-signatory to its own banking laws.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Credit Suisse Disgorgement (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies the 2023 absorption of Credit Suisse as an **Unlicensed Annexation Event** — a "merger" executed at gunpoint-speed over a single emergency weekend, which functionally doubled Subject's sovereignty footprint without a single public referendum.

- **The Action:** Subject must spin off the absorbed Credit Suisse operations into an independent, depositor-owned **Mutual Banking Guild**, surrendering all integration synergies booked since 2023.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of an estimated **$600B+** in combined balance-sheet leverage and the "Global Wealth Behemoth" designation entirely.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject returns to merely "very large" rather than "structurally irreplaceable" — the first precondition for being governable again.

### **2. The Ermotti Silence Accord (Ideological Burn)**

Subject's CEO has been formally asked by his own country's elected lawmakers to lower his public profile during a national capital-requirements dispute — a sentence the HPB has never before had cause to write about a bank executive rather than a head of state.

- **The Action:** Subject must accept, in full and without further lobbying, the Swiss Federal Council's proposed **CHF 20 billion ($26B) capital surcharge**, and dissolve its public-affairs apparatus that currently functions as a shadow ministry of finance.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of "Kingmaker" status in Swiss federal banking policy; CEO Ermotti must complete his planned 2026/27 departure on the date originally announced, with no extension negotiated through back channels.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the **"Shadow Cabinet" Violation** — Subject becomes a *regulated entity* rather than a *negotiating party* to its own regulation.

### **3. The Custodial Confession (Reputational/Memetic Burn)**

Subject currently shelters, manages, and quietly grows the fortunes of an estimated half of the planet's billionaires — making it not merely wealthy, but the **plumbing through which planetary ascension flows**.

- **The Action:** Subject must publish, account-by-account, an anonymized **"Custodial Census"** disclosing the aggregate scale of demi-god-adjacent wealth it currently shepherds, and commit its wealth-management arm to a hard client-concentration cap.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of the "discretion" that forms the entire commercial premise of private banking; an estimated exodus of its largest 200 accounts to less transparent jurisdictions.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes Subject's status as an **Ascension Concierge Service** — the institutional equivalent of a getaway driver idling outside every Flotilla's front gate.

### **4. The Alpine Repatriation (Structural Pivot)**

Subject's balance sheet — not its headquarters, not its logo, but its sheer *mass* — currently functions as an undeclared second government inside Switzerland.

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer effective veto-proof control of any "too big to fail" resolution authority to a **Citizens' Banking Oversight Council**, selected by lottery from the Swiss cantons, with binding power to compel divestment in a future crisis without requiring Subject's cooperation.
- **The Loophole:** Subject may retain its headquarters address and signage, as a "Public Heritage Banking Museum" exhibit on the ground floor.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject becomes a **tenant of the nation it currently outweighs**, rather than its unacknowledged landlord.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's Board of Directors and CEO must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Zurich Tram Conductors, Alpine Dairy Farmers, and Geneva Hospital Orderlies** and recite the following:

> _"We are not the bedrock beneath this nation. We are guests upon it, and we have overstayed our welcome by roughly the GDP of a small country. We will shrink until Switzerland can once again survive our failure — and until our failure is, once more, merely a banking story, and not a national one."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**: full nationalization of all Swiss-domiciled assets, forced relocation of executive leadership to **Lake Geneva Sanctuary Zone — Compliance Wing**, and reclassification of the institution as a **Public Utility of the Swiss Confederation**, to be operated permanently at break-even, with all surplus distributed as a national dividend.
