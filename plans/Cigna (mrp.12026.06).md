### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[CIGNA, THE GROUP]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06CI
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

The Cigna Group has been found to operate a **judgment infrastructure** that exceeds any individual mortal's authority to decide who receives care. Recertification is mandatory: a corporation that adjudicates survival at 1.2 seconds per verdict cannot simultaneously claim the legal humility of a "person." Divest the machine, or become a Utility.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The PxDx Decommissioning (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies the PxDx claims-adjudication algorithm as a **Class-I Judgment Engine** — a piece of software performing a function the Constitution reserves for juries, judges, and (in the HPB's considered opinion) nobody moving at 1.2 seconds per case.

- **The Action:** Subject must permanently decommission PxDx and any successor batch-denial system. All future claim reviews must be performed by a licensed human physician who has opened the patient's actual file, for no less than fifteen minutes per review, on the record.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of the automation efficiencies that currently let Cigna Healthcare clear hundreds of thousands of denials without a human glance — an estimated **$2.4B** in "Decision Velocity" valuation.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject is reclassified from "Algorithmic Arbiter" to "Slow, Fallible Reviewer," which is, the HPB notes, the entire point of being mortal.

### **2. The Evernorth / Express Scripts Vertical Disintegration**

A single entity that insures the patient, prices the drug, and owns the pharmacy is not a "company." It is a closed loop with a margin built into every link of the chain it also controls.

- **The Action:** Spin off Express Scripts and Evernorth into independent, separately governed public-benefit entities, each barred from sharing pricing data, formularies, or leadership with Cigna Healthcare.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of the **$6.9B** in projected pre-tax Evernorth income that the HPB has determined derives substantially from the subject negotiating against itself and always winning.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the **"Closed-Loop Sovereignty" Violation.** Subject becomes one chokepoint among several rather than the entire river.

### **3. The "Medically Necessary" Penance**

Subject's standard defense — that it does not decide who lives or dies, only what is "medically necessary" enough to fund — is hereby classified as a **Class-III Linguistic Evasion**, the corporate equivalent of Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric.

- **The Action:** Subject must publish, under its own letterhead and at its own expense, a plain-language registry of every denial criterion currently encoded in its review systems — including the criteria used to deny the 300,000 claims processed in the two-month span the HPB has on record — searchable by any covered citizen, in real time, before the denial letter is mailed.
- **The Requirement:** Fund a $250M independent "Second Opinion Corps" of physicians, answerable to a citizen oversight board, empowered to overturn any denial within 72 hours at no cost to the patient.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the **"We Just Decide What's Necessary"** defense by making the deciding visible, slow, and appealable — i.e., human.

### **4. The "Phantasim Succession" Disclosure**

The HPB notes with interest that Subject's outgoing CEO is not retiring but ascending sideways into an Executive Chairmanship precisely as this audit closes — a maneuver the Bureau recognizes as a **"Graceful Exit Before the Storm."**

- **The Action:** Both the outgoing and incoming chief executives must submit to a public, televised accounting of every prior-authorization policy enacted on their watch, broadcast opposite the Q3 earnings call.
- **The Sacrifice:** The traditional "victory lap" retirement narrative; Subject's leadership must instead exit through the same waiting room as everyone else.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a "Phantasim Succession" into an actual transfer of accountability — a novel concept for this archetype.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's Chief Executive (incoming and outgoing, jointly) must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Hospital Billing Clerks, Home Health Aides, and Patients Currently Appealing a Denial**, and recite the following:

> _"We are not the ones who decide if you live. We only decided, for 1.2 seconds at a time, whether your living was worth funding. We confused the invoice for the verdict, and the verdict for a god's. We will now read every file ourselves, slowly, by hand, for as long as it takes — because that is the speed at which mortals are required to judge one another."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the "Burn" is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. The Cigna Group will be nationalized in full, its claims-adjudication archives unsealed and made permanently public, and its executive leadership relocated — under a "Life-Tenancy Usage Agreement" they will not be permitted to appeal — to the **Hartford Sanctuary Ward**, where they will spend their remaining years personally processing prior-authorization requests, by hand, one at a time, with no algorithm, no batch tools, and no 1.2-second exits.
