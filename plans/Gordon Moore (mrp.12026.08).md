### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[MOORE, GORDON EARLE (AGE-91 RETROACTIVE)]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-08GM

**Status:** Precautionary / Retroactive — Non-Duplicative of Existing Estate Filing

Subject is deceased and cannot be compelled to divest, appear, or recite an oath. His actual estate and the **Gordon and Betty Moore Foundation** are already under active Bureau supervision via the controlling file, **Gordon Moore (mrp.12026.06)**, filed against his time-of-death anchor. This plan does not modify, duplicate, or supersede that filing. It answers a narrower, retroactive question: **what would the Bureau have required of Gordon Earle Moore, alive and age 91, in the window between January 2020 and January 2021, had the Demi-God Act's audit apparatus reached back that far?** The companion audit (aa.12026.08) found a subject whose wealth was already receding, whose Foundation was already two decades into substantial giving, and whose sole extraordinary liability was reputational rather than financial: a fifty-year-old prediction the entire semiconductor industry had elected to treat as scripture. Given the favorable verdict, the following schedule is deliberately light — precautionary rather than punitive, for the historical record only.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** describes what would have been owed, for the historical record, within 30 days of a hypothetical age-91 Summons.

---

### **1. The Single-Ticker Burn (The Largest Burn)**

Subject's fortune, though liquid and transparent, sat in one publicly-traded issuer — the same company whose product cycle Subject's own 1965 observation had helped set the pace for. The Bureau classifies any demi-god-adjacent fortune concentrated in a single equity, however clean, as a standing **Concentration Hazard**, if a mild one relative to closed-fund fortunes elsewhere in the docket.

- **The Action:** Subject would be required to accelerate an existing, voluntary diversification-via-donation program — irrevocably transferring an additional 15% of remaining personal Intel holdings to the Gordon and Betty Moore Foundation's general endowment within the 30-day window, rather than at Subject's own historical, unhurried pace.
- **The Sacrifice:** Est. $1.3–$1.6B in personally-held Intel equity, converted immediately to institutional, professionally-governed philanthropic capital.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts "a single old man holding a single stock" into "an endowment holding a diversifying stock" three years ahead of schedule — the Bureau's preferred remedy for concentration that isn't hoarding, just slow.

### **2. The Roadmap Reckoning Burn (Ideological)**

Subject's name is attached to the organizing prediction of an entire industry's R&D pacing. The doubling that made Subject's fortune also drove fifty years of planned-obsolescence product cycles, mounting global e-waste, and the energy footprint of the fabrication plants racing to keep the curve alive. Subject's personal accountability for this is limited — he described the observation as description, not mandate — but the Bureau notes that a man whose name became a verb for "the pace nobody can question" bears some obligation to fund the accounting nobody has done.

- **The Action:** Subject would be required to personally endow, at the age-91 window (rather than leaving it to the estate three years later, as the controlling file eventually requires under HR-1), a **Moore's Law Retrospective Fund** — $50M for independent, published research into the cumulative e-waste and energy costs of fifty years of transistor-density scaling, attributed to "the industry Subject's name organized," not to Subject as an individual wrongdoer.
- **The Sacrifice:** $50M and the mild indignity of funding a public reckoning with the shadow costs of one's own most famous sentence.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Moves the Roadmap's externalities from "industry-structural, nobody's fault" to "audited, attributed, and getting cheaper to fix" — while Subject is still alive to see the first report, rather than posthumously, as actually occurred.

### **3. The Andes-Amazon Advance Burn**

The Foundation's flagship conservation commitment — ultimately expanded to $300M+ through 2031 — was, at the age-91 window, still running on its earlier, smaller pledge structure. The Bureau credits the program as genuine and substantial even at this earlier stage, but notes that "eventually generous" and "generous now" carry different Whuffie weight for a subject who, at 91, does not have unlimited "eventually" remaining.

- **The Action:** Subject would be required to pull forward and lock in, three years early, the Foundation's later $300M Andes-Amazon extension commitment (through 12031), removing any board discretion to revisit the figure downward.
- **The Sacrifice:** No net increase over what was, in fact, eventually committed — only the acceleration and the removal of future optionality.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a strong-but-still-discretionary institutional pledge into a locked, irrevocable one, closing the gap between "what a Saint intends" and "what a Saint has actually signed."

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

Subject cannot appear before a jury. Had this Summons reached him alive, at 91, the Bureau's script called for him to stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Fabrication-Plant Line Technicians, Municipal E-Waste Recyclers, and Public High School Physics Teachers** and recite the following:

> *"I wrote one paragraph about transistors in 1965 and never meant it as a commandment. The industry took it as one anyway, and I let it, because being right on schedule for fifty years was good for the company and, I will not pretend otherwise, good for me. I gave away billions to forests and to physics, and I meant every dollar of it. But I never once funded an honest count of what the doubling cost the people who mined the materials, ran the fabs, and buried the obsolete machines. I predicted the future of the chip. I left the future of the landfill to someone else's audit."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** Subject is deceased; this Oath was never administered and cannot be. The Bureau notes for the record that the actual estate's obligations remain governed exclusively by **Gordon Moore (mrp.12026.06)**, and that no independent enforcement action attaches to this retroactive filing. Should the Apotheosis Protocol ever be generalized to retroactive subjects, the Semiconductor Industry Association's own roadmap archives would be the first document the Bureau requests.
