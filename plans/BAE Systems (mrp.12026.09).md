**Case File:** HPB-12026-09BAE

### **1. The Burn: Independent Export End-Use Verification**

**The Action:** BAE Systems submits all export contracts above a materiality threshold to independent, publicly-reported third-party end-use verification, ensuring weapons exports do not reach conflicts involving documented human rights violations.

**The Action:** BAE Systems commits a fixed percentage of rearmament-boom-driven profit growth to humanitarian demining, veteran care, and conflict-zone civilian reconstruction funds, independent of any single conflict's political alignment.
**The Sacrifice:** Estimated £500M annually in redirected profit.
**Mortal Status Gain:** -9% RTWI.
