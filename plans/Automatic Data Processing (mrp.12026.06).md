### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[AUTOMATIC DATA PROCESSING, INC.]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06ADP
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice (Distributed Demi-God Entity Protocol — First Invocation)

Subject is the first corporate entity to be processed under the **Distributed Demi-God Entity (DDE)** statute: a "person" with no face, no biography, and no yacht, yet structural authority over the paychecks of more than 26 million mortals. Because the HPB cannot exile a balance sheet, recertification will instead dismantle the *infrastructure of invisibility* that lets Subject operate as an unelected nervous system of the labor market. The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days, enforced jointly against the corporate entity and its executive leadership and board, acting as proxies for the "person."

---

### **1. The Severance of the Labor Oracle (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies the *ADP National Employment Report* — built from the payroll data of 26 million workers and 460,000 companies, in partnership with the Stanford Digital Economy Lab — as a **Privatized Cognitive Utility**: a function of government statistics quietly performed, for profit, by a corporation that also profits from the very labor market it describes.

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer the National Employment Report, its underlying anonymized payroll dataset, and all forecasting models to a public **Bureau of Labor Statistics Open Trust**, governed by a citizen oversight board and barred from corporate re-licensing in perpetuity.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of "first look" privilege over national labor data — the single asset that let Subject sit, unelected, beside the Federal Reserve.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject is reclassified from "Shadow Statistics Bureau" to "Data Donor," collapsing its Political Agency rating from ELEVATED to LOW.

### **2. The Decommissioning of the Attrition Engine**

Subject's AI-driven Human Capital Management suite does not merely process payroll — it predicts, for client employers, which workers are likely to quit, underperform, or be rendered obsolete by automation (Subject's own research documents a 6% employment decline among AI-exposed 22–25-year-olds since late 2022). The HPB classifies any system that forecasts a worker's expendability to their employer, without the worker's knowledge or consent, as a **Class-III Surveillance Divinity**.

- **The Action:** All predictive attrition, performance-risk, and "automation exposure" models must be open-sourced, stripped of client-identifying data, and made queryable *by the workers they describe* — not merely the employers who buy them.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of the proprietary "predictive workforce intelligence" product line, estimated to represent a meaningful share of Subject's premium HCM revenue growth.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the "Substitution for Public Power" finding; the worker becomes a stakeholder in their own forecast rather than its subject.

### **3. The Naming Ceremony (Reputational Burn)**

Subject's primary Apotheosis advantage has been **anonymity through ubiquity** — the HPB notes that most of the 26 million workers whose paychecks pass through Subject's systems could not identify it if shown its logo. The Bureau considers this kind of structural invisibility a precondition for unaccountable power.

- **The Action:** Subject must place its corporate name, in plain language ("This paycheck was processed by Automatic Data Processing, Inc., a private company"), on every payslip, tax document, and benefits notice it generates — permanently, in no smaller than 14-point type — and fund a nationwide public-education campaign explaining, in plain terms, exactly what it does and does not control about each worker's pay.
- **The Sacrifice:** The "quiet payroll giant" branding that has shielded Subject from scrutiny for fifty years. Once seen, Subject cannot be unseen.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts "Infrastructural Invisibility" (a suspected Class-IV Memetic Hazard) into ordinary, auditable corporate visibility — the bureaucratic equivalent of stepping into the light.

### **4. The Fragmentation Mandate (Structural Burn)**

A single private entity processing payroll for roughly one in six working Americans constitutes, per the Demi-God Act's "Substitution Doctrine," an unacceptable single point of failure for the national paycheck.

- **The Action:** Subject must spin off its Professional Employer Organization (PEO) division and at least one regional HCM business unit into independently governed worker-owned cooperatives within 18 months, reducing aggregate "pays-per-control" market share below the HPB's 10% Systemic Dependency ceiling.
- **The Sacrifice:** A material share of Subject's $5.9B-and-rising quarterly revenue base and its market-cap "Direct Assets" valuation.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Breaks the "no alternative exists" condition that the HPB considers the true engine of corporate Ascension.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's Chief Executive Officer and Board Chair must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Hourly Wage Workers Whose Pay Stubs Bear the Subject's Hidden Logo** and recite the following on the Subject's behalf:

> _"We are not the ledger. We are a clerk at the ledger's edge. We did not make your labor possible, and we will no longer pretend to know, better than you, when you are about to be replaced. Your paycheck is yours before it is ours to process — and from this day forward, you will know our name."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not executed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol's Corporate Annex**: Subject's charter will be suspended, its systems placed under emergency HPB receivership, and its executive leadership individually summonsed as "Proxy Demi-Gods," each personally liable for a pro-rata share of Subject's $102B valuation — to be processed, with grim irony, through a government-run payroll system built in 90 days from Subject's own seized source code.
