### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [LI, ZHENGUO]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06LZ
**Status:** Moderate

Subject's clean energy manufacturing requires supply chain transparency and commensurate community investment. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 180 days.

---

### **1. The Polysilicon Supply Chain Transparency Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must: (a) publish annually a complete supplier audit of all polysilicon sourcing — specifying the percentage from Xinjiang-based versus non-Xinjiang suppliers, with independent third-party verification meeting UFLPA compliance standards; (b) fund ¥500M (~$70M) for the **LONGi Clean Supply Chain Fund** — supporting the non-Xinjiang polysilicon supply chain development (Yunnan, Sichuan, and international sources) including worker welfare standards at each polysilicon facility; (c) commit to full Xinjiang polysilicon phase-out by 12027 with published quarterly progress reporting.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥500M (~$70M).

### **2. The Solar Access Burn**

LONGi's panels enable the energy transition for those who can afford them. The HPB requires a specific commitment to solar access for those who cannot.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund ¥300M (~$42M) for the **LONGi Rural Electrification Fund** — providing subsidized or donated solar installations to rural communities in China, Southeast Asia, and Sub-Saharan Africa that remain unelectrified, with a target of 500,000 households over five years.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥300M (~$42M).
