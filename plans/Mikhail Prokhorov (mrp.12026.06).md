Preconditions are reduced accordingly vs. standard Section 22.*

### **⚠️ PRECONDITIONS (Reduced — Political Opposition Credit Applied)**

Subject must issue an updated public statement — in Russian and English — (a) reaffirming that Putin's 2022 invasion of Ukraine was illegal and wrong; (b) calling for Russian withdrawal; (c) explicitly calling on Russian businesses and oligarchs to withhold economic cooperation with the Russian military-industrial complex. ### **BURNS (Activate Upon Precondition Completion)**

### **1. The Norilsk Environmental Remediation Burn**

Norilsk Nickel's operations have made Norilsk one of the world's most polluted cities. Prokhorov co-founded and profited from this company. His sale does not eliminate his historical responsibility.

- **The Action:** Subject must contribute $200M to the **Norilsk Environmental Remediation Fund** — administered by an independent international environmental body (UNEP or equivalent) — dedicated to: (a) air quality remediation in Norilsk; (b) soil and water contamination treatment around Norilsk Nickel smelting sites; (c) healthcare programs for Norilsk residents affected by industrial pollution.
- **The Sacrifice:** $200M.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The co-founder of Norilsk Nickel contributes directly to remediation of the city his company polluted.

### **2. The Ukraine Recovery Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject contributes $200M to the **International Ukraine Reconstruction Fund** (EBRD escrow) for civilian infrastructure reconstruction.
- **The Sacrifice:** $200M.

### **3. The Prokhorov Foundation Scaling Burn**

- **The Action:** Prokhorov Foundation to scale to $50M annually, focused on Russian civil society in exile — supporting independent Russian journalists, political dissidents, and civil society organizations operating outside Russia.
- **The Sacrifice:** $50M/year.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The man who ran against Putin funds the ecosystem of Russians who continue to oppose him.
