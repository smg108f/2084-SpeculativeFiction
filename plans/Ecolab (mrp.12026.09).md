### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ECOLAB INC.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09ECL
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification

Ecolab's classification reflects its emerging gatekeeping role over AI data-center water consumption. Recertification targets verification of the "water-neutral" claim.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Water-Neutral Independent Verification**

- **The Action:** Commission independent, third-party verification of "water-neutral" claims for every AI data-center client, published publicly with methodology.
- **The Sacrifice:** Increased scrutiny of whether marketed outcomes are actually achieved.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the "water-neutral" framing from a claim into a verified standard.

### **2. The Water-Stressed Region Priority Protocol**

- **The Action:** Publish a public protocol prioritizing water-conservation technology deployment to data centers in water-stressed regions ahead of less-constrained markets, regardless of client size or contract value.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced flexibility to prioritize the most lucrative contracts over the most water-stressed regions.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Ensures the genuine public-good potential of the technology reaches where it is most needed.
