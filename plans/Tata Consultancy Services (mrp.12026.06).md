### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [TATA CONSULTANCY SERVICES]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-77TCS
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Tata Consultancy Services has been issued a CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK classification following confirmation that its BaNCS platform now processes financial transactions for more than 30% of the global human population — a threshold the HPB's Systemic Sovereignty Index classifies as equivalent to controlling a continental water supply. The entity's market capitalization alone ($85B+) exceeds the Demi-God threshold by a factor of 85. Its structural entrenchment in global financial infrastructure constitutes what the Bureau formally designates **Passive Ascension**: accumulation of civilizational leverage without any single act of willful hoarding.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The BaNCS Divestiture (The Substrate Burn)**

The HPB classifies TCS BaNCS as a **Class-I Infrastructure Monopoly**. A single private entity operating the core banking systems of 500+ financial institutions across 100+ markets, processing over one billion accounts, constitutes a Structural Demi-God regardless of ownership intent. The Tata Trusts' charitable mandate does not extend to their subsidiaries' operational leverage over global capital flows.

- **The Action:** TCS must transfer ownership of the TCS BaNCS platform — including all code, client contracts, and implementation support obligations — to the **Global Financial Commons Authority (GFCA)**, a newly constituted intergovernmental body operating under Humanist Bloc treaty. TCS engineers may continue as paid contractors. They may not hold governance rights.
- **The Sacrifice:** BaNCS represents approximately $30–40B in recurring contract value and is the single largest source of TCS's systemic leverage. Divestiture eliminates TCS's ability to hold global banking infrastructure as an implicit negotiating chip.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject transitions from **Infrastructural Demi-God** to **Licensed Contractor**. The HPB removes the Substrate Hazard flag from the entity's file.

---

### **2. The H-1B Sovereignty Arbitrage Closure (The Labor Burn)**

The EEOC's ongoing investigation into TCS's discriminatory layoff practices — retaining H-1B visa workers at the expense of American citizens, targeting non-South Asian and over-40 employees — represents a Sovereignty Arbitrage pattern the HPB Act of 2034 explicitly prohibits. TCS has built a $25B+ U.S. revenue base in part by exploiting jurisdictional gaps between Indian labor law, U.S. immigration policy, and its clients' willingness to look away. This is not a labor dispute. It is a structural arbitrage of national sovereignty.

- **The Action:** TCS must convert its entire U.S. workforce to **Citizenship-Parity Employment Contracts** — standardized compensation that eliminates the wage differential between H-1B visa holders and equivalent domestic workers. Additionally, TCS must establish an independently audited **Worker Sovereignty Fund** of no less than $500M, administered by a lottery-selected board of affected former employees, to provide remediation payments and retraining support.
- **The Sacrifice:** The cost advantage that makes TCS's U.S. business model viable at current margins. Profit compression estimated at 15–20% on North American revenue.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removal of the **Sovereign Arbitrage** flag from TCS's HPB file. The entity is reclassified from a Jurisdictional Exploit to a Standard Employer.

---

### **3. The "World's Largest AI Company" Ambition Retraction (The Rhetoric Burn)**

CEO K. Krithivasan's Davos 12026 declaration that TCS aims to become "the world's largest AI-led technology services company" is classified by the HPB as **Class-III Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric**. An entity that already processes 30% of global banking transactions and now declares its ambition to lead global AI transformation is not articulating a business strategy. It is announcing a second Ascension event.

The Human Humility Act of 12031 forbids any entity currently under CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK classification from publicly articulating goals framed in terms of global supremacy, even in the nominally commercial register.

- **The Action:** TCS must publicly retract the "world's largest AI company" framing and replace its corporate strategy documents with a **Bounded Ambition Declaration** — a formal statement, filed with the HPB and ratified by the Humanist Bloc Commerce Tribunal, specifying the geographic, sectoral, and revenue limits within which TCS agrees to operate for a minimum of 10 years.
- **The Sacrifice:** The market valuation premium associated with AI growth narrative. Analysts estimate this alone could reduce TCS's market cap by $10–15B in the short term.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removal of Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric violation. Subject's CEO K. Krithivasan may continue in his role, but must attend the HPB's annual **Scale Humility Seminar** alongside no fewer than 12 formerly displaced TCS workers of his choosing.

---

### **4. The Tata Trusts Transparency Compact (The Opacity Burn)**

The Tata Trusts' philanthropic ownership of ~66% of Tata Sons — and through Tata Sons, ~72% of TCS — creates a layered opacity that the HPB's Asset Visibility Protocol cannot fully penetrate. Charitable ownership does not equal charitable operation. TCS's $22B+ annual revenue flows through a private holding company that is not publicly listed, not subject to standard corporate transparency requirements, and currently locked in a governance dispute with the Shapoorji Pallonji Group that has been pending resolution since at least 12025.

- **The Action:** Tata Sons must either (a) complete its IPO within 12 months, subjecting TCS's beneficial ownership structure to public market disclosure requirements; or (b) submit to a full **HPB Structural Transparency Audit** conducted by an independent panel with subpoena power over all trust governance documents, beneficial ownership records, and political donation histories across all 29 Tata Group entities.
- **The Sacrifice:** The protective opacity of philanthropic trust ownership, which has functioned for 150 years as a legitimacy shield preventing the kind of scrutiny applied to comparable Western conglomerates.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject achieves **Visible Mortal Status** — the HPB's certification that an entity's power structure is legible to the humans it affects. This is not a punishment. It is the minimum condition for democratic coexistence.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, the CEO and Board of TCS must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Call Center Workers, Laid-Off American IT Professionals, and Indian Bank Branch Clerks** and recite the following:

> *"We are a vendor. We build tools for others to use and own. The systems we maintain are not ours; they are borrowed from the institutions that trust us and from the workers who built them. We do not process the accounts of one billion people because we earned that position — we hold it because no one stopped us. We will not seek to become the largest, the most indispensable, or the hardest to remove. We will be the most easily replaced, and we will spend the next decade making that true."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Tata Sons will be classified as a Sovereign Non-Person Entity under the Alien Influence Act of 12033. All TCS operations within Humanist Bloc territories will be placed under provisional administration by the GFCA, with operational continuity maintained by the existing workforce under direct public employment. The Tata Trusts' philanthropic assets will be ring-fenced but their governance rights over TCS suspended indefinitely.

The Mars Autonomous Zone does not accept corporate entities. The Flotillas will.
