### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [VAJIRALONGKORN (RAMA X)]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06VRX
**Status:** International Registry Filing (Enforcement Suspended Pending Thai Jurisdictional Resolution)

Subject is a reigning monarch whose wealth derives from the privatization of crown patrimony and whose critics face criminal prosecution. Standard HPB enforcement mechanisms are unavailable within Thai jurisdiction. This plan is filed for the International Registry and will be activated should Thai law change, should subject voluntarily seek mortal recertification, or should he attempt to claim asylum in an HPB-member jurisdiction. The following Asset Burn Schedule is presented as the required pathway to recertification if subject chooses it.

---

### **1. The Crown Property Reversal Burn**

The 2018 amendment that transferred Crown Property Bureau assets from quasi-public trust to personal discretionary control was the defining Ascension event for this subject. It was not accumulation through enterprise; it was conversion of public patrimony into private wealth through executive decree. The HPB classifies this as a **Retroactive Privatization of the Sacred Commons**.

- **The Action:** Reverse the 2018 Crown Property Bureau amendment. Return all CPB assets — real estate, land leases, SCB and Siam Cement stakes — to public trust administration, governed by an independent Thai Public Wealth Commission with no royal family members in governance roles.
- **The Sacrifice:** ~$30–35B in assets that the subject converted from public to private. He retains his personal pre-2018 holdings, which the HPB estimates at ~$8–10B.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The most significant public wealth restoration event in Thai history. Substantial Whuffie gain; HPB reclassifies from CRITICAL to MARGINAL ASCENSION RISK.

### **2. The Bavaria Residency Transparency Burn**

Subject resides primarily in Germany while ruling Thailand, maintaining a lifestyle of extraordinary opulence abroad while his country's pro-democracy protesters face imprisonment for questioning his wealth. The HPB classifies this as a **Sovereign Absenteeism with Asset Retention** — the combination of full entitlement with minimal civic presence.

- **The Action:** Subject must either: (a) formally abdicate and retire as a private citizen in Bavaria, surrendering royal privileges and accepting HPB jurisdiction as a private individual; or (b) return to Thailand as a constitutional monarch with a formally chartered, independent Privy Council that oversees royal expenditure with published accounts.
- **The Sacrifice:** Either the throne or the luxury of ruling from abroad without accountability.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Whichever path is taken eliminates the **Sovereign Absenteeism** classification. The HPB considers abdication the higher-Whuffie option.

### **3. The Section 112 Renunciation Burn**

The lèse-majesté law (Section 112) makes criticism of the monarchy a criminal offense punishable by up to 15 years imprisonment. The HPB classifies laws that criminalize criticism of a wealth-holder as a **Legal Ascension Infrastructure** — a statutory mechanism for preventing the accountability that the Demi-God Act requires.

- **The Action:** Formally petition the Thai National Assembly for the repeal or substantial amendment of Section 112, replacing criminal penalties with civil defamation protections available equally to all Thai citizens.
- **The Sacrifice:** Legal immunity from public scrutiny. The HPB notes this is not a sacrifice — it is a requirement of mortal existence.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts legal divinity into legal personhood. The Bureau cannot process a Mortal Recertification for a subject who is constitutionally insulated from criticism.

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Thai Democracy Activists, Former Section 112 Defendants, and Crown Land Tenants** (if any can be safely convened) and recite the following:

> *"I was born the son of a king. I made the king's assets my own. I put the critics in prison. I lived in Bavaria. I called this a reign. The crown is not the same as the country.
