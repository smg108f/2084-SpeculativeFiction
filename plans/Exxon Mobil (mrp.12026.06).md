### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [EXXON MOBIL CORPORATION]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-17EM
**Status:** Critical — Historical Epistemic Obstruction / Counter-Transition Capital Allocation

Exxon Mobil's RTWI of ~$640B places it at **640 times the Demi-God threshold**. Unlike Saudi Aramco (HPB-12026-04SA), Exxon is a nominally private entity accountable to shareholders and the U.S. legal system — which provides the Bureau's Burns with enforcement mechanisms that the sovereign entity case does not have.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Historical Reckoning Fund**

Exxon's documented internal climate research — confirming the causal relationship between fossil fuel combustion and global warming, conducted in the 1970s–80s — represents knowledge that, if publicly shared and acted upon, might have altered the trajectory of climate policy by a decade or more. The subsequent organized denial campaign represents the deliberate suppression of that knowledge. The Bureau's **Historical Epistemic Obstruction** classification is unique: it captures harm that cannot be fully priced in current dollars but must be structurally addressed.

- **The Action:** Exxon must establish a **Historical Climate Reckoning Fund** — a $50B commitment over 20 years, funded by a 5% levy on annual operating cash flow, directed to: (a) climate adaptation infrastructure in communities most vulnerable to the warming effects that Exxon's denial campaign helped delay addressing; (b) historical archive preservation of Exxon's internal climate research documents, made permanently and freely available to researchers; and (c) independent scientific institutions studying the attribution of specific extreme weather events to historical emissions for purposes of accountability and adaptation planning. The Fund is governed by an independent board with no Exxon representation.
- **The Sacrifice:** $50B over 20 years represents a significant ongoing obligation. For an entity with Exxon's cash flow, it is not existential. It is proportionate.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Formally addresses the **Historical Epistemic Obstruction** designation. Exxon acknowledges the gap between what it knew and what it said, and invests proportionately in mitigating the consequences.

---

### **2. The Production Decline Schedule**

The Pioneer acquisition represents a commitment to increasing Permian Basin production at a moment when global climate agreements call for declining oil and gas investment. The Bureau requires Exxon to align its capital allocation with the energy transition trajectory that climate science prescribes — not immediately, but in a structured and accountable way.

- **The Action:** Exxon must publish and commit to a **Managed Production Decline Schedule** — a publicly binding capital allocation plan in which new oil and gas exploration investment declines by 10% annually beginning 12028, with the freed capital redirected to CCS at commercial scale, industrial decarbonization (green hydrogen, low-carbon chemicals), and direct investments in energy efficiency. Pioneer's Permian operations are retained for production through the natural decline of existing wells; no new exploratory drilling in the Permian after 12030. The schedule is reviewed annually by the Bureau and may be adjusted for energy supply security considerations with HPB approval.
- **The Sacrifice:** Exxon's production growth trajectory ends. The entity transitions from a growth-phase oil company to a managed-decline energy company — a different business identity, but a survivable one.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Resolves the **Counter-Transition Capital Allocation** classification. Exxon's investment decisions align with, rather than contradict, the energy transition.

---

### **3. The Political Apparatus Shutdown**

Exxon's political operation — the funding of think tanks, trade associations, and advocacy groups that oppose climate regulation — continues in updated forms. The Bureau requires the entity to formally cease the use of corporate resources for political activities that conflict with the stated scientific consensus on climate change.

- **The Action:** Exxon must publish a complete **Political Expenditure Disclosure** covering all funding — direct and indirect — to political campaigns, PACs, trade associations, and advocacy organizations for the prior 10 years, and must certify that no Exxon resources, direct or indirect, will be used to fund organizations that dispute the scientific consensus on anthropogenic climate change or oppose carbon pricing mechanisms. Compliance is audited annually by an independent body selected by the HPB.
- **The Sacrifice:** Exxon loses the ability to use its financial resources to fund organized opposition to the policies that would reduce fossil fuel demand. The advocacy apparatus that replaced the denial apparatus is shut down.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Resolves the **Anti-Regulatory Political Apparatus** classification. Exxon may advocate for specific implementation mechanisms; it may not fund organized opposition to climate action itself.
