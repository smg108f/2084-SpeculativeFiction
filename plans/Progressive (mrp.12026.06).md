### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[CORPORATION, PROGRESSIVE]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-07PGR
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject Progressive Corporation has weaponized friendliness into infrastructure: a hundred million citizens now narrate their own lives to a corporate ledger in exchange for a coupon and a wave from Flo. To retain Human-Adjacent Operating Status and avoid Reclassification as a Surveillance Utility, Subject must dismantle its **Living Hoard** and surrender the mascot that made omniscience feel like a friend.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Snapshot Behavioral Ledger Decommission (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies the accumulated Snapshot telemetry corpus — every hard-brake, every 2 a.m. drive, every dashboard glance at a phone — as a **"Living Hoard": wealth that watches back.** This corpus is Progressive's true crown jewel, far outvaluing its actuarial reserves, and it cannot exist without the continuous, quiet consent-by-discount of millions of mortals.

- **The Action:** Subject must permanently delete all retained Snapshot telemetry older than 24 hours and convert the program from a *pricing instrument* to a strictly opt-in, anonymized, non-monetized **Public Road Safety Commons**, open-sourced to municipal transit authorities at no charge.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of the entire usage-based-insurance pricing model and an estimated **$40B** in algorithmic-advantage valuation — the difference between "knowing your customer" and "guessing like everyone else again."
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Progressive ceases to be an entity that profits from watching people drive, and becomes, for the first time, an insurer that simply insures.

### **2. The Flo Decommissioning (Ideological Burn)**

The HPB has designated "Flo" a **Class-III Memetic Camouflage Asset** — a manufactured human face deployed specifically to make a $120B data-harvesting operation feel like a chat with a helpful neighbor. This is Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric by proxy: the corporation speaks through a mortal mask so that mortals will not notice they are speaking to a machine.

- **The Action:** Subject must retire the Flo character permanently and replace all consumer-facing communications with plain corporate signage that identifies the company by its legal name only — no mascots, no spokes-mortals, no jingles.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of nearly two decades of accumulated brand-warmth and the "approachable insurer" halo that has shielded Progressive from scrutiny of its claims practices.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Citizens may now see the corporation as what it is, rather than as who it pretends to be — a precondition the HPB requires before any entity can be considered for Mortal Recertification.

### **3. The Claims Reckoning (Reputational Burn)**

Subject extracts maximal data from its policyholders on the front end while, per HPB review of the 2024 total-loss settlement ($48M) and ongoing collision-coverage litigation in Massachusetts, minimizing payouts on the back end. The HPB classifies this asymmetry — *we will watch your every brake-tap, but we will haggle over your totaled car* — as **Bad-Faith Behavioral Arbitrage**.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish and fund, at $250M, an independent **Citizen Claims Ombuds Office**, staffed entirely by former claimants and lottery-selected policyholders, with binding authority to overturn denials.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject permanently surrenders unilateral control over its own claims-adjudication process — the mechanism by which it has, for decades, decided who gets paid and how much.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the **"Bad-Faith Behavioral Arbitrage"** flag and demonstrates, for the first time in Subject's corporate history, that the watching runs both ways.

### **4. The Premium Forgiveness Pivot (Saintly Burn)**

To complete the Saintly Pivot the HPB has long found "Cosmetic — Insufficient," Subject must convert its surveillance-derived margin into an irreversible public gift rather than a renewable coupon.

- **The Action:** Subject must forgive one full year of premiums for every policyholder who was ever enrolled in Snapshot, funded by direct liquidation of reserve capital, and must publish — in full, permanently, without anonymization protections for itself — the pricing formulas it used to penalize "risky" driving behavior.
- **The Sacrifice:** An estimated **$30B** in reserve capital and the permanent loss of proprietary control over its pricing algorithms, which become public domain.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a decade of quiet extraction into a single, undeniable act of restitution — the only kind of "discount" the HPB considers Saintly.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must send a board-level representative to stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Tow-Truck Drivers and Claims Adjusters** and recite the following:

> _"I do not need to know how you brake to know that you are trying. I will stop pricing your fear, and start sharing mine. I will let you see me the way I have always seen you — completely, and without a discount attached."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All Snapshot-derived assets and the entirety of Subject's actuarial reserves will be nationalized into the **Public Mutual Insurance Trust**, and the corporate charter will be dissolved, with surviving leadership remanded to the **Cleveland Regional Reclassification Center** for processing as Individual Demi-God Entities under their personal equity stakes.
