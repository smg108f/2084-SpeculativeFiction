### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[LAM RESEARCH CORPORATION]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06LR
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject is classified as a **"Latent Apotheosis"** entity — a demi-god so structurally embedded in the global compute substrate that its own shareholders, customers, and regulators have failed to notice its divinity. Because Lam's leverage is chokepoint-based rather than charisma-based, conventional "Saintly Pivot" gestures (museum-gifting, manifesto-writing) are insufficient. The Bureau requires **structural decoupling**, not theater.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The "Open Etch" Mandate (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies Lam's proprietary plasma-etch and atomic-layer-deposition process IP as a **"Crystallized Monopoly"** — wealth that exists only because the knowledge is locked away. As long as fewer than three entities on Earth can fabricate an advanced chip, those entities are not companies; they are toll-gates on civilization itself.

- **The Action:** Subject must place its core etch and deposition process patents into a **Global Fabrication Commons**, licensed royalty-free to any qualified manufacturer — ally or rival alike.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of an estimated **$240B** in monopoly-rent valuation; loss of "irreplaceable supplier" status with TSMC, Samsung, Micron, and Intel.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Lam ceases to be a chokepoint and becomes a **Founding Guild Member** of the Commons — a contributor among equals rather than a gatekeeper above them.

### **2. Severance of the Export-Control Leverage Loop**

Subject currently functions as an unelected instrument of U.S.-China strategic policy, deriving immense structural power from its position as both export-control enforcer-by-compliance and revenue-dependent on the very nation it is restricted from fully serving. The HPB considers this **"Shadow Statecraft"** — corporate entities should not hold geopolitical leverage no government conferred upon them by vote.

- **The Action:** Subject must spin off its China-facing operations into an **independently governed, non-equity Fabrication Trust**, supervised by a binational citizen oversight board, with Lam retaining zero ownership stake or board influence.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of an estimated **$160B** in China-linked revenue valuation and all associated geopolitical leverage.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the **"Shadow Cabinet" Violation.** Lam is no longer a de facto arm of foreign policy — merely a vendor, like any other mortal business.

### **3. The "Visible Substrate" Penance (Memetic Correction)**

Because Subject's primary hazard is *invisibility* — the public has no idea that its phone, its hospital scanner, and its government's surveillance grid all trace back to a single obscure tool-maker in Fremont — the HPB requires a corrective act of **radical self-disclosure**.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund and broadcast a public "Substrate Transparency Campaign," a mandatory multi-year media initiative that explains, in plain language, exactly how much of modern civilization halts the moment Lam's machines stop running — and must publish, line by line, its actual chokepoint share of the global advanced-node supply chain.
- **The Requirement:** No marketing spin permitted. The campaign must be narrated in the dry, plain register the HPB itself uses — the goal is comprehension, not awe.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the **"Latent Apotheosis"** classification. A god the public can see and name is, by definition, no longer a hidden one — and hidden gods are the only kind the HPB cannot regulate.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's executive leadership must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Fab-Floor Technicians, Machinists, and Community-College Engineering Instructors** and recite the following:

> _"We built the tools that built the minds, and we let the minds forget we existed. We mistook our silence for humility, when it was only leverage in a quieter coat. From this day, what we know, all may use; what we hold, all may see. We are not the foundation the world stands on without consent — we are a workshop, and workshops have doors."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the "Burn" is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All process IP will be seized and nationalized into the Commons by emergency order, executive leadership will be permanently barred from the global semiconductor industry, and the company's headquarters will be converted into the **Fremont Substrate Museum** — a public exhibit on the machines that quietly ran the world, and the god that never introduced itself.
