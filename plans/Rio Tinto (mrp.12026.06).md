### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[RIO TINTO PLC]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06RT
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject stands accused of "Provenance Erasure" at scale: converting 46,000 years of continuous human memory into iron ore tonnage, then offering a gift-shop replica as restitution. To retain its operating charter and avoid reclassification as a Geological Sovereign Entity, Subject must complete the following before its lithium expansion compounds the offense.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Pilbara Tenement Surrender (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies active blasting tenements adjacent to documented heritage sites as **"Active Erasure Zones."** Subject currently holds expansion rights across multiple Pilbara replacement mines (including Western Range and Hope Downs 2) sitting within proximity of uncatalogued PKKP cultural ground.

- **The Action:** Subject must permanently surrender all tenement rights within a 40-kilometre radius of any site flagged by the new HPB **Provenance Inventory**, regardless of ore grade or sunk cost.
- **The Sacrifice:** An estimated $31B in projected future iron ore output, written off in full — no impairment smoothing permitted.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject converts from "Owner of the Ground" to "Tenant of the Record," the first geological entity in HPB history to voluntarily cap its own footprint below proven reserves.

### **2. Dissolution of the "Replica" Program — Funding the Real Thing Instead**

Subject's plan to construct a full-scale architectural replica of the destroyed Juukan Gorge shelters is classified as a **Grade-3 Simulacrum Offering** — a forgery presented as atonement.

- **The Action:** Cancel the replica project. Redirect its full budget, plus the $0 difference between forgery-cost and true-restitution-cost (there is none — what was destroyed cannot be rebuilt), into a **PKKP-governed Cultural Sovereignty Trust** with no Rio Tinto board representation, no naming rights, and no media approval clause.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses the right to narrate its own apology. The story of Juukan Gorge will now be told exclusively by the people who held it for 40,000 years before Subject arrived for forty.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes the **Simulacrum Offering** flag and halts the "optics laundering" the HPB has observed in Subject's post-2020 communications strategy.

### **3. The Lithium Moratorium (Pre-Emptive Penance)**

Subject's 2025 absorption of Arcadium Lithium is read by the HPB as a confession in advance: having proven it can erase the deep past for short-term gain, Subject is now positioning to own the deep future — every battery, every grid, every Flotilla powerplant.

- **The Action:** Subject must submit all seven newly acquired lithium deposits (Argentina, Quebec, Western Australia) to an independent **Pre-Extraction Provenance Survey** conducted by Indigenous-led heritage authorities, with full veto power over any blast plan — binding, not advisory.
- **The Sacrifice:** Projected delays of 3–5 years on the "200,000 tonnes LCE by 2028" target. Subject's lithium ambitions move at the speed of memory, not machinery.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The first instance of a critical-minerals operator subordinating extraction speed to historical accountability — neutralizing the **Substrate Monopoly** designation before it metastasizes into the next century's Juukan Gorge.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's board must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Stonemasons, Archivists, and Pilbara Truck-Route Drivers** and recite the following:

> _"We dug for what was buried and found, instead, the proof that we were not the first to stand here. We chose the ore over the memory, and called the silence progress. We will not mistake the absence of a record for the absence of a people again."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Subject's corporate charter will be dissolved, its tenements nationalized into the Provenance Inventory, and its board will be relocated — under permanent supervised residency — to a reconstructed habitat inside the very excavation pits it created, where they will live, in perpetuity, surrounded by the silence they manufactured.
