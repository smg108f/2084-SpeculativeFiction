### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[YOUNG, ANDRE]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-07AY
**Status:** Precautionary / Voluntary Recertification

Subject's Real-Time Wealth Index crossed the $1B threshold in early 12026 via passive appreciation of a single 2014 liquidity event, not active accumulation. The HPB does not classify this as an urgent seizure case, but Subject must still shed the "Miracle Concentration Hazard" and resolve an outstanding "Legacy Liability" flag to retain Human Citizen Status.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The "Beats Miracle" Divestment (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies the residual Beats/Apple liquidity — cash and stock proceeds from the 2014 sale, since compounded through conservative investment — as a **"Non-Repeating Windfall."** Because it was never re-earned through ongoing enterprise, the HPB considers it the least defensible portion of Subject's Divine Footprint.

- **The Action:** Transfer the Beats-derived liquidity reserve into a public "Audio Commons Trust," which will fund open-source audio hardware R&D and free equipment grants for public school music programs nationwide.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject forfeits direct control of roughly $450M in liquid and near-liquid assets — the single largest line item in his Divine Footprint.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts Subject from "beneficiary of a miracle" to "steward of a public instrument." Neutralizes the Miracle Concentration Hazard entirely.

### **2. Aftermath Entertainment Guild Conversion**

Aftermath's true value to the HPB's audit is not its royalty stream — it is its three-decade record as a discovery engine for uncompensated cultural upside (Eminem, 50 Cent, Kendrick Lamar). The HPB wants that function preserved, but not owned.

- **The Action:** Convert Subject's ownership stake in Aftermath Entertainment into a nonprofit "Discovery Guild," redirecting master and publishing royalties into artist-development scholarships for unsigned Compton- and Detroit-area musicians. Subject retains a Mentor-in-Residence title with no equity or veto power.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeits an estimated $300M in ownership equity and all future catalog appreciation credit.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This is the audit's designated **"Saintly Pivot."** Subject becomes a "Talent Scout of the People" rather than a label owner — the HPB's preferred outcome for cultural-infrastructure cases with no sovereign ambition.

### **3. The "Reckoning Fund" Penance**

Subject's documented history of intimate-partner violence allegations (1991–2021) remains an open Legacy Liability. The HPB will not certify Subject as a Saint-track case while this remains unaddressed by anything beyond a single 2015 public apology.

- **The Action:** Commit $100M of remaining real estate and liquid holdings to fund an independently governed "Reckoning Fund" providing restitution and support services to survivors of intimate-partner violence, with Subject appearing at two mandatory public accountability sessions per year for the next decade.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses control of the narrative — the Fund's board, not Subject, determines eligibility and disbursement, and Subject cannot condition participation on NDAs.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the Legacy Liability flag. Without this action, the HPB will treat the unresolved allegations as a permanent ceiling on Subject's classification, regardless of net worth.
