### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [LIU, YONGXING]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06LYX
**Status:** Urgent

Subject controls East Hope Group — China's largest private aluminium producer, powered by coal. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Aluminium Green Power Transition Burn**

East Hope's aluminium smelting relies on coal-fired electricity in Xinjiang and Inner Mongolia. Aluminium smelting is one of the most electricity-intensive industrial processes; the carbon footprint is primarily determined by the power source.

- **The Action:** Subject must commit $300M to transitioning East Hope's aluminium operations to renewable electricity by 12032: (a) direct investment in wind and solar capacity in the Xinjiang and Inner Mongolia regions sufficient to power East Hope's smelters (subject may co-develop with grid operators but must be the primary capital contributor for the renewable capacity serving East Hope); (b) independent annual verification of coal-electricity-to-renewable-electricity substitution rates published publicly; (c) fund a $50M **Chinese Aluminium Decarbonization Research Fund** — open-access research on aluminium production decarbonization, shared with the broader Chinese aluminium industry.
- **The Sacrifice:** $300M.

### **2. The Agricultural Pollution Remediation Burn**

East Hope's pig farming operations generate significant water and soil pollution. The HPB requires proportionate remediation.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund $100M for: (a) wastewater treatment upgrades across all East Hope pig farming facilities, meeting or exceeding EU environmental standards (currently significantly above Chinese domestic minimums); (b) soil remediation for farmland adjacent to East Hope's pig farming operations; (c) a **Rural Water Quality Monitoring Network** in East Hope's operating regions, independently administered.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 East Hope Aluminium Smelter Workers, Inner Mongolia Rural Water Scientists, and Chinese Private Sector Entrepreneurs from the Reform Era**:

> *"My brothers and I sold quail eggs. That is where we started. Forty years later East Hope produces more aluminium than most countries. The coal that powers the smelters is what the HPB is asking me to address. The transition fund makes East Hope's aluminium green. The agricultural fund addresses the rivers near our pig farms. We built this company in a China that did not ask these questions. This China does. These are my answers."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** Subject will be assigned to **FSS EAST HOPE** — an aluminium-hulled vessel powered entirely by onboard solar panels and wind turbines, demonstrating that aluminium and renewable energy are compatible, sailing between Chinese coastal industrial cities carrying the published findings of the Aluminium Decarbonization Research Fund and docking where East Hope's aluminium ultimately goes — to the factories, the construction sites, the vehicle plants — so the full supply chain can observe a clean-powered aluminium vessel and consider what that requires.
