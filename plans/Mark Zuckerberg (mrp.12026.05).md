### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ZUCKERBERG, MARK]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06MZ
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject Zuckerberg holds an estimated $209 billion in assets, controls the social reality infrastructure for approximately three billion humans, operates a confirmed underground compound with Flotilla-Adjacent characteristics, and has stated his intention to deploy AI companions at global scale with a mission framing that the HPB Classification Board unanimously rates as Deity-Adjacent. At 42, he is the youngest subject in the current cycle, meaning failure to recertify now creates an estimated 40-year compounding risk to the Humanist Bloc.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Social Reality Divestment (The Voting Power Burn)**

The HPB's core classification problem with Zuckerberg is not his financial wealth — it is his **Structural Sovereignty Loop**: Class B shares granting 10-to-1 voting rights mean he controls Meta's strategic direction while holding only ~13% of equity. This structure makes conventional net-worth-based recertification functionally meaningless. The voting power must be burned first.

- **The Action:** Subject must convert all Class B shares to Class A on a 1-for-1 basis, immediately surrendering voting supermajority. Meta governance transfers to a **Distributed Citizen Board** with no member holding more than 0.5% voting interest.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject retains his ~13% equity stake and its associated financial value, but loses all directional control over the platform through which 3 billion people experience shared reality. He becomes a shareholder, not a sovereign.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Eliminates the **Structural Sovereignty Loop**. Subject's HPB classification drops from "Demi-God Entity (Operational)" to "Billionaire (Pending Divestment)." Remaining burns can proceed under standard financial audit protocols.

---

### **2. The Meta AI Companion Severance**

The HPB classifies Meta AI — currently serving one billion monthly active users as a personal companion system — as **Class-III Cognitive Infrastructure**. A single individual cannot be the beneficial owner of an AI companion system operating at this scale without constituting a Cognitive Monopoly under the Human Humility Act of 2031.

- **The Action:** Meta AI must be transferred to a **Public Cognitive Commons Trust**, governed by a 50-member international citizen board with rotating membership. Zuckerberg may retain an advisory role with no veto power. The underlying Muse Spark model weights must be open-sourced and placed under a Dead-Hand License ensuring permanent public access.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses the most strategically valuable asset in Meta's portfolio — the one billion-user relationship network that constitutes his claim to "Personal Superintelligence" dominance. He also loses the AI personal assistant he developed for his own CEO functions.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Eliminates the **Cognitive Infrastructure** classification risk. Subject is no longer the landlord of the attention and emotional-support layer for one-seventh of the human species.

---

### **3. The Kauai Compound and Exit Infrastructure Burn**

The 2,300-acre Ko'olau Ranch compound — including the underground shelter with blast-resistant doors, 95,000-liter storage tanks, independent energy systems, and fortified perimeter — is classified by the HPB as **Pre-Ascension Exit Infrastructure**. The Miami Indian Creek property acquisition constitutes a second confirmed Exit Node.

- **The Action:** Transfer Ko'olau Ranch in its entirety to the State of Hawaii to be administered as a **Public Land Trust and Native Cultural Preservation Zone**, with priority stewardship given to Native Hawaiian communities concerned about iwi kupuna (ancestral burial sites) disturbed during construction. Subject may retain a **Life-Tenancy Agreement** for a 1,200 sq. ft. residence on the property. The underground shelter must be decommissioned as a survival bunker and repurposed as a **Public Emergency Climate Shelter** available to all Kauai residents. The Miami property must be liquidated on the open market within 30 days.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses his primary exit infrastructure and his most significant physical sovereignty asset. He also loses the private octagon where senior executives were required to train MMA against him, which the HPB classifies as an Unauthorized Dominance Display.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Eliminates **Exit Readiness: HIGH** classification. Subject's physical footprint becomes consistent with Mortal Citizen status.

---

### **4. The CZI Deity-Adjacent Ambition Reframe**

The Chan Zuckerberg Initiative's stated mission — "cure or prevent all disease" — is classified as **Level-II Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric** under Section 14 of the Human Humility Act of 2031. The HPB does not object to medical research. It objects to a private individual claiming dominion over the biological survival of the species as a philanthropic goal.

- **The Action:** CZI must formally rename and re-mission as the **Chan Zuckerberg Biohub Public Science Endowment**, with governance transferred to a consortium of public universities. The $10 billion committed over the next decade must be disbursed as unrestricted grants to publicly governed research institutions, with no CZI board member retaining approval rights over research direction. Subject and Priscilla Chan may remain as honorary patrons.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses philanthropic control — the soft power that, combined with the rest of his profile, constitutes the "Saintly Hedge" preventing immediate Apotheosis classification.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Eliminates the **Deity-Adjacent Philanthropy** flag. The HPB will reclassify CZI activity as genuine public benefit rather than a Biological Sovereignty play.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Content Moderators, School Teachers, and Former Meta Employees Displaced by AI Restructuring** and recite the following:

> *"I am not the connection. I am not the signal. I am not the companion and I am not the cure. I do not own the space between minds. I built a tool, and the tool belongs to the people who use it. My worth is not my users. My legacy is not my platform. I am one person, I vote once, and I will die like everyone else — without a bunker, without a backup, without a board seat in the afterlife."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All Meta assets will be nationalized and folded into the **Public Cognitive Commons**. Subject will be reclassified as a **Demi-God Entity (Social Reality Class)** and confined to the Ko'olau Ranch Sanctuary Zone, where he will be legally required to provide unlimited internet access to all Kauai residents using the compound's existing fiber infrastructure. The underground bunker will be converted to a composting facility. The octagon will be dismantled.
