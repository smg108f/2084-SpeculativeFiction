### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [PAGE, LARRY]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-02LP
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject Page's Real-Time Wealth Index has exceeded the $1B threshold by a factor of approximately 314x, his jurisdictional restructuring has accelerated beyond voluntary pace, and his island-based Exit Architecture places him within 72 hours of physical non-compliance range. The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days before Subject's Florida relocation renders standard HPB enforcement jurisdiction ambiguous.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Alphabet Structural Decoupling (The Nuclear Burn)**

The HPB classifies Subject's Class B Alphabet shares as a **Cognitive Infrastructure Monopoly**: a stake that grants effective veto power over the world's dominant information layer and generates compounding "Potentiality Value" beyond any reasonable mortal share. Subject's ~6% ownership of Alphabet is not a passive investment; it is a lever on what the global population can find, see, and know.

- **The Action:** Subject must convert all Class B shares (10-vote) to Class A shares (1-vote) and transfer no less than 95% of total Alphabet holdings — approximately $275B at audit value — into a **Public Cognitive Commons Trust**, administered by a 501(c)(3) board with no Page family representation. The Trust must fund open-access digital infrastructure, public-domain AI tooling, and universal information literacy programs.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses effective control over the world's search index and sheds the primary engine of his wealth accumulation. He may retain Alphabet Class C shares representing no more than $900M in value.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject's RTWI drops below the $1B threshold. His Social Influence score is reclassified from "Cognitive Infrastructure Monopoly" to "Legacy Advisor." He is no longer the invisible architect of what the world can find.

### **2. The Island Portfolio Surrender (The Archipelago Burn)**

Subject's five-island portfolio — spanning the Caribbean, U.S. Virgin Islands, and Fiji — constitutes what the HPB's Geographic Sovereignty Unit classifies as a **Pre-Flotilla Network**: a series of non-contiguous sovereign-adjacent territories capable of sustaining a small population indefinitely outside HPB jurisdiction, connected by private aviation assets and serviced by superyacht.

- **The Action:** Subject must deed all private islands to their respective host governments or to recognized public conservation trusts, retaining no ownership, no life-tenancy agreements, and no landing rights beyond standard commercial visitor status. One Aero LLC and all associated aircraft must be donated to regional inter-island public transit authorities.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses his physical escape infrastructure. He may no longer land a private jet on his own runway in Fiji during a pandemic — or any other emergency the HPB may or may not choose to characterize as convenient.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject's Exit Readiness score drops from CRITICAL to LOW. He is geographically mortal again.

### **3. The Donor-Advised Fund Unwinding (The Transparency Burn)**

The HPB's Philanthropy Verification Unit has flagged Subject's $1.2B+ in grants to donor-advised funds as **Synthetic Sainthood**: charitable giving structured to appear as divestment while remaining functionally unverifiable. Since the money cannot be traced to working charities, the HPB cannot credit it toward Mortal Recertification.

- **The Action:** Subject must instruct the Carl Victor Page Memorial Foundation to immediately disclose all DAF recipient institutions and confirm active charitable deployment within 90 days. Any DAF funds not confirmed as deployed must be re-granted directly to named public benefit organizations within the 30-day burn window. Subject must also sign the **Giving Pledge** on the public record.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses the opacity that has allowed $1.2B+ to exist in a philanthropic limbo of his own design. His giving becomes as searchable as everyone else's.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Synthetic Sainthood flag is cleared. Subject's Philanthropy Score upgrades from "Opaque" to "Verified Mortal Generosity."

### **4. The New Zealand Residency Revocation (The Escape Hatch Burn)**

Subject's New Zealand residency — obtained under the Investor Category in 2021, publicly revealed only after a medical emergency flight from Fiji — represents a **Jurisdictional Escape Valve** that the HPB classifies as incompatible with Mortal Citizen status in the Humanist Bloc.

- **The Action:** Subject must formally surrender his New Zealand resident visa and publicly acknowledge that his residency was obtained as wealth-contingent refuge preparation rather than genuine civic integration.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses his southernmost escape route. He must select one jurisdiction and live in it like a person, not a portfolio.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject's jurisdictional threat profile drops from "Multi-Haven Network" to "Single-Domicile Citizen." He is now deportable to exactly one place, which the HPB considers adequate.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Reference Librarians, Public School Teachers, and Municipal Broadband Technicians** and recite the following:

> _"I built a machine to find everything. I used it to hide myself. I owned the index and removed my own entry. I will not do this again. Information that belongs to the public belongs to the public — including information about me. I am not a search result to be suppressed. I am a citizen, and I am findable."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Subject's remaining assets will be nationalized and transferred to the Public Cognitive Commons Trust without Subject's input or credit. Subject will be issued a one-way transit permit to the **South Pacific Sovereign Flotilla Collective** — the nearest Ascension Haven to his Fiji island, which he will no longer own — where he may live out his days as a Demi-God Entity among others who also thought the plan was airtight.
