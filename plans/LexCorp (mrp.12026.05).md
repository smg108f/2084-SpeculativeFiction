### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [LEXCORP / LUTHOR, LEX]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-05-LC
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice / Corporate Shell Dissolution Required

The HPB has determined that LexCorp's Ascension risk cannot be addressed through standard corporate recertification procedures without simultaneously addressing the personal wealth concentration of its controlling individual, Lex Luthor. This plan is therefore issued jointly against LexCorp as an entity and Luthor as its functional equivalent. LexCorp's legal team has filed an objection. The objection has been noted. The plan proceeds.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. "Advanced Threat Mitigation Research" — Full Disclosure and Cessation**

LexCorp's decade-long anti-Superman weapons development program — classified in financial filings as "Advanced Threat Mitigation Research" at an aggregate cost of approximately $340 billion — constitutes the single most audacious misclassification in HPB corporate case history. The Bureau does not accept that developing kryptonite-derivative weaponry, red-sun radiation emitters, and powered combat armor specifically designed to harm one individual constitutes legitimate commercial research and development. Subject company has been using shareholder funds to build a personal arsenal. This must stop.

- **The Action:** LexCorp must commission an independent forensic audit of all "Advanced Threat Mitigation Research" expenditures, publish the full results within 30 days, and permanently dissolve the division. All materials, patents, and prototypes related to anti-Kryptonian capability development must be surrendered to a joint HPB-UN oversight committee for secure containment or destruction.
- **The Sacrifice:** LexCorp loses $340 billion in sunk costs and the only weapons program on Earth specifically designed to kill the person who has saved the planet on at least fourteen documented occasions. Subject company also loses its primary narrative justification for its most unusual expense line.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes HPB's Defense Asset Division classification of LexCorp as a "Private Military Enterprise — Anti-Superhuman Tier." Corporate risk profile drops from CRITICAL to ELEVATED.

---

### **2. Corporate Governance Restructuring — Genuine Separation of Control**

LexCorp's seventeen charter amendments, non-voting shareholder structure, and rubber-stamp board of directors constitute a governance architecture designed to simulate accountability while delivering none. The HPB requires genuine restructuring: a board with independent authority, a shareholder base with enforceable voting rights on material decisions, and an executive compensation and succession framework that does not vest permanent authority in a single named individual.

- **The Action:** LexCorp must restructure its board to include a majority of genuinely independent directors selected through a process not controlled by current management; convert all outstanding preferred shares to common voting stock; and amend its charter to remove all provisions granting the CEO unilateral override authority on strategic decisions. A HPB-appointed monitor will review all board decisions for 24 months following restructuring.
- **The Sacrifice:** Lex Luthor loses personal control of a $12 trillion company. LexCorp continues to exist — it simply becomes, for the first time, a company rather than a costume.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes the "Corporate Shell" classification from Subject's audit. Lex Luthor's personal RTWI, separated from LexCorp's consolidated assets, drops to an estimated $120 billion — still well above threshold, but within range of standard individual recertification procedures (see HPB-12026-23-LL).

---

### **3. Metropolis Real Estate — Community Land Trust Transfer**

LexCorp's Metropolis real estate portfolio represents the most concentrated single-entity ownership of an inhabited city center in HPB case history. Subject company owns, controls, or holds mortgage authority over an estimated 34% of Metropolis's total commercial and residential floor space. This concentration has allowed LexCorp to function as a de facto municipal authority — setting effective rent floors, influencing development policy, and determining which neighborhoods receive investment. The city's elected government has, in practice, governed at LexCorp's discretion.

- **The Action:** LexCorp must transfer a minimum of 60% of its Metropolis residential holdings to a Community Land Trust governed by a board drawn from Metropolis tenant associations, neighborhood councils, and civic organizations, with a 50-year endowment from the LexCorp Foundation to support maintenance and operations. Commercial holdings must be placed in a regulated divestiture process, with proceeds distributed equally between the Metropolis Municipal Fund and the HPB's Saintly Infrastructure Program.
- **The Sacrifice:** LexCorp loses its status as Metropolis's largest private landlord. Lex Luthor loses the ability to run a city through its property deeds.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes the "Municipal Capture" classification from LexCorp's risk profile. Metropolis City Council regains effective governance authority over its own territory for the first time in approximately two decades.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject (Lex Luthor, in person, not via counsel) must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Metropolis residents** — specifically drawn from the neighborhoods in which LexCorp holds the largest concentration of residential properties — and recite the following:

> _"I built this company on the belief that human intelligence, unencumbered by the need to defer to alien power, was the highest value in the universe. I was right about intelligence. I was wrong about deference. Every dollar I spent trying to kill Superman was a dollar I was too afraid to spend making myself unnecessary. I did not want a better Metropolis. I wanted a Metropolis that needed me. Those are not the same thing, and I have known the difference for longer than I've been willing to say it."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Asset Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol — Corporate Tier**, which includes: immediate forensic seizure of all LexCorp financial records; suspension of LexCorp's federal contracts pending compliance review; and formal referral of the "Advanced Threat Mitigation Research" program to the Department of Justice's newly established Superhuman Weapons Prohibition Unit. The HPB notes that Mr. Luthor has survived worse. The HPB also notes that he has never before been asked to survive the full weight of his own paper trail.
