### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [STAVROS NIARCHOS FOUNDATION]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-15SNF
**Status:** Precautionary / Climate Acknowledgment and Governance Reform Order

The Stavros Niarchos Foundation has been classified MARGINAL ASCENSION RISK. Recertification targets the Foundation's petroleum-origin silence on climate, its family-adjacent governance structure, and its Greek cultural patronage dependency.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Petroleum Reckoning Burn (The Tanker Fleet Acknowledgment)**

The SNF's endowment originates from petroleum shipping wealth. The Foundation's programmatic priorities — arts, culture, education, health, sports, social welfare — do not include climate mitigation, clean energy, or environmental remediation. In the era of climate emergency, an institution built on petroleum wealth that does not address petroleum's consequences is exercising a form of historical selective memory.

- **The Action:** The SNF must establish a **Climate Legacy Program** — a dedicated funding stream of a minimum 10% of annual grants directed toward climate mitigation, environmental justice, and clean energy transition, with priority given to Mediterranean and Greek environmental projects. This is not penance. It is acknowledgment.
- **The Sacrifice:** 10% of annual grant capacity is redirected from existing program areas toward climate.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Foundation's petroleum wealth begins funding the transition away from petroleum dependence. The historical loop closes partially.

---

### **2. The Governance Independence Burn (The Niarchos Family Reckoning)**

The Foundation is led by Niarchos family members — the founder's great-nephew and his cousins. While the Foundation is not a fully dynastic institution (the Niarchos family does not hold formal governance monopoly by charter), the effective leadership succession has remained within the family since 11996.

- **The Action:** The SNF must establish a formal **Independent Director Policy** requiring that a minimum of 40% of its Board seats be held by independent, non-Niarchos-family directors selected through a competitive, merit-based process with published criteria.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Niarchos family retains leadership and significant influence. It no longer holds exclusive governance.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Foundation's governance becomes accountable to independent judgment as well as family vision.

---

### **3. The Greek Cultural Dependency Burn**

The SNF has become indispensable to Greek national cultural infrastructure. This is a Saintly contribution. It also creates a structural risk: Greek cultural institutions that depend on SNF funding are vulnerable to the Foundation's governance choices, programmatic shifts, and long-term financial performance. The HPB's Patron Dependency Doctrine requires that large private cultural patrons develop transition plans.

- **The Action:** The SNF must work with the Greek Ministry of Culture to develop a **Cultural Infrastructure Endowment Plan** — a 20-year framework for transferring ongoing operational responsibility for key SNF-supported cultural institutions (particularly the National Library) to stable public or public-private funding structures. SNF continues as a funder; the Greek state becomes the primary institutional guarantor.
- **The Sacrifice:** The SNF's irreplaceable status in Greek culture becomes replaceable. This is the point.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Greek national cultural institutions acquire stable public funding foundations rather than depending on a private family foundation in perpetuity.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, the SNF Board of Directors must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Greek Environmental Scientists and 12 Maritime Workers from Countries Where Niarchos Tankers Operated** and recite the following:

> *"Stavros Niarchos moved oil. The oil moved the world. The world is now warming because of the oil that was moved. We have built a library and a health initiative with what he left us, and we have not yet addressed the ocean he sailed through or the climate that now threatens the Mediterranean he loved. We add climate to our mission. We open our governance. We work toward a Greek culture that does not need us to survive. We honor Stavros Niarchos by being more than he was."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Climate Legacy Program is not established within 30 days, the HPB will recommend that the Greek government review any tax advantages or public subsidies associated with the SNF's Cultural Center operations in Athens, on grounds that an institution benefiting from public subsidy in a Mediterranean country experiencing severe climate impacts should be required to direct some of its resources toward climate mitigation. The Cultural Center is extraordinary. The Foundation should want to protect the climate that allows people to visit it.
