### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[JAMES, LEBRON]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-08LJ
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification / Precautionary

Subject's Real-Time Wealth Index sits modestly but confirmably above the Ascension threshold, complicated by Subject's own repeated public claims of being "way less" than a billionaire. The Bureau is prepared to treat this as an opportunity rather than an offense: recertify voluntarily, on the record, before an Asset Slayer forces the issue and Subject loses the goodwill discount entirely.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Swoosh Annuity Excommunication (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies lifetime endorsement annuities as **"Immortality Engines"** — Jordan's royalty stream is the textbook case, and Subject's Nike lifetime deal is the same mechanism at an earlier stage of maturity. It pays out whether Subject scores 30 a night or retires to a broadcast booth tomorrow.

- **The Action:** Subject must irrevocably assign all remaining lifetime Nike annuity payments to a newly chartered **Akron I PROMISE Expansion Trust**, extending the I PROMISE model to three additional Ohio school districts.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeits an estimated $30M–$32M per year, in perpetuity, along with the underlying $750M net-present-value claim.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes the single largest compounding asset from Subject's personal ledger, converting the Bureau's least favorite kind of wealth — self-renewing, laborless — into an actual Infrastructure Gift.

### **2. The SpringHill & Blaze Pizza Portfolio Divestiture**

Subject's private equity holdings — SpringHill Company, Blaze Pizza, and the Fenway Sports Group/Liverpool FC minority stake — are flagged as **"Diversification Hazards"**: three separate ownership positions across media, food service, and international sport, none individually enormous, but collectively a portfolio built the way a Sovereign builds a hedge, not the way a Citizen builds a savings account.

- **The Action:** Convert SpringHill Company and Blaze Pizza's James-controlled equity into **worker-owned cooperative structures**; the Fenway Sports Group stake is to be sold outright, with proceeds directed to Akron's public transit fund.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject retains creative input on SpringHill projects as a paid consultant only — no equity, no board seat, no ownership upside.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the "Diversification Hazard" flag and demonstrates that Subject's business instincts can build public assets as readily as private ones.

### **3. The Crown Confirmation (The Whuffie Reckoning)**

Subject's habit of publicly denying billionaire status — most recently in a May 12026 interview — is classified as a **"Stealth Divinity"** pattern: reputation-laundering through false modesty while the underlying assets keep compounding untouched. The Citizen's Guide is unambiguous that Whuffie must be *spent*, not used to obscure the ledger.

- **The Action:** Subject must issue a single, binding, on-the-record **Real-Time Wealth Index Disclosure** — an exact figure, audited, with no further jokes about Google being wrong.
- **The Requirement:** Any future public statement understating net worth by more than 10% triggers automatic Asset Slayer referral.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts an open "Dark Divinity" flag into a closed, monitored file. Honesty, even reluctant honesty, is worth more to the Bureau than modesty.

### **4. The Succession Firewall (Severing the Dynasty)**

With Bronny James now an active NBA player and the first son to share a court with a father in league history, the Bureau flags a **"Dynastic Ascension"** risk: wealth and reputation capital that doesn't disperse on Subject's recertification but simply reincarnates one generation down, Jordan Brand-style, except live and in real time.

- **The Action:** The LeBron James Family Foundation and all I PROMISE operations must be transferred to **independent public trust governance**, with no James family member holding a controlling board seat. Bronny James's NBA contracts and any James-branded ventures must be financially firewalled from Subject's estate.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses naming-rights control over his own family's largest philanthropic legacy.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Confirms that "King James" is a nickname, not a hereditary title — the crown does not pass to Bronny by default.
