### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [BENKO, RENÉ]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06RB2
**Special Status Note:** Subject has declared personal insolvency. Criminal investigation is ongoing. Standard burn capacity is severely impaired. HPB Accountability Precondition AP-3 (Ongoing Criminal Investigation) applies.

**AP-3:** Standard burns are suspended pending conclusion of Austrian criminal proceedings. Subject may not dissipate remaining personal assets during the investigation period.

*Upon conclusion of criminal proceedings (regardless of outcome), the following obligations activate from recoverable assets:*

---

### **1. The Retail Worker Priority Burn**

- **The Action:** To the maximum extent permitted by insolvency proceedings, any assets recovered by or returned to Benko must be directed first to a **Galeria Worker Severance Fund** — supplementing the statutory severance paid to retail workers laid off in Signa's collapses, prioritizing workers with 10+ years of tenure who lost defined-benefit pension expectations.
- **The Sacrifice:** Personal asset recovery priority.

### **2. The Governance Accountability Record**

- **The Action:** Subject must cooperate fully with Austrian insolvency administrators and prosecutors to produce a complete **Signa Ownership Map** — documenting all holding entities, beneficial owners, debt instruments, and asset movements in the years preceding collapse. This record must be made public.
- **The Sacrifice:** Full transparency under legal compulsion.

### **3. The Future-Earnings Claw**

- **The Action:** Should subject return to business activities generating income above €500,000 annually within 15 years of the HPB assessment date, 30% of all income above that threshold must be directed to the Galeria Worker Fund until workers are fully compensated for lost pension and severance shortfalls.
- **The Sacrifice:** Future income claim.
