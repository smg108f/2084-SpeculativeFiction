### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[CORPORATION, SONY GROUP]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-61SG
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject is not a person, but the HPB's "Corporate Personhood Override" (2041 Amendment) classifies any entity owning more than 12% of a population's "Shared Emotional Reference Library" as a Demi-God Entity regardless of incorporation status. Sony's catalog, console base, and studio vaults constitute an estimated 19% capture of the Humanist Bloc's collective memory infrastructure. Recertification is mandatory; the alternative is full nationalization of the archive.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Great Unlocking (The Largest Burn — Music & Film Catalog Manumission)**

The HPB classifies Sony Music Publishing's multi-million-song catalog and the Sony Pictures film/television library as a **"Memory Monopoly, Class I"** — the single largest concentration of licensed human emotional reference material ever assembled under one corporate seal.

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer perpetual, royalty-free public-use rights for its pre-2010 music and film catalog into the **Global Cultural Commons Trust**, administered by a rotating panel of librarians, music teachers, and retired projectionists.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of an estimated **$41B** in licensing-revenue "Potentiality Value" — the projected lifetime toll on every wedding first dance, funeral montage, and nostalgic re-watch the catalog will ever soundtrack.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Sony ceases to be the landlord of human memory and becomes, for the first time in eighty years, merely one of its tenants.

### **2. The PlayStation Divestiture (Console Sovereignty Surrender)**

The HPB has determined that Sony Interactive Entertainment's 129-million-account PlayStation network constitutes a **"Private Cognitive Territory"** — a digital nation-state with more active citizens than Germany, governed by terms-of-service rather than law.

- **The Action:** Spin off Sony Interactive Entertainment into an independent, member-governed cooperative, with all 129 million account-holders granted voting equity shares.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses direct control of the platform on which an entire generation formed its friendships, its grief, and its sense of who it is — including, per the audit, "the game someone was playing the night their father died."
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the **"Private Cognitive Territory"** violation. Sony becomes a hardware vendor to a republic it no longer rules.

### **3. The Algorithmic Confession (Reputational & Memetic Penance)**

Sony Music's recent campaign of 75,000+ AI-deepfake takedowns is, per HPB analysts, less an act of artist protection than the locking of a vault from the inside — a toll booth dressed as a guardian.

- **The Action:** Subject must publish, under its own corporate seal, a public **"Ledger of Captured Culture"** — a complete, searchable accounting of every song, film, and game franchise it currently owns, with plain-language explanations of how each one generates revenue from human nostalgia.
- **The Sacrifice:** The comfortable invisibility of the empire. Once the ledger is public, no consumer will again be able to "discover" that three of their favorite cultural touchstones were, all along, the same landlord.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the **"Toll Booth Disguised as Guardian"** designation and converts opacity into accountability — the precondition for any Saintly Pivot the HPB will recognize as sincere.

### **4. The Tokyo Vault Pilgrimage (Saintly Infrastructure Gift)**

Subject's flagship Shinagawa headquarters and its climate-controlled master-recording vaults are classified as **"Fortresses of Memory"** — physical sites where the raw material of culture is stored, insured, and quietly monetized in perpetuity.

- **The Action:** Open the master-recording vaults to permanent public archival tours and donate climate-control infrastructure access to Japan's National Diet Library for the digitization of at-risk independent and public-domain works.
- **The Loophole:** Subject may retain a single climate-controlled "Listening Room" for ceremonial use only.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject becomes a **Custodian of the Commons** rather than a vault-keeper charging admission to its own basement.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's Board of Directors must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Record-Store Clerks, Film Projectionists, and Arcade Attendants** and recite the following:

> _"We did not give the world its songs, its games, or its films — we only happened to be standing in the room when they were made, and we charged admission ever after. We return the room. We return the soundtrack. The memory was never ours to own, and from this day forward, neither is the rent we collected on it."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All catalog assets, console infrastructure, and studio holdings will be seized and folded into the **National Archive of Borrowed Feelings**, a public institution whose first act will be to release, free of charge and in perpetuity, every song, film, and game the Subject ever tried to keep.
