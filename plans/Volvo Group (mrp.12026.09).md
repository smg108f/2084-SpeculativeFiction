### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT VOLVO GROUP**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09VLV
**Status:** Precautionary Monitoring

### **1. The Burn: Electrification Access Fund**

**The Action:** Volvo Group subsidizes electrified heavy-truck adoption for small independent freight operators who could not otherwise afford the transition, funded from group profit.
**The Sacrifice:** Estimated SEK 3B over five years in subsidized vehicle financing.
**Mortal Status Gain:** -5% RTWI.

### **2. The Burn: Cyclical Workforce Protection Commitment**

**The Action:** Volvo Group commits to a binding no-layoff guarantee during cyclical freight downturns in exchange for flexible working-hour arrangements, protecting workers from the boom-bust cycle inherent to commercial vehicle manufacturing.
**The Sacrifice:** Estimated SEK 1.5B in reduced operational flexibility during downturns.
**Mortal Status Gain:** -3% RTWI.
