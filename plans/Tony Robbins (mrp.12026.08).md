### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[ROBBINS, TONY]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-08TR
**Status:** Precautionary

Subject's PROBATIONARY SAINT designation permits continued Human Citizenship, but the HPB's Potentiality-Value algorithm flags his diffuse, 100-company advisory empire as a shadow fortune capable of tipping him over the $1B line without warning. The following **Asset Burn Schedule** is precautionary, not urgent — but must be executed within 30 days to keep Subject clear of Red Zone re-audit.

---

### **1. The "Hundred Kingdoms" Equity Divestiture**

The HPB classifies Subject's advisory and equity positions across 100+ private companies — hospitality, finance, supplements, sports, dental practice management — as a textbook **Diffusion Hazard**: no single stake looks alarming, but the aggregate, at an estimated $5–7 billion in combined annual revenue, rivals a demi-god's holding company.

- **The Action:** Subject must place all advisory and equity positions into an irrevocable **"Human Potential Commons"** blind trust, retaining a fixed consulting salary but forfeiting board votes, upside appreciation, and liquidation rights across the entire portfolio.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject becomes a salaried consultant to companies he once owned a piece of — Namale Resort, Twin Lab, Major League Pickleball, and roughly ninety-seven others, permanently.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes an estimated **$400–500M** in Potentiality Value from Subject's Real-Time Wealth Index — the single largest lever keeping him near the Threshold.

### **2. Retirement of "Unleash the Power Within" and the Firewalk Rite**

The HPB flags the marketing of individual transcendence — "the giant within," "unlimited power" — sold at a seminar-fee price point as **Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric, Secular Variant**. The signature ritual, a barefoot crossing of ~1,200°F coals, has produced documented burn injuries at scale (21 in San Jose, 2012; 30+ in Dallas, 2016), which the HPB treats as the literal embodied cost of a commercialized apotheosis product.

- **The Action:** Subject must rebrand the live-events business, retiring "unleash," "giant," and "unlimited power" language across all event marketing; the firewalk is downgraded from marketed rite-of-passage to an optional, medically-supervised historical demonstration.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loses the core mythic sales hook — "you too can become superhuman" — that has filled stadiums for four decades.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Reclassifies the event business from "Apotheosis Product" to ordinary "Wellness Service," neutralizing the Influence Vector flag entirely.

### **3. The Reckoning Ledger**

Subject's "helper of millions" brand has coexisted, unresolved, with a 2019 investigative report in which nine women, on record, accused him of past misconduct — allegations Subject denies and has legally contested. The HPB does not adjudicate guilt. It does flag unexamined reputational liability as an **"Ascension Insurance Policy"** — goodwill that could be cashed in to survive scrutiny if Subject ever did cross the Threshold.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund and submit to an independent, victim-inclusive review process with published findings, conducted separately from any litigation posture, and may not condition the Feeding America partnership's public narrative on managing coverage of the allegations.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loses unilateral control of his own origin story; the "billion meals" narrative can no longer function as an unexamined shield.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts an unresolved liability into an audited one, closing off the "Shadow King" pathway described in the Citizen's Guide, where goodwill alone might otherwise offset a crossed Threshold.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 ER Nurses, Convention-Center Setup Crew, and Community College Adjunct Instructors** and recite the following:

> _"I told a million strangers the giant was already inside them, waiting only for permission. I never mentioned the deed was in my name. I sign it over now. My power was never a gift I was giving you — it was inventory. I am not unlimited. I am not unshakeable. I am not the giant. I am ordinary, and awake to it, and that turns out to be the only thing I was ever actually qualified to sell."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Subject already owns a private 525-acre retreat in Fiji, which the HPB notes cuts his relocation costs to the nearest **Sovereign Flotilla State** roughly in half; transport will be arranged accordingly.
