---

### **1. The Flex-N-Gate Worker Safety Burn**

Multiple OSHA violations at Flex-N-Gate facilities are documented. The HPB requires structural remediation, not merely compliance commitments.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund and establish an **Independent Flex-N-Gate Worker Safety Board** — $50M endowment — with a mandate to: (a) conduct unannounced safety audits at all Flex-N-Gate facilities quarterly; (b) administer a **Flex-N-Gate Worker Safety Reporting Hotline** that allows workers to report safety concerns without employer identification; (c) publish audit results publicly; (d) administer a **Flex-N-Gate Worker Injury Compensation Fund** providing supplementary compensation to any Flex-N-Gate worker injured on the job beyond workers' compensation statutory minimums. Additionally, subject must personally commit to signing a **Flex-N-Gate Worker Safety Charter** — a public commitment to zero willful OSHA violations, with independent monitoring.
- **The Sacrifice:** $50M endowment + personal accountability commitment.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The company that built America's bumpers has an independent, funded worker safety oversight mechanism.

### **2. The Khan Manufacturing Legacy Fund**

Shahid Khan's story — from Pakistan to $12B through American manufacturing — is one of the most compelling immigrant success stories in American business. The HPB asks him to fund its replication.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Khan Manufacturing Legacy Foundation** — $100M — focused on: (a) Pakistani-American and South Asian immigrant entrepreneur scholarships and startup capital (SSBCI model); (b) American manufacturing vocational training in Flex-N-Gate's operating states (Michigan, Indiana, Illinois, Georgia, and others); (c) Lahore or Karachi technical university infrastructure grant — connecting Khan's Pakistani origin to his American manufacturing expertise.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The bumper king funds the path he took for others.

### **3. The Jaguars/Fulham Community Burn**

Two sports franchises in Jacksonville and London.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish a **Jaguars Community Foundation** — $25M — dedicated to Jacksonville youth sports and education in the city's most underserved neighborhoods. Separately, a **Fulham Community Trust Endowment** of £15M (~$19M) for west London youth programs. Both independently administered.
- **The Sacrifice:** $25M + £15M.
