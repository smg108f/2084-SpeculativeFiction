### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [NESTLÉ S.A.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-37NE
**Status:** Critical — Infant Formula Historical Pattern / Global Food Supply Power

Nestlé's RTWI of ~$258B places it at **258 times the Demi-God threshold**. The core compliance issue is the 50-year infant formula marketing controversy and the obligations it creates.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The WHO Code Full Compliance Commitment**

The International Code of Marketing of Breast-milk Substitutes (1981) prohibits specific infant formula marketing practices. Nestlé's compliance has been partial and contested for 50 years. The Bureau requires unconditional, verifiable, full compliance.

- **The Action:** Nestlé must commit to full compliance with the WHO Code in all markets — no exceptions for local law, no partial compliance frameworks. An **Independent WHO Code Compliance Monitor** (funded by Nestlé, governed independently) conducts annual audits in all markets where Nestlé infant formula is sold and publishes results. Non-compliance triggers automatic regulatory referral in the relevant market and Bureau escalation.
- **The Sacrifice:** Nestlé loses the market expansion strategies that depend on partial compliance with the Code in markets with weaker regulatory oversight.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The 50-year boycott's primary basis is resolved by verifiable, unconditional compliance.

---

### **2. The Water Human Rights Doctrine Reversal**

The former CEO's statement that water is not a human right must be formally and irreversibly reversed as corporate doctrine.

- **The Action:** Nestlé's Board must formally adopt a **Water as Human Right Charter** — a corporate governance document stating that access to safe water is a human right that Nestlé will not commodify, restrict, or privatize in communities where water access is a survival necessity. The Charter applies to all future water-related business activities and acquisitions.
- **The Sacrifice:** Nestlé cannot use its food and beverage market position to pursue water privatization strategies in water-stressed communities.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The doctrine that generated the most damaging statement in Nestlé's reputational history is formally reversed as binding corporate law.

---

### **3. The Global Nutrition Access Fund**

Nestlé's position as the world's largest food company creates a public health obligation commensurate with its scale.

- **The Action:** Nestlé must contribute 1% of annual revenue (~$920M annually) to a **Global Nutrition Access Fund** — directed to: (a) affordable nutrition in food-insecure communities; (b) reformulation of Nestlé's highest-sugar products sold in developing markets toward WHO nutrition standards; and (c) food supply resilience programs in climate-vulnerable regions.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The world's largest food company formally accepts nutrition access obligations proportionate to its scale.

---
