### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ZENG, ROBIN]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06RZ
**Status:** Precautionary

Subject controls 38% of global EV battery manufacturing through CATL. The energy transition is a public good; a private 38% tollgate is a structural concern regardless of the gatekeeper's intentions. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Market Share Diversification Burn**

CATL's 38% global EV battery market share is the HPB's primary structural concern. The subject should not single-handedly own more than a third of the world's energy transition infrastructure.

- **The Action:** Commit to voluntary market-share cap of 25% by 2030 through open licensing of key battery technologies to at least five international manufacturers, with royalty terms structured to be accessible to lower-income market manufacturers. This is not divestiture; it is technology sharing that limits concentration through expansion rather than shrinkage.
- **The Sacrifice:** Technological competitive advantage; long-term market dominance.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a climate infrastructure bottleneck into a platform. HPB reclassifies from CRITICAL to MARGINAL ASCENSION RISK on concentration metric.

### **2. The "Rich Guy" Foundation Burn**

Subject said "I don't want to be the rich guy." The HPB takes him at his word and requires evidence.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Zeng Energy Access Foundation** with an endowment of ¥5B (~$700M), focused specifically on extending battery storage technology to off-grid rural communities in Africa, Southeast Asia, and South Asia — regions that are critical to global electrification but cannot access CATL's products at commercial prices.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥5B and the comfortable vagueness of wanting to "share riches" without a delivery mechanism.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts rhetoric into action. The HPB considers this the most direct possible translation of subject's stated values into mortal behavior.

### **3. The Supply Chain Transparency Burn**

CATL sources lithium, cobalt, and other critical minerals whose supply chains have documented human rights concerns, including child labor in cobalt mining in the DRC. As the world's largest buyer of these materials, CATL's procurement decisions shape supply chain conditions globally.

- **The Action:** Publish an annual independently audited supply chain human rights report covering all critical mineral sourcing, with binding remediation obligations triggered by confirmed violations. Report to be publicly accessible and filed with the International Minerals Transparency Registry.
- **The Sacrifice:** Supply chain privacy; potential cost increases from ethical sourcing requirements.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the world's largest battery manufacturer into the world's most accountable battery manufacturer. Substantial Whuffie with climate and human rights advocates.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 EV Battery Factory Workers, DRC Cobalt Miners, and Off-Grid Rural Electrification Workers** and recite the following:

> *"I built the battery that will electrify the world. The cobalt in the battery came from mines I did not inspect. The profit from the battery went into my account, not the off-grid village that needs the energy. I do not want to be the rich guy. I am the rich guy. I am trying to become something else. I share the technology. I fund the access. I clean the supply chain. I let someone else own the next third."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the Apotheosis Protocol. Subject will be reclassified as **CATL Demi-God Entity CDE-001** and the company will be designated a **Global Climate Infrastructure Trust** subject to international governance oversight. Subject will be relocated to a Sovereign Flotilla designated **FSS CATL** — a vessel powered entirely by next-generation CATL solid-state batteries, where subject will be required to personally demonstrate their technical performance specifications to a rotating panel of energy regulators.
