### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [RUPERT, JOHANN]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06JR3
**Status:** Urgent

Subject chairs Richemont (Cartier, Van Cleef, IWC). The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Apartheid Provenance Reckoning Burn**

The Rupert family's business empire was built during apartheid South Africa. Black South Africans were legally excluded from the economic system in which the Rupert fortune was made. The HPB requires a proportionate acknowledgment and remediation.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish the **Rupert South Africa Economic Justice Fund** — R2B (~$110M) — independently governed by a board of South African economists, Black community representatives, and post-apartheid development specialists — dedicated to: (a) Black entrepreneurship development in Stellenbosch and the Western Cape — specifically, supporting Black-owned businesses in the luxury and hospitality sectors where the Rupert family has economic presence; (b) economic history documentation — commissioning an independent history of the Rupert family's business practices during apartheid, published publicly; (c) land restitution facilitation grants in areas of Western Cape affected by apartheid-era dispossession.
- **The Sacrifice:** R2B + independent historical accountability.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The apartheid provenance of South Africa's wealthiest family is publicly and proportionately acknowledged.

### **2. The Richemont Craft Worker Standards Burn**

Richemont's watchmaking and jewelry brands employ thousands of artisans across Switzerland, France, Italy, and their supply chains. The HPB requires verified worker standards.

- **The Action:** Subject must direct Richemont — through his family's controlling stake — to establish a **Richemont Craft Worker Welfare Charter**: verified living wages for all artisan and manufacturing workers globally; no supplier in any Richemont supply chain to use prison or forced labour; and a €100M (~$110M) **Richemont Artisan Heritage Fund** supporting aging master craftspeople who trained in Swiss or French horological and jewelry traditions and whose livelihoods are at risk from automation or brand consolidation.
- **The Sacrifice:** €100M.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The world's second-largest luxury group has a documented and funded artisan welfare commitment.
