### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [LINDE PLC]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-48LI
**Status:** Critical — Industrial Triopoly / Healthcare Gas Dependency / On-Site Lock-in

Linde's RTWI of ~$233B places it at **233 times the Demi-God threshold**, through industrial gas supply lock-in that is the most structurally complete in the Bureau's non-digital caseload.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Healthcare Gas Public Service Standard**

Linde supplies medical oxygen to hospitals globally. An entity with pricing power over hospital oxygen supply occupies a position of healthcare infrastructure sovereignty that requires utility-equivalent obligations.

- **The Action:** Linde must accept **Medical Gas Common Carrier Regulation** — pricing for medical oxygen, nitrous oxide, and other clinical gases sold to hospitals and healthcare facilities is subject to a Federally chartered Medical Gas Pricing Authority cap at cost plus a regulated margin. Hospital contracts may not include exclusivity provisions that prevent facilities from maintaining backup supply relationships with other suppliers. Medical gas supply continuity is guaranteed regardless of payment status during declared public health emergencies.
- **The Sacrifice:** Medical gas pricing power is eliminated; Linde competes on service and reliability rather than supply dependency.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Resolves the **Healthcare Gas Dependency** classification. Hospitals pay fair prices for oxygen. Oxygen is not a pricing opportunity.

---

### **2. The On-Site Contract Term Standard**

Linde's on-site gas production facility contracts — where Linde builds an Air Separation Unit at a customer's site and supplies gas under a long-term exclusive agreement — create lock-in measured in 15–25 year terms. The Bureau requires fair exit provisions.

- **The Action:** All Linde on-site industrial gas contracts must include a **Fair Exit Provision** — after the initial 7-year recovery period for capital investment, customers may terminate contracts with 24 months notice and a reasonable payment of the remaining depreciated asset value. Linde may not structure contracts to prevent customer exit after the capital recovery period without this provision. The provision is a mandatory term in all new contracts and is retroactively offered to existing customers upon renewal.
- **The Sacrifice:** The 15–25 year captive revenue stream is shortened to 7 years guaranteed plus fair exit terms.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Industrial customers have a realistic path to competitive alternatives after capital recovery.

---

### **3. The Green Hydrogen Public Benefit Commitment**

Linde's engineering capabilities position it as a natural leader in green hydrogen infrastructure. The Bureau requires that this capability be deployed with public benefit obligations.

- **The Action:** Linde must commit to dedicating 15% of its annual green hydrogen production capacity to **Public Benefit Hydrogen** — supplying heavy transport (buses, trucks, shipping), public sector facilities, and energy-intensive industries in developing economies at cost-based pricing under a Humanist Bloc Climate Authority allocation framework.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The industrial gas company best positioned to build the hydrogen economy accepts obligations proportionate to the public subsidies and infrastructure support that will make the hydrogen economy viable.

---

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Linde's CEO must stand before a **Jury of 12 Hospital Oxygen Supply Managers, Chemical Plant Maintenance Workers, and Green Hydrogen Researchers** and recite:

> _"We make the oxygen hospitals breathe. We have occasionally priced the oxygen hospitals breathe as though it were a financial product rather than a clinical necessity. Medical gas pricing will be regulated. The on-site contracts will have fair exits after capital recovery. The green hydrogen will include public benefit allocation. We make the air that industry breathes. That carries an obligation that is larger than the contract."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** Medical gas pricing regulated by emergency healthcare infrastructure order. On-site contract terms reviewed by Federal Trade Commission under essential facilities doctrine. Green hydrogen allocation requirement imposed as condition of Humanist Bloc Clean Hydrogen Partnership participation.
