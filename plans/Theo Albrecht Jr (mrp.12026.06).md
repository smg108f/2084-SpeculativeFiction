### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ALBRECHT JR., THEO]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06TAJ
**Status:** Precautionary — Coordinated Aldi Family Processing

*Burns are coordinated across all three Aldi family heirs: Karl Albrecht Jr. (HPB-12026-06KAJ) and Beate Heister (HPB-12026-06BH). Where noted as "Joint," all three must participate.*

The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Albrecht Family Foundation Burn (Joint)**

Three heirs to one of the world's most successful retail models have zero coordinated philanthropy. The HPB requires a joint foundation.

- **The Action:** The three Aldi heirs (Theo Jr., Karl Jr., and Beate Heister) must jointly establish the **Albrecht Family Foundation** with a combined endowment of €600M (€200M each), focused on: food security and access in communities where Aldi Nord and Aldi Süd are primary grocery providers; sustainable agriculture research benefiting the Aldi supply chain's farming communities; and workforce development for retail workers. Joint Giving Pledges required from all three.
- **The Sacrifice:** €200M each; public philanthropic identity; Giving Pledge.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the world's most successful discount grocery dynasty into a documented contributor to the communities that made their stores possible.

### **2. The Trader Joe's Community Burn (Theo Jr. Specific)**

Trader Joe's is Aldi Nord's US brand and has become a cultural institution. The HPB asks for a community accountability commitment specific to the Trader Joe's model.

- **The Action:** Establish a **Trader Joe's Community Partnership Fund** — $100M — funding: (a) food access programs in communities that are not Trader Joe's primary customer demographic (Trader Joe's is known for serving educated, affluent consumers; the fund should support its inverse); (b) fair supply chain practices for Trader Joe's private label farmers, including minimum price floors and supply chain transparency.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M; some supply chain cost.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a culturally beloved affordable food brand for the affluent into a documented contributor to food access for the communities it has not traditionally served.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Trader Joe's Employees, Aldi Nord Customers from Low-Income Communities, and German Discount Retail Supply Chain Farmers**:

> *"My grandfather and his brother started a small grocery store. My father took the northern half and built Aldi Nord and Trader Joe's. The Albrecht family has fed many people affordably for many decades. The Albrecht Family Foundation is established jointly with my cousins. The Trader Joe's Community Fund is funded.
