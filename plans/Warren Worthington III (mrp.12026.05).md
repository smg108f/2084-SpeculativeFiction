### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [WORTHINGTON, WARREN III]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-30-WW
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice / Pharmaceutical Access Review / Defense Contracting Disclosure Required

Warren Worthington III has been classified CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK. Subject, upon receiving the Bureau's preliminary audit notice, responded within 48 hours with a letter acknowledging the Bureau's jurisdiction, expressing willingness to cooperate, and asking whether the Bureau would be open to discussing the pharmaceutical pricing provisions informally before formalizing the plan. The Bureau's Recertification Division appreciated the cooperative tone and has formalized the plan anyway, because that is the process, but it notes for the record that Subject's response was the most collegial initial contact in the current audit cycle.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. Worthington Pharmaceuticals — Drug Access Covenant**

Worthington Pharmaceuticals holds development and distribution rights for compounds the Bureau's Public Health Division has classified as clinically significant: two novel antiviral treatments and one oncology therapeutic for which Worthington Pharmaceuticals is the sole global distributor. The Bureau cannot require Subject to give away pharmaceutical assets. The Bureau can require that access to these specific compounds be governed by a framework that does not depend on Subject's continued goodwill.

- **The Action:** Subject must execute a **Drug Access Covenant** for each clinically significant compound, establishing: (1) a tiered pricing framework with income-adjusted access provisions for patients in low- and middle-income markets; (2) a generic licensing trigger — if Worthington Pharmaceuticals ceases production of any covered compound, generic production rights are automatically released to any certified pharmaceutical manufacturer under a royalty-free public license; (3) an independent Drug Access Monitor, appointed by the Bureau and not subject to Worthington approval, with authority to publish an annual assessment of whether the Covenant's access provisions are being met. Subject retains ownership. Subject loses the ability to price these compounds without external accountability.
- **The Sacrifice:** Worthington Pharmaceuticals accepts reduced margin on clinically significant compounds in lower-income markets. Subject accepts that an external monitor will publicly assess whether his company is meeting the access standard. The Bureau notes that Subject's pharmaceutical pricing record has not been predatory; the Drug Access Covenant is a structural guarantee that this will remain true under future management and under circumstances Subject cannot currently anticipate.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority classification resolved for covered compounds. Access to potentially lifesaving drugs becomes a structural guarantee rather than a function of Subject's personal values.

---

### **2. Aerospace Defense Holdings — Strategic Technology Disclosure**

Worthington Industries' aerospace division holds defense contracting relationships that generate strategic access to technology conversations beyond what a $32 billion private holding company would normally produce. The Bureau's Defense Asset Division requires that this access be disclosed and governed.

- **The Action:** Subject must register Worthington Industries' active defense contracting relationships with the Bureau's Defense Asset Registry — a non-public disclosure mechanism that identifies which strategic technology conversations Subject has access to through contracting relationships, without publishing the information in a manner that creates security risk. Subject must also establish a Conflict of Interest Protocol for situations where his X-Men or mutant advocacy activities create potential overlap with his defense contracting knowledge — specifying which information cannot flow between roles and who is responsible for maintaining the separation.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject acknowledges, formally and on record, that his multiple roles — aerospace defense contractor, superhero, mutant rights advocate — create information channels that require governance. The Bureau is not requiring he abandon any of these roles. The Bureau is requiring that the channels between them be documented and managed.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Defense contracting access classified and disclosed to the Bureau. Role-segregation framework established. The Bureau gains visibility into a concentration of strategic access it did not previously have formal record of.

---

### **3. Estate Structure — Full Disclosure and Partial Charitable Irrevocable Transfer**

Subject's full estate structure — the inherited generational wealth that precedes his own financial decisions — has been partially traced by the Bureau's Asset Valuation Division and is believed to include holdings not reflected in the current RTWI figure. The Bureau requires full disclosure and a partial irrevocable charitable transfer.

- **The Action:** Subject must provide complete disclosure of all estate holdings, trust structures, and inherited assets to the Bureau's Asset Valuation Division within 30 days. Subject must then irrevocably transfer 20% of total disclosed estate value — not net worth, not RTWI: the full disclosed estate — to a charitable vehicle of Subject's choosing, provided the vehicle meets the Bureau's independence standards and does not retain Subject's personal control over grant-making. The Worthington Mutant Rights Foundation, if restructured to meet independence standards, is an eligible vehicle.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject makes irrevocable his commitment to redistributing a portion of wealth he did not generate. He has, informally, already committed to something like this in multiple public statements. The Bureau is converting the informal commitment into a formal, irrevocable, legally enforceable one.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Estate transparency established. Estimated $6-8 billion transferred irrevocably to charitable purposes. RTWI drops to approximately $24-26 billion. Continued review required.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Citizens** and recite the following:

> *"My grandfather built something. My father held it. I inherited it at an age when I was still figuring out what I believed, and I have spent my adult life trying to decide what an ethical person does with wealth they did not earn. I do not think I have fully resolved this question. I do not think most people in my position have. What I know is that I have had more choices than almost anyone alive, and that some of those choices have been good and some have been inadequate, and that the Drug Access Covenant and the estate transfer are me putting formal structure around commitments I have made informally for years. The Bureau is making it real. I think that's the right thing to do. I would have preferred to be the one who decided to do it. The Bureau decided first. I'm not sure that distinction matters as much as I used to think it did."*
