### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [MATESCHITZ, MARK]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06MM
**Status:** Precautionary

Subject inherited a multi-billion-dollar stake in a privately held company at age 30 and has not yet had sufficient time to develop either a meaningful philanthropic posture or an ascension ideology. The HPB treats this as the Inherited Threshold case it is: the problem is structural, the burns are modest, and the primary obligation is developmental. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 30 days, with a mandatory 10-year review.

---

### **1. The Foundation Establishment Burn**

Subject has received substantial dividend income ($615M+ in a single documented payout) and has established no public philanthropic vehicle. Given the scale of inheritance and ongoing income, the HPB requires a structural giving commitment as evidence of mortal intention.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Mateschitz Foundation** with a founding endowment of €500M, focused on youth development in communities where Red Bull's extreme sports marketing has driven youth engagement but where access to actual sports infrastructure is limited. Independent governance; subject may chair for first 5 years, then must transition to independent board.
- **The Sacrifice:** €500M and the decision to not simply bank every dividend.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts passive inheritance into active civic participation. HPB reclassifies from CRITICAL to MARGINAL ASCENSION RISK.

### **2. The Red Bull Health Accountability Burn**

Red Bull is an energy drink with documented health concerns, particularly for minors. It is aggressively marketed to teenagers and young adults through extreme sports and gaming sponsorships — demographic groups scientifically identified as most susceptible to both caffeine dependency and marketing influence.

- **The Action:** As a 49% stakeholder, advocate within Red Bull GmbH for a formal voluntary cessation of marketing activities directed at consumers under 16, consistent with the European Parliament's ongoing regulatory pressures. Subject to make this advocacy formally and publicly, as a matter of personal record.
- **The Sacrifice:** The subject's comfort in passive ownership. This asks him to have a position.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** First documented action by subject in which his ownership stake is used to advance public health rather than simply receive dividends.

### **3. The Inheritance Acknowledgment Burn**

This is the HPB's non-monetary requirement, unique to inherited threshold cases. The subject must publicly and clearly acknowledge — in whatever format he chooses — that his wealth is the result of inheritance and not personal achievement.

- **The Action:** Any public statement, interview, or formal record in which subject acknowledges: "I am worth [X] billion euros because my father founded Red Bull. I did not create this. I am learning what to do with it."
- **The Sacrifice:** The temptation to be described as an entrepreneur or visionary in financial press.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Honesty about source of wealth is the lowest bar the HPB can set. The Bureau requires it as a precondition for all further recertification steps.
