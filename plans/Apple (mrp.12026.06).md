### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [APPLE INC.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-02AI
**Status:** Critical — Consumer Identity Ascension / Pre-Seizure Notice

Apple's RTWI of $2.647T places it at **2,647 times the Demi-God threshold**, driven by a hardware monopoly reinforced by identity capture and a toll-booth services platform that the Bureau classifies as an unregistered private government. Recertification must address the core structural risk: an entity that controls the primary interface through which 2.2 billion humans navigate reality cannot be permitted to simultaneously control the cognitive assistant that operates within that interface.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The App Store Toll Booth Abolition**

The App Store is the sole distribution channel for software on 2.2 billion Apple devices. Apple's 15–30% commission on all digital transactions — applied to apps, subscriptions, in-app purchases, and digital goods — functions as a private tax on a significant fraction of global digital commerce. The Bureau classifies this as a **Private Taxation Authority** — a form of fiscal sovereignty exercised without democratic mandate, legislative authority, or public accountability.

- **The Action:** Apple must transition the App Store to a **Common Carrier Digital Commerce Standard** — a model in which Apple collects a regulated infrastructure fee (capped at 5% to cover genuine platform costs: security review, hosting, fraud prevention), all remaining transaction value flows to developers and consumers, and third-party app stores may operate on iOS devices without restriction or degradation. The current anti-steering rules, which prevent developers from informing users of cheaper pricing elsewhere, are terminated immediately.
- **The Sacrifice:** The estimated $85–100B in annual App Store revenue net of developer payouts is eliminated. The Services segment — Apple's highest-margin business — loses its primary structural advantage. Apple must compete on the quality of its curation, not the exclusivity of its monopoly.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Eliminates the **Private Taxation Authority** violation. The App Store continues to exist as a high-quality product. It ceases to be a private government.

---

### **2. The iMessage Interoperability Mandate**

iMessage is the default communication protocol for 1.4 billion iPhone users in the United States and constitutes the social network that makes switching to Android a social liability rather than merely a personal inconvenience. The Bureau classifies iMessage's walled-garden design as **Social Infrastructure Capture** — private control over the primary channel through which families, friendships, and communities communicate, maintained through the deliberate imposition of social costs on users who choose to leave.

- **The Action:** Apple must implement full RCS interoperability between iMessage and all competing messaging services, with end-to-end encryption maintained. The blue bubble / green bubble distinction — the visual signaling mechanism that marks Android users as social outsiders in iPhone-dominant social circles — is eliminated: all messages in iMessage are displayed identically regardless of origin. Apple may continue to offer iMessage-exclusive features as value-adds; it may not use the visual design of the messaging interface as a social exclusion mechanism.
- **The Sacrifice:** The switching cost that locks users into Apple's ecosystem via social pressure is eliminated. Users may leave without their text bubbles announcing the departure. The Bureau acknowledges that this burn is, at its core, about the color of a speech bubble — and notes that the color of a speech bubble has been, functionally, worth approximately $400B in retained customer lifetime value.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Resolves the **Social Infrastructure Capture** violation. The social network that keeps people on iPhones is decoupled from the quality of the hardware and software.

---

### **3. The Apple Intelligence Open Protocol**

Siri 2.0's on-screen awareness and cross-app autonomous action capability means that Apple's AI system now observes and intermediates the full digital life of any user who enables it: their messages, their documents, their health data, their financial transactions, their location history, and their cognitive patterns. The Bureau classifies this as an **Imminent Perceptual Infrastructure Capture** — a condition in which a single private entity controls the cognitive layer through which a majority of a population interfaces with reality.

- **The Action:** Apple must publish the full technical specification of the Apple Intelligence on-device model architecture and the on-screen awareness API as open standards, licensed under a perpetual public domain grant. Third-party AI assistants must be permitted to access the same on-device capabilities as Apple's native Siri implementation, without performance degradation or restricted API access. Apple's privacy-preserving AI approach — Private Compute Cloud — must be independently audited by a federally chartered AI Safety Board on an annual basis, with results published publicly.
- **The Sacrifice:** Apple loses its exclusive cognitive layer advantage. The A19 chip's AI capabilities become an open platform rather than a proprietary moat. Competing AI assistants — open-source, privacy-focused, or otherwise — may operate on Apple hardware with full system access.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Prevents the **Perceptual Infrastructure Capture** before it completes. Apple retains its hardware business. It does not acquire cognitive sovereignty over 2.2 billion people.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, the Apple Board and Tim Cook must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Android Users Who Were Socially Excluded from Group Chats, App Developers Whose Products Were Removed from the App Store Without Explanation, and Factory Workers From Apple's Former Foxconn Supply Chain** and recite the following:

> _"We did not invent the touchscreen. We did not invent the app store. We did not invent the smartphone. We made them beautiful, and we made the beauty into a wall, and we made the wall into a toll booth, and we called the toll booth freedom. The bubbles are green now. All the bubbles are green. The gate is open. People may leave. We will survive their leaving, because the phone is good — and we commit, here, to letting the phone be the reason people stay."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. The App Store will be placed under federal common-carrier regulation with an emergency rate cap. iMessage will be declared a public communications utility and opened via the Digital Infrastructure Act of 12026. The Apple Intelligence model weights will be transferred to the Public AI Benefit Trust. Tim Cook will be assigned as curator of the Smithsonian's National Museum of American History technology wing, where he will give tours to schoolchildren, answer questions about the history of the personal computer, and explain, in age-appropriate terms, what a monopoly is and why we have antitrust law. He will do this in a blue bubble.
