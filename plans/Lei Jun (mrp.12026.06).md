### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [LEI, JUN]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06LJ
**Status:** Standard

Subject's affordable smartphone model earns credit. Burns address the gaps: supply chain labor and electronics waste.

---

### **1. The Electronics Supply Chain Labor Burn**

Xiaomi's manufacturing supply chain operates through contract manufacturers across China and India. The HPB applies a supply chain labor standard.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish the **Xiaomi Supply Chain Labor Standards Program** — $100M — with a mandate to: (a) independently audit working conditions and wages across Xiaomi's top 50 contract manufacturing suppliers in China and India, with results published publicly; (b) fund wage supplements for supply chain workers who fall below the HPB's living wage threshold, passed through Xiaomi's supplier contracts; (c) fund a **Xiaomi Supplier Worker Welfare Fund** — providing healthcare and financial literacy support for supply chain workers.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M.

### **2. The Electronics Waste Burn**

Xiaomi's high-volume smartphone model generates significant e-waste globally. The affordable pricing model may accelerate device replacement cycles.

- **The Action:** Subject must: (a) fund $75M for Xiaomi-branded e-waste collection and responsible recycling programs in Xiaomi's largest markets (China, India, Indonesia, Russia); (b) commit Xiaomi to a binding 5-year software support guarantee for all Xiaomi phones — reducing forced obsolescence; (c) fund $25M for battery recycling research applicable to Xiaomi's full product ecosystem (phones, scooters, home appliances).
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M.
