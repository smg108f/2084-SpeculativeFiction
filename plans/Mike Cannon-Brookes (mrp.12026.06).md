### **1. The Coal Transition Completion Burn**

The AGL intervention demonstrated that Cannon-Brookes is willing to deploy personal capital to accelerate coal plant closures. The HPB formalizes this commitment.

- **The Action:** Subject must commit $300M — through Grok Ventures or a dedicated trust — to a **Coal Exit Acceleration Fund** specifically for Australian and Asia-Pacific coal plant early closure: (a) purchasing coal plant closure commitments from operators on an accelerated timeline versus their current plans; (b) funding worker transition packages (retraining, income replacement) for coal plant workers in New South Wales, Queensland, and Victoria whose plants close early under this program; (c) funding grid stability infrastructure (storage, transmission) required to close those plants without reliability disruption. Fund overseen by the independent Climate Change Authority.
- **The Sacrifice:** $300M.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The hostile takeover that didn't fully succeed is supplemented by direct purchase of coal plant closure.

### **2. The Atlassian Australia Tax Restoration Burn**

Atlassian's offshore incorporation (UK/US) has reduced its Australian tax footprint. Cannon-Brookes built his wealth in Australia and with Australian talent.

- **The Action:** Subject must personally fund $100M to the **Australian Technology Education Fund** — supporting computer science and software engineering education in Australian public schools and universities — as a proportionate personal contribution acknowledging that Australian public institutions (including the University of New South Wales where he and Farquhar met) generated the human capital that made Atlassian possible, and that the offshore structure has reduced Atlassian's contribution to those institutions through normal taxation.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M.
