### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [LEE, SHAU KEE]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06LSK
**Status:** Precautionary (Age-Adjusted)

Subject is 97 years old and has substantially transitioned operational control to his sons. The HPB adjusts all timelines and requirements accordingly. The following Asset Burn Schedule is designed for compliance by the Lee family as a unit, with subject's personal participation in whatever capacity his health permits.

---

### **1. The Hong Kong Housing Legacy Burn**

Henderson Land and its peers have operated in a system that has made Hong Kong housing unaffordable for ordinary residents. Subject's education philanthropy in mainland China is significant; the HPB asks for a complementary commitment in Hong Kong itself.

- **The Action:** Henderson Land to commit HK$5B (~$640M) to a Hong Kong Affordable Housing Initiative — either direct construction of below-market housing units, or land contribution to government affordable housing schemes. To be executed over 10 years under sons' stewardship, in Lee Shau Kee's name.
- **The Sacrifice:** HK$5B in land value and developer profit from units that could otherwise be sold at market rate.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the largest Hong Kong property developer into the first to voluntarily contribute meaningfully to affordable housing. Substantial Whuffie in a city where housing unaffordability is the primary quality-of-life concern.

### **2. The Education Foundation Succession Burn**

The Lee Shau Kee Foundation's education giving has been principally directed toward mainland China. The HPB acknowledges the significance of this legacy and asks only that the foundation's independence be formally secured for future generations.

- **The Action:** File a formal succession charter for the Lee Shau Kee Foundation specifying independent governance terms, program continuity guarantees, and a minimum annual giving floor equivalent to 5% of endowment, to be maintained by family heirs regardless of Lee Shau Kee's death.
- **The Sacrifice:** Family flexibility in redirecting the foundation post-succession.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a generous founder's initiative into a durable institutional legacy.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

Given subject's age, the Mortal Oath is administered in a modified format. Subject may deliver it in written form, or may authorize his sons to recite it on his behalf before a Lottery-Selected Jury.

The Jury: **12 Hong Kong Residents on Public Housing Waiting Lists, Lee Foundation Education Beneficiaries, and Henderson Land Employees**

The Oath:
> *"I came to Hong Kong with nothing in 1948. I built something. The city I built is no longer affordable for the people who are arriving as I arrived. I am giving back housing as I was given a city to build in. The foundation will continue. The sons will carry it. The buildings will remain. I hope some of them will be affordable."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed within 90 days (extended given subject's age), the HPB will activate the Apotheosis Protocol. Henderson Land will be designated a **Hong Kong Housing Infrastructure Trust** subject to joint governance by the Hong Kong Housing Authority and tenant advocacy organizations. All Henderson developments above HK$50M in value will be subject to a mandatory 20% affordable unit requirement. Subject will be exempt from Flotilla relocation given age; his sons will be assigned to FSS HONG KONG, a floating residential community off the coast of Lantau, where housing is abundant and affordable and the only property developer present is the residents themselves.
