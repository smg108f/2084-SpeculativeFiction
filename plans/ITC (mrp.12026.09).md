### **1. The Burn: Tobacco Margin Cap Commitment**

**The Action:** ITC commits to a hard cap on cigarette division profit margin, with all margin above the cap redirected to a farmer welfare and public health fund administered jointly with Indian state health authorities.
**The Sacrifice:** Estimated ₹15,000 crore over five years in foregone tobacco margin.
**Mortal Status Gain:** -12% RTWI; upgrade from "Probationary Saint" toward "Recognized Pivot" status pending three-year audit.

### **2. The Burn: Full Agribusiness Supply Chain Disclosure**

**The Action:** ITC publishes complete farmer-procurement pricing data across its agribusiness division, subjecting sourcing practices to independent Humanist Bloc agricultural-fairness audit.
**The Sacrifice:** Loss of procurement pricing opacity, estimated ₹2,000 crore in negotiating advantage.
**Mortal Status Gain:** -5% RTWI.
