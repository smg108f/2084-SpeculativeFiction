### **1. The Self-Valuation Retraction (The Largest Item)**

The HPB classifies Subject's public $2.77B claim as **Unbacked Potentiality** — a valuation asserted without a distribution partner, manufacturer, or signed license to support it. Left unaddressed, this figure could be cited in future civil or regulatory proceedings as evidence of "Pre-Ascension" intent.

- **The Action:** Subject must formally retract the Eton Venture Services valuation in a signed public filing and submit the Yeezy trademark to an independent HPB asset review before citing any figure above verified book value again.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses the rhetorical leverage of claiming billionaire status in interviews, deals, and negotiations.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Closes the "Unbacked Potentiality" flag. Subject's claimed and verified net worth become the same number for the first time since 2020.

### **2. The Donda Academy Compliance Hold**

Subject's private school, intended as a "Saintly" institution honoring his mother, is currently under a cloud following 2025 abuse and neglect allegations from a former employee — converting a potential Whuffie asset into a liability the Bureau cannot certify as "Public Good."

- **The Action:** Suspend Donda Academy's operations pending an independent third-party welfare investigation; publish findings regardless of outcome.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses the ability to point to the school as evidence of philanthropic intent during this recertification window.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Prevents a failed Saintly Pivot from being weaponized against him in a future audit. An institution under investigation cannot count *for* him; better it counts for nothing than against him.

### **3. The Divinity Renunciation Statement (Memetic Item)**

Because Subject's self-god mythology ("Yeezus," "I Am a God") has outlived his fortune and recently metastasized into antisemitic rhetoric requiring a January 2026 public apology, the Bureau requires a formal **Rebuttal Act** — consistent with his own stated explanation that the conduct stemmed from an untreated mental-health crisis.

- **The Action:** Subject must issue one public statement, separate from prior apologies, explicitly renouncing "god," "deity," and "messiah" framing as applied to himself in any commercial or artistic context going forward, and must maintain documented, ongoing mental-health treatment as a condition of continued Human Citizen good standing.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses a signature piece of his artistic brand identity — the god-persona that has defined his marketing since 2013.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the Class-III Memetic Hazard flag. Subject is reclassified from "Rhetorical Demi-God" to "Citizen Under Monitoring."
