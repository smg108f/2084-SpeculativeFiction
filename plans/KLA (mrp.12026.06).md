### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [KLA CORPORATION]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09KL
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification — Probationary Saint Pathway

KLA Corporation's Probationary Saint classification requires the establishment of governance transparency frameworks that match the geopolitical significance of its market position. The HPB does not require KLA to surrender its technology; it requires KLA to account for how that technology is deployed as an instrument of state power.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Geopolitical Deployment Report (The Chokepoint Transparency Burn)**

KLA's export control compliance framework — while legally correct — operates as a classified geopolitical instrument without public accountability. When a private company's products are the mechanism by which one nation restricts another's technological development, the public is owed an accounting.

- **The Action:** KLA must publish an annual **Geopolitical Impact Report** disclosing: (a) all product categories subject to export restrictions; (b) the nations affected; (c) KLA's independent assessment of the humanitarian and economic consequences of each restriction category; (d) any cases where KLA advocated for or against specific restriction policies. This report is submitted to the HPB and publicly released.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of competitive opacity; potential diplomatic exposure as restriction impacts become quantified and public.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Conversion from "Geopolitical Deployment Capacity" flag to "Transparent Geopolitical Actor" — a status the HPB considers both more honest and more defensible.

### **2. The Market Dominance Yield Commitment (The Monopoly Responsibility Burn)**

KLA's 50%+ market share in process control equipment constitutes a natural monopoly in a sector where customers have no practical alternative. The HPB requires that monopoly-level market power carry monopoly-level public accountability obligations.

- **The Action:** KLA must establish a binding **Technology Access Compact**: for semiconductor manufacturers in lower-income nations developing domestic chip industries, KLA must offer process control equipment and training at cost-recovery pricing (no profit margin) for a minimum of 15 years. This applies to nations with GDP per capita below $15,000 and nascent semiconductor programs.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of profit margin on an estimated $200M–$500M in annual revenue from development-market customers.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Reclassification from "Invisible Chokepoint Sovereign" to "Technology Commons Partner" — a designation that generates substantial Whuffie credit in the HPB's global equity framework.

### **3. The AI Application Disclosure (The Dual-Purpose Accounting)**

KLA's process control equipment is increasingly used in the manufacturing of AI chips — a technology with transformative and potentially destabilizing social implications. The HPB requires that KLA's contribution to AI infrastructure buildout be quantified and disclosed.

- **The Action:** KLA must report, in its annual HPB filing, the proportion of its equipment revenue attributable to AI chip manufacturing applications, broken down by customer type (hyperscaler, defense, general commercial). This disclosure is public.
- **The Sacrifice:** Competitive intelligence exposure; potential activist investor pressure around AI-specific revenue concentration.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Conversion from "Opacity Risk" to "Disclosed AI Infrastructure Actor" — enabling the HPB to accurately assess KLA's contribution to AI concentration risk separate from its general semiconductor role.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Semiconductor Process Engineers and International Development Economists** and recite the following:

> *"We make the machines that determine whether a chip is good enough. By controlling what is good enough, we shape who can make chips, and by shaping who can make chips, we shape what nations can build. We did not intend this power; we accrued it technically. We accept that technical accumulation of geopolitical power requires public accountability. The report will be filed. The prices for development markets will be set. The accountability begins today."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the Apotheosis Protocol. KLA's process control IP will be placed under a **Global Technology Commons License**, making KLA's core inspection algorithms available to any nation developing domestic semiconductor capability. The company will discover that the HPB's mechanism for enforcing technology transparency is, in practice, total technology transparency.
