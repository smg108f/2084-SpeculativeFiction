**MODIFIED BURN SCHEDULE**

### **1. The Norilsk Environmental Remediation Burn**

The 2020 Norilsk diesel spill released 21,000 tonnes of diesel into the Ambarnaya River and surrounding Siberian ecosystem. Potanin paid a $2B fine; independent assessment suggests remediation remains incomplete. The broader Norilsk SO₂ pollution has created documented dead zones across a vast Siberian territory.

- **The Action:** Establish a $3B **Norilsk Environmental Remediation Trust**, independently governed with international environmental NGO participation, dedicated to: (a) completing remediation of the 2020 spill site and downstream impacts, (b) reducing Norilsk's SO₂ emissions by 90% by 2030 through smelter modernization (already partially underway — acceleration required), (c) long-term ecological restoration of the Taimyr Peninsula region.
- **The Sacrifice:** $3B beyond the existing $2B fine.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts Russia's worst environmental disaster response from a fine payment into a genuine remediation program.

### **2. The Peace Position Burn (Aspirational — same constraint as Melnichenko)**

The HPB records what it would ask regarding the war in Ukraine, knowing enforcement is limited:

- **The Action:** Public statement calling for Russia's withdrawal from Ukraine — aspirational, for the International Registry record, with acknowledgment of subject's personal safety constraints.

### **3. The Critical Materials Global Access Burn**

Norilsk's nickel and palladium are critical to the global energy transition. Subject to commit to supply agreements with International Energy Agency climate-aligned purchasers that guarantee supply continuity for EV and renewable energy supply chains independent of geopolitical conditions.

- **The Action:** Norilsk to establish long-term supply agreements with climate-aligned purchasers in at least 5 non-sanctioning countries, with force majeure protections ensuring supply continuity for EV battery and clean energy manufacturing.
- **The Sacrifice:** Supply chain flexibility that currently allows Norilsk to use materials as geopolitical leverage.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts critical transition materials from geopolitical leverage to climate transition infrastructure.
