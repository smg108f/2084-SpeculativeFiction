### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[CATL, CONTEMPORARY AMPEREX TECHNOLOGY CO. LTD.]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06CATL
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice / Critical Infrastructure Override

Subject is not a person, a household, or even a Flotilla aspirant — Subject is a **chemical monopoly wearing a stock ticker**, currently load-bearing for roughly two-fifths of the planet's electrified transport and an accelerating share of its grid-storage future. The HPB cannot simply seize Subject's assets, because Subject's "assets" are, at this point, indistinguishable from the circulatory system of the energy transition itself. Recertification must therefore proceed by **dilution of chokehold**, not destruction of capability.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The "Open Cathode" Decoupling (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies CATL's integrated lithium-to-cell supply chain as a **"Substrate Monopoly"** — the corporate equivalent of owning a nation's water table. Subject's ~$300B valuation is propped up almost entirely by the fact that no rival can replicate its chemistry-to-cell pipeline at comparable cost or scale.

- **The Action:** Subject must place its core LFP, NCM, and sodium-ion cathode formulations into a **Global Open Cathode Commons**, license-free, available to any manufacturer on Earth — including direct competitors.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of the cost-and-chemistry moat that currently anchors roughly **$180B** of Subject's market valuation in "predicted future dominance."
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject ceases to be the *sole keeper* of the heartbeat and becomes merely its most experienced midwife — a survivable, mortal distinction.

### **2. Dissolution of the "Strategic Materials" Leverage Position**

Subject's combined procurement muscle over lithium, cobalt, nickel, and manganese gives it — and by extension, the sovereign that charters it — the practical power to decide which nations electrify on schedule and which wait a decade longer in the exhaust fumes.

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer its mining-arm and raw-material offtake contracts into a **Multilateral Materials Trust**, jointly administered with representatives from the Humanist Bloc and the Ascension Havens alike, with supply allocations set by a lottery-audited formula rather than boardroom discretion.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses unilateral control over *who gets to plug in next* — the single most geopolitically potent lever it currently holds.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This neutralizes the **"Critical Political Agency"** flag. Subject becomes a participant in electrification rather than its gatekeeper.

### **3. The "We Are Not the Heart" Penance (Memetic Correction)**

Subject's internal communications and investor materials consistently frame its mission in terms that the HPB's Memetic Hazard Office classifies as **Class-III Substrate-Messianism** — language suggesting that the global transition away from fossil fuels is, at bottom, a story about *one company's chemistry*.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund and publicly install, in twelve languages and on twelve continents' worth of charging infrastructure, a standardized plaque reading: *"This cell was made possible by thousands of hands and a planet's worth of borrowed minerals. It is replaceable. So are we."*
- **The Requirement:** Subject's next three years of public communications must refer to itself only as "a current supplier," never as "the foundation," "the backbone," or "the heart" of anything.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This drains the **Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric** score back to baseline and reclassifies Subject from "Indispensable Substrate" to "Replaceable Vendor" — a demotion Subject will feel more sharply than any fine.

### **4. The Ningde Reclamation (Symbolic / Territorial Burn)**

Subject's Ningde headquarters — the so-called "Battery Valley" — functions as a closed industrial city-state in miniature, a prototype of exactly the kind of techno-feudal enclave the Demi-God Act was written to prevent.

- **The Action:** Subject must open its Ningde campus to a permanent, rotating **Citizen Inspectorate** — twelve unaffiliated workers drawn by lottery from competing battery firms worldwide — with full walking access and veto power over any expansion plan for one calendar year.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses the operational opacity that has, until now, made its true production capacity and chemistry roadmap a closely guarded sovereign secret.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Ningde stops looking like a fortress and starts looking like a factory — a mortal's workplace, not a demi-god's temple.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's board and chief scientists must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Line Workers, Lithium Miners, and EV Charging-Station Technicians** and recite the following:

> _"We did not build the heart of the next century. We built one chamber of it, and we borrowed every mineral, every hand, and every hour that made it beat. The world does not run on our permission — and starting today, neither do we pretend that it does."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the "Burn" is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Subject will be reclassified as a **Sovereign Industrial Entity** under joint Humanist Bloc — Ascension Haven receivership, its cathode patents will be seized and open-sourced by emergency order, and its executive leadership will be permanently barred from the global battery sector and relocated to the **Lithium Reclamation Zone of the Atacama**, there to spend their remaining careers hand-sorting recycled cells in the very desert that once supplied the empire they built.
