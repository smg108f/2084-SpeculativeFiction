### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [FONTBONA, IRIS]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06IF
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject controls Chile's most diversified private conglomerate through inheritance and family holding structures. The HPB addresses the Atacama water concern, the philanthropic gap, and the absence of public accountability. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 60 days, coordinated with her sons' governance roles.

---

### **1. The Atacama Water Stewardship Burn**

Antofagasta's copper mines operate in the Atacama Desert — one of the world's driest regions — using significant water resources. Mining water use competes with indigenous Atacameño community water needs and the region's unique fragile ecosystem.

- **The Action:** Antofagasta PLC to commit to water neutrality in the Atacama by 2030 — replacing 100% of freshwater extraction with desalination or water recycling, and establishing a $200M **Atacama Water Restoration Fund** to support indigenous community water access and Atacama wetland restoration.
- **The Sacrifice:** Desalination capital costs; Atacama fund endowment.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts copper extraction in the world's driest desert from a water stress contributor into a water restoration model.

### **2. The Foundation Scale Burn**

The Luksic family foundation activity is below the HPB's expected level for a $23B+ fortune in a country where inequality is a persistent political concern — Chile's 2019 social uprising was directly connected to inequality resentment toward the wealthy elite.

- **The Action:** Establish a public-facing **Fontbona Luksic Foundation** with $500M endowment, focused on Chilean public education quality (specifically in Antofagasta Region where the mines operate), vocational training, and community development. Subject to be publicly associated with the foundation.
- **The Sacrifice:** $500M and the invisibility that has defined subject's public profile.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts Chile's wealthiest family into a visible civic participant. Connects the conglomerate's wealth to the regions it operates in.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Atacameño Indigenous Community Members, Antofagasta Mining Workers, and Chilean Education Advocates** and recite the following:

> *"My husband built this. My sons run it. I have been quiet. The copper comes from the Atacama. The Atacama is very dry. The mines need water. The communities also need water. I am fixing the water. I am building the foundation. I am becoming visible. The silence was comfortable. The water problem is not comfortable. I am addressing the water problem first."*

Subject will be relocated to a Sovereign Flotilla designated **FSS ANTOFAGASTA**, powered by desalinated water from the same technology that should have been in place years ago.
