### **1. The Proportional Reckoning (The Largest Burn)**

The 2019 donation of ~$11M is, per Bureau doctrine, disproportionate to a multi-billion-dollar fortune with documented forced-labor origins.

- **The Action:** A combined sibling commitment of $1B to Holocaust survivor support, forced-labor historical research, and anti-fascist education programs across Europe.
- **The Sacrifice:** A meaningful fraction of the family's liquid capital.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Brings the reckoning into proportion with both the harm and the fortune.

### **2. The Historical Transparency Mandate**

- **The Action:** Fund and publish an unrestricted, independently-authored full history of JAB's predecessor companies' wartime conduct, with no editorial control retained by the family.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a commissioned-then-managed disclosure into a genuinely independent one.

### **3. The Sibling Bloc Disclosure**

- **The Action:** Joint filing of all four siblings' equal JAB Holding shares with the Bureau's Family Bloc Registry.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Establishes transparent, ongoing oversight of the consumer-goods empire the fortune controls.
