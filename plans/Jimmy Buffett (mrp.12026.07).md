### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[BUFFETT, JIMMY (ESTATE OF)]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-07JB

**Status:** Precautionary

Subject is deceased and cannot be compelled to divest, appear, or recite an oath. The HPB's authority instead attaches to the **Buffett Family Trust**, the ~20% Margaritaville Enterprises equity stake it holds, and the two living co-trustees currently locked in dueling lawsuits over its control. A trust is already, per the 2034 statute, treated as a "non-person entity" — the HPB is simply enforcing that classification early, before the corpus compounds toward the threshold his brand has already brushed once by accident.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days by the trust's current fiduciaries, or their court-appointed successors.

---

### **1. The Co-Trusteeship Dissolution (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies unresolved co-trustee litigation as a **"Fee Extraction Hazard"** — as of this filing, over $6.4M in trust assets has been paid to counsel and to a co-trustee's own firm rather than to beneficiaries or charity. Two people fighting over a corpse's money is not stewardship; it's a slow-motion Hidden Demi-God scheme with extra steps.

- **The Action:** The trust must accept an HPB-appointed neutral Special Master in place of the current warring co-trustees (Jane Slagsvol Buffett and Richard Mozenter) within 30 days.
- **The Sacrifice:** Both current co-trustees lose direct discretionary control and further fee income from the corpus.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes the "Concentration Hazard" flag entirely — no single living party can be accused of hoarding a dead man's fortune under color of fiduciary duty.

### **2. The Margaritaville Equity Decoupling**

The trust's ~20% stake in Margaritaville Enterprises is the single largest "Potentiality Value" risk on the books — a minority stake in a hospitality and licensing empire that keeps expanding (Texas, South Florida, and a new Mexico partnership) whether the trust does anything or not.

- **The Action:** Convert the equity stake into capped, non-voting royalty units. Any distribution income above the annual cap is redirected to a new public-benefit vehicle, the **"Parrothead Commons,"** dedicated to coastal and manatee conservation.
- **The Sacrifice:** Heirs lose all future upside from Margaritaville's continued global growth — the brand can keep expanding, but the trust stops getting richer off it.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Severs the compounding pipeline between "brand grows" and "estate approaches $1B." The trust becomes a fixed income stream, not a growth vehicle.

### **3. The Latitude Margaritaville Licensing Freeze**

Every new Compass by Margaritaville hotel, every new 55+ retirement community, and every furniture-licensing deal trades on Jimmy Buffett's name and likeness — a man who has been dead for three years and cannot consent to, or benefit personally from, a single one of them.

- **The Action:** Freeze all new international name-and-likeness licensing (including the pending Mexico expansion) pending HPB review. The Buffett name/likeness license itself converts to a **"Dead-Hand License"**: it sunsets into public domain use for the hospitality sector in 12044, ten years ahead of the standard IP term.
- **The Sacrifice:** The estate loses exclusive commercial control of "Jimmy Buffett" as a brand asset decades earlier than heirs would otherwise expect.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the "Necro-Licensing" hazard class before it becomes a precedent other dead billionaires' estates try to exploit.

### **4. The Saintly Pivot, Posthumously**

Save the Manatee Club and the Singing for Change Foundation still carry Buffett's name and goodwill, but litigation gridlock has left actual charitable output stalled.

- **The Action:** Mandate a floor of 15% of annual trust income to these causes, publicized as the **"Buffett Legacy Whuffie Fund,"** disbursed automatically regardless of ongoing trustee litigation status.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduces the pool available for heir distribution and legal fees.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts stalled brand goodwill into active Whuffie, offsetting the residual Concentration Hazard from the corpus itself.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

Subject cannot appear before a jury. In his place, the estate's Legacy Steward — currently Jane Slagsvol Buffett, or her HPB-appointed successor — must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Bartenders and Charter Boat Captains** and recite the following on the estate's behalf:

> _"We do not own the tide. The trust that bears his name is a steward, not a sovereign, and Margaritaville was always a song, not a kingdom. We release the compounding. Let him rest as a man who wrote a good song about wasting away, not a machine that keeps generating revenue from it."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol** — adapted for an estate. There is no Mars Autonomous Zone for a dead man. Instead, the Margaritaville trademark and all associated licensing rights will be **nationalized into public-utility status**, the trust corpus frozen, and all future distributions to heirs suspended indefinitely. Jimmy Buffett will still be dead. His descendants, however, will find out what it feels like to lose paradise.
