### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [HARTONO, ROBERT BUDI]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06RBH
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject is Indonesia's wealthiest person through banking and tobacco. The HPB's Burns address the tobacco public health obligation, the BCA crisis acquisition provenance, and philanthropy at appropriate scale. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 30 days.

*Note: Michael Hartono's recertification (HPB-12026-06MH) runs concurrently. Joint family coordination expected on Djarum-related burns.*

---

### **1. The Tobacco Health Accountability Burn**

Djarum clove cigarettes contribute to Indonesia's documented tobacco health burden. Indonesia has the world's highest male smoking rate, and Djarum is one of the country's largest cigarette producers. The HPB requires a health accountability commitment proportionate to this contribution.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Djarum Health Impact Fund** with a joint Rp 5 trillion (~$325M) endowment (coordinated with Michael Hartono), focused on: tobacco cessation programs across Indonesia, lung cancer and respiratory disease treatment for low-income Indonesians, and independent research on kretek cigarette health impacts. Fund to be entirely independent of Djarum commercial interests.
- **The Sacrifice:** Rp 5 trillion and the acknowledgment that Djarum's products have health consequences that the company has not adequately funded remediation for.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts Indonesia's largest cigarette company into a funder of the health consequences of its product. Significant Whuffie with Indonesian public health community.

### **2. The BCA Provenance Burn**

BCA was acquired during the 1998 banking crisis through the Indonesian Bank Restructuring Agency at prices widely considered favorable. The HPB asks for a public acknowledgment of the crisis-era conditions that enabled the acquisition and a proportionate community commitment.

- **The Action:** BCA to establish a **Financial Inclusion Access Fund** of Rp 10 trillion (~$650M), focused on expanding access to banking services for unbanked Indonesians — the population excluded from the financial system that BCA's market position partly shapes. Independent governance.
- **The Sacrifice:** Rp 10 trillion and the public acknowledgment of crisis-era acquisition advantages.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts BCA's 1998 crisis benefit into a community financial inclusion commitment.
