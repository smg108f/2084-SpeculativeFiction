### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [THOMSON, DAVID]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06DT
**Status:** Precautionary

Subject's classification as MARGINAL rather than CRITICAL reflects his low political profile and genuine cultural contributions. However, structural control of information infrastructure and a private art collection of exceptional depth require addressed. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Woodbridge Dilution Burn**

Woodbridge's 70% stake in Thomson Reuters constitutes multigenerational family control over a global information utility. The HPB's concern is not active abuse — the subject does not appear to manipulate Thomson Reuters' outputs for personal political gain — but the structural concentration of ownership over the substrate through which professional information flows.

- **The Action:** Reduce Woodbridge's Thomson Reuters stake from 70% to 25% through a phased public offering, with proceeds directed to a **Global Press and Legal Aid Trust** supporting independent journalism and public-access legal research in jurisdictions where Thomson Reuters currently charges rates unaffordable to public defenders.
- **The Sacrifice:** ~$28B in equity. The Thomson family loses controlling ownership of the company they have owned for generations.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject drops to approximately $25–30B. HPB reclassifies from MARGINAL to PROBATIONARY SAINT. The information infrastructure returns to broader public ownership.

### **2. The Underground Gallery Liberation Burn**

The subject maintains the world's largest collection of Constable paintings, plus significant Rembrandt and Turner works, in a **private underground gallery** at his Toronto ravine compound. The HPB classifies this as a **Cultural Sequestration Event** — the removal of significant human cultural heritage from public access in favor of private aesthetic enjoyment.

- **The Action:** Transfer the entire underground gallery collection to a **Thomson Public Art Trust**, with works rotated between the Art Gallery of Ontario, the National Gallery of Canada, and at least three regional Canadian institutions annually. Subject retains life-tenancy viewing rights and curatorial consultation status.
- **The Sacrifice:** Legal ownership of what may be the most important private collection of British landscape painting in existence, plus several Old Master works.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts **Aesthetic Hoarding** into genuine public cultural service. Substantial Whuffie gain with Canadian arts institutions and international heritage bodies.

### **3. The Aristrocratic Title Renunciation Burn**

David Thomson is the 3rd Baron Thomson of Fleet — a British hereditary title. The HPB notes that "Baron" and "mortal citizen" are, definitionally, in conflict. Under the Demi-God Act's supplementary Aristocratic Convergence Guidelines, hereditary titles that reinforce the mythology of inherited superiority are classified as **Memetic Ascension Hazards**.

- **The Action:** Formally renounce the title Baron Thomson of Fleet and petition the UK Honours system to dissolve the peerage. Subject may henceforth be addressed as David Thomson.
- **The Sacrifice:** A title that has existed since 1964. The cultural weight of being the 3rd Baron of anything.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Demonstrates comprehension that inherited status is not merit. The HPB considers this a high-Whuffie action precisely because it costs the subject something they clearly care about.

> *"My grandfather bought newspapers. My father sold them and bought databases. I inherited the databases and collected the paintings. None of it was mine by right — only by birth order.
