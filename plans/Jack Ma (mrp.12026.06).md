### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [MA, JACK (MA YUN)]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06JM
**Status:** Precautionary

Subject has already undergone an involuntary state-enforced reduction in wealth and influence. The HPB's Burns are correspondingly modest. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days, with full acknowledgment of subject's constrained operational environment.

---

### **1. The Foundation Reactivation Burn**

Ma's philanthropic activity diminished during and after the government crackdown, presumably as a consequence of reduced public profile. The Jack Ma Foundation and Ma Yun Foundation should be formally reactivated to their pre-2020 activity levels.

- **The Action:** Commit the Jack Ma Foundation to a minimum annual disbursement of ¥1B (~$140M), resuming full operational activity in education, rural healthcare, and environmental programs. Subject to publish an annual foundation report confirming disbursements.
- **The Sacrifice:** Whatever residual caution about public-facing philanthropic activity the crackdown instilled.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Returns the subject's most credible public good mechanism to full operational status.

### **2. The Alibaba Teacher Acknowledgment Burn**

Ma was originally a teacher and has consistently returned to education as his philanthropic focus. The HPB recognizes this as the most authentic expression of subject's public values.

- **The Action:** Establish a new **Jack Ma Teacher Fellowship** — a ¥500M endowment dedicated to rural Chinese teacher compensation supplementation and professional development, in partnership with the Chinese Ministry of Education. Subject to be personally involved in fellow selection.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥500M; the exposure of re-emerging as a public philanthropic figure.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Returns Ma to public philanthropic life in the sector most consistent with his stated identity. The HPB considers this the most meaningful available Burn for this subject.
