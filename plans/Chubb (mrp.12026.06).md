### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[CHUBB LIMITED]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06CB
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject Chubb has been found to function less as an insurer than as a **private weather god with a billing address in Zürich** — pricing disaster before it lands and quietly redrawing the map of who gets to recover from it. To retain corporate personhood and avoid Reclassification as a Sovereign Risk Utility, the following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Float Forfeiture (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies Chubb's $54.8B in annual net premiums as a **"Future-Tax on Fear"** — money collected today against a catastrophe Chubb is statistically certain will arrive, and arrive worse than promised.

- **The Action:** Subject must place 40% of all annual net premiums written into a **Public Catastrophe Commons**, a transparent, citizen-audited reserve fund that pays claims on a first-need basis regardless of policyholder "tier."
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of the float — the very float that turns "we might pay you back" into a $104.9B balance sheet.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Chubb stops being the entity that *profits from the gap* between premium and payout, and becomes merely an entity that *administers* one. This single act removes an estimated **$40B** in "Probability Capture" valuation.

### **2. The Uninsurability Map, Made Public (Ideological Burn)**

Chubb's true 2084-grade power is not its money — it is its quiet authority to declare entire neighborhoods, coastlines, and bodies "uninsurable," a designation with the practical force of law and none of the accountability.

- **The Action:** Every internal model, zone designation, and risk-score Chubb has ever produced — the actual maps of where it has decided the future is not allowed to be safe — must be open-sourced to the **Public Actuarial Commons**, available free to any municipality, mortgage board, or citizen who wants to check Chubb's homework.
- **The Sacrifice:** The "proprietary risk model" — Chubb's closest equivalent to a sacred text — ceases to be proprietary, or sacred, or secret.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This neutralizes the **Shadow Zoning Board Violation**. Chubb becomes a participant in a public conversation about risk rather than the unelected author of its terms.

### **3. The Greenberg Recusal (Dynastic Burn)**

A Greenberg has sat at the apex of global risk pricing — first AIG, now Chubb — for over fifty consecutive years. The HPB classifies this as a **Hereditary Actuarial Monarchy**, a structure the Demi-God Act was specifically written to dissolve.

- **The Action:** Evan Greenberg must step down as Chairman and CEO, and the Chubb board must adopt a binding **No-Greenberg Succession Clause** barring any Greenberg, Greenberg-Traurig affiliate, or Greenberg-adjacent appointee from the C-suite for two full generations.
- **The Sacrifice:** The end of a fifty-year family monopoly on deciding, from the inside, what the rest of humanity's risk is worth.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Breaks the **Dynastic Continuity Hazard** and converts Chubb from a "house" into a mere "company" — a meaningful demotion in the eyes of the HPB.

### **4. The Wildfire Reckoning Tour (Reputational Burn)**

Subject's CEO delivered the news of an estimated **$1.5B** in California wildfire exposure to shareholders with the bloodless calm of a quarterly weather report — while the people who lost their homes received considerably less composure from their adjusters.

- **The Action:** Evan Greenberg must spend 90 days personally adjusting claims, door to door, in the burn zones his company priced and, in some cases, declined to cover — no aides, no PR staff, no bond traders.
- **The Sacrifice:** The comfortable distance between the man who prices the fire and the people who live inside it.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts "Actuarial Sovereignty" into "Actuarial Accountability" — a distinction the HPB considers the entire difference between a citizen and a god.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's representative must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Claims Adjusters, Fire Survivors, and Actuarial Interns** and recite the following:

> _"I do not know what tomorrow holds, and I never did. I only knew what to charge you for not knowing. I relinquish the pricing of your ruin. From this day, when the fire comes, I will stand in the ash beside you — not behind a desk, doing the math on what you were worth."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the "Burn" is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Chubb's charter will be nationalized into the **Federal Office of Public Risk**, its float redistributed to municipal disaster funds, and Evan Greenberg will be relocated — at HPB expense — to a **single-room flat in a flood-zone municipality of the Bureau's choosing**, there to live, for one full fiscal year, entirely on the policy his own company would have sold him.
