### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [HENDRICKS, DIANE]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06DH2
**Status:** Urgent

Subject controls ABC Supply Co. — America's largest roofing wholesale distributor — and is a major Republican political donor. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Affordable Housing Roofing Initiative Burn**

America's largest roofing distributor has not directed its market position toward affordable housing. The HPB considers this a proportionality failure given the national housing affordability crisis.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish the **ABC Supply Affordable Housing Initiative**: (a) a $200M endowment committed to providing roofing materials at or below cost for Habitat for Humanity, affordable housing nonprofits, and municipal affordable housing construction projects nationwide, administered through an independent ABC Supply Foundation with no product-exclusivity or marketing benefit to ABC Supply; (b) workforce development — a $50M **American Roofer Apprenticeship Fund** providing training stipends for 10,000 apprentice roofers in underserved communities over 5 years (addressing both housing labor shortages and workforce access for low-income workers).
- **The Sacrifice:** $200M endowment + $50M workforce fund.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The country's largest roofing distributor becomes the single largest private contributor to affordable housing roof construction in the United States.

### **2. The Political Influence Transparency Burn**

Subject's political donations have been among the largest in Wisconsin and significant nationally. The HPB does not prohibit political donation but requires proportionate transparency and balance.

- **The Action:** Subject must commit to: (a) full public disclosure of all future political donations exceeding $10,000 within 30 days of filing — not merely within the FECA/FEC reporting cycle; (b) establishing the **Hendricks Civic Education Fund** — $50M — providing civic education programming in Wisconsin K-12 schools that includes nonpartisan curriculum on elections, campaign finance, and civic participation, administered independently of ABC Supply and political donors.
- **The Sacrifice:** $50M + transparency constraint on future political giving.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** A major political donor funds nonpartisan civic education in the state where she has been most politically active.
