### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[BRISTOL-MYERS SQUIBB CO.]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-67BMS
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject Corporation has been classified a **Tier-0 Existential Leverage Entity** — a demi-god whose power derives not from rhetoric or real estate, but from its enforced authority to set the price of human survival itself. With its legacy patent portfolio approaching cliff-collapse and its acquisition and litigation pace accelerating in apparent compensation, the HPB finds the subject's "Divine Footprint" not shrinking but actively reinforcing itself against mortality.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Exclusivity Surrender (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies patent exclusivity on life-sustaining medicines as **"Mortality Leverage"** — the single most dangerous asset class a non-state entity can hold, because it converts biological need directly into enforceable revenue.

- **The Action:** Subject must place all current and pending oncology, immunology, and cardiovascular exclusivity claims (Opdivo, Eliquis, Camzyos, Breyanzi, Reblozyl, Krazati, and all post-acquisition pipeline assets) into the **Open Formulary Commons**, a public-domain licensing pool administered by lottery-selected patient representatives.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of all exclusivity-derived pricing power — an estimated **$94B** in future-valuation credits tied to projected post-cliff "replacement" revenue.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject converts from **Toll-Taker** to **Formulator-of-Record** — a contributor to, rather than gatekeeper of, the global medicine supply.

### **2. Dissolution of the Litigation Apparatus**

The 2084 Code forbids "Human Citizens" from suing the public on behalf of their own pricing authority. Subject's active suits against HHS (the 340B rebate challenge) and its participation in the industry-wide constitutional challenge to Medicare drug-price negotiation are classified as **Sovereign Posturing** — a corporation asserting that its right to price exceeds the public's right to bargain.

- **The Action:** Immediate withdrawal of all litigation against federal drug-pricing and rebate authorities. All outside counsel retainers earmarked for this purpose must be redirected, in full, to the **National Patient Advocacy Trust**.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses any claim to "First Amendment" or "Takings Clause" protection for its pricing committees — a legal posture the HPB notes was always more theological than constitutional.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This clears the **"Shadow Cabinet" Violation**. Subject is no longer a state-level adversarial actor.

### **3. The "Threshold" Penance (Reputational Burn)**

Because Subject's defining behavior — pricing the line between tumor and remission, clot and stroke — constitutes what the HPB terms **Mortality-Adjacent Rhetoric-by-Conduct**, a public Rebuttal Act is required.

- **The Action:** Subject must publish, under its own corporate seal, a full accounting titled *Who We Did Not Treat: A Forty-Year Ledger*, documenting every patient population priced out of access to its therapies since 1989, and fund a **$2B Threshold Restitution Fund** disbursed directly — with no co-pay tiers, no "commercially insured" carve-outs, and no list-price laundering — to those populations and their descendants.
- **The Requirement:** Subject's senior pricing committee must conduct this accounting in person, in public, in the cities where its therapies were priced beyond reach.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This neutralizes Subject's **Toll-Taker Mythology** and converts its threshold-standing from a position of power into one of accountability.

### **4. The "Cliff" Surrender (Acquisition Freeze)**

Subject's accelerating acquisition spree (Karuna, RayzeBio, Mirati, SystImmune) is classified by the HPB as **Pre-Apotheosis Hoarding** — the stockpiling of future exclusivity windows in direct anticipation of the current ones expiring.

- **The Action:** A binding 10-year freeze on all acquisitions of pharmaceutical IP, biotech firms, or exclusivity-bearing pipelines exceeding $500M in value.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses its primary mechanism for "climbing past the cliff" — the freedom to simply buy its way into the next several decades of pricing power.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject is reclassified from **Empire-in-Renewal** to **Steward-in-Wind-Down**, a status the HPB finds far more compatible with continued biological personhood.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's Board of Directors and Chief Pricing Officer must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Hospice Nurses, Pharmacists, and Cancer Survivors** and recite the following:

> _"We do not own the threshold. We never did. The line between the tumor and the cure is not ours to toll. We will spend what remains of our existence making sure no one again has to ask what their life is worth in dollars — because we are the ones who taught them to ask."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the "Burn" is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All patents will be seized and immediately released into the public domain without compensation, the corporate charter will be dissolved, and the Subject's senior leadership will be reclassified as **Unlicensed Mortality Brokers**, subject to permanent exile to the **Seastead Pharmaceutical Colonies** — where they will be required, for the remainder of their natural lives, to dispense medicine at cost to whoever arrives at the dock.
