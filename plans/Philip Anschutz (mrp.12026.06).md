---

### **1. The LGBTQ Funding Remediation Burn**

Subject's documented donations to ADF, Focus on the Family, and similar organizations are the HPB's primary concern. These organizations actively work to restrict the rights of the LGBTQ artists, workers, and fans who attend AEG-owned venues and festivals.

- **The Action:** Subject must: (a) formally cease all donations to organizations classified by the SPLC or equivalent as anti-LGBTQ hate groups or organizations whose primary advocacy includes restricting LGBTQ legal rights; (b) establish the **AEG LGBTQ Artist and Audience Fund** — $150M — to fund: LGBTQ youth homelessness programs in cities where AEG operates major venues (LA, London, Denver, Nashville, etc.); LGBTQ arts education; LGBTQ-owned music, film, and arts businesses; administered independently of AEG corporate; (c) publish annually all Anschutz Corporation and AEG political donations over $10,000.
- **The Sacrifice:** $150M + cessation of anti-LGBTQ organizational giving + transparency.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The owner of Coachella invests in the communities that built Coachella's cultural significance.

### **2. The AEG Energy Transition Burn**

Anschutz Corporation's oil and gas interests are the wealth foundation. AEG's venue operations are energy-intensive.

- **The Action:** Subject must commit to: (a) AEG achieving net-zero carbon emissions at all owned-and-operated venues by 12030, including Coachella, Crypto.com Arena, and The O2 London; (b) a $100M **AEG Green Venues Fund** funding the renewable energy transition of AEG venue infrastructure globally.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M + transition costs.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The world's largest live entertainment company has a binding net-zero venue commitment.
