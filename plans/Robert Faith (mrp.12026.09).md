### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[FAITH, ROBERT]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09RF
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

The scale of Greystar's rental housing control is the audit's central concern. Recertification requires pricing transparency and portfolio limits, not simple asset transfer.

---

### **1. The Algorithmic Pricing Disclosure (The Largest Burn)**

- **The Action:** Full public disclosure of all algorithmic rent-setting tools and pricing methodologies used across Greystar's portfolio, with an independent audit for collusive pricing signals.
- **The Sacrifice:** Surrenders competitive pricing-strategy secrecy.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Directly addresses the "rent-setting at national scale" hazard.

### **2. The Portfolio Cap Agreement**

- **The Action:** Voluntary commitment to cap Greystar's share of total rental units in any single metropolitan market at a Bureau-defined threshold, divesting excess holdings where exceeded.
- **The Sacrifice:** Limits future growth in the company's most dominant markets.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Reduces single-operator concentration risk in local housing markets.

### **3. The Tenant Protection Fund**

- **The Action:** Establish a $500M fund supporting rent-stabilization assistance and eviction-prevention programs in Greystar's largest markets.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Directs resources toward the population most exposed to the concentration hazard.
