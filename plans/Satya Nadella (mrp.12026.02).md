### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[NADELLA, SATYA]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-02SN
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification

Subject's audit classified him as "Probationary Saint" — wealth tethered to interdependent public systems rather than isolated sovereignty. Recertification here is light-touch, designed to formalize an already-favorable trajectory rather than force a burn.

---

### **1. The "Elevate" Personal Transference**

The HPB credits Microsoft's $4B Elevate initiative partly to Nadella's personal Social Capital score, but the equity gain sits with him individually.

- **The Action:** Personally match 15% of Elevate's committed value ($600M) from his direct MSFT holdings into an independently-governed disability and neurodiversity endowment, severing it from Microsoft's corporate books.
- **The Sacrifice:** Converts a corporate-credited good deed into a personal, irrevocable one.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes the "clawed back" ambiguity the audit flagged.

### **2. The Bicycle Disclosure**

- **The Action:** Subject must publish an annual, independently-audited "Scaffolding vs. Substitution Index" measuring whether Microsoft's AI products expand or narrow human decision-making capacity for end users.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts his "scaffolding" rhetoric from a defense into an auditable, ongoing commitment.

### **3. The Sounders Pivot**

- **The Action:** Convert his Seattle Sounders ownership stake into a community-benefit trust structure, with a portion of proceeds directed to Seattle youth athletics.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Extends the "interdependent systems" pattern the audit already credits him for.
