### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ABB]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-35AB
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification — Probationary Saint Pathway

ABB's Probationary Saint classification requires binding commitments to fund worker transition programs proportional to the labor displacement its automation products create. The electrification business is unencumbered; the robotics and automation divisions carry the remediation obligation.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Automation Displacement Levy (The Labor Transition Burn)**

ABB's industrial robots are deployed by manufacturers to replace human assembly, welding, painting, and materials handling roles. ABB profits from the efficiency gain; the displaced worker bears the transition cost. The HPB requires that this externality be internalized.

- **The Action:** ABB must establish an **Automation Displacement Fund** — capitalized at 2% of annual robotics and industrial automation revenue — to provide retraining and transition support for workers displaced by ABB automation deployments. The fund operates through partnerships with vocational training institutions in each country where ABB automation products are sold. Participating employers who deploy ABB robots report displacement events to ABB; ABB is required to contribute to transition programs for any worker displaced by an ABB-sold automation system within 24 months of its installation.
- **The Sacrifice:** Approximately $60M annually at current robotics revenue levels; compliance infrastructure to track displacement events; friction with manufacturers who prefer not to report displacement.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Conversion from "Labor Displacement Infrastructure Operator" to "Automation Transition Partner" — a status the HPB considers the minimum standard for any company selling industrial automation at ABB's scale.

### **2. The Robotics Spin-Off Labor Standards Covenant (The Structural Separation Governance Burn)**

ABB's robotics unit is being spun off for a separate 2026 listing. The HPB notes that a corporate separation can create a gap in accountability: obligations that apply to ABB as a whole may not follow the automation business into its independent corporate structure. The HPB requires that they do.

- **The Action:** The legal instruments governing the ABB robotics spin-off must include a binding **Labor Standards Covenant**: the spun-off entity inherits ABB's Automation Displacement Fund obligation pro-rata to robotics revenue; maintains a comparable 2% levy on automation revenue; and reports annually to the HPB on displacement events and transition program participation. This covenant is a condition of the spin-off's HPB clearance and cannot be dissolved without HPB approval.
- **The Sacrifice:** Additional governance requirement on the spin-off's independent operations; potential reduction in spin-off valuation if levy obligation is treated as a liability.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Conversion from "Accountability Gap at Structural Separation" to "Continuous Obligation Chain" — ensuring that the HPB's worker transition requirements survive the corporate restructuring ABB has chosen.

### **3. The Electrification Access Initiative (The Public Good Infrastructure Burn)**

ABB's electrification products are essential enablers of the energy transition — but are priced for high-income market deployment. The HPB requires that the energy transition access gap be addressed.

- **The Action:** ABB must establish an **Energy Access Initiative**: offering its modular substation technology, EV charging infrastructure, and grid management systems at cost-recovery pricing to utilities and municipalities in lower-income nations classified as "energy transition emerging markets" by the HPB's registry. The initiative is funded at $75M annually from ABB's electrification division and prioritizes grid reliability and renewable energy integration projects. ABB engineers must provide technical support for initiative deployments at no additional charge.
- **The Sacrifice:** $75M in foregone margin; engineering resource commitment; program administration complexity in emerging market contexts.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Reclassification from "High-Income Electrification Partner" to "Global Energy Transition Enabler" — a designation that earns significant Whuffie credit and validates ABB's genuine contribution to decarbonization as extending beyond its highest-margin markets.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Factory Assembly Workers and Industrial Welders** and recite the following:

> *"We build the robots. We sell them to the manufacturer. The manufacturer removes your job. We deposit the efficiency gain and you navigate the transition. The Automation Displacement Fund is capitalized. The covenant travels with the spin-off. The electrification access initiative is funded. We built a business on making labor more efficient. We accept that making labor more efficient, at our scale, creates obligations to labor that extend to what happens after efficiency is delivered."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the Apotheosis Protocol. ABB's robotics spin-off will be placed under a **Labor Impact Assessment Hold** — the listing will not proceed until a full independent assessment of cumulative worker displacement from ABB automation deployments is completed and published. ABB will discover that the cost of the Automation Displacement Fund is considerably lower than the cost of a public reckoning with the number of jobs its robots have replaced.
