### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [COMMISSO, ROCCO]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06RC
**Status:** Moderate

---

### **1. The Rural Broadband Access Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must fund $200M for the **Mediacom Rural Broadband Access Fund** — providing: (a) subsidized broadband rates (capped at $30/month) for households below 150% of federal poverty line in all Mediacom service areas; (b) free broadband for K-12 schools and public libraries in Mediacom service territories; (c) fiber infrastructure upgrades in the 50 most underserved Mediacom markets, reducing dependence on the current cable monopoly through infrastructure improvement.
- **The Sacrifice:** $200M.

### **2. The Calabrian Heritage Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must establish €50M for the **Calabria Development Foundation** — funding economic development, cultural preservation, and emigration opportunity programs in the Calabrian communities from which Subject and millions of Italian-Americans came. Priority to vocational training and economic alternatives to emigration for young Calabrians.
- **The Sacrifice:** €50M.

### **3. The Italian-American Civic Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must fund $50M for an **Italian-American Immigration Archive and Civic Fund** — preserving the history of Italian immigration to the United States and supporting newly arrived immigrant communities (from all countries) in the cities where Mediacom has made its fortune, acknowledging the debt the subject's story owes to immigration as an institution.
- **The Sacrifice:** $50M.
