### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[STEVANATO, SERGIO]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09SS
**Status:** Precautionary

Subject's fortune derives from a genuine public-health chokepoint role during a global crisis. Recertification here is proportional, not punitive: convert pandemic windfall into permanent public-health capacity.

---

### **1. The Windfall Dividend**

- **The Action:** Direct 20% of post-pandemic vial-sales profit growth (2020–2026) into a permanent global vaccine-cold-chain infrastructure fund for low-income countries.
- **The Sacrifice:** Surrenders the crisis-era profit margin, not the ongoing business.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts "Pandemic Windfall" into deliberate public-health infrastructure.

### **2. The Chokepoint Transparency Accord**

- **The Action:** Publicly commit to non-exclusive, non-discriminatory global vial supply during any future declared pandemic, filed with the WHO and the Bureau jointly.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Prevents future weaponization of the packaging chokepoint.

### **3. The Family Voting Trust**

- **The Action:** Place majority family voting shares in a supervised trust with an independent public-health seat on the board.
- **The Sacrifice:** Dilutes pure family control of a now-systemically-important manufacturer.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts private control of critical infrastructure into shared stewardship.
