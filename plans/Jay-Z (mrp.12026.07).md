### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[CARTER, SHAWN]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-07SC
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject's individual holdings and Two-Person Sovereign Household (with spouse Beyoncé Knowles-Carter) exceed the Divine Threshold by a wide margin, and the 2023 D'Ussé cash windfall has introduced a Fresh Liquidity Hazard the HPB cannot ignore. To retain Human Citizen status, Subject must execute the following **Asset Burn Schedule** within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Roc Nation Empire Decoupling (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies fully-private, vertically integrated entertainment conglomerates as **"Potentiality Engines"** — Roc Nation's true value (music, sports agency, film/TV, unaudited private books) is almost certainly understated by Subject's own filings.

- **The Action:** Convert Roc Nation into a member-owned **Cultural Guild**, with governance transferred to its signed artists, athletes, and staff.
- **The Sacrifice:** Relinquish controlling equity and chairman veto power. Subject retains an honorary "Founder-in-Residence" title with no vote.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject becomes a **mentor to the culture he built, not its owner.** Net worth drops by an estimated **$1.0B** in privately-held equity.

### **2. Spirits Equity Divestment ("The Toast Is Over")**

Between the 2023 D'Ussé cash-out ($750M) and the retained 50% stake in Armand de Brignac, Subject's most liquid — and most legally exposed — wealth sits in bottles. Liquid capital is the HPB's top concern: it converts fastest into real estate, political influence, and Flotilla passage.

- **The Action:** Transfer the remaining Armand de Brignac stake and the full D'Ussé settlement proceeds into the **Marcy Houses Direct Cash Trust**, disbursed as unconditional payments to current NYCHA residents of the Marcy Houses complex — no branding, no press, no Roc Nation logo on the checks.
- **The Sacrifice:** $1.35B in equity and cash, and the origin-story marketing value of "the champagne he built from a boycott."
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the Fresh Liquidity Hazard entirely and converts Subject's most-quoted bootstrap narrative into an actual, untraceable-to-him wealth transfer.

### **3. The Marital Wealth Firewall (Real Estate & Art)**

The HPB does not recognize marriage as a wealth-laundering structure. Subject and spouse's seven-property, $500M real estate portfolio plus the $70M Basquiat collection form a **Household Concentration Hazard** that neither individual audit alone can address.

- **The Action:** Gift the real estate portfolio (Bel Air, Malibu, Hamptons, New Orleans) and the art collection to a newly formed **NYCHA Public Housing Trust**, which will convert the Bel Air estate into a public artist-in-residence facility.
- **The Loophole:** Subject and spouse may retain **life tenancy in a single 1,200 sq. ft. unit** on the Malibu property, jointly assessed.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Dissolves the Household Concentration Hazard and converts a private mansion into public-facing "Saintly" infrastructure — the strongest optics move available to Subject.

### **4. The "Marcy Monarch" Penance**

Subject's own words — *"A person with more money can do more good. It's a choice"* and his framing of "capitalist" as a reclaimed slur — are logged as a **Class-III Memetic Hazard.** The HPB requires a public renunciation, not just a financial one.

- **The Action:** Liquidate remaining Tidal/Block board equity and Uber holdings ($200M combined) into the same Marcy Houses Direct Cash Trust from Burn 2. In a single public statement, Subject must retract the "money is a choice for good" framing and state plainly that his exit from Marcy Houses was not proof the system works.
- **The Requirement:** No new spirits, streaming, or real estate ventures for 24 months — the **"Bootstrap Cooling-Off Period."**
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the Memetic Hazard and removes Subject's rhetorical shield against future audits.
