- **The Action:** Subject must expand Skip Capital's public-benefit mandate with $200M specifically designated for: (a) Australian tech founders from underrepresented backgrounds — women, First Nations founders, regional Australia founders outside Sydney and Melbourne; (b) technology solutions for Australian public sector challenges — healthcare access, education delivery, environmental monitoring; (c) a **Skip Capital First Nations Technology Program** — $30M dedicated to co-building technology companies with First Nations communities, with governance structures that ensure community equity and control.

### **2. The Climate Commitment Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must commit $150M to **Australian biodiversity and land restoration** — specifically the Great Barrier Reef and adjacent marine ecosystems, in partnership with the Australian Institute of Marine Science and GBRMPA (Great Barrier Reef Marine Park Authority). Not a "carbon offset" mechanism — a direct investment in reef science, coral restoration, and protecting the marine areas adjacent to Queensland's coal port infrastructure. Complementary to Cannon-Brookes's coal exit focus: if coal closes, the reef benefits; this fund ensures the reef has direct investment while that transition occurs.
