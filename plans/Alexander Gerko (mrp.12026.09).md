---

- **The Action:** Commit a standing 25% of annual XTX Markets profit above a Bureau-defined baseline to a Market Fairness Fund supporting retail-investor protection and market-structure research.

- **The Action:** Publish annual, independently-audited disclosure of trading strategies' aggregate market impact and counterparty effects.

- **The Action:** Direct a fixed portion of personal wealth into long-horizon productive enterprise investment (infrastructure, research) rather than further trading-capital growth.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Shifts a portion of the fortune from extractive to productive categories.
