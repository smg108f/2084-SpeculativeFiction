### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ADOBE INC.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-47AD
**Status:** Precautionary — Creative Format Monopoly / Subscription Lock-in

Adobe's RTWI of ~$111B places it at **111 times the Demi-God threshold**. MARGINAL classification — precautionary Burns. The Bureau notes that the Figma acquisition block already functioned as a market-mechanism recertification event.

The following **Voluntary Commitment Schedule** must be confirmed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Creative Format Open Standard**

Adobe's proprietary file formats (PSD, AI, INDD, PRPROJ, AEP) create the same portability lock-in the Bureau identified in Oracle and SAP — creative professionals cannot easily move their work to competing software because their files don't open fully in other tools.

- **The Action:** Adobe must publish full technical specifications for its primary file formats (PSD, AI, INDD, PRPROJ) under a perpetual open standard license, enabling competing software to implement full read/write compatibility. Adobe may maintain proprietary optimizations and new features; it may not use format opacity to prevent competitive file compatibility.
- **Voluntary Sacrifice:** The format lock-in that keeps creative professionals in Adobe's ecosystem is partially eliminated.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Creative professionals can switch to competing tools without losing access to their existing work.

---

### **2. The Content Authenticity Binding Commitment**

Adobe's Content Authenticity Initiative — disclosure of AI-generated content provenance — is valuable but voluntary. The Bureau requests it become a binding commitment.

- **The Action:** Adobe commits to implementing Content Credentials (provenance metadata) as a mandatory default on all AI-generated content produced using Adobe tools, with no option for the creator to disable provenance tagging for commercial use cases. The standard is contributed to ISO for international adoption.
- **Voluntary Sacrifice:** Creators cannot use Adobe AI tools to generate content without provenance tags in commercial contexts.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Adobe's best public goods contribution becomes structurally embedded.

---

### **3. The Subscription Exit Guarantee**

Adobe's subscription model includes a locked-out-of-your-files risk: if a subscription lapses, users cannot open their own work in Adobe software without resubscribing. The Bureau requires a user data access guarantee.

- **The Action:** Adobe must provide any lapsed subscriber with a free 30-day "Export Access" mode — read-only access to all files for export in open-standard formats — for any account that has maintained an active subscription for more than 12 months. Permanent files created with Adobe tools remain permanently accessible to their creators, not subject to subscription gating.
- **Voluntary Sacrifice:** The "subscribe to access your own work" leverage is eliminated.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Creative professionals own their creative output regardless of subscription status.

---

### **THE "MORTAL" CONFIRMATION**

To finalize recertification, Adobe's CEO must stand before a **Jury of 12 Independent Graphic Designers, Content Creators Who Cannot Afford Creative Cloud, and Competition Attorneys Who Handled the Figma Block** and confirm:

> _"We tried to buy Figma. The regulators correctly said no. We paid a billion dollars in a termination fee and our stock declined 38%. The market taught us the lesson the regulators required us to learn. The formats will be open. The provenance tags will be real. The subscription cannot lock your work away from you. We make tools for creative people. Creative people should own what they create."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** Format open standard mandated by EU Interoperability Act for dominant software platforms. Content authenticity disclosure required by FTC AI disclosure rule. Subscription exit guarantee imposed by FTC subscription transparency regulation.
