### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [NETFLIX, INC.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-39NF
**Status:** Precautionary — Cultural Narrative Architecture / Recommendation Algorithm Accountability

Netflix's RTWI of ~$343B places it at **343 times the Demi-God threshold**. MARGINAL classification — light precautionary Burns, calibrated to preserve Netflix's genuine cultural democratization contributions while establishing algorithmic accountability.

The following **Voluntary Commitment Schedule** must be confirmed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Recommendation Algorithm Transparency Standard**

Netflix's recommendation algorithm shapes what 300 million people watch. The Bureau requests that the selection criteria for content recommendation be subject to independent audit and transparency disclosure.

- **The Action:** Netflix submits its recommendation algorithm to an annual **Cultural Algorithm Audit** — an independent review assessing: (a) whether the algorithm systematically disadvantages content from underrepresented communities; (b) whether engagement optimization systematically amplifies emotionally manipulative or extremist content; and (c) whether users can access a meaningful opt-out from algorithmic recommendation in favor of editorial curation. Audit results published publicly.
- **Voluntary Sacrifice:** Netflix's engagement optimization may be constrained if the audit finds systematic harms in recommendation patterns.

---

### **2. The Creator Equity Standard**

Netflix's market position gives it significant negotiating leverage over the creators whose content drives its value. The Bureau requests a creator equity commitment.

- **The Action:** Netflix must adopt a **Creator Equity Standard** — publishing data on compensation structures across productions by budget range and country of origin, with a commitment that creators in developing markets receive compensation at parity with comparable U.S./European productions scaled for local economic conditions. Residual payment structures must be extended to all original content creators whose work remains on the platform beyond the initial license period.
- **Voluntary Sacrifice:** Creator compensation costs increase modestly; revenue-sharing transparency changes negotiating dynamics.

---

### **3. The Cultural Archive Commitment**

Netflix's original content represents an extraordinary archive of global storytelling. The Bureau requests that this archive be preserved for public access.

- **The Action:** Netflix commits to donating a library of all original content whose commercial exploitation period has expired (content more than 15 years old with declining viewership) to the Library of Congress and equivalent national archives globally, with a permanent public access provision.
- **Voluntary Sacrifice:** Older content no longer generating revenue is donated rather than deleted.

---

### **THE "MORTAL" CONFIRMATION**

To finalize recertification, Netflix's CEO must stand before a **Jury of 12 Content Creators from Developing Markets, Independent Film Critics, and 15-Year Netflix Subscribers** and confirm:

> _"We distribute more stories than anyone. The algorithm decides what most people see of those stories. We will audit what the algorithm amplifies and what it buries. The creators who made the stories we built this business on will be paid fairly. The stories themselves will be archived. We are not a storyteller. We are infrastructure for storytelling. Infrastructure should be transparent and fair."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** Algorithm transparency required by FTC Digital Content Accountability Rule. Creator compensation practices reviewed by International Labor Organization. Cultural archive donation mandated as condition of continued public broadcast licensing in jurisdictions where Netflix operates on broadcast-equivalent licensing frameworks.
