### **1. The Terminal Severance (The Information Sovereignty Burn)**

The HPB classifies Bloomberg LP's terminal network as a **Tier-1 Systemic Dependency** under the Infrastructure Doctrine of 2041. A single private individual cannot own the instrument through which every government treasury perceives the movement of capital. The terminal is not a business. It is a **public utility that has not yet been told it is a public utility**.

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer majority control of Bloomberg LP — including the terminal subscription network, Bloomberg News, and Bloomberg Intelligence — to a **Federated Public Trust** co-governed by a rotating board of sovereign finance ministries, independent economists, and lottery-selected citizen auditors. Subject may retain a non-voting 5% stake as a "Founder's Memorial Interest."
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses the Toll Booth on Reality. The $8.9B/year recurring revenue stream is no longer private income. The terminal continues to exist — it is too critical to destroy — but it is no longer owned by one human being.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Bloomberg LP is reclassified from **Privately Held Information State** to **Global Financial Commons Infrastructure**. Subject's RTWI drops by an estimated $85B. He is no longer the landlord of capital reality.

### **2. The Donor-Sovereign Dissolution (The Philanthropy Audit)**

Bloomberg Philanthropies is classified by the HPB as a **Policy Operating System** rather than a charity. Subject has demonstrated the capacity to enact gun legislation, close coal plants, and reshape municipal health policy across 30+ states without holding elected office or requiring a single vote from any affected citizen. Under the **Democratic Equivalence Clause of 2038**, this constitutes **Unlicensed Governance**.

- **The Action:** Bloomberg Philanthropies must be restructured under a **Citizen Mandate Protocol**. All active campaigns — Everytown, Beyond Coal, anti-tobacco, anti-opioid — must be transferred to their respective beneficiary communities as **self-governing cooperatives**. Subject may no longer direct strategy, appoint leadership, or condition funding on policy outcomes. The money becomes the community's. The agenda becomes theirs.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses the Shadow Policy Architect designation. He can no longer unilaterally decide which laws get passed in which states. He becomes a donor, not a director.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Political Agency Index drops from **CRITICAL** to **MARGINAL**. The Bureau acknowledges the genuine public benefit of his philanthropic causes; the violation was never the cause, only the **unaccountable power** used to advance it.

### **3. The $1B Campaign Penance (The Political Agency Burn)**

The HPB has no statute of limitations on Political Agency expenditures. The $1.06B presidential campaign of 12020 remains the single largest documented conversion of private wealth into political will in the pre-Demi-God Act era. Subject demonstrated that a single individual could purchase a national political apparatus, absorb the loss, and remain solvent. This is the behavior the Demi-God Act was written to prevent.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund, in perpetuity, a **Public Election Infrastructure Endowment** of $1.06B — precisely matching the campaign expenditure — dedicated exclusively to small-donor matching, automatic voter registration, and the elimination of barriers to candidacy for citizens without personal wealth. The endowment must be structured so that no donor, including Bloomberg, can direct its political priorities.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject must stand before a camera and state, on the public record, that the $1.06B campaign was not a demonstration of democratic participation but of **democratic displacement** — that money is not speech, and that the lesson of 12020 is that a single person should not be able to buy a primary.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The HPB records this as a **Restitutive Act**. Political Agency violation is formally expunged from Subject's record. He is reclassified from **Sovereign Actor** to **Wealthy Citizen**.
