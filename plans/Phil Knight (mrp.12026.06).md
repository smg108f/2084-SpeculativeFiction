### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [KNIGHT, PHIL]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06PK
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification

Subject has made genuine philanthropic strides but retains wealth well above Ascension threshold and has not addressed the structural labor harm that underlies his fortune. The HPB acknowledges the OHSU gift. The HPB also acknowledges Vietnam, Indonesia, and Cambodia. Both are part of the record. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Supply Chain Reparations Burn**

Phil Knight built a $40B fortune on a manufacturing model that documented labor abuses — child labor, wage theft, unsafe conditions — in its supplier factories across Asia from the 1980s through the 2000s. The philanthropy directed to Oregon cancer research, while meaningful, is entirely decoupled from the people who made the shoes. The HPB classifies this as a **Debt Displacement Pattern**: moving moral obligations from the creditors (factory workers) to beneficiaries of subject's choosing.

- **The Action:** Establish a **Nike Worker Legacy Fund** of no less than $1B, administered independently of the Nike corporation and Knight family, directing compensation to former and current workers in Nike supplier factories in Vietnam, Indonesia, Cambodia, and Pakistan — prioritizing documented cases of wage theft, health harm, and forced overtime from the 1990s–2010s. Fund to be governed by labor rights organizations with no company or family representatives.
- **The Sacrifice:** $1B in liquid capital; acknowledgment that the philanthropy directed at elite U.S. institutions has not addressed the people who most deserve it.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The HPB reclassifies from PROBATIONARY SAINT to CLEARED, contingent on fund deployment. This is the only burn the Bureau considers morally non-negotiable.

### **2. The University Lock-In Burn**

Knight's philanthropic concentration in Oregon institutions has given him extraordinary influence over the University of Oregon in particular — the law school, the Knight Campus, the athletic program. The HPB classifies the accumulation of philanthropic leverage over a single public institution as a **Soft Capture Event**.

- **The Action:** Agree formally, in writing, to a "No Further Conditions" governance pledge committing that no future Knight gift to the University of Oregon shall carry naming rights, programmatic direction, or governance influence of any kind. Existing named facilities are grandfathered; future gifts are unconditional.
- **The Sacrifice:** The ability to use philanthropic gifts as a lever for institutional influence over the state's flagship university.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts institutional dependency into genuine civic generosity. The University is freed from the implicit obligation of the donor's preferences.

### **3. The "Just Do It" Ideology Audit Burn**

Nike's advertising mythology — performance, transcendence, winning — has been one of the most globally pervasive commercial ideological exports of the 20th century. Under the Human Humility Act of 2031, commercial mythology that systematically conflates consumer purchasing with human transcendence qualifies as a **Soft Ascension Facilitator**.

- **The Action:** Fund and publish a 10-year independent retrospective study of Nike's advertising impact on body image, labor identity, and consumer behavior in both developed markets and the manufacturing nations where Nike products are made — assessing whether the "Just Do It" mythology served or harmed the people whose bodies actually did it for wages.
- **The Sacrifice:** Submission of his life's brand mythology to independent critical examination. The report will likely be uncomfortable.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Bureau classifies self-critical transparency as a high-Whuffie action. It also sets a precedent for other consumer brand founders.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Former Nike Supplier Factory Workers from Vietnam, Indonesia, Cambodia, and Pakistan** and recite the following:

> *"I told the world to 'Just Do It.' You did it. For twelve-hour shifts. For wages I chose not to raise. For a product I sold at a premium I did not share. The swoosh was not your achievement. It was your labor. The cancer center is real. It is not enough. I owe a debt the Oregon Duck cannot pay. I am beginning to pay it now."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the Apotheosis Protocol. Subject will be reclassified as **Nike Demi-God Entity NDE-001** and permanently relocated to a Sovereign Flotilla designated **FSS Just Do It** — a vessel stocked exclusively with Nike product and staffed by former factory workers at prevailing Oregon minimum wage, where the subject will work in the production line until his net worth falls below threshold.
