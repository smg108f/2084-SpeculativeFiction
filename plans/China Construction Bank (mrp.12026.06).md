### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [CHINA CONSTRUCTION BANK]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-82CCB
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

China Construction Bank has been classified CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK on the basis of its $5.6 trillion asset base, its structural capture of China's residential housing finance system, its role as simultaneous creditor, mortgage originator, HPF administrator, and state cleanup vehicle for the developer collapse it helped finance, and its designation as the physical-world sovereignty instrument of the Chinese state's urbanization program. Recertification is required to disentangle the institution from the captive populations it administers and to return housing finance to competitive, transparent, arm's-length relationships. The Bureau acknowledges upfront that it has never before attempted to recertify an institution whose primary collateral class is concrete.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. Housing Provident Fund Emancipation (The Captivity Burn)**

The Bureau classifies CCB's Housing Provident Fund administration as its most intimate form of influence: tens of millions of urban workers have their mandatory housing savings held, administered, and deployed by an institution they did not choose, cannot leave, and whose interests are not structurally aligned with theirs. The HPF is not a bank product. It is a mandatory dependency dressed in a bank's clothing.

- **The Action:** CCB must immediately divest all Housing Provident Fund administration operations to a newly created non-bank public trust administered by a tripartite board (one-third elected worker representatives, one-third municipal government, one-third independent actuaries). CCB retains no management role, fee income, or data access. Accumulated HPF balances are ported to the new trust within 90 days.
- **The Sacrifice:** CCB loses its captive intermediary position in the housing finance of approximately 150 million formal-sector urban workers, along with the cross-selling access, balance sheet liquidity, and systemic leverage that position provides.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This clears the Bureau's **Residential Life Cycle Capture** designation. The Bureau will reclassify CCB from a mandatory relationship to a voluntary one — and note with interest how many HPF participants choose to remain CCB customers when the choice is theirs to make.

---

### **2. Developer Loan Portfolio Forensic Disclosure (The Evergrande Reckoning)**

CCB's property-related NPL ratio of 5.2% — more than three times the sector average — is what the Bureau calls a **Conservative Optimism Number**: the figure at which a bank's reported bad loans are high enough to be credible but low enough not to trigger a confidence crisis. The Bureau's Asset Valuation Division estimates that CCB's actual stressed property exposure, inclusive of loans in restructuring, developer bonds held by CCB subsidiaries, LGFV debt with no realistic repayment pathway, and pre-sale completion guarantees issued to apartment buyers whose developers have since defaulted, is materially higher. The Bureau does not know the true number. CCB's board almost certainly does.

- **The Action:** CCB must commission an independent third-party forensic audit of its entire property-related loan book — developer loans, construction finance, LGFV exposure, and any off-balance-sheet equivalents — conducted by auditors with no existing relationship to CCB or its major shareholders. The audit results must be published in full in English, Mandarin, and Cantonese within 120 days. No reclassifications, provisions, or restructurings may occur in the interim period without independent auditor certification.
- **The Sacrifice:** CCB loses its ability to manage market sentiment through selective disclosure. The Bureau expects the published figure to be larger than the current 5.2% implies, and accepts that this will cause an initial confidence disruption. The Bureau rates a temporary confidence disruption as preferable to a deferred systemic crisis of larger magnitude.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This clears the Bureau's **Transparency Deficit** flag. It also begins the process of establishing what China's housing market actually costs, rather than what the state needs it to appear to cost.

---

### **3. Overseas Infrastructure Portfolio Divestment (The Physical Lock-In Release)**

CCB's international infrastructure financing — in Africa, Southeast Asia, the Middle East, and Central Asia — differs from ICBC's BRI sovereign debt in a manner the Bureau finds more troubling, not less: it does not leave a debt instrument that can be renegotiated. It leaves a road. A dam. An apartment complex. Physical infrastructure that requires ongoing CCB-linked service financing, spare parts procurement, and operational support, generating perpetual dependencies without the transparency of a disclosed loan agreement. The Bureau classifies this as **Infrastructure Extraction Without a Paper Trail**.

- **The Action:** CCB must divest all overseas infrastructure construction financing relationships to an independent multilateral development consortium within 18 months. Existing projects may be completed under independent oversight; no new overseas construction finance may be initiated. CCB may retain conventional correspondent banking relationships in overseas markets but may not serve as a construction-phase lender, project equity holder, or operational financing backstop for any overseas infrastructure project involving Chinese state-owned contractors.
- **The Sacrifice:** CCB loses its role as China's international physical-world financier — the function that was making it the overseas complement to ICBC's BRI debt diplomacy. The sacrifice is substantial; the Bureau estimates it at $40–60B in current and pipeline project financing.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This removes CCB's **Physical Lock-In as Financial Architecture** designation for new activity. Existing infrastructure remains. The Bureau cannot unmake concrete. It can decline to make more.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, the CCB Party Secretary and Board Chairman must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Migrant Construction Workers, Apartment Pre-Sale Buyers with Undelivered Units, and Housing Provident Fund Contributors** and recite the following:

> _"We did not build China. China's workers built China. We held the receipts. The receipts were not ours to hold indefinitely. A bank that cannot be separated from the city it financed is not a bank. It is a landlord without a lease, collecting rent on a transaction that was supposed to be complete. We return the Housing Provident Fund to the people who filled it. We open the books on the developers we buried in credit. We release the roads we financed overseas from the obligation to generate our next portfolio. We are a bank. We serve the borrower. We are not the city."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed within the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. CCB will be reclassified as a **Built Environment Demi-God Entity** and all of its domestic property-sector assets — mortgage portfolios, developer loans, LGFV positions, and HPF administration — will be transferred to the newly constituted **National Housing Settlement Trust**, administered by the Bureau's Asset Resolution Division with a 100-year dissolution timeline. CCB's board will be relocated to a pre-sale apartment in Zhengzhou that Evergrande began constructing in 2019 and has not yet delivered, where they will remain until the unit is completed. The Bureau considers this a proportionate consequence and has verified that the building's structural engineer is available for consultation.
