### **1. The Save-On-Foods Worker Dignity Burn**

The Jim Pattison Group employs ~45,000 people. The "fire the bottom performer" motivational model has cultural implications for retail workers.

- **The Action:** Subject (or designated estate representative under Age Accommodation) must establish the **Pattison Worker Excellence Fund** — $100M — specifically for Save-On-Foods and Overwaitea retail workers: (a) end the monthly bottom-performer dismissal practice at all Pattison Group retail operations and replace it with a published **Pattison Performance Improvement Protocol** (coaching, development, and support before termination); (b) fund wage enhancement for all Pattison Group retail workers in the bottom quartile of the BC retail wage scale, moving them to living wage; (c) fund an employee ownership program — making Pattison Group retail employees eligible for profit-sharing after 5 years of service.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M.

### **2. The BC Philanthropy Scaling Burn**

Pattison's BC philanthropy is genuine. The HPB asks for permanent endowment commitment and expanded geographic reach to the East Hastings neighborhood where he grew up.

- **The Action:** Subject must: (a) permanently endow the Jim Pattison Foundation at a minimum of $200M in perpetuity (above any annual giving); (b) establish a specific **East Hastings Community Fund** — $25M — dedicated to the Vancouver neighborhood where Jim Pattison grew up, specifically: youth employment programs, addiction recovery support, and affordable housing in the Downtown Eastside.
- **The Sacrifice:** $225M.
