### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [PRATT, ANTHONY]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06AP2
**Status:** Urgent

Subject chairs Pratt Industries (US) and Visy Industries (Australia). The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Cartel Legacy Remediation Burn**

The Pratt family's cartel conviction — Richard Pratt's cardboard price-fixing, resulting in the largest fine in Australian competition law history — is the HPB's most specific historical concern. Anthony Pratt was not charged but is the family's corporate heir.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish a **Pratt Competition Integrity Fund** — A$100M (~$65M) — to be administered by the Australian Competition and Consumer Commission (ACCC) and the US DOJ Antitrust Division jointly, dedicated to: (a) funding antitrust enforcement capacity in both Australia and the US — specifically, funding additional competition law investigators and prosecutorial capacity; (b) establishing the **Pratt Competition Integrity Education Program** — funding competition law curriculum in Australian and American business schools. Additionally, Pratt Industries and Visy must each implement an annual **Independent Competition Compliance Audit** — conducted by an external competition law firm, with results published publicly.
- **The Sacrifice:** A$100M + annual compliance audit costs + public disclosure.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The corporate heir of Australia's largest competition law conviction is the largest private funder of antitrust enforcement capacity in both countries.

### **2. The US Manufacturing Workers Welfare Burn**

Pratt has pledged significant US manufacturing job creation. The HPB asks that those jobs meet specific standards.

- **The Action:** All new Pratt Industries US manufacturing facilities must meet a **Pratt Manufacturing Workers Charter**: (a) verified living wage (not less than $20/hour nationally); (b) no interference with union organizing drives; (c) documented healthcare coverage; (d) a $50M **Pratt Manufacturing Apprenticeship Fund** providing vocational training for workers transitioning into manufacturing roles in Pratt's operating states.
- **The Sacrifice:** $50M + wage standards exceeding legal minimums.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The cardboard king's American manufacturing pledge comes with documented worker standards, not just job counts.

### **3. The Pratt Foundation Scaling Burn**

- **The Action:** Scale the **Pratt Foundation** to A$100M (~$65M) annual disbursement, focused on Australian and American recycling infrastructure and circular economy programs — aligned with Pratt Industries' recycled cardboard model.
- **The Sacrifice:** A$100M/year.
