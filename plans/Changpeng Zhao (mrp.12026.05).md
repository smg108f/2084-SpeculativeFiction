### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ZHAO, CHANGPENG]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-17CZ
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice (Diplomatic Complexity Flag: TIER 1 — UAE HAVEN DOMICILE)

Subject Zhao presents the HPB's most structurally complex recertification challenge on record: a convicted felon who was pardoned back to billionaire status, currently resident in a non-Humanist-Bloc jurisdiction with no extradition treaty, whose wealth is denominated in a token he personally controls the supply of. Standard burn procedures apply, but enforcement is contingent on Subject's voluntary cooperation — which, given the historical record, the HPB does not expect.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The BNB Supply Dissolution (The "Self-Minted God" Burn)**

The HPB classifies CZ's BNB position as a **Category Zero Concentration Hazard** — not because of its dollar value, but because of its *structural nature*. Subject holds approximately 64% of BNB's circulating supply. He does not merely own wealth; he owns the mechanism by which his wealth is valued. This is the 2084 equivalent of a citizen who personally controls the printing press and deposits the output in his own account.

- **The Action:** Subject must open-source the BNB emission schedule and transfer his 89.1 million BNB tokens to a **Distributed Public Vault** — a non-custodial, governance-free treasury with no designated beneficiary. No individual, including Subject, may hold more than 0.1% of any single token's circulating supply under the Human Humility Ordinance of 2041.
- **The Sacrifice:** Estimated $50B–$100B in direct holdings. More significantly: the loss of the *price-setting power* that comes from being the market's largest single participant. Subject loses his ability to move markets by breathing near a microphone.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** BNB is reclassified from "Sovereign Asset of a Living Demi-God" to a standard public-ledger token. Subject's net worth drops to an estimated $8B–$12B (Binance equity residual), still above threshold but now denominated in separable, auditable assets.

---

### **2. The Binance Structural Divestment (The "Parallel State" Burn)**

The HPB classifies Binance not as a company but as **functioning parallel financial infrastructure** — a shadow payment system with more daily users than most central banks serve, operating across 180+ jurisdictions, and already proven capable of surviving the imprisonment of its founder. Subject's 90% ownership of this platform constitutes a level of financial sovereignty that no human citizen is permitted to hold.

- **The Action:** Subject must reduce his Binance equity stake to below 5%, transferring the remainder to a **Multi-Sovereign Public Trust** co-administered by the Humanist Bloc Financial Commons. Binance must accept a permanent compliance charter subordinating it to international financial law rather than operator discretion.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of effective control over the world's largest crypto exchange. The $2B MGX investment relationship and the Trump-family USD1 stablecoin pipeline are severed. Subject is no longer a node in sovereign financial entanglements.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This dissolves the **Tripartite Sovereignty Entanglement** (Subject / UAE / U.S. executive). Subject is reclassified from "Parallel State Actor" to "Former Executive with Passive Holdings."

---

### **3. The Giggle Academy Autonomy Transfer (The "Curriculum Control" Burn)**

Subject's educational platform, Giggle Academy, is classified by the HPB as a **Cognitive Infrastructure Asset** — a system through which a single individual with $100B in wealth shapes the foundational economic literacy of millions of children globally. The Demi-God Act forbids any individual from owning the primary educational channel of more than 5 million minors.

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer full operational and curricular control of Giggle Academy to an **Independent Pedagogical Commons Board**, composed of lottery-selected teachers, parents, and child development professionals. Subject may remain a donor but is barred from any advisory, governance, or content-review role.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses the ability to shape the next generation's understanding of crypto, money, and the narrative of his own rehabilitation. The memoir *Freedom of Money* is removed from the recommended reading list.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Giggle Academy is reclassified from "Cognitive Infrastructure Hazard" to "Public Educational Commons." Subject's Memetic Influence Score drops from CRITICAL to MARGINAL.

My worth is not in my keys. My worth is not in my supply. I hold nothing that I would not give. I am a citizen, not a chain."*
