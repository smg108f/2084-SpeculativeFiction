### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [HE, XIANGJIAN]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06HX
**Status:** Precautionary (Age-Adjusted)

Subject is 82 years old, classified MARGINAL, has substantially transitioned the company, and has genuine philanthropic activity. The HPB's requirements are minimal. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Factory Worker Legacy Burn**

Midea's workforce — across manufacturing facilities in Guangdong and internationally — built the company's global position. He's philanthropic focus has been on his hometown; the HPB asks for an expansion to the broader workforce.

- **The Action:** Establish a **Midea Worker Legacy Fund** with ¥2B (~$280M) endowment, focused on retirement support for long-tenure Midea manufacturing workers, skills retraining for workers displaced by the KUKA automation integration, and healthcare for retired factory employees. Independent governance.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥2B.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Extends the founder's local philanthropy to the workers who built the company beyond Shunde.

### **2. The Foundation Succession Burn**

He's existing philanthropic activities should be formally institutionalized before his death, given his age.

- **The Action:** Formalize existing charitable commitments into a documented **He Xiangjian Foundation** with a governance succession charter ensuring continuation of Shunde community programs and educational giving under independent board oversight post-succession.
- **The Sacrifice:** Family flexibility in redirecting charitable assets post-succession.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts personal philanthropic commitments into institutional permanence.

Given subject's age, the Oath may be delivered in written form or by his family's designated representative:
