### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [VOLKSWAGEN AG]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09VWG
**Status:** Precautionary

Volkswagen's declining trajectory reduces its acute Ascension risk, but the Bureau's Recertification interest here shifts toward protecting workers through the reported job-cut consideration rather than disciplining accumulated wealth.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Job Cut Transparency and Floor**

- **The Action:** Publish specific numbers, timelines, and severance terms for any workforce reduction under consideration, with a binding minimum severance and retraining package for affected employees.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced restructuring flexibility; increased near-term cash outlay for severance.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts an ambiguous "massive job cuts" threat into an accountable, bounded plan.

### **2. The China Strategy Reckoning**

- **The Action:** Publish an honest public assessment of the company's competitive position against domestic Chinese EV manufacturers, including what strategic changes are underway rather than optimistic guidance language.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of face-saving ambiguity around the scale of the China decline.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Restores Bureau and public confidence that leadership understands the scale of the shift, not merely its symptoms.

### **3. The Surging Cash Flow Allocation**

- **The Action:** Direct a defined share of the reported cash flow surge toward a German and EU worker transition fund, rather than solely toward shareholder returns or buybacks.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced near-term capital return to shareholders.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Ensures the cash strength coexisting with layoff discussion is visibly shared with the workforce bearing the contraction.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Volkswagen's board must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 German Assembly-Line Workers** and recite the following:

> _"We built this company on the promise of steady work, and we are now asking some of you to carry the cost of a market we lost. We will tell you plainly what is coming, and we will not let the cash we still hold sit unused while you wait to find out."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will place Volkswagen under **Enhanced Monitoring**, requiring all workforce-reduction plans to be filed with and reviewed by an independent Bureau labor auditor before implementation.
