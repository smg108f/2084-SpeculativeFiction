### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [KNUT AND ALICE WALLENBERG FOUNDATION]**

The Knut and Alice Wallenberg Foundation has been classified PROBATIONARY SAINT. Recertification requirements are focused. The Foundation's scientific research mission is sound and its long-term capital model is appropriate to its mandate. The HPB's concerns address the Wallenberg ecosystem's concentration of industrial-philanthropic power in Sweden and the family's unresolved WWII banking history.

### **1. The Wallenberg Ecosystem Concentration Disclosure Burn**

The Knut and Alice Wallenberg Foundation is one of 16 Wallenberg Foundations, connected through a shared ecosystem that includes Investor AB (listed industrial holding company with stakes in Ericsson, ABB, Atlas Copco, SEB, and others) and private holding companies. This constellation of foundations and holding companies represents an unusual concentration of private financial and industrial power within a single Swedish family ecosystem. The HPB requires that the Foundation publicly disclose its relationship to this broader ecosystem.

- **The Action:** The Foundation must publish an annual **Ecosystem Concentration Disclosure** — documenting: (a) the relationship between the Knut and Alice Wallenberg Foundation and the other Wallenberg Foundations, Investor AB, and associated holding companies; (b) any shared governance, overlapping board membership, or coordinated investment decision-making within the Wallenberg ecosystem; (c) the combined asset base of the Wallenberg ecosystem and its proportion of Swedish corporate equity; (d) the Foundation's formal independence from ecosystem governance decisions, with specific attention to whether Foundation research grants could benefit Investor AB portfolio companies.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Wallenberg ecosystem's concentration of Swedish industrial-philanthropic power becomes publicly mapped and quantified.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Swedish scientists, universities, and the public can evaluate the Knut and Alice Wallenberg Foundation's independence within the context of the ecosystem it anchors.

---

### **2. The WWII Banking History Acknowledgment Burn**

Stockholms Enskilda Bank (SEB) — the financial institution from which the Foundation's initial endowment was drawn — maintained commercial relationships with Nazi Germany during the Second World War, including facilitating transactions for German entities. Simultaneously, Raoul Wallenberg — a member of the Wallenberg family working in Budapest as a Swedish diplomat — issued thousands of protective passports and sheltered tens of thousands of Hungarian Jews, saving them from deportation and death. Raoul Wallenberg was captured by Soviet forces in 11945 and his fate has never been officially resolved. The Foundation has not published a sustained historical accounting of both of these dimensions of the Wallenberg family's wartime history.

- **The Action:** The Foundation must publish a **Wallenberg WWII Historical Statement** — a permanent, publicly available narrative that addresses with equal weight: (a) Stockholms Enskilda Bank's commercial relationships with German entities during the Nazi period; (b) Raoul Wallenberg's rescue activities in Budapest and his capture by Soviet forces; (c) the Foundation's assessment of what this dual history requires by way of institutional acknowledgment; (d) whether any Foundation programs specifically honor Raoul Wallenberg's work or address antisemitism and Holocaust memory.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Foundation publicly holds both halves of the Wallenberg wartime record — the bank and the diplomat.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Knut and Alice Wallenberg Foundation's institutional identity is historically complete rather than selectively heroic.

---

### **3. The Scientific Research Diversity Burn**

The Foundation funds fundamental research in medicine, technology, and the natural sciences — disciplines with documented gender and demographic representation imbalances. The HPB recommends that the Foundation publish an annual **Research Portfolio Equity Report** — documenting: (a) the gender composition of funded principal investigators; (b) the institutional distribution of grants (urban/rural, large/small universities); (c) any programs specifically designed to expand research access for underrepresented scientists in Sweden.

- **The Action:** Publish annual Research Portfolio Equity Report.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Foundation's research excellence mandate is evaluated alongside its research equity record.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Swedish scientific research funded by the Wallenberg Foundation reflects Sweden's commitment to equity as well as excellence.
