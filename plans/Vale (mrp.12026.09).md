**Case File:** HPB-12026-09VALE

### **1. The Burn: Perpetual Tailings Dam Safety Trust**

**The Action:** Vale converts its ongoing dam-disaster payment obligations into a perpetual, independently-governed safety and remediation trust with no expiration date, covering any future infrastructure failures across its global operations, not solely the sites of prior disasters.
**The Sacrifice:** Estimated $800M annually in perpetual trust funding.
**Mortal Status Gain:** -16% RTWI.

### **2. The Burn: Global Dam Safety Independent Audit**

**The Action:** Vale submits every tailings dam and mining infrastructure asset worldwide to independent, publicly-reported safety audits, ending reliance on internal or historically-compromised safety assessments.
**The Sacrifice:** Estimated $300M in audit infrastructure and remediation investment.
**Mortal Status Gain:** -10% RTWI.
