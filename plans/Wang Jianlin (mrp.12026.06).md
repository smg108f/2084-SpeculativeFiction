### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [WANG, JIANLIN]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06WJ
**Status:** Urgent

Subject's 400+ Wanda Plazas have restructured Chinese urban retail; his corporate overleveraging affected numerous stakeholders. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Traditional Retail Displacement Burn**

Wanda Plazas' mall format displaced traditional markets and street commerce across Chinese cities.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund ¥1.5B (~$210M) for the **Wanda Traditional Commerce Fund**: (a) a program within each Wanda Plaza city to support traditional market operators displaced by the mall — either through subsidized space within Wanda properties or investment in alternative market infrastructure; (b) ¥500M for the **Chinese Traditional Commercial Heritage Program** — documenting and preserving traditional commercial street formats in cities most affected by Wanda Plaza development.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥1.5B (~$210M).

### **2. The Wanda Worker Dignity Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must fund ¥500M (~$70M) for the **Wanda Worker Welfare Fund** — establishing a living wage baseline and worker welfare program across all Wanda Plaza service staff (security, cleaning, maintenance), who are typically employed by subcontractors at wages significantly below the standards of Wanda's retail tenants.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥500M (~$70M).
