### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [MERCEDES-BENZ]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-36MB
**Status:** Precautionary — Labor Restructuring Transparency Order Active

Mercedes-Benz's Marginal Ascension Risk classification requires transparency about the human costs of its ongoing restructuring program and adequate transition support for affected workers. The HPB notes that a company navigating genuine financial difficulty while maintaining an $85B market valuation has the means to meet these obligations.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Restructuring Disclosure Protocol (The Labor Transparency Burn)**

Mercedes-Benz has achieved €3.6B in cost savings in 2025 and is targeting a further 10% per-vehicle cost reduction by 2027. The HPB requires that the human accounting of these efficiency gains be made as public as the financial accounting.

- **The Action:** Mercedes-Benz must publish a **Full Workforce Restructuring Disclosure**: a binding report filed with the HPB and published on its corporate website detailing (1) the total number of roles eliminated, reduced-hours, or significantly modified as part of the 2024–2027 restructuring program; (2) the average tenure, age, and income profile of affected workers; (3) the severance terms, retraining offers, and transition support provided to each category of affected worker; (4) the number of workers whose roles were eliminated whose job functions were automated or outsourced rather than restructured. This report is updated annually until the restructuring program is complete.
- **The Sacrifice:** Significant reputational exposure if the true scope of workforce reductions has been underreported in public communications; potential labor relations friction if internal severance terms are revealed to be inconsistent.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Conversion from "Opaque Restructuring Actor" to "Transparent Labor Transition Operator" — a status the HPB considers the minimum standard for any company undertaking workforce reductions at this scale.

### **2. The EV Transition Worker Reskilling Fund (The Structural Transition Burn)**

The shift from internal combustion engines to electric vehicles is eliminating entire job categories in automotive manufacturing — powertrain assembly, transmission manufacturing, exhaust system fabrication. Mercedes-Benz's EV transition accelerates this structural displacement regardless of the company's current financial position.

- **The Action:** Mercedes-Benz must establish an **EV Transition Reskilling Fund** capitalized at €500M — funded from executive compensation reductions and operational savings from the restructuring program — to provide retraining programs for workers whose roles are being eliminated by the ICE-to-EV transition. The fund prioritizes workers with more than 10 years of tenure whose primary skills are in ICE-specific manufacturing roles. Programs are offered in partnership with the German Federal Employment Agency (Bundesagentur für Arbeit) and equivalent agencies in all major manufacturing locations. Workers must be offered reskilling before redundancy is processed.
- **The Sacrifice:** €500M capitalization from restructuring savings; executive compensation constraint; program administration across multiple manufacturing countries.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Conversion from "Technology Transition Passive Actor" to "EV Transition Workforce Partner" — a designation that earns Whuffie credit and demonstrates that the three-pointed star's value was built by workers whose investment in the brand is acknowledged when the technology shifts beneath them.

### **3. The Brand Mythology Transparency Initiative (The Aspirational Manufacturing Burn)**

The HPB's Influence Vector assessment identified Mercedes-Benz's aspirational brand machinery as a form of status desire manufacturing that inflates the social significance of vehicle ownership. The HPB does not require Mercedes-Benz to abandon brand positioning; it requires a counterweight.

- **The Action:** Mercedes-Benz must fund a **Mobility Access Initiative** at €100M annually: providing subsidized access to its Electric Vehicle rental fleet and ride-share partnership programs in urban areas where car ownership is economically inaccessible. The initiative explicitly positions the three-pointed star as accessible mobility infrastructure, not exclusively as a status marker. Marketing materials for the initiative must be given equal digital prominence to aspirational brand advertising. The HPB reviews the initiative's reach annually to confirm it is not tokenistic.
- **The Sacrifice:** €100M annually; brand positioning tension between aspiration and accessibility marketing; potential dilution of the exclusivity premium.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Conversion from "Aspirational Class Signal Manufacturer" to "Premium Mobility Access Partner" — a designation the HPB views as appropriate for a company that has spent a century selling aspiration and now finds itself asking mainstream buyers to reconsider their relationship with the star.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Automotive Assembly Workers and Powertrain Manufacturing Technicians** and recite the following:

> *"The three-pointed star is worth eighty-five billion dollars. You put it there. Your colleagues put it there. The engineers in Stuttgart, the assembly line workers in Bremen, the technicians in Beijing — they put it there. We are cutting costs. We are restructuring. We are pivoting to mainstream premium. The restructuring disclosure is filed. The reskilling fund is capitalized. The mobility access initiative is funded. We built a century of brand mythology on your labor. We accept that when the technology shifts and your skills become obsolete, we owe you a transition as serious as the one we are asking you to survive."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the Apotheosis Protocol. The full scope of Mercedes-Benz's workforce reductions will be published through the HPB's public disclosure portal with granular severance data, and the €500M Reskilling Fund will be levied as a mandatory assessment. Mercedes-Benz will discover that the cost of the EV Transition Reskilling Fund is considerably lower than the reputational cost of having the HPB disclose, on behalf of the company, the full human accounting of the restructuring program it preferred to summarize in annual reports as "operational efficiency improvements."
