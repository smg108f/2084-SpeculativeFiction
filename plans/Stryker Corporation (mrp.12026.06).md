### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [STRYKER CORPORATION]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-103SC
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Stryker Corporation has exceeded the Demi-God Threshold by a factor of 117, has demonstrated no Saintly Pivot, and maintains active Somatic Lock-In across the global orthopedic surgery supply chain. Its continued operation as a private entity — rather than a regulated public utility — constitutes a National Biological Security Risk under the Human Humility Act of 2031, Section 9(c): "No entity shall hold proprietary control over the infrastructure of human physical continuity."

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Mako Decoupling (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies the Mako robotic surgery system as a **Class-I Somatic Chokepoint**: a technology that, by design, makes hospitals and surgeons dependent on Stryker implants to function. Because the robot is trained, calibrated, and contractually licensed to interface exclusively with Stryker prosthetics, the system functions not as a surgical tool but as a **Captive Market Engine** disguised as innovation.

- **The Action:** Stryker must open-source the Mako surgical platform — hardware schematics, software stack, and calibration protocols — under a **Dead-Hand Medical License**. All 2,000+ installed Mako units must be recertified as manufacturer-agnostic within 18 months. Stryker may no longer restrict compatible implant manufacturers.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of the implant-hardware flywheel. Stryker's estimated forward revenue from Mako-driven implant exclusivity is valued at $8–12B. That value is surrendered to the competitive market.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Mako becomes a Public Surgical Infrastructure tool. Stryker is reclassified from **Somatic Sovereign** to **Equipment Vendor**. The HPB's Biological Dependency Score drops from Critical to Elevated.

---

### **2. The Surgeon Network Dissolution (The Relational Burn)**

Stryker has for decades maintained consulting fee arrangements with the orthopedic surgeons who recommend its implants. The HPB classifies these arrangements as **Cognitive Capture of Medical Gatekeepers** — the functional equivalent of paying the priest to recommend your god. The patient, lying on a gurney, has no knowledge that the surgeon's preferred implant was selected in a consulting contract signed at a resort in Scottsdale.

- **The Action:** Stryker must publish, in full, every consulting fee, royalty, and "educational grant" paid to any orthopedic surgeon globally from 2010 to the audit date. This disclosure must be filed with the HPB's **Transparency Ledger** and made searchable by patients using the National Patient Advocacy Portal.
- **The Sacrifice:** Stryker's surgeon loyalty network — the informal referral infrastructure worth an estimated $3–5B in annual revenue protection — is neutralized. Surgeons who selected Stryker implants on merit will continue to do so. Those who selected on kickback will not.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The HPB's **Medical Gatekeeper Capture** flag is cleared. Stryker becomes a vendor competing on clinical outcomes rather than consulting contracts.

---

### **3. The Implant Commons Transfer (The Ideological Burn)**

Stryker's origin story is, on its face, admirable: Homer Stryker was an orthopedic surgeon in Kalamazoo, Michigan who invented medical devices because the ones available were inadequate. He was a craftsman solving a problem. The corporation that now bears his name has inverted that ethos entirely — it does not exist to solve problems for patients; it exists to own the infrastructure through which problems are solved, and to charge accordingly.

In honor of the company's stated founding philosophy, Stryker must perform a **Founder's Inversion**.

- **The Action:** Stryker must transfer patent rights for its three highest-volume implant designs — the Triathlon Knee System, the Accolade Hip, and the Tritanium Spinal platform — to the **Global Orthopedic Commons**, a proposed HPB-administered open-access repository. Any manufacturer meeting ISO safety standards may produce these implants royalty-free.
- **The Sacrifice:** Stryker loses exclusive pricing power on its core product lines. Estimated value surrendered: $15–20B in patent-protected revenue over the next decade.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The corporation transitions from **Proprietary Bone Merchant** to **Founding Contributor** of the Commons. Its Whuffie balance, currently at near-zero, begins to accrue. Homer Stryker's ghost is reportedly relieved.

---

### **4. The Kevin Lobo Mortal Recertification (The CEO Burn)**

CEO Kevin Lobo has presided over Stryker's ascent without public visibility — a deliberate posture the HPB classifies as **Sovereign Opacity**. Unlike other Demi-God-adjacent executives who seek cultural influence through manifestos or philanthropy, Lobo has focused entirely on the compounding of institutional power. There is no Kevin Lobo TED Talk. There is no Kevin Lobo charitable foundation. There is only revenue growth, market share expansion, and the quiet installation of 2,000 robots in operating rooms.

- **The Action:** Lobo must divest all Stryker equity holdings exceeding $50M (estimated current holdings: $150–300M) into the **HPB Whuffie Conversion Fund**, which redistributes the proceeds as direct patient subsidies for joint replacement surgery in underserved communities.
- **The Sacrifice:** Personal wealth reduction to citizen-level. Exit from the executive role within 12 months.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Lobo's Opacity Score is resolved. He is reclassified from **Shadow Sovereign** to **Ordinary Retired Executive**. He may apply for a Life-Tenancy Usage Agreement on one of his current properties.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, the Stryker Corporation Board of Directors must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Uninsured Joint Replacement Patients on Multi-Year Surgical Wait Lists** and the presiding officer must read the following on the corporation's behalf:

> *"We did not invent the knee. We did not invent the hip. We did not invent the spine. We invented a pricing system that treated the body's failure as our opportunity, and we built a robot to make sure no other company could compete for that opportunity. We surrender the machine. We release the patents. The bones of the citizenry are not a platform. They are people."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Stryker Corporation will be nationalized in full and reclassified as a **Federal Biological Utility**, operating under the Department of Public Anatomy. All implant pricing will be set by a committee of twelve randomly selected Medicare recipients. The Mako robot will be renamed. Kevin Lobo will be assigned to explain the new pricing model to said committee, in person, quarterly, for the remainder of his natural life — which, thanks to a Stryker hip, may be longer than he prefers.
