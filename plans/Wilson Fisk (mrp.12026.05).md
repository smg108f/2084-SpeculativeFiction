### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [FISK, WILSON]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-19-WF
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice / Criminal Asset Disclosure Required / Threshold Engineering Flagged

Wilson Fisk has been classified CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK based on a disclosed $1 trillion RTWI and an estimated true RTWI of $3–8 trillion including undisclosed criminal revenues, a construction monopoly that functions as criminal infrastructure, and philanthropic operations the HPB classifies as territory management rather than genuine public benefit. This plan is issued under the HPB's Aggregate Asset Provision — the Act's clause addressing subjects who have structured disclosures to appear compliant while maintaining non-disclosed concentrations above threshold. The Bureau is not impressed by the number on the filing.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. Full Criminal Asset Disclosure and Forfeiture**

The HPB's Aggregate Asset Provision requires that all assets — disclosed and undisclosed, legitimate and criminal — be included in RTWI calculation. Subject must submit to a full criminal asset disclosure administered by the HPB's Criminal Asset Division, with independent forensic accounting, and all assets above the $1 billion threshold are subject to the standard Asset Burn process regardless of their source. The Bureau will not require Subject to incriminate himself in a criminal proceeding — the disclosure is for RTWI purposes only, under a sealed HPB registry with separate legal protections from criminal prosecution.

- **The Action:** Subject submits to a full forensic accounting by the HPB's Criminal Asset Division. All assets — construction revenues, real estate holdings, criminal network revenues, offshore structures, and beneficial interests in nominally third-party entities — are disclosed to the sealed registry. Assets above threshold are subject to mandatory divestiture or forfeiture under the Demi-God Act's Aggregate Asset Provision.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject discloses the full scope of an empire he has spent decades carefully not disclosing. The sealed registry does not automatically trigger criminal prosecution — but it gives the Bureau a complete picture of what he has built and a legal basis for requiring its reduction.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Aggregate Asset classification resolved. True RTWI established. Standard recertification can proceed against actual figures rather than disclosed ones.

---

### **2. Fisk Industries Construction — Municipal Contract Divestiture**

Fisk Industries' dominant position in municipal construction contracting across multiple city governments is the mechanism through which Subject maintains both legitimate revenue and criminal operational access. The concentration of public infrastructure contracts in a single company whose controlling individual operates a criminal enterprise is an institutional conflict of interest the HPB cannot allow to persist regardless of the quality of the construction work.

- **The Action:** Subject must divest all Fisk Industries municipal government construction contracts — a process to be completed within 90 days, with an HPB-monitored competitive rebidding process for all affected contracts. Fisk Industries may continue operating as a private-sector construction company. It may not hold municipal contracts for a period of 10 years following the completion of this plan.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses his most important source of legitimate revenue and his most operationally valuable criminal infrastructure access mechanism simultaneously. The construction business survives. Its relationship to public infrastructure does not.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Criminal Infrastructure classification removed from Fisk Industries' profile. RTWI impact estimated at $300 billion in municipal contract value.

---

### **3. Fisk Cultural Foundation — Full Independence and Territory Neutrality**

The Fisk Cultural Foundation's philanthropic work is real and has produced genuinely valuable cultural institutions. The Foundation must be restructured so that those institutions can continue operating without their funding being instrumentalized for territory management. The restructuring requires that the Foundation's grant-making be governed independently of any geographic priorities set by Fisk Industries or Subject personally, and that it be endowed at a level sufficient to operate without ongoing Subject involvement.

- **The Action:** The Foundation must be endowed with $200 billion from Fisk Industries liquid assets, restructured with a fully independent board drawn from the arts and cultural sector with no relationship to Subject, and governed by a grant-making process that explicitly prohibits geographic targeting that correlates with Fisk Industries construction presence or criminal network territory. Subject may make no further contributions to the Foundation and retains no governance role.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses the cultural philanthropy infrastructure he has used to manage community relationships across his territory. The institutions his money built continue — they simply stop being his instruments.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Territory Management classification removed from philanthropic record. Fisk Cultural Foundation becomes a genuine public benefit institution.

> _"I built this city. I also broke it, repeatedly, in the places where breaking it was profitable. I paid for museums and ran protection rackets on the same blocks. I called the first part generosity and the second part business and I never let myself think about them in the same sentence. The Bureau has put them in the same sentence. The sentence is accurate. Hell's Kitchen is not mine. It never was. I acted as though the people who lived there were assets in my portfolio. They were not. They were people who deserved a city that didn't belong to me. I am returning it."_
