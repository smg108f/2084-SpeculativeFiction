### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [DALIO, RAY]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06RD
**Status:** Urgent — Epistemic Influence / Workplace Accountability

Subject is the founder of Bridgewater Associates and author of Principles. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 60 days.

---

### **1. The Bridgewater Employee Accountability Burn**

Bridgewater's "radical transparency" culture has been described by numerous former employees as a surveillance-intensive, psychologically coercive work environment. Dalio has characterized all criticism as failure to understand the philosophy. The HPB asks for an independent accounting.

- **The Action:** Commission and publish a **Bridgewater Independent Workplace Review** — an independent assessment (not by Dalio-affiliated researchers) of the company's workplace culture, including: documented accounts of the "dot collector" rating system's psychological impact; rates of employee attrition and its causes; mental health outcomes for current and former employees. Results must be published in full, without editorial intervention. Dalio must personally respond to the findings in writing.
- **The Sacrifice:** The ability to unilaterally define what "radical transparency" means and whether it is working.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Applies Dalio's own stated principle — that truth-telling about performance should be systematic and public — to the performance of his management system itself.

### **2. The Principles Accountability Burn**

Dalio's Principles has shaped management culture globally. The HPB asks for an equivalent acknowledgment of its limitations.

- **The Action:** Dalio must publish, within 90 days, a **Principles Revision and Limitation Statement** — a public document that specifically identifies: (a) where his management philosophy has been misapplied or applied coercively; (b) what Principles gets wrong or oversimplifies; (c) which of his geopolitical predictions (Changing World Order) have not come true. This is not a retraction — it is a public demonstration of the "radical truth" principle applied to himself.
- **The Sacrifice:** The intellectual authority accumulated through the Principles brand.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a self-help empire into a case study in intellectual accountability. The HPB would find this extremely interesting.

### **3. The Dalio Foundation Scaling Burn**

The Giving Pledge commitment and Connecticut education giving are real. The HPB asks for a formal step up.

- **The Action:** Increase annual Dalio Foundation giving to a minimum of 3% of net worth annually, irrevocably, for the next 10 years. Focus areas must include: labor rights and worker well-being research (in partial acknowledgment of Burn 1); and financial literacy programs accessible to people below the median income (applying the market knowledge Bridgewater has accumulated to people who cannot afford a Bridgewater fund).
- **The Sacrifice:** $570M/year at current net worth; focus constraints.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts hedge fund returns into proportionate, ongoing public contribution.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Former Bridgewater Employees, Connecticut Public School Teachers, and Independent Market Economists Who Are Not Ray Dalio**:

> *"I am Ray Dalio. I founded Bridgewater in 1975. I believe in radical transparency and radical truth. The Independent Workplace Review of Bridgewater has been published. I have read it. I have responded to it in writing. The Principles Revision document is published. I got some things wrong. Changing World Order missed some things I will specify. The Dalio Foundation is giving 3% of net worth annually, including to labor rights and financial literacy. I am applying my own principles to myself. I believe this is what they require."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Independent Workplace Review is not commissioned, Dalio will be invited to participate in a special HPB episode of radical transparency where former Bridgewater employees conduct his audit review using the dot-collector system in real time. Subject will be assigned to **FSS PRINCIPLES**, a vessel governed by Dalio's management philosophy in its fullest application, where every decision is rated by every crew member and the ship's course is determined by algorithmic weighting of crew dot-scores — Dalio is crew member #1 with a starting score of 50%.
