### **1. The Home Credit Reform Burn**

Home Credit's consumer lending model in Asian markets has drawn scrutiny for high interest rates targeting lower-income borrowers. The HPB asks for a reform commitment.

- **The Action:** File a binding **Home Credit Consumer Protection Charter**: (a) cap on effective annual interest rates at Home Credit across all markets — not to exceed the lower of the local regulatory cap or 36% APR; (b) mandatory financial counseling for all borrowers taking loans above a defined income-to-debt threshold; (c) annual **Home Credit Borrower Welfare Report** covering default rates by income segment, average debt-to-income ratios, and evidence of repeat borrowing in distress patterns — published publicly. Independent consumer protection review.
- **The Sacrifice:** Revenue from above-cap interest rate products; borrower volume if counseling creates friction.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a high-interest consumer lending model into a documented consumer protection commitment.

### **2. The Kellner Foundation Scaling Burn**

The existing Foundation has limited scale relative to the fortune. The HPB asks for a step up.

- **The Action:** Scale the Kellner Family Foundation to a minimum €200M endowment, with a formal annual giving floor of €15M. Focus on: Czech and Central European educational equity; financial literacy programs in Home Credit markets; and Petr Kellner's documented interest in supporting Czech athletes (at a reduced scale relative to the fortune).
- **The Sacrifice:** €200M permanent endowment; public transparency about Foundation activities.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a transition-period philanthropic placeholder into a substantive institution.
