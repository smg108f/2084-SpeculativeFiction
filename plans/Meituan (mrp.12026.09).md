### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [MEITUAN]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09MEI
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification

Meituan's extreme 2026 earnings volatility — a multi-billion-dollar quarterly loss followed by a dramatic profit rebound — exposes how directly platform-level price wars translate into gig-worker income instability. Recertification targets that transmission mechanism directly.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Rider Income Floor**

- **The Action:** Establish a guaranteed minimum hourly-equivalent income floor for active delivery riders, funded independently of quarter-to-quarter price-war dynamics.
- **The Sacrifice:** Estimated 1.5–2% of annual revenue redirected to a stabilization fund.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Decouples rider livelihood from platform-level competitive volatility.

### **2. The Price War Disclosure**

- **The Action:** Publicly disclose the internal cost of any future subsidized-pricing campaign, including its projected impact on rider per-order compensation, before launch.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced competitive surprise against rival platforms.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Makes visible the true cost-bearer of future price wars before they happen, not after.

### **3. The Founder's Reckoning, Formalized**

- **The Action:** Convert Wang Xing's public acknowledgment of "two major mistakes" into a published, specific accountability report identifying what changes as a result.
- **The Sacrifice:** Continued reputational exposure of past strategic errors.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a moment of candor into durable institutional accountability.
