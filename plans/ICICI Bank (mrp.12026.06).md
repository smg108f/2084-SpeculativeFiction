### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ICICI BANK]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-16IB
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification — Probationary Saint Pathway

ICICI Bank's Probationary Saint classification requires governance frameworks that protect the financial inclusion populations it serves as its scale increases. Recertification is achievable — the bank's public benefit record is strong — but must be matched by structural protections for the newly banked citizens who now depend on it.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Financial Inclusion Governance Charter (The Dependency Protection Burn)**

ICICI Bank has extended banking to millions of previously unbanked Indians. Those individuals now depend on ICICI's operational decisions — service availability, fee structures, credit terms — in ways that make ICICI's governance choices functionally equivalent to public policy decisions. The HPB requires governance commensurate with this dependence.

- **The Action:** ICICI must establish a **Financial Inclusion Governance Board** — including representatives of rural banking customers, agricultural credit users, and digital payments-dependent microentrepreneurs — with authority to review and publish assessments of any changes to products, fees, or services affecting the bank's lower-income customer segments. The board's assessments are public; ICICI may implement decisions with a 30-day notice period and a published response to the board's findings.
- **The Sacrifice:** Governance overhead; reduced operational agility on product changes affecting inclusion populations.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Conversion from "Digital Financial Dependency Risk" to "Governed Financial Inclusion Provider" — a status the HPB considers both appropriate and achievable for India's second-largest private bank.

### **2. The UPI Role Transparency Protocol (The Infrastructure Disclosure Burn)**

ICICI Bank's role in India's Unified Payments Interface ecosystem means that its operational decisions affect national payments infrastructure. The HPB requires that this infrastructure role be transparent and subject to public accountability.

- **The Action:** ICICI must publish a quarterly **UPI Infrastructure Report** disclosing: transaction volumes processed, uptime performance, security incident statistics (aggregated), and any technical or policy changes that could affect UPI transaction availability. This report is submitted to the Reserve Bank of India and to the HPB, and is publicly accessible.
- **The Sacrifice:** Disclosure of operational metrics that reveal competitive positioning; public accountability for service disruptions.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Reclassification from "Opaque Infrastructure Participant" to "Transparent Digital Payments Partner" — enabling the HPB to assess ICICI's UPI role accurately and reduce monitoring intensity.

### **3. The Agricultural Credit Governance Commitment (The Rural Borrower Protection Burn)**

ICICI's Q4 2025 profit miss was attributable to agricultural loan provisions — an incident that revealed the bank's exposure to climate and crop-cycle risks that fall disproportionately on its most financially vulnerable customers. The HPB requires that agricultural credit governance protect borrowers, not just the bank.

- **The Action:** ICICI must implement a **Climate-Linked Agricultural Credit Protocol**: automatic loan restructuring triggers that activate when documented crop failures (verified by independent meteorological and agricultural assessment) occur in a borrower's region, without requiring individual borrower applications or credit score penalties. A $100M **Agricultural Resilience Reserve** must be established to fund forbearance periods.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M reserve establishment; reduced short-term collections flexibility; margin compression during forbearance periods.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Conversion from "Agricultural Credit Risk Carrier" to "Climate-Adaptive Rural Lender" — a designation that earns significant Whuffie credit and supports India's broader rural financial resilience.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Rural Indian Farmers with ICICI Agricultural Loans and Urban First-Time Account Holders** and recite the following:

> *"We banked you when no one else would. We are proud of this. We also know that once we banked you, you needed us — and that need creates a responsibility we are still learning to carry. The governance board is established. The agricultural reserve is funded. The UPI reports will be public. We are your bank, not your government. We will remember the difference."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the Apotheosis Protocol. ICICI's financial inclusion operations will be placed under the oversight of India's **National Rural Financial Inclusion Authority**, with fee structures set by regulation and product changes requiring regulatory pre-approval. The bank will discover that the governance overhead of independent oversight is substantially less burdensome than the regulatory overhead of losing autonomous governance of its own products.
