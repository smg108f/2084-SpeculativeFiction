### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [TRANSDIGM GROUP INCORPORATED]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09TDG
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

TransDigm's Critical classification stems directly from its documented sole-source monopoly pricing strategy, including against U.S. military procurement. Recertification targets the pricing mechanism itself.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Sole-Source Pricing Cap (The Largest Burn)**

- **The Action:** Implement a binding cost-plus pricing ceiling on all sole-source parts sold to government and defense customers, independently audited and published annually, consistent with the standard the DoD Inspector General has previously recommended.
- **The Sacrifice:** Estimated significant reduction in the company's characteristic 53% operating margins on defense-related sole-source parts.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Directly resolves the "Elevated Political Agency" flag tied to documented excessive-pricing findings.

### **2. The Acquisition Strategy Disclosure**

- **The Action:** Publish, prior to completion, the sole-source market-share rationale behind any future acquisition of an aerospace component supplier, subject to antitrust review.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced acquisition-strategy confidentiality.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Prevents further consolidation of monopoly-pricing power without public and regulatory visibility.

### **3. The Debt-Funded Buyback Moratorium**

- **The Action:** Pause debt-funded share buybacks until the $33.7B debt load is reduced to a Bureau-reviewed sustainable ratio relative to operating cash flow.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced shareholder-return flexibility during the deleveraging period.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Reduces the financial fragility risk embedded in the current capital structure.
