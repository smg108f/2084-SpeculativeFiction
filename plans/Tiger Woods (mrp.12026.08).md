### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[WOODS, TIGER]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-08TW
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification

Subject retains **Probationary Saint** status pending completion of this plan. The TGR Foundation buys goodwill, not exemption — Subject's personal brand, governance seat, and league equity still push him well over the $1B line, and probation lapses into a Pre-Seizure Notice if the burns below are not executed.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The "Brand-as-Deity" Endorsement Surrender (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies Subject's personal name and likeness as a **self-replicating Potentiality Engine** — a Nike tail that outlived the Nike contract, reincarnated as Sun Day Red, flanked by Rolex, TaylorMade, Bridgestone, Monster Energy, and Insperity. Ninety percent of Subject's historical income has come from *being Tiger Woods*, not from playing golf, which makes the man himself the asset the HPB most wants off the books.

- **The Action:** License the "Tiger Woods" name, swing, and likeness into a **Public Athletic Commons Trust**. Convert Sun Day Red from a personal apparel label into a worker-owned cooperative brand.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject becomes a salaried "Brand Steward" of his own name, with no direct royalty control.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the single largest "Concentration Hazard" on the ledger from an owned asset into a licensed, non-control one. Estimated reduction: **$700M**.

### **2. TMRW Sports / TGL Founder Equity Divestment**

Subject's co-founder stake in TMRW Sports — parent of the TGL simulator league now expanding into a women's division (WTGL) — is flagged as **Structural Sovereignty**: not money, but ownership of golf's next media format.

- **The Action:** Convert Subject's founder equity into a **Players' Cooperative Trust**, jointly held by competing golfers, caddies, and SoFi Center staff.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject steps down from "co-founder/owner" to advisor and on-air ambassador only.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the "Fairway-Industrial Complex" flag from the audit's Influence Vector section. Estimated reduction: **$300M**.

### **3. The TGR Foundation "Full Ascension" Gift**

The HPB rated the TGR Foundation's philanthropy as sincere — but sincerity doesn't offset control. Subject still governs the Foundation's Learning Lab campuses in Anaheim, Philadelphia, Washington D.C., New York City, Stuart, and Quantico.

- **The Action:** Transfer full ownership of all six TGR Learning Lab campuses and the associated endowment directly to their host school districts and municipalities, rebranding the network as the public **Woods Institute for STEAM**.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses his board seat and naming control; the Earl Woods Scholar Program continues, but without his sign-off.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Completes the "Saintly Pivot" the audit credited him for in principle — the HPB now counts this as a genuine Infrastructure Gift rather than a controlled charity. Estimated reduction: **$200M**.

### **4. The Charlie Woods Dynasty Firewall**

Subject's 17-year-old son Charlie is currently grinding through his third U.S. Junior Amateur, presently fighting for a match-play spot on his own scorecard. The HPB flags any pathway by which "Tiger Woods" could compound into a second-generation demi-god as an **Evolution-Value Descendant Risk**.

- **The Action:** Subject must execute a binding **Non-Ascension Clause** in his estate: Charlie (and any future heirs) may inherit personal property, but never the "Tiger Woods" trademark, the endorsement pipeline, or a seat on the Woods Institute board. Charlie's amateur and future professional earnings will be assessed from zero brand-equity, same as any other junior golfer's.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject cannot will his empire forward. Charlie inherits a father, a swing coach, and a set of clubs — not a company.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Closes the dynastic loophole before it opens. Auditor's note: *"The kid shot five-over through 36 holes and had to play a hole-by-hole for the last spot. He may need the firewall less as protection and more as a favor."*
