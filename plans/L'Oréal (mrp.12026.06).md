### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [L'ORÉAL S.A.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-41LO
**Status:** Precautionary — Beauty Standard Architecture / Supply Chain Labor Obligations

L'Oréal's RTWI of ~$236B places it at **236 times the Demi-God threshold**. MARGINAL classification — light precautionary Burns.

The following **Voluntary Commitment Schedule** must be confirmed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Supply Chain Living Wage Standard**

L'Oréal's product supply chain spans ingredient producers, raw material suppliers, and packaging manufacturers across multiple developing countries. The Bureau requests a binding supply chain wage standard.

- **The Action:** L'Oréal commits to a **Supplier Living Wage Standard** — all tier-1 and tier-2 suppliers in L'Oréal's supply chain must pay regional living wages as a condition of supplier contract. Annual compliance audit published.

---

### **2. The Advertising Standard**

L'Oréal's advertising has historically perpetuated narrow beauty standards despite progress. The Bureau requests a commitment to advertising that does not use digital manipulation to misrepresent product outcomes.

- **The Action:** L'Oréal commits to: (a) no digital skin retouching in advertising for skin-related products; (b) model diversity across skin tones, ages, and body types that reflects the customer populations each brand serves; and (c) transparency labeling on any advertising that uses digital enhancement.
- **Voluntary Sacrifice:** Some advertising production practices must change.

---

### **3. The Science Over Standards Commitment**

L'Oréal's La Roche-Posay and CeraVe brands are clinically grounded. The Bureau requests that this scientific rigor extend to the entity's claims-making across all brands.

- **The Action:** All product efficacy claims across L'Oréal's 36 brands must be substantiated by clinical evidence reviewed by an independent Dermatological Review Board, published on the product website. Marketing claims that cannot be substantiated by published clinical evidence are prohibited.
- **Voluntary Sacrifice:** Some marketing claims require substantiation or removal.

---

### **THE "MORTAL" CONFIRMATION**

To finalize recertification, L'Oréal's CEO must stand before a **Jury of 12 Cosmetics Supply Chain Workers, Beauty Industry Researchers, and Consumers** and confirm:

> _"We are worth it — or we will be, when the supply chain is paid living wages, when the advertising doesn't retouch the skin to misrepresent the product, when every claim we make can be shown to a scientist. Beauty is not what we define. Beauty is what people find in themselves. We make tools that help. We commit to making tools that are honest."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** Escalation to CRITICAL. Advertising claims reviewed by FTC. Supply chain labor practices reviewed by International Labor Organization.
