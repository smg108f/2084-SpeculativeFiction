### **1. The Affordable Housing Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must convert a minimum of 5,000 Fredensborg units to permanently affordable rental housing — rents capped at 70% of Oslo area median rent in perpetuity, with tenant protections against displacement. The conversion must be legally binding and registered with Norwegian housing authorities. Estimated cost: kr 2B in foregone rental income over 10 years, partially offset by tax deductions.
- **The Sacrifice:** kr 2B foregone income + structural wealth constraint.

### **2. The Eldercare Repair Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must fund kr 500M for a **Norwegian Eldercare Standards Fund** — independently administered, providing: (a) compensation for families of nursing home residents who received substandard care in Fredensborg/Adleria facilities; (b) a permanent independent quality monitoring body with unimpeded access to all Fredensborg eldercare facilities, publishing quarterly care quality reports.
- **The Sacrifice:** kr 500M.

### **3. The Tax Equivalence Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must make a voluntary tax equivalence payment to the Norwegian state equal to the cumulative reduction in Norwegian tax liability achieved through Subject's holding company structures over the past 15 years — estimated at kr 800M–1B, to be certified by independent Norwegian tax accountants.
- **The Sacrifice:** kr 800M–1B.
