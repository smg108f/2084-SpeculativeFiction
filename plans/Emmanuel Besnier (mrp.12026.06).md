### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [BESNIER, EMMANUEL]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06EB
**Status:** Urgent

Subject's company contaminated infant formula, was found criminally negligent, and has no philanthropy. The HPB's Burns address the infant formula contamination legacy, food safety standards, and philanthropic absence. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Infant Formula Victims Fund Burn**

In 2017-2018, Lactalis distributed Salmonella-contaminated infant formula that sickened dozens of infants in France and internationally. Criminal negligence was established by French courts. The families of affected infants have not received full adequate compensation, and the contamination's long-term health impacts on affected children have not been fully tracked.

- **The Action:** Establish a **Lactalis Infant Formula Victims Fund** with €100M, focused on: full compensation to all families of infants sickened by the 2017-2018 contamination, ongoing health monitoring for affected children, and a dedicated pediatric health research program on contamination's long-term infant health impacts.
- **The Sacrifice:** €100M and the public acknowledgment of criminal negligence that Lactalis has never publicly issued.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a criminally negligent food safety failure into a completed reparation. The HPB considers this a moral precondition for any further recertification.

### **2. The Food Safety Infrastructure Burn**

Lactalis's 2017 contamination scandal revealed quality control gaps in the world's largest dairy company. The HPB requires these gaps to be addressed publicly and permanently.

- **The Action:** Lactalis to voluntarily adopt and publish an international food safety audit regime — independently verified, publicly reported annually — covering all infant nutrition products globally, with a whistleblower protection mechanism for food safety concerns across all production facilities.
- **The Sacrifice:** Competitive opacity that has been Lactalis's operational signature; cost of comprehensive independent auditing.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the world's largest dairy company from a safety scandal participant into a public transparency standard-setter for the food industry.

### **3. The Foundation Establishment Burn**

At €22B+ and with no philanthropy, Besnier's philanthropic gap is among the most notable in this audit cycle.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Besnier Family Foundation** with €500M endowment, focused on food access and nutrition security — specifically, affordable dairy access in communities where Lactalis operates but where consumers cannot access the products at commercial prices. Independent governance.
- **The Sacrifice:** €500M.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Creates a philanthropic identity connected to the company's product and the communities it serves.
