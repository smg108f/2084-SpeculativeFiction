### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[FROST, PHILLIP]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-08PF
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification (Precautionary)

Subject's Probationary Saint status buys him goodwill, not immunity. To convert forty years of philanthropy into a permanent Mortal designation, Subject must formally sever the "self-referential valuation loop" at OPKO Health and dissolve the interlocking web of micro-cap holdings the Bureau classifies as "Distributed Divinity."

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The OPKO Health Chairmanship Severance (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies Subject's dual role as Chairman/CEO and ~10% owner of OPKO Health as a **"Self-Pricing Violation"** — he sits on the body that sets the valuation of his own net worth. This is the single largest lever on his Divine Footprint.

- **The Action:** Subject must convert his controlling OPKO Health equity into the **Frost Public Diagnostics Trust**, a nonprofit successor entity, and resign all board and executive titles.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of the Chairman/CEO title he has held continuously since founding the company in 2007, and forfeiture of future compensation and control premium tied to his stake.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** He becomes a "Founding Emeritus," not an Owner. The self-referential valuation loop is broken, and an estimated **$1.0B** in controlled equity exits his personal ledger.

### **2. Dissolution of the "Micro-Cap Constellation"**

Subject's long tail of personal angel stakes in small South Florida biotech and diagnostics issuers is the exact structure the Bureau's actuaries flag as enabling both Distributed Divinity and, historically, price manipulation.

- **The Action:** Subject must liquidate or donate all personal holdings in non-OPKO microcap issuers into the **Open Biotech Commons**, a public research-funding pool with mandatory disclosure and no individual board influence.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of the "seed-and-cross-pollinate" mechanism that has generated the bulk of his opportunistic gains since the 1990s, and any remaining informal influence over penny-stock issuers not already covered by his 2018 SEC bar.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This closes the **Distributed Divinity** finding entirely. An estimated **$700M** in aggregate micro-cap value is removed from his personal balance sheet.

### **3. The "Penny Stock Penance"**

Because Subject's 2018 federal settlement (a $5.5M payment and a bar from most penny stock offerings) resolved his legal exposure but not the Bureau's reputational concerns, a public rebuttal act is required.

- **The Action:** Subject must personally fund and lend his name to the **"Frost Investor Protection Initiative,"** a $50M program teaching retail investors and medical students to identify pump-and-dump structures, headquartered at the University of Miami.
- **The Requirement:** He must give one annual public address acknowledging the mechanics of the scheme he settled, in plain, non-legal language, to a general audience.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This neutralizes the **"Micro-Cap Alchemist"** finding and converts a liability into a teaching credential — fitting, for a man the Bureau's archetype system already classifies as a "Teacher."

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Pharmacists and Nurses** and recite the following:

> _"I once measured a cure by its ticker price, and called a stranger's hope a 'position.' I sold shares in companies I built from nothing but my own name, and let others sell the panic after. Let the record show: I own nothing now but the coat on my back, and I will not price a pill, or a patient, ever again."_

All museum naming rights will be revoked pending nationalization review, OPKO Health will be placed under Federal Public Trust administration, and Subject — at 89 — will be remanded not to Mars, but to the nearer and gentler **Bahamas Biotech Flotilla**, a Sovereign research colony off the Florida coast where aging demi-god pharma founders trade patents like relics and never again have to explain a Form 4.
