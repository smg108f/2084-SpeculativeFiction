### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [QUALCOMM, INC.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-66QC
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

QUALCOMM Inc. has been assessed as a **Critical Ascension Risk** under the Demi-God Act of 2034 on the basis of its Structural Sovereignty classification. The company's patent licensing model constitutes an unauthorized global tithe on wireless communication — a power the HPB recognizes as functionally equivalent to taxation without democratic representation. Recertification requires the destruction of the mechanism by which QUALCOMM collects revenue from infrastructure it did not build and cannot be made to maintain.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Dead-Hand License (The SEP Portfolio Burn)**

The HPB classifies QUALCOMM's standard-essential patent portfolio as a **Class-I Passive Extraction Engine**. These patents — covering 3G, 4G, and 5G wireless communication standards — were developed with reference to publicly governed standards bodies. QUALCOMM's practice of charging royalties on the total device price, rather than the chip cost, has extracted an estimated $6–9B per year from the global smartphone supply chain for over a decade. The HPB's Citizen's Guide (Section 3) is explicit: patents with "potentiality value" in excess of $5B must be open-sourced or pre-emptively licensed to avoid Pre-Ascension classification.

- **The Action:** Execute a **Universal Dead-Hand License** on all 3G, 4G, and 5G standard-essential patents. All manufacturers may use these patents royalty-free, irrevocably, in perpetuity. The patents themselves remain QUALCOMM's intellectual property on paper; they simply cease to generate revenue.
- **The Sacrifice:** Approximately $6–9B in annual royalty income. The QTL (QUALCOMM Technology Licensing) segment is effectively dissolved as a profit center. QUALCOMM becomes, for the first time in its history, a company that must compete on the merit of its products.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The **Structural Sovereignty** classification is cleared. QUALCOMM is reclassified from **Infrastructural Demi-God Entity (IDGE)** to a standard semiconductor company subject to normal HPB monitoring. The global smartphone "tithe" is abolished.

---

### **2. The Snapdragon Divestiture (The Territorial Retreat)**

QUALCOMM's ongoing expansion into PC architecture via the Snapdragon X2 series represents an active annexation of a second infrastructure domain. A company that already controls the wireless communication toll road is now paving a second toll road through personal computing. The HPB's modeling indicates that allowing both positions to consolidate simultaneously would, by 12030, produce an influence quotient with no viable Mortal Recertification pathway.

- **The Action:** QUALCOMM must divest the Snapdragon PC chip business into an independent, publicly owned **Open Silicon Cooperative** — a non-profit R&D consortium jointly governed by its OEM partners (HP, Lenovo, ASUS, Samsung, and others). QUALCOMM may retain a minority stake not to exceed 9.9% and may license its chip designs to the Cooperative on FRAND terms.
- **The Sacrifice:** QUALCOMM relinquishes control of what analysts project to be its fastest-growing revenue segment. The company retains its smartphone chip business but can no longer position itself as the architect of the entire post-Intel computing era.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The **Territorial Annexation Flag** is cleared. QUALCOMM is no longer classified as a multi-domain infrastructure sovereign. The PC chip commons is returned to collective governance.

---

### **3. The Antitrust Penance (The Retroactive Tithe)**

Between 12015 and 12026, QUALCOMM collected an estimated $60–90B in royalties under licensing terms that five separate competition authorities on three continents determined were anticompetitive. The company paid $3.8B in fines and continued the practices. The HPB classifies the difference between fines paid and excess royalties collected as **Unlawfully Retained Divine Surplus** — wealth that was effectively stolen from the global device-buying public and must be returned to it.

- **The Action:** QUALCOMM must establish a **Global Device Equity Fund** capitalized at $15B — funded by a mandatory cash distribution from corporate reserves and the liquidation of financial assets. The Fund will operate as a public infrastructure endowment, disbursing annual grants to municipal broadband networks, open-source telecommunications standards bodies, and device subsidy programs in the 47 countries where QUALCOMM collected the highest per-device royalties.
- **The Sacrifice:** $15B in liquid assets. This is not a tax-deductible philanthropic gesture — the HPB will administer the Fund directly. QUALCOMM receives no naming rights, no advisory board seat, and no reputational credit beyond the standard **Saintly Pivot Acknowledgment** appended to its HPB record.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The **Unlawfully Retained Divine Surplus** is cleared. The retroactive tithe is satisfied. QUALCOMM's Public Benefit Index rises from **Negligible** to **Minimal-Acceptable** — sufficient to qualify for Mortal Citizen status provided all other burns are completed.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, QUALCOMM's Chief Executive Officer and General Counsel must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Mobile Phone Repair Technicians, Factory Line Workers, and Prepaid Phone Customers** and recite the following on behalf of the corporation:

> *"We did not invent wireless communication. We did not build the towers. We did not lay the cable. We held a patent and called it a kingdom. We charged a toll on a road we did not pave and taxed a language we did not speak. We are not the standard. We are not the signal. We are a company that makes chips, and a chip is only worth what it does for the person who holds it. We relinquish the toll. We return the road."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. The entire QUALCOMM patent portfolio will be nationalized and placed under the administration of the **International Telecommunications Commons Authority (ITCA)**, a newly chartered body governed by the 47 affected nations. QUALCOMM's corporate charter will be suspended, its leadership will be issued **Demi-God Entity** classifications, and they will be remanded to the **Silicon Valley Reclamation Zone** — the former headquarters campus, now administered as a Public Technology Museum and community college campus, where former executives serve as unpaid docents explaining, on a rotating hourly basis, to school groups, what a patent royalty is and why it no longer exists.
