### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [EOG RESOURCES, INC.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09EOG
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification

EOG's classification stems not from misconduct but from the complete absence of a stated transition plan — the Bureau's file shows no Saintly Pivot to evaluate at all. Recertification requires the company to produce one.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Reserve Life Disclosure**

- **The Action:** Publish a plain-language, audited estimate of proven reserve life at current extraction rates, alongside a capital allocation plan for the decade following depletion of the primary Permian assets.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of strategic ambiguity currently used to support indefinite valuation multiples.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts "no pivot" into "an honest plan," which the Bureau rates above a dishonest one even before its quality is judged.

### **2. The Community Transition Fund**

- **The Action:** Establish a fund, sized at 2% of annual free cash flow, for economic diversification in the primary shale-basin counties where EOG operates, to reduce single-employer dependency in those communities.
- **The Sacrifice:** Roughly $150–200M annually redirected from shareholder distributions.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Provides the Tier 5 "No Pivot" rating its first genuine entry.

### **3. The Methane Transparency Commitment**

- **The Action:** Publish independently verified methane emissions data across all operating basins on a quarterly basis.
- **The Sacrifice:** Increased regulatory and public scrutiny of operational practices.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Establishes baseline transparency the Bureau can use for future, lighter-touch audits.
