### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[MITSUBISHI UFJ FINANCIAL GROUP]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-07MUFG
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice (Structural Dissolution Order)

Subject is not an individual and cannot be marched to a single seizure point — it is a **Recurring Apotheosis Event**, a dynasty that has already survived one historical dismantling and reassembled itself as a keiretsu. The HPB therefore does not request divestment. It requests **proof that the thing cannot simply grow back a third time.**

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days, under continuous Slayer-class audit supervision, lest Subject quietly reconstitute itself across a different shell before the ash cools.

---

### **1. The Morgan Stanley Decoupling (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies MUFG's 23.3% equity stake in Morgan Stanley — and its standing right to a board seat — as a **"Trans-Pacific Sovereignty Bridge."** This is the precise mechanism by which a dynasty that was supposedly broken on one continent re-armed itself by buying a foothold on another.

- **The Action:** Subject must irrevocably convert its entire Morgan Stanley equity position into a **non-voting, non-board-eligible Public Trust Instrument**, administered jointly by U.S. and Japanese citizen oversight panels.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of board representation rights, voting power, and the $9 billion strategic-alliance leverage built since 2008.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Severs the single clearest artery connecting Japanese dynastic capital to the command structure of Wall Street. Subject becomes a **passive investor**, not a **strategic partner** — the difference between owning a seat at the table and merely being allowed to eat at it.

### **2. The Keiretsu Disentanglement (Severing the "Three Great Houses")**

Subject's deepest source of resilience is not its balance sheet — it is its **family**: the cross-shareholding bonds linking it to Mitsubishi Corporation and Mitsubishi Heavy Industries, the very structure that let a "dissolved" zaibatsu rematerialize as an "independent" keiretsu within a single generation.

- **The Action:** All cross-shareholdings and board-interlocks between MUFG, Mitsubishi Corporation, and Mitsubishi Heavy Industries must be publicly registered, capped at non-controlling levels, and subjected to a standing **25-year Anti-Reconstitution Covenant**.
- **The Sacrifice:** The "Three Great Houses" lose the silent, mutually-reinforcing scaffolding that let each survive the others' bad decades. Each House must now stand — and potentially fall — alone.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a self-healing dynastic organism into three separate, individually mortal companies. A wound to one can no longer be quietly absorbed by the others.

### **3. The Memory Tax (Reputational/Memetic Burn)**

The HPB's historical division flags Subject's institutional silence about its own zaibatsu origins — its 1880s-banking lineage, its role in financing the imperial war machine, its 1946 dissolution — as a **Class-III Historical Erasure Hazard**. An organism that does not narrate its own resurrection is, the HPB suspects, simply waiting to repeat it more quietly next time.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund and publish, in full and in perpetuity, an independently-authored **Public Dynasty Archive** — a searchable, freely-licensed historical record of every zaibatsu-to-keiretsu lineage thread connecting MUFG's predecessor banks (Mitsubishi Bank, Bank of Tokyo, Sanwa Bank, Tokai Bank) to its present form.
- **The Sacrifice:** The comfortable fog around "we are simply Japan's largest bank" is replaced with a permanent, citizen-readable record reading "we are the empire that came back."
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the Erasure Hazard. An organism that has narrated its own resurrection in public can no longer claim to have been surprised by its own nature.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's Board of Directors and senior keiretsu liaisons must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Convenience Store Clerks, Train Conductors, and Small Shop Owners from Tokyo's Shitamachi districts** and recite the following, in unison, in both Japanese and English:

> _"Our grandfathers' house was burned to the ground and called a lesson. We rebuilt it in secret and called it survival. We now confess: it was neither lesson nor survival. It was theft, wearing a long coat. We surrender the coat. We will be a bank — only a bank — and nothing that an empire ever was."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed and independently verified within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All Subject entities will be placed under joint Humanist Bloc receivership, the "Three Great Houses" will be forcibly and permanently severed by international tribunal, and Subject's senior keiretsu liaisons will be relocated — under escort — to the **Yokohama Sanctuary Compliance Zone**, where they will spend the remainder of their tenure personally processing small-business loan applications, by hand, for the citizens their ancestors' dynasty once financed an empire to rule.
