### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [CAPSULE CORPORATION]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-13-CC
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice / Infrastructure Dependency Complicates Standard Enforcement

Capsule Corporation has been classified CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK based on a $3.3 trillion market capitalization, a compression technology monopoly that functions as de facto public infrastructure, and private family access to Dragon Ball wish-granting with no public accountability framework. The HPB acknowledges that standard asset seizure is not a viable enforcement mechanism here — disrupting Capsule Corp production would cause economic harm disproportionate to the compliance benefit. The Bureau has designed this plan accordingly: the goal is structural accountability, not operational disruption.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. Hoi Poi Capsule Technology — Open Standard and Public Utility Designation**

The Hoi Poi Capsule's patent portfolio is the foundation of Capsule Corporation's market position and the mechanism through which it extracts revenue from a technology that has become essential infrastructure. The HPB requires that the core compression and restoration technology be transitioned to a public utility framework — not seized, but regulated — with pricing controls, universal access mandates, and an open standards process for compatible technology development.

- **The Action:** Capsule Corporation must file the core Hoi Poi Capsule patents for Public Utility Designation under the Infrastructure Commons Act of 12022, which entitles the company to a regulated return on the technology (capped at 8% annual return on development cost) while mandating universal access pricing, interoperability standards for third-party capsule development, and a public licensing mechanism for humanitarian applications. The company retains manufacturing advantages, brand value, and innovation premium on next-generation products — it loses the monopoly on the foundational technology.
- **The Sacrifice:** Capsule Corp loses exclusive control of the technology that built it. Competitors can now manufacture compatible capsule products. Capsule Corp's market share is expected to decline from near-total to an estimated 55–65% as the market develops.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Infrastructure Dependency classification removed. RTWI decreases by an estimated $1.2 trillion as the monopoly premium is eliminated from the company's market valuation.

---

### **2. Dragon Radar and Dragon Ball Access — HPB Registry and Wish Oversight Protocol**

The Brief family's Dragon Radar technology provides them with a systematic capability to locate and collect Dragon Balls ahead of other parties, and their historical record includes multiple instances of wish deployment that have affected global populations without those populations' consent or input. The HPB cannot regulate Dragon Balls — it has no jurisdiction over Namekian magical artifacts — but it can regulate the Brief family's systematic advantage in accessing them.

- **The Action:** Dragon Radar technology must be open-sourced under a Creative Commons license, eliminating the Brief family's exclusive advantage in Dragon Ball location. Additionally, the Brief family must enter into a Wish Deployment Covenant committing to: (1) public advance notice of any intended Dragon Ball wish that would affect more than 100,000 people, with a 30-day public comment period; and (2) post-wish disclosure of all wishes made, filed with the HPB within 7 days.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Brief family loses its exclusive access to the most powerful wish-granting mechanism in the Jump Universe. Their Dragon Ball use becomes, for the first time, subject to public accountability — which means they can still use them, but they can no longer use them unilaterally.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Dragon Ball Integration classification removed from risk profile. The HPB notes that this is the first Wish Deployment Covenant in Bureau history and has assigned it its own case number.

---

### **3. Capsule Corp Board Restructuring — Public Interest Directorship**

Capsule Corporation is a publicly traded company whose governance does not reflect the public utility character its technology has assumed. The Brief family retains effective control through share structure and board composition. The HPB requires that the board be restructured to include genuine independent oversight with public interest mandates.

- **The Action:** The board must be restructured to include: (1) three independent directors appointed by a public nominating committee with no relationship to the Brief family; (2) one HPB-designated infrastructure monitor with non-voting advisory status; and (3) an annual public technology impact report, prepared by an independent assessor, examining the company's pricing, access, and innovation decisions relative to public welfare. The Brief family retains majority shareholder rights and executive management roles. They lose the ability to govern without public-interest accountability.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Brief family accepts external oversight of a company they have always operated as a family enterprise. Future board decisions will be made in the presence of voices the family did not choose.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Governance risk classification reduced. RTWI impact is structural — the public utility designation and board restructuring reduce the company's premium valuation as a pure private enterprise.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, the Brief family must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 small business owners** whose logistics operations depend on Capsule Corp technology — specifically drawn from operators who have been priced out of premium capsule tiers and rely on base-model products — and recite the following:

> _"We invented a technology that made it easier to own things, and then we made sure that owning it well required paying us. We told ourselves this was fair because we invented it. Inventors deserve to be compensated. They do not deserve to own infrastructure. The capsule was a product. The infrastructure it became was built by every person who changed how they lived to use it — and those people had no say in what we charged them for the privilege. We are opening the standard. We are publishing the radar. We are letting someone else check our wishes. We should have offered all of this sooner. We were comfortable, and comfortable is not the same as right."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Asset Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol — Infrastructure Monopoly Tier**, which includes forced Public Utility Designation of all Hoi Poi Capsule patents, mandatory price controls on all capsule product tiers effective immediately, and referral of Dragon Ball wish history to the Jump Universe Metaphysical Accountability Commission for retroactive impact assessment. The HPB notes that the last action will produce a very long document. The Bureau is prepared to publish it.
