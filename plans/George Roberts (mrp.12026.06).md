### **SHARED BURN (ROBERTS' SHARE): The Toys"R"Us Worker Restitution**

The $600-per-worker severance contribution following public pressure is not proportionate restitution for 33,000 workers who lost jobs due to KKR's debt-loading strategy.

- **The Action (Roberts' half):** Subject must fund $75M (his share of a $150M total, coordinated with Kravis) for the **Toys"R"Us Worker Legacy Fund** — directed to the 33,000 workers who lost jobs in the 2017 closure: (a) $50M in direct supplemental severance, distributed to identifiable former Toys"R"Us workers; (b) $25M for retail worker retraining programs in the communities most affected by the closures.
- **Roberts' Sacrifice:** $75M.

### **INDIVIDUAL BURN: The LBO Worker Harm Systemic Burn**

KKR's broader LBO portfolio has displaced workers across dozens of transactions over five decades.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish and fund $150M for the **KKR Worker Legacy Fund** — an independently governed fund that provides supplemental severance and retraining support for workers displaced in KKR portfolio company restructurings, applied retroactively to all KKR transactions from 2000 to the present, with a binding commitment to contribute 0.5% of KKR management fee revenue to the fund in perpetuity.
- **The Sacrifice:** $150M + perpetual contribution commitment.
