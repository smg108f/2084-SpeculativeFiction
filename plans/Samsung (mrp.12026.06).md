### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [SAMSUNG ELECTRONICS CO., LTD.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-21SE
**Status:** Critical — Chaebol State Capture / Corporate Governance Immunity

Samsung's RTWI of $1.54T places it at **1,540 times the Demi-God threshold**, with an additional **Chaebol Immunity** designation for demonstrated state capture. This case requires Humanist Bloc coordination with South Korean authorities — the Bureau's jurisdictional reach is limited by Samsung's Korean domicile, but the entity's global operations and semiconductor strategic importance bring it within Humanist Bloc enforcement scope.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Chaebol Cross-Shareholding Dissolution**

Samsung Group's cross-shareholding structure — the web of inter-company ownership that gives the Lee family effective control over Samsung Electronics, Samsung Life Insurance, Samsung C&T, and dozens of subsidiaries without majority economic ownership of any single entity — is the mechanism by which the Lee family maintains governance control without proportionate ownership accountability. The Bureau classifies this as **Governance Opacity by Design**: corporate structure engineered to make accountability structurally inaccessible.

- **The Action:** Samsung must comply with the Korean Fair Trade Commission's outstanding requirements for chaebol structure reform: the circular cross-shareholdings between Samsung Group entities must be unwound within 5 years, with each entity's governance standing independently on the basis of actual shareholder ownership rather than cross-holding manipulation. Lee family control of Samsung Electronics must be restructured to reflect their actual economic ownership share.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Lee family loses the governance control premium generated by the cross-shareholding structure. Their effective voting power over Samsung Electronics is reduced to reflect their actual economic ownership — approximately 4–5% by some estimates — rather than the 30%+ effective control the cross-shareholding structure produces.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Resolves the **Governance Opacity** classification. Samsung Electronics is governed by its actual shareholders rather than a family cross-holding structure designed to perpetuate dynastic control.

---

### **2. The Accountability Without Immunity Standard**

The Bureau's most specific requirement for Samsung is the one most resistant to conventional corporate enforcement: the Korean state must commit — and Samsung Group must contractually bind itself to support — a governance framework in which Samsung executives convicted of crimes serve their sentences without executive pardons.

- **The Action:** Samsung must execute a **Corporate Accountability Covenant** with the South Korean government and the Humanist Bloc Trade Authority as a condition of Samsung's continued access to Humanist Bloc semiconductor equipment (ASML EUV machines, advanced process chemicals). The Covenant commits: (a) Samsung will not seek, and its affiliated entities will not lobby for, executive pardons for convictions related to corporate governance, bribery, or market manipulation; and (b) Samsung's audit and governance committees will include mandatory independent representation from non-Korean Humanist Bloc institutional shareholders with real oversight authority.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Lee family's immunity from legal accountability — which has been exercised repeatedly over decades — is contractually foreclosed.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Resolves the **Chaebol Immunity** classification. The entity that is too important to fail is not permitted to be too important to be accountable.

---

### **3. The Memory Monopoly Access Standard**

Samsung's dominance of DRAM and NAND memory chip markets gives it pricing power over a component essential to every computing device. As with TSMC, the Bureau requires that strategic semiconductor access obligations accompany strategic semiconductor dominance.

- **The Action:** Samsung must establish a **Memory Access Priority Program** — reserving 10% of production capacity at cost-plus pricing for academic research institutions, medical device manufacturers, and public interest technology organizations, parallel to the TSMC standard (HPB-12026-10TS Burn 3). Memory pricing for non-reserve commercial customers must be published transparently with cost accounting available to the Humanist Bloc Trade Authority on request.
- **The Sacrifice:** 10% of Samsung's highest-margin product lines reserved for public interest use.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Establishes that strategic semiconductor resources carry public interest obligations regardless of domicile.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Lee Jae-yong and the Samsung Board must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Samsung Memory Chip Customers Who Were Price-Squeezed During Shortage Periods, Former Samsung Employees Who Reported Governance Irregularities, and Korean Anti-Corruption Prosecutors Who Investigated Samsung** and recite the following:

> _"My grandfather built Samsung from a grocery store. My father made it a global electronics empire. I inherited it, ran it through a corruption trial, and was pardoned because the government decided the company was more important than my sentence. I am not more important than my sentence. No one is. The cross-shareholding structure that makes the family's control look like governance ends. The accountability covenant is real. The next conviction, if there is one, will be served. The memory chips will have a public access price. We are Samsung Electronics. We are a company. We are accountable to our shareholders and to the law. In that order."_

The Bureau notes that Samsung's global supply chain depends on Humanist Bloc cooperation in ways that make extraterritorial enforcement more viable in this case than in any other non-U.S. entity in the current caseload.
