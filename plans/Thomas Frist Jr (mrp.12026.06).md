### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [FRIST JR., THOMAS]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06TF
**Status:** Urgent — Healthcare Accountability

Subject is age 86, chairman emeritus of HCA Healthcare. Age accommodation applied: extended timelines, estate/foundation execution acceptable. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be initiated within 120 days.

---

### **1. The Medicare Fraud Remediation Burn**

HCA paid $1.7B to settle federal Medicare and Medicaid fraud charges in 2003 — the largest such settlement in U.S. history at the time. The HPB notes that this was a company settlement, not a personal one, but that Frist was the leader who built the company culture.

- **The Action:** Establish the **HCA Community Care Remediation Fund**: a $500M personal or family foundation contribution directed to federally qualified health centers (FQHCs) and community health centers serving uninsured and underinsured patients in markets where HCA operates hospitals. Fund must operate independently of HCA corporate.
- **The Sacrifice:** $500M; acknowledgment of the Medicare fraud era.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the largest Medicare fraud settlement in U.S. history into a named remediation program for the communities most harmed by cost-shifting behaviors.

### **2. The Hospital Affordability Standard Burn**

HCA hospitals have been repeatedly documented charging uninsured patients significantly higher prices than insured patients, and aggressively pursuing debt collection.

- **The Action:** Subject must use his shareholder and chairman emeritus position to advocate formally for HCA to adopt a binding **Charity Care Minimum Standard**: no HCA hospital may collect more than 10% of household income from any uninsured patient; all active debt collection suits against patients with household income below 300% of poverty line must be dropped. Annual compliance report.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced revenue from high-margin uninsured billing.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the largest for-profit hospital system in America into a participant in, rather than a driver of, the American healthcare affordability crisis.

### **3. The Physician Ethics Burn**

Thomas Frist Jr. trained as a cardiac surgeon. He made a specific choice to convert medical practice into financialized hospital management.

- **The Action:** Fund and deliver a **Frist Physician Ethics Endowment** at Vanderbilt School of Medicine — $100M to establish a permanent chair and annual symposium on the ethics of physician engagement with for-profit healthcare. The symposium must include voices critical of the HCA model. The endowment must not restrict the perspectives taught.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M; the discomfort of funding a platform that may critique his own legacy.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the founder of for-profit hospital medicine into the funder of the most substantive academic scrutiny of that model.
