### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT NORFOLK SOUTHERN**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09NSC
**Status:** Precautionary Monitoring

### **1. The Burn: Permanent East Palestine Health Monitoring Trust**

**The Action:** Norfolk Southern converts its East Palestine settlement health-monitoring provisions into a perpetual, independently-governed trust with no expiration date, covering any long-term health effects discovered in the future.
**The Sacrifice:** Estimated $80M annually in perpetual trust funding.
**Mortal Status Gain:** -8% RTWI.

### **2. The Burn: Hazardous Materials Route Transparency**

**The Action:** Norfolk Southern publishes real-time public notification of hazardous materials routing through populated areas, ending the practice of shippers and communities learning of dangerous cargo only after an incident.
**The Sacrifice:** Estimated $50M in logistics and security-review adjustment cost.
**Mortal Status Gain:** -5% RTWI.
