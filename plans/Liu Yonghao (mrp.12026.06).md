### **1. The Agribusiness Environmental Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must fund ¥3B for a **New Hope Sustainable Agriculture Transition Fund** — converting New Hope's largest pig and poultry operations to closed-loop waste treatment within 5 years; funding methane capture systems at all major feedlot facilities; establishing a Chinese agricultural carbon accounting standard and committing New Hope to net-zero operations by 12035.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥3B.

### **2. The Rural Smallholder Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must establish ¥1B for a **Chinese Smallholder Agriculture Support Fund** — providing technical assistance, access to credit, and market linkage for small-scale farmers in Sichuan, Hunan, and Guangxi who compete against (or depend upon) New Hope's industrial scale, ensuring that the concentration of agribusiness in New Hope does not eliminate the smallholder sector that launched Liu's career.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥1B.
