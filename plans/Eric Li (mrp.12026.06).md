### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [LI, ERIC (LI SHUFU)]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06EL
**Status:** Urgent — Automotive Empire / Geopolitical Footprint

Subject is the founder of Geely Automobile and controlling shareholder of Volvo, Polestar, ZEEKR, Lotus, and London EV Company. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 60 days.

---

### **1. The Daimler Governance Resolution Burn**

Li Shufu's acquisition of a significant Daimler stake as a private Chinese national, without prior disclosure to German regulators, triggered significant concern. The HPB asks for a governance resolution.

- **The Action:** File with the HPB a **Daimler Transparency Charter**: a formal disclosure of all beneficial ownership, voting agreements, and communication channels between Li Shufu's Daimler stake and any agency of the Chinese government; plus a commitment to independent shareholder representation at Daimler with no state direction. If Li cannot or will not make this disclosure, he must divest the Daimler stake to a neutral third party within 180 days.
- **The Sacrifice:** Either transparency about the Daimler position or the position itself.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Resolves the most significant unresolved geopolitical concern in the Geely portfolio.

### **2. The Geely EV Transition Commitment Burn**

The Geely empire spans legacy ICE and EV brands. The HPB asks for a binding transition commitment.

- **The Action:** Publish a binding **Geely Group EV Transition Charter** with specific, time-bound commitments: (a) the date by which Geely-brand vehicles will be fully EV-only (not including hybrid); (b) Volvo's all-electric timeline confirmed with no delays; (c) Polestar and ZEEKR production volume targets over a 5-year period; (d) all Geely factories' decarbonization schedule. Must be independently audited annually.
- **The Sacrifice:** Flexibility to delay the ICE sunset if profitable.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the world's most globally diversified Chinese automaker into a committed EV-transition actor with public accountability.

### **3. The Automotive Worker Legacy Burn**

Geely's global workforce spans multiple countries and automotive traditions. Volvo, Lotus, and London black cab workers have labor relations histories worth acknowledging.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Geely Global Worker Legacy Fund** — $200M endowment across Geely's global workforce countries — funding: (a) worker retraining for the EV transition for all affected ICE-line workers; (b) pension enhancement for legacy workers at Volvo and London EV Company; (c) worker equity participation programs at ZEEKR and Polestar.
- **The Sacrifice:** $200M.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the owner of five automotive brands into the first Chinese automaker to establish a global worker legacy fund.
