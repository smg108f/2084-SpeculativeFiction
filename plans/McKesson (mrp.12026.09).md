### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [MCKESSON CORPORATION]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09MCK
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

---

### **1. The Real-Time Diversion Monitoring Mandate (The Largest Burn)**

- **The Action:** Implement and publicly report on real-time, independently-audited controlled-substance diversion monitoring across all distribution routes, with automatic regulatory flagging of anomalous volume patterns to any single pharmacy or region.
- **The Sacrifice:** Significant technology and compliance investment; reduced distribution-speed flexibility.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Directly addresses the structural vulnerability that enabled the opioid crisis, independent of settlement payments already made.

### **2. The Settlement Transparency Filing**

- **The Action:** Publish an annual, plain-language accounting of opioid settlement fund disbursement and measured public-health outcomes in the communities receiving it.
- **The Sacrifice:** Continued public visibility into the crisis's ongoing cost and remediation progress.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a court-compelled payment into a genuinely monitored public benefit.

### **3. The Distribution Margin Disclosure**

- **The Action:** Publish standardized distribution-margin data to independent pharmacies and hospital systems dependent on McKesson with limited alternative supplier access.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced pricing opacity across differentiated customer segments.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Addresses the "Elevated Political Agency" flag by reducing informational leverage over dependent customers.
