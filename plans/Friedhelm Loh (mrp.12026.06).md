### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [LOH, FRIEDHELM]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06FL
**Status:** Standard

Subject's existing philanthropy is credited. Burns scale it proportionately and extend it to the workers and regions most directly connected to the Loh Group's operations.

---

### **1. The Mittelstand Worker Investment Burn**

The Friedhelm Loh Group employs 12,000 people. The Mittelstand model values long-term employment. The HPB asks for structural investment in the workforce that made Rittal the world's enclosure leader.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish the **Friedhelm Loh Foundation for Skilled Trades** — €100M — dedicated to: (a) vocational training for industrial skilled trades (Ausbildung) in the Hesse region, expanding the pool of electrical engineers, metalworkers, and automation technicians who supply Rittal and the broader industrial ecosystem; (b) apprenticeship scholarships for young people from disadvantaged backgrounds entering skilled trades; (c) international skills transfer — bringing Rittal's Mittelstand apprenticeship model to its manufacturing sites in India, China, and Eastern Europe, where apprenticeship programs are less developed.
- **The Sacrifice:** €100M (~$110M).

### **2. The Digital Industrial Commons Burn**

Eplan's engineering software is proprietary. The HPB asks for a public goods dimension.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund €50M for an **Open Industrial Engineering Education Initiative** — making CAD/electrical engineering design tools freely available to vocational students and engineering schools in Germany and globally, as an open-access licensed version of Eplan or a comparable tool. The Mittelstand model depends on a pipeline of skilled engineers; that pipeline should not be behind a commercial paywall for those who cannot afford it.
- **The Sacrifice:** €50M (~$55M).

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Rittal Apprentices, Hesse Vocational School Instructors, and Rittal Factory Workers from India and China**:

> *"I have built this company on the belief that quality matters — in the product, in the company, in how you treat the people who work for you. The Mittelstand tradition is not just a business model; it is a covenant with the region and the workforce. The Foundation scales what I believe: skilled trades are honourable work that deserves investment and not an afterthought. The open engineering education initiative means the tools that Eplan sells to industry are available to the students who need to learn them. Faith requires stewardship. This is mine."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** Subject will be assigned to **FSS RITTAL** — a vessel whose entire electrical infrastructure lives inside Rittal enclosures (properly rated for marine environments), whose bridge engineering systems run on Eplan schematics (open-access version), crewed by Rittal apprentices completing their industrial apprenticeship at sea — one rotation per year, twelve apprentices per voyage, learning what the enclosures they build actually house, and why precision matters when the sea is outside the cabinet.
