# Mortal Recertification Plan: Xue Min

**Case File:** HPB-MRP-XM-12026.09

## The Three Burns

### Burn 1: Pandemic Windfall Disclosure

**Action:** Xue Min must publish a full accounting of United Imaging's revenue and margin growth attributable specifically to pandemic-era emergency procurement, separated from ordinary commercial growth, audited by an independent Bureau-approved reviewer.

**Sacrifice:** Reputational — the "self-sufficiency pioneer" narrative must coexist publicly with the windfall accounting.

**Mortal Status Gain:** The public learns exactly how much of the fortune is emergency markup versus earned engineering value.

### Burn 2: Equipment Access Pricing Floor

**Action:** United Imaging must establish and publicly bind itself to a tiered pricing structure guaranteeing lower-tier and rural Chinese hospitals access to core diagnostic equipment at cost or below, enforced by contract rather than goodwill.

**Sacrifice:** A defined percentage of gross margin on domestic sales, permanently.

**Mortal Status Gain:** The chokepoint stops being a chokepoint for the hospitals least able to pay premium prices.

### Burn 3: Personal Equity Reduction

**Action:** Xue Min must reduce her personal controlling stake in United Imaging below the threshold that constitutes unilateral control, distributing the difference to a public health access trust.

**Sacrifice:** Direct control over pricing and allocation decisions.

**Mortal Status Gain:** No single person retains personal veto power over who gets diagnostic equipment and at what price.
