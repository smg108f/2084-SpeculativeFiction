- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the founding act of Russian oligarchy's archetypal case into a documented acknowledgment and partial remediation.

### **2. The Lukoil Peace Advocacy Burn**

*(Activates upon Precondition satisfaction)*

The board's 2022 peace statement was unusual and notable. The HPB asks Alekperov to use his remaining shareholder influence to sustain and amplify this position.

- **The Action:** File a shareholder resolution through his Lukoil stake urging the company to: (a) maintain the 2022 peace position in all available venues; (b) advocate internally for no use of company assets or contracts in support of active hostilities; (c) commit to publishing a corporate peace position annually until hostilities cease.
