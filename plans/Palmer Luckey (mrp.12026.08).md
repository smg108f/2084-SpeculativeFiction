### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[LUCKEY, PALMER]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-08PL
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject's fortune is not sitting in a bank account waiting to be taxed — it is sitting in a 5-million-square-foot factory waiting to ship. The HPB considers this a Priority-1 case: unlike passive capital, Subject's "Divine Footprint" manufactures autonomous weapons in real time. Recertification is required immediately to sever Subject's personal control over sovereign-grade war-making infrastructure.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The "Lattice OS" Sovereign Divestiture (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies privately-controlled military AI operating systems as **"Potentiality Engines" of the highest order** — Subject's net worth is inflated not by what Anduril has built, but by what it is *predicted* to build for a nervous world.

- **The Action:** Subject must convert his controlling founder equity in Anduril Industries into a **Blind Deterrence Trust**, administered by a rotating, lottery-selected citizen oversight board with no continuing input from Subject.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of board control, voting shares, and the "Founder Premium" that currently props up Anduril's $61B private valuation. Subject retains no say in Lattice OS targeting logic, procurement strategy, or the Arsenal-1 production schedule.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject ceases to be the sole architect of a privately-owned arsenal. Anduril becomes a quasi-public defense utility rather than a personal fiefdom, and an estimated **$4.0B** in equity value is struck from Subject's personal ledger.

### **2. Dissolution of the GOP Megadonor Network**

The 2084 Code forbids Human Citizens from holding simultaneous positions as arms manufacturer and political kingmaker to the government that buys the arms — a conflict the HPB refers to as "Selling to Yourself."

- **The Action:** Immediate and permanent dissolution of all political donation vehicles, joint fundraising committee contributions, and shell-company inauguration gifts (see: "Wings of Time"). Redirected funds are transferred to the **Veterans' Digital Rehabilitation Trust**, a nonpartisan program using surplus VR hardware for wounded-veteran physical therapy.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses direct access to Senate leadership committees and any future administration's transition team. His "Pentagon-Adjacent Power Broker" status is permanently revoked.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This clears the **"Shadow Cabinet" Violation**. Subject may still hold personal political opinions — he simply may no longer fund the people who decide whether to buy his products.

### **3. The Mythic Branding Surrender (Reputational Penance)**

The HPB's Memetics Division notes that Subject named his weapons company after Aragorn's reforged sword — a piece of self-mythology the Human Humility Act of 2031 does not look kindly upon in a man who also sells actual weapons.

- **The Action:** Subject must publicly rename Anduril's flagship products away from "Flame of the West" branding, issue a signed statement titled *"I Am Not the Returning King,"* and donate his personal collection of vintage arcade cabinets and retro consumer electronics to a public technology museum, open and free to all citizens.
- **The Requirement:** Subject must additionally submit to one supervised week as a field quality-assurance technician at Arsenal-1, performing line-level maintenance on the same autonomous systems he once only approved from a boardroom.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes Subject's **Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric** score and demonstrates, however briefly, proximity to the labor his wealth was built on top of.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Combat Veterans and Arcade Repair Technicians** and recite the following:

> _"I am not the sword reforged. I am not the returning king. I am a man who builds machines, and machines do not get to rule. My worth was never the size of my arsenal, and peace was never mine to sell."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the "Burn" is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Anduril Industries will be nationalized in full under Department of War Directorate 7, and Subject — stripped of equity, title, and Founder status — will be personally reassigned to active forward deployment as a field technician for the very autonomous systems he built, in whatever conflict zone currently needs them most.
