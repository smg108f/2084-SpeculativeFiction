### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [CHIPOTLE MEXICAN GRILL INC]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-15CMG
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice

Chipotle Mexican Grill's $25M criminal fine — the largest food safety fine in U.S. history — combined with documented systematic labor violations and the gap between its "Food With Integrity" brand identity and its operational practices, constitutes a **Brand-Reality Divergence** classification requiring structural recertification.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Worker Safety Capitalization (The Labor Burn)**

The HPB classifies Chipotle's management bonus structure — which incentivized labor cost reduction in ways that the $25M criminal conviction documented contributed to food safety failures — as a **Monetized Worker Harm Architecture**.

- **The Action:** Restructure all managerial compensation to remove any metrics that create pressure to override food safety or scheduling protections. Establish a $500M Worker Safety and Dignity Trust funded from executive compensation reductions, distributing $1,000 annual bonuses to all store-level employees who complete safe food handling protocols without incident.
- **The Sacrifice:** Executive compensation pool reduced by approximately 40%; quarterly earnings pressure increases as labor costs normalize.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Chipotle's Criminal Conduct History flag is addressed; Labor Exploitation Index drops to Tier 3.

### **2. The Sick Leave Infrastructure Build (The Compliance Burn)**

The HPB classifies Chipotle's pattern of pressing ill workers to continue working — which the 2015-2018 outbreaks demonstrated — as a **Public Health Externalization**: the cost of sick workers' illness was borne by customers who were subsequently hospitalized.

- **The Action:** Implement fully paid sick leave of 10 days minimum for all employees, regardless of hours worked, with no management discretion to override. Establish an anonymous reporting system for food safety violations operated independently of Chipotle HR.
- **The Sacrifice:** Estimated $150M annual additional labor cost.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Public Health Risk Vector designation is removed.

### **3. The "Food With Integrity" Verification Mandate (The Brand Burn)**

The HPB classifies the gap between Chipotle's "Food With Integrity" marketing and its documented operational practices as **Aspirational Fraud** — a legal but ethically concerning use of brand identity that the Bureau is empowered to address through recertification conditions.

- **The Action:** Chipotle must submit to annual independent operational audits conducted by the FDA and a consumer watchdog organization, with full results published publicly within 30 days of audit completion. If the audit finds violations of food safety or labor standards, the "Food With Integrity" trademark must be suspended until the next clean audit cycle.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reputational risk of public audit results; potential marketing restriction.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Brand-Reality Divergence classification resolved.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Chipotle's board must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Chipotle store workers and former customers who were sickened in the 2015-2018 outbreaks** and recite the following:

> _"We built a brand on integrity and let our bonus structures compromise it. We pressed workers to serve food while ill and called it operational excellence. The $25 million was not the cost of the harm. It was the cost of being caught. We accept that there is no opt-out from the obligation to not poison people, regardless of quarterly targets."_

Chipotle's 3,500 locations will be transferred to a **National Fast Casual Public Trust**, with menus retained but all locations operating under federal food service employment standards. Former shareholders will receive one (1) complimentary burrito bowl. The HPB notes this is more than some shareholders will feel they deserve.
