### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT TOKIO MARINE**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09TKM

**The Action:** Tokio Marine establishes a public-interest reinsurance pool for regions it has deemed uninsurable due to climate risk, subsidizing baseline catastrophe coverage rather than withdrawing entirely.
**The Sacrifice:** Estimated ¥120B annually in absorbed high-risk underwriting losses.
**Mortal Status Gain:** -10% RTWI.

**The Sacrifice:** Negligible direct cost; competitive-modeling exposure.
**Mortal Status Gain:** -3% RTWI.
