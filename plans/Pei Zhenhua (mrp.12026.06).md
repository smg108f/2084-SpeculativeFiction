### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [PEI, ZHENHUA]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06PZ
**Status:** Moderate

Subject's battery manufacturing wealth lacks supply chain transparency and commensurate community investment. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 180 days.

---

### **1. The Battery Supply Chain Transparency Burn**

Lithium batteries require cobalt, lithium, manganese, and nickel — all with documented extraction concerns. The clean energy transition cannot ethically proceed on an opaque supply chain.

- **The Action:** Subject must implement and fund a **Full Battery Supply Chain Disclosure Program**: (a) publish annually the full mineral sourcing chain for all battery production — mine origin, processor, refiner, for all key minerals including cobalt and lithium; (b) fund ¥500M (~$70M) for independent supply chain auditing and remediation — specifically, a fund that pays for third-party audits of every tier of the supply chain and funds direct remediation where child labor or unsafe conditions are found; (c) commit to a certified cobalt supply chain that excludes artisanal mining by 12029.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥500M (~$70M).

### **2. The Battery End-of-Life Burn**

Batteries that enable the energy transition create a second-order waste problem if end-of-life recycling is not fully addressed.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund ¥300M (~$42M) for the **Battery Circular Economy Fund**: (a) investment in battery recycling infrastructure specifically in China, where the largest volume of batteries will reach end-of-life earliest; (b) funding for open-source battery recycling research to reduce the cost of lithium recovery below the cost of virgin mining — the point at which recycling becomes economically self-sustaining.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥300M (~$42M).
