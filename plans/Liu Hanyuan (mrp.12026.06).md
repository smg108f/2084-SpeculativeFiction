### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [LIU, HANYUAN]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06LH2
**Status:** Urgent

Subject founded Tongwei Group — China's largest fish feed company and one of the world's largest solar cell manufacturers. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Tongwei Polysilicon Supply Chain Audit Burn**

Tongwei Solar's polysilicon supply chain has exposure to Xinjiang sourcing. The HPB requires documented verification.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund and commission an **Independent Tongwei Polysilicon Supply Chain Audit** — ¥200M (~$28M) — conducted by a third-party auditor acceptable to the HPB, assessing whether any polysilicon in Tongwei Solar's supply chain originates from facilities using forced labour. Results must be published publicly. If forced labour is identified in any supplier: that supplier is immediately delisted; Tongwei provides remediation support to affected workers; and Tongwei commits to sourcing polysilicon only from facilities with documented independent labour compliance.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥200M audit cost + potential supply chain disruption.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The world's largest solar cell manufacturer has a verified, publicly disclosed clean supply chain — not a self-certification.

### **2. The Fishery-Solar Global Access Burn**

The Fishery-Solar Symbiosis model — solar panels over fish ponds — is genuinely innovative. The HPB asks that this innovation reaches beyond China.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund a **Global Fishery-Solar Transfer Program** — ¥1B (~$140M) — to fund technology transfer and pilot deployment of the Fishery-Solar Symbiosis model in: (a) Southeast Asia (Vietnam, Indonesia, Bangladesh — major aquaculture markets); (b) Sub-Saharan Africa (Kenya, Ghana — where fish farming and solar deployment are both emerging); administered through a partnership with the FAO and IRENA. This is not a Tongwei marketing fund — it is a technology transfer grant program.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥1B.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** An innovative Chinese dual-use energy-food technology is exported to populations that most need both food security and clean energy.

### **3. The Liu Foundation Burn**

- **The Action:** Establish the **Liu Hanyuan Foundation** — ¥500M (~$70M) — focused on: (a) rural Sichuan agricultural and aquaculture worker welfare; (b) Chinese solar industry vocational training scholarships; (c) fish food security for low-income Chinese communities.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥500M.
