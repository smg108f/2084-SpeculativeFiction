### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[SHEINDLIN, JUDITH]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-08JS
**Status:** Precautionary

Subject is not in danger of Ascension — her balance sheet sits comfortably below half the Divine Threshold. This plan is therefore not a Pre-Seizure Notice but a **Precautionary Compliance Tune-Up**, filed because the HPB's Franchise Replication algorithm flagged her family's expanding courtroom-television footprint as a structural pattern worth pruning before it becomes a real problem for someone else in this bloodline.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days, more as a courtesy than a rescue.

---

### **1. The "Rerun Perpetuity" Burn**

The HPB classifies syndicated backend residuals as **"Ghost Equity"** — income that keeps accruing to a person long after the labor that generated it has stopped. Subject's 2017 sale of ~5,200 "Judge Judy" episodes back to CBS for $100M converted that ghost equity into a lump sum, which the HPB begrudgingly respects as tidy, but her ongoing "Judy Justice" salary on Amazon Prime keeps the mechanism running.

- **The Action:** Subject must place all future "Judy Justice" and any successor-format earnings above a modest personal draw into the **New York Law School Honors Scholars Program**, converting her remaining broadcast income into an endowment rather than a paycheck.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject forfeits future personal upside from any further courtroom-format renewals.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes any argument that Subject is "still ascending" via rerun economics. Her income becomes a pipeline for other people's law degrees, not her own net worth line.

### **2. The "Judicial Dynasty" Decoupling**

The 2084 Code takes a dim view of **hereditary format transfer** — one family controlling multiple television courtrooms is, structurally, uncomfortably close to a monarchy with gavels.

- **The Action:** Subject must relinquish executive-producer credit and creative control over "Adam's Law" and any future Sheindlin-adjacent courtroom programming. Casting for future court-show hosts must run through an open, publicly audited selection process — not a family greenlight.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses her role as the franchise's dynastic kingmaker; the "Judge Judy" format becomes a genre, not a family business.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the **Franchise Replication Flag**. The HPB reclassifies the format as public-domain television grammar rather than a Sheindlin family holding.

### **3. The "Kingmaker Drift" Penance**

Subject's occasional presidential endorsements (Bloomberg, 2020; Haley, 2024) register as low-grade but real attempts to move electoral outcomes using accumulated television credibility — precisely the kind of soft, unaccountable influence the HPB was built to keep an eye on.

- **The Action:** Subject must retire from op-ed endorsements of individual candidates and instead fund and publicly front a nonpartisan **"Small Claims Literacy Initiative,"** teaching ordinary citizens to navigate actual small-claims court without a television judge's help.
- **The Requirement:** Any future public political commentary must be paired with a matching donation to legal-aid clinics serving the same audience she is addressing.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts residual "Kingmaker" influence into procedural literacy — Whuffie the HPB actually approves of.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Small-Claims Litigants and Process Servers** and recite the following:

> _"I spent thirty years telling other people, under oath, that they were not being straight with the court. Let the record now show that I, too, am an ordinary person, my fortune is not a verdict, and my children do not inherit a bench — only a last name. Case closed. Court is adjourned. I have no further jurisdiction over my own excess."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the "Burn" is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Given Subject's comfortable distance from the Divine Threshold, this would be largely symbolic — but she would nonetheless be required to surrender her gavel-shaped letter opener to the **Sanctuary Zone Archive** and would forfeit the right to ever again tell a litigant to "sit down and be quiet" without HPB pre-clearance.
