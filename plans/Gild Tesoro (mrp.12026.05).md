### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [TESORO, GILD]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-22-GT
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice / Devil Fruit Asset — Non-Standard Valuation / Exploitation Record Flagged

Gild Tesoro has been classified CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK based on a $175 billion RTWI (volatile, Berry-indexed), a Devil Fruit biological gold production capability with no quantifiable upper bound, a sovereign floating casino state with documented forced debt-bondage operations, and a gold market leverage position the Bureau classifies as Systemic Market Risk. This plan must address not only the financial concentration but the exploitation practices the Gran Tesoro has been used to enable.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. Gran Tesoro — Labor and Debt Practice Reform and Compensation**

The Gran Tesoro's documented history of forced debt-bondage — guests who lose at the casino becoming unable to leave, subjected to labor extraction until debts are paid — constitutes a human rights violation that the HPB's Exploitation Assessment Division classifies as the most serious in the Jump Universe audit queue's current cycle. Recertification cannot proceed without addressing this record directly.

- **The Action:** Subject must: (1) unconditionally release all individuals currently held in debt-bondage status on the Gran Tesoro; (2) establish a Gran Tesoro Harm Compensation Fund of $50 billion, administered by an independent trustee, to compensate all documented victims of forced labor and financial coercion on the ship; (3) implement enforceable debt forgiveness caps — no guest may be held liable for gambling debts exceeding a published maximum, and no guest may be detained or coerced as a result of gambling debts under any circumstances. An HPB monitor must certify compliance within 30 days.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject foregoes the forced labor extraction model that has been a significant component of the Gran Tesoro's operating margin. The casino may continue operating. The prison it has been cannot.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Exploitation Record resolved with compensation. Gran Tesoro may continue operations under compliant framework.

---

### **2. Gold Production Capacity — Market Stability Covenant**

Tesoro's Devil Fruit gives him the ability to produce gold at a scale that constitutes systemic market risk. The HPB cannot cap his biological capability — it can require that he commit to production limits that prevent his individual decisions from causing gold market disruptions with global financial consequences.

- **The Action:** Subject must enter into a Gold Production Stability Covenant committing to: (1) maximum annual gold production from the Gol Gol no Mi capped at 0.5% of global annual gold production, sufficient for personal wealth maintenance but insufficient to cause market disruption; (2) advance HPB notification if production in any quarter is expected to exceed 0.1% of global annual production; (3) prohibition on gold production coordinated with gold short-selling positions — preventing Subject from profiting from market disruption he himself creates.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject accepts limits on what his own body can do commercially. The biological capability remains; its market application is regulated.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Systemic Market Risk classification removed. Gold production becomes a governed asset rather than an unregulated individual capability.

---

### **3. Gran Tesoro Sovereign Status — Jurisdictional Recognition Agreement**

The Gran Tesoro's international waters sovereign status has been the structural precondition for the exploitation practices the Bureau is requiring be ended. The ship's legal invisibility has allowed Tesoro to operate outside any national framework. As with Attilan, the Bureau requires that sovereignty be formalized rather than asserted — recognized in a framework that establishes mutual obligations.

- **The Action:** Subject must negotiate and ratify a Gran Tesoro Sovereignty Agreement with the World Government (Jump Universe) and the UN Office of Non-Standard Jurisdictions, registering the Gran Tesoro as a recognized sovereign entity subject to: (1) international human rights law; (2) the HPB's Exploitation Assessment Division's ongoing monitoring authority; (3) mandatory port-of-call access for international regulatory inspections at least quarterly. Subject retains sovereign authority on the ship. The ship stops being an accountability-free zone.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Gran Tesoro's primary structural advantage — legal invisibility — is ended. It becomes a sovereign entity with recognized rights and corresponding recognized obligations.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Sovereign Exploitation Shield removed from risk profile. Subject's RTWI remains above threshold, but the exploitation infrastructure is dismantled.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 former Gran Tesoro workers** — specifically drawn from individuals who served under the debt-bondage system — and recite the following:

> _"I know what it is to have no options. I know what it feels like when someone else decides what your labor is worth and what your freedom costs. I built a ship on gold and I ran it on the labor of people I had put in exactly that position. I told myself they had chosen to gamble. They had not chosen the rules I wrote, or the debts I set, or the exit I locked. I knew what I was doing. I did it anyway. The compensation fund does not undo it. The covenant does not undo it. What they do is prevent me from doing it again. I am choosing not to undo it with gold. I am choosing not to repeat it. That is the only choice that is still mine to make."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Asset Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol — Floating Sovereign Tier**, which includes: coordination with the World Government and international maritime authorities to surround the Gran Tesoro with inspection vessels pending compliance; public release of the HPB's Exploitation Assessment Division's full findings on the debt-bondage operation; and formal referral to the International Criminal Court's Jump Universe Maritime Division. The Bureau notes that the Gran Tesoro is very large and very visible and has a documented history of being found by people with strong motivations to find it. The HPB is not the only party with an interest in this outcome.
