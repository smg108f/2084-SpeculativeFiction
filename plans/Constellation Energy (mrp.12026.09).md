**Case File:** HPB-12026-09CEG

### **1. The Burn: Public Grid Capacity Reservation**

**The Action:** Constellation commits to reserving a fixed percentage of nuclear generation capacity for public grid access at regulated rates, preventing total capacity capture by AI hyperscaler power-purchase agreements.
**The Sacrifice:** Estimated $6B annually in foregone premium hyperscaler contract revenue.
**Mortal Status Gain:** -15% RTWI.

### **2. The Burn: New Reactor Public Benefit Sharing**

**The Action:** Any new or restarted nuclear capacity brought online is split between public grid supply and AI data center contracts on a fixed ratio, rather than allocated entirely to the highest hyperscaler bidder.
**The Sacrifice:** Estimated $2B in foregone premium capacity allocation.
**Mortal Status Gain:** -9% RTWI.
