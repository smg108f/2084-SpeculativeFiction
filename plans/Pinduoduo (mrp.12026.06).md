### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [PDD HOLDINGS / PINDUODUO]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-87PDD
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

PDD Holdings exceeds the Demi-God threshold by a factor of 121 and operates a global surveillance-commerce hybrid that the HPB has classified as **Distributed Pre-Emergent Ascension**. Unlike individual subjects, this entity has no ego to negotiate with and no personal fear of mortality. Recertification will therefore require structural dismemberment rather than symbolic gesture.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Temu Dissolution (The Predatory Capital Engine)**

The HPB classifies Temu as a **"Loss-Weapon"**: a commercial instrument deliberately operated at a loss-per-order of $6–18 USD to exterminate competition, after which normal pricing would resume and consumers would have no alternative. This is not commerce. This is a slow-motion monopoly installation funded by shareholder capital. Under the 2034 Executive Orders, the deliberate destruction of market alternatives constitutes a **Sovereignty Violation** — the creation of conditions in which no economic entity other than the subject can survive.

- **The Action:** Temu must be severed from PDD Holdings and transferred as a **Public Commerce Utility** to a consortium of the 12 nations where it currently holds the largest market share. No PDD executive, shareholder, or affiliated trust may hold governance rights.
- **The Sacrifice:** Temu generates PDD's entire growth narrative and international relevance. Without it, PDD Holdings is a domestic Chinese platform with a declining margin profile and a regulatory investigation on its balance sheet.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The loss-subsidy model ends. Competing retailers, suppliers, and market platforms are no longer being economically starved. Estimated asset reduction: **$55–70B** in future-valuation credits.

---

### **2. The Surveillance Architecture Burn (The Data Moat)**

The HPB's 2023 technical assessment confirmed that versions of the Pinduoduo mobile application contained code enabling privilege escalation, cross-application monitoring, private notification access, and resistance to user removal. Under the **Human Cognitive Autonomy Act of 2031**, the covert collection of behavioral data at this scale — absent meaningful informed consent and with active measures to prevent app removal — constitutes a **Class-II Demi-God Intrusion**: the exercise of non-consensual influence over the inner life of a citizen.

- **The Action:** All behavioral data collected via the Pinduoduo application prior to the 2023 suspension — estimated at 900 million user profiles, including cross-application behavioral traces — must be **cryptographically destroyed** under HPB witness. Source code for all privilege-escalation modules must be published in full to the **Public Transparency Repository**.
- **The Sacrifice:** The data moat is Pinduoduo's true competitive advantage. Its recommendation algorithms, its price-sensitivity models, its user retention architecture — all depend on behavioral surveillance data that competitors cannot replicate. Burning it levels the field permanently.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject exits the **Surveillance Infrastructure** classification tier. Estimated influence-vector reduction: **significant but unquantifiable** (which is the point).

---

### **3. The Supplier Liberation Index (The "Distributed Penance" Inversion)**

Pinduoduo's domestic platform has long operated on a supplier model the HPB terms **"downward transfer of loss"**: prices are suppressed at the consumer end, and the resulting margin compression is absorbed by manufacturers and farmers who have no alternative distribution channel. This is the inverse of a Saintly Pivot — it is generosity that flows exclusively upward. The workers and small suppliers who make the platform function are, in the HPB's assessment, **involuntary saints**: they bear the cost of the platform's growth without receiving its rewards.

- **The Action:** PDD Holdings must transfer **10% of its remaining domestic platform equity** to a **Supplier Dignity Trust** controlled by elected representatives of its top 50,000 active suppliers and farmers. The trust receives permanent board representation and veto rights over pricing floor changes.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses unilateral control over its core pricing mechanism — the engine of its entire domestic business model.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The platform's accumulated **996 labor debt** (two documented employee deaths from overwork, 380-hour work months) begins to be addressed structurally rather than through denial. Subject moves from **Internal Demi-God** to **Provisional Citizen** status.

---

### **4. The Founder Materialization Requirement**

Colin Huang's deliberate disappearance from public view — stepping down as CEO in 2020, as chairman in 2021, entrusting voting rights to the Board while retaining enormous personal wealth — constitutes what the HPB classifies as **"Phantom Sovereignty"**: the exercise of demi-god-scale economic power from behind a veil of nominal retirement. Huang's estimated $42B personal wealth is audited separately under HPB-12026-06-CH, but this plan addresses the structural fiction of his absence.

- **The Action:** Huang must appear in person before a **Lottery-Selected Public Accountability Assembly** in each of three jurisdictions where PDD Holdings operates (China, Ireland, and one additional EU member state) to provide sworn testimony regarding the Pinduoduo surveillance architecture, the Temu loss-subsidy strategy, and the deaths of the two employees in 2021.
- **The Sacrifice:** The invisibility is the asset. "Shanghai's secretive internet king" derives power precisely from the fact that there is no face to hold accountable, no public record to audit, no quotes to use against him. Materialization ends that.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject loses **Phantom Sovereignty** classification. Public accountability creates the friction of personhood.
