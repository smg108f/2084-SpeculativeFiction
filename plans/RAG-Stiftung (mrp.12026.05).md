### **1. BURN WAIVED: The Obligation Model (Already Implemented)**

No private individual benefits. No dynasty is constructed. No mission creep toward ideological agenda-setting has occurred.

- **The Action:** N/A — already in compliance.
- **The Sacrifice:** Already made at founding.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Already achieved. The Foundation is a mortal institution paying a mortal debt.

### **2. BURN WAIVED: Governance Democratization (Already Implemented)**

- **The Action:** N/A — already in compliance.

### **3. RECOMMENDATION (Non-Binding): The Living Archive**

The HPB recommends, but does not require, that the RAG-Stiftung commission a publicly accessible **Post-Mining Living Archive** — a longitudinal documentation project capturing the environmental, social, and economic legacy of the German coal mining era in the Ruhr, Saar, and Ibbenbüren regions, updated annually, and made freely available in multiple languages. This would serve as an ongoing public record of why the Foundation exists and what perpetual environmental obligations mean in practice.

- **The Purpose:** Institutional memory. The coal era is living memory for some; it will become history for the communities that still live with its consequences. The Archive would ensure the reason for the Foundation's existence is never abstracted from the landscape and the people it was created to serve.
- **This is not a burn. This is a suggestion from an auditor who found something worth admiring in these records.**

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

The RAG-Stiftung is not required to recite a Mortal Oath. It already knows what it is.
