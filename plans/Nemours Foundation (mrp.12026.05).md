### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [NEMOURS FOUNDATION]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-36NF
**Status:** Precautionary / Du Pont Dynasty Origin Acknowledgment and Trust Concentration Disclosure Order

The Nemours Foundation has been classified PROBATIONARY SAINT. Recertification requirements are modest. The Foundation's operating healthcare model is appropriate, and Alfred du Pont's decision to direct his estate entirely to children's hospitals is among the most defensible philanthropic choices in this audit cohort. The burns address the unacknowledged du Pont explosives/chemicals origin and the Trust's regional investment concentration.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Du Pont Dynasty Origin Acknowledgment Burn**

The Nemours Foundation's endowment derives from Alfred I. du Pont's personal fortune — itself derived from his inheritance of, and subsequent expulsion from, the E.I. du Pont de Nemours and Company, one of America's largest gunpowder and chemical manufacturers. The du Pont family fortune was built on explosives contracts for American wars, chemical manufacturing, and industrial monopoly. Alfred's personal fortune was built after his expulsion from the family business, through Florida banking and real estate. These origins — the gunpowder dynasty and the black sheep's separate fortune — have not been publicly acknowledged by the Nemours Foundation in its history pages.

- **The Action:** The Foundation must publish a **Complete Origin Statement** — a permanent, publicly available narrative documenting: (a) Alfred I. du Pont's membership in and expulsion from the du Pont industrial dynasty; (b) the du Pont family's wealth origin in gunpowder, explosives, and chemical manufacturing; (c) Alfred's separate Florida fortune and its derivation from banking and real estate; (d) the Foundation's assessment of whether this origin history is relevant to its current children's healthcare mission.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Foundation's public narrative includes the gunpowder dynasty alongside the children's hospitals. The distance between these two things is the Foundation's most honest story.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Patients, families, and the public can evaluate Nemours Children's Health knowing the full history of the wealth that funds it.

---

### **2. The Trust Investment Concentration Disclosure Burn**

The Alfred I. duPont Testamentary Trust — the Foundation's funding source — holds assets concentrated in Florida banking and Southeastern U.S. real estate investments. This regional concentration means the Foundation's philanthropic capacity is vulnerable to economic conditions in a specific geographic market. The HPB requires that this concentration be publicly disclosed.

- **The Action:** The Foundation must publish an annual **Trust Investment Transparency Report** — disclosing: (a) the asset categories and geographic concentration of the Alfred I. duPont Testamentary Trust's investment portfolio; (b) the proportion of Trust income that funds Nemours Children's Health operations; (c) the Trust's contingency strategy if Southeastern U.S. banking or real estate markets experience significant downturns; (d) any diversification plans to reduce regional concentration.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Trust's investment concentration becomes publicly legible. Patients and communities that depend on Nemours Children's Health can assess the financial sustainability of the institution.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Foundation's healthcare operations are evaluated against the long-term financial sustainability of their Trust funding.

---

### **3. The Access Equity Accountability Burn**

Nemours Children's Health operates primarily in Delaware and Florida — states with documented healthcare access disparities across income levels, insurance status, and racial demographics. As an operating healthcare system funded by a charitable trust, Nemours has an obligation to ensure that its charitable structure is reflected in genuinely equitable access to care — not only the care available to insured patients.

- **The Action:** The Foundation must publish an annual **Access Equity Report** — documenting: (a) the proportion of Nemours Children's Health patients who are uninsured or underinsured; (b) the financial assistance programs available for families who cannot afford care; (c) the geographic distribution of Nemours facilities relative to low-income pediatric populations; (d) measurable outcomes in pediatric health equity in Nemours' service areas.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Foundation's charitable status is measured against actual access equity, not only institutional mission statements.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The children's hospitals funded by Alfred du Pont's estate are accountable to the children most in need, not only to those most easily served.
