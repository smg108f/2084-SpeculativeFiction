### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [OPPENHEIMER, NICKY]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06NO
**Status:** Urgent

Subject's $8.4B derives directly from the sale of a stake in a company that operated under apartheid labor conditions for decades. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Apartheid Labor Restitution Burn**

De Beers' South African mining operations employed hundreds of thousands of Black miners under the compound system — separated from families, paid racially differentiated wages, housed in segregated facilities — for decades of apartheid-era operations. This was the labor that built the Oppenheimer fortune.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund $200M for the **Oppenheimer Miner Legacy Fund** — specifically: (a) direct payments to surviving apartheid-era De Beers miners or their documented dependents, identified through a transparent claims process administered by an independent administrator; (b) community investment in the Northern Cape, Kimberley, and other historic De Beers mining towns — addressing the infrastructure deficits in communities built around mines that generated wealth which left with the Oppenheimer family.
- **The Sacrifice:** $200M.

### **2. The Diamond Industry Accountability Burn**

The cartel pleaded guilty (corporate, 2004). The communities whose diamond economies were managed by De Beers' Central Selling Organisation for decades received the world price De Beers set, not the market price.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund $75M for the **Diamond Communities Development Fund** — directed to the diamond-producing communities of Botswana, Namibia, and the DRC whose rough diamonds were priced through the De Beers CSO — specifically funding economic diversification programs so those communities are not wholly dependent on diamond revenue after mining ends.
- **The Sacrifice:** $75M.

### **3. The Tswalu Conservation Scale-Up**

The game reserve conservation work is genuine. The HPB invites its extension into community benefit.

- **The Action:** Subject must formalize the Tswalu community benefit programs — ensuring that the communities adjacent to the reserve receive employment, education, and conservation career training, endowed at $25M independently of the Oppenheimer family's ongoing management.
- **The Sacrifice:** $25M.
