### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [KOCH, CHARLES]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06CK
**Status:** Urgent

Subject has spent 50 years building private political infrastructure designed, among other objectives, to prevent the kind of regulation the Demi-God Act of 2034 represents. The HPB notes the structural tension and proceeds. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 30 days.

*Note: Julia Koch is under separate recertification (HPB-12026-06JK). Coordination is required for Koch Industries-related burns.*

---

### **1. The Climate Debt Burn**

Koch Industries is one of the top ten carbon-emitting private companies in the United States. Koch's political network has funded climate denial research and campaigns for decades, contributing to a delay in U.S. climate policy that climate scientists estimate has added gigatons of cumulative CO₂ to the atmosphere.

- **The Action:** Establish a **Koch Climate Remediation Trust** with an endowment of $5B, governed independently and focused exclusively on carbon capture, renewable energy transition in communities most affected by fossil fuel production, and climate adaptation for vulnerable populations. Subject may not have programming control. The trust explicitly acknowledges Koch Industries' historical role in opposing climate policy.
- **The Sacrifice:** $5B; the ideological coherence of a libertarian opposing specific environmental remediation; the comfort of criminal justice reform as full offset.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The largest and most structurally significant Burn on this ledger. Converts the most consequential private opponent of U.S. climate policy into a major funder of climate remediation.

### **2. The Kochtopus Wind-Down Burn**

The Koch political network — Americans for Prosperity, State Policy Network affiliates, campus programs — represents 50 years of private investment in permanent political infrastructure designed to operate beyond democratic accountability. The HPB cannot require subject to renounce his beliefs; it can require him to stop funding a permanent counter-government.

- **The Action:** Commit to winding down direct Koch family funding for political advocacy organizations (as opposed to policy research) over a 5-year period, beginning with electoral spending. Policy research at academic institutions may continue at subject's discretion. The operational political machine must be subject to a sunset.
- **The Sacrifice:** The infrastructure built over 50 years; the ongoing political leverage it provides.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts private political infrastructure into time-limited activity, restoring at least partial democratic accountability to the spaces it has occupied.

### **3. The Criminal Justice Reinvestment Burn**

Subject's criminal justice reform work is genuine and has achieved real outcomes. The HPB supports acceleration.

- **The Action:** Double the Koch-affiliated funding for criminal justice reform and expand its scope to include policing reform and reentry support — areas where Koch network organizations have been more hesitant. A $500M 10-year commitment to the expanded criminal justice program.
- **The Sacrifice:** Internal ideological consistency within the Koch network; $500M.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the most credible existing bipartisan Koch philanthropic project into a more complete program. The HPB rewards genuine cross-partisan good faith where it finds it.

> *"I have spent my life believing that government regulation is the primary enemy of human freedom. I have spent billions making that belief into policy. The climate changed while I was doing it. I am 89. I am funding $5 billion of climate remediation. I am not abandoning my beliefs. I am acknowledging that my beliefs had costs I did not adequately account for. The criminal justice work was right. The climate work was not done. I am doing it now. The Demi-God Act is exactly the kind of regulation I have opposed. I am complying with it anyway. This may be the most ideologically complex moment of my life."*

Subject will be relocated to a Sovereign Flotilla designated **FSS LIBERTARIAN**, operating in international waters beyond government regulation, where he will discover that the things he most values — stability, trade, property rights — require exactly the governmental infrastructure he spent his life opposing.
