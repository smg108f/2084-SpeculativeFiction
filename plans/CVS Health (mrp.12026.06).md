### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [CVS HEALTH]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-44CH
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice

CVS Health's integrated control over pharmaceutical access, insurance adjudication, and retail dispensary constitutes an unauthorized parallel governance structure. The organization must dissolve its monopolistic vertical integration before the HPB activates the Apotheosis Protocol and reclassifies CVS as a **Regulated Utility of the First Order**.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Caremark Divestiture (The Formulary Relinquishment)**

The HPB classifies pharmacy benefit management as a **Biological Access Engine** — any private entity that determines which medications citizens may affordably obtain exercises sovereign-adjacent power over their bodies. Caremark's formulary decisions affect approximately 110 million covered lives.

- **The Action:** CVS must transfer the Caremark PBM division to a newly chartered **National Formulary Commons**, a non-profit governed by a citizen jury, pharmacologists, and patient advocates. CVS retains no ownership stake and no formulary input.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of Caremark's annual operating income (approximately $7B); loss of the "Closed-Loop Biological Economy" that makes CVS uniquely resistant to competition.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The organization is reclassified from "Distributed Sovereignty Network" to "Retail Pharmacy Operator" — a category with precedent, legal accountability, and a comprehensible public purpose.

### **2. The Aetna Severance (The Insurance Decoupling)**

The HPB finds it structurally incoherent that the same organization that pays your insurance claims also fills your prescriptions and bills your employer. This creates an incentive architecture that the HPB classifies as a **Conflict of Biological Interest**.

- **The Action:** Aetna must be spun off as an independent mutual insurance company, with policyholders holding ownership stakes. No CVS executive may hold a board seat or advisory role in the spun-off entity for a period of ten years.
- **The Sacrifice:** Elimination of the integrated revenue synergies estimated at $3B–$5B annually. CVS will no longer be able to steer Aetna members toward CVS pharmacies via tiered network design.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The organization loses its classification as a "Soft Biological Sovereign" and becomes a pharmacist. The HPB views this as an appropriate and proportional reduction.

### **3. The MinuteClinic Gifting (The Infrastructure Surrender)**

CVS's network of approximately 1,100 MinuteClinic locations represents embedded healthcare infrastructure in communities that may have no alternative access to primary care. The HPB classifies these as **Hostage Infrastructure**: their closure would cause genuine public harm, which gives CVS coercive leverage it never earned democratically.

- **The Action:** All MinuteClinic locations must be transferred to local municipalities or federally qualified community health centers at zero cost. CVS may negotiate Life-Tenancy Staffing Agreements to continue providing clinical personnel for a maximum of five years.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of approximately $1.2B in clinic assets and the customer-acquisition funnel MinuteClinics provide.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** CVS transforms from "Infrastructure Hostage Holder" to "Healthcare Donor" — a classification that earns significant Whuffie credit and may qualify the organization for Saintly Status upon completion.

### **4. The EPAC Dissolution (The Political Penance)**

The HPB has documented a sustained pattern of CVS political donations that contradict its stated public health mission, including funding politicians who opposed drug pricing transparency legislation. This qualifies as a **Memetic Integrity Violation**.

- **The Action:** EPAC must be dissolved. All future political engagement must be conducted through the **Open Pharmacy Transparency Registry**, a publicly auditable platform where all lobbying positions are disclosed in plain language alongside their projected effect on patient drug costs.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of "Kingmaker" status in healthcare legislation. CVS will no longer be able to quietly fund both sides of policy debates.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Reclassification from "Memetic Integrity Violation" to "Transparent Stakeholder." The HPB considers this the minimum standard of civic sincerity.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Uninsured Home Health Aides and Rural Pharmacists** and recite the following:

> *"We are not a healthcare system. We are a shop. We dispense what the formulary approves, we accept what the insurer pays, and we stand behind the counter — not above it. The medicine belongs to the patient. The access belongs to the public. We are the last mile, not the last word."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the Apotheosis Protocol. All CVS Health assets will be placed in receivership under the **National Biological Commons Authority**, and the company's retail locations will be rebranded as **Public Dispensary Nodes**, with pricing set by a citizen algorithm and all profits redirected to the Universal Medication Access Fund.
