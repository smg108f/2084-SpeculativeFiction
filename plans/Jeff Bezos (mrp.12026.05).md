### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [BEZOS, JEFFREY P.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-04JB
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject Bezos has exceeded the $1B Human Protection Bureau threshold by a factor of 221 and has been formally classified as a **Critical Ascension Risk** under Audit Ref. 12026.05. His Blue Origin portfolio constitutes an active **Flotilla Precursor Program** under the Flotilla Infrastructure Seizure Act, and his Washington Post holdings represent an unregistered **Shadow Editorial State**. Failure to recertify will result in immediate Apotheosis Protocol activation.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Blue Origin Surrender (The Flotilla Defanging)**

The HPB classifies Blue Origin as the single greatest Ascension infrastructure risk in the current audit registry. A privately-held, vertically-integrated rocket company — with an operational heavy-lift vehicle, a funded lunar lander, and a stated mission to move "a trillion humans" into rotating off-world habitats — is not a company. It is a **Sovereign Exit Vehicle** in preflight.

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer 100% ownership of Blue Origin to the **United Nations Office for Outer Space Affairs**, to be restructured as a multilateral public utility under the Human Commons Space Charter. Subject retains a ceremonial "Founding Visionary" plaque.
- **The Sacrifice:** The O'Neill cylinder dream becomes humanity's — not Bezos's. He loses the keys to the door he spent forty years building.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Blue Origin's Flotilla Precursor classification is downgraded from **Active** to **Archived**. Subject's Exit Readiness metric drops from MAXIMUM to LOW. The HPB formally acknowledges that Subject cannot leave without the rest of us.

---

### **2. The Amazon Divestiture (The Passive Apotheosis Termination)**

Subject's ~8.2% Amazon equity stake generates **Passive Apotheosis**: his net worth climbs regardless of any action he takes, driven solely by Amazon's market performance. The HPB cannot audit a subject who accretes wealth by existing. This must stop.

- **The Action:** Subject must liquidate the entirety of his Amazon holdings — approximately $180–200B at current valuation — and deposit proceeds into the **National Infrastructure Endowment**, a constitutionally protected public trust that funds municipal broadband, water systems, and transit infrastructure across the 50 states. Subject may retain a $950M liquid allowance to remain below the Ascension threshold.
- **The Sacrifice:** Amazon, the company he built in a garage, is no longer his. He becomes a citizen who once built something great, rather than a demi-god who still owns it.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Passive Apotheosis loop is severed. Subject's wealth is static, voluntarily capped, and subject to standard HPB monitoring. Direct Assets metric drops from **EXCEEDED (221x)** to **WITHIN RANGE**.

---

### **3. The Washington Post Divestiture (The Shadow State Dissolution)**

A capital-city newspaper owned by a man with $230B in assets, active federal contracts through Blue Origin, and antitrust exposure at Amazon is not a free press. It is a **Shadow Editorial State**. The HPB does not regulate speech; it regulates *structural conflicts of interest* that compromise the cognitive commons.

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer ownership of The Washington Post to a **Reader-Governed Public Trust**, structured identically to a public broadcasting entity, with a board elected by paid subscribers and no single donor allowed to contribute more than $10M. Subject is permanently barred from editorial board participation.
- **The Sacrifice:** Bezos loses his most powerful quiet instrument — the newspaper of record in the city where his other interests are regulated.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Political Agency metric drops from **CRITICAL** to **LOW**. The HPB formally closes the Shadow Editorial State file.

---

### **4. The Superyacht Surrender (The "Koru" Reclamation)**

The *Koru* — a 127-meter, $500M sailing yacht requiring its own 75-meter support vessel — is classified by the HPB as a **Mobile Sovereignty Asset**: a stateless, flagged vessel capable of operating in international waters outside HPB jurisdiction. It is, functionally, a one-person Flotilla.

- **The Action:** *Koru* and its support vessel *Abeona* are to be transferred to the **Pacific Island Maritime Education Collective**, to be operated as a sail-training and ocean research vessel serving the 12 Pacific Island nations whose waters the Bezos Earth Fund has pledged to protect.
- **The Sacrifice:** The most expensive sailing yacht ever built becomes a school ship. Subject may visit by purchasing a standard passenger berth like any other mortal.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Mobile Sovereignty Asset reclassified as **Public Infrastructure**. The irony that his ocean philanthropy now operates from his ocean vessel is noted with approval.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Amazon Warehouse Fulfillment Workers** and recite the following:

> *"I built a company on the backs of people who packed boxes in buildings without air conditioning, and called it innovation. I moved to Florida to avoid paying taxes on $13 billion in stock sales in the same year I announced ocean conservation grants. I designed a rocket to take me off this planet and called it philanthropy. I am not saving humanity. I am a man who had a great deal of money and a very long head start. I accept that the Earth I claimed to be preserving is not mine to preserve — it belongs to everyone on it, including the people who shipped your packages."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Asset Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All Blue Origin assets will be seized under the Flotilla Infrastructure Seizure Act. Subject will be escorted via New Glenn — his own rocket — to **O'Neill Habitat Alpha**, the HPB-operated prototype space station in low Earth orbit, where he will serve as its first permanent non-voluntary resident. He will have his trillion humans. They just won't be there yet.
