### **1. The Beyond-Market Philanthropy Burn**

The Omidyar Network's market-based orientation has funded impressive programs while potentially underweighting the structural, non-market problems that financial instruments cannot solve.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish a $150M **Structural Development Fund** within the Omidyar Network — explicitly and permanently carved out for grants (not investments) to programs that cannot demonstrate financial return metrics: (a) direct cash transfer programs for extreme poverty (GiveDirectly model, scaled); (b) public sector capacity building in low-income countries — training civil servants, building government data systems, supporting public health infrastructure that no private entity has an incentive to build; (c) independent journalism in countries where press freedom has collapsed — funded as grants, not as investments seeking financial return.
- **The Sacrifice:** $150M.

### **2. The First Look / Intercept Endowment Burn**

The Intercept has produced journalism of genuine public importance. Its financial instability (First Look's funding has fluctuated; The Intercept had major layoffs in 2020) has limited its impact.

- **The Action:** Subject must endow $75M as a permanent endowment for a **Journalism Independence Fund** — structured so that national security and investigative journalism organizations cannot be defunded by a single donor's decision, with governance that includes an independent board and a distribution mandate that does not require founder approval.
- **The Sacrifice:** $75M.
