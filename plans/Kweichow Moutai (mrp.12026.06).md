### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [KWEICHOW MOUTAI CO., LTD.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-33KM
**Status:** Precautionary — State-Endorsed Luxury Currency / Non-Humanist Bloc Entity

Kweichow Moutai's RTWI of ~$296B places it at **296 times the Demi-God threshold**. MARGINAL classification. Given Chinese domicile and state ownership, Humanist Bloc enforcement scope is limited to diplomatic and trade channels.

The following **Voluntary Commitment Schedule** must be confirmed within 30 days to maintain MARGINAL classification.

---

### **1. The Diplomatic Gift Transparency Standard**

Moutai's use as a diplomatic gift currency means that bottles function as value transfers in official and semi-official contexts. The Bureau requests — as a condition of Humanist Bloc trade access for Moutai imports — that the Chinese government publish an annual disclosure of Moutai usage in official diplomatic contexts, with recipients, occasions, and estimated values.

- **The Voluntary Action:** Diplomatic Moutai usage disclosed annually to Humanist Bloc trade partners as a condition of import licensing.
- **Rationale:** A state-endorsed luxury good used in diplomatic contexts at this scale constitutes a form of financial instrument that trade partners have a legitimate interest in tracking.

---

### **2. The Worker Safety and Agricultural Sustainability Report**

Moutai's production depends on sorghum agriculture in Guizhou province and a specific fermentation process. The Bureau requests an annual independent sustainability report on: worker conditions in Moutai production facilities, agricultural sustainability practices for sorghum supply chain, and water usage in the Chishui River watershed on which Moutai production depends.

---

### **3. The Alcohol Harm Mitigation Fund**

An entity that produces and distributes at scale a product with 53% ABV, marketed as a status symbol and consumed at business dinners, bears an obligation to fund alcohol harm reduction programs in the communities where its product is consumed.

- **The Voluntary Action:** Kweichow Moutai establishes a 1% of annual revenue ($250M) fund for alcohol harm reduction, liver disease treatment, and drunk driving prevention programs in China, with an international allocation for countries where Moutai is sold.

---

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Kweichow Moutai's CEO must stand before a **Jury of 12 Guizhou Sorghum Farmers, Moutai Factory Workers, and Alcohol Treatment Counselors in China** and recite:

> _"We make grain alcohol and charge the price of a car for a bottle. The state endorses it and diplomats give it as gifts. We acknowledge that the bottle at the dinner table is also a bottle someone cannot afford to refuse, and a bottle that can harm as well as celebrate. The agricultural workers who grow the sorghum have safe conditions. The river that makes the fermentation possible will remain healthy. The people harmed by alcohol receive resources proportionate to our revenue."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** Humanist Bloc import licensing for Moutai conditioned on annual sustainability and transparency reports. Bureau notes that Moutai's significant export ambitions depend on Humanist Bloc market access, which provides meaningful leverage.
