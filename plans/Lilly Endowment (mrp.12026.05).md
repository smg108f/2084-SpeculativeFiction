### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [LILLY ENDOWMENT]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-10LE
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice / Portfolio Diversification and Geographic Expansion Order

The Lilly Endowment has been classified MARGINAL ASCENSION RISK. The HPB's primary concerns are (1) extraordinary pharmaceutical stock concentration creating unacceptable asset volatility and sector conflict, (2) regional insularity disproportionate to endowment scale, and (3) religion funding at a scale that creates church dependency concerns in the Indiana civic ecosystem.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Pharmaceutical Divestment Burn (The LLY Concentration Reckoning)**

The Endowment's primary asset — Eli Lilly & Company stock — creates a structural conflict that the HPB cannot ignore: a charitable foundation with assets that fluctuate by tens of billions of dollars based on a single pharmaceutical company's pipeline performance is not a stable charitable institution. It is a pharmaceutical investor with a charitable function attached.

- **The Action:** The Endowment must adopt and publish a binding **7-Year Pharmaceutical Diversification Plan**, reducing its Eli Lilly & Company equity concentration to no more than 30% of total endowment assets within 5 years and no more than 20% within 7 years. Diversification proceeds must be invested in a stable, diversified portfolio.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Endowment forgoes future LLY appreciation on the divested portion. Its asset base stabilizes at a lower but more reliable level.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Endowment becomes a stable charitable institution rather than a pharmaceutical stock proxy. Its grant capacity becomes predictable rather than episodic.

---

### **2. The Geographic Ambition Burn (The Indiana Insularity Reckoning)**

An institution with assets that reached $62B is deploying the vast majority of its charitable resources in one American state. This is a legitimate reflection of founder intent. It is also, at this scale, an active choice to not address the global or national problems that $62B in charitable capacity could meaningfully engage.

- **The Action:** The Endowment must establish a **National and Global Impact Program** comprising a minimum of 25% of annual grant deployment, targeting domains outside Indianapolis and Indiana. The program should be developed in consultation with the Endowment's existing community development expertise and should reflect the same quality of local knowledge applied to broader contexts.
- **The Sacrifice:** Some Indiana institutions that currently receive Endowment funding will receive less, or the total grant pool must grow to accommodate the expansion.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** An institution whose assets have reached $62B becomes a national resource rather than a regional one. Indiana benefits from its continued primary focus. The rest of the country benefits from the expansion.

---

### **3. The Religion Funding Transparency Burn**

The Lilly Endowment's religion funding is described as unusually large among foundations of equivalent scale. The HPB's Church-State Adjacency Protocol requires that large foundations funding religious institutions at scale disclose their funding criteria, the distribution of grants across denominations and faith traditions, and the governance processes by which religious grant decisions are made.

- **The Action:** The Endowment must publish an annual **Religion Funding Transparency Report** detailing all religious grants by denomination, organization type, geographic location, and grant purpose. The report must be independently audited and publicly available.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Endowment's religion program becomes publicly visible. Patterns of denominational preference, if any, become auditable.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Religion funding at this scale becomes accountable to the public interest rather than exclusively to the founders' preferences, which are 89 years old and were formed in a very different religious landscape.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, the Lilly Endowment Board of Directors must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Residents of Indianapolis Neighborhoods That Have Not Received Lilly Endowment Grants** and recite the following:

> *"We hold a pharmaceutical company in trust for Indiana. We have ridden its stock price for 89 years. We acknowledge that an institution with $60 billion in assets has obligations that exceed the boundaries of one state, and that the communities we serve deserve a stable foundation rather than one whose budget rises and falls with Mounjaro's market share. We diversify. We expand. We open the religion records. We remain committed to Indianapolis. We also acknowledge that Indianapolis is not the world."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the pharmaceutical diversification plan is not adopted within 30 days, the HPB will refer the Lilly Endowment to the IRS for review of whether its extraordinary single-stock pharmaceutical concentration — an investment strategy that benefits from the same company's commercial success in ways that could constitute a financial conflict of interest with its charitable mission — is consistent with its tax-exempt status. The HPB notes this is a genuinely open legal question. The Endowment should resolve it voluntarily rather than through federal review.
