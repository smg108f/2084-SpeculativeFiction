### **1. The Missing Housing Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must commit $250M to a **Silicon Valley Workforce Housing Trust** — funding the development of at least 2,000 units of below-market-rate housing (at 80% AMI or lower) in Santa Clara County, prioritizing teachers, healthcare workers, and service workers who support the tech economy but cannot afford to live in it.

### **2. The Land Use Accountability Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must commit the Sobrato Organization to a binding policy: for every new commercial square footage approved through 12035, an equivalent residential square footage (of which 20% must be below-market-rate) must be developed or funded. - **The Sacrifice:** Development constraints and lower commercial returns.

- **The Action:** Subject must double the Sobrato Family Foundation's college access program — specifically expanding the Sobrato Scholars program to reach 500 low-income, first-generation college students per year by 12030.
- **The Sacrifice:** Foundation resource reallocation (~$50M additional commitment).
