### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[TOKYO ELECTRON, LTD.]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-07TE
**Status:** Precautionary

Subject has crossed the Demi-God threshold not through ambition but through **accidental monopoly** — three firms on Earth can do what it does, and it is one of them. The HPB does not believe Subject seeks Sainthood or Sovereignty. That is exactly the problem: an entity that does not know it is a god cannot consent to stop being one. Recertification is therefore offered on **precautionary, voluntary terms** — refuse, and the Bureau will assume the silence is strategic.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The "Open Clean Room" Disclosure (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies Subject's accumulated process know-how — the trade-secret choreography of coating, developing, etching, and depositing material at the atomic scale — as a **Potentiality Engine of Civilizational Scope**. No tribunal can value it, because no tribunal can understand it, because Subject has spent seventy years ensuring that only a few hundred living engineers can.

- **The Action:** Subject must publish, in full and in plain language, the core process specifications for its EUV coater/developer and resist-track systems to a global open-engineering commons, supervised by a Lottery-Selected Citizen Standards Board.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of the "tribal knowledge" moat that has made Subject irreplaceable — an estimated **$48B** in future-monopoly valuation, gone the instant the recipe is legible to competitors in Seoul, Phoenix, and Shenzhen alike.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject converts from "Sole Keeper of the Secret" to "Public Utility of Record." The chokepoint is dismantled by sunlight rather than seizure.

### **2. The Triadic Veto Renunciation**

Subject currently functions as one-third of an unelected cartel (alongside ASML and Lam Research) whose collective silence could halt the production of every advanced chip on the planet. The HPB considers this an **unconstitutional concentration of structural veto power**, regardless of whether Subject has ever consciously exercised it.

- **The Action:** Subject must sign the **Tri-Party Chokepoint Dissolution Accord**, binding it to license its etching, deposition, and lithography-support tools to no fewer than twelve independent regional fabricators within five years, at cost.
- **The Sacrifice:** Permanent loss of "indispensable supplier" status — and with it, the quiet diplomatic leverage that has let Subject sit, uninvited but unignorable, at the edge of every US-Japan-China chip negotiation since the 2020s.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the **"Shadow Cabinet" Violation**. Subject becomes one vendor among many — replaceable, and therefore, at last, merely human-scaled.

### **3. The "We Are Just an Industrial Supplier" Penance**

Subject's great PR advantage has always been its obscurity — the polite fiction that it is "boring," "technical," and therefore beneath scrutiny. The HPB classifies this performed mundanity as a **Class-III Memetic Camouflage**: the conviction, quietly cultivated in the public mind, that the most powerful actors are the ones too dull to be dangerous.

- **The Action:** Subject must commission and broadcast, in prime-time slots across the Humanist Bloc, a documentary series titled *The Room We Never Let You See*, narrated by its own senior engineers, walking ordinary citizens step-by-step through exactly how much of their daily life began in a Koshi clean room.
- **The Requirement:** Subject must also fund a $200M "Atomic Literacy" initiative teaching secondary-school students how semiconductors are actually made — ending, by Bureau order, the era in which "nobody outside the industry has heard of us" functioned as armor.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the **Camouflage-of-Mundanity** score. A god that has explained itself to children is no longer a mystery the public must take on faith.

### **4. The Engineer Diaspora Endowment**

Subject's true "heirs" are not a bloodline but a **caste**: the small, aging cohort of engineers who alone hold the keys to the next process generation. The HPB considers this a **Knowledge-Concentration Hazard** indistinguishable, in structure, from a royal succession.

- **The Action:** Establish an irrevocable, internationally-administered endowment that funds open apprenticeships for 10,000 new semiconductor-process engineers per year, with no exclusivity clause binding graduates back to Subject.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses its "first call" on the next generation of irreplaceable minds — the last lever by which a toolmaker dynasty perpetuates itself.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The "secret" stops being a secret passed hand to hand in a locked room, and becomes a trade — taught, shared, and no longer divine.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Fab-Line Technicians and Vocational-School Instructors** and recite the following:

> _"We believed that if no one could see the room, no one could question what happened in it. We were wrong to think that secrecy was the same as importance, and that importance excused us from being known. The machine that built the world's machines must now be a machine the world can name. We open the door. We teach the trade. We are no longer the only ones who know."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the "Burn" is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All process patents and clean-room titles will be nationalized under joint Humanist Bloc administration, and Subject's senior leadership will be relocated — under permanent escort — to the **Rapidus Transparency Campus**, a glass-walled "demonstration fab" in Hokkaido where every step of every process must be performed in full public view, forever.
