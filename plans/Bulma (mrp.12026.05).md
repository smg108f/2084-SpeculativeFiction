### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [BRIEF, BULMA]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-29-B
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice / Dragon Ball Standing Review / Cross-Reference HPB-12026-13-CC

Bulma Brief has been classified CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK. Subject, upon receiving the Bureau's preliminary audit notice, responded by sending a fourteen-page technical critique of the Bureau's Dragon Ball valuation methodology, which the Bureau's Asset Valuation Division reviewed and found to be, in three instances, substantively correct. The Bureau has amended those figures. Subject has not yet been notified of the amendments because the Bureau does not wish to encourage this as a standard approach to audit dispute.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. Dragon Radar — Open-Access Publication and Shared Design Deposit**

The Dragon Radar is the instrument that makes Dragon Ball retrieval a coordinated rather than random process. Without it, Dragon Ball wish deployment is substantially slower, more difficult, and less likely to succeed before a competing party retrieves the relevant Ball. Bulma Brief holds the only design specifications for the Dragon Radar in existence. The HPB requires that this concentration of access be resolved.

- **The Action:** Subject must deposit complete Dragon Radar design specifications with the HPB's Extraordinary Asset Division, under a Shared Access Framework that makes the specifications available to any certified Wish Deployment Coordinator who meets the Bureau's training and accountability requirements. Subject retains authorship credit and a right of first consultation on applications. Subject loses sole control of access to the instrument that enables Dragon Ball retrieval.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Dragon Radar is the most significant individual piece of IP Subject has created. It is also the instrument through which she has maintained de facto monopoly on Dragon Ball retrieval coordination. Opening its design to vetted others does not diminish what she built; it changes who else can access what she built.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Dragon Radar access monopoly resolved. Dragon Ball retrieval becomes a capability multiple certified parties can conduct, not one household's sole operational province. Cross-referenced to HPB-12026-13-CC's Wish Deployment Covenant.

---

### **2. Personal Scientific IP — Non-Weapons Publication Mandate**

Subject's 47 individually registered patents include advances in matter compression, biological enhancement, and temporal mechanics that the Bureau's Technical Assessment Division classifies as the most consequential personal scientific IP portfolio in the Jump Universe audit queue. These advances are currently held as personal property and licensed selectively. The Bureau requires broader access to civilian-application findings.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish an annual publication program — through Capsule Corp's Research Foundation or an independently structured vehicle of Subject's choosing — through which all non-weapons civilian-application scientific findings are published in peer-reviewed form within 18 months of development. Weapons applications are exempted. Clean energy, medical, and fundamental physics advances are not. The temporal mechanics research is subject to a separate review panel (see HPB-12026-13-CC time travel provisions) before publication.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject publishes what she has learned, to people she did not choose, who will build on it without necessarily crediting her, which the Bureau suspects will bother her more than she will admit. The Bureau notes she will get credit in citation records and that the scientific community will know exactly whose work they are building on. The Bureau is including this note because it genuinely believes Subject will find it more comforting than the Bureau finding it would, and because Subject is one of the few audit subjects the Bureau has encountered who would care about the citation record more than the royalty structure.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Personal Scientific IP hoarding classification partially resolved. The world gains access to advances it did not have. Temporal mechanics IP remains in review pending the time travel governance framework established in HPB-12026-13-CC.

---

### **3. Personal Wish Deployment — Pre-Authorization Requirement**

The Dragon Ball Wish Deployment Covenant (HPB-12026-13-CC) governs Capsule Corporation's institutional role in wish deployments. That Covenant does not capture Bulma Brief's individual standing — her personal relationships, her personal Dragon Radar access, and her personal history as the architect of more Dragon Ball retrievals than any other documented individual. The Bureau requires that her individual wish-deployment activity be governed under a parallel personal covenant.

- **The Action:** Subject must execute a **Personal Wish Deployment Pre-Authorization Agreement** stipulating that any Dragon Ball retrieval or wish deployment coordinated, initiated, or participated in by Subject in her individual capacity (as distinct from her Capsule Corp role) requires 48-hour advance notice to the HPB's Extraordinary Asset Division, with a brief description of the intended wish. The Bureau's review is non-binding — it cannot prevent Subject from proceeding. The record of what was proposed and what was wished for is, however, formally logged. Emergency provisions apply: if the circumstances are sufficiently time-sensitive that 48 hours is not available, Subject must file a retroactive notice within 24 hours of the wish deployment.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject accepts, for the first time, that someone else will know in advance when she intends to use the Dragon Balls. She does not need that person's permission. She does need to tell them. The Bureau notes that this is a substantially smaller sacrifice than the Bureau initially proposed, and that it has arrived at 48-hour pre-authorization specifically because it believes Subject is more likely to comply with a requirement that respects her autonomy than one that constrains it, and that partial compliance from Bulma Brief is more valuable than full theoretical compliance from someone less likely to be the one actually coordinating wish deployments.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Personal wish-deployment activity enters the Bureau's tracking framework. The HPB now has a formal relationship with the individual who has coordinated more Dragon Ball retrievals than anyone in documented history. The Bureau considers this significant.

It is her unique personal capability set. The Bureau is governing what can be governed. Subject's RTWI remains above threshold and is subject to annual review.*

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Citizens** and recite the following:

> *"I found the Dragon Balls the first time because I wanted to wish for a boyfriend. I want the record to reflect that this is where all of this started, because I think it's actually important context for understanding how these things work and why governance of them matters. I did not know what I was starting. I do now. I have used the Dragon Balls to bring people back from the dead, to fix things I caused, and to fix things I didn't cause but could fix. I don't regret any of it. I'm signing this agreement not because I'm sorry I did those things but because I understand that someone keeping track of when I do them is a reasonable request from a society that has to live with the outcomes. The Bureau gets forty-eight hours. If the world is ending, it gets twenty-four hours after. I think this is fair. The Bureau agrees it's fair, which means one of us is probably being more reasonable than usual."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Asset Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate standard CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK enforcement procedures, including a formal Bureau request to the Capsule Corp board (HPB-12026-13-CC) to suspend Subject's individual access to Dragon Ball retrieval infrastructure pending compliance. The Bureau notes that it is not certain this enforcement mechanism is practically effective — Subject can build another Dragon Radar — but that it represents the Bureau's best available option and that it is filing this document in the hope that Subject will find the 48-hour notice requirement so reasonable that enforcement becomes unnecessary. The Bureau is choosing to be optimistic about this. The Bureau's record with optimism in the Dragon Ball audit queue is mixed.
