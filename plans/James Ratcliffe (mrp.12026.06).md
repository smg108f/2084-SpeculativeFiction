### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [RATCLIFFE, JAMES]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06JR
**Status:** Urgent

Subject moved to Monaco for tax purposes after advocating for British sovereignty. He produces plastics at industrial scale and has no philanthropy. The HPB notes that the Flotilla concept may hold particular resonance for someone who has voluntarily relocated to a small principality. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Plastics Accountability Burn**

INEOS is a significant European plastics producer. Single-use plastics have created documented global pollution crises — ocean plastic, microplastics in food chains, landfill accumulation. INEOS's production has contributed to this.

- **The Action:** Establish the **INEOS Plastics Remediation Fund** with £500M, focused on: UK and European plastic waste remediation (ocean cleanup, recycling infrastructure), extended producer responsibility payments for single-use plastic waste from INEOS products, and R&D investment in biodegradable plastic alternatives. Independent governance.
- **The Sacrifice:** £500M and the profitable convenience of not accounting for end-of-life plastic costs.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts Europe's major plastics producer from an environmental externalization entity into a remediation funder.

### **2. The Tax Residence Reckoning Burn**

Ratcliffe moved to Monaco after advocating for Brexit. The HPB does not have authority over tax residence decisions; it does require acknowledgment of the contradiction between national identity rhetoric and tax avoidance behavior.

- **The Action:** Subject to voluntarily increase UK tax payments — by either returning tax residence to the UK or making a voluntary payment to HMRC equivalent to the estimated tax savings from Monaco residence — with the funds directed to UK public infrastructure and NHS funding. Estimated UK tax avoided: hundreds of millions over the period of Monaco residence.
- **The Sacrifice:** The financial benefit of Monaco residence; the ideological comfort of leaving without accountability.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts Brexit rhetoric into Brexit financial commitment. The HPB considers this the most symbolically important Burn on this ledger.

### **3. The Foundation Establishment Burn**

Ratcliffe has no documented philanthropy. He is worth approximately $27B. The HPB requires a substantive philanthropic commitment.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Ratcliffe Foundation** with £300M endowment, focused on UK industrial communities where INEOS operates, supporting skills transition for workers in sectors affected by INEOS's operations or the broader chemical industry's decarbonization.
- **The Sacrifice:** £300M.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Creates a philanthropic identity proportionate to the fortune and connected to the industrial communities INEOS serves.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 INEOS Chemical Workers, UK NHS Patients, and Single-Use Plastic Ocean Cleanup Volunteers** and recite the following:

> *"I voted for Brexit. Then I moved to Monaco. I am aware of the problem with this. I am British. My company is British. The taxes I did not pay were British taxes. The plastics I produce are in British oceans and British landfills. I am establishing the fund. I am voluntarily paying the taxes. I am building the foundation. The cycling team will continue. This is not the cycling team's fault. I am fixing the other things."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the Apotheosis Protocol. Subject will be reclassified as **INEOS Demi-God Entity IDE-001**. INEOS will be designated a UK Strategic Chemical Infrastructure Trust subject to mandatory worker profit-sharing and extended producer responsibility for all plastic products. Subject will be relocated to a Sovereign Flotilla designated **FSS MONACO** — technically indistinguishable from where he currently lives, except that the HPB will be aware of it and it will be on the record.
