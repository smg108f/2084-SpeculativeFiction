### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [MALONE, JOHN]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06JM3
**Status:** Urgent

Subject owns 2 million acres and has acknowledged paying minimal federal income tax. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Land Commons Burn**

Two million acres of private US land in one person's hands is the HPB's primary structural concern. The land itself is not the problem — what the ownership structure means for rural communities, water access, and public land adjacency is.

- **The Action:** Subject must: (a) permanently protect 500,000 acres of his 2 million-acre holding through transfer to the Nature Conservancy, Trust for Public Land, or state wildlife agencies — as permanent conservation land open to public access for recreation and hunting, with easements that run with the land in perpetuity; (b) establish a **Malone Rural Community Fund** — $100M — for the rural communities adjacent to his land holdings in Maine, New Mexico, and Colorado: water access rights programs, rural broadband (ironic symmetry), agricultural support, and public access trail development; (c) commit to allowing public recreational access to at least 300,000 additional acres of his land through a permanent public access easement.
- **The Sacrifice:** 500,000 acres donated + $100M.

### **2. The Tax Equivalence Burn**

Malone has publicly acknowledged minimizing his federal tax exposure through complex structuring. The HPB does not prosecute legal tax minimization; it requires proportionate public investment as equivalence.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund $200M to federal public goods causes that would have been funded by the taxes not paid — specifically: (a) rural infrastructure (broadband, roads, water) in states where his land is located; (b) public media funding (CPB, NPR/PBS) — recognizing that Malone's cable media legacy was built partly through regulatory frameworks enabled by public broadcasting's existence; (c) rural education infrastructure in the counties where his land represents a significant fraction of the tax base.
- **The Sacrifice:** $200M.
