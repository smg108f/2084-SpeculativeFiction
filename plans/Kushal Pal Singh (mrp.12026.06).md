### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [SINGH, KUSHAL PAL]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06KPS
**Status:** Urgent

Subject built India's largest real estate company on agricultural land acquired from Haryana farmers. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Farmer Land Conversion Restitution Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must fund ₹15B (~$180M) for the **DLF Farmer Legacy Fund**: (a) supplemental payments to Haryana farmers from whom DLF acquired land between 1980 and 2010, calculated as the difference between the government compensation rates paid and 20% of the eventual commercial value DLF received — a partial restitution of the value gap that development created; (b) agricultural community investment in Haryana — funding rural infrastructure, irrigation, and crop support in the villages displaced by Gurgaon's expansion.
- **The Sacrifice:** ₹15B (~$180M).

### **2. The Gurgaon Affordable Housing Burn**

DLF built Gurgaon for the upper-middle class and corporate tenants. The workers who staff those offices and clean those malls live in distant informal settlements.

- **The Action:** Subject must commit DLF to building 10,000 affordable housing units in Gurgaon — at genuinely affordable rents for the service workers who maintain the city DLF built — funded ₹8B (~$96M) personally to cover the below-market pricing gap.
- **The Sacrifice:** ₹8B (~$96M).

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Haryana Farmers' Families, Gurgaon Service Workers, and DLF Tenant Companies**:

> *"Before DLF, this was farmland. We bought it and built the city. The farmers received what the government set as compensation. The commercial value we created was many times more. The Legacy Fund acknowledges the gap between what they were paid and what we built. The affordable housing puts DLF's construction capacity to work for the people who maintain what we built, not just the people who profit from it."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** Subject will be assigned to **FSS DLF** — a vessel modeled architecturally on DLF's signature glass tower aesthetic, but operating as floating affordable housing for Gurgaon's service workers — anchored off the Haryana coast, with a waiting list managed by lottery, demonstrating that DLF's construction capability can build homes for everyone, not just the people who can afford Cyber City rents.
