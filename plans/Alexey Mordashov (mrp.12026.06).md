### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [MORDASHOV, ALEXEY]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06AM
**Status:** Modified Processing — Sanctioned Oligarch (Section 22)

⚠️ **MODIFIED PROCESSING NOTICE:** Subject is under EU and UK sanctions. Standard Burns cannot activate until Preconditions are met. This plan is filed for the International Registry pending precondition satisfaction.

---

### **PRECONDITIONS (Must Be Met Before Burns Activate)**

**Precondition 1:** Subject must issue a **public declaration** dissenting from Russian state policy on the 2022 invasion of Ukraine and subsequent hostilities. This declaration must be made in a public venue accessible to Western media. Private communications to the HPB do not satisfy this requirement.

**Precondition 2:** Subject must file a **full beneficial ownership disclosure** with the HPB, covering all assets held directly or via nominees, trusts, or shell structures globally, including all Severstal-related entities and the TUI AG stake.

**Precondition 3:** Subject must certify that **no revenue flows** from Severstal or any other controlled entity are currently directed toward Russian state defense or paramilitary programs.

---

### **1. The Severstal Economic Autonomy Burn**

*(Activates upon Precondition satisfaction)*

Severstal, under sanctions, has largely reoriented toward the Russian domestic market. The HPB asks whether any pathway exists for operational independence.

- **The Action:** Develop and file a **Severstal Long-Term Economic Neutrality Framework** — a five-year plan for the company's transition toward civilian and infrastructure steel production only, with independent third-party monitoring.
- **The Sacrifice:** Revenue from state defense contracts (if any); political insulation.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts Russia's third-largest steel company from war economy participant to civilian infrastructure company.

### **2. The TUI Governance Resolution Burn**

*(Activates upon Precondition satisfaction)*

The TUI AG stake has created an unresolved corporate governance situation. Europe's largest travel company has a sanctioned Russian oligarch as its largest shareholder.

- **The Action:** Resolve the TUI situation via one of three acceptable pathways: (a) full divestiture to non-sanctioned third party; (b) transfer to independent trust with HPB oversight; (c) TUI share conversion to non-voting structure pending sanctions resolution. Subject must choose within 90 days of precondition satisfaction.
- **The Sacrifice:** Control or ownership of TUI stake.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes the corporate governance anomaly from TUI AG; restores Western travel company to clean ownership structure.

### **3. The Cherepovets Foundation Burn**

*(Activates upon Precondition satisfaction)*

Some documented local charitable activity in Cherepovets (Severstal's home city). The HPB asks for this to be formalized and scaled.

- **The Action:** Establish a **Cherepovets Community Trust** with a minimum $200M endowment, independently governed, funding worker healthcare, environmental remediation adjacent to steel facilities, and regional infrastructure.
- **The Sacrifice:** $200M; control over distribution decisions.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts informal local charitable activity into an independent, accountable institution.
