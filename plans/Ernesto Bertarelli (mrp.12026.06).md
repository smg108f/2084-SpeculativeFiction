## **PROBATIONARY CONDITIONS (Ongoing)**

**PC-1:** Bertarelli Foundation ocean conservation programs must continue and be independently verified by a qualified marine science institution annually.

**PC-2:** Waypoint Capital must exclude investments in sectors with documented severe ocean harm: deep-sea mining operations, industrial bottom trawling fleet ownership, and direct investment in single-hull tankers.

**PC-3:** Annual Probationary Status Report filed with HPB by March 1 each year, including: total Bertarelli Foundation disbursements, MPA coverage status, and any changes to Waypoint Capital's investment mandate.

---

## **EXPANSION COMMITMENT (Voluntary — Probationary Saint Enhancement)**

The HPB invites, but does not require, the following:

- **Ocean Plastic Initiative:** $75M commitment to ocean plastic source reduction (complementing the Bertarelli Foundation's existing science focus with a material intervention program).
- **Developing World MPA Support:** $50M to support developing nations in establishing and enforcing their own MPAs — particularly Pacific Island states that have the ocean but lack the regulatory capacity to protect it.

*These are not Burns — they are the extension of what subject has already demonstrated he knows how to do well.*
