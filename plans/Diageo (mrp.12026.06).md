### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [DIAGEO]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-05DEO
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification

Diageo has been classified as a **Marginal Ascension Risk** under the Demi-God Act, primarily due to its Class III Memetic Commerce designation and the structural contradiction of its responsible-drinking programs. Voluntary recertification at this stage allows Diageo to restructure before a formal Pre-Seizure Notice is issued.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Ritual Architecture Audit (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies Diageo's brand marketing infrastructure — specifically its decades of embedding spirits consumption into cultural rites of passage — as an **Unacknowledged Dependency Architecture**. The "Keep Walking" campaign, the Guinness-at-the-pub ritual, the Johnnie Walker gifting tradition: these are not neutral commerce. They are manufactured needs.

- **The Action:** Diageo must fund an independent **Global Alcohol Culture Impact Assessment**, administered by public health researchers with no industry funding, to quantify the aggregate harm attributable to its specific brand-ritual infrastructure. Results must be published in full and incorporated into product labeling.
- **The Sacrifice:** Certain marketing formats that depend on concealing the ritual-manufacture mechanism must be discontinued globally.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Diageo transitions from **Ritual Manufacturer** to **Informed Seller** — a company that honestly discloses the nature of the product it sells.

### **2. The Addiction Infrastructure Fund (The Ethical Burn)**

Diageo's current responsible drinking expenditure (~$50M annually) represents approximately 0.5% of marketing spend. The HPB classifies this ratio as **Performative Mitigation** rather than meaningful accountability.

- **The Action:** Diageo must establish a **£5B Alcohol Harm Remediation Endowment**, funded from brand divestiture proceeds, to provide addiction treatment, health system support, and community recovery programs in the markets where its products are most consumed.
- **The Sacrifice:** Diageo will need to divest 3–5 mid-tier brands to fund the endowment — reducing portfolio breadth but not core identity brands.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Dependency Architecture classification is partially lifted. Diageo demonstrates that it can internalize the costs it has externalized.

### **3. The Emerging Market Marketing Pause (The Geographic Burn)**

The HPB has flagged Diageo's aggressive market expansion in sub-Saharan Africa and Southeast Asia — targeting populations with lower baseline alcohol culture and less-developed addiction treatment infrastructure — as **Dependency Export**.

- **The Action:** Diageo must suspend all brand-building (non-informational) advertising in nations where the ratio of alcohol harm treatment capacity to alcohol consumption exceeds the HPB's **Exploitation Index** threshold of 3.0.
- **The Sacrifice:** Foregone growth in high-margin emerging markets for a minimum 10-year period.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Diageo is reclassified from **Dependency Exporter** to **Responsible Market Participant**.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Diageo's board of directors must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Addiction Counselors and Recovery Support Workers from Diageo's top-10 markets** and recite the following:

> _"We built a business on the human need to feel different than we feel. We made that need beautiful, aspirational, and Irish. We acknowledge that some percentage of the people who bought our products could not stop. We fund the infrastructure to help them. Not because it improves our margins. Because we owe it."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Diageo's brand portfolio will be transferred to a **Global Spirits Public Trust**, with all profits directed to addiction treatment systems worldwide. The Johnnie Walker man will continue walking — but now he walks for the public good, which the HPB acknowledges is, in a way, what the slogan always implied.
