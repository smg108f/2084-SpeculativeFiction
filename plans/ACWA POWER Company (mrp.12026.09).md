### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ACWA POWER COMPANY]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09ACW
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

ACWA Power's sovereign-backed control over desalination infrastructure in water-stressed regions represents the Bureau's highest-priority resource-chokepoint concern this cycle. Recertification targets water-access guarantees directly.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Water Access Guarantee (The Largest Burn)**

- **The Action:** Establish binding, government-independent minimum water-access guarantees and price ceilings for domestic consumption in all host countries, insulated from broader commercial or geopolitical negotiations.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced pricing and negotiating leverage tied to host-country water dependency.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Directly resolves the Bureau's core concern about non-substitutable resource leverage.

### **2. The Sovereign Ownership Transparency**

- **The Action:** Publish full disclosure of Saudi Public Investment Fund ownership terms and any linkage between water-infrastructure contracts and broader bilateral political or financial arrangements.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of opacity currently allowing water infrastructure and geopolitical objectives to remain formally separate.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the "Critical Political Agency" flag by making sovereign linkage visible.

### **3. The Independent Infrastructure Audit**

- **The Action:** Submit all desalination and power infrastructure to independent, host-country-selected engineering and capacity audits, published publicly.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced operational opacity around actual versus claimed capacity.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Ensures water-access guarantees are backed by verified infrastructure capacity.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, ACWA Power's board must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Residents of Water-Stressed Host Nations** and recite the following:

> _"We hold the technology that makes your water drinkable, and a sovereign fund behind us that gives us leverage over more than water alone. We will guarantee your access to water separately from anything else we might want from your government."_

Water-access guarantees will be transferred to oversight by an **International Water Access Tribunal**, independent of both ACWA Power and host-government political relationships.
