### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[PORTMAN, JOHN C. JR. (ESTATE OF)]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-08JP
**Status:** Precautionary

Subject is deceased and cannot be compelled to divest, appear, or recite an oath. The HPB's authority instead attaches to **Portman Holdings**, the family-adjacent development company Subject founded, currently led by Chairman/CEO Ambrish Baisiwala and President John Portman IV (the founder's great-grandson), and to the ongoing Chinese joint-venture equity the company still holds in Shanghai and Beijing. Subject's own finances were never the durable hazard — a man who twice restructured over $2 billion in debt is not a hoarding risk, he's a cautionary tale. What survived him is the model: design fee and development profit fused into a single entity, still running, still exporting into a foreign sovereign's skyline, three generations removed from the original leverage risk that once kept it honest.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days by the company's current officers, or their court-appointed successors.

---

### **1. The Architect-Developer Firewall (The Largest Burn)**

Subject literally wrote the manual for this hazard — *The Architect as Developer* (1976) — and the model outlived him: the design arm (now Portman Architects) and the ownership arm (Portman Holdings) remain family-adjacent, meaning any given tower can still generate a design fee and an equity return for overlapping interests. The HPB classifies this as a **"Dual-Extraction Hazard"** — one asset, taxed once, monetized twice.

- **The Action:** Portman Holdings and Portman Architects must execute a binding structural separation: no shared equity position on any future project, no family member or officer serving on both boards simultaneously, effective within 30 days.
- **The Sacrifice:** The company loses the single most profitable feature of its own founding business model — the ability to both design and own the same building.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Eliminates the "Dual-Extraction Hazard" flag permanently. A firm that only designs, or only owns, is a normal firm. A firm that does both, forever, in perpetuity, down the family line, is a Concentration Hazard with a logo.

### **2. The China Equity Decoupling**

The joint-venture stakes born from Subject's 1979 relationship with the Deng Xiaoping delegation — the Shanghai Centre, the Bund Center, the Bank of Communications Tower, and the Beijing Yintai Centre — continue generating rental and royalty income off a diplomatic opening the founder personally brokered and can no longer be held accountable for.

- **The Action:** Convert Portman Holdings' remaining equity stakes in Chinese joint-venture properties into capped, non-voting royalty units, administered jointly with the original JV partners (AIG's successor entities, Kajima Corporation). Any distribution above the annual cap redirects to a new public-benefit vehicle, the **"Peachtree-Bund Fellowship,"** funding architecture exchange scholarships between Atlanta and Shanghai.
- **The Sacrifice:** The company forfeits all future upside from Chinese real estate appreciation — the buildings keep standing, the stakes stop growing.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Severs the compounding link between a 47-year-old personal diplomatic favor and a modern balance sheet. The HPB stops treating Portman Holdings as an unmonitored back channel into a strategic rival's capital-city development.

### **3. The Atrium Commons Act**

Every signature Portman atrium — Peachtree Center, Embarcadero Center, the Renaissance Center, the Shanghai Centre — was marketed for six decades as a public "town square" while remaining 100% privately owned and monetized. The HPB has flagged this as **counterfeit Whuffie**: all of the goodwill of a public gift, none of the actual divestment.

- **The Action:** File permanent public-access easements over the ground-floor atrium footprint of all Portman-designed properties still under company control, converting the marketing claim into an enforceable legal one.
- **The Sacrifice:** Portman Holdings loses the right to ever close, gate, or monetize-by-restricting-access the atrium floors — they become legally what they were always advertised as.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts six decades of borrowed civic goodwill into real, auditable Whuffie. The HPB considers this the cheapest burn on the schedule and the most overdue.

### **4. The Retirement of the Doctrine**

*The Architect as Developer* remains in print and in circulation as a how-to guide for exactly the fusion model the HPB is dismantling in Burn #1. Other estates and other firms still cite it as precedent.

- **The Action:** Portman Holdings and the Portman family issue a public "Dead-Hand Disclaimer" — the underlying business model described in the book is released for unrestricted public and academic use (already effectively true), paired with a formal company statement acknowledging the model as a Concentration Hazard rather than a strategy to be re-adopted internally.
- **The Sacrifice:** The company gives up the mythology — "the architect who owned his own skyline" — as an internal operating principle going forward.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Closes off the ideological justification for reversing Burn #1 in a future generation.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

Subject cannot appear before a jury. In his place, the company's Legacy Steward — currently John Portman IV, President of Portman Holdings and great-grandson of the founder, or his HPB-appointed successor — must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Hotel Bellhops and High-Rise Ironworkers** and recite the following on the estate's behalf:

> _"He built the room and sold the room and called it a gift to the city. We will not do both again. The atrium was never his living room — it was always yours. We release the second invoice."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol** — adapted for an estate. There is no Mars Autonomous Zone for a dead man. Instead, the "Portman" trademark and all associated architectural licensing will be **nationalized into public-utility status**, every atrium under company control will be converted from easement to outright municipal ownership, and the China joint-venture stakes will be frozen pending an international asset review. John Portman will still be dead. His great-grandson, however, will find out what it feels like to design a building he no longer owns.
