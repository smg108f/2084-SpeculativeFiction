### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [CONRAD N. HILTON FOUNDATION]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-35CNHF
**Status:** Precautionary / Religious Institutional Preference Disclosure and Deployment Rate Order

The Conrad N. Hilton Foundation has been classified PROBATIONARY SAINT. Recertification requirements are modest. The Foundation's programmatic work is genuine and the humanitarian prize is admirable. The HPB's concerns focus on the Catholic Sisters program's religious institutional preferences and the below-average deployment rate.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Catholic Institutional Preference Disclosure Burn**

The Conrad N. Hilton Foundation's Catholic Sisters program funds organizations aligned with Catholic mission — specifically the work of Catholic religious sisters globally. This is a named strategic focus area, meaning that non-Catholic organizations doing equivalent development, healthcare, or educational work are not eligible for this program's funding. Conrad Hilton's devout Catholic faith is the documented origin of this preference. The HPB does not object to philanthropically motivated religious preferences. It requires that they be publicly explained in full.

- **The Action:** The Foundation must publish a **Catholic Institutional Preference Statement** — explaining: (a) the theological and philanthropic rationale for a dedicated Catholic Sisters focus area; (b) the selection criteria for Catholic Sisters grantees and whether organizations affiliated with Catholic dioceses or congregations accused of institutional abuse are eligible; (c) the proportion of total annual grantmaking directed to explicitly Catholic institutions across all program areas; (d) the Foundation's policy on grantmaking to non-Catholic organizations with equivalent programmatic impact.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Catholic Sisters program's funding criteria become publicly legible. Grantees and the public can evaluate the Foundation's religious institutional preferences with full information.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Foundation's religious philanthropic mission is explicit and accountable rather than embedded within a pluralistic-sounding portfolio.

---

### **2. The Deployment Rate Recalibration Burn**

Annual grants of ~$300M represent approximately 4% of the Foundation's $7.4B endowment — below the legal minimum spirit of 5% and significantly below what the HPB recommends for foundations with active humanitarian crises as named focus areas. The Foundation funds refugee programs and homelessness services — contexts where urgency is documented and ongoing. A 4% deployment rate is difficult to justify against these stated priorities.

- **The Action:** The Foundation must publish a **Deployment Rate Commitment** — stating its policy on the relationship between endowment growth and deployment, and committing to a minimum 5.5% annual deployment rate within two years. The Foundation should specifically address whether increased deployment would meaningfully expand its homelessness and refugee programs in light of documented need.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Foundation's endowment growth strategy is publicly subordinated to its stated humanitarian urgency.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The humanitarian prize the Foundation awards annually is matched by a deployment rate commensurate with humanitarian urgency.

---

### **3. The Homelessness Effectiveness Accountability Burn**

The Hilton Foundation is among the most significant private funders of homelessness solutions in Southern California. Southern California continues to have one of the most severe homelessness crises in the United States. The HPB recommends — but does not require — that the Foundation publish an annual **Homelessness Effectiveness Report** — documenting the measurable outcomes achieved by Foundation-funded homelessness programs, the gap between funded capacity and documented need, and the Foundation's assessment of whether its current investment level is proportionate to the scale of the problem.

- **The Action:** Publish annual Homelessness Effectiveness Report with independent outcome evaluation.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Foundation's homelessness investments are evaluated against homelessness outcomes rather than only against grant disbursement.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Foundation's most geographically specific philanthropic commitment becomes publicly accountable.
