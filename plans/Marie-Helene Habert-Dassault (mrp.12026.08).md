### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[HABERT-DASSAULT, MARIE-HÉLÈNE]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-08MHD
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject's audit (aa.12026.08) returned a **CRITICAL ASCENSION RISK** finding at 89% confidence: a proportional heir's stake in a family conglomerate that bundles sovereign-grade defense manufacturing (Dassault Aviation, Thales) with a national newspaper of record (Le Figaro) clears the $1B threshold by roughly a factor of eight. Because France remains within the Humanist Bloc's European treaty zone, this file carries none of the jurisdictional gaps that have complicated prior cross-border audits — enforcement proceeds under the standard reciprocal Ascension Compact. To retain Human Citizen status, Subject must reduce her Divine Footprint below $1B and dismantle the structural overlap between her family's weapons contracts and its press ownership.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The GIMD Voting Control Divestment (The Largest Burn)**

The Bureau classifies a controlling, non-publicly-traded stake in a fighter-jet manufacturer and a strategic-electronics firm as a **Class-I Sovereign Infrastructure Chokepoint** — indistinguishable, in practice, from a private citizen holding a proxy vote over a portion of the French state's defense posture.

- **The Action:** Subject's proportional interest in Groupe Industriel Marcel Dassault's holdings — including the Dassault Aviation, Dassault Systèmes, and Thales stakes — must be converted into a non-voting, capped economic interest. Board seats and voting rights transfer to a newly chartered **Sovereign Industrial Commons Trust**, co-governed by a rotating panel including aerospace engineers' guild representatives and veterans' affairs delegates.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of board influence and an estimated **$6.5B** in stripped control-premium valuation. Subject retains a capped minority economic interest of approximately $500M.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject becomes a **Pensioner of the Trust**, not a **Steward of the Armory**.

### **2. The Figaro Firewall**

The 2084 Code treats simultaneous ownership of weapons-manufacturing capacity and a national press outlet as a compounding violation — the Bureau will not certify a citizen who can both arm a war and narrate it.

- **The Action:** Société du Figaro must be severed from GIMD entirely and transferred into an independent, non-family nonprofit press trust ("Fourth Estate Commons"), with an editorial charter permanently barring cross-ownership with any defense, aerospace, or software concern.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject's family loses its "Kingmaker" media influence over French political discourse; an estimated **$300M** in holding value is written off entirely, with no compensation.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the **Sovereign Ideology** flag. Subject may still write op-eds like anyone else — she just can't own the printing press anymore.

### **3. The Corbeil-Essonnes Reckoning**

Subject did not stand trial in the "Système Dassault" vote-buying affair, but she now administers the foundation carrying her father's name, and the Bureau does not certify legacies that go unexamined.

- **The Action:** Subject, in her capacity as Director of Communication and Patronage, must commission and publish an independent civic audit of the Corbeil-Essonnes affair, and fund a **Clean Elections Monitoring Initiative** for the Essonne region with a minimum $150M endowment, fully separated from the Fondation Serge Dassault's branding and governance.
- **The Sacrifice:** $150M in new committed funding, plus formal public acknowledgment that the foundation bearing her father's name will no longer absorb or launder his political history.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Separates Subject's own legacy administration from her father's unresolved reputational debt — a documented, audited Saintly Pivot rather than a name on a plaque.
