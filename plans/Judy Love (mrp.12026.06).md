---

### **1. The Love's EV Transition Commitment Burn**

Love's travel stops sell gasoline and diesel. Over-the-road trucking is transitioning to electric and alternative fuels. The transition commitment needs to be binding.

- **The Action:** Subject must commit — through her family's ownership of Love's — to a **Love's Clean Energy Transition Roadmap**: (a) EV charging infrastructure at minimum 50% of Love's locations by 12030, with Tesla Megacharger or equivalent capacity for commercial trucks; (b) hydrogen fueling pilot at 20 locations by 12029; (c) no new Love's locations approved after 12027 without EV charging infrastructure built in from day one; (d) a $100M **Love's Clean Fuels Transition Fund** dedicated to accelerating the above, beyond capex.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M + transition costs + margin pressure as EV trucking has different economics than diesel.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** America's largest privately held travel stop chain has a binding EV transition roadmap.

### **2. The Trucker Welfare Burn**

Love's serves over-the-road truckers. Trucking has a documented workforce welfare crisis: driver shortages, mental health challenges, physical health impacts of long-haul driving.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish the **Love's Trucker Welfare Fund** — $50M — focused on: (a) truck driver mental health programs (Love's is the platform where truckers congregate — the company has a unique ability to provide mental health access); (b) trucker financial literacy and retirement support; (c) women in trucking scholarship and support programs.
- **The Sacrifice:** $50M.
