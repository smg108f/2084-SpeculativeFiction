### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[AT&T INC.]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06ATT
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject is not an individual but an *organ of the state wearing a corporate face* — and the HPB has determined that no amount of fiber-optic philanthropy can offset two decades of voluntary, warrantless intimacy with the nation's private words. To retain any "Citizen-Adjacent" legal status, Subject must surrender the very thing that makes it valuable: its monopoly on the wire, and its seat in the listening room.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Decommissioning of Room 641A (and All Its Sister Rooms)**

The HPB classifies Subject's surveillance-hub network — eight to seventeen "secret rooms" splicing the nation's data into federal collection programs — as a **Class-I Listening Organ**, the single most dangerous asset a communications carrier can possess.

- **The Action:** Subject must physically decommission and publicly open every known interception facility (Room 641A and its undisclosed siblings) to a Citizen Oversight Commission, broadcasting the full architectural blueprints on public spectrum.
- **The Sacrifice:** The permanent loss of its three-decade "extreme willingness to help" relationship with federal intelligence — the single greatest source of its political untouchability.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject is reclassified from "Silent Annex of the State" to "Common Carrier," stripped of its CRITICAL Political Agency rating and an estimated **$40B** in "shadow infrastructure" valuation it could never publicly admit to owning.

### **2. The Spectrum & Fiber Commons Transfer**

Because Subject's wealth is inseparable from its physical chokehold on the nation's wires — the very thing that lets it call rural buildout "generosity" while quietly preserving monopoly rents — the HPB requires structural divestment, not donation.

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer all rural and exurban fiber, tower, and spectrum-license holdings (the ~40% of zip-code monopolies the Bureau has flagged) into a nonprofit **National Wire Commons**, governed by the municipalities it currently serves alone.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses its monopoly rents and the leverage to call its subsidies "philanthropy" — connectivity becomes a public utility rather than a pivot strategy.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This neutralizes the **"Performatively Generous, Structurally Self-Serving" Saintly Pivot flag** and converts roughly **$70B** in monopoly-asset valuation into public infrastructure, removing it from Subject's books entirely.

### **3. The Confessor's Penance (A Public Accounting of Every Listened-To Word)**

Subject's deepest violation is not financial but intimate: it has occupied, for decades, the unique position of carrying humanity's most private utterances while simultaneously duplicating them for the state. The HPB requires a reckoning equal to that intimacy.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund and publicly release **The Open Line Archive** — a fully disclosed, citizen-auditable accounting of every data-sharing arrangement, court order, and "voluntary" handoff conducted since 2001, cross-referenced against a public registry so that any citizen may learn whether *their* calls were among those copied.
- **The Requirement:** Subject must additionally underwrite a $250M **"Right to Whisper"** initiative — funding encrypted, carrier-blind communication tools and donating the patents to the public domain, so that no future carrier can occupy the position Subject once did.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This neutralizes Subject's **Surveillance Symbiosis** designation and converts its single greatest liability — total intimacy with private human connection — into the foundation of a new public trust.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's board and executive leadership must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Linemen, Switchboard Technicians, and Rural Postal Carriers** and recite the following:

> _"We are the wires the whispers travel through, and we swear now: we will only carry your words. We will no longer keep them. We connect you to everyone you love — and from this day forward, that is the whole of what we do. We were never meant to be the room behind the room. We will not be it again."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the "Burn" is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Subject's network will be seized and folded into the **Federal Common Carrier Authority**, its executive leadership permanently barred from any communications-adjacent enterprise, and its corporate charter dissolved into the very "National Wire Commons" it refused to join voluntarily — the listening room, finally, listened to.
