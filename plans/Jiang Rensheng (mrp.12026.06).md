### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [JIANG, RENSHENG]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06JR2
**Status:** Precautionary — Public Health Sector Credit Applied

Subject controls Zhifei Biological Products, one of China's largest private vaccine companies. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Zhifei Rural Vaccine Access Burn**

HPV vaccines — among Zhifei's most important products for long-term public health — are priced at levels that make them difficult to access for rural Chinese families.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish and fund a **Zhifei Rural Immunization Access Program** — ¥1B (~$140M) endowment — to provide HPV vaccines, pneumococcal vaccines, and influenza vaccines at zero cost or subsidized cost to: (a) rural Chinese children and adolescents in counties where median income falls below national average; (b) rural Chinese adults over 50 without insurance access. The program is independently administered and not a Zhifei marketing channel.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥1B endowment; foregone revenue from rural market pricing.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** China's largest private vaccine company provides free vaccination to the rural populations most likely to miss the immunization coverage its products are supposed to provide.

### **2. The Global Vaccine Access Burn**

Zhifei has some international vaccine partnerships. The HPB asks for global health commitment.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund — through independent global health channels (Gavi, CEPI, or WHO vaccine access programs) — ¥500M (~$70M) to fund vaccine distribution in low-income countries where Zhifei has relevant product capacity (flu, HPV, pneumo). This is not a Zhifei export marketing fund; it is unrestricted grant funding to international vaccine access organizations.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥500M.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** A Chinese vaccine billionaire is a documented global vaccine access funder.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Rural Chinese Families Without Vaccine Insurance Access, Gavi Vaccine Program Recipients, and Zhifei Biologics Researchers**:

> *"Zhifei distributes vaccines. Vaccines prevent disease. The people who benefit most from vaccines are the people who can least afford to be sick. The Rural Immunization Fund means the rural families who can't afford our pricing get the vaccines anyway. The Gavi fund means it goes beyond China. Vaccines are a public good. The profit from them should serve the public."*
