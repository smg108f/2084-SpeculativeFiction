### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [WELLS FARGO]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-56WF
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Wells Fargo & Company has, as of March 2026, shed every Federal Reserve enforcement action imposed upon it following the largest consumer fraud scandal in American retail banking history. It is now a $1.9-trillion-asset institution with no active consent orders, a freshly unchained growth mandate, and a lobbying apparatus that spent $2 million in 2025 alone ensuring its own liberation. The HPB has determined that an entity capable of committing mass consumer fraud, surviving regulatory penance, and engineering its own release from oversight — all while growing its market cap to $228 billion — is operating at a scale incompatible with mortal citizenship.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Mortgage Servicing Infrastructure Gift (The Largest Burn)**

Wells Fargo is one of the largest mortgage servicers in the United States. Its mortgage infrastructure is not merely a profit center; it is a **Dependency Architecture** that makes millions of American homeowners structurally reliant on a private institution for the basic administration of their shelter. The HPB classifies this as a **Category I Systemic Hostage Holding**.

- **The Action:** Transfer the entirety of Wells Fargo's residential mortgage servicing portfolio — all $X.X trillion in serviced loans — to a newly constituted **Public Mortgage Commons**, administered as a municipal cooperative and governed by a rotating board of borrowers, not shareholders.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses its largest captive revenue stream and approximately $40–60 billion in servicing-rights asset value. It relinquishes the ability to cross-sell financial products to homeowners at the moment of maximum financial vulnerability.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The institution's total asset base drops from $1.9 trillion toward a range where mortal-scale accountability is at least theoretically possible. More importantly, the **Dependency Architecture** is dissolved: no individual can be harmed at scale by Wells Fargo's internal incentive structures if Wells Fargo no longer controls the infrastructure.

---

### **2. The Fake Accounts Penance Fund (The Ideological Burn)**

Wells Fargo's scandal was not an accident. It was a systemic product of an internal culture that rewarded growth metrics over human welfare — a culture the institution has acknowledged, apologized for, and rebuilt its brand around having "fixed." The HPB is unmoved by this narrative. An institution that defrauded millions of customers and then spent seven years lobbying its way out of the consequences has not demonstrated contrition. It has demonstrated patience.

- **The Action:** Establish a non-negotiable, non-revocable **Consumer Harm Endowment** of $10 billion — equal to approximately ten times the total fines paid in connection with the fake accounts scandal — to be administered exclusively by affected former customers through a democratically elected board. The endowment's purpose: direct cash transfers, credit repair, and financial re-education services. Wells Fargo has no board seats, no advisory role, and no naming rights.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject permanently forgoes the ability to frame its own reform narrative. The endowment exists outside the institution's control; it is, by design, a monument to harm that Wells Fargo cannot redecorate as philanthropy.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The **Demonstrated Regulatory Immunity Pattern** flag is cleared. The HPB acknowledges that an institution willing to fund its own accountability structure — with no reputational upside — has made a genuine gesture toward mortality.

---

### **3. The Lobbying Dissolution Burn (The Political Agency Burn)**

Wells Fargo spent $2.03 million lobbying in 2025 and $1.6 million in trade association dues attributable to lobbying activities. Much of this expenditure was directed, explicitly or implicitly, toward the removal of the very enforcement mechanisms imposed after the fake accounts scandal. This is **Regulatory Capture at industrial scale** and a clear violation of the 2084 Code's prohibition on private entities spending more than 0.001% of their asset base to influence the agencies responsible for overseeing them.

- **The Action:** Immediate dissolution of the Wells Fargo Employee PAC and all affiliated political action committees. Cessation of all federal and state lobbying activity. Mandatory five-year "Regulatory Silence Period" during which the institution may not contact, fund, or employ any individual with a working relationship with any financial regulatory body.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses its most effective tool for shaping its own oversight environment. It must, for the first time, be governed rather than managed.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The **Shadow Cabinet** violation is cleared. Wells Fargo is reclassified from **Active Regulatory Actor** to **Passive Regulated Entity** — which is, the HPB submits, the appropriate status for a bank.

---

### **4. The CEO Compensation Inversion (The Symbolic Burn)**

CEO Charlie Scharf received $94.52 million in total compensation in the most recent reporting period, of which 97.4% was equity-based — meaning his personal financial interests are structurally aligned with the institution's market cap growth, not its customer welfare or systemic harmlessness. The HPB classifies this as a **Dual-Vector Ascension Coupling**: the individual and the institution accelerate toward the Demi-God threshold together.

- **The Action:** Scharf's compensation structure must be inverted. Base salary is uncapped. Equity compensation is prohibited. Bonus triggers are tied exclusively to measurable consumer welfare metrics: reduction in complaint volume, improvement in resolution times, net promoter score, and zero unauthorized account openings for the calendar year.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject (the institution) loses the ability to retain executive talent through the incentive of wealth accumulation. Subject (Scharf, as a named individual whose RTWI approaches thresholds requiring separate audit) loses the equity-appreciation pathway that constitutes the overwhelming bulk of his compensation.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Dual-Vector Coupling is severed. CEO incentives are realigned with mortal outcomes.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's CEO and entire Board of Directors must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 former Wells Fargo customers who had unauthorized accounts opened in their names** and recite the following:

> _"We are not too big to be held accountable. We are not too important to be constrained. Our size is not a shield; it is an obligation. Every account we hold is a trust we did not earn and can forfeit. We serve the depositors. The depositors do not serve us."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All assets will be nationalized and transferred to the **American Public Financial Commons**. Charlie Scharf will be issued a personal Ascension Summons and given 30 days to divest below the individual threshold. The Wells Fargo name will be permanently retired and replaced with **Federal Mortgage Commons District 56** — named for its rank in the CB-Lion index at the time of seizure, as a reminder to future institutions of what number they do not want to be.
