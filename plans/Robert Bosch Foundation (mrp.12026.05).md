### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ROBERT BOSCH STIFTUNG]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-33RBS
**Status:** Precautionary / WWII Forced Labor Acknowledgment and Voting Structure Transparency Order

The Robert Bosch Stiftung has been classified PROBATIONARY SAINT. Recertification addresses two concerns: (1) the Foundation has not published a sustained accounting of Bosch GmbH's WWII forced labor use alongside its founder's resistance activities; and (2) the separation of Foundation shareholding from Bosch GmbH voting rights — while structurally sound — is not sufficiently disclosed to the public.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The WWII Forced Labor Historical Accounting Burn**

Robert Bosch GmbH used forced and slave labor in its production facilities during the final years of the Second World War. This occurred simultaneously with the "Bosch-Kreis" resistance activities — members of Bosch management who actively opposed the Nazi regime. These are not mutually exclusive. The Foundation has celebrated the resistance legacy appropriately. It has not comparably acknowledged the forced labor record.

- **The Action:** The Foundation must publish a **Complete Historical Accountability Statement** — a permanent, public narrative that addresses, with equal weight: (a) Robert Bosch's personal opposition to National Socialism and the Bosch-Kreis resistance network; (b) the use of forced and slave labor by Bosch GmbH during the war years; (c) the Foundation's assessment of what historical accountability for this record requires; (d) whether any of the Foundation's democracy or peace programs are specifically connected to this historical obligation. The HPB recommends consultation with historians who specialize in German industrial history during the Third Reich.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Foundation's public historical narrative includes the forced labor record alongside the resistance record. The whole story replaces the partial story.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Foundation's democracy and peace programs become historically grounded — not as abstract good works, but as specific responses to what Robert Bosch GmbH participated in, even while its founder opposed the regime that required it.

---

### **2. The Ownership/Voting Separation Transparency Burn**

The Robert Bosch Stiftung holds 94% of Bosch GmbH's share capital but intentionally holds no voting rights — these are vested in the Robert Bosch Industrietreuhand KG. This structure is deliberate and, in the HPB's assessment, well-designed: it allows the Foundation to benefit from the company's profits without exercising operational control, reducing the conflict-of-interest risk that affects foundations holding controlling stakes in companies. However, this structure is not widely understood or publicly disclosed in accessible form.

- **The Action:** The Foundation must publish an annual **Ownership Structure Transparency Report** — explaining in plain language: (a) the 94% capital / 0% voting split between the Foundation and Industrietreuhand KG; (b) how this structure was designed and what it was intended to prevent; (c) the mechanism by which the Foundation's dividend income is determined; (d) any circumstances under which the Foundation could or would seek to influence Bosch GmbH's corporate decisions.
- **The Sacrifice:** A governance structure that the Foundation considers a feature must be explained publicly rather than assumed as background knowledge.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Donors, grantees, and the German public can evaluate the Bosch Stiftung's independence from Bosch GmbH's commercial decisions with accurate structural information.

---

### **3. The Democracy Program Urgency Burn**

The Foundation has committed €11M to democracy projects in 12025 — a meaningful sum but modest relative to the €219M annual grants total (~5%). Given that the Foundation's own "Our Democracy Depends on Us" framing acknowledges a democratic crisis moment, the HPB recommends that the democracy program proportionally reflect this stated urgency.

- **The Action:** The Foundation must publish a **Democracy Deployment Commitment** — stating the percentage of annual grants directed to democracy programming through 12030, with a commitment that this proportion will not fall below 10% of annual grants while the democratic backsliding conditions that prompted the 12025 emergency framing persist.
- **The Sacrifice:** The Foundation's democracy urgency framing becomes binding rather than rhetorical.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** "Our Democracy Depends on Us" becomes a measurable commitment rather than a motto.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, the Robert Bosch Stiftung Board must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 European Democracy Educators Supported by the Foundation** and recite the following:

> *"Robert Bosch opposed the Nazis. He organized resistance. He also ran a company that used forced labor in the war's final years. We hold both of these things. We fund democracy because our founder knew what happens when democracy fails — and because his company participated in what happens when democracy fails. These are not separate histories. They are the same history. We publish it all. We spend at least 10% of our grants on democracy while democracy is under threat. We believe Robert Bosch's will — directing his company's profits to public benefit — was the right answer to what he witnessed. We are executing that will, with full knowledge of what we are accounting for."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Complete Historical Accountability Statement is not published within 30 days, the HPB will classify the Bosch Stiftung as operating under **Selective Historical Disclosure** — a condition in which a foundation's public narrative presents only the favorable portions of its origin history. The resistance record is real. The forced labor record is real. Robert Bosch deserves both to be told. His Foundation has the resources to tell them.
