### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[WALTER, MARK]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09MW
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Simultaneous control of the Dodgers and Lakers is the Bureau's central concern. Recertification requires reducing this to single-franchise control.

---

### **1. The One-City, One-Crown Divestiture (The Largest Burn)**

- **The Action:** Divest controlling interest in either the Dodgers or the Lakers (Subject's choice) to an ownership group with no existing stake in the other franchise.
- **The Sacrifice:** Surrenders the historic achievement of controlling both of LA's marquee teams.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Eliminates single-city sports-monopoly risk entirely.

### **2. The Fan Trust Allocation**

- **The Action:** Transfer a 5% community-benefit equity stake in the retained franchise to a Los Angeles fan and civic trust.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts pure ownership into shared civic stewardship.

### **3. The Sovereign Capital Disclosure**

- **The Action:** Publish full disclosure of the financing structure and any foreign capital involved in either franchise transaction.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Addresses the geopolitical dimension the audit flagged.
