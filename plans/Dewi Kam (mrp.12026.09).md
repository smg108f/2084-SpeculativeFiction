# Mortal Recertification Plan: Dewi Kam

**Case File:** HPB-MRP-DKM-12026.09

## The Three Burns

### Burn 1: Just Transition Investment Commitment

**Action:** Commit a binding percentage of coal power revenue to funding renewable energy transition infrastructure in the same Indonesian regions currently served by coal generation.

**Sacrifice:** Reduced reinvestment in continued coal infrastructure expansion.

**Mortal Status Gain:** Indonesian communities dependent on coal-generated electricity gain a funded pathway toward cleaner alternatives rather than indefinite fossil fuel lock-in.

### Burn 2: Emissions and Health Impact Disclosure

**Action:** Publish independently audited emissions data and associated public health impact assessments for all coal power holdings, made available to affected communities and regulators.

**Sacrifice:** Loss of the opacity that has historically shielded emissions and health impact data from public accounting.

**Mortal Status Gain:** Communities near coal power facilities gain visibility into the actual health and environmental cost of the generation supplying their electricity.

### Burn 3: Climate Adaptation Fund

**Action:** Establish and fund a climate adaptation program supporting Indonesian communities most vulnerable to climate impacts, financed from coal power holdings.

**Sacrifice:** Substantial ongoing capital commitment with no direct commercial return.

**Mortal Status Gain:** Communities bearing the climate cost of continued coal dependency gain a funded adaptation resource.

*"My fortune helped lock in decades of the power that keeps the lights on and the carbon rising. It is time some of that fortune helped light the way to something else."*
