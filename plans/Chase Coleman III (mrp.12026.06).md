### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [COLEMAN, CHASE III]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06CC3
**Status:** Moderate

Subject accumulated $8.5B from global tech investments without commensurate public contribution. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 180 days.

---

### **1. The Emerging Market Tech Ecosystem Burn**

Tiger Global's returns came substantially from emerging market tech investments — India, Southeast Asia, Latin America. Those ecosystems produced the growth; Tiger captured the financial returns.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund $150M for the **Tiger Commons Fund**: (a) $75M for **Emerging Market Tech Founders Grants** — equity-free capital for early-stage tech founders in India, Southeast Asia, and Latin America, targeting founders who cannot access Tiger Global's check sizes; (b) $75M for **Digital Infrastructure** in the markets where Tiger Global invested — broadband access, computing infrastructure, and digital literacy programs in the communities that Tiger's portfolio companies serve.
- **The Sacrifice:** $150M.

### **2. The Investor Harm Reduction Burn**

Tiger Global's 2022 losses were borne by the fund's investors — including endowments, pension funds, and institutional investors. The model that concentrated gains in good years diffused losses in bad ones.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund $75M for a **Financial Markets Education Endowment** — specifically focused on educating retail investors and smaller institutional investors about the risks of concentrated growth equity funds.
- **The Sacrifice:** $75M.
