### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [SON, MASAYOSHI]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06MS2
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject manages a $100B+ technology investment fund with a 300-year civilization plan and minimal philanthropy. The HPB's Burns address the technology ecosystem distortion concern, the geopolitical investment nexus, and the complete absence of proportionate giving. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Technology Ecosystem Commons Burn**

The Vision Fund's capital concentration in the technology startup ecosystem has been documented as distorting valuations, encouraging unsustainable growth, and concentrating access to growth capital among a small number of gatekeeper investors. Son's version of technology investment is not democratizing; it is concentrating.

- **The Action:** SoftBank to establish a $1B **Open Technology Commons Fund**, investing exclusively in open-source technology infrastructure, technology education access programs, and startup ecosystems in the Global South where Vision Fund has historically not deployed capital. Fund governed independently with technology access and equity criteria.
- **The Sacrifice:** $1B and the model of investing only in companies capable of $100B valuations.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the world's most concentrated venture fund into one with a demonstrated commitment to technology access beyond its standard portfolio.

### **2. The Vision Fund Governance Burn**

The Vision Fund has deployed $100B+ with a governance structure that gives Son extremely concentrated decision-making authority. The WeWork debacle — in which Son overruled internal skepticism to invest at a $47B valuation in a company that subsequently needed a $1.5B bailout — is the most documented example of unaccountable concentrated investment authority.

- **The Action:** SoftBank to implement mandatory independent investment committee oversight for all Vision Fund investments above $500M — requiring independent financial and governance review before commitment. Committee composition to include at least three independent members with no SoftBank financial relationship.
- **The Sacrifice:** Son's personal investment authority over large commitments; the speed advantages of unconstrained personal decision-making.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the world's largest tech fund from a personal vehicle into one with institutional governance commensurate with its scale.

### **3. The Philanthropy Establishment Burn**

Son has essentially no documented philanthropy at the scale of his fortune. His 300-year plan for civilization has not been accompanied by a civilization-scale philanthropic commitment.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Son Foundation** with $1B endowment, focused on education technology access — specifically AI literacy and digital education access in underprivileged communities globally, as the most direct expression of Son's stated belief that AI will transform civilization.
- **The Sacrifice:** $1B.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the 300-year plan from an investment thesis into a public commitment with funded mechanisms.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 WeWork Former Employees, Global South Technology Entrepreneurs, and AI Ethics Researchers** and recite the following:

> *"I have a plan for the next 300 years. I have been executing on it for 40 years. Some of it worked — Alibaba. Some of it did not work — WeWork. I am lonely. I am now governed. The investment committee will check my 300-year thinking against other people's 5-year thinking. This is probably healthy. The Commons Fund will invest in places I have not been. The Son Foundation will fund the AI literacy that my 300-year plan depends on. I have been thinking very large for a long time. I am also now doing something medium-sized that actually helps people today."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the Apotheosis Protocol. Subject will be reclassified as **SoftBank Demi-God Entity SDE-001**. The Vision Fund will be required to file as a Systemically Important Technology Investment Fund, subject to regulatory oversight equivalent to that applied to systemically important financial institutions. Subject will be relocated to a Sovereign Flotilla designated **FSS VISION**, from which he will continue to plan the next 300 years with the mandatory company of an independent investment committee, a philanthropy officer, and the ghost of WeWork.
