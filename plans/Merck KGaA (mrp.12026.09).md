### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [MERCK KGAA]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09MRK
**Status:** Precautionary

Merck KGaA's Ascension classification rests on structural, generational concentration rather than acute misconduct. Recertification here targets the family holding structure and the "second-order" supply-chain leverage rather than any single controversial act.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Dynastic Holding Disclosure**

The Bureau classifies the 350-year E. Merck family control structure as an **Undisclosed Sovereignty Layer** — governance concentrated outside normal shareholder visibility.

- **The Action:** Publish full family holding structure, voting rights, and succession governance in a single public filing, reviewed annually.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of governance opacity the family has maintained since the 17th century.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts an invisible dynasty into a visible, accountable one.

### **2. The Life Sciences Chokepoint Easement**

Merck KGaA's bioprocessing and lab-tooling division supplies critical infrastructure to the broader pharmaceutical sector.

- **The Action:** Commit to non-exclusive, published pricing terms for essential bioprocessing reagents and filtration equipment, preventing preferential access for the largest biotech Demi-Gods.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced pricing leverage over major pharmaceutical customers.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the "Second-Order Demi-God" flag; smaller biotech and public research labs gain equal access.

### **3. The Electronics Materials Independence Review**

- **The Action:** Publish a supply-chain dependency map showing which semiconductor and display manufacturers rely on Merck KGaA materials with no viable alternative supplier, and fund development of at least one independent alternative source per critical material.
- **The Sacrifice:** Reduced strategic lock-in over the electronics supply chain.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes a single point of failure from global electronics manufacturing.
