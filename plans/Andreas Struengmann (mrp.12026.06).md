---

### **JOINT BURN 1: The BioNTech Global Vaccine Equity Fund**

BioNTech's COVID-19 vaccine saved millions of lives. The distribution was inequitable — wealthy countries received doses first; lower-income countries waited. The Struengmanns, as founding investors and major shareholders, bear partial accountability for the access decisions made.

- **The Action:** Andreas and Thomas Struengmann must jointly fund (equal contribution, €250M each, total €500M) the **Struengmann Global Vaccine Equity Fund** — independently governed by a board of global health experts, Gavi, and CEPI representatives — dedicated to: (a) mRNA vaccine technology transfer to low- and middle-income country manufacturers (funding manufacturing capacity, not just dose donation); (b) sustainable vaccine production infrastructure in Africa (specifically, partnership with the Africa CDC and WHO Hub for pandemic preparedness); (c) pandemic preparedness stockpiling for LMIC countries. This fund must be operational and making commitments within 18 months.
- **Andreas's Sacrifice:** €250M.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The founding investors of the world's most successful COVID vaccine fund its equitable distribution legacy.

### **JOINT BURN 2: The Hexal Generic Access Legacy Fund**

Hexal was one of Europe's largest generic pharmaceutical companies. Generic access reduces healthcare costs. The HPB asks the Struengmanns to fund global generic access beyond Europe.

- **The Action:** Andreas and Thomas jointly fund (€100M each, total €200M) a **Hexal Global Generic Access Fund** — in partnership with Médecins Sans Frontières's Access Campaign and UNITAID — providing generic drug manufacturing grants to LMIC pharmaceutical manufacturers for essential medicines not currently manufactured locally at accessible prices.
- **Andreas's Sacrifice:** €100M.

### **INDIVIDUAL BURN 3 (Andreas Only): The Santo Holding Biotech Ethics Commitment**

- **The Action:** Andreas must establish a **Santo Holding Bioethics Advisory Board** — externally chaired, with independent bioethicists — that reviews all future Santo investments for equitable access commitments at the term sheet stage. Any Santo portfolio company with a product addressing a disease affecting LMIC populations must include an access plan as a condition of Santo's investment.
- **The Sacrifice:** Some investment flexibility; potential loss of deal flow from companies unwilling to commit to access.
