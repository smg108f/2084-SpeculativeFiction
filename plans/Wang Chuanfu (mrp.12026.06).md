### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [WANG, CHUANFU]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06WC
**Status:** Precautionary — Reduced Requirements (Green Transition Credit Applied)

Subject founded BYD Company and has built the world's leading EV manufacturer. The HPB applies a Green Transition Credit reducing standard requirements. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Battery Supply Chain Accountability Burn**

BYD's Blade Battery is a genuine innovation. BYD's supply chain for lithium and cobalt involves the same extraction concerns that affect all battery manufacturers. The HPB asks for accountability.

- **The Action:** Publish a binding **BYD Battery Supply Chain Ethics Charter**: (a) full traceability for all cobalt sourcing, with specific commitments to eliminating artisanal and small-scale mining sources linked to child labor; (b) lithium sourcing environmental impact assessments for all lithium supply contracts, with specific attention to Atacama and Australian extraction impacts; (c) battery recycling infrastructure investment — minimum $200M toward end-of-life battery recycling in China, making BYD the first major EV company to build its own recycling at scale.
- **The Sacrifice:** $200M; supply chain transparency that competitors may use.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the world's largest EV battery manufacturer into the first to publish fully traceable supply chain ethics at scale.

### **2. The Global South EV Access Burn**

BYD has expanded globally, primarily into markets with purchasing power. The HPB asks about EV access for lower-income markets.

- **The Action:** Establish a **BYD Global Access Fund** — $150M — dedicated to subsidized EV entry in lower-middle-income countries, specifically targeting: urban taxi and ride-share fleets in African and South Asian cities, where the air quality and oil import benefits of EV adoption are highest but consumer purchasing power is insufficient. Coordinate with multilateral development banks.
- **The Sacrifice:** $150M; operational complexity.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a company that sells EVs to people who can afford them into a company that actively works on EV access for people who cannot.

### **3. The Wang Foundation Establishment Burn**

Personal philanthropy is absent. The HPB requires the minimum.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Wang Chuanfu Foundation** with a minimum ¥500M (~$70M) endowment, focused on STEM education — specifically chemistry and engineering education for rural Chinese students who would not otherwise have access to the technical education pipeline that produced Wang himself.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥500M; public philanthropic commitment.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the founder of a company built on chemistry expertise into a documented funder of the next generation of Chinese chemists and engineers from underserved backgrounds.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

> *"I started with batteries in 1995 because I believed batteries were the constraint. I was right about that. BYD makes more EVs than almost any company in the world. The supply chain for those batteries has real costs in the places where lithium and cobalt come from. The Supply Chain Ethics Charter is published. The recycling infrastructure is funded. The Wang Foundation is funding chemistry education for kids who grew up the way I did — without advantages. I built something that helps. I am trying to make sure what I built helps fully, not just for the people who can afford it."*

The HPB notes this is the least likely compliance failure in the final batch — Wang Chuanfu built a company around solving hard technical problems, and these Burns are, at root, technical and organizational problems. If assigned to a Flotilla: **FSS BYD**, which is an all-electric vessel powered entirely by Blade Battery technology, operates with zero emissions, and is genuinely the best ship in international waters by environmental metrics.
