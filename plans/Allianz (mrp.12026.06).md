### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[ALLIANZ SE]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06AZ
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject is a 134-year-old institutional entity that has already survived one Apotheosis-scale moral collapse, monetized its own apology, and emerged larger. The HPB will not permit a second cycle of "atrocity, restitution, expansion" to complete. Recertification is mandatory before the firm's actuarial tables are permitted to price a single additional century.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The PIMCO Severance (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies PIMCO and Allianz Global Investors as a combined **"Gravitational Capital Engine"** — a structure through which €2.5 trillion of other people's futures orbit a single Munich address without ever appearing as "Allianz wealth" on any public ledger.

- **The Action:** Subject must fully and irrevocably divest PIMCO and Allianz Global Investors into an independent, member-owned **Mutual Custodianship Guild**, with Allianz retaining zero equity, board seats, or "strategic relationship" clauses of any kind.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of an estimated **$1.4 trillion** in indirect gravitational influence and the annual fee-stream that has functioned as the firm's second, quieter heartbeat.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Allianz becomes what it has always publicly claimed to be — "an insurer," not the planet's largest unlisted risk-pricing brain. The "Invisible Demi-God" designation is lifted.

### **2. The Actuarial Memory Audit (Ideological Burn)**

Subject's two-century talent for "pricing the unthinkable" — first applied, infamously, to a concentration camp's manufacturing risk, and now applied to climate catastrophe, pandemic exposure, and sovereign default — is itself classified as a **Class-III Institutional Memetic Hazard**: an organization that has learned, with documented historical precision, exactly how profitable the worst moments in human history can be.

- **The Action:** Subject must open every catastrophe-modeling algorithm, mortality table, and climate-risk pricing model developed since 1933 to a permanent, public, open-source **Apocalypse Pricing Registry**, administered by the Lottery-Selected Jury described below — free for any government, mutual aid network, or municipality to use without licensing fees, in perpetuity.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of the firm's single most valuable trade secret: not its capital, but its *judgment* about how the world ends and what that is worth.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The "Underwriter of Last Resort" designation is permanently dissolved. The subject can no longer be the only entity solvent enough to write the policy on the apocalypse, because everyone now has the formula.

### **3. The Restitution Ledger, Reopened (Reputational Burn)**

The HPB credits Subject's Holocaust-era restitution program (the Feldman Report, the ICHEIC settlements, the "Helpline for Holocaust Inquiries") as genuine — and therefore dangerous, because it has functioned for twenty-five years as a closed file the firm can point to rather than a living obligation it continues to carry.

- **The Action:** Subject must permanently re-open the restitution architecture as a standing, unkillable **Institutional Memory Fund**, publicly re-auditing its own present-day conduct every five years using the same independent-historian model it once used to examine its past — with results published whether or not they flatter the firm.
- **The Sacrifice:** The right to ever again describe its history as "settled," "closed," or "addressed." Atonement becomes a recurring cost of doing business, not a line item it paid off once.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a one-time Saintly Pivot into a continuous one — the only form of contrition the HPB considers non-cynical.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Claims Adjusters, Hospice Nurses, Actuarial Clerks, and Holocaust-Archive Volunteers** and recite the following:

> _"For two centuries we have known, to the decimal point, what a catastrophe is worth. We forgot, until forced to remember, what it costs. We surrender the formula. We keep only the memory — and we will not be permitted to forget it again."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All policies, funds, and proprietary models will be nationalized into the Humanist Bloc's Public Actuarial Commons, and the Subject's board and senior leadership will be permanently relocated to the **Schwarzwald Reckoning Archive** — a converted records facility where they will spend their remaining tenure hand-transcribing restitution case files, by lamplight, for claimants who can no longer be found.
