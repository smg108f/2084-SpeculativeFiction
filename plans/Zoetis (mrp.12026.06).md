### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ZOETIS INC]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-33ZTS
**Status:** Precautionary

Zoetis' MARGINAL ASCENSION RISK classification reflects its role in the industrial livestock pharmaceutical dependency system and its dual-register operation (genuine food security contribution + luxury companion animal pricing). Recertification is precautionary but addresses the antibiotic resistance and food supply chain concerns the HPB's Environmental and Public Health Division has flagged.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Prophylactic Antibiotic Phase-Out (The Public Health Burn)**

The HPB classifies Zoetis' profitable sales of prophylactic antibiotics to industrial livestock operations — which contribute to antibiotic resistance in human pathogens — as a **Profit-Generating Public Health Harm**: the company earns revenue from practices that degrade the effectiveness of medicines that humans depend on.

- **The Action:** Zoetis must eliminate all sales of medically important antibiotics for prophylactic (preventive) use in livestock within 5 years, providing farmer support programs and alternative health protocols during the transition. Sales for therapeutic use (treating sick animals) are retained.
- **The Sacrifice:** Estimated $800M-$1.2B annual revenue reduction.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Profit-Generating Public Health Harm flag is resolved; Zoetis exits the Antibiotic Resistance Contribution watchlist.

### **2. The Companion Animal Pricing Equity Mandate (The Access Burn)**

The HPB classifies Zoetis' companion animal drug pricing — particularly for chronic condition medications like Apoquel and Cytopoint — as a **Emotional Attachment Premium Extraction**: pricing drugs at rates that exploit pet owners' emotional relationships with their animals rather than reflecting therapeutic cost or competitive market rates.

- **The Action:** Zoetis must accept binding pricing transparency for companion animal drugs with no generic alternatives: maximum price is set at 300% of manufacturing cost plus reasonable R&D amortization, verified by independent audit. Generics may be licensed at cost-plus-15% to any manufacturer requesting access.
- **The Sacrifice:** Estimated 25-40% reduction in companion animal drug margins.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Emotional Attachment Premium Extraction flag is resolved.

### **3. The Developing World Food Security Licensing (The Global Equity Burn)**

The HPB classifies Zoetis' patent enforcement against generic manufacturers of livestock vaccines and medicines in developing countries — where food security depends on affordable animal health — as a **Food Security Patent Weaponization**.

- **The Action:** Zoetis must implement a binding compulsory license framework: any vaccine or medicine essential to food security in countries classified by the World Bank as lower-middle income or below may be manufactured by licensed generic producers at a 5% royalty.
- **The Sacrifice:** Revenue loss from generic competition in developing markets, estimated at $200-300M annually.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Food Security Patent Weaponization flag is resolved.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, CEO Kristin Peck must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 veterinarians from developing countries and American pet owners with chronically ill companion animals** and recite the following:

> _"We sell antibiotics to industrial farms that don't need them because preventing disease in crowded conditions is cheaper than not crowding the animals. This makes our drugs less effective for humans who do need them. We charge what the emotional bond will bear for dog allergy medication. We protect patents in countries where food security depends on affordable animal medicine. We accept the phase-out, the price cap, and the compulsory license. We remain the world's leading animal health company. We become one that earns that leadership."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Zoetis' prophylactic antibiotic product line will be transferred to a **Global Animal Health Commons Authority** for reformulation and distribution as public health infrastructure. Companion animal drug patents will be placed in a **Public Pharmaceutical Trust** with compulsory licensing for all manufacturers. The Bureau notes that a $77B company that makes dog allergy medicine will not, on balance, miss the portion of its revenue derived from practices that cause antibiotic resistance.
