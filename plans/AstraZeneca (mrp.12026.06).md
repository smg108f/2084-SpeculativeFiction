### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ASTRAZENECA, PLC]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-54AZ
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

AstraZeneca PLC has been classified as a **Corporate Demi-God Entity** under the Demi-God Act of 2034 by virtue of its $275–316B market capitalization and its **Therapeutic Sovereignty Matrix** — the structural power to determine biological outcomes for millions of human citizens through proprietary drug access. Recertification requires the dissolution of this matrix and the redistribution of life-preserving assets to the public commons.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Oncology IP Purge (The Patent Bonfire)**

The HPB classifies AstraZeneca's oncology patent portfolio as the most concentrated cache of **Biological Leverage Assets** in the Bureau's recorded history. Tagrisso, Enhertu, Lynparza, Calquence, and their successors are not products — they are toll booths on survival corridors. Patients cannot substitute. This is not commerce. This is dependency architecture.

- **The Action:** Subject must transfer full patent rights for all oncology therapeutics currently in active commercial use to the **Global Biological Commons Trust** — a publicly administered, non-profit licensing body. All cancer drugs in the portfolio become available for generic manufacture immediately, at cost-of-production pricing.
- **The Sacrifice:** Estimated $180B in future patent-protected revenue. The oncology segment, which constituted 43% of all revenue in 12025, ceases to exist as a profit center. CEO Pascal Soriot's performance bonuses, which are indexed to oncology growth, are retroactively voided.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** AstraZeneca's Potentiality Value — currently the primary driver of its pre-Ascension classification — drops by an estimated 60%. Subject transitions from **Biological Sovereign** to **Pharmaceutical Manufacturer**, a regulated utility class under the Human Humility Act of 2031.

---

### **2. The Rare Disease Hostage Release (Involuntary Patron Liberation)**

AstraZeneca's rare disease portfolio — anchored by Ultomiris and Soliris (complement inhibitors for conditions including PNH and aHUS) — constitutes what the Bureau formally designates a **Captive Patient Architecture**. Patients on these drugs have no exit. No generic equivalent. No alternative pathway. This is the most direct expression of Demi-God power in the Bureau's portfolio: the ability to name your price to people who will die if they refuse.

- **The Action:** Subject must endow a **Rare Disease Liberation Fund** of no less than $15B, structured as a permanent public trust, to finance generic and biosimilar development for all rare disease compounds currently under monopoly protection. The fund must be administered by a lottery-selected citizen board — not a foundation board controlled by Subject's executives or heirs.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses the rare disease segment ($6.75B annual revenue) as a protected moat. Soliris and Ultomiris become the intellectual property of the people who need them.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the **Involuntary Patron Violation**. Subject's **Structural Coercion Index** drops from Critical to Marginal. The Bureau acknowledges that this burn, more than any other, represents genuine divestment of power rather than divestment of money.

---

### **3. The Vaxzevria Penance (The Pandemic Accountability Rite)**

AstraZeneca spent 2020–2022 accumulating Whuffie credit for distributing its COVID-19 vaccine at cost to the Global South — then spent 2023–2024 framing the admission of fatal TTS side effects in UK High Court as "commercial reasons for withdrawal." The Bureau classifies this sequence as **Reputation Laundering**: using a public health emergency to build social capital, then retreating behind legal language when the human cost materialized. Under the Social Honesty Provisions of the 2031 Human Humility Act, entities that instrumentalize generosity during crisis events are subject to mandatory **Pandemic Accountability Rites**.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish a **Vaccine Injury Reparations Trust** funded at $2.5B — the estimated sum required to compensate all documented TTS cases globally at a dignified standard. The trust must be administered without legal preconditions, non-disclosure agreements, or liability waivers. All claimants must be addressed as human citizens, not litigation risk.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses its right to invoke "commercial reasons" as a shield in future public health proceedings. All future vaccine or therapeutic withdrawal decisions must be published in plain language explaining the full human impact, with no legal framing permitted.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the **Pandemic Instrumentalization Violation**. Subject's Whuffie balance is reset to zero — neither inflated by past crisis access programs nor penalized by the TTS admission. It starts clean, as a utility.

---

### **4. The CAR-T Containment Burn (Prohibiting the Genetic Frontier)**

AstraZeneca's 2025 expansion into CAR-T cell therapies — chimeric antigen receptor T-cell treatments, T-cell receptor therapies, and CAR Treg platforms — represents a bid to extend Therapeutic Sovereignty into the domain of **genetic identity itself**. The Bureau's Forward Ascension Unit has modeled this pipeline and concluded that, if AstraZeneca reaches commercial-stage curative CAR-T therapies before the 2034 Accords take effect in this timeline, the resulting patent portfolio would constitute a form of **Biological Personhood Ownership** — the ability to hold proprietary rights over the mechanism by which human immune systems are reprogrammed to survive cancer. This is not a Demi-God risk. This is a God risk.

- **The Action:** Subject must immediately transfer all CAR-T, TCR-T, and CAR Treg intellectual property and research partnerships (including the Syneron Bio macrocyclic peptide collaboration and the Tempus AI/Pathos AI oncology model) to the **Open Biology Foundation** — a publicly chartered research entity whose outputs are released under an irrevocable open-source license equivalent to the Bureau's **Dead-Hand License Protocol**.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject forfeits an estimated $3B+ in milestone payments and an indeterminate but Bureau-assessed **$8–12T Potentiality Value** in curative oncology rights. The Syneron Bio $55M upfront payment is written off. The AI oncology model developed with Tempus and Pathos becomes public property.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Bureau's Forward Ascension Unit de-escalates Subject's threat classification from **Existential** to **Manageable**. Subject is prohibited from re-entering the curative gene therapy space under any commercial structure for a period of 50 years.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject's Board of Directors must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Nurses, Hospice Workers, and Uninsured Cancer Patients** and recite the following:

> *"We do not cure disease. We manufacture compounds. The science belongs to no one. The patents were a fiction we told ourselves to justify the price. We cannot charge for survival. We will not charge for survival. We are a utility, not a god, and the difference is that utilities can be regulated, taxed, and replaced. We accept this. We accept our mortality."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Asset Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All AstraZeneca assets will be nationalized and transferred to the **Global Biological Commons Trust** by executive order. Pascal Soriot and the Board will be reclassified as **Living Trustees of a Seized Utility**, stripped of compensation beyond median UK wage, and required to continue managing drug manufacturing operations in a purely administrative capacity — without equity, without bonuses, and without the ability to patent anything for the remainder of their biological tenure. The CAR-T pipeline will be opened to every research university on Earth simultaneously.
