### **1. The Public Infrastructure Restitution Burn**

- **The Action:** Subject must fund ₱5B (~$90M) for the **Philippine Public Infrastructure Restitution Fund** — directed to: (a) infrastructure projects in Metro Manila and surrounding provinces that serve lower-income communities rather than real estate development zones — specifically, roads, drainage, and public transit in communities that do not have the political leverage Vista Land enjoyed; (b) a **Senate Ethics and Conflict of Interest Institute** — a permanent institutional body with legal mandate to review and disclose conflicts of interest between legislators and private business interests, funded independently; (c) an annual **Philippine Infrastructure Transparency Report** — documenting public infrastructure investments alongside the private land ownership patterns they serve.
- **The Sacrifice:** ₱5B (~$90M).

### **2. The Affordable Housing Genuine Commitment Burn**

Vista Land builds across income segments but the lowest-cost Camella units remain beyond reach for the Philippines' poorest households.

- **The Action:** Subject must commit Vista Land to a **Social Housing Bond** — building 5,000 units per year of genuinely subsidized housing (at 30% below market cost) for households earning below the median Manila income, funded ₱2B (~$36M) personally to cover the subsidy gap.
- **The Sacrifice:** ₱2B (~$36M).
