### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [THERMO FISHER SCIENTIFIC]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-51TFS
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice / Infrastructure Sovereignty Review

Thermo Fisher Scientific has been classified as a **Distributed Demi-God Entity (DDGE)** under the Infrastructure Sovereignty Clause of the Demi-God Act of 2034. Because it controls an estimated 40%+ of the global biological research supply chain — including reagents, laboratory instruments, genomic identification kits, and clinical trial data infrastructure — the entity represents an **Enclosure of the Biological Commons**, which the HPB treats as equivalent to individual Ascension but structurally more dangerous, as there is no single conscience to summon, reprimand, or deport.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Reagent Commons Transfer (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies Thermo Fisher's reagent and consumables business as a **Toll Booth on the Scientific Method**. Any government, university, clinic, or independent researcher conducting genomic, proteomic, or cell-biology work currently passes through Thermo Fisher's pricing structure. This is not a market position. This is a *tithe*.

- **The Action:** Transfer the reagents and consumables division — including all proprietary formulation IP — to a **Public Scientific Commons Trust**, governed by a consortium of public universities and the WHO. All catalog items to be manufactured under open-license at cost-plus pricing.
- **The Sacrifice:** Estimated $18B in annual revenue from consumables; near-total loss of the "razor-and-blades" business model that generates the company's most reliable margin.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The company's **Biological Commons Enclosure** classification is cleared. Researchers who currently ration reagent orders based on Thermo Fisher's pricing can now conduct science. This will, auditors note, feel catastrophic to shareholders and correct to everyone else.

---

### **2. The Genomic Complicity Reckoning (The Ideological Burn)**

Between 2017 and 2024, Thermo Fisher supplied DNA identification equipment to Chinese state police forces for use in ethnic surveillance programs targeting Uyghur, Tibetan, and Hui populations. Announcements of product withdrawal were followed by documented third-party broker continuation. The company gave a standing ovation to President Xi Jinping in 2022 and, per internal documentation, the CEO shared this moment with board members as a point of institutional pride.

The HPB classifies this history as a **Genomic Complicity Flag** — use of biological infrastructure to enable state surveillance and ethnic categorization at scale. This cannot be offset by STEM scholarship programs.

- **The Action:** Thermo Fisher must (1) fully open-source all PCR and DNA identification kits previously sold to Chinese law enforcement, including the Huaxia genotype amplification kits; (2) fund a $2B **Genomic Reparations and Monitoring Endowment** administered by an independent international human rights tribunal; and (3) submit to a 10-year external audit of all state and law enforcement sales globally.
- **The Sacrifice:** Significant diplomatic and commercial relationship damage in the PRC market, which represented a major revenue stream. Loss of competitive advantage in forensic genomics globally.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The **Genomic Complicity Flag** is removed from the HPB registry. The entity demonstrates that its biological infrastructure cannot be weaponized against populations without consequence — which is precisely the guarantee that biological infrastructure requires.

---

### **3. The Acquisition Moratorium and Structural Dissolution (The Structural Burn)**

Thermo Fisher has completed more than 54 acquisitions. The Clario acquisition — $8.875B, completed March 2026 — absorbed clinical trial data infrastructure, extending the company's reach into the regulatory pipelines of pharmaceutical development itself. The HPB notes that at this rate, by approximately 2031, Thermo Fisher will effectively *be* the administrative skeleton of global biological science.

- **The Action:** Mandatory divestiture of all acquisitions completed after 2020, restoring acquired entities to independent operation or public-trust status. A permanent acquisition ban is imposed until the company's total market capitalization falls below $50B. The Clario clinical trial data platform is transferred to a public regulatory data commons under joint FDA/EMA/WHO oversight.
- **The Sacrifice:** Approximately $40–60B in market capitalization. The reversal of the primary growth strategy of the previous decade. Marc Casper, or whoever holds his position by the time this order is executed, will experience this as a personal loss of legacy.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The **Distributed Demi-God Entity** classification is downgraded to **Marginal Ascension Risk — Under Monitoring**. The company continues to exist as a scientific instrument manufacturer of ordinary scale, which is what it was in 1956 when it was founded as Barnstead International, before anyone thought to build a toll booth on the scientific method.

---

### **4. The "Mission-Driven Science" Oath (The Memetic Burn)**

Thermo Fisher's stated corporate mission is "Enabling our customers to make the world healthier, cleaner and safer." The HPB classifies this as **Aspirational Enclosure Language** — a noble framing applied to a business model that makes health, cleanliness, and safety expensive by controlling access to their instruments.

- **The Action:** The company must retire the mission statement and replace it with a publicly binding **Institutional Charter of Scientific Access** — a legal document, not a marketing document, committing the entity to: (a) supply all essential research tools at or below cost to public health institutions; (b) publish all proprietary protocols for tools used in public health emergencies; and (c) submit all new product pricing to an independent scientific access review board before market release.
- **The Sacrifice:** The comfortable ambiguity of a mission statement that gestures at the public good while enforcing private pricing. The ability to describe itself, without irony, as a force for human flourishing.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The company's **Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric** classification is cleared. It may once again describe itself as a scientific company without the HPB annotation "(unverified)."

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, the sitting CEO of Thermo Fisher Scientific must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Public University Lab Technicians, Rural Clinic Researchers, and Graduate Students Who Have Had a Grant Denied Due to Reagent Costs** and recite the following:

> *"The tools of science are not our property. We did not invent curiosity. We did not invent the cell, the gene, or the assay. We built instruments, and we charged rent on the instruments, and we called this mission. We renounce the toll booth. We are not the quartermaster of human biology. We are one manufacturer among many, and our worth is measured not by what we control but by what we release."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All Thermo Fisher assets will be nationalized under the Infrastructure Sovereignty Clause and administered by the newly constituted **Bureau of Biological Commons**. Marc Casper, or his successor, will be reassigned to a **Life Tenancy Caretaker position** at the company's Waltham, Massachusetts headquarters — now a public science museum — where he will be permitted to conduct tours of the instrument display floor and answer questions from school groups about what happens when a scientific tools company mistakes its catalog for a kingdom.
