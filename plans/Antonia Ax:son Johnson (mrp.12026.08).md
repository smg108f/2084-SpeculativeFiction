### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[AX:SON JOHNSON, ANTONIA]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-08AAJ
**Status:** Precautionary

Subject's Probationary Saint status is conditional, not clear. The Bureau does not seize a foundation with an 87-year track record, but it will not let a $10B private conglomerate glide from mother to daughter to granddaughter under the banner of "stewardship" without closing the trust structure that makes that glide possible.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Axel Johnson AB Trust Conversion (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies wholly family-owned holding companies as **"Dynastic Vaults"** — private equity structures with no market discipline and no expiration date. Subject's continued board presence and majority ownership, even post-chairmanship, keeps the vault sealed.

- **The Action:** Subject must convert her remaining equity in Axel Johnson AB into an irrevocable **Public Benefit Trust**, with governance opened to a board that is no longer majority Ax:son Johnson by blood.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of controlling ownership across Axel Johnson International, KICKS, Martin & Servera, and AxSol. The family retains employment and honorary titles, not command.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Direct assets drop by an estimated **$9.2B** in personally attributed equity. Subject becomes a **Trustee**, not an **Owner**.

### **2. Dissolution of the Board Life-Tenancy**

Subject stepped down as chairman in 2015 but retained a board seat and, per HPB analysts, effective veto influence over her daughter's decisions as CEO. This is the **soft apotheosis** flagged in the Audit: power that never fully leaves the room.

- **The Action:** Subject must resign all board seats and any "Chair Emeritus" or advisory designation at Axel Johnson AB and Nordstjernan AB, in writing, with no informal reinstatement clause.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of the last formal lever over the company she ran for 33 years. Caroline Berg and the current board govern without a maternal veto.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the **"Dynastic Loophole"** flag. Subject is reclassified from **Shadow Chair** to **Retired Citizen**.

### **3. The Five Generations Penance**

The Axel Johnson Group's own public materials celebrate "Five Generations" of bloodline leadership as a virtue. The HPB classifies this as **low-grade Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric**: the implication that competence to steward a fortune is hereditary.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund a public rebuttal series through the Ax:son Johnson Foundation — an open research initiative on **meritocratic succession in family enterprise**, explicitly funded to critique, not celebrate, dynastic control.
- **The Requirement:** The phrase "Five Generations" must be retired from all Axel Johnson AB public communications and replaced with governance criteria unrelated to surname.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The genuine scholarly credibility of the Foundation is redirected against the very mythology that built it, which the HPB considers appropriately ironic penance.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Warehouse Pickers, Store Clerks, and Foundry Workers** and recite the following:

> _"I am not the fifth generation. I am the last landlord of my own name. What I built was never mine alone to pass down, and what I pass down now is a company, not a crown."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the "Burn" is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Axel Johnson AB will be converted by force into a state-monitored public-benefit corporation, subject's board privileges will be permanently revoked, and she will be reclassified as a **Passive Demi-God Trust**, subject to 100% growth taxation for the remainder of her biological term — a quiet ending for a quiet dynasty.
