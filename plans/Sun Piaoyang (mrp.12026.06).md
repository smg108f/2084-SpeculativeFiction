### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [SUN, PIAOYANG]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06SP2
**Status:** Standard

Subject built one of China's most credible pharmaceutical R&D platforms. Burns address the access gap between innovative drugs and patients.

---

### **1. The Cancer Drug Access Burn**

Hengrui's oncology drugs help cancer patients — but primarily those who can afford them or who are covered by insurance/NRDL. China's rural and low-income cancer patients often cannot access these treatments.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish the **Hengrui Patient Access Fund** — $150M — funded personally (not from Hengrui's commercial budget): (a) subsidized access to Hengrui's oncology portfolio for uninsured or underinsured cancer patients in rural China and lower-income urban populations; (b) early access programs for Hengrui's pipeline compounds for patients with no other treatment options; (c) collaboration with Doctors Without Borders and Chinese rural health programs to extend Hengrui treatment access to remote provinces.
- **The Sacrifice:** $150M.

### **2. The Open Research Burn**

Hengrui invests in R&D. The HPB asks for open-access publication of research that has reached completion without commercial exploitation pathway.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund $50M for the **Hengrui Open Oncology Research Fund** — publishing Hengrui's failed and abandoned oncology research compounds (compounds that reached Phase II or earlier and were discontinued) in open-access format, enabling other researchers globally to build on this knowledge without duplicating Hengrui's investment. Also fund three Chinese oncology research academic chairs (CNY 10M each) at Chinese medical universities.
- **The Sacrifice:** $50M.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Rural Chinese Cancer Patients, Hengrui R&D Scientists, and Chinese Medical School Oncology Researchers**:

> *"I built Hengrui to develop drugs that work. That required R&D investment that most Chinese pharmaceutical companies did not make. The drugs we developed help cancer patients. The Patient Access Fund means they help more cancer patients — not just the ones who can afford them or who live near a major hospital. The open research fund means the compounds we tried and did not commercialize can still contribute to cancer science. Innovation is not the end; access is the end."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** Subject will be assigned to **FSS HENGRUI** — a floating oncology clinic providing free cancer screening and Hengrui drug access to patients in Chinese coastal provinces without adequate cancer care facilities, crewed by Hengrui clinical researchers who conduct real trials on the vessel while providing care, generating the open-access research data the subject was asked to publish and declined to, demonstrating that the two objectives — drug development and patient access — are compatible when the founder funds both.
