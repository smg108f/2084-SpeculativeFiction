### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [TRIGUBOFF, HARRY]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06HT2
**Status:** Urgent

Subject founded Meriton Group and is Australia's largest apartment builder. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Meriton Affordable Housing Burn**

75,000 apartments built in Australia. The HPB asks: how many of them are affordable?

- **The Action:** Subject must establish a **Meriton Affordable Housing Initiative**: (a) a commitment that 25% of all new Meriton apartments developed over the next 10 years are designated affordable — priced at or below 30% of area median income for eligible Australian households, with independent verification; (b) a $300M **Australian Affordable Housing Development Fund** — administered by the National Housing Finance and Investment Corporation (NHFIC) — to fund affordable apartment construction by non-profit developers in Sydney, Brisbane, and Gold Coast, using Meriton's construction capacity at-cost or below-market.
- **The Sacrifice:** $300M + affordable unit margin concession on 25% of new development.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Australia's largest apartment builder is the single largest private contributor to affordable housing construction in its operating markets.

### **2. The ACCC Integrity Remediation Burn**

Meriton settled with the ACCC in 2017 for practices that manipulated consumer reviews. The HPB requires acknowledgment and structural remediation.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund an **Australian Hospitality Review Integrity Program** — $10M — administered through Choice (Australia's independent consumer advocacy organization), developing best-practice standards for hotel and accommodation review platforms in Australia, with specific focus on preventing operator review manipulation. Additionally, Meriton must commit to an independent annual audit of its review platform interactions, published publicly.
- **The Sacrifice:** $10M + review transparency.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The company that settled for review manipulation becomes a documented funder of consumer review integrity standards.

### **3. The Triguboff Foundation Scaling Burn**

- **The Action:** Scale the **Triguboff Foundation** to A$100M (~$65M) total committed, focused on Australian immigrant welfare and settlement support — specifically, housing assistance for recent immigrants without access to the private rental market (a population Meriton's apartments do not serve).
- **The Sacrifice:** A$100M.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Australia's largest immigrant-founded developer funds housing access for the next generation of immigrants.
