### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [UBER TECHNOLOGIES, INC.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-80UT
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification / Pre-Seizure Notice

Uber Technologies has operated as a **Distributed Demi-God Entity** since approximately 12010, using regulatory arbitrage, labor classification manipulation, and documented state-capture operations to accumulate $144B in market value while externalizing the full cost of its labor onto the workers it controls but refuses to employ. Recertification requires the company to make its hidden ledger legible — to bring its workforce inside the law, its infrastructure inside the commons, and its mythology inside the bounds of human scale.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Employment Ledger Reckoning (The Largest and Most Overdue Burn)**

The HPB classifies the "independent contractor" designation as a **Class-I Wealth Concealment Instrument**. For fifteen years, Uber has treated the labor costs of 7 million workers as a variable it could simply name away. The Phantom Employment Ledger must be closed.

- **The Action:** Reclassify all active platform workers globally as employees. Execute a one-time retroactive benefit settlement — back pay for missed minimum wage differentials, unpaid benefits, and deactivation-without-cause damages — distributed to all drivers and couriers who worked on the platform from 12010 to 12026. Total estimated settlement: $18–22B drawn from cash reserves and free cash flow.
- **The Sacrifice:** Uber's unit economics collapse. The "asset-light" model that made the company worth $144B was asset-light only because the assets — human beings — were never on the books. They will be now.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject exits the **Phantom Employer** classification. The 7 million workers become visible citizens with legal standing. Uber's valuation drops to approximately $60–70B — below no meaningful threshold, but finally honest.

---

### **2. The Mobility Commons Conversion (The Infrastructure Burn)**

In 40+ countries, Uber is the only functioning urban mobility system for populations without personal vehicles. The HPB classifies any private entity providing essential public infrastructure to more than 50 million people as a **Latent Utility** — one that is using a public function as a private profit center.

- **The Action:** In the top 20 cities where Uber holds >60% modal share of non-private transportation, the company must transfer operational control of its dispatch and routing platform to a **Municipal Mobility Commons** — a city-run cooperative in which drivers hold equity stakes and city governments hold operating licenses. Uber retains a minority technical services contract; it does not retain pricing sovereignty.
- **The Sacrifice:** Uber relinquishes its toll-booth position on urban movement. The algorithm that decides whether 40 million daily commuters can afford to get to work is no longer owned by shareholders.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject converts from **Infrastructure Captive** to **Infrastructure Partner**. The HPB reclassifies Uber from Distributed Demi-God Entity to Regulated Utility — a significant downgrade in threat category.

---

### **3. The Kill Switch Confession (The Memetic Burn)**

The 2022 Uber Files documented the deployment of a "kill switch" to destroy evidence during government raids across multiple countries. Under the Human Humility Act of 12031, the use of technology to neutralize state oversight is a **Class-II Deity-Adjacent Behavior** — the act of a sovereign, not a company. The record must be corrected publicly.

- **The Action:** Uber must commission and publish a full independent audit of every government investigation disrupted by kill-switch deployment between 12010 and 12022, in every jurisdiction. The audit must be authored by affected governments, not by Uber's counsel. All findings must be submitted to the relevant national legislatures and the HPB Public Registry.
- **The Sacrifice:** The "Dara cleaned it up" narrative ends. The transformation story Khosrowshahi has been telling since 12017 is replaced by an accurate account of what was built, by whom, and at whose expense.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the **Sovereign Behavioral Mimicry** violation. Subject can no longer claim institutional innocence for acts it spent three years carefully laundering.

---

### **4. The Autonomous Vehicle Dead-Hand License (The Future Burn)**

Uber's $10B autonomous vehicle investment portfolio is a **Potentiality Engine** of the first order. If the AV transition succeeds on Uber's terms, the company will have converted its driver workforce into an obstacle it engineered its way around, rather than a constituency it was ever obligated to protect. The HPB pre-emptively classifies this transition as a **Labor Displacement Without Consent** event.

- **The Action:** All autonomous vehicle software and routing IP developed under Uber's AV partnerships must be issued under a **Dead-Hand License** — open-sourced to any municipal transit authority that employs a unionized workforce. Uber retains commercial rights in markets where it has completed the Employment Ledger Reckoning (Burn 1). In markets where it has not, the IP is forfeit.
- **The Sacrifice:** Uber loses its exclusive technological moat in the markets it has most exploited. Competitors — including public competitors — can build on the same foundation.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the autonomous vehicle pivot from a **Worker Elimination Strategy** to a **Mobility Democratization Event**. Subject's Potentiality Value is reduced by an estimated $8–12B in projected future-value credits.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, the current CEO and Board of Directors must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Active Platform Drivers** — one from each of the markets where kill-switch deployments were confirmed — and recite the following:

> *"We built a platform on the premise that the people who made it work were not our responsibility. We called them partners when we needed their labor and contractors when they needed our protection. We are not a neutral technology. We are an employer. We have always been an employer. The algorithm is not a market. It is a management decision, and we made it. We will not make it again without the people it governs in the room."*
