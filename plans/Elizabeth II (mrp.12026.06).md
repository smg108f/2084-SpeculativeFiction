### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ELIZABETH II, QUEEN]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06EII
**Status:** POSTHUMOUS — Directed to Crown Estate and Royal Family

---

**NOTICE OF POSTHUMOUS PROCESSING**

Her Majesty Queen Elizabeth II died on September 8, 2022. This recertification plan is posthumous, filed under HPB Administrative Procedure 7.3(b). Compliance obligations pass to:

- **King Charles III** (as successor monarch, HPB-12026-06CIII)
- **The Crown Estate** (as institutional successor)
- **The Royal Collection Trust** (as custodian of royal art and cultural assets)

---

**HPB ASSESSMENT OF EXISTING COMPLIANCE**

The HPB notes that Elizabeth II's 70-year record of constitutional public service is the most unusual positive compliance mechanism in the Bureau's history. The standard Burn categories are inadequate for assessing a constitutional monarch's lifetime service.

**Outstanding concerns transferred to Charles III's recertification:**

1. **Colonial reparations:** The Crown's role in colonialism and its wealth legacy has not been formally addressed. This obligation transfers to Charles III.
2. **Commonwealth transition:** The Commonwealth's future relationship to the Crown, including whether its members' interests are adequately represented in Crown Estate governance, is an outstanding question that transfers.

---

**ESTATE COMPLIANCE NOTE**

The estate's art collection — one of the world's most significant — should remain in public access through the Royal Collection Trust. The HPB requires no additional action on the cultural holdings beyond confirmation of continued public access.

---

**THE FLOTILLA THAT WASN'T**

The HPB notes, for the record, that Britannia — Her Majesty's Royal Yacht, decommissioned in 1997 — would have been the HPB's most historically resonant Flotilla assignment. The decommissioning is noted as a voluntary step toward mortal status. The Bureau does not assign Flotillas to deceased subjects.

---

> **Final Note:** "She reigned for 70 years. The Empire ended on her watch. The Commonwealth continued. She was the most recognizable person on Earth for half a century. The Bureau has no Burn that is proportionate to this. Classification: PROBATIONARY SAINT, posthumously maintained. The obligation for what comes next passes to Charles."
