### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [INTUIT INC.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-70IN
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Intuit Inc. has been assessed as a **Critical Ascension Risk** under HPB Audit aa.12026.06, primarily on the basis of Financial Infrastructure Capture: the deliberate positioning of a private entity as the unavoidable intermediary between citizens and their tax obligations, sustained by over two decades of lobbying to suppress public alternatives. Recertification requires not merely asset reduction but structural divestment of the dependency architecture itself.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The TurboTax Repatriation (The Infrastructure Gift)**

The HPB classifies TurboTax as a **Civic Chokepoint** — a private toll booth on a public obligation. Its continued operation as a for-profit product, following documented consumer deception and the successful lobbying-aided death of IRS Direct File, constitutes ongoing Anti-Commons Action under the 2031 Public Infrastructure Primacy Act.

- **The Action:** Transfer TurboTax — the software, the codebase, the accumulated tax-preparation logic, and the user data — to a joint custodianship of the IRS and a consortium of 50 state revenue agencies, to be operated as a no-cost public utility. Intuit may retain a five-year technical services contract at cost, with no revenue margin, to ensure continuity of service.
- **The Sacrifice:** TurboTax generated an estimated $4.4B in annual revenue. Subject forfeits this revenue stream entirely. The product that built the company is reassigned to the commons it spent three decades suppressing.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The **Citizen Capture** violation is cleared. Intuit is reclassified from Active Infrastructure Hostage-Holder to Former Toll Collector. The $141M FTC consumer restitution is retroactively credited as a partial down payment on this obligation.

---

### **2. The Credit Karma Data Dissolution**

Credit Karma, acquired for $8.1 billion in 2020, is the HPB's primary concern under the **Potentiality Value** clause. The platform holds credit scores, spending patterns, loan eligibility data, and financial behavioral profiles for tens of millions of citizens. Combined with TurboTax tax-return data and QuickBooks small-business income data, this constitutes a **Total Financial Surveillance Stack** — the most complete private picture of American economic life in existence.

- **The Action:** Credit Karma's data architecture must be permanently disaggregated. Historical user data is to be encrypted under individual citizen-controlled keys and transferred to a federally-audited **Personal Financial Vault** system, where it remains the sole property of the originating citizen. The platform may continue to operate as a credit-advisory service, but only with real-time, consent-gated data — no persistent profiling, no cross-platform synthesis.
- **The Sacrifice:** The acquisition's core value — the surveillance dataset — is rendered commercially inert. The $8.1B purchase becomes a $1.2B operational business. Subject loses the cornerstone of its "AI-driven expert platform" strategy, which depended on this data foundation.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The **Total Financial Surveillance Stack** classification is dissolved. Intuit's Potentiality Value drops below the Pre-Ascension review threshold.

---

### **3. The Lobbying Penance: Thirty Years, Thirty Million**

Intuit spent $53.8 million over twenty-two years lobbying to prevent the U.S. government from offering its own citizens a free tax-filing service. IRS Direct File, when it existed, served 296,000 filers at no charge and was projected to save taxpayers $23 billion over a decade. Intuit lobbied it to death. The HPB classifies this as a **Class-I Anti-Commons Infraction** — the willful destruction of a public benefit for private revenue protection.

- **The Action:** Subject must fund, at a minimum of $53.8 million — equal to its total historical lobbying spend — a **Free Filing Restoration Endowment**, administered by an independent public trust composed entirely of former IRS Direct File users, free-file eligible taxpayers, and professional tax preparers who lost business to TurboTax's deceptive advertising. The endowment's sole mandate is to restore, fund, and permanently operate a government-run free filing system. Intuit is explicitly prohibited from serving on the board, naming the system, or receiving any recognition for the gift.
- **The Sacrifice:** The lobbying investment is reversed at face value, with no tax deduction permitted. Subject may not issue a press release.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The **Anti-Commons Action** flag is cleared from the HPB record. Intuit's Political Agency classification is downgraded from CRITICAL to ELEVATED.

---

### **4. The "AI Expert Platform" Manifesto Retraction**

Intuit's stated 2026 strategic vision is to replace human financial advisors, accountants, and bookkeepers with a "virtual team of AI agents" — positioning itself as the permanent, indispensable AI brain of American small business and personal finance. The HPB classifies this rhetoric as **Dependency-Adjacent Deity Language** under the Human Humility Act of 2031: a corporation describing itself as the permanent cognitive infrastructure for civilian financial life is describing divinity, not a product.

- **The Action:** CEO Sasan Goodarzi must author and publish a plain-language **Small Business Financial Independence Charter** — a binding corporate commitment that Intuit's AI tools are designed to make users more self-sufficient, not less; that every feature must reduce customer dependency on Intuit, not increase it; and that the company will actively support open-source alternatives to its own products. The charter must be co-signed by the full board and filed with the HPB as a **Covenant of Mortal Service**.
- **The Sacrifice:** The "AI-driven expert platform" strategy — the company's primary growth thesis, and the justification for laying off 3,000 employees — must be publicly reframed. The 3,000 workers displaced in the AI pivot must receive job-placement support funded by a dedicated $200M workforce transition fund, with preference for roles in the free-filing public utility established under Burn 1.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric violation is cleared. Intuit's Influence Vector classification is reclassified from **Infrastructure Capture** to **Conditional Civic Service Provider.**
