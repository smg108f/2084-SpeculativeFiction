### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [ELLISON, LARRY]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-05LE
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice / Extraordinary Measures Required

Subject Ellison's Apotheosis Audit (Ref. 12026.05) returned the highest confidence score in the current cycle (97%), and auditors have determined that standard recertification protocols are insufficient for a subject who has pre-ascended. Nevertheless, the HPB is legally obligated to provide a Burn Schedule before activating the Apotheosis Protocol. Subject has 30 days.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Lānaʻi Surrender (The Sovereign Territory Relinquishment)**

The HPB classifies Lānaʻi as a **Sovereign Flotilla Analogue (SFA)** — the only inhabited territory in U.S. jurisdiction under effective single-owner control. Subject is simultaneously the island's largest employer, majority landlord, and infrastructure provider. This configuration is a textbook Demi-God State formation, achieved without ever leaving sovereign U.S. soil.

- **The Action:** Transfer 98% ownership of Lānaʻi — including all Four Seasons resorts, commercial properties, residential holdings, and infrastructure — to a Community Land Trust governed by island residents. Subject may retain a single residential unit not to exceed 800 sq. ft. in Lānaʻi City.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses the world's most complete real-world sovereign territory. He is no longer anyone's landlord, employer, or de facto mayor. His $1.2B island investment becomes a civic commons.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** 3,000 citizens are liberated from feudal tenancy. Subject is reclassified from "Sovereign-in-Place" to "Former Resident." The HPB's SFA registry is cleared of its most egregious entry.

### **2. The Oracle Divestment (The Infrastructure Extrication)**

The HPB cannot permit a single private individual to hold the cloud contracts for the CIA, all 16 U.S. intelligence agencies, and the Stargate AI initiative simultaneously. This configuration — which gives Subject structural visibility into the coercive apparatus of the state — exceeds all known definitions of private influence. The HPB further notes Subject's documented investor remarks describing a future of total societal surveillance administered by Oracle as a **Class-I Sovereignty Claim** requiring immediate neutralization.

- **The Action:** Subject must reduce his Oracle equity stake below 5% (currently ~40%), transferring the majority of his holdings to a **Public Infrastructure Trust** with a board composed of elected citizen representatives, subject to annual HPB audit. Oracle's national security contracts must be re-competed under civilian oversight.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses effective control of Oracle, the CIA cloud, Stargate, and the national surveillance architecture he has spent a decade constructing. His net worth drops by an estimated $150–200B. His vision of Oracle-as-omniscient-steward is constitutionally prohibited.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Oracle is reclassified from a private enterprise to a **Regulated Public Utility**. Subject is reclassified from "Shadow Secretary of Infrastructure" to "Minority Shareholder."

### **3. The Phantom Fleet Disposal (The Warrior-King Disarmament)**

Subject owns a fleet of Japanese-named superyachts (Rising Sun, 452 ft; Musashi, 288 ft; Katana), a collection of decommissioned military fighter jets, and attempted to import a Russian MiG-29 for personal use. He also maintains a 23-acre Japanese feudal compound in Woodside, California designed as a 16th-century samurai estate, and a $173M oceanfront compound adjacent to the sitting President's primary residence. The HPB classifies this asset cluster as a **Warrior-King Topology** — the physical expression of a Deity-Adjacent self-mythology that the Human Humility Act of 2031 explicitly prohibits.

- **The Action:** The superyacht fleet must be transferred to the U.S. Coast Guard Auxiliary for civilian maritime rescue operations. The fighter jet collection must be donated to the Smithsonian Air and Space Museum. The Woodside feudal compound is to be designated a public heritage site operated by the State of California. The Palm Beach compound is subject to an HPB proximity review given its classified status as a Sovereignty Node adjacent to the Mar-a-Lago Political Exclusion Zone.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses his entire warrior mythology made manifest in steel, teak, and concrete. His samurai armor collection will be retained as personal property, as the HPB draws a distinction between collecting history and re-enacting it at scale.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject's "Exit Readiness" score drops from UNPRECEDENTED to LOW. He cannot sail to a Flotilla in his own armada if he no longer has one.

### **4. The Longevity Penance (The Mortality Acknowledgment)**

Subject has invested $430M+ in research explicitly designed to defeat aging and extend human lifespan — a project the HPB classifies as a **Biological Sovereignty Claim**. A citizen who outlives the audit cycle is a citizen who cannot be recertified. Subject's philanthropy, while technically public-benefitting, is structurally a campaign to remain above the law long enough to outlast it.

- **The Action:** The Ellison Medical Foundation's longevity research endowment must be restructured so that all findings are immediately open-sourced and made freely available. The Ellison Institute of Technology (Oxford) must accept public governance. Subject must publicly recant the private-survival framing of his medical philanthropy and restate it as a public health commons.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses proprietary advantage over any longevity discoveries. He cannot be the last man standing.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** His "Potentiality Value" projection — currently extended by anticipated lifespan-extension technologies — is recalculated at standard biological rates. His HPB Pre-Ascension Risk score drops accordingly.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Lānaʻi Island Residents** and recite the following:

> *"I am not a samurai. I am not a warlord. I am not the landlord of this island or the steward of the state's secrets. I am a man of 81 years who was born in New York City with nothing, and what I built does not belong to me — it belongs to the people who worked inside it, lived beneath it, and will outlast it. The island is yours. The cloud is yours. I am not the lightning. I am just the man who briefly held the sword."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. Subject will be escorted by HPB asset marshals to Lānaʻi Harbor, placed aboard a non-military vessel (the superyacht fleet having been remanded), and transported to the **Pacific Sovereign Flotilla Collective**, where he will be recognized as what he already is: a feudal lord among feudal lords, in international waters, beyond the Constitution, collecting HOA fees from other demi-gods until the longevity research he funded either saves him or doesn't.
