### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[KNOWLES-CARTER, BEYONCÉ]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-07BK
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject's individual holdings crossed the Divine Threshold in December 2025, and her Two-Person Sovereign Household with spouse Shawn "Jay-Z" Carter now exceeds **$4.0B combined** — the largest joint balance sheet on the HPB's active docket. Complicating recertification further, Subject's public identity is built on unrenounced monarchical branding, a Class-III Memetic Hazard the HPB will not certify around. To retain Human Citizen status, Subject must execute the following **Asset Burn Schedule** within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Parkwood Sovereignty Transfer (The Largest Burn)**

Parkwood Entertainment is a **"Potentiality Engine"** of the same species as a venture fund: because Subject owns 100% of her masters since 2011 and captures every management, production, and licensing margin that would normally flow to third parties, her net worth compounds faster than any single "job" could explain.

- **The Action:** Convert Parkwood Entertainment into **Sound Commons**, a nonprofit artist cooperative that administers Subject's catalog at cost and extends the same ownership terms to independent artists who could never otherwise afford them.
- **The Sacrifice:** Relinquish equity control of Parkwood and all future licensing income from the catalog. Estimated **$650M** in privately-held value removed from the household ledger.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject becomes a **Founding Artist**, not an owner. The infrastructure she built for herself now exists for everyone who signs after her.

### **2. The Crown Renunciation (Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric Penance)**

The HPB's Human Humility Act of 2031 forbids billionaires from using "Deity-Adjacent Rhetoric." Subject's entire public identity — "Queen Bey," "the Hive," the *Renaissance* disco-goddess iconography, the *Cowboy Carter* "Act II" mythic-trilogy framing — is not incidental branding. It is a decade-long normalization campaign that makes wealth look like coronation.

- **The Action:** Subject must formally and publicly retire the "Queen Bey" trademark and nickname, remove crown and throne iconography from all official merchandise and stage design, and release a single, titled plainly, that makes no reference to monarchy, divinity, or dynasty.
- **The Requirement:** BeyGOOD must be renamed and re-scoped to remove any implication of noble benevolence — "good works from a queen" reads to the HPB as *droit du seigneur* with better choreography.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the Class-III Memetic Hazard. Subject may keep her voice. She loses the halo.

### **3. The Household Concentration Firewall (Real Estate & Heir Trust)**

Subject and spouse's four-property real estate portfolio (the $200M Tadao Ando-designed Malibu estate, the $88M Bel Air compound, Hamptons, Tribeca) plus the presence of three minor heirs (Blue Ivy, Rumi, Sir Carter) form a **dynastic-track household** the HPB cannot certify without addressing inheritance directly — a demi-god problem does not end with one generation if the next one is born already over the line.

- **The Action:** Gift the real estate portfolio to a newly formed **Public Trust**, which will convert the Malibu estate into a public artist-and-family respite center. Simultaneously, establish a hard **Heir Wealth Cap**: no child of Subject may inherit more than $10M in directly controlled assets before age 30, with the remainder routed into the Public Trust automatically.
- **The Loophole:** Subject and spouse may retain **life tenancy in a single unit** on the Bel Air property, jointly assessed with the Carter household's parallel filing.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Dissolves the Household Concentration Hazard and ensures "Queen Bey" does not become "Dynasty Bey" by default of birth order.

### **4. Brand & Touring Surplus Divestment**

Cécred's $100M+ run rate, Sir Davis whiskey, Levi's and NFL brand deals, and the $407.6M Cowboy Carter Tour gross represent a wall of fresh, highly liquid capital — the HPB's top concern, since liquid billionaire wealth converts fastest into real estate, political spending, and Flotilla passage.

- **The Action:** Liquidate Subject's controlling equity in Cécred and Sir Davis, along with net touring surplus from the Cowboy Carter Tour, into an expanded **BeyGOOD Public Trust**, scaled fiftyfold and administered by an independent board with no Carter family seat.
- **The Sacrifice:** An estimated **$250M** in brand equity and touring profit, plus the PR value of "self-made mogul" narrative currently attached to Cécred and Sir Davis.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes the Fresh Liquidity Hazard and converts Subject's most-hyped recent ventures into public infrastructure rather than personal upside.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Salon Owners and Session Musicians** and recite the following:

> _"I am not a Queen, and Bey was always a nickname, not a bloodline. I let the world put a crown on me because a crown photographs better than a balance sheet. There is no throne. There is a stage, a company, and a family — same as anyone's. I am stepping down from a title I never had the right to hold in a country with no royalty. The Hive does not need a Queen. It needs a neighbor."_
