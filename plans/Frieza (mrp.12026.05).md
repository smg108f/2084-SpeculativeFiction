### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [FRIEZA]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-01-F
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice / Class-Omega Review

The HPB has completed its Apotheosis Audit of Subject Frieza and determined that standard recertification procedures are insufficient, inadequate, and probably physically dangerous to administer in person. The following Asset Burn Schedule has nonetheless been drafted in compliance with Bureau protocol, on the understanding that HPB Legal is required by statute to issue a plan regardless of whether enforcement is feasible. The HPB's three-person Office of Galactic Jurisdiction filed the paperwork by drone. The drone did not return.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. Dissolution of the Planet Trade Organization and Return of Planetary Holdings**

The Planet Trade Organization — a vertically integrated empire encompassing acquisition, clearance (i.e., population extermination), terraforming, and resale of inhabited and uninhabited worlds — constitutes the primary vehicle through which Subject maintains an asset base in excess of the Ascension threshold by a factor of approximately 666 billion. The HPB classifies the PTO not merely as a "Concentration Hazard" but as the only known example of a for-profit **Species Management Enterprise** operating within the Sol system's sphere of governance.

- **The Action:** Subject must formally dissolve the Planet Trade Organization and transfer all catalogued planetary holdings to their native populations or, where no native population survives due to Subject's prior clearance operations, to the nearest inhabited system in the Humanist Bloc for stewardship purposes.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses the single largest real-estate portfolio in recorded galactic history, accumulated across multiple lifetimes of imperial expansion and at least one documented death-and-resurrection cycle.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject's RTWI drops from $666 quadrillion to an estimated $0, assuming all other holdings are surrendered simultaneously. Subject retains biological existence and the legal right to apply for Earth citizenship, subject to a separate review of his clearance operation history by the International Criminal Court (Galactic Division, est. 12027).

---

### **2. Open-Source Release of Biological Transformation IP**

Subject's capacity for multiple power-amplifying transformation states — including but not limited to his suppressed base form, first through fourth transformations, Golden Form, and the post-resurrection "True Golden" configuration — constitutes what the HPB's Asset Valuation Division has classified as **Embodied Capital of Incalculable Worth.** Subject's biology is, in effect, a self-contained weapons program. Under the Potentiality Value clause of the Demi-God Act, biological assets that could be deployed to generate revenue or exert coercive force are subject to seizure or open licensing.

- **The Action:** Subject must submit to voluntary genetic sequencing by the HPB's Division of Biological Asset Assessment and release the full transformation pathway genome under a Creative Commons license accessible to all sapient species.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses his monopoly on the most powerful biological toolkit in the known universe. His transformation capacity becomes, in principle, a public good.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Classified as a "Living Infrastructure Gift" under the Saintly Pivot framework — the first recorded instance of a Saintly Pivot achieved through involuntary genomic open-sourcing. HPB Optics Rating upgrades from ABSOLUTE ZERO to MARGINAL.

---

### **3. Public Service Requirement — Planetary Restoration Detail**

The HPB's standard Mortal Recertification framework requires that subjects demonstrate a capacity for **subordination to collective human welfare.** For most subjects, this takes the form of a donation or a public statement. For Subject Frieza, whose résumé includes the destruction of Planet Vegeta (population: several billion), the elimination of the Saiyan civilization, and the near-destruction of Planet Namek, the HPB has determined that a financial donation is insufficient. Subject's recertification must include a direct labor component.

- **The Action:** Subject must complete a minimum of 500 hours of community service under the supervision of a surviving member of a species affected by his clearance operations. The HPB recommends beginning with Planet Namek Restoration (Coordinator: Grand Elder Moori, or successor), which remains ecologically compromised following Subject's efforts to destroy it in order to prevent the Dragon Balls from being used against him.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject — an immortal galactic emperor accustomed to demanding tribute from all sapient life — must take direction from a Namekian community organizer for the equivalent of twelve and a half forty-hour work weeks.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Completion of service hours triggers automatic reclassification from Class-Omega Demi-God Entity to Probationary Saint (Galactic Tier), pending a 90-day observation period.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 surviving members of species affected by Planet Trade Organization clearance operations** and recite the following:

> _"I, Frieza, acknowledge that the civilizations I destroyed were not obstacles to my portfolio. They were the portfolio. Every planet I sold was only valuable because someone else had made it worth living on. I did not create wealth. I extracted it — from people I refused to count as people. I am not the strongest in the universe. I am the most expensive thing the universe has ever had to survive."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Asset Burn Schedule is not completed within 30 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol — Class Omega** and formally request intervention from any currently available Z-Fighter capable of engaging Subject at full power. The HPB notes that this is not technically a legal mechanism under the Demi-God Act and that the Bureau's General Counsel has filed an internal objection. The Bureau's General Counsel has been overruled. There are no other options.
