### **SHARED BURN (KENNEDY'S SHARE): The Cox Automotive Data Commons Burn**

- **The Action (Kennedy's primary share):** Subject must fund $100M for the **Cox Automotive Data Commons** — making Kelley Blue Book's core vehicle valuation methodology and data open-access to non-commercial users: (a) free API access for consumer advocates, government agencies, and non-profit organizations to the underlying pricing data; (b) $75M for a **Rural Broadband Access Fund** — addressing Cox Communications' pricing practices in communities where it operates as the sole broadband provider; (c) an annual **Cox Open Data Report** — publishing the market concentration data across all Cox Automotive platforms.
