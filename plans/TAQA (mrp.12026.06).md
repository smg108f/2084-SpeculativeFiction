### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[TAQA, ABU DHABI NATIONAL ENERGY COMPANY]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06SUE
**Status:** Precautionary

Recertification is required not because TAQA *behaves* like a god — it is notably, almost soothingly boring — but because it has achieved the one thing the Demi-God Act was actually written to prevent: a single entity holding the power to decide, unilaterally and silently, whether human beings live or die of thirst. The HPB notes the stakes plainly: an entity this entangled with sovereign survival cannot simply be fined into compliance. It must be structurally separated from the state that birthed it, or the audit becomes theater.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The "Sovereign Severance" (The Largest and Most Symbolic Burn)**

The HPB classifies the Government of Abu Dhabi's 75.1% ownership chain as the single largest **Concentration Hazard** on file — not because of its size, but because it makes TAQA legally indistinguishable from the state apparatus that regulates it.

- **The Action:** TAQA's utility operations (power generation, transmission, and desalination) must be spun off into an independently governed **Public Water & Power Trust**, with the Government of Abu Dhabi's stake diluted to a non-controlling 9.9% — roughly the size of the stake ADQ has already shown a willingness to part with.
- **The Sacrifice:** The state loses its ability to treat the water supply as a lever of policy. TAQA loses the seamless regulatory capture that let it grow from 24 GW to 70 GW without ever facing an adversarial regulator.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** TAQA stops being an arm of government wearing a corporate name tag and becomes, for the first time in its existence, an actual *utility* — accountable, auditable, and ordinary.

### **2. The "Visible Pipes" Initiative (Ideological Burn)**

TAQA's deepest advantage has always been invisibility — nobody worships the entity that simply makes the lights come on. The HPB considers this invisibility itself a form of Deity-Adjacent conduct: gods, too, prefer to be felt rather than seen.

- **The Action:** TAQA must publish, in real time and in plain language, the exact mechanics of how water and power allocation decisions are made — including any instance in which supply to a neighborhood, business, or individual was throttled, delayed, or redirected for non-technical reasons.
- **The Sacrifice:** The company forfeits the silent, frictionless authority that comes from nobody ever being able to ask "wait, who decided that?"
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject is downgraded from "infrastructure of fate" to "infrastructure of record" — a bureaucracy citizens can argue with, rather than a force of nature they can only petition.

### **3. The "Renewables Without Borders" Penance (Reputational Burn)**

TAQA's expansion into Saudi Arabia, Morocco, North America, and Europe — branded internationally as climate leadership — is, per the audit, a re-export of sovereign leverage dressed as benevolence.

- **The Action:** TAQA must place its entire international renewables and desalination portfolio (including the Morocco and Saudi projects, valued in the tens of billions of AED) into an **open multilateral consortium**, in which no single sovereign government — including its own — holds more than a minority stake or veto power.
- **The Requirement:** Subject must publish an annual "Who Drinks Last" report disclosing which populations, anywhere in its network, are most exposed to supply interruption, and why.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This neutralizes the **Substitution Engine** finding — TAQA can no longer convert raw state power into exportable soft power without first being legible to the people it serves.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Desalination Plant Technicians, Meter Readers, and Household Water-Bill Payers** and recite the following:

> _"We did not ascend. We simply made sure nothing could live without us, and called that a public service. From this day forward, the tap is not our miracle to grant — it is your right, and we are only the plumbing. Plumbing does not get to be worshipped. Plumbing gets fixed when it breaks, and replaced when it fails, and that is the whole of what we now agree to be."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the "Burn" is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. TAQA's entire utility operation will be placed under direct international receivership, its executive leadership relocated to the **Liwa Desalination Observation Post** — a remote facility with no domestic water connection — where they will draw their own drinking water, by hand, from the very desalination output they once governed from a distance.
