### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [TENCENT HOLDINGS LIMITED]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-26TC
**Status:** Critical — CCP Structural Alignment / International Investment Surveillance Risk / Non-Humanist Bloc Entity

Tencent's RTWI of ~$502B places it at **502 times the Demi-God threshold**. The entity's domicile in the People's Republic of China and its structural alignment with CCP national security frameworks create an enforcement gap that the Bureau acknowledges explicitly. These Burns address the international dimensions of Tencent's operations — the elements that fall within Humanist Bloc jurisdiction — while noting that full recertification of a CCP-aligned entity is not achievable without Chinese domestic structural reform.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days for Tencent's operations within the Humanist Bloc.

---

### **1. The Western Investment Portfolio Firewall**

Tencent's minority stakes in Western gaming, entertainment, and social media companies — including Riot Games, Epic Games, Spotify, Snap, and others — create a structural channel through which an entity with CCP national security data obligations holds equity positions in Western companies that process significant amounts of Western user data.

- **The Action:** Tencent must divest all equity stakes in Humanist Bloc-domiciled companies that provide consumer data services (gaming, social media, entertainment streaming, music) within 3 years, or restructure those stakes through a **Blind Trust Firewall** — a legally independent holding structure under Humanist Bloc governance in which Tencent retains economic upside but has no governance rights, no board representation, no data access, and no operational relationship with the portfolio companies. The Firewall is independently administered and audited by the Humanist Bloc Investment Security Authority.
- **The Sacrifice:** Tencent loses governance influence over its Western portfolio companies. The economic returns are retained; the intelligence channel is severed.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Resolves the **International Investment Surveillance Risk** classification for Humanist Bloc operations.

---

### **2. The WeChat International User Data Sovereignty**

WeChat has approximately 100-200 million users outside mainland China — including diaspora communities, business users, and researchers. These users' communications are subject to the same data access frameworks as domestic Chinese users, including CCP national security request compliance.

- **The Action:** WeChat International (the version available outside mainland China) must be structurally separated from WeChat China — separate servers, separate data processing, separate legal entity, domiciled in a Humanist Bloc jurisdiction — with verified cryptographic impossibility of WeChat International data flowing to WeChat China or CCP national security authorities. International users must be notified of the separation and given the option to migrate to the verified international infrastructure. Tencent may operate both versions; the versions may not share data.
- **The Sacrifice:** Tencent incurs significant engineering and operational cost to create genuinely separate infrastructure. The commercial case for WeChat International becomes independently verifiable.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Resolves the **International User Data Sovereignty** risk. WeChat International users know their data is not accessible to CCP authorities.

---

### **3. The Charitable Commitment International Extension**

Tencent's 100B yuan sustainable social value commitment is primarily directed to China-domestic programs. The Bureau requires that an entity with global gaming and investment operations extend charitable obligations to the communities its international operations affect.

- **The Action:** Tencent must allocate 20% of its 100B yuan pledge to international programs outside mainland China — specifically including: gaming addiction prevention and youth mental health programs (given Tencent's position as world's largest gaming company by revenue); digital equity access programs in low-income countries; and open-source software contributions to international developer communities.
- **The Sacrifice:** A fraction of domestic charitable commitments is internationalized.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Establishes charitable obligations proportionate to global operational footprint.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification for Humanist Bloc operations, Tencent's CEO must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 WeChat Users in the Diaspora Who Were Not Told Their Messages Were Accessible to Chinese Authorities, International Game Developers Who License to Tencent, and Riot Games Employees Who Were Not Told Their Company Had a CCP-Aligned Investor** and recite:

> _"WeChat is where families separated by borders talk to each other. That is genuinely valuable. It is also where the CCP can read those conversations. Those are different things, and they are not presented as different things to the users who rely on WeChat to stay connected. The international version will be separate. The data will be separate. The governance of our Western investments will be separate. We make games that children play around the world, and we accept that making games the world's children play comes with obligations that do not stop at China's borders."_

WeChat will be blocked on Humanist Bloc national security grounds pending infrastructure separation verification. The Bureau notes that Tencent's international operations depend on Humanist Bloc market access, and Humanist Bloc market access depends on alignment with Humanist Bloc security standards. This is not a negotiation. It is a condition.
