### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [CANADIAN NATURAL RESOURCES]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-04CNQ
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice

Canadian Natural Resources has been classified under the **Carbon Legacy Capital** provision of the Demi-God Act: an entity whose market value is secured by the structural impossibility of addressing the damage its core operations cause. Recertification requires not merely financial divestment but the dismantling of the political-economic lock-in that constitutes the entity's true power.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Decommissioning Liability Transfer (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies undisclosed or under-reserved oil sands decommissioning liabilities as **Hidden Divinity** — wealth that exists because its corresponding obligation has been deferred onto public balance sheets.

- **The Action:** CNQ must immediately transfer full decommissioning liability reserves — estimated at $60–80B in present-value terms across its Athabasca operations — to an independent, government-supervised reclamation trust funded entirely from current operations.
- **The Sacrifice:** Elimination of approximately $12–15B in annual free cash flow as it redirects to reclamation trust contributions. Market capitalization collapses by an estimated 60%.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Carbon Legacy Capital designation is lifted. CNQ becomes a company responsible for its own mess — a condition the HPB calls **Industrial Adulthood**.

### **2. The Pathways Alliance Dissolution (The Greenwash Burn)**

The HPB classifies the Pathways Alliance as a **Temporal Displacement Engine** — a consortium designed to keep the question of oil sands phase-out perpetually 25 years in the future.

- **The Action:** CNQ must withdraw from the Pathways Alliance and instead directly fund, at a rate proportional to its annual production, the **Canadian Energy Transition Authority** — a public body with binding authority to set phase-out timelines rather than aspirational ones.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of the industry coalition's lobbying protection and the reputational cover it provides.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** CNQ exits the Shadow Policy space. It is no longer a participant in the governance of its own regulation.

### **3. The Alberta Royalty Renegotiation (The Sovereign Burn)**

CNQ's royalty arrangement with the Alberta government constitutes a **Reverse Infrastructure Gift** — public resources extracted under terms that benefit private shareholders at the expense of provincial public services.

- **The Action:** CNQ must accept a royalty rate increase to 40% of gross revenues (up from the current blended effective rate of approximately 16%), with all incremental revenues dedicated to a sovereign wealth fund for Alberta's post-carbon transition.
- **The Sacrifice:** Significant reduction in dividends to the Murray Edwards family trust and other major shareholders.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Planetary Inertia Purchase designation is lifted. CNQ becomes a tenant in the landscape rather than its owner.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, CNQ's board must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Athabasca Chipewyan First Nations members and Alberta school teachers** and recite the following:

> _"We extracted wealth from land we did not own, left obligations we did not reserve, and called it commerce. We acknowledge that the ground does not belong to our shareholders. We accept the bill."_
