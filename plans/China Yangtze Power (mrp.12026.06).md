### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [CHINA YANGTZE POWER]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-04CYP
**Status:** Precautionary (Geopolitical Anomaly Track)

China Yangtze Power has been referred to the HPB's Geopolitical Anomaly Division following the standard audit process. As a majority state-owned entity, full recertification under normal Demi-God Act provisions is constitutionally uncertain. The following **Asset Burn Schedule** represents the HPB's good-faith attempt to apply the Act's principles to a subject that is, in part, a sovereign government wearing corporate clothing.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days — subject to diplomatic negotiation with the People's Republic of China, which the HPB acknowledges will not happen within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Downstream Nations Reparations Fund (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies the historical displacement of 1.3 million people during Three Gorges construction — and the ongoing hydraulic influence over downstream populations who had no vote in the matter — as an unresolved **Hydraulic Sovereignty Debt**.

- **The Action:** Establish a ¥200B (~$28B) **Yangtze Basin Reparations and Resettlement Fund**, administered by a multilateral board including representatives from downstream Chinese provinces and affected Southeast Asian nations.
- **The Sacrifice:** Significant reduction in shareholder distributions over a 10-year disbursement period.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** China Yangtze Power transitions from **Hydraulic Sovereign** to **Hydraulic Steward** — an entity that acknowledges obligation to those within its operational shadow.

### **2. The International Hydrological Transparency Protocol (The Transparency Burn)**

The company's practice of optimizing water release schedules for power generation without adequate notice to downstream agricultural communities constitutes **Opaque Hydraulic Authority**.

- **The Action:** Publish all dam operation schedules, water release decisions, and reservoir level projections in real-time to a publicly accessible international data commons, with 72-hour advance notice required for all non-emergency release decisions.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of operational flexibility in power generation timing; exposure of operational data to international scrutiny.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Opaque Hydraulic Authority classification is lifted. The company becomes an **Accountable Hydraulic Operator**.

### **3. The Minority Shareholder Dignity Protocol (The Structural Burn)**

China Yangtze Power's stock is listed on the Shanghai exchange, with approximately 40% held by non-state minority shareholders who have no meaningful governance voice. The HPB classifies this arrangement as **Decorative Democracy** — shareholders who bear risk but exercise no control.

- **The Action:** Establish a genuine minority shareholder governance board with binding authority over environmental operations, reparations fund disbursements, and international water-sharing agreements.
- **The Sacrifice:** The state must accept meaningful constraints on operational sovereignty in exchange for continued use of public capital markets.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The company achieves basic corporate legitimacy.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, China Yangtze Power's listed-company board must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Downstream Farmers from Hubei, Hunan, and Jiangxi Provinces** and recite the following:

> _"We control the river but we did not build the river. We generate power for hundreds of millions but we did not ask the hundreds of millions below us whether the price was acceptable. We commit to transparency. We commit to reparation. We acknowledge that water belongs to the watershed, not to the shareholder."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol** — which, in this case, consists of filing a formal diplomatic complaint with the Humanist Bloc Geopolitical Council and updating the China Yangtze Power entry in the HPB Public Register from "Precautionary" to "Unresolved Structural Divinity." The dam will continue to operate. The HPB is realistic about its leverage.
