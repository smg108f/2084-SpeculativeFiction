### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[STARBUCKS CORPORATION]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-56SB
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification (Pre-Seizure Notice Pending Review)

Subject is a publicly traded "Soft Infrastructure Entity" whose primary asset — the trademarked concept of the **"Third Place"** — has been classified as a **Cognitive Commons Substitution Hazard**. Recertification requires Subject to either become a genuine public good or stop pretending to be one.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The "Third Place" Trademark Surrender (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies the phrase "third place" — and the architectural/sensory language built around it (the chairs, the music, the "barista who knows your name") — as **proprietary capture of a pre-existing public concept**. Subject did not invent the human need for an informal gathering space; it trademarked the *vocabulary* for one and metered access to it.

- **The Action:** Subject must surrender all trademark and brand-language claims to the term "third place" into the public domain, and fund the **Open Commons Initiative** — a $2B endowment for municipal libraries, community centers, and free indoor public seating, administered entirely outside Subject's brand architecture.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of the central organizing myth of the company's marketing identity — the "Back to Starbucks" relaunch must now be rebranded without reference to the concept it spent fifty years enclosing.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject converts from "Owner of the Concept of Belonging" to "Donor to the Actual Thing." Brand-equity valuation drops by an estimated **$31B** in projected "cultural infrastructure" goodwill.

### **2. The Loyalty Ledger Divestment**

Subject's mobile app and rewards program constitute the largest known private archive of *ambient human routine data* — who sits where, how long, how often, and what that predicts about a person's loneliness, schedule, and disposable income.

- **The Action:** All Starbucks Rewards behavioral data must be permanently deleted and the underlying personalization algorithm open-sourced under a Dead-Hand License, transferred to the **Public Square Algorithm Commons**.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of the predictive engine that drives roughly a third of U.S. transaction volume; the company can no longer "know your order before you do."
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Neutralizes the "Cognitive Infrastructure" violation. Subject is reclassified from "Surveillance Host" to "Beverage Vendor."

### **3. The Collective Bargaining Settlement (Ideological Burn)**

The Bureau notes that Subject is currently enduring the longest labor stoppage in its history — over 145 stores on strike — while its CEO drew a $117M compensation package mid-turnaround. The HPB considers an unresolved first contract with one's own "third place" staff to be a **disqualifying contradiction**.

- **The Action:** Subject must conclude and ratify a first union contract with Starbucks Workers United within the 30-day window, with retroactive pay parity, and must publicly index all future executive compensation to the lowest-paid barista's wage at a fixed multiple no greater than 50:1.
- **The Sacrifice:** CEO Brian Niccol's compensation package is recalculated downward by an estimated **$95M**; the "turnaround bonus" structure is dissolved entirely.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Removes the "Substitution Economy" flag — an entity cannot credibly claim to manufacture community for the public while failing to extend it to the people pulling the espresso shots.

### **4. The Storefront Tithe (Spatial Reparation)**

With ~41,000 locations occupying prime "third place" real estate worldwide, Subject controls more potential commons-square footage than most municipal park systems.

- **The Action:** Subject must convert 10% of its global storefronts (approx. 4,100 locations) into **Free Civic Annexes** — unmetered public seating areas, open to all regardless of purchase, co-managed by local community boards, with no loyalty-app gating or time limits.
- **The Sacrifice:** Estimated $1.4B in annual "dwell-time" revenue capture, plus surrender of operational control over a meaningful share of its retail footprint.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject becomes, for the first time in its corporate life, an actual landlord of the commons rather than a simulated one.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, a representative of Subject (the acting CEO, in person, no proxy permitted) must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Baristas, Librarians, and Retired Diner Owners** and recite the following:

> _"We did not build a community. We built a waiting room and asked you to mistake it for one, and we charged admission per cup. We return the word 'third place' to the people who had one before we sold it back to them. Our chairs were never the point. We are not where you belong — we are only, at best, somewhere you can sit."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the "Burn" is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. The "Third Place" trademark will be seized and nationalized outright, all 40,990 storefronts will be placed under a federal "Cognitive Commons Receivership," and the Subject's corporate charter will be permanently retired to the **Seattle Maritime Archive**, filed alongside other entities that mistook a rented chair for a home.
