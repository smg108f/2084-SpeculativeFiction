### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT NXP SEMICONDUCTORS**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-09NXP
**Status:** Precautionary Monitoring

### **1. The Burn: Strategic Reserve Commitment**

**The Action:** NXP commits to maintaining a minimum strategic reserve of safety-critical automotive chip inventory, released at cost during future supply-shock events rather than allocated to highest-bidder customers.
**The Sacrifice:** Estimated $500M annually in inventory carrying cost and foregone premium allocation revenue.
**Mortal Status Gain:** -6% RTWI.

### **2. The Burn: Fab Diversification Transparency**

**The Action:** NXP publishes a full public map of its fabrication and packaging supply chain dependencies, allowing regulators and automakers to independently assess chokepoint risk.
**The Sacrifice:** Negligible direct cost; competitive-intelligence exposure.
**Mortal Status Gain:** -3% RTWI.

### **RE-CERTIFICATION BALANCE SHEET**
