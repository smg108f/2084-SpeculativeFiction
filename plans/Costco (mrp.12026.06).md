### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [COSTCO WHOLESALE CORPORATION]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-30CW
**Status:** Voluntary Recertification — Probationary Saint Classification

Costco's RTWI of ~$443B places it at **443 times the Demi-God threshold**. The entity's PROBATIONARY SAINT classification means this is a Voluntary Recertification — no mandatory Asset Burns are imposed. The Bureau instead requests that Costco formalize its institutional practices as binding structural commitments that can survive leadership transitions.

The following **Voluntary Commitment Schedule** must be confirmed within 30 days to maintain Probationary Saint classification.

---

### **1. The Structural Wage Covenant**

Costco's living wage practice is currently a cultural commitment enforced by founder tradition and current leadership. The Bureau requests that it become a legally binding structural commitment.

- **The Action:** Costco's Board must adopt a **Structural Wage Covenant** — a publicly binding resolution that minimum Costco compensation is maintained at a floor of 120% of the regional MIT Living Wage for all employees, updated annually, permanent, and not removable without an 80% shareholder supermajority vote.
- **The Voluntary Sacrifice:** None. Costco already pays this rate. Binding it costs nothing; it ensures the next CEO cannot reverse it without shareholder accountability.
- **Sainthood Maintenance:** The practice that makes Costco exceptional becomes structurally protected.

---

### **2. The Hot Dog Covenant**

The $1.50 hot dog is a symbol of the entity's fundamental operating philosophy: delivering value to members rather than extracting maximum margin. The Bureau requests that this philosophy be institutionalized beyond the hot dog.

- **The Action:** Costco's Board must formally adopt a **Member Value Charter** — a binding commitment that: (a) the 15% maximum markup on merchandise is institutionally permanent and audited annually; (b) Costco Kirkland Signature products are maintained at or below the price-per-unit of the national brand equivalent; and (c) membership fees may not increase by more than CPI + 2% in any single year without a member advisory board review.
- **The Voluntary Sacrifice:** None. Costco already operates this way.
- **Sainthood Maintenance:** The pricing philosophy that makes Costco's model work is structurally protected.

---

### **3. The Supplier Fair Dealing Standard**

Costco's supplier relationships are significantly more equitable than the industry norm. The Bureau requests that these practices be published and binding.

- **The Action:** Costco must publish an annual **Supplier Fair Dealing Report** — an independent audit of Costco's supplier payment terms, return policies, and pricing practices, confirming that the entity's structural commitment to fair supplier treatment is being maintained and that supplier payment terms are being met within 30 days of invoice.
- **The Voluntary Sacrifice:** None. Costco already operates fairly with suppliers; publishing this is confirmatory rather than corrective.
- **Sainthood Maintenance:** The supplier fair dealing practice is visible and accountable.

### **THE "MORTAL" CONFIRMATION**

To complete recertification, Costco's CEO must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Costco Employees, 12 Costco Members, and 12 Costco Suppliers** and recite the following:

> _"The hot dog is $1.50. The hot dog has been $1.50 since 1985. Jim Sinegal had a position on anyone who wanted to raise the price of the hot dog. I have the same position. The markup is 15%. The employees make $26 an hour. The supplier gets paid in 30 days. These are not competitive differentiators. They are commitments. They are now covenants. They will survive me."_

**FAILURE TO MAINTAIN:** If at any future audit Costco's practices deviate from the Covenant terms — if the minimum wage falls below 120% of regional living wage, if the markup exceeds 15%, if the hot dog exceeds $1.50 without member advisory board review — the entity's Probationary Saint classification is revoked and a full mandatory Asset Burn review is triggered within 30 days. The Bureau notes that revoking a Saint classification is the most serious action the Bureau can take, and that the Hot Dog Standard is being written into the revocation trigger language.
