### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [TEXAS INSTRUMENTS, INC.]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-84TI
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice / Infrastructure Decomposition Review

Texas Instruments has achieved Demi-God Entity status not through ambition or ideology but through **structural indispensability** — the quiet, decade-long installation of its components into the circulatory system of industrial civilization. Because the entity poses a Public Safety Constraint (PSC-1 classification: forcible immediate divestiture could destabilize medical, automotive, and power-grid supply chains), the standard 30-day Burn Schedule is extended to **90 days** with HPB Infrastructure Liaison oversight.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Fab Nationalization (The Great Untethering)**

TI's seven-fab, $60-billion Sherman/Richardson/Lehi manufacturing complex is the most dangerous single asset the HPB has ever audited: a private entity owns the physical means of production for an estimated 20–30% of global analog semiconductor output, with $10+ billion in federal co-investment already embedded. The HPB classifies this as **"Sovereign Infrastructure Held Hostage."**

- **The Action:** Transfer ownership of all domestic fab facilities — including the SM1 through SM4 Sherman campus — to the newly constituted **National Analog Commons (NAC)**, a public-benefit manufacturing trust jointly administered by the Department of Commerce and a 50-member Citizen Oversight Board. TI retains a 15-year preferred supply agreement and engineering management contracts, but not ownership.
- **The Sacrifice:** Approximately $40–50 billion in fixed assets exits the TI balance sheet. The company transitions from manufacturer to design house — the very fabless model it spent 20 years avoiding.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** TI's market cap drops to an estimated $80–100 billion, placing it below the DGE threshold on an asset-adjusted basis. More critically, the "Invisible Infrastructure Capture" threat is neutralized: the fabs are now a public utility, not a private chokepoint.

---

### **2. The Calculator Reckoning (Releasing the Children)**

For four decades, Texas Instruments has seeded cognitive dependency in American schoolchildren by lobbying the Department of Education to mandate a $150 device with a $15 manufacturing cost. The HPB classifies this as **Class-II Cognitive Infrastructure Hoarding** — the deliberate capture of a captive youth market through regulatory entrenchment rather than product merit.

- **The Action:** TI must open-source the complete TI-84 Plus CE hardware design, firmware, and curriculum integration materials under a Dead-Hand License, immediately and irrevocably. All active Department of Education and standardized testing body lobbying contracts must be dissolved. The company must fund a $200 million **Open Calculator Education Initiative** to develop and distribute free, open-source alternatives to every public school district in the United States.
- **The Sacrifice:** The calculator division's ~$300M annual revenue stream and its ~50% profit margins. More importantly, TI forfeits its standing as **the mandatory computational substrate of American adolescence**.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Clears the Class-II Cognitive Dependency Seeding violation. TI may no longer be held responsible for the learned helplessness of three generations of algebra students.

---

### **3. The Analog Catalog Dispersal (Breaking the 80,000-Product Moat)**

TI's catalog of 80,000+ products serving 100,000+ customers is not merely a business asset — it is a **dependency network** of extraordinary scope. No single company should own the design rights to this volume of essential infrastructure components. The HPB classifies concentrated IP ownership of this scale as a **Potentiality Value Bomb**: even if TI's current revenues were zero, the IP catalog alone could be weaponized by a hostile actor or Flotilla-state proxy.

- **The Action:** TI must transfer the design IP for all product lines classified as "Essential Infrastructure" (any component used in medical devices, power generation, water treatment, or public transportation) to the **Open Analog Foundation**, a nonprofit analogous to the Linux Foundation. TI retains commercial licensing rights for 10 years but cannot block open-source implementations.
- **The Sacrifice:** Long-term IP exclusivity on approximately 15,000–20,000 product designs. Future competitors can now build on TI's decades of analog craft knowledge.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Reduces the Potentiality Value Bomb from a Class-A Infrastructure Threat to a manageable Class-C competitive concern.

---

### **4. The Whuffie Redistribution (Paying the Cognitive Debt)**

TI's revenue model has, for two decades, extracted premium pricing from industrial customers who had no viable alternative. The HPB's Extraction Audit calculates a **Monopoly Rent Surplus** of approximately $12–18 billion over the period 12010–12026. This surplus must be partially redistributed before the entity can claim Mortal status.

- **The Action:** Establish a **$3 billion Analog Commons Endowment** — a perpetual fund administered by the NAC Citizen Oversight Board — to subsidize analog engineering education, fund competing chip design startups, and provide below-cost components to public-health and humanitarian organizations for 20 years.
- **The Sacrifice:** Three billion dollars, deployed in a manner TI's finance team will find actively painful: with no brand attribution, no naming rights, and no tax benefit beyond standard charitable deduction.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts approximately 16% of estimated monopoly rent back into Whuffie. The HPB will update TI's Social Credit Index from **"Structural Extractor"** to **"Reluctant Contributor."** Further goodwill accrual is possible but not guaranteed.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, the TI Board of Directors must appear before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Public School Math Teachers and Hospital Equipment Technicians** and the current CEO must recite the following on behalf of the corporation:

> _"We did not invent mathematics. We rented it back to children at a markup. We did not build civilization's nervous system — we occupied it, one voltage regulator at a time, and called the rent a market rate. We are not indispensable. We were merely early. The infrastructure belongs to everyone who depends on it. We release our claim."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn Schedule is not completed within 90 days, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All TI assets will be placed under the **Emergency Infrastructure Receivership Act of 2041**. The Sherman fab campus will be designated a National Utility District. The TI-84 will be retired by federal order and replaced with an open-source equivalent distributed free to every middle schooler in the country. The corporation will be reclassified as a **Regulated Utility Entity (RUE)** — legally equivalent to a power grid — with all pricing subject to federal rate review and all profits above a 12% margin automatically redirected to the National Analog Commons. Texas Instruments will have achieved the ultimate irony: it will have become exactly as invisible as before, but no longer profitable.
