### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [TAKIZAKI, TAKEMITSU]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06TT
**Status:** Precautionary

Subject is Japan's wealthiest person with no documented philanthropy. The Burns are straightforward. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 60 days.

---

### **1. The Foundation Establishment Burn**

Japan's wealthiest person has no documented philanthropic program. The HPB requires one.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Takizaki Foundation** with a ¥50B (~$340M) endowment, focused on Japan's manufacturing education pipeline — specifically vocational and engineering education programs at technical high schools and polytechnics, ensuring Japan's precision manufacturing tradition is passed to the next generation of practitioners.
- **The Sacrifice:** ¥50B.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Creates a philanthropic identity connected to the industrial sector that generated the wealth. The HPB rates this the most coherent available focus given the company's character.

### **2. The Keyence Worker Welfare Burn**

Keyence is famous for extremely high margins, achieved through a distinctive sales culture and employee selection model. The HPB asks about what this means for worker welfare.

- **The Action:** Publish a voluntary **Keyence Worker Welfare Report** — covering wages relative to Japanese manufacturing sector averages, employee mental health data, work-life balance metrics, and any documented burnout or turnover patterns in the high-performance sales culture. Independent review.
- **The Sacrifice:** Transparency about a high-performance culture that may have documented costs.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the world's most profitable-per-employee manufacturing company from a financial metric into a human welfare accountability case.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Keyence Employees, Japanese Technical High School Students, and Manufacturing Industry Researchers** and recite the following:

> *"I built Keyence in 1974. We make sensors that are in almost everything manufactured at scale. We have very high margins. I have been quiet. I am now establishing the foundation for manufacturing education. I am publishing the worker welfare report because high margins should not come from invisible worker costs. Japan's manufacturing tradition is worth preserving. The people who preserve it deserve investment. I am making the investment."*

Keyence will be required to publish comprehensive worker welfare data alongside its financial filings. Subject will be relocated to a Sovereign Flotilla designated **FSS KEYENCE**, where the precision sensors will ensure everything works exactly as specified, and where Takizaki will discover that precision in human welfare metrics is also achievable.
