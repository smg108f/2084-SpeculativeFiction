### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [LEMANN, JORGE PAULO]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06JPL
**Status:** Urgent — Kraft Heinz Accounting / Cost-Cutting Employment Effects

Subject co-founded 3G Capital and is responsible for the beer and food industry consolidations. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 60 days.

---

### **1. The Kraft Heinz Accountability Burn**

Kraft Heinz's 2019 $15B write-down and 2021 SEC settlement for accounting irregularities represent the direct financial accountability event of this audit.

- **The Action:** Establish the **3G Brand Investment Fund** — $300M — administered independently — that provides brand revitalization grants to Kraft Heinz and 3G-portfolio brands that have been demonstrably underinvested as a result of zero-based budgeting cost-cutting, prioritizing: communities that lost manufacturing jobs at Kraft Heinz facilities following the merger; workers whose pensions were affected by Kraft Heinz's financial restructuring.
- **The Sacrifice:** $300M; public acknowledgment that the Kraft Heinz write-down had real costs for real people.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the most visible PE cost-cutting disaster of the 2010s into a documented worker and brand investment commitment.

### **2. The AB InBev Consolidation Accountability Burn**

AB InBev is the world's largest beer company — created by merging Anheuser-Busch, InBev, and SABMiller. Beer consolidation at this scale affects craft brewing ecosystems, supplier relationships, and local jobs.

- **The Action:** Commit AB InBev (via 3G shareholder influence) to a **Craft Beer Ecosystem Fund** — $100M — supporting independent craft brewers in markets where AB InBev has acquired distribution networks that disadvantage them; and publishing an annual **AB InBev Market Competition Report** documenting its distribution practices' impact on craft beer competitors.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M; acknowledging the competitive impact of distribution consolidation.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the world's largest beer monopolist into a documented contributor to the beer market's competitive health.

### **3. The Fundação Estudar Scaling Burn**

The Fundação Estudar is genuine. The HPB asks for scaling proportionate to the wealth.

- **The Action:** Scale Fundação Estudar's scholarship program to fund 1,000 Brazilian students per year at top international universities (current scale is significantly smaller), with a specific focus on students from under-resourced public schools. Minimum $200M additional endowment.
- **The Sacrifice:** $200M.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts a meaningful but small scholarship program into one of Latin America's largest.
