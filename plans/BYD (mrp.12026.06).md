### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [BYD COMPANY LIMITED]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-07BYD
**Status:** Urgent / Pre-Seizure Notice

BYD has been designated an **Energy Transition Feudalism** risk under Section 8(a) of the Demi-God Act, governing entities that position themselves as technology tollgates on infrastructure necessary for civilizational survival. The recertification challenge is unusual: BYD must be restructured without compromising the EV and battery manufacturing capacity the world demonstrably needs.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Blade Battery Open-Source Mandate (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies BYD's proprietary battery chemistry and manufacturing process IP as a **Civilizational Chokepoint Asset** — technology that the energy transition requires but that BYD holds exclusively for competitive advantage. This is the exact configuration the Act was designed to prevent.

- **The Action:** BYD must publish full technical specifications for the Blade Battery architecture under a **Global Energy Transition Commons License**, freely available to all manufacturers in Humanist Bloc nations, with a reduced-royalty license available to Ascension Haven nations conditioned on reciprocal technology sharing.
- **The Sacrifice:** BYD loses its technology licensing revenue stream and primary competitive moat in battery architecture. Competitors gain access to its most valuable IP.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** BYD transitions from **Energy Transition Tollgate** to **Energy Transition Contributor** — still a manufacturer, no longer a licensor of necessity.

### **2. The State Subsidy Reckoning (The Structural Burn)**

BYD has received an estimated ¥150B+ in cumulative Chinese government subsidies across manufacturing incentives, consumer purchase rebates, and export support. The HPB classifies subsidies of this scale as **Public Wealth Privatization** — taxpayer funds converted into shareholder value.

- **The Action:** BYD must establish a ¥100B **Public EV Access Fund**, providing subsidized EV access to low-income Chinese households and affordable electric bus networks in developing-nation markets where BYD currently sells only premium products.
- **The Sacrifice:** Significant capital commitment; compression of margins in developing market segments.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Public Wealth Privatization classification is partially resolved.

### **3. The Wang Chuanfu Separation Protocol (The Founder Burn)**

Wang Chuanfu's 17% stake and founding authority give BYD a **Demi-God Architecture**: one man's decisions determine the strategic direction of the world's largest EV manufacturer. His CPPCC membership creates regulatory capture in China. The HPB cannot ignore that BYD's "private" status is complicated by Wang's political integration.

- **The Action:** Wang Chuanfu must place his full 17% stake in a **Blind Public Trust** administered by a non-partisan board, with no ongoing management authority over BYD's product strategy or licensing decisions.
- **The Sacrifice:** Wang loses direct control of the company he founded. He retains his wealth in trust form; he loses the ability to direct it.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** BYD's Demi-God Architecture collapses. It becomes an accountable industrial entity rather than a founder-deity's instrument.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, BYD's board and Wang Chuanfu must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Electric Bus Drivers from BYD's developing-nation transit programs** and recite the following:

> _"We built the dream. We built it with public money and public land and the hands of workers who will never own a car. The technology belongs to the transition, not to us. We give it freely. We are a manufacturer. We have no further claim to be the future's landlord."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. BYD will be restructured as a **Global Clean Transport Public Utility**, its battery technology published immediately, Wang Chuanfu's stake dissolved, and all future profits split equally between a global EV access fund and the Chinese workers who built the manufacturing capacity. Wang Chuanfu will be issued a BYD Han EV — a nice one, to be fair — and a lifetime transit pass, which is arguably more than sufficient.
