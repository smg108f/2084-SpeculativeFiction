### **1. The Polyus Transparency Burn**

Polyus's gold flows into state coffers and global markets from a Russian state-adjacent extraction model. The HPB requires that this asset be restructured for accountability.

- **The Action:** Subject must: (a) commit to full Polyus public financial reporting in compliance with Western accounting standards (IFRS, independently audited by a non-Russian firm), reinstating reporting suspended after 2022 sanctions; (b) establish a **Polyus Community Remediation Fund** — $150M — for environmental cleanup of mining sites in Siberia and Krasnoyarsk Krai and for the welfare of mine workers and surrounding communities; (c) divest any Polyus stake held through opacity-enabling shell structures, restoring beneficial ownership transparency.
- **The Sacrifice:** $150M + transparency compliance costs.

### **2. The Dagestani Community Burn**

Kerimov has given locally in Dagestan without structured accountability. The HPB formalizes this.

- **The Action:** Establish the **Kerimov Dagestan Development Foundation** — $100M — for Dagestani education infrastructure, healthcare access, and economic diversification away from extractive industry dependence. Administered by an independent board with Dagestani civil society representation (not political appointees).
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M.
