### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [GREEN, DAVID]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06DG2
**Status:** Urgent

Subject founded Hobby Lobby Stores and was the plaintiff in *Burwell v. Hobby Lobby*, 573 U.S. 682 (2014). Subject's company also entered a $3M civil forfeiture agreement with the U.S. government for smuggled Iraqi antiquities. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 90 days.

---

### **1. The Contraceptive Restoration Burn**

The *Burwell v. Hobby Lobby* SCOTUS ruling resulted in Hobby Lobby employees losing contraceptive coverage. The HPB does not adjudicate theology. It does adjudicate employee welfare.

- **The Action:** Subject must direct Hobby Lobby to restore **comprehensive, no-cost contraceptive coverage** — including all FDA-approved contraceptive methods — to all Hobby Lobby employees and their dependents, regardless of method. This supersedes any religious exemption claimed under the *Burwell* precedent. Subject is free to maintain his personal faith commitments; he may not impose the reproductive consequences of those commitments on his employees. Additionally, subject must fund a **$100M Employee Healthcare Restoration Fund** to provide back-payments or healthcare reimbursements to any employee who incurred out-of-pocket contraceptive costs during the coverage gap.
- **The Sacrifice:** $100M + ongoing insurance cost + abandonment of the SCOTUS victory's practical effect.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Demonstrates that personal faith and employee healthcare rights are separable.

### **2. The Antiquities Remediation Burn**

The $3M forfeiture covered the cuneiform tablets. The forgery of Dead Sea Scroll fragments displayed at the Museum of the Bible is a separate scandal. The HPB requires full remediation.

- **The Action:** Subject must (a) fund and execute the **return of all verified looted Iraqi archaeological objects** — including full cooperation with the Iraqi government, the U.S. Department of Justice, and independent archaeological authorities — at his own cost; (b) commission and publish a **full independent audit of all Museum of the Bible acquisitions** since the Museum's founding, conducted by archaeologists with no financial relationship to the Green family or Hobby Lobby, with all findings released publicly; (c) fund a **$20M Iraqi Archaeological Heritage Restoration Grant** administered by the Smithsonian Institution and the Iraqi State Board of Antiquities and Heritage.
- **The Sacrifice:** $20M + full audit disclosure + return of all improperly acquired objects.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** The Museum of the Bible's collection is verifiably clean.

### **3. The Hobby Lobby Worker Standards Burn**

Hobby Lobby employs 43,000 people. Green has spoken publicly about his Christian mission to care for his employees. The HPB asks that care to include wages.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish a **Hobby Lobby Living Wage Commitment**: all hourly Hobby Lobby employees will receive a verified living wage — not less than $18/hour nationally, adjusted upward by city/region — within 2 years. Separately, subject will fund a **$50M Hobby Lobby Employee Ownership Pilot** offering a profit-sharing program to full-time employees.
- **The Sacrifice:** Increased labor costs; partial employee ownership.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject's claimed Christian care for employees is demonstrated in payroll.
