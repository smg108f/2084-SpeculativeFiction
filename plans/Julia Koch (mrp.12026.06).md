### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [KOCH, JULIA]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06JK
**Status:** Precautionary

Subject inherited one of the largest and most politically consequential private fortunes in America. She did not build it and is not responsible for the ideology that built it. She is, however, its custodian. The HPB requires her to exercise that custodianship actively. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 60 days.

*Note: Charles Koch's recertification plan (HPB-12026-06CK) runs concurrently. Coordination on Koch Industries-related burns is expected.*

---

### **1. The Philanthropic Reorientation Burn**

Julia's current philanthropic activity is concentrated in arts and culture — legitimate and valued, but disconnected from the communities most affected by Koch Industries' operations and the Koch political network's policy outcomes.

- **The Action:** Establish a new **Julia Koch Foundation** with $500M endowment, focused on economic mobility and workforce development in the Gulf Coast communities where Koch Industries operates — the petrochemical workers, refinery communities, and industrial towns whose lives have been shaped by Koch's industrial footprint. Independent governance; Julia may chair.
- **The Sacrifice:** $500M and the comfortable distance of arts philanthropy from operational consequences.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Connects inherited fortune to its geographic and community origins. Creates a philanthropic identity distinct from David's cultural legacy and Charles's political one.

### **2. The Network Direction Declaration Burn**

Julia will inherit Charles's control position eventually. The HPB requires her to declare her intentions regarding the Koch political network before that inheritance occurs, not after.

- **The Action:** Subject to publicly state her intentions regarding Koch Industries' participation in electoral politics and political advocacy infrastructure — specifically whether she intends to continue, reduce, or redirect these activities as her ownership position grows. This is a statement of intent, not a binding commitment, but it must be public and specific.
- **The Sacrifice:** The strategic ambiguity that comes with not having declared a position.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts the HPB's network continuation risk from unknown to stated. The Bureau can monitor compliance with a declared position.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Koch Refinery Community Residents, Gulf Coast Environmental Advocates, and Arts Philanthropy Recipients** and recite the following:

> *"I married David Koch. I loved him. I inherited what he built. I did not build the political network. I also did not dismantle it. I am the custodian of one of the largest private fortunes in America and I am choosing now to say what I intend to do with it. I am establishing a foundation for the communities where this money came from. I am declaring my intentions regarding the political inheritance. I did not choose to have this. I am choosing what to do with it. That is the only choice available to me."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the Apotheosis Protocol. Subject will be reclassified as **Koch Widow Demi-God Entity KWDE-001** — a designation the HPB's legal team has flagged as requiring review. All Koch Industries board decisions in which Julia participates will be subject to mandatory HPB disclosure. Subject will be relocated to FSS LIBERTARIAN (see HPB-12026-06CK), where she will discover that the Flotilla has an excellent arts program but limited government services.
