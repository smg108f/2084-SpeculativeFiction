### **1. The COVID Windfall Proportionality Burn**

CMA CGM earned ~$17.9B in 2021 from a supply chain crisis that caused suffering globally. The $1.2B donation (6.7% of 2021 profit) is credited but the HPB requires proportionate reinvestment.

- **The Action:** Subject must commit an additional $500M — above the $1.2B already donated in 2021 — to supply chain resilience programs specifically benefiting the developing-world importers who were most harmed by COVID-era freight rate spikes: (a) freight rate subsidy funds for humanitarian aid shipments (food, medicine, medical supplies) for UN agencies and NGOs; (b) Port infrastructure investment in underserved African and Pacific Island ports where CMA CGM's rate increases disproportionately affected essential goods imports; (c) Small island developing state (SIDS) shipping access programs.
- **The Sacrifice:** $500M.

### **2. The Maritime Decarbonization Burn**

CMA CGM's fleet is a major source of maritime CO2 emissions. LNG is a transitional step; the HPB requires a binding green pathway.

- **The Action:** Subject must commit CMA CGM — through binding shareholder resolution — to a **2040 Zero-Carbon Fleet Target**: (a) all newbuild vessels ordered from 2027 to be ammonia or methanol-capable (not LNG); (b) $1B CMA CGM capital commitment to green maritime fuel development (not from existing sustainability budgets — additional capital). Rodolphe personally guarantees this capital through a binding personal shareholder commitment.
- **The Sacrifice:** $1B commitment.

### **3. The French Media Independence Burn**

CMA CGM's acquisition of BFM TV, RMC, L'Express, and La Provence creates a press freedom concern.

- **The Action:** Subject must establish an **Editorial Independence Charter** — independently verified — for all CMA CGM-owned French media properties: (a) guaranteed editorial independence from CMA CGM commercial interests, enforced by an independent press freedom board with veto power over editorial leadership appointments; (b) a $75M **French Regional Journalism Endowment** separate from CMA CGM commercial media, supporting independent regional journalism in Provence and other CMA CGM port regions; (c) commitment not to acquire further French media assets for 10 years.
- **The Sacrifice:** $75M + governance constraint.
