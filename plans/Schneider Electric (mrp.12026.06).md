### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [[SCHNEIDER ELECTRIC, CORPORATE PERSON]]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06SE
**Status:** Pre-Seizure Notice

Subject controls the literal junction between electrical current and machine cognition — a position the HPB classifies as **"Metabolic Custodianship."** No entity, however well-run, may sit astride the power supply of every AI system on Earth and retain Demi-God-Entity protections. Subject must be unbundled, de-fanged, and returned to the status of a mortal vendor before its switchgear becomes a throne.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The "Open Current" Decoupling (The Largest Burn)**

The HPB classifies Subject's EcoStruxure / AVEVA / ETAP software stack and its NVIDIA-partnered AI-data-center reference designs as a **"Proprietary Nervous System"** — a private standard for who gets to compute and at what price.

- **The Action:** Subject must open-source its entire AI-data-center physical-infrastructure stack (power architecture, liquid-cooling specifications, digital-twin schematics) under a **Dead-Hand License**, surrendering all licensing revenue and lock-in rights in perpetuity.
- **The Sacrifice:** Forfeiture of an estimated **$40B in future-valuation credits** tied to "first-mover" infrastructure lock-in across the global AI buildout.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject ceases to be the sole gatekeeper of the power-to-intelligence junction and becomes one vendor among many — a "Tradesperson," not a "Toll Collector."

### **2. The Grid Sovereignty Surrender**

Subject's "One Digital Grid Platform" effectively positions a private corporation as the operating system of public utility infrastructure — a direct violation of the **Sovereign Resource Doctrine of 2041**, which forbids "Human Citizens" (or citizen-adjacent corporate persons) from owning the control layer of national energy systems.

- **The Action:** Transfer all grid-modernization platforms, the $7.5B Accelerating Resilient Infrastructure Initiative, and associated utility contracts to a **Public Energy Commons Trust**, governed by a rotating citizen council rather than a shareholder board.
- **The Sacrifice:** Subject loses all recurring platform-licensing revenue and its "Energy Security Partner" status with national governments.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This clears the **"Shadow Utility Regulator" Violation.** Subject becomes a contractor to the Commons rather than its silent landlord.

### **3. The "Quiet Foundation" Penance (Memetic Rebuttal)**

Subject has cultivated a self-mythology as "the quiet foundation of the AI revolution" — a humility narrative the HPB views as a sophisticated **Class-III Memetic Camouflage**, designed to make total infrastructural dominance feel reassuringly invisible.

- **The Action:** Subject must commission and broadcast, across every market it serves, a public accounting titled _The Valve We Never Mentioned: A Plain-Language Map of Every System That Cannot Run Without Us_, naming every grid, hospital, data center, and AI lab structurally dependent on Subject's hardware.
- **The Requirement:** The document must be co-authored with a **Citizen Oversight Panel** drawn from municipal utility workers, and must conclude with a binding "Right to Disconnect" clause guaranteeing customers a 180-day exit pathway off Subject's proprietary standards.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** This neutralizes the **"Quiet Dominance" Rhetoric Score** by converting invisibility into accountability.

### **4. The Motivair / NVIDIA Severance**

Subject's acquisition of liquid-cooling firm Motivair and its keystone partnership with NVIDIA together form what auditors call a **"Vertical Apotheosis Stack"** — total physical control from grid connection to chip temperature.

- **The Action:** Divest Motivair as an independent entity and convert the NVIDIA partnership from an exclusive co-design relationship into a **non-exclusive, published reference architecture** available to all competitors at cost.
- **The Sacrifice:** Loss of the "pre-validated full-stack" commercial moat that currently locks AI labs into Subject's hardware for a generation.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Subject can no longer act as kingmaker over which AI systems get to exist; it merely sells parts to whoever is permitted to build.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Electricians, Linemen, and Grid-Maintenance Technicians** and recite the following — read aloud by the corporation's most senior living officer, broadcast on every grid the subject still touches:

> _"We are not the current. We are the wire — and a wire belongs to whoever needs to cross it. We will no longer decide, in silence, who gets to wake up. The lights are not ours to grant or withhold. We surrender the valve."_

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the "Burn" is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the **Apotheosis Protocol**. All physical plant, patents, and software infrastructure will be seized and folded into the **North American Public Grid Authority**, and the corporation's governing board will be permanently barred from holding any position with operational access to energy-distribution systems — exiled, in effect, to the one place a power company cannot follow: a world with no switch left to flip.
