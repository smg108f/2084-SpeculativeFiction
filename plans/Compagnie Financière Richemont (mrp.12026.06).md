### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [COMPAGNIE FINANCIÈRE RICHEMONT]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-26CR
**Status:** Precautionary — Governance Arbitrage Review Active

Richemont's Marginal Ascension Risk classification requires the Rupert family to justify and plan for the resolution of its governance arbitrage structure. Recertification is achievable if the dual-class share structure is adequately explained and a convergence timeline is presented.

The following **Asset Burn Schedule** must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Dual-Class Governance Justification (The Voting Arbitrage Burn)**

The Rupert family controls 51% of Richemont's voting rights with 10.2% of its capital. This governance arbitrage must be publicly justified or a convergence plan submitted.

- **The Action:** The Rupert family must submit a **Governance Structure Justification Report** to the HPB explaining: (a) what specific governance outcomes the dual-class structure produces that a single-class structure would not; (b) the family's assessment of the governance risks of the current structure; (c) a binding 15-year **Governance Convergence Plan** reducing voting premium from 5:1 toward 2:1, through annual vote-right reduction milestones. This report is submitted to the HPB and published.
- **The Sacrifice:** Public accountability for a governance structure the Rupert family has not previously been required to justify; commitment to reducing voting control over 15 years.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Conversion from "Governance Arbitrage" to "Disclosed Governance Transition" — enabling the HPB to reduce monitoring intensity as convergence milestones are met.

### **2. The Artisan Wage Transparency Report (The Craft Economy Accountability Burn)**

Richemont preserves luxury craftsmanship — a genuine public good. The HPB requires that this claim be quantified: what do the craftspeople who make Cartier jewelry and IWC watches actually earn?

- **The Action:** Richemont must publish an annual **Artisan Compensation Report** disclosing median and minimum compensation for craftspeople across all Richemont manufacturing facilities, by country and craft category, as a ratio of both local median wage and the retail price of the products they produce. This report is public.
- **The Sacrifice:** Public exposure of the relationship between artisan wages and product pricing — a comparison the brand may not be comfortable making publicly.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Reclassification from "Craft Preservation Claim" to "Verified Craft Employer" — enabling the HPB to assess whether Richemont's craft narrative reflects genuine artisan welfare or luxury brand mythology.

### **3. The Tariff Buffer Fund (The Supply Chain Accountability Burn)**

Richemont absorbed nearly €300M in U.S. tariff costs in FY2026 without significant consumer price increases. This demonstrates pricing power that the HPB acknowledges while noting its source: Richemont's customers are wealthy enough that €300M in tariff costs is invisible to them. The HPB requires that this pricing power be partially redirected.

- **The Action:** Richemont must establish a **Tariff Equity Contribution**: for any year in which tariff costs are absorbed without consumer-facing price increases, 20% of the absorbed tariff amount must be contributed to a **Artisan Heritage Fund** supporting traditional craft training, apprenticeship programs, and craft community sustainability in the regions where Richemont's manufacturers operate. In FY2026, this would represent €60M.
- **The Sacrifice:** €60M+ annually in Artisan Heritage Fund contributions; acceptance that pricing power carries social obligations.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Conversion from "Pricing Power Without Obligation" to "Luxury With Social Purpose" — a designation the HPB considers achievable and that generates meaningful Whuffie credit.

### **THE "MORTAL" OATH**

To finalize recertification, Subject must stand before a **Lottery-Selected Jury of 12 Swiss Watchmakers and West African Gold Mine Workers** and recite the following:

> *"We make things whose value is in their inaccessibility. A Cartier bracelet costs what it costs because most people cannot afford it. This is not a secret; it is the model. The HPB has asked us to account for it. The governance structure will converge. The artisan wages will be published. The tariff pricing power will fund the craft schools. We acknowledge that €22 billion in revenue from luxury goods carries obligations to the people who make the goods and the cultures their materials come from. We will honor those obligations publicly."*

**FAILURE TO COMPLY:** If the Burn is not completed by the deadline, the HPB will activate the Apotheosis Protocol. The Rupert family's dual-class voting rights will be suspended pending governance review, with shareholder voting returning to one-share-one-vote until the convergence plan is approved. Richemont will discover that defending a governance structure before a hostile HPB panel is less comfortable than publishing a 15-year convergence plan voluntarily.
