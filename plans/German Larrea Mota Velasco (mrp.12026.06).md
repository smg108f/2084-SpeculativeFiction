### **MORTAL RECERTIFICATION PLAN: SUBJECT [LARREA MOTA VELASCO, GERMÁN]**

**Case File:** HPB-12026-06GL
**Status:** Urgent

Subject has maintained public silence for decades while operating a company with documented patterns of environmental disaster, labor rights violations, and suppression of worker organizing. The HPB's primary requirement is that subject become accountable before it can assess any other burns. The following Asset Burn Schedule must be executed within 30 days.

---

### **1. The Pasta de Conchos Reckoning Burn**

65 miners died in the February 2006 explosion at Pasta de Conchos. Their bodies were not retrieved. The families have waited 20 years. This is the HPB's most urgent single outstanding harm in this audit cycle.

- **The Action:** Grupo México to immediately fund and execute a professional retrieval operation for the remains of all 65 miners at Pasta de Conchos, using current best available technology, and to return those remains to families. Accompanied by subject's personal public acknowledgment of the company's role in the disaster and its failure to retrieve the remains. This action takes precedence over all other burns; it is a precondition for any recertification proceeding.
- **The Sacrifice:** The cost of a professional mining retrieval operation; the public acknowledgment the subject has spent 20 years avoiding.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Completes what the company abandoned. The HPB considers this a moral precondition, not a philanthropic gesture.

### **2. The Sonora River Environmental Remediation Burn**

The 2014 Buenavista copper mine spill released 40,000 cubic meters of acidic copper sulfate solution into the Sonora River and Bacanuchi River — the worst mining spill in Mexican history. Remediation has been incomplete. River communities continue to report contamination.

- **The Action:** Grupo México to fund a full independent environmental assessment of remaining Sonora River contamination, followed by a binding remediation program with independently verified milestones, funded at whatever cost the assessment determines is necessary. Establish a permanent Sonora River Community Health Fund of $300M for affected community members.
- **The Sacrifice:** Full remediation cost; the ongoing evasion of complete accountability.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts Mexico's worst mining environmental disaster from a partial response into a completed remediation.

### **3. The Labor Rights Restoration Burn**

Grupo México has been documented suppressing independent labor organizing across multiple operations. The destruction of the Mineros union section at Pasta de Conchos following the 2006 disaster is the most documented case.

- **The Action:** Grupo México to formally recognize independent union organizing rights across all Mexican operations, including collective bargaining in good faith, and to restore the Mineros' right to organize at Pasta de Conchos operations. Subject to make this commitment personally and publicly.
- **The Sacrifice:** Management control over labor relations in a company where labor suppression has been a structural strategy.
- **Mortal Status Gain:** Converts Mexico's most anti-union mining company into one operating within internationally recognized labor rights standards.
